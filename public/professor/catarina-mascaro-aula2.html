The Code

Find the missing S

I wake up at seven. — My sister wakes up at six.
We hang out on Saturdays. — Bia hangs out on Sundays.
They chat every night. — Sam chats every night.

He, she and it get an S

Everybody else keeps the verb normal: I / you / we / they wake up. Only he / she / it say wakes up.

The Code

Same verb, two shapes

Who
Verb
Example
I / you / we / they
scroll
They scroll at night.
he / she / it
scrolls
She scrolls at night.
negative
does not scroll
She does not scroll at night.

After does not the S disappears: She does not scroll — not "does not scrolls".

every day in the morning at night on weekends after school twice a week
