The Man Who Built Ljubljana

The Man Who Built Ljubljana

In 1895 Ljubljana was hit by a strong earthquake. Many houses in the old town were damaged, and much of the city was rebuilt in the years that followed. But the face of the city that tourists photograph today was designed by one man: the architect Joze Plecnik.

Plecnik was born in Ljubljana in 1872. He worked in Vienna and in Prague, where parts of Prague Castle were renovated under his direction. In 1921 he came back home, and for more than thirty years he designed bridges, squares, a market and a library for his own city. The famous Triple Bridge is a good example. The stone bridge in the middle was built in 1842. Plecnik added two footbridges on the sides, and they were completed in 1932.

His work is everywhere along the riverbank. The Central Market, with its long line of columns, was designed by him, and the National and University Library, completed in 1941, is often called his masterpiece. In 2021 his work in Ljubljana was added to the UNESCO World Heritage list. Today his buildings are visited by thousands of people every day -- and most of them do not know his name.

Chapter 4: The Code

Was Built, Was Designed

When the building is more important than the builder

Grammar Discovery

Who Did It, or What Was Done?

"The Triple Bridge was designed by Plecnik."

"Two footbridges were added in the 1930s."

"The city was hit by an earthquake in 1895."

"Fresh fruit is sold at the market every morning."

What comes first: the person who did it, or the thing that was done? Now look at the orange words: which verb comes first, and what form is the second one?

TimeFormExample
present (always, every day)is / are + participleFresh fruit is sold every morning.
past (finished)was / were + participleThe bridge was designed in 1929.
who did it (if it matters)+ by + personIt was designed by Plecnik.
questionsWhen was it built? · Who was it designed by?When was the library completed?
irregular participlesbuild → built · make → made · hit → hitIt was built in stone.
the trapNot it was build and not it built in 1932. Say it was built in 1932.

In one line: when the building matters more than the builder, put the building first and use was or is + participle.
