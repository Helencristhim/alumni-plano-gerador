Metodologia

1. Da Leitura à Fala

A Ana já lê inglês técnico. Cada aula converte esse conhecimento passivo em produção oral ativa, com muita fala e repetição em contexto real.

Na conversa, fluência primeiro; correção depois. O professor anota padrões (perfect tenses, condicionais) e devolve feedback focado, sem interromper a produção.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Comparatives with Boosters

GRAMMAR

The comparative says WHICH way. The booster says HOW FAR.

BoosterSize of the gapExample
far / much + comparativeBigIt is far quieter here.
considerably / significantlyBig, more formalconsiderably more affordable
slightly / a littleSmallslightly colder in winter
not nearly as ... asMuch lessnot nearly as expensive as
nowhere near as ... asMuch less, spokennowhere near as busy as
by far + superlativeFirst, nobody closeby far the best decision
the -er, the -erTwo things togetherThe smaller the town, the faster you know everyone.

Never: more quieter · very cheaper · as busy than · the most best. English marks the comparison only ONCE.
