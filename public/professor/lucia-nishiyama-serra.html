1. Ativar a fala, não ensinar do zero

O inglês da Lucia está em modo leitura. O programa transforma leitura em FALA e em ESCRITA executiva — sobre a carreira e o mundo real dela, nunca frases genéricas.

5. Diagnóstico e linha de base

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

The migration ran twelve weeks, and the fallout lasted two quarters. In hindsight, the review is short: if the group had run a small test first, it would have found the gap in week two. Notice the shape of that sentence. The if half takes had plus a past participle, and the other half takes would have plus a past participle. Nothing in it happened -- it is the version of March that never existed. There was one more signal. An engineer flagged the cost risk in February, and her email received no answer, because everyone was watching the schedule. Cost was the blind spot. If the director had taken that email seriously, the team would have course-corrected three months earlier, and the group would not have lost the quarter. The team underestimated the work, and that is a fact in the past simple. Whether they should still second-guess the call is a different question: the decision was made with the information available at the time, and the process is what changed.

1. Why does the text say "if the group had run a small test first" and not "if the group ran a small test first"?
A Because the test is still possible today.
B Because the condition is in the past and it did not happen, so the if half takes "had" plus a past participle.
C Because "run" has no past simple form.
2. In "the team would have course-corrected three months earlier", did the team course-correct?
A No. "Would have" describes a result that never happened.
B Yes, three months before the deadline.
C Yes, but only after the email was answered.
3. Why is "the team underestimated the work" in the past simple?
A Because it is a condition, and conditions always take the past simple.
B Because the sentence is short.
C Because it really happened. Only the imagined version needs "had" and "would have".
