Grammar Discovery

Mine? Yours?

This hoodie isn't mine. It's Kai's.
Is this towel yours, Renato?
Jess says the pink towel is hers.
The jerseys aren't ours. They belong to the club.

What do the orange words have in common?

my hoodie = mine · your towel = yours · her board = hers

These words replace the thing, so no noun after them: "It's mine", never "It's mine hoodie" or "It's my". To ask about the owner, use Whose: "Whose hoodie is this?"

The Code

Before the noun, or alone

With a noun → alone

my hoodie → mine
your towel → yours
his board → his
her leash → hers
our van → ours
their kit → theirs

Never

It's my.
alone: It's mine.
the hoodie of Kai
a person: Kai's hoodie
Who is this towel?
owner: Whose towel is this?

The question: Whose is this? — It's mine. / It's Jess's.

Sound Check

Two small sounds

Whose hoodie is this?

"whose" is HOOZ: no w sound

It's not mine. I think it's yours.

the s in "yours" is a z: YORZ

Squad News: The Names Are Out

On Monday morning, Coach Dana Reid posted the names on the club group. The interschool squad is Kai, Harper, Jess and Renato, and the reserve is Tom. Ollie is still injured, so this year the trophy isn't his to win. "Next year it's mine," he says. The first interschool round is in three weeks.

Every surfer in the squad gets the club kit: a black hoodie with the club logo, a yellow jersey and a sticker for the board. The hoodies are all the same size and color, so Dana has one rule: write your name on the label. "If a hoodie has no name," she says, "it isn't yours. It's the club's."

The rule came one day too late. On Wednesday, Jess wore a hoodie to school, but it wasn't hers. It was Harper's. And Renato took Kai's home by mistake. "Mine is the one with the hole in the sleeve!" Kai said. Now they swap them back every morning in the parking lot.

On Saturdays the squad travels in the club van, with the boards on the roof. The van belongs to the club, not to Dana, so please keep it clean. Anything left in the van goes to the lost property box at school. Right now there are two towels, a leash and one wetsuit in the box. Are they yours? Come and get them.
