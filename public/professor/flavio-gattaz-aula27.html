Chapter 3: The Code

Expected, and If Not

Supposed to, unless and otherwise

Grammar Discovery

What Was the Deal?

"Your crew was supposed to be here at seven on Monday."

"The membrane is supposed to be finished before the pour."

"Unless your crew is here by seven tomorrow, we will bring in another company."

"Send me a recovery plan today. Otherwise, I will have to apply the penalty clause."

Did the crew come at seven? Is the membrane finished now? In the third sentence, is unless positive or negative, and why is it is and not will be? What does otherwise replace?

ToolMeaningExample
was / were supposed to + verbit was the agreement, and it did not happenYour crew was supposed to be here at seven.
is / are supposed to + verbwhat the plan or the contract saysThe membrane is supposed to be finished first.
unless + presentif ... not — never with will, never with notUnless your crew is here by seven, ...
Otherwise, ...if not, this is the consequence — a new sentenceSend the plan today. Otherwise, ...
I will have to + verba firm consequence, not a threatI will have to withhold payment.

In one line: say what was supposed to happen, then what happens unless it does — calmly.

Grammar Practice

Say What Was Expected

Say it first, then click to compare

"The crew did not start on Monday, but that was the plan. (was supposed to)" ___Your crew was supposed to start on Monday.
"The contract says the membrane is done before the pour. (is supposed to)" ___The membrane is supposed to be done before the pour.
"If the crew is not here tomorrow, we bring in another company. (unless)" ___Unless your crew is here tomorrow, we will bring in another company.
"If you do not send the plan today, I cannot approve the invoice. (unless)" ___Unless you send the plan today, I can't approve the invoice.
"Call me by five. If not, I withhold payment. (otherwise)" ___Call me by five. Otherwise, I will have to withhold payment.

Tuesday, Seven Fifteen

L
Good morning, Flavio. I saw your missed calls. I can explain.
F
Good morning, Linda. Your crew was supposed to be here at seven on Monday, and nobody called me. That is unacceptable.
L
I know. Two of my people were moved to another job at the last minute. I'm sorry about the no-show.
F
I understand that things happen, but I can't let this slide. The membrane is supposed to be finished before the pour on Thursday.
L
We can make up for lost time. If I send four people tomorrow, they can work until eight.
F
Then I need a recovery plan today, in writing. Unless your crew is here by seven tomorrow, I will bring in another company.
L
Understood. You will have the plan by five. Are you going to apply the penalty clause?
F
Not this time. But if the pour slips, I will have to withhold payment. So please, do not back out of this.
Comprehension

About Linda

1. Why, according to Linda, did her crew not come on Monday?
Two of her people were moved to another job at the last minute.
2. What does she offer to make up for lost time?
To send four people tomorrow and have them work until eight.
3. What does she promise at the end, and by when?
The recovery plan, by five o’clock today.

Real Document

Put It in Writing

NOTICE OF DELAY — Waterproofing, Block B Basement
From: Flavio Gattaz · To: Linda Morales, Morales Waterproofing · Tuesday, 9:40 a.m.

Fact — Your crew was supposed to start on Monday at 7 a.m. Nobody arrived, and we received no notice.

Impact — The basement pour on Thursday depends on the membrane.

What we agreed — Four workers on site Wednesday, 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. A recovery plan in writing by 5 p.m. today.

Consequence — Unless your crew is on site by 7 a.m. Wednesday, we will bring in another contractor and back-charge the cost, as the penalty clause in Section 9 allows.

This one-day grace period is not a waiver of our rights under the subcontract.
1. What was the crew supposed to do, and when?
Start the waterproofing on Monday at 7 a.m.
2. What must Linda send, and by when?
A recovery plan in writing, by 5 p.m. today.
3. What will happen unless the crew is on site by 7 a.m. on Wednesday?
Flavio will bring in another contractor and back-charge the cost under the penalty clause.
