Lesson 04

At the
Front Desk

From good morning to thank you.
And one question you have not seen. Do good morning ao thank you — e uma pergunta que você ainda não viu.

1 · The arrival

You arrive, and it is your turn

The situation

Same hotel as lesson 1. You arrive in the afternoon, and there is nobody in the queue. Emma is at the desk. You have your booking on your phone — and everything you need to say, you already have.

What do you say first, and what can wait? O que você diz primeiro, e o que pode esperar?

2 · Try it once

The whole thing, starting now

Nothing on the screen. Your teacher is at the desk. Nada na tela. O professor está no balcão.

Check in. Faça o check-in, do começo ao fim.

3 · What comes first

Beginning, middle, end

Cada frase pertence a um momento do check-in. Coloque cada uma no seu momento.
“Good morning.”
“Bom dia.”
“My name is Vanessa.”
“Meu nome é Vanessa.”
“It is from 14 to 18 October.”
“É de 14 a 18 de outubro.”
“Can I have a room with a window?”
“Posso ter um quarto com janela?”
“Thank you.”
“Obrigada.”
“Have a good day.”
“Tenha um bom dia.”
at the beginning = no começo · at the end = no fim · in the middle = no meio
4 · Four moves

Three of these you already have

Alguém no balcão pergunta uma coisa que você não esperava. Marque as três coisas que funcionam.

Three of these work when a question surprises you. Which three?

“Sorry, again please?”
“Desculpe, de novo, por favor?”
(saying nothing)
(sem dizer nada)
“More slowly, please.”
“Mais devagar, por favor.”
“I do not speak English.”
“Eu não falo inglês.”
“Yes, please.”
“Sim, por favor.”
You already have all three, from lesson 2. A question you did not expect is the normal shape of a real conversation, not a problem with your English.
Você já tem as três desde a aula 2. Uma pergunta que você não esperava é o formato normal de uma conversa de verdade, e não um problema com o seu inglês.
5 · Micropractice

One beginning, five requests

Can I have a room with a window?
Can I have the wifi password?
Can I have breakfast at seven?
Sorry, again please?
More slowly, please.

Which one will you actually ask for today? Qual destes você realmente pediria hoje?

6 · Round one

The whole check-in, and a surprise

Your teacher is Emma at the desk. Someone else may join. O professor faz a Emma, no balcão. Outra pessoa pode entrar na conversa.

Good morning, your name, your dates, and one thing you want. Bom dia, o seu nome, as suas datas — e uma coisa que você quer pedir.

7 · Feedback

Two things, and one of them good

What today measured
Whether you opened and closed the conversation yourself. Whether you asked for one thing. And whether, when a question arrived that nobody had shown you, you asked for it again instead of stopping.

Which part was harder — the questions you expected, or the one you did not? O que foi mais difícil: as perguntas que você esperava, ou a que você não esperava?

8 · Round two

The same, in thirty seconds

Now there is a queue behind you. Agora tem uma fila atrás de você.

Thirty seconds. What stays? Trinta segundos. O que você mantém?

Keep the two shortest
Good morning and thank you cost two seconds together, and they are the two that change how the desk answers you. Everything else is information, and information can be pointed at.
