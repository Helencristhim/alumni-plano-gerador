Chapter 3: Now, or Every Day

One Verb, Two Sentences

I measure  |  I am measuring

Grammar Discovery

Which Ones Are Happening Now?

I measure the tunnel wall every Monday.
I am measuring the tunnel wall right now.
The trucks come in through the main gate.
The trucks are coming in through the south gate today.

Two sentences are about every day. Two are about this minute. Which is which?

FormWhen you use itTime words that go with it
I measureyour routine — it is true every weekevery Monday, always, usually, never
I am measuringthis minute — the tool is in your handright now, at the moment, today
The testAsk yourself: is it happening while I speak? Yes → am / is / are + -ing. No, it is my routine → the simple verb.
Grammar Discovery

What Comes Before the -ing?

I am waiting for the truck.
My boss is having a meeting.
They are working in the north area.
What are you doing right now?

What are the two parts before every -ing word?

PersonbeExample
IamI am measuring the wall.
he / she / itisMy boss is having a meeting.
you / we / theyareThey are waiting for the truck.
QuestionThe be jumps in front of the person: What are you doing? · Are you working?
Negativenot after be: I am not waiting. · He is not answering.
Spellinghave → having · take → taking · sit → sitting · wait → waiting

Role-Play — Free

Sixty Seconds, Your Site Right Now

Sixty seconds, a voice note to Karen: what is happening on your site at this minute, and what normally happens there. Four sentences with am, is or are + -ing, and two about your routine.

No keywords. No stopping. She cannot see your site — your words are the only picture she gets.

What I Can Do

I know nine words for the phone: measure, wait for, take a look, busy, meeting, right now, at the moment, hold on, speaking.
