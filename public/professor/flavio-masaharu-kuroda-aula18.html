Chapter 4: The Code

The Right Words in the Right Order

Adjectives have a fixed order, and favorite partners.

Grammar Discovery

Which Adjective Comes First?

"a simple new cloud-based tool"

"an intuitive small finance dashboard"

"a reliable SAP connector"

"a seamless integration · customizable approval rules"

Look at the colors: red, then blue, then purple, then the noun. What kind of word is always first, and what kind is always next to the noun?

PositionKind of wordExample
1opinionsimple, intuitive, reliable
2sizesmall, large
3agenew, old
4type / purpose (next to the noun)cloud-based, SAP, finance
Nounthe thinga simple new cloud-based tool
Collocationsadjectives with their usual nounsseamless integration · intuitive dashboard · robust platform
No pluraladjectives never take -snew features, not news features
From feature to benefitfeature + so (that) + benefitIt is cloud-based, so there is nothing to install.

In one line: opinion first, type next to the noun, and every feature followed by so and what the customer gains.

Grammar Practice

Put It in Order

Say it first, then click to check

new / intuitive: "We launched an ___intuitive new dashboard in March."
cloud-based / small: "It is a ___small cloud-based tool for finance teams."
The usual partner of seamless: "The customer wants a seamless ___integration with SAP."
From feature to benefit: "The rules are customizable, ___so the tool follows your process, not ours."
No plural adjective: "We have three ___new features this year."

Quick Fire

So What? Fast

1 / 6

Feature: 'pre-built SAP connector'. Turn it into a benefit with so that.

  • It has a pre-built SAP connector, so that you go live in days, not months.
  • Feature + so that + what the customer gains.

Put these in the right order: dashboard / new / simple.

  • A simple new dashboard.
  • Opinion before age: simple, then new.

Put these in the right order: tool / cloud-based / small.

  • A small cloud-based tool.
  • Size before type: small, then cloud-based.

Marta asks: what does 'out-of-the-box' mean for my customers? Answer in plain English.

  • It means they can use the SAP connection immediately, with no extra development.
  • Explain the adjective with what the customer does.

Say the collocation: the integration is ___ (smooth, no breaks).

  • The integration is seamless. A seamless integration.
  • Seamless goes with integration, experience, transition.

Marta says every vendor calls itself 'innovative'. Answer with a number instead.

  • Fair point. We process more than two million invoices a year, with 99.9 per cent availability.
  • A number is more convincing than an empty adjective.

1. Say the second line as one full sentence with so.
It has a pre-built SAP connector, so you go live in days, not months.
2. Why does the robust line have numbers instead of more adjectives?
Because a number can be checked, and an adjective like robust only means something when a number proves it.
3. Why are innovative and world-class left off the card?
Because every vendor uses them, so they give the buyer no information.

Listening 1 · The Writer

The So What Test

A copywriter on how she fixes product pages. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What is her test for every sentence, and how does it work?
The so what test: after each feature you ask so what, and the answer is the benefit.
2. Why do engineers write pages full of features?
Because they built them and they are proud of them, but the buyer cares about what changes on Monday morning.
3. What does she say about adjectives?
Four adjectives can give zero information; one good adjective in the right place is better, and a number is better than any adjective.

Listening 2 · The Buyer

Two Minutes, Three Questions

A buyer on what he looks for on a product page. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. Which words does he skip, and why?
Innovative, world-class and next-generation, because every vendor uses them and they tell him nothing.
2. What three things does he look for?
If it connects to the system they have and how long it takes; who will use it and if they need training; and a number he can check.
3. What happens when the page does not answer his questions?
He goes to the call with doubts, and the salesperson has to work much harder.
