Chapter 3: The Code

The Action First, the Person Last

be + the third form of the verb

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The route is operated by three airlines."

"The regional office was opened last September."

"The proposal has been approved by the director."

"Clients will be notified before the first of March."

Every colored verb is built from the same two pieces. What are they — and in which sentences can you tell who did the action?

FormWhat it saysExample
be + third formThe thing that receives the action becomes the subject. Only be changes tense; the third form never moves.is operated · was opened · has been approved · will be notified
by + the agentThe person or company appears only if you choose to show them. Most corporate sentences leave it out."approved by the director"
"operated by three airlines"
When it is the right choiceWhen the process matters more than the person, when the agent is obvious, or when naming somebody would be unfair."The platform will be rolled out in March."
When it is the wrong choiceBad news, and anything where somebody is waiting to know who decided. Then the passive sounds like hiding.NOT "Your request has been refused."
Say who refused it, and why.
The trapAfter be comes the third form, not the past: revise, revised, revised, but take, took, taken and write, wrote, written.NOT "The decision was took."
"The decision was taken."

A useful habit when you read a company email: ask every sentence who did this? Half the time the answer is missing, and noticing that is already half of understanding it.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"The decision was took by the regional director."
"The decision was taken by the regional director."
"The regional office opened last September by headquarters."
"The regional office was opened last September by headquarters."
"Your waiver request has been refused."
"Compliance refused the waiver, because the contract was signed in March."

After be comes the third form, and the be itself is never optional. The third pair is not a grammar mistake at all — both sentences are correct English. It is a choice: the passive describes a process well and delivers bad news badly, because the person receiving it hears only that somebody did this and you will not say who.

Comprehension

About Felipe

1. What does he tell her at the start of the call?
That the quarterly announcement was sent to all four countries that morning.
2. Who actually revised the fare structure, and who approved it?
Pricing revised it in March, and the regional director signed off on it two weeks ago.
3. How does he explain why the announcement does not say that?
He says it is not a secret — it is just the way those emails are always written.
4. Which reassuring detail does he point out in the announcement?
That existing contracts are not affected.
The Real Thing

The Quarterly Announcement

Internal communication · commercial teams
Quarterly update — South America

1. The regional office in Bogotá has been fully staffed as of last week. The two remaining positions were filled internally.

2. The new booking platform will be rolled out in March, in stages. Training sessions are going to be scheduled for every account manager beforehand. Attendance is mandatory.

3. The corporate fare structure has been revised. Existing contracts are not affected. Clients will be notified by their account manager.

1. In point 2, is the passive a good choice? Why?
Yes — it describes a process, and who exactly schedules the training does not matter to the reader.
2. Which sentence in point 3 would be better in the active, and how would you rewrite it?
"The corporate fare structure has been revised" — better as "Pricing revised the corporate fare structure in March", because the reader wants to know who.

Quick Fire

One Question at a Time

1 / 6

You have to tell a client that the fare structure changed, without blaming a colleague.

  • The passive removes the person, and here that is fair
  • The fare structure has been revised

Your director asks who approved the discount.

  • Here the active is the honest answer
  • Compliance approved it on Tuesday -- do not hide behind was approved

You are describing the new booking platform to a client.

  • A process takes the passive naturally
  • It will be rolled out in March and everybody will be trained first

You have to tell a colleague his request was refused.

  • Do not deliver bad news in the passive
  • Say who decided and why, in one sentence

A client asks whether his contract is affected.

  • Short passive, then a person
  • Existing contracts are not affected -- I will call you if that changes

Somebody asks when the office was opened.

  • Past passive: was plus the third form
  • It was opened in September, and it has been fully staffed since last week
