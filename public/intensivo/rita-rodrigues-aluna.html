Your intensive

Six lessons before the meeting on 31 August. Each lesson gives you one thing you can say in the room, with your slides and your speaking map next to you.

The meeting

Who
The president of Carestream, visiting from the United States.
Where
Your head office in São Paulo.
When
31 August 2026.
Your part
Open the presentation, present the company or support the CEO, answer expected questions, and keep the conversation going around the meeting.

How each lesson works

Pre-class
15–20 minutes on your own. No recording.
In-class
60 minutes with your teacher. You speak; the screen is the teacher’s.
Post-class
15–20 minutes to keep what you built.

The six lessons

Pre-class · Lesson 1

About 15–20 minutes. Six short activities. You do not need to finish them before the lesson — the lesson works either way.

ACTIVITY 1

Who is coming

On 31 August, the president of Carestream visits your office in São Paulo. You open the presentation.

What does he most need in your first minute?

The full history of the company, year by year.
Who you are, what the company does, and why he is in the right place.
The financial results for 2025.
ACTIVITY 2

Six words you will use

Read each word in its sentence. You will say these words in the lesson.

distribute — We distribute medical technology in Brazil. to bring a product from the maker to hospitals and clinics
medical technology — We distribute medical technology. the equipment hospitals use to diagnose and treat
hospitals and clinics — Our customers are hospitals and clinics. where your products are used
nationwide — We work nationwide. in the whole country
corporate management — I am the Corporate Management Director. your area
business processes — My area covers business processes. how the company organises its work

ACTIVITY 4

Read and find the answer

Imagem Healthcare Solutions has worked in the healthcare market for 38 years. The company started in 1988. Today it distributes medical technology to hospitals, clinics and specialised centres in Brazil, represents international brands, and provides installation, training and maintenance. The company has 146 employees.

Which sentence is true?

The company produces medical equipment in Brazil.
The company started in 1996.
The company distributes medical technology and also provides services.
