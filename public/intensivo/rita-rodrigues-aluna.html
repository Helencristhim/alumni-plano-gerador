Alumni by Better · Rita Rodrigues

Your intensive

Six lessons before the meeting on 31 August. Each lesson gives you one thing you can say in the room, with your slides and your speaking map next to you.

The meeting

Who
The president of Carestream, visiting from the United States.
Where
Your head office in São Paulo.
When
31 August 2026.
Your part
Open the presentation, present the company or support the CEO, answer expected questions, and keep the conversation going around the meeting.

How each lesson works

Pre-class
15–20 minutes on your own. No recording.
In-class
60 minutes with your teacher. You speak; the screen is the teacher’s.
Post-class
15–20 minutes to keep what you built.

The six lessons

Pre-class · Lesson 1

Lesson 4 · 26 August

Where you are, and what you can do there

Coverage and service answer the same question: if he buys, who is near him, and who keeps it running.

1 · What this lesson is for

“And if something breaks?”

This is the question behind coverage. He is not asking for a map — he is asking whether you can support what you sell.

What would you want to hear, if you were buying?

2 · What can go wrong

Two halves that do not connect

You list the states, then list the service team, and he does not see the link.
He asks about response time — a number you do not have today.
He asks about a state you do not cover.

3 · Your first version

Tell him where you operate

Thirty seconds. No map.

“Where do you operate, and who supports the equipment?”

3 · One answer, two halves

One way to say it

Our head office and distribution centre are in São Paulo.
We also have a branch in Mato Grosso do Sul, and teams in Rio, Espírito Santo and Mato Grosso.
We have a logistics branch in Santa Catarina for imports.
And we have our own service team — technicians, coordinators and applications specialists.

Which sentence turns the list into an answer?

4 · What you take with you

Lines for coverage and service

Our head office and distribution centre are in São Paulo.
We have teams in several states, and a logistics branch in Santa Catarina.
We have our own service team.own = not outsourced. This is the operational benefit
The team includes technicians, coordinators and applications specialists.
We provide installation, training and preventive maintenance.the three services from the site

4 · The number you can say

One operational benefit

Our service team has thirty technicians.from the deck — a number she owns
We also have remote care and applications support.capability, not a promise
I would need to confirm our response time.for anything the deck does not say

If there is time: We cover installation, training and both preventive and corrective maintenance.
