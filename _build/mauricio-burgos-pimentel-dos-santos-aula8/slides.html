The Pattern

Which part is the background?

We were migrating the system when the regulator changed the rules.
I was preparing the budget when the invitation arrived.
While we were restructuring, two people resigned.
The team was working weekends, and nobody noticed.

The long thing → was / were + -ing. The event that lands in it → past simple

Every case has two layers: the situation that was already going on, and the thing that happened in the middle of it. Put the situation in was/were + -ing and the event in the past simple. That contrast is what makes a story sound like a story instead of a list.

The Pattern

Four words that carry twelve minutes

Initially…
"Initially, we were running two systems in parallel."
At that point…
"At that point, the regulator changed the deadline."
As a result…
"As a result, we lost two quarters and a lot of goodwill."
In the end…
"In the end, the root cause was not the system. It was us."

Four words, four moments. Say them out loud and the case builds itself — and the room always knows where you are.

The migration — the worst thing they did and the most useful.
2. What was going on, and what happened in the middle of it?
They were running two systems in parallel when the regulator changed the deadline.
3. What does he say the real root cause was?

Listening 1

A case, in four minutes

1. What was going on before the new finance director arrived?
They were running four sites and losing money on one of them for about three years.
2. What did the analysis show, and what did they do anyway?
The site was not the problem, the contract was — and they closed the site anyway.
3. What is his recommendation?
When the analysis and the decision disagree, write down why.
Listening 2

How the twelve minutes are judged

1. What two things does she say nobody cares about?
The number of slides and your accent.
2. What are the three things that decide whether a case lands?
The first thirty seconds, one memorable number, and whether the recommendation follows from the analysis.
3. What does she say about a case with no questions at the end?
It is not a compliment.
