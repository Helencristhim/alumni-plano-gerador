Last Quarter in Numbers -- What Happened

The quarter ends with an email and a call about the numbers. Key words: a quarter, a target, to reach, to increase, to drop, growth, market share, a contract. Structure: past simple with regular verbs.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A quarter -- a period of three months in the business year
"The third quarter ended in September."
A target -- a number that a team wants to reach
"Our sales target for the quarter was two million."
To reach -- to get to a number or a goal
"We reached ninety-five percent of the target."
To increase -- to go up, to become bigger
"Sales increased by eight percent."
To drop -- to go down, to become smaller
"Prices dropped in August."
Growth -- when something becomes bigger over time
"We had good growth with electric cars."
Market share -- the part of all sales in a market that one company has
"Our market share increased to twenty percent."
A contract -- a written agreement between two companies
"We signed a new contract with a car maker."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

The third quarter ended on September 30. Fernanda opened the results email from Mark. The good news: sales increased by eight percent, and market share increased to twenty percent. The team also signed a new contract in Argentina.

The bad news: the team didn’t reach the target. In August, prices dropped, and two customers delayed their orders.

In September, the team worked very hard and visited six customers. They reached ninety-four percent. Not perfect, but good growth.

1. What increased last quarter?
A Sales and market share.
B Prices.
C The number of customers.
2. Why didn’t the team reach the target?
A Because the team did not work.
B Because prices dropped and two customers delayed their orders.
C Because they lost the contract in Argentina.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A “We didn’t reached the target.”
B “We didn’t reach the target.”
C “We don’t reached the target.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Past Simple -- Regular Verbs

GRAMMAR

For finished actions in the past, add -ed to regular verbs. For the negative, use didn’t + the simple verb.

JobFormExample
Past, regular verbsverb + -edSales increased by eight percent.
Spelling: verb ends in -e+ dWe closed the quarter well.
Spelling: short verb, one voweldouble the last letter + -edPrices dropped in August.
Negativedidn’t + verb (no -ed)We didn’t reach the target.
Three sounds of -ed/t/ reached · /d/ signed · /id/ visitedWe visited two customers.
The trapno -ed after didn’tNot we didn’t reached. Say we didn’t reach.

Never: Last quarter sales increase · We didn’t reached · Prices droped · Market share was increased

Stage 2: Put the Update in Order

Order

You give a short update on the results call. Put it in a logical order.

?"Let me start with the numbers for the third quarter."
?"Sales increased by eight percent."
?"But we didn't reach the target."
?"In August, prices dropped."
