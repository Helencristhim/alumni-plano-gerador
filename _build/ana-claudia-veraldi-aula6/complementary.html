Lesson 6 -- The Day I Decided to Leave

Documentary
The Village Surviving Germany’s Rural Exodus -- DW, Journal Reporters

The other direction of your own story: a German village that people kept leaving, and the ones who stayed and decided to turn it around. Everybody in it is speaking English as a second language.

Tip: this is German-accented English, the sound of Listening 2. Watch it once with no subtitles and notice how clear it is once you stop expecting a film accent.

Podcast
How To Fail With Elizabeth Day -- Kate Winslet: “I remember thinking: I do not want to be famous”

Fifty-six minutes of one person telling the turning points of a life out loud, in order. The whole episode is built out of the two tenses you worked on tonight.

Tip: both speakers are British. Every time you hear a was or were plus -ing, that is the background of a story arriving — count five of them.

Talk
How to quit your life (and reboot) -- Priya Parker, TEDxUHasselt

What actually happens between the long background and the day somebody finally acts, told by a person who has spent her career watching other people take the plunge.

Tip: she narrates other people’s decisions constantly. Write down three sentences where the background and the event sit in the same sentence.
