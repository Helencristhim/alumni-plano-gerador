Lesson 6 -- The Day I Decided to Leave

Documentary
The first village for digital nomads -- DW Documentary (full film, 28 min)

People who left European capitals to work remotely from a village in Portugal. They talk about the moment they decided, the friends who told them it would not work, and what the first year actually cost them. It is the life you already chose, told by other people in English.

Tip: almost nobody in this film is a native speaker. Listen once without subtitles and count how many times somebody sets a scene with was or were plus -ing before they get to the decision.

Podcast
The Life Shift -- Identity: The Job That Wasn't the Whole Story, with David S. Bernknopf

He spent twenty years at CNN, walked away when the culture changed, moved to Alaska on a phone call from a stranger, and published his first novel at sixty eight. The whole show exists to ask one question: what was the moment your life split into before and after?

Tip: he tells the story exactly the way tonight's lesson does — the scene first, then the event. Write down the sentence he uses to open it, and then write your own version of that sentence.

Talk
How to make hard choices -- Ruth Chang, TED (15 min)

A philosopher argues that a hard choice is not hard because one option is better and you cannot see which. It is hard because neither is better, and that is precisely where you get to decide who you are. It is the intellectual version of the decision you made on your own Tuesday.

Tip: she speaks slowly and structures every argument out loud. Turn the subtitles off first and try to catch her three main points. Then watch again with subtitles and check.
