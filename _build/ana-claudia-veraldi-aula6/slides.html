Listening 1 · Britain

The Morning the Train Was Cancelled

Four years of being perfectly happy, and one Tuesday in February. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What was he doing when it happened, and what had gone wrong?
He was standing on the platform at half past six in the rain, and the train was cancelled for the third time that week.
2. Was he angry? What does he say instead?
Not at all — he says that is the part nobody believes. He was completely calm and heard himself say out loud that he was not doing it again.
3. What does he say the last straw actually was?
Not the train. The train was simply the morning he finally heard himself.
Listening 2 · Germany

Nine Years and One Friday

The first time she asked. The second time she did not. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What happened the first time she wanted to leave?
She was twenty-nine and two people talked her out of it in one afternoon. She believed them and stayed nine more years.
2. What was she doing the second time, and did she snap?
She was driving home on a Friday, the traffic was not moving, and she was listening to the same radio show. She says she did not snap — she simply understood something.
3. Who did she tell, and when?
Nobody, until the contract was signed.
Chapter 4: The Code

The Background and the Interruption

was doing · did — and which one carries which

Grammar Discovery

Which Ones Lasted?

"I was sitting in gridlock when I made the decision."

"While I was mulling it over, my husband found the house."

"It was raining and the bus never came."

"In March I packed up the apartment and took the plunge."

The orange verbs are the background: they were already going on. The blue ones are the events: they happened once and were over.

FormIts jobExample
was / were + -ingThe background: already going on, and it lasted.I was sitting in gridlock.
plain pastThe event: it happened once and it was over.I made the decision.
whenIntroduces the event: when + plain past.... when I made the decision.
whileIntroduces the background: while + was/were -ing.While I was mulling it over ...
A sequenceEvents in a row: all plain past.I packed up and took the plunge.
State verbsknow, understand, want, believe take NO -ing.I knew it before I said it.
was beingBehaving that way at the time, not permanently.They said I was being restless.

In one line: the long thing takes -ing; the thing that cut it takes the plain past.

Dialogue

Was That a Sudden Thing?

C
You said you moved out of Sao Paulo. Was that a sudden thing?
A
Not at all. I was mulling it over for two years while everybody thought I was joking.
C
What was the last straw, then?
A
There was no single one. I was sitting in gridlock one Tuesday and I just snapped. Quietly.
C
I know that feeling. I did the same thing in 2016, and three people tried to talk me out of it.
A
Two tried with me. They said I was being restless.
C
And were you?
A
For two years, yes. Then in March I packed up the apartment and took the plunge. It was daunting for about a week.
Comprehension

About Colin

1. When did Colin do the same thing, and how many people tried to stop him?
In 2016, and three people tried to talk him out of it.
2. What does he say when Ana describes sitting in gridlock?
That he knows that feeling.
3. What does he ask about the decision, and what does he never ask?
He asks whether it was sudden, what the last straw was, and whether she really was restless. He never asks why she left.
