English Words

Say It the English Way

The word that sounds like your language is not always the right one. Say the sentence with the English word.

#Not thisSay this
1The economy had an expressive growth.The economy had significant growth.
2We need to economize money.We need to save money.
3This is an actual problem.This is a current problem.
4The rates impacted on the loans.The rates had an impact on the loans. / The rates impacted the loans.
