Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Esther Duflo: “Social experiments to fight poverty” (17 min)

A Nobel prize economist shows how small decisions, tested with data, change the lives of millions of people.

Tip: note every time she says economic, economy or impact, and check the form.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Jacqueline Novogratz: “Invest in Africa’s own solutions to poverty” (18 min)

An investor on how money, data and trust work together to create real economic impact.

Tip: choose one example and explain it to your teacher in 30 seconds.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Tim Harford: “Trial, error and the God complex” (18 min)

An economist on why the economy is too complex for one expert, and why testing ideas works better.

Tip: pause twice and say, in one sentence, what impact his idea could have on your work.
