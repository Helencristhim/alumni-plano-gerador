Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Economy -- the system of money, work and trade in a country
"The Brazilian economy depends a lot on credit."
Economic -- connected to the economy of a country
"There was an economic slowdown last year."
Economical -- using little money, fuel or time
"The new car is very economical."
Economically -- in a way that is connected to the economy
"Economically, the region is growing."
Impact -- a strong effect that one thing has on another
"Interest rates have a big impact on loans."
A loan -- money that a bank lends and that you pay back
"She got a loan to open a bakery."
An interest rate -- the percentage that a bank charges for a loan
"The interest rate went up in March."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Credit bureaus collect data about how people and companies pay their bills. Banks use this data to decide who gets a loan. This has an economic role: when banks know who pays on time, they can lend more, and the economy grows.

When interest rates go up, they impact families directly: loans cost more, and people buy less. Economically, this slows the market down. A small family might choose an economical car to spend less.

In the text, what is the role of credit data?
A It increases interest rates.
B It helps banks decide who gets a loan.
C It makes cars more economical.
Which sentence uses impact as a verb?
A Interest rates have an impact on families.
B Interest rates impact families directly.
C The impact was big.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Economic or Economical? Impact or Impact On?

GRAMMAR

Economic: about the economy. Economical: costs little. Impact (verb) takes no on; an impact (noun) on.
