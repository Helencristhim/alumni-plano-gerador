Talk
Andrew Stanton -- "The clues to a great story" (TED)

The writer of Toy Story and WALL-E explains what makes people care about a story: a clear goal, a little uncertainty, and a reason to keep listening. Everything he says works for a personal statement too.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down one sentence in the past simple and one in the present perfect that he uses about his own career.

Talk
Julian Treasure -- "How to speak so that people want to listen" (TED)

A sound expert on the voice habits that make people stop listening, and on the tools that make a short answer sound confident. Useful before any interview in English.

Tip: listen without the video the second time, and repeat one of his exercises out loud before you record your ninety-second story.

Talk
Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie -- "The danger of a single story" (TED)

A Nigerian writer tells the story of her own life to show how one story can define a person. It is a model of a personal narrative told with real details, at a calm speed.

Tip: stop after the first five minutes and retell her childhood story in three sentences, with the past simple.
