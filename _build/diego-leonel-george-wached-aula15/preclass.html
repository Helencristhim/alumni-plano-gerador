Vocabulary

A background -- your education and work experience
"My background is in clinical dermatology."
To graduate -- to finish a university course and receive a degree
"She graduated from medical school in 2018."
A milestone -- an important moment or result in your progress
"Passing Step 3 will be a big milestone."
A turning point -- the moment when a situation or a plan changes direction
"That patient was the turning point in my career."
To pursue -- to try to achieve something over a long time
"He decided to pursue dermatology after his first year."
A rotation -- a period of training in one department of a hospital
"She did a four-week rotation in pediatrics."
To shadow -- to follow a doctor at work to watch and learn
"I would like to shadow a dermatologist in the United States."
A personal statement -- a short essay about you in a residency application
"Her personal statement tells one real story."
A setback -- a problem that stops or delays your progress
"Failing the first application was a big setback."
To stand out -- to be easy to notice because you are different or better
"A true story stands out more than a list of prizes."
Driven -- very motivated to work hard and succeed
"The program wants residents who are driven and kind."
In hindsight -- EXPRESSION — when you look at the past with what you know now
"In hindsight, that setback made me a better doctor."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Dr. Ana Ribeiro is a Brazilian doctor who wants to train in the United States, and her personal statement mixes two tenses on purpose. When she gives a finished time, she uses the past simple: I graduated from medical school in 2018, I shadowed a dermatologist in Miami last summer.

When she talks about her experience, or about something that started in the past and is still true, she uses the present perfect: I have worked in three hospitals since then, I have already passed two Step exams. There is no finished time in these sentences, because her career is still open.

Portuguese uses the same past tense for both ideas, so Brazilian doctors often write I have graduated in 2018 or I worked here since 2021. The test is simple: if the sentence says when, and that time is over, use the past simple. The questions follow the same rule: When did you graduate? but Have you ever worked in the United States?

1. "I ___ from medical school in 2018." Which form completes the sentence?
A have graduated, because the degree is still important today
B graduated
C am graduated, because I have the degree now
2. Why does Ana write "I have worked in three hospitals since then"?
A Because her career started in the past and it is still open now.
B Because the three jobs are finished and she says when.
C Because work is a state verb.
3. Which question is correct?
A When have you started your residency in dermatology at the university hospital?
B Did you ever work in the United States?
C When did you start your residency?

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Past Simple and Present Perfect

GRAMMAR

A finished time that you say takes the past simple. Experience, and time that is still open, take the present perfect.

JobFormExample
Finished timepast simpleShe graduated in 2018.
Experience, no timehave / has + past participleI have shadowed two attendings.
Until now, for / sincehave / has + past participleHe has lived here for six years.
Negativedid not / have notI didn’t apply last year. I haven’t done a rotation yet.
QuestionsWhen did...? / Have you ever...?When did you graduate? Have you ever worked abroad?
Time wordsyesterday, in 2018, ago · ever, never, since, yettwo years ago · since 2021

Never: I have graduated in 2019 · I work here since 2021 · When have you started? · Did you ever work abroad? (for experience) · I am graduated

Stage 2: Put the Answer in Order

Order

An interviewer says, "Tell me about yourself." Put the sentences in the order of a good answer: now, the turning point, and the next step.

?"I'm a dermatology resident from Brazil."
?"I graduated from medical school there, and since then I have focused on skin disease."
?"The turning point came in my first year, when we caught a melanoma very early."
?"After that, I decided to pursue dermatology."
?"I have read dermatology articles in English every week for years."
?"Next year, I would like to shadow a dermatologist in the United States."
