Complementary Activities

Three curated pieces for the days between classes. There is no written homework -- pick one, and bring it up in the next lesson. Mark each one as done after you watch, listen or read it.

Video · American accent · 12 min
What Does A Music Producer Actually Do? (Explained By A Pro)

A working producer answers, in English, the question you were asked today: what the job is, where it starts, and where it stops. It is the same problem you have -- a role that sits between departments and refuses to fit in one line.

Tip: write down every sentence he uses to describe the shape of the job, and mark which ones are in the base form and which ones are in the -ing form.

Podcast · British and American voices · 20 min
Song Exploder, episode 292 -- Tears for Fears, "Everybody Wants to Rule the World"

The band and the producer take the song apart track by track and explain the decisions behind it: the drum machine, the guitar line, and the lyrics that almost did not happen. This is the closest thing there is to the conversation you will have in a songwriting course.

Tip: the page has a full transcript. Listen once without it, then read it and count how many words from this lesson appear.

Video · British accents · 9 min
Inside Abbey Road: A Day in the Life of Abbey Road’s Engineers and Runners

The crew of a working studio describe an ordinary day: who books the room, who sets it up, who sits behind the glass and who fixes the thing nobody planned for. Everyone on screen is doing your job, in English, with the accent you already listen to for pleasure.

Tip: listen for the moment somebody switches from what they normally do to what they are doing today, and write the two sentences side by side.
