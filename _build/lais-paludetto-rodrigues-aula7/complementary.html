Grammar Video
"Indirect Questions" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

The other half of today's lesson: what happens to word order when a question stops being a question. Six minutes, with the mistake everybody makes played out loud so you can hear how wrong it sounds. Connection to Lesson 7: "He asked me where the room was", never "where was the room".

Tip: turn five direct questions from your own week into indirect ones out loud. Five is enough to make the word order automatic.
