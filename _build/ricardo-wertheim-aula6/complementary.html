"Comparatives and superlatives" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on exactly the rule you found in class: the small ending for short words, the whole word in front for long ones, and the irregular forms you simply have to know. Connection to Lesson 6: this is the grammar behind "smaller than" and "the most iconic".

Tip: pause at each example and count the syllables out loud before they say the answer. Then build the same sentence about two cities you have visited.

Podcast
"Croatia Backroads" -- Amateur Traveler, Episode 133

A traveller who skipped the famous coast and went inland instead: Trogir, the Plitvice lakes, the hill towns north of Zagreb. Connection to Lesson 6: the whole episode is an argument that the underrated places are better, and it is built on comparisons.

Tip: every time he prefers one place over another, write the comparison down in your own words. Aim for five sentences with "more" or "-er" and one with "the most".

"Ljubljana, Slovenia: Happy Hodgepodge" -- Rick Steves' Europe

A short piece on the city the reading calls the most underrated capital in Europe: the river, the compact centre, the bridges. Connection to Lesson 6: watch it as the second half of the comparison you read in class, with the city in front of you instead of on paper.

Tip: watch it once, then say out loud three comparisons between Ljubljana and a city you know. Use one short adjective, one long adjective and one superlative.
