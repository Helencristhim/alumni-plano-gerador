Chapter 3: The Code

Happening Now

am, is, are + verb-ing

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I am returning the car."

"The agent is checking the gas."

"My wife is not waiting inside."

"Is the shuttle leaving?"

Every blue verb has two parts. The first part is a verb from lesson 1. What is the second part? Is it happening every day, or right now?

JobFormExample
Now, with II am + verb-ingI am returning the car.
Now, with he / she / itis + verb-ingShe is checking the gas.
Now, with you / we / theyare + verb-ingWe are leaving now.
Negativeam not / is not / are not + verb-ingMy wife is not waiting inside.
QuestionAm / Is / Are + person + verb-ing?Is the shuttle leaving?
Every day or now?every day: I drive · now: I am drivingI drive every day. Right now, I am driving to the airport.

In one line: for right now: am / is / are + verb-ing.

Chapter 4: At the Return Lane

At the Return Lane

One conversation, two recordings, one receipt

Dialogue

The Return Lane

R
Hi there! Are you returning the car today?
C
Yes, I am. I am returning it a little early. Our flight is at two.
R
No problem. I am checking the car now. The gas tank is full. Great.
C
Is everything okay?
R
Hmm, there is a small scratch on the back door.
C
Yes, but that scratch was there on the first day. It is on the rental agreement.
R
Let me see. You are right. It is on the agreement. Here is your receipt.
C
Thank you. Where is the shuttle? My wife is waiting with the bags.
R
It is leaving in two minutes, to Terminal D. Have a good flight!
Comprehension

About Rachel

1. What is Rachel checking?
The car. The gas tank is full.
2. What does Rachel find?
A small scratch on the back door.
3. What does Rachel give Carlos?
His receipt.
4. When is the shuttle leaving, according to Rachel?
In two minutes, to Terminal D.
