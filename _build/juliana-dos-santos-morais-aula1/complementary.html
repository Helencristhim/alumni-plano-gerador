10 ways to have a better conversation

Radio host Celeste Headlee gives ten simple rules for a real conversation: listen, ask open questions, do not repeat yourself, and be brief. Every rule works in the first minutes of a partnership call.

Tip: turn on the English subtitles, then write down the three rules you want to try in your next meeting.

How to Succeed in Your New Job -- The Way We Work, a TED series

A short video about the first weeks with new people at work: what to ask, and how to explain what you do. A good model for saying your role and your current work.

Tip: pause after each tip and say one sentence about your own job, starting with I am in charge of.
