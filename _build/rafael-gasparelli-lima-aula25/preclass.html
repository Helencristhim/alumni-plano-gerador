Lesson 25 -- Pre-class

Plans for 2027

Get ready to talk about the future. Key words: strategy, goal, target, investment, priority, long-term, opportunity, risk. Structure: going to for plans, will for decisions, promises and predictions.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Eight words to plan the future of a company. Tap Listen to hear each one.

Strategy -- a big plan to reach an important result
"Our strategy for 2027 is to grow in Europe."
Goal -- something you want to achieve in the future
"Our goal is to become the biggest exporter in the region."
Target -- an exact result, with a number and a date
"Our target is ten percent more exports by December."
Investment -- money you spend now to get more money later
"Two new machines are a big investment."
Priority -- the most important thing to do first
"Europe is our first priority next year."
Long-term -- for a long time in the future, many years
"Our long-term goal is to be strong in Europe and Asia."
Opportunity -- a good chance to do something useful or to win
"Europe is our biggest opportunity."
Risk -- the chance that something bad can happen
"Every investment has a risk."

GRAMMAR

Hi Claire, here is our strategy for 2027. Our long-term goal is to become the most reliable fruit partner in the Midwest. Next year, we are going to open a small office in Rotterdam, and we are going to invest in two new farms. It is a big investment, but it is also a great opportunity.

Our target is 400 tons of mangoes for you by December, and your orders are our first priority. Of course, there is a risk: the weather. I think it will be a hard year, but I promise we won't be late with any shipment. You asked for the numbers, so I'll send them tomorrow morning.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Going To or Will?

GRAMMAR

Use going to for plans you decided before. Use will for decisions right now, promises and predictions.

UseStructureExample
A plan (decided before)am / is / are + going to + base verbWe are going to open an office.
A decision right nowwill ('ll) + base verbOK, I'll send them now.
A promisewill + base verbI will call you on Monday.
A prediction (opinion)I think + will + base verbI think it will be a hard year.
Negativeisn't going to · won'tWe won't close the plant.

Never: we going to open · I will to send · he is going invest · next year I travel (for a plan)

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

A "We will buy two new machines, I decide now."
B "We're going to buy two new machines."
C "We going to buy two new machines."
Claire asks for the contract. You decide to send it right now. You say:
A "Sure, I'll send it now."
B "Sure, I'm going to sending it."
C "Sure, I will to send it now."
An investor asks for your opinion about next year. You say:
A "I think the market grows next year."
B "I think the market going to grow."
C "I think the market will grow next year."
A client is worried about delays. You make a promise:
A "We won't be late with any shipment."
B "We don't will be late."
