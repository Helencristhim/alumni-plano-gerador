Chapter 3: Going To or Will?

Two Futures, Two Uses

A plan from yesterday, or a decision right now?

Grammar Discovery

Before or Right Now?

We are going to open an office in Rotterdam next year.

Rafael is going to lead the new partnership.

You need the numbers? OK, I’ll send them now.

I think it will be a hard year.

Which blue sentences were decided before? What about the red ones?

UseStructureExample
A plan (decided before)am / is / are + going to + base verbWe are going to open an office.
A decision right nowwill (’ll) + base verbOK, I’ll send them now.
A promisewill + base verbI will call you on Monday.
A prediction (opinion)I think + will + base verbI think it will be a hard year.
Negativeisn’t going to · won’tWe won’t close the plant.

After going to and will: the base verb (no to, no -s). Going to always needs am / is / are.

Chapter 4: Planning With Claire

A Call About Next Year

One call, two recordings, one plan for 2027

Dialogue

The Plan for 2027

C
Hi Rafael. Our board met yesterday. We’re going to double our orders of mangoes next year.
R
That’s great news! It’s a big opportunity for us too. What is your target?
C
Four hundred tons by December. Our long-term goal is to sell in every state in the Midwest.
R
We can do that. Our CEO is going to open a small office in Rotterdam, and Europe and the U.S. are our two priorities.
C
Perfect. But is there a risk? Can your farms produce so much?
R
Yes. We’re going to make a big investment in two new farms next year.
C
Wonderful. Can you send me the full strategy? My boss wants to see it.
R
Of course. I’ll send it to you today.
C
Thanks. I think 2027 will be our best year together.
R
I agree. And I promise we won’t be late with any shipment!

Real Document

Strategy 2027

Picunha Group · Fruit Division
Strategy 2027 — prepared by Rafael Gasparelli Lima for Harbor Fresh Foods

Long-term goal: the most reliable fruit partner in the U.S. Midwest.

Target 2027: 400 tons of mangoes for Harbor Fresh by December.

Priorities: 1. two new farms · 2. a sales office in Rotterdam · 3. cold containers for every shipment.

Main risk: bad weather at harvest time. Backup plan: fruit from partner farms.

1. What is the target for 2027?
400 tons of mangoes for Harbor Fresh by December.
2. What is the first priority?
Two new farms.
3. What is the main risk, and what is the backup plan?
Bad weather at harvest time; the backup plan is fruit from partner farms.
