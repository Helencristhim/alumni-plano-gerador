Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto em voz alta, devagar, e depois responda as perguntas.

Some years ago, Carlos was in New York at Christmas. His daughter was twenty-one. It was very cold.

At Rockefeller Center, there was a big Christmas tree and an ice rink. The crowd was big. Carlos was the teacher: all the theory was perfect.

But on the ice, his legs were not strong. His skates were on the ice, and Carlos was on the ice too! Was it a bad day? No, it was very funny.

1. Quem virou a professora no gelo? (Who was the teacher on the ice?)
A O Carlos. (Carlos.)
B A filha dele. (His daughter.)
C Um funcionário do rinque. (A rink worker.)
2. Por que "his legs WERE", e não "was"? (Why "were"?)
A Porque "legs" é plural. (Because "legs" is plural.)
B Porque é uma pergunta. (Because it is a question.)
C Porque é presente. (Because it is present.)
3. Como se diz "Não foi um dia ruim" em inglês?
A "It no was a bad day."
B "It wasn't a bad day."
C "It weren't a bad day."

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Was / were (to be no passado)

GRAMMAR

O verbo to be da aula 1 também tem passado: am e is viram was; are vira were. / The verb to be has a past: am and is become was; are becomes were.

Uso / UseEstrutura / PatternExemplo / Example
Eu, ele, ela, isso / I, he, she, itwasIt was cold. (Estava frio.)
Você, nós, eles, plural / You, we, theywereWe were at the rink. (Nós estávamos no rinque.)
Negativa / Negativewasn’t / weren’tIt wasn’t a bad day. (Não foi um dia ruim.)
Pergunta / QuestionWas...? / Were...?Were you in New York? (Você estava em Nova York?)
Tinha, havia / There was, there werethere was + um / there were + váriosThere was a big tree. (Tinha uma árvore grande.)
Ideia-chave / Key ideaEm português "foi", "era" e "estava" viram a mesma coisa em inglês: was ou were. E a idade no passado também: My daughter was 21 (nunca "had 21"). / "Foi", "era" and "estava" are all was / were in English.

Etapa 4: Quiz Situacional

Quiz

Escolha a melhor resposta para cada situação. Errou? Tente de novo.

Um amigo pergunta onde você estava no Natal. Você diz:
A "I were in New York."
B "I was in New York."
C "I am in New York last Christmas."
Você quer dizer que as ruas estavam cheias. Você diz:
A "The streets were full."
B "The streets was full."
C "The streets are full yesterday."
Você quer perguntar se a viagem foi cara. Você diz:
A "The trip was expensive?"
B "Was the trip expensive?"
C "Were the trip expensive?"
Alguém pergunta: "Was it a bad day?" Não foi. Você responde:
A "No, it wasn't. It was funny."
B "No, it weren't."
C "No, it isn't yesterday."
