Chapter 4: The Code

Was and Were

The verb to be in the past

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I was in New York at Christmas."

"The streets were full of people."

"His legs were not strong."

"Was it a bad day?"

In lesson 1: I am, it is, you are. These sentences are about some years ago. What happens to am, is and are?

JobFormExample
I / he / she / itwasI was a good skater. It was cold.
you / we / they / pluralwereWe were at the ice rink. The streets were full.
Negativewas not (wasn’t) / were not (weren’t)It wasn’t a bad day.
QuestionWas / Were + person...?Were you in New York? Was it cold?
Short answersYes, it was. / No, we weren’t.Was it funny? Yes, it was.
Past of there is / there arethere was / there wereThere was a big tree.

In one line: am and is become was; are becomes were.

Forty Years at the Rink
