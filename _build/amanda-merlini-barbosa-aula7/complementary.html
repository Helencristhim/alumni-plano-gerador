Series
Ted Lasso -- Season 1, Episode 1: Pilot

An American football coach is hired to run an English football club, a sport he has never coached. It is the steepest learning curve on television, told in thirty light minutes, and the English is slow and clear.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down every sentence where he admits he does not know something. Notice that admitting it never makes him smaller.

Podcast
The Former Lawyer Podcast -- How to Figure Out What You Want to Do Instead of Practicing Law

A lawyer talking to lawyers about changing direction. You are not leaving law, but the language of the conversation is exactly the language of this lesson: what I used to do, what I am used to, what I am still getting used to.

Tip: listen for "used to". Every time you hear it, decide which of the three expressions it is, before the sentence finishes.
