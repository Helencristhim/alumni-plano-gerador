Next Steps

A meeting is only as good as its last five minutes: who does what, and by when. This lesson gives you the words and the time expressions to close a meeting clearly.

Chapter 1: First Words

The Last Five Minutes

You can open a meeting, take the floor, give an update, compare and negotiate. Now the meeting is ending. If nobody says who does what and by when, the next meeting starts from zero.

Think of your last meeting. What were the next steps, and who was responsible for each one?

2

3

The target of the day: I will send the list by Friday is a deadline; as soon as the director signs, I will let you know connects two future actions. After as soon as and when, English uses the present.

Chapter 3: The Code

By, Until, As Soon As

The small words that make a deadline clear

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Promises Show?

"I will send you the list by Friday."

"The offer is valid until the end of the month."

"We will answer within two days."

"As soon as the director signs, I will let you know."

The blue words place the action in time. By is a deadline: on that day or before. Until is a period that continues and then stops. Within is inside a period. And look at the red verb after as soon as: is it present or future?

JobFormExample
A deadline: that day or beforeby + day / timeI will send it by Friday.
A situation that continues, then stopsuntil + day / timeThe offer is valid until May 30.
Inside a period of timewithin + periodWe will answer within two days.
Right after another actionas soon as + present, will + verbAs soon as he signs, I will call you.
At the time of another actionwhen + present, will + verbWhen the list is ready, I will send it.
The trapno will after as soon as / whenNot as soon as he will sign. Say as soon as he signs.

In one line: by = the deadline; until = up to that point; within = inside the period; as soon as / when + present.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

The Close, at Real Speed

One call, three recordings, one action list

Dialogue

The Last Five Minutes

J
We have five minutes left. Before we finish, can we agree on the next steps?
D
Good idea. First, I will send you the list of Canadian companies by Friday.
J
Perfect. And I will follow up with the hotel. As soon as they confirm the room, I will let you know.
D
Great. What about the budget? Is it still pending?
J
Yes. Our director is traveling until Wednesday. When she comes back, she will sign it.
D
OK. So we have three action items. Can we touch base again next Thursday?
J
Sure. I will send everybody a reminder on Tuesday, with the list of actions and the deadlines.
D
Excellent. Please keep me posted about the hotel. Thanks, Juliana.

About Daniel

1. What will Daniel send, and by when?
The list of Canadian companies, by Friday.
2. When does Daniel want to talk again?
Next Thursday.
3. What does Daniel ask Juliana to keep him posted about?
The hotel.
