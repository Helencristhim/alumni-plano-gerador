Lesson 13 -- On Hold

Music
Hanging On The Telephone -- Blondie (1978)

A fast, famous song about somebody who keeps calling and cannot get through. Short, fun and full of phone words: to call, to answer, to ring, and the line of the title, don’t leave me hanging on the telephone.

Tip: listen once just for the rhythm. Then listen again and count how many times you hear the word telephone. Can you say how long the singer has been calling?

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) -- How to Talk to Customer Service and Actually Get What You Want

A negotiation expert explains how to make the rep your ally: say your name and theirs, describe what has been happening, stay calm and ask clearly. The same idea as tonight’s third listening, with more tricks.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then write down two sentences you could use on your next call, with I have been...

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
Useful Telephone English: How to Talk to Customer Service -- Jon Peng English

A practical video with the phrases you hear and say on a customer service call: the automated menu, being on hold, explaining a problem and asking for the next step.

Tip: watch with English subtitles first. On the second time, pause after each phrase and say it out loud as if you were on hold with your own internet company.
