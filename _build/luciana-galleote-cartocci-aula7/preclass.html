Lesson 07 -- Pre-class

Meeting Halfway -- Negotiating Price and Terms

The partner likes the idea; now the price. Key words: a quote, a discount, payment terms, a service-level agreement, maintenance, to cover, a counteroffer, to meet halfway, a commitment, the bottom line. Structure: first conditional for offers.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A quote -- a document that says how much a job or a product will cost
"We will send you the quote by Thursday."
A discount -- a reduction in the normal price
"If you sign for three years, we can offer a discount."
Payment terms -- the rules about when and how a client pays
"Our payment terms are thirty days after each phase."
A service-level agreement -- a contract that says the quality and speed of a service, like response times
"The service-level agreement includes a response in four hours."
Maintenance -- the work of keeping equipment in good condition
"Maintenance is included for the first year."
To cover -- to include or pay for something
"The price covers installation and training."
A counteroffer -- a new offer that you make after you receive an offer
"They made a counteroffer: five percent, not ten."
To meet halfway -- to accept part of what the other side wants, so both sides agree
"If you meet us halfway on the price, we will sign today."
A commitment -- a promise to do something, often for a long time
"A three-year commitment is important for us."
The bottom line -- the final price or the most important fact
"What is your bottom line?"

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

A hospital group asks an infrastructure company for a ten percent discount. The CEO does not say yes or no. She says: “If you sign for three years, we will include the first year of maintenance.”

The hospital makes a counteroffer: two years, not three. The CEO answers: “We can accept two years if you pay in thirty days.”

At the end, she adds a limit: “Unless you confirm by Friday, the price will change.” In every sentence, the verb after if or unless is in the present, even for the future.

1. What does the CEO do when the hospital asks for a discount?
A She says yes immediately.
B She offers something with a condition: a three-year contract.
C She stops the negotiation.
2. What does “unless you confirm by Friday” mean?
A If you confirm by Friday.
B If you do not confirm by Friday.
C You confirmed last Friday.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A “If you will sign today, we give a discount.”
B “If you sign today, we will give a discount.”
C “If you signed today, we will give a discount.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- First Conditional for Offers

GRAMMAR

A condition with if + present, a result with will or can. Unless means if not. Never will after if.

JobFormExample
The conditionif + presentIf you sign for three years...
The resultwill / can + verb...we will include maintenance.
The order can changeresult + if + conditionWe can offer a discount if you pay in advance.
Negative conditionunless = if notUnless you confirm by Friday, the price will change.
A counterofferIf you..., we could...If you pay in advance, we could give five percent.
The trapno will after ifNot if you will sign. Say if you sign.

Never: If you will sign, we include · We give a discount if you will pay · Unless you don’t confirm · The price cover

Stage 2: Put the Negotiation in Order

Order

A partner says your price is too high. Put the negotiation in a logical order.

?"I understand. What exactly does your budget cover?"
?"If you sign for three years, we will include maintenance."
?"Three years is a big commitment for us."
?"Then here is a counteroffer: two years, with payment in thirty days."
?"If you meet us halfway on the terms, we will send the new quote today."
?"Unless you confirm by Friday, the price will change."

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer a real speaker would give.

A client asks for a discount. You want to answer with a condition. You say:
A "OK, ten percent."
B "If you sign for three years, we can offer five percent."
C "If you will sign for three years, we give five percent."
You want to explain what the price includes. You say:
A "The price covers installation and training."
B "The price cover installation and training."
C "The price is covering installation and training always."
You want to give the client a deadline. You say:
A "Unless you don’t confirm by Friday, the price changes."
B "Unless you confirm by Friday, the price will change."
C "If you will not confirm by Friday, the price changes."
The client asks for your final price. The best answer is:
A "This is our best offer. If you meet us halfway, we will sign today."
B "The bottom line is a secret."
C "If you will meet us halfway, we sign."
