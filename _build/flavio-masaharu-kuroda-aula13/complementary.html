Lesson 13 -- The Weekly Forecast

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Plans and strategies"

A short episode on talking about plans at work: what is certain, what is likely, and what is only an intention. Full script on the same page, at natural speed.

Tip: read the script and mark every word that says how sure the speaker is. Then rank them from most certain to least certain.

Talk
Hans Rosling -- "The best stats you've ever seen" (TED, 20 min)

The most famous talk ever given about numbers moving over time. Twenty minutes of rise, fall, level off and sharply, said by a speaker whose first language is not English either.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Pause three times and describe the chart on the screen in one sentence: which way it is moving, by how much, and how fast.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Mona Chalabi -- "3 ways to spot a bad statistic" (TED, 11 min)

A data journalist on the three questions to ask before you believe a number. The same questions a good CFO asks on Monday morning: where does it come from, how sure is it, and what is missing?

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Apply her three questions to your own forecast, and answer each one in English in one sentence.
