The BEST Questions To Ask At The End Of Your Job Interview

A former American corporate recruiter explains what he was actually listening for in the last five minutes of an interview, and which questions moved a candidate up or down in the process. This is the same block you practiced tonight, described from the other side of the table, in natural American English.

Tip: write each of his questions down, then rewrite it as an indirect question with a frame in front. Compare which version you would rather say out loud.

Podcast · BBC Learning English
English at Work -- Episode 1: The Interview

A short audio drama that drops you inside a real job interview: the candidate answers, hesitates, recovers, and the panel reacts. The page carries the audio and the full transcript, so you can listen first and only then read what she actually said and where she lost ground.

Tip: listen once without the transcript and note every question the panel asks her. Then listen again and count how many questions she asks them.

TED Talk · Hiring
Regina Hartley: Why the best hire might not have the perfect resume

A human resources director explains how she reads two candidates side by side, and why the one with the straight line through elite companies is not always the one she wants. It is the clearest picture there is of what the people in that room are really deciding while you answer, which is exactly what your own questions are there to find out.

Tip: turn on English subtitles and write down the three things she says a long, non-linear career proves about a person.
