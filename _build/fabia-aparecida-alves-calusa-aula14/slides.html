How others answer

Answer A

1. How low was revenue this quarter?
She does not say. Only "a bit low".
2. How much better was the margin?
She does not say. Only "better, I think".
How others answer

Answer B

1. Was revenue higher or lower than the forecast?
Lower. Eight hundred and twenty thousand, against a forecast of nine hundred.
2. How much higher was the margin than last quarter?
Three points higher.
How others answer

Which one answers the question?

Which answer tells the client if they are on track?
Answer B. "Lower than the forecast" and "three points higher than last quarter" show where the business is, next to where it should be.
