Psychologist Adam Waytz on what happens in the mind when two good values point in different directions — loyalty to your people, or the rule you promised to follow. It is the story of a whistleblower, told slowly and in clear American English.

Tip: listen for the moment the officer has to choose. Then tell the story back in three sentences using had to, was not allowed to and did not have to.

TED -- Dan Ariely, "Our buggy moral code"

A behavioral economist on why honest people cheat a little when they think nobody is counting, and why the small exceptions matter more than the big crimes. It is the research behind the second paragraph of today’s reading: misconduct starts with somebody who bends the rules once.

Tip: write down one experiment from the talk and explain it in two sentences. Then say one rule from your own site that exists to stop the "just this once".
