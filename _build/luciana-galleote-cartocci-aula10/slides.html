Chapter 4: The Code

Booked or Just Planned?

Two ways to talk about your plans

Grammar Discovery

Five Sentences About the Trip

"We are flying to Shenzhen on October 9."

"Our host is picking us up at the airport."

"A camera manufacturer is taking us on a factory tour on Tuesday."

"Each person is going to focus on one area."

"I am going to bring more business cards this time."

The blue verbs and the orange verbs both talk about the future. One kind has a date, a time or a person who is waiting. The other is a plan in your head. Which is which?

JobFormExample
A fixed arrangement: booked, with a date, a time or other peopleam / is / are + verb-ingWe are flying to Shenzhen on October 9.
An intention: a decision or a plan in your headam / is / are going to + verbI am going to visit five booths.
The negativearen’t + verb-ing / aren’t going to + verbWe aren’t flying direct. We aren’t going to plan meetings on day one.
The questionWhat are you doing...? / Are you going to...?What are you doing on Tuesday? Are you going to bring your engineers?
With the verb goam / is / are going + placeWe are going to Shanghai by train.

In one line: booked, with a time or a host = be + -ing; a plan or a decision in your head = be going to + verb.

An example itinerary, not a real trip. Every line with a date and a time is an arrangement, and that is why we read it with be + -ing. The free day is different: it is a plan, and a plan is going to.
1. Turn the October 12 line into a sentence with be + -ing.
We are visiting the medical technology trade show in Shenzhen on October 12.
2. October 16 is free. Say what you are going to do.
For example: I am going to meet a possible partner, or I am going to rest.
3. Who is the host on October 14, and what is happening?
Wei Zhang. His company is taking the delegation on a factory tour at 9 a.m.
Listening 1 · The Trip Organizer

How Our Trips Work

Anjali organizes business trips to China for hospital groups. She explains what is booked and what is free. Sound first — no text.

1. What is always booked before the delegation leaves?
The flights, the hotels and the main visits.
2. Why is it fine to rest in the free time?
Because jet lag is hard in the first two days.
3. When do people have to tell Anjali about their plans?
A week before.
Listening 2 · Wei Zhang

Everything Is Ready on Our Side

Wei Zhang, from a camera manufacturer in Shenzhen, leaves a message about the visit. Sound first — no text.

1. When does the factory tour start?
At nine, on Tuesday. Wei’s colleague is picking up the group at eight thirty.
2. What is happening in the evening?
The director is having dinner with the delegation.
3. What does Wei want to know?
If Luciana is going to bring her engineers. He needs their names before Friday.
Listening 3 · The Hospital Director

Three Things I Am Going to Change

A hospital director from Lyon went to China last year. He talks about his plans for the next trip. Sound first — no text.

1. What went wrong at the trade show last year?
He walked for two days without a plan.
2. How many business cards did he give away?
Two hundred in three days.
3. Why is he going to arrive one day early?
To recover from the jet lag before the first meeting on Monday.
