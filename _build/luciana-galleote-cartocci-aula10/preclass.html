Lesson 10 -- Pre-class

The China Delegation -- Arrangements and Plans

A hospital group is taking a delegation to China, and Teleinfo is going too. Key words: an itinerary, a trade show, a booth, a factory tour, a showroom, a layover, jet lag, an interpreter, a host, a business card. Structure: be going to and the present continuous for the future.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

An itinerary -- a detailed plan of a trip, with dates, places and times
"The itinerary has five days in Shenzhen and three in Shanghai."
A trade show -- a big event where companies show their new products to buyers
"We are visiting a medical technology trade show on Tuesday."
A booth -- a small area at a trade show where a company shows its products
"Their booth is next to the main entrance."
A factory tour -- a visit to see how a company makes its products
"The factory tour starts at nine in the morning."
A showroom -- a room where a company shows its products to visitors
"In the showroom, we can test the new cameras."
A layover -- a short stop at an airport between two flights
"We have a six-hour layover in Dubai."
Jet lag -- the tired feeling after a long flight to a different time zone
"China is eleven hours ahead, so jet lag is a real problem."
An interpreter -- a person who translates what people say while they are talking
"The host is bringing an interpreter to the meeting."
A host -- a person or company that receives visitors and takes care of them
"Our host is picking us up at the airport."
A business card -- a small card with your name, your job and your contact details
"In China, people offer a business card with both hands."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

On Monday, Luciana reads the itinerary of the trip with her assistant. “OK,” the assistant says. “We are flying to Shenzhen on the night of October 9, and Wei Zhang is meeting the group at the airport. On October 14, his company is taking us on a factory tour.”

“And October 16?” Luciana asks. “That day is free,” the assistant answers. “Nothing is booked yet.” Luciana thinks for a moment. “Then I am going to visit two possible partners in Shanghai. And this time, I am going to bring more business cards.”

Her assistant smiles. “Good idea. And you aren’t going to plan any meetings on the first day, right? Jet lag!” The things with a date and a host are be + -ing. Luciana’s own decisions are going to.

1. Is the factory tour already arranged?
A No. It is only an idea, and the assistant is going to call the company next week to ask for a date.
B Yes. It has a date and a host.
C The text does not say.
2. Why does Luciana say “I am going to visit two possible partners”?
A Because it is her decision, and nothing is booked yet.
B Because the meetings are already booked, with a time and a place in Shanghai, and the partners are waiting for her.
C Because it happened in the past.
3. What does “Wei Zhang is meeting the group at the airport” mean?
A Wei is at the airport right now, and he is talking to the group about the trade show in Shenzhen.
B Wei is thinking about the idea, but he has not decided.
C It is a fixed arrangement for the day of the arrival.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Be Going To and the Present Continuous

GRAMMAR

Both forms talk about the future. Use be + -ing for a fixed arrangement: something booked, with a date, a time or other people. Use be going to + verb for an intention: a plan or a decision in your head.

JobFormExample
A fixed arrangement (booked)am / is / are + verb-ingWe are flying to Shenzhen on October 9.
An intention, a decisionam / is / are going to + verbI am going to visit five booths.
Negativearen’t + verb-ing / aren’t going to + verbWe aren’t going to plan meetings on day one.
QuestionWhat are you doing...? / Are you going to...?Are you going to bring an interpreter?
With the verb goam / is / are going + placeWe are going to Shanghai by train.

Never: We are go to visit · She going to bring · Are you going bring? · Tomorrow I visit the factory at nine
