Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
"What is Strategy? by Michael Porter -- A Visual Summary"

Ten minutes on the argument behind this whole lesson, from the man who made it famous: strategy is not the list of things you are going to do. It is the list of things you are deliberately not going to do. The narrator draws while he speaks, so the English is easy to follow.

Tip: pause when he reaches the word trade-off and say, out loud in English, one trade-off from your own plan. Then let it play.

Podcast
NPR Planet Money -- "What's It Worth To You?"

Twenty minutes on the question under every budget meeting: how do you decide what something is worth when nobody can agree? Useful for you because the whole episode is people defending a number out loud, which is what you practised today.

Tip: the page carries the full transcript. Listen once with it closed, then open it and find three sentences with going to. Say each one about your own plan.

Talk
INSEAD -- "Harvard’s Michael Porter on long-term strategy in a downturn"

A short interview on the hardest version of the choice you presented today: what do you protect and what do you cut when the money gets tighter. Interview English, so the pace is a conversation and not a lecture.

Tip: after watching, answer his question about your own quarter in two sentences, out loud. One thing you are going to protect, one thing you are going to postpone.
