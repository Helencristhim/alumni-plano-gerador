Chapter 3: The Code

I Was Climbing When...

The scene and the event

Grammar Discovery

The Scene and the Event

"We were climbing to the viewpoint when the fog came."

"While I was taking a photo, a llama walked past."

"It was raining, and everybody was waiting for the bus."

"We reached the summit, took a photo and started to go down."

Blue: what was happening, the scene, like a background in a film. Red: the short actions that happen in the story, one after the other. When the red action interrupts the blue one, we often use when or while.

JobFormExample
The scene, the backgroundwas / were + verb-ingWe were climbing...
The events, the storysimple past...when the fog came.
An interruptionpast continuous + when + simple pastI was walking when it started to rain.
Two things at the same timewhile + past continuousWhile I was taking a photo...
A sequence of eventssimple past, simple past, simple pastWe arrived, looked and smiled.
The trapa short event does not take -ingNot When the fog was coming, we climbed. Say We were climbing when the fog came.

In one line: the scene: was / were + -ing; the event: the simple past.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

Peru at Real Speed

One conversation, three recordings, one ticket

Dialogue

Back from Peru

P
Mirela, I’m back! You told me you went to Machu Picchu. Well, now I’ve been there too.
M
How was it? Was it foggy when you arrived?
P
Very! We set off at five, and we were climbing the trail when the clouds came down. We couldn’t see a thing.
M
Oh no! And did the fog go away?
P
Yes, at about nine. I was standing at the viewpoint when the sun came out, and suddenly the whole city was there.
M
That’s the moment everybody waits for. Did you feel the altitude?
P
A little. I was out of breath on every staircase. And while I was taking a photo of the ruins, a llama walked right in front of me.
M
That’s the best photo of the trip. It sounds unforgettable.
Comprehension

About Peter

1. What time did Peter and his group set off?
At five in the morning.
2. What happened while they were climbing the trail?
The clouds came down, and they couldn’t see anything.
3. What happened while Peter was taking a photo of the ruins?
A llama walked right in front of him.
Listening 1 · A Tour Guide

A City in the Clouds

A tour guide explains the history of Machu Picchu to a group of visitors. Sound first — no text.

1. When was the city built, according to the guide?
In the fifteenth century, around five hundred years ago.
2. Why did the world not know about it for a long time?
Because it was hidden in the mountains, and only local farmers knew about it.
3. What happened in 1911?
An American explorer arrived and told the world about it.
Listening 2 · A Traveler

The Altitude Surprised Me

An American woman tells what happened to her on her first day in the Andes. Sound first — no text.

1. What was she doing when she started to feel bad?
She was walking up the stairs of her hotel in Cusco.
2. How did she feel?
Dizzy, with a headache, and out of breath.
3. What did the hotel staff give her?
A cup of coca tea, and they told her to rest.
Listening 3 · A Hiker

The Train or the Trail?

An Australian man compares two ways to get to Machu Picchu. Sound first — no text.

1. How did he get to Machu Picchu?
He walked the trail for four days.
2. What happened on the third day?
It was raining all day, and they were walking in the mud.
3. How did his parents get there, and what did they think?
By train; they loved the views from the window.
