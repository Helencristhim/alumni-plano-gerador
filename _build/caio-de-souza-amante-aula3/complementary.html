Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
The Finance Storyteller — “Mergers and acquisitions explained”

Twelve minutes of the exact vocabulary you learned today, used the way a buyer uses it: acquirer, valuation, synergy, due diligence. Clear, slow enough to follow, and it puts the eight words into one process from start to finish.

Tip: pause every time one of today's eight words appears and say the Dataside version of that sentence out loud before you press play again.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
Aswath Damodaran — “Valuation in Four Lessons” (Talks at Google)

The most respected valuation teacher in the world explaining, in fast unscripted American English, how a buyer decides what a company is worth. This is the argument that will be used about Dataside whether or not you are in the room.

Tip: an hour is too long for one sitting. Watch fifteen minutes, then write the one sentence you would use to defend the Dataside valuation against what he just said.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Podcast
In Good Company — Marc Rowan, CEO of Apollo

The head of one of the largest private equity firms in the world on how his team looks at a company before buying it. He is describing the person on the other side of your table, in his own words and at his own speed.

Tip: the first twenty minutes are enough. Write down every time he uses has been plus a verb -- each one is a process he is telling you has not stopped.
