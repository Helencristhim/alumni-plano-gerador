Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Somebody Feed Phil — Season 1, Episode 4 "Lisbon" (Netflix)

An hour of a man arriving somewhere and telling everybody what happened to him, in the two tenses of this lesson. Nothing sad in it, and the whole episode is one long piece of storytelling in easy American English.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on and write down five sentences with was or were plus an -ing word.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "7 tips to make holiday travel less stressful"

Real people telling short stories of nights that went wrong, and what they did about them. It is the same shape as the story you read in class: the scene, the thing that happened, and how it ended.

Tip: the full transcript is on the same page. Find every was or were plus -ing in the first five minutes.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Andrew Stanton — "The clues to a great story" (TED)

Nineteen minutes from the man who wrote Toy Story, on why a story needs a scene before it needs an event. It is the same three-part shape you practised today, explained by somebody whose job is exactly that.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Then retell one of his examples out loud, in your own words, in four sentences.
