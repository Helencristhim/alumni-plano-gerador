The Hundred-Foot Journey (2014) — official trailer

An Indian family opens a restaurant in a small French village, right across the street from a very proud French chef. Two cultures, two sets of unwritten rules, and a lot of advice nobody asked for.

Tip: watch the trailer, then say three rules of each restaurant with have to and mustn’t.
