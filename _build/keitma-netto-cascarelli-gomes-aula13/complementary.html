English with Alex (engVid) — Work English: 12 phrasal verbs for meetings

A clear lesson on the two-part verbs people use in real meetings — several of them are today's verbs: bring up, go over, point out. Alex shows each one inside a meeting sentence, not in a list.

Tip: pause after each verb and say one sentence about your own close with it. If the object is it or them, check where you put it.

A short episode built around a simulated staff meeting. Ten phrasal verbs are hidden in the conversation, and Luke explains each one after the meeting is over.

Melissa Marshall — Talk nerdy to me (TED)

Five minutes on how specialists lose their audience when they speak like a report. It is about scientists, but it is the same advice the regional director gave in today's listening: speak like a person.

Tip: after watching, take one formal sentence from your last closing report and say it out loud the way you would say it in the call.
