The Product Page in English -- From Feature to Benefit

Your own product page, read like a buyer: what the product has, what the customer gains, and adjectives in the right order. Key words: a feature, a benefit, out-of-the-box, seamless, intuitive, customizable, a dashboard, cloud-based, reliable, robust. Structure: adjective order and noun collocations in product copy.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A feature -- something a product has or does
"Automatic matching is our most popular feature."
A benefit -- what the customer gains from something a product does
"The benefit is a close in four days, not nine."
Out-of-the-box -- ready to use immediately, with no extra work
"The SAP connection works out-of-the-box."
Seamless -- smooth, with no visible breaks or problems
"The customer wants a seamless integration with SAP."
Intuitive -- easy to understand and use without training
"The dashboard is so intuitive that new users learn it in an afternoon."
Customizable -- that you can change to fit your own needs
"The approval rules are fully customizable."
A dashboard -- one screen that shows the most important numbers
"The dashboard shows every invoice that is waiting."
Cloud-based -- running on the internet, not on your own computers
"It is a cloud-based tool, so there is nothing to install."
Reliable -- that works well every time, so you can trust it
"Finance teams need a reliable tool at month-end."
Robust -- strong, and difficult to break under pressure
"The platform is robust enough for two million invoices a year."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

A product page has two kinds of sentences. A feature says what the product has: Invent Match has a pre-built SAP connector. A benefit says what changes for the customer: you go live in days, not months. The easiest way to connect them is the word so: it has a pre-built connector, so you go live in days.

When you put two or three adjectives before a noun, English has a fixed order. Opinion comes first, then size, then age, and the type or purpose stays next to the noun: a simple new cloud-based tool, a reliable SAP connector. Adjectives never take a plural s: three new features, not three news features.

Product adjectives also have their favorite nouns. We say a seamless integration, an intuitive dashboard, a robust platform and customizable rules. A seamless dashboard sounds strange to a native speaker, even if the meaning is clear.

Finally, be careful with empty adjectives. Innovative, world-class and next-generation appear on every vendor's page, so they say nothing. One good adjective in the right place is better than four, and a number, like two million invoices a year, is better than any adjective.

1. Which sentence is a benefit?
A "It has a pre-built SAP connector and an approval engine for every invoice type."
B "It is cloud-based."
C "You go live in days, not months."
2. Which order is correct?
A "a cloud-based simple new tool"
B "a simple new cloud-based tool"
C "a new cloud-based simple tool, made for finance teams"
3. Why does the text say a number is better than an adjective like innovative?
A Because a number can be checked, and innovative appears on every vendor's page.
B Because adjectives are not allowed on product pages.
C Because buyers do not read the text on product pages at all.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Adjective Order and Product Collocations

GRAMMAR

Opinion first, type next to the noun, and every feature followed by so.

PositionKind of wordExample
1opinionsimple, intuitive, reliable
2sizesmall, large
3agenew, old
4 (next to the noun)type or purposecloud-based, SAP, finance
Full phraseopinion + age + type + nouna simple new cloud-based tool
Collocationsadjective + its usual nounseamless integration · intuitive dashboard · robust platform
Feature to benefitfeature + so + benefitIt is cloud-based, so there is nothing to install.

Never: a tool simple · a cloud-based new platform · three news features · a seamless dashboard

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer a real speaker would give.

A partner asks what is special about the product. The best answer is:
A "It is an innovative, powerful, world-class, next-generation smart solution."
B "It is a tool very good."
C "It has a pre-built SAP connector, so your customers go live in days."
You want to describe the dashboard correctly. You say:
A "It is a seamless dashboard for all the finance team people."
B "It is an intuitive dashboard."
C "It is a dashboard intuitive."
A buyer says every vendor claims to be reliable. You answer:
A "We process two million invoices a year with 99.9 per cent availability."
B "We are very, very reliable, I promise, much more reliable than all the other vendors."
C "Reliable is our main adjective."
You want to describe the new features. You say:
A "We have three news features this year, and all of them are very important ones."
B "We have three features news."
C "We have three new features this year."
