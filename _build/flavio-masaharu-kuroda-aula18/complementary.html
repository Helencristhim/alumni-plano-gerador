Lesson 18 -- The Product Page in English

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Ideas"

A short episode on presenting an idea so that people want to hear more. The same skill as a product page: say what is new, and then why it matters to the listener. Full script on the same page.

Tip: read the script and find one sentence that presents an idea. Rewrite it for your product with a feature, then so, then the benefit.

Listen on BBC Learning English ↗
Talk
Rory Sutherland -- "Life lessons from an ad man" (TED, 17 min)

An advertising executive explains why the way you describe a product changes its value in the customer's mind. Funny and full of examples of features turned into benefits.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down one example where he changes how people see a product without changing the product, and try the same with one feature of yours.

Watch on TED ↗
Documentary
Fyre Festival: Fiasco in the Caribbean (2019)

A festival sold with beautiful adjectives and almost no facts: luxury, exclusive, unforgettable. The documentary shows what happens when the page promises more than the product can deliver.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down three adjectives from the advertising, and for each one, ask what number or fact should have been behind it.
