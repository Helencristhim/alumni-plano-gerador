Lesson 18 -- The Deal in the News

Talk
Margrethe Vestager -- "The new age of corporate monopolies" (TED, 16 min)

The European Union's former competition commissioner explains, in clear and calm English, why antitrust exists and what regulators look at when two big companies want to become one. It is the other side of the takeover story in this lesson.

Tip: write down every compound noun she uses (competition law, market share, a price increase...) and say each one the long way.

Podcast
The Indicator from Planet Money -- "The spite acquisition that launched Warren Buffett"

A short, funny episode about the takeover that Buffett later called a terrible mistake: he bought a failing textile mill because its owner had annoyed him, and the name of that mill became Berkshire Hathaway. A takeover story told for the dinner table.

Tip: the page has a full transcript. Find five noun phrases with two or more words in front of the noun, and read each one from the right.

Podcast
Freshfields Essential Antitrust -- "Merger challenges in US courts" (28 min)

Antitrust lawyers from an international firm discuss why the US authorities lost a series of cases against mergers in court. Professional, precise English from the lawyer's side of the deal -- the conversation the lawyer in the second listening has every week.

Tip: listen once for the main argument, then a second time only for the noun phrases in front of "challenge", "review" and "remedy".
