The shape of a story

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Four sentences, one story. What do the orange words do?

"At first everything was normal. I took the bus."

"Then I opened my bag and I understood the problem."

"Suddenly the bus stopped and everybody got off."

"In the end she walked with me and I laughed."

Grammar Rule

Past Simple + Order Words

Part of the storyExample
Open itAt first everything was normal.
Move it (regular verb)Then I opened my bag.
Move it (irregular verb)I took the bus. I forgot my phone.
Turn itSuddenly the bus stopped.
Close itIn the end she walked with me.
Negative / questionI did not forget it. Did you forget it?

Key idea: every action of the story is in the past. After did and did not, the verb goes back to its base form: "I did not forget", never "I did not forgot".

Grammar Practice

Put It in the Past

"At first I _____took the subway to the museum." (take)
"Then a man _____started to play the guitar." (start)
"Suddenly I _____forgot the museum and the map." (forget)
"In the end I _____did not see the museum at all." (not see -- careful with the negative)

2. What happened suddenly on the bus?
The bus stopped and everybody got off, but he did not remember the name of his street.
3. Who helped him, and how?
An old woman. She asked if he was lost, laughed, and walked with him to his door.

MY PARIS JOURNAL

Gabriela Pires · Tuesday, day 6

At first I did not want to go alone.

I took the subway at ten o'clock.

I got off at the wrong station!

Suddenly: a man with a guitar.

Everybody stopped. I sat on the floor.

I forgot the museum. I forgot the map.

In the end I never saw the museum.

But I still remember the music.

Your line: "At first I was scared, but in the end it was the best day of the trip."

R

"So what is your best memory of Paris? Tell me what happened."

G

"At first I did not want to go to the museum alone. But that morning I took the subway anyway."

R

"And did you find the museum?"

G

"No! I got off at the wrong station. There were no tourists, and I was a little scared."

R

"That sounds bad. So what did you do?"

G

"Suddenly a man started to play the guitar. I sat on the floor and I forgot the museum. In the end I still remember that music, and I always laugh when I tell this."

The music of the man with the guitar -- and she laughs every time she tells the story.
