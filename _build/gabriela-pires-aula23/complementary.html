Series
Stranger Things -- teenagers telling each other what happened

A group of teenagers live a strange adventure, and in every episode they tell each other what happened. It is one long story in the past.

Tip: after one episode, tell the story in English in five sentences. Use "At first", "Then", "Suddenly" and "In the end".

BBC 6 Minute English -- What makes a good story?

Six minutes about why some stories are good and how people tell them. Free transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen and write down every past verb you hear (was, went, happened, told). Then use four of them to tell your own story.

A simple BBC lesson, five minutes long, that shows you how to talk about the past with the past simple.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause and say what you did last weekend. Use "remember", "forget" and "funny" if you can.
