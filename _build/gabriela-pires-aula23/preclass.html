Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. Toque em Listen para ouvir. / Listen to each word and read the example.

At first -- at the beginning, before something changed
"At first everything was normal." (No começo / a princípio)
Suddenly -- very fast and with no warning, when you do not expect it
"Suddenly the bus stopped." (De repente)
In the end -- finally, after everything happened
"In the end I never saw the museum." (No fim / no final)
Happen -- to take place; an event arrives, often without a plan
"Something wonderful happened that morning." (Acontecer)
Forget -- to lose something from your mind, or to leave a thing behind
"I forgot my phone at home." (Esquecer)
Remember -- to keep something in your mind and bring it back later
"I remember the music and the faces." (Lembrar)
Scared -- feeling afraid, because something is dangerous or strange
"I was really scared for two minutes." (Com medo / assustado)
Laugh -- to make a happy sound because something is funny
"She looked at me and she laughed." (Rir)
Funny -- making people laugh; amusing
"It was funny and a little embarrassing." (Engraçado)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto e responda às perguntas. / Read the text and answer the questions.

At first everything was normal on Ryan's first day in Sao Paulo. He took the bus and he sat next to the window. Then he opened his bag and he understood the problem: he forgot his phone at home. Suddenly the bus stopped and everybody got off, but he did not remember the name of his street. He was really scared for two minutes. Then an old woman asked, "Are you lost?" He answered in terrible Portuguese and she laughed. In the end she walked with him for ten minutes and she took him to his door. It was funny and a little embarrassing, and he thinks about what happened every time he takes the bus.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Past Simple and the Order of a Story

GRAMMAR

Numa história, TODA ação vai no passado simples: verbo regular ganha -ed (stop → stopped), verbo irregular tem forma própria (take → took, forget → forgot). Depois de did e did not, o verbo VOLTA ao infinitivo: "I did not forget", nunca "I did not forgot". / In a story every action goes in the past simple, and after did the verb goes back to its base form.

Part / ParteStructure / EstruturaExample / Exemplo
Open / abrirAt first + pastAt first everything was normal.
Regular verbverb + -edI opened my bag. The bus stopped.
Irregular verbown formI took the bus. I forgot my phone.
Turn / viradaSuddenly + pastSuddenly the bus stopped.
Close / fecharIn the end + pastIn the end she walked with me.
Negative / questiondid (not) + base formI did not forget. Did you see it?

Key point / Ponto-chave: o passado aparece UMA vez por frase. Se você ja usou did, o verbo principal fica no infinitivo: "Did you see?" e "I did not forget". E lembre: em inglês algo acontece to você ("What happened to you?"), nunca "with you".

Stage 2: Put the Story Steps in Order

Order

Ouça primeiro, depois coloque os passos da história na ordem certa. / Listen first, then put the steps in the correct order.

?Next, you say what you did and you say, I took the subway and I got off at the wrong station.
?Finally, you close the story and you say, in the end I never saw the museum, but I remember the music.
?First, you set the scene and you say, at first everything was normal.
?Then, you say how you felt and you say, I was scared, but then I laughed.
