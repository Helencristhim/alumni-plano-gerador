TED Talk · Language
John McWhorter: Txtng is killing language. JK!!!

A linguist explains that spoken English and written English are two different systems, and that people have always mixed them up. It is the argument behind tonight's whole lesson: the long formal verb belongs to the page, the two-word verb belongs to the mouth, and neither one is a mistake.

Tip: turn on English subtitles and mark every phrasal verb he uses while speaking. He talks fast, which is exactly the speed of a real panel.
