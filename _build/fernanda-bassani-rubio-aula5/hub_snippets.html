Small Talk Before the Meeting -- The Weekend and the Trip

The two minutes before the agenda: the weekend, the trip, the flight. Key words: small talk, a trip, a flight, jet lag, a day off, a long weekend, to get back, to have a great time. Structure: past simple with irregular verbs and questions with did.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Small talk -- a short, friendly chat about easy topics
"We always have two minutes of small talk before the meeting."
A trip -- a visit to another place, for work or for fun
"I went on a trip to Florida with my son."
A flight -- a trip on a plane
"The flight was long, but my son slept."
Jet lag -- feeling tired after a long flight to a different time zone
"I had jet lag for three days after the flight."
A day off -- a day when you do not work
"I took a day off on Friday."
A long weekend -- a weekend with an extra day off
"It was a long weekend, so we had four days."
To get back -- to return from a place
"We got back on Tuesday night."
To have a great time -- to enjoy something very much
"We had a great time at the park."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

On Monday morning, Fernanda joined a call with Daniel. Before the meeting, they had two minutes of small talk. “How was your long weekend?” Daniel asked. “Great!” said Fernanda. “I took a day off on Friday, and I went on a trip with my son.”

“Did you have a good flight?” “Yes. It was long, but my son slept. We had a great time, and we got back on Tuesday.”

Then Fernanda asked: “And you? Did you go anywhere?” Daniel flew to London last month, and he had jet lag for a week.

1. Why did Fernanda have four days off?
A Because she took a day off on Friday.
B Because she was sick.
C Because the meeting was cancelled.
2. When did Fernanda and her son get back?
A On Friday.
B On Tuesday.
C On Sunday.
3. Which question is correct?
A “Did you went anywhere?”
B “Did you go anywhere?”
C “You did go anywhere?”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Past Simple -- Irregular Verbs and Did

GRAMMAR

Some verbs have their own past form: go-went, have-had, take-took, get-got. In questions and negatives, use did or didn’t + the simple verb.

JobFormExample
Irregular verbsa new form, no -edgo → went · have → had · take → took · get → got
