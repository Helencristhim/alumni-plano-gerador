Exactly tonight's grammar, in six minutes: for for a period of time, since for the moment it started, and the question "How long have you...?". Two clear voices, slow examples, and a repetition drill at the end.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause after every example and say the same sentence about your own life — how long you have lived in your neighborhood, how long you have been married, how long you have studied English.

Why We Travel — full episode (free, 27 minutes)

After a lifetime on the road, Rick Steves looks back and asks what travel is actually for. It is the same thing you did tonight, on a bigger scale: measuring a long road and deciding what the next trip is for. Slow, clear American English, with subtitles.

Tip: watch it in two halves, on two different days. After each half, say one sentence out loud: "I've been traveling to Europe since 2019, and what I want next is..." Then keep going for thirty more seconds.
