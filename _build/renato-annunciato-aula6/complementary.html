Lesson 6 -- The Performance Conversation

Documentary
Inside Bill's Brain: Decoding Bill Gates (O Codigo Bill Gates) -- Part 1

Three hours of one person explaining how he judges work and people. Watch what he does before any decision: he says what has been going well, names one problem, and asks what changes. That is the same three-part conversation you built today, only about a project instead of a consultant.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time somebody describes an activity that is still going on, write the sentence down. You will collect six or seven present perfect continuous forms in one hour, without trying.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart -- Give It to Me Straight: The Power of Honest, Constructive Feedback

A free episode from the Stanford business school on the exact problem of today's class: how to say the difficult sentence once, in plain words, without spending the next minute taking it back. Around thirty minutes, clear American English, with a transcript on the page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript, even if you lose parts of it. Then open the transcript and listen again. The gap between the two is the muscle we are training.

Listen on Stanford GSB ↗
YouTube
The Secret to Giving Great Feedback (The Way We Work, a TED series)

Five minutes, and it gives you a sentence pattern you can use tomorrow with any of your seven consultants. It is the shortest useful thing on this list, and it solves the problem you will feel in the role-play: how to describe a behaviour without describing a person.

Tip: watch once with subtitles, then once without. Then record yourself giving the same feedback to a real person on your team, using her pattern.
