Chapter 1: First Words

So, What Do You Do?

Your job is less about drawing and more about product: the logic behind the screens, the team, the people you need to convince. Most people outside design do not know that. In English, you explain it in one minute.

In two or three sentences: what do you do, and what is your team working on these days?

Today’s Goal

Three Missions

1

Ten words to describe a product design job.

2

Separate your routine from the projects of the moment.

3

Answer “so, what do you do?” in one minute, and ask the same question back.

The target of the day: I lead a team of six designers is your routine; at the moment we are rolling out a new feature is a project of now. English shows the difference with the verb.

One Question

Routine, or Right Now?

Two sentences about your work, out loud, right now.

One that is true every week. One about something you are doing this month. Do not think about grammar yet: just say them.

Chapter 3: The Code

What You Do, or What You Are Doing?

Two present tenses for one job

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Show?

"I lead a team of six designers."

"We design the credit card features of the app."

"At the moment, we are testing a new flow."

"This month, I am writing the texts for an award."

Look at the blue verbs. In the first two, the verb is simple: lead, design. That is your job every week. In the last two, look at the red words first: at the moment, this month. The verb has two parts: are testing, am writing. That is a project of now, and it will end.

JobFormExample
Routine, facts, your jobpresent simpleI lead a team. She works with data.
A project of now, temporaryam / is / are + verb-ingWe are testing a new flow.
Words for routineevery day, usually, always, on MondaysI usually meet the team on Mondays.
Words for nownow, at the moment, this month, these daysThese days I am preparing a presentation.
Verbs of stateknow, need, want, like: simple onlyI need more time. Not I am needing.
The traphe / she / it + -s in the simpleNot she work. Say she works.

In one line: simple for the job and the routine; am / is / are + -ing for the projects of now.

Chapter 4: After the Talk

After the Talk

Dialogue

After the Talk

D
Thank you for coming to the talk. Do you work here at the bank?
J
Yes, I do. I’m a product designer. I lead a team of six designers.
D
Nice to meet you. I’m Daniel. I run a design team at a payments company in Chicago. What do you design here?
J
We design the credit card features of the app. I’m in charge of the logic behind the screens, not only the screens.
D
So you think about the whole flow. That’s the hard part. And you work with a lot of stakeholders, I imagine.
J
A lot! The tech lead, the product team, the business heads. We need to align every week.
D
Same in my company. These days we’re testing AI tools in every project, and everybody has an opinion. What are you working on at the moment?
J
At the moment we’re rolling out a new feature for our users, and the deadline is next month.
D
Good luck with that. I’m in São Paulo until Friday for a design conference. Let’s keep in touch.
J
Sure. It was great to meet you, Daniel.
