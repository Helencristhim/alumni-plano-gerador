Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
Kevin Hale — “How to Pitch Your Startup” (Y Combinator)

Thirty-two minutes on the only thing this lesson asked you to do: say what your company is, in one sentence, to somebody who has never heard of it. He tears apart real founder introductions and rebuilds them. The English is fast, American and unscripted — which is the point.

Tip: write down his one-sentence test, then run the Dataside sentence you built today through it. If it fails, rewrite it before the next lesson.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
Celeste Headlee — “10 ways to have a better conversation” (TED)

Eleven minutes on the habit you said costs you meetings: going quiet instead of saying you did not follow. She is direct about what to do instead, and every one of her ten moves is a sentence you can copy.

Tip: turn English subtitles on the first time and off the second. Write down the two moves you already do, and the one you never do.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
In Good Company — Arvind Krishna, CEO of IBM

A CEO of a data and AI company answering the same questions you answered today — what the company does, how it makes money, what he is looking for — at full native speed, with no simplification for a non-native listener.

Tip: the first twelve minutes are enough. Listen once with no notes, then write the three sentences he uses to describe IBM. Compare them with yours.
