"Have to and must" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the exact pair you practiced today, including the trap: "must not" and "don't have to" mean opposite things. Connection to Lesson 6: this is the difference between "you mustn't bring food" and "you don't have to bring food".

Tip: after each example, say one rule from your own building or your own office out loud. One sentence, then play again.

"Embrace the shake" -- Phil Hansen, TED (10 minutes)

An artist who lost the steady hand he needed for his own technique explains what happened when he stopped fighting the limitation and started working inside it. Ten minutes, very clear American English. Connection to Lesson 6: it is a talk about rules you cannot break and what you can still do inside them — which is your week with the brand guidelines.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then say out loud, in one minute, three limitations in your own work: one you must accept, one you should accept, and one everybody assumes is a rule and is not.
