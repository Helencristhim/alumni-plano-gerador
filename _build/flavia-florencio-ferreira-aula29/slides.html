Chapter 3: The Future Code

Plan, Calendar, Decision

going to · -ing · will

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"We are going to show the delegation two coffee farms."

"We are meeting the producers on Tuesday at nine."

"The road is closed? Okay, I will call the other farm."

"Don’t worry. I’ll send you the itinerary by Friday."

What does each orange form do? A plan, something already in the calendar, or a decision right now?

FunctionFormExample
A plan or intentionam / is / are going to + verbWe are going to show them two farms.
Already arranged, with a timeam / is / are + verb-ingWe are meeting the producers on Tuesday.
A decision right nowwill + verbOkay, I will call the other farm.
An offer or a promiseI’ll + verbI’ll send you the itinerary.
QuestionsAre you going to...? / When are they arriving?When are they arriving?

A plan: going to. In the calendar: -ing. A decision or an offer now: will.

Chapter 4: The Planning Call

The Week in March

Dialogue

The Planning Call

G
Flavia, the delegation is confirmed for March. What are you going to show us?
F
We are going to visit two coffee farms and a cheese producer. I have arranged everything for Tuesday and Wednesday.
G
Perfect. And when are we meeting the producers?
F
We are meeting them on Tuesday at nine, at the Sebrae office. That is the venue for the workshop too.
G
One problem. Our director cannot come on Monday morning.
F
No problem. I will reschedule the welcome meeting to Monday afternoon. We have a free slot at three.
G
Great. Can you send me the plan? I am preparing a briefing for the group.
F
Of course. I’ll send you the itinerary by Friday.
