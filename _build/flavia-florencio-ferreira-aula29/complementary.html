Lesson 29 -- Planning the Next Mission

TED Talk
Tim Urban: Inside the mind of a master procrastinator

A very funny talk about plans that we do not follow, and deadlines that arrive too fast. Easy to follow, with drawings on the screen.

Tip: after the talk, say one thing you are going to do this week, and one thing that is already in your calendar.

TED Talk
Tim Harford: How frustration can make us more creative

An economist shows that when plans go wrong, people often find better solutions. It is Plan B, the idea of the second listening, with great stories.

Tip: after the talk, tell one story from your work when a plan changed on the day, and say what you decided with will.

TED Talk
Laura Vanderkam: How to gain control of your free time

A short talk about planning a week. She uses a lot of future forms to describe plans and priorities.

Tip: plan your next week out loud in English: two plans, two arrangements in the calendar, one offer to a colleague.
