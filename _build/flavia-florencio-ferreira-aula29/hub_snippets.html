Planning the Next Mission -- Plans, Arrangements and Decisions

How to plan a mission in English: what you are going to do, what is already arranged, and what you decide on the spot. Key words: itinerary, venue, slot, to arrange, to postpone, to reschedule, layover, reminder, briefing, on short notice. Structure: going to for plans, the present continuous for arrangements in the calendar, and will for decisions and offers.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example.

Itinerary -- a plan of a trip, with places, days and times
"I will send you the itinerary by Friday."
Venue -- the place where an event or a meeting happens
"The venue for the workshop is the Sebrae office."
Slot -- a short period of time that is free in a schedule
"We have a slot on Tuesday at two."
To arrange -- to plan and organize something with other people
"I have arranged a visit to two farms."
To postpone -- to move an event to a later time
"We are going to postpone the visit."
To reschedule -- to change the time or the day of a meeting
"Can we reschedule the call to Monday?"
Layover -- a stop in another city during a long flight
"They have a layover in Lisbon."
Reminder -- a message that helps you not forget something
"I will send a reminder one day before."
Briefing -- a short meeting or text with the important information
"Giorgio is preparing a briefing for the group."
On short notice -- with very little time before something happens
"The director can only meet on short notice."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

The Italian delegation is coming in March, and Flavia is planning the week. “We are going to visit two coffee farms,” she tells Giorgio. This is her plan: going to + verb.

Some things are already in the calendar, with a day and a time: “We are meeting the producers on Tuesday at nine.” For a fixed arrangement, she uses the present continuous: are + verb-ing.

Then Giorgio has a problem: the director cannot come on Monday morning. Flavia decides on the spot: “No problem. I will reschedule the meeting to the afternoon. And I’ll send you the itinerary by Friday.” For a decision now, or an offer, she uses will.

"We are meeting the producers on Tuesday at nine." What does this mean?
A Flavia is meeting the producers right now, at this moment, in her office at Sebrae.
B The meeting is already arranged, with a day and a time.
C It is only an idea.
Why does Flavia say "I will reschedule the meeting"?
A Because she decides it at that moment.
B Because she planned this change many weeks ago, before Giorgio called her about the director.
C Because it is in the calendar.
Which sentence is a plan?
A "I’ll send you the itinerary by Friday, and a reminder one day before each visit."
B "Okay, I will call the hotel."
C "We are going to visit two coffee farms."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Plan, Calendar or Decision?

GRAMMAR

Three futures, three jobs: going to for a plan, -ing for the calendar, will for a decision or an offer.

FunctionFormExample
A plan or intentionam / is / are going to + verbWe are going to visit two farms.
Already arrangedam / is / are + verb-ingWe are meeting the producers on Tuesday.
A decision right nowwill + verbOkay, I will call the other farm.
An offer or a promiseI’ll + verbI’ll send you the itinerary.
QuestionsWhen are you + -ing? / Are you going to...?When are they arriving?

Never: We going to visit · I will to call · I will meet them on Tuesday (it is in the calendar) · Okay, I book another hotel

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer that a real speaker would give.

Giorgio asks about your plan for March. You say:
A "We are going to visit two coffee farms and a cheese producer."
B "We will to visit two coffee farms and one cheese producer in the region during the week."
C "We visit farms."
The meeting with the producers is in the calendar for Tuesday at nine. You say:
A "We meet the producers on Tuesday, I think, maybe at nine or at ten in the morning."
B "We are meeting the producers on Tuesday at nine."
C "We going to meet them."
Giorgio says the hotel is full. You decide now. You say:
A "Okay, I am going to book another hotel tomorrow, after I check the prices of the hotels near the venue."
B "Okay, I book another hotel."
C "Okay, I will book another hotel."
Giorgio needs the itinerary. You offer to send it. You say:
A "I am sending it to you, maybe, if I have time this week, after all the other meetings."
B "I'll send it to you by Friday."
C "I send it."
