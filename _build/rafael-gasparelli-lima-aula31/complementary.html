Lesson 31 -- Legal Meetings: Due Diligence

Series
Suits

A legal drama in a big New York law firm. Many scenes are meetings about mergers, contracts and documents, and the lawyers ask for things in very formal, very polite English, even when they are angry.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time a lawyer asks for a document, pause and say the request again with Could you or Would you be able to.

TED Talk
Margaret Heffernan: Dare to disagree

A businesswoman explains why good organizations want people to disclose problems early instead of hiding them. Clear English and a calm pace.

Tip: after the talk, say one problem you would disclose early in a deal, and how you would say it formally.

TED Talk
Rachel Botsman: We’ve stopped trusting institutions and started trusting strangers

A talk about trust in business and technology. Useful for this lesson, because due diligence is about trust: what you check and what you believe.

Tip: write down three words she uses about trust and use each one in a formal sentence about a deal.
