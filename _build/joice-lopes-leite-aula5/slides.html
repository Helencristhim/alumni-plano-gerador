Chapter 3: The Code

In, On, At

inside · on a surface · at a point

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"My session is in room four."

"Registration is on the first floor."

"I am at the conference."

"The desk is next to the entrance."

WordIdeaExample
ininside a closed spacein room four · in the building
onon a surface or a levelon the first floor · on the table
atat a point, an address, an eventat the conference · at the desk
next to / nearvery close · closenext to the entrance · near the window
betweenwith one thing on each sidebetween the elevator and the coffee area

Small rule that solves most of it: in a room, on a floor, at an event.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"The room is in the second floor."
"The room is on the second floor."
"I am in the conference today."
"I am at the conference today."
"Where is the room four?"
"Where is room four?"

A room with a number is like a name: room four, gate twelve — no the in front.

Chapter 4: At the Conference

Fifteen Minutes to the Session

and one person in a blue shirt

Dialogue

Excuse Me, Where Is Room Four?

J
Excuse me, can you help me? I am looking for my session.
S
Of course. What is the number of the room?
J
Room four. My session is at two o'clock.
S
Room four is on the third floor, between the elevator and the coffee area.
J
Sorry, can you repeat, please? Slowly.
S
Third floor. The elevator is there, near the stairs.
J
Thank you. And where is the registration desk?
S
On the first floor, next to the main entrance. You take your badge there.
J
Perfect. Is the coffee in this building too?
S
Yes, on the third floor, near your room. Have a good conference.
