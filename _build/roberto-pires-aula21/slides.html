Chapter 3: Too or Enough

Too Big or Big Enough

Two small words that change everything

Discover the Code

Listen and Find the Pattern

"This jacket is too big for me."

"These jeans are too tight."

"The sleeves aren't long enough."

"Is this shirt big enough for you?"

FormExampleMeaning
too + adjectiveIt's too big.more than you want — a problem
adjective + enoughIt's big enough.the right amount — no problem
not + adjective + enoughIt isn't long enough.less than you need — a problem
Do you have + noun?Do you have this in a large?ask the store for another option

too comes before the adjective. enough comes after it: "too big", "big enough". Never say "big too" or "enough big".

Dialogue

At the Store

S
Good afternoon. Can I help you find anything?
R
Yes, please. I need a jacket for the evening. Do you have this one in a large?
S
Here you are, on the hanger. The fitting room is on your right.
R
Thank you. Hmm. It's too big, and the sleeves are too long. It's very loose here.
S
I see. Let me bring you a medium. It's a little more tight on the arms.
R
This one is perfect. It isn't too tight, and the sleeves are long enough. Is it cotton?
S
Yes, one hundred percent cotton, and completely plain. That dark blue really suits you.

Self-Assessment

What I Can Do Now

I can use the words of a clothes store (fitting room, sleeve, hanger, cotton).
