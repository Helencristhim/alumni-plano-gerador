Lesson 04 -- From the Plant to the Port

Talk
Rose George -- "Inside the secret shipping industry" (TEDGlobal)

Fourteen minutes on the ninety percent of world trade that travels by sea and that almost nobody thinks about. Containers, ports, crews and the invisible chain behind every product on a shelf — the exact world your own export order lives in.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on and count how many passive sentences she uses in the first five minutes. It is the natural register of this whole industry.

Podcast
NPR How I Built This -- "Warby Parker: Dave Gilboa & Neil Blumenthal"

Two people who had to build a chain before they had a company: find a factory, control quality at a distance, and get a product across a border on time. Natural American English, and the whole transcript is on the page if you want to read while you listen.

Tip: good for the car. Listen for every moment something goes wrong in the chain, and note how they describe it — in English, almost always without naming a person.

Series
How It's Made (Discovery)

Five-minute segments, each one following a product from raw material to finished pallet. The narration is slow, clear American English and it is written almost entirely in the structure of this lesson: the pieces are cut, the box is sealed, the pallet is loaded.

Tip: one segment is enough. Watch with subtitles, then describe your own product the same way, in six sentences.
