Lesson 04 -- Pre-class

From the Plant to the Port -- The Chain Behind One Export Order

Forty pallets have to leave a factory in Brazil and arrive in a warehouse in Ohio. Key words: a shipment, a container, customs, a pallet, a delay, a warehouse, a freight forwarder, to clear customs. Structure: the passive voice -- what was done, not who did it.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A shipment -- a quantity of goods sent together, at one time, to one place
"The shipment left the plant on a Tuesday."
A container -- the large metal box that goods travel in, on a ship
"Two containers were loaded in Santos on Friday."
Customs -- the government office that checks goods entering a country
"Customs opened one pallet and closed it again."
A pallet -- the wooden base that boxes are stacked on, so a machine can lift them
"Forty pallets were prepared, and thirty-eight were shipped."
A delay -- the time between when something should arrive and when it does
"The delay was nine days, and six of them were at the border."
A warehouse -- a large building where goods wait before they are delivered
"The goods were delivered to the wrong warehouse."
A freight forwarder -- a company that organizes the transport of your goods for you
"Our freight forwarder was changed in March."
To clear customs -- to get official permission for goods to enter a country
"Nothing clears customs without the right invoice."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Forty pallets were prepared at the plant on the third of May. Two containers were loaded in Santos on the fifth, and the ship left on schedule. Eighteen days later the shipment arrived at the port, exactly on the promised date.

Then the goods were held at customs for six days. The reason is printed on the release form: the product code was missing on the invoice. Nothing is cleared at that border without a code on every line. The container was not opened, nothing was inspected, and no product was rejected. A document was incomplete, and the shipment waited.

The customer received the goods nine days late. Two more days were lost after the release, because a truck was only assigned two days later, and the first delivery was made to the wrong warehouse. The transport is organized by our freight forwarder, and the invoice template was changed in March, when that forwarder was changed too. The template was corrected last week, and every invoice is checked against the order before a container is loaded.

1. "The goods were held at customs for six days." Who held them?
A The freight forwarder held them.
B The sentence does not say. That is the point of this structure: what was done matters, who did it does not.
C The customer held them.
2. "The transport is organized by our freight forwarder." What is the word by doing here?
A Putting the person back into the sentence, because here it matters who does it.
B Showing where the transport happens.
C Showing when the transport happens.
3. "Nothing is cleared without a code" and "the goods were held". Why is one is and the other were?
A Because nothing is singular and goods is plural, and that is the only reason.
B Because one is a question and the other is not.
C Because the first is a permanent rule, now, and the second is one finished event in the past.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Passive Voice

GRAMMAR

The structure every logistics report is written in. It says what was done and leaves out who did it, on purpose.

UseFormExample
Now, or alwaysam / is / are + past participleNothing is shipped without the invoice.
Finished, in the pastwas / were + past participleThe shipment was held at customs.
Naming who did it+ by + person or companyThe transport is organized by our forwarder.
Negativewas not / is not + participleTwo pallets were not loaded.
QuestionWas / Were + subject + participle?Was the order delivered on time?
Useful participlesirregular, learned one by onehold → held · send → sent · make → made · lose → lost

Never: The shipment was hold · The goods delivered late · Two containers was loaded · The order was delivered on time? (for a question, put Was first) · It was happened.
