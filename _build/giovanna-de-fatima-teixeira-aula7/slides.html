Chapter 3: It Was, It Happened

Two Small Changes

is → was  |  dance → danced

Grammar Discovery

Which Ones Are Finished?

Today I am tired.
On Sunday I was tired.
The movies are long.
Last night the movies were long.

Two sentences are about today. Two are finished. What changed?

NowFinishedExample
I am · he is · it isI was · he was · it wasThe party was great.
you are · we are · they areyou were · we were · they wereThe movies were long.
NegativeI was not at home. · They were not tired.
QuestionWas it good? · Were they good? · How was your weekend?
Time wordsyesterday · last night · last weekend · on Saturday · two hours ago
Grammar Discovery

Same Two Letters, Three Sounds

We danced until three.
They arrived at eleven.
She invited me last week.
I watched two movies.

They all end with the same two letters. Do they all sound the same?

SoundWhenVerbs
/t/after k, p, f, s, sh, chdanced · watched · stopped · talked · finished
/d/after most other soundsarrived · called · stayed · listened · played
/id/after t or d — one extra syllableinvited · started · needed · visited · waited
Spellingdance → danced · invite → invited · stop → stopped · study → studied
CarefulThe form never changes with the person: I danced, she danced, they danced.

Chapter 4: The Gate at Seven

Somebody Sees Your Face

and asks the question everybody asks on a Monday

Dialogue

Monday, Seven O'Clock

G
Morning, Giovanna. You look tired. How was your weekend?
Gi
It was great, but it was short. I danced until three on Saturday.
G
Three in the morning. A party?
Gi
A friend invited me last week. There was music, there was food, and there were forty people in a small apartment.
G
And Sunday? Was it quiet?
Gi
Very quiet. I was tired all day. I watched two movies and I cooked lunch for my family.
G
Two movies. Were they good?
Gi
The first one was great. The second one was boring, and I stopped it after twenty minutes.
G
My weekend was the opposite. I worked on Saturday, and on Sunday I was here, at the site, with Ray.
Gi
On a Sunday? Why?
G
The client called on Friday night and we needed the report on Monday. So we started at seven and we finished at six.
Gi
Then you are the tired one, not me. Last night I was in bed at nine.
Comprehension

About Greg

1. Where was Greg on Sunday, and who was with him?
At the site, with Ray.
2. Why was Greg at the site on the weekend?
The client called on Friday night and they needed the report on Monday.
3. What time did Greg and Ray start, and what time did they finish?
They started at seven and they finished at six.

Sixty Seconds, Your Real Weekend

Sixty seconds, a voice note: your last weekend, from Friday night to last night. Four verbs with -ed, and three sentences with was or were.

No keywords. No stopping. The weekend is finished, so every verb is finished too.

Self-Assessment

What I Can Do

I know nine words for the weekend: last night, yesterday, weekend, party, dance, tired, boring, great, invite.
I can use was and were: "the party was great", "the movies were long".
I add -ed to a regular verb, and the form never changes with the person.
I hear the difference between danced, arrived and invited.
I told somebody about my weekend in English, and it was finished, not happening now.
