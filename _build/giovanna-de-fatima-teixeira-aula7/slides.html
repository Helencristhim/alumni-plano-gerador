Chapter 3: The Pattern

A Fixed Place

before the verb · after "am"

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

I always wake up at five thirty.
She usually works in the office.
I never arrive late.
I am always tired on Friday.

Where does the frequency word go? And what is different in the last one?

VerbPositionExemplo
normal verbBEFORE the verbI always wake up early.
am / is / areAFTER the verbI am always tired on Friday.
how many timesat the ENDHe visits the tunnel twice a week.
CuidadoEm português a palavra anda livre ("eu acordo sempre cedo"). Em inglês ela tem lugar fixo: "I wake up always" soa errado. E never ja e negativo: "I never work on Saturday", nunca "I do not never work".

Chapter 4: One Week

The Monday Briefing

what happens always, and what never happens

Dialogue

How Often Does It Happen?

P
Good morning. The safety meeting is on Monday, as always.
G
Good morning, Paulo. How often does the engineer visit the tunnel?
P
Twice a week. Usually on Tuesday and on Thursday.
G
Perfect. I always work in the office on Tuesday, so I am here.
P
Good. And we sometimes work on Saturday, but never on Sunday.
G
I never work on Sunday. Sunday is for my boyfriend and for movies.
P
Very good. One more thing: the report is always ready on Friday morning.
G
How often do we send it to London?
P
Once a month. But I often send a short message every Friday.
G
Understood. I sometimes stop for a coffee, but I never arrive late.
Comprehension

About the Week

1. How often does the engineer visit the tunnel?
Twice a week, usually on Tuesday and on Thursday.
2. When does the team never work?
On Sunday. They sometimes work on Saturday.
3. How often does Paulo send the report to London?
Once a month, but he often sends a short message every Friday.
