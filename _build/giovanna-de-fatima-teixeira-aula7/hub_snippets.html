Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Three of them are about the days, three about the party, and three about how it felt.

Weekend -- the two days at the end of the week: Saturday and Sunday
"The weekend was short this time."
Yesterday -- the day before today
"Yesterday I was at home all day."
Last night -- the night before today -- never say "yesterday night"
"Last night I was in bed at nine."
Party -- friends together with music, food and dancing
"The party started at ten."
Invite -- to ask somebody to come to a party or a dinner
"A friend invited me last week."
Dance -- to move your body to the music
"We danced until three in the morning."
Great -- very good
"The weekend was great."
Tired -- with no energy, because you worked a lot or slept little
"I was very tired on Sunday."
Boring -- not interesting -- it makes you want to stop
"The second movie was boring."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the text out loud, then answer the questions below. Read slowly -- nobody is timing you.

It is Monday morning at the gate, and I am tired. Greg looks at my face and asks how my weekend was. It was great, but it was short. On Thursday a friend invited me to a party, and I answered the same night: yes. The party started at ten on Saturday. There was music, there was food, and there were forty people in a small apartment. We danced until three in the morning. On Sunday I was very tired, so I stayed at home. I watched two movies: the first one was great, the second one was boring, and I stopped it after twenty minutes. Last night I was in bed at nine. And yesterday is finished now, so every verb here is finished too.

1. The text says "The party was great" and "There were forty people". Why is one was and the other were?
A Because the first one is positive and the second one is negative.
B Because "the party" is one thing and "forty people" is more than one.
C Because the first one is the past and the second one is the present.
2. Which three words in the text tell you that everything is finished?
A Monday, morning, gate
B on Saturday, last night, yesterday
C always, usually, never
3. The verbs danced, invited and stopped all end with -ed. What is different about invited?
A The -ed is one extra syllable, because the verb ends with t.
B It is not a past form; it is the present.
C It changes when the person changes.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Was, Were and the -ed Ending

Grammar

Two changes take a sentence from today to yesterday: the verb be becomes was or were, and a regular verb takes -ed.

NowFinishedExample
I am · he is · it isI was · he was · it wasThe party was great.
you are · we are · they areyou were · we were · they wereThe movies were long.
NegativeI was not at home. · They were not tired.
QuestionWas it good? · Were they good? · How was your weekend?
Time wordsyesterday · last night · last weekend · on Saturday · two hours ago
Sound of -edWhenVerbs
/t/after k, p, f, s, sh, chdanced · watched · stopped · talked · finished
/d/after most other soundsarrived · called · stayed · listened · answered
/id/after t or d -- one extra syllableinvited · started · needed · visited · waited
Spellingdance → danced · invite → invited · stop → stopped · study → studied
CarefulThe past form never changes with the person: I danced, she danced, they danced.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the best answer for each situation. Wrong? Try again -- nobody is watching.

It is Monday at the gate and somebody asks about your weekend. You say:
A "It is great, but it is short."
B "It was great, but it was short."
C "It were great, but it were short."
You want to say that forty people came to the party. You say:
A "There was forty people at the party."
B "There were forty people at the party."
C "There are forty people at the party last night."
A colleague asks when the invitation came. You say:
A "A friend invited me last week."
B "A friend invite me last week."
C "A friend was invite me last week."
You stopped a movie in the middle because it was not interesting. You say:
A "I am boring, so I stopped the movie."
B "The movie was boring, so I stopped it."
C "The movie was boring, so I stop it."

Stage 5: Free Production

Reflection

Record your answer. There is no right or wrong here, and nobody corrects anything.
