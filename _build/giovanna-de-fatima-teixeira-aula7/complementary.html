Extra English -- Episode 5: "A Star Is Born"

Twenty-five minutes of the sitcom made for English learners. Hector wants to be a pop star, and the four friends spend the whole episode telling each other what happened: the audition, the phone call, the party. The English subtitles are on the screen from the first second.

Tip: watch it once for the story. The second time, stop every time you hear a verb ending in -ed and say it out loud with the right ending sound.

Podcast
Lovely English Stories -- "Emma's Stately Adventure"

A short story for A2 and B1 listeners, read slowly. A story is the most natural home of the past tense: almost every verb you hear is either was, were, or a verb with -ed, one after the other, for the whole episode.

Tip: good for the commute, when you cannot read or write but you can listen. Play it once without stopping, then again and count how many times you hear "was".

YouTube
BBC Learning English -- "Pronouncing '-ed' Endings"

Three minutes on the exact problem of today's class: the same two letters at the end of a verb, and three different sounds coming out of them. The teacher says each group of verbs twice, slowly, so you can hear where the extra syllable appears and where it does not.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause after each group and repeat the verbs out loud, then add danced, invited and stayed to the right group yourself.
