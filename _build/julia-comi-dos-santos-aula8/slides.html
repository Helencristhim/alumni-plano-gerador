while goes before the long one. when goes before the short one.

"While I was watching the waves, my phone rang." = "I was watching the waves when my phone rang."

Listening 1

The day she missed the call

1. What was she doing when the recruiter called?
She was on the bus, with her headphones in, listening to music.
2. What was happening in the background?
Two people were arguing loudly right behind her.
3. What did she do about it?
She called back forty minutes later and explained — and they were fine about it.
