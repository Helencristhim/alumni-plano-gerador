Chapter 3: The Code

What Does He Look Like?

is · has · wears

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

What does the new coworker look like?
He is tall and slim.
She has long curly hair.
He has a beard, and he wears glasses.

What do the orange words have in common? When do we use IS, and when do we use HAS?

UseFormExample
QuestionWhat does he / she look like?What does Paulo look like?
Body: tall, short, slimhe / she is + adjectiveHe is tall. She is short and slim.
Hair, eyes, beardhe / she has + (adjective) + nounShe has curly hair. He has a beard.
Glasseshe / she wears glassesHe wears glasses.
Negativeis not / does not haveHe is not tall. He does not have a beard.

Key idea: body = IS (tall, short, slim). Hair, eyes and beard = HAS. Glasses = WEARS. Adjective BEFORE the noun: long curly hair, never "hair curly".

Chapter 4: The New Coworker

Who Is Paulo?

Marcos must find him at the reception

Dialogue

What Does He Look Like?

M
Dienane, the new coworker arrives at nine. I need to meet him at the reception. What does he look like?
D
His name is Paulo. He is tall and slim.
M
OK, tall and slim. And his hair?
D
He has short curly hair, and he has a beard.
M
A beard. Does he wear glasses?
D
Yes, he wears glasses. And he has a big smile!
M
Great, now I can find him. I will take him to your office at nine thirty.
D
Perfect. Thank you, Marcos!
Comprehension

About Marcos
