Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 4 -- What Is Happening Right Now

Series
Suits -- Season 1, Episode 1 (Pilot)

A whole episode of people walking into rooms and saying what is happening right now. The English is fast, which is the point: this is the speed you are training for. Watch ten minutes at a time, not the whole hour.

Tip: watch ten minutes with English subtitles, then the same ten minutes without. Count how much more you catch the second time.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
BBC Learning English -- English at Work

A short drama set inside a company: meetings, updates, problems and the exact phrases people use in them. Free, with the language explained after each scene.

Tip: listen to one episode twice. The second time, write down every sentence you hear with -ing.

Listen on BBC Learning English ↗
YouTube
Julian Treasure -- "How to speak so that people want to listen" (TED)

Ten minutes on how to be heard in a meeting -- speed, silence, tone. He speaks slowly and he demonstrates everything he says, so it is one of the easiest TED talks to follow by ear.

Tip: watch once without subtitles, then once with English subtitles. Try one of his tips in your next call.
