Chapter 3: The Code

Always, or Only Now?

Same verb. Two tenses. Two different meanings.

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I run the engagement survey every year."

"This month I am running it in the Italian offices too."

"She works in the Milan headquarters."

"She is working with us on the integration this quarter."

Two of these are always true. Two are only true this month. Which is which -- and what changes in the verb?

FormUseExample
am / is / are + verb-ingHappening now, or around now: this week, this month, this quarter.I am leading the integration workstream.
the verb to be is obligatoryThe -ing alone is not a tense. Never leave am/is/are out.I am working. (never I working)
negative: am / is / are + notThe not goes after to be.We are not hiring this quarter.
question: Are you + verb-ingInvert to be and the subject. No do here.Are you working on the merger?
state verbs stay simpleknow, need, want, understand, have (=possess) do not take -ing.I know the numbers. (never I am knowing)

The Structure

Status in Three Sentences

This is what a global director wants to hear when he asks: how is it going?

1 · What is moving

"Right now I am leading the people integration workstream."

2 · Where it is

"We are training the store managers this month, and it is going well."

3 · What is blocked

"We are not moving on the new job titles, because I am waiting for Milan."

Moving → where it is → what is blocked. The blocker is the part that makes the update professional.

How Is It Going?

M
Viviane, hello. Quick one before the board: how is the people integration going?
V
Hello, Marco. It is ongoing and it is going well. Right now I am leading the culture workstream.
M
And the store managers? Are you training them this month?
V
Yes, we are. Thirty-one of forty-one are already trained. We are finishing the last group next week.
M
Good. And the job titles? Are we closing them before the end of the quarter?
V
Sorry, could you repeat the last part, please?
M
Of course. The job titles. Are you closing them this quarter?
V
No, we are not. We are waiting for the model from headquarters. That is my only blocker right now.
M
Understood. I am sending you the model on Friday. Put it in the minutes as an action for me.
Comprehension

About Marco

1. What does Marco want to know before the board meeting?
How the people integration is going.
2. What does he ask about the job titles?
Whether they are closing them before the end of the quarter.
3. What does he agree to do at the end?
Send her the model on Friday — and it goes in the minutes as an action for him.
Listening

Three Minutes, Every Monday

A project lead gives his weekly stand-up update. Sound first -- no text.

0:000:00
1. How many things is he running in parallel this month?
Three: the training, the systems and the job titles.
2. Why is the team using two systems at the same time?
Because the new system is not ready. It is costing about six hours a week.
3. What is his only real blocker?
The job titles — they are waiting for the model from headquarters.
4. What is the good thing he says at the end?
People are asking many more questions, and in the meetings instead of the corridor.
Listening 2

What Are You Working On?

A recruiter explains why this question decides interviews. Sound first -- no text.

0:000:00
1. Why does she ask what you are working on right now?
To hear if you are close to the work or far from it.
2. What does she learn when a candidate answers with the job description?
Nothing.
3. Which two small things does she advise?
Use the continuous for right now, and say one thing that is not going well.
4. Which candidate does she call back?
The one who says what is blocked and what they are doing about it.
