TED -- Mariana Mazzucato, "Government -- investor, risk-taker, innovator" (15 min)

An economist argues that governments take many of the biggest risks in the economy, and asks who should get the rewards. A useful view for anybody who works between public contracts and private investors.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and note every sentence with if. Then say if you agree with her in two sentences.

BBC 6 Minute English -- "How do climate scientists make predictions?"

Six minutes on how scientists build scenarios about the future, with the language of possibility and prediction. The same thinking as a risk matrix, in a different field.

Tip: listen for sentences with if and will. Write down two and change them into sentences about a project.
