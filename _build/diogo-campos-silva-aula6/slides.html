Which Lesson Is Each Sentence From?

Five sentences, five lessons. Which tool does each sentence use?

LessonToolOn the ticket
1verb to be: am / is / areI am Diogo. The ticket is open.
2don’t / doesn’t, do / doesThe email doesn’t work. Does it open?
3can / can’tUsers can’t log in. Can you help me?
4imperative: verb / don’t + verbRestart the computer. Don’t close it.
5am / is / are + verb-ingI am sharing my screen. It is loading.

In one line: who you are, what doesn’t work, what users can’t do, the steps, and what is happening now.
