Lesson 21 -- Presenting with Confidence

Talk
Nancy Duarte -- "The secret structure of great talks" (TED)

A presentation designer shows the shape behind famous talks: where they start, how they move, and how they end. A perfect match for today's outline and signposts.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down one hook and one closing line that you like, and use them in your next pitch.

Talk
Julian Treasure -- "How to speak so that people want to listen" (TED)

A sound expert explains pace, pitch, volume and silence: the tools that make an audience listen. Very useful for the delivery of your sixty seconds.

Tip: try his vocal warm-up before you rehearse. Then record your pitch twice: once fast, once with a slower pace and a pause after the key number.

Watch on TED →
Talk
Chris Anderson -- "TED's secret to great public speaking" (TED)

The head of TED explains why the best talks build one single idea, step by step, in language the audience already understands.

Tip: write your pitch's one idea in one sentence, like "Better training means safer surgery." Then check that every part of your outline supports it.
