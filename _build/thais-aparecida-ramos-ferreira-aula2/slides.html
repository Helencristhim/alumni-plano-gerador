Chapter 3: The Code

am · is · are

one verb, three forms

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am a director."

"My company is Brazilian."

"My daughter is fifteen."

"My husband and my daughter are with me."

WhoFormExample
IamI am a director.
he · she · itisMy company is Brazilian.
you · we · theyareThey are with me.
my + nounmy name, my company, my daughter, my husband

One person: is. Two people: are. Me: always am.

Grammar

Say No, and Ask

PositiveNegativeQuestion
I am a director.I am not a student.Am I late?
She is fifteen.She is not sixteen.Is she fifteen?
They are in Paris.They are not in London.Are they in Paris?

"Yes, I am. / No, I am not."

"Yes, it is. / No, it is not."

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"I am work at CVC."

✓ RIGHT

"I work at CVC."

✗ WRONG

"You are a director?"

✓ RIGHT

"Are you a director?"

One sentence, one verb: am / is / are OR work / live / travel — never both together.
