Chapter 3: The Code

Said, Told, Asked

How the words change when you report them

Grammar Discovery

What Changes When You Report It?

Mark: “The brand will stay.” → "Mark said that the brand would stay."

Mark: “Nobody is losing their job.” → "He told the team that nobody was losing their job."

Director: “Will the bonus plan change?” → "He asked if the bonus plan would change."

Priya: “Please send me the org chart.” → "Priya asked me to send her the org chart."

Compare the original words with the report. The verb in red moves one step back: will becomes would, is becomes was. Told needs a person (told the team); said does not. A question becomes asked if, with normal word order. A request becomes asked + person + to + verb.

JobFormExample
Reporting a statementsaid (that) + the verb one step backMark said that the brand would stay.
With the person who heard ittold + person + (that)He told the team that nobody was losing their job.
The verb moves backis → was · are doing → were doing · will → would · can → couldShe said that IT could build a bridge.
Reporting a yes / no questionasked + if / whether + normal word orderHe asked if the plan would change.
Reporting a wh- questionasked + when / what / why + normal word orderShe asked when the payroll would move.
Reporting a requestasked + person + to + verbPriya asked me to send her the org chart.
The trapnever said + person; no question orderNot He said me, not She asked when would it move.

In one line: said that / told + person that + the verb one step back; asked if / asked when + normal order; asked me to + verb.

Chapter 4: The Debrief

Diana Missed the Kickoff

One call, two recordings, one written debrief

Dialogue

Reporting to Diana

D
Carlos, I missed the kickoff. What did Mark Ellison tell his team?
C
He told them that the brand would stay, and he reassured them that nobody was losing their job this year.
D
Good. Did anyone raise a concern?
C
Yes. The sales director asked if the bonus plan would change. Mark said that he did not know yet.
D
That is a fair question. And how was morale in the room?
C
Better than I expected. Priya, their head of HR, is now our point person, and she asked me to send her our org chart.
D
Fine. Is there anything I need to flag to the steering committee?
C
One risk. Priya said that their payroll system could not talk to ours. I will send you the full debrief tonight.
Comprehension

About Diana

1. Why does Diana ask Carlos about the kickoff?
Because she missed it.
2. What does Diana want to know about the people in the room?
How morale was.
3. What does Diana need to decide for the steering committee?
If there is anything she needs to flag to it.
