Chapter 3: Who You Are

Four Sentences, and People Know You

my name is · I am from · I live in · I have

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"My name is Patrícia."

"I am from Brazil."

"I live in São Paulo."

"I have two children."

PatternUseExample
My name is + nameYour nameMy name is Patrícia.
I am + word about youWhere you come from, your ageI am from Brazil. I am 45.
I live in + cityWhere your home isI live in São Paulo.
I have + thing or personWhat belongs to youI have two children.

With I: am, live, have. Never I is, never I has.

P
Sorry, I don't understand. Can you speak more slowly, please?
D
Of course. Are you alone, or with your family?
P
I am with my family. I have two children. They are very tired.
D
I understand. Your room is number 304, on the third floor. Breakfast is at seven.
P
Sorry, can you say that again, please? The room number?
D
Room three, zero, four. Here is your key. Do you need help with the bags?
P
Yes, please. Can you help me? Thank you very much. Good night.
Comprehension

What Did Daniel Say?

1. What is the room number?
Room 304, on the third floor.
2. What time is breakfast?
Breakfast is at seven.
3. What does Daniel offer at the end?
He offers help with the bags.
Listening

Dinner on the Plane

The flight attendant speaks to the cabin. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What are the two options for dinner?
Chicken with rice, or pasta with tomato.
2. When do you tell her about a food allergy?
Now, before the dinner service starts.
3. What does she do if you do not understand?
She says it again, more slowly. It is not a problem for her.
