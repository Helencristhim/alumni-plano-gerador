Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 1 -- Thirty Seconds on a Call

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Describing your job"

Five minutes on the exact question this lesson trained: somebody asks what you do, and the answer has to be short, clear and useful to a stranger. Two native speakers take it apart, phrase by phrase, at a speed you can follow, with the full script on the same page.

Tip: listen once without the script. Then read it and mark every verb in the simple form and every am/is/are + -ing. Bring one phrase you want to steal for your own answer.

Listen on BBC Learning English ↗
Talk
Simon Sinek -- "How great leaders inspire action" (TED, 18 min)

The most watched talk ever made about the thing you practised today: how a company explains what it does. Sinek's whole argument is that almost everybody answers the question in the wrong order, and he shows the right order in plain, slow English with very short sentences.

Tip: turn on the English subtitles and stop at minute six. Then say your own answer out loud in his order: the problem first, the product second.

Watch on TED ↗
Film
The Intern (2015) -- the first thirty minutes

A fast-growing company where everybody has to explain, over and over, what the business actually does and who it is for. The founder describes the company to visitors, to investors and to a new employee, and each time the answer is a little different. American English, office register, nothing technical.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down two sentences in which somebody says what the company does, and mark whether the verb is in the simple form or in am/is/are + -ing.
