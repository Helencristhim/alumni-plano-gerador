Chapter 3: The Pattern

Still True Today

for · since · how long

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

I have worked here for twelve years.
I have been the plant manager since 2020.
We have had a night shift for three years.
How long have you been the director?

Did these things stop, or do they continue today? What comes after FOR, and what comes after SINCE?

UseFormExample
Still true todayhave / has + past participleI have worked here for twelve years (and I still work here).
FORfor + a period of timefor three years · for ten days · for a decade
SINCEsince + the starting pointsince 2019 · since Monday · since January
QuestionHow long have you...?How long have you been the director? — Since 2019.
CarefulPortuguese uses the present ("trabalho aqui há doze anos"). English uses the present perfect: "I have worked here for twelve years", never "I work here since twelve years".
