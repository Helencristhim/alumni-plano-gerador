Grammar Discovery

Two words, one difference

I have surfed since I was eight.
I have surfed for seven years.
He has been in the club since 2019.
He has been in the club for six seasons.

Since points at a moment. For measures a length.

Both sentences of each pair mean the same thing. What changes is what comes after: since takes the starting point (2019, January, I was eight). for takes the duration (seven years, six seasons, ten minutes).

The Code

Point, or length

since + when it started

since 2019
since January
since last season
since I was eight
since I arrived

for + how long

for seven years
for six months
for two seasons
for ten minutes
for a long time

Test it in your head: can you count it? Then it is for. Can you point at it on a calendar? Then it is since.

Sound Check

Both of them are weak

I have surfed since I was eight.

the "since" is fast and unstressed

He has been in the club for six seasons.

the "for" almost disappears before the number

Since when? For how long?

in a question they get all the stress instead

Listening 1

Nineteen years of coaching

1. How long has the coach been at that club, and how long has he known Renato?
Nineteen years at the club, and he has known Renato since he was nine.
2. What does he tell parents?
That nothing happens in the first two years.
3. Why does he disagree with people who say it happened suddenly?
Because it has been happening slowly since Renato was nine, and nobody was watching.
Listening 2

Seven years as teammates

1. How long have they been teammates, and since when?
About seven years, since primary school.
2. What does she say he was like at the start?
He was not the best one. There were four of them and he was the slowest.
3. What has he not done since she met him?
He has not missed a single Saturday, not even the week his leash broke.
