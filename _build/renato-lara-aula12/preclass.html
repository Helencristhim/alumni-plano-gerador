Stage 1.1: Milestone Words

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Eight words, two minutes.

Milestone — an important moment that you always remember
"My first podium was the biggest milestone."
Podium — the three places at the top: first, second and third
"He has been on the podium four times."
Club — a group of people who do the same sport together
"I have been in the same club since I was nine."
Trophy — the object you take home when you win
"The trophy is still on the shelf."
Coach — the person who trains you and plans your season
"My coach has known me for six years."
Junior — the category for young athletes, under eighteen
"He has been in the junior category since last year."
Season — the months of the year when the competitions happen
"The season starts in March."
Teammate — somebody who is in the same club or team as you
"My teammate has been surfing longer than me."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the timeline. Then answer the questions.

I have been surfing since I was eight. I have been in the same club since I was nine, so my coach has known me for six years. My first competition was at twelve and I finished last. At fourteen I was on the podium for the first time, and the trophy has been on the shelf for eighteen months. This season I have been in the junior category, and I have been training three mornings a week since January.
How long has his coach known him?
A For six years.
B Since he was twelve.
What happened at twelve, and what happened at fourteen?
A He was on the podium at twelve and finished last at fourteen.
B He finished last at twelve and was on the podium at fourteen.
Why is it "for eighteen months" and not "since eighteen months"?
A Because eighteen months is a length, not a starting point.
B Because the trophy is an object.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Since and for — point or length

Both go with the present perfect. What changes is what comes after them.

since + when it started

since 2019
since January
since last season
since I was eight
since I arrived

for + how long

for seven years
for six months
for two seasons
for ten minutes
for a long time

The test: can you count it? Then it is for. Can you point at it on a calendar? Then it is since.
Careful: "I have the same coach since 2019" is broken. With since or for you need the present perfect: "I have had the same coach since 2019".
The questions: How long have you...? → answer with for or since. Since when...? → answer with since.

Stage 4: At the club

Quiz
You started surfing at eight and you still surf. Which one is correct?
A I have been surfing since eight years.
B I have been surfing since I was eight.
Seven years of the same club. How do you say it?
A I have been in the same club for seven years.
B I have been in the same club since seven years.
You want to know the starting point, not the length.
A How long have you been here?
B Since when have you been here?
Same coach since 2019. Which sentence is correct?
A I have the same coach since 2019.
B I have had the same coach since 2019.
