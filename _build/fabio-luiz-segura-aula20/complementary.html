Talk
Matt Cutts — “Try something new for 30 days” (TED)

A three-minute talk about small challenges that turn into habits. It is a simple way to think about your stretch goal for the next block: one thing, every day, for a month.

Tip: choose one thirty-day challenge for your English, for example one recording a day. Say it out loud with I'm going to.

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart (Stanford GSB) — “From Dreaming to Doing: How We Set and Achieve Goals”

An episode about motivation and about how people turn a goal into daily action. Useful before you write your stretch goal for the next block.

Tip: listen for one idea about making a goal specific. Then rewrite your stretch goal using it.

Video
Carol Dweck — “The power of believing that you can improve” (TED, YouTube)

A Stanford psychologist explains the difference between "not yet" and "failed", and how that small change affects how people learn. It is the right frame for a plateau.

Tip: write one sentence about your English that starts with I'm not used to it yet, and one that starts with I'm getting used to.
