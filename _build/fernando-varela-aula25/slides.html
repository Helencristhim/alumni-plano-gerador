Chapter 3: The Code

Maybe Yes, Maybe No

might · may · might not · may not

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Orange Words Say?

"The board might approve a small pilot first."

"They may decide in March."

"Some families might not want to leave their city."

"The apartments may not be cheap."

Look at the orange words. Is the speaker sure? What form is the verb after them? Where does not go?

FormUseExample
might / may + verbIt is possible, but you are not sure. The same for all people.The board might approve a pilot.
might not / may not + verbIt is possible that it does not happen.Some families might not come.
will vs mightWill = you are sure. Might = maybe.We will send the proposal. They might say yes.
QuestionTo ask, we usually say: Do you think + subject + will / might...?Do you think the board will say yes?
The trapAfter might and may, the verb is ALWAYS the base form, with no to and no -s: it might cost — never it might costs or it might to cost.

Will is a promise. Might is a maybe. A good leader says which one is which.

Chapter 4: The Monday Call

A Pilot for the Families

Sarah calls from Boston. The board has not decided yet.

Dialogue

The Housing Pilot

S
So, Fernando, what did the board say about the apartment program?
F
They liked it, but they are still uncertain. They might approve a small pilot first.
S
A pilot makes sense. When do you think they will decide?
F
It depends on the budget. They may decide in March, but it's not certain.
S
And what is the biggest risk for you?
F
The cost. The apartments might cost more than we assume.
S
In Texas, some families didn't want to come. Will all your families want to come?
F
Probably not. Some families might not want to leave their city for a month.
S
OK. So what is the worst case?
F
The board says no this year. But I think it's likely they will say yes. A no is unlikely.
Comprehension

About Sarah

1. What does Sarah think about a pilot?
She thinks a pilot makes sense.
2. What happened with some families in Texas, says Sarah?
Some families didn't want to come.
3. What is Sarah's last question?
She asks what the worst case is.
