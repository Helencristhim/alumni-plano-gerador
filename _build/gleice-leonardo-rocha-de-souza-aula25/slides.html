A promise, a rule, a request or advice?

ToolUseExample
will + verbA promise or a prediction (Lesson 21)I will visit thirty chains.
have to / don’t have to + verbA rule / not necessary (Lesson 22)The distributor has to deliver every week.
would like (to) / Would you...?A polite wish or request (Lesson 23)Would you confirm the agreement?
should / shouldn’t + verbAdvice (Lesson 24)We should meet again in three months.

In one line: after will, should and would you: verb with no to. After have to and would like: to + verb.

Chapter 4 -- The Partner Meeting

Closing the Deal

One meeting, two recordings, one summary

Dialogue

Gleice and Thomas

T
Good morning, Gleice. Thank you for coming. The board liked your proposal very much.
G
Good morning, Thomas. I am very happy to hear that. I will visit thirty pharmacy chains in the first year.
T
Great. Who is your distributor? We need one in Sao Paulo.
G
It is LogiFarma. They have to deliver every week, and they have twenty trucks.
T
Perfect. You don’t have to pay for the samples. We will send them from Boston.
G
Thank you. Would you confirm the commission? Is it still eight percent?
T
Yes, eight percent. I think we should sign the agreement in July.
G
I agree. And we should meet again in three months. I will send you a follow-up email today.
Comprehension

About Thomas

1. What did the board think of the proposal, for Thomas?
They liked it very much.
2. What will NatureCare send from Boston?
The samples.
3. When does Thomas think they should sign the agreement?
In July.
