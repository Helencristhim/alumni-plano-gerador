Checkpoint 5 -- The Partner Meeting: Will, Have To, Would and Should

Review of the Entrepreneur block (lessons 21-24) before your final meeting with Thomas Grant. Use will for promises (I will visit thirty chains), have to for rules (The distributor has to deliver), Would you...? for polite requests (Would you confirm the agreement?) and should for advice (We should meet again). Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Review eight words from the Entrepreneur block: two from each lesson. Tap Listen to hear each one.

Partner -- a person or company that works with you on a business project
"NatureCare can be a great partner for me." (parceiro)
Investment -- money or time that you put in a project to get more later
"The first year is a big investment." (investimento)
Distributor -- a company that takes products from the brand to the stores
"My distributor will deliver to thirty chains." (distribuidor)
Agreement -- a business decision that two companies sign together
"We will sign the agreement in July." (acordo)
Proposal -- a written plan or offer that you send to someone for a decision
"The board read my proposal." (proposta)
Confirm -- to say that something is true or that it is decided
"Would you confirm the date of the launch?" (confirmar)
Trust -- the feeling that a person is honest and you can believe them
"Trust grows with small, regular contact." (confiança)
Follow-up -- a message you send after a meeting to continue the contact
"I will send a follow-up email today." (retorno)

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Your Toolbox (Review)

GRAMMAR

Read the explanation and the examples.

Revisão do bloco 5 -- quatro ferramentas, nenhuma nova. will + verbo = promessa ou previsão (I will visit = Eu vou visitar). have to / has to + verbo = regra, obrigação; don't have to = não precisa (não é proibido!). would like (to) e Would you + verbo? = desejo e pedido educado (Would you confirm? = Você poderia confirmar?). should / shouldn't + verbo = conselho (We should meet = Nós deveríamos nos reunir). Depois de will, should e would you: verbo SEM to. Depois de have to e would like: to + verbo. / Four tools from the block: will for promises, have to for rules, Would you...? for polite requests, should for advice.

Use / UsoStructure / EstruturaExample / Exemplo
Promise / Promessawill + verbI will visit thirty chains. (Eu vou visitar trinta redes.)
Rule / Regrahave to / has to + verbThe distributor has to deliver every week. (O distribuidor tem que entregar toda semana.)
Not necessary / Não precisadon't have to + verbYou don't have to bring samples. (Você não precisa trazer amostras.)
Polite request / Pedido educadoWould you + verb...?Would you confirm the agreement? (Você poderia confirmar o acordo?)
Advice / Conselhoshould / shouldn't + verbWe should meet again in three months. (Nós deveríamos nos reunir de novo em três meses.)
