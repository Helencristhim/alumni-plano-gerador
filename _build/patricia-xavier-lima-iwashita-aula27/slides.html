Chapter 3: Language Focus

It Must Be... It Can't Be...

modals of deduction — must / might / could / can't + be

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"It must be the new region. It only fails since Monday."

"It might be the token, but I am not sure."

"It could be a timeout on their side."

"It can't be the users. Nobody is online at night."

How sure is she in each sentence? Put the green words in order, from almost sure YES to almost sure NO.

StructureUseExample
must + beAlmost sure it is true: you have a reasonIt must be the new region.
might / could + bePossible: you are not sureIt might be the token.
can't + beAlmost sure it is NOT trueIt can't be the users.
must be + -ingSomething happening nowIt must be running right now.
might not + bePossibly not trueIt might not be the server.

After the modal comes the base verb — never to, never -s: not "must to be", not "might is". The opposite of must here is can't, never mustn't (mustn't means it is not allowed).

The Three Sentences of Troubleshooting

Three Sentences, Three Levels of Sure

"It can't be the server, because the status page is green."

1 — what you rule out: can't + a reason

"It might be the token, but we need to check."

2 — what you suspect: might + a next step

"It must be the extra data from the new region."

3 — what the evidence says: must

Chapter 4: The Problem Call

Third Time This Week

Daniel asks — and nobody knows the answer yet

Dialogue

Let Me Think Out Loud

D
Patrícia, our nightly sync stopped again. It is intermittent, but this is the third time this week.
P
Let me think out loud with you. If it only fails at night, it can't be the users. Nobody is online then.
D
Could it be your server? It has been slow lately.
P
It might be, but I doubt it. The status page was green all night, so I think we can rule out the server.
D
Then what is it? We need the orders in the system by seven.
P
The error message says timeout. You added a new region last week, didn't you? The file must be much bigger now.
D
Yes, on Monday. So it must be the extra data from the new region.
P
That is my hypothesis. I will ask engineering to test it today, and I will send you the evidence before five.

Free Practice

Ninety Seconds, One Real Problem

Ninety seconds: choose one real problem from your accounts — a sync, an integration, a report — and think out loud as if you were on the call: the symptoms, what you rule out, your hypotheses, and the next step.

No keywords. One rule: every must, might or can't comes with a reason.
