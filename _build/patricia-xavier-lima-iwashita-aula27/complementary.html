Lesson 27 -- What Could Be Causing It?

Film
Apollo 13 (1995) -- the mission control scenes

Three astronauts are two hundred thousand miles from Earth when something explodes, and nobody on the ground knows what it was. The engineers in Houston do exactly what you did today: they look at the symptoms, rule out what is impossible, test one hypothesis at a time, and never say must before they have the evidence. Connection to Lesson 27: the most famous problem call in history, and a masterclass in staying in control before the answer exists.

Tip: watch the mission control scenes with English subtitles. Write down 5 sentences where somebody is sure, not sure or sure it is NOT something, and rewrite each one with must, might or can't.

"How to use modal verbs of deduction" -- English In A Minute, BBC Learning English

One minute, one clear explanation: must when you are almost sure, might and could when you are not sure, can't when you are almost sure something is not true. Connection to Lesson 27: the fastest review of the grammar behind every sentence you said to Daniel.
