Lesson 26 -- Soul, Football and Culture

Documentary
Summer of Soul (2021)

The forgotten 1969 Harlem Cultural Festival: Stevie Wonder, Nina Simone, Mahalia Jackson and a crowd that lived and breathed every song. The people who were there describe it with exactly the passion of this lesson.

Tip: choose one performance and describe it out loud with one normal and one extreme adjective, and one sentence with so ... that.

TED Talk
Benjamin Zander: The transformative power of classical music

A conductor whose enthusiasm is absolutely infectious explains why music strikes a chord with almost everybody. A masterclass in talking about what you love, and in saying why.

Tip: write down every intensifier and every extreme adjective he uses. Then use three of them to talk about one soul record.

Documentary
Diego Maradona (2019)

Asif Kapadia's film about Maradona's years in Naples, built from real footage. Die-hard fans, electric stadiums and a city that was over the moon: football as passion, told in English.

Tip: after the film, describe the atmosphere in Naples in three sentences, with such a ... and so ... that.
