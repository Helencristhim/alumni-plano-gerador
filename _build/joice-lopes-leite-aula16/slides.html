Chapter 3: The Code

Two Words for the Whole Past

was · were

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I was a school director for twenty-five years."

"It was a small public school."

"There were only twelve teachers."

"The sessions were all in English."

WhoPast of to beExample
I / he / she / itwasI was a director. It was difficult.
you / we / theywereThe sessions were in English.
negativewasn't / weren'tIt was not easy. They were not there.
questionWas / Were + person?Was your first school in São Paulo?
there is / there arethere was / there wereThere were four hundred students.

Short answers: Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't. / Yes, they were.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I were a school director for twenty-five years."
"I was a school director for twenty-five years."
"Last year I am part of the Meteoro Program."
"Last year I was part of the Meteoro Program."
"That was ago twenty-five years."
"That was twenty-five years ago."

In English, ago comes AFTER the time: twenty-five years ago, two weeks ago.
