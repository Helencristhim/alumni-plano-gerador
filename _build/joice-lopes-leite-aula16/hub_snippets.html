What Happened Before? -- Sua história com was e were

As seis palavras que colocam você no passado (yesterday, last, ago, before, career, experience) e o passado do verbo to be: was e were. Duas palavras dão conta de vinte e cinco anos. Sem pressa e sem cobrança.

Etapa 1.1: Cartões de Vocabulário

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. Toque em Listen para ouvir quantas vezes quiser. (Listen = Ouvir)

Yesterday -- ontem
"The meeting was yesterday." (A reunião foi ontem.)
Last -- passado (last week = semana passada)
"Last year she was part of the program." (Ano passado ela fez parte do programa.)
Ago -- atrás (dois anos atrás)
"That was twenty-five years ago." (Isso foi vinte e cinco anos atrás.)
Before -- antes
"Before this, she was a school director." (Antes disso, ela era diretora de escola.)
Career -- carreira
"It was the best school of her career." (Foi a melhor escola da carreira dela.)
Experience -- experiência
"She has a lot of experience with public schools." (Ela tem muita experiência com escolas públicas.)

Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Leia o texto em voz alta e depois responda as perguntas. (Leia devagar -- ninguém está contando o tempo.)

Before this work, I was a school director for twenty-five years. My first school was a small public school in the east of São Paulo. There were four hundred students and only twelve teachers. It was difficult, and it was also the best school of my career. That was many years ago. In 2023 I was at the INSTI conference in the United States: the sessions were all in English, and it was my first international experience. Last year I was part of the Meteoro Program.

1. Por que "I was" e "the sessions were"? (Why the difference?)
A Porque was vai com I, he, she e it; were vai com you, we e they. (Depende do sujeito.)
B Porque "sessions" é uma palavra longa. (Por causa do tamanho.)
C Porque uma frase é pergunta. (Uma é pergunta.)
2. Onde entra a palavra "ago" na frase? (Where does "ago" go?)
A Antes do tempo: "ago twenty-five years". (Antes.)
B Depois do tempo: "twenty-five years ago". (Depois.)
C No começo da frase. (No começo.)
3. Como era a primeira escola dela? (What was her first school like?)
A Grande e particular. (Big and private.)
B Pequena e pública, com quatrocentos alunos. (Small and public.)
C Uma universidade nos Estados Unidos. (A university in the US.)

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- was e were -- o passado do verbo to be

Grammar

Duas palavras cobrem todo o passado do verbo to be. / Two words cover the whole past of the verb to be.

Sujeito / WhoPassadoExemplo / Example
I / he / she / itwasI was a director. (Eu era diretora.)
you / we / theywereThe sessions were in English. (As sessões eram em inglês.)
Negativawasn't / weren'tIt wasn't easy. (Não foi fácil.)
PerguntaWas / Were + sujeito?Was your first school in São Paulo? (Sua primeira escola era em SP?)
Ideia-chave / Key ideaO "there is/there are" também tem passado: there was (singular) e there were (plural). E o ago vem DEPOIS do tempo, ao contrário do português: twenty-five years ago. / There was/were, and ago goes after the time.

Etapa 4: Quiz Situacional

Quiz

Escolha a melhor resposta para cada situação. Errou? Tente de novo -- ninguém está vendo.

Perguntaram o que você fazia antes. Você diz: (What were you before?)
A "I were a school director."
B "I was a school director for twenty-five years."
C "I am school director before."
Você quer dizer que havia doze professores. Você diz: (Twelve teachers.)
A "There was twelve teachers."
B "There were twelve teachers."
C "There have twelve teachers."
Você quer dizer "vinte e cinco anos atrás". Você diz: (Twenty-five years ago.)
A "Ago twenty-five years."
B "Twenty-five years ago."
C "Before twenty-five years."
