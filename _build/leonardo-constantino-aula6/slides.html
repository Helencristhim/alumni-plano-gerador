Chapter 3: The Code

The Passive Voice

is filed · was approved · has been revised

The Artifact

Official Communique

Normative Instruction No. 2,187

Circulated to clients by ALMA · Financial Institutions Desk · Leonardo Constantino

1. The reporting framework was enacted on 18 December and is applied in three stages.

2. Quarterly returns are filed through a single federal portal.

3. The previous state-by-state requirement has been superseded.

4. No transition period was granted.

5. The instruction is enforced by the federal tax authority.

Five sentences. Only one of them says who acted.

Which line names the agent? Why do the other four leave it out?

Grammar Discovery

Read and Notice

"Tax returns are filed quarterly through a single portal."

"The regulation was approved by Congress in December."

"The framework has been revised three times since January."

"Fintechs are now required to disclose beneficial ownership."

In every sentence the subject receives the action instead of doing it. What are the two parts that never change?

FormUseExample
Present simple passive
am / is / are + past participle		A routine, a standing rule, the way things work now.Quarterly returns are filed through the portal.
Past simple passive
was / were + past participle		One finished event, at a known moment in the past.The regulation was approved in December.
Present perfect passive
has / have been + past participle		A finished change with a result today, when the date does not matter.The framework has been revised three times.
The agentAdd by + agent only when the agent is news: "was approved by Congress". Drop it when it is obvious, unknown or not the point: "the deadline was moved".
Negative and questionThe be carries both: "The decree has not been published." / "Has the credit been phased out?"
RegisterDescribe the regulation in the passive. Describe the action your client must take in the active: "Your team files the return by the twentieth."

Two parts, always: a form of be and a past participle. Everything else is tense.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"The regulation was approve by Congress."
"The regulation was approved by Congress."
"Tax returns filed quarterly through the portal."
"Tax returns are filed quarterly through the portal."
"The framework has revised three times."
"The framework has been revised three times."

A passive sentence always carries two pieces: a form of be and a past participle. Lose either one and the sentence changes meaning or stops working.

Detail

Keep the Agent or Drop It?

by + agent only when the agent is news

"The appeal was rejected ___by the Supreme Court — KEEP: which court decided changes everything."
"The deadline was moved to March 31 ___DROP: nobody cares which office moved it."
"The framework is enforced ___by the federal tax authority — KEEP: the client needs to know who will knock."
"The documents have been reviewed ___DROP in a bulletin, but KEEP in a memo: your client has to know who reviewed them."

Listening 2

Passive Is a Decision

A senior partner explains where the passive helps and where it hides. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. Why does he say the passive is a decision, not decoration?
Writing "a penalty was imposed" is choosing not to name who imposed it.
2. Where does the passive become a real problem?
In the last paragraph of a memo: nobody knows who is supposed to act on Monday.
3. What is the rule he uses?
Describe the regulation in the passive; describe the action in the active.
