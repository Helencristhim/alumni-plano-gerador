Film
The Laundromat (2019) -- Steven Soderbergh

The Panama Papers told as a story: shell companies, offshore structures and the moment regulators finally act. Connection to Lesson 6: the narrators explain almost every mechanism in the passive, because the whole point is that nobody is named.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down five passive sentences about money or law. For each one, decide whether the agent was dropped because it is obvious, unknown, or deliberately hidden.

Podcast
Planet Money -- "How Ralph Nader inspired a consumer movement and an anti-regulation backlash"

The story of how rules get written, enforced and later attacked in the United States: agencies created, standards imposed, decisions overturned. Connection to Lesson 6: it is thirty minutes of exactly the verbs you studied today, used by native speakers at natural speed.

Tip: read the transcript on the same page after listening once. Highlight every passive construction and mark the three tenses you learned: is enforced, was enacted, has been revised.
