Chapter 3: Finished or Still Running

For, Since, and the Line Between

The period that is over, and the period that is not

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I have been at this company since 2016."

"I joined the company in 2016."

"I have run four areas for five years."

"I ran the maintenance team for two years, and then I stopped."

FormUse it forExample
have / has + participle + forA period that is still going. for + how long it has lasted.I have worked here for ten years.
have / has + participle + sinceA period that is still going. since + the point where it started.I have been a director since 2021.
Past simpleA period that is over. It has an end, so it takes a date.I ran that team for two years, and then I left.
for or sincefor + a length (six months, ten years). since + a start (March, 2016, the merger, I joined).for six months · since March
Never the present simpleA period that started in the past cannot sit in the present simple. I am here since 2016 is wrong in every register.I have been here since 2016.

for and since describe the same period from two ends. What decides the tense is not which word you use, it is whether the period has finished.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I am at this company since 2016."
"I have been at this company since 2016."
"I work in operations for ten years."
"I have worked in operations for ten years."
"I have joined the company in March 2016."
"I joined the company in March 2016."

If the period is still open, English uses have or has plus the participle, never the present. And a fixed date closes the period, so it goes back to the past simple.

Chapter 4: Telling It to a Stranger

Dialogue

Two Directors, One Coffee

B
So, Felipe, you have the whole supply chain. How long have you been in that role?
F
Since 2021. I joined the company in 2016 and I took over the fourth area three years ago.
B
That is fast. What did you do before?
F
I studied law and I worked in a law firm for two years. I did not enjoy it, so I moved to operations.
B
A lawyer in a distribution center. I have not heard that one before.
F
It helps more than people expect. I have read every supplier contract we have signed since I joined.
B
I believe you. And the scope, has it changed since 2021?
F
It has. I had two areas then. I have had four since the merger, and the headcount has tripled.
B
We have been through a merger too. What has been the hardest part for you?
F
The first year. I have never worked so many Saturdays, and I have not repeated that since.
B
Good answer. Most people say the systems. It is never the systems.
F
It is never the systems. And you? How long have you been in Rotterdam?
B
Twenty-two years, in three companies. I have not left the city since I started.
