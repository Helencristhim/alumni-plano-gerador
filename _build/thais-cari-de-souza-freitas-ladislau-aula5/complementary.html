Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
WorkLife with Adam Grant -- "Is it Safe to Speak Up at Work?"

Thirty-five minutes on the exact move you practised today: bringing the bad news forward instead of burying it at the end. One of the guests puts it in a single line — bad news does not get better with time.

Tip: the full transcript is on the same page. Listen once, then read the first ten minutes and mark every sentence in the continuous.

Talk
Ricardo Semler -- "How to run a company with (almost) no rules" (TED)

A Brazilian executive explaining his own company in English, in front of a room that has never heard of it. Half the talk is in the present simple (what the company always does) and half in the continuous (what they are trying right now) — the two tenses of this lesson, side by side, for twenty minutes.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Write down five sentences of his about a routine and five about something in progress.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Data Science in Minutes -- "What is A/B Testing?"

A few minutes on the idea behind the third recording of this lesson: give the new rule to a small group, keep the old one for a second group, and compare. Short, slow and entirely in the present, which is why it is here.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause and say each idea back in your own words, using we are testing, we are comparing, we are watching.
