Chapter 3: Three Keys

Who You Are

my name is · I am from · I live in · I work at

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"My name is Thaís."

"I am from Brazil."

"I live in São Paulo."

"I work at CVC."

PatternUseExample
My name is + nameYour nameMy name is Thaís.
I am from + countryWhere you come fromI am from Brazil.
I live in + cityWhere your home isI live in São Paulo.
I work at + companyYour jobI work at CVC.

With I: am, live, work. No extra -s.

The Three Keys

Three Phrases That Open Paris

"I need a taxi."

Key 1 — when you want something: a taxi, water, help, a map

"Where is the metro?"

Key 2 — when you are lost: the metro, the hotel, the bathroom

"Can you help me?"

Key 3 — when nothing else works, this one always does

Chapter 4: At the Hotel

Night One in Paris

the first real conversation

Dialogue

Good Evening, Welcome to the Hotel

M
Good evening. Welcome to the hotel. Can I help you?
T
Yes, please. My name is Thaís Ferreira.
M
Nice to meet you. Where are you from?
T
I am from Brazil. I live in São Paulo, a very big city.
M
Very good. Is this your first time in Paris?
T
Yes. This trip is for my daughter. She is fifteen today.
M
Happy birthday to her. Do you need anything tonight?
T
I need a map, please. And where is the metro?
M
It is very close. Two minutes from the door, on the left.
T
Thank you. Can you help me with the bags?
