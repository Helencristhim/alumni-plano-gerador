Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 6 -- How Does She Compare

Series
Emily in Paris -- Season 1, Episode 2

Two ways of working, side by side, in the same office: the American way and the French way. Every scene is a comparison, and the characters say it out loud instead of thinking it.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down five comparisons you hear (better, faster, more expensive...).

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
WorkLife with Adam Grant -- any episode

An organisational psychologist comparing how different companies treat people, always with research behind it. It is your job, in audio, with an American accent at a normal speed.

Tip: listen at 0.75x the first time. Do not try to understand everything -- catch the main idea and three comparisons.

Listen on Spotify ↗
YouTube
Margaret Heffernan -- "Forget the pecking order at work" (TED)

Fifteen minutes comparing two ways of building a team, with a famous experiment about chickens. She speaks slowly and repeats her key sentences, which makes her one of the easiest speakers to follow.

Tip: watch once without subtitles, then once with English subtitles. Note every comparative she uses.
