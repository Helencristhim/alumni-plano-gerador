Chapter 3: The -ED Code

Two Letters, and the Action Is Over

wait → waited · ask → asked · call → called

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"We landed at six forty."

"I waited for forty minutes."

"Then I asked at the counter."

"They delivered the bag last night."

NowBeforeExample
wait · ask · call+ edI waited · I asked · I called
arrive · deliver · land+ d after e · + edit arrived · they delivered · we landed
I · you · he · she · we · theyThe SAME form for everybody: I waited, she waited, they waited. No S, no change.
Remember lesson 9?Now: he works, she lives. Before: he worked, she lived — the S is gone. The past is easier than the present.

One ending for every person. These are the regular verbs — the irregular ones (went, had) come next lesson.

Three Sounds

One Ending, Three Sounds

"I asked, I checked, I walked."

a short T sound — no new syllable: ASKT, CHECKT, WALKT

"I called, it arrived, we stayed."

a soft D sound — no new syllable: CALLD, ARRIVD, STAYD

"I waited, we landed, I needed help."

a NEW syllable, only after T or D: WAI-ted, LAN-ded, NEE-ded

"What happened?"

the question that starts every story — HAP-pend, two syllables, not three

Dialogue

You Look Tired

C
Good morning. You look tired. What happened?
L
A long day. We landed at six forty, and my bag was not on the belt.
C
Oh no. Was it missing?
L
Yes. I waited for forty minutes, and then I asked at the counter.
C
Were they helpful?
L
Yes, they were. I filled in a form with my hotel and my phone number.
C
And the bag? Is it here now?
L
It is. It stayed in Lisbon, but they delivered it at seven thirty last night. Finally.
C
That happened to me in March. My bag stayed in Chicago for three days.
L
Three days? That is terrible. What happened?
C
I shopped for new clothes, and the airline paid for them. And honestly? I liked the new clothes.
L
So it was a lucky problem.
C
Exactly. A lucky problem.
Comprehension

What Did Claire Say?

1. When was Claire's problem with her bag?
In March.
2. Where was her bag, and for how long?
In Chicago, for three days.
3. What was her solution, and who paid?
She shopped for new clothes, and the airline paid for them.
