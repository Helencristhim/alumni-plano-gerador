Stage 1.1: Feedback Words

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Eight words, two minutes.

Feedback — what somebody tells you about how you did
"The coach gave me feedback after every heat."
Praise — words that say you did something well
"He does not give praise very often."
Advice — what somebody tells you that you should do
"She gave me one piece of advice and it worked."
Warning — words that tell you about a danger or a problem
"The lifeguard gave us a warning about the rocks."
Promise — when you say that you will definitely do something
"He made a promise and he kept it."
Complain — to say that you are not happy about something
"Nobody complains about the waves here."
Admit — to say that something is true, even when it is bad for you
"He admitted that he had made a mistake."
Mention — to say something quickly, without making it the main point
"She mentioned that the tide was dropping."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the report. Then answer the questions.

The coach said that my first wave had been a mistake and that I was too slow on the take-off. Then he admitted that the conditions had been difficult for everybody. The judge told me that my second wave had been the best of the heat, and she mentioned that the tide had been dropping fast. My host mum did not talk about surfing at all. She said that I looked tired and that we were going home, and she promised that she would bring sandwiches next time.
What did the coach admit?
A That the conditions had been difficult for everybody.
B That the second wave was the best.
Who mentioned the tide?
A The host mum.
B The judge.
Why is it "he said that" but "she told me that"?
A Because "told" always needs a person after it and "said" does not.
B Because the coach is a man.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Reported speech — said, told, one step back

When you repeat somebody, the verb moves one step into the past and the pronouns move with it.

said or told

He said that I was late. (no person)
He told me that I was late. (always a person)
Never: "he said me".
The word that is optional: he said I was late.

One step back

am / is / are → was / were
do / does → did
was / were → had been
will → would, can → could

said takes no person: He said that I was late. Never "he said me".
told always takes a person: He told me that I was late. Never "he told that".
One step back: are → were, is → was, do → did, was → had been, will → would, can → could.
The pronouns move too: "your wave" becomes "my wave"; "you are" becomes "I was".

Stage 4: After the heat

Quiz
You are repeating what the coach said. Which sentence is correct?
A He said me that I was late.
B He told me that I was late.
The judge said: "Your wave was the best." How do you report it?
A She told me that my wave had been the best.
B She told that your wave was the best.
The original was "I will pick you up at six."
A She said that she will pick me up at six.
B She said that she would pick me up at six.
Somebody said something and you did not catch it.
A Sorry, what did she say exactly?
B Sorry, what she said?

Stage 5: Report your week

Reflection
Record 30 seconds. Report five things that people said to you this week: a teacher, a friend, somebody at home, and two more. Use said twice and told three times. Check one thing before you send it — every "told" has a person right after it, and every verb has moved one step back.
