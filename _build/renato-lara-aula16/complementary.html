SieSie — "a day in the life of an australian high school student"

An Australian teenager films her own school day: the bell, the timetable, the locker, recess and the canteen line. Nobody is speaking slowly for a camera here — this is the speed you land in.

Tip: watch it once with no subtitles and count how many things you understood. Then watch it again with subtitles.

BBC Real Easy English — "Talking about teachers"

Short, slow and made for your level. Two people talk about the teachers they had at school, which subjects they liked, and why. The opposite speed of the video above, on purpose.

Tip: after each speaker, say one sentence about your own school starting with "There is" or "There are".

ABC Behind the News — "Starting High School, Your Questions Answered"

Australian students answer the questions everybody has before the first day: getting lost, the lockers, the uniform, and what actually happens at lunch. Made by ABC for students your age.
