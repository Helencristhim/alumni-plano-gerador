Grammar Discovery

The same words, repeated

The coach: "You are too slow on the take-off."
He said that I was too slow on the take-off.
The judge: "Your second wave was better."
She told me that my second wave had been better.

Everything steps back one

When you repeat what somebody said, the verb moves one step into the past: are becomes was, was becomes had been. And the person changes too: "you" becomes "I", "your" becomes "my". Two verbs do the reporting: said (no person after it) and told (always with a person after it).

The Code

Said, told, and the step back

said or told

He said that I was late. (no person)
He told me that I was late. (always a person)
Never: "he said me".
The word that is optional: he said I was late.

One step back

am / is / are → was / were
do / does → did
was / were → had been
will → would, can → could

And the people move with it: you → I, your → my, my → his or her.

Sound Check

Reported speech is flat

He said that I was too slow.

no drama in the voice: you are reporting, not acting

She told me my second wave was better.

the "that" disappears completely in speech

He said, and I quote, "you are too slow".

a direct quote gets the original voice back

Listening 1

The coach, direct

1. What did the surfer tell the coach before the heat?
That he was going to wait for the sets. Then he did not wait.
2. What are they going to work on from Monday, and why?
The take-off, because it has been too slow all season.
3. What is the third thing, and what does the coach ask?
That the surfer was the only one who read the tide correctly, and he asks him not to tell anybody.
Listening 2

The same thing, reported

1. How does Zoe report the first two points?
She says he said the first wave was a mistake, and that he told him his take-off has been too slow all season.
2. Why is Zoe surprised by the third point?
Because it is the biggest compliment that coach has given anybody this year.
3. What did her brother tell her about that coach?
That in three seasons he never once said anything nice.
