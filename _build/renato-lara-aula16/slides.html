Grammar Discovery

One word holds the place

There is a canteen next to the library.
There are six periods in a day.
There is no bell at the start of period five.
Is there a surf club at this school?
Four sentences. The first word is the same, and it is not the subject.

To say a place has something, start with there

"There" holds the front of the sentence, and is or are comes straight after it. The verb agrees with the thing that comes next, not with "there": one thing → there is, two or more → there are. To ask, the two words swap places: is there, are there.

The Code

There is, there are

The form

There is a canteen. (one)
There are two labs. (more than one)
There isn't a pool.
There aren't any lockers left.
Is there a surf club? — Yes, there is.
Are there two breaks? — Yes, there are.

three traps

Never My school has a canteen for a place.
say There is a canteen at my school
Never There is two labs.
the verb looks at what comes after it
Never In my school a big canteen.
the verb is never missing — this is your one

In speech both words melt: "there is" becomes there's and "there are" becomes there're — but the verb is still in there.

Sound Check

There's a canteen next to the library.

"there is" collapses to one beat: "thez-a canteen"

Listening 1

Millie, on the way to school

1. How long is recess, and what does she say about the canteen?
Recess is only twenty minutes. She says not to go to the canteen then, because the line is massive. Go at lunch.
2. Where is his locker, and what is the trick with it?
It is upstairs in C block. He has to lift the door while he turns the key, or it does not open.
3. What is the rule about the hat, and what happens if he forgets it?
No hat, no play. If he forgets his hat they keep him inside at lunch, and she says they actually do it.
Listening 2

The front office, eight fifteen

1. What two things are in the folder, and what does he say about one of them?
A timetable with Renato's name on it, and a map of the school behind it. He says the map is not very good, but it is better than nothing.
2. What are the two things people always get wrong in the first week?
There is no bell for the start of period five, so you have to watch the clock after lunch. And there are no lockers on the ground floor, so his is upstairs in C block.
3. What does he tell Renato to do if a teacher talks too fast?
Say so. "Sorry, could you slow down a bit." Nobody minds, and everybody in the school has said it to somebody.
