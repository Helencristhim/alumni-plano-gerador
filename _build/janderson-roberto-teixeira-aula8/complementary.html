"How to use quantifiers" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on the small words that sit in front of a noun: much, many, a few, a little, plenty of, a lot of. Connection to Lesson 8: it is exactly the decision you made all through class, drilled fast enough that the shape starts arriving without a decision.

Tip: pause after each example and say the same sentence about your own week, once with a countable noun and once with an uncountable one.

Vocabulary Video
"6 Minute Vocabulary: Uncountable nouns" -- BBC Learning English

Six minutes on the eight nouns that cost most: money, information, advice, news, luggage and the rest. Connection to Lesson 8: these are precisely the words that appear all day in a bank and that Portuguese counts and English does not, which is why they survive so long in a fluent speaker's English.

Tip: after watching, write one true work sentence with information and one with advice. Say both out loud and check that neither of them has an S.
