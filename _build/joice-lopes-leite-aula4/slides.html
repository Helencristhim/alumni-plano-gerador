Chapter 3: The Code

Whose Is It?

my · her · his · our · their · Marina's

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"This is my colleague."

"Her name is Marina."

"His office is in São Paulo."

"Marina's project is about schools."

PersonWordExample
Imymy team
youyouryour group
he / shehis / herhis office · her project
we / theyour / theirour partner · their questions
a namename + 'sMarina's project · the school's platform

The owner comes first, and the thing comes after: Marina's project, not the project of Marina.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"The project of Marina is good."
"Marina's project is good."
"Marina is a researcher. His university is in Recife."
"Marina is a researcher. Her university is in Recife."
"They are my colleagues. Theirs questions are here."
"They are my colleagues. Their questions are here."

In English, his and her follow the OWNER, not the thing. Marina is a woman, so it is always her office, her team, her report.
