Lesson 12 -- The Case Study

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Selling yourself"

A short episode on talking about what you have achieved at work without sounding arrogant. Almost every sentence is either a finished story in the past simple or a result in the present perfect. Full script on the same page.

Tip: read the script with two colors. Mark every past simple in one and every present perfect in the other, and check which ones have a finished time.

Listen on BBC Learning English ↗
Talk
Bill Gross -- "The single biggest reason why start-ups succeed" (TED, 6 min)

An investor looks back at two hundred companies and tells their stories with numbers: what they did, when, and what has happened since. A good model of evidence told briefly.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down two company stories he tells in one sentence each, with the past simple for the story and the present perfect for the result.

Watch on TED ↗
Film
The Social Network (2010)

The story of how one company started, told from a deposition room years later. The film moves between what happened then and what is true now, which is exactly the structure of a case study.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. In the deposition scenes, listen for questions like "When did you..." and "Have you ever...", and notice which form the answer takes.
