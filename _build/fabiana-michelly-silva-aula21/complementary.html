Amy C. Edmondson -- How to lead in a crisis

A Harvard professor on what leaders should say when the facts are still coming in: be honest about what you do not know, and be clear about the next step. It is today's bridge call, in five minutes.

Tip: write down every sentence where she separates what is known from what is not. Compare them with "it is understood that" and "is thought to be".
