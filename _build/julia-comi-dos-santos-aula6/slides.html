The Frame

Why -er here and more there?

The startup is closer than the bank.
The bank is more formal than the startup.
This schedule is easier than the other one.
That option is more convenient for me.

Short word → -er. Long word → more.

One or two syllables take -er. Three or more take more in front. Count on your hand — you will be right almost every time.

The Frame

Count the syllables

1 or 2 syllables → -er

close → closer than
big → bigger than
easy → easier than
the closest one

3 or more → more

flexible → more flexible than
formal → more formal than
convenient → more convenient than
the most flexible one

Count the syllables on your hand before you choose. That is the whole rule.

Never both: more easier does not exist. And it is than, not "that".

better than worse than the best not as ... as much more

R

C
Clara: The other one is remote and the schedule is much more flexible.
R

Clara: Honestly? Better. The team is smaller, so I would do more.
R
Rafa: Then why are we talking about this?
C
Clara: Because the salary is lower and my mother keeps saying the bank looks better on a resume.
R
Rafa: Your mother is not going to commute fifty minutes twice a day.

Choosing between two

Advanced English and third semester or above.
