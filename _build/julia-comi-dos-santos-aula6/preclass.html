Lesson 06 — Pre-class

Better, Bigger, Closer

Eight words from a real job ad (remote, flexible, formal, located, distance, salary, benefit, convenient) and how to compare: count the syllables, then -er or more.

Stage 1.1: Words from the Job Ad

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Eight words, two minutes.

Remote — work you do from home, not from an office
"The second one is remote, so I would save two hours a day."
Flexible — it can change to fit your schedule
"This one is more flexible than the other."
Formal — serious and following strict rules, like a suit and tie
"The bank is much more formal than the startup."
Located — placed in a certain part of the city
"It is located ten minutes from campus."
Distance — how far one place is from another
"The distance is the biggest problem."
Salary — the money a company pays you every month
"The salary is lower, but the experience is better."
Benefit — something extra a company gives you besides money
"The benefits are better at the bigger company."
Convenient — easy for you, because of where or when it is
"The morning schedule is more convenient for me."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the message. Then answer the questions.

I have two options and they are very different. The first is at a bank, and the salary is higher — six hundred reais more. But it is located fifty minutes away, the distance is the biggest problem, and the dress code is formal. The second is a startup. It is remote two days a week, so it is much more convenient for me, and the schedule is more flexible than the bank’s. The benefits are better at the bank, but the team at the startup is smaller, so I think I would learn more. The salary is not as good as the first one. For me, that is not the most important thing.
What is the biggest problem with the first option?
A The distance — fifty minutes away.
B The salary.
Why is it "more flexible" and not "flexibler"?
A Because flexible has three syllables, so it takes "more".
B Because flexible is an irregular adjective.
Why would she learn more at the startup?
A Because the salary is lower.
B Because the team is smaller.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Count the syllables. That is the whole rule.

Short word → add -er. Long word → put more in front. Never both.

1–2 syllables

close → closer
big → bigger
easy → easier
the closest

3+ syllables

flexible → more flexible
formal → more formal
convenient → more convenient
the most flexible

+ The startup is more flexible than the bank.
= The salary is not as good as the other one.
++ It is the most convenient option for me.

Two that break the rule and you just learn: good → better / the best · bad → worse / the worst. And it is than, never "that".

Stage 4: In the conversation

Quiz
A recruiter asks why you chose them. Which answer is best?
A Because the other company was bad.
B This position is more flexible, and for me the schedule matters more than the salary.
Which sentence is correct?
A This one is more better than the other.
B This one is much better than the other.
You want to say two things are not equal. Which is correct?
A It is not as convenient as the first one.
B It is not as convenient than the first one.

Stage 5: Two options, one choice

Reflection
Write four comparisons first: two internships (salary and distance), two neighbourhoods you know, this semester against the last one, and which option you would choose. Count the syllables before each adjective. Then turn the paper over and record 30 seconds saying them — without reading — and finish with the choice and one reason.
