Chapter 3: The Code

What You Do / What You Are Doing

The difference between your job and your week.

Discover

What Do These Have in Common?

"I handle contract and corporate matters."

"This month I am handling the Atlas supply agreement."

"Our firm advises energy clients."

"We are waiting for the regulator right now."

Present simplePresent continuous
What is always true.
I handle · our firm advises
usually, normally, every year		What is true around now.
I am handling · we are waiting
this month, at the moment, right now

In a first call you need both, in this order: what you do, then what you are working on.

Practice

Simple or Continuous?

"Our firm ___represents three energy groups." (always)
"At the moment I ___am reviewing the shareholders' agreement."
"I normally ___draft the first version myself."
"This week the counterparty ___is asking for a longer notice period."
The Habit

Slow Is Fast

1. Pause. One second before you answer. Nobody counts it but you.

2. One short sentence first. "I handle contracts and corporate work."

3. Then expand. "At the moment I am working on a supply agreement in the energy sector."

Speed does not sound senior. Control does.

Listening 1

A Voicemail from the Other Side

0:000:00
1. Which point is already closed, and not open for discussion?

The scope of the indemnity, and who carries the risk if the regulator delays the licence.
3. What does Diane say about her own role?
