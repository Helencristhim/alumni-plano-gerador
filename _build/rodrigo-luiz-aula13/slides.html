Chapter 3: The Pattern

One Verb, and You Know the Way

turn · go · take · don't

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Turn left at the traffic light.
Go straight for two blocks.
Take the subway to Brooklyn.
Don't take a taxi now.

No I, no you, no he. What is the first word in every sentence?

UseFormExample
Turnturn + left / rightTurn left at the corner.
Continuego straight + for + distanceGo straight for two blocks.
Transporttake + the subway / a taxiTake the subway to Brooklyn.
Stop the tripget off at + stationGet off at York Street.
Say NODon't + verbDon't take a taxi now.
CarefulNo subject: the verb comes first. The negative is always DON'T, never "no take". And it is "go straight", never "go direct".

Chapter 4: The Street

Monday Morning, Lexington Avenue

one hotel, one meeting, no idea where the park is

Dialogue

Excuse Me, How Do I Get There?

R
Excuse me. Can you help me, please? I'm looking for Bryant Park.
K
Sure. Go straight on this street for two blocks.
R
Two blocks. And then?
K
At the traffic light, turn left. The park is on the corner of Sixth Avenue.
R
Is it far? Can I walk?
K
Yes, it's only ten minutes. Don't take a taxi. The traffic is terrible now.
R
Thank you. And after the meeting I'd like to go to Brooklyn. How do I get there?
K
Take the subway. The station is next to the park. Take the F train and get off at York Street.
R
The F train, York Street. Thank you very much.
K
You're welcome. Enjoy New York!
Comprehension

About the Directions

1. How many blocks does Kate tell him to go straight?
Two blocks.
2. Where does he turn left?
At the traffic light.
3. Why does Kate say "Don't take a taxi"?
Because the traffic is terrible now — and the park is only ten minutes away.
