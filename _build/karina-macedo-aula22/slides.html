Chapter 4: The Code

Click! · Contact us! · Don't miss it!

telling customers what to do

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Discover our new aircraft."

"Click the link in the bio."

"Please contact us today."

"Don't miss our special offer!"

FormUseExample
base verb + ...tell people to do somethingClick the link. Watch the video.
Don't + base verbtell people NOT to do somethingDon't miss the launch!
Please + base verba polite requestPlease contact us today.
no subjectno I / you / we before the verbVisit us. (not "You visit us.")

In an ad, the verb comes FIRST: Click, Contact, Discover, Don't miss. / No anúncio, o verbo vem PRIMEIRO, sem "you" antes.

Chapter 5: Practice

Train Your Ad

Dialogue · Listening · Detective · Speak · Build

Dialogue

Mike Reads the Ad

M
Hi Karina, I read your ad. It is a good start!
K
Thank you, Mike. It is my first ad in English.
M
My first tip: start with a verb. Write Discover the new jet, not We have a new jet.
K
OK. Discover the new jet. It is shorter and stronger.
M
Exactly. Who are your customers for this jet?
K
Business people from the Middle East and from Europe.
M
Then write one clear action for them: Click the link, or Book a visit. Only one.
K
Good. Book a private visit. And at the end: Don't miss it!
M
Perfect. Do you have a slogan?
K
Yes. Fly with confidence. We launch the ad on Monday!
