Video
Michigan Ross -- "Balancing an Executive MBA with Work and Family"

Executive MBA students and alumni talk honestly about fitting the program around a full-time job and a family: what they cut back on, who supported them, and what they would do differently. Clear American English.

Tip: write down every sentence that admits a cost and keeps the decision. Then say two of your own, one with although and one with despite.

Podcast
Freakonomics Radio -- "The Upside of Quitting"

A classic episode about opportunity cost: every hour and every dollar you spend on one thing is an hour and a dollar you cannot spend on something else. The perfect background for deciding what the program will really cost you.

Tip: listen for the phrase "opportunity cost" and write down one example from the episode. Then describe your own opportunity cost in three sentences.

Talk
TED -- Ruth Chang, "How to make hard choices"

A philosopher explains why some choices are hard because neither option is simply better — and how to decide anyway. It is a calm, clear talk about exactly the kind of decision an executive MBA is.

Tip: watch it once without subtitles. Then say three sentences about your decision, using although, even though and despite.
