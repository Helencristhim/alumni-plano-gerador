Chapter 3: How Often?

Always · Usually · Sometimes · Never

how often do you post?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I always post on Monday."

"I usually post three times a week."

"Clients sometimes share my videos."

"I never post at night."

FormUseExample
always100% -- every timeI always answer comments.
usuallyabout 80% -- most of the timeI usually post on Monday.
sometimesabout 50% -- some daysI sometimes post photos.
never0% -- not one timeI never post prices.
Question: How often do you...?ask about frequencyHow often do you post? -- Three times a week.

The word goes BEFORE the verb: I always post. With "is", it goes AFTER: My caption is always short. / A palavra vem ANTES do verbo; com "is", vem DEPOIS.

Dialogue

A Call with Mike

M
Hi Karina, I am Mike. I help companies with Instagram. Can I ask about your account?
K
Of course, Mike. I post videos about aircraft for my company.
M
Great. How often do you post?
K
I usually post three times a week. I always post on Monday.
M
Good. Do you write the caption in English?
K
No, I never write in English. My captions are in Portuguese.
M
Many of your followers are from other countries. I always say: write a short caption in English too.
K
Good idea. People sometimes write a comment in English, but I do not always answer.
M
Answer every comment. People share the content of a brand that talks to them.
K
OK. This week I will write my first caption in English!
