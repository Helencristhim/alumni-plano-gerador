BBC Learning English — Live English Class: Reported Questions

A recorded live class about the grammar of today: how a direct question becomes part of your sentence, with if, whether and a question word, and why the word order changes.

Tip: every time the teacher gives an example, pause and relay one real question from your week: The auditor asked whether… Check that there is no inversion.

Business English from All Ears English — How to Clarify Numbers in Workplace English

A short episode about the moment a number is not clear in a meeting or an email, and the phrases you use to check it before you act. It is the same skill as today: do not guess, ask.

Tip: write down two phrases they use to ask for clarification. Use one of them in your next call with the BPO or the auditors.

Talk
Ernesto Sirolli — Want to help someone? Shut up and listen! (TED)

A short, funny talk about projects that failed because people decided what others needed without asking them. The lesson for any relay: before you act on a message, ask what the person really meant.

Tip: after the talk, relay two of his stories in reported speech: He asked the people what they wanted…
