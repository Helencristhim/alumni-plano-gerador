Chapter 3: The Question Inside the Sentence

They Asked Whether…

A question that becomes part of your sentence

Grammar Discovery

Where Did the Question Go?

“Have the accruals been reviewed?” → They asked whether the accruals had been reviewed.

“Why is the variance so high?” → She wanted to know why the variance was so high.

“Can you send the breakdown by Friday?” → The auditor asked me to send the breakdown by Friday.

“Don't send the slides before the call.” → The director told us not to send the slides before the call.

Look at the orange words. In 1 and 2, is the verb before or after the subject now? What new word appears in 1? And what comes after the person in 3 and 4?

TypeThe patternExample
yes / no questionasked / wanted to know + if or whether + subject + verbThey asked whether the figures were final.
wh- questionquestion word + subject + verb (no inversion)She asked what the issue was. Not: what was the issue.
question with do / does / diddo, does and did disappear“Where do you keep the files?” → He asked where we kept the files.
a requestask + person + to + verbThe auditor asked us to provide a breakdown.
an instructiontell + person + (not) to + verbShe told us not to send the slides.
tell or ask?tell never reports a questionShe asked me why it was late. Not: told me why.

In one line: a reported question is not a question anymore — no inversion, no question mark.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

Monday Morning, After the Audit Call

One conversation, two voices, and one Teams message

Dialogue

She Relays, He Relays Back

K
Mark, do you have five minutes? The auditors had some questions on Friday, and I need to relay them before I answer.
M
Sure. What did they want to know?
K
First, they asked whether the December accruals had been reviewed. Then they wanted to know why the variance in account 3200 was so high.
M
That second one sounds ambiguous. Did they ask why it increased, or whether we can support it?
K
I wasn't sure either, so I asked them to specify. They want the supporting documentation, not a new analysis.
M
Perfect. By the way, the director called this morning. She asked me if you could present the variance in Thursday's call.
K
Of course. Did she give any instruction about the format?
M
Yes. She told us to keep it to five minutes, and she advised me not to send the slides before the call.
Comprehension

About Mark

1. Which of the auditors' questions does Mark find ambiguous?
The second one: he is not sure if they asked why the variance increased, or whether the team can support it.
2. What did the director ask Mark?
If Keitma could present the variance in Thursday's call.
3. What did the director tell them about the presentation?
To keep it to five minutes, and not to send the slides before the call.
