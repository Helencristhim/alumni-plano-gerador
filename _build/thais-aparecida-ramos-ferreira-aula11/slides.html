Chapter 3: What, When, Who

The Word at the Front

What do you want? · When does it start? · Who comes with you?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"What do you want to see first?"

"When does the tour start?"

"What does your daughter want to do?"

"Who comes with you to Paris?"

Question wordYou ask aboutExample
What + do / doesa thingWhat do you want to see?
When + do / doesa day or an hourWhen does the tour start?
Who + verba personWho comes with you?
do or does?does with he, she, it and one name (the tour, my daughter). do with I, you, we, they.

Two things to keep: what and when always take a small word (do / does) with them. Who goes alone, and the verb takes the S: who comes, who pays, who speaks.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"What you want to see?"

✓ RIGHT

"What do you want to see?"

✗ WRONG

"When starts the tour?"

✓ RIGHT

"When does the tour start?"

✗ WRONG

"What does she wants?"

✓ RIGHT

"What does she want?"

Portuguese asks Quando comeca o passeio? and the verb jumps to the front. English never does that: the order is always When · does · the tour · start. And the S lives in does, so the other verb stays clean.
