Chapter 3: The Code

Doing, or To Do?

The first verb decides the shape of the second

Discover the Rule

Why Does One Column Take -ing and the Other To?

I enjoy following fashion trends.  ·  I want to work in fashion.
I avoid buying things I never wear.  ·  I decided to try a capsule wardrobe.
I keep forgetting my deadlines.  ·  I hope to get an internship.
I finished studying at eleven.  ·  I need to finish this piece.
Click to reveal the rule
The second verb changes shape, and the first verb decides which shape. There is no meaning behind it, so do not look for one — learn them in two small groups. Take -ing: enjoy, avoid, consider, keep, finish, practise. Take to: want, need, decide, plan, hope, learn. One pair is worth knowing because the meaning really does change: I stopped buying fast fashion (I do not buy it any more) against I stopped to look at the collection (I interrupted what I was doing in order to look). And two useful openers you already have: I am really into... and I am looking forward to... — both take -ing after them.

Chapter 4: The Wardrobe

What People Actually Wear

A text about the eight pieces that come out every week

Reading

The Wardrobe Nobody Plans

The Wardrobe Nobody Plans

Ask anybody to describe their style and they will tell you about the clothes they enjoy wearing. Open the wardrobe and you find something else: about eight pieces that come out every week, and a large number of things that somebody decided to buy on a particular afternoon and has avoided wearing ever since.

This is the idea behind a capsule wardrobe. You keep a small number of pieces that work with each other, and you stop buying things that only work with one outfit. People who try it usually report the same two things. The first one is that getting dressed takes four minutes instead of twenty. The second one is less comfortable: they realize how much of what they owned was never really theirs.

Vintage sits close to this idea, and not only because it is cheaper. A piece that has already lasted thirty years has proved something that a new piece cannot prove. It is also the fastest way to stop looking like everybody else, which is what most people mean when they say they want to develop an aesthetic.

None of this needs to be a moral position. You do not have to give up shopping to think about it. But it is worth considering one question before the next purchase, and it is not about money: if this piece does not work with at least three things you already own, what exactly are you buying?
