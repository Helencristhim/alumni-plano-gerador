Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example out loud. Five come from your field, and three decide the shape of the verb after them.

Sustainable -- made in a way that does not damage the planet
"I enjoy reading about sustainable fashion, but I avoid preaching about it."
Vintage -- old, from another period, and valued for that
"She keeps finding vintage pieces nobody else notices."
Capsule wardrobe -- a small set of clothes that all work together
"I decided to try a capsule wardrobe for one semester."
Aesthetic -- the particular look somebody or something has
"Her whole aesthetic is three colours and nothing else."
To influence -- to change how somebody thinks or what they choose
"One teacher influenced everything I want to do now."
To consider -- to think carefully about doing something
"I am considering applying for that course next year."
To avoid -- to deliberately not do something
"I avoid buying anything I have to wash by hand."
To keep -- to continue doing something, again and again
"I keep forgetting to take the receipt."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

I enjoy reading about sustainable fashion, but I avoid preaching about it, because everybody in my course keeps saying the same three sentences and nobody changes anything. So this semester I decided to try a capsule wardrobe: eleven pieces, all of them working with each other. I want to see what I actually miss. I have not finished doing it yet, and I am not planning to tell anybody how to dress. I am just looking forward to finding out.
Why is it "I enjoy reading" but "I decided to try"?
A Because the first verb decides the shape of the second
B Because reading is in the present and try is in the past
C Because enjoy is an irregular verb
"I am looking forward to finding out." Why finding and not to find?
A Because the to in looking forward to is a preposition, not an infinitive
B Because find is irregular
C Because the sentence is in the past

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Verb + -ing or Verb + to

GRAMMAR

When one verb follows another, the second one changes shape — and the first verb decides which shape. There is no meaning behind it. Learn them in two small groups.

take -ingenjoy, avoid, consider, keep, finish, practiseI avoid buying things I never wear.
take towant, need, decide, plan, hope, learnI decided to try a capsule wardrobe.
the pair that changes meaningstop + -ing vs stop + toI stopped buying it (never again) · I stopped to look (I paused in order to look)
two openers you already useboth take -ingI am really into reading... · I am looking forward to finishing...

Careful 1: looking forward to ends in to, but it takes -ing. "I am looking forward to finish" is wrong.

Careful 2: do not look for logic. Enjoy and hope are both about liking something, and they take different shapes.

Stage 5: Free Production

Reflection
Two minutes about who you are right now, ten weeks after the first lesson. What you study, what you are in the middle of, what you enjoy doing, what you avoid, what you decided this semester, and what you are looking forward to. Use at least four verbs with -ing and four with to. Do not write a script first — just speak.
