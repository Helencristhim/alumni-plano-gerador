Lesson 10 -- Into It or Over It?

Talk
"The ten-item wardrobe" — Jennifer L. Scott, TEDxStGeorge

Somebody who did the experiment of this lesson and lived with ten pieces. Clear, slow English, and the whole talk is built on verbs followed by other verbs.

Tip: write down every sentence where one verb follows another. You will have a page of examples in twenty minutes, and half of them will be in the -ing group.

Listening
"Making old things last longer" — 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes about repairing instead of replacing. It is the argument of the reading text of this lesson, from the other end of the wardrobe.

Tip: listen once without subtitles. Then say three things you avoid doing and three things you decided to do, about your own clothes. Six sentences, two shapes.

Grammar
"Verb patterns" — 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

The two groups of this lesson in six minutes, with the verbs listed and the stop pair explained at the end.

Tip: after the video, write the two lists on one page and keep it. This is the one thing in the whole block that you learn by memorising a short list, not by understanding a rule.
