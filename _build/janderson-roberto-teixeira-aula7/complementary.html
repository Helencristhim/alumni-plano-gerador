Talk
"Your body language may shape who you are" -- Amy Cuddy, TED

Twenty minutes from a psychologist about the two minutes before a meeting, and about what happens to people who go quiet in a room. Connection to Lesson 7: the second listening in class was somebody who froze in a meeting and decided to speak anyway. This is the research behind why that decision works.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Her pace is slow and her sentences are short, which makes her one of the easiest speakers on TED to follow without stopping.

Six minutes about a career: how long somebody has wanted something, when they started, and what changed. Connection to Lesson 7: it is a whole conversation built on for, since and ago, and you can hear the tense switching every time a date appears. Audio, vocabulary and full transcript are free on the page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then open it and mark every for, since and ago. Check that each one is doing the job you learned in class.

Grammar Video
"Present perfect with 'for' and 'since'" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on the exact pair you drilled today, plus the question how long have you...? Connection to Lesson 7: it repeats the mechanical rule -- a period takes for, a starting point takes since -- until it stops needing a decision.

Tip: pause after each example and say the same sentence about your own job, first with for, then with since, then with ago. Three versions of the same eleven years.
