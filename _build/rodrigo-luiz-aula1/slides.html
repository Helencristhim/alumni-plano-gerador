Chapter 3: The Pattern

One Pattern, Many Sentences

my name is · my role is · I am · I am not

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

My name is Rodrigo Luiz.
My role is Operations Director.
I am from Brazil.
I am not in the sales team.

What word repeats in these sentences?

FormExampleEm português
I am + ...I am from Brazil.Sou do Brasil.
My name / My role is + ...My role is Operations Director.Meu cargo é Diretor de Operações.
I am not + ...I am not in the sales team.Não sou do time de vendas.
Short formI am = I'm · I am not = I'm not. Em inglês o "am" nunca some: "I responsible for operations" não existe — o certo é "I am responsible for operations".

Dialogue

Good Morning, I Am Claire

C
Good morning. My name is Claire Fontaine. I am the global operations director.
R
Good morning, Claire. My name is Rodrigo Luiz. My role is Operations Director in Brazil.
C
Very good. So you are responsible for the full chain in Brazil?
R
Yes, I am. I am the head of operations. My team is here in Sorocaba.
C
Perfect. And are you in the forum with the purchasing group as well?
R
Sorry, can you say that again, please?
C
Of course. Sorry. Are you in the purchasing forum too?
R
Ah, okay. Yes, I am. But I am not in the sales forum.
C
Understood. Our headquarters is in France, and I am in Brazil in October.
R
Excellent. My English is simple, but I am here in every meeting.
