One Day, Six Tools

Coffee break: I was hoping we could visit your center next year.

Panel: When we started, we had never worked with a robot.

Panel: Although every hospital is different, it seems that a fixed team makes the difference.

Plenary: Let me start by thanking the people who made this possible.

Click to reveal the map of the day
Each moment of the day has its own tools. Coffee break: indirect questions -- I was wondering if / could you tell me + normal word order -- and polite past forms -- I was hoping we could / I wanted to check. Panel: narrative tenses for the story -- had + past participle for the earlier event; hedging for results -- it seems that / tend to; concession for weak points -- although + clause, despite + noun or -ing; conditions for a deal -- provided that / as long as + present. Plenary: signposting -- let me start by + -ing / moving on to -- and discourse markers -- that said / coming back to.
