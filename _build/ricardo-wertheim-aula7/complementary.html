Grammar Video
"Uncountable nouns" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Vocabulary

Six minutes on the half of the rule that decides everything else: which nouns you can count and which you cannot. Connection to Lesson 7: once you know that seafood and olive oil are uncountable, "much", "a little" and "is" follow on their own.

Tip: pause at every noun they mention and say out loud whether you could put a number in front of it. Then build one sentence with "not much" and one with "not many" about your own kitchen.

Podcast
"Travel to Croatia -- What to Do, See and Eat" -- Amateur Traveler

A full episode where a good part of the conversation is simply about eating: what is on the coast, what is inland, and what the markets look like in the morning. Connection to Lesson 7: it is your vocabulary spoken at natural speed by people who are hungry.

Tip: listen for quantity. Write down five things they say there is "a lot of" and two they say there is "not much" of. Then say the same about a market you know in Sao Paulo.

Travel Series
"Hvar, Croatia: Made for Relaxing" -- Rick Steves' Europe

The island you reach by the ferry from Split: the harbor, the small places to eat, the vineyards behind the town. Connection to Lesson 7: this is the day trip in the reading, filmed, with the food and the wine on screen.

Tip: watch it once for pleasure. The second time, pause on three food or market shots and describe each in one sentence with a quantifier: "There is not much...", "There are a few...".
