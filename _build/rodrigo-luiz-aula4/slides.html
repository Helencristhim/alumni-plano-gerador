Chapter 3: The Pattern

There Is No "Tem"

there is · there are · in · on · next to · behind

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

There is one entrance for visitors.
There are eight machines in the plant.
The warehouse is next to the production area.
The offices are on the first floor.

When do we say "is", and when do we say "are"?

FormExampleEm português
There is + one thingThere is one entrance.Tem uma entrada.
There are + many thingsThere are eight machines.Tem oito máquinas.
NegativeThere is no food here.Não tem comida aqui.
Onde / Wherein the warehouse · on the first floor · at the entrance · next to the office · behind the machines
Cuidado"Tem oito máquinas" NAO é "have eight machines". There are eight machines. O "have" fala de quem é DONO; o "there is/are" fala do que existe NO LUGAR.
Where Things Are

Five Words That Draw the Map

"The material is in the warehouse."

in — inside a space

"The offices are on the first floor."

on — a level or a surface

"The safety glasses are at the entrance."

at — an exact point

"The tanks are behind the machines."

behind — at the back of
