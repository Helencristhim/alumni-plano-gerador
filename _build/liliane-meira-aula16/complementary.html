Lesson 16 -- Checkpoint: Five Minutes on Your Own Work

Talk
TED -- Chris Anderson, "TED’s Secret to Great Public Speaking"

The curator of TED explains what the best short talks have in common: one idea, built step by step, so the audience can follow it. A perfect companion to your five-minute benchmark.

Tip: after watching, write the one idea of your own benchmark in a single sentence, and check if your talk really builds toward it.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart (Stanford GSB) -- "Um, Like, So: How Filler Words Can Create More Connected, Effective Communication"

A Stanford communication lecturer and a linguist talk about fillers: when they hurt a talk, when they help, and how to replace them with a pause. Exactly what you will listen for in your own recording.

Tip: listen to your benchmark recording after the episode and count your fillers in the first minute and in the last one.

Listen on Apple Podcasts ↗
Video
TEDxZagreb -- David JP Phillips, "The 110 Techniques of Communication and Public Speaking"

A specialist in presentation skills shows, with live examples, how pace, pauses, voice and posture change the way an audience receives the same words.

Tip: choose two techniques from the talk and use them in a new recording of the first minute of your benchmark.
