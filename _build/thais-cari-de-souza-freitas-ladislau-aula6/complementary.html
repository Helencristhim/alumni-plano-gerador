Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Hans Rosling -- "The best stats you’ve ever seen" (TED)

Twenty minutes of a statistician reading figures out loud, which is exactly what you practised today. Percentages, averages, ranges and shares, one after another, in slow and very clear English — and he never says a number without saying what he is comparing it with.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Pause after each figure and repeat it out loud with point, not comma.

Podcast
More or Less: Behind the Stats (BBC)

Nine minutes an episode, every episode about one number somebody used badly in public. It is your own methodology argument, in English, with the vocabulary of this lesson — a sample, a baseline, an average, a range — in almost every episode.

Tip: pick any recent episode. Listen once for the argument, then once more and write down every quantifier you hear before a figure.

Listen on BBC ↗
Video
Mona Chalabi -- "3 ways to spot a bad statistic" (TED)

Eleven minutes on the three questions to ask before you believe a figure — who collected it, how big the sample was, and what the number leaves out. It is the role-play of this lesson, done by a professional, in short and very clear sentences.

Tip: write her three questions down in English and take them to your next review. Ask one of them out loud.
