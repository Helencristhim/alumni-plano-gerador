Chapter 3: The Code

Where Things Are

in · on · under · next to · between

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The key is in my bag."

"The key is on the table."

"My bag is under the chair."

"The office is between the game room and the kitchen."

Four sentences, four different places. Which word tells you where?

WordWhere exactlyExample
ininside something, so you cannot see it"The key is in my bag."
onon the surface, so you can see it"The key is on the table."
underbelow something"My bag is under the chair."
next toat the side of one thing"The box is next to the door."
betweenin the middle of TWO things"The office is between the game room and the kitchen."
The trapPortuguese uses one word for two English ones. In is INSIDE; on is on TOP. "The book is in the bag" and "the book is on the bag" are two different pictures.

Five words, and you never lose anything in English again.

P
A black bag? Is it in the game room?
B
No, it isn't. I look on the table and under the chair, and it is not there.
P

B

B

P
No problem. Nobody opens the box alone. Talk to me and we open it together.
Comprehension

About the Coach

1. Where is the lost and found box?
In the main hall, next to the door.
2. Where is the main hall?
Between the kitchen and the big window.
3. Can he open the box alone?
No, he can't. Nobody opens the box alone.
4. What does she offer at the end?
She offers to open the box together with him.
