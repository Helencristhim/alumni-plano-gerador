Chapter 3: The Pattern

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

France is the biggest plant in the group.
Brazil had the fastest deliveries.
New Jersey is the most modern plant.
Brazil had the best result in quality.

Last class: -ER and MORE, for two things. Now it is the whole group. What word comes before every orange word?

UseFormExample
Short adjectivethe + adjective + -estthe fastest, the biggest
Long adjectivethe most + adjectivethe most efficient plant
Irregulargood → the best, bad → the worstour best result, our worst year
Two or all?faster than (two) / the fastest (all)Brazil is faster than China. Brazil is the fastest in the group.
In a groupthe ... in the group / of the yearthe best in the group, the worst of the year
CarefulAlways THE before the superlative: "Brazil is fastest" sounds wrong. And never "the most biggest": it is -EST or MOST, never both.
