Lesson 5 -- Plans and Possibilities

Series
Somebody Feed Phil — Netflix

Watch the first ten minutes of any episode with a destination you would like to visit. Phil says where he is going and what he is going to do there in the first two minutes, every single time.

Tip: pause after his opening and plan the same trip out loud in four sentences: destination, dates, what you are going to do, what still depends on something.

Listening
"Internships: would you work for free?" — 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on internships, which is the word you will need every time somebody asks what comes after the fourth semester. It is a real argument, with two sides.

Tip: listen once, then say out loud which side you agree with and why, in three sentences. You do not need to be right — you need to be able to say it.

Watch on YouTube →
Grammar
"How to talk about the future" — 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Going to and will side by side in six minutes, with the difference explained through examples instead of rules.

Tip: after the video, say five sentences about your next twelve months. Then check each one: did you decide that before today, or while you were speaking?
