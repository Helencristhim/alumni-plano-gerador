Chapter 3: The Code

The Plan and the Decision

Two futures, and the question that separates them

Discover the Rule

Why Does One Side Say Going To and the Other Will?

I am going to travel to the United States next year. I already have the dates.
We are going to stay in Miami for two weeks. The hotel is booked.
Oh, the flight is delayed? OK, I will wait at the cafe, then.
You do not have the room we booked? Fine, I will take the one on the fifth floor.
Click to reveal the rule
Both sides are the future. The difference is when you decided. Use going to for a plan you made before you started speaking: the dates exist, the tickets exist, the decision is old. Use will for a decision you make at this exact second, usually reacting to something that just happened. Notice the two bottom sentences: both start with a surprise. That is why they take will. And a useful third option for the future you are NOT sure about: it depends on — It depends on the dates and on how much I save.

The First Trip He Planned Alone

Read the questions, then listen.

They always built the itinerary and booked everything. This time he did it himself.

He booked a hotel far from everything, and he took far too much luggage.

Choosing the destination himself, because it was the first trip that felt like his.

About Ryan

1. When is Ryan going to Brazil, and what has he already done?
In January. He bought the ticket yesterday.
2. What has he not solved yet?
Where he is going to stay. Everything he finds is far away or expensive.

She offers to look for somewhere near the centre. He is surprised and thanks her.
Chapter 5: Practice

The Questions About Next Year

Including the ones you cannot answer yet

Listening 2

The Internship She Almost Did Not Apply For

Read the questions, then listen.

1. Why did she almost not apply?
She was in her fourth semester and thought she was too early, and the description asked for things she had not studied yet.
2. What did the person who hired her say about the decision?
They said almost nobody who applies has everything on the list, and that they choose people who want to learn.
3. What does she say about waiting until you are ready?
You are never ready, and waiting only means somebody else applies first.
