Plans and Possibilities -- The Trip You Build Yourself

Talking about what has not happened yet. Key words: destination, itinerary, confirm, luggage, career, opportunity, internship, it depends on. Structure: going to for plans, will for decisions made as you speak.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example out loud. Four are about a trip, four are about the years after this one.

Destination -- the place you are travelling to
"My next destination is somewhere I have never been."
Itinerary -- the plan of a trip, day by day
"I am going to build the whole itinerary myself this time."
To confirm -- to say officially that something is certain
"I still have to confirm the hotel for the last two nights."
Luggage -- all the bags you take on a trip
"I always travel with one piece of luggage and nothing else."
Career -- the working life you build over many years
"I do not know which career I want yet, and that is fine."
Opportunity -- a chance to do something good that may not come again
"It was a small opportunity, but I said yes."
Internship -- a short period of work you do while you are still studying
"I might look for an internship after the fourth semester."
It depends on -- used when the answer changes according to something else
"It depends on the dates and on how much I save."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Next July I am going to travel to New York, and this time I am going to plan everything myself. The flights are confirmed and the hotel for the first ten nights is booked. The last four nights are not confirmed yet, because it depends on how much I save between now and June. I am going to take one piece of luggage and nothing else. And if the price of the last hotel goes up, I will change the itinerary and stay somewhere else.
Why is it "I am going to travel to New York" and not "I will travel"?
A Because the plan was already made before speaking
B Because it is happening right now
C Because travel is an irregular verb
Why is the last sentence "I will change the itinerary"?
A Because it is a plan she made last year
B Because it is a decision she would make at that moment, if the price goes up
C Because will is always used after if

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Going To x Will

GRAMMAR

Both are the future. What separates them is when you decided.

going toa plan you made before you started speakingI am going to travel in July. I have the tickets.
willa decision you make at this secondThe room is not ready? OK, I will wait.
negativeam not going to / will notI am not going to change the dates.
not decided yetit depends on + nounIt depends on the price.

Careful 1: after going to the verb has no -ing. "I am going to confirming" is wrong — it is "I am going to confirm".

Careful 2: it is depends on, never depends of.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz
You already booked the flights. Somebody asks about your trip. What do you say?
A "I will travel to New York in July."
B "I am going to travel to New York in July."
C "I am going to travelling to New York in July."
The hotel offers you a different room. You decide right there. What do you say?
A "I am going to take that one."
B "That is fine, I will take that one."
C "I take that one."
Somebody asks which career you are going to follow. You have not decided. What is the best answer?
A "It depends of the courses."
B "It depends on which of the two courses I enjoy more."
C "I will be a fashion designer."

Stage 5: Free Production

Reflection
Record ninety seconds about the next twelve months. What you are going to do (the things that are already decided), what still depends on something else, and one trip you would like to plan on your own. Use going to at least four times and it depends on at least once. Do not write a script first — just speak.
