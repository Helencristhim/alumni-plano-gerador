Grammar Discovery

Which person? Which thing?

Harper is the surfer who everybody watches.
Kai rides a board that he shaped with his dad.
Jess is the girl who never stops talking.
This is the wave which I love most in Brazil.

What do the orange words have in common?

person + who · thing + which · person or thing + that

These words join two ideas and say exactly which person or thing. They go right after the noun. After them, you don't repeat the person: "the girl who surfs", never "the girl who she surfs".

The Code

Two ideas, one sentence

Join them

Harper is a surfer. She is goofy.
→ Harper is the surfer who is goofy.
This is my board. I use it in comps.
→ This is the board that I use in comps.
It's a wave. It breaks left.
→ It's a wave which breaks left.

Never

the board what I use
what is not for this: that / which
the surfer which won
a person: who / that
the girl who she never stops
don't repeat the person

The question: Which one is Harper? — The one who ...

Sound Check

Two small words

Harper is the surfer who everybody watches.

"who" is HOO: no w sound

This is the board that I use in comps.

"that" is weak here: thut

Listening 1

What the coach wants

1. What kind of surfers does Dana want, not only the one who does the biggest aerial?
Surfers who are consistent, who catch good waves heat after heat.
2. What is a surfer with style, for Dana?
A surfer who makes hard things look easy.
3. Which surfers does she pick, and what else is she watching?
The ones who know their weakness and work on it. She is also watching if they cheer for their mates.
Listening 2

Three questions from Ollie

1. Why is Ollie writing the profiles?
He can't surf, and Kai said he was the guy who talks the most.
2. What three things does Ollie want to know?
The board that Renato is using on Saturday, the wave that he loves most in Brazil, and the surfer who inspires him.
3. What line did Ollie already write about Renato?
Renato is the new member who saved my life.
