Stage 1.1: Describe a Surfer

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Eight words, two minutes. Goofy or regular: which one are you?

Goofy — a surfer who stands with the right foot at the front
"Harper is goofy."
Regular — a surfer who stands with the left foot at the front
"I surf regular."
Strength — something you do very well
"Her backhand is her biggest strength."
Weakness — something you do not do well, and need to improve
"Her weakness is big crowds."
Aerial — a move where the surfer and the board go up into the air
"His aerials are amazing."
Style — a special way of doing something that is only yours
"Her style is fast and fun."
Confident — sure that you can do something well
"Jess is a confident surfer."
Consistent — good every time, not only one time
"Kai is the most consistent surfer in the club."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read Ollie's first draft of the newsletter. Then answer the questions.

This week we meet the surfers who are trying out for the interschool squad. Harper is the goofy surfer who everybody watches. Her strength is her backhand, and her weakness is big crowds. Kai is the captain who never falls. He is the most consistent surfer in the club, and he rides a board that he shaped with his dad. Jess is the girl who never stops talking. She is confident, and her style is fast and fun. And Renato is the regular surfer who came from Brazil. His aerials are the ones that everybody talks about. Good luck on Saturday, team!
"Kai is the captain who never falls." Why "who" and not "which"?
A Kai is a person. For people we use who (or that).
B Because "who" is always at the start of a question.
"He rides a board that he shaped with his dad." What does "that" do here?
A It joins two ideas and says exactly which board.
B It means "because".
Which sentence is correct?
A Jess is the girl who she never stops talking.
B Jess is the girl who never stops talking.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Who, which, that

They join two ideas and say exactly which person or thing. They go right after the noun.

Which word?

person → who / that
thing → which / that
the surfer who won
the board that I use

Never

the board what I use
the surfer which won
the girl who she surfs

Positive: She's the surfer who everybody watches.  ·  Negative: It's a wave that doesn't break every day.  ·  Question: Which one is the board that you use?
Two ideas, one sentence: Kai is a surfer. He never falls. → Kai is the surfer who never falls.
Not sure? That works for people and things.

Stage 3: Say it out loud

Speaking

Tap Listen, then Record. "Who" is HOO, with no w sound. "That" is weak in the middle of a sentence.

This is the board that I use in comps.
