Lesson 04 -- Pre-class

Data Into Action -- How Much, How Many

Every company has data, but very few have insights. Key words: a dataset, a dashboard, an insight, a funnel, a conversion rate, organic reach, a customer journey, a touchpoint. Structure: countable and uncountable nouns with how much, how many, a lot of, a few and a little.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A dataset -- a group of data about one subject, often in a table
"The client sent us a dataset with ten thousand customers."
A dashboard -- a screen that shows the most important numbers
"I open the dashboard every morning."
An insight -- a clear idea that helps you understand and decide
"The data is only useful when it gives us an insight."
A funnel -- the steps from a visitor to a client, with fewer people at each step
"Many people leave the funnel at the payment page."
A conversion rate -- the percentage of people who do what you want, like buy
"The conversion rate of the new page is three percent."
Organic reach -- the people who see your posts when you do not pay
"The brand has good organic reach on Instagram."
A customer journey -- all the steps a person takes before and after buying
"We drew the customer journey on one page."
A touchpoint -- any moment when a customer meets the brand
"The website is the most important touchpoint."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Tom opens the dashboard of a client. The website got a lot of traffic: eighty thousand visits. How many leads? Only four hundred. That is not many, so the conversion rate is low.

Priya looks at the funnel of her store. A lot of people put products in the cart, but only a few hundred buy. The payment page asks for too much information.

Their insight is the same: the problem is not the ads, but one touchpoint in the customer journey.

1. How many visits did Tom’s client get?
A Four hundred.
B Eighty thousand.
C Five thousand.
2. Why do people leave Priya’s store?
A The products are expensive.
B The payment page asks for too much information.
C There is not much traffic.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A “How much leads did we get?”
B “How many leads did we get?”
C “How many traffic did we get?”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Countable and Uncountable -- How Much, How Many

GRAMMAR

Use many and a few with things you can count (leads, clients). Use much and a little with things you cannot count (data, traffic, money, time). A lot of goes with both.

JobFormExample
Countable (leads, clients, visits)How many...? · a fewHow many leads? Only a few clients.
Uncountable (data, traffic, money, time)How much...? · a littleHow much traffic? A little money.
Botha lot ofA lot of leads. A lot of data.
Negative and questionsmuch / manyWe don’t have much time. Do you have many clients?
Data, information, feedbackno -s, singular verbThe data is clear. Some information.
The trapno plural for uncountable wordsNot datas or informations. Say data, information.

Never: a lot of datas · more informations · How much leads? · a few money
