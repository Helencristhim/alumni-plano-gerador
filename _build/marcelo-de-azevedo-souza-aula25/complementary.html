Lesson 25 -- Conditionals in Real Life

TED Talk
Stefan Sagmeister: The power of time off

A designer closes his studio for one year every seven years. He explains what he does with the year, and what happens to his work afterwards. It is Fiona’s question, answered by somebody who took the plunge.

Tip: after the talk, say what you would do if you closed your office for one year, with three sentences in the second conditional.

TED Talk
Tim Ferriss: Why you should define your fears instead of your goals

How to weigh the pros and cons of a big decision by imagining the worst case. Practical, fast, and full of what-ifs.

Tip: choose one daydream of yours and answer his three questions out loud: what could go wrong, how you would prevent it, and how you would repair it.

TED Talk
Ruth Chang: How to make hard choices

A philosopher explains why some choices are hard: no option is clearly better. Calm, clear and very useful for the trade-off vocabulary.

Tip: describe one hard choice you made, and say what you would do differently if you had to make it again today.
