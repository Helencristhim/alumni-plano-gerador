Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Acquired — “Nvidia Part III: The Dawn of the AI Era”

The clearest account anywhere of a company that spent fifteen years reinvesting in a bet almost nobody understood, told by two hosts who explain the strategy as they go. The whole episode is a chain of what would have happened otherwise.

Tip: the first forty minutes are enough. Every time the hosts say what the company could have done instead, stop and say that sentence out loud yourself, starting with if.

Listen on Acquired ↗
Video
Michael Seibel — “How To Perfectly Pitch Your Seed Stage Startup”

The head of Y Combinator on the questions investors actually ask and why, including the one about the company you have not built yet. Clear American English, delivered slowly, with the reasoning behind every question said out loud.

Tip: pause after each question he lists and answer it yourself about Dataside, out loud, before you hear his answer. Reading it silently does nothing for this.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
Roger Martin — “5 Essential Questions to Craft a Winning Strategy”

The strategist who built his whole method on the idea this lesson practises: never evaluate one option, always build several and ask what would have to be true for each. Long, so take it in pieces — the vocabulary is exactly the vocabulary of today.

Tip: the first thirty minutes are enough. Write down his question — what would have to be true — and apply it to the three columns of the scenario page you saw in class.
