Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
How I Built This -- Whole Foods Market: John Mackey

The founder of an American grocery chain telling the story of the company from one shop to hundreds of stores. It is the same answer you practiced today, given for forty minutes: what the business is, how big it has become, and how long he has been at it.

Tip: listen to the first ten minutes only. Write down every sentence where he says how long something has been going on, and count how many use "have been" plus -ing.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
CNBC -- "How Costco Became A Massive Members Only Retailer"

An American business documentary about one of the retail chains you would meet at a fair in Chicago: how it buys, how it prices, and why it keeps so few products on the shelf. The vocabulary of this lesson runs through the whole piece.

Tip: watch it once with English subtitles and once without. Note three words you already knew in reading but had never heard said out loud.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
TED -- Casey Brown, "Know your worth, and then ask for it" (9 min)

A pricing consultant on why the people who deliver the most are often the worst at saying what they deliver. It is the argument behind the whole lesson: the track record only counts if you can say it out loud, in one sentence, to somebody who has never heard of you.

Tip: it is nine minutes and she speaks clearly. Try it with no subtitles first, then repeat the one sentence you would use about your own company.
