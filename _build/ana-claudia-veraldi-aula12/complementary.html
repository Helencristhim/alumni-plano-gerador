Lesson 12 -- If I Have Time This Weekend

Video
Paige Wassel -- "I went to the largest flea market in the US, The Brimfield Antique Fair!"

An outdoor antiques fair the size of a small town, filmed over one long day: the early start, the tables, the hunt for one good piece, and the constant question of what happens to everything if the weather turns.

Tip: pause every time she says what she will do next and rebuild the sentence with a condition, out loud: "If I find one, I will buy it."

Listening
NPR Life Kit -- "How to make decisions you won't regret"

A decision coach explains why people regret the time they lose deciding far more than the decisions they make quickly, and gives two exercises for the choices that keep sitting on your table. The full transcript is free on the same page.

Tip: listen once without reading, then read along and underline every sentence that puts a condition on a future action.

Talk
TED -- Ruth Chang, "How to make hard choices"

Fifteen minutes on the decisions where there is no right answer, only two good options and a person who has to pick one. She argues that hard choices are not a problem to solve but a place where you decide who you are.

Tip: open the transcript on the same page and read her first two minutes out loud after her. Copy the pauses, not only the words.
