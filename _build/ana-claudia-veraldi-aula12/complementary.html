Lesson 12 -- If I Have Time This Weekend

Documentary
Just how orderly are the Germans? -- Meet the Germans, DW

A British presenter who lives in Berlin tests the famous German love of rules, order and planning. It is the other side of Katrin in tonight’s dialogue: why a plan, once it is made, is taken so seriously.

Tip: listen for how often a plan is described as fixed. Every time you hear one, say the same idea as a first conditional: if you invite a German, she will write it down.

Podcast
Life Kit -- The science of making and keeping friends (NPR)

A psychologist talks about what keeps friendships alive, including the friend who flakes at the last minute again and again, and what to say when it happens.

Tip: write down every sentence with if you hear. Then notice how many of them are advice, with an imperative in the second half: if a friend cancels, tell her how you feel.

Talk
How to make hard choices -- Ruth Chang, TED

A philosopher explains why some decisions feel impossible: not because one option is better, but because they are different. A calm, clear talk about everyday choices, from what to do on a Saturday to where to live.

Tip: pause after each example and say what YOU will do in that situation, with if or unless. It turns a talk into a speaking exercise.
