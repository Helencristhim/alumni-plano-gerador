Lesson 16 -- What the Market Report Says

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Clear communication"

A short episode on saying complicated things simply at work. It is exactly the move from the report to the call: the same fact, with fewer and lighter words. Full script on the same page.

Tip: read the script and find two sentences that use a heavy noun. Rewrite each one with a verb, the way you would say it on a call.

Talk
David McCandless -- "The beauty of data visualization" (TED, 18 min)

A designer shows how a mountain of numbers becomes one picture that people understand in seconds. The same idea as your one-slide, one-number rule for a partner call.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Pause on one chart and describe it in two sentences: one in report style with a noun, one in call style with a verb.

Watch on TED ↗
Documentary
The Social Dilemma (2020)

Engineers and investors from big tech companies explain, with data and simple words, how their products collect and use information. A good model of turning numbers and research into sentences anybody can follow.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Each time an expert gives a number or a trend, say it again in one sentence with a verb, the way you would say it on a call.
