Talk
TED -- Margaret Heffernan, "Dare to disagree" (13 min)

The argument for the person who walks into your office and tells you the schedule is not real, made by somebody who ran five companies. It is the theory behind every good answer to a behavioral question about conflict, and it takes thirteen minutes.

Tip: after the talk, name out loud, in English, the person in your own company who disagrees with you most often. If you cannot think of one, that is the finding.
