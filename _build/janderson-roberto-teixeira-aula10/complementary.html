"Is it OK to disagree?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes about saying what you think when the other person thinks something else. Connection to Lesson 10: advice and disagreement use the same softeners, and this episode is full of them. Audio, vocabulary and full transcript are free on the page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then note every time a speaker softens an opinion instead of stating it flat. Count them -- there are more than you expect in six minutes.

"How to give advice in English" -- British Council

Five minutes on exactly today's structures, with real dialogue rather than a table: should, ought to, had better, and the softeners that make advice easier to hear. Connection to Lesson 10: it shows the same advice at three weights, spoken, so you hear the difference instead of reading about it.

Tip: pause every time somebody gives advice and produce your own version first, using a different strength from the one they use.

"Making suggestions is easy" -- English at Work, BBC Learning English

Four and a half minutes of office drama in which somebody has to suggest a course of action to a boss who has already decided. Connection to Lesson 10: it is the same problem you will have with a client -- being clear without being heavy -- and the phrases are the ones you drilled in class.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, write down the three phrases you would actually use next week, and say each one out loud.
