Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Julian Treasure: “How to speak so that people want to listen” (10 min)

A sound expert on why some voices make a room lean in and others make it check the phone. Register, pace, pitch and silence: the parts of a first impression that are not the words.

Tip: watch it once. Then pick the vocal exercise he does at the end and do it right before your next global call. Two minutes, every time.

Talk
Stanford GSB: “Think Fast, Talk Smart: Communication Techniques” (Matt Abrahams)

A lecturer from Stanford on exactly what happens when your mind goes blank in front of important people: why it happens, and simple ways to keep talking when somebody asks you something you did not prepare.

Tip: watch the first twenty minutes on Monday or Tuesday. Pick one technique and use it in class on Wednesday, when the CFO interrupts you.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Video
Rachel’s English: “ED Endings”, part 1

The exact sound you practiced in class: why worked has one beat, joined ends in a soft D, and only started gets an extra syllable. Short, clear, American.

Tip: after the video, say your career in five sentences with five past verbs. Record it, listen, and count how many -ed endings you gave an extra syllable by mistake.
