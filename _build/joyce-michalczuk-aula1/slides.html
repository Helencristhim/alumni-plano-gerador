Real Speed

One Sound, Not One Syllable

Most -ed endings are not an extra syllable. Listen, then say it back at the same speed.

I worked there for six years.
I joined the company in 2019.
I moved into a management role.
I started my career at Deloitte.

Three Versions

Sixty, Thirty, Ten

The same introduction, three times. Every round, half the time.

60 seconds
Now → how you got here → why it matters to the people listening.
30 seconds
Now, and one piece of proof. Cut the rest.
10 seconds
One sentence. You are joining the call late.
