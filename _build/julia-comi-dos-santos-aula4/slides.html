Live from the careers fair

1. What is happening at the biggest stand?
The queue is getting longer and they are only taking CVs, not talking.
2. What are the small companies doing?
They are talking to every student, and two are interviewing on the spot.

Go to the small stands first, because nobody is waiting there.
