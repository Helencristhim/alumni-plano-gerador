Chapter 3: Was or Were?

Today and Before

am, is, are — in the past

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am in Miami today."

"I was in Orlando in 2019."

"The weather was very hot."

"My wife and my daughter were with me."

NowBeforeExample
I am / he is / it iswasI was in Orlando. It was hot.
you are / we are / they arewereWe were at a theme park.
negativewasn't / weren'tIt wasn't cold. The parks weren't quiet.
questionWas...? / Were...?Was it hot? Were you in New York?

am, is = was · are = were. The same rule as lesson 1, in the past.

Now and Then

Change the Verb

"It is hot today. It was hot in Orlando."

is → was

"We are in Miami. We were in New York."

are → were

"It wasn't cold."

was not = wasn't

"The parks weren't quiet."

were not = weren't

Short Answers

Was It? Were You?

"Were you in New York? Yes, I was."

you → I was

"Was it expensive? Yes, it was."

it → it was

"Was the weather bad? No, it wasn't."

no → wasn't

"Were the parks crowded? Yes, they were."

they → they were
