Your Words at Work

Which Word Do You Need?

1. She said something surprising and you want her to continue.
Really. "Really? Three months?"
2. The sentence is not clear, but the conversation is good.
Repeat. "Sorry, can you repeat that, please?"
3. You want to give her the floor and keep quiet for a while.
Question. "Can I ask you a question? Where are you from?"
4. She asks something you do not want to answer.
Maybe. "Maybe. I am not sure yet." — and then a question back.
Chapter 3: What, Where, Who

Three Words and One Helper

what · where · who · + do you

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Where do you live?"

"What do you do?"

"Who do you travel with?"

"Where are you from?"

Question wordAsks aboutExample
What + do youa thing, or a jobWhat do you do? What do you like?
Where + do youa placeWhere do you live? Where do you work?
Who + do youa personWho do you travel with?
With to be, no dothe verb is already thereWhere are you from? What is your name?

Portuguese has nothing in the middle: onde você mora. English puts do you there, and it never changes. Treat do you as ONE piece that clicks in after the question word.

Seven questions. None of them is about you — and that is the point.

1. She answers in one word and stops. What is your next question?
A question she cannot answer with one word: What do you like about it? Why?
2. She asks you something you do not want to answer.
Maybe. I am not sure yet. — And you? Do you travel a lot?
3. You lost the thread completely, and she is still talking.
Sorry, I lost you. Can you repeat that, please?

Chapter 5: Real Speed

Two Hours, One Seat Free

the flight is delayed and nobody is going anywhere

Dialogue

Is This Seat Free?

L
Excuse me, is this seat free?
J
Yes, please. Two hours, they said. Two hours!
L
Two hours. Where are you from?
J
Ireland. A small town near Cork, but I live in Dublin now. And you?
L
Brazil, São Paulo. Really? Ireland. What do you do there?
J
I work with horses. And now I am going to Argentina for three months, to work on a farm.
L
Three months? That is interesting. And then?
J
Then home, I think. Or maybe not. And what about you? Do you travel a lot for work?
L
Yes, a lot. I have a company in Brazil. Maybe twenty countries. Who do you travel with?
J
Alone, always. My sister says I am impossible. She is probably correct, to be fair.
L
Sorry, I lost you. Can you repeat that, please?
Comprehension

What Did Julia Say?

1. Where is Julia from, and where is she now?
She is from a small town near Cork, in Ireland. Now she is in Dublin.
2. What is her work, and where is she next?
Her work is with horses. Next: Argentina, for three months, on a farm.
3. Who is with her when she travels?
Nobody. She is always alone.

0:000:00

Ten fifteen — two hours and fifteen minutes.
2. Why is the gate the right place to stay?
Because with better weather the flight is earlier.
3. Who is needed at the desk now?
Anybody with a connection after this flight.
Listening 2

One Question, Twenty Minutes

He asked one thing, and she did the rest. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. How many questions are enough at the beginning?
Only one: where are you from? And she is there for twenty minutes.
2. How much is clear for him, and is that a problem?
Maybe half. That is not a problem, because his reactions are at the right moments.
3. How are the two hours for him?
They are not long, and his phone is in his pocket.

Five Questions in Five Minutes

Scenario: The flight is delayed and you sit next to a stranger. Ask five questions in five minutes, and react to every answer. You do not have to talk about yourself.

Keywords:

Role-Play — Semi-free

The Person Who Answers in One Word

Scenario: The person next to you answers everything with one word. Find the questions that make her talk — the ones she cannot answer with yes, no, or a place.

Keywords:

What do you like about...? Why? And then? Tell me more
Role-Play — Free

Two Hours, and You Cannot Go Quiet

Scenario: Three minutes with the person next to you, and one rule: never more than ten seconds of silence. Ask, react, ask again — and answer only what you want to answer.

No keywords this time. Different from the model is not wrong — if she kept talking, it worked.
