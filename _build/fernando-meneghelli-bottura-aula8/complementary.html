Lesson 8 -- How Do You Evaluate Your Team?

Grammar Video
"Future perfect and future continuous" -- BBC Learning English, English Class

The two tenses from this lesson side by side: finished BEFORE a date, and happening AT a date. Connection to Lesson 8: this is the grammar that turns "I will assess the team" into the sentence an HR director writes down.

Tip: after each example, build one about the twelve-month plan you would take to a real interview. Two with "by the end of...", one with "this time next year...". Say them out loud, and put a real number in at least one.

Talk with transcript
"The secret to giving great feedback" -- LeeAnn Renninger, TED (5 min)

Five minutes and a four-part formula for saying something difficult to somebody who works for you. Connection to Lesson 8: a panel that asks how you evaluate a team is really asking how you hold somebody to account without losing them, and this is the mechanics of that conversation. Full transcript beside the video.

Tip: take one real conversation you had to have with a direct report and rewrite it out loud on her four steps. Five minutes of watching, ten of speaking — the speaking is where the value is.

Leadership Talk
"Why good leaders make you feel safe" -- Simon Sinek, TED (12 min)

Twelve minutes on what people actually answer to, from a speaker whose English is deliberately slow and very clearly enunciated — which makes it useful listening as well as useful content. Connection to Lesson 8: the third criterion in your answer, the one you cannot measure, is exactly what he is describing from the other side.

Tip: listen once at normal speed with no transcript. Then write, in English, the two sentences you would use if a panel asked what kind of leader you are. Two sentences only, and no adjectives you could not evidence.
