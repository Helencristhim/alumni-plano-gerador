Lesson 24 -- Review: Travel and Business

Talk
Pico Iyer -- "Where is home?" (TED)

A travel writer who has lived in many countries talks about airports, long trips and what home means for people who travel all the time. Calm, clear English.

Tip: write down three words about travel that you hear, and use one of them to describe your last business trip.

Talk
Ernesto Sirolli -- "Want to help someone? Shut up and listen!" (TED)

A funny and honest talk about working with people from other cultures: before you help a partner, you need to listen. Full of stories about visits that went wrong.

Tip: choose one story from the talk and retell it in two sentences with said or told.

Talk
Julian Treasure -- "5 ways to listen better" (TED)

A sound expert gives five simple exercises to listen better. Useful for small talk, for feedback and for every conversation with a visitor.

Tip: try one of the five exercises this week, and tell your teacher what you noticed.
