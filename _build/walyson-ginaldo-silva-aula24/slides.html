Chapter 4: The Toolkit

Three Tools, One Visit

Review Discovery

Which Tool Is It?

"It’s always this hot in Santos, isn’t it?"

"Since the visit, he has been sending us questions every day."

"He told us that his customers wanted more information."

"We have been working on a checklist for future visits."

Look at the blue words. Which tool is it, and what is its job in the conversation?

ToolJobFormExample
Question tagskeep the conversation going, check informationpositive sentence + negative tag · negative sentence + positive tagThe flight was long, wasn’t it?
Present perfect continuousan action that started in the past and is still goinghave / has + been + -ing (+ for / since)We have been preparing the visit since Monday.
Reported speechtell someone what another person saidsaid (that) / told + person (that) + one tense backShe told me that the venue was perfect.
said or told?told needs a personsaid that... · told me / him / us that...He said that... · He told us that...

In one line: a tag keeps the talk going, have been + -ing tells what is still happening, and said / told brings someone else’s words into the room.

Listening 1 · A Visit Planner

Three Rules for Hosting

A planner of business visits on how to host a foreign client. Sound first — no text.

1. Why should you not plan an important meeting on the first morning?
Because many visitors arrive after a long layover, with bad jet lag.
2. What is the real problem with a hiccup, in her opinion?
Not the hiccup itself, but having no plan B.
3. When should the team have the debrief?
In the same week as the visit, while everything is fresh.
Listening 2 · A Trader

The Lost Reservation

A trader remembers the night a visit almost went wrong. Sound first — no text.

1. What did the restaurant manager say?
He said that they had no record of the reservation.
2. What did the buyer tell him on the last day?
She told him that the dinner on the beach was her favorite moment of the trip.
3. What has she been doing since that visit?
She has been buying coffee from them every season.
