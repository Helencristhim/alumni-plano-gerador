Lesson theme: logistics and shipping -- freight forwarder, carrier, bill of lading, lead time, customs clearance, demurrage, consignment, pallet, reroute, expedite; and the passive with modals ("The container must be cleared", "It should have been sent"). Passive exposure outside class -- tick each one when you finish it.

Talk
Rose George -- Inside the secret shipping industry

A journalist who spent five weeks on a container ship on what really happens between the port where goods are loaded and the port where they are released. Almost everything you buy made this trip.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down every sentence that says what must be done, can be done or should have been done to a ship or a container.

The story of the standard shipping container, told by following a real box from one port to another. Natural American English, with the transcript on the page.
