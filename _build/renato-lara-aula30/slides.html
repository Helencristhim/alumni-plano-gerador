Grammar Discovery

What happened?

The contest was held at Margaret River.
Finn was beaten by Renato.
The last wave was given an 8.10 by the judges.
Ethan and Noah were eliminated.

What do the orange words have in common? Is the person who did the action important?

past passive: was / were + past participle (+ by + the person)

Use it when the action or the result is more important than the person who did it. Add by only when the person is important.

The Code

Active or passive?

Active: who did it

The judges gave the wave an 8.10.
Renato beat Finn.
Dana took the photo.
The club held the contest.

Passive: what happened

The wave was given an 8.10.
Finn was beaten by Renato.
The photo was taken by Dana.
The contest was held on Sunday.

one thing: was · two or more: were · negative: wasn't / weren't · question: Was it ...? Were they ...?

Sound Check

Weak was

The wave was given an eight.

"was" is weak: wuz-GIV-en

Two surfers were eliminated.

"eliminated" ends in an "id" sound: e-LIM-i-nat-id

The contest was held on Sunday.

the strong word is "held", not "was"
