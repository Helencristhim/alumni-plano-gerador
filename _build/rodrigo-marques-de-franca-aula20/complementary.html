Lesson 20 -- Rights and Obligations

Video
Written Legal English -- "Do Lawyers Need to Use Legalese?" (YouTube)

A legal English trainer asks whether contracts really need words like shall, hereby and thereof, and shows how the same duty can be written in plain English without losing precision. A good companion to the second listening.

Tip: each time an example appears on screen, say out loud who owes the duty, and whether the sentence makes that clear.

Talk
Philip K. Howard -- "Four ways to fix a broken legal system" (TED)

An American lawyer argues that too many rules, written to cover every case, stop people from using common sense. Clear, persuasive English from a lawyer speaking to a general audience.

Tip: write down three rules he quotes, and rewrite each one as a contract clause with shall be or may be.
