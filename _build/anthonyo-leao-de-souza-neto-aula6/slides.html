Near. "Is it near here, or do I need a taxi?"
2. The person says a lot and you only caught one word: the direction.

Chapter 3: In, On, Next To

Small Words That Place Everything

in · on · next to · near · opposite

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The restaurant is on Market Street."

"The pharmacy is next to the coffee shop."

"It is opposite the bank."

"The taxis are in front of the hotel."

WordWhere exactlyExample
on + streetsomewhere along that streetIt is on Market Street.
in + a place with wallsinside the hotel, the room, the carHe is in the hotel.
next toright beside it, same sideNext to the coffee shop.
oppositeface to face — you cross the streetOpposite the bank.
near / in front ofclose · right outside the doorNear the park. In front of the hotel.

Portuguese uses one word for two of these: na rua and no hotel. English does not — it is ON the street and IN the hotel. There is no logic. It is use.

Dialogue

Excuse Me, Where Is the Station?

L
Excuse me. Where is the station, please?
G
The station? Go straight to the end of this street, then take the second left.
L
Is it near here, or do I need a taxi?
G
It is about fifteen minutes on foot. With that bag, I would take a taxi.
L
Sorry, can you say that again? The second what?
G
The second left. Not the first one — the first one is very small.
L
So, straight to the end, and then the second left. Is that right?
G
Almost. Straight, second left, and then the station is on your right, opposite a big hotel.
L
Opposite a hotel. Perfect. Can you show me on the map, please?
G
Of course. We are here, and the station is here. See? Two corners, that is all.
L
Thank you very much. You are very kind.
Comprehension

What Did Grace Say?

1. What is the way to the station?
Straight to the end of the street, then the second left. The station is on the right, opposite a big hotel.
2. How long is it on foot, and what does she advise?
About fifteen minutes. With that bag, she would take a taxi.
3. Why does she say "not the first one"?
Because the first street on the left is very small.
