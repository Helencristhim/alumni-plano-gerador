Chapter 3: The Code

Have You Ever... When Did You...?

Your life so far, and one specific day

Grammar Discovery

Which Sentences Have a Date?

"I’ve been to Edinburgh twice."

"I went there for New Year in 2019."

"I’ve never seen the castle."

"It snowed for three days, and everything closed."

Look at the blue sentences: do they say WHEN? No. They talk about your life until now. Now look at the red sentences: they have a time (in 2019, for three days) or they tell one specific story. Blue = have + past participle. Red = the simple past.

JobFormExample
Life experience, no datehave / has + past participle“I’ve been to Peru.”
Asking about experienceHave you ever + past participle?“Have you ever been to Scotland?”
Never, until nowhave never + past participle“I’ve never seen the castle.”
A specific time or storysimple past“I went there in 2019. It snowed.”
Asking for detailsWhen / Where / How did you...?“When did you go?”
The trapa past time never goes with have + participleNot “I have been there in 2019.” Say I went there in 2019.

In one line: no date = I have been; a date or a story = I went.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

Scotland at Real Speed

One phone call, three recordings, one booking

Dialogue

A Call to Edinburgh

F
Good afternoon, Old Town Tours, this is Fiona. How can I help you?
M
Hi, Fiona. I’m planning a trip to Edinburgh with my family, in April, and I’d like a guided tour.
F
Lovely. Have you ever been to Edinburgh before?
M
Yes, I have. I went for New Year, but there was a blizzard. The castle was closed to visitors, and we saw almost nothing.
F
Oh no, what a shame. Well, April is a great time. It’s not as crowded as the summer, and the gardens are beautiful.
M
That’s exactly what I want. Do I need to book in advance?
F
For the castle, yes. I’ve done this tour hundreds of times, and the view from the top is still breathtaking.
M
Perfect. This time I want to make the most of every day.
Comprehension

About Fiona

1. What is Fiona’s job?
She works for a tour company in Edinburgh: she organizes and guides tours.
2. Why does Fiona say April is a good time?
It is not as crowded as the summer, and the gardens are beautiful.
3. How many times has Fiona done the castle tour?
Hundreds of times, and she still thinks the view is breathtaking.
Listening 1 · A Tour Guide

Edinburgh in Every Season

A tour guide from Edinburgh explains when to visit the city. Sound first — no text.

1. What does she say about the city in August?
It is very crowded, because of the festival, and hotels are expensive.
2. Why does she like the spring?
The days are longer, the parks are green and the streets are calm.
3. What is her advice for the winter?
Bring warm clothes and check the opening hours, because some places close early.
Listening 2 · A Travel Podcast

The Trip That Went Wrong

An American travel podcaster tells the story of a trip that did not go as planned. Sound first — no text.

1. Where did he go, and what happened?
To Iceland; a storm closed the roads, so he stayed in the hotel for two days.
2. What did he do in the hotel?
He talked to other travelers and listened to their stories.
3. What is his lesson about bad trips?
Sometimes the trip that goes wrong is the one you remember the most.
Listening 3 · An Experienced Traveler

Why I Travel in the Off-Season

An Australian man explains why he never travels in the high season anymore. Sound first — no text.

1. Why did he stop traveling in the high season?
Everything was crowded and expensive, and he spent hours in lines.
2. What does he say about prices in the off-season?
Hotels and flights are much cheaper.
3. What is the one thing he always books in advance?
The places with a limited number of visitors, like famous museums or castles.
