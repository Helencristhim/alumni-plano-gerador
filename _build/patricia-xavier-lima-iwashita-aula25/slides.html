Chapter 3: Language Focus

The Person Who...

relative clauses — who / which / that / whose / where

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Richard is the sponsor who took over in February."

"This is the workflow that saves eleven minutes per order."

"He is a man whose calendar is full from eight to eight."

"The benchmark, which we update every quarter, is very clear."

Richard does not know any of these people or things. What does the green part do for him?

StructureUseExample
whoFor peoplethe sponsor who took over
whichFor things; always after a commathe benchmark, which we update
thatPeople or things, only without commasthe workflow that saves time
whosePossession: his, her, theira man whose calendar is full
whereFor placesthe warehouse where the team saves time

No commas = the clause tells you which one. Commas = extra information, and then only who or which — never that. Never repeat the pronoun: not "the report which I sent it".

Chapter 4: The Business Review

Twenty Minutes, One Stranger

Richard asks — and every answer says which one

Dialogue

Start With the Headline

R
Thank you for coming, Patrícia. I have twenty minutes, and I have never seen a review from your team. Start with the headline.
P
The headline is this: Northwind is a customer whose usage has come back. The two teams that had stopped using the platform are active again.
R
Good. And remind me who you are. David mentioned you, but I do not know your role.
P
I am the Customer Success Manager who looks after your account. I am the person you call when something is not working.
R
Fine. I am the decision-maker on this renewal, so give me one number that I can use with my board.
P
The workflow that your warehouse team uses now takes eleven minutes less per order. That is where most of the cost savings come from.
R
And the benchmark? How do we compare with companies like us?
P
Your efficiency is above the benchmark for logistics companies, which is the strongest argument for the renewal.
Comprehension

About Richard

1. How much time does Richard have, and what does he ask for first?
Twenty minutes. He asks for the headline first.
2. What does Richard say about his role in the renewal?
He is the decision-maker, so he needs one number that he can use with his board.
3. What does Richard want to compare Northwind with?
The benchmark — companies like Northwind, in logistics.
