Formula 1: Drive to Survive — Season 1, Episode 1

The team radio is the closest thing on television to the call you described: several people speaking at once, half of it clipped, and decisions taken from the fragments. English at full speed, from British, Italian and Australian speakers in the same conversation.

Tip: pick one radio exchange and write down only the four things — numbers, dates or laps, names, and who has to do what. Watch it once, not twice.

Question tags — 6 Minute Grammar (BBC)

Six minutes on the tag from this lesson, including the intonation: falling when you are confirming something you believe, rising when you genuinely do not know. On a call, that difference does more work than the grammar.

Tip: say each example twice, once falling and once rising, and notice how the second one invites an answer. That is the version you want when you are checking a date.
