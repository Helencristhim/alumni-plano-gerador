Chapter 4: Language Focus

Before, Now, Next

the structures of the program in one story

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"In January, she managed a portfolio of mid-size accounts."

"Since August, she has managed our twelve premium accounts."

"If we had talked to finance earlier, we would have kept them."

"By December, she will have presented at the conference."

Four structures from this program. Which green words talk about a finished past, a result until now, a past that did not happen, and a future deadline?

StructureUseExample
Past simpleA finished moment in the pastIn January, she managed one portfolio.
Present perfectFrom the past until nowSince August, she has managed twelve accounts.
Third conditionalA past that did not happenIf we had called earlier, we would have kept them.
Future perfectFinished by a date in the futureBy December, she will have presented.
Contrast linkersTwo opposite ideas togetherAlthough the year started badly, it ended well.

Your story in English needs all of them: what happened, what has changed, what you learned, and where you are going.

Chapter 5: The Conversation

Talking to Mark

The VP of Customer Success has fifteen minutes for you

Dialogue

Tell Me About Your Year

M
Patrícia, congratulations. I read Karen's performance review. It is one of the best this year.
P
Thank you, Mark. I also wrote my self-assessment, and honestly, it was a year of big changes.
M
What was your biggest breakthrough?
P
Meetings. A year ago I only answered questions. Since then, I have run business reviews and budget calls in English.
M
And the hardest moment? Every year has a setback.
P
Harbor Health. If we had talked to their finance team earlier, we would have kept them. That lesson is now a team rule.
M
That is why this promotion is well-deserved. Going forward, what do you need from us?
P
Time to keep up my English, and one more step out of my comfort zone: the global conference.
