Lesson 32 -- Looking Back, Looking Ahead

Film
Hidden Figures (2016)

Three brilliant women at NASA in the 1960s step up in rooms where nobody expects them to speak, and slowly make their work impossible to ignore. Connection to Lesson 32: a story about stepping out of your comfort zone, about setbacks and breakthroughs — and about confidence that does not depend on the day.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. After the film, describe one of the characters in five sentences: before, now and next, with the structures of this program.
