Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
The Baby-Sitters Club — Season 1, Episode 1 "Kristy's Great Idea" (Netflix)

Four school friends start a small business, and every scene is full of school, families and whose idea is whose. Light, funny and easy to watch with your daughters.

Tip: watch it with your daughters, with English subtitles on. Every time somebody says my, his, her or a name with 's, pause and repeat it.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "How Reading Aloud Can Help You Bond With Your Kids"

Practical ideas for reading with children when the week is busy: how to choose a book, how long to read, and how to make it fun and not a chore.

Tip: the page has the full transcript. Find five phrases with your, their or a name with 's, and say each one about your own family.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Rita Pierson — "Every kid needs a champion" (TED)

A teacher with forty years in the classroom talks, with a lot of humor, about what makes children proud of their work. Warm, fast and very easy to love.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Afterwards, say one thing your daughters are proud of, and one thing you are proud of in their school.
