Chapter 3: The Code

Must · Have to · Should · Can

Obligation, recommendation, possibility

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The client must file the return by the deadline."

"Every financial holding has to report the new tax base."

"The client should consider the new framework this year."

"We can restructure the exposure before the vote."

Which sentences are the law, which one is your advice, and which one is an option?

ModalMeaningExample
must + base verbA strong obligation, often written in the law itselfThey must file the return by March.
have to / has to + base verbAn obligation that comes from outside — a rule, a client, a deadlineEvery holding has to report the tax base.
should + base verbYour professional recommendation — not obligatoryYou should review the structure now.
can + base verbPossibility, option, or what your firm is able to doWe can restructure the exposure.
Negativemust not = it is prohibited · do not have to = it is not necessary. They are opposites, not synonyms.
QuestionDo we have to file this quarter? · Should we wait for the final text? · Can you send the memo today?

No to after must, should and can: "should to consider" does not exist. Only have keeps the to.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"The client should to consider the new framework."
"The client should consider the new framework."
"He musts file the return in March."
"He must file the return in March."
"You must not file this quarter, it is optional."
"You do not have to file this quarter, it is optional."

Modals never take to and never take -s. And remember: must not means it is prohibited, while do not have to means it is simply not necessary.

Chapter 4: The Client Briefing

Meet Renata

CFO of a payments company · ten minutes, one decision

Dialogue

The CFO Briefing

R
Leonardo, thanks for the time. My board meets on Friday and I need one page. What has actually changed for a company like ours?
L
The short version: the tax base is wider, and most of your revenue moves into the new indirect tax. My role involves exactly this kind of company.
R
Wider base. Does that mean our tax liability goes up?
L
Not automatically. We have already assessed the impact for eleven clients, and in four cases the cash flow impact was positive. It depends on your structure.
R
Our structure sits under a financial holding. So what do we have to do, and by when?
L
You must file a return under the new rules from January. Before that, you should review the holding, because that is where we can mitigate risk.
R
And if we wait until the final text is published?
L
Then you lose the planning window. What I recommend is starting now and staying ahead of the curve. I have not seen a client regret that yet.
R
That is the first answer today that cut through the noise. Send me the one page by Thursday.
Comprehension

About Renata

1. Why does she need the answer this week?
Her board meets on Friday and she needs one page.
2. How is her company structured?
It sits under a financial holding.
3. What does she ask about waiting?
What happens if they wait until the final text is published.
4. What does she ask for at the end?
The one page, by Thursday.

The Real Thing

Walk Me Through the Update

Project Status Update
Leonardo Constantino · ALMA
Client: Pagora Pagamentos
Week 32
DoneWe have already assessed the impact on the tax base and the cash flow impact for all three scenarios.
OpenThe client has not approved the review of the financial holding yet.
MustThey must file a return under the current rules by 20 September. The deadline does not move.
AdviceThey should start the holding review this quarter, so that we can mitigate risk before implementation.
Prepared for the finance committee. Figures depend on the final text of the reform.
1. Which line is an obligation, and which one is an opinion?
The Must line is the obligation, because the deadline comes from the law. The Advice line is the opinion, because it uses should.
2. Why does the Open line use "yet"?
Because the approval has not arrived up to now, and it is still expected.
