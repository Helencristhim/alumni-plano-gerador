Series
Suits -- Season 1, Episode 1

A New York law firm where every conversation is a partner explaining what the client must do, what they should do, and what the firm can do about it. Connection to Lesson 3: listen to how the senior lawyers describe their role in one sentence and then move straight to a recommendation.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down four sentences with must, have to, should or can, and mark which one is the law and which one is only advice.

The Indicator from Planet Money -- "What's a revenge tax?"

Nine minutes on a proposed tax on foreign companies and investors, and why the financial sector is nervous about it. Connection to Lesson 3: this is your professional world in plain English -- tax base, tax liability and cross-border payments, explained the way a CFO would want to hear it.

Tip: listen twice. The second time, note every sentence with must, have to, should or can, then write one sentence of your own about the Brazilian reform using the same structure.

BBC Learning English -- "Modal Verbs: Have to and Must" (The Grammar Gameshow, Episode 5)

A short game show that drills exactly the contrast you discovered in class: must, have to, and the trap of must not against do not have to. Connection to Lesson 3: it is a second explanation in a different voice, and the negative forms are the ones that cost a tax lawyer the most.

Tip: pause before each answer and say your own version first. Then write two sentences about a client of yours: one obligation with must, one recommendation with should.
