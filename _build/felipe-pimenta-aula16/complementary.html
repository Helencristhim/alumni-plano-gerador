Lesson 16 -- Milestone Review

Series
Formula 1: Drive to Survive (Netflix)

Team principals, engineers and drivers running billion-dollar operations under a stopwatch, on camera, in English. Connection to Lesson 16: almost none of them are native speakers, and not one of them is trying to sound like one. This is what holding your own actually looks like at the top of a profession.

Tip: pick one non-native team principal and listen to a whole pit-wall scene. Count the mistakes, then count how much authority he loses because of them.

Podcast
Freakonomics Radio -- How to Become Great at Just About Anything

A free episode with Anders Ericsson, the researcher behind deliberate practice, on why talent explains far less than people assume and why most learners stop improving the moment the work becomes comfortable. Connection to Lesson 16: it is the research behind the plateau you were warned about tonight.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Listen first without it, then read it to check the two or three ideas you were not sure you had caught.

Video
Josh Kaufman -- The First 20 Hours: How to Learn Anything (TEDx)

Twenty minutes on the shape of every learning curve: the steep, ugly beginning, the point where most people quit, and the long incremental stretch that nobody films. Connection to Lesson 16: you are past the ugly part now, and this is the map of the part that comes next.

Tip: watch it once, then write down the three things you will keep doing every week now that the lessons have finished. Keep the note where you will see it in a month.
