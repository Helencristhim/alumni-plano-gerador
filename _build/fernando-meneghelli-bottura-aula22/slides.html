Chapter 3: The Code

Not Only a New Job, but a New Team

Inversion after negative and limiting words

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

“Not only did I accept the offer, but I also asked to meet the team first.”

“Never have I joined a company with such a strong finance team.”

“Only when I have met each of you will I talk about changes.”

“At no point will you hear about a decision from somebody else first.”

Look at the orange words. What comes first, the subject or the auxiliary? And how does each sentence begin?

Opening wordsThenExample
Not onlyauxiliary + subject ..., but ... alsoNot only is this a strong team, but it also closes on time.
Never / Rarely / Seldomauxiliary + subject + verbNever have I read a handover as clear as this one.
At no point / Under no circumstancesauxiliary + subject + verbAt no point will I change a process without asking you.
Only when / Only after + clausethe inversion goes in the SECOND clauseOnly when I have taken stock will I propose changes.
No auxiliary?Add do / does / did: Not only did I accept... (never Not only I accepted). Formal register: one or two in a speech, not in every sentence.
Practice

Auxiliary First

Say it first, then click to check

"Not only ___does this team close the month in five days, but it also does it without overtime."
"Never ___have I read a handover as honest as this one."
"Only when I have taken stock ___will I propose any changes."
"At no point ___did the team complain about the late nights."
The Move

Recognize, Listen, Promise

1. Recognize the team

“Not only is this a strong team, but it is also a team that closes on time.”

2. Say what you will do first

“Only when I have met each of you will I talk about changes.”

3. Make one promise

“At no point will you hear about a decision from somebody else first.”

The first speech is short. The team will judge you by the first month, not by the first five minutes.

The First One-to-One

B
Welcome, Fernando. I hope the onboarding has not been all paperwork.
F
Not all of it. But never have I signed so many forms before lunch.
B
That is the security team, I am afraid. So, where would you like to start?
F
With you. I have read the handover notes, but I would rather hear it from you. What slows the team down?
B
Honestly, the month-end close. The bottleneck is the approvals from the regional offices. We lose two days every month waiting for them.
F
That is useful. And how is morale?
B
Not great. People work late at the end of every month, and nobody has asked them about it for a long time.
F
Then let me be clear about one thing. At no point will I change that process without talking to the people who run it.
B
That would be new. The team will want to hear it from you at the town hall on Thursday.
F
They will. And not only will I say it, but I will also come back in a month and show them what we did.
