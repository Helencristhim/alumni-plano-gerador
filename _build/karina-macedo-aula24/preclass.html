Handling Problems -- Complaints and Solutions

Responda a um cliente com um problema: peça desculpas (I'm sorry about..., I apologize for...) e ofereça uma solução (Let me..., I'll...). Sete palavras de problemas: complaint, delay, mistake, damaged, refund, solution, apologize.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. São palavras para resolver problemas com clientes. Toque Listen para ouvir. (Listen = Ouvir)

Complaint -- reclamação (a message that says you are not happy)
"Robert sent a complaint about the delivery." (O Robert mandou uma reclamação sobre a entrega.)
Delay -- atraso (when something comes late)
"I am sorry about the delay." (Sinto muito pelo atraso.)
Mistake -- erro (something that is not correct)
"There is a mistake in the invoice." (Tem um erro na fatura.)
Damaged -- danificado (broken, not in good condition)
"One box arrived damaged." (Uma caixa chegou danificada.)
Refund -- reembolso (money back to the customer)
"We will give you a refund for the box." (Vamos te dar um reembolso pela caixa.)
Solution -- solução (the answer to a problem)
"That is a good solution." (Essa é uma boa solução.)
Apologize -- pedir desculpas (to say I am sorry)
"I apologize for the mistake." (Peço desculpas pelo erro.)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Leia o texto da Karina e responda às perguntas. (Read Karina's text and answer.)

Today my client Robert sent a complaint. There is a delay with his new jet, and there is a mistake in the invoice. One box of spare parts arrived damaged, too. I called him and said: "I'm sorry about the delay. I apologize for the mistake." Then I offered a solution: "Let me check the new date with the factory. I'll send you a new invoice today, and we will give you a refund for the box." At the end I said: "It won't happen again. I'll call you on Monday."

1. What are Robert's problems? (Quais são os problemas do Robert?)

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- I'm sorry about... / Let me... / I'll...

Grammar

Pedir desculpas e oferecer uma solução / Say sorry and offer a solution.

Form / FormaUse / UsoExample / Exemplo
I'm sorry about + problemsinto muito por...I'm sorry about the delay. (Sinto muito pelo atraso.)
I apologize for + problempeço desculpas por... (mais formal)I apologize for the mistake. (Peço desculpas pelo erro.)
Let me + verbdeixa eu... (ajuda agora)Let me check the date. (Deixa eu verificar a data.)
I'll + verbeu vou... (promessa)I'll send you a new invoice. (Vou te mandar uma fatura nova.)
won't + verbnão vai... (promessa negativa)It won't happen again. (Não vai acontecer de novo.)

Dica: sorry about, apologize for. Depois de Let me e I'll, o verbo vem sem "to" (Let me check, I'll send). / Tip: no "to" after Let me and I'll.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Escolha a melhor resposta para cada situação.

A client says: "The jet is two weeks late!" (O cliente diz que o jato está duas semanas atrasado.)
A "I'm sorry of the delay with your jet. Let me to check the date."
B "I'm sorry about the delay. Let me check the date."
C "Sorry. Is not my problem."
The invoice has the wrong price. You promise a new one. (Você promete uma fatura nova.)
