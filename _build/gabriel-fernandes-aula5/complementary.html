Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Present perfect and past simple - 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes from BBC Learning English on the exact pair you learned today. Two native speakers build both forms out loud and hand you the signal words, which are the fastest way into the rule.

Tip: every time they say a signal word, decide out loud whether it closes the door or keeps it open. Do that before they explain, not after.

Video
'I've done' or 'I did'? Ask BBC Learning English

A short answer to the one question this lesson exists to settle, sent in by a learner who had exactly the same doubt you had in the warm-up. Watch it after the podcast, when the rule is already in your head and you only want it confirmed.

Tip: pause before the answer and say which one you would use. If you are right, that is the lesson working. If you are not, you have found the sentence to bring to class.

Documentary
How to make your UCAS personal statement stand out | UCAS Application Tips

From UCAS, the body that handles every university application in the United Kingdom. The form is British and your target is American, but the craft is the same one Claire described: one story told properly beats a list of everything you have done.

Tip: count how many times they say some version of "be specific". Then open your own draft and find the sentence that is least specific, and rewrite that one first.
