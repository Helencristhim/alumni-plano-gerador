Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

These are words you already know. Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Itinerary -- a plan with the dates, times, and places of a trip
"I printed my itinerary for the trip to Lyon."
Layover -- a stop between two flights
"I have a two-hour layover in Paris."
Voucher -- a paper or code you can use instead of money
"The airline gave me a voucher for dinner."
Late checkout -- leaving the hotel room later than the normal time
"My flight is at night, so I asked for a late checkout."
Complimentary -- free, offered by the hotel or the restaurant
"Breakfast is complimentary for business guests."
Receipt -- a paper that shows what you paid
"Always keep the receipt for the taxi."
Landmark -- a famous building or place in a city
"The basilica on the hill is a famous landmark in Lyon."
Small talk -- light, friendly conversation about easy topics
"We made small talk about the weather before the meeting."
Baggage allowance -- the weight or number of bags you can take for free
"My baggage allowance is one bag of twenty-three kilos."
Noise complaint -- a message to say that a place is too loud
"I made a noise complaint at one in the morning."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Lucimara used to feel nervous before international trips. Now she has a plan. Her itinerary is ready: this time next month, she will be flying to Lyon, with a short layover in Paris. "If the connection is delayed, I will ask for a voucher," she says.

She already sent an email to the hotel. She would like to have her suit pressed before the fair, and she asked for a late checkout on Sunday. The receptionist said that breakfast was complimentary. "The hotel is near a famous landmark, so the view must be beautiful," she thinks. "If I had one more day, I would visit the old city."

1. What will Lucimara do if the connection in Paris is delayed?
A She will take a train from Paris to Lyon and ask the airline for a full refund later.
B She will stay in Paris.
C She will ask for a voucher.
2. Who will press Lucimara's suit?
A Lucimara will press it in her room before the fair, because she always travels with an iron.
B The hotel will press it.
C Pierre will press it.
3. Which sentence is about an IMAGINARY situation?
A "If I had one more day, I would visit the old city."
B "If the connection is delayed, I will ask for a voucher."
C "Lucimara used to feel nervous before trips."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Your Grammar Toolkit

GRAMMAR

A review of seven tools from Lessons 11 to 23. No new grammar today.

FormUseExample
used to + verba past habit that is not true now (Lesson 20)I used to hate long flights.
will be + verb-ingan action in progress at a future moment (Lesson 21)At 2 a.m., I will be flying.
have + object + past participlesomeone else does the work for you (Lesson 22)I'd like to have my suit pressed.
must / might / can't + verba smart guess (Lesson 23)The food must be good.
if + present, will + verba real possibility (Lesson 11)If it is late, I'll ask for a voucher.
if + past, would + verban imaginary situation (Lesson 18)If I had time, I would visit Paris.
said that + pastreporting what someone said (Lesson 16)She said that the gate had changed.
RuleTwo common mistakes: no "will" after "if" in a real possibility ("If the flight is late", not "If the flight will be late"), and no "would" after "if" in an imaginary situation ("If I had", not "If I would have").

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the best answer for each real situation on the trip.

Your flight to Lyon is delayed by three hours. You say to the gate agent:
A "Give me a voucher now, because this delay is not my problem and I am very tired."
B "If my flight will be late, I want a voucher."
C "My flight is delayed. Could I get a voucher for a meal?"
It is 1 a.m., and the room next door is very loud. You call the front desk and say:
A "I'm sorry to call so late, but the room next door is very loud."
B "The room next door must to be a party. I can't sleep."
C "Hello. Can you have the room next door cleaned right now, please?"
A buyer at the fair asks: "Is this your first time in Lyon?" You say:
A "Yes."
B "Yes. I used to travel more to the US. If I had more time, I would see the old city."
C "Yes, it is my first time, and I will be leave on Sunday after the fair closes."
At check-out, your company needs proof of payment. You say:
A "Could I have a voucher for my company, please, with all my costs on it?"
B "I want the itinerary."
C "Could I have a receipt, please?"
