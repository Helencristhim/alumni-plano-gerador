Lesson 23 -- Sharing the Workload

Talk
Jason Fried -- "Why work doesn't happen at work" (TED, 15 min)

The founder of a software company explains why meetings and interruptions eat a team's time, and why some meetings should be optional. Clear English and very close to the month-two rules from this lesson.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then say two sentences: one thing a team has to do, and one thing it doesn't have to do.

Talk
Rory Vaden -- "How to multiply your time" (TEDx, 18 min)

A practical talk about what to eliminate, what to automate and what to delegate. A good companion for any manager who does too much alone.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down one task from your week that you could delegate, and say who could do it.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
Margaret Heffernan -- "Forget the pecking order at work" (TED, 15 min)

A business leader shows that the best teams are the ones where people help each other, not the ones with the strongest individuals. Useful when a team has to share a heavy workload.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then say how your team shares the work when someone is overloaded.
