Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Workload -- the amount of work a person or a team has to do
"My team's workload is too high this month."
Capacity -- the amount of work a team can do in a period of time
"Our capacity is about eighty percent this month."
Delegate -- to give a task to another person and trust them to do it
"I'll delegate the access report to Marcos."
Bandwidth -- the time and energy a person has for more work
"I don't have the bandwidth for a new project now."
On call -- ready to work outside normal hours if there is a problem
"Two engineers are on call every night."
Overtime -- extra hours you work after your normal day
"Nobody wants to work overtime every week."
Mandatory -- something you have to do; it is a rule
"The Friday update is mandatory."
Optional -- something you can choose to do or not do
"The Monday check-in is optional."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Month one of the cloud migration is over, and Priya's team is tired. Her workload is too high, and two of her engineers have to be on call every night. In the planning call, Rafael says they don't have to do every night: his team will share the nights.

Priya asks if her team has to send the long report every week. Rafael says no: a short update on Friday is enough. But there is one clear rule: the vendor mustn't change production without telling Rafael's team.

Nobody has the bandwidth for the manual access report, so Rafael delegates it to Marcos. Marcos has to automate it by the end of May.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Have to, Don't Have to, Mustn't

GRAMMAR

Have to is a rule. Don't have to is a choice. Mustn't is a no.

FormMeaningExample
Affirmativehave to / has to + verb: a rule, mandatoryHe has to automate the report.
Negative (choice)don't / doesn't have to: not necessary, optionalYou don't have to join the check-in.
Negative (a no)mustn't + verb: not allowedYou mustn't share passwords.
QuestionDo / Does + subject + have to + verb?Do we have to send the long report?
Pasthad to / didn't have toTwo engineers had to work overtime.

Never: we don't must · do we must? · he have to · you have to not
