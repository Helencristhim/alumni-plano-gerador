Chapter 3: The Code

Have to, Don't Have to, Mustn't

A rule, a choice, and a no.

Grammar Discovery

A Rule, a Choice, a No

"Every workstream has to send an update on Friday."

"You don't have to join the check-in if you have nothing new."

"Engineers mustn't share passwords in the chat."

"Last month, two engineers had to work overtime."

What do the orange words have in common? Which one is a rule, which one is a choice, and which one is a no?

FormMeaningExample
have to / has to + verbit is necessary; a rule (mandatory)She has to send an update.
don't / doesn't have tonot necessary; you can choose (optional)You don't have to join.
mustn't + verbnot allowed; it is a noYou mustn't share passwords.
Pasthad to / didn't have toThey had to work overtime.
QuestionDo / Does + subject + have toDo we have to work overtime?

In one line: have to = a rule; don't have to = a choice; mustn't = a no.

Chapter 4: The Plan

Planning Month Two with Priya

One call, two stories, one page of rules

Dialogue

Rafael and Priya, Month Two

P
Rafael, before we plan month two, I have a concern. My team's workload is too high.
R
I see. What is your capacity this month?
P
About eighty percent. Two of my engineers have to be on call every night, so they are tired.
R
Then they don't have to do every night. Let's share the nights with my team.
P
That helps a lot. And do we have to send the long report every week?
R
No, you don't have to. A short update on Friday is enough. But you mustn't change production without telling us.
P
Fair. What about the manual access report? We don't have the bandwidth for it now.
R
I'll delegate it to Marcos on my team. He has to automate it by the end of May.

Listening 1 · The Support Lead

Three Rules for a Tired Team

A support lead on how she protects her team. Sound first — no text.

1. What happens when the workload is too high, according to her?
People work overtime, and after a month they start to make mistakes.
2. Do people have to come to the weekly meeting?
No. It is optional if they have nothing new. They just have to write two lines in the chat.
3. What is the mandatory rule?
You mustn't say yes to a new request without checking your bandwidth first.
Listening 2 · The IT Manager

Learning to Delegate

An IT manager remembers the years he did everything himself. Sound first — no text.

1. What did he use to think about a good manager?
That a good manager has to know everything and do the difficult tasks himself.
2. Which three tasks does he still have to do himself?
Approving the budget, talking to the board and hiring.
3. What happens if you never delegate?
You become the bottleneck.
