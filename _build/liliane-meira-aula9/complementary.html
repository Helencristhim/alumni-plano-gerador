Lesson 9 -- The Question Behind the Question

Podcast
Talk Art -- Hans Ulrich Obrist

The curator who has recorded thousands of hours of interviews with artists is himself interviewed. Listen for how he talks about asking, and for the questions he says he asks everybody.

Tip: write down every question the hosts ask him, and mark which ones are follow-ups built on his own words.

Talk
Celeste Headlee -- "10 ways to have a better conversation" (TED, 12 min)

A radio interviewer explains what she learned from asking questions for a living: use open-ended questions, go with the flow, and stop to listen. It is the method of today’s lesson, from the other side of the microphone.

Tip: after each of the ten points, pause and say one question you would ask a curator that follows it.

Video
SHOWstudio -- "In Your Face: Interview: Hans Ulrich Obrist"

The famous interviewer sits on the other side of the camera and answers questions for once. Watch how the interviewer follows up, and how Obrist handles the questions he would rather not answer.

Tip: notice one moment when he dodges a question. How would you rephrase it?
