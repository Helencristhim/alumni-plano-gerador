Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
Nina Ubhi — “The Best Everyday Natural Makeup Tutorial for Beginners” (YouTube)

A step-by-step beauty tutorial with a clear structure: the result, the products, and every step explained before it happens. Watch it for the language of the process.

Tip: pause after each step and describe it in one sentence with a signal word: first, then, once, after that.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Series
Queer Eye — Season 1, Episode 1, “You Can’t Fix Ugly”

Five experts change one person’s life in a week, and a lot of it is getting things done: a haircut, new clothes, a house redesigned. Full of causatives in real American speech.

Tip: every time somebody has something done for them, say the sentence with have or get.

See the episode on TVmaze ↗
Talk
Sal Khan — “Let’s use video to reinvent education” (TED)

The founder of Khan Academy on how short video tutorials changed the way millions of people learn. A good talk for somebody who learned English from videos.

Tip: summarize his process in three steps, using first, then and finally.
