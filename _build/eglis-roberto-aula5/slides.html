The Conversation · Then

The Life You Used to Have

1. What did a normal working day use to look like for you? What time would you get up?
2. What did you use to do on weekends, when you were working full time?
3. Is there anything from your old routine that you miss? And anything you are happy to forget?

Real Speed

Used To Is One Sound

Used to sounds like yoos-tuh. Listen, then say it back at the same speed.

I used to work late every day.
I'm used to waking up early.
It took me a while to get used to it.
We would go sailing every summer.
The Conversation · Now

The Life You Have Now

1. What was the hardest thing to get used to in the first months after you retired?
2. After three and a half months in Europe, you decided to live in Brazil. What are you used to here that you missed over there?
3. In many countries, people retire later and later. Is that good or bad, in your opinion?

Under Pressure

The Party Question

Round 1: a party, and a young guest who will not stop asking. Round 2: a coffee with an old friend who retires next month and is nervous about it.

  • Round 1: the guest asks what you do and if you get bored. Give the short answer, the funny answer or the story.
  • Round 2: your friend is about to retire. You ask about his plans and give advice.
