Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Jane Fonda: “Life’s third act” (11 min)

The actress on the thirty extra years that people have today, and why the years after work can be a time to grow, not a time to stop. It is Margaret’s idea, told on a big stage.

Tip: watch it with English subtitles. Pause at one sentence you agree with and one you do not, and say why out loud.

TED Talk
Pico Iyer: “Where is home?” (14 min)

A writer with a passport from one country, a family from another and a life in a third, on what home means today. You are an Italian citizen who chose Brazil: this talk is about people like you.

Tip: answer his question in one minute and record it: where is home for you, and what did you use to think home was?

TED Talk
Chip Conley: “What baby boomers can learn from millennials at work, and vice versa” (17 min)

A hotel founder who went to work for a young tech company in his fifties. Funny and honest about getting used to a new world, and about what experience is good for.

Tip: listen for the things he had to get used to. Write three of them with get used to plus -ing.
