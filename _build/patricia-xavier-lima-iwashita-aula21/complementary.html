Lesson 21 -- Explaining How It Works

Series
Silicon Valley -- Season 1, Episode 1: "Minimum Viable Product"

An engineer has to explain what his product does to people who are not engineers, and the whole episode turns on how badly or well that is done. American English, in the exact world you work in. Connection to Lesson 21: explaining a technical process to a non-technical listener.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down 3 moments where someone explains how something works. Rewrite each one in the passive: "The file is compressed...", "The data is sent...".

Salesforce Admins Podcast -- "What are Security Essentials for Salesforce Admins"

Two Salesforce security specialists explain, in plain American English, how customer data is protected, how permissions are managed and what admins are expected to review. It is your own platform, described the way a customer's security team wants to hear it. Connection to Lesson 21: the exact sentences you will need on a security call.

Tip: listen twice. The second time, write down 5 passive sentences you hear ("data is stored...", "access is controlled...") and say each one out loud.

YouTube
"Present and past passives - 6 Minute Grammar" -- BBC Learning English

Six minutes on exactly today's structure: is / are + past participle for a process, plus the past form you will meet in the next lesson. It also shows when "by" is needed and when it only gets in the way. Connection to Lesson 21: the grammar behind every answer you gave in class.

Tip: pause after each example and build the same sentence about your own account: "The report is generated...", "The licenses are assigned...". Say five of them out loud.
