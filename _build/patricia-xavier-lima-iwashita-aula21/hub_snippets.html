Lesson 21 -- Pre-class

Explaining How It Works

Answering a customer's technical questions: where data is stored, who can see it, and what the system does on its own. Key words: integration, permission, trigger, sync, server, encrypt, back up, automatically, approval, dashboard. Structure: the present simple passive (is / are + past participle) and "by" for the person or system that does the action.

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Before the new integration is turned on, David's security team always asks the same three questions. Patrícia answers them one by one. Customer data is encrypted and stored on secure servers, and every file is backed up at night. Permissions are managed by the customer's own admin, so nothing is shared without written approval. When a user opens a ticket, an alert is triggered automatically and the case appears on the dashboard in real time. The two systems are synced every night. Notice what Patrícia never says: she never says "we do this" or "our team does that". In English, a process is described with the action first, and the person is left out on purpose.

1. Why does Patrícia say "the data is encrypted" instead of "we encrypt the data"?
A Because she does not know who does it.
B Because the process matters here, not the person who does it.
C Because the action happened in the past.
2. In "Permissions are managed by the customer's admin", why is "by" used?
A Because the customer really needs to know who does it.
B Because "by" is always necessary in the passive.
C Because "by" shows when the action happens.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A "Tickets is assigned automatically."
B "Tickets are assigned automatically."
C "Tickets are assign automatically."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Present Simple Passive

GRAMMAR

How to describe a process when the action matters more than the person.

StructureUseExample
is / are + past participleThe action matters; the person is left out.The data is encrypted.
by + person or systemOnly when the listener needs to know who.Permissions are managed by your admin.
is / are not + past participleSay what the system never does.Nothing is shared without approval.
Is / Are + subject + past participle?Ask about a process.Is the data backed up every night?
AvoidDo not drop the -ed of the participle ("the data is encrypt") → say "the data is encrypted". Do not use "from" for the person ("managed from your admin") → say "managed by your admin". And match the verb to the subject: one ticket is assigned, many tickets are assigned.
