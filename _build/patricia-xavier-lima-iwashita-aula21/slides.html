Chapter 3: The Code

The Data Is Stored...

is / are + past participle · by + the person who does it

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Your data is stored on secure servers."

"Tickets are assigned automatically."

"Permissions are managed by your admin."

"The two systems are synced every night."

Who does the action in each sentence — and when do we add by?

StructureUseExample
is / are + past participleThe action matters, not the personThe data is encrypted.
by + person or systemOnly when the customer needs to know whoPermissions are managed by your admin.
is / are not + past participleSay what the system never doesNothing is shared without approval.
Is / Are + subject + past participle?Ask about a processIs the data backed up every night?

A process is described with is / are + past participle. The person disappears on purpose. Add by only when who does it is part of the answer.

Chapter 4: Explaining to David

The Security Call

Three questions, three clear answers

Dialogue

Where Is Our Data Kept?

D
Before we turn on the new integration, I need to understand one thing: where is our data kept?
P
Your data is encrypted, and it is stored on secure servers here in Brazil.
D
And who can see it? My team is worried about permissions.
P
Permissions are managed by your admin. Nothing is shared without your approval.
D
One more thing. What happens when a user opens a ticket?
P
An alert is triggered automatically, and the case appears on your dashboard in real time.
Comprehension

About David

1. What does David want to understand before the integration is turned on?
Where his company's data is kept.
2. What is his team worried about?
Permissions — who can see the data.
3. What is his last question about?
What happens when a user opens a ticket.
