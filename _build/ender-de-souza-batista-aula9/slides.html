Chapter 3: The Code

Like and Want

I like going, I want to go

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I like going to the gym."

"I love listening to music."

"I don’t like watching TV."

"Do you want to come?"

Look at the blue words. After like and love, what is the form of the verb? And after want?

JobFormExample
Things you enjoylike / love + verb-ingI love listening to music.
Things you do not enjoydon’t like / hate + verb-ingI don’t like watching TV.
Things you wish or planwant to + verbI want to see live music.
QuestionsDo you like...-ing? / Do you want to...?Do you want to come?
He, shelikes / wants (the s of lesson 4)She likes dancing. He wants to run.
The trapno "want go", no "like watch"Not I want go. Say I want to go.

In one line: like / love / don't like + verb-ing. want + to + verb.

Chapter 4: After the Meeting

After the Meeting

One conversation, two recordings, one city guide

Dialogue

Dinner in Austin

J
So, Ender, no more work tonight. What do you like doing after the office?
E
I like working out. I go to the gym almost every night. It is my time to relax.
J
Really? I don’t like running, but I love dancing. Do you like music?
E
Yes, I love listening to music. I like songs with good lyrics.
J
Me too! So, what do you want to do in Austin this weekend?
E
I want to see live music. Austin is famous for that, right?
J
Yes! There is a concert on Saturday. Do you want to come?
E
Yes, I want to go. Thank you! But I don’t want to watch TV. I don’t like watching TV.
J
Ha! No TV, I promise. Only music.
