Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. Toque em Listen para ouvir. / Listen to each word and read the example.

Peaceful -- calm and quiet (Lesson 26) (tranquilo)
"Siena is more peaceful than Florence." (Siena é mais tranquila do que Florença.)
Allergic -- your body reacts to a food (Lesson 27) (alérgico)
"Paul is allergic to nuts." (O Paul é alérgico a nozes.)
Ingredient -- one of the foods in a dish (Lesson 27) (ingrediente)
"The ingredients were fresh." (Os ingredientes estavam frescos.)
Platform -- where you wait for the train (Lesson 28) (plataforma)
"The train leaves from platform nine." (O trem sai da plataforma nove.)
Delayed -- later than planned (Lesson 28) (atrasado)
"The train was delayed." (O trem estava atrasado.)
Souvenir -- a thing to remember a trip (Lesson 29) (lembrança)
"The oil is my favorite souvenir." (O azeite é minha lembrança favorita.)
Fresh -- new, made today (Lesson 29) (fresco)
"The bread is fresh." (O pão está fresco.)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto e responda às perguntas. / Read the text and answer the questions.

On her last day in Italy, Marlene takes the train to Siena. The train is delayed by ten minutes. "How long does it take?" she asks. "It takes one hour and a half." Siena is smaller and more peaceful than Florence. At the market she buys a piece of pecorino and a bottle of olive oil. At lunch Paul says: "I am allergic to nuts." Marlene asks the waiter: "Does the pesto have nuts in it?" (Revisão das quatro ferramentas: comparar, alergia, How long / Which, quantidades.)

A Ten minutes.
B One hour and a half.
C Three hours.
2. How is Siena different from Florence?
A It is smaller and more peaceful.
B It is bigger and busier.
C It has no market.
3. What does Marlene ask the waiter?
A "Is it spicy?"
B "Which platform?"

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Block 6 -- The Four Tools

GRAMMAR

Revisão das quatro ferramentas do bloco da Itália. Nada novo: tudo o que você já sabe, junto. / Review: the four tools from Lessons 26 to 29.

RuleExample (EN)Tradução (PT)
Comparar (aula 26)Siena is smaller than Florence.Siena é menor do que Florença.
Comparar com more (aula 26)The village is more peaceful than the city.O vilarejo é mais tranquilo do que a cidade.
Alergia (aula 27)I am allergic to nuts. / Does it have nuts in it?Sou alérgica a nozes. / Isso tem nozes?
Trem (aula 28)Which platform? / How long does it take?Qual plataforma? / Quanto tempo leva?
Quantidades (aula 29)A piece of cheese. / Do you have any bread?Um pedaço de queijo. / Você tem pão?

Key point / Ponto-chave: os erros mais comuns do bloco: "more small" (é smaller), "allergic nuts" (é allergic TO), "How long it takes?" (é How long DOES it take?) e "a kilo tomatoes" (é a kilo OF).
