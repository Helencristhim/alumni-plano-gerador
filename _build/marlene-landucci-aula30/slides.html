The Rule

The Four Tools

ToolPatternExample
Compare (Lesson 26)adjective + -er than / more + adjective + thanSiena is smaller than Florence.
Food safety (Lesson 27)I am allergic to / Does it have ... in it?Does it have nuts in it?
Trains (Lesson 28)Which + noun? / How long does it take?Which platform? It takes an hour.
Quantities (Lesson 29)a kilo of / a piece of + some / anyDo you have any fresh bread?

Key idea: Four tools, one day: compare places, ask about food, ask about trains, and buy the right quantity. You know all of them.

Chapter 5 -- Real Voices

Planning the Last Day

A conversation, a ticket and two messages

Dialogue

The Last Plan

P

"Marlene, it is our last day. Do you want to go to Siena or stay in Florence?"

M

"Siena, please. People say it is smaller and more peaceful than Florence. How long does it take?"

P

"It takes about one hour and a half by train. The train leaves at nine, but sometimes it is delayed."

M

"No problem. Which platform does it leave from?"

P

"Platform six. And in Siena there is a market. I want some cheese for a picnic."

M

"Me too. I would like a bottle of olive oil as a souvenir. What about lunch?"

P

"A small restaurant, but remember I am allergic to nuts."

M

"I remember. I always ask the waiter about every ingredient."

Comprehension

About Paul

1. What does Paul say about the train to Siena?
It takes about one hour and a half, it leaves at nine, and sometimes it is delayed.
2. Which platform does Paul say the train leaves from?
Platform six.
3. What does Paul want at the market in Siena?
Some cheese for a picnic.
4. What does Paul remind Marlene about at lunch?
That he is allergic to nuts.
