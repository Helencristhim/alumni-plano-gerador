A rule nobody enforces is a guideline with a serious name. Every exception carries a name, a date and a reason, and the audit reads them all.
1. What is mandatory above ten thousand reais, and what is only optional below the threshold?
A human review is mandatory above ten thousand. Below the threshold, a second review is optional.
2. What is nobody allowed to do, and who can sign a waiver?
Nobody can change a limit without a written reason. Only the Credit Director or the VP can sign a waiver.
3. Under two thousand reais, is income proof forbidden or simply not necessary?
Not necessary. The page says it is not required, which is different from not permitted.
Chapter 4: The Code

Forbidden, or Just Not Necessary?

Two negatives that mean opposite things

Grammar Discovery

Which Two Say Not Allowed?

"A human has to review every case above ten thousand."

"Nobody can change a limit without a written reason."

"You do not have to send income proof under two thousand."

"Only the Director can sign a waiver, and it must carry a date."

Every red verb is bare — not one of them has to in front of it. Now look at the blue words: one of them means the door is locked, and one of them means the door is open and you simply do not need to walk through it.

MeaningFormExample
It is necessaryhave to / has to + bare verbA human has to review the case.
It is necessary, and I am the one saying somust + bare verbThe waiver must carry a date.
It is not allowedcannot · must not + bare verbNobody can change a limit without a reason.
It is not necessarydo not have to + bare verbYou do not have to send income proof.
It is allowedcan · may (formal) + bare verbOnly the Director can sign a waiver.
It is a good ideashould + bare verbYou should read the guideline first.
The two trapsnever must to · must not is NOT do not have tomust not = forbidden · do not have to = not necessary

In one line: after every modal the verb goes bare — and must not locks the door, while do not have to leaves it open.

Listening 1 · The Policy Briefing

What You Can and Cannot Do

A compliance lead briefing new joiners. Sound first — no text.

1. What does she say is mandatory above the threshold?
A human review. Above ten thousand, a person has to look at the case, and the model cannot decide alone.
2. What does she say people do not have to do, and why does she say it twice?
They do not have to ask for a second review below the threshold. She repeats it because people confuse not necessary with not allowed.
3. What does she say about the audit?
It happens every six months with no notice, and a decision without a written reason does not exist in an audit.
Listening 2 · A Podcast

How an Exception Becomes a Rule

A risk consultant on the quiet way policies move. Sound first — no text.

1. What does he say happens to an exception that is granted often?
It stops being an exception. People start planning around it, and after a while nobody asks for it any more because everybody assumes it.
2. What number does he give, and what does he say should happen at that point?
Ten. If the same exception is granted ten times, the policy is wrong and it should be rewritten.
3. What does he say a good policy owner has to be able to do?
Say out loud which rules are actually enforced and which ones are not, without looking at the document.
