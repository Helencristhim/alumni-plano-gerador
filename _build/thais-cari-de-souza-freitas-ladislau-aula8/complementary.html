Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Barry Schwartz -- "Our loss of wisdom" (TED)

Twenty minutes on the argument behind today’s text: a rule protects a team and, past a certain point, it replaces the judgement that made the rule good. Every second sentence is a modal — has to, cannot, should, must — which is exactly what you practised.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Write down five sentences of his and mark each one as obligation, prohibition or advice.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
Planet Money (NPR) -- "What the collapse of one fintech company tells us about bank regulations"

Your own industry, in English, told as a story about which rules a company had to follow and which ones it found a way around. The vocabulary of this lesson is the vocabulary of the episode: a requirement, an audit, to comply, an exception.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Listen once, then read it and mark every must, has to and cannot you find.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
ICA -- "What is compliance?"

A few minutes of somebody whose whole job is the difference between what a company has to do, what it cannot do and what it merely should do. Slow, formal English, and the exact register of the document you sign.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause after each sentence and say the same idea about your own policy.
