Complementary Activities

Optional extras for the hour you study at home, or for the car. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 1 -- Asking for the Right Thing

Film
The Founder (2016), directed by John Lee Hancock

A salesman walks into a small restaurant in California and spends the rest of the film asking for things: a meeting, a price, a contract, a franchise. Almost every scene is a commercial conversation between two people who want different numbers, in slow and very clear American English.

Tip: watch the first forty minutes with English subtitles and write down every time somebody asks for something. Count how many requests start with could or would.

Podcast
NPR How I Built This -- "Spanx: Sara Blakely"

Forty minutes on exactly what this lesson is about: a person with one product, calling buyers she has never met, and getting a first order out of a cold conversation. Two American voices, a normal speed, and the whole transcript is on the page if you want to read while you listen.

Tip: perfect for the car. Listen once for the story, then find the part where she gets in front of the buyer and write down how she asks. Bring one of her sentences to the next class.

Talk
William Ury -- "The walk from no to yes" (TEDxMidwest)

The man who wrote the standard book on negotiation, in eighteen minutes, on the one move that changes a hard conversation: the third side. He speaks slowly, he repeats his key sentences, and he uses the vocabulary of a deal without a single difficult word.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Then say out loud, in one sentence, what his idea would change in your next meeting with a customer.
