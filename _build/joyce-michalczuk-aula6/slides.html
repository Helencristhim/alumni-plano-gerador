Real Speed

Pause, Do Not Fill

Say each group, then stop in silence where you see the line. No “uh”. Listen, then say it back.

That is a fair question. / Let me think for a second.
I do not have that number / off the top of my head.
I will get back to you / by tomorrow morning.
If the rate goes up, / our cost goes up with it.

The Hot Seat

One Phrase, One Point, Stop

Questions with no warning. Three moves, every time.

1. Buy one second
One phrase, or a silent pause. Never three phrases.
2. Point and reason
One sentence each.
3. Stop
Voice down. Wait for the next question.
Do not know?
Say so, and give a time: I will get back to you by…

Under Pressure

The Board Has Questions

A board meeting in Zurich, by video. You have one minute on the reform. Then five questions, one after the other. One of them you cannot possibly know. One of them sounds like blame.

  • Round 1: you answer five questions with no warning: one point, one reason, stop.
  • Round 2: you are on the board. You ask the tough questions and watch how the other person answers.
