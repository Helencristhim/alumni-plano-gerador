Grammar Discovery

TimeFormFrom lesson
THEN, beforewas / were · did / did not + verbLessons 16 and 17
NOW, every weekpresent simple: I work, she worksLessons 2 and 3
RIGHT NOW / this montham / is / are + verb-ingLesson 14
NOVEMBER, already setam / is / are + verb-ing + dateLesson 15
Key ideaThe TIME WORD decides the verb: then asks for the past, now for the present, a date for the plan. Choose the time word first, and the verb follows.

Already = it happened sooner than expected. Still = it continues, and it is not a problem.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"Now I did not speak in the meetings."
"Then I did not speak in the meetings."
"Eighteen lessons ago I ask questions."
"Eighteen lessons ago I did not ask questions."
"I still answer some questions -- that is new."
"I already answer some questions -- that is new."

One rule solves all three: read the TIME WORD first, and let it choose the verb.

Dialogue

How Was It Eighteen Lessons Ago?

P
Joice, we are at lesson nineteen. How was it eighteen lessons ago?
J
Then I did not say one sentence about my work in English.
P
And now? What do you do in the meetings?
J
Now I ask questions, and I already answer some of them.
P
That is a big change. Is it easy?
J
No. I am still learning, and sometimes I ask people to repeat.
P
Asking to repeat is not a problem. What are you doing this month?
J
This month I am preparing my three minutes for the event.
P
And in November?
J
Later this year, on the fifth of November, I am presenting in Belém.
