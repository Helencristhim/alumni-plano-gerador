Grammar Discovery

One of each pair says when

He has won two titles.
He won his first title in 2024.
I have never surfed a reef.
I surfed a reef last summer.

The date decides everything

A finished time (in 2024, last summer, yesterday) → past simple. No finished time, just the experience or the score so far → have / has + past participle.

The Code

With a date, or without

Present perfect — no date

He has won two titles.
I have never surfed a reef.
Have you ever been to Australia?
Two wins so far this season.

Past simple — finished time

He won his first title in 2024.
I surfed a reef last summer.
Did you go last year?
She retired in 2023.

The shortcut: if there is a finished date in the sentence, it is the past simple. No date, and the story is still open → present perfect.
