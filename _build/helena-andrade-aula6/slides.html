Chapter 4: The Code

What the Writer Put First

It was not the surveyor who... / What stopped the ship was...

Grammar Discovery

Two Ways of Pointing

"It was not the surveyor who made a mistake."

"What stopped the ship was an amendment."

"The classification society to which the owner sent the surveys worked for one state only."

"The state whose flag she flies had not ratified the protocol."

Write the simple version of sentences 1 and 2. In sentences 3 and 4, write the noun the clause is about.

FormWhat it doesExample
It was + X + who / thatthe writer points at X and excludes the othersIt was not the surveyor who made a mistake. = The surveyor did not make the mistake.
What + verb + was + XX comes at the end, where the emphasis isWhat stopped the ship was an amendment. = An amendment stopped the ship.
preposition + whichformal English: the preposition goes before whichthe society to which the owner sent the surveys = the society the owner sent the surveys to
whose + nounpossession; it works for states and companies toothe state whose flag she flies = the state of her flag
The reading trapa clause is not always about the nearest noun. Ask: which noun makes sense? A wrong option in the exam often attaches the clause to the wrong noun.

You do not need to write these structures. You need to read them and find the noun they are about.

1The text says a convention does not become law on the day it is adopted — the question says adoption alone does not give a convention legal force.
2The text says the state whose flag she flies — the question says the state of registry.
3The text says the state that controlled the water — the question says the coastal state with jurisdiction.

Writing

Write What He Said

Task: Write three short sentences about the surveyor. Use one starter for each sentence.

Start with:

He used the rules of...His authorisation comes from...In the port, ...
He used the rules of the flag state. His authorisation comes from one state only. In the port, the law of the port always wins.
Writing

From the IMO to the Ship

Task: Write the three steps that make a convention law on a ship, one sentence for each step.

Start with:

First, the convention is adopted...Then, each state...Finally, the state writes...
First, the convention is adopted at the IMO. Then, each state ratifies it. Finally, the state writes it into its own national law, with a penalty.
