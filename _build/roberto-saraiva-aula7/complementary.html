Lesson 07 -- The Feedback Conversation

Talk
Frances Frei -- "How to build (and rebuild) trust" (TED2018)

Fifteen minutes on the three things that make a team trust the person leading it, from a Harvard professor who was sent to fix a company where nobody trusted anybody. Clear, structured American English, and directly useful in the twenty minutes this lesson trains.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Then decide which of her three parts is your own blind spot, and say why out loud.

Podcast
NPR How I Built This -- "Slack & Flickr: Stewart Butterfield"

A founder who had to tell a whole team that the product they had built was not going to work, twice. The vocabulary of this lesson used in real decisions: what he had to do, what he decided, and what he only advised.

Tip: good for the car. Listen for the moments he says had to and the moments he says decided to — the difference is exactly tonight's structure.

Listen on NPR ↗
Series
Ted Lasso -- Season 1, Episode 1: "Pilot"

An American manager arrives to lead a team that does not want him, and every episode is a feedback conversation of one kind or another. Slow, warm American English, and unusually good on the difference between advice and an order.

Tip: one episode is enough. Write down three sentences he uses to tell somebody to change something without giving an order.
