"Are you here for work?" — "No, I'm not."

"Do you have children?" — "Yes, I do."

"Do you have anything to declare?" — "No, I don't."

Do you...?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.Do you have children? Yes, I do.

The answer gives back the SAME word the question started with. Never Yes, I have for Do you...?

Comprehension

What Did the Officer Say?

1. What does he do when she does not understand?
He says "no problem" and asks the question again, in easier words.
2. Which exit does he tell her to take?
The green one. She has nothing to declare.
3. How does he end the conversation?
He says "enjoy your stay".
Listening

While You Wait

The message in the arrivals line. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What must you take off before the desk?
Your hat and your sunglasses.
2. What three things does the officer ask?
Why you are here, how long you stay, and the address where you sleep.
3. What does the message say about not understanding?
Say "sorry, I don't understand" and the officer repeats more slowly. It is normal.
Listening 2

Where Are the Bags?

The message after passport control. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. Which belt, and on which level?
Belt number four, on level one.
2. What do you do if a bag does not arrive?
Do not leave the airport. Go to the baggage desk next to belt number one.
3. What do you show at the baggage desk?
Your ticket and the small paper from the check-in.
