Chapter 4: Yes or No?

Yes or No?

I am not, Are you...?, Is it...?

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I am not a U.S. citizen."

"It is not a business trip."

"Are you on vacation?"

"Is your husband with you?"

Look at the blue words. Where is not? Where is the verb in a question?

JobFormExample
No (negative)am not / is not (isn’t) / are not (aren’t)I am not a U.S. citizen.
Question with IAm I...?Am I in seat 14A?
Question with youAre you...?Are you on vacation?
Question with he, she, itIs it...? / Is he...?Is it a business trip?
Short answersYes, I am. / No, it is not.Are you from Brazil? Yes, I am.

In one line: No: the verb + not. Question: the verb goes first.

Listening 1 · About the Form

Mark and the Form

The flight attendant talks about the customs form. Sound first — no text.

1. Is it one form for one person, or one form for one family?
One form for one family.
2. Is the form on the computer?
No, it is not. It is on paper.
3. What address is on the form?
Your address in the U.S.
Listening 2 · A Woman on the Plane

Carol from Chicago

A woman on the plane talks about her trip. Sound first — no text.

1. Is Carol on vacation?
No, she is not. It is a business trip.
2. Is her family with her?
No, it is not. Her family is at home.
3. Where is her hotel address?
On her phone.
