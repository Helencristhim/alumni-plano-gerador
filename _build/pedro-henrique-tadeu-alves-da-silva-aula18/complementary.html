Song Exploder -- Lorde, “Sober”

Lorde takes her song apart, layer by layer, and explains how the ideas came up in the studio: the drums, the horns, the voices, and what she kept and what she took out.

99% Invisible -- The Sound of Sports

A sports broadcast is not only the commentary. Sound teams set up dozens of microphones to catch the crowd, the kicks and the ball, and some of the sounds you hear on TV are not real at all.

Tip: compare it with the live music show in today’s first listening. Say three things a TV sound team sets up, turns down or sorts out during a live broadcast.
