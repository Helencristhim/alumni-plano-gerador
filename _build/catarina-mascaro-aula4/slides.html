The Code

Both are present. What is different?

I watch series every day.
I am watching one right now.
She plays volleyball on Mondays.
She is playing at the moment.
They study a lot.
They are not studying right now.

Happening now → am / is / are + verb-ing

Left side = always, every week, in general. Right side = now, today, this month. Two presents, two jobs.

The Code

Two presents, two jobs

In general

I study every day.
She works on Mondays.
We do not go out much.

Now / these days

I am studying right now.
She is working at the moment.
We are not going out these days.

Question: Are you studying? — not "Do you studying?"

Listening 1

A voice note from the middle of it

1. Where is she, and what is she doing?
On the bus, going to her grandmother’s house.
2. What is she getting better at these days?
Drawing — she is practising almost every day.
3. What does she ask you at the end?
What you are doing right now, and whether you are still watching that series.
