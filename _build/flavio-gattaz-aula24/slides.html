Listening 1 · A Language Coach

The Advanced Plateau

A coach on why advanced learners stop improving. Sound first — no text.

1. Which learners does she worry about least, and why?
The ones who say they have stopped improving, because it means they are paying attention.
2. What do advanced learners do under pressure?
They fall back on the three or four structures they trust, and the others never leave the page.
3. What is the one thing she asks for, and what does it cost?
To use one structure you are not completely sure of in every conversation. It costs a few mistakes.
Listening 2 · An Engineering Director

Five Minutes a Month

A non-native speaker on how he measures his own English. Sound first — no text.

1. What does he record, and how often?
Five minutes of himself talking about his projects, without notes, once a month.
2. Why does he wait a week before listening?
Because on the same day you hear what you meant to say; a week later you hear what you actually said.
3. What improved first, and what came later?
Fluency improved first. Accuracy followed about six months later.
