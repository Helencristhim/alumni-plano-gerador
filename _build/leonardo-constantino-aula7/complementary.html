Documentary Series
MADOFF: The Monster of Wall Street -- Episode 1

Investigators, lawyers and former employees describe a structure built out of feeder funds, offshore entities and one investment company that nobody looked inside. Connection to Lesson 7: almost every explanation on screen is a relative clause identifying which fund, which entity and which jurisdiction.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down three sentences that use who, which or that to identify a company. Then say one of them about a client of yours.

Post Reports -- "A tax haven in America's heartland"

Reporters from The Washington Post explain how money moved out of the jurisdictions everyone watches and into trusts in South Dakota, where the rules are different. Connection to Lesson 7: this is your own vocabulary in the wild -- jurisdiction, domicile, exemption and provision, in native speed.

Tip: listen twice. The second time, write down every sentence that uses which or that to identify an entity, and mark where the speaker pauses for a comma.

British Council -- "How to use relative clauses" (Mini English Lesson)

A short lesson on exactly the structure you discovered in class: which pronoun goes with people, things and places, and what the two commas actually do to the meaning. Connection to Lesson 7: a second explanation in a different voice, right before you use these clauses with a real client.

Tip: pause before each answer and say your own version first. Then write two sentences about a live file: one defining, with no commas, and one non-defining, with two.
