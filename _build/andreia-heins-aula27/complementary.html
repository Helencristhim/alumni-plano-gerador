Lesson 27 -- Cross-Cultural Communication

Video
Pellegrino Riccardi -- "Cross cultural communication" (TEDxBergen)

A funny and very practical talk about how people from different countries understand time, rules and politeness in different ways. Many examples come from real work situations.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down one example that reminds you of a call with the US or with Latin America.

Video
Julien S. Bourrelle -- "How culture drives behaviours" (TEDxTrondheim)

A short talk about why the same behavior can be polite in one country and strange in another, and how to adapt without losing who you are.

Tip: after watching, explain in one minute one difference between Brazilian and American work culture, with a polite past form.

Watch on YouTube →
Talk
Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie -- "The danger of a single story" (TED)

A famous writer explains how one simple idea about a country or a culture can hide the real people behind it. A good reminder before you meet colleagues from other countries.

Tip: think of one "single story" people abroad have about Brazil, and how you would correct it in a friendly tone.
