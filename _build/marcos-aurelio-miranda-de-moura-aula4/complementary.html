Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
The Wall Street Journal -- "How Companies Are Overhauling Supply Chains to Ease Bottlenecks"

Six minutes on the word you learned today, at full size: what a bottleneck does to a company that has everything else right, and what the companies did about it. The narration is clear American English and every second sentence is in the form you practised.

Tip: watch once with subtitles, once without. On the second pass, count how many sentences use am, is or are plus -ing. There are more than you expect.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Podcast
NPR Planet Money -- "Why supermarkets are adopting dynamic pricing"

Twenty minutes on a rollout that is happening right now, in your own world: shops replacing paper price labels with screens, and what changes when a price can move during the day. It is your job description, reported as news.

Tip: the page has the full transcript. Listen once with it closed, then open it and find three sentences about something in progress. Say each one out loud about your own rollout.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
TED -- Florian Aigner, "How to make better decisions and avoid sunk cost fallacy"

The decision behind every amber line on a status board: when do you keep going because it is right, and when do you keep going only because you already spent the money? Short, plain English, and directly useful in a Monday round.

Tip: after watching, think of one initiative you are running now and ask his question about it out loud, in English, in two sentences.
