Lesson 10 -- What Somebody Else Said

Talk
Rishi Manchanda -- "What makes us get sick? Look upstream"

A doctor retells his patients' stories to make an argument. Almost every example arrives as reported speech, because he was not the one who lived it.

Tip: write down four sentences in which he reports what a patient said. Then say them again in direct speech, and notice what changes.

Talk
Mary Bassett -- "Why your doctor should care about social justice"

A public health doctor relaying what communities told her, and what she told the authorities. The whole talk lives between two mouths.

Tip: after watching, relay in three sentences what she said, using said, mentioned and recommended — not only "she said".
