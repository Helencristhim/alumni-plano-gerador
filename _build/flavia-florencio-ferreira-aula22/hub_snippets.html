Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Discipline -- the ability to do what you plan, even when it is hard
"Going to the gym at six in the morning takes discipline."
Routine -- the things you usually do every day, in the same order
"My morning routine starts with a coffee and a walk."
Workout -- a session of physical exercise
"I do a short workout before work."
Recovery -- the time your body needs to rest and get strong again
"After a hard week, recovery is important."
Priority -- something that is more important than other things
"My health is a priority, even on a work trip."
Mindset -- the way you usually think about things
"A positive mindset helps me at work."
Well-being -- feeling healthy, calm, and happy
"Sleep and exercise are good for my well-being."
Self-care -- the things you do to take care of your body and mind
"Reading before bed is my favorite self-care."
To recharge -- to get your energy back after a tired period
"On Saturdays, I recharge with my family."
To stretch -- to make your arms, legs, and back long to relax your muscles
"I always stretch after a workout."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Ten years ago, Flavia's routine was very different. She used to work late every night, and she used to skip breakfast. She didn't use to exercise, and her well-being was not a priority. Today, things are different. She goes to the gym every morning, and she stretches after each workout. On Saturdays, she does volunteer work to recharge. "It takes discipline," she says, "but a good mindset makes it easy." Her friend asks: "Did you use to feel tired all the time?" "Yes," Flavia says, "but not anymore."

1. What did Flavia use to do ten years ago?
A She used to go to the gym every morning before work.
B She used to work late and skip breakfast.
C She used to do volunteer work.
2. What does Flavia do now?
A She still works late every night, like in the past.
B She does not exercise.
C She goes to the gym every morning.
3. Which question is correct?
A "Did you use to feel tired?"
B "Did you used to feel tired all the time on work days?"
C "Do you used to feel tired?"

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Used To

GRAMMAR

How to talk about habits that changed.

FormStructureExample
Affirmativeused to + base verbI used to skip my workout.
Negativedidn't use to + base verbI didn't use to stretch.
QuestionDid + subject + use to + base verb?Did you use to run?
Nowbut now + present simplebut now I go to the gym.
RuleUse used to for a past habit or situation that is not true now. It is the same for all people (I / she / we used to). With did, write use to, with no "d". For a relaxed opinion, say "Honestly, I think..." or "For me, the most important thing is...".
