Lesson 4 -- Bangkok Is Coming

Conference Skills
"Tips for Attending Your First Scientific Conference" -- SuRE Resource Center

Eight minutes on how to plan a congress week before it starts: which sessions to choose, what to do at registration, and why the coffee break matters more than the keynote. This is your October, described by somebody who has done it many times.

Tip: watch with a pen. Every time the speaker says what you should DO, write the sentence in your own words with 'I am going to'. You should finish with at least six plans.

Listening
"What is the future of work?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes of two presenters talking about what is going to happen, at a speed built for learners. It is full of the exact structures you practised today: going to, will, and fixed times.

Tip: listen once with your eyes closed, no text. Then listen again and count how many times you hear 'going to' and how many times you hear 'will'. Notice that they are not used for the same thing.

"Scientific conference guide: Here's what to expect and how to make the most of them" -- the bumbling biochemist

A researcher walks through a congress day by day: the app, the schedule, the parallel sessions you cannot both attend, the poster hall, the dinner. Slower and more detailed than the first video.

Tip: stop the video at the part about choosing between parallel sessions. Then say out loud, in English, which of the two sessions in your own Bangkok programme you are going to choose, and why.
