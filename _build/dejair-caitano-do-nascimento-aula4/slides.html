Chapter 3: The Code

Two Futures

The difference is not the time. It is when you decided

Discover the Rule

Which Were Decided Right Now?

I am going to take part in the workshop on Wednesday.
Our group is going to have dinner on Monday evening.
"Here is my card." — "Thank you, I'll send you my paper tonight."
"There is no more coffee." — "I'll get some from the other room."
Click to reveal the rule
Use going to for a plan you already had before you spoke: it is in your diary, you signed up, you bought the ticket. Use will (usually I'll) for a decision, an offer or a promise made at the moment of speaking. Both are the future. The difference is when the decision happened.

Chapter 4: In the Room

Two Messages That Decide Your Week

The organiser from Istanbul, and a colleague from Chennai

Listening 1

The Organiser's Briefing

Read the questions, then listen.

1. What time does registration open, and what do you need with you?
Eight in the morning, on the ground floor. You need the confirmation email on your phone -- not printed.
2. How do you get a place in a workshop, and when is the deadline?
You have to sign up online. The deadline is Friday at midnight, and there is no waiting list.
3. Where is the full schedule, and why does he say so?
In the app. It is the only version they are going to update.

Comprehension

About Murat

1. What does Murat say about the workshop places?
The deadline is Friday, but they fill up in two days, so you should sign up tonight.
2. How does he answer when Dejair says his English is slow?
He says half of that room learned English at thirty, like him, and invites Dejair to come with them.
3. What is the plan for Wednesday evening, and at what time?
They are going to take the bus to the networking dinner at seven, from the main entrance.
