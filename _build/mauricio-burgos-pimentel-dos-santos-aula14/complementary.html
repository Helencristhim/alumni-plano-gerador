The Big Short

Small teams of finance people divide up the work, check the numbers, push back on each other and decide together — under pressure. A lot of banking English, and many group meetings.

Tip: in each team meeting, notice who leads, who checks the figures and who stays quiet. Say what each person has to do, with has to.
