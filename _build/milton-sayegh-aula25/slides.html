Chapter 3: The Contrast Code

Good News and Bad News, One Sentence

although · despite · whereas · because of · due to

Grammar Discovery

What Comes After the Orange Words?

"Although demand is strong, the new tariff will cut our margin."

"Despite the slowdown, luxury is still growing."

"Our costs went up because of the price of gold."

"Whereas the US market is growing, the European market is flat."

Look at the orange words. Which ones are followed by a full sentence with a verb? Which ones are followed only by a noun?

FormUseExample
although / even though + subject + verbTwo opposite ideas. Even though is stronger.Although demand is strong, the tariff will cut our margin.
despite + noun (or verb + -ing)The same idea, but with no verb after it.Despite the slowdown, luxury is growing.
whereas + subject + verbCompare two different things.Whereas the US is growing, Europe is flat.
because of / due to + nounGive a reason with a noun. (because + subject + verb)The shipment is late due to customs.
The trapNo but after although. No of after despite.Although sales are good, ... (not Although..., but...)

Complex sentences let you give the full picture in one breath: what is good, what is bad, and why.

Chapter 4: The Briefing

A Call with Miami

Video call, 9:00 a.m. Three weeks before the holiday shipment.

Dialogue

Milton Talks to Rebecca

R
Good morning, Milton. Let me start with the good news. Although the economy is slowing, demand for fine jewelry in the US is still strong.
M
That's good to hear. And the bad news?
R
The new tariff on imported jewelry. Because of it, your rings will cost your clients about fifteen percent more.
M
Even though my clients are loyal, fifteen percent is a lot. And my costs are already higher due to the price of gold.
R
Yes, gold is the commodity everybody is buying. But there's one thing on your side: the exchange rate. Despite the tariff, a strong dollar means more reais for every sale.
M
So the dollar pays part of the tariff. Will there be a recession next year?
R
We don't expect one. Inflation is slowing, and whereas middle-class families lost purchasing power, your buyers didn't.
M
What do you recommend for the holiday shipment, then?
R
Ship early. Customs in Miami is slow in November due to the volume, and you don't want your rings to miss the holidays.
M
Understood. Although the margin will be smaller, I'd rather sell on time than sell late.
Comprehension

About Rebecca

1. What is Rebecca's good news?
Although the economy is slowing, demand for fine jewelry in the US is still strong.
2. Why does Rebecca think the exchange rate helps Milton?
Despite the tariff, a strong dollar means more reais for every sale.
3. Why does Rebecca tell him to ship early?
Customs in Miami is slow in November due to the volume.

An Exporter's Story

Ricardo exports leather bags from Brazil. He tells how the economy changed his company. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What happened to Ricardo's sales in Brazil in 2015?
They fell by forty percent.
2. Why did his company survive?
He had started selling abroad, and the weak real made his products cheaper in dollars.

Read the economic news every morning, the same way you read your sales numbers.
