Chapter 3: How Much, How Many

Two Questions, One Rule

how much bread · how many apples · some · any

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"How much is the bread?"

"How many apples do you want?"

"I want some cheese, please."

"Do you have any bread?"

WordUse it withExample
how muchthings you cannot count — and every priceHow much bread? · How much cheese? · How much is it?
how manythings you can count, one by oneHow many apples? · How many tickets? · How many people?
somewhen you say YES to somethingI want some bread. · We have some apples.
anyin a question and in a NODo you have any cheese? · We don't have any apples.

Can you count it? → many. You cannot? → much. And every price in the world is How much is it?

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"How many bread do you want?"

✓ RIGHT

"How much bread do you want?"

✗ WRONG

"How much apples for five euros?"

✓ RIGHT

"How many apples for five euros?"

✗ WRONG

"We don't have some apples today."

✓ RIGHT

"We don't have any apples today."

Portuguese has one word with an S: quanto and quantos. English has two different words — and the S is not the clue. The clue is: can you count it?

Chapter 5: Real Talk

At the Bread Table

one buyer, one seller, six prices

Dialogue

The Bread Table, 9:15 a.m.

L
Good morning. Some bread today?
T
Good morning. Yes, please. How much is one baguette?
L
One euro twenty. The big one is two euros fifty.
T
One baguette, please. And do you have any cheese?
L
Of course. How many pieces do you want?
T
Two small pieces. How much is that?
L
Twelve euros for the two. It is not cheap, but it is very good cheese.
T
That's fine. And how many apples for five euros?
L
One kilo, about eight apples. They are from the south, and this week they are very cheap.
T
Perfect. My daughter always eats two apples in the morning. How much is everything?
L
Eighteen euros twenty. You can pay here with a credit card.
Comprehension

What Did Luc Say?

1. How much is one baguette?
One euro twenty. The big one is two euros fifty.
2. How many apples can you buy for five euros?
One kilo, about eight apples. This week they are very cheap.
3. How much is everything?
Eighteen euros twenty. You can pay with a credit card.
