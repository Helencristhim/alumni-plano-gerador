Lesson 05 -- Furnishing from Zero

Documentary
A Washington DC Studio, Furnished from Nothing -- Never Too Small (short film, 10 min)

A standalone ten-minute film about two renters in your city who filled 490 square feet from an empty floor. Every choice is a measurement: what fits, what was built in, what had to go back.

Tip: American English, no music over the speech. Watch once without subtitles and write down three things that were done to the apartment, using was or were plus a participle.

Podcast
Make your small space feel bigger -- Life Kit, NPR

Two reporters go room by room through cheap, renter-friendly ways to place furniture: where the sofa goes, how a room is divided, what to do when nothing fits. Exactly the conversation you are having with your own house.

Tip: the full transcript is on the same page. Listen first with the page scrolled down, then read it and count how many sentences are passive.

Talk
3 Easy Steps to Measure Doorways for Furniture Deliveries -- Furniture Academy

The three numbers a delivery crew takes before a sofa is loaded, explained in a few minutes: the width of the door, the diagonal depth of the piece, and the tightest turn in the hallway.

Tip: watch it with a measuring tape in your hand and do it on your own front door. Then say the result out loud: "It fits through" or "It does not fit through".
