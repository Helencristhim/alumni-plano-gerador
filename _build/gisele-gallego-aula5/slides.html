Chapter 3: The Code

Who Delivers It? Nobody Says.

is / are and was / were + participle — and the one time the name comes back

Grammar Discovery

Where Did the Person Go?

"The sofa is made in North Carolina."

"The legs are shipped in the box."

"The table was damaged in transit."

"The rug was not included in the price."

"The bookcase was assembled by my husband."

In all five, the orange part is the same machine: a form of be plus a past participle. The blue part appears only once, and only because there it is the whole point.

UseFormExample
Presentis / are + participleThe sofa is made in North Carolina.
Pastwas / were + participleThe table was damaged in transit.
Negativeis not / was not + participleThe rug was not included in the price.
QuestionIs / Was + subject + participleIs the rug included in the price?
Why use itThe doer is unknown, obvious or unimportant.Nobody knows which driver. Say it anyway.
by + personOnly when the doer is the point.... by my husband.

In one line: be + participle, and the name only comes back when it matters.

G
Ten percent. All right — can I take a swatch of the blue home? I want to see the color in my own light first.
Comprehension

About Marcus and the Store

1. What does Marcus say about the gray sofa, and what does he offer instead?
The gray one is backordered until the end of the month. The blue one is in stock and it is delivered on Tuesday.
2. What does the store do on delivery day, and what does it not do?
Delivery is curbside only, so the sofa is left at the street. It is not brought inside, and it is not assembled: the legs are shipped in the box.
3. What are the store rules if the sofa goes back?
Fourteen days. It is picked up at her place, but a ten percent restocking fee is taken out of the refund, and the policy is printed on the receipt.

Tuesday, between eleven in the morning and three in the afternoon. The boxes are not left without a signature.
2. Name two things the driver does not do.
The items are not carried upstairs and they are not assembled. They are only brought to the street or to the door of the building.
3. What happens if nobody is home, and what is the order number?
The order is taken back to the warehouse and a second delivery is charged at fifty dollars. The order number is four seven two nine eight one.

She measured the room, but she never measured the front door. The sectional did not fit through it.

A hundred and eighty dollars, in a restocking fee, just to give the sofa back. It was picked up two weeks later.
