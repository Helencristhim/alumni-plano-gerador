Chapter 3: And, But, So

Three Small Words, One Story

and · but · so

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I had a booking, but my name was not in the system."

"The hotel was full, so they gave me an upgrade."

"The room was big and very quiet."

"It was a mistake, but the manager was very kind."

WordWhat it doesExample
andone more thing, in the same directionThe room was big and quiet.
buta surprise, the opposite directionI had a booking, but my name was not there.
sothe result — what happened nextThe hotel was full, so they gave me an upgrade.
because (lesson 3)the reason — the other side of soThey gave me an upgrade because the hotel was full.
The commaBefore but and so, a small pause (a comma): "..., but..." / "..., so...". Before and, usually no pause.

So and because tell the same story from two sides: the result, or the reason.

Two Sides

So or Because?

"The hotel was full, so they gave me an upgrade."

first the problem, then SO and the result

"They gave me an upgrade because the hotel was full."

first the result, then BECAUSE and the reason (lesson 3)

"I was tired, but I was happy."

BUT: two ideas in opposite directions — a small pause before it

"It was a long night, but it worked."

the end of every good travel story

Dialogue

My Name Is Not There?

S
Good evening. Welcome to the Riverside. Can I have your name?
L
Good evening. Leão de Souza. I have a booking for three nights.
S
Let me see... I am sorry, but your name is not in the system.
L
But I have the confirmation here, on my phone. Look.
S
Yes, I can see it. It was our mistake. But the hotel is full tonight.
L
It is eleven at night and I am very tired. Can I talk to the manager?
S
Of course. One moment, please.
S
OK, good news. We have a suite on the tenth floor, so we can give you an upgrade.
L
Really? That is great. Is it the same price?
S
Yes, and the manager wants to give you a discount for tonight, because it was our mistake.
L
Thank you, Sofia. It was a bad start, but it worked.
S
We are very sorry. Maybe you can write us a good review?
Comprehension

What Did Sofia Say?

1. What was the problem with the booking?
His name was not in the system. It was the hotel's mistake.
2. What room can the hotel give him, and why?
A suite on the tenth floor, because the hotel is full.
3. What does the manager want to give him?
A discount for tonight.
