Chapter 3: The Code

Always vs Right Now

I advise · I am reviewing

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I advise financial institutions on tax law."

"This month I am reviewing the tax reform impact."

"I present projections to clients every month."

"Right now I am preparing a compliance report."

Why blue for things that are always true, but amber for right now?

FormUseExample
Present Simple
I / you / we + verb		Facts, routines and permanent situations — always trueI advise financial institutions.
Present Continuous
am / is / are + verb-ing		Now, this month, these days — temporary or unfinishedI am reviewing the reform impact.
He / she / itPresent simple adds -s; continuous uses isShe advises the bank. / She is advising the bank.

Time words help: every day, always, usually → simple · right now, today, this month → continuous.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"Every month I am presenting the projections."
"Every month I present the projections."
"Right now I prepare a compliance report."
"Right now I am preparing a compliance report."
"I am knowing this area of tax law very well."
"I know this area of tax law very well."

Routines use the present simple. Things happening now use the present continuous. Verbs of state — know, want, need, understand, believe — almost never take -ing.

Chapter 4: The Client Call

Meet Rachel

Head of compliance · a first call

Dialogue

A First Client Call

R
Good morning, Leonardo. I am Rachel, head of compliance here at the bank. Thanks for making the time. Could you tell me a little about your firm?
L
Good morning, Rachel. Of course. I am a partner at a boutique law firm in Brazil, and we work almost only with financial institutions.
R
Only financial clients? That is unusual. Which kind, exactly?
L
Banks, insurers, payment companies and investment funds. My area is tax law, so I advise them on what they pay and why.
R
Good. And what are you working on at the moment?
L
These days I am working on the tax reform. I am building scenarios for our clients, so they can see the impact before it happens.
R
That is exactly what my board is asking me for. Send me the short version, please. Two pages, not twenty.
Comprehension

About Rachel

1. What is Rachel's job?
She is the head of compliance at a bank.
2. What does she ask about first?
The firm: what kind of law firm it is and who its clients are.
3. What does she want at the end of the call?
The short version for her board: two pages, not twenty.
