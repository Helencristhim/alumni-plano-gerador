Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 1 -- Who Is Leonardo Constantino in English?

Series
Suits -- Season 1, Episode 1 (Pilot)

A New York law firm, seen from the inside: partners, clients, deadlines and the language people actually use when they negotiate. Listen for how the lawyers introduce themselves and describe what the firm does -- the same task you practiced today.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down three sentences a lawyer uses to explain what the firm does. Say them out loud with your own clients in them.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
Business English from All Ears English -- BE 552: How to Continue Small Talk at the Start of a Work Call

The first ninety seconds of a call with a foreign client, taken apart phrase by phrase. This is the part that decides how the rest of the meeting sounds, and it is short enough for the drive to the office.

Tip: pick two phrases from the episode and use them out loud before your next call, even alone. Say them until they stop feeling like a script.

Listen on Spotify ↗
Video
Tell Me About Yourself -- A Good Answer to This Question (Linda Raynier)

A career coach shows how to answer the first question of any professional conversation: a short story with a clear point, not a list of jobs. Exactly the structure we mapped in class today.

Tip: watch once for the idea, then build your own three-sentence answer -- what you do (present simple), who your clients are, and what you are working on now (present continuous).
