Lesson 5 -- My Dogs

Series
Dogs -- Season 1, Episode 3: "Ice on the Water" (Netflix)

An Italian fisherman on Lake Como and his old Labrador, Ice. Forty minutes of people describing an animal they love, in the accent you heard in tonight's first listening.

Tip: the family speaks Italian, so read the subtitles in English, not Portuguese. Write down every description of the dog you can catch, and check the order of the adjectives.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
TED Talks Daily -- "Why all dogs are good dogs", Alexandra Horowitz

A dog cognition scientist on what a dog actually perceives of the world, and why the animal in your kitchen is not the animal you think you are living with. Around twelve minutes, one voice, American.

Tip: she stacks adjectives constantly when she describes dogs. Listen once for the argument only, then a second time counting how many two-adjective descriptions you catch.

Listen on Spotify ↗
Video
A brief history of dogs -- David Ian Howe, TED-Ed (5 min)

How a wolf that competed with us for food turned into the animal sleeping on your floor. Five minutes, animated, and the clearest narration you will find anywhere.

Tip: five minutes is short enough to watch twice. First time with no subtitles at all, second time with English subtitles to check what you missed.
