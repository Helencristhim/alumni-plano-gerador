Lesson 5 -- The People of a Small Town

Series
Rick Steves’ Europe -- Italy’s Great Hill Towns (full episode, 26 min)

Four Italian hill towns of a few thousand people each, and the people who live in them: the stallholders, the bakers, the men who have sat in the same square for forty years. Exactly the kind of place you chose.

Tip: he describes a person or a building every twenty seconds. Write down five noun phrases with two adjectives in them and check the order against tonight’s rule.

Podcast
A New Life in Italy -- Living Like a Local: Exploring Italy’s Hidden Gems Beyond the Big Cities

An hour on what changes when you leave the city for a town where the shopkeeper knows your name: the welcome, the curiosity, and the day you realize you have become a regular somewhere.

Tip: the speakers are not reading a script and some of them are not native speakers. Listen once for the story only, without stopping.

Talk
Why you should talk to strangers -- Kio Stark, TED (12 min)

Why the small unplanned exchange with the person at the stall is not small at all, and what it does to a street when people stop having it. The best argument anybody has made for a town where you bump into everybody.

Tip: she speaks fast and clear American English. Watch it once with no subtitles, then again with them and see how much you already had.
