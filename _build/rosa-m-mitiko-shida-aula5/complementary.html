Eighteen minutes with a philosopher who studies choices where neither option is clearly better. Connection to Lesson 5: this is the language of scenarios you practiced today, applied to the moment before anybody decides. The full transcript is on the page.

Grammar Video
"The first conditional" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the structure of this lesson, including the one thing everybody gets wrong. Connection to Lesson 5: will lives in the result half only, and the condition stays in the present however far away it is.

Tip: pause after each example and say the same sentence about a real decision at work. Then say it again starting with the other half, and drop the comma. Both are correct, and switching between them is what makes it sound natural.
