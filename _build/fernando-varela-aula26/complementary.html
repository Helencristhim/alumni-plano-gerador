"Defining relative clauses" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

A short lesson on who, which, that and where: how they join two ideas and tell us which person, thing or place. Connection to Lesson 26: this is exactly "Ana is the nurse who talks to every family."

Tip: pause after each example and say one sentence about a person or a place in your network with the same structure: "... is the person who..." or "... is the place where..."
