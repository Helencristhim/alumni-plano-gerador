Chapter 4: The Code

One Sentence, Two Ideas

who · which · that · where

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Orange Words Do?

"Ana is the nurse who talks to every family."

"A supermarket which is two blocks away sends food."

"They come from cities that are very far from Sao Paulo."

"There is a small park where Pedro can play."

Look at the orange words. Which one is for a person? For a thing? For a place? Which one works for people and things?

WordUseExample
whoFor people.Ana is the nurse who talks to the families.
whichFor things.It is a pilot which the board approved.
thatFor people OR things. Very common when you speak.They are cities that are very far away.
whereFor places.It is a park where Pedro can play.
The trapDo not repeat the person or the place: the nurse who talks — never the nurse who she talks; the city where she lives — never the city where she lives there.

Who, which, that and where join two ideas in one sentence, and they tell us WHICH person, thing or place.
