Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Caregiver -- a person who takes care of a sick person, often someone in the family
"His wife is his caregiver during the treatment."
Volunteer -- a person who works without pay to help other people
"Carlos is a volunteer who helps the new families."
Rent -- the money that you pay every month to live in a place
"The rent near the hospital is very high."
Neighborhood -- a part of a city where people live
"It is a quiet neighborhood with a small park."
Donation -- money or things that you give to help other people
"A local supermarket sends a donation of food every week."
Lonely -- sad because you are alone
"She felt lonely in a hotel far from her family."
Comfortable -- relaxed: you feel good and calm in a place
"The apartment is small, but it is very comfortable."
Settle in -- to start to feel at home in a new place
"The family settled in fast and made new friends."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Fernando is the manager who planned the housing pilot. In March, the board approved three apartments which are two blocks from the hospital. They are for families who come from other states for long treatments.

Ana is the nurse that talks to every family before they arrive. Near the building, there is a small park where the children can play, and a laundry room where the families can wash their clothes. Lucia, a mother from Belem, says: "It was the first place in Sao Paulo where we felt comfortable."

1. "Fernando is the manager who planned the housing pilot." Why does the text use who?
A Because who is the word for questions, and this sentence is a question about the manager of the housing pilot.
B Because who is for a person: it links Fernando to what he did.
C Because who is for places.
2. "There is a small park where the children can play." What does where link?
A A person and an action.
B A thing and a time in the future, because the children will play there next year.
C A place and what happens there.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A Ana is the nurse that talks to every family.
B Ana is the nurse which talks to every family, because which is the formal word for nurses.
C Ana is the nurse who she talks to every family.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Who, Which, That, Where

GRAMMAR

Who, which, that and where join two ideas in one sentence. They tell us which person, thing or place.

WordRuleExample
whofor peopleAna is the nurse who talks to the families.
whichfor thingsIt is a pilot which the board approved.
thatfor people or things (very common when you speak)They come from cities that are very far away.
wherefor placesIt is a park where the children can play.
The trapDo not repeat the person or the placethe city where she lives (not where she lives there)

Two sentences become one: "Ana is a nurse. She talks to every family." becomes "Ana is the nurse who talks to every family." The word who takes the place of she.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Presenting the pilot. Choose the best thing to say.

Sarah asks who the apartments are for. You say:
A "They are for families which come from other states for long treatments, and they stay for one month or more."
B "They are for families who they come from far away."
C "They are for families who come from other states."
A new volunteer asks about the park. You say:
A "It is a park who the children play."
B "It is a small park where the children can play."
C "It is a small park where the children can play there in the afternoon, after school and after lunch."
Your director asks what the pilot is. You say:
A "It is a project which helps families stay near the hospital."
B "It is a project where helps families."
C "It is a project who the board approved in March, after a long meeting with Finance and the nursing team."
A partner asks who organizes the volunteers. You say:
A "Carlos is the person where organizes them."
B "Carlos is the person that organizes them."
C "Carlos is the person who he organizes them every Monday at the hospital, with the nurses."
