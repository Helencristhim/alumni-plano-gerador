Inventing Anna — Episode 1, "Life of a VIP"

A journalist interviews the people around a fake heiress, and almost every scene is someone telling her what Anna said and promised. It is a whole episode built on the structure of this week: she told me, she said she would.

Tip: every time a character says "she told me" or "she said", pause and say what Anna's exact words were. Then press play and check.

6 Minute English — "Is rejection good for us?"

Six minutes on hearing "no" — something every sales call can bring. The presenters keep reporting what experts told them, so you hear the pattern of the lesson in a natural conversation. Free, with the full transcript on the page.

Tip: write down one sentence where a presenter reports what an expert said. Then say it to yourself again, as if you were telling your partner.
