Lesson 15 — Pre-class

She Said Friday

Four new words from the sales call — requirement, turnaround, quote, deadline. And one thing only: telling your partner what the client said, with her exact words. Around ten minutes.

Stage 1: Four new words

Vocabulary

You use all four ideas on every sales call. What is new is only the word and the sound. Read each one and say it out loud.

Requirement — something a client needs and asks for
"Their main requirement is payroll for forty people."
Turnaround — the time it takes to finish a job and give it back
"Our turnaround on payroll is two days."
Quote — the price you offer for a job, before it starts
"We will send you a quote by Friday."
Deadline — the last day something can be done
"The deadline to sign is March thirty-first."

Stage 3: What she said

Grammar

One structure this week. When you tell someone what another person said, the verb goes one step back: she says "it is slow" → she said it was slow; she says "I will call" → she said she would call. Use said alone, or told me: she told me the deadline was Friday. Never "she said me".

Linda: "Our provider is too slow." → "She said their provider ______ too slow."
A will be
B was
Linda: "I will need a quote by Friday." → "She said she ______ need a quote by Friday."
A would
B will
Which sentence is correct?
A She said me the deadline was in March.
B She told me the deadline was in March.
Your partner asks what the client wants. Which answer can your partner use?
A She said they needed payroll for forty people.
B She talked about payroll, for a lot of people, I think, more or less.

Stage 4: Complete the line

Practice

These four lines are what you tell your partner. You will say them in class.

"She said their main was payroll."
"She told me the turnaround ten days."
"She said she need a quote by Friday."
"She told me the was March thirty-first."

Stage 5: Say it out loud

Speaking

Say "she said" and "she told me" smoothly, as one block, before the information. That small start is what makes the report sound sure.

She said their main requirement was payroll for forty people.
She told me their current provider was too slow.
She said she would need a quote by Friday.

Stage 6: Before class

Speaking
Think of one real call with a client this month. Record three sentences to tell your partner about it: what the client needed (she said...), one problem they had (he told me...), and one date they gave you (she said she would...). One sentence each — no more.
