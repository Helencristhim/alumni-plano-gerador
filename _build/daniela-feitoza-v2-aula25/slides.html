Chapter 3

The Report Code

Who said it, and what the idea was

Grammar Discovery

Who Said What?

"Karen said that customers leave because of long lines."

"Tom argued that stores still need people."

"She told us that sales increased by twelve percent."

"According to Karen, speed is a customer project."

What do the orange words have in common? What comes after "told"?

UseFormExample

Say + that (no person). Tell + person + that. According to, always.

Grammar Practice

Report It

Karen: "Speed is a customer project." → "Karen ___said that speed is a customer project."

Style

Say or Tell?

say + that (no person)

She said that lines are long.
He said that tests are important.
They said that sales went up.

tell + person + that

She told me that lines are long.
He told us that tests are important.
They told the team that sales went up.

"The moderator told the audience that the panel had thirty minutes for questions."

Chapter 4

After the Panel

Greg missed it. Daniela reports it.

Dialogue

The Two-Minute Summary

G
Hi Daniela. I missed the checkout panel. Can you summarize it for me in two minutes?
D
Sure. There were two speakers, and the moderator was Linda Park. The first speaker, Karen Lopez, said that customers leave stores because of long lines.
G
Not because of prices? That is a strong idea. Did she have any numbers?
D
Yes. Her evidence was a survey. According to Karen, forty percent of customers left because the line was too long.
G
Forty percent. And the second speaker?
D
Tom Bennett argued that technology is not enough. He told the audience that older customers did not like self-checkout at first.
G
Interesting. That is exactly our problem in the Chicago stores. So what is your takeaway?
D
My main takeaway is that we should test the new checkout in a few stores first, with one person next to the machines.

Listening 1 — The Talk

One Simple Point

A talk by Karen Lopez, VP of Digital at a fashion retailer. Sound first — no text.

1. What is Karen’s main point?
Speed is not a technology project. It is a customer project.
2. What evidence does she give?
A survey of five thousand customers: forty percent said the line was too long.
3. What happened to the average waiting time?
It dropped from six minutes to two.
Listening 2 — The Panel

A Different Argument

Tom Bennett, CTO of a grocery chain, answers the moderator. Sound first — no text.

1. Does Tom agree or disagree with Karen?
Both: he agrees with her about speed, but he adds a warning.
2. What was the problem with self-checkout in his stores?
Many older customers did not like it. They wanted to talk to a person.
3. What is Tom’s takeaway for the audience?
Start small, listen to your customers, and keep people in the store.

Detective

Spot the Error

0 / 4 errors found

"Karen said me that lines are the problem."
"Karen told me that lines are the problem." (or: "Karen said that...") — say has no person after it
"Tom told that stores need people."
"Tom told us that stores need people." — tell needs a person
"According Karen, forty percent of customers left."
"According to Karen, forty percent of customers left."
"His main point it was that we should start small."
"His main point was that we should start small." — no extra "it"
