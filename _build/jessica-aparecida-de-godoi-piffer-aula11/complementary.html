Talk
Tim Urban — “Inside the mind of a master procrastinator” (TED)

Fourteen minutes on why people keep putting things off, told by a writer who does it professionally. Fast, funny and full of the verbs from this lesson: keep doing, decide to, stop doing, avoid doing.

Tip: every time he uses keep, stop, decide or avoid, pause and check the shape of the next verb. Then say one sentence about your own procrastination.

Tip: note three sentences with regret, stop or can’t help. Then record your own voice message to Greg in the same shape.
