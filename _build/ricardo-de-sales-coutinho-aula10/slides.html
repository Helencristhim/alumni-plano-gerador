Immigration

The Same Four Questions

"What is the purpose of your trip?"

Answer: "Work." or "Business."

"Where do you stay?"

Answer: "At a hotel in Brickell."

"How long is your trip?"

Answer: "Ten days."

"Who travels with you?"

Answer: "Nobody." or "My wife and my daughter."

Short answers are the right answers here. Nobody wants a long sentence.

Reading

Arrival in the United States

What happens when you land

The flight from São Paulo to Miami is eight hours and twenty minutes. You land at six in the morning.

First you go to immigration. There is a line, and it is long. Have your passport in your hand. Do not use the phone in the line.

At the booth, an officer asks four questions: what the purpose of your trip is, where you stay, how long your trip is, and who travels with you. Short answers are good answers.

After immigration you take your bag from the belt. Then you go to customs, and then you are free. Welcome to the United States.
