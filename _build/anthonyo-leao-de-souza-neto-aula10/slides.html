Your Words at Work

Which Word Do You Need?

1. You land and you cannot find the person with your name on a sign.
Wait. "Where does the driver wait? I do not find him."
2. You want to know when the plane gets there.
Arrive. "What time does the flight arrive?"
3. It is twenty minutes on foot and you have two bags.
Taxi. "It is far. I need a taxi, please."
4. A number is on the screen and you need the right door.
Gate. "Sorry, which gate? One four, or four zero?"

Dialogue

Forty Minutes to the Hotel

N
Mr. de Souza? I'm Nadia, your driver. Welcome. How was the flight?
L
Thank you. It was fine, but I'm tired, because it was eleven hours.
N
Eleven hours! Is this your first time here?
L
No, it isn't. I'm here two or three times a year. And you? Are you from here?
N
Born here. Forty minutes to the hotel, maybe fifty with the rain. Do you have family in Brazil?
L
Yes, two children. My son plays football and my daughter studies in another city.
N
Nice. Listen, the front desk asked me to tell you: the car tomorrow is at seven thirty, not at eight.
L
Sorry, can you say that again, please?
N
Of course. Tomorrow morning. Seven thirty, not eight. The traffic is bad after eight.
L
So, seven thirty, not eight. Is that right? And is there a restaurant near the hotel?
N
That is right. And yes — the Blue Door, two streets away, on the corner, opposite the bank.
Comprehension

What Did Nadia Say?

1. How long is the drive to the hotel?
Forty minutes, maybe fifty with the rain.
2. What time is the car tomorrow, and why?
Seven thirty, not eight, because the traffic is bad after eight.
3. Where is the restaurant?
The Blue Door, two streets away, on the corner, opposite the bank.
Listening

The Announcement When You Land

Everybody is standing up and nobody is listening. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What is the local time, and how is the weather?
Six fifteen in the evening. Cold, about seven degrees, with some rain.
2. If this is your final destination, where do you go and where are the bags?
Follow the blue signs after passport control. The bags are on belt number four.
3. And if you have a connection tonight?
Do not follow the blue signs. Go to the transfer desk on the right, quickly — the last flights leave in about fifty minutes.
Listening 2

The Call After the First Day

He tells a friend how it went. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. How long is he with the driver, and how is it?
Twenty minutes, alone, in English. Not perfect — three or four times he is lost and asks for a repeat, and it is fine.
2. What is his trick at the hotel, to be sure about the room?
He says the number back: room five twelve, fifth floor.
3. What is the part he really wants to tell his friend about?
Not the words. The part where he is not quiet.
