"The first conditional" -- Conditionals with Georgie, BBC Learning English

A short and clear lesson about "if + present, will + verb". Connection to Lesson 23: this is the exact pattern you used today to set a condition in a proposal: "If you confirm this week, we will organize the visit."

Tip: pause after each example and make your own sentence about a partnership, like "If the consortium agrees, we will start in April."
