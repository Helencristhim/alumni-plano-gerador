Chapter 3: The Code

Closed Box, Open Box

I sent it on Tuesday · I have not sent it yet

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I attended the convention last March."

"I have attended three conventions this year."

"We signed the contract on Tuesday."

"We have not signed the contract yet."

In each pair, one sentence tells you exactly when and the other does not. Which is which, and what does that change?

FormWhen you use itExample
Past simple
attended · signed · sent		The moment is closed and you say it, or everybody knows it: yesterday, last March, on Tuesday, in 2019, two weeks ago."I sent it on Tuesday."
"We closed the quarter above target."
Present perfect
have attended · have not signed		The period is still open, or the moment does not matter: this year, this quarter, so far, already, yet, ever, never."I have sent three this week."
"We have not signed it yet."
The pair that goes togetherOpen with the present perfect, then give the detail in the past simple. This is how a good status update is built."We have lost two clients this year. One left in March and one left in June."
The trapA closed time expression and the present perfect can never share a sentence. If yesterday is in the sentence, have cannot be.NOT "I have sent it yesterday."
NOT "Last quarter we have launched two routes."

One test decides almost every case: does the sentence say when? If it does, use the past simple.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I have sent the proposal yesterday."
"I sent the proposal yesterday."
"Last quarter we have launched two new routes."
"Last quarter we launched two new routes."
"Did you ever work with a corporate client in Chile?"
"Have you ever worked with a corporate client in Chile?"

A closed time expression and have can never share a sentence. And when you ask about somebody's whole professional life, the natural question is have you ever — did you ever sounds like you are asking about a period that is over. If the sentence says when, drop the have.

Chapter 4: The Weekly Report

One Sentence Changed

Your update went out. Somebody edited the last paragraph.

Dialogue

The Line He Rewrote

P
Rosa, I read your status update this morning. It was clear, and short, which is rare. I changed one sentence.
R
Which one?
P
The last paragraph. You wrote that the client confirmed the contract last week. They have not confirmed anything. They asked for a new proposal.
R
You are right, and I see the difference now. I wrote it after a phone call on Monday, and on the call they sounded positive. But positive is not confirmed.
P
Exactly. Say what happened, then say what is still open. Two sentences. Your manager reads forty of these on a Monday.
R
I will send a correction this afternoon. Was there anything else?
P
The third quarter figures came out yesterday. Your region is up nine per cent. I have already sent you the file.
R
Nine per cent. Should I bring that up on Thursday, or will somebody else?
Comprehension

About Paulo

1. What did he say about the update before he criticized it?
That it was clear and short, which is rare.
2. Which sentence did he change, and why?
The last paragraph — because the client has not confirmed anything, they asked for a new proposal.
3. What rule does he give for writing an update?
Say what happened, then say what is still open — in two sentences.
4. What has he already done about the quarter figures?
He has already sent her the file.
The Real Thing

The Update, Rewritten

Weekly status update
Corporate accounts — week 41

Closed this week. We signed the renewal with the bank on Tuesday and we closed the third quarter nine per cent above target.

Still open. The client has not confirmed the new contract yet. They asked for a new proposal on Monday, and I have already sent the draft to the pricing team.

Bottom line. We have not lost the account. I will follow up on Thursday, after the regional call.

1. Why is "we signed the renewal on Tuesday" in the past simple?
Because the sentence says when — Tuesday is a closed moment.
2. Why is "the client has not confirmed the new contract yet" not in the past simple?
Because it is still open — yet means it has not happened up to now and is still expected.

The Weekly Summary

Three points for the commercial team. Sound first — no text.

1. How did the third quarter close in Brazil and in Chile?
Nine per cent above target in Brazil and four per cent below in Chile.
2. What is still open with the two contracts from August?
They have not been signed, and the client has asked for a new proposal in both cases.

The draft of the fourth quarter plan, by the end of next week — two people have already sent it.
