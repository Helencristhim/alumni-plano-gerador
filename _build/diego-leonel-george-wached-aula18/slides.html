Chapter 4: The Code

However or Therefore?

Connectors of contrast and result

Grammar Discovery

Contrast or Result?

"Although I sent the documents in May, the status is still pending."

"The deadline is on Friday. Therefore, I am writing to confirm the date."

"The form is complete. However, one letter is still missing."

"My letter arrived late. As a result, I have attached a copy."

Look at the red words, then at the blue ones. Which ones show a surprise or a contrast? Which ones show a consequence? Where is the comma?

JobFormExample
Contrast, two clauses in one sentenceAlthough + clause, main clauseAlthough I sent it in May, it is still pending.
Contrast, a new sentenceSentence. However, sentence.The form is complete. However, one letter is missing.
Contrast with a noundespite + noun / -ingDespite the delay, the file is complete.
Result, a new sentence (formal)Sentence. Therefore, / As a result, sentence.The deadline is Friday. Therefore, I am writing today.
Informal, inside one sentenceclause, but / so + clauseIt is late, but it is complete. It is urgent, so I called.
Registeremail to a program: however, therefore · message to a friend: but, soFormal emails prefer the longer connectors.

In one line: although and however show a contrast, therefore and as a result show a consequence; however and therefore start a new sentence, with a comma after them.

Listening 1 · A Program Coordinator

Two Hundred Emails a Day

A residency coordinator on the emails she answers first. Sound first — no text.

1. How many emails does she receive on a busy day?
About two hundred.
2. What does she do with an email without a name or with five questions?
She leaves it for later, and later often means never.
3. What does she want in the subject line?
The full name and what the person needs.
Listening 2 · An International Graduate

The Email I Rewrote

A doctor on the first email he sent to an American attending. Sound first — no text.

1. What was wrong with his first email?
It was long, it started with his whole life, and it had no clear request.
2. What did his friend tell him to change?
One purpose, three short paragraphs and a clear subject line.
3. What happened after he sent the new email?
The attending answered the next morning and invited him to shadow her clinic for two weeks.
