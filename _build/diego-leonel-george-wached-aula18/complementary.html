Talk
Tim Urban -- "Inside the mind of a master procrastinator" (TED)

A funny talk about deadlines and why we leave important tasks for the last minute. It is the perfect reason to send that email to the program this week, and it is full of connectors used naturally.

Tip: write down every however, so and but you hear, and say if each one shows a contrast or a result.

Talk
Margaret Heffernan -- "Dare to disagree" (TED)

A business leader tells the story of a doctor who questioned an accepted idea, and explains how professionals can disagree politely and clearly. Polite but direct: the register of this lesson.

Tip: listen for the sentences in which she disagrees politely, and turn one of them into a formal email sentence with however.

Talk
David Grady -- "How to save the world (or at least yourself) from bad meetings" (TED)

A short, funny talk about meeting invitations that have no purpose. Everything he says about meetings is true for emails: say why you are writing, and ask for one clear thing.

Tip: after you watch, rewrite one email you sent recently with one clear purpose in the first sentence.
