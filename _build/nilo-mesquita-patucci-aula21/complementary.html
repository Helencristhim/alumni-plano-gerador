Lesson 21 -- News and Current Affairs: What Was Happening When the Story Broke

Film
"Spotlight" (2015) -- Official Trailer

An Oscar-winning film about a team of journalists who investigate a big scandal and protect their sources. Connection to Lesson 21: you hear real news words (story, source, allegation) and a lot of past tenses about what was happening.

Tip: watch the trailer twice. The second time, write down two sentences with "was" or "were" + verb-ing.

Video Series
"Fake News: Fact & Fiction" -- Episode 1 -- BBC Learning English

A short BBC series about news, sources, and how to check if a story is true. Connection to Lesson 21: it is the compliance view of the news, separating a rumor or an allegation from a fact.

Tip: pause after each new word and say one sentence about a sports story you know.

Grammar Video
"Make stories interesting with the past continuous" -- British Council

A short lesson on how the past continuous sets the scene of a story. Connection to Lesson 21: this is the story code of today, the background in the past continuous and the events in the past simple.

Tip: after the video, tell one news story from your week in three sentences, with "when" or "while".
