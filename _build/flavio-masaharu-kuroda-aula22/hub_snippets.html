Pricing Pages, Side by Side -- Comparing Two Offers

The note a customer writes when it puts your price next to a competitor's, and how to answer it with one difference and one reason. Key words: a tier, a subscription, per seat, a setup fee, a free trial, an add-on, billed annually, a flat fee, usage-based, a hidden cost. Structure: contrast linkers (whereas, while, however, on the other hand, unlike).

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A tier -- one level of a product, with its own price and features
"The SAP connector is included in our Growth tier."
A subscription -- a payment you make regularly to keep using a service
"The subscription is renewed every year in January."
Per seat -- for each person who uses the software
"We charge twenty-five euros per seat per month."
A setup fee -- money you pay once, at the start, to prepare a system
"The setup fee covers configuration and training."
A free trial -- a short period when you can use a product without paying
"Their free trial lasts thirty days."
An add-on -- an extra part of a product that you buy separately
"The connector is an add-on, so it costs more."
Billed annually -- paid once a year, not every month
"The price is lower if it is billed annually."
A flat fee -- one fixed price that does not change with use
"For the first year, we can offer a flat fee."
Usage-based -- with a price that changes with how much you use
"A usage-based price grows when your volume grows."
A hidden cost -- a cost that is not clear on the price page
"Storage is often a hidden cost."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

When a customer compares two suppliers, it puts two things on the same line. To do that in English, you need a small word in the middle. Invent charges per seat, whereas Clearbook charges per invoice. The comma comes before whereas, and there is a full clause on each side.

While works in the same way, and it can also start the sentence: while their trial is free, ours is a paid pilot. Here while does not talk about time. It means the same as whereas.

However is different. It starts a new sentence, and it has a comma after it: their price is lower today. However, it grows with volume. On the other hand also starts a new idea, and it often goes in the middle, between commas.

Unlike needs a noun, not a clause: unlike Clearbook, we include the connector. Brazilian speakers often write however in the middle of one sentence, with no full stop, or put a whole clause after unlike.

1. Which sentence is correct?
A "We charge per seat, whereas they charge per invoice."
B "Their price is lower however it grows with volume."
C "Unlike Clearbook offers a free trial for thirty days, we offer a pilot."
2. In which sentence does while mean the same as whereas?
A "While I am sharing my screen, can you tell me who signs?"
B "While their setup fee is zero, ours covers training."
C "Please wait a moment while the system loads your invoices."
3. What comes after unlike?
A A full clause with a subject and a verb, like they offer a trial.
B A comma and then a new sentence.
C A noun or a name, like Clearbook.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Contrast Linkers

GRAMMAR

Two sides, one sentence. The linker decides where the comma goes.

LinkerHow to use itExample
whereasclause, whereas + clauseWe charge per seat, whereas they charge per invoice.
whileWhile + clause, clauseWhile their trial is free, ours is a pilot.
howeverSentence. However, + sentenceIt looks cheaper. However, it grows.
on the other handa new sentence, between commasClearbook, on the other hand, has one plan.
unlikeUnlike + noun, clauseUnlike Clearbook, we include support.

Never: their price is lower however it grows · unlike they offer a trial · per seat whereas, they charge

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer a real speaker would give.

Giulia says: Clearbook is cheaper. The best first answer is:
A "You are right about today. However, their price grows with your volume."
B "No, we are better, and our product is much more complete than theirs in every way."
C "Okay, we can give you a discount of thirty percent."
You want to compare the two price models. You say:
A "We charge per seat however they charge per invoice, and that is different."
B "Unlike they charge per invoice, we charge per seat."
C "We charge per seat, whereas they charge per invoice."
The CFO asks about extra costs. You answer:
A "There are no costs."
B "Unlike many suppliers, we include storage, so there is no hidden cost."
C "Our price is very good, so please do not worry about any other extra costs at all."
You want both offers to be compared fairly. You ask:
A "Why did you send me the page of our competitor, and who told you to look at it?"
B "Can we compare whereas the offers?"
C "Can we compare both offers on the same basis?"
