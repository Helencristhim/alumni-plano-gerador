Lesson 22 -- Pricing Pages, Side by Side

Podcast
6 Minute English (BBC Learning English) -- "Why are prices going up?"

Two presenters talk about prices, costs and why the same product can cost more from one month to the next. Short, clear, and with the full script on the page.

Tip: read the script and find two sentences that compare. Rewrite each one with whereas or however.

Talk
Dan Ariely -- "Are we in control of our own decisions?" (TED, 17 min)

A famous example about a magazine subscription with three prices shows how people choose when two offers sit side by side. Every pricing page uses this idea.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down the three subscription prices and compare them in two sentences with whereas.

Talk
Rory Sutherland -- "Perspective is everything" (TED, 18 min)

An advertising executive explains why the value a customer sees can matter more than the price on the page. Useful the next time a customer says you are more expensive.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Choose one example and explain it in one sentence that starts with While.
