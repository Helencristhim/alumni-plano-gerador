Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Simon Sinek: “How great leaders inspire action” (18 min)

Why some leaders inspire people and others do not: start with why, not with what.

Tip: write down one sentence with encourage someone to, about something you want your team to do.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Roselinde Torres: “What it takes to be a great leader” (9 min)

A leadership consultant on the three questions that great leaders ask themselves today.

Tip: after the talk, say in one minute what your team has difficulty doing, and how you help them.

Watch on TED ↗
TEDx Talk
Drew Dudley: “Everyday leadership” (6 min)

A short, funny talk about small moments when one person helps another and changes a life.

Tip: watch it twice: first with subtitles, then without. Count how many times you hear help.
