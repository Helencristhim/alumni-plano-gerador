Lesson theme: leading a meeting -- chair, convene, steer, digress, overrun, interject, chime in, show of hands, motion, sidebar; polite requests with "Would you mind" + -ing and "Could you" + base verb, and indirect questions. Passive exposure outside class -- tick each one when you finish it.
