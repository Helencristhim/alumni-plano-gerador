Chapter 3 -- The Code

Speaking of Which

The small words that build the bridge

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Purple Words Have in Common?

"Speaking of the future, have you thought about what comes next for the company?"

"Before getting into the numbers, I'd like to hear your goals."

"Moving on to the timeline, when would you like to start?"

"Going back to what you said about your employees, that is exactly why the buyer matters."

What does each phrase do? What kind of word comes right after it?

UseFormExample
Bridge from the chatSpeaking of + noun / -ing"Speaking of the future, ..."
Delay a topicBefore + -ing"Before getting into the numbers, ..."
Move to the next topicMoving on to + noun / -ing"Moving on to the timeline, ..."
Return to a topicGoing back to + noun / what..."Going back to your employees, ..."
Neverpreposition + to + verbNot "Before to start" / "Moving on to talk about"

Sudden Stop or Bridge?

Chapter 4 -- Getting There

The Lane Family: Ten Minutes

One conversation, two recordings, one summary

Dialogue

Eduarda and Victor

V
Eduarda, come in. Did you find the house easily? The traffic out here is terrible on Tuesdays.
E
It was fine, thank you. Is that your sailboat in the photo?
V
It is. I sail every weekend. It's the only time I don't think about the factory.
E
That sounds wonderful. Speaking of time away from the factory, have you thought about what retirement would look like?
V
Ha! That was a smooth segue. You're good at this.
E
I just like to build some rapport first. Before getting into the numbers, I'd like to touch on your goals.
V
My goal is simple. My people keep their jobs. Everything else comes second.
E
Going back to your employees, that's exactly why the buyer matters. Shall we get down to business?
V
Yes. But I warn you, my daughter will bring up the price. She always does.
E
That's fine. We'll circle back to the price at the end, once we agree on the right kind of buyer.
Comprehension

About Victor

1. What does Victor do every weekend?
He sails.
2. What is Victor's main goal for the sale?
That his people keep their jobs.
3. What does Victor say his daughter will bring up?
The price.

Your Real Meeting
