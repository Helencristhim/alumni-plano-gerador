Lesson 24 -- Small Talk to Big Talk

Series
Ted Lasso -- Season 1, Episode 1 "Pilot"

An American football coach arrives in London to manage a soccer team he knows nothing about. Connection to Lesson 24: Ted builds rapport with everyone before he talks about the job, and he moves from small talk to serious topics in a way that never feels like a sudden stop.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down two moments when a character moves from a friendly chat to a serious topic, and the exact words they use.

Talk
"Why you should talk to strangers" -- Kio Stark, TED

A short talk about the small conversations we have with people we don't know, and why they matter. Connection to Lesson 24: small talk is not wasted time. It is where rapport starts, and rapport is what makes the business part possible.

Tip: listen once without subtitles. Then write three small-talk questions you could ask a new client, and one bridge from each to a business topic.

Video
"10 ways to have a better conversation" -- Celeste Headlee, TED

A radio host who interviews people for a living shares her rules for a real conversation. Connection to Lesson 24: her advice to "go with the flow" is the secret of a good segue. The best bridge comes from what the other person just said.

Tip: watch it at normal speed. Choose the two rules you break most in client meetings, and plan how to fix them in your next one.
