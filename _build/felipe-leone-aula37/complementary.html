Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Matt Cutts: “Try something new for 30 days” (3 min)

A short talk about small plans that change a life: one new thing, every day, over the next thirty days.

Tip: say three things you are going to try over the next thirty days, and one you would like to try one day.

TED Talk
Tim Urban: “Inside the mind of a master procrastinator” (14 min)

A very funny talk about deadlines, and what happens to plans when there is no deadline at all.

Tip: note every sentence with a deadline (in, within, by) that he uses.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Pico Iyer: “Where is home?” (14 min)

A travel writer who has lived in many countries on what home means when you are always on the road.

Tip: answer out loud: where would you like to live for one year, and why?
