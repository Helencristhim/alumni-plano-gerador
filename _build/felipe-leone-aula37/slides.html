Work Situation

The Next Business Trip

Your manager wants you to visit the Atlanta office next quarter. You have a short call with the travel team to plan the trip.

1. When are you going to travel, and how long are you going to stay?
2. What would you like to do there, besides the meetings?
3. What do you need to finish within the next two weeks before the trip?
Role-Play

Planning the Trip

A call with Jessica, from the travel team in Atlanta. She needs dates, plans and deadlines. Keep your answers short and clear.

  • Round 1: Jessica asks about your dates, hotel, meetings and what you would like to see. You answer with plans and wishes.
  • Round 2: You lead. Ask Jessica what is going to happen in the office that week and what she would recommend.
