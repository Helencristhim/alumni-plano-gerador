Chapter 4: The Code

Before, Now and Next

can · could · was able to · will be able to

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Orange Words Say?

"Ten years ago, doctors in Brazil couldn't offer this therapy."

"Today, Marina can walk and work again."

"She was able to do the therapy near her family."

"In five years, every patient will be able to get it."

Look at the orange words. Which ones are about the past? Which one is about the future? Why do we say will be able to and not will can?

FormUseExample
can / can't + verbAbility or possibility now. The same for all people.Today, we can treat forty patients a year.
could / couldn't + verbAbility in the past, in general.Ten years ago, we couldn't offer it.
was / were able to + verbOne real success in the past: you did it.Last year, we were able to sign two agreements.
will be able to + verbAbility in the future. Never will can.In 2028, fifteen hospitals will be able to send patients.
QuestionCan + subject + verb? · Could + subject + verb?Can you join the call on Monday?
The trapAfter can and could, the verb is ALWAYS the base form, with no to: I can speak — never I can to speak or I could spoke.

Could tells what was possible. Can tells what is possible. Will be able to tells what is coming.
