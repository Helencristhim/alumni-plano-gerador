Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Therapy -- a kind of treatment for an illness
"CAR-T is a new therapy for some blood cancers."
Breakthrough -- an important new discovery that solves a big problem
"For some patients, this therapy was a real breakthrough."
Approve -- to officially say yes to something
"The committee approved the new budget on Friday."
Access -- the chance or the right to use or get something
"Patients in small cities need better access to cancer care."
Afford -- to have enough money to pay for something
"Many families can't afford a long trip abroad."
Side effect -- a bad extra effect of a medicine or a treatment
"The most common side effect is a high fever."
Recover -- to get healthy again after an illness
"She recovered in three months and went back to work."
Survival rate -- the percentage of patients who are alive after a period of time
"The survival rate for this cancer is much better now."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

In 2019, the Oncology Network couldn't offer CAR-T therapy, and patients couldn't get it anywhere in Brazil. Fernando remembers one family from Manaus: they couldn't afford a trip abroad.

Last year, the network was able to treat forty patients, and most of them recovered well. Today, six partner hospitals can send patients to Sao Paulo. But many families still can't stay a whole month in the city. Fernando's goal is clear: in 2028, patients will be able to do the follow-up near home.

1. "The network couldn't offer CAR-T therapy." When was this true?
A In 2019, in the past.
B Today, because the therapy is still not approved in Brazil for any type of cancer or any patient.
C In 2028, in the future.
2. "Last year, the network was able to treat forty patients." What does this mean?
A The network wanted to treat forty patients, but in the end it didn't, because the budget was too small.
B The network really treated forty patients last year.
C The network will treat forty patients next year.
3. Why does the text say "patients will be able to" and not "patients will can"?
A Because will can is only for formal documents and reports.
B Because will be able to is the past form of can.
C Because will can is not correct: for the future of can, we use will be able to.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Can, Could, Be Able To

GRAMMAR

Could tells what was possible. Can tells what is possible. Will be able to tells what is coming.

FormRuleExample
Nowcan / can't + verb (the same for all people)Today, we can treat forty patients a year.
Past (in general)could / couldn't + verbTen years ago, we couldn't offer it.
Past (one real success)was / were able to + verbLast year, we were able to sign two agreements.
Futurewill be able to + verb (never will can)In 2028, more patients will be able to get it.
QuestionCan + subject + verb? · Could + subject + verb?Can you join the call on Monday?
The trapAfter can and could, use the base form, with no toI can speak (not I can to speak)

Use can for now and could for the past in general. When you talk about one real success in the past, say was able to or were able to.
