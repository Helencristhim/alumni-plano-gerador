Lesson 20 -- The Security Questionnaire

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Emails"

A short episode on writing clear work emails: how to open, how to ask, and how to close. Exactly what you need for the email that goes with your answers to a questionnaire. Full script on the same page.

Tip: read the script and copy one sentence that promises something with a date. Rewrite it for your answers to Giulia with will be sent.

Talk
James Lyne -- "Everyday cybercrime -- and what you can do about it" (TED, 17 min)

A security researcher shows, live on stage, how easily data can be stolen. After this talk, every question on a security questionnaire makes sense.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down three things he says must be done or should be done, and say each one with must be or should be.

Watch on TED ↗
Documentary
The Great Hack (2019)

The story of how the personal data of millions of people was collected and used without their permission. It explains why European customers ask so many questions about where data is stored.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down two sentences about data that use a passive, like the data was collected, and change them to must be or cannot be.
