Chapter 4: The Code

Rules About Things, Not People

How a questionnaire talks

Grammar Discovery

What Comes After the Modal?

"All customer data must be stored in the EU."

"Permissions can be removed in one hour."

"Backups should be tested every quarter."

"Data cannot be accepted from outside the EU."

What do the purple words have in common? And who does the action in each sentence?

ModalMeaningExample
must be + past participleobligation: no choiceData must be encrypted.
must not be + past participlenot allowedPasswords must not be shared by email.
should be + past participlerecommendationBackups should be tested every quarter.
can be + past participleit is possiblePermissions can be removed in one hour.
cannot be + past participleimpossible or not acceptedData cannot be stored outside the EU.
will be + past participlea promise about the futureThe report will be sent by Friday.
Questionmodal + subject + be + participleHow fast can permissions be removed?

In one line: modal + be + past participle, and the thing the form asks about comes first.

Chapter 6: Your Turn

Two Recordings, Then Giulia’s Call

Listening 1 · The IT Manager

Five Minutes to Trust You

An IT manager on how he reads supplier answers. Sound first — no text.

1. What is wrong with the sentence we take security very seriously?
It is long and vague, and it tells him nothing.
2. What does a good answer look like?
Short and specific, like: in Frankfurt; yes, at rest and in transit; on the same day.
3. What should a supplier do when it does not have something yet?
Be honest and give a date, for example: the report will be sent next month.
Listening 2 · The Sales Director

Three Weeks, Almost Lost

A sales director on the questionnaire that nearly killed her deal. Sound first — no text.

1. Why did she nearly lose her biggest deal?
She passed the questionnaire to IT without reading it, and three weeks later it was still not answered.
2. What three kinds of questions does she mark now?
Questions that can be answered by sales, questions that must be answered by IT, and questions that should be discussed with the customer.
3. What does she send the customer, and why?
A short email with a date for the full answers, so the customer knows the deal is alive.
