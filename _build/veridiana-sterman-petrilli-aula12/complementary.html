Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Queer Eye — Season 1, Episode 1 "You Can't Fix Ugly" (Netflix)

Five friendly experts help one man change his week: what he eats, how often he cleans, what he makes time for. Warm, funny and full of small habits, with nothing sad in it.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Every time somebody says how often something happens, write the sentence down.

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "How To Make Exercise A Habit That Sticks"

A short episode on the same idea as today’s text: how to build a habit you can keep, how often is realistic, and what to do when you skip a day.

Tip: the page has the full text. Find six expressions of frequency and say each one in a sentence about your own week.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Matt Cutts — "Try something new for 30 days" (TED)

Three minutes on small habits: doing one new thing every day for a month, and what stays afterward. Short, clear and easy to follow at B1.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Afterwards, choose one thing you could do every day for thirty days, and say how often you do it now.
