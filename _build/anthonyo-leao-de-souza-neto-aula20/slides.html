The Three That Save You

When the Story Stops

"What happened was..."

the start: when you do not know how to begin, begin with this

"Sorry, how do you say...?"

the bridge: when one word is missing, ask, and go on

"Anyway, the end was good."

the exit: when the story is too long, jump to the end

Chapter 5: Real Speed

Dialogue

So, How Was the Week?

C
Leão! You are back. So, how was the week?
L
Long, but very good. The flight was three hours late, and I had jet lag for two days.
C
Oh no. And the hotel?
L
My name wasn't in the system, but they gave me a suite. And on Wednesday I couldn't sleep. A party next door.
C
Terrible! Did you go to Porto?
L
Yes, I did. There was a dinner with forty guests. I met Paul again, and I introduced Nina to him.
C
Nina told me! She liked him a lot. What was your favorite day?
L
Saturday. I went on a tour, and I sent a postcard to my daughter.
C
That is lovely. My week was quiet. I was abroad too, in Madrid, but I worked every day.
L
Every day? That is a sad journey. Was there any time for the city?
C
Only on Sunday. I saw a museum, and I ate churros. That is my only memory!
L
Next time, a week like mine. But first I need to unpack.
Comprehension

What Did Claire Say?

1. Where was Claire, and what did she do every day?
In Madrid. She worked every day.
2. When did she have time for the city?
Only on Sunday.
3. What did she do on Sunday?
She saw a museum and she ate churros.
