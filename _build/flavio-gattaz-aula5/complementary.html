Podcast
How I Built This -- Discovery Channel and CuriosityStream: John Hendricks

An hour of one man telling his own trajectory in order, out loud, to somebody who keeps asking what changed. It is the long version of what you have to do in two minutes, and you can hear exactly where the turning points are because he stops on them.

Tip: listen for the first three minutes only, twice. Write down where he starts the story, and ask yourself whether you would have started there.

The admissions team taking apart the things candidates believe about the application and the interview, including the belief that there is a right answer to the opening question. Short, direct, and spoken by the people who actually do the shortlisting.

Talk
TED -- Simon Sinek, "How great leaders inspire action" (18 min)

The talk that made the argument for starting with why instead of what. It is the same move you practiced today at the level of a sentence, applied to a whole story: the order in which you say things decides what the listener keeps.

Tip: watch it once with subtitles and once without, and then reorder your own two-minute answer so the why comes before the what.
