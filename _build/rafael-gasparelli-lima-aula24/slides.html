Chapter 3: Grammar Review

Four Tools in One Place

Report, describe, advise, compare

Grammar Review

What Does Each Sentence Do?

Claire said the visit was very productive.

The contract was signed by both directors.

We should use the same backup plan for every shipment.

How about meeting on Wednesday to plan it?

It was the best trip of her year.

Report, describe, advise or compare?

ToolStructureExample
Report words (L19)said / told me + one step back (is > was)She said the visit was productive.
Say what was done (L21)was / were + participle (+ by)The contract was signed.
Give advice (L22)should / shouldn’t + base verbWe should use a backup plan.
Suggest (L22)Why don’t we + base verb? · How about + -ing?How about meeting on Wednesday?
Compare (L23)the most / the best · (not) as ... asIt was the best trip.

Chapter 4: Claire Says Goodbye

The Last Coffee

One conversation, two recordings, one full week

Dialogue

Before the Flight

C
Rafael, thank you for everything. This was the best business trip of my year.
R
Thank you, Claire. Our CEO said the meeting was very productive.
C
I agree. And the contract was finalized in only two days! That’s a real milestone.
R
Yes. It was signed by both directors on Wednesday. What about the problem in Rotterdam?
C
It’s solved. The root cause was found, and the damaged boxes were replaced.
R
Great. I think we should use the same backup plan for every shipment.
C
Good idea. How about writing a short report for both teams?
R
Sure. And next time in Chicago, I promise I’ll be punctual!
C
Ha! And I’ll be as patient as a Brazilian. Our relationship is stronger now.
R
It is. Have a good flight, Claire, and see you in Chicago.
Comprehension

About Claire

1. How does Claire describe her trip?
She says it was the best business trip of her year.
2. What does Claire say about the problem in Rotterdam?
It is solved: the root cause was found, and the damaged boxes were replaced.
3. What does Claire suggest?
Writing a short report for both teams.
