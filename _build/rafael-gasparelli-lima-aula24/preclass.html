Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Eight words from Lessons 19 to 23. Tap Listen to hear each one.

Finalize -- to complete the last details, so that something is ready
"The contract was finalized on Wednesday."
Milestone -- an important step or moment in a project
"Claire's visit was an important milestone for the partnership."
Shipment -- a group of products that is sent together
"The next shipment of mangoes leaves Santos on Monday."
Findings -- the results of an audit or a study
"The findings were presented to the board."
Root cause -- the real reason why a problem happened
"The root cause was found: the container was too warm."
Backup plan -- a second plan you use if the first plan fails
"We should use the same backup plan for every shipment."
Punctual -- arriving at the right time, not late
"Claire was punctual for every meeting."
Relationship -- the connection between two people or two companies
"After the visit, our relationship with Claire is stronger."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read Rafael's report to the board, then answer the questions.

This week was the best week of the quarter. On Monday, the client in Rotterdam told us that twenty boxes were damaged. The root cause was found quickly: the container was too warm. The damaged boxes were replaced, and the client said she was satisfied.

On Tuesday, Claire from Chicago visited our headquarters. She was very punctual, and she said our lunch was the most useful meeting of her trip. The contract was finalized on Wednesday, an important milestone for our relationship. Next month, we should use the same backup plan for every shipment. Why don't we present the audit findings at the next board meeting?

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Four Tools From Lessons 19 to 23

GRAMMAR

Report words, say what was done, give advice or suggestions, and compare.

UseStructureExample
Report words (L19)said / told me + one step back (is > was)She said the visit was productive.
Say what was done (L21)was / were + participle (+ by)The contract was signed.
Give advice (L22)should / shouldn't + base verbWe should use a backup plan.
Suggest (L22)Why don't we + base verb? · How about + -ing?How about meeting on Wednesday?
Compare (L23)the most / the best · (not) as ... asIt was the best trip.

Never: she said the visit is · the contract signed by · we should to use · the more productive
