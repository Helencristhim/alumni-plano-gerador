Grammar Rule

Whose? my, his, her, our, their

OwnerWordExample
ImyMy aunt lives in Sao Paulo.
youyourIs your family big?
he (a man)hisRyan and his sister.
she (a woman)herKate and her cousins.
weourOur family is big.
theytheirTheir house is small.
a name'sMy mother's sister is my aunt.

Key idea: the word agrees with the owner, never with the thing after it. Ryan is a man, so it is his sister. Kate is a woman, so it is her brother. And it never changes in the plural: his cousin, his cousins.

Grammar Practice

Who Is the Owner?

"Ryan misses _____his grandparents in Chicago." (the owner is Ryan)
"Kate is with _____her cousins today." (the owner is Kate)
"My aunt and my uncle love _____their three children." (the owners are two people)
"My family is small, but _____our house is always open." (the owner is my family and me)

Spot the Error

Fix the Sentence

0 / 4 errors found

"Ryan lives with her sister Kate."
"Ryan lives with his sister Kate." (the owner is Ryan, a man)
"Kate is with his cousins today."
"Kate is with her cousins today." (the owner is Kate, a woman)
"My aunt and my uncle love theirs children."
"My aunt and my uncle love their children." (their, never theirs, before a noun)
"The sister of my mother is my aunt."
"My mother's sister is my aunt." (use the apostrophe s with people)
