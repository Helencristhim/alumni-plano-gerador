Lesson 28 -- Change and Progress

Video
Hans Rosling: 200 Countries, 200 Years, 4 Minutes

A famous statistician shows how the world has changed in two hundred years, with moving bubbles and numbers. Short, clear, and full of the language of change: has grown, has doubled, since.

Tip: watch twice. The second time, pause and say one sentence about the change with has + past participle.

TED Talk
Hans Rosling: The best stats you have ever seen

Twenty minutes on how countries have changed over the last decades. He talks slowly and uses many numbers, so it is good practice for describing progress.

Tip: write down three numbers he gives and say each one with since or for.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Hans Rosling: The magic washing machine

Nine minutes on how one machine has changed the lives of millions of women. A simple story of change, told with humor and very clear English.

Tip: after the talk, say one thing that has changed in your own life or work, using has + past participle.
