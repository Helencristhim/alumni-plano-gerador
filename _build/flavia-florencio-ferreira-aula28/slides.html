Chapter 4: The Change Code

Then, Now, and So Far

has grown · have doubled · since · so far

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The program has evolved a lot since 2016."

"The number of regions has doubled."

"So far, we have trained more than two thousand families."

"Sebrae-SP has worked with GI producers for ten years."

What do the orange words have in common? Did these changes stop in the past, or are they still true today?

FunctionFormExample
A change from then until nowhave / has + past participleThe program has grown.
How longfor + a period / since + a startfor ten years / since 2016
The result until todaySo far, + have / has + past participleSo far, we have trained 2,000 families.
A finished time (2016, last year)past simpleIn 2016, we started with three regions.
QuestionsHave / Has + subject + past participle?Have your exports grown?

Connect then and now: have / has + past participle. A finished date: past simple.

Chapter 5: The Progress Talk

The Numbers

Giorgio asks; Flavia explains

Dialogue

The Progress Talk

G
Flavia, my director asked me a simple question. How has your program changed?
F
It has evolved a lot. When I started, we had three regions. Since then, the number has doubled.
G
Impressive. Has the growth been fast?
F
Not fast, but steady. So far, we have trained more than two thousand families.
G
And have buyers noticed the change?
F
Yes. That is the biggest change: recognition. Ten years ago, nobody asked for the label. Now buyers ask for it.
G
Good. So what is the challenge for the next decade?
F
To keep the momentum and make the program sustainable.
