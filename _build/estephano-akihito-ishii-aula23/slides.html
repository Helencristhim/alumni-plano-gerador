Grammar Discovery

What Do the Orange Words Tell Us?

"My laptop does not work."

"Something is wrong with my phone."

"My headphones do not turn on."

"The Wi-Fi is not working right now."

Good news or bad news? And which little word always comes after wrong?

PatternUseExample
it / he / she + does not + verbone thing has a problemMy laptop does not work.
they + do not + verbtwo or more thingsMy headphones do not turn on.
Something is wrong with + thingthere is a problemSomething is wrong with my phone.
What is wrong with + thing?ask about the problemWhat is wrong with your laptop?
is not workinga problem right nowThe Wi-Fi is not working.
NeverNot My laptop not work. Not Something is wrong my phone.

In one line: It does not work. Something is wrong with it.

Ask and Answer

What Is Wrong?

Ask about the problem, then say what it is:

"What is wrong with your laptop?" -- "It does not turn on."

"Does your phone work?" -- "No, it does not."

"Is the Wi-Fi working?" -- "No, it is not. It is very slow."

Short form: does not = doesn’t. "My laptop doesn’t work."

Chapter 4 -- Help Desk

Something Is Wrong

One chat, two recordings, one support ticket

Dialogue

Estephano and Sara

S
Hi Estephano! You look worried. What is wrong?
E
Something is wrong with my new laptop. It is very slow today.
S
Oh no! Does it freeze?
E
Yes, it freezes every five minutes. And there is an error message on the screen.
S
Try this: restart the laptop. It fixes many problems.
E
I always restart it, but it does not work. And now it does not turn on!
S
Maybe it is the battery. Is the charger connected?
E
Yes, it is. Maybe the laptop is broken. I want to call TechStore. They can fix it.
