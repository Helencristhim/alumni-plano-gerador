Dizer o que está errado com um aparelho e pedir ajuda ao suporte (It does not work. Something is wrong with my laptop. What is wrong with...?). Vocabulário novo: broken, slow, battery, error, restart, fix, freeze, turn on.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. / Listen to each word and read the example.

Broken -- it does not work anymore; it needs a fix
"My old phone is broken." (quebrado)
Slow -- not fast; it takes a long time
"My computer is very slow today." (lento)
Battery -- the part of a phone or laptop that keeps the energy
"My phone battery is low." (bateria)
Error -- a message on the screen: something is wrong
"There is an error message on my screen." (erro)
Restart -- to turn a computer off and then on again
"Restart your phone. It fixes many problems." (reiniciar)
Fix -- to make a broken thing work again
"The technician can fix my laptop." (consertar)
Freeze -- when the screen stops and nothing moves
"My game freezes every five minutes." (travar)
Turn on -- to press the button and start a machine
"I turn on my computer at eight." (ligar)

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Describing Problems

GRAMMAR

Para dizer que algo não funciona: does not / do not + verbo. Para dizer que há um problema: something is wrong WITH + coisa. / To say something has a problem: does not / do not + verb, or something is wrong with + thing.

Form / FormaUse / UsoExample
it + does not + verbuma coisa não funcionaMy laptop does not work.
they + do not + verbduas ou mais coisasMy headphones do not turn on.
Something is wrong with ...tem algo errado com ...Something is wrong with my phone.
What is wrong with ...?Pergunta: o que tem de errado?What is wrong with your laptop?
is not workingnão está funcionando (agora)The Wi-Fi is not working.
Atenção: nunca My laptop not work (falta o does) e nunca wrong my phone (o certo é wrong with).
