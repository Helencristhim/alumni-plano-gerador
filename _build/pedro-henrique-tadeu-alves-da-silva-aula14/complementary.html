Song Exploder -- Billie Eilish and Finneas, “Everything I Wanted”

Billie Eilish and her brother Finneas take the song apart, layer by layer, and explain how each part was made and why it stayed. A quiet song built from a small number of sounds.

Tip: every time they mention a part of the song, say how many or how much of it they used: a few synths, very little reverb, hardly any drums.
