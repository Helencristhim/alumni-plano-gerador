Chapter 3: The Pattern

From 100% to 0%

always · usually · often · sometimes · never

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

I always drive to Alphaville.
I usually have a call with Paris on Monday.
The forum is sometimes difficult.
I never work on Sunday.

Four words say HOW OFTEN. Where are they — before or after the verb? And with IS?

UseFormExample
100% → 0%always > usually > often > sometimes > neveralways = 100% · never = 0%
Action verbsubject + adverb + verbI often travel to Paris.
Verb to beam / is / are + adverbThe forum is always in English.
QuestionHow often do you...?How often do you have the forum? — Every month.
Exact numberevery day / once a week / twice a monthWe have a weekly meeting: once a week.
CarefulNever at the end: "I drive always" is wrong. And never with don't: "I don't never" is wrong — NEVER is already negative.

Chapter 4: Your Week

The Calendar for Next Year

the forum, the meetings, the visits — how often?

Dialogue

How Often Does It Happen?

C
Rodrigo, I am planning the calendar for next year. How often do you have the KPI forum in Brazil?
R
We have it every month. It is always on the first Tuesday, at ten o'clock.
C
And the weekly meeting with your team?
R
It is usually on Monday morning. Sometimes it is on Tuesday, when I travel.
C
How often do the global teams visit Brazil?
R
They often come, about six times a year. They never come in December.
C
Good to know. And do you send the monthly update on Friday?
R
Yes, I always send it on the last Friday of the month. Sometimes it is late, but only one day.
C
One day is fine. And your commute? Is it long?
R
Yes. I usually drive one hour to Alphaville. But I never drive on Friday: I work from home.
