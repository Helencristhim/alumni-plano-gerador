Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
The Office (U.S.) — Season 1, Episode 1 "Pilot" (Netflix)

A normal day in a small office, told as a documentary. People explain to the camera what they do every day and what is happening this week, which is exactly the two layers of today’s lesson. Light, short and very funny.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Write down three sentences about the routine and three about what is happening right now.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "7 small things you can do to improve your to-do list"

A short, practical episode for a hectic week: how to organize what you do every day and what is only urgent right now. Clear American English at a calm speed.

Tip: the page has the full text. Listen once, then read it and find five verbs in the present simple and five with -ing.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Laura Vanderkam — "How to gain control of your free time" (TED)

A time-management writer on how busy people, many of them working mothers, really spend their week. Twelve minutes, easy to follow, and full of the routine language from this lesson.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Afterwards, describe your own week in five sentences: three about your routine, two about this week.
