How to make hard choices

A philosopher explains what makes a choice hard: the options are on a par, so no amount of extra data decides it for you. It reframes a decision memo as an argument for what you stand behind, not a search for proof.

Tip: turn her idea into one sentence of hedged English, for example this option is likely to be the stronger one because.

316. The Persuasion Equation

A Stanford business podcast on how to get attention for an idea and make the reasoning behind it stick. It is the spoken version of defending a recommendation, with a full transcript on the page.

Tip: note every phrase used to signal confidence or doubt, then sort them from strongest to weakest.

How to make faster decisions -- The Way We Work, a TED series

A short episode on deciding with incomplete information: which criteria matter, which assumptions you are allowed to make, and when to stop analysing. Exactly the structure of a decision memo.

Tip: write the criteria she names as a list, then use them as the headings of a short memo of your own.
