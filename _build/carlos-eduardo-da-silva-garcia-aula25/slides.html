Chapter 3: Was or Were?

How was it? · They were ready.

the verb to be in the past

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The meeting was on Tuesday at eleven."

"The numbers were ready."

"Were you nervous? -- Yes, I was."

"The shipment wasn't on time. It was delayed."

FormUseExample
I / he / she / it + wasone person or thing (past)The meeting was on Tuesday.
you / we / they + wereyou, or two or more (past)The numbers were ready.
wasn't / weren'tnegativeThe shipment wasn't on time.
Was / Were + subject...?yes / no questionWere you nervous? -- Yes, I was.
How / Where + was / were...?information question: How was the meeting? Where were you yesterday?

am / is = was; are = were. / am e is viram was; are vira were.
