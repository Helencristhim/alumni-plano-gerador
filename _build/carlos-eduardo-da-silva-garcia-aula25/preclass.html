Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Leia o texto do Carlos e responda às perguntas. (Read Carlos's text and answer.)

The meeting with Mr. Becker was on Tuesday at eleven. Emma and I were on video, and Mr. Becker was in Colombia. I was nervous, but his questions were simple. He asked: "How was the harvest?" I said: "It was good, but the weather was very dry." Then he asked: "Were the farms ready?" I said: "Twelve farms were ready. Three weren't." One shipment wasn't on time -- it was delayed. But the buyer wasn't worried.

1. Where was Mr. Becker during the meeting? (Onde estava o Sr. Becker durante a reunião?)
A In Colombia. (na Colômbia)
B In Geneva, in the office with Emma and the team. (Emma estava no vídeo)
C In Varginha. (não está no texto)
2. How many farms were ready? (Quantas fazendas estavam prontas?)
A Three farms, and the other farms were not ready. (três não estavam)
B Twelve. (doze)
C All the farms. (não todas)
3. Which sentence is correct? (Qual frase está correta?)
A "The questions was simple in the meeting." (questions = plural)
B "Did the harvest was good?" (sem did)
C "The questions were simple." (plural = were)

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Past of To Be: Was / Were

Grammar

O verbo to be no passado / The verb to be in the past

Form / FormaUse / UsoExample / Exemplo
I / he / she / it + wasuma pessoa ou coisa (one)The meeting was on Tuesday. (A reunião foi na terça.)
you / we / they + werevocê ou duas ou mais (you, many)The numbers were ready. (Os números estavam prontos.)
wasn't / weren'tnegativaThe shipment wasn't on time. (O embarque não chegou no prazo.)
Was / Were + sujeito...?pergunta de sim / nãoWere you nervous? -- Yes, I was. (Você estava nervoso? -- Sim.)
Atenção / CarefulCom was / were NÃO se usa did: Was the meeting good? (NÃO "Did the meeting was good?"). How was...? = Como foi...?

Dica: am / is viram was; are vira were. / Tip: am and is become was; are becomes were.
