Your Camino

1. Why did you decide to walk the Camino? What had you heard about it before you went?
2. Where did you start, and what was the hardest day? What was happening when things got difficult?
3. What did you feel when you finally arrived in Santiago?

Real Speed

The Short “I’d”

Storytellers do not say every word. Listen for the short forms, then say it back.

I'd never walked that far before.
We were walking through the rain.
By then, I'd lost my map.
It was raining when we set off.
The Conversation · People

The People on the Road

1. Who did you meet on the Camino? Tell me about one person you still remember.
2. Why do you think thousands of people walk it every year, even people who are not religious?
3. After the Camino, you traveled as a backpacker for two months. Was it different from the walk? How?
