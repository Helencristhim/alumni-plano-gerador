Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Film
“The Way” (2010), with Martin Sheen: the trailer

An American father walks the Camino after the death of his son, and meets three strangers who become his friends on the road. It is Oliver and Anna in one film.

Tip: watch the trailer first. If you like it, watch the whole film in English with English subtitles, and bring one scene to tell in class with was -ing and had never.

Video
Rick Steves’ Europe: “Galicia, Spain: The Camino de Santiago Pilgrimage”

A short travel episode about the last part of the Camino and the arrival in Santiago. Clear American English, slow enough to follow, with places you know.

Tip: watch it once at normal speed. Then pause at three places you remember and say out loud what you were doing when you got there.

Watch on YouTube ↗
TED Talk
Andrew Stanton: “The clues to a great story” (19 min)

The writer of Toy Story and WALL-E on what makes people want to hear the end of a story. Useful for your three-minute Camino.

Tip: watch the first ten minutes. Write down the one rule you like best and use it the next time you tell your Camino story.
