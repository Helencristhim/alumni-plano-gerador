Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
Airbus — “A320 production: from start to finish”

From the official Airbus channel: how an A320 is built on the final assembly line in Toulouse, the same site where the training you have talked about happens. Watch what the narration does with verbs — almost nobody is named, and the aircraft is assembled, tested and delivered.

Tip: count the passive sentences in the first two minutes. You will lose count, and that is the point of the lesson.

Podcast
Mentour Pilot — “Mentour Pilot speaks to Ben the Dispatcher”

A long conversation between an airline captain and a flight dispatcher: the person who files the plan, calculates the fuel and names the alternate before the crew arrives. This is the job behind the second recording in this lesson, explained by somebody who does it.

Tip: it is long, so do not try to finish it in one sitting. Twenty minutes is a real session. Stop when you stop understanding, and come back the next day.

Listen on YouTube →
Documentary
Mentour Pilot — “A Really Unusual Problem Tricked these Pilots!”

An incident taken apart step by step, using the official report. Reports are written entirely in the structure you learned today, because an investigation cares about what was done and not about who was on duty. This is that English at full speed.

Tip: watch it once for the story, and a second time with your ears on the verbs. Write down three passive sentences you hear and bring them to class.
