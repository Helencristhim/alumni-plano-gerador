Complementary Activities

Extra material to keep English running in your head between classes. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen. None of them takes more than fifteen minutes.

Lesson 1 -- Who Is Dejair?

Conference Talk
"The mystery of chronic pain" -- Elliot Krane, TED

Eight minutes of a pain researcher explaining his own field to a room full of non-specialists. This is exactly the register you need in Bangkok: technical content, simple words.

Tip: turn on English subtitles the first time, then watch again without them. Write down three sentences he uses to explain a difficult idea in a simple way -- you will need those bridges next week.

Watch on TED →
Listening
"Pain" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters discuss pain for six minutes, at a speed made for learners. Your subject, your vocabulary, but slow enough to catch every word.

Tip: listen once with your eyes closed and no text. Then listen again and count how many words from today's lesson you hear. Do not read the transcript before the second time.

Watch on YouTube →
Guided Study
"The mystery of chronic pain" -- TED-Ed lesson with questions

The same talk, but with comprehension questions and a guided discussion. Use it only after you have watched the TED version once on your own.

Tip: answer the questions out loud, not in writing. Speaking a full answer is the exercise -- reading it is not.
