Chapter 3: The Code

What Is Always True

One tense for facts, routines and what you do for a living

Discover the Rule

What Do These Have in Common?

I work on neuropathic pain.
My institute receives foreign researchers every year.
She studies nerve pain in diabetic patients.
We attend this congress every two years.
Click to reveal the rule
All of them are always true — facts and routines, not just today. That is the present simple. With he, she, it the verb takes an -s: she studies, my institute receives. With I, you, we, they the verb does not change.
