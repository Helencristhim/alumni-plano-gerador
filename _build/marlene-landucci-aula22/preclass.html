Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. Toque em Listen para ouvir. / Listen to each word and read the example.

Trip -- a journey to a place and back home (viagem)
"My trip to Italy was amazing." (Minha viagem para a Itália foi incrível.)
Ago -- before now, in the past (atrás)
"I traveled to Italy two years ago." (Eu viajei para a Itália dois anos atrás.)
Arrive -- to get to a place at the end of a journey (chegar)
"We arrived at the hotel at six o'clock." (Nós chegamos ao hotel às seis horas.)
Stay -- to live in a place for a short time (ficar, hospedar-se)
"We stayed in a small hotel near the old city." (Nós ficamos num hotel pequeno perto da cidade velha.)
Memory -- something you remember from the past (lembrança)
"My best memory of the trip is the food." (Minha melhor lembrança da viagem é a comida.)
Postcard -- a card with a photo that you send from a trip (cartão-postal)
"I mailed a postcard to my sister." (Eu enviei um cartão-postal para a minha irmã.)
Amazing -- very, very good (incrível)
"The view from the room was amazing." (A vista do quarto era incrível.)

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Simple Past (-ed) + was / were

GRAMMAR

No passado, o verbo regular ganha -ed: travel vira traveled, stay vira stayed, arrive vira arrived (com um d só, porque arrive já termina em e). O verbo to be tem duas formas no passado: was para UMA coisa e were para DUAS OU MAIS. Depois de did e did not, o verbo volta à forma base. / In the past a regular verb takes -ed, and to be becomes was or were.

UseExample (EN)Tradução (PT)
verb + -edI traveled to Italy.Eu viajei para a Itália.
was (uma coisa)The room was small.O quarto era pequeno.
were (duas ou mais)The people were friendly.As pessoas eram simpáticas.
NegativeI did not visit the museum.Eu não visitei o museu.
Question (?)Did you like the food?Você gostou da comida?
Palavras de tempolast year / yesterday / three days agoano passado / ontem / três dias atrás

Key point / Ponto-chave: depois de did e did not o verbo NAO leva -ed: diga Did you visit? e I did not visit (nunca "Did you visited"). E cuidado com was x were: uma pessoa = was, duas ou mais = were.
