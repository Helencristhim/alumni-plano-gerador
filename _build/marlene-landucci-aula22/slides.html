Chapter 4 -- Past Simple

Language Discovery

travel + ed / was / were

Discovery

Listen and Notice

All four sentences are about the past. What do the orange words have in common?

"I traveled to Italy last September."

"We arrived in Rome on a Sunday morning."

"The room was small."

"The people were very friendly."

The Rule

-ed, was and were

UsePatternExample
Say what you didverb + -edI traveled to Italy.
One thing in the pastwasThe room was small.
Two or more in the pastwereThe people were friendly.
Say what you did not dodid not + base verbI did not visit the museum.
Ask about the pastDid you + base verb?Did you like the food?
Time wordslast / yesterday / agoI arrived home three days ago.

Key idea: a regular verb takes -ed in the past: walk becomes walked, stay becomes stayed, arrive becomes arrived (only one d, because arrive already ends in e). The verb to be has two past forms: was for one thing and were for two or more. After did and did not the verb goes back to the base form.

Chapter 5 -- Real Voices

How Was Your Trip?

A conversation, a postcard and two messages

Dialogue

Coffee with David

M

"Hello David. How was your trip to Turkey?"

D

"It was amazing. I arrived home two weeks ago."

M

"Where did you stay?"

D

"I stayed in a small hotel in Istanbul. The hotel was old, but the view was beautiful."

M

"And the weather? Was it sunny?"

D

"It was sunny every day. Only the last day was rainy, so I stayed inside and I watched a movie."

M

"What is your best memory of the trip?"

D

"The food. I tried a new dish every day, and the people were very friendly. I mailed you a postcard!"

Comprehension

About David

1. When did David arrive home?
Two weeks ago.
2. Where did he stay, and how was the hotel?
In a small hotel in Istanbul. The hotel was old, but the view was beautiful.
3. What is his best memory of the trip?
The food -- he tried a new dish every day. The people were very friendly too.
