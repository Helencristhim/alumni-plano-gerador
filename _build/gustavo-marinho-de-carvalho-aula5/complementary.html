Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
The Crown — Season 1, Episodes 1 and 2

An institution that runs almost entirely on rules nobody wrote down, watched by somebody who has just walked into the middle of it. Every scene is a lesson in what is expected, what is merely advised, and what is quietly frowned upon — which is exactly the grammar you worked on today.

Tip: English subtitles on. Every time somebody is told what they should do without being ordered, note the words they use.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English — “Who does the housework?”

Six minutes, two British speakers, normal speed, on the most unwritten set of expectations there is — the ones inside a house. Nobody signs anything, everybody has an opinion, and the language is exactly the language of this lesson. Free transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen once with no text and count how many times you hear should, have to and be expected to. Then check against the transcript.

Video
Erin Meyer — “How Cultural Differences Affect Business”

The researcher who mapped this for a living, on why the same sentence lands as direct in one country and as rude in another. She uses British and Brazilian examples, and one of them will describe a call you have already had.

Tip: stop the video when she gives an example and say out loud where your own side would sit on that scale, and where your client would.
