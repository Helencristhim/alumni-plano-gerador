Grammar Discovery

The little question at the end

It's freezing this morning, isn't it?
You're from another school, aren't you?
Two waves count for your score, don't they?
She doesn't smile much, does she?

What do the orange words have in common?

positive sentence, negative tag? · negative sentence, positive tag?

A question tag checks something you think is true, or starts a conversation. Use the same verb as the sentence (is, are, can, was) — or do / does / did if there is no helper verb. Then the pronoun: it, you, they, she.

The Code

Copy the verb, flip it

The verb decides

It's cold, isn't it?
You can surf here, can't you?
They surf in heat two, don't they?
You surfed well, didn't you?
She isn't nervous, is she?

Never

You're in heat two, no?
English doesn't use "no?": aren't you?
Harper is quiet, isn't Harper?
the tag uses a pronoun: isn't she?
You surfed well, weren't you?
past simple, no helper: didn't you?

The answer: Yes, it is. · No, I'm not. — short, with the same verb.

The Code

The easiest way to start

With a stranger, say something you both know is true, and add a tag. The other person only has to agree.

You start

"It's freezing, isn't it?"
"The waves are small, aren't they?"
"You're in heat two, aren't you?"

They answer, and keep going

"It is! You're not from here, are you?"
"They are. It's going to get better, isn't it?"
"Yes, I am. You're in heat two too?"
It's freezing this morning, isn't it?

Something true, plus a tag: the easiest way to break the ice.

Did You Catch It?

About Mia

1. Where is Mia from, and how did she know Renato's heat?
She is from Hillarys, another school. She saw his jersey on the heat sheet.
2. What does Mia say about the horn?
When it goes, you paddle out fast.
3. According to Mia, what happens if you're not selected?
You can still be the reserve.
Listening 1

The rules of the day

1. How many heats are there, and how long is each one?
Six heats, and every heat is twenty minutes.
2. How does a heat start and end, and which waves count?
With the horn. Only the best two waves count for the score.
3. How many surfers does she pick, and when do the names come out?
Four surfers for the squad and one reserve. The names come out on Monday, on the club group.
Listening 2

After the heat, a message from Kai

1. What happened with the waves in Renato's heat?
They went flat for ten minutes, and then a set came.
2. What was Renato's best wave, and his score?
The aerial at the end. About a seven and a half.
3. Can Kai tell him anything about the squad? What does he tell him to do?
No, not until Monday. He tells him to stop checking his phone and go eat something.
