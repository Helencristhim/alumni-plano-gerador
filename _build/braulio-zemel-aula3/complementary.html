Grammar Video
"How to use modals of deduction and speculation" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the structure you built today: must have, might have and can't have for talking about a past you did not see. Connection to Lesson 3: this is the difference between they knew and they must have known -- the second one is an opinion, and the opinion is what a conversation is made of.

Tip: pause after each example and say one sentence about a news story from this week. Listen for how must have collapses into must've -- that reduction is what you will hear in a bulletin.
