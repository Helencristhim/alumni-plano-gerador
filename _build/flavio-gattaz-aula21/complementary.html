mbaMission -- "MBA Interview Tips: Questions to Ask Your Interviewer"

Admissions consultants on the last five minutes of the MBA interview: which questions help you, which ones hurt you, and why a generic question is worse than it looks. Clear American English.

Tip: write down every question they recommend, and turn two of them into questions about the program you want. Check that the grammar is right.

Podcast
Freakonomics Radio -- "Can I Ask You a Ridiculously Personal Question?"

New research on why people are afraid to ask sensitive questions, and why the fear is mostly wrong. It is useful before an interview where the best question may feel a little uncomfortable to ask.

Tip: every time you hear a question, repeat it out loud and notice if it has do or not. Then ask one sensitive question about the program, politely.

TED -- Celeste Headlee, "10 ways to have a better conversation"

A radio host who interviews people for a living explains how to ask open-ended questions and really listen to the answer — the follow-up skill from today’s lesson.

Tip: watch it once without subtitles. Then write three open-ended questions for the interviewer, each starting with what, how or who.
