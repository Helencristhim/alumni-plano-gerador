Lesson 10 -- Next Steps in Writing

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Goals"

Five minutes on committing to something out loud: how to state a date, how to say what depends on somebody else, and how to leave room for what goes wrong. Full script on the same page, at natural speed.

Tip: read the script and mark every future form. For each one, ask when it was decided — in the calendar, before the conversation, or right there as the person speaks.

Talk
Simon Sinek -- "How great leaders inspire action" (TED, 18 min)

You watched this in lesson 1, before you could say what your company does. Watch it again now, at the end of the block, and notice how much of it you follow without reading. That difference is the ten lessons.

Tip: watch it once with subtitles off. Then write down, in English, the three sentences you remember. Compare with what you wrote after lesson 1.

Documentary
American Factory (Netflix, 1h55)

A plant is bought, plans are made, dates are announced, and almost nothing lands when it was supposed to. It is the clearest picture available of what happens between a signature and a first real day, filmed in two languages and two ways of talking about time.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Note three moments where somebody announces a date. For each, decide whether it was booked, planned, or only intended — and then watch what actually happens to it.
