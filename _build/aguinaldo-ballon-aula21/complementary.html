Talk
TED -- Simon Sinek, "How great leaders inspire action" (18 min)

A famous talk about why some leaders persuade and others do not. His key idea is a cleft sentence: people don't buy what you do; they buy why you do it.

Tip: write down two sentences where he puts the focus on one idea, and make one about your own work.

BBC English Masterclass -- "Using 'what' to give emphasis"
