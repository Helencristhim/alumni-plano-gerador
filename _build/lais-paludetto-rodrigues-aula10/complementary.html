Lesson 10 -- Ready for Burnaby

Grammar Video
"A review of conditionals" -- BBC English Class, BBC Learning English

All the conditionals side by side, in one short video: the real one, the imaginary one, and the difference that decides which you use. Connection to Lesson 10: this is the pair you spent lessons four and five on, and the one you said you hated before you could do it.

Tip: watch it on the plane if you can. Then say two sentences about the flight itself, one real and one imaginary.

Talk
"How to Speak So That People Want to Listen" -- Julian Treasure, TED

Ten minutes on the mechanics of being listened to: the tools your voice has, and the habits that make people stop paying attention. Connection to Lesson 10: you will spend six months being understood by people who do not share your first language, and this is about everything except vocabulary.

Tip: try the vocal warm-up at the end once, out loud, before your presentation or before any day you know will be long.

Walking Tour
"Vancouver 4K Walking Tour" -- captions and street sound, no narration

An hour of somebody simply walking through Vancouver with a camera and a microphone. No presenter, no music: just street signs, shop names, buses, and people talking as they pass. Connection to Lesson 10: this is the sound of the place you are going to, and the signs are the reading practice.

Tip: do not watch it all at once. Ten minutes at a time, reading every sign out loud that you can catch. By the time you land, the city will already look slightly familiar.
