Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Amanda Burden -- "How public spaces make cities work" (TED)

A former New York city planner on public spaces: the benches, plazas and streets where people stop, sit and meet. An architect's talk, but about the people who use the buildings, which is exactly today's lesson.

Tip: after the talk, describe one public space in Sao Paulo with where: It is a place where people...

Talk
Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie -- "The danger of a single story" (TED)

A writer on how we see a place through one story only, and miss the people who live there. Slow, clear English, and a perfect conversation topic for a traveler.

Tip: note two sentences with who or which, and say one thing about a city you know that people often get wrong.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
Theaster Gates -- "How to revive a neighborhood: with imagination, beauty and art" (TED)

An artist and urban planner who buys empty buildings in Chicago and gives them back to the people of the neighborhood. Architecture and people, together.

Tip: describe one building from the talk with which, and the people who use it with who.
