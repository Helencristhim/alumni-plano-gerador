Chapter 3: The Code

Always, or In the Middle Of?

Two tenses that Portuguese keeps in one

Discover the Rule

What Is the Difference Between These Two Columns?

I study Administration.  ·  This semester I am studying for three exams at once.
A brand launches two collections a year.  ·  They are launching the new one in March.
I work with cotton.  ·  Right now I am working with a lighter fabric.
She designs for a small brand.  ·  At the moment she is designing her first collection.
Click to reveal the rule
The left side is the present simple: things that are always true — your course, your job, what a brand normally does. The right side is the present continuous: am / is / are + verb-ing, for what you are in the middle of right now, this week, this semester. Two markers give it away: usually, every year, always pull the simple; right now, at the moment, this semester pull the continuous. One exception worth remembering: verbs like know, like, love, want, need stay in the simple — I want a lighter fabric, never I am wanting.

Chapter 4: Inside the Studio

The calendar behind every collection

Reading

How a Collection Gets Made

How a Collection Actually Gets Made

People think a fashion collection starts with a drawing. It usually starts with a calendar. A brand launches two or three collections a year, and every launch has a fixed date, so the team works backwards from that date: first the fabric, then the pieces, then the photographs, then the shops.

The fabric comes before the design, and that surprises most students. A designer does not choose a shape and then look for material. She looks at what is available, at what it costs, and at how it moves — and the shape comes out of that. A beautiful drawing on a fabric that does not fall the right way is just a beautiful drawing.

Right now, in August, the teams are not working on the clothes you see in the shop windows. Those were finished months ago. They are working on next year: choosing colours, testing fabric, and arguing about how many pieces the collection needs. In this business, the present and the future never happen at the same speed.

And the deadline is the part nobody negotiates. A student can ask a professor for two more days. A brand cannot ask a shop to move a launch date, because the whole calendar of the year is already built around it. That is the first thing every fashion course tries to teach, and the last thing anybody actually learns.

So, What Are You Into?

R
Hey. Last time you told me you study two things. How does that even work?
G
It works because they happen at different times. I study Administration in the morning and I am taking a fashion course at night.
R
And what are you working on right now?
G
Fabric research. I am testing three fabrics for a piece I have to hand in in two weeks.
R
Two weeks? That sounds like my finals. Is fashion what you want to do after university?
G
I do not know yet. I like both, and I am not choosing this year.
R
That is a good answer, honestly. What are you looking forward to?
G
The final project. And a trip, once in a while, without three deadlines waiting for me.
