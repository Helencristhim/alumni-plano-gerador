Lesson 4 -- What Are You Into?

Series
Emily in Paris — Netflix

A marketing team and a fashion house in the same room, arguing about brands, launches and campaigns. Half the vocabulary of this lesson is spoken out loud in every episode.

Tip: watch one episode with English subtitles and write down every time somebody says brand, collection or launch. Then say three sentences about your own course using the same words.

Listening
"Could you give up fast fashion?" — 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes about the industry you are studying, at a speed made for learners. It is also the vocabulary you will need in lesson 10, when we get to sustainable fashion.

Tip: listen once without subtitles and write the three words you did not catch. Look them up only after the second listen — guessing from context is the skill being trained here.

Watch on YouTube →
Grammar
"Present Simple and Present Continuous" — 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

The exact contrast from this lesson, in six minutes, with the stative verbs explained at the end.

Tip: after the video, say four sentences about your week with the simple and four with the continuous. If any of them sounds strange, it is probably a stative verb.
