Vocabulary

"This trend started on TikTok and reached the shops six months later."
Collection -- a set of pieces a brand puts out together
"The new collection has forty pieces and one colour palette."
Fabric -- the material a piece of clothing is made of
"I am working with a lighter fabric this semester."
Style -- the particular way somebody dresses or designs
"Her style is simple, and that is why people copy it."
Brand -- a company with a name and an identity people recognise
"That brand is small, but everybody in my course knows it."
To launch -- to put a new product on the market for the first time
"They are launching the collection in March."
Semester -- one half of the academic year at a university
"I am in my fourth semester of Administration."

"I have three deadlines in the same week."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Giovanna studies Administration at university and she is in her fourth semester. She also takes a fashion course at night, and this year she is taking the fabric module. Right now she is working on a project about lighter fabrics, and she has a deadline in two weeks. She does not know yet which of the two areas she wants to follow, and at the moment she is not choosing. She is looking forward to the final project.
Why is it "she studies Administration" but "she is working on a project"?
A Because studying is always true and the project is happening now
B Because the project happened in the past
C Because "work" has no simple form
Why is it "she wants" and not "she is wanting"?
A Because want is a verb we do not use in the continuous
B Because the sentence is about the past
C Because want is an irregular verb

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Present Simple x Present Continuous

GRAMMAR

English has two present tenses, and they do different jobs. Portuguese often uses one for both — that is where the mistake comes from.

present simplealways true, routineI study Administration.
present continuousam / is / are + verb-ingThis semester I am taking a fashion course.
markers of the simpleusually, always, every yearBrands launch two collections a year.
markers of the continuousright now, at the moment, this semesterRight now they are working on next winter.

Careful: know, like, love, want, need do not go into the continuous. "I am wanting" is wrong — it is "I want".

And the -s never disappears: she loves it, not she love it.
