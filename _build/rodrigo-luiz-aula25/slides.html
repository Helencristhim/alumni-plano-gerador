Chapter 3: The Pattern

Experience, Not Dates

have + past participle

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Have you ever worked abroad?
I have visited our plants in France many times.
I've never managed a team in another country.
We've had audits from global customers.

WHEN did these things happen? Is there a day or a year? And what comes after have?

UseFormExample
Experience: yeshave / has + past participleI have visited France. She has worked in China.
Experience: nohave never + past participleI have never worked abroad.
QuestionHave you ever + past participle?Have you ever worked abroad? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
Participlesregular: -ed · irregular: been, had, seen, metI have been to Paris. I have met the CEO.
CarefulNo date with the present perfect: "I have visited Paris last year" is wrong. With a day or a year, use the past: "I visited Paris last year."

Chapter 4: Your Career

An Introduction for New York

Dialogue

Your Profile for New York

N
Rodrigo, I'm writing a short introduction about you for the New York team. Can I ask some questions?
R
Of course. Go ahead.
N
Have you ever worked abroad?
R
No, I've never worked abroad, but I've visited our plants in France many times.
N
Great. Have you ever managed a product launch?
R
Yes, I have. I've managed three launches in Brazil, with the team in Paris.
N
And have you ever had an audit from a big customer?
R
Yes, many times. We've had audits from global customers, and we've passed all of them.
N
Impressive! And how long is your career in supply chain?
R
About twenty years. It's a lot of experience, and I've learned something every year.
