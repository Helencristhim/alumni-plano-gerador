Chapter 3: The Code

Three Question Words

What, Where, How long

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Questions Have in Common?

"What is your name?"

"Where are you from?"

"How long is your visit?"

"What is the purpose of your visit?"

Look at the blue words: a question word, then the verb. Then: you, your name, your visit.

JobFormExample
What (a thing, a name)What + is / are...?What is your name?
Where (a place)Where + is / are...?Where are you from?
How long (time)How long + is...?How long is your visit?
The answershort: a name, a place, a timeMy name is Eliane. / Brazil. / One week.

In one line: Question word + verb + the rest: What is...? Where are...? How long is...?
