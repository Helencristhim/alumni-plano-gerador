Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Julian Treasure — “5 ways to listen better” (TED)

Seven minutes on the exact skill you said gets better only after a day and a half at a conference. He argues that listening is a set of positions you choose, not a talent you have, and he names four of them.

Tip: watch it once with English subtitles on and once with them off. Then pick the one position you never use and try it in your next call.

Video
“Cross-Border Payments: The Future of Money Explained”

Your own subject, in English, at normal speed. You already know this content in Portuguese, which is exactly why it works: your ear can follow the argument while it gets used to the words your counterparts will use at the conference.

Tip: write down every sentence in the present continuous you hear. That is the tense this lesson was built around, and this video is full of it.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Podcast
Fintech in Focus — “Paying cross-border”

Two specialists talking about the friction in international payments the way people talk at a stand: fast, with no explanation of the basics, and with British and American accents in the same conversation.

Tip: listen once with no notes and write the three problems they mention. Then listen again and see how many you missed the first time.
