Series
The World's Most Amazing Vacation Rentals — Season 1

Three hosts travel around the world and each one picks the place they think is the most amazing to stay in: a treehouse, a castle, a house on a cliff. They argue about which one is the best, so you will hear superlatives in almost every scene. Start with Season 1, Episode 1 and see if you agree with their choices.

Tip: after each house, pause and say one sentence out loud — "That is the most incredible place I've ever seen" — and then compare it with the best place you have ever stayed in.

Podcast
Amateur Traveler, Episode 988 — "Hidden Gems of London"

A Londoner takes the host around the places most visitors never find: a tiny park full of hero stories, a Roman temple under an office building, and a secret garden above a concrete arts centre. You will hear people explain why a place is worth it, and why the famous spots are not always the best ones. It is a conversation and not a lecture, so it is easy to follow.

Tip: write down the three places that sound best to you, then say it out loud — "The most surprising place I've ever heard about is ___, because I've never seen anything like it."

YouTube
Love and London — "London's Most Overrated Attractions (and What to Do Instead)"

A Londoner goes through the attractions that everybody visits and explains which ones are overrated, which ones are a real letdown, and what you should do instead. It is only seven minutes long and it uses exactly the words from this lesson: overrated, touristy, worth it and hidden gem. By the end you will know which famous places turn out to be disappointing.

Tip: stop the video after each attraction and finish this sentence out loud — "That is the most overrated place I've ever seen", or "That is the best idea I've ever heard."
