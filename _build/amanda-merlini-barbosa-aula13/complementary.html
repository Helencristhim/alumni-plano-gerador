Film
An Inconvenient Truth (2006)

A documentary built around one long public presentation on climate change: data on a screen, a claim for each slide, and a recommendation at the end.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Notice when the speaker is completely sure and when he says the data suggests or is likely to. Which claims get a hedge?

Podcast
Outrage + Optimism: Inside COP -- The UN's Top Climate Official on Week One of COP30

Recorded during COP30 in Belém. The UN's top climate official talks about the first week of negotiations: what moved, what is still open, and what he expects.

Tip: listen for the careful words of a negotiator in the middle of talks: likely to, may, it seems. Write down three, and the claim each one protects.

Talk
Christiana Figueres: The Inside Story of the Paris Climate Agreement (TED)

The diplomat who led the Paris negotiations explains how almost every country in the world reached one agreement. Clear, structured English from someone who has spoken on every climate stage.

Tip: listen to how she states her main point with no hedge, then supports it. Pause after one claim and say it again in your own words.
