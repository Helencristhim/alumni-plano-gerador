Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I can swim."

"I can't drive yet."

"Can I borrow your charger?"

"Can I help you?"

The same word in all four. Is it doing the same job every time?

JobWhat it meansExample
Abilityyou know how to do it"I can swim." · "I can't drive yet."
Permissionyou ask for something"Can I borrow your charger?" · "Can I sit here?"
Offeryou give help"Can I help you?"
The giftAfter can the verb is NAKED: no to, no -s, no do. "She can swim", never "she cans" or "she can to swim".
Short answers"Can you swim?" → "Yes, I can." / "No, I can't." Same shape as lesson 2.

And one small word changes everything: "I can't drive" is closed. "I can't drive yet" is a plan.

Z
You look worried. Is everything OK?
B
My phone has no battery. Can I borrow your charger?
Z
Sure, you can. It's in my bag. But you can't use it on the court.
B
No problem, I can wait in the hall. Sorry, I don't understand one thing. Why not on the court?
Z
Camp rule. No phones on the court. Can you give it back after practice?
B
Of course. Thank you. Can I help you with anything?
Z
Actually, yes. Can you carry the balls to court B?
B
Yes, I can. That is easy. I can carry all of them.
Comprehension

About Zoe

1. Where is her charger?
In her bag.
2. What can't he do with it?
He can't use it on the court. It is a camp rule.
3. What does she ask him to do?
To give the charger back after practice.
4. What help does she accept at the end?
She asks him to carry the balls to court B.
