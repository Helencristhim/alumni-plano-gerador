Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Down to Earth with Zac Efron — Season 1, Episode 4 "France" (Netflix)

Two travellers who spend the whole episode asking people for access, for time and for explanations, and getting it. Light, warm, and every conversation is a request with a reason attached.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on and write down three sentences that begin with If.

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "Flight cancelled again? Try these tips."

Worth a second listen after this lesson, for a different reason than in Lesson 2. Now listen for what the agent is allowed to authorise, what you have to ask for, and the exact moment the advice turns into an if sentence.

Tip: read the transcript and mark every conditional. There are more of them than you expect.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
William Ury — "The walk from no to yes" (TED)

Eighteen minutes from the negotiator who wrote the standard book on the subject. His central move is the one you practised today: stop pushing against the other person and stand next to them, looking at the problem together.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Then say, in three sentences, how his idea would have changed one conversation you have already had.
