Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 1 -- Twenty Years in Two Minutes

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) -- "Networking can be awkward. Here's how to make it work for you"

Twenty minutes on the exact moment this lesson trained: a room full of strangers, a cup of coffee, and the question about what you do. American English at natural speed, with the full transcript on the same page, so you can read and listen at the same time.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then read it and mark every present perfect. Bring one sentence you would like to be able to say yourself.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Emilie Wapnick -- "Why some of us don't have one true calling" (TED, 12 min)

A talk built entirely around the question you practised today, and around the expression you learned for it: what happens when the honest answer is that you wear many hats. Slow, clear delivery, and a useful model for structuring an answer that does not fit one job title.

Tip: turn on the English subtitles and stop at minute three. Say her opening question out loud, then answer it about your own career in two sentences.

Watch on TED ↗
Series
Suits -- Season 1, Episode 1 "Pilot" (Netflix)

Corporate law at television speed. The plot is not the point: the point is the register, the way partners and associates introduce themselves, describe a caseload and talk about a track record in a single corridor conversation. Watch the pilot with an ear for how people say what they do.

Tip: write down three sentences in which a character says how long they have been somewhere. Check whether each one uses have plus the participle or the past simple, and why.
