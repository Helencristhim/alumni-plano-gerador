The Frame

Has any of this happened yet?

I am going to apply for an internship in March.
She is going to send her resume tomorrow.
They are not going to hire anybody this year.
Okay, fine — I’ll send it today.

It has not happened yet → going to / will

The first three were decided before you started speaking. The last one was decided WHILE you spoke. That is the whole difference.

The Frame

Two futures, one question

going to — I decided before

I am going to apply in March.
She is going to send her resume.
They are not going to hire this year.

will — I am deciding now

Okay, I’ll send it today.
I’ll help you with that.
I’ll text you when I know.

The plan you already had → going to. The decision you make while speaking → will.

Question: Are you going to apply? — and never "going apply": the to is part of it.

Did You Catch It?

About Clara and Rafa

1. What is Clara going to ask about in the interview?
The department — because nobody explained it.
2. What is Rafa going to do tonight, and what did he decide while speaking?
He offered to help her — and he decided to send the link right then.
3. Why is Rafa applying to a different company?
Because they are going to hire six people, so the opportunity is bigger.

The academic calendar

The first of March.
2. What do you have to do before the fifteenth of February?
Register in the university system and upload your resume.
3. How many hours a week can a third-semester student work?
Twenty, and never during class hours.
