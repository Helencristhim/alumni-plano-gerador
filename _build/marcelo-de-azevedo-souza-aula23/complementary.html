Lesson 23 -- The Listening Gap

Lesson theme: following real-speed native English, listening for the gist, and the short answers natives use (So do I, Neither can I, I hope not). Extra practice outside class -- tick each one when you finish it.

Film
The Banshees of Inisherin (2022) -- directed by Martin McDonagh

Two old friends on a small Irish island, and one of them suddenly stops talking to the other. Broad Irish accents, short answers, dropped words and long silences: the perfect training for the ear you worked on tonight.

Tip: watch one scene without subtitles, then again with English subtitles, and count the short answers you missed the first time.

A real-speed American conversation about listening well: how to give people attention, ask follow-up questions and stop planning your answer while the other person talks. Free to stream, with the full transcript on the same page.
