Real songs sent in by real people, with the feedback given out loud, in order: what happened in the track, then what could change, then why. It is the same structure you practiced today, at normal speaking speed.

Tip: pause before each response and give your own note out loud first. Then compare yours with his.

Podcast · American accent · 18 min
Song Exploder, episode 296 -- Gigi Perez, "Sailor Song"

A song recorded in a childhood bedroom, taken apart by the person who wrote it. She explains every decision she made and, more usefully for you, the ones she reversed after somebody told her something she did not want to hear.

Tip: while you listen, write the one note you would have given her. Then check whether the final version already contains it.
