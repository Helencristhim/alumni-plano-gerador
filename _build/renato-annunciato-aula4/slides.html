Chapter 3: The Code

The Doctor Who

Saying which one, and adding what matters

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The doctor who runs the largest clinic sees forty patients a week."

"Doctor Bennett, who trained in London, will speak at the congress."

"The protocol that we presented last year is now in three clinics."

"Our largest account, which is a clinic group, is on the same protocol."

Two sentences tell you which one. Two only add a detail. What do you see in the punctuation?

WordWhat it points atExample
whoPeople — doctors, consultants, patientsThe doctor who runs the clinic...
which / thatThings — a protocol, an account, a programmeThe protocol that we presented...
whereA place, when something happens thereThe clinic where she sees her patients...
whosePossession — it replaces his, her, theirThe consultant whose territory is the interior...
Defining (no commas)The clause says which one. Remove it and the sentence stops making sense. that is allowed here.
Non-defining (commas)The clause only adds a detail about somebody already identified. Never use that here — use who or which.
Three trapsNever the doctor which — people take who. Never the clinic where I visited — you visit a place, so it is the clinic that I visited. Never the consultant that his territory — use whose.
Practice

Choose the Right Word

Say it first, then click to check

"The doctor ___who runs the largest clinic sees forty patients a week." (a person, and I am saying which one)
"Doctor Bennett, ___who trained in London, will speak at the congress." (extra detail, after a comma)
"The protocol ___that we presented last year is now in three clinics." (a thing, and I am saying which one)
"The consultant ___whose territory covers the interior visited her twice." (it replaces his or her)
"The clinic ___where she sees her patients is in the west of the city." (a place, and something happens there)
The Structure

Three Layers, One Visit

This is how a visit is reported in English to somebody who was not in the room.

1 · Who they are, and what they run

"Doctor Prado, who runs a private practice in the west of Sao Paulo, is our strongest prescriber."

2 · What they asked for, or objected to

"She asked for a summary of the safety data that she can show to the residents she trains."

3 · What you committed to do next

"Medical Affairs will send it within ten working days, and I will map the three clinics she refers to."

Who → what they asked → what you promised, with a date. Never a visit report without layer three.

Dialogue

Twenty Minutes Between Consultations

A
Renato, I have twenty minutes and then I have a patient. What do you need?
R
Twenty minutes is more than I need. One question: the new indication that was approved in March, have you started using it?
A
No, and I will tell you why. Nobody has shown me the safety data, and my waiting list is eight weeks long. I do not put a new protocol into a queue like that on somebody's word.
R
That is fair. So the barrier is evidence and time, not the product itself.
A
Exactly. And I will not endorse anything in front of the residents I train unless I have seen the data myself. My credibility with them is the only thing here that is really mine.
R
Then let me send the safety summary first, in a form you can show them. I am not asking you for anything today.
A
That is the first time somebody from the industry has said that to me this year. There is one more thing. I send patients to three colleagues in the east, and the referral stops there.
R
You want those clinics in the same support programme. I will map them and confirm whether they qualify, and I will come back to you either way.
A
Come back with the summary and the answer on the clinics, and I will find you thirty minutes instead of twenty.
Comprehension

About Doctor Bennett

1. Has she started using the indication that was approved in March?
No, she has not.
2. What two reasons does she give?
Nobody has shown her the safety data, and her waiting list is eight weeks long.
3. What condition does she put on endorsing anything?
She has to see the data herself first — her credibility with the residents she trains is the only thing that is really hers.
4. How does she react when he says he is not asking for anything today?
She says it is the first time somebody from the industry has said that to her this year.

The Visit With No Message

Three years of doing it the other way. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What did she do in every visit for her first three years?
Prepare the message, deliver the message, and leave with a commitment she wrote down and reported.
2. What had the most important doctors stopped doing?
Telling her anything — they answered her questions and nothing else.
3. What instruction did her colleague give her?
Go into the next four visits with no message at all, and come out with three things she did not know.
4. What does she say about a visit where you learn nothing?
It is not a short visit — it is a visit you will have to repeat.
Chapter 5: Practice

Your Doctors

Spot the Error

Fix the Sentence

Say the correction out loud first, then click

1. "The doctor which prescribes the most is in Campinas."
"The doctor who prescribes the most is in Campinas." (people take who, never which)
2. "This is the clinic where I visited last week."
"This is the clinic that I visited last week." (you visit the place itself, so it is the object: that)
3. "The consultant that his territory is the interior called me."
"The consultant whose territory is the interior called me." (possession takes whose)
4. "Doctor Bennett, that runs the largest clinic, will speak at the congress."
"Doctor Bennett, who runs the largest clinic, will speak at the congress." (after a comma, that is not allowed)
