Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Film
Before Sunrise (1995)

An American man and a French woman meet on a train and decide to spend one night walking around Vienna. The whole film is a conversation: about life, love, travel and getting older. Natural, real-speed English.

Tip: watch one scene twice, then tell a friend what they talked about, with He told her that… and She said that…

TED Talk
Lýdia Machóvá: The secrets of learning a new language (13 min)

A woman who speaks many languages explains what good language learners do: they enjoy it, they find their own method, and they keep going after the course ends. Clear English and good ideas for your next step.

Tip: after the talk, say which of her ideas you are going to use, and what you will be doing in English this time next year.

Film
The Best Exotic Marigold Hotel (2011)

A group of British retirees move to an old hotel in India. They used to have quiet lives at home; now they have to make new friends, go out and start again. Funny, warm and full of small talk.

Tip: after the film, choose one character and say what his or her life used to be like, and what it is like now.
