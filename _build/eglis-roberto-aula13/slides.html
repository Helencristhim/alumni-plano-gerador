The Night Out

The Farewell at the Marina

It is a night out at the sailing club bar, and many people are there. Round 1: three guests come to talk to you, one at a time. Round 2: you are the host. You welcome a guest, introduce people, buy a round and make a short toast.

  • Round 1: an old friend, a fast talker and a curious guest. Keep each conversation going.
  • Round 2: you take care of the night, from the first round to the last toast.
