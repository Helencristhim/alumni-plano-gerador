One Texas High School’s Efforts to Help Students Graduate -- FRONTLINE (full documentary)

A whole year inside an American high school, filmed with the teenagers themselves: counselors, deadlines, families, and what actually decides whether a student finishes. The same building your daughters walk into every morning.

Talk
Gap Year: A Path to Purposeful Education -- Jay Gosselin, TEDxKanata

Fifteen minutes on the question one of your daughters may ask you next year: start college now, or take a year first? He worked with twelve thousand students before deciding what he thinks.

Tip: clear North American English and a slow pace. Watch once with no subtitles, then say his position back in three sentences of your own.
