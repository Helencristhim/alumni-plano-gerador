Chapter 3: The Missing S

One Letter, and Only for Somebody Else

I work · he works

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I work in Brazil."

"My son plays football."

"My daughter studies in another city."

"We live in São Paulo."

WhoThe verbExample
I / you / we / theyno SI work · we live · they play
he / she / it · my son · my wife+ She works · she lives · my son plays
A small spelling changestudy → studies · go → goes · do → does. Same idea, the S is just louder.

In Portuguese every person changes the verb, so you expect change everywhere. In English it changes in ONE place only — and that is exactly why it disappears. It will disappear many times. That is normal, and it is not a problem.

Whose Is It

"This is my house."

mine

"My son and his football."

"My daughter and her books."

a company in Brazil I travel a lot But tell me about you Do you have children?

Tell Me About Your Family

Scenario: One question, and then two minutes with nobody helping you. Your family, your house, your weekend — whatever you want to say about your life.

No keywords this time. Two minutes, no stopping. Different from the model is not wrong — if she knows your family now, it worked.
