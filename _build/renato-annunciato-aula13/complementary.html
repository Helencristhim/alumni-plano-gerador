Lesson 13 -- Explaining a Drop

Film
Apollo 13 (1995)

The true story of the NASA mission that went wrong on the way to the Moon. Most of the film is people in a control room explaining a problem under pressure: what happened, what the root cause is, what the knock-on effects are, and what the recovery plan is. It is today's lesson, with a spaceship.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time a character explains a problem, pause and ask yourself: did he give the number, the cause, or the plan?

Podcast
Planet Money -- Why Do Hospitals Keep Running Out of Generic Drugs?

A free episode from NPR about drug shortages in American hospitals. The hosts explain why cheap, simple drugs like IV fluids keep running short, from the factory to the hospital, and what happens as a result. It is your industry, and almost every sentence uses the linking words of today's lesson.

Tip: listen once without the transcript, then again with it. Write down three sentences with because of, due to or as a result, and say them out loud.

YouTube
The Agile Business Analyst -- Before You Do a 5 Whys Root Cause Analysis, Watch This

A short video on the 5 Whys, the method that keeps asking why until it reaches the root cause of a problem. He also explains the most common mistake: stopping at the first cause, which is often only a symptom. It is the thinking behind step two of today's explanation.

Tip: watch it once with subtitles. Then take one real drop from your region and ask why five times, out loud, in English.
