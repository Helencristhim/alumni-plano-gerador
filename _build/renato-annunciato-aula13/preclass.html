Lesson 13 -- Pre-class

Explaining a Drop -- Cause, Consequence and Recovery

Explain a bad quarter without sounding like you are making excuses. Key words: downturn, to decline, setback, root cause, stockout, price cut, knock-on effect, seasonality, temporary, to offset, to bounce back, recovery plan. Structures: linking words of cause and result (because, because of, due to, as a result, so), and the three steps of explaining a drop.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each term and read the definition. Four are about the drop, four about the causes, four about the recovery.

Downturn -- a period when sales or business activity goes down
"The South had a downturn in the third quarter."
To decline -- to become smaller in number or amount
"Units declined by twelve percent in August."
Setback -- a problem that stops your progress for a time
"The stockout was a setback, but we are back on track."
Root cause -- the main reason why a problem happened
"The root cause was a late shipment, not the price."
Stockout -- when a product is not available because there is none in stock
"The stockout in August lasted almost three weeks."
Price cut -- a reduction in the price of a product
"A competitor made a price cut in Porto Alegre."
Knock-on effect -- an indirect result of something that happened before
"The knock-on effect of the delay was worse than the delay."
Seasonality -- regular changes in sales at the same time every year
"Some of the July dip is just seasonality."
Temporary -- lasting only for a short time
"The drop is temporary, not a new trend."
To offset -- to balance a loss with a gain somewhere else
"Growth in Curitiba offset part of the drop in the South."
To bounce back -- to return to a good level after a problem
"We expect to bounce back in the fourth quarter."
Recovery plan -- a list of actions to bring results back to normal
"Our recovery plan has three actions and a deadline."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the note about the third quarter and answer the questions. Notice the words in bold that connect a cause to a result.

The third quarter was difficult for Renato's team. Units in the South declined by twelve percent, but it was not a downturn in the market. The root cause was a stockout in August. A shipment from Europe was held at customs for eleven days, so three distributors ran out of product.

Because of the delay, some clinics had nothing to use for almost three weeks. As a result, a few of them tried a competitor. Due to a price cut from that competitor, two clinics in Porto Alegre stayed with it. That was the knock-on effect, and it was worse than the stockout itself.

Part of the drop is also seasonality, because July is always a quieter month. Renato believes the problem is temporary. Growth in Curitiba will offset part of the loss, and his recovery plan starts with joint visits to the two clinics. Therefore, he expects to bounce back in the fourth quarter.

1. Why is it "because of the delay" but "because July is always a quieter month"?
A Because "because of" comes before a noun, like the delay, and "because" comes before a full sentence, with a verb.
B Because "because of" is for problems and "because" is for normal things.
C Because "because of" is more formal, so it is better in a written note.
2. "As a result, a few of them tried a competitor." What does "as a result" introduce?
A The reason why the shipment from Europe was held at customs for eleven days.
B The result of the delay.
C A new and different topic in the note.
3. What was the root cause of the drop?
A A downturn in the whole market.
B The price cut from a competitor in Porto Alegre, which made two of the clinics switch.
C The stockout in August.
4. Which sentence is correct?
A "Due to the stockout, two clinics switched to a competitor."
B "Due to the clinics had no product for three weeks, they switched to a competitor."
C "Because of the clinics had no product, they switched."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Linking Words of Cause and Result

GRAMMAR

Two questions decide the linking word: is it the cause or the result? And what comes after it: a full sentence or a noun?

JobLinking wordsExample
Cause + full sentencebecause, since, asUnits fell because the shipment was late.
Cause + nounbecause of, due to, owing toUnits fell because of the late shipment.
Result, same sentenceso, which led toThe shipment was late, so the clinics ran out.
Result, new sentenceAs a result, Therefore, ConsequentlyThe clinics ran out. As a result, units declined.
Negative and questionThe same words work: Sales did not fall because of the price. Was it because of the stockout?Ask Why did...?, answer Because...
Two trapsNever because of the shipment was late — with a verb, it is just because. And never due to the clinics had no product — due to takes a noun: due to the stockout.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the best answer for each situation.

The director asks why your numbers are down. Your first sentence is:
A "Well, it was a very complicated quarter, with many problems in the region and in the market."
B "Units in the South fell by twelve percent this quarter."
C "It was not my fault."
A shipment was late and the clinics had no product. You call it:
A A stockout.
B A price cut, because the clinics could not buy the product at the normal price.
C A recovery plan.
Sales always dip in July. The director asks about it. You say:
A "Part of the July drop is seasonality."
B "July was a downturn in the market, and we do not know the reason for it yet."
C "July was a root cause."
Two clinics switched to a competitor after the stockout. You say:
A "Because of the two clinics switched, units fell."
B "As a result of the stockout, two clinics switched to a competitor."
C "Due to two clinics switched to a competitor, units fell by twelve percent this quarter."
The director asks: is this a new trend? You believe it is not. You say:
A "Maybe, I am not sure, we will see what happens next quarter with the numbers."
B "It is a trend."
C "No, the drop is temporary. We expect to bounce back in the fourth quarter."
Curitiba grew while the South fell. You say:
A "Growth in Curitiba offset part of the drop."
B "Curitiba was a setback for the South, because the team spent too much time there."
C "Curitiba is the root cause."

Reflection
