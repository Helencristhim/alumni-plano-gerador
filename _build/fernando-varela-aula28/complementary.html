"Using Since and For with the Present Perfect" -- English with Alex, engVid

A clear lesson on for and since: a period of time or a start point, with many examples on the board. Connection to Lesson 28: this is exactly "Marta has worked here for fifteen years" and "Jorge has been a volunteer since 2020."

Tip: pause after each example and say one true sentence about your work with the same structure: "I have... for..." or "I have... since..."
