Look at the words in color. Which lesson does each structure come from? What job does it do in the sentence?

LessonJobExample
16 · Comparatives and superlativescompare then and nowMy listening is much faster than before. Emails are the easiest part.
17 · Adjective order and compound adjectivesdescribe precisely, in few wordsa two-hour, case-based exam
18 · Connectors of contrast and resultbalance a strength and a weaknessAlthough I am fluent, I make small mistakes. Therefore, I keep track of them.
19 · Participle clausesgive a lot of information in one sentenceResidents trained abroad... / doctors preparing for Step 3...

In one line: compare with than, describe with a compound adjective before the noun, balance with although or however, and compress with a participle.

Listening 1 · An Attending

The Halfway Conversation

Dr. Parker on how she checks the progress of international residents. Sound first — no text.

1. What two things does she measure separately?
Fluency and accuracy.
2. What does she ask every resident at the halfway point?
What is easier now than in the first month, and what still makes you nervous.
3. What does she tell residents to keep track of?
Their own mistakes, in a list, for two weeks.
Listening 2 · An International Graduate

The Recording I Avoided

A Brazilian doctor on the day he listened to himself. Sound first — no text.

1. Why did he think his English was good?
Because he was fast and people understood him.
2. What blind spot did the recording show him?
He dropped the word the in almost every sentence.
3. What did he do to fix it?
He recorded one case presentation a week and counted the mistakes.
