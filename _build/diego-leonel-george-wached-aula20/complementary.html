Talk
Atul Gawande -- "Want to get great at something? Get a coach" (TED)

A surgeon tells how he asked a retired colleague to watch his operations and give him feedback, years after his training ended. It is exactly the idea of today: experts also need somebody to show them their blind spots.

Tip: write down every word about progress you hear (feedback, improve, better than) and say one thing a coach could tell you about your English.

Talk
Eduardo Briceño -- "How to get better at the things you care about" (TED)

A short talk about two zones: the zone where you perform and the zone where you practice. It explains why consistent practice, with small mistakes, is the way to make real progress.

Tip: after you watch, say which of your weekly activities in English is practice and which is performance.

Talk
Carol Dweck -- "The power of believing that you can improve" (TED)

A psychologist explains the idea of not yet: you have not learned something yet, but you are on track. A good way to look at a blind spot without frustration.

Tip: make three sentences about your English with not yet, for example: I cannot write a case report with no help yet.
