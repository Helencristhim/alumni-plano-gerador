Lesson 01 -- Pre-class

What I Do -- The Head of HR

Somebody asks what you do, and you answer in English. Key words: human resources, payroll, benefits, to hire, training, a department, to be in charge of, to report to. Structure: present simple with -s for he and she, and questions with does.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Human resources -- the department that takes care of the people in a company
"I work in human resources. We take care of the people."
Payroll -- the list of employees and the money the company pays them every month
"Payroll is ready on the fifth day of every month."
Benefits -- extra things a company gives employees, like a health plan
"Our benefits include a health plan and a meal card."
To hire -- to give somebody a job in your company
"We hire about ten people every month."
Training -- classes and activities that teach employees new skills
"New employees have two days of training."
A department -- one part of a company, like sales or finance
"The finance department is on the third floor."
To be in charge of -- to be the person responsible for something
"She is in charge of the whole HR team."
To report to -- to have somebody as your boss
"I report to the CEO."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Emma works in human resources in London. She reports to the HR director, and she is in charge of training and benefits. Every month her company hires new people, and Emma organizes their first days.

Payroll is not her job: a colleague in the finance department takes care of it. On Mondays she meets her team, and they plan the week.

Daan lives in Amsterdam. He hires people for an international company, and he goes to the office by bike. Does he speak Dutch at work? No, he doesn’t. He speaks English, because his colleagues come from many countries.

1. Who does Emma report to?
A The HR director.
B The finance department and the CEO of the company.
C A colleague in London.
2. Why does Daan speak English at work?
A Because he wants to practice for a new job in London.
B Because his colleagues come from many countries.
C Because he lives in Amsterdam.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A “Daan go to the office by bike.”
B “Daan goes to the office by bike.”
C “Daan is go to the office by bike every day.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Present Simple -- I Manage, She Manages

GRAMMAR

Use the present simple for jobs, routines and facts. With he, she and it, the verb takes -s. For negatives and questions, use don’t / doesn’t and do / does + the simple verb.

JobFormExample
I / you / we / theyverbI manage the HR team.
he / she / itverb + sShe manages the payroll.
Negativedon’t / doesn’t + verbHe doesn’t work in finance.
QuestionDo / Does + subject + verb?Does she report to you?
Special formshave → has · go → goes · do → doesShe has a team of six.
The trapno -s after does or doesn’tNot she doesn’t works. Say she doesn’t work.

Never: She manage the team · He doesn’t works · Do she report to you? · I am report to the CEO
