Series
Succession — Season 1, Episode 4 ("Sad Sack Wasp Trap")

A family company where control depends on who signed what, years earlier. The board scenes are built on the exact problem of this lesson: a structure agreed in the past that nobody can undo now.

Tip: listen for the moment someone says what should have been agreed. Write the sentence down and check the weak form of have.

Podcast
a16z Podcast — "Founder Control and the Cap Table"

Two investors explaining, in unscripted American English, why exit provisions get postponed in fast rounds — and what happens years later. The vocabulary of this lesson is the vocabulary of the episode.

Tip: count how many times you hear should have and would have. Native speakers almost never say the full form.

Harvard Law School — "Deal Structuring and Shareholder Rights"

A lecture-style explanation of tag-along, drag-along and put options, in slow academic English. Useful because it names in English what you already negotiate in Portuguese.

Tip: watch at 0.75x first. Then say each term out loud in a full sentence about a case of your own.
