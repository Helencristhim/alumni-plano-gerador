Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Celeste Headlee: “10 ways to have a better conversation” (12 min)

A radio host on what makes a conversation interesting: real questions, real listening, and short answers.

Tip: choose two of her ten ways and use them in the small talk in class.

TED Talk
Kio Stark: “Why you should talk to strangers” (12 min)

A writer on how short conversations with people you do not know make you feel more connected.

Tip: write down two ways she starts a conversation and turn one into a Have you ever...? question.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Pico Iyer: “Where is home?” (14 min)

A travel writer on what it means to belong to many places. Full of strong adjectives to describe cities and people.

Tip: note five adjectives he uses to describe places and use two of them in your weekly text.
