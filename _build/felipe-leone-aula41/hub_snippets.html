Small Talk on a Business Trip -- Have You Ever...? and Strong Adjectives

Ten to twenty minutes before class. Questions that open a conversation at a dinner with clients, and adjectives that make your answers interesting. Key words: fascinating, stunning, charming, vibrant, crowded, exhausting.

Vocabulary

Charming -- pleasant and attractive, often small and old
"We stayed in a charming old neighborhood."
Vibrant -- full of energy, life and color
"Mexico City has a vibrant food scene."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

At business dinners, small talk often starts with a question about experience: “Have you ever been to Rio?” The answer usually has two parts: “Yes, I have. I went there in 2022.”

Good small talk also needs good adjectives. Instead of “very beautiful”, people say stunning. Instead of “very interesting”, they say fascinating. One strong word is better than very plus a weak word.

Which question is common at the start of a business dinner?
A "Did you ever went to Rio?"
B "Have you ever been to Rio?"
C "Are you ever been to Rio?"
What does the text say about adjectives?
A One strong adjective is better than very plus a weak one.
B Adjectives are not important in small talk.
C Always use very before an adjective.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Have You Ever...? and Strong Adjectives

GRAMMAR

Experience question: Have you ever + third form? Strong adjectives take no very.

JobFormExample
Experience questionHave you ever + third form?Have you ever been to Lisbon?
Short answerYes, I have. / No, I haven’t.Yes, I have. I went in 2021.
Strong adjectiveno veryIt’s a stunning city.
Normal adjectivevery is fineIt’s a very nice hotel.

Never: Did you ever been to Lisbon? · Have you ever gone to Lisbon? (visit) · It’s a very stunning city

Stage 2: Put the Conversation in Order

Order

Two people talk at a business dinner. Put the conversation in a natural order.

?"You should. Have you ever heard of Salvador?"
?"It is, and the food is amazing."
?"No, never. But I would love to go."
?"So, have you ever been to Brazil?"
?"Yes, a colleague told me it’s stunning."

Stage 3: Pronunciation

Speaking

Listen, then record yourself. In fast questions, have you ever sounds almost like one word.

Have you ever tried Brazilian food?
It's a charming little city.
The trip was exhausting but great.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer a real speaker would give.

You want to know if a client has visited Rio. You ask:
A "Were you ever in Rio before?"
B "Did you ever go to Rio?"
C "Have you ever been to Rio?"
A client asks if you know New York. You went there in 2018. You say:
A "Yes, I have. I went there in 2018."
B "Yes, I have been there in 2018."
C "Yes, I go there in 2018."
You want to say that the view was very, very beautiful. You say:

Stage 5: Free Production

Reflection
