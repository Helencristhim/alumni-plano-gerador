Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

At business dinners, small talk often starts with a question about experience: “Have you ever been to Rio?” The answer usually has two parts: “Yes, I have. I went there in 2022.”

Good small talk also needs good adjectives. Instead of “very beautiful”, people say stunning. Instead of “very interesting”, they say fascinating. One strong word is better than very plus a weak word.

Which question is common at the start of a business dinner?
A "Did you ever went to Rio?"
B "Have you ever been to Rio?"
C "Are you ever been to Rio?"
What does the text say about adjectives?
A Adjectives are not important in small talk.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Have You Ever...? and Strong Adjectives

GRAMMAR

Experience question: Have you ever + third form? Strong adjectives take no very.

JobFormExample
Experience questionHave you ever + third form?Have you ever been to Lisbon?
Short answerYes, I have. / No, I haven’t.Yes, I have. I went in 2021.
Strong adjectiveno veryIt’s a stunning city.
Normal adjectivevery is fineIt’s a very nice hotel.

Never: Did you ever been to Lisbon? · Have you ever gone to Lisbon? (visit) · It’s a very stunning city
