Small Talk on a Business Trip

Dinner with clients in another city: questions that open a conversation, and adjectives that make it interesting.

Retomada

Your Text, Five Marks

Say each sentence in the right form, then make one more sentence of yours with it.

Model

Dinner in Chicago

Last month I had dinner with two clients in Chicago. One of them asked me: “Have you ever been to Chicago before?”

I said: “Yes, I have. I came here in 2019. It’s a fascinating city, and the architecture is stunning.”

Then I asked them: “Have you ever been to Brazil?” One of them said no, so I recommended Salvador. I said the food is amazing and the people are very warm.

It was a relaxed dinner, and the meeting the next day was much easier.
