Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
How I Built This -- Leatherman Tool Group: Tim Leatherman

An engineer who spent years designing one tool and then spent the rest of his career running the company that makes it. Guy Raz opens the way every American opens this conversation, and Leatherman answers it in plain sentences about what he does, what the company is, and what it was building at each stage.

Tip: listen to the first ten minutes only. Write down the exact sentence he uses to say what the company does today, and use the same shape for yours.

Video
Big Think -- "The key to better conversations", Charles Duhigg

Duhigg spent years studying why some people leave every room with a new contact and others do not. His answer is almost mechanical: they hand the question back. That is exactly the move you practiced at the end of this lesson, and here you hear a native speaker explain why it works.

Tip: watch it once for the idea, then a second time and note three questions he says a good listener asks. Try one of them this week, in English or in Portuguese.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
TED -- Amy Cuddy, "Your body language may shape who you are" (21 min)

A social psychologist on what happens to people in the two minutes before a high-stakes conversation, and what they can do with those two minutes. It is the same two minutes you worked on today, seen from the other side: not the words, the state you arrive in.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on for the first half and off for the second. Her delivery is slow and very clear, so it is a good place to test how much you catch without text.
