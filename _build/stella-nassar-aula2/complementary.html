Film
Admission (2013)

Tina Fey plays a Princeton admissions officer whose carefully ordered world is shaken during a recruiting trip. A light, funny look at the people who read application essays and what they are actually looking for.

Tip: pay attention to the scenes in the admissions committee. What makes the readers argue for or against an applicant?

Podcast
Essays: What Works -- Inside the Yale Admissions Office

Two Yale admissions officers and a colleague explain, in plain terms, what makes an application essay work and why specific detail beats impressive vocabulary.

Tip: write down three phrases they use to praise an essay. Notice whether they ever say that an essay was very good.

Reading
Essays That Worked -- Johns Hopkins University Admissions

Real essays by admitted students, each followed by a short note from the admissions committee explaining why it worked. The best free library of personal statements you can study.

Tip: read two essays and underline every adverb and adjective pair. Then read the committee note and see which details they chose to praise.
