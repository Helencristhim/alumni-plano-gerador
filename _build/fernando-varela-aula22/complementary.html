"Have to and must" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

A short lesson on how to talk about obligation in English, with a clear part about the negative: don't have to means it is your choice. Connection to Lesson 22: this is exactly "You don't have to print the protocol."

Tip: pause after each example and say one rule from your own team with the same structure: "We have to..." or "We don't have to..."

Podcast
"Is rejection good for us?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters talk about how people feel after a "no", a failure or a mistake, and what they can learn from it. Connection to Lesson 22: a problem at work is information, and every team can learn from it.

Tip: listen once without the transcript and write down one lesson learned from the episode. Then listen again with the transcript and check it.

Sully (2016)

The true story of a pilot who lands a plane on the Hudson River, and of the investigation that comes after: what happened, what the crew had to do, and what everybody learned. Connection to Lesson 22: an incident, a protocol and a lesson learned.

Tip: watch the investigation scenes with English subtitles. Notice how people say what the pilot had to do and what the rules say.
