Chapter 3: The Code

Where Does the Object Go?

rule it out · not rule out it

Grammar Discovery

Read and Notice

"We ruled out three categories."

"We ruled them out in January."

"I brought the delay up on Friday."

"I would push back on the timeline."

In two of them the object sits in the middle. In the last one it cannot. What is different about it?

TypeRuleExample
Separable
(two words)		The noun can go after or in the middle.rule out the category / rule the category out
With a pronounit, them and him always go in the middle.rule it out — never rule out it
Three words
(with a preposition)		Never separate. The object comes at the end.push back on the timeline / follow up on it
RegisterIn a written report, prefer the formal twin (eliminate, reduce, expand). In a meeting, the phrasal verb is what people say.

One line to remember: a pronoun never goes at the end of a two-word phrasal verb.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"The data was too thin, so we ruled out it."
"The data was too thin, so we ruled it out."
"I would push it back on."
"I would push back on it."
"She asked me to follow up the delay with finance."
"She asked me to follow up on the delay with finance."

A pronoun always goes in the middle of a two-word phrasal verb. A three-word one never separates, and it keeps its preposition.

Detail

Report or Meeting?

Written document → formal verb  ·  spoken meeting → phrasal verb

REPORT: "Three categories were eliminated." → MEETING: "We ___ruled three categories out."
REPORT: "The pilot will be expanded in March." → MEETING: "We are ___scaling the pilot up in March."
REPORT: "The tool will be discontinued." → MEETING: "We are ___phasing it out."
REPORT: "The matter was raised with finance." → MEETING: "I ___brought it up with finance."

Two Minutes in the Stand-Up

A quick update, spoken at natural speed. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. How many categories are left, and why were three removed?
Six are left. Three were ruled out because the data is too thin.
2. What is happening to the old spreadsheet?
It is being phased out, so everyone should stop using it this week.
3. What did the commercial director ask him to do?
To follow up on the sign-off delay with finance. He will report back on Thursday.
