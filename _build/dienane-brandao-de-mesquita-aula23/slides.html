Chapter 3: The Code

The Verbs Without -ED

go → went · have → had · see → saw

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Last month I went to Belém.
I took a flight in the morning.
At the airport I had a coffee.
At the conference I saw a great presentation.

What do the orange words have in common? Do they end in -ed?

VerbPastExample
gowentI went to Belém.
havehadWe had lunch at the hotel.
seesawShe saw a great presentation.
taketookI took a flight.
meetmetI met a coworker at the airport.
make / come / getmade / came / gotHe came back on Friday.

Key idea: these verbs do NOT use -ED: they change. The past form is the SAME for everyone: I went, she went, they went.

Chapter 4: The Conference

Back from Belém

Dialogue

How Was the Trip?

M
Welcome back, Dienane! How was the trip to Belem?
D
It was great! I went on Tuesday. I took an early flight, at six.
M
Six! Did you have breakfast at the airport?
D
Yes, I had a coffee there, and I met Ana from Finance.
M
Nice. And the conference?
D
Very good. I saw a presentation about new employees. I have many ideas now!
M
I went to Belem last year, and I loved the Ver-o-Peso market. I bought a lot of fruit there!
D
Oh, I saw the market too, but only from the bus.
