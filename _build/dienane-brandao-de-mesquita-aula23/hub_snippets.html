Irregular Past -- A Work Trip to Belém

Verbos que NÃO usam -ed no passado: go → went, have → had, see → saw. New vocabulary: trip, flight, airport, suitcase, conference, presentation. You will learn to tell a trip story.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. (Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo.)

Trip -- a journey to another place (viagem)
"I have a work trip next month." (Tenho uma viagem de trabalho mês que vem.)
Flight -- a journey on a plane (voo)
"My flight is at seven in the morning." (Meu voo é às sete da manhã.)
Airport -- where planes arrive and leave (aeroporto)
"The airport in Altamira is small." (O aeroporto de Altamira é pequeno.)
Suitcase -- a big bag for clothes (mala)
"My suitcase is very heavy." (Minha mala está muito pesada.)
Conference -- a big meeting about one subject (congresso)
"The HR conference is in Belem." (O congresso de RH é em Belém.)
Presentation -- a talk to a group (apresentação)
"Her presentation was about new employees." (A apresentação dela foi sobre novos funcionários.)

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Irregular Verbs (Verbos Irregulares)

GRAMMAR

Alguns verbos muito comuns não usam -ed: eles mudam. E a forma é igual para todas as pessoas. / Some common verbs do not use -ed: they change.

Verb / VerboPast / PassadoExample / Exemplo
go (ir)wentI went to Belém. (Eu fui a Belém.)
have (ter, tomar)hadI had a coffee. (Eu tomei um café.)
see (ver)sawShe saw the river. (Ela viu o rio.)
take (pegar)tookI took a flight. (Eu peguei um voo.)
meet (encontrar, conhecer)metI met Ana. (Eu encontrei a Ana.)
come / make / getcame / made / gotHe came back on Friday. (Ele voltou na sexta.)

Key point / Ponto-chave: nunca "goed", "haved" ou "seed". Estes verbos têm forma própria — e são os mais usados no dia a dia, então vale decorar.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the best answer for each situation. (Escolha a melhor resposta.)

Marcos asks about your trip last week. You say:
A "I goed to Belem."
B "I went to Belem."
C "I go to Belem yesterday."
You drank a coffee at the airport. You say:
A "I had a coffee."
B "I haved a coffee."
C "I have a coffee yesterday."
You found a coworker at the conference. You say:
A "I meeted Ana."
B "I meet Ana last week."
C "I met Ana."

Stage 5: Free Production

Reflection

Record yourself. There is no right or wrong answer. (Grave você respondendo.)

Tell a story about a real trip: work or family. Where did you go? How did you go? What did you see? Who did you meet? Use went, took, saw, had, met. (Conte uma viagem real: aonde foi, o que viu, quem encontrou.)
