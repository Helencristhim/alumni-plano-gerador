Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Suits -- Season 1, Episode 1, "Pilot"

A law firm where nobody says their title. Every lawyer in the room introduces himself by the case he is holding and the client he answers for, in one line, at speed. That is the whole grammar of this lesson, spoken in a corridor.

Tip: stop at every introduction and write it down in one line. Then say your own version out loud in the same shape.

Talk
Rebecca Okamoto -- "How to introduce yourself, and get hired" (TED)

Twelve minutes on the exact problem of this lesson: how to say what you do in twenty words or fewer, so the other person answers "tell me more" instead of nodding politely. Her rule is the one you heard in today’s second recording: a soundbite, never a data dump.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Write her five openings down, then write yours next to them.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
Odd Lots (Bloomberg) -- "Javier Blas Explains How Commodity Trading Shops Really Work"

Your clients, described from the other side of the table. Two journalists ask a commodities reporter how the houses that move grain, oil and metal actually operate, and half the vocabulary of this lesson turns up on its own: a shipment, a contract, a trader, a deadline.

Tip: listen to the first fifteen minutes only, twice. The first time for the argument, the second time for the words you already know.
