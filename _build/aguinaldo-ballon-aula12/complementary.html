TED -- Dan Gilbert, "Why we make bad decisions" (33 min)

A Harvard psychologist shows why people are bad at estimating the odds and the value of things, with examples about money and risk. Useful for anybody who explains risk to clients and committees.

Tip: watch the first fifteen minutes. Write down two sentences he uses to talk about probability.
