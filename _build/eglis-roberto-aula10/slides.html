Stories from the Water

Close calls at sea, told at the marina bar: what would have happened if, and what you should have done.

The Question

Have You Ever Had a Close Call?

You are at the marina bar after a long day on the water. A young sailor sits next to you and asks: “Have you ever had a close call at sea? What happened?” Tell him, and ask him the same question.

The Conversation · You

Your Close Calls

1. What is your closest call at sea? What happened, and how did you get back?
2. Think of the Camino or a backpacking trip. Was there a moment when something almost went wrong? What would have happened if…?
3. Is there a decision on the water that you still think about? What should you have done?

Language you can use

The Conversation · The World

Luck or Preparation?

1. Are most accidents at sea bad luck, or mistakes? Give one example.
2. Should it be illegal to sail without a lifejacket, or is that a personal decision?
3. Young sailors sometimes say the old ones are too careful. Who is right?

Language you can use

Close Calls

Four Cards, Four Stories

One minute for each card: what happened, what would have happened if, and what you should have done.

Card 1: The Storm
A sunny morning, no forecast, and a gust of wind in the afternoon.
Card 2: The Engine
A quiet night, no wind, and an engine that stops.
Card 3: On Land
A close call on the Camino, on the road, or on a trip.
Card 4: Your Turn to Ask
