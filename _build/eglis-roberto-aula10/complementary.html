Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Roz Savage: Why I am rowing across the Pacific (6 min)

A woman who rowed alone across three oceans talks about fear, risk and the moments when things almost went wrong. Short and very clear.

Tip: after the talk, say one thing she should have done differently, and one thing that would have happened if she had stayed in her office job.

Film
All Is Lost (2013)

Robert Redford alone on a sailboat in the Indian Ocean, after a container hits his boat. There is almost no dialogue, so watch it for the sailing, and then tell the story yourself.

Tip: after the film, tell the story in one minute with three sentences in the third conditional: If he had..., he would have...

TED Talk
Ben Saunders: To the South Pole and back (18 min)

An explorer tells the story of a hard expedition and what he learned from it. He talks a lot about decisions, luck and regret.

Tip: write down one decision he made, and say what would have happened if he had made a different one.
