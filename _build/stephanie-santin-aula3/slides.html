Chapter 3: The Code

Two Tenses, One Question

Does this sentence carry a finished moment, or a line to today?

Discover the Rule

What Do These Have in Common?

I graduated from medical school in 2007.
I have worked in healthcare since then.
She left the hospital three years ago.
She has not gone back yet.
Click to reveal the rule
Look at the second highlighted part of each sentence. When the sentence carries a finished moment (in 2007, three years ago, yesterday, last year), English uses the past simple. When it carries a line to today (since then, so far, yet, already, for ten years), English uses the present perfect. The time expression decides — not how important the event feels.

Do You Regret It?

S
Mark, can I ask you something? When did you leave the operating room?
M
In 2015. I worked as a general surgeon for eleven years, and then I stopped.
S
And what have you done since then?
M
I have worked in hospital management. I have not operated since 2015.
S
Do you regret it?
M
I did at the beginning. For two years I thought about going back every single week.
S
And now?
M
Now, no. I have found a different way to be useful, and I have made my peace with it.
Comprehension

About Mark

1. When did Mark leave the operating room, and how long did he work as a surgeon?
He left in 2015, and he worked as a general surgeon for eleven years.
2. What has he done since 2015?
He has worked in hospital management, and he has not operated since then.
3. Does he regret the decision?
He did at the beginning — for two years he thought about going back every week — but he does not anymore.

Six Questions About Time

Listening 2

A Problem with Your Documents

Read the questions, then listen.

1. When did the office receive the documents, and what did they do that week?
They received them on the fifteenth and opened the file the same week.
2. What is the problem with the diploma?
It is a copy, and copies have not been accepted since January, when the rule changed.
3. What does the caller ask her to do, and by when?
To bring the original document, or a certified version, before the end of next week.
