Lesson 3 -- When English Wants the Date Back

Talk
Brian Goldman -- "Doctors make mistakes. Can we talk about that?"

An emergency physician tells three stories from his own career. The whole talk moves between what happened once and what has changed since — exactly the contrast you practised today.

Tip: listen for the moment he switches from "I did" to "I have". Write down two sentences of each kind.

Talk
Jill Bolte Taylor -- "My stroke of insight"

A neuroscientist describes the morning she had a stroke, minute by minute, and what she has learned since. A career told as a before and an after.

Tip: watch the first eight minutes and then retell them out loud in the past simple. Then say in one sentence what has changed for her since that morning.
