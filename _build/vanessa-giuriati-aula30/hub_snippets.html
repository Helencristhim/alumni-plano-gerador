Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Speaking of which -- Used to connect a new topic to what was just said
"Speaking of which, did you ever book that trip?"
That reminds me -- Says that something you just heard made you remember something
"That reminds me, how was your sister's birthday?"
By the way -- Adds a small extra piece of information to the conversation
"By the way, I loved your presentation."
Anyway -- Moves the conversation on, or back to the main point
"Anyway, how's the new director?"
Going back to -- Used to return to something that was said earlier
"Going back to what you said about Chile, which city do you recommend?"
On a different note -- Used to start a completely different topic
"On a different note, are you coming to dinner on Friday?"
As I was saying -- Used to continue after an interruption
"As I was saying, the south is beautiful in March."
To sum up -- Used to give the main points briefly at the end
"To sum up, it was a very good year."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Ten lessons ago, I would plan every sentence in Portuguese before I said it. Lately, I have been speaking without a script, and it feels different. Last week, at a dinner with my husband's colleagues, the conversation jumped from travel to work to children. When someone mentioned Chile, I said, "Speaking of which, we are going to the south in March." Later, a story about a bad boss made me say, "That reminds me of my first job." One guest came across as very serious, but she turned out to be the funniest person at the table. By the way, nobody noticed my accent, or if they did, they did not care. Anyway, I am not fluent yet. Going back to my first point: the script is gone, and I do not miss it. To sum up, it was the first dinner in English that I actually enjoyed.

1. What did she use to do before she spoke?
A She would speak without thinking.
B She would ask her husband to translate.
C She would plan every sentence in Portuguese.
2. When did she say "Speaking of which"?
A When someone mentioned Chile.
B When dinner ended.
C When she met a new boss.
3. Which phrase did she use to return to an earlier point?
A "By the way"
B "Going back to my first point"
C "That reminds me"

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Your toolkit, Lessons 21-29

GRAMMAR

Nine lessons, one toolkit. These are the structures to bring into every conversation now.

StructureUseExample
have been + -ing (Lesson 29)Something still goingI've been speaking without a script lately.
come across as / strike me as (Lesson 28)An impressionShe comes across as serious.
I'd rather you + past form (Lesson 27)Ask someone to changeI'd rather you told me earlier.
present continuous (Lesson 26)A booked planWe're flying to Chile in March.
would + base verb (Lesson 25)A repeated past habitBack then, I would plan every sentence.

Topic bridges glue it all together: 'speaking of which' (not 'about which'), 'that reminds me' (never 'that remembers me'), 'going back to what you said'.

Stage 2: Change Topics in a Conversation, in Order

Order

Listen first, then put the steps in the right order.

?Then I connect a new topic to it: speaking of which, how was your weekend?
?In the end, I move to something completely new: on a different note, are you free on Friday?
?Next, I return to an earlier point: going back to what you said about work.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the best answer for each situation.

A friend mentions a holiday, and you want to ask about her trip. Best choice:
A "Speaking about which, how was your trip?"
B "Speaking which, how was your trip?"
C "Speaking of which, how was your trip?"
Something your friend said brought back a memory. Best choice:
A "That remembers me of my first job."
B "That reminds me of my first job."
C "That reminds my first job."
You want to return to an earlier point. Best choice:
A "Going back to what you said..."
B "Going back what you said..."
C "Going back on what you said..."
You want to start a completely new topic. Best choice:
A "In a different note, are you free on Friday?"
B "At a different note, are you free on Friday?"
C "On a different note, are you free on Friday?"
