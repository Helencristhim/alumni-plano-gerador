Toolkit Review

Which Lesson Is This?

"I've been feeling much more confident lately."

"She comes across as calm, but she is very driven."

"I'd rather you told me before the meeting."

"Back then, I would plan every sentence in Portuguese."

FormUseExample
have been + -ing (Lesson 29)Something that started before and is still goingI've been feeling more confident lately.
come across as / strike me as (Lesson 28)An impression, not a factShe comes across as calm.
I'd rather you + past form (Lesson 27)Ask someone else to changeI'd rather you told me earlier.
present continuous for arrangements (Lesson 26)A booked planWe're flying to Chile in March.
would + base verb (Lesson 25)A repeated past habitBack then, I would plan every sentence.
must be / might be (Lesson 24)A logical guessHe hasn't answered. He must be busy.
⚠ CarefulToday the goal is FLUENCY, not perfection: keep talking, correct yourself if you notice, and move on. The bridges glue it together: 'speaking of which', 'that reminds me' (never 'that remembers me'), 'going back to what you said'.

Listening

Bridges, Not Bumps

Listen first. No text. Sound only.

1. What does "speaking of which" do?
It connects the new topic to the last one.
2. Which phrase helps you return to an earlier point?
"Going back to what you said."
3. How many bridges do you need, according to the speaker?
Three or four, used naturally, are enough.
Listening

The Plateau

A different voice, a different message. Listen carefully.

1. What happens around the intermediate level?
Progress becomes invisible: you are still improving, but you cannot feel it, and many people give up.
2. What kind of questions should you ask instead of "Am I fluent?"
Smaller ones: did I speak for two minutes without stopping, change topics without freezing, disagree without apologizing?
3. What does he suggest doing once a month?
Record yourself and listen to the old recordings.

"That reminds me, how was the party?" ("remind" = make someone remember)
