Lesson 21 -- Small Talk Mastery

Talk
Celeste Headlee -- "10 ways to have a better conversation" (TED)

A radio host explains how to have real conversations: ask open questions, listen, and do not repeat yourself. It is the homework talk for this lesson.

Tip: write down the three tips you like most, and try one of them in your next meeting at Sucafina.

Talk
Kio Stark -- "Why you should talk to strangers" (TED)

A writer talks about the value of short conversations with people we do not know. Clear, calm English about starting a chat with anyone.

Tip: listen for the phrases she uses to start a conversation, and say two of them aloud.

Talk
Julian Treasure -- "How to speak so that people want to listen" (TED)

A sound expert talks about voice, rhythm and intonation. It connects with the falling and rising voice of question tags.

Tip: notice how his voice goes up and down, and repeat three of his sentences with the same melody.
