Chapter 3: The Code

One Idea, Not Two

Five little words that hold sentences together

Grammar Discovery

What Does the Little Word Do?

"We did not sell in March, because the price was low."

"The price was low, so we kept the corn."

"Although the library was cold, nobody missed a class."

"The texts were long. However, every student wrote one paragraph."

The red words connect a reason and a result. The blue words connect a problem and a decision that stays.

JobFormExample
Reason (why)because + subject + verbWe waited because the price was low.
Result (what happened next)so + subject + verbThe price was low, so we waited.
Contrast, simplebut, in the middleFriday is long, but I like the group.
Contrast, one sentencealthough + subject + verb, at the start or in the middleAlthough Friday is long, I like the group.
Contrast, a new sentenceHowever + comma, at the start of the second sentenceFriday is long. However, I like the group.
The directionbecause points back to the reason, so points forward to the resultSame two facts, two orders.
The two trapsnever although ... but in the same sentence, and never however where you mean although — however starts a new sentence

In one line: because looks back, so looks forward, and although and however keep the decision standing next to the problem.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

Back From Singapore

One conversation, two recordings, one memo

Dialogue

Six Weeks, Three Decisions

E
Welcome back. How was Singapore?
C
Very good, but very different. I moved the seminar to the evening, because most of my students work during the day.
E
Was that a good decision?
C
Yes. The room was small, so we met in the library. Although the library was cold, nobody missed a class.
E
And the reading list? Did you keep the same texts?
C
No. The texts were long, so I cut the list from twelve to eight. However, every student wrote one paragraph a week.
E
One paragraph a week? Why only one?
C
Because one good paragraph teaches more than ten bad pages. And you? Did you choose Miami or London in the end?
Comprehension

About Claire’s Six Weeks

1. Why did Claire move the seminar to the evening?
Because most of her students work during the day.
2. Where did they meet?
In the library, because the room was small.
3. How many texts are on the list now?
Eight. She cut the list from twelve to eight.
