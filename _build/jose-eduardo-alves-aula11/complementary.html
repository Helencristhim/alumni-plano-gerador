Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Harvest Public Media (NPR) — "Storing Grain Can Aid Farmers In Commodity Pricing"

Four minutes about the decision Paulo explains in this lesson: farmers who keep their grain in storage, because they want to choose when to sell. Every sentence is a decision with a reason, and the words are his world.

Tip: listen once, then say the farmer’s decision in two sentences: one with because, one with so.

Listen on HPPR ↗
Talk
Tom Griffiths — "3 ways to make better decisions, by thinking like a computer" (TED)

A cognitive scientist on how to choose when you cannot check every option. Clear speech, simple examples, and every idea comes with its reason.

Tip: subtitles are available. Pick one of the three ideas and justify it out loud: the idea, the reason, and one drawback.

Watch on TED ↗
Grammar page
British Council LearnEnglish — "Contrasting ideas: although, despite and others"

A short page on although and the other contrast words, with two online tests at the end. It goes one step further than the lesson, and that is fine: read the although part first.

Tip: do Grammar test 1, then write three sentences about your own week with although.
