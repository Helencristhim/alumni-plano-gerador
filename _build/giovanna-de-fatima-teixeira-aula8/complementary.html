English at Work — Episode 35: "Briefing the Manager"

Anna and Tom finish a business meeting, and then their flight is cancelled. Anna has to call her boss and explain what happened. It is the same job you did in class today: tell somebody who was not there what went wrong, and what you did about it.

Tip: watch it once with English subtitles. The second time, stop the video before Anna speaks to her boss and tell the story yourself, in the past.

Podcast
English Unleashed — "The Necklace" (short story, A2–B1)

A classic short story, read slowly and clearly for A2 and B1 listeners. A story is the natural home of the irregular past: went, took, had, said and came arrive one after the other, for the whole episode, inside a sentence that makes sense.

Tip: good for the commute, when you cannot read or write but you can listen. Play it once for the story. The second time, count how many times you hear "she said".

Verb Ninja — "English Irregular Past Tense Verbs, Vol. 1"

Four minutes of the exact problem of today's class, in quiz form. A verb appears inside a sentence, you say the past form out loud, and only then the answer comes on the screen. It is practice, not explanation.
