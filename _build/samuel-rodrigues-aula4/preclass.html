Lesson 04 -- Pre-class

Getting Things Done Through Others -- The Grammar of Delegation

Eleven days to closing, ten items on the checklist, and not one of them done by you alone. Before the lesson: the eight words of a closing — appraisal, due diligence, escrow, closing, encumbrance, lien, covenant, novation — and the structure that says who is answerable without claiming you did it yourself: we had the title searched.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Eight words from a closing. Listen to each one and read the example out loud.

Appraisal -- an expert's statement of what something is worth
"We have already had the property appraised."
Due diligence -- the investigation you run before you commit
"The buyer will have due diligence carried out before signing."
Escrow -- money held by a third party until the conditions are met
"The funds sit in escrow until every condition is met."
Closing -- the day the deal is signed and the money moves
"We go to closing in eleven days."
Encumbrance -- any claim or restriction attached to an asset
"Every encumbrance has to be cleared before closing."
Lien -- a creditor's hold on an asset until a debt is paid
"We are having the lien discharge confirmed by the registry."
Covenant -- a promise in a contract to do or not do something
"The lease sets out a covenant not to sublet without consent."
Novation -- replacing one party in a contract with a new one, with everyone's consent
"I'd recommend having the novation agreement reviewed first."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the client email. The verbs in bold say who is answerable, not who typed.

Eleven days to closing on the Pinheiros lease. The landlord side is not idle: they are having the title searched by their own firm, and they have already had the property appraised.

What they want from our side is the due diligence report, confirmation that the lien on the parking structure has been discharged, and an opinion letter on signing authority. They also want to have the novation agreement reviewed before the funds go into escrow. Note what these sentences do not say: who typed the report, who walked into the registry. They say who is answerable for it.

In "we are having the title searched", who searches the title?
A We do, ourselves.
B Somebody else does — we arranged it and we answer for it.
C Nobody yet; it is only planned.
Why would a senior lawyer prefer this to "I will review the agreement"?
A It shows coordination, and it avoids promising work another team delivers.
B It is shorter and easier to say.
C It hides who is responsible for the result.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Causative — have something done

You arrange it. Someone else does it. You answer for the result.

  • have + object + past participle
  • "We had the agreement reviewed by outside counsel."
  • In progress: "They are having the register updated."
  • Already done: "We have already had the property appraised."
  • After recommend, use the -ing form: "I'd recommend having it reviewed" — never to have.

Three registers, one fact

  • informal — "We checked the title."
  • formal, active — "Our team reviewed the agreement."
  • professional, causative — "We had the agreement reviewed by outside counsel."

get works the same way and is less formal: "we got it done". Keep have for the client, get for the corridor.

Say it slowly. The participle links to the object: had-it-reviewed, getting-it-done. Rushed, the participle disappears and the sentence goes back to being active.

Stage 3: Pronunciation

Speaking

Keep the participle linked to the object: had-it-searched, having-it-reviewed. Rushed, the participle disappears.

We are having the title searched by our office in Sao Paulo.
We have already had the property appraised.
I'd recommend having the novation agreement reviewed first.
We will have due diligence carried out before signing.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz
The general counsel asks who is checking the title. Which answer fits a senior lawyer?
A "I will search the title this week."
B "We are having the title searched by our office in Sao Paulo."
C "The title, I check it myself on Monday."
You want to recommend a step before the funds move. Which form is right?
A "I'd recommend to have the agreement reviewed first."
B "I'd recommend having the agreement reviewed first."
C "I'd recommend have reviewed the agreement first."
It is already finished. Which sentence says so, without claiming you did it?
A "We have already had the property appraised."
B "We have already had appraised the property."
C "We already appraised the property ourselves."
He wants due diligence in two days and it takes five. What do you say?
A "That is impossible."
B "I'd push back slightly on that timeline: I can have the report carried out by Thursday."
C "I will try to do everything myself by Tuesday."
