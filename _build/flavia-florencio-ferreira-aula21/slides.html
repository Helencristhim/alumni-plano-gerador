Chapter 3: The Approximation Code

Round It, Say It Well

approximately · nearly · over · more than

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"We support approximately five hundred producers."

"Nearly half of our producers are women."

"More than twelve thousand people visited the fair."

"Over thirty percent of them sell outside Brazil."

What do the orange words have in common? Is each number exact? Is it a little more, or a little less?

WordMeaningExample
approximately / about / aroundclose to the number (more or less)approximately 500 producers
nearly / almosta little LESS than the numbernearly half (48%)
over / more thana little MORE than the numbermore than 12,000 people
fewer thana smaller number (things you count)fewer than ten people

Put the word before the number, with no "of": "approximately five hundred". For parts of a group, use of: "half of our producers", "thirty percent of them", "the majority of our producers are...".

Chapter 4: The Data Meeting

The Numbers Talk

A partner asks; Flavia presents the figures

Dialogue

The Data Meeting

L
Thank you for coming, Flavia. Our members want to understand your program. How big is it?
F
To give you an idea of the scale, we work with approximately five hundred producers.
L
That is a lot. What percentage of them sell outside Brazil?
F
Right now, about ten percent. But the figures show that the number is growing.
L
Interesting. In Parma, the average cheese producer has more than twenty employees.
F
Ours are smaller. The majority have fewer than ten people.
L
I see. And what is the trend for the next three years?
F
Very positive. Our growth in the last three years is nearly forty percent.
