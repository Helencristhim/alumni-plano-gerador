Lesson 21 -- Numbers That Tell a Story: Presenting Data and Scale

Short Documentary
"Hans Rosling's 200 Countries, 200 Years, 4 Minutes" -- BBC

In four minutes, Hans Rosling turns a huge amount of data into a clear story about health and money in the world. Connection to Lesson 21: this is exactly the skill of today, numbers that tell a story, with a clear trend at the end.

Tip: watch once for the story. Then watch again and write down three numbers he says with words like "more than" or "nearly".

Business English Video
"How to Describe a Graph - Business English lesson" -- Roxana Pascariu

A practical lesson with simple structures to describe charts and numbers before a meeting. Connection to Lesson 21: it gives you more words for trends, like "increase" and "growth", to use with your program data.

Tip: pause after each structure and say one sentence about your own program, like "The number of producers is growing."

TED Talk
"The best stats you've ever seen" -- Hans Rosling, TED

A famous talk about how to present statistics so that people feel them. Connection to Lesson 21: watch how a speaker rounds numbers, compares them, and always gives each number a meaning, like the presentation coach in today's listening.
