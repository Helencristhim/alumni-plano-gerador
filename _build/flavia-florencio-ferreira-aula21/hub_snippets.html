Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Figure -- a number, especially in official information
"The figures show that the program is working."
Statistic -- a number that comes from studying a lot of information
"One statistic surprised the delegation."
Percentage -- a part of a total, as a number out of one hundred
"A small percentage of our producers sell abroad."
Average -- the usual or middle amount of a group of numbers
"On average, a producer sells two hundred jars a month."
Total -- the full amount after you add everything
"In total, we have thirty-three regional offices."
Approximately -- close to a number, but not exact
"Approximately five hundred producers joined the program."
Growth -- an increase in size or in number
"The growth of the program is strong."
Majority -- more than half of a group
"The majority of our producers are small businesses."
Trend -- the general direction in which something is changing
"The trend is positive: the number is growing."
Scale -- the size or level of something
"To give you an idea of the scale, we work in the whole state."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Flavia is preparing a short fact sheet for a chamber of commerce in Italy. She does not read long, exact numbers. She rounds them. She writes: "To give you an idea of the scale, we work with approximately five hundred producers." The real number is 497. Then she writes about women in the program: "Nearly half of our producers are women." The real percentage is 48 percent, a little less than half. She also writes: "More than twelve thousand people visited our fair." The real figure is 12,400. Finally, she ends with the trend: "The figures show that the program is growing. The majority of our producers are small businesses, and our growth in three years is nearly forty percent."

1. The real number is 497 producers. What does Flavia write?
A "Exactly five hundred producers, more or less."
B "Approximately of five hundred producers in the state."
C "Approximately five hundred producers."
2. Why does Flavia say "nearly half" and not "more than half"?
A Because the real number, 48 percent, is a little less than half.
B Because the real number, 48 percent, is a little more than half of the producers.
C Because she does not know the number.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A "The majority of our producers is small businesses in the state."
B "The majority of our producers are small businesses."
C "The majority our producers are small."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Approximation Words

GRAMMAR

How to make a number short, round, and professional.

WordMeaningExample
approximately / about / aroundclose to the number (more or less)approximately 500 producers
nearly / almosta little LESS than the numbernearly half (48%)
over / more thana little MORE than the numbermore than 12,000 people
fewer thana smaller number (things you count)fewer than ten people
RulePut the word before the number, with no "of": "approximately five hundred". For a part of a group, use of: "half of our producers", "thirty percent of them". After "the majority of" + plural noun, use a plural verb: "the majority of our producers are...". To present data, open with "To give you an idea of the scale, ..." or "The figures show that ...".
