Lesson 21 -- The Interview

Interview
NYU School of Law -- Oral history interview with Justice Ruth Bader Ginsburg (2016)

A long, relaxed interview in which a Supreme Court justice tells the story of her own career: law school, the firms that would not hire her, and the cases that changed everything. A model of a career told as a series of moments, not a resume. The page has the full video and a transcript.

Tip: choose one story she tells and write down the first sentence she uses. Then tell the same story in under a minute, starting with an inversion.

Talk
Larry Smith -- "Why you will fail to have a great career" (TED, 15 min)

An economics professor explains, with humor and a lot of provocation, the excuses people use to avoid the career they really want. Fast, rhetorical English, full of emphasis.

Tip: listen for the moments when he puts the strong word first. Write down two of his sentences and say them again with your own career in them.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
NPR Life Kit -- "How to ace a job interview and combat your nerves"

A career coach explains which questions to prepare for and what to do when you get nervous, and then the host and the coach role-play a real interview. Useful for any interview, not only for a job.

Tip: when they practice the role-play, pause and answer the same question yourself before you hear the model answer.
