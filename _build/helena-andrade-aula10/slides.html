The Exam

The Verb That Looks Wrong

"The Code requires that the master notify the maritime authority immediately."

A real kind of regulatory sentence. It is not a mistake.

The subject is singular and the verb has no s. Is it a mistake? Choose: A) yes, a typing error, or B) no, it is correct formal English. In the exam, a wrong option often changes notify into notifies.

Chapter 4: The Code

The Verb With No Ending

requires that he notify · recommends that she complete · it is essential that it stay

Grammar Discovery

Where Did the S Go?

"The convention requires that the master notify the authority at once."

"It recommends that all crew members complete the drill before departure."

"It is essential that a standard be independent."

Look at the words before that in each sentence. Then look at the verb after the subject. What is missing?

FormWhat it doesExample
require / recommend + thatthe next verb is in the base form, with no sThe Code requires that the master notify the authority.
it is essential thatthe same base formIt is essential that the standard be independent.
be, not isthe base form of be is berequires that every ship be open to inspection
The reading trapthis verb looks like a mistake, but it is correct. In the exam, a wrong option often changes notify into notifies.

You do not need to write this form. You need to read it and know that it is correct, not a mistake.

What an Inspector Actually Decides
