Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each term and read it inside a real sentence. These are the words the exam text will use.

Maritime authority -- the government office that is responsible for safety at sea in a country
"The master must notify the maritime authority at once."
Surveyor -- the official who inspects a ship and says if she meets a standard
"A surveyor may issue a certificate."
Certificate -- the official document that shows a ship or a seafarer meets a standard
"The certificate is issued for the flag state."
Endorsement -- an official addition to a certificate that records an extra skill
"An endorsement shows a new competency."
Port state control -- the inspection of foreign ships by the country of the port
"Port state control inspects ships from other countries."
Deficiency -- a problem found during an inspection, when a rule is not met
"Any deficiency is recorded against the ship."
Detention -- the act of keeping a ship in port until a problem is corrected
"Detention follows only when the ship is not seaworthy."
Seafarer -- any person who works on board a ship
"The cook is a seafarer too."
Competency -- a proven ability to do a professional task
"The endorsement records a competency, not a course."
Seaworthiness -- the condition of a ship that is safe and ready for the voyage
"No document can see a loss of seaworthiness."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Base Form in Regulations (Reading)

GRAMMAR

After requires that, recommends that and it is essential that, formal English uses the base form: notify, not notifies; be, not is. It is not a mistake. You only need to read it and keep the meaning.

FormWhat it doesExample
require / recommend + thatthe next verb is in the base form, with no sThe Code requires that the master notify the authority.
it is essential thatthe same base formIt is essential that the standard be independent.
be, not isthe base form of be is berequires that every ship be open to inspection
The reading trapthis verb looks like a mistake, but it is correct. In the exam, a wrong option often changes notify into notifies.

You do not need to write this form. You need to read it and know that it is correct, not a mistake.
