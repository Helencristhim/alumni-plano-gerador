Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each term and read it inside a real sentence. These are the words the exam text will use.

Maritime authority -- the government body responsible for shipping safety in a state
"The master shall notify the maritime authority immediately."
Surveyor -- the official who inspects a ship and reports whether she meets a standard
"A surveyor acting under authorisation may issue both documents."
Certificate -- the document confirming that a ship or a seafarer meets a required standard
"A certificate is issued by, or on behalf of, the flag state."
Endorsement -- an official addition to a certificate, recording an extra qualification
"An endorsement records a competency the seafarer has demonstrated."
Port state control -- the inspection of foreign ships by the country whose port they enter
"Port state control exists because a flag state cannot inspect a ship abroad."
Deficiency -- a failure to meet a requirement, found during an official inspection
"Any deficiency found is recorded against the ship."
Detention -- the official holding of a vessel in port until a deficiency is corrected
"Detention follows only when the vessel is judged unseaworthy."
Seafarer -- any person employed or engaged in any capacity on board a ship
"An endorsement is added to the seafarer's own certificate."
Competency -- a proven ability to carry out a defined professional task
"The endorsement records a competency, not a course attended."
Seaworthiness -- the condition of a ship being fit, in every respect, for the voyage intended
"No document detects a loss of seaworthiness on the morning of an inspection."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the passage, then answer the questions about how it is written.

The regime requires that every vessel calling at a member port be liable to inspection, and that any deficiency found be recorded against the ship. The convention requires that the master notify the maritime authority immediately of any condition affecting seaworthiness.

It recommends that all crew members complete the relevant drill before departure, and that the master not sail without clearance. It is essential that the standard be independent of the thing it measures, and it is essential that the cost of a deficiency stay with the ship that has it.

1. The convention requires that the master notify the authority. Why is there no s on notify?
A It is a printing error that has survived in the text.
B It is the subjunctive: the base form after require, whatever the subject.
C Because master is treated as a plural in legal English.
2. How would you write the negative of requires that the master sail with clearance?
A requires that the master does not sail without clearance
B requires that the master not sail without clearance
C requires that the master no sail without clearance
3. It is essential that the standard be independent. Why be and not is?
A Because be is the base form, and the base form is what this construction takes.
B Because standard is an uncountable noun.
C Because the sentence describes the future.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Subjunctive of a Regulation

GRAMMAR

English kept the subjunctive almost only where somebody is being told what to do. That is why a regulation is full of it and a newspaper is not -- and why a verb with no ending, in a formal text, is a signal rather than a mistake.

FormWhat it doesExample
require / recommend / demand / insist + thatthe verb in the that-clause goes into the BASE form, whatever the subjectThe Code requires that the master notify the authority.
it is essential / vital / imperative thatsame base form, in the impersonal construction regulations preferIt is essential that the standard be independent.
be, not is or arethe base form of be is be, in every personrequires that every vessel be liable to inspection
the negativenot goes straight in front of the base form, with no auxiliaryrequires that the master not sail without clearance
The reading trapthe base form looks like a plural, a typing error, or a missing auxiliary, and every one of those misreadings is offered to you as an option. Requires that the master notifies is not the same sentence: it is not regulatory English at all, and choosing it means you did not recognise the form.

The subjunctive survives in English almost only where somebody is being told what to do. That is why a regulation is full of it and a newspaper is not -- and why recognising it is worth more marks than it looks.
