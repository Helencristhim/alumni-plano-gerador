We Used To Do It By Hand

Four new words for change — transition, legacy, restructure, outgrow — plus two you already have. And one structure only: used to, for what has ended. Around ten minutes.

Stage 1: Four new words, and two you have

Vocabulary

Four are new. The last two came from lessons 5 and 6 and are here because this lesson uses them again.

Transition — the period of moving from one way of working to another
"The transition to digital filing took us two years."
Legacy — old, still running, and expensive to replace
"Their legacy system still ran payroll in 2019."
Restructure — to change how the teams and the work are organised
"We restructured the firm around three service lines."
Outgrow — to become too big for something that used to fit
"We outgrew the old system long before we replaced it."
Audit trail — the record of who changed what, and when
"The new client wanted every report with an audit trail."
Regulatory — required by the law, not by the company
"The audit trail is regulatory, not a preference."

Stage 3: Used to, and nothing else

Grammar

One structure this week. used to = it happened often before, and it does not happen now. Negative: did not use to, with no d. Question: did you use to. For one single moment with a date, use the past simple instead: we replaced the system in 2018.

"We ______ do everything on paper." (a habit that has ended)
A used to
B use to
Which negative is correct?
A We did not used to work with foreign clients.
B We did not use to work with foreign clients.
Which question is correct?
A Did you use to handle payroll internally?
B Did you used to handle payroll internally?
One single moment, with a date. Which one?
A We used to replace the whole system in 2018.
B We replaced the whole system in 2018.
