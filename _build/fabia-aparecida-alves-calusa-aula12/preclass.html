Review -- Sorry, the Tenth?

No new words this week. The same four from lesson 11 — catch, confirm, note down, summarize — and the checks one more time: one fact, voice up. Around ten minutes.

Stage 1: The same four words

Review

Nothing new here. Read each one and say it out loud. If it comes back on its own, that word is yours.

Catch — to hear and understand something as it goes past
"Sorry, I didn't catch the last part."
Confirm — to check that what you understood is right
"Just to confirm: the tenth, not the twentieth."
Note down — to write something quickly, while it is being said
"Let me note down the reference number."
Summarize — to say the long thing again, but short
"Let me summarize what we agreed, in three lines."

Stage 3: One fact, voice up -- one more time

Grammar

No new rule this week. When you miss a number or a date, repeat only the fact, with your voice going up: The fifteenth? For a clear yes, add a tag that follows the person: Paula approves it, doesn't she? · Ralph signs it, doesn't he? And is that right? still works after anything.
