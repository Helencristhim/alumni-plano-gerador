Chapter 3: The Code

Two Facts, Both True

Concede one of them without losing the other

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

“Although the margin fell, the cash position improved every quarter.”

“The top line grew, whereas the margin did not.”

“We cut the overhead first. Having said that, the savings only landed in the second year.”

“It was a hard year. Even so, we closed three days earlier than in the previous one.”

Which of these are still true today, and what comes after each expression?

FormMeaningExample
Although / even though + a full sentenceAlthough the margin fell, the cash improved. (Two facts, both true, one of them uncomfortable.)I used to report to New York.
Whereas / while, to compare two thingsThe top line grew, whereas the margin did not.I am used to reporting in English.
Having said that / that said, as a bridgeWe fixed the cost base. Having said that, it took two quarters longer than I promised.I got used to being challenged.
Even so, to keep your position after concedingThe market was difficult. Even so, two competitors held their margin.

The -ing after be used to is the single most common advanced mistake in this family, and it survives for years because it never blocks understanding.

The Move

Concede, Contrast, Stop

1. Concede the fact

“Yes, the margin fell in those two years.”

2. Contrast it with what is also true

“Although the margin fell, the cash position improved every quarter.”

3. Stop there

“And that is the whole answer. No third sentence.”

Never blame the market, the parent company or the previous CFO. Every one of those may be true, and every one of them costs you the room.

Chapter 4: In the Room

Three at Once, No Pause

A group CEO who asks them together

Dialogue

Three Questions, One Order

S
Three things, and I will be direct. The margin fell, you left without another role, and you have never worked outside Brazil. Start wherever you like.
F
There are three parts to that. I will take them in your order.
S
Good.
F
Although the margin fell in those two years, the cash position improved every quarter. I own the first year of that decline: I moved late on the overhead.
S
And leaving without a role?
F
That is accurate. I left at the end of 2025, after the reporting line moved abroad and the mandate changed. Having said that, I would rather be honest about the timing than invent a better story.
S
Most people argue with me by now.
F
The third part is the one I would reframe. I have never lived outside Brazil, whereas I have reported to New York and to a French group for nine years.
S
That is a distinction I will accept. Why did you not say it first?
F
Because you asked in that order, and I did not want to choose the easy one first.
Comprehension

About Sandra

1. What are the three things Sandra raises?
The margin that fell, leaving without another role, and never having worked outside Brazil.
2. What does he say about the first year of the decline?
That he owns it, because he moved late on the overhead.
3. Which part does he reframe, and how?
The third one: he has never lived outside Brazil, but he has reported to New York and to a French group for nine years.
Listening

What Three in a Row Is Testing

A chief executive on why she asks them together. Sound first — no text.

1. What does she say she is watching?
The two seconds between the questions, not the answers themselves.
2. What are the three things candidates start doing after the first answer?
They get faster, they get louder, or they start answering before she has finished asking.
3. What does she say nobody loses a process for?
For a decline in a margin. They lose it for what the decline does to them in front of her.
Listening 2

Asked the Same Thing Three Times

A CFO on a panel that would not move on. Sound first — no text.

1. What did she think the panel believed?
That she was lying, because they asked about the same number three times.
2. What was the panel actually checking?
Whether her answer would change under pressure.
3. What did she offer the third time?
The same figure and reason again, plus the audited page that afternoon.
