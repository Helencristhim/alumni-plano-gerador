Chapter 3 -- The Story Code

Grammar Discovery

What was happening, and what happened

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I was waiting at the gate when they changed the gate."

"She was presenting the project when the fire alarm started."

"We were having lunch when my boss called."

"While I was reading, the train stopped."

What do the orange words have in common? Which action is long, and which is short?

long action: was / were + -ing · short action: past simple

The past continuous is the background -- an action in progress. The past simple is the short event that interrupts it. Use when before the short action and while before the long action.

The Story Code

Was or Were?

FormStructureExample
AffirmativeI / he / she / it + was + -ing
you / we / they + were + -ing		I was waiting.
They were talking.
Negativewasn't / weren't + -ingI wasn't listening.
QuestionWas / Were + subject + -ing?What were you doing?
The storylong action + when + past simpleI was driving when you called.

The short action uses the past simple: called, noticed, changed, realized.

Sound Check

"was" is small

I was waiting at the gate.

"was" is short and soft

We were having lunch.

"were" is short too

What were you doing?

the voice goes down at the end

Grammar Practice

Join the Actions

1. I / drive to work -- my boss / call
I was driving to work when my boss called.
2. We / have a meeting -- the lights / go off
We were having a meeting when the lights went off.
3. She / check in at the hotel -- she / notice the mistake
She was checking in at the hotel when she noticed the mistake.
4. While / I / wait for the taxi -- it / start to rain
While I was waiting for the taxi, it started to rain.

Dialogue

Tom's Story

A
Aline: Good morning, Tom! How was your trip to Chicago?
T
Tom: Good, but I have a funny anecdote for you. Something embarrassing happened at the hotel.
A
Aline: Really? What happened?
T
Tom: I was having breakfast in the lobby when my phone rang. It was a video call with the director.
A
Aline: Oh no! What were you wearing?
T
Tom: A suit jacket and my pajama pants! I was talking about the budget when suddenly I noticed the camera was on my legs.
A
Aline: No way! Did the director see it?
T
Tom: Luckily, no. The director was reading a report, so she didn't look at the screen.
A
Aline: That's so funny! I have a story like that, too. I'll tell you at lunch.
Did You Understand?

About Tom

1. What was Tom doing when his phone rang?
He was having breakfast in the lobby.
2. What did Tom notice during the call?
He noticed the camera was on his legs, and he was wearing pajama pants.
3. Why didn't the director see it?
Because she was reading a report.
Listening 1

Sarah's Presentation

1. What was Sarah doing when the fire alarm started?
She was presenting a new project to the directors. She was showing the first chart.
2. What was the weather like outside?
It was raining.
3. What did Sarah realize when they went back in?
The directors were reading her slides without her.
Listening 2

The Wrong Station

1. Where was Daniel traveling to?
He was traveling by train from Zurich to Basel.
2. What was he doing while the train was moving?
He was reading a book.
3. Where was he when he opened his eyes?
In a small town near the French border, not in Basel.
Artifact

Aline's Email

From: Aline Sberci · To: Basel Project Team

Subject: Sorry I was late this morning

Hi team,

Sorry I was late for the kickoff. I was waiting at the gate in São Paulo when I noticed that my phone was dead. While I was looking for a charger, the airline changed the gate. Luckily, a kind man told me. I got on the plane, but it left forty minutes late.

See you at 2 p.m.!
Best regards,
Aline

1. What was Aline doing when she noticed the problem?
She was waiting at the gate in São Paulo.
2. What happened while she was looking for a charger?
The airline changed the gate.
3. Was it bad luck or good luck? Why?
Both: the phone and the gate were bad luck, but luckily a kind man helped her.
