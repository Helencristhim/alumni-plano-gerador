Lesson 21 -- Telling Stories: Anecdotes

Today's topic: telling a short story with the past continuous + past simple (I was waiting at the gate when they changed it). Practice outside class -- mark each one as done when you finish.

YouTube
BBC Learning English -- "Past simple and past continuous" (6 Minute Grammar)

A short, clear lesson on exactly today's grammar: the long action (was/were + -ing) and the short action that interrupts it.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Pause after each example and say it out loud. Then write two sentences about your week with "when".
