Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Anecdote -- a short, funny or interesting story about something that happened to you
"I have a funny anecdote about my trip to Basel."
Happen -- to take place; to occur
"What happened at the airport?"
Suddenly -- quickly, and when you do not expect it
"I was walking to the hotel when suddenly it started to rain."
Notice -- to see or hear something for the first time
"I noticed that my phone was dead."
Realize -- to understand something you did not understand before
"I realized I was at the wrong gate."
Embarrassing -- making you feel a little ashamed or uncomfortable
"It was an embarrassing moment in the meeting."
Scared -- feeling afraid of something
"I was scared because the plane was shaking."
Luckily -- because of good luck; fortunately
"Luckily, a colleague found my phone."
Unfortunately -- because of bad luck; sadly
"Unfortunately, the train was late."
Meanwhile -- at the same time, while another thing is happening
"Meanwhile, my boss was waiting in the meeting room."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Here is Aline's favorite anecdote. Last spring, she was flying to Basel for a project meeting. She was waiting at the gate in São Paulo when she noticed that her phone was dead. While she was looking for a charger, the airline changed the gate. She didn't hear the announcement because she was talking to another passenger. Suddenly, she realized that the waiting area was empty. She was scared for a moment, but luckily, a kind man told her about the new gate. Meanwhile, her team in Basel was preparing the meeting room. Notice: the long action in progress uses was/were + -ing; the short action that interrupts it uses the past simple.

1. What was Aline doing when she noticed the problem with her phone?
A She was waiting at the gate.
B She was having a project meeting with her team in Basel.
C She was checking in at the hotel in Basel.
2. What happened while she was looking for a charger?
A The plane left São Paulo forty minutes before the time on her ticket.
B She lost her passport.
C The airline changed the gate.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A "Her team were prepare the meeting room in Basel."
B "She was waiting at the gate."
C "She waiting at the gate."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Past Continuous + Past Simple

GRAMMAR

Use these forms to tell a story: the background and the event.

UseFormExample
Background (long action)was / were + verb-ingI was waiting at the gate.
Event (short action)past simple...when they changed the gate.
Negativewasn't / weren't + verb-ingI wasn't listening.
QuestionWas / Were + subject + verb-ing?What were you doing?

Key point: I / he / she / it + was; you / we / they + were. Use when before the short action ("I was driving when you called") and while before the long action ("While I was driving, you called"). Don't forget the was: say "I was waiting", not "I waiting".
