Talk
Eduardo Briceño — “How to get better at the things you care about” (TED)

Eleven minutes on the difference between doing something and getting better at it — which is the whole subject of this lesson. He argues that people who plateau are usually people who spend all their time performing and none of it practising.

Tip: at the end, name one thing in your week that is performance and one that is practice. If the second list is empty, that is the finding.

Podcast
Freakonomics Radio — “How to Become Great at Just About Anything”

An hour with the researcher who spent his career studying how people actually get very good at things. The answer is not talent and it is not hours — it is what happens in the hours, which is the argument behind every exercise in this lesson.

Tip: it is fast, unscripted American English with several speakers. Listen once without stopping, then again for the part where he explains why most practice does nothing.

Video
Veritasium — “The Expert Myth”

Twenty minutes on why experience and expertise are not the same thing, and on the conditions a field has to meet before practice makes anybody better. It explains, with evidence, why feedback matters more than repetition.

Tip: write down the conditions he lists, then decide which ones your English lessons already meet and which one you would have to add yourself.
