Lesson 13

What Is in the Room

What there is, how much there is, and what is missing

Warm-up

From the Week to the Room

First, one habit from your week, and how often you really do it. Then: a patient opens the door of your practice. What is the first thing they see?

The Target

Three Layers of a Room

1. The big picture. Is it small, big, bright, quiet?

2. What is in it, and how much: two chairs, a lot of light, a few books.

3. What is missing. The towel, the space, the coffee.

At home, the third layer is a detail. In a hotel, it is the whole phone call.

Diagnostic

A Question on the Phone

A new American patient calls before her first visit and asks: “What is the practice like? Is there a place where my son can wait for me?”

Answer her in three sentences.

Chapter 3: The Code

One Thing, or Many?

How English says what a room has, and how much

Grammar Discovery

Is, Are, Much, Many

There is a small sofa in the waiting room.

There are four chairs by the window.

There aren't many magazines in English.

There isn't much space in the consulting room.

When do we say there is, and when there are? Why many magazines, but much space?

JobFormExample
One thing, or a thing you cannot countthere is / there isn'tThere is a sofa. There isn't any coffee.
Two or more thingsthere are / there aren'tThere are four chairs.
Questionsis there...? / are there...? + anyIs there any hot water? Are there any towels?
Things you countmany · a few · a couple ofThere aren't many magazines. There are a few books.
Things you don't countmuch · a littleThere isn't much space. There is a little coffee.
Both kindssome · any · a lot of · plenty of · noThere is a lot of light. There are no towels.
The trapnever have to describe a room, never there is with two thingsNot in my office have a window. Say there is a window in my office.

In one line: there is for one thing, there are for many, many for things you count, much for things you don't.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

A Visitor From Miami

One visit, two recordings, one shelf of supplies

Dialogue

Showing Mark Around

V
Mark, welcome! Come in. This is the waiting room. It's small, but it's cozy.
M
Thanks for showing me around before my flight back to Miami. It's lovely, and it's spotless. How many patients come here in a day?
V
About twelve. There are only four chairs, so it gets a little cramped at four o'clock. But there's plenty of coffee, and there are a couple of magazines in English.
M
In English? Are there many foreign patients here?
V
Not many, but there are a few, and there are more every year. Come and see my consulting room. There's a big window, so there's a lot of light.
M
Very nice. Is there any space for a second chair?
V
Not really. There isn't much space at all. My supplies are in these drawers, and there are some boxes on the shelf over there.
M
Then you're just like me. At my practice there are three dentists and only two rooms, and we always run out of gloves on a Friday.
Comprehension

About Mark

1. When is Mark visiting the practice?
Before his flight back to Miami.
2. What does Mark ask about the consulting room?
He asks if there is any space for a second chair.
3. What is Mark's practice like, and what happens there on Fridays?
There are three dentists and only two rooms, and they always run out of gloves on a Friday.
Listening 1 · An Apartment for Two

A Trip With Mom

What the apartment has, and what it doesn't. Sound first — no text.

1. Why are two bedrooms perfect for her and her mother?
Because her mother goes to bed at nine, and she doesn't.
2. What is in the kitchen, what isn't, and what does that mean for the trip?
There is a coffee machine, but there isn't a dishwasher, so they are going to eat out a lot.
3. What are two problems with the apartment, and what is she doing about the towels?
There is only one bathroom, with not much space, and there is no elevator. There aren't many towels, so she is packing a few of her own.
Listening 2 · A Hotel Welcome

Your Room, in One Minute

What is in the room, what is free, and what is missing. Sound first — no text.

1. What is free in the room, and what isn't?
The two bottles of water on the desk are free. The drinks in the minibar are not.
2. There is no hair dryer and no coffee machine. How can you get each one?
For a hair dryer, you call the front desk and they bring one up. For coffee, there is free coffee in the lobby from six to ten every morning.
3. Where can you park, and how much is it?
In the building next door, for twenty dollars a day. There aren't any parking spaces at the hotel.
