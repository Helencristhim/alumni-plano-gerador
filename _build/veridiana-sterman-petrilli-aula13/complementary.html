Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Tidying Up with Marie Kondo — Season 1, Episode 1 "Tidying with Toddlers" (Netflix)

A young family in Los Angeles goes room by room: what there is in every closet, every drawer and every shelf, and what they really need. Light, kind and full of the words of this lesson.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Every time somebody says there is or there are, pause and repeat the sentence.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "Make your small space feel bigger"

Practical ideas for a room that feels cramped: what to keep, where to put it, and how to make a small space cozy. A good match for your waiting room.

Tip: the page has the full transcript. Find five sentences with there is or there are, and say each one about a room in your home.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Graham Hill — "Less stuff, more happiness" (TED)

Six minutes about living in a small apartment with fewer things, and why there is more space in your life when there is less stuff. Clear and easy to follow at B1.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Afterwards, describe one room in your house with many, much, a few and a little.
