Real Document

The Recipe Card

LINDA’S DATIL PEPPER SAUCE
Makes 6 small jars · Wear gloves!
Step 1Wash the peppers. Remove the stems.
Step 2Chop with 1 onion + 2 cloves of garlic.
Step 3Add tomatoes, vinegar, a little sugar.
Step 4Boil 20 minutes. Stir often.
Step 5Blend until smooth.
Step 6Pour into jars. Write the date.
Keep the jars in the fridge. Best in the first three months.
1. Say step 1 and step 2, with First and Then.
First, the peppers are washed and the stems are removed. Then, they are chopped with an onion and garlic.
2. What happens while the sauce is boiling?
It is stirred often, so it does not burn.
3. Say the last step, with Finally.
Finally, the sauce is poured into jars, and the date is written on every jar.
Chapter 4: The Code

Grammar Discovery

What Is the Job of These Words?

"First, the peppers are washed."

"Then, they are chopped with an onion."

"After that, everything goes into a big pot."

"Finally, the sauce is poured into small jars."

What do the colored words tell you? And what comes right after them?

JobWordsExample
The first stepFirst, / First of all,First, wash the peppers.
The next stepThen, / Next,Then, chop them.
The step after the one you saidAfter that,After that, boil the sauce.
Two things at the same timeWhile + subject + verbWhile it is cooking, stir it.
Something you do earlierBefore + -ing / Before you + verbBefore tasting it, wait.
The last stepFinally,Finally, pour it into jars.
The trapnever After, alone at the start — say After that,

Put a comma after First, Then, Next, After that and Finally. They tell the listener where you are in the process.

Grammar Practice

Which Linker?

Say it first, then click to check

"___First, the coffee beans are ground." (the first step)
"___Then, hot water is poured over the coffee." (the next step)
"___While the coffee is dripping, the cups are warmed." (same time)
"___After that, a little milk is added." (the step after that one)
"___Before drinking it, wait one minute." (earlier)
"___Finally, the coffee is served." (the last step)
Detective

Spot the Error

0 / 4 errors found

"Before cooking, wash the peppers." — before + -ing
"First, the peppers washed."
"First, the peppers are washed." — the passive needs be
