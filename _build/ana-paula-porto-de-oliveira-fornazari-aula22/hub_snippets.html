Explaining a Process -- Step by Step

A process explained in the right order. Key words: a recipe, an ingredient, a step, to chop, to boil, to stir, to blend, to pour, a jar, spicy. Structure: first, then, after that, finally, while and before.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A recipe -- a list of things and steps to cook a dish
"My grandmother's recipe is in an old notebook."
An ingredient -- one of the foods you put in a dish
"Cheese is the main ingredient."
A step -- one part of a process, done in order
"Do not forget the last step."
To chop -- to cut food into small pieces
"Chop the onion before you start."
To boil -- to cook in very hot water or liquid
"Boil the eggs for ten minutes."
To stir -- to move food round and round with a spoon
"Stir the soup so it does not burn."
To blend -- to mix food in a machine until it is smooth
"I blend fruit for breakfast every day."
To pour -- to make a liquid go from one thing into another
"Pour the coffee slowly."
A jar -- a small glass container with a lid
"The honey is in a glass jar."
Spicy -- with a strong, hot taste
"This sauce is too spicy for me."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Brazil grows more coffee than any other country, and the process takes many months. First, the coffee cherries are picked by hand or by machine.

Then, the seeds are taken out of the cherries and dried in the sun. While they are drying, they are turned many times every day. After that, the dry beans are put in big bags and sent to the port.

Before arriving at your kitchen, the beans are roasted, so they become brown. Finally, they are ground, and the coffee is ready for your cup.

1. What is the first step?
A The beans are roasted.
B The beans are sent to the port.
C The coffee cherries are picked.
2. What happens while the seeds are drying?
A They are put in big bags.
B They are turned many times every day.
C They are ground.
3. What is the last step?
A The beans are ground.
B The cherries are picked.
C The seeds are dried.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Words for a Process

GRAMMAR

These words tell the listener where you are in a process. Put a comma after them.

JobWordsExample
First stepFirst, / First of all,First, wash the rice.
Next stepThen, / Next,Then, add the water.
The step afterAfter that,After that, cook it for twenty minutes.
Same timeWhile + subject + verbWhile it is cooking, chop the onion.
EarlierBefore + -ingBefore serving it, add salt.
Last stepFinally,Finally, serve it hot.

Never: After, add the water · Before to serve · Finally in the middle of the process

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer a real speaker would give.

You explain the first step of a recipe. You say:
A "Finally, wash the rice."
B "First, wash the rice."
C "After, wash the rice."
You explain the step after "add the water". You say:
A "After, cook it for twenty minutes."
B "Before that, cook it for twenty minutes."
C "After that, cook it for twenty minutes."
You do two things at the same time. You say:
A "While the rice is cooking, I chop the onion."
