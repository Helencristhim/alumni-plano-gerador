Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Article
Florida’s Historic Coast — "The Datil Pepper: A History of Heat and Flavor in St. Augustine"

The official tourism website of St. Augustine tells the story of the datil pepper: where it may have come from, and how it is used in the city today.

Tip: read it, then explain to a friend how datil sauce is made, with First, Then and Finally.

TV segment
News4JAX River City Live — "The History of the Datil Pepper"

A short local TV segment from Jacksonville: farmers and sauce makers talk about the datil pepper. Real American speed, real local accents.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, write down every step of a process you hear.

Watch on News4JAX ↗
Food article
Tasting Table — "What Are Datil Peppers And Why Are They Special To St. Augustine, FL?"

A food magazine explains what makes the datil different from other hot peppers, and how people cook with it.

Tip: find three kitchen words from this lesson in the article.
