Chapter 3: The Code

The Scale of Certainty

may, might, could, probably, it seems that

Grammar Discovery

Strong or Careful?

The drug reduces pain. → The drug may reduce pain in some patients.
The dose is the cause. → It could be a question of dosage.
It works for older patients. → It probably works for older patients too.
The effect is related to age. → It seems that the effect is related to age.
Click to reveal the rule
Hedging shows how sure you are. may / might / could + base verb = possible, not sure (may reduce, never may reduces). probably = more sure, about 80%: it goes before the main verb (probably works) or after is (it is probably true). It seems that / Our data suggest that + a full sentence = you show the evidence, not your feeling. For the opinion itself: I'm not 100% sure, but I think... One hedge per sentence is enough: maybe it might possibly sounds like you have no opinion at all.

Chapter 5: Practice

Careful Answers, Polite Doubts

Two voices from a Q&A session

Listening 1

An Honest Answer

Read the questions, then listen.

1. Why does she say the result is preliminary?
Because they had only forty patients, so the effect may be smaller in a bigger group.
2. Which patients seemed to respond better?
The patients with recent symptoms.
3. What do they need before they can make a strong claim?
A second study.
Listening 2

I See Your Point, But...

Read the questions, then listen.

1. What does he think the improvement might be related to, and why?
The placebo effect, because the patients knew they were getting a new drug.
2. What is his second possible explanation?
Dosage: the dose was high, and patients generally cannot take that dose for a long time.
3. What next step does he suggest?
A trial where the patients and the doctors do not know who gets the real drug.
Quick Fire

How Sure Are You?

Answer out loud first, then click to compare

"Does your drug work for every patient?"Probably not for every patient. It seems that it works better with recent symptoms.
"Is the pain caused by the dose?"It could be a question of dosage, but we are not sure yet.
"So your study proves it?"Not yet. It's a preliminary finding. Our data suggest an effect, but we need a bigger sample.
"Will this work in Thailand too?"It's likely, but it's still uncertain. Leprosy patients there may arrive at a different stage.
"I think your result is only placebo."I see your point. It might be part of it, but the effect lasted eight weeks.
"What do you think about the new guidelines?"I'm not 100% sure, but I think they are a step in the right direction.
