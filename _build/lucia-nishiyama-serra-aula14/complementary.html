Present continuous and going to -- 6 Minute Grammar

The other half of tonight's grammar: when the present continuous marks a fixed arrangement with a time and other people, and when going to reports a decision you had already made. Short, spoken, and full of examples you can copy straight into a status update.

Tip: after watching, open your own calendar and say five real arrangements out loud: "I am meeting... on Thursday."
