Lesson 27 -- Pre-class

Handling Pushback

Answer objections from a skeptical partner: acknowledge the concern, reframe the idea, and keep your position politely. Key words: objection, concern, clarify, reframe, acknowledge, maintain, skeptical, doubt, reassure, track record.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Objection -- a reason that someone gives to say they do not agree
"His main objection was the price."
Concern -- a feeling that something could be a problem
"I understand your concern about the timeline."
To clarify -- to make something easier to understand
"Let me clarify one point about the price."
To reframe -- to show the same idea from a different point of view
"She reframed the problem as an opportunity."
To acknowledge -- to say that you hear and accept what someone feels or says
"First, acknowledge the concern. Do not argue."
To maintain -- to continue to say that something is true
"I still maintain that the pilot is a good idea."
Skeptical -- not easy to convince; with doubts
"The partner was skeptical about the timeline."
Doubt -- a feeling that something may not be true or may not work
"He has doubts about the quality standards."
To reassure -- to say something that helps a person feel less worried
"The new figures reassured our partners."
Track record -- the good or bad results that a person or group had in the past
"Sebrae-SP has a strong track record with small businesses."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Flavia has a meeting with Lorenzo, from a trade association in Verona. Lorenzo is skeptical. He says: "Your certification process is too slow." Flavia does not argue. First, she acknowledges his concern: "I can see why you might think that, but let me put it a different way: what we are building is a framework that protects producers for many years." Then Lorenzo asks a hard question about the budget. Flavia says: "That is an important question. Let me think." At the end, Lorenzo still has doubts, but Flavia keeps her position: "With respect, I would maintain that this pilot is a win-win."

1. What does Flavia do first when Lorenzo pushes back?
A She acknowledges his concern.
B She gives him a long list of numbers about the program and its thirty-three offices.
C She changes the subject.
2. What does Flavia say when she needs time?
A "I do not know, sorry, maybe we can talk about this question at another meeting."
B "That is an important question. Let me think."
C "Wait, wait."
3. Which sentence is correct?
A "I can see why you might thinking that, but the pilot is a good idea for everyone."
B "Let me to put it a different way."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Responding to Objections

GRAMMAR

Four strategies to answer an objection calmly.

StrategyPhraseExample
AcknowledgeI understand your concern. / That is a fair point. What I would add is that...That is a fair point. What I would add is that our producers export to Europe.
ReframeLet me put it a different way. / What I am suggesting is not X, but Y.What I am suggesting is not a fast process, but a strong one.
Hold your positionI hear your point, and I still believe that... / With respect, I would maintain that...With respect, I would maintain that this is a win-win.
Buy timeGive me a moment to consider that. / That is an important question. Let me think.Give me a moment to consider that.
SoftenI can see why you might think that, but...I can see why you might think that, but...
RuleAfter might and let me, use the base verb: "you might think", "let me put it" (not "might thinking", not "let me to put"). After would maintain and would add, use that + a full sentence. First acknowledge, then reframe or hold your position.
