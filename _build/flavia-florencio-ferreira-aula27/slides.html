Chapter 3: The Response Code

Acknowledge, Reframe, Hold

I understand your concern · Let me put it a different way · With respect...

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I understand your concern, and I think it is worth clarifying the timeline."

"I can see why you might think that, but let me put it a different way."

"With respect, I would maintain that this pilot is a win-win."

"That is an important question. Let me think."

What does each orange phrase do? Which one accepts the feeling, which one shows the idea in a new way, which one keeps your position, and which one gives you time?

StrategyPhraseGrammar inside
AcknowledgeI understand your concern. / That is a fair point. What I would add is...I would add + that + sentence
ReframeLet me put it a different way. / What I am suggesting is not X, but Y.not + noun, but + noun
Hold your positionI hear your point, and I still believe that... / With respect, I would maintain that...would maintain + that + sentence
Buy timeGive me a moment to consider that. / That is an important question. Let me think.let me + base verb
Soften the objectionI can see why you might think that, but...might + base verb

Use one strategy at a time: first acknowledge, then reframe or hold. Do not argue right away. After might and let me, use the base verb: "you might think", "let me put it".

Chapter 4: The Skeptical Partner

Stand Your Ground

Lorenzo pushes back; Flavia stays calm

Dialogue

The Hard Meeting

L
Flavia, I have to be honest. I am skeptical about this program.
F
I understand your concern, and I think it is worth clarifying a few points.
L
In our experience, programs like this rarely deliver results.
F
That is a fair point. What I would add is that we have a strong track record with nearly five hundred certified producers.
L
But your certification process is too slow for our market.
F
I can see why you might think that, but let me put it a different way: what we are building is a framework that protects producers for many years.
L
I still have doubts about the timeline. When will we see results?
F
That is an important question. Let me think. If we start the pilot in July, you will see the first results in December.
L
December. That would reassure our members.
F
With respect, I would maintain that this is a win-win. Shall we look at the timeline together?
