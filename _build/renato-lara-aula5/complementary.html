Bondi Rescue — "Bondi's Most Dangerous Days"

One hour of real rescues on an Australian beach. After each one the lifeguards tell the story of what happened, in the past — and half of those stories start with a rip.

Tip: after each rescue, pause and tell the story back in three sentences, all in the past.

BBC Real Easy English — "Talking about yourself"

Short, slow and made for your level. People tell small pieces of their own story, so the past simple is everywhere. Free, with the full transcript on the page.

Tip: write down every irregular past you hear. Those are the ones you have to learn one by one.
