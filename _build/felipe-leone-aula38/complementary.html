Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Hans Rosling: “The best stats you’ve ever seen” (20 min)

A doctor and statistician turns numbers into a story everybody wants to follow. A master class in presenting data.

Tip: write down three sentences he says with there was, there were or a present perfect, and say them out loud.

TED Talk
David McCandless: “The beauty of data visualization” (18 min)

How to show numbers so that people see the story in seconds, not in minutes.

Tip: choose one idea and use it when you present your team’s numbers in class.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Nancy Duarte: “The secret structure of great talks” (18 min)

A presentation expert on the shape that the best talks share: what is, and what could be.

Tip: after the talk, plan your results presentation in three parts: what there was, what has changed, what comes next.
