Lesson 38 · Write & Speak

Presenting Results

Your team’s numbers in a few clear sentences: what there was last quarter, and what has changed since.

Retomada

Your Text, Five Marks

Say each sentence in the right form, then make one more sentence of yours with it.

Model

A Quarter in Numbers

Last quarter was a good quarter for our team. There was a 15% increase in new clients, and there were 40 new contracts in Brazil and Mexico.

There was one problem: there were too many late reports in February. We changed the process in March.

Since then, the numbers have improved. Late reports have fallen by half, and sales have grown since January.

Next quarter, we want to keep this rhythm.
