Talk
Laura Vanderkam — How to gain control of your free time (TED)

Twelve minutes on what people really mean when they say there is no time, and on the sentence that changes a negotiation about a deadline: not I do not have time, but it is not a priority. Direct, slow and very quotable.

Tip: after watching, take one thing you are late on and say it out loud in her words. Then say it again as a first conditional, with what happens if it moves.

Video
Randy Pausch — Lecture on Time Management

Worth a second visit, this time only for the part about renegotiating a commitment. He is blunt about the difference between telling somebody early and telling them at the last minute, which is exactly today's listening.

Tip: skip to the section on deadlines and write down every conditional sentence you hear. Then rewrite two of them about your own close.

Podcast
The Indicator from Planet Money — Could politics put your city's funding at risk?

Nine minutes built almost entirely on conditions: if this happens, then that follows. It is the grammar of this lesson used by native speakers at full speed, about money.

Tip: count the conditional sentences. Then notice which half carries the future word — it will never be the if half.
