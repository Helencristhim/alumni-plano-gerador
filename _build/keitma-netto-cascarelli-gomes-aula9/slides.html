Chapter 3: If You Send It, I Can Close It

The Sentence That Gets an Answer

Not about the past, and not about the person

Grammar Discovery

Where Does the Future Go?

"If you send me the file today, I can still close the account this month."

"If the tax file does not arrive by Tuesday, I will have to push the review back."

"Send me the reconciliation by Wednesday." → "Could you send me the reconciliation by Wednesday?"

"Would you mind sending it before the call, so that I can check one number?"

The first two sentences are about the future. Which half carries the blue future word, and what tense is the green half in? And what do the orange openings do to the same request?

ShapeWhat it doesExample
if + present, will / cana real future condition — the present is in the if halfIf you send it today, I can still close it.
if + negative presentthe consequence of nothing arrivingIf it does not arrive by Tuesday, I will have to push it back.
unlessthe same as if not, in one wordUnless it arrives by Tuesday, I will push it back.
could you + bare verba request, not an instructionCould you send me the reconciliation?
would you mind + -ingsofter still — and note the form after itWould you mind sending it before the call?
so thatthe reason that makes the request easy to say yes to... so that I can check one number before the call.
the trapno will after if, and no to after would you mindNot if you will send. Not would you mind to send.

In one line: put the present in the if half and the future in the other one — and always attach the reason, because the reason is what gets the answer.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

Day Two of the Close

One call, two voice notes and one message thread

Dialogue

One File, Two Days Late

M
I saw your note. Are we still closing on day five?
K
Not without one file. The tax file is the only dependency left, and I have been chasing it since Monday.
M
What happens if it does not arrive tomorrow?
K
If it does not arrive by Tuesday, I will have to push the review back to Wednesday morning. If it arrives today, we close on time.
M
Is there anything I can do from here?
K
Yes. Could you ask the tax team directly? A message from you moves faster than three from me. And if the BPO takes the postings, that frees up half a day on my side.
M
Done. So what can you commit to?
K
I can commit to Wednesday morning, not to Tuesday. I will keep you posted through the afternoon. And you — do you need the variance note at the same time, or can that follow?
Comprehension

About Mark

1. What does Mark want to know at the beginning of the call?
Whether they are still closing on day five.
2. What does he offer, and how does she use the offer?
He asks whether there is anything he can do from his side, and she asks him to contact the tax team directly, because a message from him moves faster than three from her.
3. What is the last thing he asks her for?
What she can commit to.
Listening 1 · A Voice Note

The Sentence That Gets an Answer

A controller on how to chase somebody and actually get the file. Sound first — no text.

1. What two sentences does she compare, and what is the difference between them?
“You have not sent me the file yet” is about the past and about the person; “if you send me the file today, I can still close the account this month” is about the future and about the work.
2. Which one does she say gets an answer, and how fast?
The second one, usually within the hour.
3. What does she say she is really talking about, and what is she not talking about?
She is not talking about being nice — she is talking about giving somebody a reason stronger than their own to-do list.
Listening 2 · A Manager

Ask Early, and Without Apologies

A finance manager on the hardest sentence in his team.

1. What does he say is the hardest sentence for most people on his team?
Not asking for something — asking for more time.
2. What is the difference between telling him on day two and telling him on day five?
On day two it is planning and he can move something; on day five at six in the evening it is a problem, and now it is his problem too.
3. What three things does he want in the sentence, and what does he say the answer usually is?
Tell him early, tell him exactly what you need, and tell him what he gets in exchange. He says the answer is almost always yes.
Real Document

The Message Thread

TEAMS · CLOSE — MARCH
keitma gomes · bpo team · tax team
Mon 09:12 — Keitma: Morning. If you send me the tax file today, I can still close the account this month. Would you mind confirming whether that is possible?
Mon 14:40 — Tax team: It is with our reviewer. Probably tomorrow.
Tue 08:05 — Keitma: Thanks. Unless it arrives before midday, I will have to push the review back to Wednesday morning, so I am flagging it now rather than on day five.
Tue 08:31 — Tax team: Understood. You will have it by eleven.
Note: the first message took nineteen minutes to write and five hours to answer. The second took forty seconds and was answered in twenty-six minutes.
1. What does Keitma offer in the first message, in exchange for the file arriving today?
That she can still close the account this month.
2. What does she say on Tuesday, and why does she say she is saying it now?
That unless the file arrives before midday she will have to push the review back to Wednesday morning — and she is flagging it now rather than on day five.
3. According to the note, which message was faster to write and faster to answer?
The second one: forty seconds to write, and answered in twenty-six minutes.

Chapter 6: Your Turn

One Deadline, Three Times

Guided, half free, and then on your own

Role-Play 1 of 3 — Guided

Chasing the File

Scenario: It is day two and the file you need has not arrived. Ask for it, attach the reason, say what happens if it does not come, and offer one thing that would make it easier. Finish with a question back.

Keyword chips:

if you send it today, I can unless it arrives by would you mind so that
Role-Play 2 of 3 — Semi-free

Asking for One More Day

Scenario: You need until Wednesday morning and it is only day two. Ask your manager in one sentence: what you need, by when, and what he gets in exchange. No apologies, and hold the date when he pushes.

Keyword chips:

could you give me until and I will send you I can commit to I will keep you posted
Role-Play 3 of 3 — Free

Ninety Seconds, No Notes

Scenario: Take a real deadline from your own last close that was at risk. Say what the dependency was, what you asked for and from whom, what would have happened if it had not arrived, and what you committed to. Finish with a question back.

No keywords, no notes, ninety seconds.
