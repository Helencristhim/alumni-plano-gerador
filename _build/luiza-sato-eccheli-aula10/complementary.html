Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
Magoosh TOEFL — “How to Write a Perfect TOEFL Email in 7 Minutes (2026)” (YouTube)

A step-by-step guide to the new Write an Email task: how to plan in one minute, organize the email, and keep time to check it.

Tip: pause before the sample answer and make your own one-minute outline for the same prompt.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Series
Ted Lasso — Season 1, Episode 1, “Pilot”

An American coach moves to London with no plan and a lot of optimism. Funny, warm, and a good preview of American and British English side by side, in the city you may study in.

Tip: after the episode, say what Ted will have learned by the end of the season, and what he will be doing in his first month.

See the episode on TVmaze ↗
Talk
Tim Urban — “Inside the mind of a master procrastinator” (TED)

A very funny talk about deadlines, and about the goals that have no deadline at all. Perfect for a plan that lasts two years.

Tip: summarize his idea of the Panic Monster in three blocks, and say how a timeline with milestones solves it.
