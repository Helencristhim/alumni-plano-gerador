The Code

Why many here and much there?

How many episodes did you watch?
How much time do you spend on your phone?
I have too many messages to answer.
I have too much homework this week.

Can you count it, one by one?

One episode, two episodes → countable → many. Time, homework, advice: you cannot say "two homeworks" → uncountable → much.

The Code

Two columns, three safe words

Countable (1, 2, 3...)

episodes · friends · videos · messages
How many episodes?
a few friends · too many messages

Uncountable (no 1, 2, 3)

time · homework · advice · money · stuff
How much time?
a little money · too much homework

These three work for BOTH sides — use them when you are not sure

a lot of · plenty of · some / any

too much and too many are not neutral: they mean it is a problem.

The Code

One block, not three words

a lotof time

plentyof stuff

a coupleof hours

Say it fast, as one piece. The of is barely there.

Did You Catch It?

The numbers

1. How much screen time does Bia have a day?
Seven hours — forty percent more than before.
2. How many group chats does she answer?
Eleven.
3. What advice does Sam give her, and does he answer her question?
To leave three chats. And no — he avoids saying his own screen time.
Listening 1

Too much of a good thing

1. How many episodes did he watch, and in how long?
Nine episodes, in one weekend.
2. How much homework did he do?
Hardly any — almost none.
3. What is his rule for next weekend?
Two episodes a day, and homework first.
Listening 2

The homework announcement

1. How many students complained about the amount of homework?
More than a hundred.
2. What is the new limit?
One hour a night, and nothing at the weekend.
3. What does the school ask students to do?
Tell a teacher if they still have too much — and be honest about it.
