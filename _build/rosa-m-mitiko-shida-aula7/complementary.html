Talk
"Want to help someone? Shut up and listen!" -- Ernesto Sirolli, TED

Seventeen minutes on why advice given before listening almost always fails. Connection to Lesson 7: should is the grammar of advice, and this talk is about when it works and when it does not — which matters when the person in front of you already knows they made the mistake.

Tip: watch it once with English subtitles. Then think of the last time a client asked you what he should do, and say your answer again out loud, this time after two questions instead of none.

Grammar Video
"'Have to' and 'must'" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the two words of obligation, including the negative forms that are opposites. Connection to Lesson 7: this is the difference between you must not promise it and you do not have to promise it, which is the most expensive mistake in this whole lesson.

Tip: pause at every negative example and say the other version out loud, then say what each one would mean to a client. Hearing the two meanings side by side is what fixes the pair for good.
