Mr. English Channel — "Learn How to Talk About Yourself in English"

Nineteen minutes of one person doing exactly what you did in class today: name, country, studies, job, normal day, free time. The speech is slow and every sentence is short, so you can hear where each part of the answer begins and ends.

Tip: listen once for the whole thing. The second time, stop after each section and say your own version out loud before you press play again.

Simple English Podcast — "Talking about Daily Routine"

Part three of your ten minutes, in audio only: a normal day from the alarm to the evening. It is full of the two things you use there without thinking — always, usually, sometimes, never, and the prepositions at, on and in.

Tip: good for the commute, when you cannot read or write but you can listen. Count how many times you hear "usually" and "at" in the first five minutes.

YouTube
Simply Street English — "Speaking about your weekend in English"

Two minutes of real people on the street answering the last question of your ten minutes: what did you do last weekend? Nobody prepared anything, the answers are short, and every one of them is in the past simple.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause after each question and answer it yourself before the person on the screen does.
