Chapter 4: The Code

Can and Can’t

What is okay and what is not okay

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"You can ask questions."

"You cannot take photos."

"Can I drive the car?"

"He can understand every step."

Look at sentence 4: He can understand. In lesson 4 we said he works, with -s. Is there an -s here? And is there do in the question?

JobFormExample
Permission (it is okay)can + verbYou can ask questions.
No permissioncannot (can’t) + verbYou can’t take photos.
Abilitycan + verbMy son can speak English.
QuestionCan + I / you / he + verb?Can I drive the car?
Every person, same formno -s, no do, no toHe can drive. (Not he cans, not can to drive.)
Short answersYes, you can. / No, you can’t.Can I touch it? No, you can’t.

In one line: can + verb for every person: no -s, no do, no to.

Listening 2 · The Test Track

Can I Drive?

An instructor talks to a visitor at the test track. Sound first — no text.

1. What does the visitor need to drive?
His driver's license.
2. Can the visitor drive alone?
No. The instructor is in the car with him.
3. How many laps can he drive?
Three laps.
