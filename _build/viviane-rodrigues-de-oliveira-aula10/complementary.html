Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 10 -- What Should HR Do Now?

Series
The Office (U.S.) -- Season 3, Episode 1 (Gay Witch Hunt)

A whole episode about a company giving people advice they did not ask for, and a policy nobody read. Painful and funny, and full of should, must and have to.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down five sentences with should, must or have to.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
WorkLife with Adam Grant -- any episode

Every episode ends with advice based on research, which is exactly what you practised today: not an opinion, a recommendation with a reason behind it.

Tip: listen at 0.75x the first time. Write down the recommendation he gives at the end, in one sentence.

Listen on Spotify ↗
YouTube
Simon Sinek -- "How great leaders inspire action" (TED)

Eighteen minutes on why some recommendations move people and others do not. It closes this block the way it started: with the difference between describing what you do and explaining why it matters.

Tip: watch once without subtitles, then once with English subtitles. Compare how much you catch now with how it felt in lesson 1.

Watch on YouTube ↗
