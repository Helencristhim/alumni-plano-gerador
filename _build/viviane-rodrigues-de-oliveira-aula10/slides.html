Chapter 3: The Code

How Hard Do You Push?

Four verbs, four levels of pressure

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"We could start with one region and see what happens."

"We should start with the north region."

"I would recommend starting with the north region."

"We must answer the compliance audit before Friday."

All four give advice, but they do not push with the same force. Put them in order, from the softest to the strongest -- and look at what comes after each verb.

FormUseExample
could + base formThe softest: one option among others.We could start with one region.
should + base formYour professional advice. The default in a recommendation.We should start with the north. (never should to start)
I would recommend + verb-ingThe most diplomatic, and the safest with a board.I would recommend starting with the north.
must / have to + base formObligation, not advice: law, audit, deadline.We must answer the audit before Friday.
negativeshould not for advice, must not for a prohibition.We should not promise a date yet. We must not share the numbers.

The Structure

A Recommendation in Three Sentences

A board can say yes or no to this. It cannot say yes or no to a report.

1 · What you recommend

"I would recommend starting with the north region, with two trainers from May."

2 · Why

"It has the lowest retention in the group, and the root cause is the first month."

3 · The risk of waiting

"If we wait until the next quarter, we should expect to lose two or three more store managers."

Recommend → why → the cost of waiting. The third sentence is what turns a good idea into a decision today.

So What Do You Recommend?

L
Viviane, I read the memo. I do not want the analysis again. What do you recommend?
V
I would recommend starting with the north region, with two trainers from May.
L
Why not all four regions? We have the budget.
V
Because we do not have the trainers. A rollout without owners looks good in a slide and dies in June.
L
And if we wait until September, what happens?
V
Sorry, could you say that again, please?
L
If we wait until September. What is the risk?
V
We should expect to lose two or three more store managers. But if you prefer September, we could still do the onboarding quick win now — it costs nothing.
L
Approved. Two trainers from May, and put the quick win in the action plan with your name on it.
Comprehension

About Luca

1. What does Luca say he does not want?
The analysis again — he wants the recommendation.
2. Why does he think all four regions are possible?
Because they have the budget.
3. What does he approve at the end?
Two trainers from May, and the quick win in the action plan with her name as the owner.
