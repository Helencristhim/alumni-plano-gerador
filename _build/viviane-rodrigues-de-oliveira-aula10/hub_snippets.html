Lesson 10 -- Pre-class

What Should HR Do Now? -- Modal Verbs for Recommendations

Turning data into a recommendation the board can act on: what to do, why, and the risk of waiting. Key words: recommendation, action plan, owner, risk, guideline, policy, escalation, compliance, quick win, roadmap. Structures: should and should not, could for a softer option, I would recommend + -ing, and must for real obligations.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the definition. These are the words of the moment somebody has to decide.

Recommendation -- what you advise someone to do, after looking at the facts
"My recommendation is to start with the north region."
Action plan -- the list of steps, each one with a person and a date
"The action plan has four steps and two owners."
Owner -- the person responsible for one specific action
"Every action needs an owner, or nothing happens."
Risk -- something that can go wrong if you act, or if you wait
"The risk of waiting is losing two more store managers."
Guideline -- advice that tells people how to act, but is not obligatory
"We should publish a guideline, not a new rule."
Policy -- an official company rule that everybody has to follow
"The overtime policy has not changed since 2019."
Escalation -- taking a problem to a higher level when it is not solved
"After two weeks with no answer, escalation is the right move."
Compliance -- following the rules and the law
"Compliance is not optional, even when it is slow."
Quick win -- a small result you can get fast, while the big work continues
"A quick win would be one extra day of onboarding."
Roadmap -- the plan of what comes when, over the next months
"The roadmap covers the next three quarters."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Viviane has one recommendation for the board, not six. She thinks the company should start with the north region, because it has the lowest retention and the root cause is the first thirty days. She would recommend putting two trainers there from May. The company could wait until September, but then it should expect to lose two or three more store managers. There is also one thing that is not advice: the company must review the overtime policy, because compliance flagged it last month, and that one is not optional. And there is one thing the board should not do: launch all four regions at the same time. Without enough trainers and without an owner for each action, the action plan looks good in a slide and dies in June. Her quick win costs nothing: one conversation with the manager in week one.

1. Why does the text say "must review the overtime policy" but "should start with the north"?
A Because compliance is an obligation and the north region is professional advice.
B Because "review" is a stronger verb than "start".
C Because "must" is more polite than "should".
2. Which sentence is correct?
A "The company should to start with the north region."
B "The company should start with the north region."
C "The company should starting with the north region."
3. The text says "she would recommend putting two trainers there". Why "putting"?
A Because recommend is followed by the -ing form, not by to.
B Because the action is happening right now.
C Because "put" is an irregular verb.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Modals of Recommendation -- Choosing How Hard to Push

GRAMMAR

Same advice, four levels of pressure. Choosing the right one is the whole skill.

FormUseExample
could + base formThe softest: one option among others.We could run a pilot first.
should + base formYour professional advice, and the default in a recommendation.We should start with the north. (never should to)
I would recommend + -ingThe most diplomatic form, and the safest with a board.I would recommend starting in May.
must / have to + base formObligation, not advice: the law, the audit, the deadline.We must answer the audit before Friday.
negativeshould not for advice, must not for a prohibition.We should not promise a date. We must not share the numbers.
the trapIf everything is a must, nothing is.Keep must for what is really obligatory.
