Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TED -- Enrique Penalosa, "Why buses represent democracy in action" (14 min)

The former mayor of Bogota on public transport, road space and who the city is for. Strong opinions about mobility from a person who built a transit system, with clear English and lots of numbers.

Tip: pause twice and summarize his argument with one hedge and one nominalization: the creation of..., it is likely that...

Talk
TED -- Janette Sadik-Khan, "New York's streets? Not so mean any more" (13 min)

New York's former transportation commissioner on how the city changed its streets. Useful vocabulary for congestion, commuters and public space, and a good model of an official explaining a policy to the public.

Tip: note three numbers she gives and say them back with a hedge: roughly, about, it is likely that.

Talk
TED -- Jeff Speck, "The walkable city" (17 min)

A city planner on why people choose to walk, take transit or drive, and how a city can change that. It connects with ridership and congestion, and with the question in the second listening: why do people really use a line?

Tip: choose one of his four conditions for walking and explain it in plain English, in thirty seconds, as if to a journalist.
