Bondi Rescue — "The Deadliest Trap at Bondi Beach?"

Australian lifeguards on the rip that catches swimmers every summer. Every rescue is told the same way: what the person was doing, and the thing that went wrong.

Tip: pause after each rescue and say the story back in one sentence, with "was" or "were" plus -ing.

BBC Real Easy English — "Talking about the news"

Short, slow and made for your level. People say where they were and what they were doing when they heard about something — this lesson, without the water.

Tip: after each speaker, answer the same question about yourself out loud: where were you, and what were you doing?
