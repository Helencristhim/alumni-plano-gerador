Grammar Discovery

Which one was already happening?

I paddled out at six. (one action, done)
I was paddling out when I saw it. (two, and one cuts the other)
She broke her board on Sunday.
While she was riding the wave, her board snapped.

The long action, and the thing that cut it

was / were + verb-ing paints the scene: it was already running, you do not say when it started or when it ended. The past simple is the short thing that arrives in the middle of it. That is why a story almost always needs both.

The Code

Two pieces, and two little words

The form

I was paddling.
They were watching.
I wasn't looking.
What were you doing?

when or while

when + the short action
when I saw it, when the board snapped
while + the long scene
while I was paddling, while she was waiting

The scene can come first or second: I was paddling out when it happened = While I was paddling out, it happened.

Sound Check

The word that almost disappears

I was paddling out when I saw it.

"was" is not stressed — it sounds like "wuz"

What were you doing when it happened?

the question you will hear every time a story starts

While I was waiting, the wind changed.

one small pause after "waiting", and the sentence is clear

Did You Catch It?

About Zoe

1. What was Zoe doing when the set came?
She was paddling out, and the set came from nowhere.
2. How did she find out about the board, and what happened to it?
When she came up she noticed it was gone. It snapped in two pieces.
3. What was her brother doing, and why did she not answer him?
He was watching from the beach and he shouted, but she was still coughing and did not hear him.
Listening 1

The beach briefing

1. What was the surfer doing when the rip took him?
He was paddling out on the north side.
2. What was he NOT doing?
He was not panicking and he was not swimming against the water.
3. What are the two instructions for today?
Do not surf the north corner on a falling tide, and stay with your board and shout.
Listening 2

Two pieces

1. What was he doing when he heard the crack?
He was riding a wave into the shallow part, thinking about breakfast.
2. Was he doing something difficult on that wave?
No. No air, no big turn. He was standing there going straight.
3. What was his friend doing, and why is that a problem for him?
He was filming from the beach, so now the whole club has seen it.
