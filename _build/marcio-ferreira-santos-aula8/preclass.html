Lesson 08 -- Pre-class

Say Exactly Which One -- Precision at the Top

Turning a vague claim about your work into a credible one. Key words: specification, criterion, scope, deliverable, cross-functional, proprietary, due diligence, milestone. Structure: defining and non-defining relative clauses with who, which, that and where.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Specification -- a document that describes exactly what something must be or do
"The first deliverable is the specification that defines the scope."
Criterion -- one of the conditions you use to judge or compare (plural: criteria)
"The first criterion is a candidate who has built a model."
Scope -- what a project includes, and what it deliberately leaves out
"The first six months are about defining the scope."
Deliverable -- something concrete you hand over at the end of a stage
"The first deliverable is not a number."
Cross-functional -- bringing together people from different areas of the company
"A cross-functional team that includes analysts, category managers and finance."
Proprietary -- owned by one company and not available to anyone else
"The proprietary data the group collects is a strong asset."
Due diligence -- the careful check you run before you commit to something
"The due diligence will include two references."
Milestone -- a fixed point on the way that shows the project is on track
"The first milestone is the model in one country."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

This document describes a role that does not exist yet in the group. It is written for candidates who have already owned pricing in a large retail business. The mandate, which was approved by the board in March, is to build a model that works at SKU level in markets which are structurally different from each other. The first criterion is a candidate who has built a pricing model, not only used one. The second is evidence of work in a business where price is the main commercial lever. The proprietary data that the group already collects is a strong asset, and it is under-used.

1. Why does "the mandate, which was approved in March," have commas?
A The information is extra. We already know which mandate it is.
B The commas tell the reader which mandate of several it is.
C Every relative clause needs commas in a formal document.
2. Why is "a candidate who has built a model" written without commas?
A The clause defines which candidate. Without it, the sentence loses its meaning.
B "Who" never takes commas in English.
C The sentence is too short for commas.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A "The model that we built it in 2022 reduced variance."
B "The model that we built in 2022 reduced variance."
C "The model, that we built in 2022, reduced variance."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Relative Clauses

GRAMMAR

The clause that tells your listener exactly which one you mean.

TypeWordsWhat it does
Defining
(no commas)		who for people, which or that for things, where for placesTells the listener which one. Remove it and the sentence loses its meaning.
Non-defining
(two commas)		who, which — never "that"Adds extra information about something already identified.
Dropping the pronounthe model (that) we builtIn a defining clause you can leave "that" out when it is the object.
Two rules to rememberNever repeat the object pronoun ("the model that we built it"), and never use "that" after a comma.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the best answer for each real professional situation.

An interviewer asks which pricing model you built. The strongest answer is:
A "I built a pricing model. It was very good."
B "I built the pricing model that our board approved in 2022."
C "I built a model which it was approved in 2022."
You are adding extra information about your company. Which is correct?
A "My company, which is one of the largest wholesalers in Brazil, publishes its results."
B "My company, that is one of the largest wholesalers in Brazil, publishes its results."
C "My company which is one of the largest wholesalers in Brazil publishes its results."
You want to describe the environment, not the company name. You say:
A "I work in a business where price is the main commercial lever."
B "I work in a business which price is the main commercial lever."
C "I work in a business who price is the main commercial lever."
A recruiter asks about your team. The most credible answer is:
A "I lead a cross-functional team that includes analysts, category managers and finance."
B "I lead a big team and we are very dedicated."
C "I lead a team who includes analysts and finance."
