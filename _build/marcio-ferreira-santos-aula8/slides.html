Chapter 3: The Code

Relative Clauses

who · which · that · where

Grammar Discovery

Read and Notice

"The model that we implemented in 2022 reduced variance by twelve percent."

"We want a candidate who has built a model, not only used one."

"He worked in a business where price is the main lever."

"The mandate, which was approved in March, covers eight countries."

Three of them tell you which one. One only adds extra information. How can you see the difference?

TypeWordsWhat it does
Defining
(no commas)		who (people), which or that (things), where (places)Tells the listener which one. Remove it and the sentence loses its meaning.
Non-defining
(two commas)		who, which — never thatAdds extra information about something already identified. Remove it and the sentence still works.
Dropping the wordthe model (that) we builtIn a defining clause you can leave that out when it is the object.
The comma testRead the sentence without the clause. If you still know which one, use commas. If you do not, no commas.

In an interview, the defining clause is the one that makes a claim credible.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"The mandate, that was approved in March, covers eight countries."
"The mandate, which was approved in March, covers eight countries."
"The model that we built it in 2022 reduced variance."
"The model that we built in 2022 reduced variance."
"We want a candidate which has built a model."
"We want a candidate who has built a model."

After a comma, use which, never that. Never repeat the object pronoun. And people take who.

Listening

The Role, Described

A recruiter explains what the advertisement does not say. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What kind of person are they NOT looking for?
Someone who runs a team of analysts. They want someone who can own pricing across eight countries.
2. What has the candidate they want already done?
Built this at least once before, in a business where price is the main lever.
3. What are the first six months about?
Defining the scope, not delivering a number, because the role is new.
Listening 2

Two Candidates

An interviewer compares the two people he saw. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What did the first candidate do well?
He gave the cleaner presentation and described a well-known framework very well.
2. What did the second candidate talk about?
A model that he actually built himself, in a business where the data was terrible.
3. What is the difference, for the interviewer?
The person they need is the one who has lived the problem, not the one who explains a framework best.
