Your First Week

From Linda Ruiz — Your First Week

Hi Ricardo, welcome to Miami! Here is your first week.

Sunday: your flight lands at six in the evening. The hotel is near the office, and you have a reservation for seven nights.

Monday: the office opens at nine. Take the elevator to the fifth floor. Your desk is near my desk.

Tuesday: lunch with the team at twelve thirty, at the Brickell Deli.

Wednesday: a call with São Paulo at ten. It is eleven in São Paulo.

Your room key is at the reception. See you on Monday! Linda
