Listen and Notice

Two kinds of question. Which one uses is, and which one uses do?

WithQuestion / NoExample
am / is / are (Lessons 1-3)Is it...? / It is notIs the taxi here? No, it is not.
other verbs (Lesson 8)Do you...? / I don'tDo you like London? I don't have a map.
polite requests (Lessons 5-6)Can I have...? / I'd like...Can I have a taxi? I'd like a coffee.

Is it? with be. Do you? with the other verbs.

Reading

My Trip Story

My First Trip Alone — by Bruna

This is my first trip alone. I am in London for one week. My hotel is near Hyde Park, and my room is on the fourth floor.

Every morning I train at the hotel gym. Then I have a coffee with milk at a small café. I don’t need a map for the café now!

I love the parks, and I have a lot of photos. I have a black jacket from Oxford Street, and a souvenir for my sister.

Today is Saturday, my last day. A taxi takes me to the station, and the train takes me to the airport.

Is my English perfect? No, it is not. But I am here, alone, and I am happy.

Travel diary — Bruna Defrain
