Grammar Video
"Make polite requests" -- BBC Learning English, English at Work, Episode 5

Anna needs three things from three different people in one morning, and the episode shows exactly which form she uses for each. Connection to Lesson 10: this is the difference between Could I, Could you and Would it be possible to, acted out instead of explained.

Tip: pause every time she asks for something and say the same request about your own trip in August. Then say it again in the most careful form you know.

Podcast
"Travel to Croatia -- What to Do, See and Eat" -- Amateur Traveler

An hour on the Dalmatian coast from Dubrovnik to Split, with the islands, the ferries and the practical parts nobody writes on a postcard. Connection to Lesson 10: half of this episode is logistics, which is exactly where your English will be tested in August.

Tip: write down every problem the guest mentions -- a ferry, a booking, a closed office. For each one, say out loud the question you would ask at the front desk.

Travel Series
"Split, Croatia: Modern City on Ancient Roots" -- Rick Steves' Europe

The city where the role-play of this lesson happens: a Roman palace with apartments, cafes and a fish market still living inside it. Connection to Lesson 10: the ferry terminal, the old town and the restaurants you will be asking questions in.

Tip: stop the video three times and describe what is on screen in one sentence each. Then choose one place you want to eat in, and say how you would ask for a table without a reservation.
