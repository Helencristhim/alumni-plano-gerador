Chapter 4: The Code

So Good, Such a Place

so + adjective · such a + adjective + noun · so ... that

Grammar Discovery

So or Such?

"The wine was so good."

"It was such a quiet place."

"They were such friendly people."

"The boat was so expensive that I stayed on the shore."

Look at what comes right after the blue word and after the green words. Is there a noun or not? And in the last sentence, what does that introduce?

FormRuleExample
so + adjectiveno noun after itThe wine was so good.
such a / an + adjective + nounone thing (singular)It was such a charming village.
such + adjective + nounplural, or uncountable: no asuch friendly people · such good wine
so / such ... thatthat + the resultIt was so expensive that I stayed on the shore.
so / such vs veryvery gives information. so and such carry your feeling: The view was so beautiful!
The trapNever so a beautiful view and never such beautiful with no noun. Say such a beautiful view or so beautiful.

One question decides it: is there a noun? No noun: so. A noun: such.

Transform

From Very to So and Such

Make it stronger. Say it first, then click to compare

1. "The view was very beautiful."
"The view was so beautiful!" or "It was such a beautiful view!"
2. "It was a very long line."
"It was such a long line!" or "The line was so long!"
3. "The fans were very passionate."
"The fans were so passionate!" or "They were such passionate fans!"
4. "The museum was very dull. I left after twenty minutes."
"The museum was so dull that I left after twenty minutes."
5. "It was a very charming town. We stayed an extra day."
"It was such a charming town that we stayed an extra day."
6. "The fish was very fresh."
"The fish was so fresh!" Uncountable noun: "It was such fresh fish!"
Chapter 5: What People Said

Two Famous Things, Two Verdicts

A restaurant in Zagreb, and a stadium in Split

Listening

The Restaurant Everybody Talked About

A traveler on a famous restaurant in Zagreb. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. Why was the famous restaurant disappointing?
They waited forty minutes for a table, the room was so noisy that she could not hear her husband, and the portions were very small.
2. What did they do on the way back to the hotel, and why?
They bought a sandwich, because the portions were so small.
3. What was the real pleasant surprise of the trip?
A tiny place near the market, with no website and no reviews: the fish was so fresh and the owner was so kind that they went back twice.
Listening 2

A Night at the Stadium

A visitor who is not a football fan, at a game in Split. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What did he expect, and was he right?
He expected a dull evening, and he was wrong: the atmosphere was so electric that he forgot to look at the field.
2. Was the game itself impressive?
No. It ended zero to zero. The fans made it special.
3. What did he do the next morning?
He bought a Hajduk scarf. Now it hangs in his office, next to his books.
