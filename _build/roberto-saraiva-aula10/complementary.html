Lesson 10 -- When It Goes Wrong

Talk
Adar Cohen -- "3 ways to lead tough, unavoidable conversations" (TEDxKeene)

Eleven minutes from somebody who mediates conflicts for a living, on the three moves that keep a hard conversation from becoming a fight. Directly useful for the phone call this lesson trains, and every example is a real disagreement.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Then pick the move you never make, and try it in your next difficult call.

Podcast
NPR How I Built This -- "Whole Foods Market: John Mackey"

A founder who had to explain, publicly and repeatedly, decisions that customers hated. Forty minutes of natural American English on what a company owes an angry customer and what it does not, with the transcript on the page.

Tip: good for the car. Listen for how he admits something and holds his position in the same sentence — that is tonight's although, used under pressure.

Film
Sully (2016), directed by Clint Eastwood

Ninety minutes of one man explaining a decision to people who are looking for the root cause, and doing it without ever raising his voice. Slow, clear American English, and the whole second half is an investigation about what really went wrong.

Tip: watch the hearing scenes with English subtitles. Count how many times he agrees with a fact and still refuses the conclusion.
