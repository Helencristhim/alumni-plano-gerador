Lesson 33 — Pre-class

Pause and Rewind

Eight words for watching a replay with your coach (pause, rewind, mistake, position, patience, waste, interference, penalty) and how to give rules: you must stay near the peak, you mustn't waste waves, you don't have to catch ten.

Stage 1.1: The Replay

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Eight words, two minutes.

Pause — to stop a video for a short time
"Pause it there. Look at my feet."
Rewind — to go back to an earlier part of a video
"Rewind it ten seconds, please."
Mistake — something you do wrong
"Taking that small wave was a mistake."
Position — the place where you are, for example in the water
"My position was too far from the peak."
Patience — the ability to wait calmly for the right moment
"Patience wins heats."
Waste — to use something badly, for no good reason
"Don't waste your energy on small waves."
Interference — when you get in the way of a surfer who has priority
"Paddling behind him is interference."
Penalty — a punishment when you break a rule in a sport
"The penalty for interference is really big."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read Renato's notebook after the replay session with Dana. Then answer the questions.

Thursday. Replay with Dana. We watched my quarterfinal, and Dana asked me to pause and rewind it many times. My first wave was a mistake: it was too small. Rule 1: I must stay in position near the peak. Rule 2: I mustn't waste waves in the first minute. Patience wins heats! Rule 3: I mustn't paddle behind a surfer with priority. That's interference, and the penalty is huge. Good news: I don't have to catch ten waves. Two good waves are enough. On Saturday I must check in at seven, but I don't have to bring a jersey. They give us one on the beach.
Why was Renato's first wave a mistake?
A Because he paddled behind a surfer with priority.
B It was too small, and it was in the first minute of the heat.
"I don't have to catch ten waves." What does Renato mean?
A It isn't necessary. Two good waves are enough.
B He can't catch ten waves, because the judges give a penalty after two.
Does Renato have to bring a jersey on Saturday?
A Yes. He must bring his own yellow jersey from the club in Perth.
B No. They give him one on the beach.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

must, mustn't and don't have to

Use must when something is necessary. Use mustn't when something is not allowed: don't do it! Use don't have to when something is not necessary: you can choose.

must

You must check in.
He must wear a jersey.

mustn't

You mustn't be late.
Surfers mustn't drop in.

don't have to

You don't have to hurry.
She doesn't have to come.

Positive: You must stay calm.  ·  Negative: You mustn't panic.  ·  Question: Do I have to bring a jersey?
Remember: no "to" after must ("I must go", not "I must to go"), and no "-s" with he or she ("he must"). "Mustn't" and "don't have to" are NOT the same!
Your voice: in "mustn't", the first "t" is silent: MUS-nt.

Stage 4: Semifinal rules

Quiz
A grommet wants to paddle behind a surfer with priority. What do you say?
A You mustn't do that. It's interference.
B You don't have to paddle there, but you can if you want to catch the wave.
Jess asks: "Do I have to bring a jersey?" The organizers give them on the beach.
A No, you mustn't bring a jersey, because the judges give a penalty for it.
B No, you don't have to.
Check-in is at seven. If you're late, you can't surf. Tell Tom.
A We must check in at seven.
B We must to check in at seven, at the tent near the parking lot.
Dana wants Renato to stay near the peak. What does she say?
A He musts stay near the peak, and he must watch the sets very carefully.
B You must stay near the peak.
Bev says: "Watch the replay again tonight!" You watched it twice today.
A I don't have to. I watched it twice today.
B I mustn't watch it tonight, because the coach says it's against the rules.

Stage 5: My rules

Reflection
Think about your rules for a big day: a contest, a test or a trip. Record 30 seconds: two things you must do, one thing you mustn't do, and one thing you don't have to do. Check one thing before you send it: no "to" after must (I must go, not I must to go).
