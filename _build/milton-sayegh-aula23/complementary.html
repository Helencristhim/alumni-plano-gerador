The Money Pit (1986)

A young couple buys a big old house, and every repair goes wrong: contractors who disappear, costs that never stop. Connection to Lesson 23: every bad repair is a "if we had..., we would..." moment.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down two sentences about the contractors with a mixed conditional.
