Chapter 3: I'd Like...

I'd Like...

a, an, and the polite way to order

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I'd like a coffee, please."

"I'd like an orange juice."

"I'd like a sandwich."

"I'd like an egg sandwich."

Why a coffee, but an orange juice? Listen to the first sound of the next word.

WordsUse itExample
I'd like (= I would like)to order, politelyI'd like a tea, please.
abefore a consonant sound (c, t, s, b...)a coffee · a sandwich
anbefore a vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u)an orange juice · an egg

I'd like + a / an + the thing + please.

The Café Menu

The Corner Café — Breakfast Menu

Good morning! Breakfast is from seven to eleven, every day. Here is our menu.

Hot drinks: coffee, three pounds. Tea, two pounds. With milk or without: you choose.

Cold drinks: orange juice, four pounds. Water is free.

Food: egg sandwich, six pounds. Cheese sandwich, five pounds. Chocolate cake, four pounds.

Today’s special: a coffee and a piece of cake for six pounds. Order at the counter, please!
