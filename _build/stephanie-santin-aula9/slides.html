Chapter 3: The Code

The Action Without the Person

Be plus the participle, and the tense hiding inside be

Discover the Rule

What Do These Have in Common?

The antibiotic is administered before the procedure.
The study was conducted at three hospitals.
The protocol has been updated twice this year.
The report must be submitted by Friday.
Click to reveal the rule
None of the four says who does it — and that is the point, not an accident. The passive is be + past participle, and the tense lives in be: is administered (present), was conducted (past), has been updated (present perfect), must be submitted (after a modal). Use it when the action or the result matters more than the person — which is why protocols, methods sections and patient histories are full of it. If you do want to name the agent, use by: analyzed by an independent team.

Chapter 4: The Protocol

The Step Remains, the Name Disappears

A protocol briefing and a decision about a paper

Listening 1

The New Protocol

Read the questions, then listen.

1. What happens within ten minutes of arrival?
Patients are assessed in the emergency room, and blood is collected immediately.
2. What is new in this protocol, and why was it added?
Every chart is reviewed by a second doctor, not by the one who operated. It was added after the audit last year.
3. How many times has the protocol been updated since March?
Three times.

EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT

TRAUMA PROTOCOL · VERSION 4

1. The patient is assessed within ten minutes of arrival.

2. Blood is collected and the results are sent to the surgical team.

3. The family is informed before the patient goes to the operating room.

4. Every chart is reviewed by a second doctor.

5. The patient is discharged only after a written assessment.

Two corrections before the abstract goes out

What Was Flagged?

G
Stephanie, your abstract was received last week. Two things have to be corrected before it goes out.
S
Of course. What was flagged?
G
The first line says we analyzed the data. In this journal the methods are always written in the passive.
S
So it should be: the data were analyzed at three centers.
G
Exactly. And the second point: it is not clear when the protocol was approved.
S
It was approved in March, and it has been updated once since then.
G
Put that in one sentence and send it back today.
S
It will be submitted before the end of the day.
Comprehension

About Dr. Grant

1. What two things does he say have to be corrected?
The first line, which is in the active voice instead of the passive, and the missing date on which the protocol was approved.
2. What does he say about how the methods are written in this journal?
They are always written in the passive.

Chapter 5: Practice

Six Answers Without a Subject

The questions a colleague asks about a procedure or a study

Listening 2

A Decision About Your Manuscript

Read the questions, then listen.

1. What has happened to the manuscript so far?
It was submitted in April, it has been reviewed by two specialists, and both reports are now with the editor.
2. What did the reviewers say about the methods and about the discussion?
The methods section was praised; the discussion was considered too long.
3. What was flagged, and what should be done about it?
The English was flagged by one of the reviewers. The text should be checked by a native speaker before it is resubmitted.
