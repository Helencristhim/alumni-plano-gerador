Lesson 9 -- When Nobody Says Who Did It

Talk
Pardis Sabeti -- "How we will fight the next deadly virus"

A geneticist describes an outbreak response: what was collected, what was sequenced, what was shared. The passive is everywhere, because the work belonged to hundreds of people.

Tip: write down five passive sentences from the talk. Then say each one again in the active, and notice what you have to invent to do it.

Talk
Anthony Atala -- "Printing a human kidney"

A surgeon explains a procedure step by step in front of the machine that does it. Exactly the register of a methods section, spoken out loud.

Tip: after watching, describe the process in six passive sentences without looking at anything.

Guided Study
TED-Ed -- "How do vaccines work?"

A short animated lesson with comprehension questions. A process described the way a protocol describes one: steps first, people last.

Tip: answer the questions out loud. Then explain the same process to a patient, in the active voice, and see which version is easier to say.
