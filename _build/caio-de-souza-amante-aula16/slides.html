The Core

The Data Room Call

I am the buyer’s analyst. I have your numbers on my screen, and I want to understand every line. Answer with the number first, then the reason.

  • Round 1: the walk-through. Three slides: revenue, margin, clients. One minute each.
  • Round 2: the hard line. I point at your worst number; you describe it, then explain it.
  • Round 3: swap. You are the analyst and question my numbers.
