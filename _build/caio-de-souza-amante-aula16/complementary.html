Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Storytelling with Data, Episode: “Story” (Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic)

A data communication expert explains what turns a set of numbers into a story, and why people who present data should care. It is the idea behind every noun phrase in this lesson: the number needs a shape.

Tip: listen for the difference between showing data and telling a story with it. Then write the story of one Dataside number in three sentences.

Listen on Storytelling with Data ↗
Talk
David McCandless: “The Beauty of Data Visualization”

A data journalist shows how the same numbers tell a completely different story when you give them the right shape. Short, visual and full of comparisons, which is exactly the skill of this lesson.

Tip: eighteen minutes. Pause on two of his charts and describe each one in one sentence with a noun phrase: “an increase in…”

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Hans Rosling: “200 Countries, 200 Years, 4 Minutes” (BBC)

Four minutes, two hundred years of data, and a speaker who describes every trend clearly while the chart moves. A perfect model for describing a trajectory out loud.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause three times and describe the trend on the screen with a noun phrase: “a rapid increase in…”
