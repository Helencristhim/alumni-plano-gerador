Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. Toque em Listen para ouvir. / Listen to each word and read the example.

Haggle -- talk with a seller for a lower price (pechinchar / negociar)
"In the bazaar, you can haggle." (No bazar, você pode pechinchar.)
Offer -- a price you say you can pay (oferta)
"My offer is two hundred euros." (Minha oferta é duzentos euros.)
Deal -- an agreement about a price (acordo / negócio fechado)
"OK, it's a deal!" (Ok, negócio fechado!)
Carpet -- a big cloth for the floor (tapete)
"This carpet is handmade." (Este tapete é feito à mão.)
Silk -- a soft, shiny material (seda)
"Silk carpets are expensive." (Tapetes de seda são caros.)
Lamp -- a thing that gives light (luminária)
"The glass lamps are colorful." (As luminárias de vidro são coloridas.)
Pattern -- shapes and colors that repeat (estampa / desenho)
"I love the pattern." (Eu adoro a estampa.)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto e responda às perguntas. / Read the text and answer the questions.

Marlene walks into a carpet shop in the Grand Bazaar. The seller shows her a silk carpet with a red and blue pattern. "It is very beautiful," she says. The price is three hundred and fifty euros. "That is too expensive for me. How about two hundred?" The seller makes a new offer: three hundred. They haggle a little more, and he adds a small lamp. At two hundred and eighty, Marlene says: "OK, it's a deal!" (Neste texto, very = muito, só informa; too = demais, é um problema; How about...? = que tal...?)

1. What is the first price of the carpet?
A Two hundred euros.
B Three hundred and fifty euros.
C Eighty euros.
2. Why does Marlene say "too expensive"?
A Because she cannot pay that price.
B Because the carpet is ugly.
C Because it is silk.
3. What is in the final deal?
A Only the carpet, for 200.
B Two carpets.
C The carpet and a small lamp, for 280.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- too + adjective / How about...?

GRAMMAR

Use too + adjetivo quando é DEMAIS, um problema (too expensive = caro demais, não dá para comprar). very só quer dizer "muito". Para fazer uma oferta: How about + preço? / Too means it is a problem; How about...? makes an offer.

RuleExample (EN)Tradução (PT)
ProblemaIt is too expensive.É caro demais.
Problema para mimIt is too big for me.É grande demais para mim.
Só informaçãoIt is very beautiful.É muito bonito.
OfertaHow about two hundred euros?Que tal duzentos euros?
AceitarOK, it's a deal!Ok, negócio fechado!

Key point / Ponto-chave: cuidado: too beautiful parece uma reclamação ("bonito demais")! Para elogiar, use very. E não diga "too much expensive" -- é só too expensive. Em italiano: troppo caro = too expensive.
