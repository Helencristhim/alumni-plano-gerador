Chapter 3 -- Too Expensive

Language Discovery

It is too expensive. / How about 200?

Discovery

Listen and Notice

Compare "very expensive" and "too expensive". Which one means you cannot buy it?

"This carpet is very beautiful."

"But it is too expensive for me."

"This lamp is too big for my suitcase."

"How about two hundred euros?"

The Rule

Too and How About

UsePatternExample
A problemtoo + adjectiveIt is too expensive.
A problem for a persontoo + adjective + for meIt is too big for me.
Only informationvery + adjectiveIt is very beautiful.
Make an offerHow about + price?How about two hundred euros?
AcceptOK, it's a deal.OK, it's a deal.

Key idea: very only says "a lot". too says it is a problem: too expensive means you cannot pay. To make an offer, say How about + a price? When you both agree, say It's a deal!

Chapter 4 -- At the Carpet Shop

Haggling for a Carpet

A seller, a price tag and two messages

Dialogue

In the Carpet Shop

M

"Welcome, welcome! Please sit down. Would you like some tea? Look at this carpet."

M

"Thank you. It is very beautiful. I love the pattern. How much is it?"

M

"It is silk, handmade. For you, three hundred and fifty euros."

M

"Oh, that is too expensive for me. How about two hundred?"

M

"Two hundred is too low, madam. My offer is three hundred."

M

"Hmm. How about two hundred and fifty? And you give me that small lamp too."

M

"You know how to haggle! Two hundred and eighty with the lamp."

M

"OK, two hundred and eighty. It is a deal!"

Comprehension

About Mehmet

1. What does Mehmet offer Marlene when she sits down?
Some tea.
2. What is Mehmet's first price, and what is the carpet made of?
Three hundred and fifty euros -- it is silk and handmade.
3. What does Mehmet say about two hundred euros?
It is too low -- his offer is three hundred.
4. What is Mehmet's last price, and what is in the deal?
Two hundred and eighty euros, with the small lamp.
