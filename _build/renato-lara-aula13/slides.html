Grammar Discovery

What comes after should?

You should wait your turn.
He shouldn't paddle for every set. (he, and still no -s)
Should I bring my own board?
You should be polite in the water. (the verb is "be" — it cannot disappear)

should + the plain verb

Nothing changes for he or she, there is never a to after it, and the verb never disappears — when the sentence is about how somebody IS, the verb is be: "you should be polite". Advice, not an order: it is your opinion about the best thing to do.

The Code

One word, three traps

The form

I / you / he / she should go.
You shouldn't go.
Should I go?
What should I do?

Never

You should to wait.
no "to" after should
He shoulds apologize.
should never takes -s
You should be polite.
the verb "be" cannot be missing

Softer, and more Australian: Maybe you should sit wide · You should probably ask the coach.

Sound Check

The silent L

the "l" is silent — it sounds like "shud"

You shouldn't paddle for every set.

the final "t" almost disappears in normal speech

What should I do?

The Code

The rule, or your opinion?

have to — the rule

"You have to wear a leash. It is the club rule."
"Members have to sign in at the tower."
Somebody else decided. You cannot say no.

should — my advice

"You should wear a leash. It is safer out there."
"You should talk to the coach first."
It is my opinion. You can still say no.

Same action, two different messages. Choose the one you really mean.

Did You Catch It?

About Tess

She is from Perth, and she is here for three weeks.
2. What does she say about the first day at school?
He should show up early and talk to one person, not to twenty.
3. What are her three pieces of advice for the water?
Sit wide, wait your turn, and apologize straight away if you drop in on somebody.
Listening 1

The coach's briefing

1. What time should the new members show up, and why?
Ten minutes early, because the session starts at six thirty and late people paddle out alone.
2. What should they do in the water in the first week?
Sit wide, watch and wait their turn. They shouldn't paddle for every set.
3. What is his most important piece of advice?
If you make a mistake, apologize. The club has no problem with a beginner, only with people who do not say sorry.
Listening 2

The voice note

1. What is her advice about lunch on the first day?
He shouldn't sit alone. He should ask somebody what they are listening to.
2. What should he answer when people ask how he is?
Good thanks, how are you, and keep walking. They are not really asking.
3. What is the last thing she asks him to do?
He should call her mum when he lands in Perth.
