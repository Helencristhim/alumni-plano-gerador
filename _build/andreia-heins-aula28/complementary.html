Lesson 28 -- Defending Your Position

Talk
Daniel H. Cohen -- "For argument's sake" (TED)

A philosopher explains why the goal of an argument is not to win, but to learn something. A great match for a lesson about defending your position and finding common ground.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then write one sentence about the talk with although or despite.

Talk
Robb Willer -- "How to have better political conversations" (TED)

A sociologist shows that we persuade people more when we use their values, not only ours. The examples are about politics, but the idea works in any meeting with a different team.

Tip: think about Paul in Boston. What does he care about? Say one argument for forty hours that uses his values.

Talk
Atul Gawande -- "How do we heal medicine?" (TED)

A surgeon defends a simple idea -- the surgical checklist -- with real data from hospitals around the world. Watch how he makes a claim and backs it with compelling evidence.

Tip: write down his main claim and one piece of evidence. Then explain them in English in one minute.
