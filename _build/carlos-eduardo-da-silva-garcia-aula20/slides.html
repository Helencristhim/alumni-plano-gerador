Chapter 4: Structure Check

Five Moments, Five Forms

Which one do you use, and when?

Grammar Discovery

Read and Notice

"Right now I am preparing the files for the audit."

"There are twelve producers in our group."

"Last month I spoke with the producers, and there was no record."

"On Monday I am going to send the corrective action."

"The certificate is renewed in December."

MomentFormExample
Nowam / is / are + verb-ingI am preparing the files.
Numbers and placesThere is (1) / There are (2+)There are twelve producers.
The last visitpast verb / There was / There wereI spoke. There was no record.
The planam / is / are + going to + base verbI am going to send it on Monday.
The ruleis / are + past participle (passive)The certificate is renewed in December.
The questionWhat / When / Where / Who / How many + verb: How many producers are in the group?

The time word tells you the form. Find the time word first. / A palavra de tempo indica a forma. Procure a palavra de tempo primeiro.

Common Mistake

Wrong vs Right

✗ WRONG

"Right now I prepare the files."

✓ RIGHT

"Right now I am preparing the files."

✗ WRONG

"There was two gaps in the training list."

✓ RIGHT

"There were two gaps in the training list."

Right now needs verb-ing, and two or more needs were. / Right now pede verbo com -ing, e dois ou mais pede were.

Dialogue

Emma Asks: Where Are We?

E
Hi Carlos. Let us start with the numbers. How many producers are in the group now?
C
There are twelve producers, Emma. And there are two new properties in the region.
E
Good. And what are you doing right now?
C
I am preparing the files for the next audit. Ana is updating the records in the system.
E
Perfect. What did you find on the last visit?
C
There was no record for one processing unit. It is my main concern, but it is not a critical finding. The evidence is in my report.
E
Thank you for saying it early. Is the corrective action ready?
C
Yes. I am going to send the corrective action to the producer on Monday. That is my top priority.
E
Very good. And remember, the certificate is renewed in December, so we have time.
C
Understood. Our progress is good -- eighty percent of the actions are closed.

About the Conversation

With the numbers -- how many producers are in the group.
2. What does Emma thank Carlos for?
For saying the concern early.
3. When does Emma say the certificate is renewed?
In December -- so the team has time.
