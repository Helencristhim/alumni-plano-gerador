A aula que fecha o bloco: tudo das aulas 11 a 19 numa unica reuniao de status. Seis palavras que voltam (progress, concern, finding, corrective action, priority, evidence) e as cinco estruturas do bloco lado a lado: agora (present continuous), numeros e lugares (there is / there are), a ultima visita (simple past + there was / there were), o plano (going to) e a regra (voz passiva). Etapa 1.1: Cartoes de Vocabulario

Vocabulary

Estas seis palavras voltam das aulas 14, 17, 18 e 19. Ouca cada uma e leia o exemplo. Toque Listen para ouvir. (Listen = Ouvir)

Progress -- progresso (quanto do trabalho ja esta feito)
"Our progress is good -- eighty percent of the actions are closed." (Nosso progresso esta bom -- oitenta por cento das acoes estao fechadas.)
Concern -- preocupacao (algo que preocupa e que voce quer falar na reuniao)
"This is our main concern, but it is not a critical finding." (Esta e nossa principal preocupacao, mas nao e uma constatacao critica.)
Finding -- constatacao / achado (o que voce descobre e anota numa verificacao)
"There was no record for the processing unit. It is a finding." (Nao havia registro da unidade de beneficiamento. E uma constatacao.)
Corrective action -- acao corretiva (o que o produtor precisa fazer para corrigir)
"I am going to send the corrective action on Monday." (Vou enviar a acao corretiva na segunda-feira.)
Priority -- prioridade (o mais importante, o que voce faz primeiro)
"The corrective action is my top priority this week." (A acao corretiva e minha prioridade maxima esta semana.)
Evidence -- evidencia (a prova de que algo foi feito)
"The evidence is in my report -- photos and the signed list." (A evidencia esta no meu relatorio -- fotos e a lista assinada.)

Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Leia a nota de status da Emma e responda as perguntas. Repare nas palavras de tempo. (Read Emma's status note and answer.)

Before our meeting on Friday, here is the status. Right now Carlos is preparing the files for the next audit. There are twelve producers in our group, and there are two new properties in the region. Last month Carlos visited three farms and he spoke with the producers. There was no record for one processing unit, and there were two small gaps in the training list. This is our main concern, but it is not a critical finding -- the evidence is in his report. On Monday Carlos is going to send the corrective action to the producer. This is our top priority. And remember: the certificate is renewed in December. Our progress is good.

1. What is Carlos doing right now? (O que o Carlos esta fazendo agora?)
A He is visiting three farms. (isso foi no mes passado)
B He is preparing the files for the next audit. (agora = is preparing)
C He is sending the corrective action. (isso e o plano para segunda-feira)
2. Why does the note say "there were" two gaps? (Por que a nota usa "there were" para as duas falhas?)
A Because the gaps are in the plan. (o plano usa going to)
B Because they are two (plural) and in the past. (dois ou mais no passado = there were)
C Because a gap is always plural. (nao -- one gap = there was)
3. How does the note say a rule of the standard? (Como a nota fala de uma regra da norma?)
A "The certificate renews in December." (falta a voz passiva)
B "The certificate is renewed in December." (is + participio = a regra)
C "The certificate is going to renew in December." (going to e plano, nao regra)

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Cinco Momentos, Cinco Formas

Grammar

Tudo das aulas 11 a 19 numa tabela so. A palavra de tempo indica a forma. / Everything from lessons 11 to 19 in one table. The time word tells you the form.

Moment / MomentoForm / FormaExample / Exemplo
Now / Agoraam / is / are + verbo-ingRight now I am preparing the files. (Agora estou preparando os arquivos.)
Numbers and places / Numeros e lugaresThere is (1) / There are (2 ou mais)There are twelve producers in the group. (Ha doze produtores no grupo.)
The last visit / A ultima visitaverbo no passado; There was (1) / There were (2 ou mais)I spoke with the producer. There was no record. (Falei com o produtor. Nao havia registro.)
The plan / O planoam / is / are + going to + verbo baseI am going to send it on Monday. (Vou enviar na segunda-feira.)
The rule / A regrais / are + participio (voz passiva)The certificate is renewed in December. (O certificado e renovado em dezembro.)
The question / A perguntaWhat / When / Where / Who / How many + verbo: How many producers are in the group? (Quantos produtores ha no grupo?)

Atencao aos dois erros que mais voltaram no bloco: right now pede verbo com -ing (nao "I prepare"), e dois ou mais no passado pedem there were (nao "there was"). / Right now needs verb-ing, and two or more in the past needs there were.

Etapa 4: Quiz Situacional

Quiz

Escolha a melhor resposta para cada momento da reuniao.

The coordinator asks: "What are you doing?" (Ela quer saber o que voce esta fazendo NESTE momento.)
A "I prepare the files for the audit."
B "I am preparing the files for the audit."
C "I am going to prepare the files for the audit."
You report two problems from the last visit. (Voce relata dois problemas da ultima visita.)
A "There was two gaps in the training list."
B "There were two gaps in the training list."
C "There are two gaps in the training list last month."
You explain a rule of the standard, not your plan. (Voce explica uma regra da norma, nao o seu plano.)
A "The farm audits every twelve months."
B "The farm is audited every twelve months."
C "The farm is going to audit every twelve months."
