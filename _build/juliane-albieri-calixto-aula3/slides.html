Chapter 3: The Code

What Happened, and What Has Changed

Two tenses for one story

Grammar Discovery

One Story, Two Tenses

"At first, we talked to many customers."

"Then we built a prototype and tested it."

"Since the launch, many customers have earned a limit."

"We have learned a lot about our users so far."

Last lesson you met the present perfect. Today it meets the past simple. Look at the red words. At first and then are steps of a story that is finished: the verb is in the past simple (talked, built). Since the launch and so far go from the past until today: the verb is have + third form.

JobFormExample
Steps of the story, finishedpast simpleWe talked to customers. We built a prototype.
Words for finished timeat first, then, last year, in 2024, when...When we tested it, users loved it.
Result until nowhave / has + third formMany customers have earned a limit.
Words for until nowso far, since, up to now, this yearSo far we have learned a lot.
QuestionsWhen did...? / Have you ... yet?When did you launch it? Have you measured it yet?
The trapwhen + present perfect does not existNot When have you launched it? Say When did you launch it?

In one line: the steps of the story in the past simple; the result until today in the present perfect.

Chapter 4: The Rehearsal

The Rehearsal

One conversation, two recordings, one entry card

Dialogue

Tom Plays the Jury

T
OK, Juliane. Imagine I’m on the jury, and I know nothing about Brazil. Tell me about your project.
J
Sure. In Brazil, many people have a credit card, but their credit limit is zero. That was the biggest pain point.
T
Interesting. I worked at a bank in Boston for five years, and we never had that problem. Why is it so important here?
J
Our key insight was this: here, a credit card is an extension of the salary. So at first, we talked to many customers.
T
Good. And then?
J
Then we built a prototype: a game. The customer completes simple behaviors, like paying bills on time, and earns a limit as a reward.
T
I love that. When did you launch it?
J
We launched it inside the app, and since the launch many customers have earned a limit.
T
That’s your ending. Two years ago I was on a design jury, and I read hundreds of entries. The good ones always finished with a result.
J
Thanks, Tom. So far, this is the best version of my story.
Comprehension

About Tom

1. Where did Tom work for five years?
At a bank in Boston.
2. What did Tom do two years ago?
He was on a design jury and read hundreds of entries.
3. According to Tom, how did the good entries always finish?
With a result.
Listening 1 · A Savings Story

Arjun’s Product Story

A product designer in Mumbai tells the story of a savings feature. Sound first — no text.

1. What was the pain point of the young customers?
They wanted to save money, but at the end of the month nothing was left.
2. What did the team build first?
A prototype that saved a small amount every time the customer paid with the card.
3. What has happened since the launch?
More than two hundred thousand people have used it, and the average savings have doubled.
