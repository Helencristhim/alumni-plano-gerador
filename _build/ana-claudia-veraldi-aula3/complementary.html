Lesson 3 -- How Long Have You Been Doing That?

Series
Restored -- 1911 Craftsman Cottage Renovation (full episode)

A restorer takes a house built in 1911 and brings back what a century of bad renovations covered up: original floors, original windows, original details. Room by room, exactly the way you work.

Tip: he constantly says what he has finished and what he has been working on. Write down three sentences you hear with have been -ing, and three with have + past participle.

Podcast
The Urban Exodus Podcast, E68 -- Hand-building a homestead in Belize

A London restaurant manager left the city and has been building her own home by hand, with her family, for years. She talks about what is finished, what is still going, and what she underestimated.

Tip: she is not a native speaker of the English you hear in films, and she is completely clear. Listen once for the story only, without trying to catch every word.

Talk
Why you should make useless things -- Simone Giertz, TED (11 min)

A Swedish inventor who builds machines that do not work on purpose, and explains why the making matters more than the result. The best eleven minutes anybody has ever spent defending a labor of love.

Tip: the speaker is Swedish, not American, and she is very easy to follow. This is the English of your own meetings. Watch once with no subtitles.
