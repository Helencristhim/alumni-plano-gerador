Direct or polite?

Left: a direct question. Right: the same question, but polite. Look at the verbs. What changes?

"When does the presentation start?"↔"Do you know when the presentation starts?"
"Did you rehearse?"↔"I wonder if you rehearsed."

Q&A mistakes

0 / 5 errors found

"Could you tell me where is the projector?"
"Could you tell me where the projector is? — in a polite question, the verb goes after the subject."
"Do you know when does it start?"
"Do you know when it starts? — no does inside the polite question."
"I wonder if did she like it."
"I wonder if she liked it. — no did: the verb takes the past."
"Have you ever given a presentation in English? Yes, I had."
"Yes, I have. — your life up to now: have, not had."
"Can you tell me what time is it?"
"Can you tell me what time it is? — subject first, then the verb."
