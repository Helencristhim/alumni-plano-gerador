Documentary
FYRE: The Greatest Party That Never Happened — Netflix

A luxury festival in the Bahamas where nothing that should have been done was done, told by the people who watched it happen and said nothing. It is ninety minutes of American English built almost entirely out of we should have, they had to and nobody had to, which is the grammar of this lesson at full speed.

Tip: write down five sentences where somebody says what should have been done differently. Then say your own five about a trip of yours, out loud, in the same shape.

Podcast
NPR Life Kit — "Flight cancelled again? Try these tips"

What you are entitled to when an airline cancels on you, which line not to stand in, and what to do before the trip so the bad day costs you nothing. Practical, American, normal speed, full transcript on the same page.

Tip: find three sentences that tell you what you have to do and three that tell you what you should do. The difference between those two lists is exactly today's grammar.

Video
Rachel's English — "Shoulda, Woulda, Coulda"

The reduction that makes an American sentence unrecognizable to somebody who learned the written form. She takes should have apart and shows exactly how it collapses into one syllable, and what the idiom means when somebody says all three in a row.

Tip: after the video, say "I should have bought travel insurance" five times, faster each time, until should have becomes one sound.
