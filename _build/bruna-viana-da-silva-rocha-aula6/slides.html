Closing

One Minute, No Notes

Somebody is leaving on their first big trip tomorrow and they ask you for one minute of advice. Go.

I can say what I should have done, with the right form of the verb after it.
I can tell somebody what they have to do and what they do not have to do, without mixing up “no need” and “not allowed”.
I understand shoulda, coulda and woulda when an American says them at full speed.
