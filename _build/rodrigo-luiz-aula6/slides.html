Chapter 3: The Pattern

One Shape for Right Now

am · is · are · + -ing

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

We are working on it right now.
The lab is testing two samples.
I am sending the report today.
The schedule is not changing.

What do these four sentences have in common?

FormExampleEm português
I am + -ingI am sending the report.Estou enviando o relatório.
He / she / it is + -ingThe lab is testing the sample.O laboratório está testando a amostra.
We / they are + -ingWe are loading the truck.Estamos carregando o caminhão.
NegativeThe schedule is not changing.O cronograma não está mudando.
CuidadoO verbo to be nunca some: "we working now" não existe — o certo é "we are working now". E o contraste vale ouro: we work with two divisions (sempre) x we are working on it (agora).
