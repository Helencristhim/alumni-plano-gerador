Grammar Discovery

One word different

Today the ocean is flat.
Yesterday the ocean was flat.
The conditions are perfect this morning.
The conditions were perfect last Saturday.

Three words become two

In the present you choose between am, is and are. In the past there are only two: was and were. The verb is still there — it just changed clothes.

The Code

Only two forms

was

I was third in my heat.
He / She was the runner-up.
It was my first contest.

were

You were fast that day.
We were at the beach at seven.
They were angry about the delay.
− I wasn't nervous.  —  They weren't ready.
? Was it a good final?  —  Were you there on Saturday? (no did — the verb jumps to the front)
Sound Check

Strong at the end, weak in the middle

It was a close final.

the was is short and soft here

The waves were tiny.

the were almost disappears

Yes, I was. No, we weren't.

at the end of the answer it comes back, loud and clear

Listening 1

The results, on the loudspeaker

1. Who won, and what was he last season?
Toby Nguyen. He was a rookie last season.
2. How close was the final?
Very close: 16.4 against 15.1. Both scores were excellent.
3. What do the organisers apologise for?
Yesterday's delay. They say it was their fault, not the ocean's.
Listening 2

Two days, one weekend

1. How long were they at the beach on Friday?
Five hours, and nothing happened.
2. How was Saturday different?
The conditions were unbelievable and the crowd was huge.
3. What was her result?
She was third in her heat, so she is out.
