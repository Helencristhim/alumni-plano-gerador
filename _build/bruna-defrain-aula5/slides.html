Chapter 3: Can I Have...?

Can I Have...?

one polite question for everything

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Can I have the Wi-Fi password, please?"

"Can I have one more towel, please?"

"This is my key."

"That is the elevator."

The first two: what do they do? This and that: near or far?

WordsUse it forExample
Can I have...?to ask for a thing, politelyCan I have a pillow, please?
thisa thing near you (here)This is my key.
thata thing far from you (there)That is the elevator.

Can I have + the thing + please? This = here. That = there.
