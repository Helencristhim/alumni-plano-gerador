Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Julia Dhar — “How to disagree productively and find common ground” (TED)

A world debating champion on how to disagree without making it personal: start from common ground, separate the idea from the person, and stay curious. Everything a moderator needs when a panel gets hot.

Tip: note the three steps she proposes and test one of them in your next tense meeting.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
Freakonomics Radio — “Can We Disagree Better?”

Why public disagreement has become so hostile, and what researchers have learned about arguing in a way that actually moves people.

Tip: listen for the phrases people use to concede a point. Write down two you would use on stage.

Listen on Freakonomics ↗
Talk
Margaret Heffernan — “Dare to disagree” (TED)

A former CEO on why the best organizations need people who push back, and how productive conflict protects everyone from bad decisions.

Tip: retell her main story in reported speech, and add one conditional without if about what would have happened.

Watch on TED ↗
