Lesson 14 -- The Panel

Real Interview
HARDtalk -- Akinwumi Adesina, African Development Bank

One interviewer, but the same mechanics: a question comes back later in different words, and the answer has to be the same one. Connection to Lesson 14: in a panel, three people will do that to you in forty minutes.

Tip: find a moment where a question returns, and write down both answers. If they differ, that is exactly what a panel compares in the calibration meeting.

Grammar Video
"Linking words of contrast" -- BBC Learning English, English Class

The connectors from Lesson 11, now used to move between people instead of between facts. Connection to Lesson 14: "although I can give you the finance side" is how you pass a question without dropping it.

Tip: build three sentences that pass a question to somebody else and still deliver your part. Each one needs a concession and an offer.

Talk
"How to stay calm when you know you will be stressed" -- Daniel Levitin, TED

Deciding the behaviour before the pressure, not during it. Connection to Lesson 14: your closing sentence, the one that hands the turn back, is decided now and used four times in the room.

Tip: choose one hand-back sentence and use it in every answer of the next lesson. It should be so automatic that it costs you nothing to say.
