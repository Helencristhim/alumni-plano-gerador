Chapter 3

Where, Which, Whose

One small word, one more detail

Grammar Discovery

One Word, One More Detail

"It's a coffee shop where the owner knows your name."

"I live on a street which goes down to the river."

"It's an old house whose windows are always open."

"Salvador, which I visited last year, is incredibly vibrant."

WordUseExample
whereA place + what happens therea coffee shop where I work
which / thatA thing (a street, a building, a city)a street which goes to the river
whose + nounPossession: its or theira house whose windows are open
, which ...,Extra information, with commas (never "that")Salvador, which I visited, is vibrant.
⚠ CarefulDon't repeat the place: 'the coffee shop where I work', not 'the coffee shop where I work there'. Use 'which', not 'who', for places and things. 'Whose' is possession; 'who's' means 'who is'.

Listening

Start with One Detail

Listen first. No text. Sound only.

1. What kind of words do good describers NOT start with?
Big words like "beautiful" or "nice".
2. What do they start with instead?
One small, real detail.
3. What happens to the listener, according to the speaker?
They stop thinking about your English and start seeing your place.
