TED -- Barry Schwartz, "The paradox of choice" (20 min)

A psychologist explains why more options can make decisions harder and people less satisfied. Useful for anyone who has to present alternatives to a client or a board.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and note every verb followed by -ing or to. Then say one recommendation you would give a client after this talk.
