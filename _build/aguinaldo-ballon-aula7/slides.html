Chapter 3: The Code

Doing or To Do?

The verbs that decide the form

Grammar Discovery

Why -ing After Some Verbs, and To After Others?

"I recommend waiting until the rule is published."

"You should avoid signing before the audit."

"The board decided to postpone the vote."

"The company agreed to pay the penalty."

The verbs in blue decide the form of the next verb. After some of them, the next verb takes -ing. After others, it takes to. Can you make two groups?

JobFormExample
Verbs + -ingrecommend, suggest, consider, avoid, risk, involveI recommend waiting. / We considered selling.
Verbs + todecide, agree, plan, expect, refuse, fail, needThe board decided to wait. / They refused to sign.
Advise + person + toadvise / ask / tell somebody to doI advise you to avoid signing now.
After a preposition, always -ingbefore, after, without, instead of + -ingDo not sign before reading the clause.
A verb as the subject-ing at the start of the sentencePostponing the vote is the safest option.
The traprecommend never takes toNot I recommend to wait. Say I recommend waiting.

In one line: recommend, suggest, consider and avoid take -ing; decide, agree, plan and refuse take to.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

The Client Wants an Answer

One conversation, three recordings, one page

Dialogue

The Client Needs a Decision

H
Aguinaldo, my board meets on Thursday. They want to know if we should bid for the port concession.
A
I have read the documents. My recommendation is to bid, but I recommend waiting for the final rule.
H
Waiting is expensive for us. Our competitors plan to bid in the first round.
A
I understand. That is the trade-off. If you bid now, you should avoid accepting the current tariff clause.
H
What is the risk if we accept it?
A
The regulator plans to change the tariff rules. Without a safeguard, you risk losing fifteen percent of the revenue.
H
Okay. Can you put that in a legal opinion? My board will not decide without it.
A
Of course. I will include the risk assessment and one alternative: entering in the second phase.
Comprehension

About Hannah

1. When does Hannah's board meet?
On Thursday.
2. Why does Hannah say waiting is a problem for her company?
Because it is expensive and the competitors plan to bid in the first round.
3. What does Hannah say her board will not do without a legal opinion?
Decide.

JustificationThe project is feasible and the traffic study is solid
Main riskThe regulator plans to change the tariff rules in 2027

The recommendation comes first, in one line. Everything below it explains why, what the client risks and what else the client could do.
1. What does the opinion recommend, and what does it advise against?
It recommends bidding in the first round, and it advises against accepting the current tariff clause.
2. Make one sentence with the alternative, using consider.
"The client should consider entering in the second phase, after the final rule."
3. Why does the recommendation come in the first line?
Because the client wants to know what to do before reading the justification.
