Complementary Activities

Extra material to keep English running in your head between classes. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen. None of them takes more than fifteen minutes.

Lesson 1 -- Your Own Words

Series
Somebody Feed Phil — Netflix

Phil travels to a new city in every episode and spends it meeting strangers. Watch how he opens every single conversation: a name, one sentence about himself, and then a question back.

Tip: watch fifteen minutes with English subtitles. Write down three questions he asks people he has just met — you will use them in class.

Watch on Netflix →
Listening
"Scared to speak English?" — 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on exactly the thing you described: understanding well and freezing when it is your turn to speak. Two presenters, slow and clear.

Tip: listen once with your eyes closed and no subtitles. Then listen again with subtitles and count how many words you missed the first time. The number is usually smaller than it feels.

Watch on YouTube →
Grammar
"How to use present tenses" — 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

The present simple in six minutes, with the third person -s explained where Portuguese speakers lose it.

Tip: after the video, say five true sentences about your own week out loud. Three with I, two with she or he — the ones with the -s are the ones to practise.
