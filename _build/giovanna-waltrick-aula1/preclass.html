Lesson 01 -- Pre-class

Your Own Words -- The Conversation That Starts Everything

Saying who you are, what you study and where you have been. Key words: travel, trip, flight, airport, alone, understand, schedule, course. Structure: present simple for facts and routines.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example out loud. These are the words you need every time somebody asks about your life.

To travel -- to go from one city or country to another
"I travel to the United States with my family."
Trip -- the whole journey, from leaving home to coming back
"My last trip to the United States was in January."
Flight -- the part of a trip that happens on the plane
"The flight from Sao Paulo to Miami takes eight hours."
Airport -- the place where you take a plane
"I always arrive at the airport two hours early."
Alone -- with nobody else
"This time I want to travel alone."
To understand -- to know what a person means when they speak
"I understand English well, but speaking is harder."
Schedule -- the plan of your week, hour by hour
"My schedule is full from Monday to Friday."
Course -- a programme of classes on one subject
"I study Administration and I also do a Fashion course."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Ryan is nineteen and he lives in Denver, in Colorado. He studies Business at a state university. His week is busy: he has classes in the morning, and three afternoons a week he works in a coffee shop near the campus. He is also learning Portuguese, because his grandmother is Brazilian. He understands a lot when people speak slowly, but when he tries to answer, he stops in the middle of the sentence.
Why does the text use "studies", "works" and "stops" with an -s?
A Because the actions happened in the past
B Because the subject is "he" — third person singular
C Because the actions are happening at this exact moment
What does "three afternoons a week he works in a coffee shop" tell you?
A It is a routine — something that repeats
B He is in the coffee shop right now
C He worked there only once

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Present Simple

GRAMMAR

Use the present simple for facts and routines — things that are always true, not only today.

I / you / we / theyverb with no changeI travel every year.
he / she / itverb + -sShe studies fashion.
negativedo / does not + verbHe does not work on Sundays.
questiondo / does + subject + verbDo you travel a lot?

Careful 1: after does the verb loses the -s. "Does she works?" is wrong — it is "Does she work?"

Careful 2: one pronoun per sentence. If you start with she, finish with she.
