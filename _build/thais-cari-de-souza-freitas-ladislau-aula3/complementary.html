Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Jason Fried -- "Why work doesn’t happen at work" (TED)

Fifteen minutes on the calendar as a weapon: why the meetings other people book on your day cost more than the hours they take. He speaks slowly and clearly, and every second sentence has a day, an hour or a place in it.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on and write down every time expression you hear with in, on or at. You will fill half a page.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) -- "How to be on time"

Twenty minutes about clocks, days and the ten minutes nobody plans for. It is the densest source of this lesson’s grammar you will find anywhere: the whole episode is people saying at what time, on what day, in what week.

Tip: the full transcript is on the same page. Read three paragraphs out loud and mark every in, on and at before you press play.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
Nigel Marsh -- "How to make work-life balance work" (TED)

Ten minutes on the hours a person decides to defend, and who ends up designing the day when nobody defends them. It is the argument behind the second recording of this lesson, told by somebody with small children and a full calendar.

Tip: after it ends, say out loud, in English, which hour of your own day you protect, on which days, and why.
