Lesson 15 -- Checkpoint: The Whole Call

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Meetings"

A short episode with the phrases people use to open a meeting, keep it on track and close it. It is the same shape as the call you ran in the benchmark today. Full script on the same page.

Tip: read the script and mark three phrases: one to open, one to bring the meeting back to the agenda, and one to close with a next step. Use them on your next real call.

Talk
Julian Treasure -- "How to speak so that people want to listen" (TED, 10 min)

A talk about the habits that make people stop listening and the tools that make them pay attention: pace, pauses and tone. Useful for a call where your English is good enough and now your voice has to do the rest.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Choose one habit he describes that you recognize in your own calls, and one tool you will try this week.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
Chris Anderson -- "TED's secret to great public speaking" (TED, 8 min)

The head of TED explains why every good talk carries one idea, built step by step from what the listener already knows. A sales call works the same way: one idea, connected to what the prospect told you.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. After watching, say in one sentence the one idea you want a prospect to remember from your next call.
