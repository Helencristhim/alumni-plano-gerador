Lesson 34 -- Pre-class

Feedback for the Team -- Results and Moments

Ten to twenty minutes before class. How to write and say feedback to your team: results until now with the present perfect, moments with a date with the past simple. Key words: a target, to reach, a result, a deadline, to improve, to thank.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A target -- a number or a result that a team wants to reach
"Our target for the quarter is two million in new contracts."
To reach -- to get to a number or a level that you wanted
"The team reached the target one week early."
A result -- the thing that happens because of work you did
"The results of the new model are very good."
A deadline -- the last day or time to finish something
"The deadline for the report is Friday at noon."
To improve -- to become better, or to make something better
"Our response time has improved a lot."
To thank -- to tell a person that you are happy with what they did
"I want to thank the team for the extra hours."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Laura manages a data team in Atlanta. Every month she sends a short email. “This month we have finished the new dashboard, and our clients have used it more than ten thousand times.” These are results until now.

Then she gives the moments: “We launched the dashboard on the 3rd, and we had a small problem on the 10th. Marcos fixed it in two hours.” Each moment has a date, so she uses the past simple.

Why does Laura say "we have finished the new dashboard"?
A Because the dashboard is old.
B Because she is giving a result until now, with no date.
C Because she is talking about next month.
Why does she say "we launched the dashboard on the 3rd"?
A Because the 3rd is a finished moment.
B Because she is asking a question.
C Because the dashboard is not finished.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Result or Moment?

GRAMMAR

A result until now: present perfect. A moment with a date: past simple.

JobFormExample
Result until nowhave / has + third formWe have reached 90% of the target.
Moment with a datepast formWe reached it last quarter.
Not done, but expectedhaven't + third form + yetWe haven't finished the review yet.

Never: We have reached it last quarter · We have lost a client on Monday · We didn’t finish it yet

Stage 2: Put the Conversation in Order

Order

Put this short feedback email in a natural order.

?"We have reached 85% of the target."
?"Thank you all for the great work."
?"Hi team, a quick update about September."
?"We haven't solved the report problem yet, but we are close."
?"We closed two big contracts in the last week of the month."

Stage 3: Pronunciation

Speaking

Listen, then record yourself. Say the contractions: we’ve, haven’t.

We've closed two new contracts this month.
We haven't sent the report yet.
We launched the new dashboard on the third.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer a real speaker would give.

Your manager asks about the target. The month is not over. You say:
A "We have reach 90% of the target."
B "We reached 90% of the target until now."
C "We have reached 90% of the target."
You want to say when you signed a contract. You say:
A "We have sign it on Tuesday."
B "We signed it on Tuesday."
C "We have signed it on Tuesday."
The report is not ready, but it will be soon. You say:
A "We haven't finished the report yet."
B "We didn't finish the report yet."
C "We don't finish the report yet."

Stage 5: Free Production

Reflection

Think, then record your answer. There is no wrong answer here.
