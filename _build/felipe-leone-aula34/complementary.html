Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
LeeAnn Renninger: “The secret to giving great feedback” (5 min)

A cognitive psychologist gives a four-part formula for feedback that people can hear and use.

Tip: use her four parts in your weekly email to the team, then compare it with the email you wrote before.

TED Talk
Joe Hirsch: “The joy of getting feedback” (11 min)

An educator on how to ask for and receive feedback in a way that helps you grow.

Tip: write down two sentences he says in the present perfect and two in the past simple.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Margaret Heffernan: “Dare to disagree” (13 min)

A former CEO on why good teams need people who say what they think, and how to disagree well.

Tip: pause after each story and say, in one sentence, if your team has ever had a moment like that.
