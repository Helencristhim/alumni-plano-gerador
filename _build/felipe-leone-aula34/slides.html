Lesson 34 · Write & Speak

Feedback for the Team

Your team email about the month: results with the present perfect, moments with a date in the past simple. Then you say it without the text.

Retomada

Your Text, Five Marks

Say each sentence in the right form, then make one more sentence of yours with it.

Model

A Feedback Email

Hi team,

This month we have reached 90% of the target, and we have closed two new contracts. We reached the same number last quarter, but this time with fewer people. Thank you.

Some things have not worked yet. Response time has gone up, and we lost one client on the 12th. We talked about it on Friday, and we have already changed the process.

Next month I want us to keep the same focus. Great work, everyone.

Guided Rewrite

Your Sentences, the Right Form

Typical sentences in a feedback email. Say the right form, then rewrite your own sentences below.

#BeforeAfter
1We reached 90% of the target this month (still the same month).We have reached 90% of the target.
2We have reached it last quarter.We reached it last quarter.
3We have lost one client on the 12th.We lost one client on the 12th.
4We already changed the process (result now).We have already changed the process.
5The team grew this year a lot and is still growing.The team has grown a lot this year.

Drill 3

Yet, Already

Answer the question about the month. Full sentence.

#QuestionAnswer pattern
1Have we reached the target yet?Yes, we have already… / No, we haven't… yet.
2Has the new analyst started yet?Yes, she started… / No, not yet.
3Have we sent the report to the client?Yes, we sent it on…
4Has the team finished the review?We have finished…, but…
