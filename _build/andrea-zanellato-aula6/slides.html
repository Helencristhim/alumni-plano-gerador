Chapter 4: The Code

She Was Dancing When...

A background action, and an action that interrupts

Grammar Discovery

Four Sentences From Films

"She was walking in the garden when she saw him."

"They were dancing when the music stopped."

"While the family was having dinner, a letter arrived."

"What were you doing at nine last night?"

Look at the blue verbs. Was walking and were dancing are long actions: the background of the scene. Saw and stopped are short actions that interrupt. Now the red words: when comes before the short action, while before the long one.

JobFormExample
Long action (background)was / were + verb-ingShe was walking in the garden.
I / he / she / itwas + -ingHe was reading a letter.
you / we / theywere + -ingThey were dancing.
Short action (interrupts)past simple...when she saw him.
when / whilewhen + short action · while + long actionWhile they were eating, a letter arrived.
The trapno -ing without was / wereNot she walking. Say she was walking.

In one line: was / were + -ing for the long background action; past simple for the short action that interrupts.
