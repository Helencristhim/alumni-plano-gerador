Lesson 2 -- Four Paragraphs, Four Englishes

Film
Fantastic Fungi (Fungos Fantásticos)

Eighty minutes of biology narrated slowly and clearly, by people who study the thing they are describing. It is the easiest English on this page: the narration is calm, the sentences are short, and the images carry half the meaning, so you can follow it even on a night when your head is full.

Tip: put the English subtitles on and stop once, at any point, to write down two words you did not know. Two is the whole task.

Podcast
Fighting Misinformation With Science Journalism : Short Wave

Short Wave is NPR’s daily science podcast and the episodes are about twelve minutes, which is the point: it fits in the walk to the bus. This one is about what happens to a finding between the paper and the headline, and it is the other half of what you did in class today.

Tip: the page has the full text next to the audio. Listen once without reading it, then read while it plays again, and mark every sentence where the speaker is being careful rather than certain.

Video
How to read a scientific paper

Ten minutes on exactly what you practiced in class, from somebody who does it for a living: which section to open first, which one to skip on the first pass, and how to decide quickly whether a paper deserves your afternoon. Plain spoken English, no jargon beyond the words you already met today.

Tip: watch it with an abstract from your own field open next to it, and do what the video says on that abstract while it plays.
