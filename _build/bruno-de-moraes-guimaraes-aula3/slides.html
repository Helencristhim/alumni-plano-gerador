Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I wake up at five thirty."

"I don't practice on Sunday."

"She wakes up at six thirty."

"She doesn't practice on Sunday."

Two sentences are about I. Two are about she. What changes?

WhoYesNo
I · you · we · theyI wake up early.I don't wake up early.
he · she · itShe wakes up early.She doesn't wake up early.
The trapAfter doesn't, the verb loses the -s: "She doesn't wake up", never "doesn't wakes". The -s is already inside doesn't.
Key ideaOne letter for he and she. One little word to say no. That is all the present simple asks of you.

With this you can tell anybody, in any country, what your day looks like.
