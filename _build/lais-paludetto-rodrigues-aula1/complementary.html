Complementary Activities

Extra material to keep English running in your head between classes. Tick each one when you finish it. None of this is homework you have to be perfect at — it is input, and input is what makes the first week in Burnaby easier.

Lesson 1 -- Countdown to Burnaby

Grammar Video
"Asking Questions" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on the exact thing you practised today: where the helper goes, when the main verb loses its -s, and how yes/no questions are built. Connection to Lesson 1: this is the shape behind every question in the car with Greg.

Tip: pause after each example and say one question about your own life out loud. Two or three is enough — do not turn it into a study session.

Podcast
"What does 'small talk' mean?" -- The English We Speak, BBC Learning English

Three minutes of two British hosts explaining the expression you met today, with real examples of when people use it. Connection to Lesson 1: small talk is what happens in the car, in the school hallway and at the dinner table for your whole first month.

Tip: listen once with your eyes closed and no subtitles. Then listen again and write down the two example sentences they give.

Vlog
"Exchange Students in Canada -- vlog #1" -- EleExplore

Two exchange students film their first days in Canada: the airport, the host family house, the school, the food. Normal speed, normal accents, no teacher voice. Connection to Lesson 1: this is your next month, filmed by somebody who has already been through it.

Tip: watch with no subtitles first and count how many things you understand without the text. Then write down three questions you would ask them if you met them.
