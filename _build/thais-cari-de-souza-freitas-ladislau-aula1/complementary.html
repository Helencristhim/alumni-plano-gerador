Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Rebecca Okamoto -- "How to introduce yourself, and get hired" (TED)

Twelve minutes on the exact problem of this lesson: how to say what you do in twenty words or fewer, so the other person answers "tell me more" instead of nodding politely. Her rule is the one you heard in today’s second recording: say the work, never the title.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Write her five openings down, then write yours next to them.

Podcast
Planet Money (NPR) -- Episode 798, "Bad Credit Bureau"

Your own subject, told as a story: where the idea of scoring a person before you lend to them comes from, and who the real customer of a credit bureau is. Half the words of this lesson turn up on their own: credit, risk, a customer, a policy.

Tip: the full transcript is on the same page. Listen once without it, then read it and listen again.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
Crucible Moments -- "Nubank: An Outsider Upends the Brazilian Banking System"

Founders and early employees telling, in English, the story of the digital bank that changed your market. It is the vocabulary of your own week — customers, credit, risk, growth, an app instead of a branch — spoken by people who lived it.

Tip: listen for every sentence that describes what somebody decides. Write three of them down and say them out loud.
