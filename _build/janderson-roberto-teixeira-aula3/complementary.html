"How to speak so that people want to listen" -- Julian Treasure, TED

Ten minutes from a sound expert on why some people are listened to and others are not. Connection to Lesson 3: he shows, out loud, that pace and pause matter more than vocabulary -- which is good news for you, because pace costs nothing and vocabulary takes years.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and copy one of his warm-up exercises out loud. Thirty seconds before a meeting is enough to change how your voice lands.
