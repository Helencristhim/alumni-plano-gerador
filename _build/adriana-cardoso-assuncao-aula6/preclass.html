How It Was Built

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example out loud. These are the words of a document other countries are going to read.

Milestone -- a point in a project where something important is finished
"The second milestone was reached three weeks late."
Framework -- a structure of rules and steps that other teams can copy
"The framework has been embedded in the onboarding of every manager."
Findings -- what a study or an investigation discovered
"The findings were not the ones anybody expected."
To streamline -- to make a process simpler and faster
"Once the process was streamlined, the results were reproduced."
To embed -- to make something part of how the company normally works
"It is not a project any more. It has been embedded in the process."
Rationale -- the reasoning behind a decision
"The rationale for each decision has to be made explicit."
To standardize -- to make everybody do something the same way
"Nothing had ever been standardized, so every manager had a version."
Case study -- a written description of what one company did and what happened
"The case study has been adopted by three regions."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

The program was designed in Brazil and was never meant to travel. A first version was tested in two sites for six months, and the findings surprised everybody: nothing had ever been standardized, so every manager had his own version of the process. Once the process was streamlined and the framework was written down, the same results were reproduced in the other sites. The document was rewritten twice before it was published, because the rationale for each decision had to be made explicit. It has since been adopted by three regions, and the framework has been embedded in the onboarding of every new manager. Next month the case will be presented at the global conference.
"The program was designed in Brazil." Why does the text not say who designed it?
A Because nobody knows
B Because in a report the action matters more than the person
C Because it is a mistake in the text
In "has been adopted", which word carries the tense?
A has been — the verb be; the participle never changes
B adopted — the participle
C Neither — the passive has no tense
"The rationale had to be made explicit." What kind of structure is this?
A An active sentence with a modal
B A passive with a modal: modal + be + past participle
C A question

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Passive Voice

GRAMMAR

The passive is the right form of be plus the past participle. The tense lives in be; the participle never changes.

presentis / are + participlePolicies are reviewed every year.
pastwas / were + participleThe program was designed in Brazil.
present perfecthas / have been + participleIt has been adopted by three regions.
with a modalmodal + be + participleThe document has to be cut. The talk will be given in English.
the agentby + personIt was designed by a team of three — only when that is part of the information.

Two traps for a Portuguese speaker: half the participles are irregular (written, given, taken, shown, made), and English has no se construction — "fez-se o levantamento" becomes "the survey was carried out", never "it made the survey".
