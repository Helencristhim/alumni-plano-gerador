Chapter 4: Will or Going To

Decided Before, Decided Now

Two ways to talk about the future

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am going to travel for ten days in December."

"It is sold out? OK, I will book another hotel."

"Look at those clouds. It is going to snow."

"I think you will love the coast."

FormUseExample
be going toa plan you decided before you speakI am going to travel in December.
willa decision you make at the moment you speakOK, I will book another hotel.
be going toa prediction with evidence you can see nowLook at those clouds. It is going to snow.
willa prediction from opinion (I think, probably, I am sure)I think you will love it.

One question decides it: did I decide this before, or am I deciding it now? Before: going to. Now: will. Offers and promises are also decisions of the moment: "I will send you the link."
