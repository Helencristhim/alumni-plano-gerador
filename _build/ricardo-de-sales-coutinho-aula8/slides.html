Chapter 3: It Is Big, It Is New

Saying What Something Is Like

is · very · and the word order

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The office is new."

"It is a new office."

"The building is very big."

"It is a quiet floor."

PositionPatternExample
After "is"thing + is + wordThe office is new.
Before the thinga + word + thinga new office — never "an office new"
More of itvery + wordIt is very big.

In Portuguese the word comes after. In English it comes before. That is the whole difference.

Reading

An Email from the Director

Subject: The new office — first information

Hi Ricardo. Here is the first information about the new office in Miami. It is a small office today, and it is very new.

The building is on Brickell Avenue. It is big and modern, with twenty floors. Our floor is quiet, and that is good for calls. The meeting room is small, so we are only six people in the room.

The beach is near — ten minutes by car. Many people go there after work on Friday. The traffic is bad at six in the evening, so we start early.

Your desk is near the window, and the view is the water. See you in Miami.

Email -- Paul Adams, Miami office
