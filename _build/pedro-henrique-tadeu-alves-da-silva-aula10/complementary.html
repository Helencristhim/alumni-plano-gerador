Podcast · American accent · 99% Invisible
99% Invisible -- Sounds Natural

The animal sounds in nature documentaries are almost never recorded in nature. They are made by Foley artists in a small room full of sand, gravel and grass. A perfect example of work that goes unnoticed when it is done well.

Tip: every time the host describes a person, notice the word after the noun: who, which, that or where. Then describe one Foley artist in your own sentence.
