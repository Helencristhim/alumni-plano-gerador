Chapter 4: The Code

For Years, Since 2015

From the past until now

Grammar Discovery

Four Sentences From Profiles

"I have worked in HR for eighteen years."

"She has been the Head of HR since 2019."

"We have used this system since March."

"How long have you lived in Brazil?"

Look at the blue verbs: have / has + past participle, for something that started in the past and continues now. The red words: for + a period of time (eighteen years), since + the starting point (2019, March).

JobFormExample
Past until nowhave / has + past participleI have worked in HR for years.
for+ a period of timefor eighteen years · for six months · for a long time
since+ the starting pointsince 2019 · since March · since I was 25
QuestionHow long have / has + person + past participle?How long have you worked here?
behave / has beenShe has been the Head of HR since 2019.
The trapnot the present simpleNot I work here for ten years. Say I have worked here for ten years.

In one line: have / has + past participle from the past until now; for + a period, since + the start.

1. Turn the experience line into a sentence with since.
I have been the Head of HR since 2019.
2. Which areas does the profile list?
Payroll, benefits, hiring, training and internal communication.
3. Why does the achievement line need a number?
Because recruiters understand a result faster with numbers.
Listening 1 · What a Recruiter Reads

Tom Again

Tom, the recruiter from Miami, explains what he reads first in a profile. Sound first — no text.

1. How long has Tom worked as a recruiter?
For twelve years.
2. What three things does Tom read first?
The headline, the first two lines of the summary and the dates.
3. Why is a profile in English an advantage?
Because he can send it to clients in other countries.
Listening 2 · A Summary Out Loud

Rachel’s Summary

An HR director from Edinburgh reads her profile summary. Sound first — no text.

1. How long has Rachel worked in HR?

2. Since when has she been an HR director?

3. What is her biggest achievement?
They reduced the time to hire from sixty days to thirty.
