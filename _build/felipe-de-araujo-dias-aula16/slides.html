Chapter 4: Who, Which, That, Where

Two Sentences, One Idea

Defining relative clauses

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"We met a taxi driver who told us the history of the city."

"We ate at a pizzeria which has no menu."

"It was the city that surprised me most."

"This is the street where Giulia grew up."

WordUse it forExample
whoPeople.the driver who took us to the hotel
whichThings and animals.a pizzeria which has no menu
thatPeople or things. Very common when you speak.the city that surprised me
wherePlaces. It replaces there.the street where she grew up
No relative wordWhen the next part has its own subject, you can drop it.the hotel (that) we booked

The relative word replaces he, she, it or there. Never say both: the street where she lived there is one place too many.

Grammar Practice

Person, Thing or Place?

Say the full sentence, then click to compare

1. Giulia is the colleague ______ grew up in Naples.
"Giulia is the colleague who grew up in Naples."
2. The funicular is a little train ______ goes up the hill.
"The funicular is a little train which goes up the hill."
3. We stayed in a neighborhood ______ the locals eat out.
"We stayed in a neighborhood where the locals eat out."
4. It was the trip ______ changed my opinion.
"It was the trip that changed my opinion."
