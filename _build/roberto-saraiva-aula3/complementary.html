Lesson 03 -- Where the Market Is Going

Talk
Martin Reeves -- "Your strategy needs a strategy" (TED@BCG)

Twelve minutes on the question behind this whole lesson: how do you decide when a market is predictable enough to plan for, and when it is not. Slow, structured American English, and every example is a company choosing between a bet and a forecast.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. When it ends, say out loud which of his four environments your own category is in, and why.

Podcast
NPR How I Built This -- "Airbnb: Joe Gebbia"

The clearest story you will hear of somebody betting on a shift in behavior before anybody had numbers to prove it. Forty-five minutes of natural American English about demand, competitors and a market that did not exist until somebody decided it would.

Tip: good for the car. Listen for the moment he stops saying what he thinks will happen and starts saying what they are going to do — that is exactly tonight's structure, in the wild.

Talk
Hans Rosling -- "The best stats you've ever seen" (TED2006)

Twenty minutes of a man showing what a real trend looks like when you put fifty years of it on a screen. The vocabulary of this lesson — consumption, demand, shift, growth — used to describe whole countries instead of one category.

Tip: watch it once for pleasure and once with a notebook. Write down five sentences he uses to describe a curve going up, and steal them for your next review.
