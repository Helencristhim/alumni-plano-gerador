"How predictions took over our lives" -- TED Radio Hour, NPR

Fifty minutes on why humans forecast at all, and what gets lost when everything about the future is priced and modelled. Connection to Lesson 15: the speakers hedge every claim about what is coming, which is the exact register you practiced today. The full English transcript sits under the player.

Tip: listen in two sittings and write down five sentences where a speaker marks how sure they are. Then rebuild each one about your own next five years.

"The best stats you have ever seen" -- Hans Rosling, TED

Twenty minutes on reading the trajectory of the world from the data rather than from the headlines. Connection to Lesson 15: he never once predicts a date and a size in the same sentence, and the page carries the full English transcript beside the video.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles and once without. Then say out loud, in one sentence, what his outlook is and how sure he sounds.

Grammar Video
"Future perfect and future continuous" -- English Class, BBC Learning English

A short lesson on the two shapes you stacked today: finished by a future point, and still running at a future point. Connection to Lesson 15: it drills the only difficult half of the rule until choosing between them stops being a decision.

Tip: pause after every example and say your own version out loud BEFORE the presenter does. Use your own June and your own December.
