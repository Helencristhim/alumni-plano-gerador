Complementary Activities

Extra material to reinforce your learning outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen. None of it is homework — one item a week is already a habit.

Lesson 1 -- The Question You Did Not Expect

Grammar Video
"Present Perfect and Past Simple" — 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes and three clear rules for choosing between the two tenses you worked on today. British English, spoken slowly, with the examples written on screen. Connection to Lesson 1: this is the grammar behind "I have worked here since 2023" and "I drafted it last year".

Tip: pause after each rule and say one sentence about your own job. If the sentence contains since or for and the verb is not have, say it again.

Talk
"10 Ways to Have a Better Conversation" — Celeste Headlee, TED

A radio host on what actually makes a conversation work: brevity, honesty, real listening, and the discipline of not preparing your answer while the other person is still talking. Eleven minutes, with an English transcript on the page.

Tip: her rule number nine is the one for you — be brief. Watch it once for pleasure, then pick the two rules you break most often in Portuguese. You will break the same two in English.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
"Working In-House: Perspectives from Corporate Counsel"

American lawyers describing what in-house work is really like — how it differs from a firm, what lands on your desk, and how the legal team sits inside the business. Connection to Lesson 1: it is your job, in their words, and it is where the vocabulary of today came from.

Tip: write down every expression you already knew and every one you did not. The second list is your vocabulary for next week — bring it to class.
