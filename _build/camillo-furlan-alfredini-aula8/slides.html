Chapter 4: The Code

How Much? How Many?

Counting things and money

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"How many customers were there?"

"How many cars are on the lifts?"

"How much profit is there?"

"How much money does he need?"

Look at the word after the orange words. Can you count it: one, two, three? Many or much?

JobFormExample
You can count itHow many + pluralHow many customers? How many cars?
You can't count itHow much + singularHow much money? How much time?
The priceHow much is it? / How much are they?How much is the new car?
Big numbershundred / thousand / million (no -s)four hundred, twelve thousand
Percentnumber + percentI save twenty percent.
The trapmoney is not countableNot How many money. Say How much money.

In one line: count it (customers, cars): how many; can't count it (money, time): how much.
