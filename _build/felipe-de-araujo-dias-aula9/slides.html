Chapter 3: The Diary and the Photograph

Already Arranged, Already Happening

Present continuous for plans, will be doing for moments

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am taking my daughter to her match on Saturday."

"We are having a get-together at one."

"This time next week I will be flying home."

"At nine thirty you will be sitting in the cold."

FormUse it forExample
am / is / are + -ingA fixed arrangement. There is a time, a place and usually another person.I am taking her to the match on Saturday.
will be + -ingA picture of a moment in the future, seen while it is happening.This time next week I will be flying home.
The time expressionThe future continuous almost always needs one: at nine, this time next week, all morning.At ten I will be watching the match.
Not for decisionsA decision taken at this second still takes will: I will get you a ticket.
Negative and questionI am not coming on Friday. Are you going to the game? Will you be working on Monday?

The present continuous is an appointment already in your diary. will be doing is a photograph of a moment that has not arrived yet.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"What do you do this weekend?"
"What are you doing this weekend?"
"This time next week I will fly home."
"This time next week I will be flying home."
"He is arrive on Thursday evening."
"He is arriving on Thursday evening."

The present simple is for what always happens, not for this Saturday. And after will be, the verb needs the -ing or the picture does not move.

Chapter 4: Finding a Window

Two Diaries, One Saturday

Arranging a day with somebody who does not know your city

Dialogue

Two Diaries, One Saturday

L
Felipe, before we start, what are you doing on Saturday?
F
In the morning I am taking my daughter to her match. Kick-off is at nine.
L
Does she play every Saturday?
F
Almost. And after that we are having a get-together at my sister house, so I am not free until four.
L
That is fine. I am not doing anything until the evening. Is the match far?
F
Forty minutes, and I am picking up two of her friends on the way, so it is a full car.
L
I understand. And in the evening? Are you going to the game?
F
I am. I am meeting two friends at seven and the game starts at nine thirty.
L
Perfect. This time next week I will be flying home, so this is my only chance.
F
Then come. I will get you a ticket tomorrow. Where are you staying?
L
In Pinheiros. If I am running late, I will send you a message.
F
Do that. And bring a jacket. At nine thirty in July you will be sitting in the cold for two hours.
Did You Catch It?

About Lars

1. What is Lars doing on Saturday until the evening?
Nothing. He says he is not doing anything until then.
2. Why does he say this is his only chance to see a game?
Because this time next week he will be flying home.
3. Where is he staying, and what will he do if he is running late?
In Pinheiros. He will send Felipe a message.
