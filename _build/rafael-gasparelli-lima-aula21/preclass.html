Lesson 21 -- Pre-class

The Quarterly Report

Get ready to present a report. Key words: output, shipment, harvest, audit, findings, summary, chart, to export. Structure: the passive voice -- the report was prepared, iron is produced.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Eight words you see in every business report. Tap Listen to hear each one.

Output -- the amount that a plant or a company produces
"Our iron output grew by eight percent this quarter."
Shipment -- a load of goods that is sent to a client
"The first shipment was sent to Europe in November."
Harvest -- the time when fruit or crops are picked, or the amount picked
"The mango harvest was very good this year."
Audit -- an official check of a company’s accounts and records
"The annual audit was completed in March."
Findings -- the results or facts that someone discovers in a check
"The findings of the audit were positive."
Summary -- a short text with only the most important points
"Please read the one-page summary before the meeting."
Chart -- a picture that shows numbers, like bars or a circle
"All the numbers are shown in the chart on page three."
To export -- to sell and send products to another country
"Ten percent of our iron is exported to China."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the report summary, then answer the questions.

This is the summary of our quarterly report. The report was prepared by the finance team and was sent to the board on Tuesday. At our plant, iron is produced every day, and this quarter our output grew by eight percent. Most of the iron is sold in Brazil, and ten percent is exported to China.

On the farms, the mango harvest was very good. The fruit was picked in October, and three shipments were sent to Europe. The annual audit was completed in March. The findings were positive: no serious problems were found. All the numbers are shown in the chart on page three.

1. Who prepared the report?
A The board of directors.
B The finance team.
C The client in China.
2. Why does the text say "iron is produced" and not "iron was produced"?
A Because it happens every day -- it is a fact now.
B Because it happened only once, in October.
C Because the plant is closed now.
3. What were the findings of the audit?
A Many serious problems were found.
B The audit was not completed.
C They were positive: no serious problems were found.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Passive Voice

GRAMMAR

Use the passive when the result is more important than the person who did it. It is the language of reports.

FormStructureExample
Present (affirmative)is / are + past participleIron is produced every day.
Past (affirmative)was / were + past participleThree shipments were sent to Europe.
Negativeisn’t / wasn’t / weren’t + past participleThe shipment wasn’t delayed.
QuestionIs / Was + subject + past participle?Was the audit completed on time?
Who did it?by + person (only if important)The report was prepared by the finance team.

Never: the report prepared by... (no be) · the report was prepare (no -ed) · iron are produced · completed from the auditors
