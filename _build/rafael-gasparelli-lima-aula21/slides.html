Chapter 3: Who Did It?

The Result Comes First

In a report, what happened matters more than who did it

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Colored Words Have in Common?

The report was prepared by our finance team.

Iron is produced at our plant every day.

The mangoes were picked in October.

Ten percent of our iron is exported to China.

Look at the verb in each sentence. Who did the action? Is it important?

FormStructureExample
Present passiveam / is / are + past participleIron is produced every day.
Past passivewas / were + past participleThe mangoes were picked in October.
Who did it? (optional)... by + the person or teamThe report was prepared by our finance team.
Negativeisn’t / wasn’t + past participleNo serious problems were found. The shipment wasn’t delayed.
QuestionIs / Was + subject + past participle?Was the audit completed on time?

Use the passive when the result is more important than the person. Never forget be: not “the report prepared”, but “the report was prepared”.

Chapter 4: Inside the Report

The Report Goes to the Board

One call, two recordings, one real document

Dialogue

A Call About the Report

C
Rafael, thank you for the quarterly report. It was sent to the board yesterday, right?
R
Yes. It was prepared by our finance team, and I checked it on Monday.
C
Great. I have a question about the output. How much iron is produced at the plant every month?
R
About fifty thousand tons. Most of it is sold to steel companies here in Brazil.
C
And the fruit? I see the harvest was very good this year.
R
It was. The mangoes were picked in October, and the first shipment was sent to Europe in November.
C
Excellent. Was the audit finished on time?
R
Yes, it was completed in March. The findings are in the summary on page two.
C
Perfect. Can you update the chart before Friday? The board likes to see the numbers.
R
No problem. I will update it on Thursday and send you the new version.
Comprehension

About Claire

1. When was the report sent to the board, according to Claire?
Yesterday.
2. What does Claire say about the harvest?
She says the harvest was very good this year.
3. What does Claire ask Rafael to do before Friday, and why?
To update the chart, because the board likes to see the numbers.
