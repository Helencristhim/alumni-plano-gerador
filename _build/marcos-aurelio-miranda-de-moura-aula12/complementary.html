Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TED -- William Ury, "The walk from "no" to "yes""

A negotiator who has worked on some of the hardest conflicts in the world, explaining why the best proposals leave room for the other side. It is today’s lesson on a much bigger table: keep the door open, and people walk through it with you.

Tip: count the times he says could or might. Then say one of his ideas again in your own words, starting with "We could...".

Series
Succession -- any scene where somebody brings a deal to the family

Watch the difference between the characters who push a proposal and the ones who offer options. The ones who say "we must" almost always lose the room. The ones who say "we could" usually get what they wanted.

Tip: pause after a proposal and say it again as a soft proposal: two options, one risk with might, and a question at the end.

Podcast
NPR Life Kit -- "4 negotiation tactics to try in everyday life"

One of the four tactics is exactly the shape of today’s proposal: instead of a yes-or-no question, offer a menu of options so the other person can choose one. Short, practical, and easy to follow.

Tip: the transcript is on the page. Find the part about the menu of options and use it to plan the two options for your homework.
