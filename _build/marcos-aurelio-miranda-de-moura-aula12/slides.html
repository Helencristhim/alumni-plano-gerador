Do Not Push. Propose.

1. The Words

an option, an alternative, a downside, an upside, a risk, feasible...

2. One Structure

Chapter 3: The Code

We Could, and It Might

What is possible, and what is uncertain

Grammar Discovery

Open the Door, Name the Risk

"We could move one field team from the north to the south."

"Coverage in the south might go up by three points."

"The north might not stay ahead of plan without that team."

"Could we talk about it on Thursday?"

What do the orange words have in common? Look at the verb after could and might. Is there a to? Is there an -s? And which sentences are an idea, and which are a warning?

JobFormExample
An option, a possible ideacould + base verbWe could move a team.
Something uncertain, maybemight + base verbCoverage might go up.
Maybe notmight not + base verbThe north might not stay ahead.
A polite questionCould + subject + base verb?Could we talk on Thursday?
Same form for everybodyno -s, no toI could, she could, it might, they might
The trapnever to, never -sNot we could to hire or it might goes up. Say we could hire, it might go up.

In one line: could opens a door, might warns you about what is behind it, and both take the verb with nothing added.

Listening 1 · A CEO

Must Is a Closed Door

A CEO on how he reacts to proposals. Sound first — no text.

1. What is his first reaction when somebody says "we must do this"?
He looks for the problem with it, because must is a closed door and people want to open closed doors.
2. How many options does he say you should bring, and what should you say about each one?
Two. What each one could do and what might go wrong, and then which one you would suggest, and why.
3. What does he say is "the strange thing" about could and might?
People think you are more confident, not less.
Listening 2 · A Negotiation Coach

Finish With a Question

A coach on could, might, and the end of a proposal. Sound first — no text.

1. According to her, what is could about, and what is might about?
Could is about what is possible: it opens a door. Might is about what is uncertain: it warns you about the other side of the door.
2. Why do you need both in a good proposal?
Could gives the room options, and might shows that you have already thought about the risk.
3. How should a proposal end, and why?
With a question, like could we talk about it on Thursday. It gives the decision back to the room, and a room that decides supports you.

The Options Paper, One Page

OPTIONS PAPER · COVERAGE IN THE SOUTH
prepared by Marcos Aurélio Miranda de Moura · for discussion, not for decision
Option 1 — move a teamno extra cost · +3 to 4 points by March
Downsidethe north might fall behind
Option 2 — hire a teammore expensive · bigger long-run upside
Riskhiring might take two months
Suggestionoption 1 as a pilot, option 2 next year
Next stepCould we discuss it on Thursday?
Every option on this page has a downside or a risk, and both are written down. That is not weakness. It shows the board that the thinking is already done, and the only thing left for them is the choice.
1. Which option is cheaper, and what might it cost the company in another way?
Option one, moving a team, has no extra cost, but the north might fall behind.
2. Why does the paper say "for discussion, not for decision"?
Because it gives options and a suggestion. The choice belongs to the board.
3. According to the note, why are the downsides written on the page?
To show the board that the thinking is already done, so the only thing left for them is the choice.
