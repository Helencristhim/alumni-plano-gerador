Documentary
Formula 1: Drive to Survive — Season 1, Episode 1

A sport where every decision is a first conditional said out loud under pressure: if it rains, we pit; if he holds that gap, we win. Ten episodes of native speakers reasoning about outcomes they cannot control, which is exactly the language you practised today.

Tip: watch one episode with English subtitles and write down five sentences with if. Then mark which are real plans and which are pictures.

Talk
Ruth Chang — “How to make hard choices” (TED)

Fifteen minutes on why the hardest decisions are hard, and why looking for the obviously better option is the wrong move. Her whole argument is built in second conditionals, so you get the grammar and the content at the same time.

Tip: after watching, say her main point out loud in three sentences, and make at least one of them start with If I were.

Podcast
BBC Learning English — “The first and second conditional”

A short, spoken class on exactly the choice you worked on today, with native examples and no written scaffolding. Free, and short enough for the bus.

Tip: pause after every example and say the other version out loud, then explain in one sentence why it would change the meaning.
