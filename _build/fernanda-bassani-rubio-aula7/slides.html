Chapter 3: The Code

Going To, or Will?

A plan decided before, or a decision made now

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Show?

"We are going to visit three new car makers." (decided last month)

"I’m not going to hire anyone new." (decided last month)

"Sure, I’ll send it tomorrow." (decided now, on the call)

"Don’t worry, I will call the customer." (a promise, now)

All four sentences are about the future. Look at the notes in parentheses. When was the decision made — before the call, or during the call?

JobFormExample
A plan, decided befoream / is / are + going to + verbWe are going to visit three car makers.
Negative planam not / isn’t / aren’t + going to + verbI’m not going to hire anyone.
A decision made now, an offerwill (’ll) + verbSure, I’ll send it tomorrow.
A promisewill + verbDon’t worry, I will call the customer.
QuestionAre you going to...? / Will you...?Are you going to go to the trade show?
The trapno to after willNot I will to send it. Say I will send it.

In one line: going to for plans you made before; will for decisions, offers and promises you make now.

Chapter 4: The Planning Call

The Planning Call

One call, two voices, one plan

Dialogue

The Planning Call

M
Fernanda, thanks for joining. So, what are your plans for next quarter?
F
Well, our main goal is to expand in Argentina. We are going to visit three new car makers there.
M
Great. Are you going to go to the trade show in São Paulo?
F
Yes, we are. We are going to have a small stand this year.
M
Good. And the budget? You know it is not very big.
F
I know. I’m not going to hire anyone new. The team is going to do a training session instead.
M
That sounds good. Could you send me the plan in writing?
F
Sure, I’ll send it tomorrow morning.
M
Perfect. I’m looking forward to reading it.
Comprehension

About Mark

1. What does Mark ask about first?
Fernanda’s plans for next quarter.
2. What does Mark say about the budget?
That it is not very big.
3. What does Mark ask Fernanda to send?
The plan, in writing.
