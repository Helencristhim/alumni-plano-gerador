Grammar Discovery

More than OK, or not enough?

The presentation was too long.
The slides had too many words.
Your part was clear enough.
We didn’t have enough time for questions.

Too + adjective = more than OK — a problem. Adjective + enough = OK, the right amount.

Too goes BEFORE the adjective: too long. Enough goes AFTER the adjective (clear enough) but BEFORE the noun (enough time). With nouns: too many slides, too much information.

The Pattern

Where does it go?

Form

more than OK, with a noun
"too many slides" · "too much text"
adjective + enough
OK, the right amount
"It was clear enough."
enough + noun
the right amount of something
"We didn’t have enough time."

Too = a problem. For a compliment, say very: "very clear", not "too clear".
