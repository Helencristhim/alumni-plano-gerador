Little Women (2019) -- official trailer

Jo March writes, rewrites and defends her own stories. If you want to see a character who is a writer -- with plot, chapters and imagination -- it is this one.

Tip: watch the trailer twice. The second time, describe out loud what Jo is doing in each shot: "She is writing", "She is running".

BBC Learning English -- What makes a good story? (6 Minute English)

Neil and Georgina talk about storytelling: what turns a plot into a good story, and what makes a character stay with you. The vocabulary of the class in a real conversation.

Tip: after listening, describe your own story idea in three sentences using scene, character and plot.
