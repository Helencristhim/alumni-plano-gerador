Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English — "How do we adapt to the cold?"

Six minutes on what a body does when the situation changes and refuses to change back. It is the word of this lesson used in a completely different place, which is the best way to learn what a word actually means.

Tip: listen once, then find every sentence in the transcript about getting used to something. Say two of them out loud about your own first month abroad.

Talk
TED — Matt Cutts, "Try something new for 30 days" (3 min)

Three minutes on what happens to a routine when you deliberately break it, from somebody who did it thirty days at a time for years. Short, plain and directly about the thing you are trying to keep now that you are home.

Tip: it is three minutes, so watch it twice. Then say one true sentence with "I used to" and one with "I am used to", about the same habit.

Video
TED on YouTube — Dan Pink, "The puzzle of motivation" (18 min)

A researcher arguing that what makes people keep going is not what companies and schools assume. It is the other half of self-discipline: not making yourself work, but building a situation that does not need you to.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then answer out loud: which of your habits from Canada survived because of discipline, and which survived because the situation carried them?
