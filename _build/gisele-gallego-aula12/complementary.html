Lesson 12 -- Reading the Program

Film
All About Eve (1950) -- Joseph L. Mankiewicz, with Bette Davis and Anne Baxter

The classic film about the theater from the inside. A young fan becomes the understudy of a great Broadway star, and one night she gets her chance to steal the show. Start with the three-minute trailer, then watch the whole film when you can.

Tip: in the trailer, listen for the words understudy, play and audience. Then watch the film with English subtitles, not Portuguese.

Podcast
With COVID outbreaks, Broadway's understudies take center stage -- NPR (4 min)

A short radio story about the actors who learn a role and wait. In one winter, a lot of them went on stage almost every night, and Hugh Jackman stopped a curtain call to thank them.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Listen once with the page closed, then read it and count how many times you hear understudy and curtain call.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
A Day in the Life: An Actor on His Very First Day on Broadway -- Playbill

The magazine that makes the playbill follows one actor from the morning to the curtain call of his first show: the stage door, the dressing room, the nerves and the applause.

Tip: watch it once with no subtitles and write down every theater word you recognize. Then watch it again and listen for many, much, a few and a little.
