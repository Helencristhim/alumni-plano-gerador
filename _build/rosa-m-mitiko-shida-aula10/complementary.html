"Dare to disagree" -- Margaret Heffernan, TED

Thirteen minutes about the colleague who tells you what nobody else will, and why organizations need that person. Connection to Lesson 10: she is describing one of the people on your real map, the one whose value never appears on any org chart.

Tip: watch it once with English subtitles. Then name, out loud, the one person at your company who plays that role, in a single sentence with a relative clause.

Grammar Video
"Defining relative clauses" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the clause that tells you which person or thing is meant. Connection to Lesson 10: this is the version without commas, which is the one you use every time you identify somebody on a call.

Tip: pause after each example and say the same sentence about a real colleague. Then say it again with commas and notice how the meaning changes. That contrast is the whole lesson.
