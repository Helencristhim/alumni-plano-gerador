Series
Somebody Feed Phil — Season 1, Episode 1, "Bangkok"

An American in his sixties lands somewhere he has never been, eats everything and talks to strangers all day. He is not a travel expert and he does not pretend to be one — which is exactly why every conversation in it is the kind you will actually have at a table abroad.

Tip: count how many times he opens a conversation with Have you ever…? or answers with I have never…. Write down two of them with the words around them, and use one this week.

Podcast
NPR Life Kit — "Want a trip of a lifetime? Try traveling solo"

American hosts and guests on what changes when you travel alone: how you choose a destination, what you do with the hours nobody planned for you, and what happens the evening loneliness shows up. Normal conversational speed, and the full transcript sits on the same page.

Tip: listen once with the transcript closed. Then open it and find three sentences built on have plus a third form. Read those three out loud at the speaker's speed, not slower.

Rachel's English — "JJ sound in 'Could You?' — American English Pronunciation"

Two words, one sound. She takes apart what an American mouth really does to could you and what you, and it is the same move that turns have you into the single sound you heard in class today.
