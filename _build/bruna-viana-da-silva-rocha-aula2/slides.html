Everybody Has Been There

Closing

One Minute, No Notes

Somebody at the next table asks: “So, where have you been?” You have one minute. Go.

I can talk about a trip without saying when it happened.
I can ask somebody else what they have done, at full speed.
I can keep a conversation with a stranger going for five minutes.
