Chapter 4: The Code

Avoid Watching, Decide to Stop

Which verbs take -ing, and which take to

Grammar Discovery

Watching or To Watch?

"Some people avoid starting a new series during the week."

"Others decide to watch only one episode a night."

"If you enjoy watching series, watch them in English."

"Streaming companies want you to keep watching."

Look at the red verbs, then at the blue words after them. After avoid and enjoy, the next verb ends in -ing. After decide and want, it comes with to. In sentence 4, look again: keep is followed by -ing too.

JobFormExample
Verb + -ingenjoy, avoid, finish, keep, mind + -ingI avoid watching series at night.
Verb + towant, decide, need, plan, hope, would like + toI decided to stop after one episode.
After a prepositionof, about, at, before, after + -ingI’m tired of waiting for the next season.
Stop + -ingstop doing something: you do not do it anymoreI stopped watching This Is Us.
Stop + tostop to do something: you pause in order to do itI stopped to make some popcorn.
The trapavoid and enjoy never take toNot I avoid to watch. Say I avoid watching.

In one line: enjoy, avoid, keep, finish + -ing; want, decide, need, plan + to.

Listening 1 · A Psychologist

Why Your Brain Wants More

A British psychologist explains what happens in the brain when an episode ends. Sound first — no text.

1. What happens in the brain at the end of a cliffhanger, according to him?
The brain feels that the story is not finished, so it wants to continue.
2. Why does he say autoplay is so powerful?
Because you do not need to decide to continue; you only need to do nothing.
3. What is his simple advice?
Turn off autoplay and decide before you start how many episodes you will watch.
Listening 2 · A Sitcom Fan

The Show I Always Rewatch

An American woman explains why she keeps rewatching the same old sitcom. Sound first — no text.

1. How many times has she watched her favorite sitcom?
About six times.
2. Why does she rewatch it after a hard day?
Because she knows every joke and she does not need to think; it helps her relax.
3. Why does she avoid starting new series during the week?
Because new series are too addictive, and she needs to sleep.
Listening 3 · An English Teacher

How Series Taught Me English

An English teacher from India talks about how she learned with series and subtitles. Sound first — no text.

1. How did she watch series at first?
With subtitles in her own language.
2. What did she decide to change, and what happened at the start?
She decided to use English subtitles; at the start it was hard and she understood very little.
3. What happened after a few months?
She stopped needing the subtitles for most of the dialogue.
