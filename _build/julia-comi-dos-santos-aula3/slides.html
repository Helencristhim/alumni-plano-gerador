Did You Catch It?

About Rafa’s weekend

1. What time did they plan to leave, and what time did they leave?
They planned to leave at one, but they left at four.
2. Why did they go back?
Because his brother forgot his bag.
3. What did Rafa forget about?
The assignment — it is due tomorrow.
Listening 1

A weekend that went wrong

1. What did she forget?
Her keys — inside the house.
2. How long did they wait?
Two hours, until her uncle arrived with the spare key.
3. How does she feel about the weekend now?
She enjoyed it in the end — she says it was the best part of the month.
Listening 2

What happened at the fair

1. How many students went to the careers fair?
Almost four hundred.
2. What surprised the organisers?
That the small companies had the longest queues.
3. What do they want from the students now?
A short answer to the survey, before Friday.
