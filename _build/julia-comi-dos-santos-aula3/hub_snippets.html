Stage 1.1: Story Words

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Eight words, two minutes.

Arrive — to get to a place
"We arrived at the beach house at midnight."
Relax — to stop working and rest
"I relaxed all Sunday and I regret nothing."
Enjoy — to like something while it is happening
"I really enjoyed the weekend."
Hang out — to spend free time with people, with no plan
"We hung out at the beach all afternoon."
Traffic — all the cars on the road at the same time
"The traffic on Friday was terrible."
Beach house — the house your family keeps by the sea
"We drove to the beach house on Friday night."
Plan — to decide what you are going to do before you do it
"We planned everything and it still went wrong."
Forget — to not remember something
"I forgot my charger. Again."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the message. Then answer the questions.

Last weekend was strange. We planned to leave at one, but my brother forgot his bag, so we went back and left at four. The traffic was terrible and we arrived at the beach house at half past ten. On Saturday we hung out on the beach all afternoon and I relaxed for the first time this semester. I really enjoyed it. On Sunday I did not open my laptop once, and I forgot about the deadline completely. It is due tomorrow.
Why did they leave at four instead of one?
A Her brother forgot his bag and they went back.
B The traffic was terrible.
Why is it "I did not open" and not "I did not opened"?
A Because "open" is irregular.
B Because "did" is already the past, so the verb goes back to normal.
What happened on Sunday?
A She did not open her laptop and forgot the deadline.
B She worked on the assignment.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Past simple — it happened, and it is over

Same frame as always. Only the verb box changes — and the S from lesson 2 is gone.

Regular — add -ed

arrive → arrived
relax → relaxed
enjoy → enjoyed
plan → planned

Irregular — new word

go → went
forget → forgot
hang out → hung out
be → was / were

+ We arrived at midnight. (I, she, they — all the same)
− I did not open my laptop. (after did, the verb goes back to normal)
? Did you enjoy the weekend? (not "Did you enjoyed")

When: yesterday · last weekend · last night · two days ago · on Friday

Stage 4: In the conversation

Quiz
It is Monday. Someone asks about your weekend. Which answer works best?
A "Beach. Good."
B "We went to the beach house on Friday, and we arrived really late. What about you?"
Which question is correct?
A "Did you went to the beach?"
B "Did you go to the beach?"
You want to say the traffic was the problem. Which is correct?
A "The traffic was terrible, so we arrived at ten."
B "The traffic were terrible, so we arrive at ten."

Stage 5: The story of your weekend

Reflection

Then turn the paper over and record 30 seconds telling the story in order — without reading. Use "and then" to join at least two parts.
