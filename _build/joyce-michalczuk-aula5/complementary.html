TED WorkLife with Adam Grant: “Is it safe to speak up at work?”

An organizational psychologist on why people wait too long to raise a problem, and what changes when a team makes it safe to flag a risk early. Laura against Mike, with research.

Tip: listen on Monday on your way to the office. Then decide: in your company, is it safer to be Laura or Mike? Bring one sentence from the episode to class.
