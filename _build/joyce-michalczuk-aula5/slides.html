Real Speed

Thirteen or Thirty?

One syllable of stress changes the number on the screen. Listen, then say it back.

We closed thirteen entities, not thirty.
The difference is fifteen thousand, not fifty.
I will send the file by the eighteenth.
Revenue went up by eighty thousand.

The Status Board

Done, Not Done, The Risk

One sentence per column. Always in this order.

Done
What we have already finished.
Not done yet
What is still pending, and why.
The risk
What could go wrong, and when you will know.
The date
By when you will deliver.
