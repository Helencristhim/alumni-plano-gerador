Inside the Mind of a Master Procrastinator -- Tim Urban

A very funny talk about deadlines: why some people only start working when the date is close, and what happens to the plans that have no deadline at all. Perfect for somebody who lives between a TV calendar and an online course.

Tip: after the talk, say three things about your own week: one plan with going to, one arrangement with the present continuous, and one promise with will.

Song Exploder -- Stranger Things (Main Title Theme)

Kyle Dixon and Michael Stein explain how they made the theme of a TV series: the first ideas, the synths, and the decisions they made before the show went on air. A real look at music made for a television calendar.

Tip: pause twice and predict what they are going to do next with the track. Then listen and check your prediction.

Video · American accent · TED, 7 min
The Psychology of Your Future Self -- Dan Gilbert

A short talk about why we are bad at predicting who we will be in ten years. A good companion for a lesson about plans and predictions, and a good question for somebody who is starting a new course.

Tip: write two sentences: “In five years, I think I will...” and “Next year, I am going to...”. Which one is a plan, and which one is an opinion?
