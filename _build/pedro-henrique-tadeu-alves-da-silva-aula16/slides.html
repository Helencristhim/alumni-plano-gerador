Chapter 4: The Code

A Plan, a Date, a Promise

Three kinds of future. Three forms.

Grammar Discovery

When Was It Decided?

"This year, they are going to record all the backing tracks in their own studio."

"The band is coming in on the fifteenth."

"The music director did not wait. ‘I’ll call the studio myself.’"

"The last ones will probably arrive a few days late."

Look at the red words. Which plan was made before? Which one has a date and other people? Which one was decided at that moment?

JobFormExample
A plan you already haveam / is / are going to + verbWe are going to record everything in-house.
An arrangement: a date, a place, other peopleam / is / are + verb-ingThe band is coming in on the fifteenth.
A decision at this moment, an offer, a promisewill (’ll) + verbI’ll call the studio. I’ll send it tonight.
An opinion about the futurewill + verb, often with probably, I think, I’m sureThey will probably arrive late.
A prediction from what you can see nowam / is / are going to + verbLook at the calendar: we are going to fall behind.
Negativewon’t · am not going to · am not + -ingI won’t be late. We are not recording on Sunday.
The trapwill + base verb, never will + toNot I will to send, not I will going to. Say I will send or I am going to send.

In one line: ask when it was decided. Before, with a date and people: -ing. Before, only in your head: going to. Right now, or a promise: will.

Listening 1 · The Busiest Week

They Will All Be Ready

A vocal coach on the week before a live show. Sound first — no text.

1. What is she doing on Monday?
She is meeting the six singers who are still in the competition, forty minutes each, one by one.
2. Why is Friday tentative?
It depends on the week. If everything is on track, the singers are going to get the day off; if not, they will use Friday to fix what is still not working.
3. What is she going to do about the singer with a sore throat?
She is going to keep her rehearsals short.
Listening 2 · A Realistic Plan

Three Mornings a Week

A man with a TV job on the online course he is starting. Sound first — no text.

1. Why is he not going to study every night?
Because he has a full-time job in television, and that plan never works.
2. When is he going to study?
Three mornings a week, before work, from six to eight.
3. What is he doing on a Sunday, and why on a Sunday?
He is recording with a friend who plays the drums, because Sunday is the only day the studio is free.
