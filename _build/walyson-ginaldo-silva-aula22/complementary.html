Lesson 22 -- Idioms in Business

Talk
Margaret Heffernan -- "Dare to disagree" (TED)

A business leader explains why the best teams discuss and disagree openly. The language of meetings and projects, full of natural business expressions.

Tip: write down every idiom you hear, with the whole sentence, and check the meaning after the talk.

Talk
Simon Sinek -- "How great leaders inspire action" (TED)

One of the most famous business talks. Clear, energetic English about companies that stay ahead of the curve.

Tip: notice the sentences about what companies have been doing for years, and repeat two of them aloud.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
Tim Harford -- "How frustration can make us more creative" (TED)

An economist shows how problems force people to think outside the box. Good for idioms about plans that fail and start again.

Tip: find a moment in the talk where someone goes back to the drawing board, and describe it in one sentence.
