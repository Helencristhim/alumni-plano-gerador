Chapter 4: The Code

Who Stands at the Front of the Sentence

The fact, or the author

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The concession was granted in twenty twenty-one."

"The agreement is governed by Brazilian law."

"The report has been submitted and the findings are pending."

"The discrepancy is being reviewed by the technical team."

In three of these, who did the action? Does the sentence tell you — and does it need to?

FormWhenExample
Present passive
is / are + past participle		a permanent fact or a ruleThe agreement is governed by Brazilian law.
Past passive
was / were + past participle		a finished event, usually with a dateThe concession was granted in 2021.
Present perfect passive
has / have been + past participle		done, with the result still openThe report has been submitted.
Present continuous passive
is being + past participle		happening right nowIt is being reviewed by the team.
The agent, with byonly when the identity matters: "verified by an accredited body". If the reader needs the by and it is missing, that is a defect in the report, not a style choice.
Why it existsa record must stay true after everybody who wrote it has left. So the sentence is built around the action, not around the author.

Sound Check

Say It Clearly

Stacked auxiliaries · the sentences of this lesson

Why this one is hard for a Portuguese speaker

The shape: a passive sentence puts two or three small words in a row before the meaning arrives: has been submitted, is being reviewed.

In English: those small words shrink and join. The stress lands on the participle, which is where the information is.

Why it matters for listening: this is the hardest stretch of a regulatory call, because the part that carries the meaning arrives last and everything before it is almost inaudible.

Chapter 5: Real Speed

The Call and the Reason

Three open points, and why the person disappears from the page

Listening 1

The Verifier Calls First

Stefan lists his three points before writing. Sound first, no text.

1. What does he say at the very start, and why does it change the tone of the call?
That nothing is a problem yet. It turns the call into a review, not an accusation.
2. What is the likely explanation for the discrepancy, and what does he still require?
Probably a rounding question, but it has to be explained in writing.
3. Why is the third point the one that will take time?
Because the baseline study was carried out before the current methodology came into force, so parts of it may have to be redone.
Listening 2

Why the Person Disappears

A short explanation of how regulatory documents are written. Sound first, no text.

1. What do you almost never read in a regulatory report?
That the technical team measured the area, or that the manager approved the change: the person disappears.
2. What is the reason given, and why is it not evasion?
A report is a record of facts that must stay true after everybody who wrote it has left the company, so the sentence is built around the action.
3. What is the exception, and what happens when it is missing?
When the identity matters it comes back with by. If the reader needs it and it is not there, that is a defect in the report.
