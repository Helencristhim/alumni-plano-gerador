The Frame

Look at the second verb

I enjoy studying in the morning.
I want to work in marketing.
I avoid speaking English on the phone.
I decided to apply for both.

Some verbs take -ing. Some take to.

There is no rule to work out — it is two lists. What helps: the -ing group looks at something that already happens; the to group looks forward.

The Frame

Two lists, not a rule

+ verb-ing

I enjoy studying
I avoid speaking
I keep saying
I do not mind working

+ to + verb

I want to work
I decided to apply
I promised to send
I tend to leave it late

There is no rule here. It is two lists.

What helps is the meaning: the -ing group looks at something that already happens. The to group looks forward, at something not done yet.

like, love and prefer take both: "I like studying" = "I like to study".

Listening 1

Habits and goals

1. What does she avoid doing, and why?
Speaking English on the phone — she cannot see the person’s face.
2. What did she decide to do about it?
To make one phone call a week in English, even a short one.
3. What does she want to improve this year?
Speaking without translating in her head first.
Listening 2

The mentoring programme

1. What do you have to write in the form?
Three things: what you enjoy doing, what you want to learn, and what you do not mind doing.
2. How long does the programme last?
Four months, with one meeting every two weeks.
3. What do they say about the third question?
It is the one mentors read most carefully — it shows how you work.
