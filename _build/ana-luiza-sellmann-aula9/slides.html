Chapter 3: The Code

Everything Takes One Step Back

The same move you already know from imagined situations

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am leaving." → She said she was leaving.

"We will review it." → They said they would review it.

"I have handed in my notice." → She said she had handed it in.

"Can you wait?" → They asked whether we could wait.

The words on the left were actually said. Look at what happened to each verb on the right.

They saidYou reportExample
present → pastis / are → was / were
work → worked		She said she was tired.
will → wouldcan → could
may → might		He said he would call.
have done → had donedid → had doneShe said she had sent it.
say or tell?tell needs a person right after it: "She told me that..." say does not: "She said that..." Never "she told that".
QuestionsNo question mark and normal word order: "He asked what the timeline was", never "what was the timeline". For yes or no questions use if or whether.
Other useful verbsmention, explain, confirm, suggest, ask — they make you sound far more precise than repeating "said" five times.

If the information is still true right now, you can leave the tense alone: "She just said the concept is approved." Both are correct.

The Trap

Say, Tell or Ask?

SAY — NO PERSON AFTER IT

"She said she was leaving in September."

TELL — ALWAYS A PERSON RIGHT AFTER

"She told me she was leaving in September."

ASK — FOR QUESTIONS AND REQUESTS

"They asked whether we could wait until November."

Tell needs somebody. Say does not. That single test solves almost every case.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"She told that she was leaving."
"She told me that she was leaving."
"He asked me what was the timeline."
"He asked me what the timeline was."
"They said they will review the budget in October."
"They said they would review the budget in October."

After tell there is always a person. A reported question uses normal word order and no question mark. And after a past reporting verb, will becomes would.

Comprehension

About Dante

1. Which version did Dante hear, and from whom?
From Marcos, who said she was moving because of a man.
2. Why did he believe it?
Because Marcos said it as if he were repeating her, almost word for word.
3. What does he admit at the end?
That he should have asked Julia directly instead of repeating a rumor.
Listening

Before Somebody Else Tells You

Two versions, and only one of them is true. Sound first — no text.

1. What did Julia tell her on Saturday?
That she was leaving the country in September, and that she had already handed in her notice.
2. What did she ask her not to do?
Not to tell anybody until she had spoken to her parents.
3. What did Marcos say, and is it true?
That she was moving because of a man. It is nonsense — she never said that.
Listening 2

The Meeting You Missed

Daniel, before the minutes arrive. Sound first — no text.

1. What did the client say about the concept and the budget?
They said they loved the concept, and explained that the budget would be reviewed in October.
2. What did they ask, and what did Laura answer?
They asked whether the launch could be held until November. Laura told them it could.
3. What did Daniel promise, and by when?
He said they would send a follow-up by Wednesday.
