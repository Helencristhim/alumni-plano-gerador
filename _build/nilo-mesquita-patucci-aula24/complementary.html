Lesson 24 -- Block 2 Simulation: The Mini Conference

TED Talk
Chris Anderson -- "TED's secret to great public speaking" (TED)

The head of TED explains the one thing that all great talks have in common: one clear idea. Connection to Lesson 24: it is the key message of your own talk, said in the first thirty seconds.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then write your key message in one sentence that starts with "What I want to stress is that...".

Grammar Video
"Cleft sentences" -- Arnel's Everyday English

A clear lesson on cleft sentences, with a short test at the end. Connection to Lesson 24: it is the emphasis code of today, What... is, It was... that.

Tip: do the test at the end of the video. Then make two cleft sentences about your work at Corinthians.
