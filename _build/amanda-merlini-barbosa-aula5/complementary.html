Documentary
Kiss the Ground (2020)

Ninety minutes on soil, farming and carbon, narrated slowly and clearly. It is the bridge between the sector you know and the market you are moving into, and half the vocabulary of this lesson is in it.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down every sentence where somebody explains a technical idea to a non-specialist. That is the skill of role-play 2.

Podcast
The Climate Question (BBC) -- How do we save Africa's forests?

Thirty minutes on forests, the carbon they store and who pays to keep them standing. The English is clear and unhurried, and the subject is the one on your desk from Monday.

Tip: listen once without stopping. Then listen again and write down three sentences you could use yourself when somebody asks why the company is buying into a forest.

Video
Sarah Milne: Beyond Carbon Credits

A short, critical look at what happens on the ground when a carbon project arrives. Useful precisely because it is not an advertisement: these are the questions the other side of your table will ask.

Tip: watch it and write down the strongest objection she makes. Then answer it out loud, in one sentence, as if you were the lawyer for the project.
