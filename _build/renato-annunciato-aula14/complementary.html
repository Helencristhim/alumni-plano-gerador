Lesson 14 -- The Elevator Pitch

Film
Air (2023)

The story of how a small team at Nike convinced the family of a young Michael Jordan to sign with them, when the competition was bigger and richer. The film builds up to one long pitch in a meeting room. Watch how it starts with a hook, says what makes the team different, and ends with a clear ask.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. During the big pitch scene, pause after each part and name it: hook, who we are, what is different, or the ask.

Podcast
How I Built This -- Spanx: Sara Blakely

A free episode from NPR. Sara Blakely tells how she sold an idea with no money, no experience and no contacts, and how she got ten minutes with a buyer from a big department store and used them well. It is a real story about a pitch that changed a company.

Tip: listen once without the transcript, then again with it. Write down how she got the buyer's attention, and compare it with your own hook.

YouTube
Patrick Dang -- Create the Perfect Elevator Pitch and Sales Pitch

A sales trainer explains how to build a short pitch, with examples and a simple template. Most of his advice matches today's four moves: start with the problem or the result, say what you do differently, and finish with a small next step.

Tip: watch it once with subtitles. Then use his template with your own team and say your pitch out loud in under sixty seconds.
