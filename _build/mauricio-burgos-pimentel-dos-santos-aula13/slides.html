Grammar Discovery

What you can — and could — say

I can’t give you the exact figure, but I can tell you the trend.
Five years ago, my team couldn’t answer clients in one day.
Last quarter we were able to cut the waiting time in half.
Could you give me an example?

Can = now. Could = in the past, in general. Was / were able to = you did it, one time.

All three are followed by the bare verb. Could you ...? is also the polite way to ask for something. For a tough question, put the two together: "I can’t ..., but I can ..."

Listening 1

Three steps for a tough question

1. What is the first step?
Say what you cannot answer — do not hide it.
2. What is the third step?
Offer to get back to the person, with a day.
3. What do people in the room expect?
That you are honest about what you know — not that you know everything.
