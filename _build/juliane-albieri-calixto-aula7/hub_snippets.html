Decided in Threes -- How Decisions Are Made

You explain how decisions are made in your team. Key words: a tech lead, a product manager, a trade-off, a requirement, feasible, to prioritize, a roadmap, the scope, to push back, a consensus. Structure: passive voice in the present and the past.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A tech lead -- the person who leads the technical decisions of a development team
"The tech lead says if an idea is possible to build."
A product manager -- the person who decides what a product team builds first and why
"The product manager owns the roadmap."
A trade-off -- a choice where you get one good thing but lose another
"Speed or quality? Every decision is a trade-off."
A requirement -- something that a product must have or must do
"Security is a requirement for every feature."
Feasible -- possible to do in the real world, with the time and money you have
"The idea is great, but it is not feasible this year."
To prioritize -- to decide which things are most important and do them first
"We prioritize the problems of the users with no limit."
A roadmap -- a plan that shows what a team will build and when
"The new flow was added to the roadmap."
The scope -- the limits of a project: what is in it and what is not
"We reduced the scope to finish on time."
To push back -- to say no to an idea or a request, or to argue against it
"The tech lead pushed back on the animation."
A consensus -- an agreement of everybody in a group
"In the trio, decisions are made by consensus."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

In Juliane’s area, every decision is made by three people: the designer, the tech lead and the product manager. The price of the card is set by the product team.

Last month, an animation was proposed by the design team. The tech lead pushed back: it was not feasible by the deadline. After a short discussion about the trade-off, the animation was cut from the scope.

The rest of the feature was approved by consensus, and it was added to the roadmap.

1. Who is the price of the card set by?
A By the tech lead.
B By the users.
C By the product team.
2. Why was the animation cut from the scope?
A Because the users did not like it.
B Because it was not feasible by the deadline.
C Because the designer forgot it.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A “The feature was approved by consensus.”
B “The feature was approve by consensus.”
C “The feature approved by consensus.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Passive Voice -- Present and Past

GRAMMAR

Use the passive when the thing (the decision, the price, the flow) is more important than the person. Present: is / are + third form. Past: was / were + third form. Add by + the person only if it matters.

JobFormExample
Present: how things are doneis / are + third formDecisions are made in a trio.
Past: how one thing was donewas / were + third formThe flow was approved last week.
Who did it (optional)by + the person...by the product team.
Negativeisn’t / wasn’t + third formThe idea wasn’t chosen.
QuestionsHow is / was ... + third form?How are decisions made?
The trapthe third form, never the simple verbNot is make, was choosed. Say is made, was chosen.

Never: It is make by three people · The price is setted · The flow approved last week · It was choosed
