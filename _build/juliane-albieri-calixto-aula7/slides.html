Chapter 3: The Code

Made, Set, Approved

When the decision is more important than the person

Grammar Discovery

The Decision Is the Hero

"Every decision is made by three people."

"The price of the card is set by the product team."

"The new flow was approved last week."

"The idea wasn’t chosen because it wasn’t feasible."

Look at the beginning of each sentence: every decision, the price, the new flow, the idea. The sentence starts with the thing, not with the person. Now the blue verbs: is or was + the third form (made, set, approved, chosen). If you want to say who did it, use the red word: by.

JobFormExample
Present: how things are doneis / are + third formDecisions are made in a trio.
Past: how one thing was donewas / were + third formThe flow was approved last week.
Who did it (optional)by + the person...by the product team.
Negativeisn’t / wasn’t + third formThe idea wasn’t chosen.
QuestionsHow is / was ... + third form?How are decisions made?
The trapthe third form, never the simple verbNot is make, was choosed. Say is made, was chosen.

In one line: is / are or was / were + third form; add by + the person only if it matters.

Chapter 4: How It Is Done

How It Is Done

One conversation, two recordings, one decision record

Dialogue

Raj Wants to Know

R
Juliane, I’m curious. In my company in Pune, most decisions are made by the product manager alone. How are decisions made here?
J
Here, every decision is made by three people: the designer, the tech lead and the product manager.
R
Three people? Doesn’t that take forever?
J
Sometimes. But each person brings something. I bring the user, the tech lead says if it’s feasible, and the product manager decides how we prioritize.
R
And if you don’t agree?
J
Then we talk about the trade-off. Last month, my animation was cut from the scope. The tech lead pushed back, and he was right.
R
Ha! That happens to me every week. So, in the end, is it a consensus?
J
Most of the time, yes. And when a decision is made, it’s added to the roadmap, with the requirements.
R
I like it. I’m here for two weeks to work on our loan app. Can I join your next trio meeting?
J
Of course. It’s on Thursday morning.
Comprehension

About Raj

1. How are most decisions made in Raj’s company?
By the product manager alone.
2. How often does Raj lose a design idea, like the animation?
Every week, he says.
3. Why is Raj in São Paulo?
To work on their loan app, for two weeks.

Listening 2 · How the Roadmap Is Made

Priya Explains

Priya, the product manager from Bangalore, explains how her team’s roadmap is made. Sound first — no text.

1. How often is the roadmap reviewed?
Every three months.
2. What is every idea compared with?
The goals of the company and the data from users.
3. What happens to ideas that are not chosen?
They are kept in a list, and some come back later.
