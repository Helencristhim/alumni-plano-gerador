Lesson 21 -- The Demo Without Slides

Video
Steve Jobs introduces the iPhone (Macworld 2007)

The most famous live product demo ever. There is a slide behind him, but the real demo is on the phone: he narrates every step while he does it, and the audience always knows where to look.

Tip: watch with English subtitles from minute 20, when the live demo starts. Write down five sentences he says in the present continuous while he shows the phone.

Talk
David JP Phillips -- "How to avoid death by PowerPoint" (TEDx, 21 min)

A communication trainer explains why slides full of text make people stop listening. A good argument for the demo without slides, and full of practical rules.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down two of his rules and say how each one changes the way you would show your product to Giulia.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
John Underkoffler -- "Pointing to the future of UI" (TED, 15 min)

A designer shows a new kind of computer interface live on stage, and narrates everything while his hands move the images on the screen. A master class in telling people where to look.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time he says what is happening on the screen, pause and repeat the sentence. Then narrate one screen of your product the same way.
