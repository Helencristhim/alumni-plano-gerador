Lesson 06 -- Pre-class

The Progress Report -- Saying Where the Project Stands

One page a month, for a reader who was in none of the meetings. Key words: a progress report, to issue, a revision, a baseline, a variance, an approval, to sign off, a design review, outstanding, to flag. Structure: the passive voice in reporting language.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A progress report -- a regular written summary of where a project stands
"The progress report goes out on the first Monday of the month."
To issue -- to send out an official version of a document
"The foundation drawings were issued on Friday."
A revision -- a numbered version of a document after a change
"We are on revision C of the general arrangement."
A baseline -- the approved plan that later progress is measured against
"Against the baseline, we are eleven days late."
A variance -- the difference between the plan and what actually happened
"The variance on the steel package is four weeks."
An approval -- official permission for something to go ahead
"Approval of the layout is expected next week."
To sign off -- to approve something formally, so that work can continue
"The client has not signed off the equipment base yet."
A design review -- a formal meeting where a design is examined before it goes further
"Two comments from the design review are still open."
Outstanding -- still not done or not answered
"Three outstanding items are listed at the end of the report."
To flag -- to mark something so that people notice it early
"I flagged the soil report in last month’s update."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

The monthly progress report opens with the exceptions. The foundation drawings were issued on April 12. Revision C of the general arrangement has been approved by the client.

Against the baseline, the steel package shows a variance of eleven days. That delay was flagged in the March report, so nobody is surprised by it now.

Two items are still outstanding. The equipment base has not been signed off by the client, and two comments from the last design review are still being resolved. Notice that no line names a person: the owners live in the action list at the end, each one with a date.

1. Why does the report say “the drawings were issued” instead of naming the engineer?
A To hide who is responsible.
B Because the document, not the person, is the subject of a report; the owner appears in the action list.
C Because nobody knows who issued them.
2. What does “has not been signed off” tell you?
A The client approved it last week.
B It is still waiting for formal approval.
C It was rejected permanently.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A “Two comments are still being resolved.”
B “Two comments are still being resolve.”
C “Two comments is still been resolved.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Passive Voice in Reports

GRAMMAR

Be plus the past participle. The work comes first, the name comes back only after by, and only when it is the point.

JobFormExample
The basic shapebe + past participleThe drawings were issued on Friday.
When the doer does not matterleave the name outApproval is expected next week.
When the doer does matter... by + the doerRevision C has been approved by the client.
Present perfect passivehave / has been + past participleThe base has not been signed off yet.
Something in progressis / are being + past participleThree comments are being resolved.
The trapthe participle, never the infinitiveNot was issue, not were sign off. Say was issued, were signed off.

Never: the drawings was issue · revision C is been approved · the base were not sign off · the report was sent by we

Stage 2: Put the Status Update in Order

Order

You are opening a monthly report for a director with four minutes. Put it in a logical order.

?"Two exceptions this month, both on the equipment side."
?"The equipment base has not been signed off by the client."
?"The client review has been booked for May 9."
?"Against the baseline, the steel package shows a variance of eleven days."
?"That variance was flagged in the March report and the drawings have been re-sequenced."
?"Everything else is on plan: the foundation drawings were issued on April 12."

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

A "Revision C has been approved by the client."

C "Revision C has approved by the client."

A "The equipment base did not sign off yet."

A "Two comments are still being resolved."
