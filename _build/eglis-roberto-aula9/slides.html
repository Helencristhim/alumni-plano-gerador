Eat Like a Local

A tourist at the bar asks you where to eat. What do you tell them, and how do you say it?

You are at a bar in your city. A tourist sits next to you and says: “I’m only here for two days. Where should I eat? What should I try?” Give her your best advice, and ask her what she likes.

Real Speed

I’d, You’d Better, Why Don’t You

Listen for the short forms, then say it back at the same speed.

If I were you, I'd skip that place.
Why don't you try the fish?
You should order the dish of the day.
You'd better book a table.

The Local Guide

Four Tourists, Four Tips

One tourist for each card. One minute each: where they should go, what they should try, and what they had better not do.

Tourist 1: The Couple
Their last night in your city. They want something special.
Tourist 2: The Backpacker
Very little money, very hungry, and open to anything.
Tourist 3: The Careful One
Afraid of street food and does not eat spicy food.
Tourist 4: Your Turn to Ask
You are in Lisbon. Your teacher is the local. You ask for tips.

Under Pressure

The Harbor Bar and the Stubborn Tourist

Round 1 is in a small harbor in Galicia: you have just arrived by boat, and the owner of the bar knows everything about the local food. Round 2 is on a beach in Brazil: a tourist wants to eat at the worst tourist trap on the beach.

  • Round 1: you ask the questions: what to order, how big the portions are, what is worth it.
