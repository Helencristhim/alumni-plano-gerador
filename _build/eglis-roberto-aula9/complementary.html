Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Street Food (Netflix, 30 min per episode)

Each episode follows one cook at one street stall in one city, in Asia and Latin America. It is Laura’s rule on the screen: the best food in the city, at a plastic table.

Tip: after each episode, record thirty seconds of advice for a friend going to that city: what they should try, and what they had better not miss.

Series
Anthony Bourdain: Parts Unknown

A chef who travels the world and eats where the locals eat, never in tourist traps. He talks a lot, and he gives his opinion about everything: perfect for advice language.

Tip: choose an episode about a place you have been to. Do you agree with his advice? Say what you would tell him if he asked you.

TED Talk
Dan Barber: “How I fell in love with a fish” (20 min)

A chef tells the story of a fish farm in Spain that changed how he thinks about food. Funny, clear, and full of food words. A good bridge to the next lesson, about the sea.

Tip: after the talk, say which dish from the sea you would recommend to a tourist in Brazil, and why it is worth it.
