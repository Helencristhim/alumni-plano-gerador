The Perfect Acai Bowl

About ten minutes. Eight food words — toppings, crunchy, texture, flavor, scoop, drizzle, craving, refreshing — and the sentences that put one idea in the spotlight: What I love is the granola, It's the toppings that make it.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example out loud.

Toppings -- the things you put on top of food, like fruit or granola
"What makes a bowl special is the toppings."
Crunchy -- hard and noisy when you bite it
"It's the crunchy granola that I love most."
Texture -- how food feels in your mouth: soft, thick or crunchy
"The thing I hate is a watery texture."
Flavor -- the taste of something, like strawberry or chocolate
"What I like about this flavor is that it isn't too sweet."
Scoop -- a round ball of ice cream or acai
"All I want right now is one more scoop."
Drizzle -- to pour a thin line of honey or sauce on food
"What I always do is drizzle honey on top."
Craving -- a strong feeling that you want to eat something
"What I have right now is a craving for something cold."
Refreshing -- it makes you feel cool and less tired on a hot day
"What makes acai so refreshing is that it's frozen."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read Sophia's food review. The words in bold put one idea in the spotlight.

My acai review — I have tried acai in a lot of places, but what I love most is the bowl at the little place near my school.

It's the granola that makes it special: it is always crunchy, never soft. The thing I hate in other places is too much ice.

And on a hot day? All I want is a big, cold bowl with honey on top.

"It's the granola that makes it special." What is in the spotlight?
A The granola.
B The place near her school, because it is close.
C The honey.
"All I want is a big, cold bowl." What does Sophia mean?
A She wants a lot of different things, and the bowl is one of them.
B She wants only that, nothing else.
C She doesn't want a bowl.
Which sentence is correct?
A What I love it is the granola.
B What I love are the granola, and the honey too.
C What I love most is the bowl near my school.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Three ways to put an idea in the spotlight

  • What + I + verb + is + the important part: What I love about acai is the cold texture.
  • It's / It was + the important part + that / who + verb: It's the toppings that make it perfect.
  • All I want / need + is = only this: All I want is one more scoop.

Watch out

  • No extra it: What I love is... (not What I love it is). And it is the thing that, never the thing what.

Remember: your life up to now is still have: Have you ever tried acai with peanut butter?
