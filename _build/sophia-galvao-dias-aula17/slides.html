What I love is...

Normal or in the spotlight?

Left: a normal sentence. Right: the same idea, with a spotlight on one part. What changes?

"I love the granola."↔"What I love is the granola."
"The toppings make it perfect."↔"It's the toppings that make it perfect."
"I hate watery acai."↔"The thing I hate is watery acai."
"I only want one more scoop."↔"All I want is one more scoop."
You want toUse
put the important part at the END, after a pauseWhat + I + verb + is / was + the important part
What I love about acai is the cold texture.
point at the ONE thing that makes the differenceIt's / It was + the thing + that / who + verb
It's the banana that makes it sweet.
say: only this, nothing elseAll I want / need + is
All I need is a spoon.
a softer spotlightThe thing (that) I + verb + is
The thing I miss most is the beach.

The question of the day: What do you want in the spotlight? With What I love is... it comes at the end. With It's ... that it comes right at the start.
