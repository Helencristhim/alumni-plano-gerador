Grammar Discovery

Two verbs have an extra letter

I check the forecast every morning.
Dave checks the forecast every morning.
We paddle out at six.
The tide comes in at seven.

Things that are always true → present simple

Routines, habits, facts. The verb never changes — except after he, she and it, which add an -s.

The Code

One -s per sentence

I / you / we / they

I check the forecast.
I don't check it on Sundays.
Do you check it?

He / she / it

He checks the forecast.
He doesn't check it on Sundays.
Does he check it?

Look at the right column: the -s moves to does, and the verb goes back to normal.

Sound Check

The -s has three sounds

checks, gets

sounds like s — no extra syllable

comes, paddles

sounds like z — no extra syllable

watches, finishes

this one does add a syllable: -iz

A student's school day

1. What does Emma do before she surfs?
She drinks water. She never eats before she surfs.
2. Why does her brother carry the gear?
Because he loses every argument.
3. What does Emma do after the session?
She goes home, has a shower and catches the bus at half past seven.
