Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
Stanford GSB — “Think Fast, Talk Smart: Communication Techniques”

The single most useful hour on the exact problem of this lesson: speaking well when you are put on the spot and have not prepared the sentence. Matt Abrahams is talking to MBA students, at full native speed, about the committee room you were just in.

Tip: watch the first twenty minutes. Write down his structure for answering an unexpected question, and use it in your next real call before the next lesson.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
Adam Galinsky — “How to speak up for yourself” (TED)

Fifteen minutes on the range between sounding too soft and sounding too strong -- which is exactly what choosing between might, can and will does to you in a room full of people you do not control.

Tip: he describes a range with a narrow band. Write down where your three warm-up promises would sit on it, and which one you would move.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
In Good Company — Ethan Mollick on AI in companies

A Wharton professor on what AI is actually doing inside businesses right now. It is your industry, said by somebody who is careful with certainty -- listen to how rarely he says will and how often he says could and should.

Tip: the first fifteen minutes are enough. Count his modals. He is doing deliberately what this lesson asked you to do.
