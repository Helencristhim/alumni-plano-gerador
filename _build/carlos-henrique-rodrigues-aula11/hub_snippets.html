Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto em voz alta, devagar, e depois responda as perguntas.

Carlos is in Orlando. The plane was a little late, and now he is waiting in the line at immigration.

The officer asks for his passport and visa. "What is the purpose of your visit?" "Vacation," Carlos says. "I own a car rental company in Brazil, and we are going to stay ten days."

"Welcome back," the officer says. His wife is waiting at baggage claim. He can see her from the line!

1. Onde o Carlos está esperando? (Where is Carlos waiting?)
A Na esteira de bagagem, junto com a esposa e as malas. (At baggage claim, with his wife and the bags.)
B No hotel. (At the hotel.)
C Na fila da imigração. (In the line at immigration.)
2. Quanto tempo eles vão ficar? (How long are they going to stay?)
A Dez dias. (Ten days.)
B Uma semana inteira em Orlando, e depois mais uma em Miami. (One week in Orlando and one in Miami.)
C Ele não sabe. (He does not know.)
3. Por que "was" na primeira frase? (Why "was"?)
A Porque é um plano para amanhã. (Because it is a plan for tomorrow.)
B Porque o atraso já passou. (Because it is in the past.)
C Porque está acontecendo agora, neste minuto. (Because it is happening right now.)

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Revisão: os seis tempos do bloco 1

GRAMMAR

Não tem gramática nova hoje. A pergunta é sempre a mesma: é todo dia, passado, agora ou plano? / No new grammar today. Ask: every day, past, now or plan?

Uso / UseEstrutura / PatternExemplo / Example
Quem e o quê / Who and what (aula 1)I am / you are / it isI am a businessman. (Eu sou empresário.)
Todo dia / Every day (aulas 3 e 4)I own / he ownsI own a car rental company. (Eu tenho uma locadora.)
Poder / Can (aula 6)can / cannot + verboI can see her. (Eu consigo vê-la.)
Passado / Past (aula 8)was / wereI was here in 2019. (Eu estive aqui em 2019.)
Agora / Now (aula 9)am / is / are + verbo-ingMy wife is waiting. (Minha esposa está esperando.)
Plano / Plan (aula 10)am / is / are + going to + verboWe are going to stay ten days. (Vamos ficar dez dias.)
Ideia-chave / Key ideaNa imigração cada pergunta já diz o tempo: "What do you do?" pede presente simples, "Were you here before?" pede was, "How long are you going to stay?" pede going to. / The question tells you the tense: answer with the same one.
