Every blue verb is from a different lesson. Which one is every day? Which one is the past? Which one is right now? Which one is the future?

JobFormExample

Comprehension

About Officer Davis

1. What does Officer Davis ask for first?
The passport and the visa.
2. What does she want to know about the visit?
The purpose of the visit, and how long Carlos is going to stay.
3. What does she say after Carlos talks about 2019?
"Welcome back."
4. Where are the bags, according to Officer Davis?
At baggage claim, carousel four.

Your Driver Is Here

He can call her.
