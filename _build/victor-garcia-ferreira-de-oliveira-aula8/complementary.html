Talk
TED — Eduardo Briceño, "How to get better at the things you care about" (11 min)

The difference between doing something and practising it, and why people who work hard for years still plateau. It is the missing half of the reading: measuring tells you that you stopped, and this tells you why.

Tip: he separates the learning zone from the performance zone. Decide which one your last week was in, and say the answer out loud in one sentence with a reason.
