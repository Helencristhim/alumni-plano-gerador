Chapter 3: The Pattern

Past, Now, Future

was · usually · going to

Pattern Review

Listen and Notice

Last month the forum was difficult.
Yesterday I went to the plant.
I usually drive to Alphaville.
I am going to travel to New York in October.

Past, now or future? Which sentence is which, and which word tells you?

UseFormExample
Past: to bewas / wereThe forum was difficult.
Past: actionverb + -ed / irregularWe finished on time. I went to the plant.
Now: routinepresent simple + always, usually...He usually sends the update on Friday.
Now: this momentam / is / are + -ingWe are preparing the forum.
Future: planam / is / are + going toI am going to travel to New York.
CarefulThe time word tells you: yesterday, last month (past) · every day, usually, now (present) · next week, in October (future).
