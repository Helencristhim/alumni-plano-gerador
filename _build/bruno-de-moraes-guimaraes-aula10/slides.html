Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"How much is this T-shirt?"

"How many T-shirts do you want?"

"I want some socks, please."

"Sorry, we don't have any small sizes."

After "how many", can you count the thing? And why "some" in one sentence and "any" in the other?

WordUse it withExample
how manythings you can COUNT: one, two, three"How many T-shirts?" · "How many pairs of socks?"
how muchthings you can't count, and the PRICE"How much money?" · "How much is it?"
someYES sentences"I want some socks." · "We have some red caps."
anyNO sentences and QUESTIONS"We don't have any small sizes." · "Do you have any black caps?"
Key ideaIn a store, one question does everything: "How much is it?" Point at the thing and ask.

Money is "how much", but dollars are "how many": "How many dollars do you have?"
