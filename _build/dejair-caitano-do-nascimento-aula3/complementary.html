Lesson 3 -- Where Have You Been?

Conference Skills
"How to Deliver an Effective Poster Presentation" -- Enago Academy

Ten minutes on what actually happens at a poster session: who stops, what they ask, and how you answer without reading your own poster out loud. Every word from today is in it -- abstract, poster, session, feedback.

Tip: watch it once. Then stand up, imagine your board in Bangkok, and give the two-minute version of your own poster out loud. Nobody is watching; that is the point.

Listening
"Improving your memory" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes of two presenters telling what happened in past experiments, at a speed built for learners. Almost every verb in it is a past simple, which is exactly what you practised today.

Tip: listen once with your eyes closed, no text. Then listen again and write down every irregular past verb you hear -- went, gave, took, said. Aim for eight.

Conference Talk
"This is your brain on communication" -- Uri Hasson, TED

A neuroscientist shows what happens inside the listener's head when somebody tells a story well. He is talking about your problem from the other side: not how to speak, but what makes another brain follow you.

Tip: watch with English subtitles the first time. Then notice how he tells his own experiments -- always in the past, always one step at a time. Copy that shape when you tell the 2024 event.
