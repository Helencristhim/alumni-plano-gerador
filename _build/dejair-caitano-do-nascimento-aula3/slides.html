Chapter 3: The Code

One Tense Back

Half of them take -ed. The other half you already know by ear

Discover the Rule

Which Ones Follow a Rule?

I presented a poster on the second day.
I went to a congress in Lisbon last year.
She asked me three questions.
He gave the keynote on Friday.
Click to reveal the rule
All four are finished actions at a finished time, so all four are past simple. Presented and asked follow the rule: verb + -ed. Went and gave are irregular — you learn them one by one, and there are about twenty you will actually need. The form is the same for I, you, he, she, we and they: no extra -s ever.

Ayse, from Istanbul

Comprehension

About Ayse

1. Where and when did Ayse present a poster?
In Lisbon, last year.
2. What happened at her poster, and how did it end?
Nobody came for twenty minutes. Then a professor stayed half an hour and gave her hard but useful feedback.
3. What does she say about the people she met that week?
Two of them are her co-authors now.
