How to turn a group of strangers into a team

Amy Edmondson studies teaming, which is what happens when people from different disciplines have to work together without ever having worked together before. Her examples come from engineering and construction, including a mine rescue.

Tip: listen for how she defines each specialist, then describe your own team with the engineer who and the architect that.
