Lesson 04 -- Pre-class

The People We Need -- Asking for the Right Profile

A job title finds a category; a profile finds a person. Key words: a structural engineer, a piping engineer, to staff, to mobilize, a secondment, a skill set, headcount, to backfill, an assignment, seniority. Structure: defining relative clauses with who, which, that and whose.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A structural engineer -- the engineer responsible for the frame that carries the loads of a building
"The structural engineer signs off every foundation drawing."
A piping engineer -- the engineer who designs the pipe systems that move fluids through a plant
"We need a piping engineer with experience in food processing."
To staff -- to give a project the people it needs to be done
"We cannot staff a third project before March."
To mobilize -- to bring people and equipment onto a project so that work can start
"We mobilize the design team two weeks after the contract."
A secondment -- a period when an employee is temporarily transferred to another office or project
"She is here on a six-month secondment from Helsinki."
A skill set -- the particular set of abilities a person brings to a role
"His skill set is unusual: structures plus industrial process."
Headcount -- the number of people a team or a project is allowed to have
"The headcount for this quarter is frozen."
To backfill -- to replace somebody in their old role when they move to a new one
"If we second her to Houston, who backfills her here?"
An assignment -- a specific piece of work given to one person for a period
"His first assignment is the equipment base drawings."
Seniority -- the level of experience and responsibility a person has
"For this interface we need seniority, not just availability."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

The request that leaves the Brazilian office names two people. The first is a structural engineer who has signed off industrial foundations. The second is a piping engineer whose experience is in food processing rather than oil and gas.

Annika reads it in Helsinki. The skill set is clear, the assignment is clear, and the date is real. She can mobilize both of them in April, as a six-month secondment.

One question stays open, and it is the one that always stays open: who will backfill the piping engineer in Helsinki? The headcount in Brazil is frozen until July, so the hole cannot be covered from that side.

1. Why does the request say “a structural engineer who has signed off industrial foundations”?
A Because the detail narrows the search to the right person.
B Because the job title alone would be impolite.
C Because the engineer owns the foundations.
2. Why is “whose” used before “experience”?
A Because the next idea is a possession: the experience belongs to the engineer.
B Because experience is a thing, not a person.
C Because the clause is a question.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A “The engineer who he signed the drawings is on leave.”
B “The engineer who signed the drawings is on leave.”
C “The engineer which signed the drawings is on leave.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Defining Relative Clauses

GRAMMAR

The small word that adds the detail: who for people, which for things, that for both, whose for possession.

JobFormExample
The detail is about a PERSONwho (or that) + verban engineer who has worked on food plants
The detail is about a THINGwhich (or that) + verbthe package that worries me
The detail is a POSSESSIONwhose + nounsomebody whose drawings we can issue
The detail is the object of the clausewho / which / that can be left outthe engineer (who) we mobilized last month
Why it matterswithout the clause, the sentence asks for anybodyWe need a piping engineer finds ten. The clause finds one.
The trapnever repeat the pronoun after the relativeNot the engineer who he has worked. Say the engineer who has worked.

Never: a piping engineer which has worked · the engineer who he signed · somebody who drawings we can issue · the package what worries me

Order
