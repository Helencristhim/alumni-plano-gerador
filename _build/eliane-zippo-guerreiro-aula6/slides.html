Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"How much is the check?"

"How much is the chicken?" "It is nineteen dollars."

"How much are the drinks?" "They are twelve dollars."

"The total is thirty-one dollars."

Look at the blue words. When is it How much is, and when is it How much are?
