Chapter 3: Left and Right

On, In, At — and Two Blocks

where it is, and how far it is

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The hotel is on Oxford Street."

"The bank is on the corner."

"The shop is in the square."

"I am at the station."

WordYou use it withExample
on + street, corner, sidea line: a street, an edgeThe hotel is on Oxford Street.
in + square, park, citya space with sides around itThe shop is in the square.
at + station, hotel, doorone exact pointI am at the station.
two blocks / five minutesHOW FAR, not whereIt is two blocks from here.

Line = on. Space around you = in. One exact point = at. And distance in the street is counted in blocks or in minutes, almost never in metres.

The Four Street Sentences

Four Sentences and You Are Never Lost

"Excuse me, where is Oxford Street?"

the question — and you always start with excuse me

"Is it far? Can I walk?"

the second question, and it decides taxi or feet

"Can you show me on the map, please?"

when the words go too fast, the finger solves it

"So, straight and then left at the corner. Is that right?"

you repeat it back — and now you are sure

P

P

P

P

Exactly right. The hotel is next to the pharmacy. You cannot miss it.
P
Thank you very much. Have a good day.
Comprehension

What Did Peter Say?

1. How long does it take, with children?
About twelve minutes — eight without them.
2. Where does he say to turn left?
At the corner with the big bank, after two blocks straight.
3. What is next to the hotel?
The pharmacy. He says she cannot miss it.

A Message About the Meeting Point

0:000:00
