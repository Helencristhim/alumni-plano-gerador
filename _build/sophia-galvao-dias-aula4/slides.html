Listening 1

Ruby and the pistachios

1. What did she not say, and why not?
That she has a nut allergy — she was embarrassed about her English.
2. What arrived at the table?
A dessert covered in pistachios, which the waiter had recommended.
3. What are her two plan-B sentences now?
If the menu is not clear, she asks; if they do not have something without nuts, she orders the fruit.
Listening 2

At the table

1. What does he recommend, and what warning does he give about it?
The moussaka — but it is a very large portion, so perhaps share it between two.
2. What is not available tonight, and what does he offer instead?
The pistachio dessert; he offers the yoghurt with honey, at the same price.
3. What will he bring at the end?
The receipt.
