Grammar Discovery

Real, or imagined?

Kai: "If I were you, I'd wait for the big ones."
Renato: "If I advanced, I'd call my parents."
"If I had your experience, I wouldn't be nervous."
"Imagine you won. What would you do?"

What do the orange words have in common? Is it about yesterday?

imagined: If + past simple, would ('d) + verb

The past after if is not about yesterday. It means: this is not real, it's in my imagination. For advice: If I were you, I'd... Negative: wouldn't.

The Code

Two parts, one dream

If + past simple

If I won the heat,
If I didn't have school,
If I were you,
If you got knocked out,

would ('d) + verb

I'd call my parents.
I'd surf every morning.
I'd wait for a set.
what would you do?
Watch out: would never goes after if. For advice, use were with every person: If I were you, if he were here. Negative: I wouldn't hesitate.
The Code

Possible, or a dream?

Real and possible today: if + present, will. Not real, only in your mind: if + past, would.

Possible

"If the wind changes, I'll move."
"If I advance, I'll surf again at two."
"If it rains, we'll eat in the van."

Imagined

"If I won the World Tour, I'd buy a house here."
"If I were a pro, I'd surf every day."
"If Gabriel Medina came, I'd ask for a photo."
What would you do if you were me?

The best question to ask a coach or an older surfer.

Sound Check

Short and soft

If I were you, I'd wait.

"I'd" is one sound: AID

What would you do?

"would you" sounds like WUD-JA — the L is silent

I wouldn't hesitate.

"wouldn't" is two short sounds: WUD-NT
