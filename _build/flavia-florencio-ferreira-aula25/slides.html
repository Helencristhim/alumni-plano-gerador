Chapter 3: The Meeting Code

Open, Move, Close

Thank you for having me · Moving on to... · To summarize...

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Thank you for having me. I would like to start by presenting our figures."

"Moving on to the proposal, if your board agrees, we will start in April."

"On the subject of products, Canastra cheese comes from Minas Gerais."

"To summarize what we discussed, the next step would be to share the minutes."

What do the orange words do? Which one opens, which ones move to a new topic, and which one closes? What comes after "start by" and after "moving on to"?

Part of the meetingPhraseWhat comes next
OpenThank you for having me. / I would like to start by...verb + -ing: start by presenting
Move to a new topicMoving on to... / On the subject of...a noun: moving on to the proposal
Add a last topicBefore we finish, I would like to mention...a noun: our coffee producers
CloseTo summarize what we discussed, ... / The next step would be to...to + verb: would be to share
Say goodbyeI look forward to...verb + -ing: following up on this

Use these phrases as signposts: they tell the other person where the meeting is going. After "by" and "look forward to", use the -ing form. After "would be", use to + verb.

Chapter 4: The Bilateral Meeting

Flavia Takes the Chair

Dialogue

The Meeting

P
Good morning, Flavia. Let's get down to business. We have three agenda items today.
F
Thank you for having me, Paolo. I would like to start by presenting our figures.
P
Of course. How big is the program now?
F
We have approximately thirty-three regional offices and nearly five hundred certified producers.
P
That is a big scale. And what about the partnership?
F
Moving on to the proposal: if your board agrees, we will start the field visits in April.
P
April works for us. I think we have a consensus.
F
Great. Before we finish, I would like to mention Canastra cheese. Just like Parmigiano, it is made by hand.
P
Our members will love that. Who will take the minutes?
F
My colleague Renata. To summarize what we discussed: the visits are in April, and the next step would be to share the minutes.
