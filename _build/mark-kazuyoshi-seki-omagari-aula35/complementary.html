Lesson 35 -- The Trip Was Organized By...

Travel video
15 Japan Travel Mistakes Tourists Always Make (and how to avoid them)

A traveler who lives in Japan goes through the mistakes first-time visitors make, from bookings to trains to rules at the airport. A lot of the problems are described through what was done, or not done, before the trip.

Tip: after each mistake, say in one passive sentence what should have been done: "The train pass should be bought...", "Bags must be..."

An airline expert explains what happens behind the scenes of a flight -- bookings, seats, luggage and delays -- and later in the episode a traveler talks about rural Japan. It is the part of a trip that nobody sees, told by people who work in it.
