Listening 1

Ruby's first time alone

1. Where does she still get nervous, and how many times has she flown alone?
At customs. She has done it twice now.
2. Why did she end up with a layover in Madrid?
Her flight to Lisbon was overbooked, so they put her on a later one.
3. What does she do differently since that day?
She always asks somebody for help — and she has not missed a flight yet.
The Airport

At the desk

A
Next, please. Boarding pass and passport?
S
Here you are. Excuse me, could you tell me where gate C12 is?
A
C12? They have changed it. Your flight leaves from C7 now. Check the screens, not the boarding pass.
S
Oh. And my bag hasn't arrived yet. Is that normal?
A
Belt four, it is slow today. Have you been to Athens before?
S
I've been here once. I came in July with my family and it was forty degrees.
A
Forty. That is Athens in July. Do you need anything else?
S
Yes — where can I change money? I mean, where is the currency exchange?
A
Ground floor, next to the pharmacy. It is just around the corner from the shuttle stop.
S
The thing that goes to the metro? We have already booked a taxi, actually.
A
Even better. Enjoy Greece — and the Acropolis is the one landmark you cannot miss.
S
Thank you so much. I've never asked for help in English before. That was easier than I thought.
The Airport

What did the agent say?

1. What happened to gate C12?
It changed — the flight leaves from C7 now, and he says to check the screens, not the boarding pass.
2. Where is the currency exchange?
On the ground floor, next to the pharmacy, just around the corner from the shuttle stop.
3. Which landmark does he say she cannot miss?
The Acropolis.
Listening 2

The airport announcement

1. Which belt is the baggage on?
Belt four.
2. Where is the currency exchange, and how often does the shuttle leave?
On the ground floor next to the pharmacy; the shuttle leaves from exit three every fifteen minutes.
3. What should you NOT trust today, and why?
Your boarding pass — the gate numbers for connecting flights have changed, so check the screens.
