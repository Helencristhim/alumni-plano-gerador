Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Lera Boroditsky — “How language shapes the way we think” (TED)

A cognitive scientist on how the languages we speak change what we notice. A good fourteen minutes for a law student who has been living between two languages since childhood.

Tip: watch with English subtitles once, then again without them. Pause after each example and say it back in one sentence.

Watch on TED ↗
Series
One Tree Hill — Season 1, Episode 1, “Pilot”

The series you are already watching, so use it as listening practice. Fast, natural American conversation between students who are deciding what comes next in their lives.

Tip: choose one two-minute scene, watch it with English subtitles, then without, and repeat one line at the same speed as the character.

See the episode on TVmaze ↗
Video
TST Prep — “TOEFL Speaking Tips for a Perfect Score, 2026 Edition” (YouTube)

The speaking section changed in January 2026: two short tasks, Listen and Repeat and Take an Interview, and no preparation time. This free video explains both, two years before your exam.

Tip: pause after each tip and answer one interview question out loud with no preparation, the way the new test asks.
