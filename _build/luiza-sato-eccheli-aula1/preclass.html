Vocabulary

An exchange program -- an agreement that lets a student spend time studying at a university in another country
"The law school has an exchange program with universities in Europe and the United States."
To apply for -- to make a formal request for a place, a job or a program
"I plan to apply for the exchange in my third year."
A requirement -- something you must have or do before you are accepted
"A TOEFL score is a requirement for every partner university."
A deadline -- the last day or time when something can be done or sent
"The deadline for the application is the first week of March."
A host university -- the university abroad that receives an exchange student
"My host university will decide which courses I can take."
A semester abroad -- half an academic year spent studying in another country
"My brother spent a semester abroad before he moved to London."
A cut-off score -- the minimum result a candidate needs in order to be considered
"The best universities have a higher cut-off score."
Competitive -- describes a process where many people want the same thing and only some get it
"The exchange is competitive, so a good score is not enough on its own."
To stand out -- to be noticed easily because you are different or better
"Your interview is where you stand out from the other candidates."
A track record -- all the things a person has done in the past that show what they can do
"She has a strong track record in her law school debates."
To keep up with -- to move or learn at the same speed as other people or things
"I read every day so that I can keep up with the classes."
A head start -- an advantage you get by starting something earlier than other people
"Starting two years before the test gives me a head start."
To be shortlisted -- to be chosen as one of the few candidates who go to the final stage
"Only twenty students are shortlisted for an interview."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Every exchange interview starts with the same kind of question: tell us about yourself. Most candidates answer with a list of schools and dates, and the list sounds the same for everybody. The candidates who stand out tell a story instead, and English has a tense that was made for stories that are not finished yet.

When something started in the past and is still going on, you use have been + -ing: “I have been studying English since I was seven.” It tells the committee that the effort is still alive. When you want to show a result, a number or an achievement, you use have + past participle: “I have read forty novels in English this year.”

Two little words decide the time. Since goes with a starting point, and for goes with a length of time: since 2019, for eleven years. Some verbs, like live, know and be, describe a state, so they usually take the simple form: “My brother has lived in London for three years,” never “has been knowing”.

And there is one border you cannot cross. When the time is finished, like last year or in 2024, English uses the simple past: “I went to Scotland last year.” A candidate who says “I have gone to Scotland last year” is understood, but the committee notices. With a clear story and the right tense, a competitive interview becomes a conversation, and that is what gives you a head start.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Present Perfect Simple and Continuous

GRAMMAR

Started in the past and still true: have been + -ing for the activity, have + past participle for the result.

JobFormExample
An activity still going onhave / has been + verb-ingI have been studying English since I was seven.
A result or a numberhave / has + past participleI have read forty novels this year.
A state (live, know, be)have / has + past participleShe has known about it since March.
A starting pointsince + a momentsince I was seven · since 2019
A length of timefor + a periodfor eleven years · for two months
A finished timesimple pastI went to Scotland last year.

Never: I study English since I was seven · I have been knowing him for years · I have gone to Scotland last year · I am preparing for two months
