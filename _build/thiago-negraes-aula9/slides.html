Chapter 3: Now and Always

What Is Happening, What Always Happens

Two tenses in the same breath

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"He is overtaking on the outside right now."

"He usually starts well from the second row."

"They are changing the tyres at the moment."

"A pit stop takes about two seconds."

TenseSignal wordsExample
Present continuousnow, right now, at the moment, todayHe is overtaking right now.
Present simplealways, usually, normally, every raceHe usually starts well.
General truthno time word at allA pit stop takes two seconds.
Never continuousknow, want, mean, need, believeI know this circuit. (not am knowing)

One question decides it: is it happening while I speak? If yes, am / is / are plus -ing. If it is a habit or a fact, the simple form. And the verbs of the mind never take -ing.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"He overtakes right now."

✓ RIGHT

"He is overtaking right now."

✗ WRONG

"I am knowing this circuit well."

✓ RIGHT

"I know this circuit well."

Portuguese uses the same simple form for both ideas, so the hand writes he overtakes for something happening this second. English separates them. And know, want, mean, need and believe never take -ing.
