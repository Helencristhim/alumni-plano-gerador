Lesson 5 -- Ask Before You Pitch

Video
Y Combinator -- "How To Talk To Users" (Startup School, 22 min)

The whole talk is about the question this lesson trained: how to ask what actually happens today instead of asking what somebody would like to buy. Clear American English, slides on screen, and every example is a real conversation with a real customer.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down four of his questions and rebuild each one for an ERP partner. Mark which of them need do and which must not have it.

Talk
Ernesto Sirolli -- "Want to help someone? Shut up and listen!" (TED, 17 min)

An economist on twenty years of arriving with the solution already prepared, and what changed when he started asking first. It is funny, it is uncomfortable, and it is the same mistake this lesson is about, told by somebody who made it professionally.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Stop at the point where he describes his first project and say out loud the three questions he should have asked.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Cold calls"

Five minutes on the call where you have no relationship and no right to anybody's time, and on the questions that buy you the next two minutes. Full script on the same page, and the question forms in it are exactly the ones this lesson drilled.

Tip: read the script and find every question. For each one, ask yourself who is doing the action, and check whether the sentence has do. Read the five hardest ones out loud.
