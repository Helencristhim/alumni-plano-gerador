Chapter 3: The Code

The Small Verb

I am, you are, it is

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I am Carlos Rodrigues."

"I am not from Miami."

"Are you from Brazil?"

"Is insurance included?"

Look at the words in blue. They are all the same verb. What changes: the person, or the verb?

JobFormExample
II am / I’mI am Carlos. I’m from Brazil.
Youyou are / you’reYou are very kind.
It (the car, the insurance)it is / it’sIt is in the parking lot.
Negativeam not / are not / is notI am not from Miami. It isn’t included.
QuestionAm I...? / Are you...? / Is it...?Is insurance included?
AgeI am + number + years oldI am 65 years old. (Never: I have 65 years.)

In one line: I am, you are, it is. For a question, the verb goes first: Is it...? Are you...?

Chapter 4: At the Counter

At the Counter

Dialogue

Next, Please!

J
Hi, welcome to Sunshine Car Rental. Are you here for a car?
C
Yes. I am Carlos Rodrigues. I have a reservation.
J
Great. Are you from Brazil, Mr. Rodrigues?
C
Yes, I am.
J
Your passport and your driver's license, please.
C
Here you are. Is insurance included?
J
No, it is not. It is twelve dollars a day.
C
Okay, that is fine. Where is the car?
J
It is in the parking lot, row B. Have a nice trip!
Comprehension

About Jessica

1. Where does Jessica work?
At Sunshine Car Rental.
2. What does Jessica ask for?
His passport and his driver's license.
3. How much is the insurance, according to Jessica?
Twelve dollars a day.
4. Where is the car, according to Jessica?
In the parking lot, row B.
