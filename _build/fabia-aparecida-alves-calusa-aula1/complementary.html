Series
Suits — Season 1, Episode 1

A professional services firm that lives on winning clients. Almost every scene is a meeting: someone pitching what the firm does, someone deciding, and someone in the room who was not on the invite. Drama with the pace you like, in the world you work in.

Tip: watch one episode with English subtitles and write down three sentences somebody uses to say what their firm does. You will hear "we handle" and "we specialise in" more than once.

TED Talk
Celeste Headlee — 10 ways to have a better conversation

Eleven minutes from a professional interviewer on how to ask, how to listen and when to say nothing. It is behaviour, not grammar — which is the reading you already choose for yourself. Subtitles available in English.

Tip: rule number four is "ask open-ended questions". Compare her list with the questions on the call cards from this lesson — you will find three of them there.
