Little Women (2019)

Greta Gerwig's adaptation follows Jo March as she fights to become a writer, and it opens, famously, in the middle of the story. A warm, funny film about finding your own voice on the page.

Tip: notice how the film starts in medias res and only later shows the past. Ask yourself why the director chose that door into the story.
