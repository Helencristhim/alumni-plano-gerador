Explained — Season 1, Episode 1: “The Racial Wealth Gap”

A short documentary series that takes one big public issue per episode and explains it in about twenty minutes. This one is about inequality in the United States, so it gives you the vocabulary of today’s lesson about a country that is not yours.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down every sentence that starts with “What” or “It is... that”. Then say one of them again, about Brazil.

Talk
Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie — “The danger of a single story” (TED)

A Nigerian writer on what happens when the world knows only one story about a place. It is exactly the question of your homework: what the world misunderstands, and why.

Tip: after the talk, say in one minute what the single story about Brazil is, and which story you would tell instead.

Talk
Hans Rosling — “The best stats you’ve ever seen” (TED)

A doctor and statistician shows that most people, even students at top universities, have an old picture of the world. It connects today’s topic with the next lesson, which is about talking with numbers.

Tip: pause after the first chart and guess what comes next, starting with “What I expect to see is...”.
