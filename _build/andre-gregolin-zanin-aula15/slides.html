Chapter 3: Where the Stress Goes

Put It in the Spotlight

Two sentence shapes that choose what the listener remembers

Grammar Discovery

What Is in the Spotlight?

"It was basketball that taught me discipline."

"It is the waiting time that people complain about."

"What I miss most is the team."

"What surprised me was the size of São Paulo."

Read each sentence without the colored words. The information is the same. What did you lose? Which part of each sentence is in the spotlight?

ShapeFormExample
It-cleftIt + be + FOCUS + that / who + the restIt was the coach who taught me.
What-cleftWhat + clause + be + FOCUSWhat I miss is the team.
Other openersThe thing that... is · The reason (why)... is that · All I want isThe reason I chose medicine is that I like people.
Timebe follows the time of the storyIt was in Franca that... · What surprised me was...
Your voicethe FOCUS gets the strongest stressIt was BASKETBALL that taught me discipline.
Notno extra subject · no “is because”Not What I miss it is... · Not The reason is because...
With last lessonsa cleft + a connector or a hedgeWhat I would tentatively argue is that the system needs reform.

A cleft is a spotlight, not a decoration. One or two in an answer sound confident. Ten sound like a speech somebody wrote for you.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

What the World Gets Wrong

One conversation, two recordings, one podcast plan

Dialogue

What People Never See

H
Can I tell you something? Before I moved here, everybody in Chicago warned me about the same things. What they talked about was crime, football and beaches.
A
That is the mainstream picture, yes. But it is not the beaches that surprise people when they get here. It is the size of everything.
H
That is true. What surprised me most was the inequality. Two streets apart, and it feels like two different countries.
A
The way I see it, it is not only money. What people outside Brazil rarely see is the difference in access: to hospitals, to good schools, even to sport.
H
Even to sport? I thought sport was the one thing everybody here had.
A
Football, maybe. When I played basketball in Franca, it was the infrastructure that made the difference. The interior has the talent. What it often lacks is the courts, the coaches and the money.
H
That is a perspective I never hear in Chicago. Somebody should bring that to light.
A
Maybe somebody will. What I would like to know is the opposite. What is one thing about Chicago that people get completely wrong?
Comprehension

About Hannah

1. What did people in Chicago warn Hannah about before she moved?
Crime, football and beaches.
2. What surprised Hannah most in Brazil?
The inequality. Two streets apart, it feels like two different countries.
3. What did Hannah believe about sport in Brazil?
That it was the one thing everybody here had.

The Story Nobody Asked For

A reporter on the one Brazilian story her editors did not want. Sound first — no text.

The Amazon, or football.
2. Why do the people take the Monday bus?

It sparked more debate than anything she wrote about the Amazon.
