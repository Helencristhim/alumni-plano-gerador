Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Margaret Heffernan: “Dare to disagree” (13 min)

Why good disagreement is central to progress, and how the best teams use conflict well.

Tip: note one sentence with an adverb that she uses to describe people.

Watch on TED ↗
TEDx Talk
Celeste Headlee: “10 ways to have a better conversation” (12 min)

A radio host on how to listen and talk better, especially when people do not agree.

Tip: choose one of her ten ideas and use it in the role-play in class.

Watch on TED ↗
TEDx Talk
William Ury: “The walk from no to yes” (18 min)

A famous negotiator on how to calm down a conflict and find a next step.

Tip: after the talk, say one conflict that is still open in your work, and one next step.
