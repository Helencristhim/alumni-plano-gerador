Lesson 32 -- Pre-class

Conflict Management -- Adverbs, Still and Yet

Ten to twenty minutes before class. How to give feedback in a conflict: how people did something, and what is still open. Key words: a complaint, to apologize, to handle, a misunderstanding, to calm down, a next step.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A complaint -- a statement that something is wrong or not good enough
"The client sent a complaint about the report."
To apologize -- to say that you are sorry for something
"I apologized to the client for the delay."
To handle -- to deal with a situation or a problem
"She handled the angry client very well."
A misunderstanding -- a situation when people understand something in a different way
"It was a misunderstanding about the deadline."
To calm down -- to become less angry or less nervous
"He calmed down after we explained the plan."
A next step -- the action that comes after this one
"The next step is a call with the client on Monday."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Last week, two teams at a data company had a conflict about a deadline. The manager talked to both teams and listened carefully.

In her feedback, she said: you all reacted quickly, and you were honestly trying to help. But we still haven't agreed on the process, and we haven't fixed the calendar yet.

How did the manager listen?
A Quickly.
B Badly.
C Carefully.
"We still haven't agreed on the process." What does it mean?
A They agreed last week.
B There is no agreement now, and it is taking longer than expected.
C They will never agree.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Adverbs, Still and Yet

GRAMMAR

Adverbs say how. Still: something continues, often longer than expected. Yet: in questions and negatives, until now.

JobFormExample
How someone did itverb + adverbYou handled it calmly.
It continuesstill + verb / still haven'tWe still haven't solved it.
Until now (question)Have you… yet?Have you called him yet?
Until now (negative)haven't… yetWe haven't agreed yet.

Never: You handled it calm · We haven’t still solved it · Have you yet called him?

Stage 2: Put the Conversation in Order

Order

Listen first, then put the lines in order. A manager gives feedback after a conflict. Put the conversation in a natural order.

Step 1: listen to the whole conversation. Step 2: put the lines in the order you heard.
?"You handled it calmly, and that was important."
?"I know. Let's agree on a next step today."
?"Sure. I know it was a difficult call."
?"Do you have five minutes to talk about the client call?"
?"Thanks. But we still haven't fixed the data."

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer a real speaker would give.

A colleague managed an angry client very well. You say:
A "You handled it very well."
B "You handled it very calm."
C "You handled it very good."
The problem started last week and it is not solved now. You say:
A "We yet haven't solved it."
B "We still haven't solved it."
C "We haven't still solved it."
You want to know if the client answered. You ask:
A "Has the client answered yet?"
B "Has yet the client answered?"
C "The client answered already yet?"

Stage 5: Free Production

Reflection

Think, then record your answer. There is no wrong answer here.

Think of a conflict at work. In about one minute: what happened, how people reacted (use two adverbs), and what is still open or not solved yet.
