Work Situation

An Upset Colleague

Dan, a manager on the US team, is upset. Your team sent a client report two days late, and the client complained to him. Your team had a data problem, and it is still not completely fixed.

1. What do you say first to Dan?
2. What did your team do well, and how?
3. What is still open, and when will you solve it?
Role-Play

The Difficult Call

A video call with Dan. He is upset. Stay calm, recognize the problem, say what is still open, and agree on a next step.

  • Round 1: Your teacher is Dan. He is upset about the late report. You answer calmly and directly.
  • Round 2: Now you are the manager. Your teacher is an analyst on your team who made a mistake. You give feedback.
