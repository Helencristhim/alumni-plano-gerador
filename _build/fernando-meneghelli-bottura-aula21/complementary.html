Lesson 21 -- The Offer Call

Grammar Video
“‘Unless’ vs ‘As long as’: What’s the difference?” -- English In A Minute, BBC Learning English

A one-minute explanation of the two linkers that carry most of an offer conversation. Connection to Lesson 21: unless shows your limit, and as long as sets your condition.

Tip: pause after each example and make your own sentence about a CFO offer before the presenter gives the next one.

“3 steps to getting what you want in a negotiation” -- The Way We Work, TED

A five-minute guide to preparing a negotiation: what you want, what the other side wants, and where the middle ground is. Connection to Lesson 21: it is the preparation you do before the offer call.

Tip: write down your two real points for a CFO offer and one middle ground for each.

Lecture
“How to Negotiate Your Job Offer” -- Prof. Deepak Malhotra, Harvard Business School

A long lecture on negotiating a job offer without damaging the relationship. Connection to Lesson 21: he explains why you should put the whole package on the table at once, not one change at a time.

Tip: watch the first fifteen minutes and note one rule you would use on the call with the chief people officer.
