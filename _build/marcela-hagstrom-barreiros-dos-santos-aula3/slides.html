Chapter 3: The Code

I Work, She Works

One tense for jobs, companies and facts

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Show?

"I work with data and marketing."

"My consultancy helps companies grow."

"Ryan’s team doesn’t track its leads."

"What does your company need?"

Look at the blue verbs. With I, the verb is simple: work. With my consultancy (it), the verb gets an -s: helps. Now the red words: in questions and negatives with he, she and it, does takes the -s, and the verb is simple again.

JobFormExample
I / you / we / theyverbI work with data.
he / she / itverb + s (or es)My consultancy helps companies. She does the analysis.
Negative: he / she / itdoesn’t + verbThe team doesn’t track its leads.
Wh-question: he / she / itWhat / How / Who + does + subject + verb?What does your company need?
Wh-question: I / you / we / theyWhat / How / Who + do + subject + verb?How do you work?
The trapthe -s goes to does, not to the verbNot What does it needs? Say What does it need?

In one line: with he, she and it the verb gets -s; in questions the -s moves to does.

Chapter 4: The First Call

The First Call

One call, two voices, one profile

Dialogue

The First Call

R
Hi, Marcela. Thanks for your time. So, what does your consultancy do?
M
Hi, Ryan. I am the founder. My consultancy looks at a company’s data and turns it into actionable marketing.
R
Interesting. We sell furniture online, and we spend a lot on paid media. But sales don’t grow.
M
I see. How do you track your results today?
R
Honestly, we don’t track much. My team looks at the ads, but nobody looks at the leads.
M
That is very common. Who talks to the leads after a campaign?
R
The sales team calls them, but they don’t use a CRM. They use a spreadsheet.
M
OK. So the first step is simple: we organize your data, and then we plan for growth.
Comprehension

About Ryan

1. What does Ryan’s company sell?
Furniture, online.
2. What is Ryan’s problem?
They spend a lot on paid media, but sales don’t grow.
3. What does Ryan’s sales team use to follow the leads?
A spreadsheet, not a CRM.

Katrin’s Team

A short, actionable report.
