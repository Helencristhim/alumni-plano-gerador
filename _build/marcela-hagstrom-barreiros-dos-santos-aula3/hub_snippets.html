What My Consultancy Does -- Your Work in One Minute

A new client asks what your company does. Key words: a founder, a consultancy, growth, a lead, a campaign, to track, actionable, paid media. Structure: present simple with he, she and it, and wh-questions with do and does.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A founder -- the person who starts a company
"I am the founder of a growth marketing consultancy."
A consultancy -- a company that gives expert advice to other companies
"My consultancy works with companies that want to grow."
Growth -- when a business gets bigger: more clients, more sales
"Our main focus is growth."
A lead -- a person or a company that may become a client
"The campaign brought two hundred new leads."
A campaign -- a group of actions to sell or promote something
"We plan every campaign with the client’s data."
To track -- to follow and measure something over time
"We track the results of every campaign."
Actionable -- you can use it to decide and to act
"A good report gives the client actionable ideas."
Paid media -- ads that a company pays for, like Google or Instagram ads
"The client spends a lot on paid media."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Arjun is the founder of a startup in India. His company makes an app for small restaurants. His partner writes the code, and Arjun talks to the clients. The company doesn’t have a marketing team, and it doesn’t track its campaigns.

Katrin works in Germany. Her team sends emails every week and tracks everything: who opens, who clicks and who buys.

And Marcela? She is the founder of a consultancy. What does it do? It turns data into actionable marketing, so companies can get more leads and more growth.

1. Who talks to the clients in Arjun’s company?
A His partner.
B Arjun.
C The marketing team.
2. What does Katrin’s team track?
A Only the number of emails.
B Who opens, who clicks and who buys.
C The prices of the products.
3. Which question is correct?
A “What does your consultancy does?”
B “What does your consultancy do?”
C “What your consultancy does?”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Present Simple -- He, She, It and Wh-Questions

GRAMMAR

Use the present simple for jobs, companies and facts. With he, she and it, the verb gets -s. In questions and negatives, the -s goes to does and the verb is simple.

JobFormExample
I / you / we / theyverbI work with data.
he / she / itverb + s (or es)My consultancy helps companies. She does the analysis.
Negative: he / she / itdoesn’t + verbThe team doesn’t track its leads.
Wh-question: he / she / itWhat / How / Who + does + subject + verb?What does your company need?
Wh-question: I / you / we / theyWhat / How / Who + do + subject + verb?How do you work?
The trapthe -s goes to does, not to the verbNot What does it needs? Say What does it need?

Never: My consultancy help companies · What does it needs? · He don’t track leads · How you track the results?
