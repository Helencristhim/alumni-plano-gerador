Real Speed

Stress the Word You Change

When you correct or rephrase, your voice lands on the new word. Listen, then say it back.

Not May. The deadline is in March.
I said the state tax, not the federal one.
Let me put it another way.

Read the Room

Four Faces, Four Moves

Explain something real. When the face changes, change your move, not your speed.

Confused
Slow down. Say it another way, with a comparison.
In a hurry
Jump to the headline and what you need.
Skeptical
Give one fact or one number that proves it.
Silent
Finish the sentence. Wait two seconds. Ask a specific question.

Language you can use

Let me put it another way.In other words, …Which part should I go over again?What would this mean for your team?

Under Pressure

The Camera Is On

A global call with Munich. The director has his camera on, says almost nothing, and frowns from time to time. You have five minutes on the tax impact for the quarter.

  • Round 1: you explain. He only reacts with his face. You check his understanding with specific questions.
  • Round 2: somebody explains too fast and goes around the point. You ask for clarification, and you stay in control.
