Talk
Erin Meyer: “How Cultural Differences Affect Business”

The author of The Culture Map on why the same silence, the same face and the same “yes” mean different things in Germany, India, France and the United States. Your five countries, explained.

Tip: while you watch, write the name of each country she mentions next to one colleague of yours. Is the face you see on calls really confusion?

TED Talk
Celeste Headlee: “10 ways to have a better conversation” (12 min)

A radio host who interviews people for a living on how to listen, how to ask, and how to stop filling every silence. Clear, warm American English at a normal speed.

Tip: pick the one rule you break most on calls. Use its opposite in one meeting this week, and tell your teacher what happened.

TED Talk
Julian Treasure: “5 ways to listen better” (8 min)

From the same speaker as lesson 1: this time about the other side of the call. What happens in your head while somebody talks, and how to follow them better.

Tip: try his first exercise before a call with the French team: three minutes of silence. Then notice how much more of the call you follow.
