The Award

Note on award: Meridian Foods v. Cortez Logistics (arbitration, London)

Meridian and Cortez signed a three-year distribution contract. The commercial terms were negotiated for six weeks. The clauses at the back were copied from a previous deal in a single afternoon.

In year two, Cortez missed eleven deliveries. Meridian terminated and claimed liquidated damages. Cortez argued that the parties had agreed a longer cure period in a side letter signed the same week as the contract. The tribunal never reached that question. The entire agreement clause was drafted so widely that the side letter fell outside the contract altogether, and with it the longer cure period. What decided the case was not the deliveries and not the damages formula. It was one sentence in the boilerplate.

The award runs to sixty pages. Two of them deal with the failures. The rest deal with the entire agreement clause, the absence of a severability provision, and whether notice had been validly given to an address that Cortez had left the year before.

Grammar

Same Facts, Different Focus

You do not raise your voice. You move where the sentence points.

Discover

Where Does the Sentence Point?

"The indemnification clause triggered the dispute."

"It was the indemnification clause that triggered the dispute."

"They did not disclose the lien."

"What they failed to disclose was the lien."

"We need to revisit the governing law provision."

"It is the governing law provision that we need to revisit."

"I am concerned about the scope of the representations."

"What I find most concerning is the scope of the representations."

It-cleftWhat-cleft
It is / It was + the thing + that...
Points at ONE element and excludes the others.
"It was the clause that decided it, not the deliveries."		What + subject + verb + is / was...
Names the topic first, the content after.
"What they failed to disclose was the lien."

Neither one adds information. Both decide where the listener looks — which is why they work when the discussion has drifted and you do not want to raise your voice.
