Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Down to Earth with Zac Efron — Season 1, Episode 1 "Iceland" (Netflix)

Two friends travelling and asking questions, in easy American English, with nothing heavy in it. Every episode is one long conversation with strangers who explain things — and half of what they say is in the tense of this lesson: what has happened, and what has not happened yet.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Write down five sentences that start with they have or it has.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "How to pack for your summer travel"

Fifteen minutes on what goes in the bag you keep with you and what goes in the one the airline takes away. It is the practical answer to the whole problem of this lesson, and it is full of the vocabulary you have just learned.

Tip: the full transcript is on the same page. Listen once, then decide what will be in your own hand luggage in October.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
Wolters World — "5 Things You Will Love & Hate About Wolters World Travel Videos"

Ten minutes of unscripted American English about what actually goes wrong when you travel and what to do about it. No slowing down and no subtitles written for learners — which is exactly the ear training this lesson needs.

Tip: play it once at normal speed with subtitles, then once at 0.75x with the subtitles off, and see how much you keep.
