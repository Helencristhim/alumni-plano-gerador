Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Celeste Headlee -- "10 ways to have a better conversation" (TED)

A radio host on how to ask questions that get real answers: open questions, short questions, and the courage to say I do not know. It connects directly with today: asking clearly, and admitting when you will get back to somebody.

Tip: pick two of her ten rules and say how you will use them in your next call with the VP, with could you or I was wondering if.

Talk
Julian Treasure -- "How to speak so that people want to listen" (TED)

A talk about the voice: tone, pace and the calm, falling intonation you worked on today. Useful for anybody who has to ask a senior person for something and wants to sound sure.

Tip: listen to the part about register and prosody, then record one of your survival phrases twice: once fast and high, once slow and falling.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
Adam Grant -- "Are you a giver or a taker?" (TED)

An organizational psychologist on how people ask for help at work, and why the people who ask well get more of it. A good bridge between polite language and real results.

Tip: note one sentence Grant uses to ask or to offer something, and rewrite it with would you mind or I was wondering if.
