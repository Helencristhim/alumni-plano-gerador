Chapter 3 -- The Top Code

Grammar Discovery

Number one in a group

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Zurich is the most expensive city on the list."

"The Basel center is the cheapest option."

"Lucerne has the coziest hotel of the three."

"It was the best trip of my life."

What do the orange words have in common? How many options are we comparing?

three or more: the + -est · the most + long adjective

The superlative says which one is number one in a group. Short adjectives take the ... -est (the cheapest). Long adjectives take the most (the most expensive). Two irregular ones: good → the best, bad → the worst.

The Top Code

-est or the most?

AdjectiveRuleExample
Shortthe + adjective + -estcheap → the cheapest
big → the biggest
Ends in -ythe + -iestcozy → the coziest
lively → the liveliest
Longthe most + adjectiveconvenient → the most convenient
Irregularmemorizegood → the best
bad → the worst
After itin + place · of + groupthe biggest city in Brazil
the best day of the week

Two options: cheaper than. Three or more: the cheapest.

Sound Check

"-est" is short

It's the cheapest option.

no extra sound after -est

It's the most convenient hotel.

"most" is long, the t is soft

Chapter 4 -- The Team Offsite

Three Options, One Choice

Listen, compare, and decide

Dialogue

Tom's Offsite

A
Aline: Hi, Tom! Did you choose the place for the team offsite?
T
Tom: Not yet. I have three options: Zurich, Lucerne or Basel. Can you help me?
A
Aline: Sure. Which one is the most convenient?
T
Tom: Zurich. The hotel is next to the airport, but it's the most expensive: four hundred francs a night.
A
Aline: Wow. And Lucerne?
T
Tom: Lucerne is the most beautiful. The hotel is small and cozy, with an impressive view of the lake. But it's the farthest from the airport.
A
Aline: And the conference center in Basel?
T
Tom: It's the most affordable, and it has the biggest meeting room. Basel isn't the liveliest city, but the trains are very reliable.
A
Aline: We are forty people, so Basel is the best choice. And we can have the team dinner in Lucerne!
T
Tom: Great idea. A dinner by the lake is worth it.
Did You Understand?

About Tom's Options

1. According to Tom, which option is the most expensive?
Zurich: four hundred francs a night. But it is next to the airport.
2. What does Tom say about the hotel in Lucerne?
It is small and cozy, with an impressive view of the lake, but it is the farthest from the airport.
3. What are the good points of Basel, for Tom?
It is the most affordable, it has the biggest meeting room, and the trains are reliable.
