Chapter 3: The Code

-ing or to

The form the verb before it decides

Grammar Discovery

What Comes After the Verb?

I recommend starting with the two worst sites.
We decided to fund the pilot.
The role involves managing four countries.
She stopped to talk to him. / She stopped talking to him.
Click to reveal the rule
There is no logic to deduce — there are groups to learn. Verbs followed by -ing: recommend, suggest, consider, avoid, involve, keep, finish, practice, appreciate. Verbs followed by to + verb: decide, plan, agree, manage, expect, need, want, offer, prepare, refuse. After a preposition, always -ing: committed to delivering, in order to get — careful, the to of commit to is a preposition, so it takes -ing; the to of decide to is an infinitive, so it does not. And four verbs change meaning: She stopped to talk (she paused in order to talk) is the opposite of She stopped talking (she went quiet). Same with remember, forget and try.

What Are You Promising?

M
So what do you recommend? In one sentence.
A
I recommend starting with the two sites that lose the most people, and postponing the global rollout until the second quarter.
M
The board will not like waiting. What do we get for the delay?
A
Evidence. That is the trade-off, and I would rather say it out loud: we give up speed in order to get numbers we can defend.
M
They will want something before March. Can you give them a quick win?
A
I can. I am prepared to deliver the exit interview report in three weeks. What I would avoid is promising the whole program on that date.
M
Understood. And what are you actually committing to?
A
To reporting the first numbers on the fifteenth of March. That one has my name on it. The rest is a recommendation until this room decides.

Chapter 5: Practice

How Much Did You Just Promise?

The verb form is the answer

Listening 2

Keep the Trade-off Visible

Read the questions, then listen.

1. What does she say you are asking them to give up, and in exchange for what?
Speed, in order to get evidence — and she says that is a fair exchange as long as you say it out loud.
2. What would she avoid doing?
Presenting the recommendation as if there were no cost.
3. What happens to teams that refuse to give a quick win?
In her experience, they end up losing the whole programme.
