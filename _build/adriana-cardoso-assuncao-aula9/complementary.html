Lesson 9 -- Recommend, Decide, Commit

Talk
Ruth Chang -- "How to make hard choices"

A philosopher on the decisions where there is no best option, only a trade-off. It is the argument you will use every time somebody asks why you did not do everything.

Tip: listen for every "I recommend", "we decided" and "I chose", and notice which verb form follows each one.

Video
BBC Learning English -- 6 Minute English, "How important is politeness?"

Six minutes at natural speed on how people soften what they ask for. Useful here for the verbs: suggest, recommend, offer, refuse — each one with its own form after it.

Tip: open the transcript and mark every verb followed by -ing and every verb followed by to. Two lists, five minutes.

The Diplomat -- Season 1, Episode 2 "Fair Winds and Following Seas"

An hour of people recommending, deciding and refusing to commit, in rooms where the difference between those three has consequences. Fast dialogue, corporate rhythm.
