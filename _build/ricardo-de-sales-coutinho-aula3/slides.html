Chapter 3: I Wake Up At

What You Do

the verb, and then the clock

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I wake up at five."

"I go to the gym at half past five."

"I have breakfast at six thirty."

"I start work at nine o'clock."

PartFormExample
Who + what you doI + verbI wake up / I go / I have
The clockat + hourat five, at nine o'clock
The part of the dayin the...in the morning, in the evening

With I the verb never changes. No extra -s.
