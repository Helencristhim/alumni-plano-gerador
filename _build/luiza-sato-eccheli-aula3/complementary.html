Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Julian Treasure — “How to speak so that people want to listen” (TED)

Ten minutes on pace, pitch and silence: the tools behind a clear summary that does not trail off. Useful for the seminar and for the new TOEFL speaking tasks.

Tip: try his warm-up exercises once, then record a thirty-second summary of the talk in three blocks.

Watch on TED ↗
Series
How to Get Away with Murder — Season 1, Episode 1, “Pilot”

A law professor, a seminar full of ambitious students and a lot of fast arguing about evidence. Much darker than your classes, but full of the language of today’s lesson.

Tip: after each scene in the classroom, pause and report in one sentence what the professor argued.

See the episode on TVmaze ↗
Video
TST Prep — “TOEFL Speaking: Listen and Repeat Training (2026)” (YouTube)

Practice for the new Listen and Repeat task: you hear a sentence once and repeat it with the same rhythm, in one block. It is the exact skill that stops you from stumbling.

Tip: repeat each sentence without pausing in the middle. If you stop, start the whole sentence again.
