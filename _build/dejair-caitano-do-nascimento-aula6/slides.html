Chapter 3: The Code

Two Things, Side by Side

More than, less than, and the one at the top

Discover the Rule

Why Did Two of Them Change Ending?

Her explanation was clearer than mine.
This design is more complex than the previous one.
That was the smallest room in the building.
It was also the most useful session of the week.
Click to reveal the rule
Count the syllables. Short adjectives (one syllable, or two ending in -y) take -er and the -est: clear, clearer, the clearest. Longer adjectives take more and the most: useful, more useful, the most useful. Never both at once. Three you have to learn by heart: good, better, the best — bad, worse, the worst — far, further, the furthest.

Chapter 4: In the Room

The Rules, and Then Somebody Else First

A facilitator from Russia, and a participant from India

Listening 1

The Facilitator's Three Rules

Read the questions, then listen.

1. How do people work in this session, and what does each pair get?
In pairs. Every pair gets two studies to compare.
2. How long does each person have, and what is the most common problem in the room?
Two minutes each. The problem is not silence -- it is people who talk for eight minutes.
3. What does she NOT want to hear, and what does she want instead?
She does not want to hear which study is better. She wants to hear why.

Chapter 5: Practice

The Answer You Will Have to Give

Two minutes, out loud, in front of twenty people

Listening 2

Bigger Is Not the Same as More Useful

Read the questions, then listen.

1. How many patients are in each study, and which one is more detailed?
About four hundred in the first and only sixty in the second. The small one is far more detailed.
2. Give two reasons why he calls the small study more detailed.
They followed each patient for two years, and they measured pain at home instead of at the hospital. Their method is also clearer.
3. What is the difference between the better study and the one he is going to use?
The better study is the large one. The one he is going to use is the small one, because his patients also live far from the hospital.
