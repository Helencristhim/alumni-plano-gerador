Lesson 22 -- Navigating Opinions

Lesson theme: agreeing, disagreeing and debating politely, and measuring a difference with comparatives (the more..., the less..., nowhere near as ... as, far more). Extra practice outside class -- tick each one when you finish it.

Film
12 Angry Men (1957) -- directed by Sidney Lumet

Twelve men in one hot room must agree on a verdict, and one of them begs to differ. The whole film is a debate: people concede points, back up claims, play devil's advocate and, one by one, come around. Clear, slow American English, almost all of it dialogue.

Tip: after each vote, pause and say who changed his mind and why, in one sentence.

Podcast
NPR Short Wave -- The science of disagreeing well

What happens in the brain during an argument, why we want to shut down, and what helps two people who disagree keep listening. Free to stream on the NPR page, in natural American English at real speed.

Tip: listen once, then write down three phrases the guests use to disagree politely.
