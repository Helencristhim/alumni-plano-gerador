Chapter 3: The Code

One Word, Three Jobs

ability · permission · asking

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I can understand almost everything."

"I cannot speak very fast."

"Can I ask a question?"

"She can present the platform on Thursday."

JobFormExample
abilitycan + verbI can understand.
negativecannot / can't + verbI can't speak fast.
permissionCan I + verb?Can I ask a question?
asking someoneCan you + verb?Can you repeat, please?
Key ideaAfter can the verb is CLEAN: no to, no -s. She can speak -- never can to speak, never cans.

And can never changes: I can, you can, he can, we can. One form for everybody.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I can to speak a little English."
"I can speak a little English."
"She cans present on Thursday."
"She can present on Thursday."
"Do you can repeat, please?"
"Can you repeat, please?"

Can is already the helper. It does not need do in the question, and it never takes an -s.

Can I Use My Notes?

P
Hello, Joice. Can I ask you something before the call?
J
Of course. But please, slowly -- I cannot speak very fast.
P
No problem. Can you present the platform on Thursday?
J
I can present it, yes. But can I use my notes?
P
You can use your notes, and you can speak slowly. Everybody can wait.
J
Thank you. And can I ask a question in the chat?
P
Yes, always. If you cannot understand a person, write it in the chat.
J
Good. I can understand almost everything. Speaking is the hard part.
P
It is the hard part for everybody. Can you attend the meeting on Monday?
J
I can attend on Monday, but I cannot on Friday.
Comprehension

About Paul

1. What can Joice use in the presentation, according to Paul?
She can use her notes.
2. What does Paul say about speaking slowly?
That she can speak slowly and everybody can wait.
3. What can she do if she cannot understand a person?
She can write it in the chat.
