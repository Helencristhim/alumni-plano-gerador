Complementary Activities

Extra material to keep English running in your head between classes. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen. None of this is homework you have to be perfect at — it is input.

Lesson 1 -- Who Is Ana Luiza?

Grammar Video
"Present Simple and Present Continuous" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six calm minutes on the only decision you had to make in this lesson: is it always true, or is it true right now? Connection to Lesson 1: this is the grammar behind "I work in advertising" and "this month I'm working on a festival".

Tip: after each example, pause and say two sentences about yourself out loud — one always true, one true only this week. Two sentences, then stop. Six minutes total.

Podcast
"S1E04 -- Great Brands That Never Advertise" -- Under the Influence, with Terry O'Reilly

Terry O'Reilly spent his career inside advertising agencies, and here he tells the story of the huge brands that built themselves with almost no advertising at all. Slow, warm North American English, and a host who tells stories instead of listing facts. Connection to Lesson 1: it is your own industry in English, and it is full of the two present tenses used naturally.

Tip: listen once without stopping, even if you lose some parts. Then write down three brands he mentions and say one sentence about each: "They don't advertise, but everybody knows them."

Series
"Mystic Mountain" -- Bob Ross, The Joy of Painting (full episode)

Half an hour of a man painting a mountain and describing every single thing he does while he does it. For a beginner painter this is a class, and for a listener it is the clearest present continuous you will ever hear: "I'm just going to take a little bit of white and pull it across here." Connection to Lesson 1: the whole episode is the grammar of right now.

Tip: watch with no subtitles and count how many times he says "I'm going to" or "I'm just" plus a verb. Then paint something small yourself and describe three steps out loud in English while you do it.
