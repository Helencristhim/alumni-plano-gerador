Chapter 3: The Code

Always True, or True Right Now?

Two options. That is the whole decision.

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I work in an advertising agency."

"This month I am working on a music festival."

"I go to the gym at six every morning."

"These days I am studying English again."

Two of them are always true. Two are only true these days. Which is which?

FormUseExample
Present simple
I work / she works		Always true: facts about you, routines, things that do not change this weekI live in Sao Paulo. She works from home.
Present continuous
I am working		True right now, or temporary around now: today, this week, this month, these daysI am working on a festival this month.
The signal wordsalways, usually, every day, never point to the simple. Right now, today, this week, at the moment point to the continuous.
The short listSome verbs stay simple even when you mean now: know, want, like, love, need, mean, understand. Say "I want a coffee", never "I am wanting".

One question, two answers. Is it always, or is it now? When you are not sure, choose the simple and keep talking — the sentence matters more than the form.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I am living in Sao Paulo."
"I live in Sao Paulo."
"I am liking this series a lot."
"I like this series a lot."
"Every morning I am going to the gym."
"Every morning I go to the gym."

Where you live is a fact, not a temporary situation. Verbs like like, want, know never take the continuous. And every morning is a routine, so it takes the simple.

Dialogue

Dinner at a Friend's Place

N
So you must be Ana. I'm Nathan, a friend of Rafa's. I'm from Austin, Texas.
A
Nice to meet you, Nathan. Are you living here now, or just visiting?
N
Just visiting. I'm staying for three weeks. I'm shooting a photo series about street art here.
A
Three weeks is a good amount of time. What do you usually do on a normal day back home?
N
I'm an early riser, so I shoot in the morning and I wind down in the afternoon. And I play a lot of tennis.
A
Tennis? I'm really into tennis too. I'm terrible at it, but I'm into it.
N
That's the only honest way to start. And what do you do, when you're not losing at tennis?
A
I work in advertising. I come up with ideas for brands, and this month I'm pitching a music festival.

Free Practice

The Whole Conversation

Scenario: From "nice to meet you" to a restaurant recommendation: introduce yourself, describe a normal day, say what you are working on this month, ask him two questions, and tell him where to eat in Sao Paulo.

No keywords, no interruptions. If a sentence breaks, start it again and keep going.

Diagnostic Baseline

Your Starting Point

Record two minutes, as if it were the first minute of a video interview for a remote international role: your name, your city, what you do, one project you are proud of, and why you want to work internationally.

Do not write a script. Speak, even if it comes out imperfect — that is the point.

Nobody is grading this. In a few weeks we record it again and hear the difference.
