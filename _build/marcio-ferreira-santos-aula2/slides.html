Chapter 3: The Code

Present Perfect vs Past Simple

I have delivered · In 2022, I delivered

Grammar Discovery

Read and Notice

"I have worked in pricing for more than ten years."

"In 2022, I led the new pricing project."

"We have delivered three cost reductions since 2021."

"Last quarter, we reached the target."

Look at the time in each sentence. Why is one verb orange and the other blue?

FormUseExample
Present perfect
have / has + past participle		Experience and results, with no closed moment. It is still true today.I have delivered three projects.
Past simple
verb + -ed or new form		A closed moment in the past: in 2022, last year, two years ago.In 2022, I led the project.
for / sinceOnly with the present perfect: for + a period, since + a starting point.for ten years / since 2019
Negative & questionPresent perfect: Have you ever presented to a board? / I have not presented yet. — Past simple: Did you present in 2022? / I did not present.

One question decides everything: is there a closed moment in the sentence? If yes, past simple. If no, present perfect.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I work in pricing since 2019."
"I have worked in pricing since 2019."
"I have led the project in 2022."
"I led the project in 2022."
"I am working in this company for ten years."
"I have worked in this company for ten years."

A closed year (in 2022) always takes the past simple. For and since always take the present perfect.
