Lesson 23 -- Persuasion Techniques in Business English

Series
Better Call Saul -- Season 1, Episode 1 "Uno"

Jimmy McGill is a lawyer with no clients and no money, so he has to sell himself in every conversation. Connection to Lesson 23: watch how he tries to win over a hesitant couple, and how questions do much of the work in his pitch.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down every rhetorical question you hear, and say which one works best and why.

Talk
"How great leaders inspire action" -- Simon Sinek, TED

One of the most watched TED talks: people do not buy what you do, they buy why you do it. Connection to Lesson 23: it is a lesson in value propositions. Notice how Sinek starts with a question before he gives any answer.

Tip: listen once without subtitles. Then write your team's value proposition in one sentence, starting with "why".

Watch on TED ↗
Video
"Nancy Duarte uncovers common structure of greatest communicators" -- TEDxEast

A presentation designer shows the pattern behind persuasive speeches: move between "what is" and "what could be", then call the audience to action. Connection to Lesson 23: "What if...?" is how you take a client from what is to what could be.

Tip: watch the first ten minutes. Then plan a two-minute pitch for a real client with one "what is" and one "what could be".
