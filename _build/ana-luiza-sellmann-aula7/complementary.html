Grammar Video
"Used to" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the structure you practiced today, including the part everybody gets wrong: after "did", the D disappears. Connection to Lesson 7: this is "I used to walk", "I didn't use to eat" and "Did you use to live here?".

Tip: repeat every example out loud and listen to your own S sound. The word is YOOS-to, one block. If you hear a Z, say it again.

Podcast
"Exit Interview with Michael Bierut" -- 99% Invisible

A designer looks back at a whole career and says out loud what the work used to be like, what changed, and what he thinks was better before. Calm American English, with the full transcript on the page. Connection to Lesson 7: it is an hour of somebody describing an industry that is not what it used to be, which is exactly your free practice.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then find three sentences where he compares before and now, and say your own version of each one about advertising.

Talk
"Design and discovery" -- David Carson, TED (17 minutes)

A designer who used to be a professional surfer and a high school teacher before he changed the look of magazines forever. He talks about the accidents and the wrong turns that built the work. Clear American English, lots of images. Connection to Lesson 7: a whole talk about what somebody used to be before becoming what they are.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then answer out loud in one minute: what did you use to want to be, and what did you become instead?
