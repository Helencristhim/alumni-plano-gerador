Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"It is twenty-four pounds."

"Two glasses of wine, please."

Two of something: what is new at the end of the word?

To sayFormExample
two or moreword + sone table → two tables
words with s, ss, ch, sh, xword + esone glass → two glasses
the priceHow much is it?How much is the wine? It is nine pounds.

Two or more: add s (or es). The price: How much is...?
