Grammar Video
"Present and past passives" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the shape you practiced today: be plus the participle, in the present and in the past. It also covers when to add "by somebody" and when to leave it out. Connection to Lesson 8: this is "the workshops are held" and "the concept was approved".

Tip: after each example, say the same fact twice out loud, once with the person and once without. Notice which one you would actually send to a client.

How copies are made, who is allowed to make them and why fashion is treated differently from every other industry. It is about brands, ownership and imitation, which is your world seen from the outside. The full transcript is on the page.

Tip: listen once for the story. Then find three sentences describing how something is made or sold, and say each one again with the person removed.

Talk
"How giant websites design for you (and a billion others, too)" -- Margaret Gould Stewart, TED (9 minutes)

A designer explains how a change of a few pixels is tested, argued about and finally shipped to a billion people. Nine minutes, clear American English. Connection to Lesson 8: almost every sentence in it describes a decision without naming who made it, which is exactly the register of your status updates.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and count how many times you hear "was designed", "is tested", "has been changed". Then describe one project of yours the same way.
