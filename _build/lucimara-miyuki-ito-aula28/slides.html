Chapter 4: What They Asked

He Asked Me To...

asked me to · told us to · told her not to

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

""Could you choose the wine?" → The host asked me to choose the wine."

""Please, wait at the bar." → The waiter asked us to wait at the bar."

""Arrive on time." → My colleague told me to arrive on time."

""Don't talk about prices." → He told her not to talk about prices."

Look at the orange words. What comes right after asked and told?

FormUseExample
ask + person + to + verbto report a request ("Could you...?", "Please...")She asked me to choose the wine.
tell + person + to + verbto report an instruction or an orderHe told us to arrive at seven.
ask / tell + person + not to + verbto report a negative request or order ("Don't...")He told me not to talk about prices.
say (that) + sentence (Lesson 16)to report a statement, not a requestShe said that the food was great.

After ask and tell, always say the person: "He told me to...". Never "He said me to..." and never "He told to me...".

Dinner with a Client

A manager tells the story of a business dinner in New York. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What did her boss tell her to do before the dinner?
To choose a quiet place. She also asked her not to order too much food.
2. Why did she send the menu to her client the day before?
Because he is a picky eater. She asked him to tell her what he didn't eat.
