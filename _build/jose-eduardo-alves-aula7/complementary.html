Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Documentary
Bloomberg Originals — "How Florida Became 'Wall Street South'"

Miami against New York, argued by people who moved: cheaper, warmer, closer to Latin America, but smaller and younger. It is the dialogue of this lesson, with the same two cities and real money behind the opinions.

Tip: every time somebody prefers one city, pause and rebuild the sentence yourself with than. Ten sentences is enough.

Podcast
The Indicator from Planet Money (NPR) — "Can American cities grow AND stay affordable?"

Three American cities compared on one question, with the numbers on the table. Nine minutes, and almost every sentence in it puts one place above another and then says why.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Find five comparatives in it, and mark which ones changed their ending and which ones borrowed more.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
Euronews Next — "What makes Vienna the world's most liveable city?"

A superlative defended point by point: the highest score, the cheapest housing, the best public transport. Short, clear, and built entirely out of the structure you learned today.

Tip: at the end, say out loud which city you would put first and why, in three comparisons. Nobody has to agree with the ranking.
