Talk
"10 ways to have a better conversation" -- Celeste Headlee, TED

Eleven minutes from a radio interviewer about listening properly and asking questions you actually want answered. Connection to Lesson 4: rule number one in her list is the same one the column gave you — one real conversation beats six polite ones.

Tip: watch it once with English subtitles and write down the two rules you break most often. Then use only those two in your next call with a colleague from another country.

Grammar Video
"Present perfect with 'ever' and 'never'" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the two words that open and answer every question about experience. Connection to Lesson 4: ever lives in the question, never lives in the answer, and neither of them ever takes a second negative.

Tip: pause after each example and ask the question out loud about a colleague, then answer it about yourself. Two sentences each time. If the word lands in the wrong place, say the pair again.
