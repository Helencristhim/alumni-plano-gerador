Podcast
Hidden Brain (NPR) -- "Creatures of Habit"

A psychologist explains how habits form and why we keep doing things we have decided to stop doing. It is the lesson’s homework topic, what you plan to do differently, told by a researcher. Audio and transcript on the same page.

Tip: every time you hear keep, stop, try, avoid or decide, write down the verb that follows it and whether it is -ing or to.

Talk
Tim Urban -- "Inside the mind of a master procrastinator" (TED)

A funny, fast American talk about putting things off: what he avoided doing, what he kept doing instead, and what he finally decided to do. It is study routine English at natural speed, with interactive subtitles.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles, then a second time without. Pause after every avoid, keep and decide and say the full sentence aloud.

Talk
Angela Lee Duckworth -- "Grit: The power of passion and perseverance" (TED)

Six minutes on why some people manage to finish what they start. She talks about students, but it is exactly the language of a long exam preparation: keep going, fail to, manage to, decide to.

Tip: write down three sentences from the talk with manage to, keep or fail to, then make one true sentence about your own Step 3 preparation with each.
