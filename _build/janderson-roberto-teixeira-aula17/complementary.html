"Used to" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on exactly the structure you practiced in class, including the part everybody gets wrong in writing: used to, and use to after did. Connection to Lesson 17: the presenters also compare it with the past simple, the same test you used for one time versus a long time.

Tip: pause after each example and say a sentence about your own life ten years ago, once positive and once negative.

"Was Christmas better in the past?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes in which two presenters talk about nostalgia and whether things really used to be better. Connection to Lesson 17: the whole episode compares how things were with how they are, so you will hear used to and back then again and again. Audio, vocabulary and full transcript are free on the page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then find three sentences about the past that are not true now, and say one of your own about your hometown.

Talk
"The psychology of your future self" -- Dan Gilbert, TED

Seven minutes on a surprising idea: we all know how much we changed in the last ten years, and we all think we will not change much in the next ten. Connection to Lesson 17: it is a talk about who we used to be and who we are nowadays, which is exactly the story you told in class.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then say three things you used to believe ten years ago that you do not believe now.
