Which Word Do You Need?

1. You arrive at three in the morning and the room is not ready.
Luggage. "Can I leave my luggage here, please?"
2. The first sentence you say at the desk, before your name.
Reservation. "Good evening. I have a reservation."
3. You want to know where to go tomorrow at eight in the morning.
Breakfast. "What time is breakfast, and where is it?"
4. You have the number 512 and you need to know how high to go.
Floor. "What floor is the room on?"
Chapter 3: There Is, There Are

How to Describe Any Place

there is · there are

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"There is a big bed in the room."

"There are two elevators next to the desk."

"There is no restaurant here at night."

"Are there two keys for the room?"

How manyFormExample
One thingThere is · short: There'sThere is a desk near the window.
Two or moreThere areThere are two elevators.
ZeroThere is no / There are noThere is no restaurant here.
To askIs there...? / Are there...?Is there a safe in the room?

Portuguese uses the same word for one and for many. English does not: the number decides between is and are, every time.

Numbers That Open Doors

Your Room, Your Floor

"Room five twelve."  ·  512

in a hotel, never "five hundred and twelve"

"It is on the fifth floor."

the floor takes fifth, not five

"What floor is the room on?"

"Room five twelve, on the fifth floor. Thank you."

repeat the number back — this is what a careful traveler does

Dialogue

Do You Have a Reservation?

S
Good evening. Welcome to the Hotel Marlowe. Do you have a reservation?
L
Good evening. Yes, I have a reservation. My name is Leão de Souza.
S
Thank you. Your passport, please... Perfect. Five nights, is that right?
L
Yes, five nights.
S
Here is your key. It is a card. Room five twelve, on the fifth floor.
L
Room five twelve, fifth floor. Thank you. Where are the elevators?
S
There are two, right there, next to the desk. And breakfast is from seven to ten on the first floor.
L
Sorry, can you say that again, please? What time is breakfast?
S
Of course. From seven to ten, in the morning. First floor.
L
Perfect. One more thing: is there a safe in the room?
S
Yes, there is. It is in the closet. Have a good night, Mr. de Souza.
