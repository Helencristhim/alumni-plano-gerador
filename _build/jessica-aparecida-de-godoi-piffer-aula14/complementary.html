Talk
Hans Rosling — “The best stats you’ve ever seen” (TED)

Twenty minutes in which a public health professor makes a room full of people cheer for a chart. The best example there is of the lesson’s idea: a number only matters when it tells a story.

Tip: pause three times and describe the moving line in one sentence with a trend word and by or to. Then listen to how he does it.

Podcast
99% Invisible — “Florence Nightingale: Data Viz Pioneer” (episode 433)

How a nurse used a chart to change the way a whole army treated its soldiers — and the uncomfortable question of whether her chart told the whole truth. History, investigation and data in one story.

Tip: write down every word the narrator uses for a number going up or down, and the small word after it.

Video
BBC — “200 Countries, 200 Years, 4 Minutes” (Hans Rosling)

Four minutes, two hundred years of health and wealth, one chart that moves. Fast, clear English and a perfect model for describing a trend while it happens.

Tip: watch it once with the sound off and describe it yourself. Then watch it with sound and compare your verbs with his.
