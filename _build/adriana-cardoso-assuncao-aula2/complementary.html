Lesson 2 -- Reading the Numbers

Talk
Mona Chalabi -- "3 ways to spot a bad statistic"

A data journalist shows how a single headline number can be technically correct and completely misleading. It is the argument of today's reading, told with other examples.

Tip: after each of her three points, pause and say out loud which HR number in your own reports has the same problem.
