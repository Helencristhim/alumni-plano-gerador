Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Suits — Season 1, Episode 1, “Pilot”

A New York law firm, a lot of fast legal English and a hero who reads faster than anyone in the room. It is corporate law, not international law, but the vocabulary of obligations, agreements and breaches is everywhere.

Tip: every time a character says signed, binding or breach, pause and say the sentence again in the passive.

Video
BBC Learning English — “Where do international laws come from?” (YouTube)

A short animated explainer on where international law comes from and why countries follow it. Clear British English, and exactly the argument of today’s reading.

Tip: watch once without subtitles and write down three passive sentences you hear. Then check with subtitles on.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
TED-Ed — “Why do we need international law? An animated explainer”

A TED-Ed lesson with the video, extra questions and a short reading, on how international law bridges the gap between sovereign states. Short, and useful for your Global Law course.

Tip: answer the questions on the page out loud before you read the answers.
