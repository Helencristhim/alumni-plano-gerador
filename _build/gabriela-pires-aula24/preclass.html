Series Marathon Recap

Recapping an episode and recommending it: screen vocabulary (episode, season, plot, ending, actor, boring, exciting, spoiler, binge-watch) and the language to give an opinion with a reason ("It is boring, because nothing happens", "The season is short, so I finished it in a weekend"). Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. Toque em Listen para ouvir. / Listen to each word and read the example.

Episode -- one single part of a series, usually 30 to 60 minutes long
"I watched the first two episodes yesterday." (Episódio)
Season -- a group of episodes that tell one part of the whole story
"He finished the whole first season last night." (Temporada)
Plot -- the events of the story: what happens, and in what order
"The plot is simple: a girl moves to a new town." (Enredo / trama)
Ending -- the last part of the story, and how it finishes
"I will not tell you the ending." (Final)
Actor -- the person who plays a part in a film or a series
"The actor who plays the brother is fantastic." (Ator / atriz)
Boring -- not interesting, so you want to stop watching
"The first episode is slow and a bit boring." (Chato / entediante)
Exciting -- so interesting that you want to keep watching
"Episode two is exciting, because everything changes." (Emocionante)
Spoiler -- information about the story that ruins the surprise for other people
"I will not tell you the ending: no spoilers." (Spoiler / estraga-prazeres)
Binge-watch -- to watch many episodes one after the other, without stopping
"I want to binge-watch the rest this weekend." (Maratonar)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto e responda às perguntas. / Read the text and answer the questions.

Ryan finished the whole first season in one night. "It is only eight episodes, so it is a perfect weekend," he says. The plot is strange at the beginning, and he almost stopped, because the first episode is slow and a little boring. But in episode three everything changes and it becomes really exciting. The actor who plays the brother is only seventeen, and Ryan thinks he is fantastic. He will not talk about the ending, because he does not want to give Gabriela a spoiler. He asks one thing: do not binge-watch it in one night, because tomorrow there is school.

1. Why is the season perfect for a weekend?
A Because it has only eight episodes.
B Because the plot is strange.
C Because the actor is seventeen.
2. Why did Ryan almost stop watching?
A Because the ending is bad.
B Because the first episode is slow and a little boring.
C Because he had school the next day.
3. Why does he not talk about the ending?
A Because he does not remember it.
B Because the ending is boring.
C Because he does not want to give a spoiler.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Opinion with because and so

GRAMMAR

Opinião sozinha é fraca; opinião com motivo é uma resenha. Use because quando vem o MOTIVO e so quando vem a CONSEQUÊNCIA. As duas dizem a mesma ideia na ordem inversa, e NUNCA aparecem na mesma frase. / Use because before the reason and so before the result — never both in one sentence.

Word / PalavraWhat comes after / O que vem depoisExample / Exemplo
becausethe reason / o motivoIt is boring, because nothing happens.
sothe result / a consequênciaNothing happens, so it is boring.
boring / boreda coisa / a pessoaThe episode is boring. I was bored.
a little / a bitsuaviza a opiniãoIt is a little boring at the start.
You shouldrecomendar + verbo simplesYou should watch it, because it is short.

Key point / Ponto-chave: "It is boring, because nothing happens" e "Nothing happens, so it is boring" dizem a MESMA coisa. Escolha uma das duas — nunca "Because..., so...". E cuidado com o falso amigo: o episódio é boring, você fica bored.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Escolha a melhor frase para cada situação. / Choose the best sentence for each situation.

Nothing happens in the episode. You give your opinion with the reason:
A "It is boring, so nothing happens."
B "It is boring, because nothing happens."
C "Because it is boring, so nothing happens."
You did not enjoy the first episode at all. About yourself you say:
A "I was very boring."
B "I was very bored."
C "I was very boredom."
A friend is going to tell you the ending. You stop them:
A "Stop! I hate spoilers, so do not tell me the ending."
B "Stop! I hate spoilers, because do not tell me the ending."
C "Stop! I am a spoiler, so tell me the ending."
