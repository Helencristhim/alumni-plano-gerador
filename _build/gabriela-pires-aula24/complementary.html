Never Have I Ever -- a teen series to watch and then talk about

A funny teen series about a fifteen-year-old girl at school. The episodes are short, so it is perfect for one recap a week.

Tip: watch one episode, then answer out loud: who is your favorite actor, is the plot exciting or boring, and how does the episode end? Do not give a spoiler to a friend.
