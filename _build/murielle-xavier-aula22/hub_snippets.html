Numbers That Tell a Story -- Used To

Telling the story behind HR numbers with used to for past habits and states (used to, didn't use to, Did you use to...?) and the present simple for today. New words: back then, nowadays, paperwork, timesheet, payroll, outdated, digitize, replace, hierarchy, commute, hybrid, evolve.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example.

Back then -- at that time in the past
"Back then, we used to print every contract."
Nowadays -- at the present time, different from the past
"Nowadays, we sign contracts online."
Paperwork -- the documents and forms you have to complete
"We used to spend hours on paperwork."
Timesheet -- a record of the hours a person works
"Employees used to fill in paper timesheets."
Payroll -- the process of paying employees their salaries
"Payroll used to take three full days."
Outdated -- old and not useful anymore
"Our old evaluation form was outdated."
Digitize -- to change paper information into digital files
"We digitized all employee records in 2019."
Replace -- to put something new in the place of something old
"An app replaced the paper timesheets."
Hierarchy -- a system where people have different levels of power
"The hierarchy used to be very rigid."
Commute -- the trip between your home and your work
"My commute used to take two hours."
Hybrid -- partly at the office and partly at home
"Most of the HR team has a hybrid schedule now."
Evolve -- to change and develop slowly over time
"Our HR policies evolved a lot."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

When I joined Minerva Foods, HR was very different. Back then, we used to do everything on paper, and the paperwork never ended. Employees used to fill in paper timesheets every week, and payroll used to take three full days. We didn't use to have a hybrid schedule, so many people had a long commute. Turnover used to be eighteen percent. In 2019 we digitized all employee records, and an app replaced the paper timesheets. Nowadays, turnover is around nine percent, and the old process looks outdated. Our HR has really evolved.

1. What did employees use to fill in every week?
A Online forms on the new mobile app.
B Nothing at all.
C Paper timesheets.
2. Which sentence is correct?
A "We didn't use to have a hybrid schedule."
B "We didn't used to have a hybrid schedule."
C "We not use to have a hybrid schedule."
3. Why do we say "Nowadays, we sign" and not "Nowadays, we used to sign"?
A Because sign is an irregular verb.
B Because used to is only for the past; for today we use the present simple.
C Because nowadays is a question word.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Used To (Past Habits and States)

GRAMMAR

Use used to + base verb for habits and states in the past that are not true now: We used to print every contract. In negatives and questions, use didn't use to and Did ... use to (no -d). For the situation today, use the present simple.

FormUseExample
used to + base verbpast habit or state, not true nowWe used to print every contract.
didn't use to + base verbnegative (no -d after didn't)We didn't use to have a hybrid schedule.
Did + subject + use to ...?question (no -d after did)Did you use to work on Saturdays?
present simplethe situation todayNowadays, we sign contracts online.

Tip: used to is only for the past. After did and didn't, write use to without -d. For today, say Nowadays + present simple: Nowadays, we work hybrid.
