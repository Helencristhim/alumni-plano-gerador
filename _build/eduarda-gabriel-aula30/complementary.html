Lesson 30 -- Review & Simulation: Investment Committee

Film
Too Big to Fail (2011)

The 2008 financial crisis seen from inside the meetings: bankers, regulators and CEOs present deals, defend numbers and look for a decision under pressure. Connection to Lesson 30: almost every scene is a small committee, with a base case, a dissent and a vote.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Each time a group decides something, pause: who has the conviction, and who is the dissent?

Talk
"How to disagree productively and find common ground" -- Julia Dhar, TED

A world debate champion explains how to disagree with someone without a fight, and how to find the point where both sides can agree. Connection to Lesson 30: it is what Eduarda does when David is not sure about the cap.

Tip: listen once without subtitles. Then write down one technique that you can use the next time a committee member disagrees with you.

Video
"Dare to disagree" -- Margaret Heffernan, TED

A business leader explains why the best organizations need people who disagree, and why dissent makes decisions better, not worse. Connection to Lesson 30: a good committee listens to the dissent before it votes.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Use it for your reflection homework: in your last real committee or meeting, was there a dissent? What happened to it?
