Chapter 3: The Code

Gerunds and Infinitives

recommend reviewing · decided to rebuild

Grammar Discovery

Read and Notice

"I recommend reviewing the model every quarter."

"We avoided changing the price during the peak season."

"We decided to restructure the pricing team."

"I managed to reduce variance by fifteen percent."

Look at the first verb in each sentence. Two groups. What decides the form of the second verb?

GroupVerbsExample
verb + -ingavoid, consider, recommend, suggest, keep, finish, practice, risk, involveI recommend reviewing the model.
verb + todecide, plan, expect, manage, need, want, offer, agree, fail, tendWe decided to restructure the team.
-ing as the subjectat the start of a sentenceMonitoring margins requires discipline.
Both, different meaning"I stopped using intuition" (I no longer use it) vs "I stopped to review the data" (I paused in order to review it).

There is no logic to learn here. There is a list, and the fastest way in is three verbs at a time.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I recommend to review the model every quarter."
"I recommend reviewing the model every quarter."
"We decided restructuring the pricing team."
"We decided to restructure the pricing team."
"She suggested me to start with one category."
"She suggested starting with one category."

Recommend and suggest take -ing and never take a person straight after them. Decide and manage take to.

Practice

-ing or to?

Say it first, then click to check

"I recommend ___reviewing the model every quarter." (review)
"We decided ___to rebuild the model at SKU level." (rebuild)
"Avoid ___announcing a big plan in week two." (announce)
"I managed ___to reduce variance by fifteen percent." (reduce)
"___Monitoring margins at SKU level requires discipline." (monitor, as the subject)
Detail

Same Verb, Different Meaning

stop · try · remember · forget

"I no longer use intuition-based pricing." → "I stopped ___using intuition-based pricing."
"I paused, in order to look at the data." → "I stopped ___to review the data."
"We experimented with a lower price, to see what happened." → "We tried ___lowering the price."
"We made an effort, and it was difficult." → "We tried ___to align three departments."
Chapter 4: The Handover

She Is Leaving the Role You Want

One hour to ask everything

Dialogue

The Handover

V
You start in three weeks. What do you want to know that is not in the documents?
M
What would you recommend doing in the first month?
V
Spend it in the stores. I regret not doing that. I decided to start with the data, and it cost me a quarter.
M
And the model? Do you suggest rebuilding it, or fixing what exists?
V
Fix one category, end to end. Avoid announcing anything before you have one number that works.
M
That is a pilot, then. With a rollback plan before it starts.
V
Exactly. And one more thing. The team has learned to wait for permission. If you expect them to decide, you will have to say it out loud.
M
Then I plan to say it in the first week, and to keep saying it until they believe me.
Comprehension

About Vera

1. What does Vera regret?
Not spending the first month in the stores. She decided to start with the data instead.
2. What does she suggest doing with the model?
Fixing one category end to end, and avoiding any announcement before one number works.
3. What has the team learned to do?
To wait for permission.
Listening

The First Ninety Days

Your new manager explains what is expected. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What do they NOT expect in the first ninety days?
A number. They expect you to understand the business.
2. What does she recommend for the first month?
Spending it in the stores, not in the office.
3. Who succeeds in that company, according to her?
The people who manage to finish one thing properly before starting the next.
Listening 2

The Handover Note

Three things the outgoing director would tell a successor. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What decision does he now think was wrong?
Pricing at category level, decided three years ago.
2. What does he say about the data?
They keep collecting data that nobody uses, and somebody needs to decide what to delete.
3. What does he regret?
Not telling the team earlier, out loud, that he expected them to decide without him.
