Masterclass
"'stop buying' or 'stop to buy'?" -- BBC English Masterclass

A short lesson on the verbs that change meaning with the form: stop, regret and go on. This is the half that costs you precision in a real conversation.

Tip: say three true sentences about your career with "I stopped -ing", and one with "I stopped to". Notice how different the second one feels.
