Chapter 3: The Code

Two Verbs, One Sentence

What goes between them is not a choice of style

Grammar Discovery

What Comes After the Verb

"We recommend restructuring the entity before the end of the quarter."

"This route avoids triggering the new withholding obligation."

"The client has decided to challenge the ruling before the filing deadline."

"They risk losing the benefit if they fail to file on time."

"The group stopped filing under the old regime in June."

"On the way to the board meeting he stopped to call the tax director."

Six sentences, two shapes. What decides which one?

GroupVerbsExample
Always + -ingavoid · consider · recommend · suggest · risk · involve · deny"We recommend restructuring the entity."
Always + toagree · decide · offer · refuse · manage · tend · afford · fail"The client decided to challenge the ruling."
Both, same meaningbegin · start · continue · prefer"They began filing / began to file in June."
Both, meaning changesremember doing = the memory of a past act · remember to do = do not forget it · stop doing = the act ends · stop to do = you pause in order to do something else · try doing = an experiment · try to do = an effort that may fail
The one that costs yourecommend and suggest never take a person + to. Not "we recommend the client to restructure". Say "we recommend restructuring", or "we recommend that the client restructure". Advise is the verb that does take a person: "we advise the client to restructure".

The pattern is not decoration. It decides who has to act, and how much of the risk you are carrying.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"We recommend the client to restructure the holding."
"We recommend restructuring the holding." / "We advise the client to restructure the holding."
"The board decided restructuring the group this year."
"The board decided to restructure the group this year."
"The structure avoids to trigger the withholding obligation."
"The structure avoids triggering the withholding obligation."

All three come from the same place: a Portuguese sentence that puts a person after the verb. English decides that per verb, not per sentence.

Listening 2

Two Words That Are Not the Same

A senior partner on the verb that carries the risk. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What sentence does he cross out in every junior lawyer's memo?
"We recommend the client to restructure" — recommend does not take a person plus an infinitive.
2. Which verb DOES take a person, according to him?
Advise: "we advise the client to restructure".
3. Beyond grammar, what does he say the verb tells the reader?
How much of the risk you are carrying: suggest is soft, recommend commits you, advise commits you and names who must act.
