Your Words at Work

Which Word Do You Need?

1. You slept four hours and you have been up since five.
Tired. "I'm a little tired today."
2. You want a subject that is safe with anybody, anywhere.
Weather. "The weather is very nice today, isn't it?"
3. Nothing is wrong, nothing is special, and you do not want to talk about it.
Fine. "I'm fine, thank you. And you?"
4. You have to go, but you do not want to be rude.
Later. "Sorry, I have to go. See you later."
Chapter 3: The Word Because

One Word Doubles Every Answer

because

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I'm tired because it was a long day."

"The week is busy because I am here for five days."

"Today is great because the weather is perfect."

"I'm fine, thank you. And you?"

PartWhat it doesExample
1. How you arethe short answerI'm tired.
2. because + reasonanswers why before anybody asks...because it was a long day.
3. And you?gives the conversation backAnd you? How is your week?

Three parts, ten seconds. That is the whole difference between a dead conversation and a live one.

The Shape of the Answer

How · Why · And You?

the airport version

how to leave without being rude — you need this one too

Dialogue

Long Day, Long Drive

D
Good morning. Long drive today, forty minutes at least. How are you?
L
Good morning. I'm fine, thank you. And you?
D
Honestly? Not great. I am so tired. And how is your week?
L
Busy, very busy, because I am here for only three days. And you?
D
Three days? That is short. I am here for two weeks, so I have time.
L
Two weeks is good. Is this your first time here?
D
No, it is my fourth. And the weather is always the same. Always.
L
Sorry, can you say that again, please?
D
Of course. The weather. It is always cold and grey here, every time I come.
L
Ah, the weather. In Brazil it is great now. Very hot.
D
Do not say that. Okay, we are here. Have a good day, and see you later.
Comprehension

What Did Diane Say?

1. How long is Diane in the city?
Two weeks. She says she has time.
2. Is it her first time there?
No. It is her fourth time.
3. What does she say about the weather?
It is always the same — cold and grey, every time she comes.
Listening

A Message Late at Night

She answers "how are you" the long way. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. Why is Diane so tired?
Because it was a long day and she only slept four hours.
2. Why was the afternoon busy?
Because they had three meetings and nobody stopped for lunch.
3. Why is tomorrow better?
Because she has only one meeting, in the morning, and then she is free.
Listening 2

The Morning Briefing

Weather, schedule, and one thing you must not miss. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. How is the weather today, morning and afternoon?
Cold in the morning, about four degrees, and much better in the afternoon, around fifteen. No rain.
2. What time is the car, and why?
At eleven, not at ten thirty, because the traffic is very bad on Fridays.
3. What is the one thing you must not miss tonight?
Dinner at eight — not eight fifteen, because the restaurant is small and they are eleven people.
