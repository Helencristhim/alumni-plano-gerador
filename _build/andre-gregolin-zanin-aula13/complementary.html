Series
Ted Lasso — Season 1, Episode 1

An American coach who talks constantly, and almost never in finished sentences. He hesitates, repairs, changes direction and keeps going, and nobody thinks he is less fluent for it. It is today’s lesson, at full speed, with a lot of humor.

Tip: watch ten minutes with English subtitles and count the phrases he uses to buy time or to repair a sentence. Then copy two of them into your recovery toolkit.

Talk
Celeste Headlee — “10 ways to have a better conversation” (TED)

A radio interviewer on what keeps a conversation alive. One of her rules is that it is fine to say you do not know, and another is that you should not repeat yourself — both are about the moments when your mind goes blank.

Tip: after each of her ten rules, pause and say in one sentence whether you already do it in English.

Talk
Julian Treasure — “How to speak so that people want to listen” (TED)

A sound expert on pace, pause and rhythm. He explains why a short, deliberate pause sounds confident and a long, empty one does not — exactly the line between a good filler and a freeze.

Tip: try his vocal warm-up before your three-minute homework recording, and notice whether your first sentence comes out faster.
