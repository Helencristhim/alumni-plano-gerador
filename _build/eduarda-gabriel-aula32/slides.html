Chapter 3 -- The Code

Yes, and Even Though

Agreeing and disagreeing in one sentence

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Purple Words Have in Common?

"Although the courts are slow, the outcomes are good."

"Even though Brazil is risky, investors made money last year."

"While I agree with Mark, I see the risk differently."

"Despite the delays, the company survived."

Do the two ideas agree or pull against each other? What comes after "although"? And after "despite"?

FormExample
although + subject + verb"Although the courts are slow, the outcomes are good."
even though + subject + verb (stronger)"Even though Brazil is risky, investors made money last year."
while + subject + verb (formal, at the start)"While I agree with Mark, I see the risk differently."
despite / in spite of + noun or -ing"Despite the delays, the company survived."
NeverNot "Although..., but..." / "despite of" / "despite the courts are slow"
Grammar Rule

A Wall or a Bridge?

Direct (a wall)Concessive (a bridge)
"No, Brazil is not too risky.""Although Brazil has risks, the returns are strong."
"The courts are slow. It doesn't matter.""Even though the courts are slow, the outcomes are good."
"You are wrong about the currency.""While I agree that the real is volatile, many deals are now in dollars."
"There were delays. The company survived.""Despite the delays, the company survived."
The other panelist defends himself.The other panelist keeps listening.

Chapter 4 -- Getting There

Friday, Eleven O’Clock

One moderator, four panelists, one disagreement

Dialogue

Eduarda and Mark

M
Let me play devil's advocate here. Brazil is too risky for most foreign investors.
E
I'd like to build on that, Mark. Although the risk is real, the returns are real, too.
M
But the courts are so slow. A restructuring can take years.
E
I concede that the courts are slow. Even though the process is long, the outcomes are improving.
M
Improving how much?
E
Last year, creditors in our cases recovered sixty percent, despite the delays.
M
That's a fair point. But what about the currency risk?
E
While I agree that the real is volatile, many deals are now in dollars.
M
So your view is that the risk is overpriced?
E
Exactly. That's my counterargument. And I'd love to hear the moderator weigh in.
Comprehension

About Mark

1. What role does Mark play at the start?
He plays devil's advocate.
2. What is Mark's problem with the Brazilian courts?
They are slow. A restructuring can take years.
3. What other risk does Mark mention?
The currency risk.

Listening 2 -- The Coach

Make the Discussion Better

A communication coach explains how to contribute on a panel. Sound first -- no text.

1. Why do some smart people say almost nothing on a panel?
They wait for the perfect moment, and it never comes.
2. What are the three steps?
Acknowledge the other speaker, add your view, and support it with a fact or an example.
3. Who does the audience remember?
The panelist who made the discussion better.
