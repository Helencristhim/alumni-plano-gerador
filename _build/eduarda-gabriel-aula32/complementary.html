Lesson 32 -- Panel Discussions -- Contributing Actively

Film
The Big Short (2015)

A small group of investors bets against the American housing market before the 2008 crisis. Connection to Lesson 32: in the conference scene in Las Vegas, an investor in the audience challenges a confident panelist. It shows a panel discussion that goes wrong: no acknowledging, only attack.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. In the conference scene, pause: how could the investor say the same thing with "although" and keep the panelist listening?

Talk
"How to have better political conversations" -- Robb Willer, TED

A sociologist shows why arguments fail when we only use our own reasons, and how to present a view in the language of the other side. Connection to Lesson 32: it is the "accept, then add" move from the panel, with research behind it.

Tip: listen once without subtitles. Then write one sentence with "although" that accepts the other side's value and adds your view.

Video
"14 Ways to Agree and Disagree -- Business English Meetings" -- Derek Callan

A business English teacher presents fourteen phrases to agree, partly agree and disagree in professional discussions. Connection to Lesson 32: many of them work on a panel, and you can combine them with "although" and "despite".

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Choose three phrases for partial agreement and use them in your homework recording.
