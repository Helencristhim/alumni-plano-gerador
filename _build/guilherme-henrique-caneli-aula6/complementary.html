Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Freakonomics Radio — “Why Is Academic Writing So Bad?”

Why experts write in a way nobody wants to read, and what clear writers do differently. Every point applies to briefing notes and press statements.

Tip: after listening, take the last text your team sent you and cut three adjectives from the first paragraph.

Listen on Freakonomics ↗
Talk
David McCandless — “The beauty of data visualization” (TED)

How to turn a mass of numbers into one clear message — the same discipline as choosing the key message of a release about a multi-billion infrastructure deal.

Tip: note how he states the point of each chart in one sentence before he explains it. That sentence is a lede.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
Hans Rosling — “The best stats you’ve ever seen” (TED)

A master class in making complex data understandable to a room of non-specialists, with the relationships between facts made visible at every step.

Tip: listen for the connectors he uses between facts. Write down three and use them in your next briefing note.
