Chapter 3: The Pattern

Have To or Don't Have To?

have to · has to · don't have to

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Visitors have to wear a badge.
The plant director has to sign the report.
You don't have to print the report.
Do I have to do the training? Yes, you do.

What do the orange words have in common? Which one is NOT a rule?

UseFormExample
It is a ruleI / you / we / they + have to + verbVisitors have to wear a badge.
It is a rule (he, she, it)he / she / it + has to + verbThe director has to sign the report.
Not a rule: your choicedon't / doesn't have to + verbYou don't have to print it.
QuestionDo / Does ... have to + verb?Do I have to do the training? — Yes, you do.
CarefulAfter "doesn't": HAVE, never "has" — "She doesn't have to sign." And the verb after "to" never changes: "has to sign", never "has to signs".
Rule or Choice?

A Rule, or Your Choice?

a rule — no choice
