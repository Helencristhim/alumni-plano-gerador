Chapter 4: The Code

I Work, He Works

The present simple with he and she

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"He gets up at eight."

"His son works with him."

"He does not work late."

"Does he watch movies?"

Last class: I need, Do you have...? Today the person is he. Look at the blue words. What letter appears?

JobFormExample
Affirmativehe / she / it + verb + sHe works. She gets up at seven.
Special verbsgo → goes · have → has · watch → watchesHe goes home. He has dinner.
Negativehe / she + does not (doesn’t) + verbHe doesn’t work late.
QuestionDoes + he / she + verb?Does he work on Saturday?
Short answersYes, he does. / No, he doesn’t.Does your son work with you? Yes, he does.
The traponly one -sNot Does he works? Say Does he work?

In one line: he, she and it take an -s; with does, the -s goes to does and leaves the verb.
