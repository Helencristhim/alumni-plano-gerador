Chapter 4: The Code

Who Made It Happen?

The difference between a sentence that names the agent and one that hides it

Grammar Discovery

Three Sentences Name the Agent. One Hides It.

"The fund had its exposure reviewed by an outside adviser."

"The operator got the concession extended for ten years."

"The concession was extended for ten years."

"We had the project structured as blended finance."

The red sentences tell you who caused the action, even when somebody else did the work. The blue one does not. Why would an investor reading the blue sentence want to ask a question?

JobFormExample

Ask or persuade a personhave + person + verb / get + person + to + verbWe had the bank run the numbers. We got the ministry to publish the date.
The trapnever make + object + to beNot We made the contract to be reviewed. Say We had the contract reviewed.

In one line: Have and get tell the room who made it happen; the passive lets you stay silent about it.

Listening 1 · Business Radio

The Price of Safety

A markets segment on brownfield assets in Latin America. Sound first — no text.

1. What return could a brownfield toll road offer five years ago, and what can it promise today?
A double-digit return five years ago; today it struggles to promise nine percent.
2. According to the segment, why have returns come down?
Because so many pension and sovereign wealth funds are bidding for the same operating assets that prices went up. It is yield compression, not traffic.
3. What are development banks doing to close the gap?
Offering guarantees, so that a new greenfield project looks more like an old one to a cautious fund.
Listening 2 · The Roundtable

What the Fund Needs to Hear

A sovereign fund investment director speaks at a roundtable. Sound first — no text.

1. What does the director say her fund starts with, if not the return?
With what can go wrong, and in what order.
2. What are the three risks she lists, in order?
Construction risk first, then currency, then the contract itself.
3. What has happened to her fund in other markets, and what does she say about the first deal?
It has had concessions renegotiated after an election. The first deal has to be structured carefully enough to defend; after that, capital deployment becomes much easier.
