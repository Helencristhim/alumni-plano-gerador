Why do buildings fall in earthquakes?

An engineering professor explains how loads travel through a structure and why some structural systems resist them far better than others. The animation makes the vocabulary of loads, columns and bracing visible.

Tip: pause on each structural system and compare them out loud, for example steel is more ductile than, or the stiffest option is.

Why Concrete Needs Reinforcement

A civil engineer breaks concrete beams in his garage to show why tensile strength, and not compressive strength, decides the design. It is the clearest ten minutes available on concrete against steel.
