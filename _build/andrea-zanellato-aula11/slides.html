Chapter 3: The Code

Love It, Hate It

What comes after enjoy, don’t mind and can’t stand

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Show?

"I enjoy reading physical books."

"She can’t stand waiting in traffic."

"I don’t mind working late on Monday."

"We avoid having meetings on Friday afternoon."

Look at the blue words: they say what you feel about an activity. Now the red words: after enjoy, can’t stand, don’t mind and avoid, the next verb ends in -ing. Never to + verb.

JobFormExample
Likeslove / like / enjoy + verb-ingI enjoy reading physical books.
Dislikeshate / can’t stand + verb-ingI can’t stand waiting in traffic.
It is OK for medon’t mind + verb-ingI don’t mind working late.
To stay away fromavoid + verb-ingWe avoid having meetings on Friday.
Spellinghave → having · sit → sittingDrop the final -e, or double the last letter.
The trapno to after enjoy, mind and avoidNot I enjoy to read. Say I enjoy reading.

In one line: enjoy, love, like, hate, don’t mind, can’t stand and avoid + verb-ing.

Chapter 4: The Team Day

The Team Day

One call, two offices, one survey

Dialogue

A Call to Sydney — The Team Day

A
Hi, Liam! Thanks for the call. I have some questions about the team day in March.
L
Sure, Andrea! I love talking about our team. What do you want to know?
A
First, what do people in Sydney enjoy doing together?
L
They enjoy cooking together, and they love being outside. But they can’t stand long meetings!
A
Like my team in Brazil. And at work, what do they avoid doing?
L
Paperwork! Everybody avoids doing it. And my pet peeve is people working overtime every day.
A
I agree. I don’t mind working late sometimes, but on my day off I want to unwind.
L
Good idea. So how do you unwind?
A
I enjoy reading physical books, and I love listening to U2 in the car. And you?
L
I’m a people person, so I enjoy meeting friends at the beach. I’ll send you the team list today.
Comprehension

About Liam

1. What do people in Sydney enjoy doing together?
They enjoy cooking together and being outside.
2. What is Liam’s pet peeve?
People working overtime every day.
3. How does Liam unwind?
He meets friends at the beach.
