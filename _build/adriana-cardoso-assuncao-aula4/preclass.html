Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example out loud. These are the words a lawyer reads before anybody else does.

Workload -- the amount of work one person is expected to do
"Managers must review the workload of the team every quarter."
Psychosocial risk -- something in the work itself that can damage mental health
"Every manager must complete the psychosocial risk training."
Harassment -- behavior that humiliates or intimidates somebody at work
"A complaint of harassment cannot be investigated by the same area."
To comply with -- to do what a law or a rule requires
"We have to comply with the new rule before the end of the year."
Guidelines -- the written instructions that say how something has to be done
"The guidelines say you should document the conversation."
Whistleblower -- somebody who reports wrongdoing inside their own company
"A whistleblower may use the anonymous channel at any time."
Grievance -- a formal complaint an employee makes about the workplace
"A written grievance has to be answered within five working days."
Duty of care -- the legal responsibility a company has for the health of its people
"If it is part of the duty of care, the verb cannot be should."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

The wellbeing policy has four lines that matter. Every manager must complete the psychosocial risk training each year. A written grievance has to be answered within five working days, in writing. Nobody may investigate a complaint about their own team; a complaint of harassment goes to the compliance officer. After that, the register changes: managers should document the conversation, and employees may use the anonymous channel. The company does not have to open an investigation for every concern, but it must not ignore one that is formally filed. Because the company can be held to what it wrote, everything that belongs to the duty of care is written with must, and everything the company recommends but cannot guarantee is written with should.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Modals of Obligation and Recommendation

GRAMMAR

All of these are followed by the base verb: must complete, never must to complete. What changes is the STRENGTH.

obligationmust / have toEvery manager must complete the training. A grievance has to be answered in five days.
recommendationshouldYou should document the conversation.
permissionmay / canEmployees may use the anonymous channel.
prohibitionmust not / cannotYou must not investigate a complaint about your own team.
no obligationdo not have toYou do not have to use the template — it is optional.

The trap that costs money: must not and do not have to are opposites, not variations. "You must not report" forbids it. "You do not have to report" makes it optional. In a policy, choosing the wrong one changes what the company promised.

Stage 3: Pronunciation

Speaking

Listen, then record yourself. Keep the modal unstressed and the main verb strong — that is how a policy sounds when it is said out loud.

Every manager must complete the training this quarter.
A written grievance has to be answered within five working days.
Nobody may investigate a complaint about their own team.
The strength of the verb has to match the strength of the obligation.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz
Legal says the workload paragraph is part of the duty of care. How do you rewrite it?
A "Managers should review the workload every quarter."
B "Managers must review the workload every quarter."
C "Managers may review the workload every quarter."
Nobody can be obliged to report a concern. Which sentence is right?
A "Employees must report any concern."
B "Employees may report any concern."
C "Employees must not report any concern."
A manager wants to investigate a complaint about his own team. What do you say?
A "You must not investigate it. It goes to compliance."
B "You do not have to investigate it."
C "You should not to investigate it."
Which sentence is grammatically correct?
A "All managers must to comply with the guidelines."
B "All managers have to comply with the guidelines."
C "All managers must complying with the guidelines."
