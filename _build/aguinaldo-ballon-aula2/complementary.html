Talk
TED -- David Grady, "How to save the world (or at least yourself) from bad meetings" (6 min)

A short, funny talk about meetings that waste everybody's time, and one simple question that makes them better: what is the goal of this meeting? Clear American English at a normal pace.

Tip: watch once without subtitles, then again with English subtitles. Note the question he asks before accepting a meeting.

BBC 6 Minute English -- "How important is politeness?"

Six minutes on how politeness changes the way people react to what we say, with six useful words explained. Useful for the moment in a meeting when the investor pushes back and you want to answer firmly but politely.

Tip: listen once, then read the transcript and copy two polite phrases you could use with a fund.

Derek Callan -- "28 Advanced Phrases For Meetings"

A business English teacher presents phrases for opening a meeting, giving updates, disagreeing and closing with next steps. Many of them fit directly into a status meeting with an investor.

Tip: choose five phrases, write them on a card and use at least two in your next real meeting.
