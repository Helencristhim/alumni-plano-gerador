Chapter 3 -- The Tag Code

Didn't We? · Isn't It? · Can't He?

A short question that checks agreement and passes the turn

Discovery

Listen and Notice

Look at the orange words at the end of each sentence. Where does the verb in the tag come from? When is the tag positive, and when is it negative?

"We agreed on mid-April, didn't we?"

"The indemnity covers the interest, doesn't it?"

"Tom can send the documents by Friday, can't he?"

"Paulo isn't on the call yet, is he?"

SentenceTagExample
positive, with beisn't it? / aren't we?"The date is fixed, isn't it?"
positive, present simpledon't / doesn't + pronoun"The indemnity covers the interest, doesn't it?"
positive, past simpledidn't + pronoun"We agreed on April, didn't we?"
positive, with a modalthe same modal, negative"Tom can send them, can't he?"
any negative sentencepositive tag"Paulo isn't here yet, is he?"

Key idea: the tag repeats the auxiliary of the sentence (is, do, did, can) and uses a pronoun, never the name: "Tom can send them, can't he?" Positive sentence, negative tag; negative sentence, positive tag. With falling intonation, the tag checks agreement -- and passes the turn.

Practice

Which Tag?

Say it first, then click to check

"The indemnity covers the interest, ___doesn't it?" (present simple)
"Tom can send the documents by Friday, ___can't he?" (a modal)
"Paulo isn't on the call yet, ___is he?" (a negative sentence)
"We are all on the same page, ___aren't we?" (be, we)
The Structure

Check, Pass, Park

Three jobs a chair does on every call. The tag helps with all three.

Check agreement · a falling tag

"So we agreed on the fifteenth, didn't we?"

You expect yes. You are confirming, not asking.

Pass the turn · a name + a tag

"Paulo, the tax debt is limited, isn't it?"

The tag hands over the turn to one person, by name.

Park a topic · then move on

"Let us park the interest. We can come back to it on Thursday, can't we?"

The tag gets quick consensus to move on.

On a call with three cities, a question tag is the polite way to say: your turn, and please agree.
