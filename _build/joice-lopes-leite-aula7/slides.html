Chapter 3: The Code

Where the Word Goes

before the verb · after to be

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"We usually meet every two weeks."

"I always prepare before the call."

"We never meet on Friday."

"The meeting is always online."

SentenceWhereExample
normal verbBEFORE the verbI always prepare.
verb to beAFTER am / is / areThe meeting is always online.
questionHow often + do / doesHow often do you meet?
answer with a numberevery / once / twiceevery two weeks · twice a month

One line to remember: before the verb, after to be.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I prepare always before the call."
"I always prepare before the call."
"We don't never meet on Friday."
"We never meet on Friday."
"How many times you meet?"
"How often do you meet?"

In English never is already negative. It does not need don't next to it.

Chapter 4: The Real Question

The Question That Opens the Door

how often, and what you answer

Dialogue

How Often Do You Meet?

G
Hi, Joice. How often do you have meetings with the program?
J
We usually meet every two weeks, on Monday.
G
And do you always prepare before the meeting?
J
Yes, I always prepare. I read the document twice.
G
Twice? That is a lot. Do you often speak in the meeting?
J
Sometimes. I am a little afraid, but I speak anyway.
G
That is the right way. I never speak on my first day in a group.
J
Really? And how often do you speak now?
G
In every meeting. It takes time, but it comes. And you? Every week?
J
No, I never miss a meeting, but I usually speak only once.
Comprehension

About Greg

1. What did Greg never do on his first day in a group?
He never spoke.
2. How often does Greg speak now?
In every meeting.
3. What does Greg say about time?
That it takes time, but it comes.
