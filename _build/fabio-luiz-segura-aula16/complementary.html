Talk
Kio Stark — “Why you should talk to strangers” (TED)

A writer who studies conversations between strangers explains why a short, unplanned exchange with somebody you do not know is worth more than it seems, and how people open those conversations naturally. It is the idea behind the coffee station and the shuttle queue.

Tip: write down two ways she says people start a conversation with a stranger. Then write one version of each for the first morning in Miami.

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart (Stanford GSB) — “Don't Get Lost in Translation: How Non-Native Speakers Can Communicate with Confidence”

The host talks with a communication coach about specific tactics non-native English speakers can use in the moment: when they miss a word, when they need time, and when the conversation moves fast. It connects the listening work of lesson 14 with the cold starts of this lesson.

Tip: choose one tactic from the episode and use it in your homework recording. Say which one you chose.

Video
Vanessa Van Edwards — “You are contagious” (TEDxLondon, YouTube)

A researcher of human behavior explains what happens in the first seconds of meeting somebody new, and how your energy and your first question change the whole conversation. Useful before a day when every conversation starts from zero.

Tip: note the questions she recommends instead of "What do you do?". Pick one you could use at a conference and say it out loud three times.
