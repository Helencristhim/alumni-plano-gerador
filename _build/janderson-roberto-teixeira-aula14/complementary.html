Podcast
"What's it like to be a twin?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes in which two presenters spend the whole episode describing people and telling one apart from another. Connection to Lesson 14: that is precisely what a defining relative clause is for, and you will hear a dozen of them without anybody naming the grammar. Audio, vocabulary and full transcript are free on the page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then find three sentences that identify one person rather than another, and notice how short they are.

"Are you a giver or a taker?" -- Adam Grant, TED

Thirteen minutes describing three kinds of person you meet at work, which means thirteen minutes of exactly today's structure. Connection to Lesson 14: watch how he identifies each type in one sentence rather than three, and how quickly you understand who he means.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down the sentence he uses to define each of the three types, and then write one of your own about somebody in your bank.
