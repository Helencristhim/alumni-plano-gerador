Julian Treasure -- "How to speak so that people want to listen" (TED, 10 min)

A sound expert on the habits that make people stop listening, and the tools of the voice that make them lean in: pace, pitch and silence.

Tip: record your pitch once, apply one of his tools, and record it again.
