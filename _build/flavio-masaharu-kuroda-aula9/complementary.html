Lesson 9 -- But It Is Too Expensive

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Negotiating"

Five minutes on the language of a negotiation that has to continue afterwards: how to disagree, how to hold a position, and how to make an offer that promises nothing. Full script on the same page, at natural speed.

Tip: read the script and mark every could, might, would and may. For each one, ask what exactly the speaker has committed to. In most cases the answer is nothing.

Talk
William Ury -- "The walk from no to yes" (TEDxMidwest, 18 min)

The co-author of Getting to Yes on what to do when the other side says no, and on the third position that makes agreement possible without giving in. Slow, deliberate English, and every idea is illustrated with a real negotiation.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down the two questions he says you should ask before you concede anything, and rehearse both in English with your own deal in mind.

Series
Suits -- Season 1, Episode 1 "Pilot"

Forty minutes of people refusing things without ending conversations. The plot does not matter: what matters is the register — how a hard no is delivered, how a condition is attached to an offer, and how often the answer is a question instead of a number.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Note three moments where somebody says no, and write down the sentence that comes immediately after each one. That second sentence is the one doing the work.
