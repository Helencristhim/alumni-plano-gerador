Lesson 25 -- Review & Simulation: Full Deal Discussion

Series
WeCrashed -- Season 1, Episode 5 "Hustle Harder"

The story of WeWork and its founder, Adam Neumann. In this episode, after a huge investment, the company is in the big leagues and Adam goes after his competition. Connection to Lesson 25: watch how money changes who has the power at the table, and who has the final say.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down one moment when someone sets a condition, and one moment when someone tries to persuade instead of giving an order.

Talk
"How to speak up for yourself" -- Adam Galinsky, TED

A Columbia Business School professor who studies negotiation explains how to be assertive without creating a backlash. Connection to Lesson 25: it is the balance Eduarda needs with Mark, firm on what is non-negotiable and flexible on everything else.

Tip: listen once without subtitles. Then write one non-negotiable point and one flexible point from a deal you are working on.

Video
"Negotiation: Getting What You Want" -- Margaret Neale, Stanford GSB

A Stanford professor explains how to prepare for a negotiation and why it is a problem-solving conversation, not a fight. Connection to Lesson 25: her advice on preparation is what turns a list of tools into a full deal discussion.

Tip: watch the first twenty minutes. Then prepare one page for your next negotiation: your deal-breakers, your offer, and the question you will use to close.
