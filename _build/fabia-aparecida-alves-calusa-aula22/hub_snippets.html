Review — What the Fee Covers

Nothing new this week. The same four words — cover, bookkeeping, tax return, additional — and the same shape: saying what is in the fee and what is not, with "however" and "although". A new client, a new question. Around ten minutes.

Stage 1: The same four words

Vocabulary

Nothing new here. Read each one and say it out loud. If it comes back on its own, that word is yours.

Cover — to include something in a price or a service
"The fee covers payroll for both offices."
Bookkeeping — keeping the daily records of what a company pays and receives
"The bookkeeping for the new branch is in the fee."
Tax return — the form a company sends to the government about its taxes
"Your tax returns do not cost more."
Additional — extra; more than what was agreed
"The new employees were additional work."

Stage 3: However and although — one more time

Grammar

No new rule this week. The same shape as lesson 21, with a new client: Your fee covers payroll. However, this month we did additional work. Although the fee does not change, additional work has its own price. However starts a new sentence; although goes before the first idea, then a comma. One contrast word is enough: never "although..., but...".

"The fee is the same. ______, this month we did additional work."
A However
B Although
"______ the fee does not change, additional work has its own price."
A However,
B Although
Which sentence is correct?
A Although the invoice is higher, the fee is the same.
B Although the invoice is higher this month, but the monthly fee is still the same as always.
The client asks why the invoice is higher. Which answer can he check?
A There was more work this month, I think, with the new office and the people, so it is a bit higher.
B We set up the payroll for twelve new employees. It is in item four.

Stage 5: Say it out loud

Speaking

Three lines. Make a small stop after "however" and after the "although" idea. Listen first, then record yourself.

Your monthly fee covers bookkeeping and payroll.

Although the fee does not change, additional work has its own price.

Stage 6: Before class

Speaking
Think of one month when a real client of your firm (no name needed) got a higher invoice. Record three sentences as if you were talking to that client: what the monthly fee covers, what the extra work was (However, this month...), and that extra work has its own price (Although the fee does not change, ...). One sentence each — no more. In class you will use this client.
