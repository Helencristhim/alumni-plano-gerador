Lesson 10 -- Turquoise What?

Documentary
The truth behind fast fashion -- DW Documentary (42 min)

A film crew follows a cheap garment from the factory to the store window, and asks the brands what the label actually promises. Every word from tonight is in it: the fabric, the pattern, the size chart, the price on the tag.

Tip: watch the first fifteen minutes with no subtitles and write down five words you hear about clothing. Then watch the same part again with subtitles on.

Podcast
Blue Jeans -- Articles of Interest, episode 5 (99% Invisible)

Twenty-five minutes on one garment: why denim was built to be worn out, why the color fades where your hands go, and how a work uniform became the most common piece of clothing on earth.

Tip: the full transcript is on the same page. Listen once with the page closed, then read it and count how many times the word fade comes up.

Talk
Why women’s clothing sizes don’t make sense -- Vox (5 min)

Five minutes that explain the whole problem: where American sizes came from, why a medium in one store is a small in the next one, and why the number on the label is not a measurement.

Tip: short and clear American English. Watch it once with no subtitles, then say the main idea out loud in three sentences.
