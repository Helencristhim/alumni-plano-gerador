Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Maurizio Seracini: “The secret lives of paintings” (14 min)

An Italian engineer uses science to look under famous paintings in Florence and finds what was hidden there by the artists. Full of the passive: what was painted, what was covered, what was found.

Tip: every time you hear was or were plus a verb, pause and say the sentence again.

TED Talk
Amit Sood: “Every piece of art you’ve ever wanted to see, up close and searchable” (6 min)

How millions of works of art from museums all over the world were photographed and put online. You can visit a museum in Italy from your sofa.

Tip: after the talk, find one painting online that you saw in Europe, and describe it out loud in one minute.

TED Talk
Thomas Heatherwick: “Building the Seed Cathedral” (17 min)

A British designer shows buildings and objects that were designed by his studio, including a pavilion made of thousands of long thin rods. Great for the vocabulary of buildings and materials.

Tip: choose one building from the talk and say what it is made of and why it was built.
