Chapter 3

Once You've Done That

Step one has to finish before step two starts

Grammar Discovery

Finished, Then Next

"Once you've logged in, tap Expenses."

"As soon as you've added the photo, tap Send."

"Don't close the app until you've seen the green message."

"After you've saved the file, send it to Daniel."

FormUseExample
once / as soon as + you've + past participleThe first step must be complete before the next oneOnce you've logged in, tap Expenses.
until + you've + past participleKeep doing (or don't do) something up to that momentDon't close it until you've seen the message.
after / when + you've + past participleThe order of two stepsAfter you've saved it, send it.
Make sure you + verb / Don't forget to + verbHighlight an important stepMake sure you check the total.
⚠ CarefulAfter once, as soon as and until, don't use will: 'Once you've finished', not 'Once you will finish'. Use the past participle: 'once you've logged in', not 'once you've log in'. And no 'to' after 'make sure you': 'Make sure you save it'.
