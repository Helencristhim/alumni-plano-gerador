Chapter 3: The Code

I and Me

before the verb · after the verb

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"She sent the report to me."

"He invited them to our call."

"They answered him this morning."

"Can you introduce me to them?"

Before the verbAfter the verbExample
ImeI sent it. / She sent it to me.
he / she / ithim / her / itShe helps. / I write to her.
we / theyus / themThey answer. / He invited them.
youyouThe only one that does not change.
Key ideaWho DOES the action goes before the verb. Who RECEIVES it goes after the verb, or after to / with / for.

One position, one word: She helps me. I help her.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"She sent the report to I."
"She sent the report to me."
"He invited they to our call."
"He invited them to our call."
"I contact with the technical team."
"I contact the technical team."

In English contact takes no preposition: you contact somebody, never contact with somebody.
