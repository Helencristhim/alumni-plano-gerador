One city, one episode: the cooks of Osaka show you what to eat, where to stand and how things are done at their counters. It is the city that the hostel message and the travel page were preparing you for, seen from the inside.

Travel with Rick Steves -- 576: Budget Travelers; Beginner's Guide to Japan

A travel writer gives eight tips for traveling on very little money, and then the writer Pico Iyer talks about what still surprises him about Japan after more than thirty years of living there -- a beginner's guide from somebody who has already been. Tip: write down every instruction you hear in the budget tips, then mark the ones that come with extra weight -- "make sure", "always", "never", "don't".
