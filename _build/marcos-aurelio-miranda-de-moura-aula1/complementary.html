Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Mad Men -- Season 1, Episode 1, "Smoke Gets in Your Eyes"

A room full of advertising people, and every one of them introduces himself by what he decides, never by his history. That is the whole grammar of this lesson, played out in the first fifteen minutes of the pilot.

Tip: stop at every introduction and write it down in one line. Then say your own version out loud in the same shape.

Talk
TED -- Adam Galinsky, "How to speak up for yourself" (15 min)

A social psychologist on the exact situation you described: the room where everybody has more authority than you, and your voice gets smaller. He explains why it happens and what changes it. Clear American English, slow enough to follow without subtitles.

Tip: watch it once for the idea. Then pick the one sentence you would want to be able to say in your next board meeting, and say it out loud five times.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
CNBC Explains -- "What’s next for oil?"

Six minutes on the market that sets your price list: where the demand is going, what moves the barrel, and what the industry expects next. It is your own working world, said in English by people who report on it for a living.

Tip: watch it once with English subtitles and once without. Note three words you already use in Portuguese every day but had never heard said out loud in English.
