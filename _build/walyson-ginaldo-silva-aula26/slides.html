Chapter 4: The Code

Because of the Drought...

Cause and effect: how one thing leads to another

Grammar Discovery

From Cause to Effect

"Because of the drought, the yield fell by a third."

"Prices went up. As a result, many smallholders lost money."

"The trees keep the plants cool. Therefore, the farms lose less coffee."

"A smaller carbon footprint leads to better relationships with buyers."

Look at the blue words. What is the cause? What is the effect? What comes after because of?

LinkerWhat comes afterExample
because of / due to+ a nounBecause of the drought, the yield fell.
because+ subject + verbThe yield fell because it did not rain.
so+ result (same sentence)It did not rain, so the yield fell.
As a result, / Therefore,new sentence + commaThe yield fell. As a result, prices went up.
lead to / causecause + lead(s) to + effectThe drought led to higher prices.
Grammar Practice

Connect the Facts

Say it first, then click to check

"___Because of the drought, the yield was lower." (because / because of)
"Prices went up. ___As a result, many smallholders lost money." (a new sentence: the result)
"Deforestation ___leads to higher temperatures." (lead to -- present)
"The farm protected its forest, ___so it kept its certification." (same sentence: the result)
"We lost the contract ___due to the new regulation." (due to / because)
"The coffee was shade-grown. ___Therefore, the plants survived the dry season." (formal: the result)

Listening 1 · A Cooperative

Why We Are Changing

A cooperative manager in Minas Gerais on the three steps of their program. Sound first — no text.

1. What happened to the weather in the last five years?
They had two long droughts and one frost.
2. Why do they plant trees between the coffee plants?
Because shade-grown coffee needs less water.
3. What is the result of the certification for the smallholders?
They can sell to buyers who pay a premium.
Listening 2 · A Buyer

The Questions Have Changed

A green coffee buyer in Hamburg on what his customers ask today. Sound first — no text.

1. What was the first question ten years ago, and what is it today?
Before: price and quality. Today: where the coffee comes from and how it was produced.
2. Why can't he buy a lot without the exact farm?
Due to the new European regulation on deforestation.
3. What is his opinion about the new rules?
He is not convinced it is just paperwork: he strongly believes it is good for everyone, and he signs longer contracts with exporters he trusts.
