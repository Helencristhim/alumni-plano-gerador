The Code

One was long. One cut it.

I was watching a video when my phone died.
She was talking when I walked in.
While I was cooking, my laptop crashed.

The background is was / were + -ing

The long action was already happening. Then the short one arrived and cut it — and the short one is the past simple you learned in lesson 3.

The Code

When cuts. While runs alongside.

The long one (background)

I was watching
She was talking
They were not sleeping

The short one (the event)

my phone died
I walked in
the lights went out

I was watching a video when my phone died. — when + the SHORT one
While I was watching a video, my phone died. — while + the LONG one
While I was cooking, she was talking on the phone. — two long ones, side by side

Swap when and while and the sentence changes meaning. That is the whole lesson.

I WAS! (only here)

Only stress was when you are arguing with somebody. In a story, let it fall.

Did You Catch It?

About Bia

1. What was Bia doing when she missed her stop?
She was listening to music and reading at the same time.
2. What happened right after she realized she was on the wrong bus?
Her phone died — zero percent.
3. Why did the story end well?
She ran into her cousin, who was going to the same place.
Listening 1

The most embarrassing minute

1. What was he doing when the teacher called his name?
He was drawing, and not listening at all.
2. What did he answer, and why was it wrong?
He said "yes" — but the question was not a yes-or-no question.
3. How did the class react, and what did he do?
Everyone laughed, and he froze for a few seconds.
Listening 2

What was happening during the blackout

1. What time did the lights go out?
At twenty past two.
2. What were the students in the science lab doing?
They were finishing an experiment.
3. Why does the school thank the students?
Because nobody panicked — everyone waited quietly.
