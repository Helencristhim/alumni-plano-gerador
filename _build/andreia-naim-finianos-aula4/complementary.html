Lesson 4 -- The Pitch That Opens Doors

Podcast
NPR Life Kit -- "4 negotiation tactics to try in everyday life"

Twenty minutes on the move you practised today: finding out what the other side is actually worried about before you make an offer. The tactics are the same whether the table is a salary or a licence. American English at natural speed, with the full transcript on the page.

Tip: listen once without reading. Then find the two moments where somebody asks a question instead of making a claim, and write both questions down in English.

Talk
Nancy Duarte -- "The secret structure of great talks" (TED, 18 min)

She takes famous speeches apart and shows the shape underneath: what is, what could be, what is, what could be. It is the same movement you built today — from where the client is now to where they will be if they say yes — and she makes the pattern impossible to unsee.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then map your own two-minute pitch onto her shape and say it out loud once. Where is your "what could be"?

Film
Print the Legend (Netflix, 100 min)

Two companies race to sell the same new technology, and the documentary follows what happens between the promise on stage and the product in the box. It is the lesson of today at full scale: the gap between eight weeks and twelve, filmed from the inside. American English, several accents, real meetings.

Tip: watch the first forty minutes with English subtitles. Write down one promise somebody makes on camera, and note what it costs them later.
