Lesson 15 -- When the Answer Is No

Podcast
WorkLife with Adam Grant (TED) -- "How to say no"

Adam Grant talks with the psychologist Vanessa Bohns about why saying no feels so hard, and why people take a clear refusal much better than we expect. The research behind today’s lesson.

Tip: every time you hear a refusal or a counter-proposal, pause and say how you would phrase it with I’m afraid, I’d rather or would it work if.

Talk
TEDx -- Alina Sharaf, "But, I Was Just Being Polite"

A short talk about the line between being polite and being unable to say no, and about setting boundaries without being rude. A good starting point for your own idea of a gracious refusal.

Tip: write down two sentences she uses to set a boundary, then rewrite them for a curator or a client of the agency.

Grammar Video
engVid (English with Emma) -- "7 Ways to Say NO Politely in English"

Seven polite ways to refuse, with the structure behind each one: a kind start, but, and a reason. The same moves you practiced today, in everyday situations.

Tip: after watching, take the five sentences of your survival card and make a new version of each one for a friend instead of a curator.
