Chapter 4: Always or Right Now

Your Life or Your Week

Present simple and present continuous

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I visit the warehouse twice a month."

"This week I am visiting the warehouse twice."

"The night shift starts at ten."

"Tonight the shift is starting later than usual."

TenseUse it forTime words
Present simple
I visit		Your life: facts, routines, things that were true last year and will be true next year.always, usually, every month, twice a week, never
Present continuous
I am visiting		Your week: right now, this period only, and fixed plans in the near future.now, today, this week, at the moment, tonight

Some verbs almost never take the -ing form because they describe a state, not an action: know, want, need, believe, belong. "I know the supplier", never "I am knowing".

Grammar Practice

Which Tense?

Say the full sentence, then click to compare

1. Every Monday the team ______ (meet) at headquarters.
"Every Monday the team meets at headquarters."
2. This month we ______ (deal) with a much heavier workload.
"This month we are dealing with a much heavier workload."
3. I ______ (not / work) on site today. I am at the office.
"I am not working on site today."
4. ______ the warehouse ______ (receive) four trucks every day?
"Does the warehouse receive four trucks every day?"
Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"I am knowing that supplier very well."

✓ RIGHT

"I know that supplier very well."

✗ WRONG

"I am living in Sao Paulo for twenty years."

✓ RIGHT

"I live in Sao Paulo."

✗ WRONG

"Every Monday I am meeting the senior team."

✓ RIGHT

"Every Monday I meet the senior team."

Look at the time word before you choose the tense. Every and for twenty years pull the simple form; today and this week pull the -ing.

Dialogue

Where Are You This Week?

M
Felipe, I cannot find you at your desk. Where are you?
F
I am at the warehouse. I am spending two days on site this week.
M
Two days? You normally go there for a morning.
F
Normally, yes. But the night shift is dealing with seven trucks a day instead of four.
M
That is a heavy workload. Is it permanent?
F
No, only this month. Two suppliers moved their dates. I am following up on that today.
M
And the report? The deadline is Friday, and the senior team meets at headquarters on Monday.
F
I know. I write it on Thursday nights, always. This week will be no different.
Did You Catch It?

What Megan Learned

1. Why is this week different from a normal week?
He is spending two days on site instead of one morning, because two suppliers moved their dates.
2. How many trucks is the night shift receiving, and how many is normal?
Seven a day this week, instead of the usual four.
3. When does he always write the report?
On Thursday nights, every week.
