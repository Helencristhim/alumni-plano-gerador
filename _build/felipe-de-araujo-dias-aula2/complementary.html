Grammar Video
"Verb patterns" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on what comes after a verb in English, including when the -ing form is the only possible choice. Connection to Lesson 2: it is the other half of the tense you practised today, and it explains why "I am knowing" never sounds right to a native ear.

Tip: stop the video every time you hear an -ing and decide, out loud, whether it is there because of the tense or because of the verb before it.

Podcast
"How to better manage your time" -- Life Kit, NPR

A short American English episode about weeks that never look the same, and about the difference between the routine you plan and the week you actually get. Connection to Lesson 2: the whole conversation moves between what people always do and what they are doing right now.

Tip: listen once for pleasure. The second time, write two sentences about your own week, one in each tense, using something you heard.

"How to speak so that people want to listen" -- Julian Treasure, TED

A British speaker, on a stage, at natural speed. Connection to Lesson 2: this is your British accent training for the week, and the content is directly useful for the meetings you avoid.

Tip: watch it once with no subtitles, even if you catch only half. Then watch it again with English subtitles and see how much you had actually understood.
