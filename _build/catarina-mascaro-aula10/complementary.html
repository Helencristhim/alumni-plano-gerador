Queer Eye — Season 1, Episode 1

Five people whose entire job is giving advice, and they do it in every strength there is — "you should", "you could", "you might want to". Warm, slow enough to follow, and packed with the grammar of this lesson.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down five pieces of advice. Which are strong, which are soft, and which are just a maybe?
