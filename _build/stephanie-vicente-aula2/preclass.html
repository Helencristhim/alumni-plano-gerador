Lesson 02 · Block 1 — Build

Three People, One Material

Get your ear ready for three voices you have never heard.
▼

Before class

In class you will hear a call between three people about a coursebook: a teacher who already uses it, someone from the publisher, and the author. Nobody decides anything — each one says what matters to them.

Write two lines, no more: what would you want to ask about a new book before your school adopted it? Bring them. We will see whether anyone on the call asks the same thing.
