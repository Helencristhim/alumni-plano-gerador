Lesson 26 -- Pre-class

Networking Mastery -- The Welcome Reception in Miami

Meet the people of your cohort at the welcome reception of the Miami module and keep in touch after it: use networking phrasal verbs and put the pronoun in the right place (set it up / run into him). Key words: run into, catch up, hit it off, set up, come across, pass on, hand out, keep in touch, look up, get together.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each phrasal verb and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Run into -- to meet someone by chance, without a plan
"I ran into an old colleague at the airport in Miami."
Catch up -- to talk with someone you have not seen for some time and share your news
"Let's catch up over coffee after the session."
Hit it off -- to like each other and get along well from the first moment
"We hit it off in the first five minutes."
Set up -- to arrange or organize a meeting, a call or an event
"Can you set up a call with the federation next week?"
Come across -- to give a particular impression to other people
"He comes across as confident and friendly."
Pass on -- to give information, a message or a contact to another person
"Please pass on my contact details to your director."
Hand out -- to give something to each person in a group
"Don't hand out fifty business cards in one evening."
Keep in touch -- to continue to communicate with someone after you meet
"Let's keep in touch after the Miami module."
Look up -- to find someone or some information, for example online
"I looked her up on LinkedIn before the reception."
Get together -- to meet other people socially or for work
"Our cohort gets together online every Thursday."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Phrasal Verbs and Pronouns

GRAMMAR

Where the object goes in a phrasal verb.

TypeRuleExample
Separable: set up, pass on, hand out, look upa noun goes before or after the particle; a pronoun goes in the middleset up a call / set a call up / set it up
Inseparable: run intothe object always goes after the particleI ran into Daniel. / I ran into him.
No object: catch up, get together, come across (as)no object; add a person with withLet's catch up. / Let's catch up with Sofia.
Fixed: hit it off, keep in touchthe words never change placeWe hit it off. / Let's keep in touch.
RuleSay "set it up", never "set up it". Say "run into him", never "run him into". In speech, link the sounds: catch up, set it up, hit it off.
