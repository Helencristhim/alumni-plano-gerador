Series
The Playlist — Episode 1

Six episodes about a decision that nobody could see the end of, told once from each person point of view. It is a study in what people commit to, what they end up doing instead, and what they persist in when it stops being fun.

Tip: watch one episode with English subtitles and write down six sentences where one verb is followed by another. Then mark which took -ing and which took to.

Talk
Matt Cutts — “Try something new for 30 days” (TED)

Three minutes, and it is the shortest useful thing on this list. It is about the second column: what happens when you stop intending to do something and simply do it for thirty days. Watch it in the queue for lunch.

Tip: three minutes is short enough to shadow. Play a sentence, pause, and say it back at his speed. Do the whole talk that way once.

Podcast
BBC Learning English — “Gerund or infinitive?”

A short answer to the exact question this lesson is built on, from the Ask BBC Learning English series. Free, spoken, and it covers the meaning-change verbs that most courses skip.

Tip: after they explain each group, say one sentence about your own week using a verb from it, out loud, before pressing play again.
