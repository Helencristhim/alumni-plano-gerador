Chapter 3: Routine or Now?

I check · I am checking

every week -- or right now?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I check the IMS system every Monday."

"Right now I am preparing the EUDR maps."

"Ana usually visits the farms on Tuesdays."

"This week she is checking the documents."

FormUseExample
Present simple: check / checksa routine, a habitI check the IMS system every Monday.
Present continuous: am / is / are + -ingnow, or this week onlyRight now I am preparing the maps.
Negative: don't / doesn't -- am not / isn't / aren'troutine -- nowI don't visit farms on Mondays. I am not visiting farms this week.
Question: Do you...? -- Are you...-ing?routine -- nowDo you work on Fridays? Are you working on the project today?
Time wordsevery day, usually, on Mondays -- right now, today, this week, currently

Routine = present simple. Now = am / is / are + -ing. / Rotina = present simple. Agora = am / is / are + verbo com -ing.
