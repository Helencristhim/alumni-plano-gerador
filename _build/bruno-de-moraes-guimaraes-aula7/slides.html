Chapter 3: The Code

At, On, In

From the smallest to the biggest box of time

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"School starts at seven o'clock."

"I play basketball on Saturday."

"I do my homework in the afternoon."

"We travel in December."

One has a clock time. One has a day. Two have something bigger. Which word goes with which?

WordGoes withExample
ata POINT on the clock"at seven o'clock" · "at half past ten"
ona DAY on the calendar"on Monday" · "on Friday night" · "on my birthday"
ina BIG BOX of time"in the morning" · "in December" · "in 2027"
The one exceptionIt is in the morning, in the afternoon — but at night. There is no reason. Learn it as one block: at night.
Key ideaSmall to big: at the clock, on the day, in the month. If you can see it on a clock, it is at.

Three words, and you can book a flight, a class or a game without anybody getting confused.

Chapter 4: The Flight Home

The Flight Home

Last day, and everything depends on the clock

Dialogue

What Time Is Your Flight?

Z
So, today is your last day. What time is your flight?
B
At eleven forty, at night. It's a long flight.
Z
And when do you arrive in Brazil?
B
At nine in the morning. And school starts again on Monday.
Z
On Monday? That's fast. What time do you go to the airport?
B
In the afternoon, at four. I don't want to be late.
Z
Good. Are you coming back next year?
B
I want to. Maybe in July. Send me a message and we talk.
Comprehension

About the Conversation

1. What does Zoe ask first?
She asks what time his flight is.
2. What does she say about Monday?
She says that it is fast.
3. What does she want to know at the end?
She wants to know if he is coming back next year.
4. Which month does he say?
July. He says "maybe in July".
Listening

At the Airport

An announcement for the flight home. Sound first — no text.

At eleven forty tonight, from gate fifteen.
2. What time do you need to be at the gate?
At half past ten.
3. What time does the flight arrive?
At nine in the morning, local time.
Listening 2

Tyler's Week

Your roommate describes his whole week. Sound first — no text.

1. What does he do on Tuesday?
He works in his father's shop, in the afternoon.
2. Why doesn't he like Thursday?
Because he has a test at seven thirty, so he wakes up early.
3. What happens in December?
He goes to his grandmother's house for two weeks, every year.
