Lesson 24 -- Not Only... But Also

Film
The Mitchells vs. the Machines

Something to review, chosen because it is genuinely groundbreaking in how it looks and genuinely uneven in the middle -- which means an honest verdict on it needs both halves, exactly like the review you read in class. Watch it in English and decide your score before you look up anybody else's.

Tip: write your five line review the same night: verdict, praise, one shortcoming, the condition, and the closing line. One inversion, on the sentence you want quoted.

Podcast
Pop Culture Happy Hour (NPR) -- Terrible but bingeable TV shows

Four critics arguing about things they know are not good and watched anyway. It is the whole vocabulary of this lesson in use -- overrated, underwhelming, compelling, verdict -- and it shows how a review stays honest without turning into an attack. The transcript sits next to the audio.

Tip: pick one show they discuss and write the verdict line yourself before they give theirs. Then compare: whose sentence would you quote?

YouTube
BBC English Class -- Inversion

A short class on exactly the structure you practised, with the trigger words listed and the auxiliary explained. Watch it after the lesson rather than before, so it confirms a rule you already worked out from the examples instead of handing it to you.

Tip: pause after each example and say the normal, uninverted version out loud. If you can move between the two in both directions, the structure is yours.
