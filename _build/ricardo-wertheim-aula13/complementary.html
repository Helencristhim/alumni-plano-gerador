Grammar Video
"Adverbs of Manner -- English Grammar Lessons" -- Ellii

A clear lesson on the structure of this class: adjective plus -ly, the spelling changes, and the irregular ones like well and fast. Connection to Lesson 13: these are the words that turn "the waiter answered" into a real story about a person.

Tip: after each example, say one sentence about a local you met on a trip, with the same adverb. Then check: is it about the person, or about HOW they did it?

Six minutes on gestures that mean different things in different countries, exactly like the head in Listening 2. Connection to Lesson 13: gesture, misunderstanding and custom, in a real conversation between two presenters.

Tip: listen once without the transcript and write down every gesture they mention. Then say which ones you use in Brazil, and what they mean here.
