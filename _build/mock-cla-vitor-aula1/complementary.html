Series
The White Lotus — HBO

A hotel, its staff, and guests who complain about everything except what is actually wrong with them. Written by people who clearly worked in hospitality: the manager's lines are painfully accurate.

Tip: watch the first episode with English subtitles and note every time a guest complains about one thing while meaning another. You will fill a page.

Podcast
Hidden Brain — Are You Listening?

A psychologist who researches listening explains why we are bad at it: we are busy preparing our defence while the other person is still talking. That is the exact mistake this lesson was built around.

Tip: listen on your way to work. Then, on your next shift, try the one thing from the episode that costs nothing — do not answer until they have finished.

Talk
Julian Treasure — 5 ways to listen better (TED)

Seven minutes, and the most useful part is the last one: a set of listening positions you can switch between on purpose. A guest at the desk at eleven at night needs a different one from a colleague in a meeting.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, decide which position you use by default — and which one you should have used in your last difficult complaint.
