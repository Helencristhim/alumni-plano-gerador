Lesson 01 — Before class

The Real Complaint

Warm up your mouth, and bring your own cases. In class you will spend half the hour talking to a difficult guest — come with the lines ready and two real complaints in your pocket.

Before class 1: The lines you will need

Vocabulary

Eight lines a front desk manager says every week. Tap Listen once for each. Do not memorise them — just let your ear meet them before class.

What happened tonight? — opens the real story instead of the complaint
The guest says the room is unacceptable. You ask this.
Just so I understand correctly — checks the facts before you act on them
"Just so I understand correctly, the noise starts before six?"
I completely understand why you feel that way — validates the feeling without admitting fault
Notice what it never says: that the hotel was wrong.
That would frustrate me too — puts you on the same side as the guest
Shorter and warmer. Use it while the guest is still angry.
What I can offer is — states what is possible, without promising more
"What I can offer is two nights, and I can do that now."
Would it help if — tests a solution without committing to it
"Would it help if I moved you to the second floor?"
I'm afraid that's not something I can do, but — says no and keeps the door open
The word that does the work is the last one: but.
I'll take care of it personally — closes with ownership, not with an apology
Say it only when it is true. A guest remembers this one.

Before class 2: Until they are automatic

Speaking

This is the part that matters. In class you will say these to a guest who is not being reasonable, and under pressure you only reach for what your mouth already knows. Record each one until it comes out without thinking.

What happened tonight?
Just so I understand correctly, the problem started on Monday?
I completely understand why you feel that way.
I'm afraid that's not something I can do, but I can hold your room until two.
I'll take care of it personally.

Before class 3: Bring two real ones

Reflection

In class the conversation will be about your hotel, not a textbook one. Come with the raw material.

Think of a complaint you handled where the thing the guest pointed at was NOT the real problem. Record 60 seconds: what they said first, what it actually turned out to be, and what you offered in the end. If none comes to mind, describe the complaint you get most often — and say what you think is really behind it.
Now the uncomfortable one. Think of a time you said no to a guest and it went badly. Record 40 seconds: what they asked for, what you said, and what you would say differently today. You will need this in the second half of the class.
