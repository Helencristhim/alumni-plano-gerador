Before class 1: Eight lines

Vocabulary

These are chunks, not words. Listen and say each one until it sounds like you.

Just so I understand correctly — checks understanding before you act
"Just so I understand correctly, the problem started on Monday?"
What happened tonight? — opens the real story instead of the stated one
"Instead of explaining, I asked one question: what happened tonight?"
I completely understand why you feel that way — validates the feeling without admitting fault
"I completely understand why you feel that way."
What I can offer is — states what is possible, in your own terms
"What I can offer is two nights, and I can do that now."
Would it help if...? — tests a solution without promising it
"Would it help if I moved you to the second floor?"
I'll take care of it personally — closes with ownership
"I'll take care of it personally."
I'm afraid that's not something I can do — refuses without closing the door
"I'm afraid that's not something I can do, but here is what I can."
What would work best for you? — hands the choice back to the guest
"What would work best for you?"

Before class 2: Which job does it do?

Quiz
A guest says: "Nobody here listens to me." Which line comes FIRST?
A I completely understand why you feel that way. Tell me what happened.
B What I can offer is a free breakfast.
You cannot give the refund the guest is asking for. What do you say?
A That is our policy, I am sorry.
B I'm afraid that's not something I can do, but what I can offer is...
When do you ask "What would work best for you?"
A After you understand the problem, when there is more than one way out.
B First, before the guest explains anything.

Before class 5: Bring a real one

Reflection
Think of a complaint you handled badly — everyone has one. Record 30 seconds: what the guest said, what you said, and what you would say now. We will use it in class.
