Lesson 28 -- Although It Works...

Film
Chef (2014)

A famous chef gets a terrible online review, answers it in anger on social media, and loses his job. Then he starts again with a small food truck and learns to listen to his customers. Connection to Lesson 28: it is the perfect example of how NOT to answer negative feedback — and of what changes when you answer with balance.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. When the chef reads the review, pause and write how you would answer it with although, however and despite.

Podcast
BBC Learning English Grammar -- "Linking devices of contrast"

A short BBC episode that explains, with clear examples, how although, despite, in spite of and however join two opposite ideas, and which words come after each one. Connection to Lesson 28: exactly the grammar of your answer to the Brightwave survey.

Tip: listen twice. The second time, stop after each example and say a sentence about one of your own customers with the same word.

"Linking words of contrast" -- BBC English Class

A BBC teacher shows the differences between despite, in spite of, though, although and even though, with the most common mistakes that learners make. Connection to Lesson 28: a fast review of the sentences you used to answer mixed feedback.

Tip: pause before each answer and try the exercise first. Then write three sentences about your own work with although, despite and however.
