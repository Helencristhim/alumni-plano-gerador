Chapter 4: Language Focus

Although, However, Despite

contrast linkers — although / even though / however / despite / in spite of

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Although the platform works well, this was a difficult quarter."

"The dashboards are clear. However, the sync failed three times."

"Despite the problems, they are planning to renew."

"Even though nobody knew the cause, she called the same day."

Every sentence joins a good idea and a bad idea. Look at what comes after each green word: a subject and a verb, a new sentence, or only a noun?

StructureUseExample
although / even though + subject + verbTwo opposite ideas in one sentence. Even though is stronger.Although the fix was fast, the orders were late.
However, + new sentenceStarts a new sentence with the opposite idea. Comma after it.The dashboards are clear. However, the sync failed.
despite / in spite of + nounOnly a noun after it, never a subject and a verb.Despite the problems, they renewed.
despite / in spite of + -ingOr the -ing form of a verb.Despite having three failures, they stayed.
despite the fact that + subject + verbWhen you need a full sentence after despite.Despite the fact that it failed, they renewed.

Never "despite of": it is despite or in spite of. Never "although the problems" → say "despite the problems". And never "despite it failed" → say "although it failed".

The Three Sentences of a Balanced Answer

Problem, Reassurance, Plan

"Although the fix was fast, I know the delay was frustrating."

1 — accept the problem: although + subject + verb

"Despite the delay, your data was never at risk."

2 — reassure: despite + noun

"However, next time we will warn you before your team sees it."

3 — the plan: however + new sentence

Chapter 5: The Call

Calling Daniel

The complaint first, the thanks at the end

Dialogue

Thank You — and Sorry

D
Patrícia, I sent the survey yesterday. I gave you a seven. I hope that is not a problem.
P
Not at all. Thank you for the genuine feedback. Although a seven is not a ten, it tells us exactly what to fix.
D
The sync was the big one. Those three mornings were really frustrating for the stores.
P
I understand. Despite the quick fix, your team found the problem before we did. That should not happen.
D
Exactly. That was my main complaint. What are you going to do about it?
P
We have added an alert. However, I want to go further: if the sync is late, I will call you before seven.
D
Good. And thank you for the calls in August. The praise in the survey was for you.
P
That means a lot. Overall, I hope the benefits still outweigh the problems for you.
Comprehension

About Daniel

1. What rating did Daniel give in the survey?
Seven.
2. Why were those three mornings bad for Daniel's company?
The sync failed, and it was frustrating for the stores.
3. Who was the praise in the survey for, according to Daniel?
For Patrícia — for her calls in August.

Free Practice

Ninety Seconds, One Real Survey

Ninety seconds: think of real feedback that one of your customers gave you — a survey, an email or a call. Answer it with balance: the complaint first, then what changed, and the thanks at the end.

No keywords. One rule: at least three contrast words — although, however, despite or even though.
