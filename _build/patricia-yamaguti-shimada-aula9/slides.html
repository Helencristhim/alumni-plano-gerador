Chapter 3: Can and Cannot

One Word: Ask, Allow, Refuse

can · cannot · can I · you can

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Can I try it on?" (you ask)

"Yes, you can." (he allows)

"You cannot get the money back." (he refuses)

"Can you check in the back, please?" (you ask him)

PatternWhat it doesExample
Can I + verb?YOU ask for permissionCan I try it on?
Can you + verb?you ask THEM to do itCan you check in the back?
You can + verbthey allow itYou can exchange it in 30 days.
cannot / can't + verbthey refuse itYou cannot get the money back.

After can the verb is naked: no to, no -s. It never changes: I can, she can, they can. And can't is just the short form of cannot.

Comprehension

What Did Daniel Say?

1. Why can she not have a refund?
The dress was in the sale, so it is exchange only.
2. What does Daniel find in the back?
The dress in a large size, in blue and in black.
3. Where is the fitting room, and what is inside?
At the back, on her right. There is a big mirror inside.
Listening

The Rules of the Store

The recorded message at the exchange desk. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What two things do you need to exchange?
The receipt, and the tag still on the piece.
2. When can you get your money back?
Only if the piece has a defect. Then you can do it at any time.
3. What is the rule for sale items?
Always exchange only, even in the first week.
