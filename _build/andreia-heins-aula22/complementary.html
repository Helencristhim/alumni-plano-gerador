Lesson 22 -- Telling a Clinical Story

Talk
Catherine Mohr -- "Surgery's past, present and robotic future" (TED)

A surgeon and robotics engineer tells the story of surgery, from the past to the robot you work with every day. A great model of telling a story in the past.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down three sentences in the past simple and one in the past continuous that you hear.

Talk
Brian Goldman -- "Doctors make mistakes. Can we talk about that?" (TED)

An emergency doctor tells real cases from his career, with the scene, the event and what had happened before. Honest clinical storytelling in clear English.

Tip: choose one of his cases and retell it in one minute, with at least one past perfect (had + past participle).

Watch on TED →
Talk
Atul Gawande -- "Want to get great at something? Get a coach" (TED)

A surgeon tells the story of his own operations: what he was doing, what had gone wrong, and how a coach in the operating room changed his results. A long clinical story full of narrative tenses.

Tip: write down two verbs in the past continuous and one in the past perfect that you hear. Then tell one moment from your career when someone helped you get better.
