Chapter 4: The Code

There Is, There Are

Two phrases for every place

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"There is a red train."

"There are big mountains."

"There is not a lot of noise."

"Is there a station in the village?"

Look at the blue words and the word after them. When is it is, and when is it are?

JobFormExample
One thingthere is + a / anThere is a lake.
Two or more thingsthere are + pluralThere are mountains.
Negativethere is not / there are noThere are no Brazilian restaurants.
QuestionIs there...? / Are there...?Is there a station?
Short answersYes, there is. / No, there are not.Is there a lake? Yes, there is.
The trapnever "have" for a placeNot Have a lake. Say There is a lake.

In one line: one thing: there is; two or more: there are. Never "have" to describe a place.
