Aula 04 · ESP / E1 — Lesson 04 — Before you sit down with him

1 · What the meeting is

This is what the school tells new teachers about observation feedback.

After an observation
The observer meets the teacher within two working days. The purpose is development, not assessment. Nothing said in the meeting goes into a performance record, and the teacher decides what to do with the feedback.

Mark the two things that follow from that.

The teacher can decline a suggestion
The observer should avoid saying anything critical
The observer has to give reasons, not instructions
The meeting has no consequences at all
The teacher has to agree by the end
The observer decides what changes
If the decision is his, then what you bring has to be arguable. That is not softness — it is what makes the meeting worth having.

3 · Who decides

Six sentences from feedback meetings. In each one, who is left deciding what happens next?

“You need to change the order of those two stages.”
“Have you thought about changing the order?”
“Something to think about for next time.”
“One thing you might try is giving it earlier.”
“Please make sure the instruction comes first.”
“We could look at that again after the next class.”
Politeness is not the same as room
Number 5 is the politest sentence in the list and it leaves him nothing to answer. The question is never how nice it sounds — it is who is left deciding.
