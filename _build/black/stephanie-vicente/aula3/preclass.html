Aula 03 · Grammar / G1 — Lesson 03 — Five moments, before you tell them

1 · What an observation note is for

This is the form a school gives an observer to fill in.

Observation record — teaching practice
Section A: what happened, in the order it happened
Section B: what the observer concluded
Note: Section B is discussed with the teacher. Section A is not open to discussion.

Mark the two statements that follow from that note.

Section A has to be something both people saw
Section B is the more important one
Putting an opinion in Section A takes away the teacher’s right to reply
The observer should avoid concluding anything
Section A is written after the class
Both sections say the same thing in different words
The two sections exist because they have different statuses. What is in A is shared ground; what is in B is the observer’s, and can be argued with. Mixing them removes the argument without winning it.

The test
Could the teacher who taught the class disagree with the sentence? If yes, it is Section B, whatever it looks like.
