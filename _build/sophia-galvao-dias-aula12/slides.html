0 / 6 errors found

"I had already went to both shops."
"I have already been to both shops. — have, not had; and the third form of go here is been."
"I have went to the U.S. last July."
"I went to the U.S. last July. — last July is a finished time, so no have."
"I have ate one."
"I have eaten one. — eat, ate, eaten."
"Have you ever tried it? — No, I hadn't."
"No, I haven't. — the answer repeats the have of the question."
"Have you ever already watched it?"
"Have you ever watched it? — one is enough: ever asks about your whole life."
"Have you ever made a video with friends? — Yes, with my friends."
"Yes, I have! With my friends. — say the short answer first, then the story."
