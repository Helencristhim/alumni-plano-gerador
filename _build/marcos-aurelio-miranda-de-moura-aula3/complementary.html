Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
NPR Planet Money -- "How are gas prices set?"

They follow one barrel of crude oil from the global market to the refinery to the pump, and count the cents at every step. It is the chain that decides your own price list, told in the past tense from beginning to end.

Tip: the page has the full transcript. Listen once with it closed, then open it and mark every past simple verb in the first five minutes. Count how many are irregular.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
TED -- Elizabeth Lyle, "How to break bad management habits before they reach the next generation of leaders"

Nine minutes on the habit that makes a bad quarter worse: a room where nobody wants to say the number out loud. It is the other side of the lesson you just did, seen from the chair of the person listening.

Tip: watch it once for the idea. Then pick the one sentence you would want to hear from somebody on your own team, and say it out loud as if you were them.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
CNBC Explains -- "What’s next for oil?"

Six minutes on the market that moved your cost last year: where the demand went, what moved the barrel, and what the industry expected next. Almost every sentence in it is a past simple verb doing exactly what you practised today.

Tip: watch it once with English subtitles and once without. On the second pass, say out loud what happened, in three sentences, before the video tells you.
