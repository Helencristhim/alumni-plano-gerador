Have or had?

Have or had?

Left: your life up to now. Right: a story. Which thing happened first?

"I have been to three concerts in my life."↔"When we arrived, the show had already started."
"I have never seen Bruno Mars live."↔"Before that night, I had never seen him live."
"Have you ever lost your voice at a show?"↔"I had lost my voice before the encore."
"The tickets have sold out!"↔"The tickets had sold out, so we watched it at home."
You want to sayUse
your life up to now, no timehave / has + third form
I have been to three concerts.
the story, at a finished timepast simple
We arrived at nine.
inside the story: what happened BEFORE thathad + third form
The show had already started.

Had lives only inside a story, before another past moment. Your life up to now is still have.
