Chapter 3: The Code

Let’s, Why Don’t We, How About

Four ways to make a suggestion

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Do?

"Let’s order takeout tonight."

"Why don’t we walk the dogs on the beach?"

"How about watching a movie?"

"Would you like to go out for dinner?"

All four sentences are suggestions. Look at the blue verbs. After let’s, why don’t we and would you like to, the verb is simple: order, walk, go. After how about, the verb takes -ing: watching.

JobFormExample
Let’slet’s + verbLet’s order takeout.
Why don’t wewhy don’t we + verb?Why don’t we walk the dogs?
How abouthow about + verb-ing?How about watching a movie?

AnswersGood idea! · Sure! · I’d rather stay home.Let’s go out. — Good idea!
The trap-ing only after how aboutNot let’s ordering or how about watch.

In one line: let’s, why don’t we and would you like to + simple verb; how about + -ing.

Chapter 4: At Home

At Home

One evening, two voices, one list on the fridge

Dialogue

The End of the Day

H
Hi! You’re home. How was your day?
M
Long. I had five calls with clients, and I read two articles for my master’s. I’m exhausted. How was yours?
H
Not bad. I finished a big project, and in the afternoon I ran errands: the bank and the vet.
M
The vet? Are the dogs OK?
H
Yes, they’re fine. It was only a check-up. But they want to go out now. Why don’t we walk the dogs on the beach?
M
Good idea. And after that, how about ordering takeout? I don’t want to cook.
H
Sure. Sushi? And let’s watch something to wind down.
M
Perfect. And tomorrow is my day off from the master’s, so we can catch up properly.
Comprehension

About her husband

1. What did her husband finish today?
A big project.
2. Where did he go in the afternoon?
To the bank and to the vet.
3. What does he suggest for after dinner?
To watch something to wind down.

Listening 2 · A Day Off

Giulia’s Day Off

Giulia, back home in Milan, talks about her day off with her partner. Sound first — no text.

1. What did Giulia do in the morning?
She ran errands: the post office and the supermarket.
2. What did her partner suggest?
Going to the lake for lunch.
3. Why was the day perfect for her?
Because they had time to catch up.

The Same Evening, Three Times

Role-Play 1 of 3 — Guided

He Asks About Your Day

Scenario: You arrive home after a long day. Your husband asks: how was your day? What did you do? How do you feel? Answer each question, and then ask him about his day.

Keywords:

It was I had exhausted How was yours? ran errands
Role-Play 2 of 3 — Semi-free

Plan the Evening

Scenario: It is Friday evening. You are both tired. Make at least three suggestions for the evening: dinner, the dogs and something to watch. Answer his suggestions too: good idea, or I’d rather...

Keywords:

Let's Why don’t we How about Good idea! I'd rather
Role-Play 3 of 3 — Free

Your Real Evening, No Notes

Scenario: Tonight, at home. In one minute: tell the story of your day, how you feel, and suggest a plan for the evening. Talk as if he is here.

No keywords, no notes, two minutes.
