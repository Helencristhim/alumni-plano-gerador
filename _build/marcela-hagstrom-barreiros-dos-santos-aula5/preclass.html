Lesson 05 -- Pre-class

How Was Your Day? -- English at Home

The work day is over and someone asks how your day was. Key words: exhausted, to walk the dogs, to run errands, a to-do list, to catch up, to wind down, to order takeout, a day off. Structure: suggestions with let’s, why don’t we, how about + -ing and would you like to.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Exhausted -- very, very tired
"I’m exhausted. I had six calls today."
To walk the dogs -- to take the dogs out for a walk
"We walk the dogs on the beach every morning."
To run errands -- to go out and do small tasks, like the bank or the supermarket
"I ran errands after lunch: the bank and the pharmacy."
A to-do list -- a list of the things you need to do
"My to-do list for tomorrow is very long."
To catch up -- to talk and tell each other the news
"Let’s have dinner and catch up."
To wind down -- to relax after a busy day
"I watch an old series to wind down."
To order takeout -- to ask a restaurant for food and eat it at home
"We are tired, so let’s order takeout."
A day off -- a day when you do not work
"Wednesday is my day off this week."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Mark is still at the office. He is exhausted, so he sends a message: “How about ordering takeout tonight? And can you walk the dog? Let’s watch a movie after dinner to wind down.”

Giulia had a day off. In the morning she ran errands and finished her to-do list. Then her partner said: “Why don’t we go to the lake for lunch?”

They talked for hours. They work a lot, so it was a good day to catch up.

1. Why does Mark want takeout?
A Because he loves Thai food.
B Because he is exhausted and late.
C Because it is his day off.
2. What did Giulia do in the morning?
A She ran errands.
B She went to the lake.
C She watched a movie.
3. Which suggestion is correct?
A “How about go to the lake?”
B “How about going to the lake?”
C “Let’s going to the lake.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Making Suggestions -- Let’s, Why Don’t We, How About

GRAMMAR

Use these structures to suggest a plan. After let’s, why don’t we and would you like to, use the simple verb. After how about, use the verb + -ing.

JobFormExample
Let’slet’s + verbLet’s order takeout.
Why don’t wewhy don’t we + verb?Why don’t we walk the dogs?
How abouthow about + verb-ing?How about watching a movie?
Would you like towould you like to + verb?Would you like to go out?
AnswersGood idea! · Sure! · I’d rather stay home.Let’s go out. — Good idea!
The trap-ing only after how aboutNot let’s ordering or how about watch.

Never: Let’s to order takeout · How about watch a movie? · Why we don’t go? · Would you like go out?
