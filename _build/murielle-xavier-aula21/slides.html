Patterns at a Glance

-ing or to?

Some verbs take -ing; others take to + base verb

VERB + -ING

avoid, consider, suggest, postpone, practice, look forward to

VERB + TO

plan, decide, agree, need, manage, offer, tend, decline

Chapter 3

Verb Patterns

Verb + -ing or verb + to + base verb

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I suggest starting with the turnover data."

"We need to send the agenda today."

"Let's avoid discussing salaries in this call."

"I look forward to meeting the Sydney team."

PatternVerbsExample
verb + -ingavoid, consider, suggest, postpone, practice, mindI suggest starting early.
verb + to + base verbplan, decide, agree, need, manage, offer, affordWe decided to hire two people.
preposition + -inglook forward to, before, after, aboutI look forward to meeting you.
negativenot + -ing / not + toI decided not to postpone it.

Chapter 4

The Prep Call

Preparing a call with the Sydney team

Dialogue

The Prep Call

E
Murielle, the call with the Sydney team is tomorrow. Are we ready?
M
Almost. I suggest starting with the turnover data, and I plan to keep it short.
E
Should we talk about the new bonus policy too?
M
I would avoid discussing the bonus tomorrow. Let's postpone it until the numbers are final.
E
Good idea. Australians tend to speak fast. Are you worried?
M
A little. I practice listening to Australian podcasts every morning, and I won't hesitate to ask them to repeat.
E
Great. Can you manage to send the agenda today?
M
Yes, I offered to prepare it. Honestly, I look forward to meeting the team.
