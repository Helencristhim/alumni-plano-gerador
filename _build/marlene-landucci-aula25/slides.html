Chapter 3 -- Four Tools

Four Tools for a Conversation

I love walking. / It was amazing. / Would you like...? / There is...

Discovery

Listen and Notice

Which one is about the past? Which one offers? Which one says what a place has? Which one says what you like?

"I love walking in small towns."

"Last weekend I visited Siena. It was amazing."

"Would you like some wine?"

"There is music on the terrace tonight."

The Tools

Four Tools in One Box

ToolPatternExample
What you like (Lesson 21)like / love / enjoy + verb-ingI enjoy chatting with new people.
Your story (Lesson 22)verb + -ed / was / wereI visited Rome. It was amazing.
Offer and recommend (Lesson 23)Would you like...? / You should try...You should try the cheese.
What a place has (Lesson 24)There is + one / There are + two or moreThere are drinks on the long table.

Key idea: a conversation is a small circle -- you say what you like, you tell a short story, you offer something, and you talk about the place. Then you ask the other person the same things.

Chapter 4 -- At the Party

A Night on the Terrace

A conversation, an invitation, and two messages

Dialogue

Paul at the Party

P

"Marlene! Good to see you again. Would you like a glass of wine?"

M

"Yes, please. Thank you, Paul. How was your weekend?"

P

"It was amazing. I visited Siena on Saturday. I love walking in small towns."

M

"That sounds amazing! Two years ago I stayed in Rome for a week. My best memory is the food."

P

"Then you should try the cheese from Tuscany. Would you like to share this plate with me?"

M

"Yes, thank you. Is there Wi-Fi here? I want to send a message to my family."

P

"Yes, there is. The password is on the card. I really enjoy chatting with you, Marlene."

Comprehension

About Paul

1. What does Paul offer Marlene to drink?
A glass of wine -- he asks "Would you like a glass of wine?"
2. Where was Paul on Saturday, and what does he love doing?
He was in Siena. He loves walking in small towns.
3. What does Paul recommend, and what does he want to share?
The cheese from Tuscany -- and he wants to share his plate with Marlene.
4. Where is the Wi-Fi password, according to Paul?
On the card.
