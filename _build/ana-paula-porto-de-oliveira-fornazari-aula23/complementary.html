Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Celeste Headlee — "10 ways to have a better conversation" (TED)

A radio host explains how to have a real conversation with anyone. Clear American English and very practical ideas.

Tip: turn on English subtitles. Choose two of the ten ideas and use them at your next dinner.

A public radio story with simple tips for talking to people you do not know at a party. Very useful for your dinners with American friends.

Tip: write down one opening sentence from the story and add a question tag to it.
