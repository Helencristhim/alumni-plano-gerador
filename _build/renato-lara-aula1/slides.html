Grammar Discovery

Look at the highlighted word

I am from Brazil.
Zoe is a local.
The line-up is crowded.
We are stoked.

Every English sentence needs a verb.

To say who somebody is, where they are, or how they feel, that verb is to be: am, is, are. In Portuguese you can drop it. In English you never can.

The Code

Three forms, that is all

Full form

I am
You are
He / She / It is
We / They are

How people really say it

I'm
You're
He's / She's / It's
We're / They're

The verb is small, and it disappears in fast speech — but it is never gone.

The Code

Say no. Then ask.

+ I am nervous.  —  The swell is big.
− I am not nervous.  —  The swell is not big. (I'm not / isn't / aren't)
? Are you nervous?  —  Is the swell big? (the verb jumps to the front — no do)
→ Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
