Chapter 3: The Code

You Should

Advice and recommendations

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Show?

"You should have three topics, not more."

"You shouldn’t put too many numbers on one slide."

"How many slides should I have?"

"I think we should send the samples by air."

Look at the red word: should or shouldn’t. It gives advice or a recommendation. Now look at the blue verbs after it: is there to, -s or -ing?

JobFormExample
Advice: a good ideashould + verbYou should have three topics.
Advice: a bad ideashouldn’t + verbYou shouldn’t put too many numbers.
Ask for adviceShould I...? / What should I...?How many slides should I have?
A recommendationI think we should + verbI think we should send the samples by air.
Same form for everybodyI / you / he / she / we / they + shouldShe should present first. (not shoulds)
The trapno to after shouldNot You should to practice. Say You should practice.

In one line: should + simple verb for advice; shouldn’t for a bad idea; I think we should for a recommendation.

Chapter 4: Getting Ready

Getting Ready With Anna

One call, two voices, one slide plan

Dialogue

Getting Ready With Anna

A
Fernanda, you are presenting at the quarterly review on Thursday, right?
F
Yes, and I’m a little nervous. I have only two minutes.
A
Two minutes is good. You should have three topics, not more.
F
OK. First of all, the results. Then the trial. And to sum up, our plan.
A
Perfect. And you shouldn’t put too many numbers on one slide.
F
Good point. How many slides should I have?
A
Four: one for the agenda and one for each topic.
F
And what should I say at the end?
A
Say your recommendation clearly, and then ask: any questions?

Listening 2 · What Customers Want

Julien’s Advice

Julien, a customer in France, says what he likes in a supplier presentation. Sound first — no text.

1. How long should a supplier presentation be, for Julien?
Short: ten minutes is enough.
2. What shouldn’t suppliers do, according to Julien?
They shouldn’t read the slides.
3. What should they always say at the end?
Their recommendation, clearly.
