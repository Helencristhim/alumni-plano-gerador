Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
The Office (U.S.) — Season 2, any episode

A documentary record of an office that no longer exists: the desks, the printer, the meeting that exists because it has always existed. Watch it as an archive rather than as a comedy and half the jokes turn into the text you read in this lesson.

Tip: after one episode, name three habits in it that have disappeared from your own work, using used to.

Watch on Netflix →
Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English — “How reading shapes your brain”

Six minutes, two British speakers, normal speed, on a habit that changes the person who has it. It is the other half of this lesson: you spent an hour on habits that ended, and this is about one that lasts. Free transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen once with no text, then say three sentences about how you used to read and how you read now.

Listen on BBC Learning English →
Talk
Matt Mullenweg — “Why working from home is good for business” (TED)

Recorded before the change everybody lived through, by somebody whose company had already done it. Worth watching for that reason alone: it is an argument made when it was still an argument, and you can hear which parts turned out to be right.

Tip: stop halfway and say what you would ask him now that nobody could have asked him then.
