The Core

The Due Diligence Call

One subject, three follow-ups. You cannot change the subject, and every answer starts with a short verb.

  • Round 1: the escalation. Three follow-ups on the same question.
  • Round 2: the trap. I ask with the wrong word order; you repeat it correctly, then answer.
  • Round 3: swap. You are the buyer.
