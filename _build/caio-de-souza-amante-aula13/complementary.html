Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Coaching for Leaders, Episode 681: “The Way to Handle Q&A” (with Matt Abrahams)

How to prepare for the questions you know are coming, and how to answer them in a short structure instead of a long story. It is exactly the skill of this lesson: the first sentence decides how the rest of the answer is heard.

Tip: about forty minutes. Listen for bring up, follow up and figure out, and use each one in a sentence about a hard question a buyer could ask you.

Listen on Coaching for Leaders ↗
Talk
Daniel Levitin: “How to stay calm when you know you’ll be stressed”

A neuroscientist explains why stress makes clear thinking difficult, and why the time to prepare for a hard moment is before it happens. Due diligence is a very good place to use his idea.

Tip: twelve minutes. After watching, list three hard questions about Dataside and the one sentence you would open each answer with.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Steve Jobs answers a hostile question (WWDC 1997)

In front of a full room, a developer insults him. Watch how long he waits before he answers, how calm the first sentence is, and how he takes the question seriously instead of fighting it.

Tip: five minutes. Watch the first thirty seconds of his answer three times and copy the pause before he speaks.
