Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TED -- Elizabeth Lyle, "How to break bad management habits before they reach the next generation of leaders"

The other side of today’s lesson. She is talking to the person asking the questions, not the person answering them, and what she describes is exactly the room you are learning to survive: one where nobody feels safe hesitating.

Tip: watch it once and then ask yourself one honest question out loud, in English. When somebody on your own team hesitates in a meeting, what do you do with the silence?

Watch on TED ↗
Series
Succession -- any scene where somebody is questioned in front of the family

The show is built out of the exact moment you practised today: a question arriving with no warning, in front of people who matter. Watch what the ones who survive do in the first two seconds. Almost none of them answer.

Tip: pause right after the question lands and say out loud what you would say to buy four seconds. Then let it play and compare.

Watch on HBO Max ↗
Podcast
NPR Planet Money -- "What's It Worth To You?"

Listen to it this time for the interviews rather than the economics. Every few minutes somebody is asked something they were not expecting, and you can hear exactly what they say while they think. It is the technique of this lesson, unrehearsed.

Tip: the transcript is on the page. Find three moments where somebody repeats the question back before answering it, and count the seconds it bought them.
