Chapter 3: Hearing Numbers

The Pair That Costs You Money

-teen at the end · -ty at the start

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"It is thirteen pounds fifty."

"It is thirty pounds fifty."

"That is fifteen ninety-nine."

"That is fifty ninety-nine."

You hearThe strong part isIt means
thirTEEN, fourTEEN, fifTEENat the END, with a clear T13, 14, 15
THIRty, FORty, FIFtyat the START, T sounds like D30, 40, 50
"fifteen ninety-nine"two numbers, no and£15.99
"forty-two eighty"pounds first, then pence£42.80

The rule that saves you: -teen pushes the voice to the END, -ty keeps it at the START. And when in doubt, you never guess — you ask.

Comprehension

What Did Nick Say?

1. What was the total, and how did Nick help her get it?
Forty-two eighty. He wrote it down: four, two, point eight, zero.
2. What does he say about paying by card?
Either is fine, but card is quicker.
3. What was wrong with the change, and what did he do?
He gave six twenty instead of seven twenty, so he gave one more pound.
