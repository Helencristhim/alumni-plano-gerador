Lesson 2 -- Experience Counts

Podcast & Reading
"How to tell a good story: 8 tips for captivating your audience" -- Life Kit, NPR

Twenty minutes on how to turn something that happened to you into something worth listening to. Connection to Lesson 2: at the convention you have five minutes to talk about one client you have worked with for years. This is how you keep a room awake while you do it. Full transcript on the page.

Tip: read the transcript and pick the one tip you would use in your five minutes. Then say your own client story out loud, once, applying only that tip. Send the page to your Kindle for the flight.

Talk
"Why the best hire might not have the perfect resume" -- Regina Hartley, TED

Ten minutes about track records that do not look impressive on paper and turn out to be the strongest ones in the room. Connection to Lesson 2: every sentence in this talk is about what people have done, which is exactly the tense you practiced today.

Tip: count how many times she uses have or has plus a verb in the first three minutes. You will be surprised. Then say three sentences about your own career using the same structure.

Grammar Video
"Present perfect with 'for' and 'since'" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the exact pair that decides the answer to "how long have you been here?". Connection to Lesson 2: for gives the length of the period, since gives the point where it started, and both need have plus the third form.

Tip: pause after each example and say the same sentence about yourself, once with for and once with since. If one of the two feels harder, that is the one to repeat.
