Lesson 19 -- The Second Interview

Grammar Video
"How to use reported speech" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

A short grammar episode on reporting what other people said, with the changes in tense and modal verbs. Connection to Lesson 19: it is the structure you use when the chief executive asks what you heard in round one.

Tip: pause after each example, say what the person originally said, then report it again with the modal one step back.

Talk
"10 ways to have a better conversation" -- Celeste Headlee, TED

A radio host on listening well, and on why most of us listen to reply instead of listening to understand. Connection to Lesson 19: you can only report accurately what you listened to carefully.

Tip: choose one of her ten rules and say, out loud, how you would use it on the call with the headhunter after round one.

Talk
"How to speak so that people want to listen" -- Julian Treasure, TED

A sound expert on the habits that make people stop trusting what we say, gossip and exaggeration among them. Connection to Lesson 19: secondhand information said as fact sounds like gossip; reported and checked, it sounds like preparation.

Tip: write down the seven habits he lists, and mark the one you would most want to avoid when you report what you were told.
