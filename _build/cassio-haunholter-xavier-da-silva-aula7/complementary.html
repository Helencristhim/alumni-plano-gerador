LeeAnn Renninger: The secret to giving great feedback

A cognitive psychologist gives a four-step formula for feedback: the micro-yes, the data point, the impact, and the question at the end. That closing question is a model of how to invite a concern from a client politely.

Tip: copy her four steps and use them to write one sentence of feedback about your own last design review.

The Problem With Hyper Real Renders
