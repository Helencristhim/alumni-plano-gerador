Documentary
Greenwashing: The Climate Killer (2023)

An investigation into companies whose carbon neutrality claims rest on offset projects that do not hold up. It is the other side of the file on your desk, and it is the reason the rule is written the way it is.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down every sentence where somebody says what a company is required to do, and every sentence where somebody says what it merely chose to do. That is this lesson in the wild.

Podcast
Law, Policy & Markets -- How to Improve Carbon Credit Markets: "Trust but Verify"

A lawyer and a policy specialist on what separates a voluntary market from a compliance market, and what the new rules actually require. This is your vocabulary, spoken by people who use it professionally.

Tip: listen once for the argument. The second time, count the modals: every time you hear must, have to, may or should, note what kind of rule it was carrying.

TED Countdown: Is there a role for carbon credits in the transition to a fair, net-zero future?

Scientists, executives, activists and community leaders arguing about the same question, in the same room. Useful for the ear: the accents are many and the register changes every few minutes.

Tip: pick one speaker you find hard to follow and replay that section three times. It is the most efficient listening practice there is, and it costs ten minutes.
