Grammar Video
"The zero conditional" -- BBC Learning English, Conditionals with Georgie

Four minutes on the exact structure of this lesson: if plus present simple, present simple, for facts and for things that are always true. Connection to Lesson 11: this is the sentence shape a guide uses all day, and the one word you must not add is "will".

Tip: after each example, say the same sentence about a building you know. Then say it again with "will" and hear how it stops being a general truth and becomes a promise about tomorrow.

Podcast
"99% Vernacular: Volume 1" -- 99% Invisible

Three ordinary building types -- the Dingbat facade, the A-frame and the Southern front porch -- told as social history rather than as engineering. Connection to Lesson 11: an hour of people describing buildings out loud, with no photograph, which is exactly the skill of this lesson.

Tip: choose the segment you like most and listen to it twice. The second time, write down every word they use for a surface, a shape or a colour. That list is your next eight words.

Travel Series
"Croatia: Adriatic Delights" -- Rick Steves' Europe, Season 6

Twenty-five minutes along the coast you are visiting in August, from the walls of Dubrovnik to the Roman palace in the middle of Split. Connection to Lesson 11: a professional describing buildings out loud for the whole episode, and almost every sentence is a general truth about a place.

Tip: watch with the sound on and the subtitles off. Every time he describes a facade, a column or a dome, pause and repeat his sentence. Then say your own version about a building in your city.
