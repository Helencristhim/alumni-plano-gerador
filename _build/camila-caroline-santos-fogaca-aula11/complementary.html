The Company Men (2010)

A film about a large company that cuts costs, and about the managers who have to explain the decisions to the people who stay and to the people who go.

Tip: listen for how each manager states the decision in one sentence before anything else. Compare it with the rule first, then the exception.

HBR IdeaCast

Short interviews from Harvard Business Review with leaders and researchers. Many episodes discuss cost decisions, workforce planning and how managers communicate difficult news.

Tip: choose an episode about cost cutting or workforce planning and note every sentence in the present simple that states a rule or a policy.
