Grammar Discovery

Done, or not yet?

The organizers have just announced the date.
Renato has already repaired the ding.
He hasn't watched the replay yet.
Have you seen the countdown yet?

What do the orange words have in common? Where do just, already and yet go?

present perfect: have / has + past participle

just = a very short time ago · already = done, maybe sooner than expected · yet = until now, in negatives and questions. just and already go before the participle; yet goes at the end.

The Code

Just, already, yet

just: a short time ago

I've just bought a leash.
Dana has just called.

already: done

I've already repaired it.
Kai has already booked the van.

yet: not done, or a question

I haven't packed yet.
Has Jess confirmed yet?

I / you / we / they: have ('ve) · he / she / it: has ('s) · short answer: Not yet.

Sound Check

Small 've

I've just bought a new leash.

"I've" is one sound: aiv-JUST

I've already repaired the ding.

the strong part is in the middle: al-RED-y

Have you seen the countdown yet?

your voice goes up at the end
