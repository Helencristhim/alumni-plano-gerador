Stage 1.1: Dragon Words

Vocabulary

Tap Listen. Say the word out loud. Do the action with your body!

Wings -- the two big parts a bird or a dragon uses to fly
"The dragon opens his wings."
Claws -- the sharp, hard nails on the feet of an animal
"His claws are very sharp."
Tail -- the long part that grows at the back of an animal
"Storm hits the rock with his tail."
Scales -- the small hard pieces that cover the skin of a fish or a dragon
"His scales are red and hot."
Cave -- a big dark hole inside a mountain or a rock
"The dragon sleeps in a cave."
Brave -- not afraid to do something difficult or scary
"Be brave. Climb on his back!"
Breathe fire -- to send hot flames out of your mouth
"Storm can breathe fire!"
Ride -- to sit on an animal or a bike and travel with it
"I can ride a dragon."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the story. Then answer the questions.

Storm lives in a dark cave in the mountain. He is a big red dragon. His scales are hot and his claws are very sharp. Every morning he opens his wings and he flies over the river. Storm can fly very fast and he can breathe fire, but he can't swim. Nina is his rider. She is small, but she is very brave. She can ride Storm with no rope!
What can Storm do?
A He can fly and breathe fire.
B He can swim and breathe fire.
Which sentence is TRUE about Nina?
A She is big and she can't ride Storm.
B She is small, but she is very brave.
Why does Storm stay away from the river?
A Because he can't fly.
B Because he can't swim.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

can / can't + verb

Use can to say what someone is able to do. Use can't to say what someone is not able to do.

+   Storm can fly.
-   Storm can't swim.
?   Can Storm fly?  --  Yes, he can. / No, he can't.
Put two ideas together with but:   He can fly, but he can't swim.

Careful: the verb after can never changes. Not can to fly. Not cans fly. Always can fly.

Stage 4: What Do You Say?

Quiz
Your friend asks: "Can your dragon swim?" Storm can't swim. What do you say?
A "No, he don't swim."
B "No, he can't. He can fly, but he can't swim."
A new rider is afraid of the dragon. What do you say to help?
A "Be brave! He is friendly."
B "Be scary! He is friendly."
You want to know about your friend's dragon. What do you ask?
A "What your dragon can do?"
B "What can your dragon do?"

Stage 5: Your Own Dragon

Reflection
Invent your own dragon. What is his name? What can he do? What can't he do? Record your answer -- try to say three sentences!
Example: "My dragon's name is Rocket. He is blue and he has big wings. He can fly very fast and he can jump, but he can't breathe fire. I am brave, so I can ride him!"
