Can & Can't

Look at the orange words

Storm can fly over the mountain.
Storm can breathe fire.
But Storm can't swim.

Storm + can / can't + fly

The verb after can never changes. No to. No s.

Dragon Trap

Find the mistake

One word is wrong. Tap to see the fix.

Storm can to fly over the mountain.
Storm can fly over the mountain.
My dragon cans breathe fire.
My dragon can breathe fire.
He can't to swim in the river.
He can't swim in the river.
Practice

Make the sentence

A baby dragon + NOT + breathe fire
A baby dragon can't breathe fire.
Quick Game

Can it, or can't it?

Two sentences. Use but in the middle.

A fish -- swim? fly?
A fish can swim, but it can't fly.
A robot -- walk? sleep?
A robot can walk, but it can't sleep.
A car -- go fast? fly?
A car can go fast, but it can't fly.
