Talk
TED -- Dan Gilbert, "The psychology of your future self" (7 min)

A Harvard psychologist on why the person you will be in ten years is almost never the person you predict today. Seven minutes, and it is the most useful argument against writing a goals essay full of predictions.

Tip: after the talk, say out loud which line of your own road map is a prediction and which one is a decision. The two need different verbs.

Video
The B1M -- "The $135BN Bullet Train That Could Save Australia"

A construction project described entirely in the future: what is due to start, what is only proposed, and what will have changed by a given year. Your industry, and every future form from this lesson used the way a professional uses them.

Tip: pause whenever a date is named and decide which form the narrator used, and why that one. There are at least four different ones in the video.

Podcast
How I Built This (NPR) -- "Planet: Will Marshall and Robbie Schingler"

Two founders describing a plan that changed direction several times without ever losing its horizon. Long-form spoken American English, and a whole hour of people talking about what they were going to do and what they ended up doing instead.

Tip: listen for the moment they describe a pivot. Then say, in one sentence, what your own company would keep if you pivoted.
