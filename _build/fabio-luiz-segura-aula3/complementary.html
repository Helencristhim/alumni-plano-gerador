Nancy Duarte — “The secret structure of great talks” (TEDx)

Eighteen minutes on the same idea this lesson is built around: a presentation has a shape, and once you know the shape you stop improvising. She takes two famous talks apart and shows the identical skeleton underneath.

Tip: map her shape onto your own five sentences — country, bank, division, problem, ask. Where does your version go flat?
