Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TED -- Julian Treasure, "How to speak so that people want to listen"

A sound expert on the voice of a person the room listens to: slow, calm and firm. It is the voice you need in the five seconds after an interruption, when the room decides if you are still the one leading.

Tip: watch the part about the voice tools. Then record one sentence from today, like "I'll come back to that", slowly and with the tone going down at the end.

Video
Stanford GSB -- Matt Abrahams, "Think Fast, Talk Smart: Communication Techniques"

A Stanford lecturer on speaking when you did not prepare: a question in the middle of your talk, a comment you did not expect. His advice is close to today’s lesson: stay calm, answer short, and get back to your structure.

Tip: watch the first twenty minutes. Every time he gives an idea, say how you will use it in your next presentation, starting with "I'll...".

Watch on YouTube ↗
Podcast
NPR Life Kit -- "How to not get interrupted at work"

A short, practical episode about being heard in meetings: what to do when somebody talks over you, and how to keep your point in the room without getting angry. Calm and firm, the two words from today.

Tip: the transcript is on the page. Find two sentences people use to keep the floor, and say them again in your own words.
