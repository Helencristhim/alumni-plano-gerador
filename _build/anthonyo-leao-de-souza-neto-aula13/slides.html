Chapter 3: Went, Had, Saw

No -ED. A New Face

go → went · have → had · see → saw

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I went on a tour of the old city."

"We had lunch by the river."

"I saw the view from the castle."

"She took a hundred photos."

NowBeforeExample
go · have · seewent · had · sawI went to the beach · we had lunch
take · eat · buy · gettook · ate · bought · gotI took photos · I bought a souvenir
EverybodySame form for every person: I went, she went, they went. No S, no -ED.
How to learn themThere is no rule. Learn them like new words, in pairs: go – went. These seven cover almost every weekend story.

Goed, haved, taked: that mistake means your brain learned the -ED rule. Now it learns the exceptions.

No, and the Question

didn't · Did you...?

"I didn't go to the museum."

didn't + the verb as it is NOW — go, not went

"Did you see the view?"

did first, then the person, then the normal verb

"Yes, I did.  /  No, I didn't."

the short answer — no need to repeat the verb

"What did you do on the weekend?"

the Monday question — and the one you give back

Self-Assessment

What I Can Do

I know went, had, saw, took, ate, bought and got.
I can ask "What did you do on the weekend?"
After did, I use the normal verb: did you go, not did you went.
I can answer "Yes, I did" and "No, I didn't."
I can tell my weekend and ask the question back.
