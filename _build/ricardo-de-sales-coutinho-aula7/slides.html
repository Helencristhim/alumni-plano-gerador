Chapter 3: Do You, Does It

The Same Word, at the Front

do · does

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Do you work with dollars?"

"Do you buy euros too?"

"Does the company need dollars?"

"Does she pay in euros?"

WhoQuestionShort answer
you / we / theyDo you work...?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
he / she / it / the companyDoes she pay...?Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
The -s againIt is already inside does. Never "does she pays".

The question word goes first. The verb stays plain.

Four Answers That Always Work

Yes, I do.

Do you work with dollars?

No, I don't.

Yes, it does.

Does the company import?

No, it doesn't.

Four answers. They cover almost every yes-or-no question in a meeting.
