Chapter 3: The Pattern

Competing or to Compete?

avoid competing · plan to open · telling the story

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Orange Words Do?

"We avoid competing on price with the big houses."

"We plan to open a showroom in New York."

"Telling our story is the key to the brand."

"Olivia recommends testing two taglines first."

Look at the orange words. Which verbs take -ing, and which take to + verb? What is the subject of sentence 3?

FormUseExample
verb + -ingavoid, enjoy, consider, recommend, suggest, keep, riskWe avoid competing on price.
verb + to + verbwant, plan, decide, aim, expect, refuse, needWe plan to open a showroom.
-ing as the subjectThe action is the topic of the sentence.Telling our story is the key.
preposition + -ingAfter in, at, about, before, by, and the to of look forward toI look forward to working with you.
stop + -ing / stop + tostop selling = no more selling · stop to call = pause in order to callWe stopped selling to outlet stores.

The verb in front decides the form that follows. Learn them in pairs: avoid competing, plan to open.

Chapter 4: The Strategy Meeting

The Strategy Meeting

Manhattan, Tuesday morning. Olivia Grant has one question for Milton.

Dialogue

Coffee with Olivia

O
Milton, thanks for flying in. Before we design anything, I need to understand one thing. How do American buyers see your brand today?
M
Honestly? Their perception is nice Brazilian jewelry at a good price. I want to change that.
O
Then the first step is to stop competing on price. Luxury buyers don't want a deal. They want a story.
M
We have a story. My family has been making jewelry for three generations. That heritage is real.
O
Perfect. Telling that story is how we differentiate you from the big houses. What makes the pieces different?
M
The craftsmanship. Every piece is finished by hand in our workshop. We refuse to mass-produce.
O
Good. So I recommend positioning you in a small niche: handmade Brazilian gold for collectors. Exclusivity, not volume.
M
I like that. I would also like to have a tagline that people can remember.
O
We'll get there. First, I suggest testing two ideas with your best clients in New York. Can you send me a list by Friday?
M
Of course. I'm looking forward to seeing your first proposal.
Comprehension

About Olivia

1. According to Olivia, what do luxury buyers want instead of a deal?
A story.
2. What position does Olivia recommend for Milton's brand?
A small niche: handmade Brazilian gold for collectors. Exclusivity, not volume.
3. What does Olivia suggest doing before choosing a tagline?
Testing two ideas with Milton's best clients in New York.
