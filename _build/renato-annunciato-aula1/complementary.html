Complementary Activities

Optional extras for the days between classes. Nothing here is homework — pick what you enjoy and mark it as done. Listening is the muscle we are training, so audio counts double.

Lesson 1 -- The Role You Run

Series
Mindhunter -- Season 1, Episode 1

Two FBI agents build a new unit from nothing, and they spend the whole season explaining to sceptical rooms what their unit is and where its authority stops. It is investigation with a psychological core, which is the kind of story you said you enjoy, and the English is calm, slow and clear.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time a character explains their role to somebody new, write the sentence down. You will collect five versions of the lesson you did today.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart -- Simple Is a Superpower: How to Communicate Any Idea to Any Audience

A free episode from the Stanford business school on the exact problem of today's class: saying what you do in plain words, so that a room full of people from other countries and other areas actually understands it. Around thirty minutes, clear American English, and a transcript on the page if you need it.

Tip: listen once without the transcript, even if you lose parts of it. Then open the transcript and listen again. The gap between the two is exactly what we are training.

Listen on Stanford GSB ↗
YouTube
Julian Treasure -- How to Speak So That People Want to Listen (TED)

Ten minutes on why some people are heard and others are not, and almost none of it is about vocabulary. He works on the voice itself: pace, pitch, silence and weight. For someone who runs meetings every day in one language and is now building the same authority in another, this is the shortest useful thing on the internet.

Tip: watch it once with subtitles on, then once with the subtitles off. Then record yourself saying your three sentences again, using one thing he suggests.
