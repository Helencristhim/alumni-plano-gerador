Chapter 3: The Finish-Line Code

Will Have Done

a finished action · a point in the future

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"By the end of the Miami module, I will have met all the participants."

"By the time we arrive in Zurich, we will have completed two modules."

"By December, my mentor will have read my first assignment."

"I won't have finished the assignment by Friday."

What do the orange words have in common? At that moment in the future, is the action finished or not?

FormStructureExample
Affirmativewill have + past participleI will have met the cohort.
Negativewon't have + past participleI won't have finished it.
QuestionWill + subject + have + past participle?Will you have finished by May?
Time wordsby + time / by the time + present simpleby Friday, by the end of the module, by the time we arrive

Use the future perfect for an action that will be finished before a point in the future. "By" means "not later than". After by the time, use the present simple: "by the time we arrive", not "by the time we will arrive".
