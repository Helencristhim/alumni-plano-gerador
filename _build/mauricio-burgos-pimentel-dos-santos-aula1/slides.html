The Pattern

What comes right after the verb?

I lead a team of eight people.
We handle twelve hundred corporate clients.
I deliver the annual budget.
My team manages the largest accounts.

The verb and its object never separate

Subject → verb → the thing. Everything else (when, where, how) goes at the end. So: I have to deliver the budget — never "I have to the budget deliver".
