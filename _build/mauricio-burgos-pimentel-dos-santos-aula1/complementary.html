Film
The Intern

You said you would watch one film a week — start with this one. A seventy-year-old walks into a company where he knows nobody, and spends the whole film introducing himself, explaining his background and asking people what they do. Clear American English, office register, nothing shouted.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down three ways people introduce themselves in the first twenty minutes — then say your own version out loud. On Netflix Brazil the film is listed under its translated title, so use the link.
