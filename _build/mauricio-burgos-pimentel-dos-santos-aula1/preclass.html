Lesson 01 — Pre-class

So, What Do You Do?

Eight words for your role (role, be responsible for, be in charge of, report to, handle, portfolio, background, based in) and the sentence shape that keeps the verb next to its object. Around 30 minutes, in one sitting.

Stage 1.1: Words for Your Role

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say each word out loud with your own numbers. Eight words, three minutes.

Role — the job you do inside a team or a company
"My role is commercial head of the division."
Be responsible for — to have something as your job or your duty
"I am responsible for the annual budget."
Be in charge of — to be the person who leads something or someone
"I am in charge of a team of eight people."
Report to — to have someone as your direct boss
"I report to the head of the division."
Handle — to take care of something as part of your work
"My team handles the largest accounts."
A portfolio — the group of clients or products you take care of
"I manage a portfolio of twelve hundred clients."
Background — your studies and the jobs you had before this one
"My background is in corporate banking."
Based in — the city or the office where you work
"I am based in São Paulo."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the introduction. Then answer the questions.

My name is Daniel Ferraz and I am based in São Paulo. I work for a bank, in the corporate division. My role is head of credit for large companies, so I am responsible for the risk of every deal we sign. I am also in charge of a team of six analysts. We handle a portfolio of around four hundred clients. I report to the commercial director, and she reports to the board. My background is all in banking — I have never worked anywhere else.
What exactly is Daniel responsible for?
A The whole corporate division.
B The risk of every deal the bank signs.
Who is Daniel’s boss?
A The commercial director.
B The board.
In "we handle a portfolio of four hundred clients", what comes immediately after the verb?
A The thing he handles — the portfolio.
B The number of clients.

Stage 4: In the room

Quiz
Someone at the coffee break asks: "So, what do you do?" Which answer works best?
A "I work in a bank."
B "I run a commercial team at a bank in São Paulo — eight people, twelve hundred clients. And you?"
In the middle of a sentence, the English word does not come. What do you do?
A "Sorry — how do you say this in English?"
B Say the Portuguese word and keep going.
Which sentence is correct?
A "I have to this budget deliver every year."
B "I have to deliver this budget every year."

Stage 5: Your thirty seconds

Reflection
Record thirty seconds in English. Introduce yourself to an executive you have just met at Stanford: your name, the bank, your role, your team and your numbers, and one sentence about why you are there. Do not write a script first — speak. Then listen to your own recording once and note the one thing you would fix.
