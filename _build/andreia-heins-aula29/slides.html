Chapter 3: The Mix

Everything You Know, in One Talk

Five parts, five structures

Discover the Mix

One Talk, Five Jobs

Open: Let me start with a question.

Story: When we started, we had never worked with a robot.

Findings: It seems that the learning curve gets flatter after fifty cases.

Limits: Although our sample size was small, the results were clear.

Close: A small team can do it, provided that the nurses train together.

Click to reveal the map of the talk
A good talk is not one structure: it is a sequence of jobs, and each job has its own language. Open: signposting -- let me / I'd like to + verb, I'll start by + verb-ing. Story: narrative tenses -- we were + verb-ing for the background, we had + past participle for the earlier event. Findings: hedging -- it seems that / tend to / be likely to: strong, but honest. Limits: concession -- although + clause, despite + noun or -ing. Close: a condition -- provided that / as long as + present -- and the bottom line. Q&A: that said / coming back to and indirect questions.
