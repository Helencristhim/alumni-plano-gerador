Chapter 3: How About This?

Making an Offer

Two words that open a negotiation

Discover the Code

Listen and Find the Pattern

"How about forty euros for the bag?"

"What about an exchange instead of a refund?"

"How about taking two for sixty?"

"What about coming back on Friday, when it is on sale?"

FormExampleUse
How about + nounHow about forty euros?offer a thing or a price
What about + nounWhat about an exchange?the same, in other words
How about + verb-ingHow about taking two?offer an action
Saying yesThat works for me.you accept the offer
Saying noI'm afraid I can't do that.you say no, politely

After about comes a noun or a verb with -ing. Never "How about to take two".

Roberto's Two Deals

Read the questions first. Then listen — sound first, no text.

Forty-five each if he took two, and yes, the seller said yes.
