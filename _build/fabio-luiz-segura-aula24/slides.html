Chapter 4: The Milestones

By March, We Will Have Signed

Talking about what will be finished by a future date

Grammar Discovery

Finished Before When?

"By March, the working group will have mapped the main corridors."

"By the end of the second quarter, we will have signed the MOU."

"By the time we meet in Frankfurt, our teams will have tested the first messages."

"Will your legal team have finished the review by June?"

When are these actions finished: before the date or after it? Which words give the date?

UseFormExample
Finished before a future momentwill have + past participleBy March, we will have signed the MOU.
The future momentby + date / by the time + present simpleBy the time we meet, we will have tested it.
Negativewon't have + past participleWe won't have finished the tests by June.
QuestionWill + subject + have + past participleWill you have finished the review by June?
The trapno will after by the timeNot by the time we will meet. Say by the time we meet.

In one line: use will have + past participle for a milestone that will be complete before a date in the future.

Chapter 5: Real Speed

Two Voices Before the Meeting

A veteran of correspondent banking, and a message from Frankfurt

Listening 1 · The Veteran

How She Runs a Bilateral

A correspondent banking executive explains her method. Sound first — no text.

1. What does she send before the meeting, and how many points does it have?
A short agenda, a week before, with five points, never more.
2. How long does she present, and what does she do next?
About three minutes. Then she hands the floor over with a real question, like: how does this look from your side?
3. What does she leave for the working group, and what does she do before anyone leaves?
The commercial details. Before anyone leaves, she reads the next steps out loud, with names and dates.
Listening 2 · The Voicemail

A Message from Frankfurt

The partner bank's legal counsel leaves a voicemail. Sound first — no text.

1. Who is Marco Ferri, and what is the good news?
He is legal counsel at Main River Bank in Frankfurt. His side has reviewed the draft MOU and is happy with the structure.
2. Which two points do they still need to iron out?
The governance of the working group, and who pays for the testing environment.
3. What will they have done by the end of next week, and what does he hope for the Frankfurt meeting?
They will have sent their comments in writing. He hopes both legal teams will have agreed on a final version by then.
