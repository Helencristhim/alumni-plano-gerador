Talk
Margaret Heffernan — “Dare to disagree” (TED)

A business leader shows why the best partners are not the ones who always agree, and how good disagreement makes decisions better. Useful before any negotiation between two institutions.

Tip: write down one sentence about “good conflict”. Think of how you could disagree with a partner bank and still build consensus.

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart (Stanford GSB) — “What’s Normal? How Culture Affects Communication Styles”

A cultural psychologist explains why some cultures prefer direct rules and others prefer flexibility. Very useful for a meeting between a Brazilian bank and a German bank.

Tip: note one difference between a “tight” and a “loose” culture. Which one is closer to your partner, and which one to Bradesco?

Listen on Stanford GSB ↗
Video
Simon Sinek — “How Great Leaders Inspire Action” (TED, YouTube)

A famous talk about starting with “why” before “what”. It is a good model for the first phase of a bilateral: explain the shared problem before the proposal.

Tip: write your own “why” for a partnership between Bradesco and a European bank, in one sentence.
