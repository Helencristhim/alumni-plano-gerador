Chapter 3: The Code

If This, Then That

One structure for predictions

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Show?

"If brands use AI well, customers will get better answers."

"If a company doesn’t protect data, it will lose trust."

"Customers won’t stay if the service is bad."

"What will happen if Google changes the rules?"

Every sentence has two parts: a condition with if, and a result. Look at the blue verbs: after if, the verb is in the present (use, doesn’t protect). The result has will or won’t. The if part can come first or second.

JobFormExample
Conditionif + present simpleIf brands use AI well...
Resultwill / won’t + verb...customers will get better answers.
Orderif first (with a comma) or if secondCustomers won’t stay if the service is bad.
Negative conditionif + don’t / doesn’t + verbIf a company doesn’t protect data...
QuestionWhat will happen if...?What will happen if Google changes the rules?
The trapno will after ifNot if brands will use AI. Say if brands use AI.

In one line: if + present for the condition, will for the result; never will after if.

Grammar Practice

Choose the Right Form

Say it first, then click to check

"If brands ___use personalization, customers will feel special." (use)
"Customers ___won't come back if the chatbot is bad." (not come)
"If we ___don't protect privacy, we will lose loyalty." (not protect)
"What ___will happen if the budget is cut?" (question)
Chapter 4: On the Podcast

On the Podcast

One interview, two voices, one trend report

Dialogue

On the Podcast

J
Welcome to Growth Talk. Today my guest is Marcela, a growth consultant from Brazil. Marcela, what is the biggest trend right now?
M
Thanks, Josh. For me, it is AI search. Many people ask a chatbot now, not Google. If brands don’t prepare for that, they will lose brand awareness.
J
Interesting. I have a small company. What will happen if I do nothing?
M
Honestly? If you do nothing, your competitors will appear in the answers, and you won’t.
J
OK, that is scary. And personalization? Everybody talks about it.
M
It is powerful, but be careful with privacy. If customers feel watched, they won’t trust the brand.
J
So, can you predict one thing for the next five years?
M
One thing: loyalty will be more valuable than traffic. If you keep your customers, you will grow.
