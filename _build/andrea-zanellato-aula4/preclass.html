The Job I Didn’t Try -- A Story in the Past

A good job posting, one requirement in English, and a decision. Key words: a job posting, to apply for, a requirement, fluent, a recruiter, a deadline, to give up, a second chance. Structure: past simple, regular and irregular verbs, did and didn’t.

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Two years ago, Marina saw a job posting for an HR director. She had the experience and she wanted the job, but one requirement was fluent English. She closed the page.

The deadline was Friday. She opened the posting every day, but she didn’t apply. Later, she decided not to give up, and she started English classes.

Last month a recruiter called her. Did she send her CV? Yes, she did. She got an interview and a second chance.

1. Why did Marina close the page?
A Because she did not have the experience for an HR director job in another country.
B Because fluent English was a requirement.
C Because the deadline was over.
2. What happened last month?
A A recruiter called her, and she sent her CV.
B She got the job.
C She saw the same posting again on a Monday morning at work.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A “Marina didn’t applied for the job.”
B “Marina didn’t apply for the job.”
C “Marina not apply for the job two years ago on Friday.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Past Simple -- Regular, Irregular and Did

GRAMMAR

Use the past simple for finished actions. Regular verbs take -ed. Irregular verbs have their own form: see-saw, send-sent, get-got. For negatives and questions, use didn’t and did + the simple verb.

JobFormExample
Regular verbsverb + edwant → wanted · call → called · apply → applied
Irregular verbsa new form, learn it as a groupsee → saw · send → sent · think → thought · get → got
Negativedidn’t + verbI didn’t apply.
QuestionDid + subject + verb?Did you send your CV?
Short answersYes, I did. / No, I didn’t.Did you apply? No, I didn’t.
The trapno past form after didNot I didn’t applied. Say I didn’t apply.

Never: I see the posting yesterday · I didn’t applied · She sended her CV · You called him? (as a question)
