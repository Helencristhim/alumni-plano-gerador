Chapter 3: The Passive

is checked · was sent · is renewed

the action matters -- the person does not

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The documents are checked every year."

"The evidence is kept in the IMS system."

"The report was sent to Geneva on Friday."

"The farm was approved after the audit."

FormUseExample
is / are + past participlea rule; it happens every timeThe documents are checked every year.
was / were + past participleit happened one time, in the pastThe report was sent on Friday.
is not / was not + past participlenegativeThe certificate was not renewed.
by + person (optional)only when you need the personThe farm is audited by an external team.
Question: is / was + subject + participleask about the actionHow often is the farm audited?
I check the documents = the person The documents are checked = the action

In a standard, the person is not important, so the sentence starts with the thing. / Numa norma a pessoa nao importa, entao a frase comeca pela coisa: be + verbo no participio.

Common Mistake

Wrong vs Right

✗ WRONG

"The report was send on Friday."

✓ RIGHT

"The report was sent on Friday."

✗ WRONG

"The documents checked every year."

✓ RIGHT

"The documents are checked every year."

The passive always needs two parts: be + past participle. / A passiva sempre pede duas partes: o verbo be + o participio.

Scope: the coffee area only. Audit date: June 15.

Dialogue

Emma Explains the New Standard

E
Hi Carlos. The new standard is published, and it starts in June. Did you read it?
C
Yes, Emma. I read the first part, but some sentences are difficult for me.
E
Which ones?
C
For example: "Records are kept for three years." Who keeps them? The sentence does not say.
E
That is normal in a standard. The action is important, not the person. The farms keep the records.
C
I understand now. And the audit? How often is a farm audited?
E
Every farm is audited every twelve months. The certificate is renewed after a good audit.
C
And the evidence?
E
Evidence is requested for every criterion. Please send me your evidence folder by Friday.
C
Of course, Emma. I am going to send it tomorrow.

The evidence folder.
