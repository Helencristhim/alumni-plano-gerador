Lesson 11 — Pre-class

Sorry, the Tenth?

Four new words for checking — catch, confirm, note down, summarize. And one thing only: how to check one number or date you did not hear well, without stopping the call. Around ten minutes.

Stage 1: Four new words

Vocabulary

The four words of this lesson. Read each one and say it out loud.

Catch — to hear and understand something as it goes past
"Sorry, I didn't catch the last part."
Confirm — to check that what you understood is right
"Just to confirm: the tenth, not the twentieth."
Note down — to write something quickly, while it is being said
"Let me note down the reference number."
Summarize — to say the long thing again, but short
"Let me summarize what we agreed, in three lines."

Stage 3: One fact, voice up

Grammar

One thing this week. When you miss a number or a date, do not ask for everything again. Repeat only the fact, with your voice going up: Forty-eight? · The tenth? When you want a clear yes, add a short tag: positive sentence, negative tag — Ralph signs it, doesn't he? If the tag is hard in the moment, is that right? works after anything.

He said a number, and you think it was 48. What do you say?
A Can you repeat everything, please?
B Forty-eight?
"Ralph signs the contract, ______ ?"
A doesn't he
B isn't he
"You said Tuesday, ______ ?"
A did you
B didn't you
You are not sure about the date. Which one keeps the call moving?
A The tenth, is that right?
B Sorry, I did not understand anything.

Stage 5: Say it out loud

Speaking

Three lines. Let your voice go up at the end of the first two. Listen first, then record yourself.

Stage 6: Before class

Speaking
Think of the last call or meeting where you missed a number or a date. Record three sentences: the fact you check with your voice up, one line with is that right?, and one short summary. One sentence each — no more.
