Lesson 25 -- Pre-class

A Trip to Remember -- Irregular Past and the Past Perfect

Ten to twenty minutes before class. How to tell a trip with irregular past verbs, and how to say what you had never done before. Key words: a layover, to land, jet lag, a conference, sightseeing, a delay.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A layover -- a stop between two flights
"I had a four-hour layover in Miami."
To land -- to arrive on the ground, for a plane
"We landed in Atlanta at six in the morning."
Jet lag -- the tired feeling after a long flight to a different time zone
"I had jet lag for two days."
A conference -- a big meeting with talks, usually for a few days
"The conference had more than two thousand people."
Sightseeing -- the activity of visiting famous places in a city
"We did some sightseeing on the last day."
A delay -- the time that something is late
"There was a three-hour delay because of the snow."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Marco works for a bank in Lima. Last spring he flew to Toronto for a meeting. He had never been to Canada before, and he had never seen so much snow in April.

When he got to the office, the meeting had already started. He sat at the back, took notes and spoke at the end. His director said it was the best part of the meeting.

"He had never been to Canada before." What does it mean?
A He went to Canada many times.
B Before that trip, he was never in Canada.
C He is going to Canada in the future.
When Marco got to the office, what was the situation?
A The meeting was cancelled.
B The meeting started before he arrived.
C The meeting started after he arrived.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Past and the Past Before the Past

GRAMMAR

The story: the past simple. Something that happened before that moment: had + third form.

JobFormExample
The storypast simple (went, took, saw)I went to Chicago in May.
Before that momenthad + third formI had never been there before.
Already finished beforehad already + third formThe session had already started.

Never: I have went · We taked a taxi · I never had been there before

Stage 3: Pronunciation

Speaking

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer a real speaker would give.

You want to say it was your first time in Chile. You say:
A "I never had been to Chile before."
B "I have never been to Chile before that."
C "I had never been to Chile before."
You took a taxi from the airport. You say:
A "I took a taxi."
B "I taked a taxi."
C "I have took a taxi."
You arrived late, and the meeting was in the middle. You say:
A "The meeting already started."
B "The meeting had already started when I arrived."
C "The meeting has already started when I arrived."

Reflection
