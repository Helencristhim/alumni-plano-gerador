Model

A Trip I Will Never Forget

Two years ago, I went to Chicago for a conference. I had never been to the United States for work before, so I was a little nervous.

I took a night flight from Brazil and slept very little. When I got to the hotel, the first session had already started, so I left my bag and ran to the conference center.

In the afternoon, I met a group of analysts from Canada. We had lunch together and spoke about credit models for two hours. I had never seen a city with so many tall buildings, and I had never eaten a hot dog with so many things on it.

That trip taught me one thing: I can do my job in English.
