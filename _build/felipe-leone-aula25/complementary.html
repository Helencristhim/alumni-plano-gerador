Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Pico Iyer: “Where is home?” (14 min)

A travel writer who has lived in many countries talks about travel, home and what a trip changes in a person.

Tip: write down five irregular past verbs he uses and say a sentence of yours with each one.

Watch on TED ↗
TEDx Talk
Rick Steves: “The value of travel” (TEDxRainier)

A famous American travel guide on what travel really gives you: a new way to see the world and the people in it.

Tip: pause after one of his stories and tell it again in one minute, in the past.

Watch on YouTube ↗
TED Talk
Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie: “The danger of a single story” (19 min)

A writer on what happens when we only know one story about a country or a people.

Tip: say in two sentences what you had never understood about a country before you went there.
