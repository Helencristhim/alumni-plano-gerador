Talk
How to speak so that people want to listen

Sound consultant Julian Treasure explains the habits that make people stop listening, and the vocal tools that make them pay attention. It is the closest thing there is to a manual for the first three minutes of an international kick-off call.

Tip: note the four cornerstones he calls HAIL, then use them the next time you say who you are and what you do.

6 Minute English -- Chatting to new people

Two presenters talk to a social psychologist about why we dread speaking to strangers, and why those conversations almost always go better than we expect. The vocabulary is exactly the vocabulary of a first call with a new team.

Tip: read the transcript after the first listening and mark every phrase you could use to open a call.

How to Succeed in Your New Job -- The Way We Work, a TED series

A short, practical episode on the first weeks in a new role: what to ask, what to say about yourself, and how to explain what you are working on right now. Perfect for practising the permanent role against the current project.

Tip: pause at each tip and say your own version out loud, starting with I manage and then At the moment I am working on.
