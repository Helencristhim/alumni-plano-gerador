Chapter 3: The Little Question at the End

It's a Long Day, Isn't It?

The question that invites the other person to answer

Grammar Discovery

The Question at the End

"You're based in Mexico City, aren't you?"

"It's been a long close, hasn't it?"

"We met at the Toronto meeting, didn't we?"

"You don't use the same ERP, do you?"

What do the orange words have in common? When the sentence is positive, what happens at the end? And when it is negative?

TypeThe ruleExample
positive sentencenegative tagIt's a big event, isn't it?
negative sentencepositive tagYou haven't presented here before, have you?
with an auxiliary (be, have, can, will)repeat the same auxiliaryWe can share the slides, can't we?
no auxiliary (present / past simple)use do, does or didYour team closes on day five, doesn't it?
the subjectalways a pronoun in the tagDiego works in Mexico, doesn't he?
intonationfalling = you expect agreement · rising = you really checksmall talk: falling · a real doubt: rising

In one line: find the auxiliary, flip positive and negative, and end with a pronoun.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

The Coffee Break in Toronto

One conversation, two voices, and the agenda of the day

Dialogue

You Start, He Follows

K
Excuse me, you're Diego from the Mexico team, aren't you? I'm Keitma, from R2R in São Paulo.
D
That's right! Nice to finally meet you. We're on the same closing call every month, aren't we?
K
We are, but I usually just listen. Today I want to speak up more. How was your flight?
D
Long, but fine. Mexico City to Toronto is easy. You didn't fly in this morning, did you?
K
No, last night. So your team closes on day five too, doesn't it? I think we have some common ground there.
D
Exactly. And the BPO topic this afternoon is a hard one. Are you going to chip in?
K
Actually, I volunteered to present our reconciliation process. My manager told me to put myself forward.
D
That's great. If you want, I can jump in after you and build on it with the Mexico numbers.
Comprehension

About Diego

1. Which team is Diego from?
The Mexico team.
2. How does Diego describe his flight?
Long, but fine: Mexico City to Toronto is easy.
3. What does Diego offer to do after Keitma's presentation?
To jump in and build on it with the Mexico numbers.
