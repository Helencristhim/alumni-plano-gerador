Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto em voz alta, devagar, e depois responda as perguntas.

Here is an idea for next year. Carlos and his wife are going to take a vacation in March. They are going to take a flight to Miami.

Carlos is going to rent a car. Then they are going to start a road trip along the coast, with a paper map.

They are not going to take a big suitcase. Is Carlos going to do the talking? Yes, he is!

1. Quando é a viagem no texto? (When is the trip?)
A Em março do ano que vem. (In March, next year.)
B No Natal. (At Christmas.)
C Hoje. (Today.)
2. "They are going to take a flight" fala de: (talks about:)
A Algo que aconteceu. (Something in the past.)
B Um plano para o futuro. (A plan for the future.)
C Algo que está acontecendo agora. (Something happening now.)
3. Como se diz "Nós não vamos levar uma mala grande" em inglês?
A "We not going to take a big suitcase."
B "We are not going to take a big suitcase."
C "We are going not take a big suitcase."

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Be going to (planos)

GRAMMAR

Para planos já decididos: am / is / are + going to + verbo. É o nosso "vou fazer", "vamos viajar". / For plans: am / is / are + going to + verb.

Uso / UseEstrutura / PatternExemplo / Example
Eu / II am going to + verboI am going to rent a car. (Vou alugar um carro.)
Ele, ela / He, sheis going to + verboShe is going to take the photos. (Ela vai tirar as fotos.)
Nós, eles / We, theyare going to + verboWe are going to travel in March. (Vamos viajar em março.)
Negativa / Negativeam / is / are not going to + verboWe are not going to visit Disney. (Não vamos visitar a Disney.)
Pergunta / QuestionAre you going to + verbo?Are you going to drive? (Você vai dirigir?)
Ideia-chave / Key idea"Vou viajar" = I am going to travel. O am/is/are não some ("I going to" está errado) e o verbo depois de going to fica na forma básica (going to rent, nunca "going to rents"). / Never drop am / is / are, and use the base verb after going to.
