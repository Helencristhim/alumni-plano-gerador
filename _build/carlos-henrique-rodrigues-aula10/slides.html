Chapter 4: The Code

Going To

Plans and decisions for the future

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"We are going to take a flight to Miami."

"Carlos is going to rent a car."

"They are not going to visit Disney."

"Are you going to drive?"

Last class: I am returning (now). These sentences are about next year. Look at the blue words: am / is / are + going to + what?

JobFormExample
A plan, with II am going to + verbI am going to rent a car.
A plan, with he / sheis going to + verbShe is going to take one suitcase.
A plan, with we / you / theyare going to + verbWe are going to travel in March.
Negativeam / is / are not going to + verbWe are not going to visit Disney.
QuestionAm / Is / Are + person + going to + verb?Are you going to drive?
Short answersYes, I am. / No, we are not.Are you going to rent a car? Yes, I am.

In one line: for a plan: am / is / are + going to + verb (the base form: going to rent, never going to rents).
