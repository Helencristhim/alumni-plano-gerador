Movie
WALL·E — Disney+

A robot who does the same job every single day, alone, for hundreds of years: he lifts, he carries, he fixes, he never stops. The first forty minutes have almost no words, so you understand everything from the pictures — and then the English starts.

Tip: watch in English. After twenty minutes, say three things WALL·E does every day. Don't forget the -s.

Podcast
Who decides what robots look like? — But Why

A free podcast made for curious kids. In this episode they visit a real factory with hundreds of robots, and two engineers answer children's questions: why robots are made of metal, and whether robots cry.

Tip: listen in the car. Afterwards, tell somebody one thing the factory robots do every day.

Video
Atlas Gets a Grip — Boston Dynamics

A real robot, not a cartoon. Atlas picks up a heavy bag of tools, carries it across a building site and throws it up to a worker. Every verb from this lesson happens on screen in two minutes.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, say what the robot does out loud, while it does it: "He lifts the bag. He carries it."
