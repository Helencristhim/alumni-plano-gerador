He Works, She Checks

What is different?

I work at night.
He works at night.
You check the battery.
She checks the battery.
We lift the boxes.
It lifts the boxes.
They carry the boxes.
Bolt carries the boxes.

He / She / It + verb + S

Only he, she and it. Never I, you, we or they.

carry → carries  ·  fix → fixes  ·  go → goes

Listening 2

The broken arm

1. Which arm has the problem?
The left arm.
2. How does the arm sound?

3. How many boxes does he carry now, and how many normally?
Now: one box every two minutes. Normally: one box every ten seconds.
