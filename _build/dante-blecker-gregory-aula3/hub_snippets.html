Stage 1.1: Factory Words

Vocabulary

Tap Listen. Say the word out loud. Do the action with your body!

Check -- to look at something carefully to see if it is right or safe
"He checks the machines every night."
Carry -- to hold something and take it to another place
"She carries the boxes to the truck."
Lift -- to move something up, usually something heavy
"The robot lifts a car with one arm."
Fix -- to repair something that is broken
"The engineer fixes the arm at four o'clock."
Battery -- the part that gives electric power to a machine
"Nina checks the battery every morning."
Machine -- a thing with motors that does a job for people
"There are nine machines on this floor."
Shift -- the hours when a person or a machine works
"Bolt works the night shift."
Heavy -- difficult to lift because it weighs a lot
"These boxes are too heavy for me."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read about Bolt's night. Then answer the questions.

Bolt is a robot and he works the night shift. He starts at ten o'clock. First he checks the nine machines on the first floor. Then he carries the heavy boxes to the truck. He lifts two hundred kilos with one arm. He never stops and he never sleeps. At six in the morning he goes to the charging room. Nina arrives at seven. She checks his battery and she writes the report. When something is broken, the engineer fixes it.
What does Bolt do first?
A He checks the machines.
B He carries the boxes.
Who checks the battery?
A The engineer checks it.
B Nina checks it.
Which sentence is correct?
A Bolt never stop and he never sleep.
B Bolt never stops and he never sleeps.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

present simple: the letter S

Use the present simple for things that happen every day. After he, she and it, add -s to the verb.

no S   I / you / we / they  work at night.
+ S   he / she / it  works at night.
-   He doesn't work in the morning. (the S goes to does)
?   Does he work at night?  --  Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.

Three special ones

carry → carries fix → fixes go → goes

Careful: never I works, and never he work.

Stage 5: Somebody in Your House

Reflection
Choose one person in your house: your mother, your father, or somebody else. What does that person do every day? Record your answer -- try three sentences, and don't forget the -s.
Example: "My mother wakes up at six. She makes breakfast and she goes to work at eight. In the evening she watches TV with me. She never plays video games."

Lesson 3 — Robot Factory

Movie
WALL·E — Disney+

A robot who does the same job every single day, alone, for hundreds of years: he lifts, he carries, he fixes, he never stops. The first forty minutes have almost no words, so you understand everything from the pictures — and then the English starts.

Tip: watch in English. After twenty minutes, say three things WALL·E does every day. Don't forget the -s.
