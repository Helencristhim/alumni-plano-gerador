Talk
"10 ways to have a better conversation" -- Celeste Headlee, TED

A radio interviewer on what makes a conversation real: be brief, do not repeat yourself, and listen. Connection to Lesson 20: her rules are the difference between a back-and-forth and a long-winded exam answer.

Tip: choose one of her ten rules and write one sentence about how you would use it in a second interview.

Grammar Video
"Short Answers in English" -- Rebecca, engVid

A short class on answering with the auxiliary only: Yes, I have. No, I didn’t. Connection to Lesson 20: this is the first move of every answer in the second interview.

Tip: pause after each example and give your own short answer about your career before she does.

"Better Ways to Say Yes and No in English" -- Speak Confident English

Natural alternatives to a flat yes or no, including I think so and I’m afraid not. Connection to Lesson 20: these are the answers that replace a whole clause and keep the conversation moving.

Tip: write down three expressions you do not use yet and say each one in an answer about your work.
