Chapter 3

The Modal Code

Must, have to, don’t have to, should, might

Grammar Discovery

Rule, Choice, Tip or Maybe?

"You must wear your badge at all times."

"You have to connect to the VPN first."

"You don’t have to use a company laptop."

"You should save your work on the shared drive."

"The Wi-Fi might be slow in the afternoon."

Which ones are rules? Which one is your choice? Which one is a tip? Which one is a “maybe”?

UseFormExample
A rule (mandatory)must / have to + verbYou must wear your badge.
Not necessary (optional)don’t / doesn’t have to + verbYou don’t have to use a company laptop.
Not allowedmustn’t + verbYou mustn’t share your credentials.
Adviceshould / shouldn’t + verbYou should save your work on the shared drive.
Possibility (maybe)might + verbThe Wi-Fi might be slow.

Modal + base verb: never “must to”, never “should to”. Have to is the only one with “to”.

Careful

Mustn’t or Don’t Have To?

mustn’t = not allowed

You mustn’t share your password.
You mustn’t change the system on Saturday.
Visitors mustn’t enter the server room.

don’t have to = not necessary

You don’t have to come on Saturday.
You don’t have to wear a suit.
She doesn’t have to use a company laptop.

"You don’t have to come to the office on Saturday, but you mustn’t change anything in the system."

Chapter 4

The Workshop

Nine o’clock, Tuesday. The test system will not open.

Dialogue

Can I Use Mike’s Login?

G
Good morning, Daniela. Is everything okay? You look a little worried.
D
My laptop cannot open the test system. It asks for a code on my phone.
G
That is our security policy. Everyone has to confirm the login on the phone. It is mandatory.
D
I see. Can I use Mike’s login this morning? We must start the tests at nine.
G
Sorry, you mustn’t share credentials here. It is a compliance rule, and the auditors check it every month.
D
Of course. I don’t want to create a risk for the project. So what should I do?
G
You should call Karen from IT security. It might take ten minutes, but you don’t have to wait here. Start with the test plan.
D
Good idea. At SPF we have a similar guideline, but it is not as strict as yours!
Comprehension

About Greg

1. According to Greg, what does everyone have to do?
Confirm the login on the phone. It is mandatory.
2. Why does Greg say no to using Mike’s login?
Because you mustn’t share credentials: it is a compliance rule, and the auditors check it every month.
3. What is Greg’s advice?
Call Karen from IT security, and start with the test plan.
