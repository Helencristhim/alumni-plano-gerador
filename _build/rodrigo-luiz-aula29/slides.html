Chapter 3: The Pattern

Open, Sequence, Close

Today I'm going to · First · Next · To sum up

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Today I'm going to talk about three KPIs.
First, delivery. Next, cost.
As you can see on this chart, delivery increased.
To sum up, it was a good quarter.

Which words OPEN, which words MOVE to the next point, which words SHOW, and which words CLOSE?

UseFormExample
OpenToday I'm going to talk about...Today I'm going to talk about three KPIs.
SequenceFirst... / Next... / Finally...First, delivery. Next, cost. Finally, safety.
ShowAs you can see on this chart...As you can see on this chart, the cost decreased.
Numbersincreased / decreased + to or byDelivery increased to 96%. The cost decreased by 3%.
CloseTo sum up... / Any questions?To sum up, it was a good quarter. I'm happy to answer any questions.
CarefulTO = the final number (increased to 96%). BY = the difference (increased by 3%). And it is "To sum up", never "For sum up".

Chapter 4: The Forum

Brazil Has the Floor

five minutes at the global forum

Dialogue

Five Minutes at the Forum

C
Good morning, everybody. Now it's Brazil. Rodrigo, the floor is yours.
R
Thank you, Claire. Today I'm going to talk about three KPIs: delivery, cost and safety.
C
Perfect. Please start.
R
First, delivery. As you can see on this chart, on-time delivery increased to ninety-six percent.
C
A great trend. And the cost?
R
Next, cost. The cost per kilo decreased by three percent, because of the new supplier.
C
Very good. And safety?
R
Finally, safety: zero accidents this quarter. To sum up: better delivery, lower cost, and a safe plant.
C
Thank you, Rodrigo. Clear and short. Any questions for Brazil?
R
Thank you. I'm happy to answer any questions.
Comprehension

About Claire

1. How does Claire start Brazil's turn?
She says: Rodrigo, the floor is yours.
2. What does Claire say about the delivery?
It is a great trend.
3. What does Claire say at the end?
That it was clear and short, and she asks for questions.
