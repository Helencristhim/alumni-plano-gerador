Chapter 3: The Code

One Letter Changes Everything

I work · she works

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I work with public schools."

"I evaluate educational platforms."

"He manages the research group."

"She teaches in a public school."

WhoVerbExample
I / you / we / theyworkI work with schools.
he / she / itworksShe works with schools.
he / she / itteachesHe teaches education.
he / she / itmanagesShe manages the team.

After ch, sh, s and ge, the ending is -es: teaches, manages.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"She teach in a public school."
"She teaches in a public school."
"I works with schools."
"I work with schools."
"He manage to evaluate platforms."
"He evaluates platforms."

Only he, she and it take the s. With I, the verb stays clean.
