Lesson 2 -- My Life, My Story

Series
Somebody Feed Phil — Netflix

Pick any episode and watch the first ten minutes: Phil lands, gets through the airport and tells you what happened. Almost every sentence he says about the journey is in the past simple.

Tip: write down five past simple verbs you hear in the first ten minutes. Three regular, two irregular — the irregular ones are the ones worth memorising.

Listening
"The art of conversation" — 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on what actually keeps a conversation alive. Useful the moment you have to answer more than one question at a counter.

Tip: listen twice. The second time, stop the audio whenever you hear a verb in the past and say the base form out loud.

Watch on YouTube →
Grammar
"The past simple tense" — 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

The whole rule in six minutes: regular -ed, irregular verbs, and the negative and question forms with did.

Tip: after the video, tell the story of your last trip out loud in six sentences. Count how many irregular verbs you needed — those are your list for the week.
