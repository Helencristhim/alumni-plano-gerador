Chapter 3: The Code

The Small Verb

I am, you are, it is

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I am the technology director."

"I am not from New York."

"Are you the partner in São Paulo?"

"Is your team on the call?"

Look at the words in blue. They are all the same verb. What changes: the person, or the verb?

JobFormExample
II am / I’mI am Ender. I’m from Brazil.

Chapter 4: On the Call

On the Call

One conversation, two recordings, one invitation

Dialogue

Is Everybody Here?

S
Good morning, everyone. I am Sarah Miller, from Mastercard in New York. Is everybody here?
E
Good morning, Sarah. I am Ender, from Brazil. I am the technology director.
S
Nice to meet you, Ender. Are you our partner in São Paulo?
E
Yes, I am. My company is in São Paulo. We are a payments company.
S
Great. Is your team on the call too?
E
No, they are not. They are not online now.
S
No problem. Is it eight a.m. in São Paulo?
E
No, it is nine a.m. here. Is it eight in New York?
S
Yes, it is. Okay, welcome to the call, Ender!
Comprehension

About Sarah

1. Where is Sarah from?
From Mastercard, in New York.
2. What does Sarah ask about the team?
If the team is on the call.
3. What time is it in New York, according to Sarah?
It is eight a.m.
