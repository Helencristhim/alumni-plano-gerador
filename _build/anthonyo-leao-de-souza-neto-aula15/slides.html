Chapter 3: Need and Want

One for the Problem, One for You

I need to · I want to · do you want to...?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I need to call my wife."

"I want to eat something."

"She needs to change her flight."

"Do you want to get a coffee?"

UseFormExample
It is necessaryneed to + verbI need to call the hotel.
It is your wishwant to + verbI want to sleep.
He / Sheneeds to / wants toShe needs to change her flight. He wants to go home.
Nodon't / doesn't + need to / want toI don't need to hurry. She doesn't want to wait.
QuestionDo you want to...? · Do you need to...?Do you want to get a coffee? — Yes, I do. / No, thanks.

Here the TO stays: need to call, want to eat. Remember can (lesson 14)? Can has NO to. Need and want DO.

Can or Need?

With To or Without?

"I can call my wife."

CAN (lesson 14): no TO, and the same for everybody

"I need to call my wife."

NEED: with TO — and the TO is weak, almost "NEED-tuh"

"She wants to go home."

WANT with she: an S, like in lesson 9 — WANTS-tuh

"Do you want to get a coffee?"

the question that starts a conversation at the gate — fast it sounds "wanna"
