Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 9 -- What Was Happening When It All Changed

Series
The Crown -- Season 1, Episode 1

One hour about the week everything changed for one family and one organisation. Every scene is people reacting to news they did not choose. The English is slow and very clear, and it is a different accent from the American you usually hear.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down five sentences with was or were plus -ing.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
TED Talks Daily -- any recent episode

Short talks, one a day, many of them about change: companies, careers and what people did when the ground moved. Ten to fifteen minutes is the size that survives a busy week.

Tip: listen at 0.75x the first time. Choose one episode and listen to it twice in the same week.

Listen on Spotify ↗
YouTube
Kelly McGonigal -- "How to make stress your friend" (TED)

Fourteen minutes about what happens to people under pressure -- which is exactly what you were managing during the transition. Clear American English at a comfortable speed.

Tip: watch once without subtitles, then once with English subtitles. Note three sentences in the past continuous.
