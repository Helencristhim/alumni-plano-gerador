On track -- going as planned, with no delay
"The new farms are on track."
Behind schedule -- later than the plan said
"Last year, the irrigation work was behind schedule."
Overview -- a short description of the main parts of something
"Let me give you a quick overview of the contract."
Trend -- the general direction in which something is changing
"The trend of our sales to Chicago is positive."

"The key takeaway for the directors: Picunha is ready."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Five Tools From Lessons 25 to 29

GRAMMAR

One meeting, five tools: a plan, a deadline, a signpost, a link and how long.

ToolStructureExample
Plans (L25)going to + verb (plan) · will + verb (decision now, promise)We are going to plant two farms. I’ll call you.
Deadlines (L26)by (not later than) · until (all the time up to)Send it by Friday. Wait until Monday.
Signposts (L27)to begin with · moving on to · finally · to sum upTo sum up, Picunha is ready.
Links (L28)so / as a result (result) · however / although (contrast)Although it rained, we finished.
How long (L29)have/has + participle + for (length) / since (start)We have worked together since 2025.

Never: will to send · send it until Friday (use by) · although ... but · we work together since 2025 (use have worked)
