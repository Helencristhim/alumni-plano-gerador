Chapter 4: The Code

One Story, Many Tenses

Then, until now, and next

Grammar Discovery

Then, Until Now, Next

"I moved to Jacksonville in 2019."

"At parties, I used to hide in the kitchen."

"Since that day, I have taken a risk every week."

"He told me that my English was much better."

"Next month, I am going to celebrate six years in Florida."

Which sentence is about a finished moment? An old habit? The past until now? What someone said? A plan?

TimeStructureExample
A finished moment (in 2019, last year)past simpleI moved here in 2019.
An old habit, not true nowused to + verbI used to hide in the kitchen.
From the past until now (for, since, ever)have / has + past participleI have lived here for six years.
What another person saidtold me that / said that + tense backShe told me that it was easy.
A plan for the futurebe going to + verbI am going to take a course.

Ask yourself first: WHEN? Then choose the structure.

Detective

Spot the Error

0 / 4 errors found

"I have moved to Florida in 2019."
"I moved to Florida in 2019." — a finished time (in 2019) takes the past simple
"I use to hide in the kitchen at parties."
"I used to hide in the kitchen at parties." — an old habit: used to
"I live here since 2020."
"I have lived here since 2020." — since = from the past until now
"She told me that the test is easy."
"She told me that the test was easy." — told me that: the tense goes back
