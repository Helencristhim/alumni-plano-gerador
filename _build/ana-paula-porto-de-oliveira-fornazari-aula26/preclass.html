Lesson 26 -- Pre-class

Finding Your Voice -- Fluency and Confidence

The last lesson of the program: your story with every tense. Key words: proud, nervous, a mistake, to make progress, to take a risk, a comfort zone, to give up, to keep going, to celebrate, a recording. Structure: review of the past simple, used to, the present perfect, reported speech and going to.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Proud -- feeling happy because you, or someone you love, did something well
"My parents are very proud of me."
Nervous -- a little afraid and worried before something important
"She gets nervous before exams."
A mistake -- something you do wrong, without wanting to
"I made a mistake in the email."
To make progress -- to get better, little by little
"My son is making progress in math."
To take a risk -- to do something when you are not sure it will go well
"He took a risk and opened a restaurant."
A comfort zone -- the situations where you feel safe and relaxed
"Traveling alone took me out of my comfort zone."
To give up -- to stop trying
"The test was hard, but she did not give up."
To keep going -- to continue, even when it is difficult
"We were tired, but we kept going."
To celebrate -- to do something special because something good happened
"We celebrated my birthday at the beach."
A recording -- sound that you save so you can listen to it again
"I listened to a recording of my voice."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Carlos is a chef from Salvador. He moved to Miami in 2018. In Brazil, he used to cook for his whole family every Sunday. Now he works in a small restaurant, and he has worked there for five years.

At first, Carlos was nervous with American customers. Then one day, a customer told him that his moqueca was the best dish in Miami. He felt proud for the first time.

Next year, Carlos is going to open his own restaurant. It is a big risk, but he is not going to give up.

1. What did Carlos use to do in Brazil?
A He used to work as a chef in a big hotel in Salvador.
B He used to cook for his family on Sundays.
C He used to sell fish.
2. What did the customer tell him?
A That his moqueca was the best dish in Miami.
B That the restaurant was too expensive and too far from the beach.
C That he was nervous.
3. Which sentence is about a plan?
A He has worked there for five years.
B He moved to Miami in 2018.
C He is going to open his own restaurant.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- One Story, Many Tenses

GRAMMAR

First ask WHEN. Then choose the structure.

TimeStructureExample
A finished moment (in 2019, last year)past simpleI moved here in 2019.
An old habit, not true nowused to + verbI used to hide in the kitchen.
From the past until now (for, since, ever)have / has + past participleI have lived here for six years.
What another person saidtold me that / said that + tense backShe told me that it was easy.
A plan for the futurebe going to + verbI am going to take a course.

Never: I have moved here in 2019 · I live here since 2020 · I use to hide · she told me that it is easy (yesterday)
