Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TEDx -- Marianna Pascal, "Learning a language? Speak it like you're playing a video game" (17 min)

An English teacher explains why adults stop talking in a new language: they are afraid of mistakes. Clear, friendly English, and a message for the last lesson of your program.

Tip: write down one thing she said, and tell it to a friend in reported speech: she said that...

Talk
TED -- Carol Dweck, "The power of believing that you can improve" (10 min)

A psychologist talks about the power of 'not yet': you have not learned something yet, but you are making progress. Slow, clear English.

Tip: make three sentences about your English with not yet and already.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
TED -- Amy Cuddy, "Your body language may shape who you are" (21 min)

A famous talk about confidence: how your body can help you feel strong before a difficult moment, like an exam or a conversation in English.

Tip: watch the first ten minutes with English subtitles, then try her idea before your next conversation in English.
