TED -- Celeste Headlee, "10 ways to have a better conversation" (11 min)

A radio host on listening for what is actually being said rather than waiting to speak. Several of her ten points are exactly the habit this lesson trains: asking the question that gets a real answer instead of a polite one.

Tip: eleven minutes, fast but clear. Pick the one rule you break most often and use it in your next call in English.

Shark Tank US -- "Negotiating With Barbara Isn’t Easy!"

Unscripted American business English at speed, and a masterclass in hedged refusal: watch how often somebody declines without ever saying the word no, and how the other side hears it immediately.

Tip: watch once for the deal and once with a pen. Write down every phrase used to decline without refusing.

Podcast
How I Built This -- Airbnb: Joe Gebbia

Years of meetings in which almost everybody said no, told by somebody who had to learn to hear it early. The rejections in this story are all polite, which is what makes it useful listening for this lesson.

Tip: listen to any twenty minutes. Note two moments where somebody declines without using the word.
