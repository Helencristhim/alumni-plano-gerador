Lesson 02 -- Pre-class

The Three Companies: Bank, Finance and Rental

An illustrated glossary of the automotive finance business, with audio: what each company does, who the clients are and the number that measures the whole system. Key words: captive finance, retail financing, dealer network, floor plan, fleet rental, leasing, loan portfolio, penetration rate. Structure: finance, financing and financial.

Stage 1: Start with the Text

GRAMMAR

Start with the text. Read it once before you look at anything else: the language of this lesson comes out of it.

Every car that leaves a showroom carries two decisions: who sells it and who pays for it. The group sells the car through its dealer network, and a captive finance company pays for it, first for the dealer and then for the driver.

For the dealer, the product is the floor plan. The cars in the showroom were produced and delivered long before any customer arrived, and somebody has to finance them while they wait. For the driver, the product is retail financing: a loan for the person who takes the car home. The two products have the same name in Portuguese conversation and very different names in an English meeting.

The rental company sits next to them. Its clients are companies with fleets, and its products are fleet rental and leasing. The idea is simple: the client uses the car and gives it back. The group takes care of the rest.

All these loans together form the loan portfolio, and the share of the cars sold that the group itself finances is the penetration rate. When the financial results are presented, these two words appear in almost every slide, and they are not interchangeable: the portfolio is the size, the rate is the share.

A last point about form. Finance is the action and the area. Financing is the activity as a thing: retail financing grew, fleet financing was stable. Financial describes another word: financial results, financial services, financial institution. Three forms, one family, and a mistake in the middle of a sentence is enough to make a clear idea sound uncertain.

Vocabulary

captive finance -- a finance company owned by the manufacturer, which finances its own products

retail financing -- the loans given to the final customer who buys a car

leasing -- paying to use a car for a period, with the option to buy it or return it

fleet rental -- renting a large number of cars to companies, not to individuals

"The dealer network is the main channel of the business."

Stage 4: Grammar Tip -- finance / financing / financial, lease / leasing / leased

GRAMMAR

The same family, three jobs in the sentence. The action, the activity and the description are different words, and English does not accept them in the wrong place.

FormWhat it doesExample
finance (verb)the action: somebody pays for somethingWe finance four out of ten cars sold.
financing (noun)the activity as a thing you can measureRetail financing grew last year.
financial (adjective)describes another nounThe financial results, a financial institution.
lease / leasing / leasedthe same three jobs in the rental businessThe client leases a car; leasing grew; the leased cars come back.
Watch outusing the -ing form as a description. "The financing results" and "the leasing cars" are the two mistakes a native ear hears immediately.

Quick test: before a noun, use financial. Alone, as the thing itself, use financing. With a subject doing it, use finance.
