Chapter 4: The Code

Finance, Financing, Financial

The word family of the business you work in

Grammar Discovery

Which Form Goes Where?

"We finance the cars that the dealers sell."

"Retail financing grew in Brazil last year."

"The financial results come out in March."

"The client wants to lease the car for three years."

Two of these words are actions. One describes another word. One is the name of an activity. Which is which?

FormWhat it doesExample
finance (verb)the action of giving money for somethingWe finance about forty per cent of the cars sold.
financing (noun)the activity itself, as a thing you can count and compareRetail financing grew. Fleet financing was stable.
financial (adjective)describes another noun: results, services, statementThe financial results, a financial institution.
lease / leasing / leasedthe same three forms in the rental businessThe client leases a car. Leasing grew. The leased cars come back after three years.
Watch outthe adjective. "The financing results" and "a finance institution" sound wrong to a native ear. Use financial before a noun.

Two Messages, One Structure

A colleague in Italy and a short internal briefing

Listening 1

A Message from a Colleague in Italy

Sofia, HR business partner in Italy, leaves a voice message. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. Why is Sofia calling Camila?
She has a call with the global team on Friday and she has to present the South American structure.
2. What does Sofia find confusing?
The difference between the bank, the finance company and the rental company, because in Italy it is one entity.
3. What exactly does she ask Camila to send, and when?
Three lines, one for each company, saying what it does and who its clients are, before Thursday evening.
Listening 2

The Two-Minute Business Briefing

An internal briefing for new joiners. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. What does the finance company do for the dealer, and what does it do for the customer?
It finances the cars in the showroom through the floor plan, and it gives retail financing to the customer who buys the car.
2. What is the penetration rate mentioned, and what does it mean?
Close to forty per cent: four out of ten cars sold are financed by the group.
3. Who are the clients of the rental company?
Companies with large fleets, not individual drivers.
