Chapter 3: The Pattern

The Process, Not the Person

is made · are tested · is shipped

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

The formula is created in the lab.
Every batch is tested in the quality lab.
The ingredients are weighed and mixed.
The flavor is shipped to the customer.

WHO does the action? Is it important? Look at is / are and the verb after it.

UseFormExample
Process: one thingit is + past participleThe flavor is made in Brazil.
Process: many thingsthey are + past participleThe ingredients are weighed.
Who? Not importantthe action is more important than the personEvery batch is tested (by the lab team).
QuestionHow is it made? / Is it tested?How is it made? — It is mixed in tanks.
CarefulThe verb is the PARTICIPLE: "it is make" is wrong, "it is made" is right. And is/are cannot disappear: "it made in Brazil" is wrong.

Chapter 4: The Tour

A Visitor at the Plant

a customer asks how it is made

Dialogue

The Tour with Laura

L
Rodrigo, thank you for the tour. Can you explain how a flavor is made here?
R
Of course. First, the formula is created by our team in the lab.
L
And the ingredients? Where do they come from?
R
Most of them are imported. Every ingredient is checked when it arrives.
L
Interesting. What happens next?
R
The ingredients are weighed very carefully, and then they are mixed in these tanks.
L
Is every batch tested?
R
Yes. Every batch is tested in the quality lab. If it is OK, it is approved.
L
And after that?
R
The flavor is packed, it is stored in the warehouse, and it is shipped to the customer.
