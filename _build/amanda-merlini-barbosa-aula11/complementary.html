Film
Erin Brockovich (2000)

An environmental case against a large company, built from documents, settlements and what each side agreed to do. The legal English is real, but the dialogue is fast and natural.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time somebody refuses, agrees, promises or avoids something, pause and say the sentence again with the right form of the second verb.
