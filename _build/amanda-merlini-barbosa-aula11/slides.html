Chapter 3: Before He Signs

Twelve Pages, Two Minutes

Rafael asks what the clause really says

Dialogue

What the Clause Says

R
I saw the buyer's draft. Before I read twelve pages, what are they asking us to do?
A
Mainly three things. We undertake to deliver a monitoring report within sixty days of each verification, and we commit to keeping the registry up to date.
R
Sixty days is fine. What else?
A
We have to refrain from selling the same credits to anybody else until the transfer is complete.
R
That worries me. What if a better buyer appears next month?
A
Then we would be in breach. I recommend accepting it, because after paying they are entitled to some certainty.
R
Can we postpone signing until the spring? The market may go up.
A
I would avoid postponing it. They are already seeking other projects, and they have agreed to link the payment to the issuance.
R
And if they object to our price review clause?
A
Then I suggest waiving the review for the first year only. We keep it for the years after that.
Comprehension

About Rafael

1. What worries Rafael about the exclusivity point?
That a better buyer might appear next month, and they could not sell to it.
2. What does Rafael want to do with the signature, and why?
He wants to postpone signing until the spring, because the market may go up.
3. Which clause is Rafael afraid the buyer will object to?
Their price review clause.
Chapter 4: The Code

Doing or To Do

the verb that comes after the verb

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"We undertake to deliver the report within sixty days."

"I would avoid postponing the signature."

"We must refrain from selling the same credits."

"They are entitled to terminate the agreement."

Which verbs are followed by -ing, and which by to? What always comes after from?

PatternVerbsExample
verb + -ingavoid, consider, recommend, suggest, postpone, delay, risk, involveI recommend accepting the clause.
verb + toagree, undertake, seek, refuse, fail, decide, intend, be entitledThey agreed to link the payment.
preposition + -ingrefrain from, commit to, object to, look forward toWe must refrain from selling them.
verb + somebody + towant, ask, require, expect, would likeThey want us to deliver it in sixty days.
The to testIf you can put a noun after to (object to the price), to is a preposition: use -ing. If you cannot, it is the infinitive: entitled to terminate.

Sound Check

Say It Clearly

the -ing ending and the weak to · the sentences of this lesson

Why this one is hard for a Portuguese speaker

The habit: adding a vowel after -ing, so that selling sounds like selling-ee, or opening it into an -in sound.

In English: -ing ends in a nasal sound at the back of the mouth, with no vowel after it. And to before a verb is weak and short: the stress goes on the verb, never on to.

The test: say "refrain from selling the credits" without any sound between selling and the.

Chapter 5: Real Speed

The Voicemail and the Trap

What the other side wants, and three verbs that change the fact

Listening 1

The Draft, Explained

The buyer's lawyer leaves a voicemail. Sound first, no text.

1. What has the buyer agreed to?
To link the payment to the date the credits are issued. They have stopped insisting on a calendar date.
2. Why do they want the monitoring report within sixty days?
Because they want to avoid paying for credits that nobody has checked.
3. Which point is sensitive, and what does she suggest?
Refraining from selling the same credits to anybody else until the transfer is complete. She suggests having a call on Tuesday.
Listening 2

Three Verbs, Two Facts

A senior lawyer on stop, remember and try. Sound first, no text.

1. What is the difference between "we stopped negotiating" and "we stopped to negotiate"?
The first means the negotiation ended. The second means they interrupted something else in order to negotiate.
2. What does "I remembered to send the notice" say?
That there was a duty to send it, and the person did not forget it.
3. What does he advise you to do?
When stop, remember or try appears in a statement you are going to sign, read the sentence twice and ask which fact it states.

Clause 7

Clause 7 · Delivery, Exclusivity and Payment · sample data
7.1 ReportingThe Seller undertakes to deliver a monitoring report within 60 days of each verification.
7.2 ExclusivityThe Seller shall refrain from offering or selling the Contracted Credits to any third party until the transfer is complete.
7.3 PaymentThe Buyer agrees to pay within 30 days of the issuance of the Contracted Credits.
7.4 Price reviewThe Parties commit to reviewing the price after the first monitoring period.
7.5 TerminationThe Buyer shall be entitled to terminate this Agreement if the Seller fails to transfer the credits within 15 days of issuance.
Sample data for this lesson. Nothing here is a real contract.
1. Find one verb + to and one preposition + -ing in the clause.
Verb + to: undertakes to deliver, agrees to pay, entitled to terminate. Preposition + -ing: refrain from offering, commit to reviewing.
2. When is the buyer entitled to terminate the agreement?
If the seller fails to transfer the credits within 15 days of issuance.
3. Which line will Rafael want to discuss, and why?
7.2, exclusivity: he is worried that a better buyer might appear before the transfer is complete.
