Talk
"Inside the mind of a master procrastinator" -- Tim Urban (TED2016)

A free talk of about fourteen minutes about what happens between a deadline and the day the work actually starts, and why a project with no date in the middle is the most dangerous kind. Connection to Lesson 19: it is the argument for milestones, told as a joke you will not forget.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time he mentions a deadline, pause and say one sentence about your own week with "by" and one with "until".

Podcast
"How to better manage your time" -- Life Kit, NPR

A free episode of about twenty minutes in clear American English, with the full text on the same page. The guest is Oliver Burkeman, and his point is the one the committee makes you face: you cannot commit to everything, so the honest move is to choose what you will not do. Connection to Lesson 19: it is the reasoning behind the date you refuse to promise.

Tip: listen once without reading. Then write three commitments for next week, one with will, one with going to and one in the present continuous, and say each of them out loud.

Video
"Present continuous and going to" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

A short free class on exactly the choice you practiced today: the plan you already decided (going to) against the arrangement already in your diary (present continuous). Connection to Lesson 19: it drills the two forms a committee reads as commitment, and the moment each one is the wrong answer.

Tip: pause after each example and say the same idea about your own quarter. If the date is in your diary, the present continuous should come out first.
