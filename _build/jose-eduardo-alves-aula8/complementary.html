Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Documentary
FRONTLINE (PBS) — "Trump's Trade War" (full documentary)

A negotiation between two countries told the way a negotiation actually goes: while one side was preparing an offer, the other was already announcing something else. Tariffs, deadlocks and the weeks when nothing moved.

Tip: turn the subtitles on and write down five sentences that put two things at the same moment. They will all have was or were plus -ing.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Podcast
Freakonomics Radio — "Get Your Share of the Pie"

A Yale professor who negotiates for a living, telling real deals from the inside: what each side was doing while the other side was waiting, and the moment somebody finally moved. Negotiation as a story, not as a theory.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Pick one story he tells and retell it out loud in six sentences, in the past.

Listen on Freakonomics ↗
Talk
William Ury — "The walk from 'no' to 'yes'" (TED)

The author of the most quoted negotiation book in the world, telling how deadlocks break. His central idea is the third side: while two people are arguing, somebody else is the reason they stop. That is the shape of the story you read today.

Tip: watch it once with subtitles. Then answer, out loud: in your last deadlock, who was the third side, and what were they doing?
