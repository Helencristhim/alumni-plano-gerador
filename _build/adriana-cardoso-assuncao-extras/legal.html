Legal English for HR

The Brazilian labour code has no twin in English, and a literal translation is usually wrong. What works is a short, correct English term plus one line that says what it is. That is what this tab gives you: the term, and the sentence you can actually say to the global legal team.

Why this tab exists. In your class on 18 September you stopped at one word, and it was the right word to stop at. The English column below is not a dictionary: it is what a lawyer in Seattle will understand on the first try.

4 · Explaining the code to someone who has never heard of it

You have had this conversation before, in Portuguese and with a board in Germany. These are the six lines that carry it in English. Say each one out loud once.

  • Brazil has a single labour code that applies to almost every employee. It is called the CLT.
  • The CEO here is not a statutory officer. He is an employee, so the code applies to him too.
  • The pay rise was not a management decision. It was negotiated by the union and it is an obligation.
  • If we do not apply it, the company is in breach, and the difference is claimed later with interest.
  • We can take him out of the code and make him a statutory officer, but that costs more, not less.
  • What the company writes in a policy can be used in a hearing, in our favour or against us.

7 · Three questions from a real room

Quiz
Your global counsel asks what a "passivo trabalhista" is. Which answer works best on the first try?
A It is a labour passive of the company.
B It is the liabilities we may have to pay if an employee wins a claim.
C It is a Brazilian law about employees.
Which sentence tells a foreign board why the CEO gets the union pay rise?
A He is an employee under the labour code, so the rise is an obligation, not a decision.
B He is the president, so he receives the rise of the union.
C The union decided that he must receive a rise this year.
A manager wants to write "employees must report any concern" in the policy. What is the legal problem?
A Nothing. Reporting is always an obligation.
B Nobody can be obliged to report, so that line has to become may.
C It should be should, because reporting is a recommendation.
