Lesson 7 -- What They Actually Said

Talk
Margaret Heffernan -- "Dare to disagree"

A talk about what happens in the room when nobody says what they think, and about the person who wrote down what everybody else preferred to forget.

Tip: report three of her claims out loud in English, using a different reporting verb each time: she said, she argued, she warned.

Video
BBC Learning English -- English at Work, Episode 1: The Interview

A scene in a British office, broken down line by line afterwards. Useful here for a different reason: the narrator spends the whole episode reporting what the characters said.

Tip: pause every time the narrator reports a line and predict the tense he is going to use before he says it.

Series
Succession -- Season 1, Episode 1 "Celebration"

An hour of people reporting to each other what somebody else said, and of decisions that depend entirely on who repeats what. Fast, corporate, and full of reporting verbs.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down five sentences that begin with "he said", "she told me" or "they asked". Say them again out loud.
