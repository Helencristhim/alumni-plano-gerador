Chapter 3: The Code

One Step Back

Statements, questions and instructions, all reported

Grammar Discovery

What Happened to the Verb?

"The budget is approved."  →  He said the budget was approved.
"We have finished the training."  →  She said they had finished the training.
"Have you considered April?"  →  He asked whether we had considered April.
"Send the list today."  →  He told me to send the list that day.
Click to reveal the rule
Reported speech: the verb moves one tense back. Present → past (is → was), past and present perfect → past perfect (finished / have finished → had finished), will → would, can → could. Time words move too: today → that day, yesterday → the day before, next week → the following week. Questions lose the inversion and the question mark, and take whether / if when there is no question word — exactly like the indirect questions of Lesson 5. Instructions become told somebody to + verb. And the difference that matters in writing: say takes no person (he said that…), tell always does (he told me that…).

Chapter 4: The Escalation

When It Goes on Record

Listening 1

The Meeting You Missed

Read the questions, then listen.

1. What did the finance director say, and why was it a surprise?

2. What did the head of operations ask, and what was the answer?

3. What was said off the record, and why does it matter?
The operations director had already decided to leave the company — so he should not be made the owner of the initiative.
Artefact

The Minutes

REGIONAL PEOPLE COMMITTEE

MINUTES — 14 MARCH

1. Finance confirmed that the budget had already been approved.

2. Operations said they could not release people in March, and asked whether April had been considered.

3. The VP told the committee to appoint one owner, not three.

Action item: A. Cardoso to circulate the owner list by Friday.

CONFIRMED · SAID · ASKED · TOLD

Debrief, twenty minutes after the meeting

What Goes in the Minutes

M
You were not there. Give me the short version before I read the minutes.
A
Finance said the budget had already been approved, which nobody in the room knew. Operations said they could not release people in March.
M
Did anybody propose an alternative?
A
Operations asked whether we had considered April. Nobody answered, so we left the meeting in a deadlock on the date.
M
And the owner? I told them I wanted one name.
A
That is the part I want to talk about before I write anything. Somebody told me, off the record, that the person we were about to name has already decided to leave.
M
Then that does not go in the minutes. But the decision does.
A
Agreed. Let me recap what I am writing: the budget was confirmed, the date is open, and the owner will be named by Friday.
Comprehension

About Michael

1. What does he ask for at the beginning?
The short version of the meeting, before he reads the minutes.
2. What had he told the committee about the owner?
That he wanted one name, not three.
3. What does he decide about the off-the-record information?
That it does not go in the minutes, but the decision does.
