Lesson 2 -- Reading the Other Side

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Client management"

Five minutes on the language people use about the companies they work with: who the client is, what they expect, and how the relationship is described out loud. The script is on the same page, and it is full of the long sentences this lesson took apart.

Tip: read the script and underline every that, which, who and where. For each one, ask yourself whether you could remove the clause and still know which company or which person is meant.

Video
Y Combinator -- "How To Talk To Users" (Startup School, 22 min)

Eric Migicovsky on what to find out about a company before you try to sell it anything, and on the difference between what a company writes about itself and what is actually true. Clear American English, slides on screen, and every example is a real conversation.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and stop at minute eight. Write down two questions he suggests, and rewrite each one so it fits a call with a European ERP partner.

Documentary
Explained (Vox) -- any episode, 18 minutes each

Every episode is one subject explained in long, well-built sentences at natural speed — exactly the register of a company page, but spoken. It is the fastest way to hear relative clauses used the way native speakers actually use them, several times a minute.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Pause five times and write the sentence on screen. Mark whether each relative clause has commas, and say why.
