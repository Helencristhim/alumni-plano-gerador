Talk
Dan Gilbert — “The psychology of your future self” (TED)

Six minutes, and it is the exact study from the second recording of this lesson, told by the researcher who ran it. Nineteen thousand people, all convinced they had just finished changing. Short, funny and hard to argue with.

Tip: after watching, write three sentences about what you used to be certain of and are not certain of now. Then predict one thing that will change by 2028 and keep the note.

Radio
99% Invisible — “Exit Interview with Michael Bierut”

A designer at the end of a forty-year career, looking back on all of it out loud. It is an hour of exactly what you practised today: what he used to do, what he did not use to be able to do, and what he never got the hang of.

Tip: write down five of his used to sentences word for word. Then write the same five about yourself, changing only the content.

Video
Angela Duckworth — “Grit: the power of passion and perseverance” (TED)

Six minutes from a researcher on why staying with something is a better predictor than talent. It is the counterweight to the first recording of this lesson: getting the hang of something almost always takes longer than the person expected.

Tip: think of one thing you fell out of the habit of and one you did not. Say out loud, in English, what was different about the second one.
