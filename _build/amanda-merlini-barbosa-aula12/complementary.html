Dark Waters (2019)

A corporate lawyer takes on an environmental case against a chemical company. Meetings, depositions and settlements, full of people looking into things, ruling things out and turning offers down.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down every phrasal verb you hear in a meeting scene, then say the formal verb a contract would use.
