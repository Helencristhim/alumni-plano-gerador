Lesson 10 -- Milestone Review

Series
The Lincoln Lawyer -- Season 1, Episode 1 ("He Rides Again")

Mickey Haller spends the whole episode choosing shapes: an obligation for a client who wants to know what he must do, a flat report of what the file says, a conditional when somebody asks how bad it is. Watch a lawyer who never explains more than the moment needs.

Tip: pick three answers he gives and label each one -- modal, reporting verb, conditional or passive. That is the map of Lesson 10, in the wild.

Podcast
Rough Translation (NPR) -- "How to Speak Bad English"

A communication specialist spent years teaching people to sound native, and then changed her mind. Her argument is the same one you heard in this lesson: in an international meeting, the clearest speaker wins, and clarity has nothing to do with accent.

Tip: listen once for the argument, then a second time and note every moment where somebody is understood better because they said less.

YouTube
TED -- Alan Siegel: "Let's simplify legal jargon!"

Five minutes on what happens when a legal document is rewritten in plain English: the same obligations, a third of the words, and a reader who finally acts on it. American English, clear and slow enough to shadow.

Tip: take the longest clause you reviewed this week and do what he does -- say it in one sentence, out loud, with a reporting verb at the front.
