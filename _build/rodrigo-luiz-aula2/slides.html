Chapter 3: The Pattern

One Letter Changes Everything

he · she · it

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

I work in operations.
He works in purchasing.
She sends the product to the client.
Our factory starts at seven.

What is different in the last three sentences?

SubjectVerbExample
I / you / we / theyworkI work in operations.
he / she / itworksHe works in purchasing.
my team / the factorystartsOur factory starts at seven. (a fábrica é it)
Ideia-chaveSó he, she e it levam s. Em português cada pessoa muda o verbo; em inglês só uma muda — e por isso ela some.
My, Our, Their

Whose Operation Is It?

"My team is here in Sorocaba."

my — only me

"Our factory makes flavor and fragrance."

our — me and my company

"Their office is in France."

their — the other people
