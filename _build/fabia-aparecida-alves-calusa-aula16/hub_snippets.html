Review — She Said Friday

Nothing new this week. The same four words — requirement, turnaround, quote, deadline — and the same shape: telling your partner what a client said. A new call, a new client. Around ten minutes.

Stage 1: The same four words

Vocabulary

Nothing new here. Read each one and say it out loud. If it comes back on its own, that word is yours.

Requirement — something a client needs and asks for
"Their main requirement is tax filing for three companies."
Turnaround — the time it takes to finish a job and give it back
"With their accountant, the turnaround is three weeks."
Quote — the price you offer for a job, before it starts
"He wants our quote on Monday."
Deadline — the last day something can be done
"The deadline is the end of June."

Stage 3: What he said — one more time

Grammar

No new rule this week. The same shape as lesson 15, now with a male client: he says "it is slow" → he said it was slow; he says "I will call" → he said he would call. He said, or he told me — never "he said me".

Tom: "Our accountant is too slow." → "He told me their accountant ______ too slow."
A was
B will be
Tom: "I will need your quote on Monday." → "He said he ______ need our quote on Monday."
A will
B would
Which sentence is correct?
A He told me the deadline was in June.
B He said me that the deadline was in June.
Your partner asks what the client needs. Which answer can your partner use?
A He talked about taxes, for some companies, I think, more or less.
B He said they needed tax filing for three companies.

Stage 4: Complete the line

Practice

A different client, a different call. In class you will say these lines with nothing on the screen.

"He said their main was tax filing."
"He told me the turnaround three weeks."
"He said he need our quote on Monday."
"He told me the was the end of June."

Stage 5: Say it out loud

Speaking

Three lines. Say "he said" and "he told me" as one block, then the information. Listen first, then record yourself.

He said their main requirement was tax filing for three companies.
He told me their accountant was too slow.
He said he would need our quote on Monday.

Stage 6: Before class

Speaking
Choose one real call with a client of your firm (no name needed). Record three sentences as if you were telling your partner: what the client needed (he said / she said...), one problem they had (he told me...), and one date they gave you (she said she would...). One sentence each — no more. In class you will use this call.
