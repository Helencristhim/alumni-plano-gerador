Grammar Review

Three tools

Boston is quieter than São Paulo.
Ibirapuera is the most beautiful park in the city.
You should come back next year.
You could visit Rio, too.

Which sentence compares two things? Which one is number one? Which ones give advice?

two things: quieter than · the group: the most beautiful · advice: should / could + verb

Two things: -er or more + than. Three or more: the -est or the most. Advice: should (strong idea) or could (soft idea) + the verb, with no to.

The Code

Compare, number one, advice

How it works

big → bigger than → the biggest
quiet → quieter than → the quietest
beautiful → more beautiful than → the most beautiful
good → better than → the best
You should + verb: You should come back.
You could + verb: You could visit Rio.

Never

Boston is more quiet than São Paulo.
short word: quieter
It's the most good gift.
special: the best
You should to come back.
no to: should come

Question: Should I come back next year? — Yes, you should!

E

M
Mark: It's beautiful. It's the best gift of my trip!
E
Érica: So, tell me. Is São Paulo better than Boston?
M
Mark: Boston is quieter and smaller. But here the food is better, and the people are friendlier!
E
Érica: Then you should come back next year. You could visit Rio, too.
M
Mark: Good idea! And you should visit Boston. You could stay with my family.
