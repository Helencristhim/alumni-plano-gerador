Grammar Discovery

So, or such?

I feel so restless.
It was such a long drive.
It was such an amazing sunset.
They are such nice people.

What do the orange words have in common? What comes after so, and what comes after such?

so + adjective · such + (adjective) + noun

Both make the feeling stronger, like "very". With one thing, use such a / such an. With plural or uncountable nouns, use only such.

The Code

So and such

so + adjective

I'm so tired.
The scenery was so beautiful.

such a / an + adjective + noun

It was such a long drive.
It was such an amazing sunset.

such + adjective + plural noun

They're such nice people.
We had such good waves.

No noun? Use so. A noun? Use such. Same idea, two ways: The drive was so long. = It was such a long drive.
