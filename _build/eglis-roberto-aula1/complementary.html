Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Kio Stark: “Why you should talk to strangers” (12 min)

A writer who talks to strangers everywhere: on the street, on buses, in bars. Why these short conversations make us feel more alive, and simple ways to start one.

Tip: watch it with English subtitles. Write down two ways she starts a conversation, and use one of them in class when the backpacker sits next to you.

TED Talk
Pico Iyer: “The art of stillness” (15 min)

A travel writer who has been almost everywhere explains why the best trip can be the one where you stop moving. This is Claire’s side of the argument, told by a professional.

Tip: after the talk, answer out loud in one minute: are you more Jake or more Claire today? Record it and compare it with your answer from class.

Watch on TED ↗
TEDx Talk
Rick Steves: “The value of travel” (TEDxRainier)

The most famous travel guide in the United States, after thirty years living out of a suitcase, on what travel really gives you: not photos, but a new way to see the world and the people in it.

Tip: watch it on a weekend. Pause after each story he tells and ask yourself the lesson question: have you ever had a moment like that? When was it?
