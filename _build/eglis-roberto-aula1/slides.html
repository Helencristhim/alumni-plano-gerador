2. Which country in South America has surprised you the most? What happened there?

Real Speed

Short Sounds, Fast Questions

In a noisy bar, short questions win. Listen, then say it back at the same speed.

Have you ever been to Peru?
Where have you been this year?
I have been there twice.
How long have you been here?

Under Pressure

The Backpacker Who Talks Fast

A hostel bar, eleven at night. Mick, a backpacker from Sydney, talks fast and talks a lot. You want to understand him, and you want him to listen to your stories too.

  • Round 1: Mick talks fast. You ask him to slow down, you answer, and you tell your own story.
  • Round 2: you are the local, and a tourist has just arrived in Brazil. You ask the questions.

Closing

One Minute, One Stranger

Back in the bar in Lisbon, the traveler asks: “So, what is the best place you have ever been to?” You have one minute. Go.

I can talk about places I have been to without giving a date.
I can give the details of one trip with the past simple.
I can keep a conversation going and ask questions back.
