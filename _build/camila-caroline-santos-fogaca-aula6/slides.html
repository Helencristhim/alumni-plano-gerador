Chapter 4: The Code

Entering the Conversation

The signals that give you the floor and give it back

Grammar Discovery

Who Is Asking for the Floor?

"Let's get started, everybody is here."

"If I may add, the effective date is a holiday in Chile."

"Going back to the point, we still need a decision on the two positions."

"To sum up: two decisions, three action items, one open point."

Each of these sentences does one job in the meeting. Which one opens, which one enters, which one returns and which one closes?

FormWhat it doesExample
openingstarts the meeting and gives the first signalLet's get started. / Let me kick off with the numbers.
enteringtakes the floor politely, in the middle of somebody elseIf I may add, ... / Sorry to interrupt, but ...
returningbrings the discussion back to the point that mattersGoing back to the point, ... / Coming back to the headcount, ...
closingsummarises and names the next actionTo sum up, ... The next step is ... / Let us wrap up.
Watch outentering with an apology and losing the sentence. "Sorry, sorry, maybe, I don't know, but..." gives the floor back before you use it. One short signal is enough.

Sound Check

Say It Clearly

Key-word stress (point 9 of 14) — one strong word per phrase, and the small words stay light

Why this one is hard for a Brazilian speaker

In Portuguese: A sentence is fairly even, and emphasis is usually carried by word order or by an extra word.

In English: One word in the phrase is made longer and louder, and the small words around it shrink to almost nothing.

If it slips: With every word equally strong, the listener cannot tell which part of the sentence is the news.

Chapter 5: Real Speed

The Opening and the Closing

Five minutes at the start, five minutes at the end

Listening 1

The First Five Minutes

A regional HR meeting opens. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. What three points are on the agenda?
The headcount of the month, the remote work policy, and the open positions in Chile.
2. Which point is for decision, and which one is for information?
The positions in Chile are for decision; the policy point is for information.
3. What does the person who opens ask everybody to do?
To keep each point to ten minutes and to send any document before the meeting, not during it.
Listening 2

The Last Five Minutes

The same meeting closes. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. What are the two decisions in the wrap-up?
One position in Chile is approved, and the second one waits for the budget confirmation.
2. How many action items are there, and who are the owners?
Three: HR sends the local adaptation, finance confirms the budget, and the country manager talks to his team.
3. What is the deadline for the minutes?
The same day, before the end of the afternoon.
