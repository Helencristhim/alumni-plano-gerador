Lesson 21 -- The Project Kickoff

Video
Henrik Kniberg -- "Agile Product Ownership in a Nutshell" (15 min)

A clear, animated explanation of how a product owner decides what is in the scope, what comes first, and how the team and the stakeholders agree on expectations. Simple language and lots of project vocabulary.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down three sentences with plan to, need to or avoid + -ing that describe your own project.

Talk
Simon Sinek -- "Why good leaders make you feel safe" (TED, 12 min)

Why teams work better when everyone trusts that the leader will protect them. A good reminder for the first meeting of any project: people need to know what to expect from you.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then say, in two sentences, what you expect from your vendor team and what they can expect from you.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
Julian Treasure -- "How to speak so that people want to listen" (TED, 10 min)

Simple tools to make your voice clear and calm in a meeting. Useful for the first minute of a kickoff, when everyone is deciding if they trust the person who is speaking.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Choose one of his tools and use it when you record your kickoff for the next class.
