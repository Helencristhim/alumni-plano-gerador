Lesson 21 -- Pre-class

The Project Kickoff -- Roles and Expectations

The contract is signed and the project starts: the first meeting with the vendor team. Key words: scope, timeline, workstream, point of contact, expectation, responsibility, check-in, ramp up. Structure: verb + to (plan to, agree to) and verb + -ing (avoid changing, suggest having).

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Scope -- everything a project will do, and what it will not do
"Data migration is in the scope, but training is not."
Timeline -- the plan of dates for each part of a project
"The timeline shows go-live in June."
Workstream -- one part of a big project, with its own team and tasks
"We have three workstreams: network, data, and security."
Point of contact -- the one person you talk to about a topic
"Priya is our point of contact at the vendor."
Expectation -- what you believe someone will do or deliver
"Let's agree on our expectations in the first meeting."
Responsibility -- a task or decision that is your job
"Testing is the vendor's responsibility."
Check-in -- a short, regular meeting to see how things are going
"We have a short check-in every Monday."
Ramp up -- to slowly increase people or work until it is at full speed
"The vendor plans to ramp up the team in April."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

The cloud contract is signed, and today is the first meeting with the vendor team. Rafael and Priya, the delivery lead, talk about the scope. Training is not part of it: Rafael's team plans to train its own people.

There are three workstreams: network, data and security. Rafael suggests having one point of contact for each one. He expects to see the network part finished by the end of April, and he asks Priya to avoid changing the timeline without telling him.

Priya needs to ramp up her team first, so the first two weeks will be slow. At the end, they agree to have a short check-in every Monday at nine.

1. Who will do the training?
A Rafael's team.
B Priya's team, in the first two weeks of the project.
C Nobody.
2. Which sentence is correct?
A "Rafael suggests to have one point of contact for each workstream."
B "Rafael suggests having one point of contact."
C "Rafael suggests have one point of contact."
3. Why will the first two weeks be slow?
A Because the contract is not signed yet and the lawyers are still reading it.
B Because of the check-in.
C Because Priya needs to ramp up her team.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Verb + to or Verb + -ing

GRAMMAR

Some verbs take to + verb. Other verbs take verb + -ing. Learn them as a list.

PatternVerbsExample
+ to + verbplan, agree, need, expect, decide, wantWe plan to finish in June.
+ verb-ingavoid, suggest, finish, keep, considerLet's avoid changing the scope.
Pastonly the first verb changesThey agreed to send a report.
Negativenot + to / not + -ingWe decided not to add training.
Questiondo / does + verb + to / -ingDo you plan to ramp up in April?

Never: I suggest to have · avoid to change · we plan finish · they agreed sending
