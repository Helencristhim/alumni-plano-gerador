Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Andrew Stanton: “The clues to a great story” (19 min)

The writer of Toy Story and WALL-E on what makes people want to hear a story until the end.

Tip: note one tip and use it when you tell your week in class.

TED Talk
Nancy Duarte: “The secret structure of great talks” (18 min)

A presentation designer on the shape that great talks and stories share.

Tip: after the talk, tell one meeting of your week in three parts: before, during, after.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie: “The danger of a single story” (19 min)

A novelist tells stories from her life to show why one version of a story is never enough.

Tip: count the past continuous sentences in the first five minutes.
