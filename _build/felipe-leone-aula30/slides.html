Telling the Week: Meetings and Decisions

Your week as a story: what happened, what was happening, and what had already happened.

Retomada

Your Text, Five Marks

Say each sentence in the right form, then make one more sentence of yours with it.

Model

A Week in Ten Sentences

On Monday, I had a long meeting with the risk team. While we were discussing the new model, the client called. They had already seen the first numbers, and they wanted more details.

On Tuesday, I was preparing the report when my manager asked for a quick update. By the end of the day, we had already sent it.

On Wednesday, we made an important decision. We had tested two options, and we chose the simpler one. On Thursday, I was traveling to Rio while the team was finishing the slides. On Friday, we presented the plan, and the client approved it.

Guided Rewrite

Your Sentences, the Right Form

Typical sentences from a week text. Say the right form. Then write two of your own sentences below and rewrite them together.

#BeforeAfter
1While we discussed the budget, the client was calling.While we were discussing the budget, the client called.
2When I arrived, the meeting already started.When I arrived, the meeting had already started.
3We decided it before the call.We had already decided it before the call.
4On Tuesday I was having a meeting with the risk team.On Tuesday I had a meeting with the risk team.
