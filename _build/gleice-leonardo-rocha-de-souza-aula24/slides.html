Chapter 4 -- Good Advice

A Rule or a Good Idea?

should · shouldn’t · Should I...?

Grammar Discovery

What Is a Good Idea?

"You should be punctual."

"You should start with small talk."

"You shouldn't ask personal questions."

"Should you be very formal all the time? No, you shouldn't."

Rules or good ideas? What comes after should? Do you see to or -s?

UseFormExample
Good idea (all people)should + verbYou should be punctual. He should send a follow-up.
Not a good ideashouldn't + verbYou shouldn't ask personal questions.
Ask for adviceShould + subject + verb?Should I bring my catalog?
Short answerYes, you should. / No, you shouldn't.Should I be formal all the time? No, you shouldn't.
Comparehave to = a rule · should = adviceI have to send a report. I should send a follow-up.
Nevershould + to / shoulds / don't shouldNot you should to arrive. Not she shoulds.

In one line: should + verb = a good idea; shouldn’t + verb = not a good idea.

Grammar Practice

Good Idea or Not?

Say it first, then click to check

Good idea: "You ___should arrive (arrive) five minutes early."

The Follow-up Email

From: Gleice Leonardo Rocha de Souza
To: Thomas Grant, NatureCare Skin
Subject: Thank you for the meeting

Dear Mr. Grant,

Thank you for the meeting on Tuesday. It was a pleasure to meet you in Sao Paulo.

I hope you enjoyed the feijoada, and good luck to your team on Sunday!

As a next step, I would like to schedule a call next week with the two distributors from the fair. Would Thursday at ten work for you?

Best regards,
Gleice

1. Which detail from the conversation does Gleice write?
The feijoada and his football team.
2. What next step does she suggest?
A call next week with the two distributors, on Thursday at ten.
3. Is it a good follow-up? Answer with should.
Yes. It is short, it says thank you and it suggests a next step, like a follow-up should.

Listening 1 -- Tips About Thomas

A Message from Jennifer

Jennifer Lowe, NatureCare Skin, gives tips for the meeting. Sound first -- no text.

1. When does Thomas want to meet Gleice?
On Tuesday at ten.
2. What topic should Gleice use for small talk?
Football: she should ask him about his team.
3. Why should she bring business cards?
Because Thomas wants to introduce her to two distributors at the fair.
Listening 2 -- A Networking Coach

After the Meeting

Daniel Reed teaches networking to sales teams. Sound first -- no text.

1. When should you send your follow-up?
In twenty-four hours, when the person still remembers your face.
2. How long should the email be?
Short: three or four sentences.
3. How many files should you attach to the first email?
One is enough. You shouldn't attach ten.
