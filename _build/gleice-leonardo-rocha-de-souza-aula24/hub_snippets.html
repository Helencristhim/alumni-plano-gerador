Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Learn the eight new words for building trust. Tap Listen to hear each one.

Trust -- the feeling that a person is honest and you can believe them
"Trust grows slowly." (confiança)
Relationship -- the connection between two people or two companies
"A good relationship starts with a good conversation." (relacionamento)
Small talk -- a short, light conversation about easy topics
"Start with small talk about the trip." (conversa leve, papo informal)
Follow-up -- a message you send after a meeting to continue the contact
"Send a short follow-up the next day." (mensagem de retorno)
Networking -- meeting new people to make professional contacts
"She met two distributors at a networking event." (fazer contatos profissionais)
Business card -- a small card with your name, your job and your contact
"Here is my business card." (cartão de visita)
Punctual -- on time, not late
"You should be punctual for the meeting." (pontual)
Personal -- about your private life, not about work
"Don't ask personal questions in the first meeting." (pessoal)

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Should and Shouldn't

GRAMMAR

Read the explanation and the examples.

Use should + verbo para dar um CONSELHO, uma boa ideia (You should be punctual = Você deveria ser pontual). Shouldn't = não é uma boa ideia (You shouldn't ask personal questions = Você não deveria fazer perguntas pessoais). Should é igual para todas as pessoas: sem to e sem -s (She should send, nunca she shoulds). Compare: have to = regra, obrigação; should = conselho. / Use should for advice and shouldn't for things that are not a good idea.

Use / UsoStructure / EstruturaExample / Exemplo
Good idea / Conselhosubject + should + verbYou should be punctual. (Você deveria ser pontual.)
Not a good idea / Não é boa ideiasubject + shouldn't + verbYou shouldn't ask personal questions. (Você não deveria perguntar.)
Question / PerguntaShould + subject + verb?Should I bring my business card? (Eu devo levar?)
Short answer / Resposta curtaYes, you should. / No, you shouldn't.Should I be formal all the time? No, you shouldn't.
Rule x advice / Regra x conselhohave to x shouldI have to send a report. I should send a follow-up.
