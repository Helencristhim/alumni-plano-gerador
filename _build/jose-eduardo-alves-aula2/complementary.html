Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
A Day in the Life of an NYC Corporate Lawyer

A corporate lawyer films one ordinary working day: the documents she reviews, the calls she takes, the hours nobody sees. It is your own week, in another city and another language.

Tip: after every scene, say one sentence out loud about her, in the third person. "She reads the contract." "She does not go to court." Ten sentences is enough.

Podcast
Planet Money (NPR) — Episode 877: The Laws Of The Office

Two rules that explain why an office week looks the way it does: work grows to fill the time you give it, and people do whatever you decide to measure. Marina’s rule about Friday afternoons is the same idea, told by a lawyer.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Listen once with the page closed, then read it and see how much you already had.

Listen on NPR ↗
Documentary
Rotten — Season 1, Episode 1, "Lawyers, Guns & Honey"

The largest food fraud case ever prosecuted, told through the commodity chain that carries it: a producer, a trader, a shipment, a contract and, at the end of the line, a lawyer. Your market, filmed.

Tip: write down five verbs the narrator uses about a company — they will all be in the third person, and they will all carry the -s.
