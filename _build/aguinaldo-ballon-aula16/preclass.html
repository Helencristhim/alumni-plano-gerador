Lesson 16 -- Pre-class

Midpoint Review -- Fifteen Lessons, One Toolbox

A review of lessons 1 to 15 through a reading about the intermediate plateau. Key words: to take stock, a plateau, fluency, accuracy, a blind spot, to fine-tune, self-assessment, a setback, consistency, to brush up on. Structures: a review of the toolbox, no new grammar.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

To take stock -- expression: to stop and think carefully about a situation before you decide what to do next
"After fifteen lessons, it is a good moment to take stock."
A plateau -- a period when you stop making progress after a time of fast progress
"Many learners reach a plateau at the intermediate level."
Fluency -- the ability to speak easily, without stopping too often
"His fluency improved when he stopped translating in his head."
Accuracy -- the quality of being correct, without mistakes
"In a contract, accuracy is more important than speed."
A blind spot -- an area that you do not notice or understand well
"Prepositions are my blind spot: I never notice my mistakes."
To fine-tune -- to make small changes to something to make it work better
"Now we only need to fine-tune your pronunciation."
Self-assessment -- the act of judging your own progress or work
"An honest self-assessment shows what to practice next."
A setback -- a problem that stops or delays progress
"Missing two weeks of lessons was a small setback."
Consistency -- the quality of doing something regularly, in the same way
"Consistency matters more than long study sessions."
To brush up on -- to practice and improve a skill that you partly forgot
"Before the exam, I need to brush up on my reading."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

After fifteen lessons, Aguinaldo decides to take stock. He has worked on panels, funds, rules and debates. His fluency is much better: he has spoken for two minutes without notes several times.

His teacher told him that his accuracy still needed work. His blind spot is the present perfect with a date: "I have stepped down three months ago." If he practices it every day, he will fix it in a few weeks.

His plan for the second half was written together with his teacher: less new grammar, more real tasks, and consistency. Notice how many different structures from lessons 1 to 15 are in this short text.

1. What is Aguinaldo's blind spot?
A Reading academic texts about regulation and artificial intelligence for his doctorate.
B The present perfect with a date.
C Speaking for two minutes without notes on a panel in front of an audience.
2. Which sentence from the text reports what the teacher said?
A "His teacher told him that his accuracy still needed work."
B "If he practices it every day, he will fix it in a few weeks with consistency."
C "His plan for the second half was written together with his teacher last week."
3. Which sentence is correct?
A "I have stepped down from the company three months ago, after five years as CEO."
B "I stepped down three months ago."
C "I am stepped down from the company three months ago, after five years as CEO."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Fifteen Lessons, One Toolbox

GRAMMAR

No new grammar today. Choose the structure by the job you need to do.

JobFormExample
Experience and career (lessons 1, 2)present perfect: have + past participleI have advised clients since 2015.
Risk scenarios (lesson 3)If + present, will · If + past, wouldIf the regulator delays it, we will ask for more time.
Regulatory facts (lesson 4)be + past participleThe framework was published in March.
Reporting a meeting (lesson 5)told us (that) + one tense backHe told us the fund had approved it.
Precise and polite (lessons 9, 10, 12)would / could / might · Could you tell me what... · must / may / be likely toCould you tell me what the risk is?
Conversation and meaning (lessons 11, 15)seem to + verb · question tagsShe seems to agree, doesn’t she?

Never: I have stepped down three months ago · If the regulator will delay · The rule published in March · He said us that...
