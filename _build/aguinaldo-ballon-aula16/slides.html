Grammar Discovery

Which Tool Does Which Job?

"I have advised infrastructure clients since 2015."

"If the regulator delays the license, we will ask for more time."

"The new framework was published in March."

"The investor told us that the fund had approved the deal."

No new rule today. For each sentence, answer two questions: what JOB is the structure doing (experience, a risk scenario, a regulatory fact, a report), and which lesson did it come from?

JobFormExample
Experience and career (lessons 1, 2)present perfect: have + past participleI have advised clients since 2015.
Risk scenarios (lesson 3)If + present, will · If + past, wouldIf the regulator delays it, we will ask for more time.
Regulatory facts (lesson 4)be + past participleThe framework was published in March.
Reporting a meeting (lesson 5)told us (that) + one tense backHe told us the fund had approved it.
Precise and polite (lessons 9, 10, 12)would / could / might · Could you tell me what... · must / may / be likely toCould you tell me what the risk is?
Conversation and meaning (lessons 11, 15)seem to + verb · question tagsShe seems to agree, doesn’t she?

In one line: choose the structure by the job you need to do, not by the rule you remember.

Listening 1 · The Researcher

Why Progress Becomes Invisible

A researcher in adult language learning explains the intermediate plateau. Sound first — no text.

1. What do almost all intermediate learners say in her interviews?
That they are not improving.
2. What surprises them when they listen to an old recording?
Their sentences are longer, their vocabulary is more precise and they understand much more.
3. What is her advice?
Record yourself every few months and listen to the old recordings.
Listening 2 · The Executive

English After Forty-Five

A Brazilian executive remembers how he learned English late in his career. Sound first — no text.

1. When did he start to study English seriously?
When he was forty-six, after he became the CEO of a company with American investors.
2. What did his teacher ask him to do in the second year?
To give the same five-minute presentation from his first lesson.
3. What changed in the method after that?
Less new grammar, more real tasks from his work, and a lot of correction.
