You Already Know the Words

Stage 1.1: Words for Your World

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Eight words, two minutes.

Major — the main subject you study at university
"My major is Business Administration."
Internship — a short job where a student learns the work
"I want an internship next semester."
Semester — one half of a university year
"I am in my third semester."
Degree — what you get when you finish university
"I finish my degree in two years."
Apply for — to officially ask for a job or a place
"I apply for three internships every month."
Look for — to try to find something
"I am looking for my first internship."
Fluent — able to speak a language easily, without stopping
"I want to be fluent by the end of the year."
Abroad — in another country
"I want to spend a month abroad next year."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the message. Then answer the questions.

My name is Clara. I study Business Administration and I am in my third semester. I live near the university during the week, and on Friday I go to my family’s house. My brother studies at the same university, but he does not live with me. I do not work yet — I am looking for an internship for next semester. Every company wants English, so I want to be fluent this year. And next January I want to spend a month abroad.
Where does Clara go on Friday?
A To the university.
B To her family’s house.
Why is it "he does not live" and not "he does not lives"?
A Because "does" already carries the S.
B Because "live" is irregular.
Why does Clara want to be fluent this year?
A Because she wants to study abroad.
B Because every company wants English.

Stage 4: In the conversation

Quiz
An interviewer asks: "Tell me about yourself." Which answer works best?
A "Administration, third semester."
B "I study Business Administration in São Paulo, and I am in my third semester."
The sentence is not coming out. What do you say instead of stopping?
A "Give me one second, I am thinking."
B Say nothing and wait.
Which sentence is correct?
A "My brother do not lives with me."
B "My brother does not live with me."

Stage 5: Four sentences, no paper

Reflection
Write four sentences about yourself first, using the frame: who + verb + what + where or when. One about where you live, one about what you study, one about something you do every week, one about something you want this year. Then turn the paper over and record 30 seconds saying them — without reading. Keep this recording: we compare it at the end of the programme.
