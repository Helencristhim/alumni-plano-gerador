Lesson 17 -- Say It Again, Better

Conference Talk
"Dance vs. PowerPoint, a modest proposal" -- John Bohannon, TED

A science writer explains a laser physics experiment with dancers on the stage instead of slides. Funny and very short -- and a strong argument that a complex idea can be shown in a simple way.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down one sentence he uses to define a technical word, and say how you could "show" your own research without slides.

Podcast
"Restoring trust in science" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters discuss why some people do not trust science, and how scientists can explain their work so that more people understand it. Six minutes, with a transcript and a vocabulary list.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then paraphrase the main idea in two sentences: one for a colleague, and one for a student.

Listen on BBC Learning English →
Video
"How to sound smart in your TEDx talk" -- Will Stephen, TEDxNewYork

A comedy talk with no content at all, only the tricks speakers use to sound important. Six minutes that show exactly what jargon and a confident voice can hide.

Tip: watch once for fun. Then watch again and list three "tricks" you will NOT use in Bangkok, and what you will do instead.
