Chapter 3: The Code

The Word That Defines

who, which, that

Grammar Discovery

Which Word Comes After the Noun?

A patient who has this damage can burn a hand and not notice it.
Small fibers are the nerve fibers that carry pain and temperature.
Allodynia is pain which comes from something that should not hurt.
We need a drug that calms the nerve without switching it off.
Click to reveal the rule
To define a word inside the sentence, put who, which or that right after the noun, and then the explanation. who = people (a patient who has...). which = things (pain which comes from...). that = people or things — the safest choice when you speak. No comma: the clause tells us which one we mean. Do not repeat the subject: a nerve that is damaged, never a nerve that it is damaged. This is the grammar of every good definition: X is a Y that...

Poster session, row C

What Does That Refer To?

R
Excuse me, I'm a medical student. Your poster says small fiber neuropathy. What does that refer to?
D
Good question. Small fibers are the nerve fibers that carry pain and temperature. In leprosy, they are often the first ones that get damaged.
R
So the patient can't feel heat?
D
Exactly. A patient who has this damage can burn a hand and not notice it.
R
And this word here, allodynia? Sorry, it's jargon for me.
D
No problem. Let me put it this way: allodynia is pain which comes from something that should not hurt, like a bedsheet on the foot.
R
Wow. So the same patient feels no heat, but feels pain from a bedsheet?
D
That's what makes it so interesting. What I mean is, one nerve can lose some signals and exaggerate others.
R
Now it makes sense. Thank you, that was much clearer than my textbook.
D
Then my poster did its job. If you have more questions, here's my card.

Quick Fire

Explain It Simply

Answer out loud first, then click to compare

A student asks: "What is neuropathic pain?"It is pain that comes from a damaged nerve, not from an injury in the skin or the muscle.
A journalist asks: "What does a pharmacologist do?"A pharmacologist is a scientist who studies how drugs work in the body.
Somebody asks: "What is a placebo?"It is a pill that has no real drug in it. We use it to compare.
A nurse asks: "What do you mean by sensory loss?"What I mean is a part of the body that cannot feel touch, heat or pain anymore.
Your listener looks confused after you say "sensitization".Let me put it this way: the nerve is like an alarm which goes off too easily.
