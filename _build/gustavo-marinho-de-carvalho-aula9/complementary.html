Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Suits — Season 1, Episodes 1 and 2

A firm in which the question that drives every scene is the question from this lesson: who actually decides, and who only appears to. The dialogue is fast, it is American, and half of it is people describing other people in a single sentence.

Tip: English subtitles on. After each episode, describe three of the characters out loud, one sentence each, with a relative clause inside.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English — “The Enhanced Games”

Six minutes, two British speakers, normal speed, on an organisation, the people behind it, and everybody whose view has to count. It is a short lesson in stakeholders with none of the vocabulary of a management book. Free transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen once with no text, then name the four groups of stakeholders out loud, one sentence each, using who or which.

Talk
Simon Sinek — “Why good leaders make you feel safe” (TED)

Eleven minutes on what an organisation looks like from inside it, and on the difference between the person with the title and the person people actually follow. It is the human half of the map you drew in this lesson.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles. Then say in two sentences which person on your client’s side has the title, and which one has the influence.
