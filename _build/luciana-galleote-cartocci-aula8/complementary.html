‘Have to’ and ‘must’ -- 6 Minute Grammar (BBC Learning English)

Two BBC presenters explain, with many short examples, when to use have to and when to use must, and the difference between don’t have to and mustn’t. Exactly the grammar of this lesson, in six minutes.

Tip: watch once for the gist. The second time, pause after each example and say one sentence about a real tender with the same verb.
