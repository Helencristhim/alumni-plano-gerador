Lesson 08 -- Pre-class

The Tender -- Rules, Needs and Options

Before a hospital buys, it publishes a tender with rules for every company. Key words: a tender, a bidder, a requirement, to submit, eligible, to comply with, a specification, a certificate, a penalty, a warranty. Structure: must, have to, need to, don’t have to.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A tender -- a public document that invites companies to compete for a contract
"The hospital has published a tender for new cameras."
A bidder -- a company that competes for a contract in a tender
"Five bidders sent a proposal to the hospital."
A requirement -- something that a client says you must have or do
"Read every requirement before you start the proposal."
To submit -- to send a document officially, so that someone can make a decision
"We have to submit the proposal by Friday."
Eligible -- allowed to take part, because you meet all the rules
"Only certified partners are eligible for this tender."
To comply with -- to follow a rule or a standard exactly
"The cabling needs to comply with the hospital standard."
A specification -- a detailed technical description of what a client needs
"The specification asks for three hundred and twenty cameras."
A certificate -- an official document that proves a skill or a level
"Our engineers have a valid certificate from our partner."
A penalty -- money that you pay when you do not follow the contract
"A late repair means a penalty for the supplier."
A warranty -- a promise to repair or replace a product for a period of time
"The cameras come with a five-year warranty."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

On Monday morning, Luciana’s assistant reads a new hospital tender. “OK,” she says. “Every bidder must submit the proposal by May 30. We have to show two partner certificates, and the cameras need to comply with the specification.”

“What about the site visit?” Luciana asks. “It is optional,” the assistant answers. “We don’t have to visit the hospital. But there is one important rule: after the contract, we mustn’t change the engineers without the hospital’s approval.”

Luciana remembers the last tender. “Last year we had to prove three similar projects, and we almost lost because of one missing document. This time, we will have to check everything twice.” Must has no past form, so for last year she says had to.

1. Does the team have to visit the hospital?
A No. The visit is optional.
B Yes. The tender says that every bidder must visit the hospital before May 30.
C The text does not say.
2. Why does Luciana say “last year we had to prove” and not “we must prove”?
A Because the rule last year was optional and the team could choose to prove the projects or not.
B Because must has no past form, so the past is had to.
C Because it is a plan for the future.
3. What does “we mustn’t change the engineers” mean?
A It is not necessary to change the engineers, but the team can change them if it wants to.
B The team has to change the engineers.
C Changing the engineers without approval is not allowed.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Must, Have To, Need To, Don’t Have To

GRAMMAR

Use must, have to and need to for something necessary. Use don’t have to when something is not necessary (you can choose), and mustn’t when something is not allowed. Must has no past: use had to.

JobFormExample
A written rule, a strong obligationmust + verbEvery bidder must submit by May 30.
An obligation from outside (client, law, partner)have to / has to + verbShe has to sign every page.
Something necessary for the resultneed to / needs to + verbThe cameras need to comply with the specification.
Not necessary: you can choosedon’t / doesn’t have toWe don’t have to visit the site.
Not allowedmustn’t + verbYou mustn’t send it late.
Past and futurehad to / will have toLast year we had to prove three projects.
The questionDo we have to / Do we need to...?Do we have to send the original?

Never: We must to submit · She have to sign · Do we must send? · Last year we must show
