Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Celeste Headlee — “10 ways to have a better conversation” (TED)

Eleven minutes on exactly what this lesson practised: how a conversation with somebody you have just met stays alive, and what kills it. She is a radio interviewer, so every one of her ten moves is a sentence you can copy word for word.

Tip: turn English subtitles on the first time and off the second. Write down the two moves you already do, and the one you never do.

Video
Think Fast Talk Smart (Stanford) — “How to Start Small Talk: Asking Follow-up Questions”

Twenty-five minutes from the Stanford Graduate School of Business podcast, on the single move that decides whether small talk continues: the follow-up question. Two American speakers, unscripted, at full speed, with no simplification for a non-native listener.

Tip: pause after each of their examples and say your own version out loud, about your own job and your own weekend.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Podcast
Criminal — “The Poison Squad” (episode 364)

An investigation into the chemist who spent years proving what was being put into American food, and the regulation that came out of it. It is a true crime series built entirely out of questions and follow-ups — the same mechanism you practised today, at native speed, for thirty minutes.

Tip: listen once with no notes. Then listen again and write down five questions the host asks. Notice how few of them are more than six words long.
