Chapter 3: The Code

What You Do, Every Day

I work · my team analyzes · do you negotiate?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I work in pricing."

"I negotiate with our suppliers every week."

"My team analyzes the market every morning."

"The company publishes its results four times a year."

Look at the coloured verbs. Why do two of them end in -s?

FormUseExample
I / you / we / they + verbFacts about you and things you do regularlyI work in pricing.
he / she / it + verb + sThe same idea, but for one other person or thingMy team analyzes the market. The company publishes its results.
Negative
don't / doesn't + verb		The -s moves to does, so the verb goes back to plain formI don't work with one category. She doesn't set the price alone.
Question
Do / Does + person + verb		Same rule: does carries the -sDo you negotiate every week? Does your team meet on Monday?

One rule, four shapes: the -s belongs to he, she and it — and when does appears, it takes the -s for itself.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I am work in pricing."
"I work in pricing."
"My team analyze the market every day."
"My team analyzes the market every day."
"I no work with only one category."
"I don't work with only one category."

One verb is enough: I work, never I am work. The -s belongs to he, she, it — and my team is an it. English never negates with a bare no before a verb: it uses don't and doesn't.

Chapter 4: The Coffee Break

Meeting a Colleague at the Conference

Chicago, half past ten in the morning

Dialogue

The Coffee Break

K
Hi, I don't think we have met. I'm Karen Mitchell, and I work for a grocery chain here in Chicago.
M
Nice to meet you, Karen. I'm Marcio Santos, and I'm from Sao Paulo, in Brazil.
K
Brazil, very nice. And what do you do there?
M
I work in pricing. My team decides the price of the products in a large wholesale company.
K
That is my world too. I look after the margin of the fresh categories. How often do you change your prices?
M
Almost every day. We follow the market, and we negotiate with every supplier before we decide.
K
Then you know the hard part. Most people think pricing is only a number on a shelf.

About Karen

2. Which part of the business does she look after?

3. What does she say most people think about pricing?
