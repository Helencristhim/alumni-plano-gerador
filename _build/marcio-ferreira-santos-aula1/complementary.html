Complementary Activities

Extra material to reinforce your learning outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen.

Lesson 1 -- Who Is Marcio?

Grammar Video
"Present Simple and Present Continuous" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the tense you used all through this lesson: the present simple for facts and routines, and the little -s that belongs to he, she and it. Connection to Lesson 1: this is the grammar behind "I work in pricing" and "my team analyzes the market".

Tip: pause after each example and say one sentence about yourself, then the same sentence about your team. If the second one has no -s, say it again.

Podcast
"Is Dynamic Pricing Coming to a Supermarket Near You?" -- Planet Money, NPR

Twenty-five minutes of clear American English on electronic shelf labels, food waste and prices that change during the day. Connection to Lesson 1: every word you learned today is in this episode -- retail, pricing, margin, supplier -- said by people who do your job in another country. The page also has the full transcript.

Tip: listen once without the transcript and write down three things they say about prices. Then read the transcript and check. Reading it in English, not in a translator, is the habit this programme wants to build.

Business Video
"Is Personalized Pricing the Future of Shopping?" -- Chicago Booth Review, The Big Question

A short panel with Chicago Booth professors on how companies use data to set a different price for every customer. Connection to Lesson 1: this is the school you want to study at, and this is how the people there talk about your own subject.

Tip: watch the first five minutes only. Write one sentence with your opinion, in the present simple, and bring it to the next class: "I think personalized pricing works when..."
