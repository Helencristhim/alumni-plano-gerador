Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
This American Life -- Episode 355, "The Giant Pool of Money"

The hour that created Planet Money, and the best story ever recorded about credit rules that somebody loosened and nobody wrote down. It is one long past-simple narrative, told by the people who were inside it, in plain American English.

Tip: the full transcript is on the same page. Listen to the first twenty minutes, then read that part and mark every irregular past verb you did not recognise by ear.

Listen on This American Life ↗
Talk
Kathryn Schulz -- "On being wrong" (TED)

Seventeen minutes on the moment a person discovers a decision was wrong, and on how much of a career is spent avoiding that sentence. It is the language behind the last paragraph of today’s text: the team that read the policy instead of blaming the model.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Write down three sentences she says in the past, then say the same three about a decision of your own.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
60 Minutes -- "The real-life Big Short and the 2008 financial crisis"

Reporters and investors telling, in the past, what they saw in a portfolio before anybody else did. Interview English at normal speed, and the whole vocabulary of this lesson: a cohort, a default, a write-off, a loss.

Tip: stop the video after each answer and repeat one sentence out loud, keeping it in the past all the way to the end.
