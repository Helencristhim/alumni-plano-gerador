Chapter 4: The Code

Long Nouns, Clear Sentences

Complex noun phrases in academic writing

Grammar Discovery

Where Is the Main Noun?

"The use of automated systems in public decisions raises new legal questions."

"A recent study of credit algorithms suggests that bias may be common."

"The lack of transparency in high-risk systems is a problem for the courts."

"New rules for AI developers will be published next year."

Each blue part is ONE noun phrase. Find the main noun in each one: use, study, lack, rules. What comes before the main noun? What comes after it? Now look at sentence 3: why is the verb is and not are?

JobFormExample
Noun + of + nounthe use of / the lack of / the impact ofthe lack of transparency
Words before the main nouna / the + adjective or noun + main nouna recent credit study, a high-risk system
Details after the main nounmain noun + in / for / by / on + nounnew rules for AI developers
Turn a verb into a nounuse → the use of; regulate → the regulation ofThe regulation of AI is difficult.
Agreementthe verb agrees with the main noun, not with the last nounThe lack of rules is a problem.
The trapadjectives go before the noun and have no pluralNot systems high-risks. Say high-risk systems.

In one line: build the phrase around one main noun, and the verb agrees with that noun.

Listening 1 · The Professor

The First Lecture of the Seminar

A law professor opens a doctoral seminar on technology and regulation. Sound first — no text.

1. What is new about the use of algorithms by public agencies, according to her?
The scale: one system can make a million decisions in one day.
2. What is the guiding question of the semester?
How the law can guarantee accountability at that scale.
3. What does she expect from the students who present a paper?
To disagree with the authors at least once.
Listening 2 · The Founder

A Startup Founder on Regulation

The founder of an AI company speaks at a technology conference. Listen for the main nouns. Sound first — no text.

1. What does he say startups really hate?
Uncertainty, not regulation.
2. What does his company do every month?
It tests the system for bias.
3. What does he ask governments for?
Clear rules and one agency for oversight.
