Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Accountability -- the duty to explain your decisions and to accept responsibility for them
"Without accountability, nobody answers for the mistakes of the system."
Transparency -- being open, so that people can see how decisions are made
"The law requires transparency in public algorithms."
Bias -- an unfair preference for or against a group of people
"The study found bias against applicants from poor areas."
A dataset -- a collection of data that is used for a specific purpose
"The system was trained on a dataset from 2015."
Oversight -- the act of watching and checking an activity to make sure it is done correctly
"The agency has oversight of all high-risk systems."
Automated decision-making -- decisions that a computer system makes without a person
"Automated decision-making is common in credit and insurance."
Explainability -- the ability to explain how a system reached a result
"Courts want explainability, not only accuracy."
A high-risk system -- a system that can seriously affect people's rights or safety
"A system that selects job candidates is a high-risk system."
To deploy -- to start using a system in real situations
"The city deployed the system before it was tested."
Human in the loop -- expression: a person who checks or approves the decisions of an automated system
"For high-risk decisions, the law requires a human in the loop."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Aguinaldo reads a paper for his doctorate. The first sentence is long: "The use of automated systems in public decisions raises new legal questions." He finds the main noun: use.

The second paragraph says that "the lack of transparency in high-risk systems is a problem for the courts". The verb is is, not are, because the main noun is lack, not systems. The paper also says that bias in old datasets may cause unfair results.

At the end, the authors argue that the real test of new frameworks will be accountability: a citizen must know who answers for a wrong decision. Notice how much information each noun phrase carries.

1. What is the main noun in "The use of automated systems in public decisions"?
A Use.
B Systems, because it is the most important word in the whole phrase.
C Decisions, because it is the last noun before the verb in the sentence.
2. Why is the verb "is" in "the lack of transparency in high-risk systems is a problem"?
A Because the verb always agrees with the noun that is closest to it in the sentence.
B Because the main noun, lack, is singular.
C Because "systems" is an uncountable noun in academic English and takes is.
3. Which noun phrase is correct?
A "the systems high-risks of the public agencies in Brazil"
B "high-risk systems"
C "the high-risks systems of the public agencies in Brazil"

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Complex Noun Phrases

GRAMMAR

Academic English packs information into one long noun phrase. Find the main noun: the verb agrees with it.

JobFormExample
Noun + of + nounthe use of / the lack of / the impact ofthe lack of transparency
Words before the main nouna / the + adjective or noun + main nouna recent credit study, a high-risk system
Details after the main nounmain noun + in / for / by / on + nounnew rules for AI developers
Turn a verb into a nounuse → the use of; regulate → the regulation ofThe regulation of AI is difficult.
Agreementthe verb agrees with the main noun, not with the last nounThe lack of rules is a problem.
The trapadjectives go before the noun and have no pluralNot systems high-risks. Say high-risk systems.

Never: The lack of rules are a problem · The use the systems · systems high-risks · a study recent
