Series
Suits — Season 1, Episode 1

A series about people who argue for a living. Almost every scene is somebody conceding a small point to win a bigger one, at a speed close to real conversation. It is the concede-and-counter move from today, done by people who do it every day.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and pause every time somebody says to be fair, that said, granted or however. Write down three of those lines and say them out loud.

Talk
Julia Dhar — “How to disagree productively and find common ground” (TED)

A world debate champion on how to disagree without turning it into a fight. Her first rule is the one from today: start from what you actually agree on, and only then turn.

Tip: watch it once for the ideas. The second time, write down every connector she uses to move from one point to the next.

Talk
Daniel H. Cohen — “For argument’s sake” (TED)

A philosopher on why the person who loses an argument is often the one who learns the most. Short, funny, and full of exactly the language of conceding and countering.

Tip: pause after each of his main points and answer it out loud with Granted... that said... before you hear what he says next.
