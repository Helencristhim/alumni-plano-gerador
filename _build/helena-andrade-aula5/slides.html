Chapter 4: The Code

Reading Formal Emphasis

Rarely is... Not only does... Not until... did

Grammar Discovery

The Verb Came First

"Rarely is a maritime casualty caused by a single failure."

"Not only does low manning make the crew tired, but it also removes the second person."

"Not until a computer beat the world champion did people accept that a machine could think."

Write the first sentence in normal word order. Is the meaning the same?

FormWhat it doesExample
rarely / seldom / never + is, does, hadthe verb comes before the subject; the meaning does not changeRarely is a casualty caused by one failure. = A casualty is rarely caused by one failure.
not only ... but alsoonly the first half changes the word orderNot only does it make the crew tired, but it also removes...
not until ... didthe second half changes the word orderNot until a computer won did people accept it.
The reading trapRarely does not mean never. In the exam, a wrong option often changes rarely into never.

You do not need to write these sentences. You need to read them and keep the meaning: rarely = not often, but sometimes.

Listening 1

The Drill That Nobody Ran

A safety officer talks about drills. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. How many of the drills did he really watch?
About a third of them.
2. What happens after an eleven-minute drill?
Everyone goes back to work, and nobody learns anything.
3. Why do people report near misses honestly?
Because nobody is punished for an accident that did not happen.
Writing

Write What He Said

Task: Write three short sentences about the safety officer. Use one starter for each sentence.

Start with:

He signed...On paper, the ship...In practice, the drills...
He signed hundreds of drill records, but he watched only a third of the drills. On paper, the ship has a lifeboat drill every month. In practice, the drills are short and nobody learns anything.
Writing

One Sentence per Text

Task: Write the main idea of each text in one sentence. Use your own words, not the words of the text.

Start with:

Text 1 says that...Text 2 says that...
Text 1 says that ships have the right papers, but the crew do not really practise safety. Text 2 says that people change the meaning of intelligence every time a machine learns a new task.
