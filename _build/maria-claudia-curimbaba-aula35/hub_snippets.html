Lesson 35 -- Pre-class

Vermiculite and Beyond -- Your Creative Process

Talk about your art and your process with the present perfect continuous ("I have been painting for ten years", "lately I have been experimenting...", "how long have you been...?") and -ing subjects ("Getting the mix right is..."). New vocabulary: medium, canvas, sketch, pigment, technique, trial and error, creative block, inspiration, body of work, self-taught.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Medium -- the material an artist uses, like oil, acrylic or watercolor
"My medium is acrylic with vermiculite."
Canvas -- the strong cloth that artists paint on
"This canvas is almost two meters wide."
Sketch -- a quick, simple drawing that you make before the real work
"I always make a small sketch before I paint."
Pigment -- a powder that gives paint its color
"I mix the vermiculite with a brown pigment."
Technique -- a special way of doing something that needs skill
"It took me years to find my own technique."
Trial and error -- learning by trying different things and seeing what works
"Getting the mix right is pure trial and error."
Creative block -- a period when you cannot create anything new
"After a creative block, a museum visit always helps me."
Inspiration -- something that gives you new ideas or makes you want to create
"My inspiration comes from the colors of Minas Gerais."
Body of work -- all the works that an artist has made, seen as a group
"This body of work took me three years."
Self-taught -- having learned a skill by yourself, without classes or a teacher
"I am self-taught. I never went to art school."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Next week Maria Claudia's paintings will hang at the gallery evening in Houston, and David, the curator, has asked her for a short artist note. She writes: "I am self-taught. I have been painting for about ten years, mostly at night and on weekends. Lately I have been experimenting with vermiculite: I mix it with the pigment, so the canvas has a texture you want to touch. Getting the mix right is pure trial and error, and I have ruined many canvases. When I have a creative block, visiting a museum always helps. My inspiration comes from the colors of Minas Gerais, and you can see them in this whole body of work."

B Since last week.
C For about ten years.
2. What has she been experimenting with lately?
A Oil paint on very small pieces of paper that she buys in Houston.
B Vermiculite mixed with pigment.
C Photography.
3. What helps her when she has a creative block?
A Visiting a museum.
B Buying a new canvas.
C Working longer hours at the office until the ideas come back on their own.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Present Perfect Continuous

GRAMMAR

Use have / has been + -ing for an activity that started in the past and is still going on, or that you have been doing again and again lately. The focus is the activity and how long, not the result.

UseFormExample
started before, still goinghave / has been + -ing + for / sinceI have been painting for ten years.
recent, again and againlately / recently + have been + -ingLately I have been experimenting with vermiculite.
question about durationHow long have you been + -ing?How long have you been working on it?
the result (not the activity)have + past participleI have finished three canvases.
activity as the subject-ing + singular verbMixing the colors is the hardest part.

Key point: say "I have been painting since 2016", never "I am painting since 2016". Verbs like know and like do not take -ing here: "I have known him for years".
