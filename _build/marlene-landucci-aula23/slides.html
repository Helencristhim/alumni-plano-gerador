Chapter 3 -- Would You Like...?

Language Discovery

Would you like some water? / You should try the fish.

Discovery

Listen and Notice

Who wants the food in each sentence -- you or the other person?

"Would you like some water?"

"Would you like to share the appetizer?"

"You should try the grilled fish."

"I would like the mushroom pasta, please."

The Rule

Offer and Recommend

UsePatternExample
Offer a thingWould you like + thing?Would you like some water?
Offer an actionWould you like to + verb?Would you like to share this?
Recommend a dishYou should try + dishYou should try the grilled fish.
Ask for a recommendationWhat do you recommend?What do you recommend here?
Say what YOU want (review)I would like + thingI would like the pasta, please.

Key idea: Would you like is for the OTHER person, and I would like is for you -- that one is a review from Lesson 6. After like you put a thing (some water) or to plus a verb (to share). After should the verb has no to: say You should try, never "You should to try".

Chapter 4 -- At the Trattoria

Dinner for Six

A conversation, a menu, and two messages

Dialogue

At the Dinner Table

P

"Good evening, Marlene. Would you like some water or some wine?"

M

"Water, please. Thank you, Paul. I do not know this menu. What do you recommend?"

P

"The tomato bread is the best appetizer here. Would you like to share it with the table?"

M

"Yes, please. And for the main course? I would like to try something typical."

P

"You should try the grilled fish. Carla is vegetarian, so she always takes the mushroom pasta."

M

"Perfect. Let us make a toast first, and at the end we split the bill."

Comprehension

About Paul

1. What does Paul offer Marlene to drink?
Water or wine -- he asks "Would you like some water or some wine?"
2. Which appetizer does Paul like, and what does he want to do with it?
The tomato bread -- he wants to share it with the whole table.
3. What does Paul recommend for the main course, and what does Carla take?
He recommends the grilled fish. Carla is vegetarian, so she takes the mushroom pasta.
