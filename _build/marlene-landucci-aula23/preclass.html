Aula 23 -- Pre-class

Dining with Friends

Jante com amigos no exterior: oferecer ("Would you like some water?"), recomendar ("You should try the grilled fish") e dividir a mesa e a conta. Vocabulário novo: recommend, appetizer, main course, share, vegetarian, split, toast.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. Toque em Listen para ouvir. / Listen to each word and read the example.

Recommend -- to say that something is good and worth trying (recomendar)
"I recommend the grilled fish." (Eu recomendo o peixe grelhado.)
Appetizer -- a small dish you eat before the main food (entrada)
"The tomato bread is a great appetizer." (O pão com tomate é uma entrada ótima.)
Main course -- the biggest and most important dish of the meal (prato principal)
"For the main course I would like the fish." (De prato principal eu gostaria do peixe.)
Share -- to eat the same dish together with other people (dividir um prato)
"We share the appetizer with the whole table." (Nós dividimos a entrada com a mesa toda.)
Vegetarian -- a person who does not eat meat or fish (vegetariano)
"Carla is vegetarian, so she takes the pasta." (A Carla é vegetariana, então ela pede a massa.)
Split -- to divide the bill so that everybody pays a part (dividir a conta)
"At the end we split the bill." (No final nós dividimos a conta.)
Toast -- to lift your glass and say Cheers with other people (brinde)
"Let us make a toast to this trip." (Vamos fazer um brinde a esta viagem.)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto e responda às perguntas. / Read the text and answer the questions.

Paul and Carla invite Marlene to dinner at a small trattoria. Six friends sit at one table. Paul looks at Marlene and asks: "Would you like some water or some wine?" Marlene does not know the menu, so she asks: "What do you recommend?" Paul says: "You should try the tomato bread. It is the best appetizer here. Would you like to share it with the table?" For the main course Paul recommends the grilled fish. Carla is vegetarian, so she takes the mushroom pasta. Before they eat, everybody lifts a glass and they make a toast. At the end they split the bill, and each person pays a part. (Repare: Would you like é para oferecer ao OUTRO, e You should try é para recomendar.)

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Would you like...? + You should try...

GRAMMAR

Para OFERECER alguma coisa ao OUTRO, use Would you like + a coisa: "Would you like some water?". Para oferecer uma AÇÃO, use Would you like to + verbo: "Would you like to share?". Para RECOMENDAR, use You should try + o prato -- e depois de should o verbo NUNCA leva to. E I would like (revisão da aula 6) continua sendo para pedir algo para VOCÊ. / Would you like is for the other person, I would like is for you, and after should there is no to.

UseExample (EN)Tradução (PT)
Oferecer uma coisaWould you like some water?Você gostaria de um pouco de água?
Oferecer uma açãoWould you like to share this?Você gostaria de dividir isto?
RecomendarYou should try the fish.Você deveria provar o peixe.
Pedir uma recomendaçãoWhat do you recommend?O que você recomenda?
Pedir para si (revisão)I would like the pasta, please.Eu gostaria da massa, por favor.
Responder a uma ofertaYes, please. / No, thank you.Sim, por favor. / Não, obrigada.

Key point / Ponto-chave: depois de would you like entra uma coisa (some water) ou to + verbo (to share). Depois de should o verbo vai sozinho: diga You should try, nunca "You should to try".
