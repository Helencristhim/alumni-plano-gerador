Grammar Video
"The first conditional" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the shape you practiced today: if plus the present, will plus the verb. It also covers the mistake that costs everybody the most, which is putting will inside the if half. Connection to Lesson 4: this is the sentence behind "If it rains, we'll move to my place".

Tip: pause after each example and finish the sentence yourself with something from your own Saturday. One sentence, then play again.

Podcast
"Questlove" -- Song Exploder

Twenty minutes of one musician taking a single song apart and explaining every decision: what he tried, what he dropped, what would have happened if he had kept it. Short, calm, and the audio itself is half the story. Connection to Lesson 4: it is a whole episode of creative decisions with consequences, which is exactly the conversation you practiced with the budget.

Tip: listen once for the music and once for the language. On the second listen, write down two decisions he made and turn each one into your own conditional sentence.

Talk
"A powerful way to unleash your natural creativity" -- Tim Harford, TED (14 minutes)

An economist explains why the best creative work often comes from the plan that went wrong, using a famous studio session where the piano was broken. Clear British English, one story at a time. Connection to Lesson 4: the whole talk is about backup plans, and about what happens if the first plan fails.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then answer out loud in one minute: what will you do next time a plan of yours falls apart? Start the answer with "If".
