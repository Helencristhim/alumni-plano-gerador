Chapter 4: The Code

It Is Filed, It Is Approved

When the action matters more than the person

Grammar Discovery

Who Does It? Does It Matter?

"The case is filed in a bankruptcy court."

"The debtor is protected by an automatic stay."

"The claims are checked, and some of them are rejected."

"Finally, the plan is approved by a judge."

Look at the red verbs: is or are, then a past participle (filed, protected, checked, approved). Now ask: who files the case? Who checks the claims? The text does not say, because it is not important. The action is the important part. Only in sentence 4 do we see the person, after by.

JobFormExample
Formam / is / are + past participleThe petition is filed online.
Negativeis not / are not + past participleThe business is not closed.
QuestionIs / Are + subject + past participle?Is the plan approved by the judge?
By + persononly when the person is importantThe plan is approved by a judge.
When to use itthe action matters more than the person: legal, formal and process textsThe creditors are paid over five years.
The trapdo not forget is / are, and use the past participleNot “The plan approved by the judge.” Say The plan is approved by the judge.

In one line: is / are + past participle: the petition is filed, the claims are paid.

Listening 1 · A Bankruptcy Attorney

The First Day of a Case

An American bankruptcy attorney explains what happens on the first day of a Chapter 11 case. Sound first — no text.

1. When does the automatic stay start?
At the moment the petition is filed.
2. What can creditors not do during the stay?
They cannot call the debtor, sue the company or take its assets.
3. What is his advice for a lawyer who represents a creditor?
Do not call the debtor; follow the court and read the case file every day.
Listening 2 · A Business Owner

The Year My Company Almost Closed

An Australian businessman remembers the Chapter 11 case of his hotels in California. Sound first — no text.

1. How much did his company owe?
More than twenty million dollars.
2. What surprised him when they filed for Chapter 11?
The hotels were not closed, and he kept his job.
3. How were the creditors paid?
One hotel was sold, and the money was used to pay them.
Listening 3 · A Bank Lawyer

On the Side of the Creditor

A lawyer at a bank in India explains her work when a U.S. client files for bankruptcy. Sound first — no text.

1. How is the bank told about a new case?
By a notice from the court.
2. What is the most important date in the notice?
The last day to file a claim.
3. Who files the claim for the bank?
Its American lawyers.
