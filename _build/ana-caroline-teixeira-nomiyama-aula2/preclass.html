Chapter 11 in Plain English -- It Is Filed, It Is Approved

How a U.S. bankruptcy case works, from the petition to the plan. Key words: bankruptcy, to file, a petition, a bankruptcy court, a judge, assets, a reorganization plan, an automatic stay, to approve, a claim. Structure: the present simple passive.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Bankruptcy -- the legal situation of a person or company that cannot pay its debts
"The company went into bankruptcy last year."
To file -- to give an official document to a court so that it is recorded
"Our client filed for Chapter 11 on Monday."
A petition -- a formal written request to a court
"The petition is filed online, with a list of all the creditors."
A bankruptcy court -- a federal court in the U.S. that only handles bankruptcy cases
"The case is in a bankruptcy court in Miami."
A judge -- the person who controls a court and makes the decisions
"The judge asked for more information about the assets."
Assets -- everything that a person or a company owns and that has a value
"Its assets include two buildings and a fleet of trucks."
A reorganization plan -- a document that explains how a company will pay its debts and continue to work
"The reorganization plan pays the creditors over five years."
An automatic stay -- an order that stops creditors from collecting money as soon as a bankruptcy case starts
"Because of the automatic stay, we cannot call the debtor."
To approve -- to say officially that something is accepted
"The plan was approved by the court in March."
A claim -- a formal request for money that a creditor says it has the right to receive
"Every creditor files a claim with the amount it is owed."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

In her new job, Ana is given a file from the Miami office. The debtor is a food company in Florida, and it is in bankruptcy. Its petition is filed in a bankruptcy court under Chapter 11, so the company is not closed: it continues to work.

From the first day, the company is protected by an automatic stay. Ana’s client, a bank, cannot call the company or take its assets. What can the bank do? It files a claim with the amount it is owed, before the deadline.

Later, a reorganization plan is written and is sent to all the creditors. If the plan is approved by the judge, the bank is paid over five years. Ana thinks it is slow, but it is better than nothing.

1. Why does the text say “the company is protected” and not “the company protects”?
A Because the company does not do the action: it receives the protection.
B Because the sentence is in the past, and every sentence about the past needs is before the verb.
C Because protect is a verb that is never used in the active form.
2. What can the bank do during the automatic stay?
A Call the company every week and ask for a payment plan outside the court.
B File a claim with the amount it is owed.
C Take some of the assets.
3. When is the bank paid?
A Over five years, if the judge approves the plan.
B On the first day of the case.
C When the managers of the company decide to sell the assets and close the business forever.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Present Simple Passive

GRAMMAR

Use am / is / are + past participle when the action is more important than the person who does it. It is very common in legal texts. Add by + person only when the person is important: the plan is approved by a judge.

JobFormExample
Formam / is / are + past participleThe petition is filed online.
Negativeis not / are not + past participleThe business is not closed.
QuestionIs / Are + subject + past participle?Is the plan approved by the judge?
By + persononly when the person is importantThe plan is approved by a judge.
When to use itthe action matters more than the person: legal, formal and process textsThe creditors are paid over five years.
The trapdo not forget is / are, and use the past participleNot “The plan approved by the judge.” Say The plan is approved by the judge.

Never: The plan approved by the judge · The petition is file · The claims is paid · The debtor is protect
