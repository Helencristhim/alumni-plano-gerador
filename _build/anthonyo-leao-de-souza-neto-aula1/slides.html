Chapter 3: I Am, You Are

Three Words, Every Sentence

am · is · are

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am from Brazil."

"My name is Leão."

"You are very kind."

"It is my first time here."

WhoWordExample
IamI am from Brazil.
He / She / It / My nameisMy name is Leão.
You / We / TheyareYou are very kind.

In English the sentence is never complete without one of these three. "My name Leão" does not exist. "My name is Leão" does.

Real Speech

How People Really Say It

I am  →  I'm

"I'm from Brazil."

You are  →  You're

"You're very kind."

It is  →  It's

"It's my first time here."

"I'm Leão. I'm from Brazil. It's my first time here."

the three short forms together — this is real speed

No, and the Question

Say No, and Ask

"Are you from Brazil?"

Comprehension

What Did Claire Say?

1. Where is Claire from?
She is from Chicago.
2. Why is Claire in the city?
She is here for work. She is here for a meeting.
3. Is this her first time in the city?
Yes, it is. It is her first time.
