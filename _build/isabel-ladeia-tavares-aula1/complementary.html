Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Nothing here takes more than twenty minutes. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 1 -- Back in the Rehearsal Room

Series
The Good Place (O Bom Lugar)

You are already rewatching it, so this costs you nothing extra: switch one episode to English audio with English subtitles. The characters spend half of every episode explaining who they used to be before they arrived, which is exactly the structure of this lesson, in short and very clear sentences.

Tip: pick one twenty-two minute episode and count how many times somebody says used to. Write down two of those sentences and change them into sentences about yourself.

Podcast
Nervous about making a speech? Life Kit can help

Fifteen minutes of American English on the exact thing this lesson is about: what happens to a person who is fine in a small group and freezes in front of a room. A speechwriter and a speech coach take it apart, and the page has the audio and the full text of the episode side by side.

Tip: listen once with the text hidden, then read it while it plays a second time. Write down the three expressions they use for being nervous, and see which one is closest to stage fright.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Julian Treasure -- How to speak so that people want to listen

Ten minutes on the voice itself: how to project, what pace does to an audience, and six vocal exercises that any actor would recognise as a warm-up. He speaks slowly and articulates every word, which makes this one of the easiest talks on the site to follow without subtitles.

Tip: do the six exercises at the end out loud, once, in your room. Then read one paragraph of anything in English and notice what changed.
