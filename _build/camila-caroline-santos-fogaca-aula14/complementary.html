Film
Margin Call (2011)

One night inside an investment bank, when a young analyst finds that the numbers do not add up. A film about how people explain numbers to the people above them, from the analyst to the CEO.

Tip: listen for the numbers the characters say: percentages, millions and billions. Write down three sentences with a number and read them to your teacher.

Podcast
The Indicator from Planet Money -- NPR

Short episodes, about ten minutes each, that explain one number from the economy: a percentage, a price or a change year over year. Clear American English.

Tip: choose one episode and write down every number you hear. Then check: fifteen or fifty? thirteen or thirty?

Video
How to Say BIG NUMBERS in English -- Speak English With Vanessa

A clear lesson on how to read big numbers out loud in American English: thousands, millions, decimals and the words in between.

Tip: pause the video at each example and say the number before the teacher does. Then read the total cost of a request out loud the same way.
