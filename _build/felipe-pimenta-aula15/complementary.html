Lesson 15 -- Food, Culture & Daily Life

Series
Somebody Feed Phil (Netflix)

Phil Rosenthal travels the world and does exactly one thing: he sits down with strangers and talks about food until they are friends. Connection to Lesson 15: this is small talk at native speed, in restaurants and kitchens, full of the question tags and short responses you practised.

Tip: watch one episode with English subtitles and count how many times he hands a question back instead of ending on his own answer.

Podcast
Gastropod -- Canned Tomatoes and the Myth of the San Marzano

A free episode on how one Italian tomato became the most overrated and most counterfeited ingredient in the world. Connection to Lesson 15: it is exactly the kind of story that turns a dinner conversation from polite into genuinely interesting, and it lives in your own territory of food and cooking.

Tip: listen once without notes, then tell the story out loud in ninety seconds and finish by asking a question back.

Video
Celeste Headlee -- 10 Ways to Have a Better Conversation (TED)

A former radio host on why most people listen only to reply, and the small habits that keep a conversation alive. Connection to Lesson 15: her core advice is the move you drilled tonight — answer, then hand the turn back, instead of stopping on yourself.

Tip: pick the two habits you break most often and try only those at your next dinner.
