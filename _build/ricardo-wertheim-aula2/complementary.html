Grammar Video
"The Past Simple Tense" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the tense you used all through this lesson: the -ed of regular verbs, the new word of irregular ones, and the didn't that carries the past on its own. Connection to Lesson 2: this is the grammar behind "we walked", "I went" and "we didn't look at a map".

Tip: every time they give an example, pause and say the same sentence about one of your own trips. If the verb is irregular, say it three times before you continue.

Podcast
"AT#763 -- Travel to Croatia" -- Amateur Traveler

A traveler tells the whole story of her trip through Croatia, from Dubrovnik up the coast to Split and the islands. One hour of exactly what this lesson is about: a past trip, told out loud, in the past simple. Connection to Lesson 2: listen for went, saw, ate, took and stayed — they are everywhere.

Tip: listen to the first fifteen minutes only. Write down five past verbs you hear, then use all five to tell your own Croatia plans back to yourself.
