Chapter 3: The Code

Four Futures, Four Jobs

will · going to · I am meeting · will have done

Grammar Discovery

Read and Notice

1. "Food inflation will stay high next year."

2. "We are going to review prices every quarter."

3. "I am meeting the board on Monday at nine."

4. "By March, we will have finished the rollout."

Four sentences about the future. Which one is an opinion? Which one is a decision? Which one is in the calendar? Which one is finished before a date?

FormJobExample
will + verbA prediction or opinion, a promise, or a decision you make now.Inflation will stay high. I will send you the file.
be going to + verbA plan already decided, or a prediction with evidence now.We are going to review prices every quarter.
Present continuousA fixed arrangement: date, place, other people.I am meeting the board on Monday.
will have + participleFuture perfect: finished before a point in the future. Look for by + a date.By March, we will have finished the rollout.
Negatives and questionswon't review · aren't going to match · Are you meeting the board? · Will you have finished by March?

One line to remember: opinion = will, decision = going to, calendar = -ing, deadline = will have.

Food inflation, which will stay high in the first half.
2. How many new stores are they opening, and is it still only a plan?
Eight, in the first half. It is not only a plan: the contracts are already signed.

By October, at the current run rate.

Your Plans After the Course

Advice before a visa interview. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00

That you are going home after the course, not your dreams.
2. What are the three parts of a good answer?
The fixed arrangement (the flight back), the plan (using the pricing models at work), and a date with what will be finished by then.

The return ticket, because a date on paper says more than any promise.
