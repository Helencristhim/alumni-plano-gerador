Chapter 3: The Code

Big, Medium, Small

in November · on Monday · at ten o'clock

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The event is in November."

"The meeting is on Monday."

"The meeting is at ten o'clock."

"I work in the afternoon, but at night I rest."

WordSize of timeExample
inBIG: months, years, parts of the dayin November · in 2026 · in the morning
onMEDIUM: days and dateson Monday · on October 15th
atSMALL: the exact hourat ten o'clock · at half past two
exceptionat night — but in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening.

A funnel: in the big piece, on the day, at the hour.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"The meeting is in Monday."
"The meeting is on Monday."
"My class is in eight fifteen."
"My class is at eight fifteen."
"I work in the night."
"I work at night."

One exception to learn by heart: at night. Everything else in the day is in: in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening.

What Time Is the Meeting?

T
Hi, Joice. Did you see the calendar for this month?
J
Yes, I did. What time is the group meeting?
T
It is on Monday, at ten in the morning. One hour.
J
Ten in the morning is perfect. And the technical session?
T
On Thursday, at three in the afternoon, in room four.
J
Sorry, can you repeat the day, please?
T
Thursday. T, H, U, R. And the big event is in November.
J
Thank you. My schedule is very full this month.
T
I know. But there is no meeting on Friday, so you have the day.
J
Very good. And what time does the event start in November?
