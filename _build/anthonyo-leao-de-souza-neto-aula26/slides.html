ExampleHow?
Short wordcold → the coldest · cheap → the cheapestthe + word + -est
Short, one vowelbig → the biggest · hot → the hottestdouble the last letter
Word with -yfriendly → the friendliest · happy → the happiesty becomes -iest
Long wordbeautiful → the most beautifulthe most + word
Specialgood → the best · bad → the worstlearn them by heart
Lesson 23colder THAN Oslo (two things) — the coldest OF the trip (all of them)

Always THE before it: the best, the coldest, the most beautiful.

The Sound

How It Sounds

"the coldest day"

-EST is short: COLD-est, the T is soft

"the best meal"

BEST, with ST — not BES

"the most beautiful view"

the stress goes on BEAU: the most BEAU-tiful

"This is the best day of the trip!"

the sentence for the last night

The Best and the Worst

C
So, the last night. What was the best day of the trip for you?
L
The picnic with Ingrid and Mads. They are the friendliest people we met.
C
I agree. And the most beautiful place?
L
The view from the top of the tower, here in Oslo.
C
Three hundred stairs! I was the slowest person on the tower.
L
Yes, but you climbed them all. And the worst moment?
C
Easy. Tuesday by the lake. The coldest day of my life.
L
Of my life too! I'm from Brazil. Minus two degrees is not normal for me.
C
Ha! And the best meal? The fish on the island?
L
No. For me, the fish soup at the market yesterday. It was the cheapest and the best.
C
Really? I think the island was better. But OK. Would you like to come back next year?
L
Sure, I'd love to. But in the summer, please!
Comprehension

What Did Claire Say?

1. Who was the slowest person on the tower?
Claire. She was the slowest, but she climbed all the stairs.
2. What was the worst moment of the trip for Claire?
Tuesday by the lake, the coldest day of her life.
3. What was the best meal for Claire?
The fish on the island. She thinks it was better.
