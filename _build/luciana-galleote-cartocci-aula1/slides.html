Chapter 3: The Code

What You Do, or What You Are Doing?

Two present tenses for one company

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Show?

"We provide infrastructure for hospitals every year."

"This month we are installing cameras in a new hospital wing."

"Our partners usually come from the United States and Europe."

"At the moment we are working with a company from Silicon Valley."

Look at the time words in red. Some mean always, in general: every year, usually. Others mean now, not forever: this month, at the moment. The verb in blue changes with them.

JobFormExample
A fact about the companypresent simple: we + verbWe provide infrastructure for hospitals.
A fact with he / she / itpresent simple + -sThe company specializes in hospitals.
A project happening nowam / is / are + verb-ingAt the moment we are expanding our work.
A project for this montham / is / are + verb-ing + this monthThis month we are installing cameras.
The two questionsWhat does your company do? / What are you working on?What does your company do? — What are you working on now?
The trapstate verbs stay simpleNot we are having many clients. Say we have many clients.

In one line: the simple form says what the company is and does; the -ing form says what it is doing now.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

The Call, at Real Speed

One call, three recordings, one company profile

Dialogue

The First Call

A
Good morning, Luciana. I am Anna Keller. I manage partnerships in Latin America for a European technology group. Thank you for your time.
L
Good morning, Anna. Thank you for the invitation. I am the CEO of Teleinfo, a family-owned company in Brazil.
A
Nice to meet you. So, what does Teleinfo do, exactly?
L
We specialize in infrastructure: structured cabling, cameras, access control and data centers. Most of our clients are hospitals.
A
Hospitals are exactly our focus. We are looking for a local partner in Brazil this year. Which hospitals do you work with?
L
Our client base includes Einstein, Sírio-Libanês, HCor and Oswaldo Cruz. We manage the projects end to end.
A
That is a strong track record. Our group has offices in Munich and Madrid, and at the moment we are opening an office in São Paulo.
L
Great timing. We want to expand with international partners. I will send you our company profile after the call.
Comprehension

About Anna

1. What does Anna do, and for which region?
She manages partnerships in Latin America for a European technology group.
2. What is Anna’s group looking for this year?
A local partner in Brazil.
3. Where does the group have offices, and what is it doing at the moment?
In Munich and Madrid. At the moment it is opening an office in São Paulo.
Listening 1 · The Partner Manager

What I Want to Hear in the First Minute

A partner manager at an American technology company explains what he wants to know about a new local partner. Sound first — no text.

1. How many local partners does he talk to every month?
About ten.
2. What is the problem with a long history of the company?
He does not have time for it: he wants to know what the company does now.
3. What three things does he want to hear?
What the company does, who its main clients are, and what it is working on at the moment.
Listening 2 · The Hospital IT Director

What a Hospital Needs

The IT director of a hospital in London describes the infrastructure of his hospital. Sound first — no text.

1. How many people work in his hospital?
About four thousand people.
2. Why is the network so important for him?
Because every machine, every camera and every door depends on it. If the network stops, the hospital stops.
3. What is his team doing this year?
They are building a new data center and they are changing the access control in the old building.
Listening 3 · The Conference Host

Three Companies, Three Minutes

The host of a technology conference in Singapore introduces the next three speakers. Sound first — no text.

1. Where is the first company from, and what does it do?
From India. It builds software for hospitals.
2. What is the second company doing this year?
It is opening its first office in Europe.
3. What does the host ask each speaker to do?
To present the company in one minute and say what it is working on now.
