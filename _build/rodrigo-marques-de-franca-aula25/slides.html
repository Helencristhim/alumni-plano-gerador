Chapter 3: The Code

Signs on the Road

Start, add, conclude, finish: one small word for each step.

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Small Words Do?

1. To begin with, the contract set a minimum speed of 120 packs a minute.

2. Furthermore, the seller knew about the problem in March.

3. Consequently, my client lost four months of production.

4. Finally, I will turn to the damages.

Which word opens? Which one adds? Which one gives a result? Which one closes? And what comes right after each one?

JobMarkersExample
OpenTo begin with, · First of all, · First,To begin with, the contract set a speed.
Next pointSecondly, · Next, · Turning to + noun,Turning to the damages, ...
AddFurthermore, · What is more, · In addition to + noun / -ingIn addition to the delay, the quality was poor.
ResultConsequently, · As a result, · It follows that + clauseIt follows that the seller must pay.
Last pointFinally, · Lastly,Finally, I will turn to the damages.
CloseTo sum up, · In short,To sum up, the machines were not the machines in the contract.
The trapsat last = after a long wait · resuming = starting againNot At last, the damages; not Resuming, ...; not In addition of

In one line: the marker goes first, then a comma, then a full sentence; each one tells the listener what kind of step comes next.

Chapter 4: Real Voices

In the Hearing Room

One hearing, two recordings, one schedule

Dialogue

The Claimant’s Opening

J
Good morning. We are ready to hear the opening statement for the claimant. You have forty minutes, Mr. França.
R
Thank you, Madam President. I will take the floor for no more than thirty. To begin with, let me lay out what this case is about.
J
Please. The tribunal has read both written submissions, so you can be brief.
R
In short, Atlântica bought four packaging machines from Brenner, and none of them ever worked at the agreed speed. I have three points. First, the contract set the speed. Secondly, the machines never reached it.
J
And your third point? I don't want to lose the thread.
R
Thirdly, Brenner knew about the problem in March and said nothing. Consequently, Atlântica lost four months of production.
J
Will you call a witness on that point? I imagine counsel for the respondent will want to cross-examine.
R
Yes. Our plant manager will give evidence this afternoon. Furthermore, the emails from March are in Exhibit C-12.
J
Thank you. The tribunal is following you. Please wrap up in the next five minutes.
R
Certainly. Finally, to drive the point home: a machine that cannot reach the speed in the contract is not the machine in the contract. It follows that Brenner must pay.
Comprehension

About Judith

1. How much time does Judith give for the opening statement?
Forty minutes.
2. Why does Judith say the opening can be brief?
Because the tribunal has already read both written submissions.
3. Who does Judith expect to cross-examine the witness?
Counsel for the respondent, Brenner’s lawyer.

Chapter 7: Wrap-Up

Five Signs to Keep

Survival Card

Five Phrases for the Floor

I have three points, and I will take them in order.
To begin with, let me lay out the facts.
Furthermore, the seller knew about the problem in March.
It follows that the seller must pay.
Finally, and this is my last point, I will turn to the damages.
Self-Assessment

What I Can Do Now

I say how many points I have before I start.
I open, add and close with to begin with, furthermore and finally.
I show a result with consequently or it follows that.
I don't say "at last" or "resuming" when I mean "finally" or "to sum up".
I know the words: the claimant, the respondent, to cross-examine, to signpost, to lose the thread.
