Lesson 19 -- Qualifying the Deal

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Help"

A short episode on asking for help and information at work politely. Many of the questions end with a preposition, exactly like the qualification questions in this lesson. Full script on the same page.

Tip: read the script and underline every question that ends with a small word like on, for, with or to. Say each one out loud.

Talk
Margaret Heffernan -- "Dare to disagree" (TED, 13 min)

A business leader explains why the best teams ask uncomfortable questions early instead of avoiding them. The same courage you need to ask a buyer whose budget it is and who else decides.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down one uncomfortable question from her examples, and then write the uncomfortable question you have not asked in one of your real deals.

Watch on TED ↗
Documentary series
Challenger: The Final Flight -- Episode 1

A documentary series about the space shuttle disaster of 1986. It shows a long chain of people, committees and approvals, and how one decision depended on many voices that were not heard.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Draw the decision map: who was the decision-maker, whose approval was needed, and who should have been asked.
