Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
MIT Interviewer: What We TRULY Look For | Crucial Tips for College Interviews

Somebody who interviews candidates for MIT explains what actually happens in that half hour, including the questions everybody is asked and the difference between an answer that was prepared and an answer that is real. This is the other side of the conversation you practised in class.

Tip: every time she describes a question, pause and turn it into a buried one before she moves on. “Tell me about yourself” becomes “Could you tell me a little about yourself?”

Podcast
Indirect Questions - 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on exactly the structure you learned today, from BBC Learning English. Two native speakers build the form out loud, with the word order and the disappearing helper verb explained in the same order the lesson used.

Tip: six minutes is the right length for one sitting. Listen once without stopping, then a second time with a pen, and write down the three examples they give.

Talk
The power of youth volunteerism | Timothy Lann | TEDxShuksanMiddleSchool

A student roughly your age, on a TEDx stage at his own middle school, talking about the volunteering he started himself. He is not describing an activity he joined for an application. He is describing a commitment, which is the word the interviewer used.

Tip: listen for what he does NOT say. He never explains why it looks good on paper, and that is exactly why it does.
