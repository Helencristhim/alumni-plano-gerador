Chapter 4: Language Focus

Recommend Doing, Remember to Do

verb patterns — verb + -ing / verb + to-infinitive

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I recommend keeping the call on Thursdays."

"Avoid promising new features on the call."

"Richard has agreed to support the renewal."

"Remember to send the report before the fifth."

After some verbs the next verb takes -ing; after others it takes to. Which verbs go in each group?

StructureUseExample
verb + -ingrecommend, suggest, avoid, keep, consider, finish, mindI recommend keeping the call.
verb + toagree, need, plan, decide, promise, want, remember (a task)Remember to send the report.
prefer + -ing / toBoth are correctDavid prefers talking to reading.
remember + -ingA memory of the past, not a taskI remember sending it in May.
stop + -ingYou do not do it any moreStop sending PDFs to finance.

There is no rule that tells you which verb takes -ing: you learn them in groups. The most common mistake in Brazil: "I recommend to keep" → "I recommend keeping".

The Three Sentences of a Handover

Three Sentences and Nothing Gets Lost

"I recommend keeping the monthly call on Thursdays."

1 — what to keep: recommend + -ing

"Remember to send the report before the fifth."

2 — what not to forget: remember + to

"Avoid promising new features without checking with product."

3 — what to avoid: avoid + -ing

Chapter 5: The Handover Call

Tom Has Questions

A new colleague, a note, and thirty minutes

Dialogue

What Should I Know?

T
I read your handover note twice. It is very clear, but I have a few questions before I take over.
P
Of course. The most important thing is continuity. I recommend keeping everything the same for the first two months.
T
Understood. What about David? Should I send him an email to introduce myself?
P
Please avoid sending a cold email. I will give you a warm introduction on Tuesday. And one quirk: he prefers talking on the phone.
T
Good to know. And what is their biggest pain point right now?
P
The monthly report. Remember to send it before the fifth, and send the spreadsheet, not a PDF.
T
I need to ramp up quickly. Can I shadow you on a call first?
P
Yes. I suggest joining my call with them in July. And I have agreed to stay on your first two calls.
Comprehension

About Tom

1. How many times did Tom read the handover note?

2. What was Tom planning to do to introduce himself to David?

3. What does Tom ask to do before he takes over the calls?
He asks to shadow Patrícia on a call first, because he needs to ramp up quickly.
