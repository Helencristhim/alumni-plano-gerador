Lesson 26 -- The Account Handover

Film
The Intern (2015)

Ben, a seventy-year-old retired executive, starts again as an intern at a fast online company. He has to shadow a founder who has no time for him, ramp up in a world that he does not know, and earn trust one small touchpoint at a time. The film is full of the advice that you practised today: what to keep doing, what to avoid, what somebody has agreed to do. Connection to Lesson 26: every handover is really a story about trust moving from one person to another.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down 5 sentences with recommend, suggest, avoid, agree or remember, and check the verb that comes after each one: -ing or to?

Podcast
All Ears English -- "Grammar Part 5: How to Avoid Misunderstandings with Infinitives and Gerunds"

Two American teachers explain, with real conversation examples, why some verbs want -ing and others want to, and which mistakes make a native speaker stop and think. They also explain the verbs that change meaning, like remember and stop. Connection to Lesson 26: the exact structure behind every line of your handover note, in a relaxed conversation that trains your ear.

Tip: listen once for the ideas. Listen again and write down 5 verbs with their pattern: recommend + -ing, agree + to... Then say one sentence about your own work with each one.

"Gerund or infinitive?" -- Ask BBC Learning English

A short, clear answer to the question that every intermediate student asks: when two verbs come together, is the second one -ing or to? The video gives the most common verbs in each group with simple examples. Connection to Lesson 26: a quick review of the grammar you used to hand over Northwind, in less than five minutes.
