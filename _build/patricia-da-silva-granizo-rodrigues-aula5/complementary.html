Talk
How to speak so that people want to listen -- Julian Treasure, TED

Ten minutes on the habits that lose a room and the ones that hold it. The section on register and prosody is the one for you.

Tip: after it, record thirty seconds of your own claim and listen for the two things he names. You will hear at least one of them.

Talk
The secret structure of great talks -- Nancy Duarte, TED

Eighteen minutes on how a persuasive case is shaped -- the same shape as a three-minute update to an executive team.

Tip: watch the first eight minutes. Then say your own claim using her shape: what is, what could be, what is.

Podcast
The art of conversation -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes, with the transcript on the same page. British accents, which is useful contrast after an hour of American ones.

Tip: listen once without the transcript, then once with it, and mark only the words you did not catch the first time.
