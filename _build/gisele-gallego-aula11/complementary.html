Lesson 11 -- Having People Over

Film
Guess Who’s Coming to Dinner (1967) -- Stanley Kramer, with Sidney Poitier, Spencer Tracy and Katharine Hepburn

A whole film about one dinner invitation in San Francisco: who is coming, who is staying, and what a host does when the evening does not go as planned. Every scene is somebody being welcomed, offered a drink or asked to stay.

Tip: start with the trailer below, then watch the film with English subtitles. Write down every offer you hear with would you like or shall.

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) -- Hosting Tips for Book Clubs, Dinner Parties and Gatherings, with Priya Parker

An expert on gatherings explains what a host is really for: how many people to invite, how to start the conversation and how to take care of the group. Clear American English, and the same idea as tonight’s third listening.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then write down three things you could do at your own housewarming.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
How to Host an Amazing Dinner Party -- Vanessa Van Edwards

A short, practical video about receiving people at home: what to say at the door, how to make guests feel welcome and how to keep the table talking. Fast, natural American speech.

Tip: watch with English subtitles first. On the second time, pause after each tip and say it as an offer: Would you like... or Shall I...
