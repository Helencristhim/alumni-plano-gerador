Comprehension

About the Call

1. What condition did the buyer's lawyer set?
They wanted two hundred thousand credits, but only if the credits were issued by the end of March.
2. What did the verifier confirm, and what did he note?
He confirmed that the map annex had arrived, and he noted that the discrepancy was still under review.
3. What was the disagreement about the price, and how did the call end?
The finance director warned that the price might fall and argued against a fixed price; the buyer said a fixed price was not negotiable. They said they would send a draft by Friday.

What did they actually say? And what happened to each verb when it moved into your report?

They saidYou reportExample
present simple: "We want..."past simpleShe stated that they wanted more credits.
present perfect: "It has arrived."past perfectHe confirmed that it had arrived.
will / may / canwould / might / couldHe warned that the price might fall.
Verb patternsstate, confirm, note, acknowledge, point out, argue, warn + that. advise and warn + somebody + to: "I advised them to link the payment."
say or tell?say something; tell somebody something: "She told me that...", never "she said me".
Why it matters heresaid is neutral. The other verbs tell the listener what the speaker was doing: giving a fact, a warning, a reason or an admission.

Sound Check

Say It Clearly

the -ed of reporting verbs · the sentences of this lesson

Why this one is hard for a Portuguese speaker

The habit: saying every -ed as a separate syllable, so that warned sounds like warn-ed and confirmed has three syllables.

In English: the -ed is a new syllable only after a t or a d: stat-ed, point-ed. Everywhere else it is just a d or a t glued to the verb: warned is one syllable, confirmed is two.

The test: count the syllables of warned, acknowledged and stated before you say them. One, three, two.

Chapter 5: Real Speed

The Summary and the Verb

How the meeting closed, and how to report it without taking sides

Listening 1

Before We Close

The buyer's lawyer summarizes the meeting. Sound first, no text.

1. What was decided, and on what condition?
They agreed in principle to buy two hundred thousand credits, subject to verification.
2. What was the objection, and why does the buyer keep its position?
The finance director argued that a fixed price exposed them if the market fell. The buyer needs a fixed price because its board approves budgets a year in advance.
3. What is the next step, and what did she refuse to confirm?
They will send a draft clause by Friday, with comments due within a week. She said linking the payment to the issuance could work, but she could not confirm it that day.
Listening 2

The Verb Is a Decision

A senior lawyer on reporting what other people said. Sound first, no text.

1. Why does he say the reporting verb is a decision?
Because each verb carries information, and the reader will trust it: it tells them what the speaker was doing, not only what they said.
2. What does acknowledged tell the reader?
That the person accepted a point they did not like.
3. Which verb signals doubt, and when is it a problem?
Claimed. In your own notes it may be fine, but in an email the other side will read, it is close to an accusation.
