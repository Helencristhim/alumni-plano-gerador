Film
12 Angry Men (1957)

Twelve jurors in one room retell, question and reinterpret what the witnesses said. Almost every scene is an argument about who said what, and whether it was true. Short, clear and in one room.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Pause after each juror retells a testimony and report it yourself in one sentence, one step back.

Podcast
Learning English Grammar (BBC) -- Reported speech

Six minutes from BBC Learning English on exactly what this lesson practices: what happens to tenses, pronouns and word order when you report what somebody else said.

Tip: every time they give an example, pause and report it yourself before you hear their version. Then compare.

Video
Julian Treasure: 5 Ways to Listen Better

You can only report well what you heard well. A short talk on active listening, with five exercises that fit into a working day.

Tip: after watching, report the five ways to somebody in five sentences, each one starting with "He argued that..." or "He pointed out that...".
