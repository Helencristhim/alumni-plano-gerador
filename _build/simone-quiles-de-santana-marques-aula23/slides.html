Chapter 3 -- The Obligation Code

Required · Permitted · Prohibited

The passive voice of a rule

Discovery

Listen and Notice

Look at the orange words. Who is doing the requiring, and which form of the verb follows each one?

"The company is required to file the report within thirty days."

"We are permitted to request an extension, but only once."

"Staff are prohibited from contacting the person who raised the concern."

"The report must be signed by a director before it is filed."

FormUseExample
be required to + base verban obligation the rules impose"We are required to file within thirty days."
be permitted to + base verba permission, usually with a limit"We are permitted to request one extension."
be prohibited from + -inga hard ban, no exceptions"Staff are prohibited from contacting the whistleblower."
must be + past participlethe rule in the shortest passive form"The report must be signed by a director."
be not required to + base verbno obligation -- very different from a ban"We are not required to disclose the name."

Key idea: the passive leaves out who requires it, because the rule stands on its own and not on a person. Watch the shape of each one: required to and permitted to take the base verb; prohibited from takes the -ing form. And not required to is not a ban: it only means there is no obligation.

The Structure

One Rule, Three Levels

Same regulator, same letter. Only the level of obligation moves.

Level 1 · Obligation

"The company is required to file the report within thirty days."

No choice. Miss it and you are explaining yourself in writing, as we are this week.

Level 2 · Permission

"We are permitted to request an extension, in writing, before the deadline."

A door that exists, with conditions attached. Permission is not the same as no obligation.

Level 3 · Prohibition

"Staff are prohibited from contacting the person who raised the concern."

A hard line. There is no version of this with a good reason attached.

Writing "we are not required to" where the rule says "we are prohibited from" is not a language mistake. It is a compliance one.

Artefact

Letter from the Regulator

REQUEST FOR INFORMATION

Ref. RFI/2026/114 · Response due within 15 working days

Two matters, for the attention of the legal department.

1. Late filing

The company is required to file the quarterly report within 30 days. The report was received on day 34. Explain, and describe the safeguard now in place.

2. Internal report of March

Describe the steps taken after the concern was raised through the whistleblower channel, with dates.

3. Extension

The company is permitted to request one extension. Any request must be made in writing before the deadline.

4. Protection of the reporting person

All staff are prohibited from contacting the person who raised the concern while this request is open.

5. Evidence

Every statement in the response must be supported by the audit trail. Disclosure must be complete.

Your first line: "The company is required to file within thirty days. The report was filed on day thirty-four."

S

"We are permitted to request one extension, in writing, before the deadline. I will decide by Monday, and the disclosure goes out complete or not at all."

3. What is Daniel worried about with the deadline?
That fifteen working days is tight for the March matter, so he asks whether they should request more time.
