WordUse it forExample
thisone thing, NEAR youthis kitchen
thatone thing, FAR from youthat school
thesetwo or more, NEAR youthese bedrooms
thosetwo or more, FAR from youthose buildings
name + 'swhose is it?my daughter's school · Ricardo's apartment

Near: this / these. Far: that / those. Whose: Ricardo's.

Whose Is It?

Ricardo's Home

"This is Ricardo's apartment."

Ricardo's = of Ricardo

"That is my wife's car."

my wife's = of my wife

"My daughter's bedroom is small."

my daughter's = of my daughter

"Amy's desk is near my desk."

Amy's = of Amy

Apartment for rent in Brickell, Miami

This is a new apartment on the tenth floor. The living room is big, with three big windows.

The kitchen is small, but it is new. There are two bedrooms. The big bedroom is for the parents, and the small bedroom is perfect for a child.

The balcony is the best part: from the balcony, you see the ocean.

The neighborhood is quiet, and the subway station is near. The school is ten minutes from the building.
