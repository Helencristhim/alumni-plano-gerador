Chapter 3: The Code

Reported Speech

He said that · she asked whether · they warned that

Grammar Discovery

Read and Notice

"The plan is approved." → He said that the plan was approved.

"Margins have improved." → She noted that margins had improved.

"The budget will not grow." → He warned that the budget would not grow.

"Can the model be extended?" → They asked whether the model could be extended.

In each pair, what happens to the verb when you repeat it?

DirectReportedExample
present simplepast simple"It is approved" → he said it was approved
present perfect / pastpast perfect"Margins have improved" → she noted they had improved
will / canwould / could"We will start" → they confirmed they would start
yes or no questionwhether / if"Can it be extended?" → they asked whether it could be extended
Beyond "said"stated, confirmed, indicated, suggested, warned, argued, noted, emphasized — each one tells the listener how strong the statement was.

The tense moves one step back. The word that is optional; whether is not.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"He asked what was the rationale."
"He asked what the rationale was."
"She said that margins have improved last quarter."
"She said that margins had improved last quarter."
"He said me that the budget was frozen."
"He told me that the budget was frozen."

In reported questions the word order is normal, not inverted. The tense moves one step back. And you tell someone, but you say something.

Detail

Beyond Said

The verb tells the listener how strong the statement was

A risk, said with concern: he ___warned that the timeline was too short.
An idea, offered gently: she ___suggested that we restructure the team.
A fact, made official: the board ___confirmed that the plan had been approved.
A position, defended hard: two directors ___argued that the budget was too small.

Dialogue

The Debrief

N
I could not join yesterday. What happened with the southern plan?
M
The chief executive confirmed that the plan had been approved, with one condition.
N
One condition. Which one?
M
The finance director warned that the budget would not be increased before the second quarter.
N
So we deliver phase one with the team we already have. Did anyone push back?
M
Two directors argued that the timeline was too short, and the board noted their position in the minutes.
N
And the fresh categories? I need to tell my stakeholders something today.
M
They asked whether the model could be extended this year, and we said that it could, but not before September.
Comprehension

About Nadia

1. Why does Nadia need the debrief?
She could not join the board meeting yesterday.
2. What does she conclude about phase one?
That they will deliver it with the team they already have.
3. Why does she ask about the fresh categories?
Because she has to tell her stakeholders something today.
