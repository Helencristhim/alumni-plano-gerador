Video
"How to use reported speech" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on the step back: is becomes was, have becomes had, will becomes would. Exactly the move you practised in today's debrief.

Tip: after the video, report three things a colleague actually said to you this week, out loud, each with a different verb: confirmed, warned, suggested.

Talk
"10 ways to have a better conversation" -- Celeste Headlee, TED

Eleven minutes from a professional interviewer on listening well enough to repeat what someone actually said. That accuracy is the whole skill behind a debrief.

Tip: turn on the English transcript. Pick her three strongest rules and report each one out loud: "She argued that...", "She warned that...", "She suggested that...".

Research on how a difficult message is received, depending on how it is delivered. Every paragraph reports what somebody said, so it is reported speech in its natural habitat.
