The Time Map

Six tenses you know — one map

Review Discovery

Where Is Each Verb on the Map?

"I used to plan every sentence in Portuguese."

"Last month I gave the SAP talk at the conference."

"I have run three project calls in English."

"I have been using smart fillers since July."

"I am going to lead the status call in November."

"If I prepare three key points, I will feel confident."

Past, up to now, or future? Put each orange verb on the map.

TimeFormExample
Old habit (past)used to + verbI used to plan every sentence.
Finished, with a timepast simpleLast month I gave the talk.
Up to now, no timehave / has + past participleI have run three calls.
Started and still goinghave been + -ingI have been using fillers since July.
Plan for the futuream / is / are going to + verbI am going to lead the call.
Real conditionif + present, will + verbIf I prepare, I will feel confident.

A time word like last month or in 2024 takes the past simple. Since and so far go with have.

Chapter 4

The Check-in

Greg and Daniela look back at six months

Dialogue

The Mid-Year Check-in

G
So, Daniela, it is our mid-year check-in. How do you feel about your English now?
D
Much better, honestly. I used to plan every sentence. Now my fluency is much better, and I just speak.
G
I can see that. Your SAP talk last month was great. What else have you done since our last review?
D
I have run three project calls in English. That was outside my comfort zone, but I did it.
G
Real progress. Was there any setback?
D
Yes. On the London call, I hesitated a lot on the budget question. Since then, I have been using a smart filler: let me think.
G
Good strategy. So what is your goal for the next quarter?
D
I am going to lead the weekly status call. And if I forget a word, I will rephrase and keep going.
