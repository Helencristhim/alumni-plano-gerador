Complementary Activities

Extra material to reinforce your learning outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen.

Lesson 1 -- Who Is Ricardo?

Grammar Video
"Present Simple and Present Continuous" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the tense you used all through this lesson: the present simple for facts and routines, and the little -s that belongs to he, she and it. Connection to Lesson 1: this is the grammar behind "I live in Sao Paulo" and "my wife speaks English".

Tip: pause after each example and say one sentence about yourself, then the same sentence about your wife. If the second one has no -s, say it again.

Podcast
"578b Walking Ljubljana; Munich and Bavaria; Wild Animals" -- Travel with Rick Steves

A local guide walks the listener through Ljubljana, the capital you will visit in August. Slow, clear American English, and two people simply talking about a city. Connection to Lesson 1: this is the register you practised in the lobby -- asking a local what they love about their own town.

Tip: listen only to the first segment on Ljubljana. Write down three things the guide says about the city, then say them out loud as your own opinion: "In my opinion, Ljubljana is..."

Travel Series
"Dubrovnik and Balkan Side Trips" -- Rick Steves' Europe, Season 6 Episode 3

The full episode about your first destination: the old town, the six hundred year old walls, the stone streets and the local food. Connection to Lesson 1: every word from today appears here -- destination, architecture, heritage, cuisine, landmark, and a lot of wandering.

Tip: watch it once with English subtitles, then a second time with the subtitles off. Count how many of the eight new words you can hear without reading them.
