Lesson 18 -- Listening Under Pressure

Conference Talk
"5 ways to listen better" -- Julian Treasure, TED

A sound expert explains why we are losing our listening and gives five simple exercises to train it. Short, clear British English -- and practical tools for the listening problem you described in our first class.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Choose one of his five exercises and do it every day this week, for three minutes, with an English podcast.

Podcast
"Why are we all so stressed?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters talk about stress: what it does to the body and to the mind, and what helps. Six minutes, with a transcript and a vocabulary list -- useful for the pressure of a Q&A.

Tip: listen once at normal speed, without the transcript, and write only the key words. Then check with the transcript: how much could you infer?

Video
"How to make stress your friend" -- Kelly McGonigal, TED

A health psychologist presents a study that changed how she sees stress: the body's stress response can help you perform. A researcher telling the story of a finding -- and good news before your Q&A.

Tip: pause after each study she mentions and ask yourself an indirect question about it: "I'd like to know how they measured..."
