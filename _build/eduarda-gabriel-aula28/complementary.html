Lesson 28 -- Term Sheets and LOIs

Film
Barbarians at the Gate (HBO, 1993)

The true story of the fight to buy RJR Nabisco in 1988, one of the biggest deals in history. Bankers, lawyers and two groups of buyers argue about the price and the terms, offer after offer. Connection to Lesson 28: you see how every letter and every term decides who wins the deal.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Each time a new offer arrives, pause and say what changed: the price, the payment or the conditions.
