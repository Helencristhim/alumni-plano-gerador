Chapter 3 -- The Code

Shall, May or May Not?

The small words that decide who pays

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Purple Words Have in Common?

"The buyer shall pay the purchase price on the closing date."

"The seller may not talk to other buyers for sixty days."

"NorthPack is entitled to keep ten million in escrow for eighteen months."

"The seller is required to give warranties about the accounts."

Which words mean "you have to", "you have the right to" and "you can't"?

MeaningIn a contract
Obligation (you have to)shall / must / is required to + verb
Right (you can, if you want)may / is entitled to + verb
Prohibition (you can't)may not / shall not / must not + verb
Formshall / may / must + base verb. Is entitled / is required + TO + verb.
NeverNot "shall to pay" / "is entitled keep" / "doesn't may talk"
Grammar Rule

Contract English or Plain English?

In the term sheetWhat Eduarda tells Victor
"The Buyer shall pay the purchase price at closing.""NorthPack has to pay you on the closing date."

Chapter 4 -- Getting There

Before He Signs

One call, two recordings, one term sheet

Dialogue

Eduarda and Victor

V
Eduarda, I read the letter of intent twice. Why do they want to keep ten million of my money?
E
That's the escrow. The money isn't theirs. A bank holds it for eighteen months, and then it comes back to you.
V
Unless there's a problem, I imagine. Is this about the supplier case?
E
Exactly. If Lane loses the case, NorthPack is entitled to take the cost from the escrow.
V
And this part about warranties? It sounds like a trap.
E
It isn't. A warranty is just a promise that the facts are true: the accounts, the pension plan, the contracts.
V
What happens if one of those promises turns out to be wrong?
E
Then the indemnity applies. You pay for the loss, but there's a cap: twenty percent of the purchase price.
V
Twenty percent is a lot of money. Can we ask for less?
E
We can. The term sheet isn't binding yet, except for exclusivity. I'd ask for ten percent.
Comprehension

About Victor

1. How many times did Victor read the letter of intent?
Twice.
2. What does Victor think the part about warranties sounds like?
A trap.
3. What does Victor say about the twenty percent?
That it is a lot of money, and he wants to ask for less.

How to Read a Term Sheet

A lawyer explains what to look for in a term sheet. Sound first -- no text.

1. Is most of a term sheet binding?
No, most of it is not binding.
2. Which two clauses are usually binding?
Exclusivity and confidentiality.
3. What does may mean in a contract?
That something is allowed.
Real Document

The Term Sheet

From: Sarah Kim, Corporate Development, NorthPack
To: Victor Lane; Eduarda Gabriel, Houlihan Lokey
Re: Project Lane -- Letter of Intent and Term Sheet (non-binding)

1. Purchase price: The Buyer shall pay USD 105 million on the closing date.
2. Escrow: USD 10 million shall be held in escrow for 18 months.
3. Warranties: The Seller is required to give warranties on the accounts, the pension plan and the main customer contracts.
4. Indemnity: The Buyer is entitled to claim under the indemnity, up to a cap of 20% of the purchase price.
5. Exclusivity (binding): The Seller may not talk to other buyers for 60 days.
6. Closing date: March 31.

Kind regards,
Sarah Kim

1. How long will the money stay in escrow?
Eighteen months.
2. Which clause is binding?
Exclusivity: the seller may not talk to other buyers for sixty days.
3. What is the maximum that Victor could pay under the indemnity?
Twenty percent of the purchase price: twenty-one million dollars.
