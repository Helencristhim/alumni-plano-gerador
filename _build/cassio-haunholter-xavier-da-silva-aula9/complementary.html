5 ways to listen better

Five concrete exercises for listening consciously, including one for filtering an unfamiliar voice out of noise. Short, and directly applicable to a call with Finnish, Swedish and American voices at the same time.

Tip: after each exercise, say back what he said using reported speech, starting with he said that we should.

307. Accent-uate, Don’t Compensate

A Stanford business podcast arguing that clarity, and not a native accent, is what makes you understood, and showing how to ask for and give clarification. The single most on-topic episode for an international call.

Tip: write down three ways the speakers check understanding, and use one of them on your next call.

A dialect coach takes apart what actually differs between English accents: vowels, rhythm and stress, including regional American varieties. Once you know what to listen for, an unfamiliar accent stops being a wall.
