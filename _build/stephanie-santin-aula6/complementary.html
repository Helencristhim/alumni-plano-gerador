Lesson 6 -- If I Had a Year, I Would Go

Talk
Adam Grant -- "The surprising habits of original thinkers"

On the people who imagine an alternative and then act on it. Full of hypothetical sentences, said fast, by somebody who is enjoying himself.

Tip: count how many sentences start with "if". Then rewrite two of them about your own decision, out loud.

Talk
Nina Tandon -- "Could tissue engineering mean personalized medicine?"

A short talk about a medicine that does not exist yet. The whole argument lives in the conditional, which is how research is described before it works.

Tip: after watching, say three sentences about what would change in your own field if her idea worked.

Guided Study
TED-Ed -- "How does anesthesia work?"

A short animated lesson with comprehension questions. Familiar science, unfamiliar words, and a good test of listening without a dictionary.

Tip: answer the questions out loud. Then explain in three sentences what would happen if a patient reacted badly.
