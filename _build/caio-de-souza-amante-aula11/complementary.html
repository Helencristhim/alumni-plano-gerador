Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
BBC Learning English — “Gerund or infinitive?”

A short, clear lesson on the structure of this class: which verbs take to, which take -ing, and the small group that changes meaning. It is a few minutes long, so it fits between two meetings.

Tip: pause after every example and say your own version about Dataside before the teacher gives the next one.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Podcast
Freakonomics Radio — “The Upside of Quitting”

An economist's case for stopping things on purpose: projects, jobs, plans that no longer pay back. For a founder deciding whether to sell, it is a useful conversation about the cost of continuing. The page has a full transcript.

Tip: every time you hear stop, decide, keep or avoid, notice the next word — is it to or -ing? That is this lesson, in natural speech.

Listen on Freakonomics ↗
Talk
Julian Treasure — “How to speak so that people want to listen” (TED)

A sound expert on the habits that make people stop listening — and one of them is exactly the vague language this lesson removes. Ten minutes, and he gives exercises you can do before an important call.

Tip: try his warm-up before your next meeting in English, then check whether your first sentence was a decision or a mood.
