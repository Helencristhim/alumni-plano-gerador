The Core

The Term Sheet

The buyer has sent four demands. Every answer has to say exactly what you will and will not do.

  • Round 1: the clock. One demand, one sentence: agree to, refuse to, insist on.
  • Round 2: the lawyer. Every try has to become a commitment.
  • Round 3: swap. You are the buyer, and you catch my mistakes.

The Promise You Can Keep

One sentence a buyer could copy into the contract: what will you keep doing after the sale?

I can choose between to and -ing after the verbs of a negotiation.
I can say what I stopped doing and what I intend to do, without try.
I can answer a buyer’s demand with agree to, refuse to or insist on.
