Lesson 23 -- Closing Gaps

Podcast
"Is rejection good for us?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters talk about how we feel when somebody says no, and what we can learn from it. Six minutes, with a transcript -- useful before a congress, where not every question will be friendly.

Tip: listen once and count the hedges (might, maybe, probably) and the boosters (clearly, definitely) the presenters use. Which do they use more?

Conference Talk
"The power of believing that you can improve" -- Carol Dweck, TED

A psychologist from Stanford presents her research on the "growth mindset": people who see a weak spot as "not yet" learn more. A researcher who is direct about her data, in clear American English.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write one sentence where she is very sure (a booster) and one where she is careful (a hedge).

Video
"How to practice effectively... for just about anything" -- Annie Bosler and Don Greene, TED-Ed

A short animated lesson about what happens in the brain when we practice, and why short, focused practice on our weak spots works better than long, easy practice. Five minutes.

Tip: watch it, then plan your own practice for Bangkok in three sentences, out loud: what, how long and how often.
