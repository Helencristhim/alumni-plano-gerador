Chapter 4: This or That?

This or That?

Near and far, one and many

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"This bag is beautiful."

"That bag is sixty dollars."

"These shoes are forty dollars."

"Those sunglasses are expensive!"

Look at the blue words. Near you or far from you? One thing or many?

JobFormExample
Near + onethisThis bag is beautiful.
Far + onethatThat bag is sixty dollars.
Near + two or moretheseThese shoes are forty dollars.
Far + two or morethoseThose sunglasses are expensive.
With the verbthis / that + is; these / those + areThis is a gift. These are gifts.

In one line: Near: this / these. Far: that / those. One: is. Many: are.
