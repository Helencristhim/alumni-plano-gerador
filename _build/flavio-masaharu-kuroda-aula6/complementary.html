Lesson 6 -- What the Requirements Say

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Deadlines and logistics"

Five minutes on dates, dependencies and who is waiting for whom — the three things a requirements document buries in the passive. Two native speakers at natural speed, with the full script on the same page.

Tip: read the script and underline every sentence built with is or are plus a participle. For each one, write in the margin who you think is doing it.

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Rules"

Five minutes on the language of company rules: what is required, what is allowed, and who decides. Almost every sentence in it is built the way requirements documents are built, with the action in front and the person nowhere in sight.

Tip: after listening, take five sentences from a real document in your inbox and rewrite each one in the active voice, naming the person you think is responsible.

Documentary
Skandal! Bringing Down Wirecard (1h32)

A story that is entirely about documents: what they said, what they did not say, and who was assumed to be responsible. The journalists on screen read corporate English out loud and explain what each sentence actually commits somebody to — which is the skill this lesson trained.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Note two sentences from a document on screen that hide who is doing the action, and say out loud the question you would have asked.
