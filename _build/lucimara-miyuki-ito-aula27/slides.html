Chapter 3: It Has Been Fixed

What Is Being Done?

is being cleaned · has been fixed · hasn't been cleaned yet

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I'm sorry, your room is being cleaned right now."

"Good news: the leak in your bathroom has been fixed."

"The second charge has been canceled, and the fee has been waived."

"It's eleven p.m., and my room hasn't been cleaned yet."

Look at the orange words. Who is doing the action? Is it important?

FormUseExample
is / are being + past participleit is happening now (we don't say who does it)Your room is being cleaned.
has / have been + past participleit is finished, and the result is important nowThe leak has been fixed.
hasn't / haven't been + past participle ... yetit has not happened up to nowThe room hasn't been cleaned yet.
Has / Have ... been + past participle?to ask if it is doneHas my bill been corrected?
is / was + past participle (Lesson 10)the simple passive: a fact or a finished past actionThe room was cleaned yesterday.

In the passive, the important thing is the room, the leak, or the bill, not the person who does the work. Never forget been: "has been fixed", not "has fixed".

Chapter 4: At the Front Desk

Eleven p.m. in Lyon

Lucimara has a problem; Julien, the manager, has to solve it

Dialogue

At the Front Desk

L
Good evening. I have a reservation under Ito, but the receptionist says my room isn't ready.
J
I'm so sorry, Ms. Ito. I'm afraid your room was double-booked. Another guest checked in this afternoon.
L
Double-booked? I booked it three months ago, and my rate is non-refundable.
J
I understand. I've looked into it, and a new room is being prepared for you right now. It's a suite on the fifth floor.
L
Thank you. But isn't the fifth floor under renovation? I saw a sign in the elevator.
J
Only the fourth floor, and the work has been finished for today. It will be quiet, I promise.
L
Good. And one more thing: I think I was overcharged. There are two charges on my card.
J
Let me check... You're right. The second charge has been canceled, and the hold will be released tomorrow.
L
Perfect. I'm here for a trade fair, so I really need a good night's sleep.
J
Of course. As a goodwill gesture, breakfast is on us, and the late checkout fee has been waived.
Comprehension

About Julien

1. What does Julien say happened to Lucimara's room?
It was double-booked. Another guest checked in that afternoon.
2. According to Julien, which floor is under renovation?
Only the fourth floor, and the work has been finished for today.
3. What does Julien offer as a goodwill gesture?
Free breakfast, and he waives the late checkout fee.

Listening 2

How to Complain

A corporate travel manager gives advice about hotel problems. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What does she say instead of "Everything is terrible"?
Be specific: "The air conditioning hasn't been fixed, and I asked two times."
2. Why was the engineer overcharged?
He was charged for three nights of parking, but he didn't have a car.
3. What goodwill gestures do most hotels offer?
A discount, a free breakfast, or an upgrade.
