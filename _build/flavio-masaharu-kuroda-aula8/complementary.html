Lesson 8 -- Numbers on a Page

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Pitching"

Five minutes on presenting a commercial case out loud: what to put first, how to say the number, and what to do when the room goes quiet. Two native speakers at natural speed, with the full script on the same page.

Tip: read the script and underline every comparison — cheaper than, up to, at least, about. Then say your own price out loud three times, each time against a different benchmark.

Article and audio
NPR Planet Money Summer School -- "Negotiating: price, salary and benefits"

Thirty minutes on how a number becomes a negotiation, with real examples and an economist explaining why the first figure moves everything after it. Audio and full transcript on the same page, American English at normal speed.

Tip: listen once, then find three sentences in the transcript where a number is put next to another number. Write each one down and replace the figures with your own.

Film
Moneyball (2011)

Two hours of people arguing about numbers out loud. Every scene is somebody defending a figure against a different benchmark, and the film is unusually careful about how the numbers are spoken. American English, business register, no technical vocabulary.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down five sentences in which somebody compares two numbers, and mark whether they use -er than, more ... than, or as much as.
