Find the subject in each question. Why does the blue one need does, and why does the green one not?

FormWhat it doesExample
question word + do / does + subject + verba direct question about a fact or a routineWhat does the team do?
question word + is / are + subjecta direct question about a stateWhy is the workload growing?

Sound Check

Long and Short

Long and short vowels · point 4 of 14

Why this one is hard for a Brazilian speaker

In Portuguese: There is one i sound, and its length never changes the meaning of a word.

In English: There are two. The long one is stretched, with the lips in a smile: need, team. The short one is quick and relaxed, close to an e: business, fill.

If it slips: A long i in "fill" turns it into "feel", and "live" becomes "leave". The listener understands a different word, and in a conversation about people leaving a team, that matters.

Chapter 5: Real Speed

What the Form Needs, What the Form Has

The regional manager on requisitions, and a voice message from a manager

Listening 1

Three Answers on Every Form

Claire, the regional manager, talks to the HR team about requisitions. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. What does Claire receive every week?
Requisitions with a title, a salary range and the word urgent, and nothing else.
2. What are the three answers she wants on every form?
The business need, the workload, and the time: permanent or only in the short term.
3. What does she suggest when the need is only in the short term?
To look into a temporary solution first.
Listening 2

A Request with a Gap

Diego, a manager in collections, leaves a message. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. Is Diego asking for a replacement or a new position?
A new position: nobody is leaving.
2. Why does the workload of his team grow?
Because the dealers pay later and later.
3. What is not ready yet?
The job description.
