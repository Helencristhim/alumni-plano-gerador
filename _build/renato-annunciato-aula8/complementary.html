Lesson 8 -- The Development Plan

Series
Explained (Explicando) -- any episode on work and money

Short episodes, around twenty minutes each, that take one idea apart and rebuild it. The narration is clear, the pace is fast but never rushed, and every episode ends by projecting forward — which means you hear the three futures of this lesson used naturally, again and again.

Tip: pick one episode and watch it with English subtitles. Every time the narrator talks about what happens next, write the sentence down. You will collect will, going to and the present continuous without hunting for them.

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart -- Get Hired: How the Right Communication Can Advance Your Career

A free episode from the Stanford business school on the exact conversation you practised today: how to talk about yourself when somebody senior is deciding whether you are ready. Clear American English, around thirty minutes, with a transcript on the page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript, even if you lose parts of it. Then write your own three layers in English — aspiration, gap, action with a date — and read them out loud.

Listen on Stanford GSB ↗
YouTube
Dan Pink -- The Puzzle of Motivation (TED)

Eighteen minutes on what actually moves people at work, and it is not the bonus. He argues for autonomy, mastery and purpose — which is a useful frame the day somebody asks you why you want the next role, and "more money" is not the answer you want to give.

Tip: watch once with subtitles, then again with them off. Afterwards, say out loud in English which of his three words describes your own reason for wanting the next step.
