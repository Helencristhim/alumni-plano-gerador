Easy English Conversations -- Episode 13: "Talk about what you did last weekend"

Five minutes of the BBC series for beginners. Two people ask each other the exact question of today's class, "What did you do at the weekend?", and answer it slowly, with the words on the screen while they speak.

Tip: watch it once for the story. The second time, stop at every question and say it out loud before the person on the screen does.

Podcast
Cool English -- "Simple Past Explained"

A short grammar episode that goes through the three shapes of the past simple, one after the other: the sentence, the negative, and the question. The third one is today's class, and hearing it explained in English is different from reading it.

Tip: good for the commute, when you cannot read or write but you can listen. Every time you hear an example with "did", repeat it out loud and change one word to make it yours.

engVid -- "Basic English Grammar: Past Tense Questions"

Seven minutes on the exact problem of today's class: the one small word that learners forget when they ask about the past, and what happens to the main verb when that word arrives. The teacher writes every example on the board before she says it.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause before each example and build the question yourself, then compare.
