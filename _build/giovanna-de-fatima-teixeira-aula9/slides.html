Chapter 3: The Pattern

One Word, Three Jobs

able · allowed · please

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

I can read technical drawings.
I cannot understand people on the phone.
We cannot start before seven.
Can you speak slowly, please?

Same word in all four. Is the meaning the same?

JobMeaningExemplo
Abilityyou know how, you are ableI can read technical drawings.
Permissionit is allowed, or it is notWe cannot start before seven.
Polite requestplease do this for meCan you speak slowly, please?
CuidadoDepois de can o verbo fica NU: sem "to" e sem "s". "I can to read" e "She can reads" nao existem — e sempre I can read, she can read. Forma curta da negativa: cannot = can't.
