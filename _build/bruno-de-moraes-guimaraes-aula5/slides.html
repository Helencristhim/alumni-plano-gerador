Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I like basketball."

"I like watching series at night."

"I want to learn English."

"I would like a water, please."

After like, what comes? And after want?

PatternWhat comes afterExample
I like + thinga noun"I like basketball."
"I like comedies."
I like + verb-inga verb with -ing"I like watching series."
"I like playing games."
I want to + verbto + the normal verb"I want to learn English."
"I want to travel alone."
I would likethe polite version of want"I would like a water, please."
"I would like to see the menu."
Key ideaIn a shop, a restaurant or an airport, always use would like. I want a coffee sounds like an order; I would like a coffee, please sounds like a person.

Opinion and desire. That is where a conversation stops being a form and becomes a talk.
