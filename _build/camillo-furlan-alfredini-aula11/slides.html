Chapter 3: The Code

Likes and Wants

What you enjoy, and what you want in the future

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I love fishing on Sunday."

"Laura hates cooking."

"Do you like going to the gym?"

"I want to visit Germany next year."

Look at the orange words. After like, love and hate, what is at the end of the verb? And after want?

JobFormExample
Activities you enjoylike / love + verb-ingI love fishing. I like watching F1.
Activities you don't enjoyhate / don't like + verb-ingI don't like running. She hates cooking.
QuestionDo you like + verb-ing?Do you like going to the gym?
A wish, a dreamwant to + verbI want to visit Germany.
He / shelikes / loves / hates / wants (+ s)Laura wants to see Porto Seguro.
The traplike fish ≠ like fishingI like fish = the food. I like fishing = the hobby.

In one line: like / love / hate + verb-ing for hobbies; want to + verb for wishes.

Chapter 4: The Dinner

The Dinner

One conversation, two recordings, one profile page

Dialogue

What Do You Like Doing?

L
This restaurant is great, Camillo. So, no more brakes tonight. What do you like doing in your free time?
C
I love fishing. In Porto Seguro, I go on a boat every Sunday.
L
Fishing! Nice. Do you like cooking the fish?
C
No, I don't like cooking. But I love a barbecue with my family. And you? Do you have a hobby?
L
I love cycling, and I go to the gym after work. But I hate cooking too!
C
I like watching Formula 1. My dream is to see a race in Germany. I want to visit Munich next year.
L
Great idea! I want to show you the city. And I want to see your beach in Porto Seguro.
C
Perfect. You can come fishing with me!

Klaus and His Free Time

He wants to see a Formula 1 race at Interlagos.
