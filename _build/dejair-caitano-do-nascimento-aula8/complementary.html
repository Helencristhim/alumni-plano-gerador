Lesson 8 -- When Things Go Wrong

Listening
"How important is politeness?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on why the same request sounds different depending on the words around it. That is exactly the difference between can and could that you worked on today.

Tip: listen once with your eyes closed. Then listen again and write down every request you hear. Mark which ones use could or would, and which ones are direct. Notice who they are talking to in each case.

Conference Talk
"Don't insist on English!" -- Patricia Ryan, TED

An English teacher of forty years argues that good ideas get lost when people are judged by their English instead of by their thinking. This is the talk to watch on a day when your English feels like the problem.

Tip: watch it once, without subtitles, and see how much you follow. Then watch again with subtitles and count what you missed. That number is your listening measure -- write it down and repeat the test in four weeks.

Listening
"How do you learn to speak a language?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes about what actually makes speaking improve, and what does not. Useful now, when you are eight lessons in and starting to hear your own progress.

Tip: listen for what they say about making mistakes. Then say out loud, in English, one thing you do today that you could not do eight lessons ago. One sentence, and be specific.
