Chapter 4: The Code

I Work, He Works

The small letter at the end

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"Marco works on a Formula 1 crew."

"He wakes up at six o'clock."

"He doesn't drive the car."

"Does he like his job?"

Look at the orange words. What is different with he? And where does the -s go in the negative and the question?

JobFormExample
He / She / Itverb + sHe works. She watches.
Spelling-es after ch, sh, s, x, owatch → watches, go → goes
Negativedoesn't + verb (no -s)He doesn't drive the car.
QuestionDoes + he + verb (no -s)?Does he travel a lot?
Short answersYes, he does. / No, he doesn't.Does he drive? No, he doesn't.
The traponly one -sNot He doesn't drives. Say He doesn't drive.

In one line: with he and she, the verb takes -s. In the negative and the question, the -s goes to does.
