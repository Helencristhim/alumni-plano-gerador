Real Document

A Quote for the Kitchen

Casa Nova Renovations · Scope of Work · Client: Rodrigo M. de França · Fictional document, written for this lesson

Kitchen Renovation — Quote No. 214

1. Definitions. “The Work” means the renovation of the kitchen described below. “Completion” refers to the day on which the kitchen is working and has been cleaned.

2. Included. New cabinets, countertop and sink; tiles on two walls; all plumbing in relation to the new sink.

3. Excluded. Electrical work, the floor and painting outside the kitchen.

4. Price. R$ 48,000, limited to the items in Section 2. “Any other work that may be necessary” will be charged separately.

1. Which two words does the quote define? Which phrases does it use?
"The Work and Completion. It uses means and refers to."
2. The old floor is broken. Is fixing it part of the price?
"No. The floor is excluded, so it falls outside the scope of the quote."
3. Which sentence is a catch-all? Why is it risky for the client?
"‘Any other work that may be necessary.’ It has no limit, so the final bill can grow: classic scope creep."
4. Rewrite the catch-all so that it protects the client.
"Other work will be charged only to the extent that the client approves it in writing before it starts."

Listening 1 · The Project Manager

A Hundred Small Requests

A software project manager, on how projects grow. Sound first — no text.

1. According to her, how do most projects fail?
Not with one big mistake, but with a hundred small requests.
2. What does her team write down before a project starts?
What the project includes and what it doesn't include, and the meaning of words like finished, tested or live.
3. What does she say when a new request comes in?
That it's outside the current scope, so they will estimate it as a change.
Listening 2 · The Builder

The Most Expensive Word

A kitchen builder, on the one-page scope of work. Sound first — no text.

1. What is the most expensive word in his business, and why?
Everything, because nobody knows what it includes: the floor, the lights, moving the sink.
2. Which part of the scope of work do people forget?
The list of what's excluded.
3. How does he define finished?
The kitchen works and has been cleaned, not that every handle is the one the client saw online.
