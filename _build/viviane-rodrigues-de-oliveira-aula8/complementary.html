Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 8 -- How Much? How Many?

Series
Chef's Table -- Season 1, Episode 2 (Dan Barber)

A chef who measures everything: how much land, how many animals, how much time. It is a whole episode of somebody explaining numbers in plain English, at a calm speed.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down five sentences with numbers or quantifiers.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
How I Built This -- any episode

Founders telling their story, and every one of them says numbers out loud: how many employees, how much money, how many years. Numbers spoken fast are the hardest part of listening, and this is the gym for it.

Tip: listen at 0.75x and write down every number you hear in the first five minutes. Then listen again at 1x.

Listen on Spotify ↗
YouTube
Shawn Achor -- "The happy secret to better work" (TED)

Twelve minutes of research about work, told with numbers and jokes. He speaks fast, which makes it a good challenge, but the story is easy to follow even when the words are not.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles, then once without. Note three quantifiers he uses (a lot of, too much, most of).
