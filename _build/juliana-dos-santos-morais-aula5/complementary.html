Don’t insist on English!

Patricia Ryan, an English teacher who worked in the Gulf for many years, talks about English as the international language and what we lose when it is the only one. A good talk for anyone who meets people from many countries.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and note the countries and experiences she describes.
