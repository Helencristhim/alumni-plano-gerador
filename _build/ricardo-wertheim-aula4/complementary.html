Grammar Video
"Talking about the future" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the two futures you practiced in class: "going to" for what you already decided, and "will" for what you decide while you speak. Connection to Lesson 4: this is the grammar behind "I am going to spend four nights in Dubrovnik" and "The ferry is full? Then I will take the bus".

Tip: every time they give an example, decide out loud whether it was decided before or in the moment. Then say the same sentence about your own trip in August.

"Travel to Croatia's Istrian Peninsula" -- Amateur Traveler, Episode 881

A guest who lives in Croatia builds a seven day itinerary out loud, town by town, with the reasons for every choice. Connection to Lesson 4: it is one long answer to the question you practiced today, "what are you going to do there?".

Tip: listen with a pen. Every time she names a place, write one sentence with "going to" as if the plan were yours. You should have six sentences by the end.

"Croatia & Slovenia Travel Skills" -- Rick Steves' Europe, with Cameron Hewitt

A full planning talk on exactly your route: the Dalmatian coast, Split, Dubrovnik, the ferries to the islands, and then north to Lake Bled and Ljubljana. Connection to Lesson 4: this is a person planning the trip you are going to take, in the language you learned today.

Tip: watch it with your map open. Stop three times and say out loud what you are going to do in that place, and one thing you will decide only when you arrive.
