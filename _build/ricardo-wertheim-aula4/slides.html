Chapter 4: The Two Futures

Decided Before, or Decided Now

I'm going to... · I think I'll...

Grammar Discovery

When Did You Decide?

"I am going to visit Dubrovnik in late August."

"We are going to take the scenic road from Split."

"The ferry is full? Then I will drive instead."

"I am sure the coastline will be incredible."

Two of these were decided weeks ago. Two were decided one second ago. Which is which?

FormUseExample
am / is / are going to + verbA plan you already decided, before this conversationI am going to visit Dubrovnik in August.
will + verbA decision you make right now, while you speakThe ferry is full? Then I will drive.
will for predictionsWhat you believe about the future, with I think / I am sureI am sure it will be incredible.
Time expressionsnext week · in August · at the end of the month · soon · in a few weeks. They open or close the sentence: In August we are going to fly to Split.

Same future, two different histories: going to already existed before you spoke; will was born in the sentence.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I go to Croatia at the end of August."
"I am going to Croatia at the end of August."
"We already have the tickets, so we will fly on the fourth."
"We already have the tickets, so we are going to fly on the fourth."
"I am looking forward to visit the islands."
"I am looking forward to visiting the islands."

A ticket in your hand means going to, never will. And after looking forward to the verb always takes -ing.
