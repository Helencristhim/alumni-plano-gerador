How others answer

Which one can the client plan with?

Which answer helps the client prepare her budget?
Answer B. "In January" and "not above our cap of six percent" give her a date and a limit.

What B is going to do

In January, we are going to adjust the fee.
The adjustment is going to follow the inflation index.
The increase is not going to go above our cap.

we are going to + verb · it is going to + verb · it is not going to + verb
A plan your firm already decided. Never "we going to".
