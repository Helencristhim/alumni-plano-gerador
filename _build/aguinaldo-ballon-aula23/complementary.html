Talk
TED -- Ben Goldacre, "Battling bad science" (14 min)

A doctor shows how claims in the news and in research are often attributed to studies that do not say what people think. Great for the language of sources, findings and evidence.

Tip: write down three reporting verbs he uses (claims, shows, suggests...) and use each one in a sentence about your own research.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English -- "Is rejection good for us?"

Six minutes on rejection and what we learn from it. Every researcher knows the feeling: a journal says no, and you rewrite.

Tip: after listening, say what one of the experts argues, starting with: As the expert argues, ...

Academic Bites -- "How to Use Reporting Verbs in Academic Writing"

A short, clear video on reporting verbs and how each one shows the author's position: neutral, strong or critical.
