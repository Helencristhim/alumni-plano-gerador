Talk
Adam Grant — “The surprising habits of original thinkers” (TED)

Fifteen minutes on why some of the most original people put things off on purpose. A smart, funny talk about the exact habits of this lesson — procrastinating, coming up with ideas, giving up too early.

Tip: every time he uses a two-word verb, write it down with its formal version. Aim for five.

Podcast
Criminal — “The Informant” (episode 379)

An old friend calls an investigator from jail, asks to be picked up, and says he has information about a case. A true story told in clear, unhurried American English, full of the verbs from this lesson.

Tip: listen for picked up, set up and turned down, and note what the object was each time. Then say the sentence again with a pronoun.
