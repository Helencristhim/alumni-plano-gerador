Lesson 32 -- Closing Deals

TED Talk
William Ury: The walk from no to yes

A famous negotiator from Harvard explains how to move from conflict to agreement. Calm, clear English and many stories from real negotiations.

Tip: after the talk, say one offer you would make in a real deal, with if and would.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Margaret Neale: Negotiation -- getting what you want

A professor of negotiation shows how to prepare for the last meeting and how to ask for something back. Short and practical.

Tip: write down your bottom line for a real negotiation, and say two concessions you would ask for in return.

Series
Billions

A series about big money in New York, where almost every episode has a hard negotiation: offers, concessions, bottom lines and people who walk away.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Pause after each offer and say it again in your own words, with if + past and would.
