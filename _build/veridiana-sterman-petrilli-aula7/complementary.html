Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Somebody Feed Phil — Season 7, "'The Real' Orlando" (Netflix)

Worth a second visit after Lesson 1, this time for the language of the body: what people eat, what they cannot eat, how they feel afterwards. Florida, warm, and nothing in it is sad.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on and write down every sentence you hear with for or since in it.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "How to be a supercommunicator"

Listen to it again with today’s question in mind: how do you say something difficult in few words, to somebody who has thirty seconds for you? The clinic desk is the hardest version of exactly that.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Pick the three shortest sentences in it and say why they work.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
George Zaidan — "How do pain relievers work?" (TED-Ed)

Four animated minutes on what happens between the ache and the tablet. It is your own professional territory in English, and it hands you the exact words of this lesson without a single sad story in it.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Then explain the whole thing back, out loud, in five sentences, as if to a patient.
