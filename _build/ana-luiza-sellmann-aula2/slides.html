Chapter 3: The Code

Does the Date Matter?

Two options. That is the whole decision.

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I have never been to Asia."

"My mother has lived in Shanghai since 2024."

"I have just finished a big project."

"Have you ever eaten dumplings for breakfast?"

Four sentences about the past. Not one of them says the day. Why not?

FormUseExample
have / has + past participleThe past when the day does not matter, or when it is still true nowI have worked on festivals.
She has lived here since 2024.
Negative
have not / has not		Something that has not happened up to nowI have not been to Asia.
He has not called yet.
Question
Have / Has + you + participle		Asking about a life experience, at any momentHave you ever been to Brazil?
The six little wordsever and never for a whole life, just for a minute ago, already for sooner than expected, yet for still missing, for and since for how long.

One question: does the date matter? If you can say the day and the day is the point, use the past simple. If not, this is your form.

The Contrast

With a Date, or Without

THE DAY IS THE POINT

"I went to New York in January."

"We launched the campaign in March."

THE EXPERIENCE IS THE POINT

"I have been to New York twice."

"We have already launched the campaign."

If the sentence has a finished time word, the past simple wins. In January, last year, in 2022, yesterday.

If you are talking about your life so far, and nobody asked for a date, you do not have to find one.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"My brother is in Shanghai since 2024."
"My brother has been in Shanghai since 2024."
"I have gone to New York in January."
"I went to New York in January."
"Have you ever went to a launch?"
"Have you ever been to a launch?"

With since and for, English uses have or has, never the present. A closed date like in January always takes the past simple. And after have or has the verb is the participle: been, not went.

Listening

Rachel Has Moved

Three weeks in a new city. Sound first — no text.

1. How long has Rachel been in Lisbon?
For three weeks, and she has not unpacked half of her boxes.
2. What has she already found there?
A gym and a decent coffee place.
3. What has she started doing, and how is it going?
Guitar lessons. She says she is terrible, but her neighbor has not complained yet.
Listening 2

A Voicemail from London

Tom is booking a call for Thursday. Sound first — no text.

1. What has Tom's team already done?
They have received her portfolio and read it, including the client list.
2. What does he say he wants to hear on the call?
The story of the work she is most proud of, and why.
3. How long is the call, and what should she prepare?
Fifteen minutes, and he says there is nothing to prepare.
