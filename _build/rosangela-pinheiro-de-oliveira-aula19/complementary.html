Indirect Questions — 6 Minute Grammar (free, 6 minutes)

Six minutes on exactly tonight's tool. Two teachers take the short question apart and put it back together with an opening in front, and you hear the moment the verb moves back behind the subject: could you tell me where the station is, not "where is the station".

Tip: watch it twice. The first time, only listen. The second time, pause after every example and say the same question about a place in your city. One note: they are British and your material is American. The frames are identical; only the music of the voice changes.

The Swiss Alps, Paris and London, and twenty-five minutes about the part of a trip nobody films: arriving, finding the place you booked, and getting across a big city with a suitcase. Slow, clear American English, and it is the same journey you rehearsed tonight.
