Chapter 4: The Code

Enjoy Doing, Want to Do

Two families of verbs

Grammar Discovery

Two Families of Verbs

"I enjoy mentoring junior designers."

"We need to hire a senior designer."

"Avoid asking questions with yes or no answers."

"She decided to apply because she is interested in working with data."

Look at the red verbs and the blue form after them. Enjoy and avoid take -ing: mentoring, asking. Need and decide take to + verb: to hire, to apply. And after a small word like in, about or of, the verb always takes -ing.

JobFormExample
The -ing familyenjoy, avoid, finish, keep, consider, recommendI enjoy mentoring people.
The to familywant, need, decide, plan, hope, would likeWe need to hire a designer.
After in, about, of, atverb + -ingShe is good at presenting.
A tendency, not a lawto often points to the futureI want to grow. I plan to mentor her.
Like, love, startboth forms are fineI like working / I like to work with data.
The trapenjoy to, want -ing do not existNot I enjoy to mentor. Say I enjoy mentoring.

In one line: enjoy, avoid, finish + -ing; want, need, decide + to; after a preposition, always -ing.

A scorecard turns an interview into a decision. When you explain it to your trio, the verbs need the right form after them: he enjoys talking..., he decided to practice..., we need to decide...
1. Turn the line “Strength” into a sentence.
He enjoys talking to users and simplifying flows.
2. Turn the line “Weakness” into a sentence.
He avoided presenting to big groups, so he decided to practice every month.
3. What is the next step?
A second interview with the tech lead and the product manager.
Listening 1 · The Candidate

Alex Answers

Alex, a product designer from Denver, answers three questions in an interview. Sound first — no text.

1. What is Alex’s background?
Four years in a health app and two years in a payments company.
2. What does he enjoy doing most?
Talking to users and turning research into simple flows.
3. What weakness does he mention, and what did he decide to do?
Presenting to big groups; he decided to give a short talk every month.
Listening 2 · Advice From a Hiring Manager

Ananya Hires

Ananya, the designer from Delhi, now leads a team and hires designers. She shares her advice. Sound first — no text.

1. How many designers has Ananya hired this year?
Four.
2. What does she recommend doing before the interview?
Looking at the portfolio and choosing one project to discuss.
3. What is her biggest red flag?
A candidate who never mentions the users.
