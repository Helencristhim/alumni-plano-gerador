Chapter 3: The Code

Turn Left, Go Straight

Short orders and small place words

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"Go straight for two blocks."

"Turn left at the corner."

"The station is next to the bank."

"The park is across from the hotel."

Sentences 1 and 2: where is the person? There is no you. The verb in blue comes first. Sentences 3 and 4: the red words say where.

JobFormExample
Direction (an order)verb first, no "you"Go straight. Turn left.
Negative directionDon’t + verbDon’t turn right.
Next toright besideThe station is next to the bank.
Across fromon the other side of the streetThe park is across from the hotel.
On the corner / on the lefton + the corner / the left / the rightIt is on the corner. It is on your right.
AskingExcuse me, where is...? / How do I get to...?Excuse me, where is the subway?

In one line: for directions, start with the verb: Go, Turn, Take, Walk.

Chapter 4: Asking the Way

Asking the Way

One conversation, two recordings, one map card

Dialogue

Excuse Me

C
Excuse me. Where is the subway, please?
E
Which subway? Where do you want to go?
C
To Central Park. My wife and I are tourists.
E
Oh, you don't need the subway. Central Park is close. Go straight ahead for four blocks.
C
Four blocks. Okay. And then?
E
Then turn left on 59th Street. The park is across from the big hotel.
C
Sorry, can you say that again, slowly?
E
Sure. Straight four blocks, turn left, and the park is on the corner.
C
Thank you very much!
Comprehension

About Emily

1. What does Emily ask first?
Where do you want to go?
2. Does Emily say to take the subway?
No. She says they don't need the subway.
3. How many blocks does Emily say?
Four blocks.
4. Where is the park, according to Emily?
On 59th Street, on the corner, across from the big hotel.

Listening 2 · New York Streets

How Manhattan Works

A New Yorker explains the streets and avenues. Sound first — no text.

1. Do streets and avenues go in the same direction?
No. Streets go east and west, and avenues go north and south.
2. How long is a short block?
About one minute on foot.
3. What does she say about the subway?
It is on the corner of many streets, and it is fast.
