Lesson 25 -- The Difficult Stakeholder

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Disagreements"

How to disagree with a colleague or a client in English without sounding rude, with short phrases you can use in your next meeting. Full script on the same page.

Tip: copy two phrases from the script and use each one to answer a concern a customer really told you.

Talk
Julia Dhar -- "How to disagree productively and find common ground" (TED, 12 min)

A world debating champion explains how to disagree with someone and still move forward together. Her three steps fit a difficult stakeholder very well.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down her three ideas and say each one with I see your point or I'm not sure I agree.

Podcast
6 Minute English (BBC Learning English) -- "How to talk to a climate denier"

What works when you talk to someone who strongly disagrees with you: listening, respect and facts instead of arguments. The same is true for a skeptical CFO.

Tip: read the script and write two sentences you could say to a skeptic, one with with respect and one with that is a fair concern.
