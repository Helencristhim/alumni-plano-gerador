Chapter 3: The Code

How a polite no sounds

Grammar Discovery

No, but Politely

"I see your point, but distance is not the main risk."

"With respect, I'm not sure I agree that the fee is too high."

"I take your point about the setup fee."

"That's a fair concern. Let me show you the numbers."

Does Flávio ever say you are wrong? What does he do before he disagrees?

JobPhraseExample
Show you heardI see your point, but + clauseI see your point, but our support is in Italian.
Accept part of itI take your point about + nounI take your point about the fee.
Disagree softlyI'm not sure I agree (that) + clauseI'm not sure I agree that distance is the risk.
Disagree formallyWith respect, + clauseWith respect, our data says something different.
Accept the worryThat's a fair concern.That's a fair concern. Here is how we handle it.
Offer a way forwardWould it help if we + past simple?Would it help if we linked the fee to the go-live?
Nevera direct attackNot: you are wrong · not: I am not agree

In one line: first show you heard, then disagree softly, then offer something.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

One Call, One Skeptic

One call, two recordings, one map

Dialogue

Paola Ferri, Group CFO

P
Mr. Kuroda, I'll be direct. I don't think a supplier without an office in Italy is a good idea for us.
F
I see your point, and it's a fair concern. However, our support team already works in Italian, every day.
P
Support by phone is not the same as people here. If the go-live fails, who comes to Milan?
F
With respect, I'm not sure I agree that distance is the main risk. Our last three go-lives in Europe were done remotely.
P
Maybe. But your setup fee is five thousand euros, and the other finalist charges nothing.
F
I take your point about the fee. Would it help if we linked half of it to a successful go-live?
P
That is more interesting. But I still need to see a reference in Italy, not in Spain.
F
That's reasonable. I'll ask our customer in Turin to call you this week.
Comprehension

About Paola

1. What is Paola's first concern?
Invent has no office in Italy.
2. What does Paola say about the setup fee?
It is five thousand euros, and the other finalist charges nothing.
3. What does Paola still need to see?
A reference in Italy, not in Spain.
Listening 1 · The Sales Coach

Not Against You, Against a Risk

A coach who trains enterprise sellers, on the person who can stop any deal. Sound first — no text.

1. What are the two common mistakes?
Going around the skeptic to the CEO, and arguing with opinions.
2. How should you disagree, according to him?
With data, never with feelings.
3. Why should you give the skeptic something to win?
So they can say yes without losing face.
Listening 2 · The CFO

The Person They Call Difficult

A CFO on what she tests when she disagrees with a supplier. Sound first — no text.

1. Why does she disagree a lot, on purpose?
Because her job is to find what can go wrong, and she wants to see what the supplier does next.
2. What did the first supplier do?
He took her point about the risk and came back two days later with a reference she could call.
3. What did the second supplier do, and what happened?
He asked the CEO to overrule her, the CEO forwarded the email to her, and the conversation ended.
