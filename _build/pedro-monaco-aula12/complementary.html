Lesson 12 -- The Rules of the Game

Grammar Video
"Passive Reporting Verbs in English | IT IS SAID THAT, IT IS THOUGHT THAT" -- Oxford English Now

Seven minutes on the exact structure of this lesson: the impersonal it is said that with a full clause, and the personal he is said to be, he is believed to have taken. Connection to Lesson 12: this is the form that carried the reading, both listenings and every role-play answer you gave.

Tip: pause at each example and rebuild it from your own files -- it is alleged that the supplier withheld the invoice, the supplier is alleged to have withheld the invoice. The pattern only sticks when the sentence is yours.

Eighteen minutes on a sport where, as the episode puts it, you cannot win unless you outrun your own regulator. Connection to Lesson 12: it is the incentive story underneath the governing body, the integrity unit and the sanction you met in the vocabulary.

A plain-English explainer of the tribunal behind the lesson text: how it was set up, who its arbitrators are, how the Olympic division decides inside twenty-four hours, and the independence question that has followed it since 1994. Connection to Lesson 12: it is the reading of the lesson with the real names and dates attached.
