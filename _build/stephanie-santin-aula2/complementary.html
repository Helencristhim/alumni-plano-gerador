Lesson 2 -- The Tense That Connects Two Careers

Talk
Atul Gawande -- "Want to get great at something? Get a coach"

A surgeon explains why he asked another doctor to watch him operate after years of experience. The whole talk is built on the tense you learned today: what he has done, and what it has changed.

Tip: listen for every "I have" and "I had". Write down three sentences and say them out loud with your own career in the place of his.

Talk
Dan Gilbert -- "The psychology of your future self"

Why we are so bad at imagining who we will become. Useful for a conversation you will have often this year: what you have been, and what you want to be next.

Tip: after watching, answer out loud: how have you changed in the last ten years, and what has not changed at all?
