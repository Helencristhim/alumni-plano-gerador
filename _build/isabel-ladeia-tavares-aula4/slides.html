Chapter 3: The Code

The Sentence That Starts With a Verb

No subject, no opinion, and four small words that carry everything

Grammar Discovery

What Is Missing From All Four?

Weigh out the powder. Then make up the solution.

Once the mixture is clear, incubate the tubes.

Do not rinse the glassware until the batch is complete.

Waste must be discarded in the marked container.

Three of them have no subject at all: the sentence opens on the verb. The fourth has a subject, and it is not a person. The blue words are the ones that decide the order, and they are not interchangeable.

UseFormExample
An instructionbase verb, no subjectDilute the sample.
A prohibitionDo not + base verbDo not rinse the glassware yet.
OrderFirst, then, after that, finallyFirst weigh out the powder. Then make up the solution.
Something must already be trueonce / afterOnce the mixture is clear, incubate.
Where a step stopsbefore / untilDo not rinse until the batch is complete.
The rule you cannot breakmust / must not (not should)Waste must be discarded in the marked container.

In one line: the verb comes first, the small word decides the order, and must is never a suggestion.

Listening 1 · The Induction

Three Rules, and Why the Third One Exists

Your first morning in a new lab, and somebody is talking fast. Write down the three rules. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What is rule one, and what happened to him because of it?
Label every tube before you fill it, not after. He once threw away an entire afternoon of somebody else’s work because four identical tubes came back to the rack with nothing written on them.
2. Where does waste go, and which three places does he specifically rule out?
In the marked container by the door. Not the sink, not the general bin, and not a beaker you plan to deal with later.
3. Why does he say a must not sentence is in a protocol at all?
Because somebody was hurt, and the sentence was added afterwards. It is not a recommendation and it is not somebody being careful.
Listening 2 · A Voice Message

The Copy on the Bench Is the Old One

A colleague caught you too late and left this. Reconstruct the correct order of the steps. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. Which step changed, and what exactly is different about it?
Step four. You now allow the tubes to stand for five minutes before you add the reagent. The old version had you adding it straight away.
2. Which buffer do you use, and why is that a real risk?
The one from the fridge, not the one on the shelf. They look identical and the labels are almost the same.
3. When can you rinse the glassware, and what happens if you rinse earlier?
Not until the whole batch is finished. If you rinse halfway through, you have nothing to compare the last four tubes against.
Real Document

The Sheet on the Bench

PROTOCOL 7B — SAMPLE PREPARATION
revised copy · bench 3 · I. Ladeia Tavares
1. Before you start, label every tube.
2. Weigh out 2.5 g of the powder.
3. Make up the solution with the buffer from the fridge, not the one on the shelf.
4. Allow the tubes to stand for five minutes before adding the reagent.
5. Dilute each sample one to ten.
6. Once the mixture is clear, incubate at 37°C for 20 minutes.
7. Rinse all glassware thoroughly, except after the final incubation.
8. Do not rinse until the batch is complete.
Warning. This reagent must not be handled outside the fume hood. Waste must be discarded in the marked container by the door.
If a step is unclear, stop and ask. Do not improvise a step and write it up afterwards.
1. You have just finished step 6 on the last four tubes. Do you rinse now?
No. Step 7 says rinse thoroughly except after the final incubation, and step 8 says not until the batch is complete.
2. Two steps in this protocol are not instructions to you at all. Which are they, and what makes them different?
The two warning sentences. They use must and must not, they have a subject that is not a person, and they are rules rather than steps.
3. A colleague added the reagent immediately in step 4. What did they do wrong, and what would you expect to see?
They skipped the five minutes of standing time. That is the old version of the step, and the batch used to come out cloudy every time.
