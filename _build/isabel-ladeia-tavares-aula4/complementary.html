Lesson 4 -- Read the Protocol, Do Not Guess

Documentary
Picture a Scientist (Ciência no Feminino)

Ninety minutes inside real laboratories, with three women who work at benches like yours talking about their own careers. The English is unscripted and the pace is normal, which is the point: this is what a colleague actually sounds like when she explains what she does.

Tip: watch the first twenty minutes with English subtitles and write down two sentences in which somebody describes a procedure. Notice how little the person doing it is mentioned.

Podcast
Why are so many Coho salmon dying? The answer might be in your tires : Short Wave

Twelve minutes, and it is a detective story where the method is the plot: a team spends years narrowing down thousands of chemicals to find the one killing the fish. Everything they do is the English you worked on today, spoken out loud instead of printed on a bench sheet.

Tip: listen once for the story. Then listen again and write down, in order, the steps they took. Six lines is enough, and it is exactly the exercise you did in class.

Video
How to pipette correctly — a short step-by-step introduction into proper pipetting

Three minutes, and it is a spoken protocol from beginning to end: every sentence starts on a verb, the order words are all there, and you already know the technique in Portuguese. That is what makes it easy listening rather than a lesson.

Tip: play it once with the sound only, no picture, and see how much you follow. Then watch it and count how many instructions had no subject.
