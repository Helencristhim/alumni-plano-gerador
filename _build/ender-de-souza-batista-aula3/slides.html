Chapter 3: The Code

Do and Don’t

I work, we work, do you work?

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I manage the technology team."

"We work with payments."

"We don’t send text messages."

"Do you travel to the US?"

Look at the blue words. There is no "am" or "are" here. What word do we use for a negative and for a question?

JobFormExample
I, you, we, theyverb (no am / are)I manage the team. We work with banks.
Negativedon’t (do not) + verbWe don’t send text messages.
QuestionDo + you + verb?Do you travel for work?
Question with whatWhat + do + you + verb?What do you do?
Short answersYes, I do. / No, I don’t.Do you travel? Yes, I do.
The trapnever "am" + verbNot I am work. Say I work.

In one line: I work, we work. Negative: don't work. Question: Do you work? Never "I am work".

Chapter 4: Small Talk

Small Talk

One conversation, two recordings, one business card

Dialogue

Before the Meeting

J
Hi, Ender! I am Jessica, from TextFlow. We send text messages for big companies.
E
Hi, Jessica. Nice to meet you. What do you do at TextFlow?
J
I manage the projects for new clients. And you? What do you do?
E
I am the technology director. I manage the system team. We work with payments.
J
Interesting! Do you travel to the US for work?
E
Yes, I do. I travel to the US sometimes. Do you work in Austin?
J
Yes, I do. I live and work in Austin. Do you send text messages to your clients now?
E
No, we don’t. That is why we need a provider.
J
Perfect. We do that every day. Okay, let’s start!

In Austin.
