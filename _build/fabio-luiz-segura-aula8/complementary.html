Melissa Marshall — “Talk nerdy to me” (TED)

Four minutes, and the shortest thing you will watch all week. Her argument is the one from this lesson: the audience is interested in your subject and will follow it, as long as you give them somewhere to stand before you give them the detail.

Tip: she is explicit about what to cut. Take the explanation you recorded for homework and cut it by a third using her rule.

Talk
Eric Berlow — “Simplifying complexity” (TED)

Three minutes on a diagram with hundreds of nodes, and how to find the small part of it that actually matters. It is the bottleneck argument from this lesson, made visually and without a single word of finance.

Tip: after watching, mark the one bottleneck on your own page. If you cannot pick one, that is the finding, not a failure.
