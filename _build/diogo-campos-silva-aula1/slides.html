Chapter 3: The Code

The Small Verb

I am, you are, it is

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I am an IT analyst."

"The email account is not working."

"Are you the support analyst?"

"Is the ticket open?"

Look at the words in blue. They are all the same verb. What changes: the person, or the verb?

WhoFormExample
II am / I’mI am Diogo. I’m from Brazil.
Youyou are / you’reYou are the support analyst.
He, she, it (the ticket, the system)is / ’sThe ticket is open.
We, theyare / ’reWe are the IT team. They are in India.
Negativeam not / is not / are notThe email account is not working.
QuestionAm I...? / Are you...? / Is it...?Is the ticket open?

In one line: I am, you are, it is, we are. For a question, the verb goes first: Is it...? Are you...?

Chapter 4: On the Phone

On the Phone

One conversation, two recordings, one ticket

Dialogue

How Can I Help You?

K
Microsoft Support, good morning. I am Karen. How can I help you?
D
Good morning, Karen. I am Diogo. I am an IT analyst in Brazil.
K
Nice to meet you, Diogo. What is the problem?
D
An email account is not working. The user is very worried.
K
Okay. Is the ticket open?
D
Yes, it is. The ticket number is four five two.
K
Thank you. Are you the security team too?
D
Yes, I am. We are a small team. Infrastructure and security.
K
Great. I am here to help. Your account is my priority now.
Comprehension

About Karen

1. Where does Karen work?
At Microsoft Support.
2. What does Karen ask about the ticket?
If the ticket is open.
3. What is Karen’s priority now?
The email account.
