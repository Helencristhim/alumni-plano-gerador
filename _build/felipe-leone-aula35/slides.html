Structure Hunt

Adjective or Adverb?

Find the comparison in the audio. Is it about a person or thing (adjective), or about how people do it (adverb)?

#From the audioFind
1Americans are more direct.more + adjective (what they are)
2They speak more directly.more + adverb (how they speak)
3Meetings start more slowly.more + adverb (how meetings start)
4The small talk is shorter.adjective + -er
5They react more quickly.more + adverb (how they react)
