Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Derek Sivers: “Weird, or just different?” (2 min)

A very short talk about how the opposite of a good idea, in another culture, can also be a good idea.

Tip: after watching, say two comparisons with an adjective and two with an adverb about Brazil and the US.

TED Talk
Ernesto Sirolli: “Want to help someone? Shut up and listen!” (17 min)

Funny stories about what happens when people from one culture arrive with all the answers for another culture.

Tip: note one sentence where he compares how two groups do something.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Julian Treasure: “5 ways to listen better” (8 min)

A sound expert on why we listen less carefully than before, and five simple exercises to listen more closely.

Tip: use one of his exercises before your next call with the US team.
