Chapter 4: The Code

Not Just Cheaper: Far Cheaper

How to say how much

Grammar Discovery

How Much Cheaper?

"The online test is far cheaper than the others."

"For universities in the US, the TOEFL is by far the most common test."

"The Duolingo test isn’t nearly as long as the TOEFL."

"The more mock tests you take, the calmer you feel."

Look at the blue words: you already know comparatives and superlatives. Now look at the red words before them: they say HOW MUCH. Far, by far, not nearly. And in sentence 4, two comparatives work together, each one with the.

JobFormExample
A big differencefar / much / a lot + comparativefar cheaper, much more affordable
A small differenceslightly / a bit + comparativeslightly longer, a bit easier
The clear winnerby far + the + superlativeby far the most common test
A big difference, negativenot nearly as + adjective + asisn’t nearly as long as the TOEFL
Two things change togetherthe + comparative ..., the + comparative ...The more you practice, the calmer you feel.
The trapvery does not go with a comparativeNot very cheaper or more cheaper. Say much cheaper.

In one line: say how much: far or much for a big difference, slightly for a small one, by far the for the winner.

Listening 1 · An IELTS Teacher

Three Tips for the IELTS

A British teacher who prepares students for the IELTS gives advice to a new group. Sound first — no text.

1. What do most of her students worry about, and what does she say?
The speaking test; she says it is far less scary than they think.
2. Why does she ask students to take a mock test early?
Because the format is the hardest part at first, and the more you know it, the better you do.
3. What is her advice about the band score?
Know the cutoff score you need and aim slightly higher.
Listening 2 · An HR Manager

What the Employer Sees

An HR manager at a multinational company explains how they assess English when they hire. Sound first — no text.

1. What does a certificate tell him, according to him?
That the candidate can read and understand English at a certain level.
2. What is more important for him than the certificate?
The interview: how the candidate speaks and reacts.
3. What happened with a candidate last year?
She had a lower score than another candidate, but she was much better in the interview, so she got the job.
Listening 3 · A Student Advisor

Start With a Cheap Test

An American student advisor talks about how to choose a test without wasting money. Sound first — no text.

1. What does she recommend as a first step?
A cheaper online test, to check the level.
2. Why do some students waste money, according to her?
They book the expensive test too early, before they know their level.
3. What does she say about time and results?
The more time you give yourself to prepare, the better your result.
