Grammar Video
"How to use the third conditional" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the structure you built today: past perfect in the if half, would have plus participle in the result. Connection to Lesson 2: this is the difference between I retired this year and if I had retired earlier, I would have seen Japan already.

Tip: pause after each example and say one sentence about your own life. Listen for how would have collapses into would've -- that reduction is what you will hear at a real table.
