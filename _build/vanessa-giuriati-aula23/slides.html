Chapter 3

The Long Way Round

In English, longer means softer

Grammar Discovery

Count the Words

"Can you send the report?"

"Could you send the report, please?"

"Would you mind sending the report?"

"I was wondering if you could send the report."

FormUseExample
Could you + base verb?Everyday polite — safe with anyoneCould you send the report, please?
Would you mind + verb-ing?More polite; easy to refuseWould you mind sending the report?
I was wondering if you could + base verbThe softest — for a big favor or a busy personI was wondering if you could look at this today.
I would love to, but ... / I have to turn it downA clean no that keeps the door openI would love to, but I am swamped this week.
⚠ Careful'Would you mind' is followed by the -ing form: 'Would you mind sending?', never 'to send'. 'I was wondering' takes if you could plus the base verb. And after 'sorry to', use the base verb: 'Sorry to bother you', not 'sorry for bother you'.

Comprehension

Did You Catch It?

1. What does Daniel ask Vanessa to do, and for when?
He asks her to cover the client call on Friday.
2. How does Daniel show he is being careful with her time?
He says sorry to bother you, he admits it is short notice, and he says he does not want to impose — if she is swamped, she can turn him down.
3. What does Daniel say at the end?
That it is a real help, and that he owes her one.
Listening

The Longer, the Softer

Listen first. No text. Sound only.

1. What is the rule the speaker describes?
The longer the request, the softer it sounds.
2. When would you use the longest version, "I was wondering if you could..."?
For something big, or with somebody who is already swamped.
3. Why do the longer versions work, according to him?
Because they give the other person a way out. A request with no exit is not a request, it is an instruction.
Listening

How to Say No

A different voice, a different message. Listen carefully.

1. What did the speaker used to do instead of saying no?
She would go quiet for two days and hope the person forgot.
2. What are the three parts of the formula?
Say no early, give one reason (not four), and offer something small.
3. Why does saying no clearly help you?
Because people trust your yes more. If you never refuse anything, nobody knows what your yes is worth.
