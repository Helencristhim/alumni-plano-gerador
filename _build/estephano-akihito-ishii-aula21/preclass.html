Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. / Listen to each word and read the example.

Channel -- a person or company on YouTube with many videos
"My favorite channel is about cybersecurity." (canal)
Subscribe -- to click a button and get all the new videos of a channel
"I always subscribe to good channels." (se inscrever)
Playlist -- a list of videos that play one after the other
"This playlist has ten videos about passwords." (lista de reprodução)
Episode -- one part of a series or of a show
"I usually watch one episode at night." (episódio)
Series -- a long story on TV or online, in many episodes
"Mr. Robot is a series about cybersecurity." (série)
Stream -- to watch a video or a series on the internet, with no download
"I stream series on the weekend." (assistir online)
Tutorial -- a video that teaches you how to do something, step by step
"I watch a tutorial about strong passwords." (vídeo-aula)
Recommend -- to tell a friend that something is good and they can try it
"Can you recommend a good series?" (recomendar)

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Like, Enjoy, Prefer + -ing

GRAMMAR

Depois de like, enjoy e prefer, o verbo termina em -ing. / After like, enjoy and prefer, the verb ends in -ing.

Form / FormaUse / UsoExample
like + verb-inggostar de fazerI like watching videos.
enjoy + verb-ingcurtir fazer (é divertido)I enjoy learning online.
prefer + verb-ingpreferir fazer (gosto MAIS)I prefer watching short videos.
do not + like + verb-ingNegativoI do not like reading long manuals.
Do you + enjoy + verb-ing...?PerguntaDo you enjoy streaming series?
Atenção: com he / she, o like / enjoy / prefer ganha -s (Sara enjoys streaming). Nunca: I enjoy watch / I prefer to watching.
