Chapter 3 -- The Pattern

I Enjoy Watching

like, enjoy, prefer + verb-ing

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Orange Words Have in Common?

"I like watching videos about games."

"I enjoy learning about cybersecurity."

"I prefer watching short videos."

"Sara enjoys streaming series on the weekend."

Look at the verb after like, enjoy and prefer. What is at the end?

VerbMeaningExample
like + verb-ingit is good for meI like watching videos.
enjoy + verb-ingit is fun, it makes me happyI enjoy learning online.
prefer + verb-ingI like this one MOREI prefer watching short videos.
He / She + verb-s + verb-ingremember Lesson 18Sara enjoys streaming series.
NeverNot I enjoy watch. Not I prefer to watching.

In one line: like / enjoy / prefer + verb-ing.

Negative and Question

Not and Do You...?

The verb-ing stays the same. Only do not / do you comes in:

"I do not like watching long videos."

"Do you enjoy streaming series?" -- "Yes, I do."

"Do you prefer watching videos or reading articles?"

Short form: do not = don’t. "I don’t like watching long videos."
