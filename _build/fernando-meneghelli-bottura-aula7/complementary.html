Lesson 7 -- The Numbers Behind the Story

Grammar Video
"Stop buying or stop to buy?" -- BBC Learning English, Masterclass

Exactly the six verbs from this lesson, including stop, regret and go on. Connection to Lesson 7: with these verbs the form is not style, it is fact — and in front of a finance panel the wrong one says something different happened.

Tip: after each pair, build both versions with something from your own 2019: one thing you stopped doing, one thing you stopped to do. Say both out loud and notice how different the year sounds.

Talk with transcript
"What is economic value, and who creates it?" -- Mariana Mazzucato, TED (14 min)

Fourteen minutes of a professor of economics arguing about what a number actually measures. Connection to Lesson 7: the whole lesson is about numbers that people misread — billings for revenue, EBITDA for cash — and she is doing the same operation on a much bigger scale, in very clear English. Full transcript beside the video.

Tip: listen once with no transcript and write down every financial term you catch. Then read the transcript and check. Whatever you missed by ear, and not by meaning, is exactly what to work on.

Executive Interview
"Howard Marks, co-founder of Oaktree" -- In Good Company (48 min)

An investor who has spent forty years explaining risk to people who do not want to hear it, talking without a slide in sight. Listen for how he gives a number and then one sentence of interpretation, and never the other way round. That order is the habit this lesson is building.

Tip: take twenty minutes of it, and every time he gives a figure, pause and say out loud what you think the next sentence will be. Then listen. You are training prediction, which is the fastest way to stop translating.
