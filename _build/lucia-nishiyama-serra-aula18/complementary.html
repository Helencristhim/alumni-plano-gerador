Lesson 18 -- The American Executive Conversation

YouTube · Speak Confident English
English Small Talk with Your Boss -- Build Rapport and Credibility

An American teacher walks through the small talk that happens with someone senior to you: how to open, which topics are safe in a US office, how long to stay on them, and how to close without disappearing. She gives full example sentences you can use exactly as they are, and she explains why the same three minutes work differently in different cultures.

Tip: write down three of her openers and use one of them in your next call, before the agenda starts.

YouTube · Alexander Lyon, Communication Coach
How to Ask Good Questions in Conversations

An American communication professor explains the difference between a question that closes a conversation and a question that opens it, and he spends most of the video on the follow-up: the short question you ask about what the other person just said. It is the exact habit this lesson is built on, explained by a native speaker at a calm, clear pace.

Tip: listen for how short his best examples are -- most of them are under five words, like the ones you practiced tonight.

TED Talk · Conversation
Celeste Headlee: 10 Ways to Have a Better Conversation

A radio interviewer who has talked to people for a living for decades gives ten concrete rules, and several of them are exactly what you practiced tonight: ask open questions, go with the flow instead of preparing your next line, and stop equating the other person's experience with your own. Her English is fast, natural and American, and the talk is only twelve minutes long.

Tip: watch it once with subtitles for the ideas, then pick the two rules you break most often and use them in one real conversation this week.
