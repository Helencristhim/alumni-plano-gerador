Chapter 4: Comparing

Cheaper, and the Cheapest

Two against each other, or one against all

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The second plan is cheaper than the first one."

"This is the most expensive option on the list."

"The exchange rate is better than it was yesterday."

"That is the worst deal in the store."

AdjectiveTwo thingsOne against all
Short (cheap, big)cheaper thanthe cheapest
Long (expensive)more expensive thanthe most expensive
goodbetter thanthe best
badworse thanthe worst

One or two syllables take -er and -est. Three or more take more and the most. Never both at the same time.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"This plan is more cheaper than that one."

✓ RIGHT

"This plan is cheaper than that one."

✗ WRONG

"It is the most good option."

✓ RIGHT

"It is the best option."

Portuguese builds the comparative with two words, so the hand wants to write more even when the -er is already there. Choose one, never both. And good and bad have their own forms: better, best, worse, worst.

Dialogue

At the Counter

T
Excuse me. Is this the same model as the one in the window?
C
It is. This one is fifty dollars more, but it comes with a longer warranty.
T
How much longer? And is the price on the label the final price?
C
Three years instead of one. And no, we add sales tax at the till.
T
So the cheaper one is not really cheaper. Is there any discount this week?
C
Ten per cent if you buy today. That makes it the best offer we have had this month.
T
I am paying with a Brazilian card. Is there a fee for that?
C
Not from us. But your bank may add one, and our exchange rate is worse than theirs.
T
Then I will pay in dollars. Can I still afford the extended warranty?
C
With the discount, yes. It is the cheapest it has been all year.
T
I will take it. Could I have the receipt by e-mail as well?
Did You Catch It?

About the Clerk

1. What does she say the extra fifty dollars pays for?
A longer warranty: three years instead of one.
2. What does she say about the price on the label?
That it is not final, because sales tax is added at the till.
3. What does she warn him about paying with a Brazilian card?
That his bank may add a fee, and that the store's exchange rate is worse than the bank's.
