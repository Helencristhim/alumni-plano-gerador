"The second conditional" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on today's structure, including the two things that cost most: no would in the if half, and why English keeps were for every subject. Connection to Lesson 13: it repeats the shape often enough that the past tense stops feeling like a mistake.

Tip: pause after each example and say the same sentence about your own life. Then say the real version of it, with the present and will, and listen to the distance between the two.

"How important is play?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes about the things adults stop doing and wish they had not. Connection to Lesson 13: it is the same conversation you had in class -- what you would do with more time -- and the presenters use the structure repeatedly without ever naming it. Audio, vocabulary and full transcript are free on the page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then answer the episode's own question out loud, in three imaginary sentences about yourself.

Talk
"The surprising science of happiness" -- Dan Gilbert, TED

Twenty minutes from a psychologist about the lives we imagine for ourselves and how badly we predict them. Connection to Lesson 13: the whole talk is built on imaginary situations -- what would happen if, what we think we would feel -- so it is this structure at native speed, with a transcript.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Then say out loud one thing you are certain you would enjoy if you had more time, and one thing you suspect you would not.
