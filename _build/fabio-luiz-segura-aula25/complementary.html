Talk
Eduardo Briceño — “How to get better at the things you care about” (TED)

An educator explains the difference between the “performance zone” and the “learning zone”, and why people stop improving when they only perform. The perfect talk for the halfway point of a program.

Tip: decide which of your three focus areas needs more time in the learning zone.

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart (Stanford GSB) — “Give It to Me Straight: The Power of Honest, Constructive Feedback”

An episode about how to give and receive honest feedback without losing motivation. Useful for the rubric: your score, the teacher’s score, and the conversation about the difference.

Tip: write down one idea about receiving feedback. Use it when you compare your scores with the teacher.

Listen on Stanford GSB ↗
Video
Josh Kaufman — “The first 20 hours: how to learn anything” (TEDx, YouTube)

A short talk about how adults learn new skills, and why the beginning feels so hard. It explains the learning curve that you are now in the middle of.

Tip: listen for his four steps. Which one could help you with your third focus area?
