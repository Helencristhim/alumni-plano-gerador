Chapter 3: The Pattern

One Pattern, Three Small Words

I work · I have · in · on · at

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

I work on a metro site.
I have a degree in civil engineering.
My shift starts at seven.

What do the orange words have in common?

SubjectVerbExample
I / you / we / theywork, have, startI work on a metro site.
he / she / it / my shiftworks, has, startsMy shift starts at seven.
Negativedo not / does not + verbI do not work on Sunday.
Use it forThings that are true every day: your job, your shift, your commute, your routine.
Grammar Discovery

In, On, At

My shift starts at seven. The crew meets at the gate.
I work on a metro site. The visitor arrives on Monday.
I have a degree in civil engineering. We start in the morning.

Which word comes before a clock time? And before a day?

WordPlaceTime
ata point: at the gate, at the office, at homea clock time: at seven, at noon, at night
ona surface or a line: on the site, on Line Nine, on the tablea day or a date: on Monday, on July 3
ininside or an area or a field: in the tunnel, in Brazil, in civil engineeringa long period: in the morning, in September, in 2027
Quick trickat = a point · on = a surface or a day · in = inside something big

Gi

G
