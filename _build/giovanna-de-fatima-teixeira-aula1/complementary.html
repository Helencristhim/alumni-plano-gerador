Extra Practice

None of this is homework and nothing has to be delivered. It is English coming in through your ears while you do something else. Tick the box when you watch or listen, just so you remember what you have already seen.

Series
Extra English -- Episode 4: "Hector Looks for a Job"

A sitcom written for people who are coming back to English. In this episode the characters talk about jobs, interviews and what they do every day -- the same present simple you used today. They speak slowly and the subtitles are on screen.

Tip: watch the first ten minutes and listen for one thing only -- how each character says what they do for a living. You do not have to understand everything.

Podcast
5-Minute English Practice -- "Talking About Your Job"

Two friends catch up in a cafe and talk about what they do, where they work and what their day looks like. Five minutes, spoken slowly, with exactly the sentences you practised today. Good for the commute, when you cannot read or write but you can listen.

Tip: listen once from start to end without stopping, then again and say out loud the sentences you recognise. Three sentences is already a win.

YouTube
BBC Learning English -- Six short lessons on "in", "on" and "at"

Twenty minutes, six small lessons, one subject: the three little words that decided everything in class today. Each lesson is under four minutes, so you can watch one at a time.

Tip: watch one lesson a day, not all six at once. After each one, say three sentences about your own week using the word of the day.
