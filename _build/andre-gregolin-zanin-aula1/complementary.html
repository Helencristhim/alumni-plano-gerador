Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Ted Lasso — Season 1, Episode 1

An American lands in a country he does not know and has to explain who he is, in one line, to a new person every ten minutes. That is this whole lesson, played out in a corridor. The English is fast, modern and full of the expressions you said you never catch the first time.

Tip: stop at every introduction and write it down in one line. Then say your own version out loud, in the same shape.

Watch on Apple TV ↗
Talk
Julian Treasure — “How to speak so that people want to listen” (TED)

Ten minutes on the thing you said you want: sounding natural, not sounding correct. He works on register, pace and the habits that make a room stop listening — and none of it is about grammar.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on the first time, off the second. Write down the four habits he names and check which one you already do.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
BBC Learning English — “The art of conversation” (6 Minute English)

Six minutes, two native speakers, normal speed, on how a conversation is actually kept alive. It is exactly the level above where you are now, and the transcript is free on the page.

Tip: listen once with no text. Listen again with the transcript open and mark every phrase you understood by ear but would never have produced.
