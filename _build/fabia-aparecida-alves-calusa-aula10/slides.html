The Pattern Again

Which one blames nobody?

The revised proposal will be sent by Wednesday.
The employee file needs to be sent to us by Friday.
The budget has not been approved yet.
We completed the onboarding two days early.

Passive = be + past participle. Nobody is named, so nobody is blamed.

The last line is the only active one, on purpose: the credit is yours, so keep your name on it.
