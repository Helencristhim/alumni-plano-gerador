Stage 1: The same six words

Review

Nothing new here. Read each one and say it out loud. If it comes back on its own, that word is yours.

As per — exactly as agreed before, pointing back to something
"As per our call, here is what was agreed."
Pending — waiting, not decided yet, still open
"The budget is still pending on their side."
Approval — the formal yes that lets the work start
"We start as soon as we have your approval."
Acknowledge — to say the message arrived, before you answer it
"Just acknowledging receipt, a full answer follows Monday."
Transition — the period of moving from one way of working to another
"The transition took us two years."
Outgrow — to become too big for something that used to fit
"We outgrew the old system long before we replaced it."

Stage 3: The passive, one more time

Grammar

No new rule this week. The passive is be + past participle: will be sent, needs to be sent, has not been approved. Use it for the problem, so nobody is blamed. Keep the active for what your team did well.

"The signed contract ______ to you by Tuesday." (you are the one sending it)
A will be sent
B will send
Which one does NOT point a finger?
A The budget has not been approved yet.
B You have not approved the budget.
Which line is correct?
A The bank details have not been confirmed yet.
B The bank details have not confirmed yet.
Your team finished early. Which one keeps the credit?
A We completed the onboarding two days early.
B The onboarding was completed two days early by us.

Stage 6: Before class

Speaking
Record the same four sentences, but this time without reading them: what will be sent and by when, what you need from them and by when, what is still pending, and one thing your team did well. One sentence each. If the four come out, you are ready for the class.
