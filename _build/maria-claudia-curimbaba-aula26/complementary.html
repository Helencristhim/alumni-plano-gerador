Lesson theme: passing a message on -- relay, readout, debrief, paraphrase, verbatim, heads-up, clarify, misquote, downplay, flag; and reported speech for statements, questions and requests ("He said the container had not sailed", "She asked whether we could wait"). Passive exposure outside class -- tick each one when you finish it.

Talk
Celeste Headlee -- 10 ways to have a better conversation

A radio host on listening well enough to repeat somebody correctly, and on saying "I do not know" instead of filling the gap. Rule number nine is the one this class is built on.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down the two rules you break most often when you relay a message at work.
