Grammar Video
"The second conditional" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the shape you practiced today: if plus the past, would plus the verb, for situations that are imagined rather than planned. Connection to Lesson 5: this is the difference between "If they offer me the role" and "If they offered me the role".

Tip: pause after each example and say the same sentence twice, once as real and once as imagined. Two versions, then play again.

"The Upside of Quitting" -- Freakonomics Radio

An hour about the decisions people do not take, and the cost of staying where you are. It follows real people through the question you practiced today: what would happen if I walked away? The full transcript is on the page, so you can read along whenever you lose the thread.

Tip: listen in two halves on two different days. After each half, say one sentence out loud starting with "If I were her, I would...".

Talk
"The power of believing that you can improve" -- Carol Dweck, TED (10 minutes)

A psychologist explains the difference between "I am not good at this" and "I am not good at this yet", and what that one word changes. Ten minutes, very clear American English. Connection to Lesson 5: the whole talk is about the imagined version of yourself, which is exactly what the second conditional describes.

Tip: watch with English subtitles, then answer out loud in one minute: what would you try in English if you were sure you could not fail?
