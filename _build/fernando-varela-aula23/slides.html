Chapter 3: The Code

Not True Anymore

used to · didn't use to · Did you use to...?

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Orange Words Say?

"Twenty-four years ago, the hospital used to be much smaller."

"I used to work in finance, with budgets and paperwork."

"I didn't use to work with partners in other countries."

"Did you use to travel a lot, Sarah?"

Look at the orange words. Are these things true now? And in sentences 3 and 4, what happens to the letter D?

FormUseExample
used to + verbA habit or a situation in the past that is not true now.I used to work in finance.
didn't use to + verbNegative. After did, no D: use to.We didn't use to have video calls.
Did + subject + use to + verb?Question. Again, no D after did.Did you use to travel a lot?
Nowadays + present simpleTo compare with now.Nowadays, I work in the Oncology Network.
The trapUsed to is ONLY for the past. For a habit now, use the present simple: I usually drive to work — never I use to drive to work.

Used to tells the old story. Nowadays tells the new one.

Chapter 4: Sarah Visits

A Walk Through the Hospital

Sarah is in Sao Paulo for the first time. Fernando shows her around.

Dialogue

Then and Now at Einstein

S
This building is beautiful, Fernando. Was the hospital always this big?
F
No, not at all. When I started, twenty-four years ago, it used to be much smaller.
S
And did you use to work in oncology?
F
No, I didn't. I used to work in finance. I spent my days on budgets and paperwork.
S
Paperwork! I remember that. In Boston, everything used to be on paper too.
F
Here too. Patients used to wait weeks for an appointment. Nowadays, the waiting time is a few days.
S
And the treatments? Ten years ago, CAR-T was very rare in Latin America.
F
Yes, it used to be rare. Now it is more common, and our network takes it to partner hospitals.
S
That is a huge change. Is there anything you miss from the old days?
F
Honestly? The small team. We used to have lunch together every day.
Comprehension

About Sarah

1. What does Sarah ask about the hospital at the start?
She asks if the hospital was always this big.
2. What does Sarah remember about Boston?
That everything used to be on paper there too.
3. What does Sarah say about CAR-T ten years ago?
That it was very rare in Latin America.
