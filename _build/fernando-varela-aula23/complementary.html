"Used to" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

A short, clear lesson on used to: habits and situations in the past that are not true now, with the negative and the question. Connection to Lesson 23: this is exactly "I used to work in finance."

Tip: pause after each example and say one sentence about your first years at the hospital with the same structure: "I used to..."

"Is social media dead?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters talk about how people used social media in the past and how they use it nowadays. Connection to Lesson 23: a conversation about then and now, with a lot of used to and nowadays.

Tip: listen once without the transcript and write down two things people used to do. Then listen again with the transcript and check them.

The Intern (2015)

A seventy-year-old man starts working at a modern online company and compares how work used to be with how it is nowadays. Connection to Lesson 23: experience, change, and a young team that learns from an older colleague.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time Ben talks about his old job, notice how he describes the past.
