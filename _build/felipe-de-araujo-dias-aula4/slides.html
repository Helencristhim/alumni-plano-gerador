Chapter 4: What Already Happened

One Tense for Everything Finished

The past simple, and the -ed that only appears once

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I printed my boarding pass on Sunday night."

"The officer asked three questions."

"He took a taxi to the hotel."

"I did not catch the last one."

FormUse it forExample
Regular verbsAdd -ed. The spelling is easy; the sound is not.ask → asked · print → printed · walk → walked
Irregular verbsA different word. There is no rule, only a list worth learning.take → took · come → came · speak → spoke · catch → caught
Negativedid not + the BASE form. The past is already inside did.I did not catch it. Never: I did not caught it.
QuestionDid + subject + BASE form. Same rule, same reason.Did you check in online?
To bewas and were, with no did at all.The flight was fine. Nobody was rude.

The past appears once per sentence. If did is there, the verb goes back to the base form and gives the past away.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I did not caught the question."
"I did not catch the question."
"Did you checked in online?"
"Did you check in online?"
"He was went to Chicago in March."
"He went to Chicago in March."

Two pasts in one sentence is one too many. After did and after was, the other verb goes back to its simplest form.

The Hotel Desk

F
Good evening. I have a booking for four nights. The name is Dias.
D
Good evening, Mr Dias. Let me find it. Did you book directly with us, or through the fair?
F
Through the fair. I made the booking in January, and I asked for a quiet room.
D
I can see the note. You asked for a floor away from the elevator, and we kept one for you. May I see your passport?
F
Of course. Here it is.
D
Thank you. Your room is on the ninth floor, and breakfast is from six thirty to ten.
F
Perfect. One question. I arrived without an adapter. Do you have one?
D
We do. I will send it up. Did you fly in this afternoon?
F
This morning. Immigration took an hour, and then the taxi took another one.
D
That sounds like a long day. The trade fair shuttle leaves from the front door at eight.
F
Good to know. Could you tell me where the shuttle stops at the convention center?
D
At the south entrance. Show your badge to the driver and he will let you on.
