Lesson 24 -- Pre-class

A Meeting with American Colleagues -- There Is, There Are

Ten to twenty minutes before class. How to say what there is and what there are in a meeting, with countable and uncountable nouns. Key words: the agenda, an item, room to grow, an impact, a source, a late payment.

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Room to grow -- the space or the chance to get bigger or better
"There is a lot of room to grow in Chile."
An impact -- a strong effect on something
"The new rule had a big impact on the bank."
A source -- a place or a person that gives you information
"We only use sources that we can check."
A late payment -- money that a person pays after the date
"There were fewer late payments this year."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

In a meeting, Americans often describe the situation with there is and there are. “There is a problem with the report. There are two mistakes in the numbers.”

With nouns you cannot count, like information, data, work and time, they use there is and much: “There isn’t much time.” With nouns you can count, they use there are and many: “There aren’t many options.”

Which sentence is correct?
A "There is a lot of information."
B "There are a lot of information."
C "There are many informations."
Why do we say "There aren’t many options"?
A Because options is a noun you cannot count.
B Because options is a noun you can count.
C Because the sentence is a question.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- There Is or There Are?

GRAMMAR

One thing, or a noun you cannot count: there is. Two or more things: there are.

JobFormExample
One thingthere is + a / anThere is a meeting at ten.
A noun you cannot countthere is + (much / a lot of)There is a lot of data.
Two or more thingsthere are + (many / a lot of)There are three new clients.
A questionIs there…? / Are there…?Are there any questions?

Never: There are a lot of information · There is many problems · Have a meeting today (for there is a meeting)
