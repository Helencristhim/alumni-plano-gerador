Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
David Grady: “How to save the world (or at least yourself) from bad meetings” (6 min)

A short and funny talk about meetings with no agenda, and what you can do about them.

Tip: count how many times he uses there is and there are, and repeat two of his sentences.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Jason Fried: “Why work doesn’t happen at work” (15 min)

A company founder on why there are so many interruptions at the office, and why there is so little time to think.

Tip: after the talk, say in one minute what there is too much of and what there is too little of in your week.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Margaret Heffernan: “Dare to disagree” (12 min)

Why the best teams are the ones where people disagree in meetings, with real stories from business and science.

Tip: choose one story and retell it in two minutes, as you did in class with the 4/3/2.
