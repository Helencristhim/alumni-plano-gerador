Chapter 3: The Extra Information

One Sentence, More Information

Greg, who works in New York, ... · my company, which ... · Tom, whose team ...

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Greg, who you met in New York, put me in touch with Tom."

"My company, which is based in Sao Paulo, makes ingredients for cosmetics."

"Houston, where the conference is this year, is a big center for the chemical industry."

"I'm meeting Tom Reyes, whose team buys our ingredients."

Look at the orange words. Cover them. Does the sentence still work?

WordUseExample
whoextra information about a personGreg, who works in New York, is a good friend.
whichextra information about a thing or a company (never 'that' here)My company, which is based in Sao Paulo, ...
whereextra information about a placeHouston, where the conference is, is very hot.
whosethe person or company that something belongs toTom, whose team buys our ingredients, ...
defining (Lesson 15)no commas: the information is necessary to know WHO or WHICHThe supplier who I met in Lyon called me.

The extra information goes between commas. When you speak, make a small pause before and after it. With commas, never use that: use who or which.

Chapter 4: At the Reception

Seven p.m. on the Terrace

Lucimara meets Tom, the contact that Greg recommended

Dialogue

At the Reception

L
Excuse me, are you Tom Reyes? I saw your name badge. I think we have a mutual contact.
T
I am! And you must be Lucimara. Greg, who I've known for years, told me to look for you.
L
Yes, Greg put me in touch with you last month. I'm sorry I didn't reach out sooner.
T
No problem at all. So, you're based in Sao Paulo, right?
L
That's right. I'm a director at a chemical company, which makes ingredients for cosmetics and cleaning products.
T
Interesting. My counterpart in Brazil, whose team buys from local suppliers, mentioned your company.
L
Really? That's great to hear. Are you going to the keynote tomorrow morning?
T
Of course. And after that, I'm speaking on a panel, which is about supply chains in Latin America.
L
Then let's meet at the breakout session in the afternoon, where we can talk business.
T
Perfect. Here's my card. And please, reach out anytime.
Comprehension

About Tom

1. How long has Tom known Greg?
For years. Greg told him to look for Lucimara.
2. Who mentioned Lucimara's company to Tom?
His counterpart in Brazil, whose team buys from local suppliers.
3. What is Tom doing after the keynote?
He is speaking on a panel about supply chains in Latin America.
