Have You Closed the Deal Yet?

2Have you sent the proposal yet?No, not yet. We still need… / Yes, we’ve already…
3Have you signed the contract yet?No, not yet. We still need… / Yes, we’ve already…
4Have you met the new client yet?No, not yet. I still need… / Yes, I’ve already…

Drill 3

Not Yet

Hear the sentence, make it negative with yet, then add what you still need.

#You hearCue: still need
1We have closed the deal.the final price
2The client has paid the invoice.a new purchase order
3I have finished the report.the numbers from Mexico
4They have answered our email.a call with the director
5We have approved the credit line.two more documents
Drill 4

Still Waiting

Something did not happen and it is late. Use still + haven’t.

#SituationSay
1The client promised the documents last week.They still haven’t…
2The bank wanted to sign in May.We still haven’t…
3You asked for the data on Monday.I still haven’t…
4The director said he would call.He still hasn’t…
Drill 5

3sell this product in BrazilWe have never…
4have a complaint from themWe have never…
Drill 6

All Four Together

Now you ask. Your teacher is the client. Use already, yet, still, never.

#Ask the clientThen report
1Have you… yet?They have already…
2Have you already…?They haven’t… yet.
3Have you ever…?They have never…
4Is there anything you still…?They still need…
Work Situation

The Status Call

A big client in the United States wants a status update on the new credit project. Some things are done, some are not done yet, and you still need two things from them.

1. What have you already done for this client?
2. What haven’t you done yet, and why?
3. What do you still need from them?
Role-Play

Keep the Client Happy

A video call with Jennifer, the client's head of risk. She is not happy: the project is late. Be honest, be direct, and keep the relationship.

  • Round 1: Jennifer asks what is done and what is late. You answer with already, yet and still.
  • Round 2: You lead. Ask Jennifer what she still needs and what she has never had from a supplier.
