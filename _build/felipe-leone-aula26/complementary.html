Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Celeste Headlee: “10 ways to have a better conversation” (11 min)

A radio host with ten simple rules for better conversations, very useful for calls with clients.

Tip: choose two rules and use them in your next client call. Then say what you have already tried and what you haven’t tried yet.

TED Talk
Ernesto Sirolli: “Want to help someone? Shut up and listen!” (17 min)

Funny and true stories about what happens when you do not listen to the client.

Tip: say in two sentences what the people in his stories had never asked the clients.

TED Talk
Simon Sinek: “How great leaders inspire action” (18 min)

Why clients buy why you do it, not what you do: the golden circle.

Tip: explain your company in three sentences, starting with why.
