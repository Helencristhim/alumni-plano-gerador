Chapter 4: The Code

He Works, She Works

One letter that changes the verb

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"He gets up early."

"He goes to the gym after work."

"He doesn’t watch TV."

"Does she work on Saturdays?"

Look at the blue words. Last class it was "I get up", "I go". What letter is new with he and she? And in the negative and the question, where does it go?

JobFormExample
He, she, itverb + s / esHe works. She goes to the gym.
Havehas (not haves)He has a lot of meetings.
Negativedoesn’t + verb (no s)He doesn’t watch TV.
QuestionDoes + he / she + verb (no s)?Does she work on Saturdays?
How oftenalways > usually > often > sometimes > neverHe usually gets up early.
Where it goesbefore the verb, after am / is / areHe never has a long lunch. His day is always busy.

In one line: he works, she goes, it has. In the negative and the question, the s goes to does: Does he work? He doesn't work.
