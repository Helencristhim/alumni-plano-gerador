Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example out loud. These are the words a brand uses every day behind the counter.

Process -- all the steps you follow to make or do something
"Let me explain the process, from the drawing to the store."
Supplier -- a company that sells materials or products to another company
"Our supplier sends the fabric from another city."
Sample -- one test piece that a brand makes before it makes many
"If the sample does not fit well, we make another one."
Stock -- the products that a store or company has and is ready to sell
"Old stock costs money every month it stays in the warehouse."
Invoice -- a document that tells a company how much it has to pay
"If the invoice is wrong, we send it back."
To approve -- to officially say yes to a plan, a product or a payment
"When the manager approves the sample, the factory starts."
To deliver -- to take something to the person or place that ordered it
"If the supplier delivers late, the whole process stops."
Sold out -- when there is nothing left to buy, because everybody bought it
"When a jacket is popular, it is sold out in a week."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Ryan sent me a sad message: his favorite jacket is sold out again. So I explained the process to him. If a brand makes too much, it loses money, because old stock is expensive. Every jacket starts with one sample. If the manager approves the sample, the factory starts. If she doesn't approve it, the designer makes a new one. And when the supplier delivers late, the whole process stops. That is why a popular jacket is sold out in a week. If Ryan joins the waiting list, he gets an email when it is back.
Why does the text say "If the manager approves the sample" and not "If the manager will approve the sample"?
A Because will is only for questions
B Because the manager is a woman, and will is only used for men when we talk about the future
C Because it is a process that is always true, so we use the present simple after if
What happens when the supplier delivers late?
A The whole process stops
B The manager approves a new sample and the factory works faster than before to finish the order
C The jacket is cheaper

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- If and When for Things That Always Happen

GRAMMAR

Use if or when + present simple, then present simple again, for facts, rules and processes: things that happen every time.

affirmativeIf the manager approves the sample, the factory starts.
negativeIf you don't check the invoice, you pay too much.
questionWhat happens if the supplier delivers late?
when = ifWhen a jacket is popular, it sells out fast.

Careful 1: no will after if. Not if the manager will approve; it is if the manager approves.

Careful 2: he, she, it need the -s on BOTH sides. If the supplier delivers late, the process stops.
