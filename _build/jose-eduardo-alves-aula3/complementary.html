Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Documentary
FRONTLINE (PBS) — "Boeing's Fatal Flaw" (full documentary)

An entire investigation told in the past: what the engineers wrote, what the executives decided, what the regulator did not check. Almost every sentence of the narration is the grammar of this lesson, at professional speed.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on and write down twelve past verbs in the first twenty minutes. Mark which ones are irregular.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Podcast
Planet Money (NPR) — Episode 563: "I Was Wrong"

A typing mistake inside a signed contract, and the twenty-five million dollars it cost. The whole story is told the way you told your week today: what somebody wrote, what nobody read, and what happened next.

Tip: stop the episode at the halfway point and say out loud, in five past sentences, what has happened so far. Then finish it.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
CNBC — "The Rise And Fall Of Theranos"

Fifteen minutes of business history in the past simple: investors put money in, journalists asked questions, regulators arrived, and the company closed. Short sentences, clear narration, one story with a beginning and an end.

Tip: watch it once for the story. Then tell it back in ten sentences, out loud, in the order it happened.
