Lesson 26 -- Pre-class

The Sales Pitch -- Negotiating Terms

A supplier pitches a training bundle, and you negotiate the terms. Key words: quote, discount, warranty, trial period, upfront, installment, bundle, cost-effective, win-win, meet halfway. Structure: conditions -- as long as, provided that, on condition that, unless.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Quote -- a written document that shows the price a supplier will charge
"I asked three suppliers for a quote."
Discount -- a reduction in the normal price
"Can you give us a discount if we buy two?"
Warranty -- a promise that the company will repair or replace a product for a period of time
"The robot is still under warranty."
Trial period -- a short time when you can use a product before you decide to buy it
"During the trial period, ten nurses tested the simulator."
Upfront -- paid at the beginning, before you receive the product or service
"They want half of the money upfront."
Installment -- one of several smaller payments you make over time
"We paid for the new console in twelve installments."
Bundle -- a group of products or services sold together for one price
"The bundle comes with software and support."
Cost-effective -- giving good results for the money you spend
"Simulation is a cost-effective way to train a team."
Win-win -- good for both sides of an agreement
"A longer contract for a better price is a win-win."
Meet halfway -- to accept part of what the other side wants, and they accept part of what you want
"They wanted forty days and we wanted twenty, so we met halfway at thirty."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Mark, a sales rep from SimSurg, visits Einstein Hospital. He presents a training bundle: a simulator, software and forty hours of training. Andreia listens and asks for a written quote. The price is high, but Mark offers a ten percent discount and a free trial period.

Andreia sets her conditions. "We can pay thirty percent upfront, as long as the rest is in monthly installments." And: "We will not sign unless the warranty covers three years." Notice the verbs after the linkers: the rest is, the warranty covers -- present simple, even for the future.

Mark cannot give a bigger discount, so they meet halfway: three years of warranty, provided that the hospital signs a maintenance contract. It is a cost-effective solution and a win-win deal.

1. What does Mark offer at the start?
A A discount and a trial period.
B A three-year warranty and free maintenance for every robot in the hospital.
C A lower price if Andreia pays everything upfront today.
2. Which sentence is correct?
A "We will not sign unless the warranty will cover three years."
B "We will not sign unless the warranty covers three years."
C "We will not sign unless the warranty doesn't cover three years."
3. What is the condition for the three-year warranty?
A The hospital must pay the full price before the end of the month.
B Andreia must buy a second simulator next year.
C The hospital signs a maintenance contract.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Conditions

GRAMMAR

Linker + present simple, even for the future. Unless means if ... not.

LinkerMeaningExample
as long asonly ifWe can sign, as long as the price includes training.
provided thatonly if (more formal)We will pay upfront, provided that the rest is in installments.
on condition thatonly if (contracts)They will extend the warranty on condition that we sign.
unlessif ... notWe will not sign unless they give us a trial period.

Remember: no will after the linker (provided that you give, not provided that you will give), and no second not after unless.
