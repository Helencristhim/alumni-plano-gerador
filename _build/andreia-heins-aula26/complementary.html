Lesson 26 -- The Sales Pitch

Talk
William Ury -- "The walk from 'no' to 'yes'" (TED)

One of the most famous negotiators in the world explains how to find a deal that is good for both sides, even in a very difficult situation. Perfect after a lesson about meeting halfway.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then write down one idea you can use with your next supplier, as a sentence with as long as or unless.

Talk
Adam Galinsky -- "How to speak up for yourself" (TED)

A social psychologist explains why it is hard to ask for what we want in a negotiation, and gives simple ways to do it with more confidence.

Tip: pause after each tip and say in English how it could help you in a meeting with a sales rep.

Watch on TED →
Talk
Simon Sinek -- "How great leaders inspire action" (TED)

Why do some pitches convince us and others do not? Simon Sinek says it starts with why. Watch it and think about the sales pitches you hear from suppliers at work.

Tip: after watching, explain in one minute why your team needs a new tool, starting with why.
