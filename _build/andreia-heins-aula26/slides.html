Three Moves of a Negotiation

1. Listen to the pitch and ask for a written quote.

2. Set your conditions clearly and politely.

3. Meet halfway and close a deal that is good for both sides.

Diagnostic

Your Conditions

A sales rep offers your team a simulator with training for 42,000 dollars. You like the product, but you want a better price, a longer warranty and time to pay. Tell him two things you need before you sign.

Chapter 4: The Code

Only If...

Words for conditions

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Colored Words Have in Common?

"We can sign this month, as long as the price includes training."

"We will pay forty percent upfront, provided that the rest is in installments."

"They will extend the warranty on condition that we sign a maintenance contract."

"We will not sign unless the warranty covers three years."

Do these sentences talk about now or about the future? Which verb form comes after the colored words?

LinkerMeaningExample
as long asonly if (friendly, common)We can sign, as long as the price includes training.
provided thatonly if (more formal)We will pay upfront, provided that the rest is in installments.
on condition thatonly if (very formal, contracts)They extend the warranty on condition that we sign.
unlessif ... notWe will not sign unless the warranty covers three years.
The verbAfter the linker: present simple, even for the future. The will goes in the other part of the sentence.

Linker + present simple. Unless already means not: no double negative.

Grammar Practice

Set Your Conditions

Say it first, then click to check

"We can sign today, as long as the price ___includes the software." (include)
"We will buy the bundle, provided that you ___deliver it in May." (deliver)
"We will not sign ___unless you give us a trial period." (= if you do not give us)
"They will lower the price on ___condition that we sign this month."
"I can pay thirty percent upfront, as long as the warranty ___covers three years." (cover)
"Unless the supplier ___agrees, we will look for another company." (agree)
Detective

Spot the Error

0 / 4 errors found

"We will sign, provided that you will give us a discount."
"We will sign, provided that you give us a discount." — no will after the linker
"We will not sign unless you don't lower the price."
"We will not sign unless you lower the price." — unless already means not
"We can pay upfront, as long as the warranty cover two years."
"...as long as the warranty covers two years." — he / she / it: verb + s
"They will add training on condition of we sign today."
"They will add training on condition that we sign today." — on condition that + subject + verb

A Message from SimSurg

The sales rep summarizes his offer. Sound first — no text.

The simulator, the software and forty hours of hands-on training.
2. What must the hospital do to get the ten percent discount?
Sign the contract before the end of the month.
3. What is the condition for the two-year warranty?
SimSurg technicians must do the maintenance.
Listening 2 · The Deal

Good News for Claudia

After the call, Andreia updates her manager. Sound first — no text.

1. How will the hospital pay?
Thirty percent upfront and the rest in six monthly installments.
2. Why will SimSurg extend the warranty to three years?
Because the hospital will sign a maintenance contract.
3. How did they meet halfway?
The discount stays at ten percent, but SimSurg adds twenty extra hours of training for free.
