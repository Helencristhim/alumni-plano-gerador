Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Julian Treasure — “How to speak so that people want to listen” (TED)

Ten minutes on register, pace, pitch and silence — the tools behind a voice that a room of international investors can follow. It is the practical side of today’s first recording.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles. Then choose one of his vocal exercises and try it before your next opening.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
Freakonomics Radio — “How to Have Great Conversations”

An hour on what makes a conversation work, including why people remember how a conversation ended and how the best talkers hand the floor back. Useful for the dinner before every panel.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Listen once without it, then find the three sentences you would steal for a GRI dinner.

Listen on Freakonomics ↗
Video
Patrick Winston — “How to Speak” (MIT, YouTube)

A legendary MIT lecture on how to start a talk, how to end it, and why the last slide matters more than the first. Everything in it applies to a conference opening.

Tip: watch the first fifteen minutes on how to start. Compare his advice with the way you opened your last event.
