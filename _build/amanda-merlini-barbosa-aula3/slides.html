Chapter 3: The Alignment Call

Item Three Is Yours

A weekly alignment call, line by line

Dialogue

Ten Minutes on the Agenda

P
Right, item three. Amanda, the floor is yours, and I have a flight at six.
A
Thank you. On item three, we have been reviewing the delayed shipment since Friday, and I have two options for you.
P
Two options. Good. But first, what has legal actually been doing all week?
A
We have been writing to the agent every day and we have been reading the contract line by line. We have not sent a formal notice yet.
P
Why not? The vessel has been sitting at that port for six days.
A
That is exactly where I want to raise a concern. If we send the notice today, the contract gives them ten days to answer, and the ship waits. If we wait one week, we lose the argument later.
P
So what do you want from this meeting?
A
A decision today, not a discussion. I recommend the notice, and I will follow up on the answer myself.
P
Agreed. Send it. Anything else before I run?
A
Let me wrap up. Two action items: I send the notice today, and you confirm the new delivery date by Friday.
Comprehension

About the Conversation

1. Why is Paul in a hurry, and how does that change the way the item is handled?
He has a flight at six, so the item has to end with a decision instead of a discussion.
2. What is the concern about sending the formal notice today?
The contract gives the other side ten days to answer, so the ship waits. But waiting a week loses the argument later.
3. What are the two action items at the end, and who owns each one?
Amanda sends the notice today; Paul confirms the new delivery date by Friday.
Chapter 4: The Code

What You Do, and What You Have Been Doing

The job, and the week

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I oversee the legal department."

"We have been reviewing the shipment since Friday."

"I liaise with the commercial team every day."

"The vessel has been waiting at the port for six days."

Two of them are true every week of the year. Two are only true right now. Which is which?

FormUseExample
Present simplethe job itself: what is true every week, with or without you in the roomI oversee the department. I liaise with the commercial team.
Present perfect continuous
have / has been + ing		work that started in the past, is still going, and is the reason you are talking about itWe have been reviewing the contract since Friday.
for and since, againfor six days, since Friday. They work here exactly as they worked in the last lesson.
Result or activity?"I have read the contract" = it is done, you know what is in it. "I have been reading the contract" = you are in the middle of it. In a meeting, the second one explains why you have no answer yet.
Never continuousknow, want, need, understand, mean. Say "I have known this buyer for years", never "I have been knowing".

Sound Check

Say It Clearly

The weak form of have and has · the sentences of this lesson

Why this one is hard for a Portuguese speaker

The habit: every word gets its full weight, so the sentence comes out as four heavy blocks: I. HAVE. BEEN. WORKING.

In English: the helper shrinks and the main verb keeps the stress. "I've been WORKING." "She's been CALLING." "We've been REVIEWING."

Why it matters twice: it makes you sound natural, and it makes the listening easier, because this is how you will hear it in the room.

Chapter 5: Real Speed

What Arrives Before the Room

The agenda change, and the update nobody wants to repeat live

Listening 1

The Agenda Changed

Clara leaves a message about Wednesday. Sound first, no text.

1. What are the two changes to the meeting, and why did the time move?
It moves from four o'clock to two, because the commercial director is flying out that evening, and the agenda is shorter: the pricing review was cut.
2. Which item belongs to Amanda, and how long does she have?
Item three, the training on the compliance policy, and she has ten minutes.
3. What has gone wrong since March, and what is Clara asking each owner to do?
Nobody has been taking minutes, so two decisions were repeated. Each owner writes the action items for their own item and sends them the same day.
Listening 2

The Update Before the Update

Paul records where things stand, so the meeting does not have to. Sound first, no text.

1. What have the new buyer's team been asking for in every round?
A shorter payment term.
2. What has his own team been doing about the delayed shipment, and with what result?
Calling the agent twice a day, and getting nothing.
3. What does Paul say he needs on Wednesday, and what does he not want?
He needs a decision, with both options and the risk on each. He does not want a legal opinion.

1. Two items say "for decision" and one says "for information". What changes in the way you prepare them?
A decision item needs options and a recommendation. An information item only needs a clear, short status.
2. The agenda has a hard stop at 2:40. What does that mean for item three?
It is the last item, so it can lose its ten minutes. The status has to be short and the decision has to come first.
3. Say the first sentence you would use to open item three, out loud.

Chapter 6: Your Turn

Ten Minutes That Are Yours

Quick Fire

Answer in One Sentence

Role-Play 1 of 3 — Guided

Open Item Three

Task: Paul says: "Item three. Amanda, the floor is yours." Four sentences, in this order: open the item and say where things stand, give the one fact everybody needs, say what you recommend, and close with the action items.

Support:

Thanks. On item three, we have been...The one thing you need to know is...I recommend...Let me wrap up. Two action items: ...
Role-Play 2 of 3 — Semi-free

Raise the Concern

Task: Paul wants the full legal review of a hundred-page contract in two weeks. You do not think it is possible. Raise the concern, give the reason and propose something else. Sixty seconds.

Support:

I would like to raise a concern about...We have been working on...What I can offer is...Can we agree on...?
Role-Play 3 of 3 — Free

The Whole Item, Then Again

Task: Run item three from start to finish. You will be interrupted twice: once off the point, and once with "we have five minutes left". Then hear the feedback and run it again.

No support on the screen. Target: one status with have been + ing, one concern with a reason, the conversation brought back to the agenda, and a close with owner and date.
