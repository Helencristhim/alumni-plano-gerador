Film
Margin Call (2011)

Almost the whole film is one long meeting: a technical person has to explain a complicated problem to a director who does not understand it, in a room with a hard stop. That is the skill of this lesson, at feature length.

Tip: watch the boardroom scene with English subtitles and count how many times somebody says "speak to me as you would to a young child". Then notice what changes in the explanation after that line.

Podcast
How I Lawyer -- #148: C.J. Mahoney, Microsoft General Counsel, Former Deputy US Trade Representative

A general counsel on what it takes to run a legal team inside a business: how he decides what reaches him, how he keeps the other departments close, and why he thinks trust is the whole job.

Tip: listen for the present simple when he describes the role, and for "we have been" when he describes what the team is in the middle of. Write down one of each.

Video
David Grady: How to save the world (or at least yourself) from bad meetings

Six minutes, funny, and about the exact room you sit in every week. He also gives you the sentence for asking why you were invited, which is a useful thing to own in English.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, stop after each joke and say what happened in one sentence, out loud.
