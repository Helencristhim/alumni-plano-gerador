Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Cathy O’Neil -- "The era of blind faith in big data must end" (TED)

Fifteen minutes on the line this lesson draws: the model decides, and a person still answers for the rule behind it. She is a mathematician, she speaks slowly, and almost every sentence is in the third person — which is the grammar you practised today.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Write down five sentences she says about what an algorithm does, and five about what it does not do.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
The Indicator from Planet Money -- "Your Lifetime Value Score"

Nine minutes on the scores companies build about a customer who never meets them — exactly the situation of a bank with no branches. Short, fast, and full of the words of this lesson: data, a model, a target, to approve, to decline.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Listen once without it, then read it and listen again.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
"Day in the Life of a London Credit Risk / Financial Analyst"

Somebody with your analyst’s job, narrating a whole morning in English: the screens he opens, the numbers he reads first, and the cases he sends up. It is the reading of this lesson, spoken, at real speed.

Tip: stop the video every time he says what he does and repeat the sentence in the third person: "He opens...", "He reviews...", "He does not decide...".
