Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Somebody Feed Phil — Season 7, "The Real Orlando" (Netflix)

Florida, light from the first minute, and nobody dies. Phil talks to a stranger every four minutes and never accepts a one-sentence answer — watch how he asks why, and how the other person opens up the moment he does.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Count how many times you hear because and so in the first ten minutes.

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "How to be a supercommunicator"

Twenty minutes on the exact skill of this lesson: why a conversation dies on a short answer, and how one extra sentence turns a polite exchange into a real one. The idea is the same one you heard in today’s first recording.

Tip: the full transcript is on the same page. Listen once without it, then read it and listen again.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Celeste Headlee — "10 ways to have a better conversation" (TED)

Eleven minutes from a radio host who has interviewed people for a living. Two of her ten rules are today’s lesson: ask questions that cannot be answered with one word, and never be afraid to say you do not know.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Choose the two rules that are hardest for you and say why, out loud, in two sentences.
