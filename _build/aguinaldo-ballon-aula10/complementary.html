TED -- Patricia Ryan, "Don't insist on English!" (10 min)

A teacher who worked for decades in the Gulf argues that the world should not measure intelligence by English, and that ideas should not be lost in translation. A different point of view for somebody who works in four languages.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles. Then say if you agree with her, starting with "I would say that...".

BBC 6 Minute English -- "Can apps teach you a language?"

Six minutes on what apps can and cannot do when you learn a language, with two presenters with different accents. Good listening practice, and a topic close to your own routine with English and Italian.

Tip: listen once for the gist only. Then listen again and write down one indirect question you would ask the presenters.

Video
English Speaking Success -- "How to Understand Native English Speakers: Advanced Listening Skills"

A teacher explains why native speakers are hard to understand (linking, reduced sounds, speed) and gives practical exercises to train your ear. Directly connected to your biggest challenge.

Tip: do one of the exercises every morning for a week, at 0.75x speed first and then at normal speed.
