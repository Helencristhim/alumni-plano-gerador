Not the Look-Alike

These words look like the ones you know, but in English the meaning changes. Say the sentence with the English word.

#Not thisSay this
1She is very sympathetic. (for nice and easy to talk to)She is very friendly.
2He is very educated. (for good manners)He is very polite.
3My boss is comprehensive. (for he accepts your problems)My boss is understanding.
4She is a very exigent manager.She is a very demanding manager.

Straight to the Point

30 Seconds

Describe one person in 30 seconds. Five adjectives, at least one adverb. No detours.

1. Your manager: what is this person like, and how does this person work?
2. The best analyst on your team: why?
3. A client you like to work with: what makes the meetings easy?
