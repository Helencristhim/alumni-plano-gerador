Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Susan Cain: “The power of introverts” (19 min)

A writer on the quiet people in every team: how they think, how they work, and why companies often describe them in the wrong way.

Tip: write down five adjectives she uses for people, and one adverb for how they work.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Adam Grant: “Are you a giver or a taker?” (13 min)

An organizational psychologist on three types of people at work, and how to recognize each one on your team.

Tip: after the talk, describe one person on your team as a giver, a taker or a matcher, in three sentences.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Simon Sinek: “Why good leaders make you feel safe” (12 min)

What makes a good leader, described with simple adjectives and clear examples from companies and the military.

Tip: pause twice and say how the leaders in the talk act, with an adverb: calmly, generously, quickly.
