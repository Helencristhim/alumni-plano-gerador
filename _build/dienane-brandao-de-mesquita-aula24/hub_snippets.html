Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. (Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo.)

Tour -- a visit to many places (passeio, tour)
"We took a city tour on Monday." (Fizemos um passeio pela cidade na segunda.)
Guide -- a person who shows a place (guia)
"The guide was very friendly." (O guia foi muito simpático.)
Monument -- a famous building or statue (monumento)
"This monument is very famous." (Este monumento é muito famoso.)
Cathedral -- a very big church (catedral)
"The cathedral in Brasilia is beautiful." (A catedral de Brasília é linda.)
Souvenir -- a small thing to remember a trip (lembrancinha)
"I bought a souvenir for my son." (Comprei uma lembrancinha para meu filho.)
View -- what you can see from a place (vista)
"The view from the TV Tower is amazing." (A vista da Torre de TV é incrível.)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the dialogue and answer the questions. (Leia e responda.)

Marcos: "Where did you go last month?" Dienane: "I went to Brasília with my family." Marcos: "Did you take a city tour?" Dienane: "Yes, I did. The guide was great. We saw the cathedral and many monuments." Marcos: "Did you buy a souvenir?" Dienane: "No, I didn't, but I took many photos of the view from the TV Tower." To ask about the past, we use did + the base verb.

1. Did Dienane take a city tour? (Ela fez o passeio?)
A Yes, she did.
B No, she didn't.
C The text does not say.
2. Did she buy a souvenir? (Ela comprou lembrancinha?)
A Yes, she did.
B No, she didn't.
C Yes, she bought two.
3. Which question is correct? (Qual pergunta está certa?)
A Did you went to Brasilia?
B Did you go to Brasilia?
C You did go Brasilia?

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Questions in the Past (Perguntas no Passado)

GRAMMAR

Para perguntar sobre o passado, usamos DID + o verbo na forma base. / To ask about the past, use DID + the base verb.

Use / UsoPattern / EstruturaExample / Exemplo
Pergunta sim/nãoDid + you + verbo base?Did you visit the cathedral? (Você visitou a catedral?)
Resposta curtaYes, I did. / No, I didn't.Yes, I did. (Sim, visitei.)
Pergunta de informaçãoWhere / What + did + you + verbo base?Where did you stay? (Onde você ficou?)
Cuidado / Carefuldepois de DID, verbo SEM passadoDid you go? (nunca "Did you went?")

Key point / Ponto-chave: o DID já carrega o passado, então o verbo volta para a forma base: Did you see? — nunca "Did you saw?". E na resposta curta não repita o verbo: Yes, I did.

Stage 3: Pronunciation

Speaking

Listen, then record yourself. Focus: "Did you" sounds like DIDJU, and the voice goes UP at the end of a yes/no question.

Aonde você foi nas férias?
Did you like the city?
Você gostou da cidade?
Yes, I did. It was beautiful.
Sim, gostei. Era linda.
