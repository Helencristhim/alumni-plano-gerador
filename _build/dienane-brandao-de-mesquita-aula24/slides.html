Chapter 3: The Code

One Word to Ask: Did

Did you...? · Where did you...?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Did you go to Brasília? — Yes, I did.
Did you like the food? — No, I didn't.
Where did you stay?
What did you see?

What do the orange words have in common? Is the verb after DID in the past?

UseFormExample
Yes / No questionDid + you / she + base verb?Did you visit the cathedral?
Short answerYes, I did. / No, I didn't.Did she buy a souvenir? — Yes, she did.
Information questionWhere / What / When + did + you + base verb?Where did you stay?
Carefulafter DID, NO past formDid you go? (not "Did you went?")

Key idea: DID carries the past. So the verb after it goes back to the base form: Did you see? — never "Did you saw?"
