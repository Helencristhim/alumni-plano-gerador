Chapter 3: The Code

I Recommend Doing, and I Plan to Do

Two lists, and no rule

Grammar Discovery

What Decides -ing or to?

"I recommend saying the number out loud."

"I plan to be finished in ten minutes."

"People avoid doing this because it feels mechanical."

"I need to have a decision today."

Two red verbs carry -ing and two carry to. Nothing in the second half of the sentence decides it. Look at the blue verb instead: the first one chooses for the second.

JobFormExample
These take -ingrecommend, suggest, consider, avoid, keepI recommend saying the number.
These take toplan, need, want, decide, expect, agreeI plan to be finished in ten minutes.
What decides itthe FIRST verb, never the secondsuggest starting · decide to start
Negativeavoid + not... use avoid aloneWe avoid sending it on a Friday.
After a prepositionalways -ingbefore starting · instead of waiting
Start and beginboth forms work, same meaningI started talking / I started to talk
The trapthere is no logic to learn — there is a listNot I recommend to say. Say I recommend saying.

In one line: the first verb decides, and eleven verbs cover almost everything you say in a meeting.

Listening 1 · A Director

Three Points, Ten Minutes

A director running his own slot at a board. Sound first — no text.

1. What two things does he say in his opening, before any content?
How many points he is going to cover, and that he plans to be finished in ten minutes so there is time for questions.
2. What are his three points, and which one does he say he wants to spend longer on?
Coverage numbers, the discount decision, and what he needs from the room before the fifteenth. The third one.
3. What does he offer the room before he moves to his last point?
He asks whether anybody wants him to clarify anything from the first two points.
Listening 2 · A Podcast

It Is Not Confidence. It Is Structure.

A business podcast on running a meeting. Sound first — no text.

1. What does she say is the real reason confident people lose a room?
Because nobody could tell which point they were on. She says it is structure, not confidence.
2. What are her first two habits?
Say how many points there are at the start, and make the transitions full sentences instead of just so or moving on.
3. What is the third habit, and what does she say about it?
Plan to finish early, not on time. She says the two minutes you give back are the only part of the meeting anybody will thank you for.
