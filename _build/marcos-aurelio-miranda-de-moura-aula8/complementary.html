Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TED -- Casey Brown, "Know your worth, and then ask for it" (9 min)

Nine minutes that are also a demonstration. Watch what she does structurally: she says how many things she is going to cover, she moves between them with full sentences, and she closes early. The content is about pricing. The lesson is about running the room.

Tip: on the second viewing, ignore what she is saying and write down only her transitions. There are four, and every one of them is a complete sentence.

Video
Harvard Business Review -- "The Explainer: Don’t Just Sell Stuff, Satisfy Needs"

Two minutes, and it does in two minutes what most people fail to do in twenty: one idea, announced, developed and closed. Worth watching again now that you know what to look for in the structure rather than in the argument.

Tip: watch it once, then close your eyes and rebuild it out loud in three sentences. If you can, the structure worked. If you cannot, it is not your English.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Series
Succession -- any board or family meeting scene

Pick any scene where somebody tries to hold a room and loses it. There are several in every episode, and they all fail the same way: nobody announced how many points there were, so the room took control after the first one.

Tip: pause the moment the room turns against the speaker and say out loud, in English, what sentence would have prevented it.
