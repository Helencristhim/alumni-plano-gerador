Chapter 3: The Code

Where, and How Many

in · on · at · next to · near · between · there is / there are

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The hotel is on this street."

"The metro is next to the hotel."

"There is a bathroom near the park."

"There are two restaurants between the hotel and the station."

PatternUseExample
in + cityA big place around youWe are in Paris.
on + streetA line of buildingsThe hotel is on this street.
at + one pointOne exact placeI am at the station.
next to · near · betweenWhere, from another thingThe metro is next to the hotel.
There is + one thingSay that one thing existsThere is a bathroom here.
There are + two, three...Say that more than one existsThere are two restaurants here.

Count first, then choose: one thing → there is. Two or more → there are.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"Have a metro near here?"

✓ RIGHT

"Is there a metro near here?"

✗ WRONG

"The hotel is in this street."

✓ RIGHT

"The hotel is on this street."

Portuguese says "tem" and "na rua". English says there is and on the street. Two small words, and people understand you the first time.

Chapter 5: The Street

Excuse Me, Sir

one question, one stranger, one answer

Dialogue

On Rue de Rivoli

T
Excuse me, sir. Where is the metro station?
H
It is on this street, next to the big church.
T
Is it near? We have our bags with us.
H
Very near. Two minutes. There is a map here, on the wall.
T
Thank you. And is there a restaurant near here?
H
There are two restaurants on this street. One is between the hotel and the station.
T
Perfect. One more question. Where is the bathroom?
H
There is one in the restaurant, but it is for clients. There is a public one in the park.
T
In the park. Is the park on this street too?
H
No, it is not. It is at the end of the street, on the left.
T
Thank you very much. You are very kind.
Comprehension

What Did Hugo Say?

1. Where is the metro station?
On this street, next to the big church. It is two minutes away.
2. How many restaurants are there on this street?
Two. One is between the hotel and the station.
3. Where is the public bathroom?
In the park, at the end of the street, on the left.
