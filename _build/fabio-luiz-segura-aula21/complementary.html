Talk
Kio Stark — “Why you should talk to strangers” (TED)

A writer explains why short conversations with people we do not know are good for us, and how to start them without feeling strange. It is the theory behind every opening line on a conference floor.

Tip: write down one way to start a conversation that she describes. Try it on the first day in Las Vegas.

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart (Stanford GSB) — “Question Everything: Why Curiosity Is Communication’s Secret Weapon”

An episode about how strong questions build trust and open conversations. Perfect for this lesson: the best opener is a good question.

Tip: write down two questions from the episode. Then check: are they subject questions or object questions?

Video
Vanessa Van Edwards — “You are contagious” (TEDxLondon, YouTube)

A researcher of human behavior shows how the first seconds of an interaction change how people see you, and why a good conversation starter matters more than a perfect introduction.

Tip: listen for the questions she suggests instead of “What do you do?”. Choose one for your homework recording.
