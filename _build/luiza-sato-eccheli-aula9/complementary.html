Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
TOEFL (ETS) — “TOEFL iBT Speaking Question 1: Independent Speaking” (YouTube)

From the official TOEFL channel: how to structure a spoken opinion with a clear position, reasons and examples. The task format has changed since 2026, but the structure of a good opinion has not.

Tip: pause after each tip and answer one opinion question in forty-five seconds, adding a concession with although.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Series
The Good Wife — Season 1, Episode 1, “Pilot”

A lawyer returns to work and has to argue every case, often accepting the other side’s best point before she wins. Excellent for the language of concession in real American English.

Tip: when a lawyer says although, despite or however, pause and repeat the sentence with the same rhythm.

See the episode on TVmaze ↗
Talk
Julia Dhar — “How to disagree productively and find common ground” (TED)

A world debating champion on how to disagree well: find the common ground, separate the idea from the person, and accept a good point. Everything in this lesson, in eleven minutes.

Tip: summarize her three tips, and give one example from a discussion at GV.
