Kathryn Schulz -- "Don't regret regret" (TED)

A short talk about the decisions we wish we had made differently, and what regret can teach us. It is full of the past that did not happen: the exact language of this lesson.

Tip: write down every sentence with if... had... that you hear, and say if the result is in the past or now.

Amy Edmondson -- "How to turn a group of strangers into a team" (TED)

A Harvard professor who studied mistakes in hospitals explains why the best teams talk openly about what went wrong. It is the idea behind every debrief in an American hospital.

Tip: listen for how she talks about mistakes without blame, and compare it with Dr. Parker in the dialogue.

Leana Wen -- "What your doctor won't disclose" (TED)

An emergency physician talks about transparency between doctors and patients, and why honesty builds trust. It connects with accountability and with the patient who asks a difficult question.

Tip: after you watch, say in one minute what you would have done in her place, with I would have...
