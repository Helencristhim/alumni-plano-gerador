Chapter 3: At, In, On

Three Words That Never Change

at + hour · in + part of the day · on + day

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The car is here at seven thirty."

"In the afternoon there is nothing."

"The meeting is on Friday."

"On Friday at ten, in the morning."

WordGoes withExample
atan HOUR on the clockat seven thirty · at nine · at noon
ina PART of the day (and months, years)in the morning · in the afternoon · in the evening
ona DAY of the week, or a dateon Friday · on Saturday morning
The exception to rememberat night, not in the night. That one is just like that.

Portuguese uses almost the same word for all three, so there is nothing to transfer. Three boxes: hour → at, part of the day → in, day → on.

Dialogue

What Time Is the Car Tomorrow?

L
Good evening. What time is the car tomorrow?
E
Good evening, Mr. de Souza. Seven thirty, in the morning. It changed today.
L
Sorry, can you repeat that, please?
E
Of course. Seven thirty, not eight. The traffic is very bad after eight.
L
So, seven thirty, not eight. Is that right? And breakfast?
E
That is right. Breakfast is from seven, so you have half an hour.
L
Perfect. And in the afternoon? Is there anything?
E
Nothing until five thirty. And dinner is at eight, in the evening. Please be early — the restaurant is small.
L
One more thing. The meeting on Friday — morning or afternoon?
E
Morning now. On Friday at ten. It changed this afternoon.
L
So: car at seven thirty, dinner at eight, and Friday at ten in the morning. Thank you. Good night.
Comprehension

What Did Elena Say?

1. What time is the car, and why is it not eight?
Seven thirty, not eight, because the traffic is very bad after eight.
2. What is there in the afternoon?
Nothing until five thirty.
3. When is the Friday meeting now?
On Friday at ten, in the morning. It changed that afternoon.

Her Day, From Five Thirty to Ten

A whole day in forty seconds. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What time is she up, and where is she from six to seven?
She is up at five thirty, and from six to seven she is outside — the only hour that is only hers.
2. When is she at work on Saturday?
In the morning, but never in the afternoon.

Sunday is empty, and that is not laziness — it is the reason the other six days are good.
