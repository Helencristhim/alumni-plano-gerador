Lesson 03 -- Pre-class

Could You Say That Again? -- When You Do Not Understand

Someone on the call speaks fast and you lose the meeting. Key words: to repeat, to spell, to catch, to speak up, a shipment, a delay, a sample, to confirm. Structure: can and could for polite requests.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

To repeat -- to say something again
"Could you repeat the date, please?"
To spell -- to say the letters of a word, one by one
"Could you spell your name, please?"
To catch -- to hear and understand what someone says
"Sorry, I didn’t catch that."
To speak up -- to speak louder
"Can you speak up a little? I can’t hear you."
A shipment -- products that travel together to a customer
"The shipment leaves Shanghai on Monday."
A delay -- when something happens later than planned
"There is a delay of five days."
A sample -- a small amount of a product to test
"The customer wants to test a sample first."
To confirm -- to say that something is true or correct
"Can you confirm the new date by email?"

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

It is Tuesday, and Fernanda is on a call with Wei, from the plant in Shanghai. Wei speaks fast, and the connection is bad. Fernanda doesn’t catch the first part.

“Sorry, Wei. Could you repeat that, please?” Wei says it again: there is a delay with the shipment of samples. “Can you speak up a little?” asks Fernanda. Wei speaks louder.

Then Fernanda asks: “Could you spell the name of the port?” Wei writes it in the chat: NINGBO. At the end, Fernanda says: “Just to confirm: the samples arrive on the twentieth?” “Yes,” says Wei. The call ends well.

1. Why doesn’t Fernanda catch the first part?
A Because Wei speaks fast and the connection is bad.
B Because Wei speaks Chinese.
C Because she is late for the call.
2. What does Wei write in the chat?
A The date.
B The name of the port.
C The price.
3. Which request is correct?
A “Could you to repeat that?”
B “Could you repeat that, please?”
C “Could you repeating that?”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Can and Could for Polite Requests

GRAMMAR

Use Can you...? with colleagues and Could you...? when you want to be more polite. After can and could, the verb is always simple.

JobFormExample
Ask someone to do somethingCan you + verb?Can you speak more slowly?
More polite, for a customer or a managerCould you + verb?Could you repeat that, please?
Ask for permissionCan I + verb?Can I ask a quick question?
Answer yesSure. / Of course. / No problem.Could you spell it? — Sure. N-I-N-G-B-O.
Make it softeradd please and a little / moreCould you speak a little more slowly, please?
The trapno to, no -ing after can and couldNot Could you to repeat. Say Could you repeat.

Never: Could you to repeat? · Can you repeating? · Could you speak more slow? · I didn’t catched that

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer a real speaker would give.

A customer speaks too fast. You say:
A "Speak slow!"
B "Could you speak a little more slowly, please?"
C "Could you to speak slowly?"
You do not understand a name. You say:
A "Could you spell that, please?"
B "Spell, please."
C "Can you spelling that?"
You want to ask a question in a big meeting. You say:
A "I have question now."
B "Can I ask a quick question?"
C "Can I asking a question?"
At the end of the call you check the date. You say:
A "Just to confirm: the shipment arrives on Friday?"
B "Confirm me the date."
C "You confirm Friday?"
