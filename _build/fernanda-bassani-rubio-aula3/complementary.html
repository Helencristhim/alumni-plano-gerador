English at Work -- Talking sense

In this BBC drama, a message is not clear and the team is confused. Anna and Tom use short, polite sentences to ask, check and clear up the confusion.

Tip: listen for every question that starts with could you or can you, and write them down.

40 English Phrases You Need for Online Meetings

A business English teacher shows the phrases people really use on Zoom and Teams: when the connection is bad, when you did not hear, and when you want to check something.

Tip: choose five phrases for the part about not hearing or not understanding. Use one of them on your next call.

Series
The Proposal (2009) -- Trailer

A romantic comedy with Sandra Bullock and Ryan Reynolds: a boss, her assistant and a very strange plan. The dialogue is fast, so it is good training for your ear.

Tip: watch it with English subtitles. When you do not catch a line, pause, say sorry, I didn’t catch that, and play it again.
