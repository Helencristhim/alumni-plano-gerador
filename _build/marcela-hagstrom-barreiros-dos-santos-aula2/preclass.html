Lesson 02 -- Pre-class

New York Stories -- What Did You Do There?

The trip ends in New York: the subway, the free museums and one night on Broadway. Key words: a borough, the subway, a skyline, to sell out, to wait in line, free admission, a Broadway show, stand-up comedy. Structure: irregular verbs in the past, and questions and negatives with did.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A borough -- one of the five big parts of New York City
"Brooklyn is my favorite borough."
The subway -- the train that runs under the streets of the city
"We took the subway to Central Park every day."
A skyline -- the shape of the tall buildings against the sky
"The skyline at night was beautiful."
To sell out -- to sell all the tickets, so there are no more
"The show sold out in two days."
To wait in line -- to stand with other people and wait your turn
"We waited in line for an hour at the museum."
Free admission -- you can go in and you do not pay
"The museum has free admission on Friday evenings."
A Broadway show -- a musical or a play in the theaters of Broadway
"We saw a Broadway show on our last night."
Stand-up comedy -- a show where one person tells jokes to the public
"The stand-up comedy was very fast, but very funny."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Ana went to New York for ten days. She didn’t take taxis: she bought a card and took the subway everywhere. She saw a different borough almost every day.

Some museums have free admission in the evening, so she went to three of them. At one museum she waited in line for forty minutes. From the Brooklyn Bridge she saw the skyline at sunset.

Did she see a Broadway show? Yes, she did. The famous shows sold out, but she got a cheap ticket for the same night.

1. How did Ana travel in the city?
A By taxi.
B By subway.
C By bus.
2. Why did Ana go to three museums for free?
A Because she had a student card.
B Because some museums have free admission in the evening.
C Because her hotel gave her tickets.
3. Which question is correct?
A “Did Ana saw a show?”
B “Did Ana see a show?”
C “Ana did see a show?”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Past Simple -- Irregular Verbs and Did

GRAMMAR

Many common verbs are irregular in the past: go-went, see-saw, take-took. For questions and negatives, use did and didn’t + the simple verb.

JobFormExample
Irregular verbsa new form, no -edgo → went · see → saw · take → took
More irregular verbslearn them as a groupeat → ate · buy → bought · sell → sold
QuestionDid + subject + verb?Did you see a show?
Negativedidn’t + verbWe didn’t take a taxi.
Short answersYes, I did. / No, we didn’t.Did you go to Brooklyn? Yes, we did.
The trapno past form after didNot Did you saw. Say Did you see.

Never: We goed to Brooklyn · Did you saw the show? · We didn’t took a taxi · You went to the museum? (as a question)
