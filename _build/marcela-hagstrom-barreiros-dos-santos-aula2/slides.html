Chapter 4: The Code

Did You Go? We Didn’t.

Irregular verbs and questions with did

Grammar Discovery

Four Sentences About New York

"We went to Brooklyn on Sunday."

"I saw the skyline from the bridge."

"Did you see a Broadway show?"

"We didn’t take a taxi."

Look at the blue verbs. Went and saw do not end in -ed: they are irregular. Now the red words: after did and didn’t, the verb goes back to the simple form: see, take.

JobFormExample
Irregular verbsa new form, no -edgo → went · see → saw · take → took
More irregular verbslearn them as a groupeat → ate · buy → bought · sell → sold
QuestionDid + subject + verb?Did you see a show?
Negativedidn’t + verbWe didn’t take a taxi.
Short answersYes, I did. / No, we didn’t.Did you go to Brooklyn? Yes, we did.
The trapno past form after didNot Did you saw. Say Did you see.

In one line: irregular verbs have their own past form; after did and didn’t the verb is simple.

Giulia on Broadway
