Podcast
6 Minute Vocabulary: How to use irregular verbs

A short BBC program about the verbs that do not take -ed in the past: go, see, take, buy and many more. It explains them with clear examples.

Tip: make a list of every irregular verb you hear, with the simple form and the past form: go - went.
