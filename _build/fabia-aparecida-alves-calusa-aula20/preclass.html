Lesson 20 — Pre-class

Review — The Credit on Every Purchase

Nothing new this week. The same four words — replace, gradual, credit, purchase — and the same shape: saying which tax or which companies with "that". A new client, a new question. Around ten minutes.

Stage 1: The same four words

Vocabulary

Nothing new here. Read each one and say it out loud. If it comes back on its own, that word is yours.

Replace — to take the place of something
"The CBS and the IBS replace five old taxes."
Gradual — happening slowly, step by step
"The change is gradual, until 2033."
Credit — money you can take off the tax you pay
"His company gets a credit on the tax in its costs."
Purchase — something a company buys
"Every purchase of computers has tax in the price."

Stage 3: The one that... — one more time

Grammar

No new rule this week. The same shape as lesson 19, with a new client: you pay the CBS, the tax that replaces PIS and Cofins; companies that buy a lot get a credit; the client who called. That + verb, right after the noun. Never "what", and no extra "it".

"The IBS is the tax ______ replaces ICMS and ISS."
A that
B what
"Daniel is the client ______ is opening an office in Campinas."
A which he
B who
Which sentence is correct?
A Companies that buy a lot get a credit.
B Companies that they buy a lot of computers every year get a credit.
The owner asks what changes for his company. Which answer can he plan with?
A A lot of things change, little by little, and maybe you get some money back on some things.
B From 2027, you pay the CBS, the tax that replaces PIS and Cofins.
