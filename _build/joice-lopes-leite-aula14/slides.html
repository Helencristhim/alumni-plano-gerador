Chapter 3: The Code

Two Pieces, One Now

am / is / are + verb + ing

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am joining from São Paulo."

"We are waiting for the German team."

"I usually work from the office."

"But today I am working from home."

IdeaFormExample
NOW, at this momentam / is / are + verb-ingI am joining now.
routine, every weekpresent simple (Lesson 2)I usually work from the office.
negativeis / are + not + verb-ingMy camera is not working.
questionAre / Is + person + verb-ing?Are we discussing the platform?
Key ideaWords that go with NOW: now, right now, at the moment, today.

In speech: I am becomes I'm, we are becomes we're.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I joining from São Paulo."
"I am joining from São Paulo."
"Sorry, I listen to you now."
"Sorry, I am listening to you now."
"We are waiting the German team."
"We are waiting for the German team."

The present continuous always has TWO pieces. Without am / is / are, it is not a sentence.
