Complementary Activities

Three curated pieces for the week between classes. There is no written homework -- pick one, and bring it up next Wednesday. Mark each one as done after you watch, listen or read it.

Video · British accent · 5 min
What does an agronomist do?

An agronomist walks through an ordinary working day in wheat and cabbage fields, checking plant health and testing the soil. It is the clearest short example of a professional in your own sector explaining what she does and who she does it for.

Tip: note every verb she uses in the present simple to describe her routine, then say three sentences about your own working day using the same pattern.

Video · Australian accent · 4 min
Occupation Spotlight: Agronomist -- Career Stories

Emily, an agronomist in Tasmania, describes the crops she follows from planting to harvest and how she keeps growers informed. She moves between what she does now and how she got there, which is exactly the contrast you worked on this week.

Tip: listen for the moment she switches from present simple to past simple, and write down the sentence where the change happens.

Article · Irish author · 5 min read
How do we reduce pesticide use while empowering farmers?

A researcher explains why crop protection sits between food production, thin farm margins and environmental pressure. It gives you the vocabulary to describe what your own company sells and why growers buy it.

Tip: collect ten words you could use to explain your company in one minute, and check which ones you already knew.
