Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Documentary
Downfall: The Case Against Boeing (2022)

An hour and a half of exactly the thing this lesson is about: internal documents, procedures and public statements in which almost nobody appears. Listen to how the company describes what happened and how the families describe the same events. Same facts, and one of the two versions has people in it.

Tip: English audio, English subtitles. Write down three sentences from the company statements and say them again out loud with a name in front.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English — “Should we eat ultra-processed food?”

Six minutes, two British speakers, normal speed, on something that is made by a process rather than by a person — which is why half the episode is in the structure you worked on today. Free transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen once with no text, then open the transcript and underline every passive. There will be more than you expect.

Listen on BBC Learning English →
Talk
Margaret Heffernan — “The dangers of willful blindness” (TED)

Fourteen minutes on what happens in organizations where everybody knew and nobody said. She is British, she speaks at normal speed, and the second half is the argument behind the rule you took from this lesson: a line with no name is a line nobody is doing.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles, then say in three sentences what you would have done in the situation she describes.
