Lesson 11 -- Getting Used to the Quiet

Video
Kirsten Dirksen -- "Left BBC's Planet Earth to start dream family homestead (full tour)"

A wildlife cameraman who filmed Planet Earth left that life to build a self-sufficient homestead with his wife on eighty acres of old quarry land in Wales. Forty minutes of exactly what this lesson is about: the trade-offs, the first hard winters, and the slow way a difficult place becomes home.

Tip: write down every "used to", "we are used to" and "we got used to" you hear. He switches between all three in the same interview, so it is the best listening test for tonight's structure.

Listening
NPR Life Kit -- "How solitude can help you regulate your mood"

Researchers who study time alone explain why solitude can restore you instead of making you lonely, and how much of it is too much. The full transcript is free on the same page, so you can listen first and read afterwards.

Tip: listen once with the transcript hidden and write the gist in one sentence. Then read along and see how much you already had.

Talk
TED -- Susan Cain, "The power of introverts"

Nineteen minutes on why quiet, low-stimulation places are where some people do their best thinking, and why the modern world keeps telling them they are wrong. It starts with a summer camp story that will sound very familiar after tonight.

Tip: open the transcript on the same page and read her opening story out loud after her. Copy the pauses, not only the words.
