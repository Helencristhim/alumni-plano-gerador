Chapter 3: Is It...?

Is It...? It Is Not

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Is this gate 12?"

"Yes, it is."

"Is the flight on time?"

"No, it is not. It is delayed."

Where is the orange word in a question? And where is not?

To do thisSayExample
Ask a questionIs / Are firstIs the flight on time?
Say noam / is / are + notIt is not on time.
Short answerYes, it is. / No, it is not.Are you Bruna? Yes, I am.

Question: the word goes first. No: add not after it.
