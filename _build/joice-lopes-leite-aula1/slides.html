Chapter 3: Who You Are

Three Little Words

am · is · are

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"My name is Joice."

"I am from Brazil."

"I am 52 years old."

"They are from Canada."

WhoWordExample
IamI am from Brazil.
YouareYou are in São Paulo.
He / She / ItisShe is from Canada. My name is Joice.
WeareWe are in the meeting.
TheyareThey are from Canada.

With I, always am. Age uses am too: I am 52 years old.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I have 52 years."
"I am 52 years old."
"My name Joice."
"My name is Joice."
"She are from Canada."
"She is from Canada."

Portuguese says tenho 52 anos. English says I am. Age is something you are, not something you have.

The Three Keys

Three Phrases That Save a Meeting

"Sorry, I speak a little English."

Key 1 — say it first, and everyone slows down for you

"Can you repeat, please?"

Key 2 — when they speak too fast, in any meeting

"Can you help me, please?"

Key 3 — when nothing else works, this one always does
