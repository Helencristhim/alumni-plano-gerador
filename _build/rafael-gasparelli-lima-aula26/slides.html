Chapter 3: By or Until?

Four Small Time Words

A deadline, a long action, and the order of events

Grammar Discovery

Deadline or Duration?

Please send the report by Friday.

The team is going to work until six today.

Check the containers before sending the fruit.

We’ll plant the trees after the rain.

Which one is a deadline? Which one is an action that continues?

WordMeaningExample
by + timea deadline: at that time or beforeSend it by Friday.
until + timean action continues, then stops at that timeWe work until six.
before + time / -ingearlier thanbefore sending the fruit
after + time / -inglater thanafter the rain · after checking

Deadline = by. Continuing action = until. After before and after, a verb takes -ing.

Grammar Practice

Chapter 4: The Cold Chain Project

A Status Call

One call, two recordings, one deadline

Dialogue

Are We On Track?

C
Hi Rafael. Quick question about the cold containers. Are we on track?
R
Mostly, yes. The first ten containers will be ready by March 15.
C
Great. And the temperature tests? My boss wants the results before signing the next order.
R
That task is a little behind schedule. The test company is closed until Monday.
C
I see. So when can you submit the results?
R
By next Friday. Can you give us a one-week extension?
C
Yes, that’s fine. We can postpone our meeting until after the tests.
R
Thank you. My team has a heavy workload this month, but the rest is going well.
C
Good. Please send me a progress report every Monday until the end of the project.
R
Of course. You’ll get the first one before ten a.m. on Monday.
Comprehension

About Claire

1. What does Claire’s boss want before signing the next order?
The results of the temperature tests.
2. What can Claire postpone?
Their meeting, until after the tests.
3. What does Claire ask for every Monday?
A progress report, until the end of the project.
Listening 1 · Why Projects Finish Late

A Date Is Not a Wish

A project manager gives a short talk. Sound first — no text.

1. If you say “by Friday”, when must the task be finished?
On Friday or before.
2. How often should you check the progress?
Every week.
3. When should you ask for an extension?
Early, before the deadline, not after it.
