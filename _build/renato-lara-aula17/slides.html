Grammar Discovery

What happens to the verb?

I love surfing beach breaks.
Nobody enjoys getting up at half past five.
We don't mind helping new people.
Kai can't stand waiting for people.

What do the orange words have in common?

love / like / enjoy / don't mind / can't stand / hate + verb-ing

After these verbs, the next verb ends in -ing. Enjoy, don't mind and can't stand never take to: "I enjoy surfing", never "I enjoy to surf". And surf is the wave; surfing is what you do: "I love surfing", not "I love surf".

The Code

From love to can't stand

The scale

I love surfing.
I really enjoy training.
I like watching the WSL.
I don't mind waiting.
I don't like losing.
I can't stand getting up early.

Never

I enjoy to surf.
after "enjoy" the verb ends in -ing
I love surf beach breaks.

The question: Do you like ...ing? · What do you enjoy ...ing?

The Code

Ask it back

In a new group, the answer is only half. The other half is the question you send back.

They ask

"Do you like surfing big waves?"
"What do you enjoy doing at the weekend?"
"Do you mind training at six?"

You answer, then ask

"Yes, I love it. What about you?"
"I enjoy watching the WSL. Do you?"
"I don't mind. Do you like it?"
I love surfing beach breaks. What about you?

Answer plus a question: that is how a conversation keeps going.

Sound Check

Three blocks, one sound

I can't stand getting up early.

the t in "can't" almost disappears: can-stand

I don't mind waiting my turn.

"don't mind" is one block: dohn-mind

I really enjoy surfing with a group.

"-ing" stops on the ng: no extra sound after it

Listening 1

The captain's welcome

1. When does the club train?
On Tuesday and Thursday after school, and on Saturday at six in the morning if the swell is good.
2. What does nobody in the club like doing?
Waiting for somebody who is late. Be on the sand by a quarter past four.
3. What do they usually do after training, and why should Renato go?
They hang out at the kiosk and get chips. That is where you actually meet everybody.
Listening 2

The cheat sheet from Millie

1. Why is Jess the right person to stand next to if you are shy?
She loves talking, so he won't have to say anything.
2. What does Harper enjoy doing before she talks to new people?
Watching them. She is quiet at first, but she is the best surfer there.
3. What is Millie's trick to break the ice?
Complain about the cold water. Everybody hates it in the morning.
