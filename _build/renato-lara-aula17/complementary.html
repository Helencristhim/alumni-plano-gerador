Surfing Australia — "Molly's Way"

A short film about Molly Picklum, the 2025 World Champion, who started as a grom surfing with the local kids on the Central Coast. Her friends, her coaches, her home breaks — this is what a surf community in Australia actually looks like.

Tip: every time somebody talks about what Molly loves or can't stand, pause and say the sentence again with -ing.

Podcast
BBC Real Easy English — "Talking about hobbies"

Short, slow and made for your level. Two people talk about what they love doing in their free time and what they don't enjoy. You will hear love, enjoy and like + -ing again and again.

Tip: count how many times you hear a verb with -ing after love, like or enjoy. Then say one about you.

Woodward English — "Like + Verb-ING, Enjoy + Verb-ING"

A clear grammar video about today's rule: why "I enjoy surfing" is right and "I enjoy to surf" is not, with lots of examples and a short quiz.

Tip: pause before each answer in the quiz and say it out loud first.
