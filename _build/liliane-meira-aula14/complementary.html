Lesson 14 -- Two Reviews of the Same Show

Podcast
The Week in Art (The Art Newspaper) -- "Renoir and Love in London, political storm around Kanal in Brussels, Ronald Moody"

An episode that opens with an exhibition everybody disagrees about: Renoir still divides critical opinion, and the curators of the new show at the National Gallery say so openly. It is the real version of the two reviews you read in class.

Tip: write down every adjective you hear about Renoir, with the small word in front of it, and decide how strong each verdict is.

Video
The Art Assignment (PBS) -- "The Case for Mark Rothko"

Sarah Urist Green argues for an artist many people call overrated, answering the usual criticisms one by one. A model of how to give a verdict with nuance: what is weak, what is strong, and why it matters.

Tip: pause each time she grades an opinion (very, rather, absolutely) and say whether you agree, using a different intensifier.

British Council -- "Intensifiers will absolutely totally improve your English"

A short lesson on the words that make an adjective stronger, and which ones go with which kind of adjective. Exactly the grammar of today's reviews, in a few minutes.
