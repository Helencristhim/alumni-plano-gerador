"Present Simple and Present Continuous" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the contrast you practiced in class: the simple form for your permanent life, the -ing form for what is temporary. Connection to Lesson 5: this is the grammar behind "I live in Sao Paulo" and "I am traveling through Croatia".

Tip: after every example, say the same idea twice about yourself -- once about your life, once about your trip in August. If the verb is know, want or like, remember it never takes -ing.

"Voice Note 008: Split, Croatia" -- Travel Voice Notes

A short voice note recorded in Split, the city you fly into. One person, one microphone, describing what is happening around her while it happens. Connection to Lesson 5: almost every sentence is the -ing form, used exactly as you will use it on a terrace.

Tip: it is short, so listen twice. The second time, write down four sentences she says in the -ing form. Then say each one out loud about the room you are in right now.

Twelve minutes on the exact thing this lesson asks of you: speaking first to a person you have never met. She explains why it feels uncomfortable, and gives concrete openers that work anywhere. Connection to Lesson 5: this is the reason behind every phrase in today's toolkit.
