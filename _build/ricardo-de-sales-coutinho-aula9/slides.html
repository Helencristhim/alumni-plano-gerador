Chapter 3: Can and Can't

One Word, Two Jobs

what you are able to do · how you ask

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I can speak a little English."

"I can't hear you well."

"Can you repeat that, please?"

"Can you speak more slowly?"

JobFormExample
What you are able to doI can + verbI can speak a little English.
What you are not able to doI can't + verbI can't hear you well.
Asking politelyCan you + verb + please?Can you repeat that, please?

No "to" after can. And the verb never changes: he can speak, never "he cans".

The Five Phrases

Five Ways to Not Go Silent

"Sorry, can you repeat that?"

"Can you speak more slowly, please?"

"Sorry, I don't understand."

"What does that word mean?"

Dialogue

The Line Is Bad

D
Ricardo, the report is due on Wednesday, not Thursday.
R
Sorry, I can't hear you well. Can you repeat that?
D
Of course. The report is due on Wednesday.
R
Can you speak more slowly, please?
D
Sure. The re-port is due on Wed-nes-day.
R
Wednesday. Sorry, what does "due" mean?
D
It means the day you send it. Wednesday is the last day.
R
Now I understand. Wednesday. Thank you.
D
No problem. Your English is better every week.
R
Can you send me an email with the date, please?
Comprehension

About the Call

1. What day is the report due?
On Wednesday.
2. What does "due" mean?
The day you send it — the last day.
3. What does Diane say about his English?
That it is better every week.
