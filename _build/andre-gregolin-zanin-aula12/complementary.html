Series
The Last Dance — Episode 1

A documentary series about one basketball season that keeps jumping back and forth in time. Every interview is somebody putting events in order: by the time he arrived, as soon as the season started, since nobody believed in them. It is today’s grammar, spoken by people who lived the story.

Tip: watch the first twenty minutes with English subtitles and write down five sentences that start with when, once, by the time, since or although.

Talk
Tim Urban — “Inside the mind of a master procrastinator” (TED)

A very funny talk about what happens to a day that has no deadline and no sequence. It is the problem from today’s text, explained by somebody who suffered from it for years.

Tip: listen for until, as soon as and by the time. Each time you hear one, pause and say the sentence again about your own week.

Talk
Laura Vanderkam — “How to gain control of your free time” (TED)

A time-management researcher on planning a week backwards, from what matters to what fills the gaps. It is the same idea as the second listening from class, with real examples from people with very full lives.

Tip: after watching, say out loud what you will have done by the end of next week, using by the time and so that at least once each.
