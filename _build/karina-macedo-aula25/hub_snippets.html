Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. São palavras de redes sociais e networking online. Toque Listen para ouvir. (Listen = Ouvir)

Profile -- perfil (your page on a social network)
"Click my profile to see my aircraft." (Clique no meu perfil para ver as minhas aeronaves.)
Hashtag -- hashtag (a word with # to find posts)
"I always use the hashtag aviation." (Eu sempre uso a hashtag aviation.)
Tag -- marcar alguém num post (with @)
"I tag Mike in my photo from Miami." (Eu marco o Mike na minha foto de Miami.)
Link -- link / endereço para clicar
"The link to my website is on my profile." (O link do meu site está no meu perfil.)
Invite -- convidar (ask a person to come)
"I want to invite you to our next event." (Eu quero te convidar para o nosso próximo evento.)
Online -- online / na internet
"We meet online every Tuesday." (A gente se encontra online toda terça.)
Audience -- público (the people who see your posts)
"My audience is pilots and aircraft buyers." (O meu público são pilotos e compradores de aeronaves.)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Leia o texto da Karina e responda às perguntas. (Read Karina's text and answer.)

I usually post on Instagram three times a week, and I always use three hashtags. After the event in Miami, I posted a photo and I tagged Mike. My caption says: "Click the link on my profile. Don't miss my next video!" Then Mike called me: "A pilot from Dubai wants your link, and he can't find it." I said: "I'm sorry about that. I'll send you the link now." Mike wants to invite me to his online event next month. I asked him: "Have you been to the aviation fair in Sao Paulo?" He said: "No, I haven't." My audience is online, but the big deals happen offline.

1. How often does Karina post on Instagram? (Com que frequência a Karina posta?)
A Every day, in the morning and in the evening, with a new video. (não está no texto)
B Three times a week. (três vezes por semana)
C Never on Instagram, only on her website. (não está no texto)
2. What is the problem with the pilot from Dubai? (Qual é o problema do piloto de Dubai?)
A He can't find Karina's link. (ele não acha o link da Karina)
B He wants a refund for his aircraft and a new contract. (não está no texto)
C He doesn't like the photo. (não está no texto)
3. Has Mike been to the aviation fair in Sao Paulo? (O Mike já foi à feira de aviação em São Paulo?)
A Yes, he has. He goes to the fair every year with Karina. (ele diz No, I haven't)
B Yes, he goes every month. (não está no texto)
C No, he hasn't. (não, ainda não foi)

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Review: Four Tools (Lessons 21-24)

Grammar

Revisão: as quatro estruturas das aulas 21 a 24, juntas. / Review: four structures together.

Form / FormaUse / UsoExample / Exemplo
always / usually / sometimes / never + verbocom que frequência você faz algo (aula 21)I never post on Sunday. (Eu nunca posto no domingo.)
Verbo! / Don't + verbo!call to action: diga ao público o que fazer (aula 22)Click the link! Don't miss my video! (Clique no link! Não perca o meu vídeo!)
Have you met + pessoa? / Have you been to + lugar?perguntar sobre experiência num evento (aula 23)Have you met Sofia? -- No, I haven't. (Você já conhece a Sofia? -- Não.)
I'm sorry about + problema. Let me... / I'll + verbopedir desculpas e oferecer uma solução (aula 24)I'm sorry about the link. I'll send it now. (Desculpe pelo link. Vou mandar agora.)

Dica: always, usually e never vêm ANTES do verbo (I always post), nunca depois. Depois de Don't, o verbo vem sem to (Don't miss). / Tip: always goes before the verb.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Escolha a melhor resposta para cada situação.

A new follower asks: "How often do you post?" You post on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. (Você posta três vezes por semana.)
A "I post usually three times in the week, on Monday, Wednesday and Friday."
B "I usually post three times a week."
C "I am post usually."
You want your audience to click the link in your caption. (Você quer que o público clique no link.)
A "Click the link on my profile!"
B "You can to click the link on my profile, please, if you want."
C "Don't to click the link!"
A buyer at the fair says: "Your brochure is only in Portuguese." (O catálogo está só em português.)
A "I sorry. I send the English brochure for you in the next week, maybe."
B "Yes, it is in Portuguese."
C "I'm sorry about that. I'll send you the English version today."
