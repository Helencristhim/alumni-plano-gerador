Chapter 3: Review the Code

Four Tools from Lessons 21-24

how often · call to action · networking · problems

Review the Code

Four Tools, One Story

Lesson 21 · How often
"I usually post three times a week."

Lesson 22 · Call to action
"Click the link! Don't miss my next video."

Lesson 23 · Networking
"Have you been to Miami? -- Yes, I have."

Lesson 24 · Problems
"I'm sorry about the link. I'll send it now."

FormUseExample
always / usually / sometimes / never + verbsay how often you do somethingI never post on Sunday.
Verb! / Don't + verb!tell your audience what to do (call to action)Follow my page! Don't miss the fair!
Have you met + person? / Have you been to + place?ask about experience at an eventHave you met Sofia? -- No, I haven't.
I'm sorry about + problem. Let me... / I'll + verbsay sorry and give a solutionI'm sorry about the delay. Let me check.

always, usually, sometimes, never go BEFORE the verb: I always post. Short answers: Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
