The City on the Shelf

The City on the Shelf

My name is Max and I build cities. My city is on a big shelf in my bedroom, next to the window. It is not finished, and I think it will never be finished. That is the best part.

In the middle of the city there is a tall grey tower with a red light on top. In front of the tower there is a small park with four trees. Behind the tower there are eleven houses, and every house has a different roof. My favorite one is the blue house, because the roof is round.

There is a river under the shelf -- well, it is not a real river, it is a long piece of blue paper. Over the river there is a bridge. The bridge is red and it has two towers, one on each side. Under the bridge there are two boats and a fire truck. I know a fire truck cannot go under a bridge in the water. It is my city, so it can.

There is one problem. There are twelve wheels in the box and I need sixteen. So four cars in my city have no wheels. They are in the street in front of the park, and they never move. My sister says they are broken. I say they are parked.

Did You Catch It?

True or false?

1. The city is on the floor.
False -- it is on a shelf, next to the window.
2. There are eleven houses behind the tower.
True.
3. The river is real water.
False -- it is a long piece of blue paper.
4. Max has enough wheels for every car.
False -- there are twelve wheels and he needs sixteen.
