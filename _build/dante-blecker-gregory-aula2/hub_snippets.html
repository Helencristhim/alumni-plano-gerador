Stage 1.1: City Words

Vocabulary

Tap Listen. Say the word out loud. Then find the real thing in your room!

Brick -- a small hard block used to build something
"I need one more red brick."
Wheel -- the round part of a car that turns and makes it move
"This truck has six wheels."
Roof -- the top part of a house that covers it
"The roof is not finished."
Tower -- a very tall narrow building
"There is a tall tower in the middle."
Bridge -- something you build over a river so you can cross it
"The red bridge goes over the river."
Street -- the road in a town where cars drive and people walk
"There are three streets in my city."
Shelf -- a flat board on a wall where you keep books or toys
"My city is on the shelf."
Piece -- one part of something that has many parts
"One piece is missing!"

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read about Max's city. Then answer the questions.

Max builds cities. His city is on a big shelf in his bedroom. In the middle there is a grey tower. In front of the tower there is a small park. Behind the tower there are eleven houses, and every house has a different roof. There is a red bridge over the river, and under the bridge there are two boats. Max has a problem: there are only twelve wheels in the box, and he needs sixteen. So four cars in the street have no wheels.
What is in the middle of the city?
A There is a grey tower.
B There are eleven houses.
Where are the two boats?
A They are on the bridge.
B They are under the bridge.
Why do four cars have no wheels?
A Because there are only twelve wheels in the box.
B Because the cars are broken.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

there is / there are

Use there is and there are to say what exists in a place. Count first, then choose.

1 thing   There is a tower in the middle.
2+ things   There are three streets.
-   There isn't a park. / There aren't any cars.
?   Is there a bridge?  --  Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.

Where is it?

in on under next to behind in front of

Careful: it is in front of the house, never in front the house.

Stage 4: What Do You Say?

Quiz
Your friend asks how many towers your city has. You built two. What do you say?
A "There is two towers."
B "There are two towers."
Your mom cannot find the Lego box. It is on the shelf. What do you say?
A "It's on the shelf, next to the books."
B "It's in front the books on the shelf."
You want to know if the set has a bridge. What do you ask?
A "There is a bridge?"
B "Is there a bridge?"

Stage 5: Your Room

Reflection
Look around your bedroom. Describe it: what is there, and where is it? Record your answer -- try to say three sentences with there is and there are.
Example: "In my bedroom there is a big bed next to the window. There are two shelves behind the door. On the shelf there are eleven Lego cars and one dragon. There isn't a TV."
