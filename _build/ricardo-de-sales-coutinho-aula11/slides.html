Chapter 3: Are You...?

The Word That Moves

same three words · new job

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Are you busy this morning?"

"Is it hot in Miami today?"

"I am not busy now."

"It is not cold in Florida."

JobFormExample
SayingYou are busy.You are the new manager.
AskingAre you busy?Are you the new manager?
Asking (it / he / she)Is it hot?Is the coffee free?
Saying noI am not · it is notI am not busy. It is not cold.

To ask, the word moves to the front. To say no, not comes after it. And there is no "do" here: never "Do you are busy?"

Short Answers

Three Words, Always

"Yes, I am."

"No, I am not."

"Yes, it is."

"No, it is not."

A single "yes" sounds cold in English. Three words sound like a person.

The Second Half

Answer, Then Ask Back

"Are you busy?"

"No, I am not. Are you?"

"Is it hot in Brazil?"

"No, it is not. It is winter. Is it hot here?"

"Are you tired?"

"Yes, I am. The flight was long. And you?"

"Is this your first day?"

"Yes, it is. Are you here for a long time?"

Two words — "And you?" — and the conversation keeps going.

Chapter 6: Your Turn

For Real

Guided > Semi-free > Free

Role-Play — Guided

Amy Asks, You Answer

Scenario: Amy is at the coffee machine and asks you four questions. Answer every one with three words first, then add one more sentence.

Keywords:

Yes, I am. No, I am not. Yes, it is. No, it is not.
Role-Play — Semi-free

You Start the Conversation

Scenario: A person you do not know is in the break room. Nobody speaks first. Find out two things: the name, and if the person is busy.

Keywords:

Good morning Are you...? busy Nice to meet you
Free Practice

Thirty Seconds

Thirty seconds, no stopping: a real coffee break. Answer every question in three words, and ask one back every time.

No keywords. The conversation does not die while you keep asking.

Self-Assessment

What I Can Do

I can ask "Are you...?" and "Is it...?"
I can answer in three words: yes, I am / no, it is not.
I can say no with "not" in the right place.
I can ask the question back, so the conversation continues.
Thirty seconds at the coffee machine is not small. It is how a team starts.
