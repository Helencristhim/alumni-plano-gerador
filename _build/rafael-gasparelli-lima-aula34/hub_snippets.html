Crisis Communication -- The First Hours

How to speak in the first hours of a crisis: say what you know, what you do not know yet, and what is not possible. Key words: crisis, statement, spokesperson, to recall, reputation, rumor, transparent, to apologize. Structure: modals of deduction with it must be, it might be, it could be and it can't be.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example.

Crisis -- a sudden, serious problem that needs action now
"The damaged shipment became a crisis in one day."
Statement -- official words that a company writes or says in public
"We will publish a short statement this afternoon."
Spokesperson -- the one person who speaks for a company to the press
"Rafael is the spokesperson for this case."
To recall -- to ask buyers to return a product because it may be unsafe
"The company recalled all the boxes from that shipment."
Reputation -- what people think and say about a person or a company
"Our reputation took thirty years to build."
Rumor -- a story that people repeat, but nobody knows if it is true
"The rumor spread on social media in two hours."
Transparent -- open and honest; you do not hide information
"We want to be transparent with our clients."
To apologize -- to say that you are sorry for a problem
"If it is our mistake, we will apologize."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

A container of fruit arrives in Rotterdam, and many boxes are damaged. Rafael does not know the cause yet, so he speaks carefully. The temperature log shows twelve degrees, and he says: “It must be the container.” He is almost sure.

But he does not know which part of the container failed. He says: “It might be the cooling unit, or it could be the port.” Here he is not sure. These are only possibilities.

His farms passed every quality test last month, so he says: “It can’t be the fruit.” He is sure that this is not true. After must, might, could and can’t, the verb is always be, with no to.

Rafael says "It must be the container." How sure is he?
A He has no idea, and he is only guessing because the journalist wants an answer now.
B He is sure it is not true.
C He is almost sure.
Which sentence is correct?
A "It might to be the cooling unit, but our technician in Rotterdam is still checking it."
B "It might be the cooling unit."
C "It might is the cooling unit."
The farms passed every test. What does Rafael say about the fruit?
A "It can't be the fruit."
B "It mustn't be the fruit."
C "It might be the fruit, because all the boxes in the container were damaged during the trip."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- How Sure Are You?

GRAMMAR

must = almost sure · might / could = maybe · can't = sure it is not.

How sure?FormExample
Almost sure it is truemust + beIt must be the container.
Maybemight + beIt might be the cooling unit.
Maybecould + beIt could be the port.
Sure it is not truecan’t + beIt can’t be the fruit.
Different meaningmust + verb = obligationWe must inform the client.

Never: It must to be · It might is · It mustn't be (for sure it is not) · It can be the port (for a maybe now)

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer that a real speaker would give.

A journalist calls. You do not know the cause yet. You say:
A "It is not our fault, and our fruit is perfect, so please do not write anything about our company."
B "No comment."
C "We do not know the cause yet. It might be the container."
The log shows the cooling unit stopped for six hours. You say:
A "It must be the cooling unit."
B "It must to be the cooling unit, because the log shows that it stopped for six hours."
C "It can't be the cooling unit."
Three directors want to talk to the press. You say:
A "Good idea. Everybody can speak."
B "Let us choose one spokesperson, so the message stays the same."
C "Let us wait one week, and then we can all talk to the journalists together at the same time."
The problem is your mistake. You say:
A "It is a rumor."
B "It might be a rumor from our competitors, so we will wait and see what happens next week."
C "We apologize, and we are recalling the shipment."

Stage 5: Free Production

Reflection
