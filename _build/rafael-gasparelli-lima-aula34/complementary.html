Lesson 34 -- Crisis Communication: The First Hours

Series
Succession

A drama about a family that controls a giant media company. In many episodes, the company has a public crisis, and the directors argue about the statement, the spokesperson and the reputation of the business.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. When the characters discuss a crisis, pause and say what the cause must be or might be.

TED Talk
Jon Ronson: When online shaming goes too far

A journalist tells the story of a woman who lost her job and her reputation in one day because of one message on social media. A strong example of how fast a story spreads online.

Tip: after the talk, say what she could do differently in the first hours, with might and could.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Kathryn Schulz: On being wrong

A writer explains why people feel so sure that they are right, even when they are wrong. Useful for this lesson, because in a crisis it is dangerous to say "it can't be our fault" too early.

Tip: write down three things you were sure about at work and later discovered were wrong. Say each one with It must be... and It can't be...
