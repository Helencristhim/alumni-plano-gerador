Chapter 4: The Deduction Code

Must, Might, Can’t

How sure are you?

Grammar Discovery

How Sure Are You?

"The log shows twelve degrees. It must be the container."

"It might be the cooling unit, or it could be the port."

"Our farms passed every test. It can’t be the fruit."

"Daniel is not answering. He might be in a meeting."

Look at the orange words. In which sentence is the speaker almost sure? Not sure? Sure it is not true?

How sure?FormExample
Almost sure it is truemust + beIt must be the container.
Maybemight / could + beIt might be the port.
Sure it is not truecan’t + beIt can’t be the fruit.
Obligation (lesson 17)must + verbWe must inform the client.
Nevermust to be · might isIt might be (not: it might is)

In one line: must = almost sure, might / could = maybe, can’t = sure it is not.

Chapter 5: The Call

Container Forty-Two

One call, two recordings, one statement

Dialogue

The News From Rotterdam

C
Rafael, have you seen the news? A website says that your fruit in Rotterdam is bad.
R
Yes, I saw it an hour ago. It is a rumor for now. Our farms passed every test last month, so it can't be the fruit itself.
C
But the photos show damaged boxes. What happened?
R
The temperature log shows twelve degrees, so it must be the container. It might be the cooling unit, but we are still checking.
C
My client is nervous. This is a crisis for their reputation too.
R
I understand. We will publish a short statement today, and we will be transparent: what we know, and what we do not know yet.
C
Good. Who will speak to the press?
R
I will be the spokesperson. And if it is our mistake, we will apologize and recall the shipment.
Comprehension

About Claire

1. What did Claire see on a website?
A story that says Rafael’s fruit in Rotterdam is bad.
2. What do the photos show, according to Claire?
Damaged boxes.
3. Why is Claire worried about her client?
Her client is nervous, and the crisis is a problem for their reputation too.

The Statement

PUBLIC STATEMENT — DRAFT
Grupo Picunha · Spokesperson: R. Gasparelli Lima, Director
What happeneddamaged fruit in one container (No. 42), Rotterdam
What we knowthe cooling unit stopped for six hours in transport
What we do not know yetthe exact cause of the failure
Our farmsall quality tests passed last month

Next updatetomorrow, 10 a.m. (Rotterdam time)
Simulated example for class. Not an official document.

The cooling unit stopped for six hours in transport.
2. When is the next update?
Tomorrow, at 10 a.m., Rotterdam time.
3. Is the problem with the farms? Answer with a modal.
It can’t be the farms. All quality tests passed last month.
