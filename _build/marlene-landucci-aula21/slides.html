Chapter 3 -- Like + -ing

Language Discovery

I like playing / I love watching / I enjoy walking

Discovery

Listen and Notice

What happens to the second verb in each sentence?

"I like playing tennis."

"I love watching movies."

"I enjoy walking in the park."

"Do you like traveling?"

The Rule

Like + -ing

UsePatternExample
Say what you likelike / love / enjoy + verb-ingI love watching movies.
Say what you do not likedo not like + verb-ingI do not like cooking.
Ask another personDo you like + verb-ing?Do you like traveling?
Say a habit (review)Present simple + usually / alwaysI usually walk in the morning.

Key idea: after like, love and enjoy, the next verb always takes -ing: play becomes playing, watch becomes watching, walk becomes walking. The last line is a review from Lesson 2: a habit stays in the present simple.

Key Phrase

The Small Talk Phrase

"I love traveling. Do you like traveling too?"

Answer, then give the question back. That is how a chat continues.

Chapter 4 -- At the Hotel

Breakfast with a Stranger

A conversation, a board, and two messages

Dialogue

At the Breakfast Table

P

"Good morning. Is this seat free? Can I join you?"

M

"Good morning. Yes, please. It is a beautiful morning."

P

"It is. The weather is sunny today. Are you here on vacation?"

M

"Yes, I am. I love traveling, and I enjoy walking in a new city every morning."

P

"Nice. I like walking too, but my favorite thing is watching movies at night."

M

"Me too. And on the weekend I play tennis. Do you like playing tennis?"

Comprehension

About Paul

1. What does Paul ask before he sits down?
If the seat is free -- he asks to join her at the table.
2. What does Paul say about the weather?
That it is sunny today.
3. What is Paul's favorite thing to do?
Watching movies at night. He also likes walking.
