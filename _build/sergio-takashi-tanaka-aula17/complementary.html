Talk
"How do we heal medicine?" -- Atul Gawande (TED2012)

A free talk of about twenty minutes by the surgeon who put the checklist into the operating room. His argument is that results come from systems and named roles, not from individual heroes: fewer cowboys, more pit crews. Connection to Lesson 17: it is your control map seen from a hospital -- every step with an owner, a deputy and a check.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time he describes a role, stop the video and say one sentence about it with who, which or that.

Podcast
"Be A Leader At Work And Take Control Of Your Career Development" -- Life Kit, NPR

A free episode of about twenty minutes in clear American English, with the full text on the same page. Connection to Lesson 17: it is about the line you drew today between doing the work and answering for it -- what you hand to your team, what stays with you, and how you say both out loud.

Tip: listen once without reading. Then write down three tasks you delegate at work and say each one twice: "I delegate the task to the person who...", and "I am the one who is accountable for..."

Video
"Defining relative clauses" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

A short free class on exactly the code you learned today: who for people, which for things, that for both, and the moment the pronoun can disappear. Connection to Lesson 17: it drills the two errors that cost you the sentence in a real meeting -- the wrong pronoun, and the second subject that should not be there.

Tip: pause after each example and say the same idea about a control on your own desk. If your sentence still works with the pronoun dropped, you have understood the pattern.
