Lesson 16 -- The Numbers Room

Talk
"Making data mean more through storytelling" -- Ben Wellington, TEDx

A data scientist on why a number only lands when it answers a question the audience already has. Connection to Lesson 16: the sentence for the year is exactly that, a number attached to a question the board already asks.

Tip: each time he gives a number, pause and say in one sentence what question it answers. Then do the same with one number from your own career.

Real Interview
"GM CFO: Tariffs have been a headwind" -- CNBC Television

A chief financial officer explaining a quarter on live television, to an audience that is not made of accountants. Connection to Lesson 16: listen for how he separates what will repeat from what will not.

Tip: write down every headwind he names and whether he says it will stay. Then say his first answer again in one sentence of your own.

Grammar Video
"Passive reporting structures" -- BBC Learning English, English Class

A short class on it is said that, it is believed that and the decline is thought to be. Connection to Lesson 16: the impersonal passive is how a CFO reports the market without pretending to own the view.

Tip: after the video, say three sentences about your market with it is expected that, and one about your own work with we had ... reviewed.
