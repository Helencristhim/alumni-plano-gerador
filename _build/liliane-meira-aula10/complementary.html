Lesson 10 -- The Limits of a Project

Podcast
The Nocturnists -- "Uncertainty in Medicine: The Art of Not Knowing"

Clinicians tell true stories about the moments when they did not know, and what they did next. It is the humanities side of medicine speaking in its own voice, full of must have and might have.

Tip: every time a speaker makes a guess about the past, pause and say how sure they are: almost certain, possible, or impossible.

Talk
Kathryn Schulz -- "On being wrong" (TED, 18 min)

A writer who studies error argues that the feeling of being right tells us nothing about whether we are. A good companion to today’s reading: the report you trust is the one that admits its limits.

Tip: note three sentences where she says how sure somebody was, and rewrite one of them with a modal of deduction.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Dr Kriukow -- "How to write research limitations section (and what NEVER to write there)"

A qualitative researcher explains how to write the limitations of a study that works with interviews and personal accounts, which is exactly the kind of evidence your project uses.

Tip: pause after each piece of advice and apply it to one sentence of your own limitations paragraph.
