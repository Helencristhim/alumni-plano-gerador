Lesson 7 -- Yes, But

Podcast
"The Basic Income Experiment" — Planet Money, NPR

A real policy tested on real people, with results that neither side found convenient. Connection to Lesson 7: it is a half-hour of economists conceding points to each other in public, which is exactly the register today's lesson trains. Transcript beside the audio.

Tip: count the concessions. Every time somebody says granted, admittedly or to be fair, note what they conceded and what they held. Fifteen minutes is plenty.

Talk
"Poverty Isn't a Lack of Character, It's a Lack of Cash" — Rutger Bregman, TED

A historian arguing a position many people disagree with, and doing it by taking the objections seriously first. Connection to Lesson 7: watch the structure rather than the politics — where he concedes, where he pivots, and what he refuses to give up. Transcript on the page.

Tip: whether or not you agree, write down the two strongest objections he does NOT answer. That is the exercise, and it is more useful than agreeing.
