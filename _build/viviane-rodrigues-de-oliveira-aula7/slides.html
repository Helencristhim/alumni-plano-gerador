Chapter 3: The Code

The Best, The Biggest

One small word makes the superlative work

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Store retention is the biggest challenge we have."

"The head office is the strongest team in the company."

"The first month is the most difficult moment for a new employee."

"That was the best insight of the whole survey."

One small word appears in all four sentences, right before the adjective. Which one -- and what happens if you take it out?

FormUseExample
the + short adjective + -estOne syllable, or two ending in -y.the biggest · the lowest · the easiest
the most / the least + long adjectiveTwo syllables or more. Never add -est.the most difficult · the least expensive
the is obligatoryThe superlative always carries the.It is the biggest challenge. (never is biggest challenge)
irregularThe same three from last class.good → the best · bad → the worst · far → the furthest
one of the + superlative + pluralWhen it is not the only one.It is one of the biggest challenges in retail.

The Structure

A Diagnosis in Three Sentences

This is five minutes with a board, compressed. Nothing else fits, and nothing else is needed.

1 · The best result

"Our strongest number this year is engagement: seventy-four per cent, the highest we have ever had."

2 · The biggest problem

"Our biggest challenge is store retention. The north region has the lowest number in the company."

3 · The root cause

"And the root cause is not salary. It is the first month, and the manager who receives them."

Best → biggest problem → root cause. A board can act on the third sentence. It cannot act on a list.

Chapter 4: The Board Meeting

Five Minutes with the Board

Luca · CEO of the Brazilian operation

Dialogue

What Is the Real Problem?

L
Viviane, you have five minutes. Start with the good news, please.
V
Engagement is seventy-four per cent. It is the highest number we have ever had.
L
Good. And the bad news?
V
Store retention. It is our biggest challenge, and the north region has the lowest number in the company.
L
Is it the salary? Everybody tells me it is the salary.
V
Sorry, could you repeat that, please?
L
The salary. Is that the reason people leave?
V
No. The root cause is the first month. People leave the manager, not the money, and the north region has our newest managers.
L
That is the most useful thing I have heard this morning. What do you need?
Comprehension

About Luca

1. How much time does Luca give her?
Five minutes — and he asks her to start with the good news.
2. What does he believe is the reason people leave?
The salary — because everybody tells him that.
3. How does he react to her answer about the root cause?
He says it is the most useful thing he has heard that morning, and asks what she needs.
Listening

The Slide That Changed the Meeting

An HR director tells how she stopped presenting spreadsheets. Sound first -- no text.

0:000:00
1. What did the finance director tell her?
That her data was the best in the company and her slide was the hardest to read.
2. What did she put on the new slide?
Three sentences: the strongest number, the biggest problem, and the root cause.
3. What happened the first time she used it?
The meeting was silent for about ten seconds.
4. What did the CEO ask?
What do you need from us — a question twenty numbers had never produced.
Listening 2

What Is Your Biggest Weakness?

A recruiter on the worst question in every interview. Sound first -- no text.

0:000:00
1. Why does he still ask about weaknesses?
Because the answer tells him more than the CV.
2. What is the most common answer, and what does he do with it?
The fake one — I am a perfectionist, I work too hard. He writes nothing down.
3. What are the two parts of the best answer?
The real weakness said simply, and what you are doing about it.
4. What does he advise about a language weakness?
Never say I do not speak English; say I am learning, and here is where I am.

The Real Thing

One Slide for the Board

People dashboard
People -- Quarterly Headlines
Prepared by: Head of People
Board meeting: 5 minutes

Three headlines. No spreadsheet.

  • Best result: engagement 74% — the highest we have ever had (2023: 66%)
  • Biggest challenge: store retention. North region: 31% turnover, the lowest retention in the group
  • Root cause: the first 30 days. 62% of the people who leave, leave in month one
  • Bottleneck: new store managers — the north region has the newest managers in the company
  • Trend: three quarters going down — the impact of the training is the biggest in the oldest stores

Ask: two trainers for the north region, from May.

1. Which line is the one the board can actually act on?
The root cause and the bottleneck — the first 30 days and the new managers in the north. A percentage alone is not an action.
2. Why does the ask sit at the bottom, in one line?
Because the three headlines earn it. You name the problem and the cause first; then the ask is obvious.
