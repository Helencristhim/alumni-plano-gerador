Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
Think Fast, Talk Smart: Communication Techniques

Fifty-eight minutes from Stanford on the exact problem you described: speaking when there was no time to prepare the sentence. Matt Abrahams works with people who are fluent in their own field and still freeze the moment somebody asks a question they did not expect.

Tip: watch the first fifteen minutes only, twice. The second time, write down the three things he says to do while your head is still looking for the word.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
Julian Treasure — How to speak so that people want to listen (TED)

Ten minutes on why some voices are followed on a call and others are not, and almost none of it is about grammar. He is a sound engineer, so he talks about pace, pitch and silence — the three things that decide whether a room waits for the end of your sentence.

Tip: turn English subtitles on the first time and off the second. Then read your five survival sentences out loud using his advice about pace and silence.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
The Indicator from Planet Money — Is bankruptcy really all that bad?

Nine minutes of American business journalism at full native speed, on a subject you already understand in Portuguese: what happens to the numbers when a company cannot pay. Because you know the accounting, your ear can work on the English instead of the content.

Tip: this episode is full of dates and times. Listen once and count how many times you hear on before a day, in before a month or a year, and at before a clock time.
