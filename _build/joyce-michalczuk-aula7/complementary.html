TED Talk
Simon Sinek: “How great leaders inspire action” (18 min)

The famous talk about starting with why. It is Tony’s argument from class, told for whole companies: people give more when they understand the reason, not only the task.

Tip: after the talk, take one real request you have to make this week and say the why first. Record it and send it to your teacher.

TEDx Talk
Kim Scott: “How to lead with radical candor” (TEDxPortland)

A former manager at Google and Apple on how to say what you mean without being mean. Diane’s argument from class: friendly and firm at the same time is possible, and she shows how.

Tip: listen for her two words for the mistakes managers make. Which one is closer to the way you talk to your provider?
