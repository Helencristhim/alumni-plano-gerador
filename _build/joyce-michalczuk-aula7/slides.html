Real Speed

I Will Becomes I’ll

In normal speech, will almost disappears. Listen, then say it back at the same speed.

I'll send it by Thursday at noon.
We'll follow up tomorrow morning.
It'll be ready before the close.
I'll get back to you by Friday.

Two Teams

One Request, Two Ways

The same real request, first to your team, then to your provider.

What
Exactly what you need. One sentence.
Why
The reason, the way Tony wants it.
By when
Day and hour. Never “as soon as possible”.
If it is late
The consequence, as a fact, the way Diane says it.

Language you can use

I need… by… at…The reason is that…If it is late, we are going to…Can you commit to that?

Under Pressure

Both Sides of the Table

Two calls on the same afternoon. First, your provider’s account manager, who is friendly and never gives a date. Then your global manager, who is not happy that a report is late.

  • Round 1: you chase the provider until you get a real commitment: a day, an hour, and what happens if it is late.
  • Round 2: you are the one being chased. Own it, explain in one sentence, and commit to a new date.
