The scene

Just the facts, or the scene?

Left: just the facts. Right: the same story, with the scene around it. What changes?

"I watched the finale."↔"I was watching the finale when the screen froze."
"My mom cooked pasta."↔"My mom was cooking pasta while I was watching."
"I watched for three hours."↔"I had been watching for three hours when the Wi-Fi died."
"My brother downloaded a game."↔"My brother had been downloading a game all night."
You want to sayUse
the scene: what was happening at that momentwas / were + -ing
My mom was cooking and my brother was playing.
the thing that cut into the scenepast simple (when...)
I was watching when the screen froze.
two things at the same timewhile + was / were + -ing
While we were arguing, my friend sent a spoiler.
how long something had been going on before that momenthad been + -ing (for / since / all night)
I had been binge-watching for three hours.

The question of the day: Is it the scene, or the thing that happened? The scene is was / were + -ing. The surprise is the past simple. To say how long it had been going on before, use had been + -ing.
