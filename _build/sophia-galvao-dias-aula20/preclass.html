The Night the Wi-Fi Died

About ten minutes. Eight screen words — binge-watch, spoiler, cliffhanger, plot twist, subtitles, season finale, rewatch, buffering — and how to tell a story with the scene around it: I was watching when..., I had been waiting for...

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example out loud.

Binge-watch -- to watch a lot of episodes, one after another
"I had been binge-watching for three hours when the Wi-Fi died."
Spoiler -- information that tells you the end of a story too early
"My friend sent me a spoiler while I was watching."
Cliffhanger -- an exciting end of an episode that makes you want more
"The episode ended with a cliffhanger, and I screamed."
Plot twist -- a surprise in the story that changes everything
"I was eating popcorn when the plot twist came."
Subtitles -- the words at the bottom of the screen
"I always watch with subtitles in English."
Season finale -- the last episode of a season
"I had been waiting for the season finale all week."
Rewatch -- to watch something again
"I rewatched my favorite part three times."
Buffering -- when a video stops and loads before it plays
"The video was buffering while the music was getting louder."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Telling a story with the scene around it

  • was / were + -ing = the scene: My parents were talking in the kitchen.
  • past simple = what cut into the scene: I was watching when the screen froze.
  • had been + -ing = how long it had been going on before: I had been watching for three hours when the Wi-Fi died.

Watch out

  • The sudden thing is past simple: the screen froze, not was freezing. And it is had been waiting, never had been wait.

Remember: your life up to now is still have: Have you ever rewatched a series? Yes, I have.
