The Code

Why -er here and more there?

short → shorter
This week is shorter than last week.
boring → more boring
Monday is more boring than Friday.
good → better
The book is better than the film.
bad → worse
The traffic is worse than yesterday.

Count the syllables

One syllable → add -er. Three or more → put more in front. And two of them refuse to follow any rule: good → better, bad → worse.

Did You Catch It?

Who said what

1. What does Bia think about the ending?
That it is amazing.
2. What is Sam’s reason for calling season two boring?
Nothing happens for forty minutes.
3. Why does Bia say season one is more popular?
Only because it was first — not because it is better.
Listening 1

Two concerts, one opinion

1. Which concert was more expensive, and by how much?
The second one — almost twice the price.
2. Why does she say the first one was better?
It was smaller, so she was much closer to the stage.
3. What is her final answer about the expensive one?
It was not worth it — amazing, but not for that price.
Listening 2

This year vs. last year

1. How many people came this year, compared to last year?
Three hundred, against two hundred last year.
2. What was the most popular stand?
The food stand — it sold out in one hour.
3. What was worse than last year, and why?
The music — the speakers were too quiet outside.
