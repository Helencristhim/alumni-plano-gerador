Lesson 06 — Pre-class

Better, Worse, or the Same?

Eight opinion words (boring, exciting, stressful, relaxing, popular, terrible, amazing, worth it) and how to compare: shorter than, more boring than, better than — and the best of all.

Stage 1.1: Opinion Words

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Eight words, two minutes.

Boring — it makes you want to do something else
"The film was long and boring."
Exciting — it makes your heart go faster
"The last ten minutes were really exciting."
Stressful — it makes you worried and tense
"Test week is the most stressful week of the term."
Relaxing — it makes you calm
"A rainy Sunday at home is relaxing."
Popular — a lot of people like it
"That song is the most popular one right now."
Terrible — very, very bad
"The ending was terrible. Everyone hated it."
Amazing — very, very good
"The concert was amazing. Best night of the year."
Worth it — good enough for the time or money it costs
"Two hours in the car, but it was worth it."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the review. Then answer the questions.

Season two is better than season one, and I will defend that. Yes, the first three episodes are slower, and yes, episode four is more boring than a maths class. But the ending is the most exciting thing they have ever made. Season one is more popular only because it came first. And the soundtrack this time is amazing — much better than the old one. Eight hours of my life, but honestly? It was worth it. The worst part is that now I have to wait a year.
Why does the writer say season one is more popular?
A Only because it came first.
B Because it is better.
Why is it "more boring" and not "boringer"?
A Because "boring" is a negative word.
B Because "boring" is a long adjective, so it takes "more".
What is the worst part, according to the review?
A Episode four.
B Having to wait a year for the next season.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Count the syllables, then choose

Short adjective → -er. Long adjective → more. Two of them follow no rule at all.

Short

cheap → cheaper
long → longer
big → bigger

Long

more boring
more exciting
more popular

Irregular

good → better
bad → worse
far → further

2 things → Friday is better than Monday.
All of them → Friday is the best day of the week. (never forget "the")
✗ more better · ✗ the most stressfulest · ✗ boring that Friday

In speech, than almost disappears. Say the two things loud and let it fall between them.

Speaking

Stage 4: In the conversation

Quiz
Someone says your favourite series is boring. Which answer keeps the conversation alive?
A "Ok."
B "I don’t think so — it’s more exciting than the one you like. Why do you say that?"
You are not sure — sometimes yes, sometimes no. What do you say?
A "It depends on the day."
B "It depends of the day."
Which sentence is correct?
A "This film is more better than the book."
B "This film is better than the book."

Stage 5: Defend an opinion

Reflection
Record 30 seconds in English. Compare two things you actually care about — two series, two songs, two apps, two months of the year. Say which one is better and give TWO reasons, not one. Use at least three words from Stage 1.1. Finish with a question for your teacher.
