Chapter 3: The Code

Three Pieces, One Order

Wh- word + did + person + verb

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"What did you study in your project?"

"Where did you go last year?"

"When did the project start?"

"Why did you choose small schools?"

PieceWhat it isExample
1the Wh- wordWhere ...
2didWhere did ...
3the person + the clean verbWhere did you go?
answer with whyBecause + sentenceBecause nobody studied them before.
Key ideaThe order never changes: Wh- + did + person + verb. And the verb is CLEAN -- did you go, never did you went.

In speech, did you sounds like one word: DI-djoo.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"Where did you went last year?"
"Where did you go last year?"
"Why you chose small schools?"
"Why did you choose small schools?"
"Why did you choose them? Nobody studied them before."
"Why did you choose them? Because nobody studied them before."

An answer to why starts with because. Without it, the answer sounds unfinished.

Dialogue

Can I Ask You Some Questions?

N
Joice, I read your report. It was very good. Can I ask you some questions?
J
Of course. Please, slowly.
N
Where did you go last month?
J
I went to four public schools, all in the east of São Paulo.
N
And who did you talk to in the schools?
J
I talked to the teachers and to two directors. I did not talk to the students.
N
Why did you choose those four schools?
J
Because they used the platform every day. The other schools did not.
N
That makes sense. And how did you organize the visits?
J
That is a very good question. I did not organize them alone — my colleague helped me.
