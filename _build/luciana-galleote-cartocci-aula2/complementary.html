Film
The Terminal (2004) Trailer #1

Tom Hanks plays a traveler who lands in New York and understands almost nothing at the airport. Funny and warm, and a good reminder that asking again is how everybody starts.

Tip: watch the trailer with English subtitles, then again without them. Write down one question he asks when he does not understand.

Talk
5 ways to listen better

Sound expert Julian Treasure says that we are losing our listening, and gives five short exercises to get it back. Seven minutes, and the exercises take less than a minute a day.

Tip: listen for the gist the first time, without subtitles. The second time, turn on English subtitles and write the five exercises.

Podcast
Scared to speak English? -- 6 Minute English

Two BBC presenters talk about why so many people are afraid to speak a second language, and what helps. Short, clear, and with a transcript on the BBC site.

Tip: listen once at 0.75x speed and once at normal speed. Pause three times and say: sorry, I didn’t catch that.
