Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Film
The Bucket List (2007)

Jack Nicholson and Morgan Freeman are two men in a hospital who write a list of things to do before they die, and then they go and do them: a safari, the pyramids, the Himalayas. Funny, warm and full of travel plans.

Tip: after the film, write your own bucket list of three things, with I’m going to… and This time next year, I’ll be…

TED Talk
Pico Iyer: Where is home? (14 min)

A travel writer who has lived in many countries asks a simple question: when you travel all your life, where is home? Slow, clear English and a lot of ideas for a conversation about your next trip.

Tip: after the talk, say where you will be living in ten years, and why.

Film
The Secret Life of Walter Mitty (2013)

Ben Stiller plays a quiet man who only dreams about adventures, until one day he gets on a plane to Greenland. There is a famous scene on a small boat in a cold sea.

Tip: after the film, tell a friend about the trip Walter takes, and the trip you are going to take next.
