Lesson 2 -- The Director's Voice

Documentary
Inside Bill's Brain: Decoding Bill Gates -- Part 1

Three hours of one person explaining how he decides things. Ignore the technology and listen to the structure: he names the scope of a problem before he touches the detail, every single time. That is the habit this lesson is trying to build in you, in English.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time he describes a decision he did not take, write the sentence down — you will collect five or six third conditionals in an hour without trying.

Podcast
WorkLife with Adam Grant -- any episode on careers and identity

An organisational psychologist on why people describe their own work badly, why titles say so little, and how professional identity is built. He speaks clearly for an American and the episodes are short enough for a commute.

Tip: listen once at normal speed, without stopping. Then pick one idea and say it out loud in your own words, in English, as if you were explaining it to your counterpart in Mexico.

YouTube
Amy Cuddy -- Your Body Language May Shape Who You Are (TED)

Twenty minutes on the gap between how competent you are and how competent you appear — which is exactly the gap you described when you said you feel senior in one language and junior in another. Her argument is that the gap is closable, and that it starts before you speak.

Tip: watch it once with no subtitles. You will understand more than you expect, and noticing that is part of the point.
