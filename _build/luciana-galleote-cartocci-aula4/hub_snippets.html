Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Footage -- the video recorded by a camera
"The footage is stored on the hospital servers."
A feed -- the live video that a camera sends to a screen
"The security team watches forty feeds at the same time."
To detect -- to notice or find something, often automatically
"The system detects a fall in two seconds."
To flag -- to mark something so that people pay attention to it
"Unusual movements are flagged on the screen."
An alert -- a message that tells someone about a problem immediately
"An alert is sent to the nurse’s phone."
In real time -- at the same moment that something happens
"The doctors see the information in real time."
Facial recognition -- technology that identifies a person from the face
"Some hospitals do not use facial recognition, for privacy reasons."
Privacy -- the right of a person to keep personal information protected
"Patient privacy is the first question every client asks."
To store -- to keep information or objects in a place to use later
"The video is stored for thirty days."
A false alarm -- an alert when there is no real problem
"A good system has very few false alarms."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

A large hospital uses video analytics in its emergency area. Every camera feed is analyzed in real time by software. When a person falls, the fall is detected in two seconds, and an alert is sent to the security team.

The footage is stored on a server inside the hospital for thirty days. After that, it is deleted automatically.

Privacy is important. Faces are not recognized, and the images can be seen only by three people. Nobody needs to say who does each action: the system is the subject of the story.

1. What happens when a person falls?
A The security team watches the footage the next day.
B The fall is detected in two seconds and an alert is sent.
C The person is recognized by the camera.
2. Why does the text say “the footage is stored” and not “somebody stores the footage”?
A Because the action is more important than the person who does it.
B Because it happened in the past.
C Because the footage is a person.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A “The images can be see by three people.”
B “The images can be seen by three people.”
C “The images can seen by three people.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Present Simple Passive

GRAMMAR

Use is / are + the third form of the verb when the thing is more important than the person. Add by + person only if it matters who does it.

JobFormExample
One thingis + third formThe footage is stored on site.
Many thingsare + third formThe alerts are sent to the nurse.
Negativeis not / are not + third formFaces are not recognized.
QuestionIs / Are + subject + third form?Is the footage stored in the cloud?
With cancan be + third formThe images can be seen only by security.
Who does it (if important)by + personThe alerts are checked by the nurse.

Never: The footage stored on the server · An alert is send · The footages are kept · The alerts is checked
