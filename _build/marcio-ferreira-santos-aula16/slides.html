Chapter 3: The Code

Four Words to Join Two Sides

although · whereas · despite · however

Grammar Discovery

Read and Notice

1. "Although the price cut is expensive, it brings volume."

2. "The bull case is volume, whereas the bear case is margin."

3. "Despite the price war, our margin stayed stable."

4. "The app is growing. However, only a third of customers use it."

What do the orange words have in common? Now look at the words that come after each one. Which one is followed by a noun, and not by a full sentence?

WordWhat comes afterExample
althoughA full sentence (subject + verb). Concession: you accept one fact, but the other one is more important. Never although ... but.Although the risk is real, the upside is bigger.
whereasA full sentence. Contrast: two facts side by side, with the same weight. Comma before it.Option A protects volume, whereas Option B protects margin.
despite / in spite ofA noun or a verb with -ing. Never a full sentence. Never despite of.Despite the price war · despite being cheaper
howeverStarts a new sentence: full stop before it, comma after it.The test was positive. However, it was small.

One line to remember: although + sentence, whereas + sentence, despite + noun, however + new sentence.

Both Sides of the Private Label

A category manager presents a plan to the committee. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. Why is the private label good for margin?
Our own brands make about eight points more margin than the national brands.
2. What are the two problems?
Cannibalization, and the suppliers, who may reduce their promotions.
3. What does he recommend?
To move forward slowly, in three categories first, with a review after six months.
