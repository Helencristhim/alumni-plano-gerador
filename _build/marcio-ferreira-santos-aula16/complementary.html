"Why you think you're right -- even if you're wrong" -- Julia Galef, TED

A short talk about two mindsets: the soldier, who defends his side at any cost, and the scout, who wants to see the other side clearly. It is the attitude behind every good analyst note and every good case discussion.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Pause after each example and say one sentence with although or however about the two sides.
