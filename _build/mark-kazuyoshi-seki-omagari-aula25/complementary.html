Lesson 25 -- The Game, a Game, Games

Series
GDLK (High Score) -- Season 1, Episode 1 ("Boom and Bust")

The Brazilian catalogue lists it under the name GDLK, so search for that. It is a documentary series that spends every episode doing exactly what this lesson asks of you: explaining a game to people who have never played it. The narration introduces a machine, a company or a designer, and then keeps referring back to it, so the whole thing is one long article exercise you can hear.

Tip: watch the first five minutes twice. The second time, write down every noun phrase the narrator uses for the same object, in order, and see the moment it turns from "a" into "the".

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English -- How important is play?

Six minutes of two presenters explaining what play is and why it matters, to an audience that may never have thought about it. It is the same job you did in class, in a slightly slower accent, and it is full of general statements about whole categories: children, games, animals, adults.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Listen once without it, then read it and underline every plural noun that appears with no article at all. That list is the habit you are building.

Listen on BBC Learning English ↗
YouTube
Arnel's Everyday English -- A, An, The & Zero Article

The same three options you worked out in class, explained by a teacher who treats the zero article as a real choice and not as an exception. Watch it after the lesson, not before: it will confirm the rule you already found yourself, and it adds the cases with names of countries and places.

Tip: after watching, take the five lines of your recommendation card and check each article against the video. Any line you have to change is the one worth memorising.
