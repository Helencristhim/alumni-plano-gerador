Drill 3

At the Airport

Answer about you. First the experience, then one detail with the past simple.

#QuestionAnswer pattern
1Have you ever missed a flight?Yes, I have. I missed one in… / No, I've never…
2Have you ever lost your luggage?Yes, once. It happened in…
3Have you ever slept at an airport?Yes, I have. I slept at… when…
4Have you ever had a flight cancelled?Yes, I have. Last time, I…
5Have you ever been upgraded to business class?Yes, once. I went to…
Drill 4

At the Hotel

Same structure, a new place. Full sentences.

#QuestionAnswer pattern
1Which hotel have you stayed at the most for work?I've stayed at… many times. I last went…

3Have you ever been to a hotel with no room for you?Yes, once. I went to… and…
4How many times have you been to the US this year?I've been there… times. I went in… and in…

Work Situation

A Cancelled Flight

You are at the airport in Atlanta after a client meeting. Your flight to Sao Paulo is cancelled. You need a new flight and a hotel for tonight. The agent at the desk is tired, and there is a long line behind you.

1. Have you ever had a flight cancelled? What did you do?
2. What is the first thing you say to the agent?
3. What do you need tonight, and what can wait until tomorrow?
Role-Play

At the Desk, Then the Hotel

Round 1 at the airline desk, round 2 at the hotel reception. Keep calm, be direct, and get what you need.

  • Round 1: Your teacher is the airline agent. You ask for a new flight and a hotel voucher.
  • Round 2: Your teacher is the hotel receptionist. Your booking is not in the system. Explain and solve it.
