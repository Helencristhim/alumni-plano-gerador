Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Pico Iyer: “Where is home?” (14 min)

A writer who has lived in airports and hotels around the world asks what home means when you travel all the time.

Tip: note two sentences with have been and two with went. Then say one of each about your own trips.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Chris Hadfield: “What I learned from going blind in space” (17 min)

An astronaut on how to stay calm when something goes wrong, and how to prepare for the unexpected.

Tip: after the talk, say in one minute what you do when a trip goes wrong.

Watch on TED ↗
TEDx Talk
Rick Steves: “The value of travel” (TEDxRainier)

The most famous travel guide in the United States on what travel really gives you.

Tip: pause after each story and ask yourself: have I ever had a moment like that? When was it?
