Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Documentary
DW Documentary — "New work: The future of toil"

A film about work that is changing while it is being filmed: what companies are trying now, what people are giving up, what nobody has decided yet. Every second sentence is the tense of this lesson.

Tip: pause at three different moments and describe, out loud, what is happening on screen. One sentence each time, with is or are plus -ing.

Podcast
The Indicator from Planet Money (NPR) — "The evidence on remote work is changing"

The title alone is the grammar of this lesson. Nine minutes on a situation that has started and has not settled: what the numbers are showing now, and what companies are doing about it this year.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Listen once with the page closed, then read it and count how many verbs carry -ing.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Nilofer Merchant — "Got a meeting? Take a walk" (TED)

Three minutes, one idea, and the exact vocabulary of a working week: what she is doing instead of sitting in meetings, and why. Short enough to watch twice in the time it takes to drink a coffee.

Tip: watch it once without subtitles and once with them. Then say her whole argument back in four sentences.
