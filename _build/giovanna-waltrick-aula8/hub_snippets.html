Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example out loud. All eight belong to the part of a trip that nobody plans.

Queue -- a line of people waiting for their turn
"The queue for security was not moving at all."
Security -- the place where they check you and your bags before the gate
"We were still standing in security when they announced the gate."
Gate -- the door at the airport where you get on the plane
"They changed the gate twice while we were waiting."
To board -- to get on a plane, a bus or a train
"We were boarding when the storm started."
Delay -- when something happens later than it should
"The delay was four hours and nobody explained why."
To announce -- to tell a lot of people something officially
"They announced the delay when everybody was already at the gate."
Crowd -- a large group of people in one place
"A crowd was forming in front of the desk."
To realize -- to suddenly understand something that was already true
"I realized I was in the wrong queue."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

I was standing in the queue for security when they announced the first delay. Nobody around me was listening, so I realized what was happening only because everybody suddenly started walking in the other direction. While we were waiting at the new gate, a crowd was forming in front of the desk and nobody was answering. We were boarding, finally, at one fifteen in the morning, when somebody told me they had given out food vouchers three hours earlier. I did not ask, so I did not get one.
Why is it "I was standing" but "they announced"?
A Because standing was already going on and the announcement cut through it
B Because standing is in the present and announced is in the past
C Because stand is an irregular verb
"While we were waiting, a crowd was forming." Why are both verbs continuous?
A Because the two things were happening at the same time
B Because one interrupted the other
C Because while always takes the continuous in both halves

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Past Continuous

GRAMMAR

The past continuous is was / were + verb-ing. It paints the scene; the past simple is the short event that cuts through it.

the scenewas / were + verb-ingI was waiting at the gate.
the interruptionpast simple...when they called my name.
whencomes before the short one...when the storm started.
whilecomes before the long oneWhile I was standing in the queue...
two long onesboth continuous = same timeA crowd was forming while nobody was answering.

Careful 1: it is we were, never we was. I and he/she/it take was; you, we and they take were.

Careful 2: I waited when they called is not wrong English, but it means something else — a sequence, not an interruption.

Stage 5: Free Production

Reflection
Tell the story of the longest wait you have ever had — an airport, a queue, a hospital, anything. Record two minutes: where you were, what was happening around you, what other people were doing, and what finally happened. Use was or were plus -ing at least six times. Do not write a script first — just speak.
