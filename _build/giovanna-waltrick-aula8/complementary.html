Lesson 8 -- What Was Happening

Conversation
"Talking about travel" — Real Easy English, BBC Learning English

Real people telling real travel stories, at a speed made for learners. Listen for how often they describe the scene instead of only the events.

Tip: pick one story and retell it out loud in four sentences, adding a background layer that was not there. That is the whole exercise of this lesson.

Listening
"Are you lonely in a crowd?" — 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes about being surrounded by people and still alone — which is exactly what four hours at a gate feels like. The word crowd is used a dozen times.

Tip: listen once without subtitles, then say three sentences describing the last crowded place you were in, using was or were plus -ing.

Grammar
"Past simple and past continuous" — 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

The exact contrast of this lesson in six minutes, with when and while explained side by side.

Tip: after the video, tell one true story from your week in three sentences, and put a while in the middle one. If it sounds strange, the two halves are probably the wrong way round.
