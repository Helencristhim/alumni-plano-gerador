Lesson 21 -- Saying It Like a Pro

Podcast
"What English phrases really mean" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters talk about polite phrases that do not mean what they say, and how the same idea changes with the situation. Six minutes, with a transcript -- register in real life.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then write two phrases you heard and say each one in a more formal way and in a more casual way.

Conference Talk
"What makes a word 'real'?" -- Anne Curzan, TED

A language researcher explains who decides which words are correct, and why casual words and formal words both have their place. Short, clear American English about the topic of today's class.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Note one casual word and one formal word she talks about, and the situation where each one works.

Video
"This app knows how you feel" -- Rana el Kaliouby, TED

A researcher from Egypt, who learned English as a second language, presents her research on emotions and technology. A non-native speaker who explains complex science in clear, simple English -- the model for your homework.

Tip: use it for your listening log. Write one hedging expression, one connector and one moment when she switches from formal to casual.
