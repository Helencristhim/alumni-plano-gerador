Chapter 3: The Code

Panel Verbs, Hallway Verbs

conduct or carry out? discover or find out?

Grammar Discovery

Same Meaning, Different Room

Panel: We conducted the study in two clinics. — Hallway: We carried out the study in two clinics.
Panel: We discovered an unexpected pattern. — Hallway: We found out something unexpected.
Panel: The pain increased after six weeks. — Hallway: The pain went up after six weeks.
Panel: We will investigate this next year. — Hallway: We're going to look into it next year.
Click to reveal the rule
Casual English often uses a phrasal verb: a short verb + a small word (find out, carry out, go up, look into, set up). Formal English often uses one longer verb with the same meaning (discover, conduct, increase, investigate, establish). Both are correct — the room decides. The formal verb takes no small word: we investigated the problem, never investigated into. Casual English also likes contractions (we're, didn't) and openers like basically and honestly.
