Angelica Dass -- "The beauty of human skin in every color" (TED)

A Brazilian photographer talks about her project that matches every person to an exact color. A short, personal talk, with precise and careful language about color and description.

Tip: listen for the compound adjectives she uses, and describe your own skin color in one sentence with two of them.

A Canadian emergency doctor tells honest stories about his own clinical mistakes and about how doctors talk to each other. It is the language of the phone call and the handover, in a calm, clear voice.
