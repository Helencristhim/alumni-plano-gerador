Chapter 3: The Code

Decided Before, or Now?

English has two futures and they do different jobs

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Next quarter we are going to run the survey in Italy."

"I am going to ask for two more people in January."

"Fine — then I will send you the numbers today."

"I think it will take about six months."

Two of these were decided before this conversation. Two are being decided right now. Which is which?

FormUseExample
be going to + base formA plan you already made, before this conversation.We are going to run the survey in June.
will + base formA decision you take at this moment, in the conversation.Fine, I will send it today.
will for opinionA prediction: with I think, probably, maybe.I think it will take six months.
negativeam/is/are not going to · will not (won't)We are not going to hire in January. I will not promise that.
never to after willTwo traps for Brazilian speakers.I will send it. (never I will to send, never I go to send)

The Structure

A Priority in Three Sentences

This is how you present a plan to people who do not know your company yet.

1 · The priority and the plan

"My first priority is retention, and we are going to start with the store managers."

2 · The number

"The target is to bring store turnover below twenty per cent by December."

3 · The ask

"I will need your approval for a small budget, and I will send you the numbers this week."

Priority → target with a number → what you need. Three sentences and they know exactly what to say yes to.

Chapter 4: The Planning Call

Milan Wants Your Three Priorities

Andrea · group HR director · the quarterly planning call

Dialogue

The Planning Call

A
Viviane, good morning. Before we start: what are your three priorities for the next quarter?
V
Good morning, Andrea. Number one is retention in the stores. We are going to start with the store managers in April.
A
Good. And the target?
V
Store turnover below twenty per cent by December. Today it is twenty-six.
A
That is ambitious. What do you need from us?
V
Sorry, could you repeat that, please?
A
Of course. What do you need from Milan to do it?
V
A small budget for the training, and your approval before April. I will send you the numbers this week.
A
Send them on Monday and I will give you an answer in the same week. And Viviane — do not put five priorities on that page. Three is already a lot.
Comprehension

About Andrea

1. What does Andrea ask for at the beginning of the call?
Her three priorities for the next quarter.
2. What does he say when he hears the target?
That it is ambitious — and he asks what she needs from Milan.
3. What advice does he give her at the end?
Not to put five priorities on the page: three is already a lot.
Listening

Three Priorities, Not Nine

An HR director explains how she plans a quarter. Sound first -- no text.

0:000:00
1. What does she write down every December?
Everything her team could do next year — usually about nine projects.
2. What happens to the other six projects?
They go on a second page called not this year, and she shows it to her director too.
3. Where are they going to start, and why?
With the store managers in April, because that is where the turnover is.
4. What does she say will happen if she puts nine projects on the page?
Nothing.
Listening 2

Where Do You See Yourself?

A recruiter on the future question everybody fears. Sound first -- no text.

0:000:00
1. Why does he ask where you see yourself in five years?
Not for a five year plan — to know if you have a direction.
2. What is the worst kind of answer?
The one that promises everything: I will be a director, I will change the company.
3. What does a good answer sound like?
Smaller and honest: I am going to keep working on culture, in an international company.
4. What does he tell candidates who are not native speakers?
Do not apologise for your English at the start. Just answer — he will ask if he needs something repeated.

The Real Thing

Your Quarter, on One Page

Quarterly plan
People Plan -- Next Quarter
Owner: Head of People
Board review: April

Three priorities. Nothing else on this page.

  • 1. Retention in the stores — we are going to start with the store managers in April. Target: turnover below 20% by December (today: 26%).
  • 2. Leadership programme — we are going to run two groups of twelve. Budget needed: yes, small.
  • 3. New performance process — pilot in the head office first. Scope: 60 people, not the stores.

Not this quarter: employer branding, new HR system, benefits review.

1. Why is there a second list at the bottom, with what is not happening?
Because a plan is also what you decide not to do — and your director needs to see it, so nobody expects it.
2. Which sentence would you use for priority 1, and which for the budget answer?
We are going to start with the store managers — already decided. I will send you the numbers — decided now, on the call.
