Chapter 3: The Code

Finished, and When

The tense of every interview question about your experience

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Last week I finished the payroll on Friday."

"On Tuesday I ran three interviews."

"I did not finish the backlog."

"What did you do on Monday?"

Two verbs only added -ed. One changed completely. And after did, why is the verb short again?

FormUseExample
regular verb + -edMost verbs. The past is one word.I finished the report. We talked on Friday.
irregular verb changesA small group changes shape. You learn them one by one.run → ran · do → did · have → had · send → sent
did not + base formThe past is already in did, so the verb goes back to base.I did not finish it. (never did not finished)
Did + subject + base formQuestions work the same way.Did you send the payroll? Did she talk to him?
time markersThey tell you the past is closed.last week, yesterday, on Monday, two days ago

The Structure

Three Sentences for Any Week

This is the shape of a weekly report -- and of the interview answer that starts with "tell me about a recent project".

1 · What you finished

"Last week I ran three final interviews and we closed the shortlist."

2 · One number

"We interviewed six people and we shortlisted three."

3 · What is still open

"I did not finish the backlog, so it moves to this week."

Finished → a number → what is still open. The third one is what makes it sound true.

The Monday Call

M
Good morning, Viviane. Before the board call — tell me about last week.
V
Good morning, Marco. Last week I ran three final interviews and we closed the shortlist for the store manager role.
M
Good. How many people did you interview in total?
V
Six. We interviewed six and we shortlisted three.
M
And the payroll? Did it go out on time?
V
Sorry, could you say that again, please?
M
Of course. The payroll. Did you send it on Friday?
V
Yes, we did. We sent it on Friday morning. The only thing I did not finish was the backlog of absence reports.
M
That is fine, it can wait. Send me a short follow-up on Wednesday and we close it there.
Comprehension

About Marco

1. What does Marco ask for before the board call?
A report of her week — what happened last week.
2. What does he want to know about the payroll?
Whether she sent it on Friday, on time.
3. What does he ask for at the end?
A short follow-up on Wednesday, and they close the subject there.
