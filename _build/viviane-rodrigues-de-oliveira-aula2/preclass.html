Lesson 02 -- Pre-class

What Did You Do Last Week? -- Talking About Work Events in the Past

Reporting a real week of work: what you finished, the numbers, and what is still open. Key words: headcount, payroll, shortlist, deadline, workload, absence, backlog, handover, follow-up, line manager. Structures: past simple of regular and irregular verbs, did not + base form, and questions with did.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the definition. All of them are on your desk every week.

Headcount -- the total number of people working in a company
"Headcount went down by two people this month."
Payroll -- the list of employees and the money the company pays them
"The payroll went out on Friday morning."
Shortlist -- the small group of final candidates you choose
"We closed the shortlist on Thursday."
Deadline -- the day or time when a task must be finished
"The deadline for the report was Wednesday."
Workload -- the amount of work one person has to do
"Her workload doubled after the acquisition."
Absence -- a day when an employee does not come to work
"I signed twelve absence forms last week."
Backlog -- work that is waiting and is not done yet
"I did not finish the backlog of absence reports."
Handover -- passing your tasks to another person before you leave a role
"She wrote a handover document before she left."
Follow-up -- a second message or meeting to check what happened
"I sent a follow-up on Wednesday morning."
Line manager -- the person a team member reports to directly
"Every line manager talked to me last week."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Last week was a busy week for Viviane. On Monday she had a system problem and the payroll almost went out late. She spent the whole morning with the finance team and they fixed it before lunch. On Tuesday she interviewed six candidates and she closed the shortlist for the store manager role. On Wednesday she talked to every line manager about absence and workload. She did not finish the backlog of reports, and she did not write the handover document either. On Friday Marco asked her one question: "Did you send the payroll on time?" She said yes, and she sent him a follow-up on Wednesday.

1. Why does the text say "she interviewed" and not "she interviews"?
A Because the action is finished and it happened last week.
B Because she is a woman.
C Because "interview" is an irregular verb.
2. The text says "she did not finish", not "she did not finished". Why?
A Because the sentence is a question.
B Because the past is already inside did, so the verb goes back to the base form.
C Because "finish" has no past form.
3. Why is there a "did" in "Did you send the payroll on time?"
A Past questions need did, and the verb goes back to the base form.
B It makes the question more polite.
C It is a mistake in the text.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Past Simple: Regular, Irregular and Questions

GRAMMAR

How to say what happened, what did not happen, and how to ask about it.

FormUseExample
regular verb + -edMost verbs. One word, and it is finished.I finished the report. We talked on Friday.
irregular verb changesA small group changes shape. Learn them one by one.run → ran · send → sent · have → had · write → wrote
did not + base formThe past is already in did. Never two pasts in one verb.I did not finish it. (never did not finished)
Did + subject + base formQuestions work the same way as the negative.Did you send the payroll? Did she talk to him?
was / wereThe verb to be is the exception: no did.It was a busy week. They were in the store.
time markersThey close the past: the action is over.last week, yesterday, on Monday, two days ago

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the best answer for each situation.

Marco asks: "What did you do last week?" You answer:
A "Last week I ran three interviews and we closed the shortlist."
B "Last week I run three interviews."
C "I am running three interviews last week."
You want to know if your colleague sent the report. Which question is correct?
A "Did you sent the report?"
B "Did you send the report?"
C "You sent the report yesterday?"
The payroll was not ready on Friday. You say:
A "We did not finished the payroll."
B "We not finished the payroll."
C "We did not finish the payroll."
An interviewer asks about a recent project. The strongest answer is:
A "I do many things every week: payroll, absence, interviews, reports."
B "Last month I ran the hiring for four stores. We interviewed twenty people and we hired five."
C "It is difficult to explain in English."
Your manager asks how the week was. You want to sound honest and organised:
A "Everything was perfect, no problems."
B "I finished the interviews and the payroll, but I did not finish the backlog."
C "I do not remember, sorry."
