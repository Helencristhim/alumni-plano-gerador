Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 2 -- What Did You Do Last Week

Series
Emily in Paris -- Season 1, Episode 1

An American arrives in a European company where nobody planned for her. The English is slow and clear, the situations are all office situations, and almost every scene is somebody explaining what happened yesterday.

Tip: watch with English subtitles, never Portuguese. Write down five verbs in the past that you hear.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
BBC Learning English -- 6 Minute English

Six minutes, two speakers, one topic, and a full transcript on the same page. It was built for exactly your problem: fast English that you cannot follow yet. Start with any episode about work or careers.

Tip: listen once with no transcript, then again reading it. The second time will feel easy -- that gap is the training working.

Listen on BBC Learning English ↗
YouTube
Tim Urban -- "Inside the Mind of a Master Procrastinator" (TED)

Fourteen funny minutes about deadlines, backlogs and the work we push to next week. Everything he says is in the past simple, and the drawings tell you what the words mean.

Tip: watch once without subtitles, then once with English subtitles. Note three irregular past verbs he uses.
