Tim Ferriss — “Why you should define your fears instead of your goals” (TED)

The contingency word from this lesson, turned into a method. He writes down what could go wrong, then what he would do about each one — which is exactly what a dress rehearsal does to the question you cannot answer.

Tip: do the exercise for your own conference week. Three things that could go wrong, and one line each on what you would do. Say the lines out loud.

Talk
Laura Vanderkam — “How to gain control of your free time” (TED)

Bandwidth and priorities, argued from real data rather than opinion. Her central claim — that we do not build the life we want by saving time, but by deciding what the time is for — is the four-day week in this lesson, applied to the rest of the year.

Tip: her line about "I do not have time" meaning "it is not a priority" is worth trying in English on your own calendar. Rewrite three of your own sentences that way.
