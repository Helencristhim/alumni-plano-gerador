Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 1 -- What You Actually Do

Series
The Office (US) -- Season 1, Episode 1 (Pilot)

An HR department seen from the inside, with jokes. The English is slower than a real meeting and the topics are exactly yours: hiring, performance, people leaving, culture. A gentle first step for listening in English.

Tip: watch with English subtitles, not Portuguese. Write down three words you already know from today's lesson when you hear them.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
WorkLife with Adam Grant -- any recent episode

An organisational psychologist talking about the exact things you manage: motivation, culture, why people leave, what makes teams work. He speaks clearly and slowly for an American, which makes him a good listening partner while you build the muscle.

Tip: listen at 0.75x speed the first time. Do not try to understand everything -- try to catch the main idea and three words.

Listen on Spotify ↗
YouTube
Simon Sinek -- "Why good leaders make you feel safe" (TED)

Twelve minutes on leadership and why people stay or leave a company -- the heart of your retention problem. TED talks are the best listening training there is: the speaker is clear, the subtitles are accurate, and you can slow it down.

Tip: watch once with no subtitles, then once with English subtitles. The second time will feel much easier -- that feeling is the training working.
