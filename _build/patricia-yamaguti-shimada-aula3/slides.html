Chapter 3: Asking Politely

From "I Want" to "I Would Like"

the same request, a completely different person

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I would like the chicken."

"I want the chicken."

"Can I have some water, please?"

"Give me some water."

PatternHow it soundsExample
I would like + thingpolite, always safeI would like the chicken.
Can I have + thing?polite, and even easierCan I have some water?
I want + thingnot wrong, but it sounds hardI want the chicken.
Give me + thingan order, never in a planeGive me some water.

Short form: I'd like = I would like. And please at the end never hurts.

P

P
Sorry, I don't understand. Can you speak more slowly, please?
J
Sorry. Something to drink? Water, juice, tea, coffee?
P
Water, please. One more thing: my son has an allergy. He is allergic to nuts.
J
Thank you for telling me. The chicken is fine. The cake is not.
P
Does the rice have nuts in it? Can you check, please?
J
I can check now. No nuts in the rice. Here is your tray.
P
Thank you. And can I have a blanket for my daughter? She is cold.
J
Certainly. I come back in two minutes. Enjoy your meal.
Comprehension

What Did James Say?

1. Which food is not safe for her son?
The cake. The chicken is fine.
2. What does he do when she asks about the rice?
He checks, and says there are no nuts in the rice.
3. When does he come back with the blanket?
In two minutes.

Chapter 6: Your Turn

From Guided to Free

Role-Play — Guided

Order for the Whole Family

Scenario: The crew arrives at row 14 with the dinner cart. Order one meal for you, one for your husband and two for the children, and ask for something to drink.

Keywords:

I would like... chicken beef Can I have some water?
Role-Play — Semi-free

They Offer the Cake to Your Son

Scenario: The crew offers the chocolate cake to your son. The cake has nuts, and he is allergic. Say no, explain, and ask them to check the rest of the tray.

Keywords:

allergic to nuts It is serious. Does it have nuts? Can you check?
Role-Play — Free

Three in the Morning, and Nobody Is Sleeping

Scenario: It is the middle of the night. Your daughter is cold, everybody is thirsty, and you want to ask about your son's breakfast. Stop a member of the crew and solve all three.

No keywords this time. Two minutes, no stopping. Different from the model is not wrong — if he understood you, it worked.
