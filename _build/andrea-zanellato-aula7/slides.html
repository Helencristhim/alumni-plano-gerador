Chapter 3: The Code

Better, Bigger, the Best

How to compare

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Show?

"The bonus is bigger than last year."

"Remote work is more popular than free lunch."

"The health plan is the most important benefit."

"This offer is better than the other one."

Look at the blue words: they compare TWO things, with than. Short words take -er (bigger); long words take more (more popular). The red words compare one thing with ALL the others: the most / -est.

JobFormExample
Short adjectivesadjective + er + thanbig → bigger than · cheap → cheaper than
Long adjectivesmore + adjective + thanmore flexible than · more popular than
The top of the groupthe + adjective + est / the most + adjectivethe biggest · the most important
Irregulargood → better → the best · bad → worse → the worstThis offer is better.
Ending in -yy → iereasy → easier · busy → busier
The trapnever more + erNot more better. Say better.

In one line: short words take -er / the -est, long words take more / the most; good becomes better and the best.

Chapter 4: Two Countries

Two Countries

One conversation, two HR managers, one comparison table

Dialogue

An HR Conference — Coffee Break

S
Hi, I’m Sara. I’m an HR manager in Stockholm. Are you here for the talk about benefits?
A
Yes! I’m Andrea, Head of HR in Brazil. Benefits are one of my areas.
S
Great. So tell me: what is the most important benefit in Brazil?
A
The health plan, for sure. And the meal allowance. In Sweden?
S
Our public health system is good, so a health plan is less important. People want flexible hours and more paid time off.
A
Interesting. Here, candidates ask about remote work more than about a bonus.
S
Same in Sweden. Time is more valuable than money for young people.
A
And the best perk in your company?
S
Fika! A coffee break every afternoon, with cinnamon buns. It’s cheaper than a bonus, and people love it.
Comprehension

About Sara

1. Where does Sara work?
In Stockholm, as an HR manager.
2. Why is a health plan less important in Sweden?
Because the public health system is good.
3. What is the best perk in Sara’s company?
Fika: a coffee break every afternoon, with cinnamon buns.
