Chapter 3: I Need

Two Words That Open Any Counter

I need · a bottle of · a box of

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I need medicine for my daughter."

"I need something for a headache."

"Two bottles of water, please."

"A box of tea, please."

PatternUseExample
I need + thingyou say what you want, simplyI need medicine.
I need something for + problemwhen you do not know the nameI need something for a headache.
a bottle / box / packet of + thingthe package becomes the unitTwo bottles of water.
for my son / for my daughterwho it is for — never leave this outMedicine for my daughter, she is six.

You cannot count water, milk or rice — but you CAN count the bottle, the box and the packet. The package is the unit, and of joins the two.

Dialogue

The Pharmacy Closes in Fifty Minutes

P
Good evening. How can I help you?
P
Good evening. I need something for a fever.
P
Is it for you?
P
No, it is for my daughter. She is six years old, twenty kilos, and she has thirty-eight now.
P
Perfect, that is exactly what I need to know. This one is for children from two to twelve.
P
Is it safe with a nut allergy? My son is allergic, and sometimes they share.
P
Good question. This one has no nuts. I would avoid the chewable ones, though, just in case.
P
Sorry, I don't understand. Can you say that again, more slowly?
P
Sorry. Not the ones you chew. Take the liquid.
P
The liquid, thank you. And I need a box of plasters too.
P
Here. Anything else?
P
One more thing. How often can I give it to her?
P
Every six hours, four times a day maximum. If the fever is still there tomorrow, see a doctor.
Comprehension

What Did Paul Say?

1. What does Paul say about the chewable ones?
He would avoid them — he says to take the liquid.
2. How often can she give the medicine?
Every six hours, four times a day maximum.
3. What does he say to do if the fever continues?
See a doctor tomorrow.
Listening

The Pharmacy at Night

The recorded message when you call the pharmacy after eight. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What three things do they need for children medicine?
Who it is for, the age, and the weight if you know it.
2. Where is the night pharmacy, and when is it open?
On King Road, next to the station, open all night after ten.
3. When do you NOT need a prescription?
For a fever, a headache or a small cut.

The Supermarket Announcement

The message in the supermarket, twenty minutes before closing. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What is half price after nine, and where is it?
The fresh bread and the cakes, at the back near the milk.
2. What is the offer on water?
Big six-bottle packs, two packs for five pounds.
3. Where are the biscuits with no nuts?
On the second shelf, in the blue boxes.
