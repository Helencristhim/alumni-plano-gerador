Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Acquired — “Due Diligence”

Two investors walking through exactly what a buyer checks, in what order, and which founder answers make them slow down. It is the other side of the table talking openly, and the whole episode is a list of the questions this lesson taught you to answer before they are asked.

Tip: pause every time they name something a buyer verifies, and say out loud how you would answer it about Dataside, with a clause attached to every claim.

Listen on Acquired ↗
Talk
Steven Pinker — “Linguistics, Style and Writing in the 21st Century” (The Royal Institution)

A Harvard linguist on why clear writing is difficult even for people who know exactly what they mean. The section on what he calls the curse of knowledge is the single best explanation of why a founder's own description of his company is the hardest one for a stranger to read.

Tip: British accent, spoken quickly, one hour. Watch twenty minutes at a time rather than all of it, and note every sentence where he attaches a clause instead of starting a new sentence.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Video
Steven Pinker — “The Sense of Style” (Talks at Google)

The shorter, faster, American-audience version of the same argument, with more examples of real sentences rewritten in front of you. If the hour-long talk is too much, start here: it is under an hour and the pace suits a second listen at full speed.

Tip: take three sentences from your own company description into this one. Every time he rewrites an example, stop and do the same thing to one of yours.
