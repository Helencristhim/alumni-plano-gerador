Chapter 3: The Code

Three Patterns, One You

I am · I live · I play

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am Bruno."

"I am fifteen years old."

"I live in Osasco."

"I play basketball."

Two sentences use am. Two use another verb. When do we use am?

PatternWhat it saysExample
I am + name, ageWho you are and how old you are. Not an action — a fact about you."I am Bruno."
"I am fifteen years old."
I live in + cityWhere your home is. The city comes after in."I live in Osasco."
"I live in Brazil."
I play / study / like + thingWhat you do. The thing comes straight after the verb, with no in."I play basketball."
"I study at Anglo."
Key ideaWith I the verb never takes an -s: I am, I live, I play. And age uses am, never have: "I am fifteen years old."

Three patterns. That is the whole code for talking about yourself.
