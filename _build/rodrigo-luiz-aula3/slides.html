Chapter 3: The Pattern

Two Words Open Every Question

do · does · don't · doesn't

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Does the order include the new material?
I don't have the price here.
The supplier doesn't change the price.

Which word starts a question? And which word makes a "no"?

UseI / you / we / theyhe / she / it
QuestionDo you have a minute?Does he work here?
NegativeI don't have the price.She doesn't work on Friday.
Short answerYes, I do. / No, I don't.Yes, it does. / No, it doesn't.
CuidadoDepois de does e doesn't, o verbo perde o "s": "Does he works?" → Does he work? O "s" ja esta no DOES.

Chapter 4: The Supplier Call

Wednesday, Four in the Afternoon

a call with no agenda

Dialogue

Do You Have a Minute?

S
Good afternoon, Rodrigo. Do you have a minute?
R
Yes, I do. How can I help?
S
I have a question about the order from Brazil. Does it include the new material?
R
No, it doesn't. This order doesn't include the new material.
S
I see. And when do you need the delivery?
R
We need it in October. The deadline is October fifteen.
S
October fifteen. Does the price change with the new deadline?
R
I don't know. I don't have the price here. Marcos has it, and he confirms it today.
S
Perfect. Do you want a call tomorrow?
R
Yes, I do. But I don't work after six. Ten in the morning is good.
Comprehension

About the Call

1. What is Sophie's first question about?
The order from Brazil, and if it includes the new material.
2. Does the order include the new material?
No, it doesn't.
3. When does Sophie want the call?
Tomorrow — at ten in the morning.
