Lesson 11 -- Say It Like You Mean It

Conversation
"How to talk about your home town" — Easy English Conversations, Episode 3, BBC Learning English

Two people describe the places where they grew up: the streets, the shops and the people. It is the exact situation of this lesson, at a calm speed.

Tip: every time you hear a place, pause and describe it in one sentence with that or where. Then describe one place in your own neighborhood the same way.

A tailor explains what he changes so that a jacket fits. It connects the fashion course with two words of this lesson: tailor and designer.

Tip: watch the first five minutes with English subtitles. Then explain to yourself, out loud, what a tailor is, using who, without the word tailor.

Grammar
"Defining relative clauses" — 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on the structure of this lesson: who, that and where to say exactly which person, thing or place you mean.

Tip: after the video, describe three things in your room without their names, one with who, one with that and one with where.
