Podcast
How I Built This -- Patagonia: Yvon Chouinard

A founder who talks about his own bad calls without softening them and without performing regret either. The interesting part for this lesson is how he separates the decisions that were wrong from the ones that were reasonable and simply ended badly.

Tip: listen for every sentence where he talks about a past that did not happen. Write two of them down and change the facts to your own company.

TED -- Astro Teller, "The unexpected benefit of celebrating failure" (15 min)

The head of a research lab on why a company that punishes bad outcomes stops learning from them. It is the argument the consultant makes in the second recording of this lesson, told from the inside of an organisation that built a process around it.

Tip: watch it once without subtitles. Then answer, out loud, the question he keeps returning to: at what point could somebody have seen this coming?

Video
Shark Tank US -- "Negotiating With Barbara Isn't Easy!"

Fast, unscripted American business English in the exact register of this lesson: quotes, margins, upfront money and people backing out of deals in real time. Nobody is speaking slowly for you, which is the point.

Tip: watch it twice. The first time, just follow who wants what. The second time, count how many times somebody says what they would have done differently.
