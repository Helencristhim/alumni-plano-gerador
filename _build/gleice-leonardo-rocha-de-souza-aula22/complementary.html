Lesson 22 -- Brand Representation

Podcast
BBC Learning English -- 6 Minute English: "How advertisers make us spend money"

Two presenters talk about how brands and advertisers get our attention in stores and online. Simple English, with the full script on the page.

Tip: read the script while you listen. Write down three words about brands and selling, and say how they connect to your work in pharmacies.

Talk
Rory Sutherland -- "Life lessons from an ad man" (TED, 17 min)

A famous advertising man explains why the way we present a product changes how people see it. Funny and full of examples from real brands.

Tip: watch with English subtitles, at your own speed. Choose one example and explain it in two sentences: what the brand did, and what customers had to see.

A short grammar program about have to and must, with many examples. It is the grammar of this lesson, explained slowly and clearly.
