TEDx Talk
Heather Hansen: “2 Billion Voices: How to speak bad English perfectly”

A global communication coach on the English that most of the world actually speaks at work: not the native kind, but the kind that gets understood. A great talk for somebody who wants to hear “your English is beautiful”.

Tip: write down her three pieces of advice. Which one would help you most on a call with Paris?

TEDx Talk
Melissa Baese-Berk: “Learning to understand accented English”

A linguist explains, with research, that understanding accents is a skill the listener can train, and that it gets easier with exposure. Good news for somebody who already understands Indian English well.

Tip: after the talk, listen once more to the three questions from class. Which accent is now the easiest? Which one still needs practice?

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart, episode 17: “You Asked, We Answered”

Listeners send their questions and the host answers them live, including how to deal with people who interrupt. Listen to how he checks each question before he answers it.

Tip: count how many times he says the question back before answering, like Arjun, and how many times he answers first, like Charlotte.
