Lesson 21 -- Conditional Structures in Negotiations

Series
Suits -- Season 1, Episode 1 ("Pilot")

Harvey Specter, a top New York lawyer, closes a deal in the first minutes of the series and then negotiates his way through the episode. Connection to Lesson 21: listen for how he puts an idea on the table without promising anything, and how he uses his leverage.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time someone says "if", pause and decide: is it an idea (would) or a firm offer (will)?

Talk
"The walk from 'no' to 'yes'" -- William Ury, TED

Nineteen minutes from the co-author of Getting to Yes on how to get out of a deadlock: step back, look at the interests behind the positions, and find the third option. Connection to Lesson 21: it is the thinking behind every trade-off and every earn-out.

Tip: listen at 0.75x first. Write down one idea you could test in your next negotiation, as a second conditional sentence.

Video
"Never Split the Difference" -- Chris Voss, Talks at Google

A former FBI hostage negotiator explains why meeting halfway is not always the best deal, and how to ask questions that make the other side move. Connection to Lesson 21: compare his advice with the earn-out solution in Project Vertex.

Tip: watch the first twenty minutes. Note two techniques and say how you would use each one with a creditor or a buyer.
