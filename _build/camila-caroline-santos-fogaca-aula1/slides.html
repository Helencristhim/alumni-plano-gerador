Chapter 3: The First Meeting

Sixty Seconds With a New Director

A conversation at headquarters, line by line

Dialogue

Meeting the New Global Director

M
Camila, good morning. I am Mark Ridley. I start today, and I am meeting every HR Business Partner this week.
C
Good morning. I am the HR Business Partner for South America, so I look after three entities.
M
Three entities. Tell me which ones, slowly. I am still learning the structure here.
C
The bank, the finance company and the rental company. Brazil, Argentina and Chile are inside my scope.
M
And your reporting line? I ask because the region is new for me.
C
I report to the Global HR Director at headquarters, and I have a dotted-line reporting to the regional managing director here.
M
Good. Who do you work with every day?
C
I work closely with the local managers and with finance. I act as a bridge between headquarters and the three countries.
M
One last question. What is not your responsibility?
C
Payroll operations. A shared services team takes care of that, and I deal with the people decisions.
Comprehension

About the Conversation

1. Why does Mark ask Camila to say the names of the three entities slowly?
Because he is new and he is still learning the structure of the region.
2. What is the difference between the two reporting lines Camila describes?
She reports to the Global HR Director at headquarters, and she has a dotted line to the regional managing director.
3. What is outside her responsibility, and who takes care of it?
Payroll operations. A shared services team takes care of it.
Chapter 4: The Code

One Word, Three Forms

responsible, responsibility, report, reporting line

Grammar Discovery

Adjective or Noun?

"I am responsible for three entities."

"Hiring is my responsibility in these three countries."

"I report to the Global HR Director."

"My reporting line goes to headquarters."

Two of the colored words come after the verb be. Two of them are things, not descriptions. Which is which?

FormWhat it doesExample
be + responsible fordescribes the person: an adjective after the verb beI am responsible for the people agenda.
have / be + responsibilitynames the thing itself: a nounThat is my responsibility. I have responsibility for three entities.
report to + person or rolethe action: a verbI report to the Global HR Director.
reporting linethe thing in the structure: a noun phraseMy reporting line is to headquarters.
Watch outthe preposition. It is responsible FOR and report TO — never "responsible of" and never "report for".

Sound Check

Say It Clearly

H and R · point 1 of 14

Why this one is hard for a Brazilian speaker

In Portuguese: The letter H is silent (hora, homem), and an initial R is made back in the throat (rato).

In English: The H is a puff of air you can feel on your hand, and the R is made with the tongue pulled back, touching nothing.

If it slips: "HR" arrives as "a-R", and "report" arrives as "heport".

Listening 1

A Voice Message from Your Manager

Claire, at headquarters, leaves a message about Thursday. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. How much time does each HR Business Partner have with Mark on Thursday?
One minute each, because he has sixty minutes for the whole region.
2. What three things does Claire ask Camila to say?
Which entities she looks after, who she reports to, and what is inside her scope this quarter.
3. What does Claire need by Wednesday at noon, and why?
Two lines from Camila, so that she can put them in the agenda.
Listening 2

The New Director Introduces Himself

Mark Ridley records a short message for the region. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. Where is Mark based, and what did he look after before this job?
He is based at headquarters in Paris, and before this he looked after the rental business in Europe.
2. How many countries are inside his scope now?
Twelve countries, with the bank, the finance company and the rental company.
3. What three things does he want to hear from each partner on Thursday?
What they are responsible for, who their stakeholders are, and one problem they deal with every week.
