Vocabulary

Listen to each expression and read it in context. You already do all of this. Today it gets its English name.

HR Business Partner -- an HR professional who works inside the business and advises its leaders
"As an HR Business Partner, I sit in the business, not outside it."
business unit -- one part of a company with its own activity, its own leader and its own results
"Each business unit has a different way of hiring."
reporting line -- the role you report to in the structure of the company
"My reporting line is to the Global HR Director."
scope -- the area and the limits of what a role or a project covers
"Argentina and Chile are inside my scope; Mexico is not."
be responsible for -- to have the duty to look after something or someone
"I am responsible for three entities in three countries."
report to -- to have someone as your manager in the structure
"I report to the Global HR Director at headquarters."
work closely with -- to work with someone often and in a direct way
"I work closely with the finance team in Buenos Aires."

Stage 3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the passage, then answer the questions about how it is written.

In a large group, HR is not one team. It is organised in three parts, and each part has a different job. The first part is the HR Business Partner. The business partner sits inside the business, next to the leaders of each business unit, and is responsible for the people agenda there: hiring, performance, organisation and cost of the team.

The second part is the centre of excellence. This team designs the rules: the policy on pay, the policy on promotion, the way performance is measured. It usually sits at headquarters, and it has a global scope. The centre of excellence writes the policy; the business partner explains it to the managers and makes it work in each country.

The third part is shared services. This team runs the operations: contracts, payroll, documents, systems. When a manager asks for a certificate or a payslip, shared services takes care of it. The business partner does not.

A global business partner has one more line on the organisation chart. She has a reporting line to the global HR director at headquarters, and often a dotted-line reporting to a regional leader. The solid line is the manager; the dotted line is the person she also keeps informed. Both are stakeholders, and so are the country managers, the finance team and the employees themselves.

This is why the same person can be responsible for three entities in three countries and still say, without any contradiction, that payroll is not her responsibility. Knowing where your scope ends is part of explaining what you do.

Stage 4: Grammar Tip -- responsible / responsibility, report / reporting line

GRAMMAR

The same idea has more than one form in English, and the form depends on the place in the sentence. After the verb be, you need the adjective. After have, or after my, you need the noun.

FormWhat it doesExample
be responsible for + somethingadjective after the verb beI am responsible for three entities.
my / a responsibilitynoun: the thing itselfHiring is my responsibility.
report to + person or roleverb: the actionI report to the Global HR Director.
my reporting linenoun phrase: the line in the structureMy reporting line goes to headquarters.
Watch outthe preposition. It is responsible for and report to. "Responsible of" and "report for" do not exist in English.

Quick test before you speak: after "I am", use responsible. After "It is my", use responsibility.
