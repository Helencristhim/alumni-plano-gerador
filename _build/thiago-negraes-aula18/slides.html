Chapter 3: Read the Terms

The Agreement Rachel Reviewed

East Side pumping stations · scope, responsibilities, and exceptions

Reading

Routine Service and Emergency Work

Before you read: Which services do you expect to be included? Which costs might be separate?

The agreement covers routine inspections and basic maintenance at the East Side pumping stations. The contractor visits once a month and checks pumps, valves, and control panels. The monthly inspection is included in the service fee.

Although the contractor supplies standard tools, the water utility provides replacement pumps and spare parts. The contractor must report a safety issue immediately. Emergency repairs are available around the clock; however, the utility pays a separate call-out fee for work after 6 p.m.

Despite the four-hour response target, the agreement does not guarantee that every repair will be completed on the same day. The response time starts only after the utility confirms site access. The contractor is responsible for its own staff and safety equipment. The utility must keep the access road clear.

The agreement excludes major construction work and damage caused by a third party. Rachel and the utility manager are reviewing these terms next Thursday before they sign.

Chapter 4: Grammar Discovery

How to Show a Concession

Acknowledge one term and explain its limit

Grammar Discovery

The Service Is Included, Although...

Although the contractor supplies standard tools, the utility provides replacement pumps.

Despite the four-hour response target, same-day completion is not guaranteed.

The response target is four hours; however, the clock starts after site access is confirmed.

What changes in each sentence: the condition, or the other side of the agreement?

Grammar Discovery: Concession

Show a Limit and the Other Side

Although / even though + a complete clause

Although inspections are included, emergency repairs cost extra.

Despite + a noun or -ing form

Despite the short response time, same-day completion is not guaranteed.

However connects two sentences

The fee is included. However, after-hours work costs extra.

Use a concession to acknowledge one term and explain the condition that still matters.

Grammar Discovery: Even Though

Acknowledge the First Term

Even though emergency work is available, after-hours work has a separate fee.

Even though + subject + verb. It has a similar meaning to although.

Rewrite: The inspection is included. Replacement parts are provided by the utility.
