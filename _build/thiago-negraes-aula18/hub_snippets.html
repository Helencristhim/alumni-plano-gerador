Lesson 18 -- Pre-class

Contracts and Concessions

A service agreement for two Houston pumping stations. Key words: scope, contractor, emergency, exclusion, fee, sign, be responsible for, inspection. Structure: although / even though + clause, despite + noun or -ing form, and however between contrasting sentences.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Read each meaning and example. Tap Listen to hear the word.

Coverage -- The work or service an agreement includes
"Routine inspections are within the scope."
Contractor -- A person or company that provides a service
"The contractor checks both stations."
Emergency -- A situation that needs urgent action
"Emergency repairs are billed separately."
Exclusion -- A limit or condition that is not included
"Replacement parts are an exclusion."
Clause -- A section of a legal document
"This clause explains after-hours work."
Sign -- To officially agree to a document
"Both sides sign the agreement."
Be responsible for -- To be responsible for something
"The contractor is responsible for its staff."
Inspection -- A regular check of equipment or work
"The monthly inspection is included."

Stage 1.2: Read the Situation

Reading

Rachel is reviewing an agreement for the East Side stations. Routine inspections are included. Emergency repairs are available at any time, but after-hours work has a separate fee. The utility provides replacement parts, and the contractor is responsible for its staff. The agreement excludes major construction work.

Tap Listen and notice how although and however introduce a condition.

Although inspections are included, emergency repairs have a separate fee.
