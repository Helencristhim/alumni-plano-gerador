Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TED -- Nancy Duarte, "The secret structure of great talks"

A presentation designer who studied hundreds of famous speeches and found one shape in all of them: what is, and what could be, again and again. It is today’s lesson on a bigger stage: a before, a turning point, and a now.

Tip: draw the shape she shows on a piece of paper. Then draw the line of one number from your area and mark the turning point.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Talks at Google -- Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic, "Storytelling with Data"

A former Google analyst shows how one chart can become a clear story for a busy audience: take away what does not help, and say in words what the line means. Long, but you do not need to watch all of it.

Tip: watch the first fifteen minutes with English subtitles. Pause on one of her charts and describe it with used to, sharply or gradually.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Podcast
NPR TED Radio Hour -- Tyler Cowen, "Do the numbers give us the full picture?"

An economist explains why numbers alone never tell the whole story, and why you need to say what is behind them. Short and easy to follow, from an episode called The Story Behind the Numbers.

Tip: listen once without reading. Then write one sentence about something that used to be true in your market and is not true now.
