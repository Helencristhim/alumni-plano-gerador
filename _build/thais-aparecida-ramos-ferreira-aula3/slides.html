Chapter 3: The Code

Say No, Ask Back

I don't · Do you...? · Where · Who

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I don't work on Sunday."

"I don't live in Buenos Aires every day."

"Do you have children?"

"Where do you live?"

PatternUseExample
I don't + verbSay noI don't work on Sunday.
Do you + verb?Ask yes or noDo you have children?
Where do you + verb?Ask about a placeWhere do you live?
Who is...?Ask about a personWho is with you?

After don't and do, the verb never changes: work, live, have.

Grammar Focus

Whose? my · your · her · his

I → "My daughter is fifteen."

you → "Where is your family?"

she → "This trip is for my daughter. Her name is on the ticket."

he → "My husband is here. His seat is next to mine."

The word goes with the owner, not with the thing: a woman → her, a man → his.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"I no have children."

✓ RIGHT

"I don't have children."

✗ WRONG

"Where you work?"

✓ RIGHT

"Where do you work?"

English needs don't for no, and do for the question. Portuguese and Spanish do not — that is why this one takes time.

Comprehension

About David

1. Where is David's home?
In Chicago.
2. Does David have children?
Yes, one son. His name is Ethan and he is eight.
3. Why is David on this plane?
For work. He does not travel with his family.
