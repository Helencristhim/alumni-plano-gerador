Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

In my view, we should centralize purchasing.
I think it is the best option.

Which words GIVE an opinion, and which words ANSWER an opinion?

UseFormExample
Give an opinionI think... / In my view... / In my opinion...In my view, local teams are faster.
AgreeI agree (with you). / Good point.I agree with you about speed.
Disagree, politelyI don't agree. / I'm not sure.I'm not sure. The price is important too.
ReactReally?Really? That's interesting.
Ask the groupWhat do you think?What do you think, Rodrigo?
CarefulAGREE is a verb: "I am agree" is wrong, "I agree" is right. And one starter is enough: not "In my opinion, I think...".
