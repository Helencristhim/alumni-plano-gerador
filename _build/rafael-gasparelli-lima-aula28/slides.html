Chapter 3: Linking Ideas

Connect the Numbers

Words for a reason, a result and a contrast

Grammar Discovery

Result or Contrast?

It rained a lot in March. As a result, exports dropped.

Exports dropped in March. However, they went up again in April.

Although the weather was bad, we recovered quickly.

We expect more growth, so we need more containers.

Which blue words show a result? Which red words show a contrast?

WordJobExample
soa result, in the middle of a sentenceSales went up, so we hired people.
As a result,a result, at the start of a new sentenceIt rained. As a result, exports dropped.
However,a contrast, at the start of a new sentenceMarch was bad. However, April was good.
Although + sentencea contrast inside one sentence (no “but”)Although it rained, we recovered.

Result = so / as a result. Contrast = however / although. Never although ... but.
