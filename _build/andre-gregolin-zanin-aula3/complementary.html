Series
Suits — Season 1, Episode 1

A first day inside a job where nobody sleeps and everybody is already three hours ahead of you. The dialogue is fast, American and full of exactly the shapes you worked on today — people saying what they have already done and what has not happened yet, at speed.

Tip: watch ten minutes with English subtitles and write down every sentence with already, just or yet. You will get more than you expect.

Talk
Matt Walker — “Sleep is your superpower” (TED)

Nineteen minutes on what the two ends of the candle actually cost, from the researcher who wrote the book on it. Worth it for the content alone this year, and the English is clear, slow and full of numbers you can hold on to.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Then say his main argument out loud in three sentences, using in the long run and a trade-off.

BBC Learning English — “Using adverbs of frequency to describe your daily routine”

A few minutes on the other half of today lesson: the routine itself, and where always, usually and never actually sit in the sentence. Short, spoken, and free.
