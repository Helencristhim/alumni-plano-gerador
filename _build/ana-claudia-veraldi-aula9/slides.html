Chapter 3: The Code

Gonna. Wanna. Gotta.

What English does when nobody is being careful

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am gonna call you tonight."

"Do you wanna see the workshop?"

"Sorry, I gotta go."

"It is kind of an old house."

In the green ones, two words became one and a whole word disappeared. Which word? And what happens to the blue one at full speed?

WrittenSaid at full speedExample
going togonna — the "to" is goneI am gonna call you.
want towanna — only before a verbDo you wanna see it?
have got to / got togotta — the "to" is already insideI gotta go.
kind of / sort of / a lot ofkinda / sorta / a lottaIt is kinda old.
LinkingA final consonant jumps onto the next vowelan old house → "a nold house"
The flap TA T between two vowels sounds like a fast Dwater → "wader" · a lot of → "a lodda"

The rule in one line: English is spoken in groups, not in separate words. You say these too — you just write the long version.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I gonna call you tomorrow."
"I am gonna call you tomorrow."
"I wanna a coffee."
"I want a coffee."
"I gotta to leave now."
"I gotta leave now."

A reduction replaces two words, never three. Gonna still needs be in front of it, wanna only works before a verb, and the to is already inside gotta.

Expand each one out loud, then click to check

Detail

Where the Words Disappear

Listen for the join, then say it joined

"It is an old house." → sounds like "i-tsa-nold house"

"Give it a try." → sounds like "gi-vi-da try"

"What about it?" → sounds like "wha-da-bou-dit"

"I need a little water." → sounds like "a li-dle wader"

There is no silence between words. Your ear is looking for gaps that are not there.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

A Call with Rick

He is American. He is fast. And that is fine.

Dialogue

Sorry, I Did Not Catch That

R
Hey Ana, so I am gonna send you the photos tonight, and then we can kinda figure out what you wanna do with that old cabinet.
A
You lost me for a second. Could you say that again, a little more slowly?
R
Sure. Photos tonight. Decision tomorrow. I know I mumble — my wife says nobody can keep up with me.
A
It is not only you. When people speak fast I only get the gist, so I ask them to rephrase it. Nobody follows anything word for word.
R
That is smart. Most people just nod and hope for the best. My sister lived in Chile for a year and she did exactly what you are doing.
A
Good. Then say the fast version one more time, and I will tell you what I caught.

Survival Card

Five Phrases for a Fast Speaker

Self-Assessment

What I Can Do Now

I recognize gonna, wanna, gotta and kinda when I hear them.
I know that words are linked together, so I stop looking for gaps.
I can ask someone to slow down or rephrase without freezing.
I aim for the gist first, and I do not expect every word.
I can say the fast version myself, not only understand it.
