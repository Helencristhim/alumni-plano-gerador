Lesson 9 -- Understanding Fast English

Video
Tim’s Pronunciation Workshop: Consonant to vowel linking -- BBC Learning English (2 min)

Machine number one from tonight, in two minutes: what happens to a word ending in a consonant when the next word starts with a vowel. Short, and it is the single mechanism behind most of what disappeared in Todd’s voice note.

Tip: watch it once, then go back to any thirty seconds of English and count the links you can hear.

Radio
Wait Wait... Don’t Tell Me! -- NPR, March 29, 2025 (with Eric Idle)

A live news quiz recorded in Chicago, which is exactly where Todd is from. Four Americans joking over each other in front of an audience, at the speed people actually use when nobody is being careful. Free, no account needed.

Tip: this is not for understanding every word. Play five minutes and try only to catch what the joke is about.

Video
Jacques Pépin Makes A Perfect Omelet -- Jacques Pépin Foundation (5 min)

A French chef explaining something in English, at home, with no script. The other half of tonight: a strong accent and almost no reductions, which is why he is easier to follow than a native speaker at full speed.

Tip: watch this one straight after the NPR quiz. The difference you feel is the whole lesson.
