Lesson 9 -- Understanding Fast English

Pronunciation
How to Pronounce 'Gonna' and 'Wanna' — Rachel's English (American)

Four minutes on the two reductions that account for a large slice of everything you lose on an American call. She shows the mouth, slows the audio down, and then plays it at real speed so you hear what you have been missing.

Tip: watch it once, then go back to any American recording you have and count how many times gonna appears. It will be more than you expect.

Podcast
How I Built This — NPR (free, full episodes)

Long interviews with people telling the story of something they built, at the speed Americans actually talk to each other. No teaching, no slowing down, and every reduction from tonight, live.

Tip: this is the fast version. Listen to five minutes with no transcript, then open the transcript on the NPR page and read what you heard. That order, never the reverse.

Pronunciation
Tim's Pronunciation Workshop: Assimilation of /t/ and /p/ — BBC Learning English

The British version of the same phenomenon, in three minutes. Sounds do not just disappear — they change into their neighbours. Once you have seen it named you cannot unhear it.

Tip: compare this with the American video above. The rules are not identical, and knowing which accent you are hearing is half of understanding it.
