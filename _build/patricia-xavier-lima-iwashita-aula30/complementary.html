Lesson 30 -- By the End of the Year

Film
Moneyball (2011)

The manager of a baseball team with a small budget decides to plan the season with data and forecasts instead of opinions. Everybody says it will not work. Connection to Lesson 30: a story about a plan with numbers, checkpoints and a stretch goal — and about presenting it to people who do not believe in it yet.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Pause when the team talks about the season and say where they will be by the end of the year, with will have.

Podcast
BBC Learning English Grammar -- "Future perfect"

A short BBC episode about the future perfect: how to talk about things that will be finished before a moment in the future, with by and by then. Connection to Lesson 30: the grammar of every deadline in your account plan.

Tip: listen twice. The second time, write three sentences about your own year with by + date and will have + past participle.

YouTube
"English Class: Future perfect and future continuous" -- BBC Learning English

A BBC teacher compares the two forms that you used today: will have done for a finished action and will be doing for an action in progress, with clear timelines. Connection to Lesson 30: a fast review before you present your own twelve-month plan.

Tip: pause at each timeline and make one sentence about one of your accounts with the same form.
