Chapter 4: Language Focus

Will Have Moved, Will Be Training

future perfect — will have + past participle · future continuous — will be + -ing

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"By February, we will have moved the twelve stores."

"By then, every manager will have received a login."

"In March, our team will be training the store managers."

"This time next year, we will be preparing the next renewal."

Make two groups. Which green words describe something FINISHED before a date, and which describe something IN PROGRESS at a moment in the future?

StructureUseExample
will have + past participleFinished before a moment in the futureBy February, we will have moved the stores.
by + date / by then / by the end ofThe deadline that goes with will haveby March · by then · by the end of the year
will be + -ingIn progress at a moment in the futureIn March, we will be training the managers.
this time next year / in MarchThe moment that goes with will be + -ingThis time next year, we will be preparing the renewal.
won't have + past participleNot finished by that dateBy May, we won't have finished phase three.

Never the base verb after will have: not "will have move" → "will have moved". Never forget be: not "we will training" → "we will be training". And by means "not later than", not "until".

The Three Sentences of an Account Plan

Deadline, Activity, Horizon

"By the end of February, we will have moved all twelve stores."

1 — what will be finished: will have + past participle

"In March, our team will be training your store managers."

2 — what will be in progress: will be + -ing

"This time next year, we will be preparing the renewal with real numbers."

3 — the horizon: this time next year

Chapter 5: The Planning Call

The Planning Call

Daniel has questions about the calendar

Dialogue

Can We Do All This?

D
Patrícia, I read the account plan. It is ambitious. Can we really do all this in one fiscal year?
P
I think so. The kickoff is in January, and by the end of February we will have moved the twelve stores.
D
And in March? My store managers are very busy in March.
P
I know. In March our team will be training them in small groups, one hour a week, not more.
D
Good. What about the go-live of the app? My CEO asks me about it every week.
P
The app goes live in May. By then, every manager will have received a login, and we will be sending you weekly reports.
D
And the twenty new stores? That is the part that worries me most.
P
That is our stretch goal. If the openings are ahead of schedule, we will add a checkpoint in September.
Comprehension

About Daniel

1. What does Daniel think about the account plan?
It is ambitious; he asks if they can really do it all in one fiscal year.
2. Why is March a difficult month for Daniel's store managers?
They are very busy in March.
3. Who asks Daniel about the app every week?
His CEO.

Owner: Patrícia Xavier Lima Iwashita · Stores today: 40 · Forecast: 60

Phase 1 · January to March

Kickoff in January. By the end of February, the 12 stores in the new region will have moved. March: training for store managers.

Phase 2 · April to June

Go-live of the mobile app in May. Checkpoint before the finance budget review in June.

Phase 3 · July to December

Stretch goal: 20 new stores. By December, 60 stores will have joined the platform.

Forty today; the forecast is sixty.
2. Why is the checkpoint in phase two so important?

Sixty stores will have joined the platform.
