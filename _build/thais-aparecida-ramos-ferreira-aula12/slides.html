Chapter 3: How, How Long, How Far

One Word, Three Questions

How do I go? · How long does it take? · How far is it?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"How do I get to the museum?"

"How long does the train take?"

"How does your husband go to work?"

"How far is the tower from here?"

QuestionYou ask aboutAnswer
How + do / doesthe way: bus, train or feetBy train. / You can walk.
How long + does it take?the minutesIt takes twenty minutes.
How far + is it?the distanceIt is two kilometers. / It is near.
by + transportby bus · by train · by taxi — but with your feet it is on foot, or simply I walk.

The trap is in the middle one: how long takes does, but how far takes is. Time is something the train DOES; distance is something the place IS.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"How I get to the station?"

✓ RIGHT

"How do I get to the station?"

✗ WRONG

"How long takes the bus?"

✓ RIGHT

"How long does the bus take?"

✗ WRONG

"How far does the park?"

✓ RIGHT

"How far is the park?"

Portuguese says Quanto tempo demora? with nothing in the middle. English puts does in and keeps the verb at the end. And distance is never a thing you DO — it is a thing that IS.

1. How do you go from the hotel to the Louvre?
By train. You walk to Bastille, take line one, and change at Chatelet.
2. How long does the whole trip take?
Twenty-eight minutes: six on foot, nine on line one and five on line seven.
3. Where do you change trains?
At Chatelet. You leave line one and take line seven.

Dialogue

Bastille Station, 9:00 a.m.

P
Good morning. Do you need help with the map?
T
Good morning. Yes, please. How do I get to the Louvre?
P
By train. Take line one, and change at Chatelet.
T
Line one, and I change at Chatelet. How many stops is that?
P
Four stops, then two more on line seven. It is very simple.
T
And how long does it take?
P
About twenty-eight minutes, with the walk to the platform.
T
Perfect. And the tower? How far is it from the Louvre?
P
It is four kilometers. You can take the bus, number sixty-nine.
T
And can we walk? My daughter wants to see the streets.
P
Yes, you can. It is fifty minutes on foot, and it is a beautiful walk.
Comprehension

What Did Pierre Say?

1. How do you get to the Louvre?
By train. Line one, and you change at Chatelet for line seven.
2. How long does the trip take?
About twenty-eight minutes, with the walk to the platform.
3. How far is the tower from the Louvre, and how can you go?
Four kilometers. You can take bus sixty-nine, or walk fifty minutes.

0:000:00

Chatelet. You can change to line four, line seven and line eleven.
2. How long does it take from Chatelet to the last station?
Fourteen minutes.
3. What does the voice ask people with big bags to do?
To stay near the door and let the other people go out first.
