Real Speed

Long Words, One Strong Beat

Every long word has one strong syllable. Put it in the right place and the word arrives. Listen, then say it back.

The reconciliation is done.
The transition period runs until 2033.
Compliance is our main risk this year.
The consolidated numbers arrive on Friday.

The 3-Minute Update

Open, Run, Land

Your real topic. Three minutes. Then three questions. Then again, better.

Open (30 s)
One sentence of context, then the ask.
Three points (2 min)
Say when you move from one to the next.
Land (30 s)
The takeaway and the ask again. Stop.

Under Pressure

Two Directors, Two Styles

The quarterly global call. Priya wants the number in dollars. Julien wants to know why it is happening now. Both interrupt, and you have three minutes.

  • Round 1: you present. Answer each interruption in one sentence, then come back to your structure.
  • Round 2: you chair the meeting. The speaker runs over time. You stop him politely and ask for the takeaway.
