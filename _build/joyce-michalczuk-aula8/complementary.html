TED Talk
Nancy Duarte: “The secret structure of great talks” (18 min)

A presentation designer shows the shape that the best talks share: what is, what could be, and the move between them. A useful map for Julien’s idea of context before the ask.

Tip: after watching, draw the shape of your real presentation on paper: where is the context, where is the ask? Show it to your teacher.

TEDx Talk
David JP Phillips: “How to avoid death by PowerPoint” (TEDxStockholmSalon)

A Swedish presentation expert on why audiences stop listening when you read your slides, and five simple rules for slides that help the speaker. Priya’s point, with examples.

Tip: open your real slide deck after the talk. Apply one rule to one slide, and tell your teacher which one you changed.

Pronunciation
Rachel’s English: “English Pronunciation: Stressed Syllables within a Word”

How American speakers make one syllable long, high and clear, and the others short and quiet. The reason reconciliation and compliance arrive, or do not arrive, on a global call.

Tip: take the five longest words from your real slide deck. Say each one with her method, and record them.
