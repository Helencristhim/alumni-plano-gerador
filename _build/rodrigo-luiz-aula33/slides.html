Chapter 4: The Call

Claire Is Calling

three messages in one phone call

Dialogue

Three Messages, One Call

C
Hi Rodrigo, it's Claire. Are you available for a minute?
R
Sure, Claire. I'm available now.
C
Tom called you this morning. Can you call him back today?
R
Yes, I saw a missed call from him. I can call him back after lunch.
C
Thanks. And Ana left a message for me. She wants your process document.
R
No problem. I'll send it to her this afternoon.
C
Great. And the badges for us, for the visit?
R
They are ready. I'll give them to you at the entrance.
C
Perfect. If you have a question, text me.
R
I will. Thanks, Claire. Talk to you soon.
Comprehension

About Claire

1. Who does Claire want Rodrigo to call back?
Tom.
2. What does Ana want, in Claire's message?
Rodrigo's process document.
3. How does Claire want Rodrigo to contact her with a question?
By text: "text me".
