Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Emily in Paris — Season 1, Episode 1, “Emily in Paris”

An American who moves abroad for a year and gets every piece of culture shock on the first day. Light, fast, and full of the language of settling in.

Tip: every time Emily makes a mistake, say what she would have done if she had known, in one sentence.

See the episode on TVmaze ↗
Video
Utah State University — “Study Abroad Culture Shock & Reverse Culture Shock” (YouTube)

Real students talking about the culture shock of going abroad and the surprise of coming home. Natural, unscripted American English at normal speed.

Tip: choose one student and report what they said in three sentences, with at least one conditional.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie — “The danger of a single story” (TED)

A novelist on what happens when we know only one story about a place or a people. Essential before you live in another country, and beautifully clear English.

Tip: pause after her story about her American roommate and say what the roommate would have thought if she had known more.
