Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I always wake up early."

"I sometimes eat a snack before the game."

"He never drinks soda."

"I am always busy on school days."

In three sentences the frequency word is in the same place. In one it moves. Which one, and why?

WordHow muchExample
always100% — every single time"I always wake up early."
usuallyabout 80% — almost every time"I usually have a big breakfast."
oftenabout 60% — many times"I often go to the gym."
sometimesabout 30% — from time to time"I sometimes play until late."
never0% — not one time"He never drinks soda."
The placeBEFORE the verb: "I always wake up", "he never drinks".
The exceptionWith am / is / are it goes AFTER: "I am always busy", never "I always am busy".

And one more thing: never is already negative. "I never drink soda" — not "I don't never drink".

The Habits Talk

P
Bruno, one minute. How many hours do you sleep?
B
Six, usually. I always wake up at five thirty for school.
P
Six is not enough. And do you drink water during practice?
B
Sometimes. I often forget it, because I am always busy.
P
Busy is not a reason. A tired body never plays well. What about the gym?
B
I go often, but I sometimes play video games until late. That is my problem.
P
Good. You know your problem. Change one habit, not five.
B
OK. I never go to bed after midnight. From today.
Comprehension

About the Coach

1. What does she say about six hours of sleep?
That it is not enough.
2. What does she say about being busy?
That busy is not a reason, because a tired body never plays well.
3. What is her advice at the end?
To change one habit, not five.
4. What does she say when he names his problem?
She says "Good. You know your problem."
