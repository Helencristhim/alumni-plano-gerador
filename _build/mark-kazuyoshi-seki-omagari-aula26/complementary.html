Lesson 26 -- The Director Insisted That...

Series
FIFA Uncovered -- Episode 1 ("Money Ball")

Four hours of officials confirming, denying, admitting and refusing, about the organization behind the game you play. Every minute of it is this lesson: the narrator almost never writes "said", because each person in the room is either defending themselves or accusing somebody else, and the verb has to say which.

Tip: watch the first episode with English subtitles and write down ten reporting verbs the narrator uses. Next to each one, write who was under pressure -- the speaker or the person being reported.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English -- How are influencers affecting politics?

Six minutes in which two presenters report what researchers claim, what platforms deny and what critics accuse influencers of doing. It is a whole episode of secondhand information, which is exactly why the reporting verbs are doing all the work.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Listen once without it, then read it and underline every verb that introduces somebody else's words. Any verb that is not "said" is telling you the presenter's opinion.
