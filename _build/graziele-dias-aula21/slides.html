Chapter 3: The Order Code

Show the Order

One step, one sentence

Discover the Pattern

What's the Pattern?

First, the patient arrives at reception.

Then, the nurse checks the patient.

Next, the doctor asks for an exam.

After that, the doctor reads the result.

Finally, the patient goes home.

What do the orange words show?

Order wordWhen to use it
First,the first step
Then, / Next, / After that,the steps in the middle
Finally,the last step

Order word + comma + one sentence: "Then, the nurse checks the patient."

Chapter 4: The Patient Journey

"Step by Step"

Graziele explains the process to Dr. Carter

Dialogue

Graziele Explains

G
Welcome back, Doctor Carter. Today I explain our patient process, step by step.
C
Great. In Toronto, our process has six steps. How many steps do you have?
G
We have five steps. First, the patient arrives at reception for the admission.
C
I see. And what happens then?
G
Then, the nurse checks the patient. Next, the doctor asks for an exam.
C
Is the laboratory in the same building?
G
Yes, it is on the second floor. After that, the doctor reads the result.
C
In my hospital, the results take two days. And the last step?
G
Finally, the patient goes home. We call this the discharge.
C
Very clear, thank you. Could you send me the process by email?
Comprehension
