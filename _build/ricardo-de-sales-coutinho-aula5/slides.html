Chapter 3: She Studies, He Works

One Extra Letter

she · he · and an -s at the end

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I work at a bank."

"My wife works in São Paulo."

"My daughter studies at a school."

"She speaks English at school."

WhoVerbExample
I / you / we / theyno changeI work, we travel
he / she / it / my wife+ sshe works, he speaks
Verb ending in -y+ iesstudy → she studies

One other person, one extra letter. That is the whole rule.

Who It Belongs To

My, His, Her

my

from me

"my wife, my daughter"

his

from a man

"Paul and his son"

her

from a woman

"Diane and her office"

The word follows the OWNER, not the thing.
