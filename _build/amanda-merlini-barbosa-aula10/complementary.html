Film
The Big Short (2015)

A film about the 2008 financial crisis that stops, again and again, to explain a complicated idea in simple words. It is a lesson in the difference between a short answer and a complete one.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time a character explains something to the camera, count the links: therefore, whereas, although, on the other hand.

Podcast
Learning English Grammar (BBC) -- Linking devices of contrast

Six minutes from BBC Learning English on although, whereas and the other ways of putting two contrasting ideas together, with the punctuation that goes with each one.

Tip: write down every example they give, then say it again about your own work, changing only the content.

Video
Chris Anderson: TED's Secret to Great Public Speaking

The head of TED on building one idea in the listener's mind, piece by piece, where every piece connects to the one before. It is the long answer, explained as a method.

Tip: after watching, summarize his method in ninety seconds using position, reason, evidence, caveat and conclusion.
