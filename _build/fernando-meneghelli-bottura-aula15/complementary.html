Talk
"How to stay calm when you know you will be stressed" -- Daniel Levitin, TED

The closing item of the programme, and the one to watch again the night before any real interview. Connection to Lesson 15: the card next to your keyboard is exactly the pre-mortem he describes.

Tip: watch it the evening before an interview, not the morning of. Then read your card once and put it away.

Real Interview
HARDtalk -- Akinwumi Adesina, African Development Bank

A full interview to use as a mirror now that the programme is over: cold start, hostile blocks, and a closing line that travels. Connection to Lesson 15: listen for the sentence he wants repeated about him.

Tip: write down the one sentence you would repeat about him if somebody asked you what you made of him. Then write the sentence you want people to repeat about you.

Grammar Video
"Linking words of contrast" -- BBC Learning English, English Class

Keep this one for the weeks after the programme: it is the structure that holds an answer together when a question is uncomfortable. Connection to Lesson 15: concede, contrast, stop is still the shape of every hard answer.

Tip: once a week, say three sentences about your own results using although, whereas and having said that. Three minutes is enough to keep it alive.
