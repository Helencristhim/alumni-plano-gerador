Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I think it's beautiful."

"I don't think it's interesting."

"I agree. It's amazing."

"What do you think?" — "Me too."

Look at the blue words. Which ones give an opinion? Which ones answer a friend?

ExampleWhen?
My opinionI think it's beautiful.you say what you think
Not my opinionI don't think it's interesting.the NO goes on think
AgreeI agree. / Me too. / I think so too.your friend is right
Don't agreeI don't agree. / I don't think so.you think different
Ask backWhat do you think? / What is your opinion?the conversation goes on
The trapI agree — never I am agree.

I think + a full sentence: I think it's amazing — never I think is amazing.

The Sound

How It Sounds

"I think it's amazing."

TH in think: the tongue between the teeth — not TINK, not FINK

"I agree."

the stress goes at the end: a-GREE

"I don't think so."

one block, fast: I-don't-THINK-so

"What do you think?"

the question that keeps the conversation alive
