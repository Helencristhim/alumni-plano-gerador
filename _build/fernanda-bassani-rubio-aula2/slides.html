Chapter 4: The Code

Do You? Does She?

Questions and how often

Grammar Discovery

Four Questions From the Team

"Do you visit your customers every month?"

"Does Priya send the sales report on Friday?"

"I usually follow up the next day."

"How often do you place an order?"

Look at the red words. Questions start with Do or Does. With he and she, it is does — and the blue verb has no -s. In sentence 3, usually comes before the verb.

JobFormExample
Question: I / you / we / theyDo + subject + verb?Do you visit your customers?
Question: he / she / itDoes + subject + verb (no s)?Does Priya send the report?
Short answersYes, I do. / No, she doesn’t.Does he place an order every week? No, he doesn’t.
How often?How often + do / does + subject + verb?How often do you follow up?
Frequency wordsalways · usually · often · sometimes · never + verbI usually follow up the next day.
The trapthe frequency word goes after beNot I always am late. Say I am always on time.

In one line: Do or Does starts the question; the frequency word goes before the verb, but after am, is and are.

Listening 1 · The Sales Coordinator

Priya’s Week

A sales coordinator in India talks about her normal week. Sound first — no text.

1. How many meetings does Priya have on Monday?
Two: one with her team and one with the global team.
2. Why does she often follow up by phone?
Because many customers do not read long emails.
3. When does she send the sales report?
On Friday. She never sends it late.
Listening 2 · The Customer

How Julien Buys

A purchasing manager at a car parts company in France talks about his orders. Sound first — no text.

1. What does Julien’s company make?
Car seats.
2. How often does he place an order?
Usually every two weeks, on Tuesday.
3. What does he do with a quote that arrives after the deadline?
He does not read it.
