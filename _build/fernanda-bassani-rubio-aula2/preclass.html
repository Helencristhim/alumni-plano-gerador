Lesson 02 -- Pre-class

A Week in the Sales Team -- How Often, and When

Every team has a rhythm: a meeting on Monday, a report on Friday. Key words: a sales report, a forecast, a quote, to place an order, to follow up, a customer visit, a deadline, a weekly meeting. Structure: questions with do and does, and frequency words.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A sales report -- a document that shows how much a team sold
"I send the sales report to my director every Friday."
A forecast -- what you think will happen, for example next month’s sales
"We update the forecast at the end of every month."
A quote -- a document with the price you offer to a customer
"The customer wants a quote by Wednesday."
To place an order -- to ask a supplier to send products
"This car maker places an order every two weeks."
To follow up -- to contact someone again after a meeting or a message
"I always follow up after a customer visit."
A customer visit -- a meeting at the customer’s office or factory
"I usually have one customer visit a month."
A deadline -- the last day or time to finish something
"The deadline for the forecast is Thursday."
A weekly meeting -- a meeting that happens once every week
"Our weekly meeting is on Monday at nine."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Priya works in India. Does she have a weekly meeting? Yes, she does. It is always on Monday. She usually prepares a quote on Tuesday, and she often follows up by phone.

Julien is a customer in France. How often does he place an order? Every two weeks. He never reads a quote after the deadline.

And Fernanda? She sometimes has a customer visit, and she always sends the sales report on Friday.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Questions With Do and Does + Frequency Words

GRAMMAR

Start the question with Do (I, you, we, they) or Does (he, she, it). Put always, usually, often, sometimes and never before the verb.

JobFormExample
Question: I / you / we / theyDo + subject + verb?Do you visit your customers?
Question: he / she / itDoes + subject + verb (no s)?Does Priya send the report?
Short answersYes, I do. / No, she doesn’t.Does he place an order every week? No, he doesn’t.
How often?How often + do / does + subject + verb?How often do you follow up?
Frequency wordsalways · usually · often · sometimes · never + verbI usually follow up the next day.
The trapthe frequency word goes after beNot I always am late. Say I am always on time.

Never: Do she send the report? · I follow always up · How often you visit? · Does he places an order?
