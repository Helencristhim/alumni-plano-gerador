Podcast
NPR Short Wave -- "Six common sunscreen mistakes"

Exactly the content of this lesson, said on air by American dermatologists to an audience that is not medical: how much to apply, how often, what a cloudy day really costs, and why the number on the bottle matters less than the habit. Full transcript on the same page.

Tip: write down each of the six mistakes as a conditional sentence -- three in the zero conditional (always true) and three in the first (what will happen to you). Say all six out loud.

Thaler & Sunstein -- "Nudge: Improving Decisions About Health, Wealth and Happiness" (audiobook preview)

The book behind every prevention campaign that works: why telling people the truth changes nothing, and what has to be added so that it does. The second half of the message you practised today -- one action small enough to do now -- is this book’s whole argument.

Tip: listen to the preview and note every example where a small change in wording changed behaviour. Then rewrite one of your own recommendations the same way.

Music
The Beatles -- "Here Comes The Sun"

Three minutes of the clearest sung English there is, and a lyric that runs on the exact structure of this lesson: little darling, it’s been a long cold lonely winter -- and then the repeated present that says what is always true. Every word is common and every ending is audible.

Tip: read the lyrics and find every present-tense verb describing something that always happens. Then write two sun-safety sentences in the same shape -- if this, then that.
