Chapter 3: Can and Can't

One Word for Yes, One Word for No

you can · you can't · can I...?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"You can use the gym all night."

"You can't smoke in the room."

"Can I have a late checkout?"

"I can swim, but I can't dive."

UseFormExample
It is OK (rules)can + verbYou can use the pool until ten.
It is not OKcan't + verbYou can't smoke in the room.
You know howcan / can't + verbI can swim. I can't dive.
QuestionCan + person + verb?Can I use the gym? — Yes, you can. / No, you can't.
EverybodyThe SAME form: I can, he can, she can, they can. No S, no TO: never "he cans", never "can to swim".

Can is the same for everybody, and the verb after it is always the simple one.

The Sound

Can or Can't? Listen to the Vowel

"I can swim."

CAN is short and weak: "I k'n SWIM" — the stress goes on the verb

"I can't dive."

CAN'T is long and strong: "I KÉNT DIVE" — both words are strong

"Yes, you can."

at the end of a sentence, CAN is strong again: KÉN
