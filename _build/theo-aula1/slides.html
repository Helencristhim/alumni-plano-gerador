The Replay

Look at the highlighted words

We played six matches last night.
I practiced for an hour.
We won four rounds.
He beat me three times.

It already happened → past simple

Most verbs just add -ed. Some verbs change completely — you learn those one by one.

The Code

Two groups

Regular — add -ed

play → played
score → scored
practice → practiced

Irregular — new form

win → won
lose → lost
beat → beat (same word!)

Question and negative use did — and the verb goes back to normal: Did you play? / We didn't win.

Sound Check

-ed has three sounds

played, scored

sounds like d — no extra syllable

practiced, watched

sounds like t — no extra syllable

wanted, started

this one does add a syllable: -id

Common Mistake

One past per sentence

Did you played with Nina?
Did you play with Nina?

did is already the past. The verb after it stays normal.

Practice

Fix the verb

Last night we (play) six matches.
Last night we played six matches.
My team (win) the first round.
My team won the first round.
I (not / practice) yesterday.
I didn't practice yesterday.
(you / lose) the last match?
Did you lose the last match?
