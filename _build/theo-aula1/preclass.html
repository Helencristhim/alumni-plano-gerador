Lesson 01 — Pre-class

Game Night

Eight game words (match, round, teammate, score, beat, lose, skill, practice) and the past simple: played, won, lost, beat. Get ready to tell the story of a real game night.

Stage 1.1: Game Words

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Eight words, two minutes.

Match — a game between two teams or players
"We played six matches last night."
Round — one short part of a match
"We won the last round."
Teammate — a person who plays on your side
"My teammate scored twice."
Score — the number of points you have
"My score was the best of the week."
Beat — to win against someone
"He beat me three times."
Lose — to not win a game
"We lost the third match."
Skill — something you can do well
"Aiming is a skill. You learn it."
Practice — to do something many times to get better
"I practiced the new map for an hour."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the message. Then answer the questions.

Last night we played six matches. We won the first four, and my teammate scored in every round. Then we lost two in a row. The other team beat us nine to two. After that I practiced the new map for one hour.
How many matches did they lose?
A They lost four.
B They lost two.
What did the writer do after the matches?
A He practiced the new map.
B He watched a stream.
Which verb in the text is irregular?
A practiced
B won

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Past simple — it already happened

Most verbs add -ed. Some verbs change into a new form.

Regular

play → played
score → scored
practice → practiced

Irregular

win → won
lose → lost
beat → beat

+ We won four matches.
− We didn't win the last one. (after did, the verb goes back to normal)
? Did you play last night? (not "Did you played")

Stage 4: In the chat

Quiz
A friend asks how your night was. You played and you won. What do you say?
A I play and I win yesterday.
B We played four matches and we won three.
You want to ask your teammate about last night. Which question is correct?
A Did you play with your cousin?
B Did you played with your cousin?
Your team lost 9-2. How do you tell the story?
A They beated us nine to two.
B They beat us nine to two.

Stage 5: Your game night

Reflection
Think about the last time you really played — a game, a match, anything. What happened? How many matches? Did you win? What did you do after? Record 30 seconds in English, all in the past.
