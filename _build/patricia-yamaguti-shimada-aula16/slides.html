Chapter 3: Telling and Asking

The Short Way and the Polite Way

Stop here · Could you stop here?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Take me to this address, please."

"Stop here, please."

"Could you take me to this address?"

"Could you open the boot, please?"

FormHow it soundsWhen to use it
Verb + pleaseshort, direct — NOT rudeStop here, please. (in a taxi, everybody says this)
Could you + verb?softer, a favourCould you open the boot?
after could youthe verb is NAKED, no toCould you take me... (never to take)
no subject in the short formyou do NOT say youStop here. (never you stop here)

In a taxi the short form is normal and polite, because it is an instruction about the route. Could you is for favours: the boot, the window, waiting two minutes.

Comprehension

What Did Dave Say?

1. How long does he say the trip takes, and how much?
About forty-five minutes at that hour; about fifty-five pounds (it was fifty-two).
2. What does he ask her to do with the door?
Close it hard, because it does not lock.
3. What does he say about the bus?
Number 12, on the corner, going to the centre every ten minutes.

Change at Central Station and take the blue line, direction west.

Because tickets are checked on this train, until the end of the journey.

Role-Play — Guided

From the Airport to the Hotel

Scenario: You get into a taxi with your family and three bags. Give the address, ask the price, ask him to open the boot, and tell him where to stop.

Keywords:

Take me to this addressHow much is it to...?Could you open the boot?Stop here, please
Role-Play — Semi-free

Bus or Metro?

Scenario: You are at the station and you do not know which one to take. Ask a member of the staff: which bus, which line, how long, and how much.

Keywords:

Which bus goes to...?How long does it take?How much is the fare?Where is the stop?
Role-Play — Free

He Is Going the Wrong Way

Scenario: You are in the taxi and you recognise nothing. The hotel is on Oxford Street and you are going away from it. Say something, check the address, and get the car turned around.

No keywords this time. Two minutes, no stopping. Different from the model is not wrong — if they understood you, it worked.
