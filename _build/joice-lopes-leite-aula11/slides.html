Chapter 3: The Shape

Four Lines, in Order

who · what · one number · what you want

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"My name is Joice, and I am from São Paulo."

"I work in education and technology. I also visit schools."

"For example, we work with four hundred schools."

"I want to improve my English this year."

LineWhat goes thereWords from
1. Who you arename, city, countryLesson 1
2. What you dowork, evaluate, teach + alsoLessons 2 and 4
3. One numberfor example + how manyLesson 10
4. What you wantI want to / I would like toLesson 9
Key ideaFour sentences are enough. Nobody asks for more in the first round, and a short answer said SLOWLY sounds better than a long one said fast.

And if you have more time, line 2 grows: your background, your project, your team.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"Sorry, my English is very bad. My name is Joice."
"Hello. My name is Joice Lopes Leite."
"I am director of school since twenty-five years."
"I was a school director for twenty-five years."
"I work with schools and platforms and teachers and technology and data and..."
"I work in education and technology. I also visit schools."

You never have to apologize for your English. Say the four sentences slowly, and the room waits for you.

Dialogue

It Is Your Turn

N
Hello, Joice. It is your turn. Tell us about yourself.
J
My name is Joice Lopes Leite, and I am from São Paulo, in Brazil.
N
Nice to meet you. And what do you do?
J
I work in education and technology. I evaluate platforms, and I also visit schools.
N
Interesting. How many schools do you work with?
J
For example, this year we work with four hundred public schools.
N
That is a big number. And what is your background?
J
I was a school director for twenty-five years, so my background is in schools.
N
Wonderful. And what do you want this year?
J
I want to improve my English, and I would like to learn from everybody here.
