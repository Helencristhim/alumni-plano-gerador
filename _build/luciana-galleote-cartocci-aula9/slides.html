Chapter 3: The Code

As Soon As, Once, Before

One structure for every next step

Grammar Discovery

Four Next Steps

"As soon as the draft is ready, I will send it to you."

"Once we receive it, our lawyers will review every clause."

"We will send the purchase order when both sides sign off."

"Before we roll out the first phase, I will introduce you to Marcos."

Each sentence has two future actions, connected by a red time word. Look at the blue verbs: one of them is in the present, even though it is about the future. Which one, and why?

JobFormExample
Immediately afteras soon as + presentAs soon as it is ready, I will send it.
After something is completeonce / after + presentOnce we receive it, we will review it.
At that momentwhen + presentWe will send the PO when both sides sign off.
Earlier thanbefore + presentBefore we roll out, I will introduce you.
Up to that momentuntil + presentI will send updates until the project is complete.
Not later than a datewill + verb + by + timeWe will finish it by the end of July.
The trapno will after the time wordNot as soon as it will be ready. Say as soon as it is ready.

In one line: time word + present, then will for the other action; never will after as soon as, once, when, before or until.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

The Next Steps, at Real Speed

One call, three recordings, one plan

Dialogue

The Call After the Yes

R
Luciana, good news. Our board has approved the deal, and we are ready to move forward.
L
That is great news, Ryan. Thank you. What are the next steps on your side?
R
Our legal team is preparing the draft of the contract. As soon as it is ready, I will send it to you.
L
Perfect. Once we receive it, our lawyers will review every clause. It usually takes about a week.
R
That works for us. And when both sides sign off on the contract, we will send the purchase order.
L
Great. As soon as we get the purchase order, we will kick off the project with a meeting in São Paulo.
R
Sounds good. Who will be our point of contact before the project starts?
L
Our project manager, Marcos. I will introduce you before we roll out the first phase.
Comprehension

About Ryan

1. What has Ryan’s board done?
It has approved the deal.
2. When will Ryan send the draft of the contract?
As soon as his legal team finishes it.
3. When will Ryan’s company send the purchase order?
When both sides sign off on the contract.
Listening 1 · Giulia, the Lawyer

Not Before the Signatures

Giulia, a contract lawyer in Milan, explains the four steps before a project can start. Sound first — no text.

1. When can a project start, according to Giulia?
Only when the signatures are on the contract, not before.
2. What happens after one side sends the draft?
The lawyers of the other side review it and send comments. It can take two or three rounds.
3. What is her advice?
Send the draft early and answer the comments quickly.
Listening 2 · Raj, the Project Manager

The Plan for the First Month

Raj, a project manager at a technology partner, explains the plan for a hospital rollout. Sound first — no text.

1. What will Raj do as soon as he receives the purchase order?
He will book the kick-off meeting.
2. What is the first milestone, and when will they finish it?
The main building, by the end of March.
3. How often will he send an update, and until when?
Every Friday, until the project is complete.
Listening 3 · Anna

A Door to Munich

Anna, from the European technology group, congratulates Luciana and has an idea for the next months. Sound first — no text.

1. Why is the first milestone very important, according to Anna?
Because the hospital will watch every detail: if it goes well, people will talk about Teleinfo.
2. What is her group preparing?
A similar project for two hospitals in Munich.
3. What does she need before she plans the visit?
The dates of the first milestone.

A one-page plan like this is what a partner wants after the yes. Every step depends on another step, and that is why the language of next steps is as soon as, once and when, always followed by the present.
