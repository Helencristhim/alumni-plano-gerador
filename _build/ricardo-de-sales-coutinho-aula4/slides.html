Reading

Santana Station Notice

Line 1 — Blue · Santana Station

Santana Station is on Line 1, Blue. The station opens at four thirty in the morning and closes at midnight.

To Vila Olímpia: take Line 1 from Santana to Paraíso. At Paraíso, change to Line 2, Green. At Pinheiros, take Line 9 to Vila Olímpia.

The trip is one hour and ten minutes from Santana to Vila Olímpia. The ticket is five reais. The traffic on the street is bad in the morning, but the train is not in the traffic.

Vila Olímpia Station is near the offices. It is five minutes on foot. Have a good trip.

Notice -- São Paulo Transit, Santana Station
