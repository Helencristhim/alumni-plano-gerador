Complementary Activities

Extra material to keep English running in your head between classes. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen. None of them takes more than twenty minutes.

Lesson 1 -- Who Is Stephanie? -- The English You Already Have

Talk
Eric Topol -- "The wireless future of medicine"

A cardiologist explains how data and technology are changing the way doctors work. It is exactly the bridge you live in every day, told in clear, slow English.

Tip: watch it once with English subtitles, then again without them. Write down three sentences he uses in the present simple to say what is always true about medicine.

Talk
Abraham Verghese -- "A doctor's touch"

A physician talks about what happens between a doctor and a patient in the room. Simple words, strong ideas, and the register you will need when you talk to patients in English.

Tip: stop the video after five minutes and say out loud, in your own words, what he has said so far.

Guided Study
TED-Ed -- "How does the immune system work?"

A short animated lesson with comprehension questions. Familiar content in unfamiliar words: the perfect place to practise listening without a dictionary.

Tip: answer the questions out loud, not in writing. Speaking a full answer is the exercise; reading it is not.
