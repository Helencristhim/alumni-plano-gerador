Chapter 3: The Code

Always True, or True Right Now

Two tenses, one decision you make in every sentence about your work

Discover the Rule

What Do These Have in Common?

I work in healthcare.
Right now I am working with data and artificial intelligence.
She bridges two teams every day.
This month we are testing a new system with the doctors.
Click to reveal the rule
The first and the third are always true — facts and routines. That is the present simple, and with he, she, it the verb takes an -s: she bridges. The second and the fourth are true right now, and temporary. That is the present continuous: am / is / are + verb-ing.
