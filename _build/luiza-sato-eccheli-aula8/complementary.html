Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
Computerphile — “Blindly Accepting Terms and Conditions?” (YouTube)

A short, clear conversation about why people accept terms without reading them and what that can cost. Good listening practice on today’s exact topic.

Tip: every time you hear a rule, say whether it is a duty, an option or a right.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Series
Silicon Valley — Season 1, Episode 1, “Minimum Viable Product”

A comedy about building a tech company, where contracts, equity and fine print decide everything. Fast American English and a lot of business and legal vocabulary.

Tip: when a contract or an offer appears, say what the character must, may or may not do.

See the episode on TVmaze ↗
Talk
Glenn Greenwald — “Why privacy matters” (TED)

A journalist and lawyer on why people who say they have nothing to hide still care about privacy. Useful for the data clauses in any terms of service.

Tip: summarize his main argument in three blocks, and say whether you agree and why.
