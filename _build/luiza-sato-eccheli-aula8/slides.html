It may offer a credit. A refund is offered only if the order never arrives.
2. Which change uses shall, and what does that mean for the user?
Disputes shall be resolved by arbitration, so arbitration is an obligation, not an option.

By continuing to use the app after 30 days. A user who does not agree may close the account before that date.
Chapter 4: The Code

Must, May, May Not

Four clauses from real-looking terms, and the one word that turns an option into a duty

Grammar Discovery

An Option, or a Duty?

"The user shall keep the password confidential."

"The company may change the delivery fee at any time."

"Users may not share their account with a third party."

"Customers are entitled to a refund if the order never arrives."

In each clause, who has a duty, who has a choice, and who has a right? Which words make it a duty, and which make it only an option? And what changes when you add not?

JobFormExample
A duty, in formal legal textsshall + base verbThe user shall keep the password confidential.
A duty, in everyday and legal Englishmust / is required to + base verbThe company must protect personal data.
A permission or an optionmay + base verbYou may cancel at any time.
A prohibitionmay not / shall not / is prohibited from + -ingUsers may not share their account.
A rightis entitled to + noun / verbCustomers are entitled to a refund.
No dutyis not required to / does not have toYou are not required to give your phone number.
The trapmust not ≠ does not have toYou must not share it (forbidden). You do not have to share it (optional).

In one line: Shall and must create duties, may creates options, may not forbids, and must not is never the same as do not have to.

Quick Fire

Listening 1 · A Consumer Lawyer

Three Clauses Worth Reading

A consumer lawyer on how to read terms of service in five minutes. Sound first — no text.

1. Why does the lawyer say she would never ask you to read all the terms?
Because nobody is going to read forty pages before ordering dinner.
2. What are the three words or ideas she searches for, and what does each clause tell you?
Liable: what the company will not pay for. Dispute or arbitration: where you have to go if something goes wrong. Share or third parties: who else receives your data.
3. What can a court decide about a clause that says the company is never responsible?
That it is void.
Listening 2 · An App Notice

We Have Updated Our Terms

An app notice read aloud. Sound first — no text.

1. What may a user receive if an order is more than forty-five minutes late, and when is a full refund given?
A credit for the next order. A full refund only if the order never arrives.
2. What will the app share, and what does it promise it will not do?
Some personal data with payment and delivery partners. It shall not sell data to advertisers.
3. What happens to disputes the support team cannot solve, and how does a user consent to the changes?
They are resolved by arbitration. A user consents by continuing to use the app after thirty days.

Survival Card

Five Sentences for Any Contract

The fine print is not decoration; it is the contract.

You must not share your account, but you do not have to give your phone number.

If the order never arrives, the customer is entitled to a refund.
