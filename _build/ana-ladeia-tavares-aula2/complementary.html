LegalEagle -- "Celebrities Sued for Posting Photos of Themselves"

An American lawyer walking through the cases where a famous person put a photograph of herself online and was sued by the photographer who took it. Fast, informal American English, and exactly the question of today's file: who owns the image, and what did the licence actually cover.

Tip: he speaks quickly, so use the subtitles the first time and turn them off the second. Count how many times he says "would have" and note what he is imagining each time.

TED -- Kirby Ferguson, "Embrace the remix"

Ten minutes arguing that nothing is original, built on music, film and Bob Dylan. It is the other side of the argument you defended today, and it is the debate that runs under every copyright dispute in the entertainment industry you are interested in.

Tip: watch it once and then decide whose side you are on. Write four sentences: two for him, two against him, and use at least one third conditional in each pair.
