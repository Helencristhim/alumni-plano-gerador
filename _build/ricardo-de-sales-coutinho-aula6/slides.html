Chapter 3: I Do Not, He Does Not

How to Say No

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I don't know the lyrics."

"My daughter doesn't like rock."

"The singer doesn't sing slowly."

WhoFormExample
I / you / we / theydon't + verbI don't like slow songs.
he / she / it / the singerdoesn't + verbHe doesn't like rock.
The -s of lesson 5It moves to doesn't: he likes → he doesn't like. Never "doesn't likes".

Only one word carries the -s. Never two.

The Scale

Love, Like, Don't Like

I love

a lot

"I love rock."

I like

yes, it is good

"I like this band."

I don't like

no

"I don't like slow songs."

"I don't like it" is a correct, normal, adult sentence. You are allowed to say it.

Reading

Music and English

Four steps, one song

Music is a good way to learn English. Many people learn their first English words in a song, not in a classroom.

Step one: listen to the song one time. Do not read anything. Just listen.

Step two: read the lyrics in English. Do not open the translator yet. Look for the words you know. There are always some.

Step three: open the translator, and read the lyrics again. You do not need to understand every word. Three new words is a good day.
