Chapter 3

The Passive Code

When the action matters more than the person

Grammar Discovery

Who Did It?

"A bug was found in the payment page on Friday."

"All the servers were restored by six p.m."

"The store data is backed up every night."

"The patch was approved by the security team."

What do the orange words have in common? Which verb comes first, and what form is the second verb?

UseFormExample
Routine, factsis / are + past participleThe data is backed up every night.
Finished past actionwas / were + past participleThe servers were restored at six.
Who did it (when it matters)... + by + the person or teamThe patch was approved by the security team.
Negative and questionwas not + participle / Was ... + participle?No data was lost. Was the patch tested?

Use the passive when the action or the result is more important than the person. Irregular verbs use the third form: find → found, write → written, send → sent.

Grammar Practice

Make It Passive

Say it first, then click to check

"Somebody found a bug on Friday." → "A bug ___was found on Friday."
"The QA team tested the patch." → "The patch ___was tested by the QA team."
"We back up the data every night." → "The data ___is backed up every night."
"They restored the servers at six." → "The servers ___were restored at six."
"Carlos wrote the fix on Saturday." → "The fix ___was written on Saturday."
Style

Active or Passive?

Passive (the report, the system)

A bug was detected.
The patch was released.
No data was lost.

Active (the person matters)

Carlos found the bug.
My team released the patch.
Thank you for your help.

"The bug was detected at nine, and the patch was approved by the security team at noon."

Chapter 4

The Patch Call

Greg and Daniela check the facts for the report

Dialogue

Checking the Facts

G
Hi Daniela. Your report goes to the US directors tomorrow, so let us check the facts. When was the bug found?
D
On Friday night. It was detected by the monitoring tool at about nine.
G
Good. And the patch? The directors will ask who said yes to it.
D
It was tested by the QA team on Sunday morning, and it was approved by the security team at noon.
G
Perfect. Was any customer data lost? That is always their first question.
D
No. No data was lost. All the servers were restored by six p.m.
G
Great. One more thing: the Brazilian stores use the same system. Is the patch installed there yet?
D
Not yet. The task was assigned to my team this morning. It is planned for Wednesday night.
Comprehension

About Greg

Tomorrow.
2. According to Greg, what is always the directors' first question?
Whether any customer data was lost.
3. Why does Greg ask about the Brazilian stores?
Because they use the same system, so they need the patch too.

Real Document

The Incident Report

INCIDENT REPORT — IR-1107
System: Checkout — payment page (US stores)
Prepared by: Daniela Feitoza, IT Manager, SPF Group
Status: Resolved (US) · Planned (Brazil)

Fri 9:00 p.m. — A bug was detected in the payment page by the monitoring tool.

Sat — The fix was written and the patch was prepared.

Sun 10:00 a.m. — The patch was tested by the QA team.

Sun 12:00 p.m. — The patch was approved by the security team.

Sun 6:00 p.m. — All servers were restored. No customer data was lost.

Next step: The patch is scheduled for the Brazilian stores on Wednesday night. The task is assigned to the SPF Brazil IT team.

1. How long did it take from the bug to the restored servers?
From Friday at 9 p.m. to Sunday at 6 p.m. — about two days.
2. Which team is responsible for the next step?
The SPF Brazil IT team. The task is assigned to them.
3. Find two passive sentences: one in the past and one in the present.
Past: "The patch was tested by the QA team." Present: "The task is assigned to the SPF Brazil IT team."

Detective

Spot the Error

0 / 4 errors found

"The bug detected on Friday night."
"The bug was detected on Friday night." (passive = be + participle)
"The fix was wrote on Saturday."
"The fix was written on Saturday." (write – wrote – written)
"All the servers was restored by six."
"All the servers were restored by six." (plural = were)
"The patch was approved for the security team."
"The patch was approved by the security team." (the agent = by, not for)
