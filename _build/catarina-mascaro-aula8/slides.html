Did You Catch It?

About Bia

1. Why was leaving at seven terrible for her?
Because she had only slept four hours.
2. What did she really think of the museum?
The first part was boring, but the second part was amazing.
3. She did not know the word "shakes". What did she do instead of stopping?
She described it: "when the floor moves under you" — and Sam gave her the word.
Listening 1

Someone thinking out loud

1. What is she trying to decide?
Which club to join — music or film.
2. What is the real reason she is hesitating?
The music club is on the same day as her cousin’s house.
3. What word did she not know, and how did she get around it?
"Clash" — she said "when two things are on the same day".
Listening 2

What changes when you stop translating

1. What does he say is the slowest way to speak?
Building the sentence in your own language first and then translating it.
2. What does he tell students to do when a word is missing?
Describe it instead of stopping — say what it does or what it is like.
3. What does he say about mistakes?
A sentence with a mistake still works. Silence does not.
