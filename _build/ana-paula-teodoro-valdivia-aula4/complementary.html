Lesson 4 -- Risk and Possibility

Documentary
Downfall: The Case Against Boeing (2022)

Engineers who saw a real risk, and a company that heard it as a remote scenario. The whole film is a case study in what happens when likelihood, impact and mitigation are not said clearly to the people who sign. American English, one hour and a half.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down two sentences the engineers use to describe risk. Which conditional would you have used in their place?

Podcast
TED Radio Hour (NPR) -- "Risk"

Speakers who work with risk for a living explain how they separate what is likely from what is merely possible, and how they say it out loud. One of them studied professional gamblers and found that the best decisions come from people who are comfortable putting a number on the likelihood.

Tip: the transcript is on the page. Listen first without it, then open it and mark every "if... will" and every "if... would".

Listen on NPR ↗
YouTube
BBC Learning English -- "A review of conditionals" (English Class)

A short review of the conditionals side by side, so you can hear the difference between the real one and the hypothetical one in the same lesson. That contrast is exactly what carries the likelihood in a risk call.

Tip: after the video, take three risks from a contract you are reading and say one sentence about each -- two with "if... will" and one with "if... would".
