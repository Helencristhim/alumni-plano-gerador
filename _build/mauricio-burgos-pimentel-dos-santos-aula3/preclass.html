Lesson 03 — Pre-class

What We Are Doing Now

Eight words for an operation (restructure, oversee, coordinate, workflow, priority, deadline, quarter, headcount) and the difference between what you always do and what you are doing this quarter. Around 30 minutes, in one sitting.

Stage 1.1: Words for an Operation

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say each word out loud with your own numbers. Eight words, three minutes.

Restructure — to change how a team or a company is organised
"We are restructuring the commercial desk."
Oversee — to watch over work and make sure it is done well
"I oversee eight people and their accounts."
Coordinate — to make different people or areas work together
"I coordinate with risk and with legal."
Workflow — the order in which work moves from one person to the next
"We are changing the workflow this quarter."
Priority — the thing that has to come first
"Right now, the budget is the priority."
Deadline — the day when something has to be finished
"The deadline is the end of the month."
Quarter — a period of three months in the business year
"This quarter is harder than the last one."
Headcount — the number of people a team is allowed to have
"We are asking for more headcount."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the text. Then answer the questions.

Normally my team handles around forty deals a month. I oversee the commercial side and I coordinate with risk and with legal every week. That is the normal picture. This quarter, though, nothing is normal. We are restructuring the desk, so the workflow is changing every ten days. We are running two systems at the same time and it is costing us a day a week. We are not getting extra headcount this year, and the deadline does not move. So the priority right now is simple: finish the migration and close the budget with the same eight people.
How many deals does the team normally handle a month?
A Forty.
B Ten.
Why is it "we are restructuring" and not "we restructure"?
A Because it is happening this quarter, not every quarter.
B Because it is a big change.
What is the priority right now?
A Hiring more people.
B Finishing the migration and closing the budget.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Two presents, two jobs

Always true → present simple. True this quarter, not forever → am / is / are + verb-ing.

The job

I oversee the desk.
We handle 1,200 clients.
I report to the head.

The quarter

We are restructuring it.
We are migrating the system.
I am preparing for November.

+ We are running two systems at the moment.
− We are not getting more headcount this year.
? Are you hiring? — not "Do you hiring?"

In speech nobody says it long: we’re restructuring · I’m preparing · they’re not hiring.

Stage 4: In the room

Quiz
Someone asks what your team does in general. Which answer fits?
A "We are handling 1,200 clients."
B "We handle 1,200 clients."
You want to ask about their current projects. Which question is correct?
A "Are you working on anything new?"
B "Do you working on anything new?"
Which update sounds like a professional and not a complaint?
A "It is a disaster and nobody is helping."
B "We are at thirty-one deals against forty, and we are freezing new requests until the migration is done."

Stage 5: Your operation in one minute

Reflection
Record one minute in English. Explain your operation to somebody who has never worked in a bank: what your team normally does, what is different this quarter, what the priority is, and what the deadline is. Use at least three words from Stage 1.1 and one sentence with am/is/are + -ing. Finish with a question for your teacher.
