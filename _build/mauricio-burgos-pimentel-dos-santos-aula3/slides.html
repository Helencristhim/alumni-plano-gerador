The Pattern

Both are present. What is different?

I oversee eight people. — Right now I am covering for two of them.
We handle corporate clients. — This quarter we are restructuring the desk.
I report to the commercial head. — At the moment I am reporting to the board.
We close the budget every year. — We are not closing it on time this year.

Temporary → am / is / are + verb-ing

The left side is the job description — always true. The right side is what is on your desk right now, this quarter, this week. Two presents, two jobs.

Did You Catch It?

About David’s operation

1. What is the normal order of a deal on his desk?
The client asks, they price it, risk approves, and legal signs.
2. What is different right now, and what does it change?
They are restructuring the desk, so the workflow changes every ten days.
3. Why is the restructuring a problem for him?
They are not getting more headcount, and the deadline does not move.
Listening 1

A team update, in one minute

1. What is different about this quarter?
They are running two systems at the same time because they are migrating the old one.
2. Where are they against target, and what is not moving?
Thirty-one deals, below target — and the quarter deadline does not move.
3. What two things are they doing about it?
Freezing all new requests until the migration finishes, and asking for two temporary people.
Listening 2

Introducing a colleague to the group

1. What does Marta do, and how many people does she oversee?
She runs operations for a logistics company in Lisbon — about sixty people across three warehouses.
2. What is she doing this year?
They are moving everything to one single system, and she is holding that together.
3. What is she asked to tell the group about?
The workflow she is building.
