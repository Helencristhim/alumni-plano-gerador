Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto em voz alta, devagar, e depois responda as perguntas.

Last October, Bruno was in Paris for the Motor Show. It was his first time in France.

On Saturday, the weather was rainy. The halls were very crowded, and the new cars were amazing.

On Sunday, he was tired. The croissants were delicious, but they weren't cheap: they were expensive. Was it a good trip? Yes, it was.

1. Como estava o tempo no sábado? (How was the weather on Saturday?)
A Ensolarado e quente, perfeito para passear. (Sunny and hot.)
B Chuvoso. (Rainy.)
C Com neve. (Snowy.)
2. Como se diz "Os pavilhões estavam lotados" em inglês?
A "The halls were crowded."
B "The halls was crowded with many people from the world."
C "The halls are crowded last October."
3. Por que o texto diz "they were" e não "they was"? (Why "were"?)
A Porque é uma pergunta sobre os croissants de Paris. (Because it is a question.)
B Porque é presente. (Because it is the present.)
C Porque com they (plural) o passado é were. (With they, the past is were.)

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Was / were (passado do to be)

GRAMMAR

O verbo to be no passado tem duas formas: was (com I, he, she, it) e were (com you, we, they). Serve para "foi", "era" e "estava". / The past of to be: was (I, he, she, it) and were (you, we, they).

Uso / UseEstrutura / PatternExemplo / Example
I / he / she / itwasI was in Paris. (Eu estava em Paris.)
You / we / theywereThe halls were crowded. (Os pavilhões estavam lotados.)
Negativa / Negativewasn't / weren'tIt wasn't cheap. (Não foi barato.)
Pergunta / QuestionWas...? / Were...?Was it a good trip? (Foi uma boa viagem?)
Ideia-chave / Key ideaCom "last year", "yesterday" ou "last October", use o passado: "Paris is amazing last year" está errado; o certo é "Paris was amazing". / With last year or yesterday, use was / were.

Etapa 4: Quiz Situacional

Quiz

Escolha a melhor resposta para cada situação. Errou? Tente de novo.

Um amigo pergunta: "How was Spain?" Você adorou. Você diz:
A "It was amazing."
B "Spain is amazing last year, the best country for me."
C "It were amazing."
Você quer dizer que os restaurantes eram caros. Você diz:
A "The restaurants was expensive in the center of the city."
B "The restaurants were expensive."
C "The restaurants are expensive yesterday."
Você quer perguntar se o seu amigo estava cansado depois da viagem. Você diz:
A "You were tired after the long trip to Europe?"
B "Was you tired?"
C "Were you tired after the trip?"
O hotel não era caro. Você diz:
A "The hotel wasn't expensive."
B "The hotel no was expensive for one night."
C "The hotel weren't expensive."
