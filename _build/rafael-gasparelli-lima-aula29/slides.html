Chapter 3: How Long?

From Then Until Now

Things that started in the past and are still true

Grammar Discovery

For or Since?

I have worked at Picunha for ten years.

We have worked with Harbor Fresh since 2025.

Claire has been the head buyer since 2023.

How long have you known Daniel? I have known him for three years.

Which words give a length of time? Which words give a starting point?

FormStructureExample
for + lengthhave/has + participle + forI have worked here for ten years.
since + starthave/has + participle + sinceShe has been the buyer since 2023.
QuestionHow long + have/has + subject + participle?How long have you known Daniel?
Negativehaven’t/hasn’t + participleWe haven’t seen Claire since May.

for ten years, six months, a long time · since 2025, March, my first job. Started in the past, still true now.

A Call From Chicago

Claire, a coach and Daniel talk about trust

Dialogue

Partners Since 2025

C
Rafael, your presentation was great. Our directors were very satisfied.
R
Thank you, Claire. That’s good news. How long have you worked at Harbor Fresh?
C
I’ve worked there for eight years. And I’ve been the head buyer since 2023.
R
And our companies have worked together since 2025, right?
C
Yes. And you have always kept your promises. You’re a reliable partner.
R
Trust is very important for us. We try to be honest, even with bad news.
C
I know. Remember the rain in March? You called me the same day. That was important to me.
R
We want a long-standing relationship, not just one contract.
C
Me too. Our customers are loyal, and your mangoes always meet their expectations.
R
Great. Then let’s talk about the 2027 contract next week.
