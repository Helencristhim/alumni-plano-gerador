Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Present Perfect with For and Since

GRAMMAR

Use the Present Perfect for something that started in the past and is still true now. Use for + a length of time. Use since + a starting point.

FormStructureExample
Affirmative + forhave/has + participle + for + lengthI have worked here for ten years.
Affirmative + sincehave/has + participle + since + startShe has been the buyer since 2023.
Negativehaven’t/hasn’t + participleWe haven’t seen Claire since May.
QuestionHow long + have/has + subject + participle?How long have you known Daniel?

Never: I work here for ten years (use I have worked) · since twenty years (use for twenty years) · How long do you work here? (use How long have you worked here?)
