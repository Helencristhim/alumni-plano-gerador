Lesson 24 -- At an International Congress

Talk
Kio Stark -- "Why you should talk to strangers" (TED)

A short, warm talk about the small conversations we have with people we do not know, and why they matter. The perfect mindset for a coffee break at a congress.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then write three icebreakers you could use at your next congress, as indirect questions.

Talk
Susan Cain -- "The power of introverts" (TED)

Not everybody loves a crowded reception. Susan Cain explains how quieter people can build strong relationships in their own way — one good conversation at a time.

Tip: note one idea that helps you feel calmer at networking events, and tell it in English in one minute.

Watch on TED →
Talk
Amy Cuddy -- "Your body language may shape who you are" (TED)

A famous talk about how posture and body language change how others see us, and how we feel. Useful before you walk into a reception full of people you do not know.

Tip: after watching, record your 30-second networking introduction twice and listen to which one sounds more confident.
