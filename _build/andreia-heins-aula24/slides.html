The Coffee Break

Warm-up

The Target

Three Moves of Networking

1. Start a conversation with a polite question.

2. Find something in common and exchange contacts.

3. Reach out after the congress and keep in touch.

Diagnostic

Next to a Stranger

You are in the coffee line at a congress in Orlando. The person next to you has a name badge from a hospital in Chicago. Start a conversation and ask two questions.

Chapter 4: The Code

Direct or Polite?

Indirect questions

Grammar Discovery

What Changes in a Polite Question?

Where is the main hall? → "Do you know where the main hall is?"

What time does it start? → "Could you tell me what time it starts?"

Which hospital do you work at? → "Could you tell me which hospital you work at?"

Do you work with robots? → "I was wondering if you work with robots too."

What happens to the word order? Where did do / does go? When do we use if?

StartThenExample
Do you know ...?question word + subject + verbDo you know where the hall is?
Could you tell me ...?no do / does / didCould you tell me what time it starts?
I was wondering ...if + subject + verb (yes / no)I was wondering if you work with robots.
Do you know if ...?if for yes / no questionsDo you know if the keynote has started?
Why use it?It sounds softer and friendlier with people you do not know.

Polite question = normal sentence order. No do, does or did. Yes / no questions use if.

Grammar Practice

Make It Polite

Say it first, then click to check

"Do you know where the coffee ___is?" (Where is the coffee?)
"Could you tell me when the session ___ends?" (When does the session end?)
"I was wondering ___if you have a minute." (Do you have a minute?)
"Do you know who ___is speaking after lunch?" (Who is speaking after lunch?)
"Could you tell me how long you ___have worked there?" (How long have you worked there?)
"Do you know ___if the reception is on Thursday?" (Is the reception on Thursday?)

At nine thirty, in the main hall.

They need it to enter every session.
3. When and where is the networking reception?
On Thursday evening, from six to eight, on the terrace.
Listening 2 · Reaching Out

A Message to Dr. Chen

After the congress, Andreia reaches out to a new contact. Sound first — no text.

1. Where did they meet?
At the coffee break on Thursday, near the Medtronic booth.
2. What does Andreia ask Dr. Chen to send?
The article about her simulation program.
3. What will Andreia do today?
Send an email with her contact details.
