What You Did

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"We stayed seven nights."

"I visited New York in 2016."

"My wife loved the show."

TodayIn the pastHow
walk · stay · visitwalked · stayed · visitedverb + ed
like · loveliked · lovedverb with e + d
I · you · he · she · we · theywalkedthe same for everyone

No -s in the past: he walked, she visited.

Three Sounds

/t/, /d/ or /id/?

"We walked a lot."

/t/ — walked, watched, liked

"It rained on Tuesday."

/d/ — rained, stayed, loved

"We visited a museum."

/id/ — visited, wanted, waited

"My wife wanted a photo."

/id/ — after t or d, one more syllable

Reading

The Diary

New York — My Diary, April 2016

Monday: We arrived in New York at night. Our hotel was near Times Square. We stayed there for seven nights.

Tuesday: It rained in the morning, so we visited a museum: the Met. In the afternoon, we walked in Central Park.

Wednesday: My wife wanted a photo on the Brooklyn Bridge. We walked on the bridge for an hour. It was amazing.

Thursday: We watched a show on Broadway, The Lion King. I liked the music. My wife loved it.

Sunday: We waited for the taxi to the airport. Seven days, many photos, and very tired feet!

Ricardo's travel diary -- New York, 2016
