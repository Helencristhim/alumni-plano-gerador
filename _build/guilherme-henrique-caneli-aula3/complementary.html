Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Nancy Duarte — “The secret structure of great talks” (TED)

A presentation designer shows the shape that great speeches share: the constant movement between what is and what could be. It is the rhetorical pivot, drawn as a map.

Tip: after watching, draw the shape of your last opening. Where was your pivot?

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
Freakonomics Radio — “If Everyone Hates Meetings, Why Do We Have So Many of Them?”

Why so many meetings fail, and what the good ones do differently in the first minutes. Everything that applies to a meeting applies twice to a panel.

Tip: listen for one idea you could use in the first two minutes of your next panel.

Listen on Freakonomics ↗
Talk
Chris Anderson — “TED’s secret to great public speaking” (TED)

The head of TED on the one thing every great talk has in common: a single idea, built carefully in the audience’s mind. Seven minutes, and a perfect model of a clean opening.

Tip: listen to his first thirty seconds twice. Note what he does not say.
