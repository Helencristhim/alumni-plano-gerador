Lesson 33 -- International Travel: The Missed Connection

Film
The Terminal (2004)

A man arrives at JFK airport in New York and cannot enter the country or go home, so he lives in the terminal. Many scenes happen at airline desks and with officers who ask him questions and give him instructions.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. After a scene with an officer, pause and report it: He asked him if... He told him to...

Film
Up in the Air (2009)

George Clooney plays a businessman who flies almost every day of the year. Airports, layovers, hotels and upgrades are part of his life, and the English is clear and professional.

Tip: write down three travel words from the film and use each one in a sentence about one of your own business trips.

TED Talk
Pico Iyer: Where is home?

A travel writer who spends much of his life in airports talks about home, travel and moving between countries. Calm pace and very clear English.

Tip: after the talk, tell a friend what he said, in two sentences: He said that... He asked us if...
