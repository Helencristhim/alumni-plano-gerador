Chapter 3: The Report Code

She Asked, She Told Me To

How to report questions and instructions

Grammar Discovery

What the Agent Said

("Do you have a checked bag?") → "She asked me if I had a checked bag."

("Where is your final destination?") → "She asked me where my final destination was."

("Go to gate D12.") → "She told me to go to gate D12."

("Do not leave the gate area.") → "She told me not to leave the gate area."

Look at the orange words. Where did the question marks go? What comes after told me?

The agent saysFormYou report
A yes/no questionasked me if + subject + past verbShe asked me if I had a bag.
A where / when / what questionasked me where + subject + past verb (no do / did)She asked me where I was going.
An instructiontold me to + base verbShe told me to go to gate D12.
A negative instructiontold me not to + base verbShe told me not to leave the area.
A polite requestasked me to + base verbShe asked me to wait at the desk.

In one line: a reported question has no question mark and no do / did. After told me, use to + verb.

Chapter 4: The Airport

Stuck in Miami

One call, two recordings, one document

Dialogue

A Call From Miami

C
Rafael, hi! Are you in Chicago already?
R
Not yet. My flight from Brazil was late, and I missed my connecting flight in Miami.
C
Oh no. What did the airline say?
R
The agent asked me if I had a checked bag. Then she told me to go to the desk at gate D12.
C
And did they rebook you?
R
Yes. She asked me where my final destination was, and she put me on the eight p.m. flight. I also got a meal voucher.
C
Good. Ask them about compensation too. And do not worry about tomorrow. I will move the signing to the afternoon.
R
Thank you, Claire. The agent told me not to leave the gate area, so I will send you a message when I land.
Comprehension

About Claire

1. Where does Claire think Rafael is at the start of the call?
She thinks he is already in Chicago.
2. What does Claire tell Rafael to ask the airline about?
She tells him to ask about compensation.
3. What will Claire do about tomorrow?
She will move the signing to the afternoon.
