Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Succession -- Season 1, Episode 1, "Celebration"

You told the consultant you watch this one. Watch the first twenty minutes again and listen for one thing only: how each character describes what somebody else does. Every one of those sentences carries the s you practised today.

Tip: write down four sentences where a character says what another character does. Then rewrite them about four people on your own team.

Watch on HBO Max ↗
Podcast
NPR Planet Money -- "The Birth And Death Of The Price Tag"

Twenty minutes on the thing you look after every day: how companies decide what to charge, and why the fixed price on a shelf is a recent invention. Clear American English, and a story rather than a lecture.

Tip: the page has the full transcript. Listen first with the transcript closed, then open it and check only the parts you missed.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
Harvard Business Review -- "The Explainer: Don’t Just Sell Stuff, Satisfy Needs"

Two minutes, animated, on the question behind every campaign you approve: what the customer is actually buying. Useful for you because the argument is made in the same words you use in a briefing, and slowly enough to follow without subtitles.

Tip: it is short enough to watch three times. On the third pass, pause after each sentence and say it back out loud, keeping the s on every he and she.
