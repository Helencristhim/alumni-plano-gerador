Lesson 9 -- What Did They Actually Say?

Talk
Julian Treasure -- "5 ways to listen better" (TED, 8 min)

Eight minutes on the half of this lesson that happens before you repeat anything: hearing what was actually said instead of what you expected. His last technique — receive, appreciate, summarise, ask — is precisely the move you practised when you read the sentence back to the person.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down his four-step acronym. Use the summarise step once this week, out loud, in a real call.

Podcast
NPR Life Kit -- "How to use classic negotiation tactics in everyday life"

Listen to this one for the language, not the tactics: the guests spend the whole episode repeating what somebody else said, and every one of those sentences is the structure you worked on today. The transcript on the page lets you count them.

Tip: read the transcript and highlight every sentence that starts with he said, she told me or they asked. Note what happened to the verb in each one.

Series
The Diplomat -- Season 1, Episode 1 (Netflix)

An entire series about the job you did today: somebody says something in one room, and somebody else has to repeat it in another without changing it. Half the dialogue is reported speech, at high speed, with the caveat carrying the whole scene. American and British English, side by side.

Tip: watch the first episode with English subtitles. Find one moment where a character repeats what somebody said and deliberately changes the strength of it, and note what happens next.
