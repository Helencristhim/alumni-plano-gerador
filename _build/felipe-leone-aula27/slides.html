Not the Look-Alike

These words look right, but in English the meaning changes. Say the sentence with the English word.

#Not thisSay this
1We realized an analysis of the client.We carried out an analysis of the client.
2The client pretends to pay in May.The client intends to pay in May.
3The data is actual.The data is up to date.
4We have a lot of informations.We have a lot of information.
5There is a lot of space to grow.There is a lot of room to grow.
