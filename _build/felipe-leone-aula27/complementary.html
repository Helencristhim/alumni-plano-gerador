Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Hans Rosling: “The best stats you’ve ever seen” (20 min)

A doctor and statistician shows how to explain data in plain language, with energy and humor.

Tip: choose one chart he explains and explain it again in 30 seconds, as in class.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Kenneth Cukier: “Big data is better data” (16 min)

A journalist explains in simple words how data helps us predict what people and machines will do.

Tip: write down every form of predict and analyze he uses, and make one sentence about your job with each.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Tim Harford: “Trial, error and the God complex” (20 min)

An economist on why experts cannot predict everything, and why testing is better than guessing.

Tip: say in two sentences when a prediction at your work was wrong, and what you learned.
