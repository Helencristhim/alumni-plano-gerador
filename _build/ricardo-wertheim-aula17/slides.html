Chapter 3: The Code

Walk Along, Go Through

the imperative · along · across · through · past

Grammar Discovery

Along, Past or Through?

"Walk along the harbor, toward the old town."

"Go past the big hotel and the palm trees."

"Go through the gate and up the stairs."

"Turn left at the crossroads, and don't take the bridge."

Look at the orange words: is there a person before them? Now the blue words: what does each one tell you about the way you move?

FormRuleExample
verb, with no personthe plain verb: no you, no to, no mustWalk along the river. · Turn right.
Don't + verbthe negativeDon't take the bridge.
alongfollow the line of somethingalong the harbor · along the river
acrossfrom one side to the otheracross the square · across the road
throughinto one side and out of the otherthrough the gate · through the park
pastgo by it, and do not stoppast the hotel · past the market
turn ... atat for the point where you turnTurn left at the crossroads.
The trapNever go until the church (that is Portuguese). Say go to the church, or walk until you see the church: until needs a full sentence after it.

One verb, one small word, one place: walk along the harbor. That is a direction.

Chapter 4: Off the Ferry in Split

Excuse Me, Which Way?

A ferry terminal, a harbor and a small gate in the wall

Dialogue

Off the Ferry

R
Excuse me. How do I get to Diocletian's Palace? Am I heading in the right direction?
I
Yes, you are. Walk along the harbor, toward the old town. It's about ten minutes.
R
Along the harbor. Is there a shortcut?
I
Not really. Just stay by the sea. Go past the big hotel and the palm trees.
R
And then? I always get lost in old towns.
I
Look for a small gate in the wall, on your right. It's the Bronze Gate. You can't miss it.
R
Do I go through the gate?
I
Yes. Go through it and up the stairs. The Peristyle is right in front of you.
R
Perfect. And the cathedral? Is it far from there?
I
No, it's right next to the Peristyle. Follow the signposts for the bell tower.
Comprehension

What Did Ivana Tell Him?

1. How long is the walk to the palace, according to Ivana?
About ten minutes, along the harbor.
2. What does Ivana tell him to walk past?
The big hotel and the palm trees.
3. Where is the cathedral, and how can he find it?
Right next to the Peristyle. He can follow the signposts for the bell tower.
