Lesson 27 -- The Passive Voice in Action

TED Talk
Rose George: Inside the secret shipping industry

A journalist spends five weeks on a container ship and tells what happens behind the scenes of almost everything we buy. Full of processes described in the passive: what is loaded, where it is checked, how it is moved.

Tip: write down five passive sentences you hear, then say one about your own warehouse for each.

TED Talk
Tom Wujec: Got a wicked problem? First, tell me how you make toast

A designer asks people to draw how toast is made, and shows how teams describe a process. Short, visual and very useful for steps and systems.

Tip: after the talk, describe how an order is processed in your company in six steps, all in the passive.

TED Talk
Thomas Thwaites: How I built a toaster -- from scratch

A designer tries to build a simple toaster from raw materials and discovers how many steps, places and people are hidden behind one cheap product. A funny lesson in supply chains.

Tip: say how the metal, the plastic and the wire were made, using was and were + past participle.
