Chapter 3: The Code

Who Did It, or What Was Done?

When the action matters more than the person

Grammar Discovery

Who Does the Action?

"Every box is scanned at the door."

"The batch was dispatched at six this morning."

"The shipment has been cleared by customs."

"Forty trucks are loaded every night."

Who scans the boxes? Who loads the trucks? Look at the colored verbs: what do they have in common?

UseFormExample
A routine or a processam / is / are + past participleEvery box is scanned.
A finished eventwas / were + past participleThe batch was dispatched at six.
A result that matters nowhas / have been + past participleThe shipment has been cleared.
Who did it, only if it mattersby + agentIt was approved by the health agency.
The questionIs / Was / Has ... been + past participle?Has the batch been flagged?

In one line: the passive puts the object first. The tense is in BE, the meaning is in the past participle.

Chapter 4: The Cold Chain Tour

Fiona Visits the Warehouse

One tour, two recordings, one document

Dialogue

Behind the Shelf

F
So, Marcelo, what happens when a batch of vaccines arrives here?
M
First, every box is scanned at the door. Then it is stored in the cold room, at two to eight degrees.
F
And who checks the temperature? Is it done by hand?
M
Not anymore. The temperature is recorded every five minutes, and if anything changes, the pallet is flagged automatically.
F
What was the biggest bottleneck when you started here?
M
The hand-off to the carriers. Trucks were held up for hours at the gate. We have streamlined the process since then.
F
And if a medicine has a problem after it leaves the building?
M
Then a recall is started. Every box can be traced to the minute. Traceability is our safety net.
