Chapter 4: The Code

The Decision, Not the Person

The passive voice in three tenses

Grammar Discovery

Who Did It? Is That in the Sentence?

"The new rule was published in March."

"Tariffs are reviewed every year."

"The framework will be approved next month."

"The ruling was overturned by a higher court."

In three of the four sentences, we do not know who did the action, and it does not matter. The verbs in blue have two parts. Which part changes with the time, and which part stays the same?

JobFormExample
Present (a regular action)am / is / are + past participleTariffs are reviewed every year.
Past (a finished action)was / were + past participleThe rule was published in March.
Futurewill be + past participleThe framework will be approved next month.
Present perfecthas / have been + past participleThe contract has been amended twice.
When the agent matters... by + the agentIt was overturned by a higher court.
The trapthe verb be is never missingNot The rule published in March. Say The rule was published.

In one line: be in the right tense + past participle; add by only when who did it matters.

Chapter 5: Real Speed

The Notice, and Two Voices

One document, two recordings

Real Document

The Notice From the Agency

NATIONAL TRANSPORT AGENCY — PUBLIC NOTICE
Resolution on highway concessions · summary for the public
Draft preparedJanuary — by the technical team
Public consultationFebruary 1 to March 15 — 312 comments received
Text amendedApril — 41 changes
Final ruleWill be published on May 20
New requirementAnnual safety report for all concessions
EnforcementPenalties will be applied after 12 months
ContactComments on the transition can be sent by email
The notice does not say who wrote each comment or who made each change. It focuses on what was done and what will be done, which is exactly why regulatory texts are written in the passive voice.
1. When will the final rule be published?
On May 20.
2. Turn the line "Public consultation" into a full sentence in the passive.
"A public consultation was held from February 1 to March 15, and 312 comments were received."
3. When will penalties be applied?
After twelve months.
