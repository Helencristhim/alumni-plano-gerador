Lesson 7 -- Holding the Floor

Conference Talk
"How to disagree productively and find common ground" -- Julia Dhar, TED

A world debate champion explains how to disagree without turning it into a fight. Her first rule is the one you used today: say what you agree with before you say what you challenge.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down the exact phrase she uses to open a disagreement, and put it next to the one from today: 'May I raise one point? I say this with respect.'

Listening
"Debating veganism: How to change someone's opinion" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes about how people actually change their minds, at a speed built for learners. Full of the language of agreeing, challenging and giving evidence.

Tip: listen once with your eyes closed. Then listen again and write down every phrase that introduces an opinion or a disagreement. You should find at least four.

Conference Talk
"Dare to disagree" -- Margaret Heffernan, TED

Twelve minutes on why scientific progress needs somebody in the room who says no. She uses a real case from medicine, so the examples are your world, not a business metaphor.

Tip: after watching, say out loud in English one occasion when you did NOT disagree and should have. One minute. Then say what you would say today, with the three steps from this lesson.
