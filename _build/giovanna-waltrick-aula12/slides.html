Chapter 3: The Code

The Words Between the Sentences

Each one does a different job

Discover the Rule

What Job Do the Orange Words Do?

I prefer vintage because it lasts longer.
This jacket is versatile, so I wear it every week.
Trend-driven pieces are cheap, but they do not last.
I have a small budget. That's why every choice matters.
Quality is good value. For example, my coat is ten years old.
Click to reveal the rule
Each word does one job. because + the REASON. so + the RESULT, in the same sentence. but + the other side. That's why + the RESULT, at the start of a NEW sentence. For example + the proof. A strong opinion turn uses three of them in a row: I prefer vintage because it lasts. For example, my jacket is from 1998. That's why I rarely buy new. Careful: because and so are opposites in direction — It is cheap, so I bought it and I bought it because it is cheap say the same thing.

Chapter 4: The Debate Page

Reading

Trends or Personal Style?

The Debate Page: Trends or Personal Style?

Every month, the student newsletter asks two students the same question and prints both answers. This month the question is simple: what matters more, following trends or having your own style?

Marina, third year, Fashion Design. I would argue that trends matter more, because fashion is a conversation. If you ignore what is happening now, you are not part of that conversation. For example, my final project started with a trend I did not like at first. I studied it, and I found my own version of it. That's why I tell first-year students to follow trends closely, but not to copy them.

Lucas, second year, Administration. I see Marina's point, but I prefer personal style. A trend-driven wardrobe is expensive, so it is not a real choice for most students. The reason is simple: trends change every few weeks, and my budget does not. I have a few versatile pieces that I wear in many different ways. In my opinion, that is better value, and it is also more interesting to look at.

Which answer do you agree with? Send us your opinion in three sentences, with one reason and one example.
