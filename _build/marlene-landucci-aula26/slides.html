Chapter 4 -- Bigger Than

Language Discovery

bigger than / more peaceful than

Discovery

Listen and Notice

What do the orange words have in common?

"Brazil is bigger than Italy."

"Florence is smaller and quieter than São Paulo."

"The village is more peaceful than the city."

"Brazilian coffee is better than Italian coffee."

The Rule

Compare Two Things

AdjectiveRuleExample
Short: small, cheap, quiet+ -er thanFlorence is smaller than São Paulo.
Short, one vowel: big, hotdouble the last letter + -erBrazil is bigger than Italy.
Ends in -y: busy, sunnyy becomes -ierSiena is busier than the village.
Long: beautiful, peaceful, expensivemore + adjective + thanThe village is more peaceful than the city.
Irregular: good / badbetter / worseThe coffee here is better.

Key idea: use -er OR more, never both: say bigger, never "more bigger". And after the adjective, the word is than, not "that".

Comprehension

About Paul

1. What does Paul say about the market in Florence?
It is huge -- and he asks if it is bigger than the markets in Brazil.
2. What does Paul say about the streets of Florence?
He loves Florence, but the streets are very narrow.
3. Where does Paul want to go next week, and why?
To a village on the coast -- because it is quieter than the city.
4. What does Paul ask about the coffee?
If the coffee in Italy is better than Brazilian coffee.
