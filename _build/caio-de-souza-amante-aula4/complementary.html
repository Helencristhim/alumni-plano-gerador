Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
William Ury — “The walk from no to yes” (TED)

The co-author of the most used negotiation book in the world on the move this lesson is built around: what to do instead of saying no. Eighteen minutes, and the third idea in it is exactly the sentence you practised today.

Tip: watch once with subtitles and once without. Write down the moment he describes the third side, and say out loud what the third side would be in a Dataside negotiation.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Oxford Online English — “How to Negotiate in English”

A focused lesson on the exact phrases used to make conditional offers, push back and close in a business negotiation. Slower than the other recommendations, and the closest thing here to a rehearsal.

Tip: pause at every phrase and rebuild it with a real Dataside condition before you continue. Say it out loud -- reading it silently does nothing for this.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Podcast
In Good Company — Howard Marks, co-founder of Oaktree

One of the most careful thinkers in finance on how he decides what a deal is worth and when he walks away. His English is unhurried and precise, which makes it the easiest of these three to follow at full speed.

Tip: the first fifteen minutes are enough. Listen for every conditional he builds -- he almost never states a position without attaching a condition to it.
