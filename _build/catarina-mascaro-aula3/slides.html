The Code

Look at the highlighted words

I stayed home on Saturday.
She called me three times.
We met at the park.
I felt so tired on Sunday.

It already happened → past simple

Most verbs just add -ed. A few change into a new word — those you learn one by one. And the form is the SAME for everybody: no S here.

The Code

Two groups, one form

Regular — add -ed

stay → stayed
call → called
visit → visited
watch → watched
miss → missed

Irregular — new word

meet → met
feel → felt
have → had
go → went
see → saw

Same for everybody: I stayed, she stayed, they stayed. The S from last lesson is gone here.

yesterday last weekend last week two days ago on Saturday last night
The Code

Three sounds of -ed

sounds like T

watched
missed
talked

sounds like D

called
stayed
listened

extra syllable

visited
wanted
needed

Only after t or d does the -ed become a whole new syllable.
