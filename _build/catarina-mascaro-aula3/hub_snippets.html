Stage 1.1: Story Words

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Watch the past form next to each one.

Meet — to see a friend somewhere, or to see someone for the first time (past: met)
"I met my cousins at the park on Saturday."
Feel — to have something inside you: happy, tired, nervous (past: felt)
"I felt so tired on Sunday night."
Visit — to go and see a person or a place (past: visited)
"We visited my grandmother on Sunday."
Call — to talk to someone on the phone (past: called)
"She called me three times on Friday."
Stay home — to not go out at all (past: stayed)
"I stayed home all Saturday and I loved it."
Miss out — to lose the chance to do something fun
"I missed out on the party. Everyone went."
Have a great time — to enjoy something a lot (past: had)
"We had a great time at the concert."
A piece of cake — very easy — it is not really about cake
"The maths test? A piece of cake."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the message. Then answer the questions.

Last weekend was strange. On Friday I stayed home and watched two films. On Saturday my cousin called me at nine in the morning, so I woke up earlier than I wanted. We met at the mall at two and we had a great time. Then we visited my aunt, and I felt so tired that I fell asleep on her sofa. On Sunday everyone went to the beach and I missed out, because I did not wake up in time. The homework, at least, was a piece of cake.
Why did the writer wake up early on Saturday?
A Because her cousin called at nine.
B Because they went to the beach.
Why is it "did not wake up" and not "did not woke up"?
A Because "wake up" is irregular.
B Because "did" is already the past, so the verb goes back to normal.
What happened on Sunday?
A She went to the beach with everyone.
B She missed out, because she woke up too late.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Past simple — it happened, and it is over

Most verbs add -ed. A few become a new word. The form is the same for everybody — no S here.

Regular

stay → stayed
call → called
visit → visited
watch → watched

Irregular

meet → met
feel → felt
have → had
go → went

+ I met my friends on Saturday.
− I did not meet them on Sunday. (after did, the verb goes back to normal)
? Did you meet them? (not "Did you met")

When: yesterday · last weekend · last night · two days ago · on Saturday

The -ed has three sounds: watched (T) · called (D) · visited (a whole extra syllable).

Stage 4: In the conversation

Quiz
It is Monday. A friend asks about your weekend. Which answer works best?
A "Normal."
B "I stayed home on Saturday, and on Sunday I met my cousins. What about you?"
Everyone went to the party and you did not. What do you say?
A "I missed out."
B "I missed on."
Which question is correct?
A "Did you had a good time?"
B "Did you have a good time?"
