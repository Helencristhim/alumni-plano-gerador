Lesson 15 -- The More You Look

Podcast
"When a Poor Country Gets a Lot Richer" — Planet Money, NPR

The story of an economy that doubled overnight because of a change in how it was counted. Connection to Lesson 15: it is the case from today's first listening, told at length by the statistician who wrote the book about it. Transcript on the page.

Tip: fifteen minutes is enough for the first half. As you listen, decide at what point you would have started distrusting the figures — and whether you would have noticed at all.

Talk
"The Best Stats You've Ever Seen" — Hans Rosling, TED

The talk that changed how a generation looks at international comparison, from a statistician who was also, unexpectedly, a performer. Connection to Lesson 15: every claim he makes is a comparison with the yardstick attached, out loud. English transcript on the page.

Tip: watch it for pleasure the first time. The second time, note how often he says what is being measured BEFORE he says who is ahead. That order is the lesson.

Grammar Video
"The More, the Better — Comparative Correlatives" — Advanced Grammar

A focused lesson on the double comparative, including the cases where the second half inverts and the ones where a whole clause follows. Connection to Lesson 15: the grammar kept outside the class, as you asked.

Tip: afterwards, write five sentences of the form the more … the more about your own week. Saying them aloud is what fixes the second the, which is the part that disappears.
