Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Taiye Selasi: “Don’t ask where I’m from, ask where I’m a local” (16 min)

A writer with roots in two continents on why one country is not the right answer for people like her. You have an Italian passport and a Brazilian life: this is your question too.

Tip: answer her question out loud: where are you a local? Use if and would at least twice.

TED Talk
Dan Buettner: “How to live to be 100+” (20 min)

A journalist who studied the places where people live longest, including a village in Sardinia. Food, friends, rhythm: the lifestyle question from the lesson, with real data.

Tip: after the talk, say which of these places you would choose, and what you would change in your routine if you lived there.

TED Talk
Dan Gilbert: “The psychology of your future self” (7 min)

A short and funny talk about why we always think we will not change, and then we do. Useful before you decide where you would live in five years.

Tip: watch it twice. Then finish this sentence in three ways and record it: five years from now, I wish I…
