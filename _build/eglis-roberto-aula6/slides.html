Where Would You Live?

Italy or Brazil, the life you have and the life you could have, and the things you wish were different.

The Question

Anywhere in the World

A friend at dinner asks: “If you could live anywhere in the world for the next five years, where would you live, and why?” Money is not a problem. Answer, and ask your friend the same question.

The Conversation · You

Italy or Brazil?

1. You are an Italian citizen. If you lived in Italy, which city would you choose, and what would your week look like?
2. After three and a half months in Europe, you decided to live in Brazil. What would you miss most about Brazil if you left?
3. Is there anything about your life in Brazil that you wish were different?

Real Speed

I’d, Not I Would

Listen for the short forms, then say it back at the same speed.

If I lived there, I'd learn to cook.
I wouldn't change a thing.
If I were you, I'd stay.
I wish it were a bit cooler.
The Conversation · The World

Home and Away

1. Of all the places you have visited, which one would be the best for a retired person? Why?
2. What makes a foreigner feel at home in a new country: the language, the people or the food?
3. Some people say: travel is for visiting, home is for living. Do you agree?

The Offer

Would You Say Yes?

Each card is a real offer. One minute: what would you do, what would you lose, and what would make you say yes?

Offer 1
A free house in a village in Tuscany for one year. You cannot come back to Brazil during that year.
Offer 2
A paid job as crew on a sailing boat in the Caribbean, from November to April.
Offer 3
A small apartment in Lisbon, near the river, at half the normal price.
Offer 4
Stay in Brazil, and travel three months a year, all paid.

Language you can use

If I accepted, I would…I would say yes if…The problem is that I would lose…I wish I could…

Under Pressure

The Cousin from Italy

Round 1 is a video call with your Italian cousin, who thinks you should live in Italy. Round 2 is a coffee with a friend from abroad who is thinking of moving to Brazil.

  • Round 1: your cousin talks fast and has an answer for everything. You defend your choice.
  • Round 2: your friend wants to move to Brazil. You ask the questions and give advice.
