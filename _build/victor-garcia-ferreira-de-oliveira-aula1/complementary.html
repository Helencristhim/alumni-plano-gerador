Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TED — Julia Dhar, "How to disagree productively and find common ground" (14 min)

A world debate champion explaining what actually happens when two people argue well. It is the whole point of this lesson said by somebody who wins arguments for a living: a position, a reason, and a way of disagreeing that keeps the other person talking.

Tip: watch it once with English subtitles. Then write down the three questions she says a good disagreement needs, and try them on the person at home who disagrees with you most.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English — "What’s in a footballer’s brain?"

Six minutes on what happens inside the head of somebody who plays at a high level: reading the game, deciding faster, and what training actually changes. Free, with the full transcript and the vocabulary list on the same page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript and once with it. Then pick one fact from the episode you could use to back up an argument about sport, and say it out loud in a full sentence.

Video
TED on YouTube — Angela Lee Duckworth, "Grit: the power of passion and perseverance" (6 min)

A researcher arguing that what predicts success is not talent but staying with something for years. Short, clear, and full of the exact words this lesson gave you: commitment, evidence, and the long run.

Tip: she makes a claim and then backs it up with data, in six minutes. Count how many times she gives a number, and notice that she never once says believe me.
