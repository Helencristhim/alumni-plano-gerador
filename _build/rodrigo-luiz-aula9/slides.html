Chapter 3: The Pattern

Three Letters at the End

-ed · did · didn't

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

The guests arrived on Monday.
I showed them the two lines.
Did you show them the plant?
They didn't visit the quality lab.

What is at the end of the verb? And what happens to it after DID?

UseFormExample
Afirmativa / Affirmativeverbo + edThe guests arrived on Monday.
Mesma forma para todosed para I, you, he, we, theyHe showed · They showed — sem "s", sem excecao.
Pergunta / QuestionDid + verbo baseDid you show them the plant?
Negativa / Negativedidn't + verbo baseThey didn't visit the lab.
CuidadoDepois de did e didn't, o verbo PERDE o -ed: "did you showed" nao existe. O passado ja esta no DID.

Dialogue

How Was the Visit?

C
Hi Rodrigo. How was the visit last week?
R
It was very good. The guests arrived on Monday morning.
C
Good. Did you show them the production area?
R
Yes, I did. I showed them the two lines and the warehouse.
C
And did they visit the quality lab?
R
No, they didn't. We finished the tour at twelve, and the lab was closed.
C
I see. Did they ask a lot of questions?
R
Yes, they did. They asked about the delay from India.
C
And what did you answer?
R
I explained the reason, and I showed them the new plan. They liked it.
Comprehension

About the Visit

1. When did the guests arrive?
On Monday morning.
2. Did they visit the quality lab?
No, they didn't. The tour finished at twelve and the lab was closed.
3. What did they ask about?
They asked about the delay from India.
