Lesson 8 -- Fluency Under Pressure

Series
Formula 1: Drive to Survive -- any episode of Season 1

Team principals and drivers from a dozen countries answering hostile questions in English, live, seconds after something has gone wrong. Most of them are not native speakers, and none of them has a script. Watch what they do in the two seconds before they answer.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down every phrase someone uses to buy time or to postpone an answer. You will hear "bear with me" and "I will get back to you" in their natural habitat.

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) -- "7 common issues people face when speaking in public"

A speechwriter and a speech coach on what actually goes wrong when you have to speak and the pressure is on: the blank moment, the rush, the apology nobody asked for. Clear American English, about twenty minutes, and every point transfers straight to a call in a second language.

Tip: there is a full transcript on the page. Listen first with the transcript closed, then open it and mark the phrases the guests use to slow themselves down.

Listen on NPR ↗
YouTube
Derek Callan -- "28 Advanced Phrases For Meetings"

Twenty minutes of ready-made meeting phrases from a business English teacher, including the ones you practised today: asking someone to wait, checking what a person meant, and postponing an answer without losing authority. Slow, clear delivery, with every phrase on screen.

Tip: pause after each phrase and say it out loud once. Then pick the three you would actually use with your manager and add them to the note on your monitor.
