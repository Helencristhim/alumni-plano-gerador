Lesson 7 -- How It Actually Works

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Presentations"

Five minutes on explaining something to a room that did not build it: how to open, how to sequence, and when to stop. Two native speakers at natural speed, with the full script on the same page.

Tip: read the script and find every sequencer — first, then, once, after that. Then say your own five steps out loud using the same four words.

Talk
Nancy Duarte -- "The secret structure of great talks" (TEDxEast, 18 min)

How an explanation is built so that somebody who is not an expert can follow it, and can repeat it afterwards. The examples are famous speeches, but the structure is the one you need for a five-step description of a system.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then take your own explanation and check whether a listener could repeat step three to a colleague an hour later.

Documentary
Inside Bill’s Brain: Decoding Bill Gates -- Part 1 (Netflix)

An hour of somebody technical explaining technical things to a camera, and of the film explaining them back in plain language. Watch how each process is broken into steps you can hold in your head, and how the narration never uses a word it has not defined.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Note three sentences that start with when, if or once, and write down the two verbs in each one. Neither of them will be in the future.
