Chapter 3: Could and Couldn't

The Past of Can

I could · I couldn't · could you...?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I couldn't sleep last night."

"I could hear the music through the wall."

"This morning I could change rooms."

"When I was a boy, I couldn't swim."

NowBeforeExample
cancouldToday I can sleep. Last night I could hear everything.
can'tcouldn't (could not)I couldn't sleep. She couldn't find the key.
EverybodyThe SAME form: I could, he could, they could. No S, no TO, no -ed.
QuestionCould you...? — a polite request, NOWCould you give me a quiet room, please?
Remember lesson 13?With could there is no did: "I couldn't sleep", never "I didn't could sleep".

Could is the past of can. And "Could you...?" is the most polite way to ask for something today.

Two Jobs

Could: the Past, and the Polite Question

"I couldn't sleep last night."

the past: COULDN'T — the L is silent, it sounds KUDNT

"I could hear the music."

the past: COULD — it rhymes with good (KUD)

"Could you send a blanket, please?"

NOT the past: a polite request, more polite than "Can you...?"

"Could I have a quiet room, please?"

the sentence that ends the long night

He couldn't sleep, he could hear everything — and he asked: Could I have a quiet room?

1. What was the problem in room 714?
It was very noisy. There was a party in room 716, and he couldn't sleep.
2. Could he use the air conditioning?
No, he couldn't. It was broken.
3. What did he ask for?
A quiet room, far from the elevator: "Could I have a quiet room, please?"

Chapter 5: Real Speed

One in the Morning, on the Phone

Grace is at the front desk all night

Dialogue

Could You Help Me?

G
Front desk, good evening. This is Grace.
L
Hi, Grace. This is room 714. I am sorry, but it is very noisy.
G
Oh, I am sorry. What is the problem?
L
My neighbors have a party. I couldn't sleep. I could hear the music through the wall.
G
I understand. Could you wait ten minutes? I can call room 716.
L
I called them at midnight, but they couldn't hear the phone.
G
OK. Then I can give you a new room. Room 1102, on the eleventh floor. It is very quiet.
L
Thank you. And the air conditioning is broken. I couldn't turn it on.
G
The new room has a new one. Do you need anything else?
L
Could you send an extra pillow, please?
G
Of course. A pillow and a blanket to room 1102. Your new key is here at the desk.
L
Perfect. Thank you, Grace. Good night.
Comprehension

What Did Grace Say?

1. What was her first idea?
To call room 716, in ten minutes.
2. What room can she give him, and where is it?
Room 1102, on the eleventh floor.
3. What can she send to the new room?
A pillow and a blanket.
