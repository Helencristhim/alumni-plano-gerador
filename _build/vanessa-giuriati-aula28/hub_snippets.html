Describing People and Personalities

Describe people beyond "she is nice": impressions with "come across as", "strike me as" and "seem", plus precise qualities and the proof behind them. Key words: perceptive, composed, driven, straightforward, down to earth, hard to read, open up, click.

Straightforward -- Honest and direct, with no hidden meaning
"He's straightforward: if he disagrees, he tells you."
Down to earth -- Practical, simple and friendly, never arrogant
"She's the director, but she's very down to earth."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

People who meet my sister Paula for the first time often think she is unfriendly. She comes across as quiet and a little serious, and she is definitely hard to read. But she strikes me as the most perceptive person in our family: she always knows when something is wrong, even when nobody says a word. She is also very driven (she finished her degree at forty while working full time), and yet she is completely down to earth. She is straightforward, too: if she does not like your idea, she tells you, kindly. She is not the kind of person who opens up quickly, and some people never click with her. In a crisis, she seems calmer than anyone else: she is always the most composed person in the room.

1. How does Paula come across to new people?
A Loud and very funny.
B Quiet, a little serious and hard to read.
C Nervous in every situation.
2. What shows that Paula is driven?
A She always knows when something is wrong.
B She is calm in a crisis.
C She finished her degree at forty while working full time.
3. Which sentence gives the writer's PERSONAL impression?
A "She strikes me as the most perceptive person in our family."
B "She comes across as quiet."
C "Some people never click with her."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- come across as / strike me as / seem

GRAMMAR

Use these verbs to describe an IMPRESSION, not a fact. They are polite, and they leave space to change your mind.

StructureUseExample
come across as + adjective / nounHow a person looks to other peopleShe comes across as quiet.
strike me as + adjective / nounMy personal impressionHe strikes me as very driven.
seem + adjectiveA softer impressionShe seems a little serious.
seem like + nounThe same, with a nounHe seems like a straightforward person.

Always 'come across as' and 'strike me as' (no 'to'). After 'seem', use an adjective ('seems friendly') or 'like' + a noun ('seems like a friendly person'), never 'seems like friendly'.
