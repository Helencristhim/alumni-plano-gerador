Chapter 3: The Pattern

A Decision Now

I'll · will · won't

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Don't worry. I'll call the maintenance team right away.
The samples will be ready on Friday.
I promise: it won't be late.
Will the spare part arrive on Wednesday?

A plan from last week, or a decision now? And what comes after will?

UseFormExample
Decision nowI'll + base verbThe phone is ringing. I'll answer it.
Deadlinewill be ready / will arrive + dayThe samples will be ready on Friday.
PromiseI promise + will / won'tI promise it won't be late.
QuestionWill + subject + verb?Will the part arrive on Wednesday?
Plan or decision?going to = a plan from before · will = a decision nowI'm going to travel in October. / OK, I'll call him now.
CarefulAfter will: no TO and no -S. "It will to arrive" and "it will arrives" are wrong.

Chapter 4: On the Spot

A Machine Stops

a call from Paris, no time to plan

Dialogue

The Samples for Paris

C
Rodrigo, the client in Paris needs the samples on Friday. Is it possible?
R
We have a problem: one machine is broken. But don't worry. I'll call the maintenance team right away.
C
Do you have the spare part?
R
No, but I'll check with Tom in New Jersey. They have the same machine.
C
And the team? Is it enough?
R
We'll work overtime on Thursday, and the night shift will help too.
C
So the samples will be ready on Friday?
R
Yes. I promise: they'll be ready on Friday morning.
C
Thank you, Rodrigo. I'll tell the client.
R
Perfect. I'll send you a message on Thursday night.
