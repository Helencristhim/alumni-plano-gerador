Chapter 3: My, Your, His, Her

My, Your, His, Her

small words that say whose it is

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"My booking is for Saturday."

"What is your room number?"

"His name is Sam."

"Our breakfast is from seven to ten."

Who does it belong to? Look at the orange word.

PersonWordExample
I / youmy / yourMy room is 305. What is your name?
he / shehis / herHis name is Sam. Her booking is for Saturday.
weourOur restaurant is on the first floor.

His = a man. Her = a woman. In English the word never changes for room or rooms.

Reading

Your Booking Email

Park Hotel — Your Booking Is Confirmed

Dear Ms. Defrain, thank you for your booking at the Park Hotel.

Your room is a double room, number 305. Your check-in day is Saturday, and your check-out day is the next Saturday: seven nights.

Breakfast is in our restaurant, from seven to ten, Monday to Friday. On Saturday and Sunday, it is from eight to eleven.

Our Wi-Fi is free for every guest. The password is in your room.

Questions? Answer this email or call Sam, at the front desk. See you on Saturday!

Email — Park Hotel, London
