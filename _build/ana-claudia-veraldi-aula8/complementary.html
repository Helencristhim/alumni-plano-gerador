Lesson 8 -- Checkpoint -- Block 1

Talk
2 Billion Voices: How to speak bad English perfectly -- Heather Hansen, TEDxOdense (19 min)

Two and a half billion people speak English, and two billion of them learned it in a classroom. The whole talk is about which of those Englishes is actually the standard, and why the answer is not the one you were taught.

Tip: this is the argument behind every listening you have done in eight weeks. Watch it once with no subtitles.

Podcast
Comedy with an Accent -- S02E35, Xhuliano Dule (Italian and Albanian speaker)

A Taiwanese host interviews comedians who perform stand-up in a language they were not born into. Fifty-one minutes of two non-native speakers holding a fast, funny conversation in English, and never once apologizing for it.

Tip: not a lesson and not for learners — a real conversation. Notice how often they lose a word and simply keep going.

Talk
Learning a language? Speak it like you’re playing a video game -- Marianna Pascal, TEDxPenangRoad

Why two people with exactly the same vocabulary sound completely different in a real conversation, and why the difference has nothing to do with how much English they know.

Tip: watch this one the evening before your next lesson. It is the shortest possible answer to why the benchmark is timed.
