Lesson 8 -- Checkpoint: Block 1

Video
Homeworthy -- "House Tour: A 600-Year-Old Farmhouse in the English Countryside"

A woman walks a visitor through the house she has been restoring for years: what was already there, what was replaced, and what is still unfinished. It is your own house tour, in English, told by a native speaker.

Tip: every time she says how long something has taken, pause and say the same sentence about your house: "I have been working on it for ...".

Listening
BBC Learning English -- "Why are fewer people going to the countryside?" (6 Minute English)

Six minutes on why people move to the countryside, and why others stop. Two natural British speakers at a speed you can follow, on exactly the question this lesson asks about your own life.

Tip: listen once with no text and answer one question only -- what is their main reason? Then listen again and count three different tenses.

Listen on YouTube ↗
Talk
TED -- Carl Honore, "In praise of slowness"

A journalist explains why he stopped rushing, and what a slower, steadier life gave him instead. It is the argument behind your own move, made by someone who took stock and changed everything.

Tip: turn on the English subtitles the second time. Catch one sentence in the present perfect and repeat it until it feels easy.
