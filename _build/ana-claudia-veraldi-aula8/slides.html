Chapter 4: The Code

One Job Each

Seven forms, and how to choose between them

Grammar Discovery

Same House, Four Forms

"She left the city in 2015."

"She has been working on that house for nine years."

"The roof was replaced in the first winter."

"The kitchen is being rebuilt right now."

The green ones start with the person. The blue ones start with the thing. Inside each pair, one is finished and one is not.

FormIts one jobExample
Present simpleA fact or a routine that is true now.She gets by on very little.
Past simpleA finished moment, usually with a time.She left the city in 2015.
Past continuousThe background around that moment.While they were saying that, she was packing.
ComparativeTwo things side by side.The town is much quieter than the city.
Present perfectLife experience. No time is given.She has never hired a designer.
Present perfect continuousHow long it has been going on, and it still is.She has been working on it for nine years.
Passive (be + past participle)The thing matters more than the person.The roof was replaced. / The kitchen is being rebuilt.

The whole block in one line: first decide what the sentence is FOR, then pick the form.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I live here since 2015."
"I have lived here since 2015."
"How long are you working on the house?"
"How long have you been working on the house?"
"This town is more quiet than Sao Paulo."
"This town is much quieter than Sao Paulo."
"The roof replaced in the first winter."
"The roof was replaced in the first winter."

Three of these four errors come from Portuguese. Since and for pull the perfect forms, one-syllable adjectives take -er, and the passive always needs be.

Dialogue

Two Years Later

T
Two years without a word, Ana. So, tell me honestly: was it the right decision?
A
On the whole, yes. It took me a long time to settle in, but I would not go back now.
T
I did the opposite, you know. I sold the workshop in March and I moved back to Sao Paulo.
A
You sold the workshop? You had that place for twenty years.
T
I know. I was worn out. I was working every weekend and I was still only getting by. I needed a fresh start.
A
And now? Do you look forward to Monday morning?
T
Honestly, yes. It is noisy and it is expensive, but I have found something I can stick with. And my son lives ten minutes away.
A
Then it was worth it. We took stock at the same time and we walked in opposite directions.
Comprehension

About Tom

1. What did Tom sell, and when?
His workshop, in March. He had that place for twenty years.
2. Why did Tom want a fresh start?
He was worn out, working every weekend and still only getting by.
3. Besides the work, what is good about the city for him?
His son lives ten minutes away.
