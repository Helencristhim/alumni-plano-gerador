Common Mistake

Three Traps from Portuguese

I have 33 years.
I am 33 years old.
Where you live?
Where do you live?
Have a bathroom here?
Is there a bathroom here?

English needs three little words that Portuguese does not: am for age, do for the question, and there is for what a place has.

Dialogue

Check-in at Hotel Le Marais

M
Good afternoon, and welcome to Hotel Le Marais. Do you have a reservation?
T
Yes, I do. My name is Thaís Ferreira, and the room is for three people.
M
Ferreira, room four zero two. Is this your first trip to Paris?
T
Yes, it is. My husband and my daughter are with me. We travel together.
M
Very good. Are you here for work?
T
No, I am not. I don't travel for work this time. This trip is for my family.
M
That is wonderful. Here is your key, and a map of our street.
T
Thank you. Excuse me, is there a bathroom near the elevator?
M
Yes, there is. It is on the left, next to the small green door.
T
Perfect. And where is the metro station?
M
It is on this street, two minutes on foot. Have a very good day in Paris.

About Marc

Her key and a map of the street.
2. Where is the bathroom, in Marc's words?
On the left, next to the small green door.
3. How far is the metro station, in Marc's words?
On this street, two minutes on foot.
