The trapWould you like to go? — never Would you like go?

Would you like...? is the polite question. Do you want...? is for close friends and family.

What Did Claire Say?

She doesn't think she likes jazz, but she will try.
2. Why can't Claire go to the market in the morning?
She has a call with her sister at ten.
3. Does Claire want a ride to the park? Why?
Yes. The park is far from the hotel.
