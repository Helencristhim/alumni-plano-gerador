Lesson 7 -- If You Move on Price

Podcast
Planet Money Summer School (NPR) -- "Negotiating and the empathetic nibble"

Half an hour on the mechanics of a negotiation, including the one idea the lesson is built on: knowing your alternative before you sit down, so that you know when to hold firm and when to walk away. Everything is explained with examples, at natural speed, with the transcript beside it.

Tip: write down every conditional sentence they use to describe a move. Sort them into two lists: the ones that commit the speaker and the ones that do not.

Talk
William Ury -- "The walk from no to yes" (TED, 18 min)

The co-author of Getting to Yes, and the man who taught negotiation at Harvard for decades, on how agreement actually gets built. He speaks slowly and structures everything, which makes him unusually easy to follow and unusually easy to steal from.

Tip: his central idea is the third side. After the talk, describe a negotiation of your own from that third position, out loud, in five sentences.

Watch on TED ↗
Film
The Trial of the Chicago 7 (Netflix)

Aaron Sorkin dialogue in a courtroom, which means fast, argued English with a point behind every line. Watch it for the negotiation that happens outside the courtroom as much as for the arguments inside it: almost every scene is somebody testing what the other side would accept.

Tip: pick one exchange and write down the two positions. Then write the sentence that would have tested a compromise without committing either side to it.
