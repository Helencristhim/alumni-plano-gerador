Chapter 4: The Code

Much or Many?

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Questions Have in Common?

"How many clients are there?"

"How many transactions are there in a month?"

"How much money is in one transaction?"

"How much is the fee?"

Look at the blue words and the word after them. Clients, transactions: can you count them? Money: can you say "one money, two moneys"?

JobFormExample
Things you can countHow many + pluralHow many clients? How many calls?
Things you cannot countHow much + singularHow much money? How much time?
PriceHow much is it?How much is the fee? Two percent.
Answersa lot / not many / not muchNot many problems. Not much money.
Big numbersthousand, million (no s)two million cards, fifty thousand clients
The trapmoney is not countableNot How many money. Say How much money.

In one line: can you count it? How many + plural. You can't count it? How much. Price: How much is it?
