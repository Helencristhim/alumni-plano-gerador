Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart: “Give It to Me Straight: The Power of Honest, Constructive Feedback”

Matt Abrahams, from Stanford, talks with David Dodson about feedback that actually helps: structured reviews, honest conversations, and why a manager who avoids the hard part is not being kind. It is the conversation from this lesson, from the side of the person who has to give the review.

Tip: about thirty minutes, free on the Stanford page. Write down every sentence that starts with an activity (giving feedback, waiting too long) and say one about your own team.

Listen on Stanford GSB ↗
Talk
LeeAnn Renninger: “The secret to giving great feedback”

A five-minute method in four steps for giving feedback that people actually use. Short, practical, and full of the -ing forms from this lesson.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, use her four steps to give one piece of feedback to a person on your team, in English, out loud.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Amy Edmondson: “Building a psychologically safe workplace” (TEDxHGSE)

A Harvard professor on why teams that perform well are the ones where people feel safe saying they made a mistake, and what a leader does every day to make that possible.

Tip: eleven minutes, calm American accent. Stop after each example and say whether it is worth trying at Dataside, and why.
