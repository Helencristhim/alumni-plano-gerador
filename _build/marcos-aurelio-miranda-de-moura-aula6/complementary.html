Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
CNBC -- "How Costco Became A Massive Members Only Retailer"

A whole business built on one trade-off: fewer products, thinner margins, and a fee at the door. The narration compares Costco with every other retailer in the country, sentence after sentence, which makes it a free hour of the structure you practised today.

Tip: keep a piece of paper next to you and write down every comparative and superlative you hear in the first ten minutes. You will fill the page.

Podcast
NPR Planet Money -- "The Birth And Death Of The Price Tag"

Twenty minutes on the number you defend every quarter: how the fixed price on a shelf was invented, and why it is quietly disappearing. Half the episode is people comparing one pricing model with another, out loud.

Tip: the page carries the full transcript. Listen once with it closed, then open it and mark three comparisons. Rebuild each one about your own two segments.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
TED -- Casey Brown, "Know your worth, and then ask for it" (9 min)

A pricing consultant on the gap between what something is worth and what people manage to say it is worth. It is the same muscle as the last sentence of today’s lesson: the comparison is easy, the recommendation is the hard part.

Tip: nine minutes and she speaks very clearly. Try it once with no subtitles, then say out loud the one comparison from your own business that you have never said in a meeting.
