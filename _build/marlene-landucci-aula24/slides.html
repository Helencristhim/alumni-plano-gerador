Chapter 4 -- There Is / There Are

Language Discovery

what exists, and what does not

Discovery

Listen and Notice

All four sentences are from the hotel card. What do the orange words have in common?

"There is a phone next to the bed."

"There are two chargers at reception."

"There is no phone in the bathroom."

"Is there Wi-Fi in the room?"

The Rule

There is / There are

UsePatternExample
One thingThere is + a / anThere is a phone next to the bed.
Two or moreThere are + numberThere are two chargers at reception.
NothingThere is no / There are noThere is no signal in my room.
Ask about oneIs there ...?Is there Wi-Fi in the room?
Ask about manyAre there ...?Are there chargers at reception?
Short answerYes, there is. / No, there is not."Is there a problem?" "No, there is not."

Key idea: there is and there are say that something exists in a place. Use there is for one thing and there are for two or more. In a question the two words change places: Is there Wi-Fi here? -- never "Have Wi-Fi here?"

Dialogue

Calling Paul

M

"Hello Paul, this is Marlene. Is there Wi-Fi in your hotel?"

P

"Hi Marlene! Yes, there is. The password is on a card in the room."

M

"I can hear you, but your voice is strange. Is there a problem?"

P

"On my floor there is no signal. In the lobby the signal is good, so I go down."

M

"My battery is low too. Is there a charger at your reception?"

P

"Yes, there are two chargers at reception, and they are free."

M

"Perfect. Send me a message with the address of the restaurant."

P

"I send it now. Look at your screen in two minutes -- there is a map in the message."

Comprehension

About Paul

1. Is there Wi-Fi in Paul's hotel, and where is the password?
Yes, there is. The password is on a card in the room.
2. Why does Paul go down to the lobby?
Because on his floor there is no signal, and in the lobby the signal is good.
3. How many chargers are there at his reception?
There are two, and they are free.
