Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. Toque em Listen para ouvir. / Listen to each word and read the example.

Wi-Fi -- internet without a cable (internet sem fio)
"Is there Wi-Fi in the room?" (Tem wi-fi no quarto?)
Password -- a secret word that opens something (senha)
"What is the password, please?" (Qual é a senha, por favor?)
Charger -- the cable that gives your phone energy (carregador)
"Is there a charger at reception?" (Tem um carregador na recepção?)
Battery -- the energy inside your phone (bateria)
"My battery is low." (Minha bateria está fraca.)
Screen -- the flat glass part that you look at (tela)
"The password is on the television screen." (A senha está na tela da televisão.)
Signal -- the bars that let your phone work (sinal)
"There is no signal in my room." (Não tem sinal no meu quarto.)
Message -- a short text that you send to a person (mensagem)
"Send me a message with the address." (Me manda uma mensagem com o endereço.)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto e responda às perguntas. / Read the text and answer the questions.

Marlene arrives at her hotel in Florence. In the room there is a small card next to the television. On the card there is the name of the Wi-Fi and the password. There is a phone next to the bed, and there are two chargers at reception. There is no charger in the room. Marlene looks at the screen of her phone: the battery is low and the signal is weak. She goes down to the lobby, and there the signal is good. She sends a message to her friend Paul: "I am in the hotel. Is there Wi-Fi in your hotel too?" (Neste texto, there is = há / tem UMA coisa; there are = há / tem DUAS ou mais; there is no = não tem.)

1. Where is the card with the Wi-Fi password?
A Next to the television.
B Next to the bed.
C At reception.
2. How many chargers are there at reception?
A There is one charger.
B There are two chargers.
C There are no chargers.
3. Why does Marlene go down to the lobby?
A Because there is a restaurant in the lobby.
B Because there is no television in the room.
C Because in the lobby the signal is good.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- There is / There are

GRAMMAR

There is e there are dizem que alguma coisa EXISTE num lugar -- é o nosso "há" / "tem". Use there is para UMA coisa e there are para DUAS ou mais. Para dizer que não tem nada: there is no / there are no. Na pergunta, as duas palavras trocam de lugar: Is there Wi-Fi here? / In English, there is and there are say that something exists in a place.

UseExample (EN)Tradução (PT)
Uma coisaThere is a phone next to the bed.Tem um telefone ao lado da cama.
Duas ou maisThere are two chargers at reception.Tem dois carregadores na recepção.
Não temThere is no signal in my room.Não tem sinal no meu quarto.
Pergunta (uma)Is there Wi-Fi in the room?Tem wi-fi no quarto?
Pergunta (várias)Are there chargers at reception?Tem carregadores na recepção?
Resposta curtaYes, there is. / No, there is not.Sim, tem. / Não, não tem.

Key point / Ponto-chave: em português a gente pergunta "TEM wi-fi aqui?", e por isso dá vontade de dizer "Have Wi-Fi here?". Em inglês isso não existe: a pergunta é sempre Is there Wi-Fi here? Se você fala italiano, o atalho é fácil: there is = c'è e there are = ci sono.
