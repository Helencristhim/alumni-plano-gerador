Tom Wujec — Got a wicked problem? First, tell me how you make toast (TED)

Nine minutes on exactly what Marta did in today's text: he asks people to draw a process, step by step, and shows what happens to a team when the order finally becomes visible to everybody at once.

Tip: watch it once, then do what he asks — draw your own close on one page, one box per step, with an arrow only where one step really has to wait for another.

Video
Randy Pausch — Lecture on Time Management

A university lecture on deadlines, order and the difference between what is urgent and what is important. He speaks slowly and repeats himself on purpose, which makes it one of the easiest long talks in English to follow at native speed.

Tip: twenty minutes are enough. Write down every sentence where he says before, until, as soon as or by the time — you will find more than you expect.

The Indicator from Planet Money — Did you know companies can vote in Delaware?

Nine minutes on where companies are legally headquartered and why it matters — a subject that sits right next to the company codes and entities you deal with every month, told in fast, unscripted American English.

Tip: listen once with no notes. Then listen again and write down the three steps of the process they describe, in your own words, using first, then and once.
