Chapter 4: The Code

Long Idea, Short Phrase

Two words joined before the noun

Grammar Discovery

The Long Way and the Short Way

Long: "a port that is driven by AI" → Short: "an AI-driven port"

Long: "a contract that lasts ten years" → Short: "a ten-year contract"

Long: "a system that uses a lot of data" → Short: "a data-heavy system"

Long: "an investment for the long term" → Short: "a long-term investment"

The meaning does not change. What changes? Look at the blue words: where are they, and what happened to "years"?

PatternExampleWatch out
noun + past participlean AI-driven port · a cloud-based systembefore the noun, never a port AI-driven
number + nouna ten-year contract · a two-hour delaythe noun stays singular: never ten-years
noun + adjectivea data-heavy system · a labor-intensive businessthe noun comes first: data-heavy, not heavy-data
adjective + nouna long-term investment · a full-time teamone idea, so one stress group: LONG-term INvestment
adjective/adverb + participlea fast-growing market · a well-known casefast-growing, not fastly growing
after the verbThe contract is ten years long. The port is driven by AI.after be, use the long form

In one line: join two words, put them before the noun, and keep the number singular.

Listening 1 · The Moderator

One Question, One Minute

The moderator opens the panel on disruption. Sound first — no text.

1. How long should each answer be?
One minute. Not a five-minute speech.
2. What is her first question to every panelist?
Which technology will be mainstream in their sector in ten years, and who will lose if they wait too long.
3. Who will answer first?
The guest from the legal side.
Listening 2 · The Former CEO

The Biggest Mistake

A former terminal CEO looks back. Sound first — no text.

1. What did he say to his engineers every year?
That the technology was not ready, and that they should wait one more year.
2. What happened when the competitor opened its terminal?
It was a game changer: ships moved to the new terminal in months.
3. What is his final advice?
You do not need to be the first, but do not be the last.
