Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TED -- David Autor, "Will automation take away all our jobs?" (18 min)

An MIT economist on why machines have not ended work in two hundred years, and what really changes when a process is automated. Clear, calm and full of the vocabulary of today's lesson.

Tip: write down every compound adjective you hear, like labor-saving or high-skill, and use three of them to describe your own sector.

Talk
TED -- Erik Brynjolfsson, "The key to growth? Race with the machines" (12 min)

An economist argues that the best answer to new technology is not to fight it, but to work with it. A good model of short, quotable phrases on a big topic.

Tip: pause after each main idea and say it again in one sentence, as if you had only one minute on a panel.

Talk
TED -- Andrew McAfee, "What will future jobs look like?" (14 min)

A researcher on how digital technology changes work, and what institutions and rules need to do about it. Useful for the legal and regulatory side of disruption.

Tip: note one idea that could go into a long-term contract or a regulation, and explain it out loud in your own words.
