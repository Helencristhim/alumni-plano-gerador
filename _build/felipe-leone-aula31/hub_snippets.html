Lesson 31 -- Pre-class

False Friends at the Office -- The English Word

Ten to twenty minutes before class. Words that look familiar and mean something else. Key words: eventually, actually, currently, occasionally, room, a summary, to attend.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Eventually -- in the end, after a long time or many problems
"The negotiation was long, but eventually they signed."
Actually -- in fact, used to give the true information
"Actually, the default rate went down."
Currently -- at the present time
"We are currently testing a new score."
Occasionally -- from time to time, but not often
"I occasionally work from home."
Room -- the free space or the chance for something
"There is room for improvement in the report."
A summary -- a short text with the main points
"Can you send me a summary of the call?"
To attend -- to go to a meeting or an event
"I attended a conference in Miami."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Bruno is a credit analyst. In his first email to the US team, he wrote: we eventually have problems with the data. He wanted to say that the problems happen occasionally, from time to time.

His manager read it and asked: so, in the end, you will have problems? Bruno changed the sentence. Now he writes: we are currently fixing the data, and actually it is better than last month.

What did Bruno want to say in his first email?
A That the problems will happen in the end.
B That there are no problems.
C That the problems happen from time to time.
Which word means at the present time?
A Eventually.
B Currently.
C Actually.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Look Familiar, Mean Something Else

GRAMMAR

Learn the English meaning. Do not trust the word that looks like Portuguese.

JobFormExample
in the endeventuallyEventually, the client agreed.
from time to timeoccasionallyI occasionally travel to Atlanta.
at the present timecurrentlyWe are currently hiring.
in factactuallyActually, the numbers are good.

Never: We eventually have meetings (for from time to time) · Actually we are hiring (for now) · There is space to grow

Stage 2: Put the Conversation in Order

Order

Two colleagues talk before a call. Put their conversation in a natural order.

?"Yes, and actually it is going better than we expected."
?"OK. Eventually we need to decide, so let's talk on Friday."
?"Are you currently working on the Mexico account?"
?"Maybe. We occasionally take a small one."
?"Good. Is there room for one more client this month?"

Stage 3: Pronunciation

Speaking

Listen, then record yourself. The stress is on the first syllable: E-ven-tually, AC-tually, CUR-rently.

We are currently updating the data.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer a real speaker would give.

You want to say that you have calls with the US team from time to time. You say:
A "We occasionally have calls with the US team."
B "We eventually have calls with the US team."
C "We actually have calls with the US team."
You want to say what you are doing now. You say:
A "Eventually, I am working on the new score."
B "Currently, I am working on the new score."
C "Actually, I am working on the new score."
Your manager asks for a short version of the report. She wants:
A an agenda
