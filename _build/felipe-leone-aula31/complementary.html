Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Lera Boroditsky: “How language shapes the way we think” (14 min)

A cognitive scientist shows how the words of each language change the way people see the world.

Tip: note two English words that do not work like the Portuguese word you expected.

Watch on TED ↗
TEDx Talk
Anne Curzan: “What makes a word real?” (17 min)

A linguist on how words change their meaning over time, and who decides.

Tip: after the talk, say three sentences with eventually, actually and currently.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Erin McKean: “Go ahead, make up new words!” (7 min)

A dictionary editor, fast and funny, on how English grows and changes.

Tip: watch it twice: the second time without subtitles.
