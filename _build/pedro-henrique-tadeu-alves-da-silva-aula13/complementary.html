Film · trailer, 2 min
Amadeus (1984) -- Official Trailer

Salieri is a good composer. Mozart is far better, and Salieri knows it. The whole film is about one musician comparing himself to another, every single day, and the trailer shows exactly that.

Tip: after the trailer, compare the two composers in three sentences: one with much or far, one with nowhere near as ... as, and one with by far the.

Twenty Thousand Hertz -- The Loudness War: Can streaming finally end it?

Why did records get louder and louder for twenty years, and did they get any better? The same trap as today’s second listening, told as the story of a whole industry, with mastering engineers who fought back.

Tip: every time somebody compares an old record with a new one, pause and say how much louder or better it is, with a qualifier: slightly, a lot, nowhere near as.

Video · American accent · TED, 20 min
The Paradox of Choice -- Barry Schwartz

More options should make us happier. Barry Schwartz shows why they often make us less satisfied: the more versions we compare, the worse the one we choose seems to be. Useful for anybody who has to pick one take out of ten.

Tip: write down two comparisons he makes, then say them again with a qualifier of your own: much, slightly, not quite as ... as.
