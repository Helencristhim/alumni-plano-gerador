Podcast
NPR Life Kit -- "4 tips for understanding your rights as a renter"

The same lease you read in class, from the tenant’s side: which fees are legal, how to keep a deposit from disappearing, and what to document before you move in. It is the vocabulary of this lesson used by people who are about to sign something.

Tip: listen once, then write down every fee or payment the episode names and mark which ones come back. Say each sentence out loud with the right future form.

Audiobook
Imbolo Mbue -- "Behold the Dreamers" (audiobook preview)

A family who arrives in New York and spends the novel doing what this lesson is about: paperwork, rent, documents and the offices that each want what another office has. Read at a comfortable pace, in the American English of people who are new to it.

Tip: listen to the preview and note every sentence about something that has not happened yet. Decide which future form each one uses, and why that one.

Music
John Denver -- "Leaving on a Jet Plane"

Three minutes about the day before a departure, sung slowly, and built almost entirely on the futures of this lesson: I’m leavin’ on a jet plane (arranged), I’ll be back again (a promise made while speaking). The two forms sit side by side in the chorus.

Tip: read the lyrics and separate every future into two columns -- what is arranged and what is being promised. That is the same distinction you practised on the board.
