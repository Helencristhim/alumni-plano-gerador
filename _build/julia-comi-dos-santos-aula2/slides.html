The Frame

Find the missing S

I attend classes on Tuesday. — My brother attends on Monday.
We take a break at three. — Clara takes hers at four.
I focus in the morning. — She focuses at night.

He, she and it get an S

After ch, sh, s, x and o it becomes -es: focus → focuses, watch → watches, go → goes.
