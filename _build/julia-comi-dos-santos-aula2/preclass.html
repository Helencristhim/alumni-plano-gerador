Stage 1.1: Words for Your Week

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Eight words, two minutes.

Schedule — the plan of what you do at each hour
"My schedule changes every semester."
Commute — the trip you make to work or school every day
"My commute is short. I walk."
Campus — all the buildings and grounds of a university
"I live two blocks from campus."
Deadline — the last day you can deliver something
"The deadline is Friday at midnight."
Break — a short rest between two activities
"We take a break between classes."
Focus — to put all your attention on one thing
"I focus better in the morning."
Manage — to organise your time or your work well
"I manage my week with a calendar."
Attend — to go to a class or a meeting
"I attend classes four days a week."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the message. Then answer the questions.

My schedule is full this semester. I attend classes four days a week and I usually take a break at three. My commute is short — I live near campus and I always walk. My brother attends the same university, but he lives further away, so he takes the bus and he never arrives on time. I focus better in the morning; he focuses at night. We both have a deadline on Friday, and neither of us manages to start early.
How does the brother get to university?
A He walks.
B He takes the bus.
Why is it "he focuses" and not "he focus"?
A Because "he" takes the S — and after "s" it becomes "-es".
B Because "focus" is an irregular verb.
Where does the word "never" go in "he never arrives on time"?
A Before the verb.
B At the end of the sentence.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

The frame, with one more box: how often

Who · how often · verb · what · when

No S

I attend classes.
You manage your time.
They take the bus.

With S

He attends classes.
She manages her time.
He focuses at night.

After ch, sh, s, x, o the S becomes -es: focuses, watches, goes.

Before the verb: always · usually · often · sometimes · never  →  "I never go on Tuesday."
At the end: every day · twice a week · on Fridays  →  "I go to the gym three times a week."
− He does not take the bus. (the S moved into does)

Stage 4: In the conversation

Quiz
Someone asks how often you go to the gym. Which answer works?
A "I go three times a week never on Tuesday."
B "I go three times a week, but I never go on Tuesday."
You want to describe your brother’s routine. Which is correct?
A "He always takes the bus."
B "He take always the bus."
A recruiter asks about your availability. Which sounds best?
A "Classes morning. Afternoon free."
B "I attend classes in the morning, so I am free every afternoon."

Stage 5: A normal Tuesday

Reflection

Then turn the paper over and record 30 seconds saying them — without reading.
