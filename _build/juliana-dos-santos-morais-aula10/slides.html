Chapter 4: The Code

Said, Told, Asked

Reporting other people’s words

Grammar Discovery

What They Said, What We Report

Daniel: “The list is ready.” → "Daniel said that the list was ready."

Emma: “I am traveling next week.” → "Emma told me that she was traveling the next week."

Daniel: “I will send the contract.” → "Daniel said that he would send the contract."

The partner: “Please send the logo.” → "The partner asked us to send the logo."

Compare the original words with the report. The verb in red moves one step back: is becomes was, will becomes would. Told needs a person (told me); said does not. And a request becomes asked + person + to + verb.

JobFormExample
Reporting a statementsaid (that) + sentenceDaniel said that the list was ready.
With the person who heard ittold + person + (that)Emma told me that she was traveling.
The verb moves backis → was · am doing → was doing · will → wouldHe said he would send it.
Reporting a requestasked + person + to + verbThe partner asked us to send the logo.
Reporting a questionasked + if / what + normal orderShe asked if the date was confirmed.
The trapnever said + personNot He said me. Say He told me or He said that.

In one line: said that / told me that + the verb one step back; asked me to + verb.

Listening 1 · The Coordinator

How I Write the Follow-up

A partnership coordinator explains how she writes the email after a meeting. Sound first — no text.

1. When does she send the follow-up?
Within one hour after the meeting.
2. What does she write in the first line?
A short recap: the main decision of the meeting.
3. Why does she ask people to reply?
So that anyone who understood something differently can correct it.
Listening 2 · The Partner

What the Client Told Us

A business partner reports a conversation with an important client. Sound first — no text.

1. What did the client say about the proposal?
She said that she liked it, but the price was too high.
2. What did she ask the team to do?
To send a new version with two options.
3. What concern did she point out?
She said that her company was changing its budget rules.
