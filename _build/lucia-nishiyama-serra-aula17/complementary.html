Podcast · BBC Learning English
6 Minute English -- Is it good to disagree?

Two BBC presenters argue about whether disagreement helps or destroys a conversation, and they do it the way educated speakers actually do: they qualify almost every claim instead of asserting it flatly. It is six minutes of natural hedging in the middle of a real disagreement, with the transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript and count how many times they soften a claim. Then read the transcript and mark every marker you find.

TED Talk · Judgment
Julia Galef: Why you think you're right -- even if you're wrong

Galef contrasts the soldier, who defends a position at any cost, with the scout, who goes out to find what is actually there and updates the map. It is the argument underneath tonight's whole lesson: admitting where your knowledge ends is a sign of judgment, which is exactly why a careful answer reads as senior in an American room.

Tip: turn on English subtitles and write down every phrase she uses to mark a claim as her own view rather than a fact.
