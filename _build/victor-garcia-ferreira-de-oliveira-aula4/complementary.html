Talk
TED — Ruth Chang, "How to make hard choices" (15 min)

A philosopher on why the hardest decisions are hard: not because you lack information, but because neither option is better. It is exactly the shape of the choice in front of you, argued by somebody who has thought about nothing else for years.

Tip: she uses conditionals constantly. Write down two of her sentences, one first conditional and one second, and say why she chose each one.

Video
TED on YouTube — Tim Urban, "Inside the mind of a master procrastinator" (14 min)

Funny, fast and uncomfortably accurate about what happens between you and a deadline. His real point arrives in the last two minutes and it is about the deadlines nobody sets for you, which is the application year exactly.

Tip: watch it once for fun and once for language. Count how many times he sets up an imagined situation, and notice the verb he uses after "if" every single time.
