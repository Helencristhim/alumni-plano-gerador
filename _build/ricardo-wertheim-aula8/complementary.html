Grammar Video
"May, might and could" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the half of today's lesson that decides how certain you sound: the difference between saying something is true and saying it may be true. Connection to Lesson 8: once "might" feels natural, you stop guessing in a voice that sounds like a fact.

Tip: every time they use "might" or "could", pause and say the same sentence about your own trip to Croatia. Then say it again with "will" and hear how much stronger it becomes.

"Travel to Eastern Slovenia" -- Amateur Traveler, Episode 816

A full episode about the part of Slovenia nobody puts on a list: Maribor, Ptuj, the wine hills and the thermal towns. Connection to Lesson 8: this is one hour of two people arguing, politely and with reasons, that a place is worth it.

Tip: write down three places they say you should visit and one they say you could skip. Then say out loud, in one sentence each, whether you agree and why.

"Dubrovnik, Croatia: Pearl of the Adriatic" -- Rick Steves' Europe

The city everyone in this lesson is arguing about, filmed from the walls, the harbor and the empty morning street. Connection to Lesson 8: watch it and decide for yourself whether the writer of the reading was right.

Tip: watch it once for pleasure. The second time, stop it three times and say one sentence each with should, could and might about what is on screen.
