Chapter 3: The Camera

What Was Already Happening

I was walking · the sun was setting · when I heard music

Grammar Discovery

The Long and the Short

"I was strolling along the harbor when I heard music."

"The sun was setting and people were sitting outside."

"While we were waiting, a crowd appeared around the fountain."

"I was looking for a table when I noticed a small door."

In each sentence, one action is long and one is short. Which one was already happening?

FormUseExample
was / were + verb -ingThe background: what was already happening around youI was strolling. The sun was setting.
I / he / she / it → was
you / we / they → were		Only two words to choose from, for every verb in EnglishI was walking. People were sitting.
past simpleThe event: the short thing that cut into the background...when I heard music. ...when I noticed a door.
when vs whilewhen goes with the short event; while goes with the long backgroundI was walking when I heard it.
While I was walking, I heard it.

The long one sets the scene. The short one is what happened. That is the whole rule.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I walked in the square when I heard the music."
"I was walking in the square when I heard the music."
"People sitting outside every terrace."
"People were sitting outside every terrace."
"When I was strolling, I stumbled upon a courtyard."
"While I was strolling, I stumbled upon a courtyard."

The background needs was or were plus -ing — never the bare -ing alone. And the long action takes while, the short one takes when.

The Night You Missed

E

R
I did. I was strolling along the harbor when I heard music, so I followed it.
E
That was the summer festival. I was working here until ten, so I missed the beginning.
R

E
The old women dance there every year. My grandmother was dancing in that square when she was twenty.
R
At that point, I realized I was the only tourist. Nobody was taking photographs.
E
That is the real festival. The tourists usually stay on the terraces near the gate, so they never see it.
Comprehension

About Elena

1. Why did Elena miss the beginning of the festival?

2. Who was dancing in that square when she was twenty?
Her grandmother.
3. Where do the tourists usually stay during the festival?
On the terraces near the gate, so they never see the real festival.
