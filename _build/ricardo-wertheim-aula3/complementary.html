Grammar Video
"Past simple and past continuous" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on exactly the contrast you practiced in class: the long action that was already happening, the short one that cut into it, and the when and while that hold them together. Connection to Lesson 3: this is the grammar behind "I was strolling along the harbor when I heard music".

Tip: every time they give a pair of actions, pause and say which one is the background and which one is the event. Then build the same sentence about a street you know.

Podcast
"AT#987 -- Travel to Slovenia" -- Travel with Amateur Traveler

A writer who lived in Slovenia for five years walks through Ljubljana, the alpine lakes and the wine country. It is one long spoken travel story, told the way you will tell yours in August. Connection to Lesson 3: listen for the scenes he sets before he says what happened.

Tip: listen to the first twenty minutes. Write down three sentences where he describes what was happening around him, then say each one out loud about a place you have been.

Travel Series
"The Best of Slovenia" -- Rick Steves' Europe, Season 6 Episode 4

The full episode on Ljubljana, Lake Bled and the Julian Alps -- the second half of the trip you take at the end of August. Connection to Lesson 3: the narration is built on scenes, and the squares, fountains and terraces from today's vocabulary are all on screen.

Tip: watch the Ljubljana section twice. The second time, pause on three street shots and describe each one out loud: what the people were doing, what the light was doing, what you would have noticed first.
