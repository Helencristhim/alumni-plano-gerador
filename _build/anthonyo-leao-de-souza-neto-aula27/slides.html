The Sound

How It Sounds

"I'm waiting."

I'm = AIM, short — and -ING is soft, almost no G

"It's raining."

it's = ITS, one sound

"They're boarding now."

they're sounds like THÉR, with TH

"What are you doing?"

the question for every phone call: WHAT-ar-yu-DOING
