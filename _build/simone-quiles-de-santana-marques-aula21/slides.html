Chapter 3 -- The Timeline Code

Three Ways to Say When

will · be going to · present continuous

Discovery

Listen and Notice

Look at the orange words. Which one is a fixed date, which one is a plan, and which one is a promise made right now?

"We are signing the letter of intent on Friday at ten."

"The seller is going to keep a minority stake for two years."

"Due diligence will take about six weeks."

"Do not worry -- I will send you the report on the tenth."

FormUseExample
Present continuousa date already in the diary, with a time and a place"We are signing on Friday at ten."
Be going to + base verba plan or an intention decided before you speak"They are going to divest the warehouse arm."
Will + base verba prediction, or a promise you make in the room"Due diligence will take six weeks."
Will not / is not going tothe negative of each one, same difference"They are not going to divest it early."

Key idea: ask yourself when the decision was made. Already in the diary → present continuous. Decided before this conversation → be going to. Decided or predicted right now → will. All three are correct English; the wrong one only makes the client hear the wrong level of certainty.

The Structure

One Calendar, Three Levels of Certainty

The same deal, said three ways. Only the certainty moves.

Level 1 · It is in the diary

"We are signing the letter of intent on Friday at ten."

Present continuous. Use it only when there is a real date, a real time and a room booked.

Level 2 · It is decided, not booked

"We are going to sign the letter of intent this month."

Be going to. The client has decided; nobody has opened the calendar yet.

Level 3 · It is my reading of it

"I think we will sign the letter of intent before the holidays."

Will. Your prediction, or your promise. Safe when the date is not yours to fix.

A client who hears Level 1 puts it in the calendar. Choose the level you can defend.

Acquisition of a logistics target · Legal lead: Simone Quiles

Five steps. Signing on the twelfth.

1. Valuation · complete

Delivered by the bank. The legal team does not comment on the price.

2. Letter of intent · Friday the twelfth, 10:00

Not binding, except for exclusivity and confidentiality.

Contracts, filings and property files of the three companies inside the target.
