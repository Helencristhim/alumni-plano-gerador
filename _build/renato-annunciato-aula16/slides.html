Every sentence comes from your presentation. Which part is each one, and why does it use the blue structure?

PartStructureWhy
Who you arepresent simpleyour role is permanent: I lead
Resultspast simplefinished time: last year, my team grew
The dropcause and resultbecause of + noun · as a result
The planfirst conditionalif we get …, we will … (no will after if)
The updatereported speechshe told us she would come back
Two trapsNever last year we have grown — finished time takes the past simple. And never if we will get — after if, the present.

The Structure

Five Parts, Twelve Minutes

Talk for twelve. Leave three for questions.

1 · Opening · 2 min

"Good morning, and thank you for your time. Last year, my team grew market share by three percentage points."

2 · Results · 3 min

"Let me walk you through the numbers." One chart, one message, a pause after each number.

3 · The drop · 3 min

"Units in the South fell by twelve percent. The root cause was a stockout."

4 · The update · 2 min

"We are on track with the plan. One doctor told us she would come back in December."

5 · Close · 2 min

"To close: we need one thing from the global team." Then: questions.

The Dry Run

C
Thank you, Renato. That was clear, and you finished in eleven minutes, so you did not run over. Now let me ask the questions my team will ask.
R
Of course. I am ready.
C
You said units in the South fell in the third quarter. Why should we believe the recovery plan?
R
Because we have already started it. We did joint visits to both clinics, and one doctor told us she would come back in December.
C
Good answer. But you rushed it. Slow down your pace when you give a number.
R
Fair point. I will pause after every number.
C
Next question. What do you need from the global team?
R
One thing: six weeks of stock in the South, and a weekly call with the supply team.
C
That is a strong closing line. Use it at the end, not in the middle.
R
Understood. And the opening? I used a cue card.
C
Keep the card, but look up. Eye contact is half of the message. The rehearsal is over. Next week, you do it for real.

Why they should believe the recovery plan.
3. What does she criticize in his answer?
He rushed it: he has to slow down when he gives a number.
4. What is her advice about the cue card?

0:000:00
1. How should he divide his fifteen minutes?
Twelve minutes to talk and three for questions.
2. Why is a slower pace a gift, according to her?
Because some people on the call are not native speakers either.

Chapter 5: Practice

Clean It Up

Spot the Error

Fix the Sentence

Say the correction out loud first, then click

1. "I am working at Ipsen for eight years."
"I have worked at Ipsen for eight years." (for + a period up to now)
2. "Units fell because of the shipment was late."
"Units fell because the shipment was late." (with a verb, just because)
3. "The doctor said me she would come back."
"The doctor told me she would come back." (tell + person)
4. "We are a company French well-established."
"We are a well-established French company." (adjectives before the noun)
5. "If we will get the stock, we will recover."
"If we get the stock, we will recover." (no will after if)
