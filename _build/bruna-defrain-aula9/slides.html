Chapter 3: A Black Jacket

A Black Jacket

in English the color comes first

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I like this black jacket."

"A red dress, please."

"Do you have these shoes in white?"

"Two small jackets, please."

Where is the color: before or after the thing? And in two small jackets, where is the s?

To sayFormExample
the color or the sizecolor / size + thinga black jacket · a small dress
two or moreonly the thing takes stwo red dresses (never reds)
ask for a colorDo you have it in + color?Do you have it in white?

Color first, thing second. The color never takes s.
