"What makes siblings bond?" -- Jeff Kluger, TED Radio Hour on NPR

A writer on why the relationship with a brother or a sister outlasts every other one in a life. Connection to Lesson 17: he describes people with specific facts and specific moments, never with adjectives, which is the whole method of this lesson. The full English transcript sits under the player.

Tip: listen once without the text and write down three specific facts he gives about a real person. Then do the same about one person in your own family.

Grammar Video
"Mixing conditionals" -- English Masterclass, BBC Learning English

A short lesson on the exact structure you built today: a conditional whose two halves sit in different times. Connection to Lesson 17: it drills the choice between a past condition and a present one until it stops being a decision.

Tip: pause after each example and build your own version out loud BEFORE the presenter does. Use your own family and your own decade.
