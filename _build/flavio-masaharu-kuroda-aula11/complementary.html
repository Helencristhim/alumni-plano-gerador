Lesson 11 -- Say That Again

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Misunderstandings"

A short episode on exactly this lesson: what people say at work when they are not sure they understood, and how they check without making the other person feel accused. Full script on the same page, at natural speed.

Tip: read the script and underline every checking question. For each one, write down what the speaker already knew and what they were asking for.

Talk
Julian Treasure -- "5 ways to listen better" (TED, 8 min)

A sound consultant on why we lose track of what people say, and five small exercises to listen more precisely. Clear, deliberate English, and short enough to watch twice.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles and once without. The second time, pause after each of the five ways and paraphrase it out loud in one sentence.

Watch on TED ↗
Series
Emily in Paris -- Season 1, Episode 1 "Emily in Paris"

An American marketing executive arrives at a French agency, and half of the episode is two sides using the same words and understanding different things. Light, short, and full of business meetings where nobody checks what the other person meant.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Note two moments where a misunderstanding could have been stopped with one checking question, and write that question down.
