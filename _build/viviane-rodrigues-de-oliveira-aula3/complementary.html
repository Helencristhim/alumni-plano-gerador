Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 3 -- The GPTW Story

Series
Chef's Table -- Season 1, Episode 1 (Massimo Bottura)

Fifty minutes about one Italian chef and one project nobody believed in. It is the same shape as your GPTW story: the problem at the beginning, the small experiment, the turning point, and the result. He is Italian, so the accent is a gentle first step for the Milan calls.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down the three moments of his story: the beginning, the turning point and the end.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
How I Built This -- any episode

Every episode is one person telling the story of one project, from the first idea to the result. It is your homework in audio form: listen to how they open the story and how they close it with a number.

Tip: listen at 0.75x the first time. Do not try to understand everything -- catch the beginning, the turning point and the end.

Listen on Spotify ↗
YouTube
Adam Grant -- "The surprising habits of original thinkers" (TED)

Fifteen minutes about people whose ideas nobody wanted at first. He tells three short stories, each with a problem, a decision and a result -- exactly the structure you practised today.

Tip: watch once without subtitles, then once with English subtitles. Note how he starts each story.
