Chapter 3: The Code

Two Letters for the Past

work → worked

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Yesterday I interviewed two candidates.
Last week the manager hired a new employee.
On Monday we printed the contracts.
Marcos finished the report at five.

What do the orange words have in common? Is it the same for I, she and we?

RuleFormExample
Most verbsverb + -edwork → worked · print → printed
Verb ends in -everb + -dhire → hired · organize → organized
Consonant + yy → -iedstudy → studied
All peoplethe SAME formI finished · she finished · they finished
Time wordsyesterday · last week · on MondayYesterday I received the documents.

Key idea: in the past, a regular verb gets -ED, and it is the same for everyone: I, you, he, she, we, they. No -S in the past.
