What Did You Do? -- A Busy Week in HR

O passado dos verbos regulares: verbo + -ed. New vocabulary: interview, hire, organize, print, receive, finish. You will learn to say what you did last week at work: We interviewed six candidates.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. (Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo.)

Interview -- to ask a candidate questions (entrevistar)
"I interview candidates every week." (Eu entrevisto candidatos toda semana.)
Hire -- to give a person a job (contratar)
"We hire new employees in January." (Nós contratamos novos funcionários em janeiro.)
Organize -- to put things in order (organizar)
"I organize the files on Monday." (Eu organizo os arquivos na segunda.)
Print -- to put a document on paper (imprimir)
"Please print the contract." (Por favor, imprima o contrato.)
Receive -- to get something someone sends (receber)
"I receive many emails every day." (Eu recebo muitos e-mails todo dia.)
Finish -- to come to the end (terminar)
"I finish work at six." (Eu termino o trabalho às seis.)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions. (Leia o texto e responda.)

Last week was busy for Dienane. On Monday, she interviewed four candidates. On Tuesday, the manager hired two of them. On Wednesday, Dienane organized the training room, and on Thursday she printed the new contracts. On Friday, she received a thank you email from the manager, and she finished the week very happy. All these verbs are in the past: they end in -ed or -d.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Past with -ED (Passado com -ED)

GRAMMAR

Para falar do passado, o verbo regular ganha -ed — e é igual para todas as pessoas. / For the past, a regular verb gets -ed, the same for everyone.

Rule / RegraForm / FormaExample / Exemplo
Maioria / Most verbsverbo + -edI printed the contract. (Eu imprimi o contrato.)
Termina em -everbo + -dWe hired a new employee. (Contratamos um funcionário.)
Consoante + yy → -iedShe studied English. (Ela estudou inglês.)
Todas as pessoasa MESMA formaI finished / she finished / they finished
Palavras de tempoyesterday · last week · on MondayYesterday I received the documents. (Ontem recebi os documentos.)

Key point / Ponto-chave: no passado não existe -S: she finished, nunca "she finisheds". E o -ED tem três sons: /t/ (finished), /d/ (hired), /id/ (printed).

Stage 3: Pronunciation

Speaking

Listen, then record yourself. Focus on the -ed sound: finished (/t/), hired (/d/), printed (/id/).

Last week we interviewed six candidates.
Na semana passada entrevistamos seis candidatos.
Yesterday the manager hired a new employee.
Ontem o gerente contratou um novo funcionário.
On Wednesday I printed the new contracts.
Na quarta eu imprimi os novos contratos.
We finished the report on Friday.
Nós terminamos o relatório na sexta.
