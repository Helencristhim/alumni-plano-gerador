Reading

A Note from Diane

The Miami Team Sheet

Good morning, Ricardo. This is our team in Miami. It is a small team today, and it is a new office.

There is one director. His name is Paul Adams. There are two managers: Diane Foster and Carlos Ruiz. There are seven people in the commercial area.

Our office is on floor twelve. There are ten desks and there is one meeting room. There is a coffee machine near the window.

On Monday there is a meeting at nine. All the people are there. Your name is on the list too.

Note -- Diane Foster, Miami office
