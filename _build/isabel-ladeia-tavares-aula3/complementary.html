Lesson 3 -- The Choice You Have Not Made Yet

Series
Community

You said you were looking for a comedy and could not find one that held you. This one is set entirely at a college, the episodes are twenty minutes, and the whole first season is seven people arguing about what they are going to be when they grow up, which is the conversation you had in class today.

Tip: one episode with English subtitles is enough. Write down two sentences where somebody defends a choice, and change them so they are about you.

Podcast
How to make decisions you won’t regret : Life Kit

Twenty minutes on the exact problem of this lesson: a decision with two reasonable answers, no deadline pressure yet, and a lot of people with opinions. The argument is that the useful question is not which option is right, but which version of yourself you are willing to commit to.

Tip: the full text sits next to the audio on that page. Listen once without it, then read it while it plays again, and note every conditional sentence you hear.

Talk
Emilie Wapnick -- Why some of us don’t have one true calling

Twelve minutes arguing that the question you keep being asked, the one about what you are going to be, is built on an assumption that does not fit everybody. It is the best defence of keeping your options open that exists in a form short enough to watch on a Tuesday night.

Tip: watch with subtitles on and steal one sentence from it. The next time somebody asks you at a family lunch, use it.
