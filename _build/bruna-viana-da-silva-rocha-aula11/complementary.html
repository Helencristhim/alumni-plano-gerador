Series
Grace and Frankie — Netflix, Season 1, Episode 1

Two women in their seventies whose lives change overnight, and who spend seven seasons taking up new things, giving up old ones and putting off what they should not. Clear American English, everyday conversation, and almost every scene has somebody saying what they want to do, can't stand doing or keep promising to start.

Tip: write down eight sentences with two verbs in a row. Mark each second verb: -ing or to. Then say why the first verb chose it.

Podcast
NPR Life Kit — "A proven method to make a habit stick"

A behavior scientist explains why we give up new habits after three weeks, and how starting tiny keeps you from putting them off. American hosts, normal speed, and the full transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen once with the transcript closed. Then tell somebody, out loud, one habit you want to start and one you want to stop doing, using the method from the episode.

Video
Rachel's English — "How to Pronounce 'Gonna' and 'Wanna'"

Why the to in want to and going to almost disappears at real speed. This is the reason the to vanishes from your ear first, and then from your own sentences: I want start.

Tip: after the video, say "I want to start running again" and "I'm going to take up pottery" five times each, at full speed, and listen for the to that is still there.
