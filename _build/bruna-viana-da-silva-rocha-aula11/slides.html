Chapter 3: The Code

Two Verbs in a Row

One of them gives the orders

Grammar Discovery

Who Decides?

I enjoy cooking for my friends on Sundays.

This year I decided to take a real vacation.

I can't stand waiting in line.

I am planning to drive to Alaska again.

The red verbs come second. Two end in -ing and two start with to. Look at the blue verb in front of each one — that is where the answer is.

JobFormExample
Something you live, like or hateenjoy · mind · avoid · keep · finish · miss · can't stand + -ingI enjoy cooking for my friends.
A plan or a decisionwant · decide · plan · hope · need · learn · would like + toI decided to take a real vacation.
Both work, same meaninglike · love · hate · start · begin + -ing or toI started running / I started to run.
After a prepositionlook forward to · think about · be good at + -ingI am looking forward to seeing you.
Stop changes the meaningstop + -ing = not anymore · stop + to = pause in order toI stopped checking my phone. / I stopped to check my phone.

In one line: the first verb decides. Plans take to, experiences take -ing, and after a preposition it is always -ing.

Listening 1 · The Runner Who Hated Running

“When Would You Not Mind Doing It?”

How a habit she gave up came back. Sound first — no text.

1. Why did she give up running the first time?
She hated getting up early, she hated the cold, and she hated being passed by people twice her age. She gave up after three weeks.
2. What change made running work for her?
A friend suggested running at night, after work, instead of in the morning.
3. What is her advice for starting a new habit?
Do not ask what you should enjoy. Ask when you would not mind doing it, and start there.
Listening 2 · The Fire Pit

“Do Not Wait to Feel Ready.”

An RV park host on the sentence he hears every night. Sound first — no text.

1. What does he hear almost every night at the fire pit?
That people always wanted to do this trip and kept putting it off — first the kids, then work, then paying for the house.
2. What rule did the young couple make?
Every time one of them said someday, the other one had to name a month.
3. What does he tell people who are thinking about coming?
Do not wait to feel ready. Pick a month. The mountains are not going anywhere, but you might.
