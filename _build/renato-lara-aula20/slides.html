Grammar Discovery

The same facts, joined

I started at eight. I finished last at twelve. I was on the podium at fourteen.
I started at eight. At first I finished last, but eventually, at fourteen, I was on the podium.
I got injured. I came back. I am going to Australia.
I got injured, and that was the turning point. Thanks to the physio I came back, and now I am going to Australia.

The facts do not change. The joins do.

Read the first version of each pair out loud: three true sentences, and it sounds like a list. Now read the second: same facts, same tenses, but somebody is telling you something. The difference is four or five small words. That is the whole lesson.

The Code

Six words that turn a list into a story

To move through time

At first, ... (the beginning)
Then / After that, ...
Eventually, ... (after a long time)
Now, ... (where you are today)

To show what it meant

but ... (the turn)
because ... (the reason)
Thanks to ... (the person)
Looking back, ... (what you know now)

And the shape underneath is always the same three parts: where I started → what changed → where I am going.

Sound Check

Connectors carry a small pause

At first I could not stand up at all.

a tiny pause after "at first", then straight on

Listening 1

The long version

1. How did it start, and how does he describe the first two years?
His father pushed him into a wave at eight. At first he was terrible and he lost every heat for two years.
2. Why did he not quit at twelve?
Thanks to a coach who refused to let him leave.
3. What does he say the real turning point was?
Not a good result: the day he understood that the training was the point and the results were just the weather.
Listening 2

Why the story matters

1. What does she say every exchange student does on the first night?
They tell their story at the dinner table.
2. What does she say is the most interesting thing they own there?
Their story. Nobody at that school has it.
3. What do the students who make friends have in common?
They are the ones who told their story.
