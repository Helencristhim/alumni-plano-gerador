Listening
"Rude emails" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes about tone in professional email: why the same message reads as friendly or cold depending on a few words. You are about to write the follow-up emails Murat described, so this is not theory.

Tip: listen once, then write the two follow-up emails from your homework. Two lines each: where you met, and the one thing you promised. Read them out loud before you decide they are finished.

Conference Talk
"How language shapes the way we think" -- Lera Boroditsky, TED

A cognitive scientist on what changes inside your head when you use another language. Fourteen minutes, dense but clear, and it is a talk about your own situation, given by a researcher to researchers.

Tip: this is your listening benchmark. Watch once with no subtitles and write down how much you followed as a percentage. Keep the number. Watch it again after Lesson 20 and compare.

"Try something new for 30 days" -- Matt Cutts, TED

Three minutes, and the shortest talk in this whole programme. It is here for one reason: you are at lesson 10 of 24, and the next thirty days decide how much of Bangkok you actually get.
