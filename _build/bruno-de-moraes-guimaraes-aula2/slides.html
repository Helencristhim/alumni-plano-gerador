"I am not from here."

"He is not a beginner."

In the first two, where does not go? In the last two, which word moves to the front?

Key ideaIn English his and her follow the OWNER, not the thing: Tyler is a boy, so it is his sister. Emma is a girl, so it is her brother.

Add one word to say no. Move one word to ask. Nothing else changes.

Short Answers

Yes, I Am. No, I Am Not.

1. "Are you Bruno?" (yes)
"Yes, I am. Nice to meet you."
2. "Are you American?" (no)
"No, I am not. I am from Brazil."
3. "Is Tyler your brother?" (no)
"No, he is not. He is my friend."
4. "Is Coach Parker a teacher?" (yes)
"Yes, she is. She is my basketball teacher."
