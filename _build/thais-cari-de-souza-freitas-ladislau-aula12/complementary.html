Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Hans Rosling -- "The best stats you've ever seen" (TED)

The most famous talk ever given about a chart. Rosling describes lines that rise, fall and recover, and he does it with energy and very simple English. Watch how he says the shape first and the numbers second, exactly what you practiced today.

Tip: pause twice and describe what the line on the screen is doing, with sharply or gradually, before he does.

Video
BBC Four -- "Hans Rosling's 200 Countries, 200 Years, 4 Minutes"

Four minutes, one chart, two hundred years. Rosling narrates the whole thing live, and almost every sentence is a trend sentence: income rose, life expectancy fell, the countries recovered.

Tip: count how many times you hear rose, fell or recovered. Then describe the last ten seconds in your own words.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
David McCandless -- "The beauty of data visualization" (TED)

A designer on how the same data tells a different story depending on how you draw it. It connects with the second listening of today: the axis, the start date, and the comparison.

Tip: write down one chart from the talk that surprised you and describe it in three sentences, with from, to and by.
