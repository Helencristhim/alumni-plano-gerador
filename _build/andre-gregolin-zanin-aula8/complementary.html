Series
The Diplomat — Season 1, Episode 1

An entire series about people repeating what other people said, and about what is lost or added on the way. Almost every scene is somebody reporting a conversation they were not in, at speed, with the exact reporting verbs you learned today.

Tip: watch fifteen minutes with English subtitles and write down five sentences that report speech. Then mark which reporting verb was used and what it added.

Talk
Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie — “The danger of a single story” (TED)

Eighteen minutes on what happens when one account of a place or a person is repeated until it is the only one anybody has heard. It is the human version of the argument you read today, and the English is slow, precise and beautiful.

Tip: watch it once for the argument. The second time, write down three things she reports other people saying, and notice which verb she chooses each time.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
BBC Learning English — “How to use reported speech” (6 Minute Grammar)

Six minutes on the step back, the pronouns and the questions, spoken at native speed with examples you can shadow. Free, and the shortest way to make the question form automatic.

Tip: pause at every direct quotation and say the reported version out loud before they do it. That is the whole exercise.
