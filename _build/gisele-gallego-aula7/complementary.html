Lesson 07 -- At the Doctor’s Office

Documentary
The Healthcare Divide -- FRONTLINE, PBS (full documentary, 54 min)

Reporters follow real patients and real hospitals through the American health system: who gets billed, who gets covered, and why two people with the same symptom end up in very different rooms. This is the country you now live in, explained by the people inside it.

Tip: pause every ten minutes and say out loud, in one sentence, what just happened. Write down every time you hear coverage, a co-pay or a referral.

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) -- Here’s how to get the most out of your doctor’s appointment

Twenty minutes on the practical side of a visit: how to choose a provider, what to write down before you go, which questions to ask, and what to do when the doctor is clearly not listening to you.

Tip: the page has the full transcript. Listen once with the transcript closed, then open it and check the three tips you missed.

Talk
What the US health care system assumes about you -- Mitchell Katz, TED (13 min)

The doctor who runs the largest public health system in the country lists everything a clinic quietly assumes: that you can take a morning off, that you speak English, that somebody will explain the paperwork to you. Nobody says it better for a person who just arrived.

Tip: slow, clear American English with subtitles available. Watch once without them, then say back his main point in three sentences.
