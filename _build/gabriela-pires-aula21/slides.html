Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

What is different after the orange words?

"There is a small square near my house."

"There are shops and cafes on both sides."

"Is there a market near your house?"

"There are not any cars here on Sunday."

Grammar Rule

There is / There are

FormExample
One thing (+)There is a food truck outside.
Many things (+)There are three museums on this avenue.
QuestionIs there a market near here? / Are there shops?
NegativeThere is not a subway station here.
Short formsThere's a square. / There aren't any cars.

Key idea: look at the word AFTER it. One thing goes with there is; two or more go with there are. In a question the verb moves to the front: Is there a bike lane?

Grammar Practice

One or Many?

"_____There is a big market near the square." (one market)
"_____There are two food trucks on my street." (two trucks)
"_____Is there a bike lane on this avenue?" (a question about one lane)
"_____There are not any cars here on Sunday." (negative, plural)
