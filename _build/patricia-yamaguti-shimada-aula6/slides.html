Chapter 3: This and That

You Do Not Need the Name

this · that · these · those

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"How much is this?" (in your hand)

"How much is that?" (in the window)

"How much are these?" (shoes in your hand)

"How much are those?" (shoes on the shelf)

WordWhere the thing isExample
this + one thingNEAR you, in your handHow much is this?
that + one thingFAR from you, over thereHow much is that?
these + two or moreNEAR youHow much are these?
those + two or moreFAR from youHow much are those?

Near = this / these. Far = that / those. One thing = is, two or more = are. With your finger, you never need the name.

Comprehension

What Did Ryan Say?

1. How much is the dress, and which colors are there?
Forty-five pounds, in blue, black and green — same price.
2. What does Ryan say about the shoes?
They are eighty pounds, and they are new this week.
3. Where is the fitting room?
On her right. He stays there in case she needs another size.
Listening

The Store Announcement

The message in the store, on a Saturday afternoon. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. Which floor has the sale, and how much off?
The first floor — all dresses are thirty percent off until Sunday.
2. Who do you ask for another size or color?
Any person with a green shirt. They look in the back for you.
3. What do you need to return something?
The tag and the receipt, within thirty days.
Listening 2

A Message About Your Order

A voice message from the store, two days later. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What arrived, and until when do they keep it?
The blue dress in a large size — kept at the desk until Friday.
2. What should she bring, and who should she ask for?
The receipt of the black one, and she should ask for Ryan.
3. What is the new price of the shoes?
Sixty pounds, because they are now in the sale (they were eighty).

Role-Play — Semi-free

Buy Something for Each Child

Scenario: Now you buy one thing for each child. You need two different sizes, and one of them wants a different color. Ask, decide, and say what you take.

Keywords:

for my sonfor my daughtera bigger sizeI take this one
Role-Play — Free

Go In, Ask, and Leave Without Buying

Scenario: You go into a store alone with the children. You ask about two things, you do not like the price, and you leave politely, without buying anything.

No keywords this time. Two minutes, no stopping. Different from the model is not wrong — if they understood you, it worked.

What I Can Do

I can say no and leave, politely, without buying.
