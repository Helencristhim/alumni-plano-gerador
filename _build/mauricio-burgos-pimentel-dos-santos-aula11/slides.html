Grammar Discovery

Four words he forgot — and still said

It is the money that a company makes in one year.
She is the person who approves the budget.
It is a report which shows the numbers every month.
It is the office where the clients sign the contracts.

Who = a person. Which / that = a thing. Where = a place.

The blue word joins a general noun (person, money, report, office) to the information that makes it specific. When you forget a word, say the general noun and add the information: you never need the exact word to give the exact idea.

Listening 1

What happens to everybody

1. How often does the speaker lose a word herself?
Three or four times a day.
2. What is the difference between her and the people who stop?
She does not stop — she describes the thing.
3. What are the people in the room waiting for?
The idea, not the perfect word.
