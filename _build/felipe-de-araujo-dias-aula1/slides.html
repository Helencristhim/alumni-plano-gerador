Chapter 3: The Shape of a Fact

How English Says Always

The present simple, and the one letter that decides everything

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I oversee four areas."

"We buy from suppliers in Asia."

"She reports to the finance director."

"He does not handle maintenance."

SubjectPositiveNegativeQuestion
I / you / we / theyI overseeI do not overseeDo you oversee?
he / she / itshe overseesshe does not overseeDoes she oversee?

The -s appears once in the sentence. If does takes it, the verb gives it back: she does not oversee, never "does not oversees".

Grammar Practice

Finish the Sentence

Say the full sentence, then click to compare

1. My colleague ______ (work) in loss prevention.
"My colleague works in loss prevention."
2. We ______ (not / buy) from that supplier any more.
"We do not buy from that supplier any more."
3. ______ she ______ (report) to you?
"Does she report to you?"
4. The maintenance team ______ (check) the equipment every month.
"The maintenance team checks the equipment every month."
Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"He work in retail."

✓ RIGHT

"He works in retail."

✗ WRONG

"I am work with suppliers."

✓ RIGHT

"I work with suppliers."

✗ WRONG

"Does he works on Fridays?"

✓ RIGHT

"Does he work on Fridays?"

One verb, one -s, one place. English never uses am, is or are next to another verb in the present simple.

Chapter 4: The First Call

Nice to Meet You, Finally

A first video call with a new supplier

Dialogue

The First Video Call

L
Good morning, Felipe. I am Laura, the account manager for South America. Thank you for the time.
F
Good morning, Laura. Nice to meet you. I work in retail, and I oversee four areas here.
L
Four areas. Which ones, if I may ask?
F
Supply, facilities, maintenance and loss prevention. So I see the product before the store and the building around it.
L
That is a wide job. Who do you report to?
F
I report to the CFO. Four managers report to me, and each one runs one area.
L
Perfect. My company is a supplier of shelving and lighting. We work with eleven retail groups in Brazil.
F
Good. And how long does a delivery take? Our supply chain does not wait.
L
Thirty days, normally. I do not decide the price, but I do decide the date. Shall I send you the calendar today?
F
Please do. And send it to me, not to my team. I sign this one myself.
Did You Catch It?

About Laura

1. What does Laura's company sell?
Shelving and lighting. It is a supplier for retail groups.
2. How many retail groups does she work with in Brazil?
Eleven.
3. What does she decide, and what does she not decide?
She decides the delivery date. She does not decide the price.
