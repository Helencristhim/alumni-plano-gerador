Complementary Activities

Extra material to reinforce your learning outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen.

Lesson 1 -- What I Actually Do

Grammar Video
"Present Simple and Present Continuous" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the tense that carries every sentence you said in this lesson: the present simple for facts, jobs and routines, and the small -s that belongs only to he, she and it. Connection to Lesson 1: this is the grammar behind "I oversee four areas" and "she reports to the CFO".

Tip: pause after each example and say the same sentence twice, first about yourself and then about a colleague. If the second one has no -s, say it again.

Podcast
"Patagonia: Yvon Chouinard" -- How I Built This, NPR

The founder of a global retail brand explains, in slow and very clear American English, what his company sells, how the products reach the stores and who does what inside the business. Connection to Lesson 1: it is one long answer to the question "so, what do you do?", full of the words you learned today.

Tip: write down every sentence in the present simple that describes the company, not the past. Then say three of them again, replacing his company with yours.

Career Video
"Tell Me About Yourself -- A Good Answer to This Interview Question" -- Linda Raynier

A career coach takes apart the exact answer you practised today: how to describe what you do in a structure that lasts more than one sentence, and where most people stop too early. Connection to Lesson 1: same question, same problem, at a conference table instead of an interview.

Tip: watch it once, then record sixty seconds of yourself answering in her structure, about your four areas. Listen back and count how many times you stopped.
