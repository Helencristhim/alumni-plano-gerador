Grammar Video
"Stop buying or stop to buy?" -- BBC English Masterclass

Three minutes on the exact pair from today's lesson: the verbs that change meaning depending on whether -ing or to comes next. Connection to Lesson 9: this is the difference between the afternoon you interrupted and the afternoon you could not end.

Tip: after each example, say the same sentence twice about one of your own trips -- once with -ing and once with to -- and say out loud what changed between them.

Storytelling Podcast
"Two Ways to Travel" -- Eric Scheur, The Moth

One person, one microphone, no notes and no script -- the purest version of what this lesson is about. The Moth is where people learn to tell a true story out loud, and this one is about a journey that does not go as planned.

Tip: listen once for pleasure. The second time, find the four moves: where does he set the scene, where does he keep the ordinary short, where does he pause, and what does he reveal at the end?

Travel Series
"Slovenia: A Visit with Friends" -- Rick Steves' Europe

Not a monument in sight: a house, a kitchen table and a family talking about their own country. Connection to Lesson 9: every good travel story ends up being about a person, and this is what that looks like on screen.

Tip: watch it and choose the one moment you would keep if you had to retell the whole thing in ninety seconds. Then retell it out loud, and stop without explaining why you chose it.
