Chapter 4: The Code

There Is, There Are

Two phrases for every place

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"There is a small reception."

"There are six lifts."

"There are no tires in the shop."

"Is there a waiting room?"

Look at the orange words and the word after them. When is it is, and when is it are?

JobFormExample
One thingthere is + a / anThere is a waiting room.
Two or more thingsthere are + pluralThere are six lifts.
Negativethere is not / there are noThere are no tires in the shop.
QuestionIs there...? / Are there...?Is there a waiting room?
Short answersYes, there is. / No, there are not.Is there coffee? Yes, there is.
The trapnever "have" for a placeNot Have six lifts. Say There are six lifts.

In one line: one thing: there is; two or more: there are. Never "have" to describe a place.
