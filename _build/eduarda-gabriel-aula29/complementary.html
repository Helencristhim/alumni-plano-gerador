Lesson 29 -- Reporting to Senior Partners

Film
Margin Call (2011)

One night at an investment bank: a junior analyst finds a serious risk and has to explain it to people who are more and more senior, until he reaches the CEO. Connection to Lesson 29: the CEO asks him to "speak as you might to a young child". It is the briefing problem of this lesson.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Each time someone reports up, pause: did they start with the answer or with the story?

Talk
"How to speak so that people want to listen" -- Julian Treasure, TED

A sound expert explains what makes people stop listening, and how to use your voice so that they listen to you. Connection to Lesson 29: a senior partner listens for one minute. This talk helps you use that minute well.

Tip: listen once without subtitles. Then record your briefing from class again and use two of his tips.

"Start with the Answer: The Minto Pyramid Principle" -- Conversations On Careers and Professional Life

An explanation of the pyramid principle, the method that consultants and bankers use to structure reports: the answer first, then the reasons. Connection to Lesson 29: it is the structure that the consultant describes in the second listening.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Use it for your homework: plan your ninety-second summary as a pyramid before you record it.
