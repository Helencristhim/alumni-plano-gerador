Chapter 3 -- The Code

What Matters Is This

Putting a spotlight on one idea

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Purple Words Have in Common?

"What NorthPack accepted is a twelve-month escrow."

"What still worries them is the cap."

"It's the supplier case that creates the exposure."

"What we need from you is the green light."

Where does the most important information go in each sentence?

Neutral sentenceCleft sentence (with a spotlight)
"The cap still worries them.""What still worries them is the cap."
"The supplier case creates the exposure.""It's the supplier case that creates the exposure."
"We need your green light.""What we need is your green light."
FormWhat + subject + verb + is / was + FOCUS. It's / It was + FOCUS + that + clause.
NeverNot "What worries me it is the cap" / "It's the case what creates..."
Grammar Rule

Five Facts or One Message?

Neutral (every fact is equal)With a spotlight (one fact matters)
"NorthPack accepted twelve months.""What NorthPack accepted is a twelve-month escrow."
"The cap is still open.""What is still outstanding is the cap."
"The supplier case is the risk.""It's the supplier case that creates the exposure."
"I recommend fifteen percent.""What I recommend is that we accept fifteen percent."
The partner hears five facts.The partner remembers the one that matters.

Chapter 4 -- Getting There

Ten Minutes With Richard

One briefing, two recordings, one page

Dialogue

Eduarda and Richard

R
Eduarda, I have ten minutes before my next call. Give me the bottom line on Lane.
E
The bottom line is that the deal is on track. NorthPack accepted most of our changes to the term sheet.
R
Good. What did they accept, exactly?
E
What they accepted is a twelve-month escrow instead of eighteen. That mitigates the risk for Victor.
R
And what's still outstanding?
E
The cap. They offered fifteen percent, not ten. It's the supplier case that worries them.
R
How big is our exposure there?
E
About two million dollars, and the escrow covers it. It isn't material for a deal this size.
R
So what's your recommendation?
E
What I recommend is that we accept fifteen percent and close in March. I need your green light before the committee.
Comprehension

About Richard

1. How much time does Richard have?
Ten minutes, before his next call.
2. What does Richard ask for first?
The bottom line on Lane.
3. What is the last thing that Richard asks for?
Eduarda's recommendation.

From: Eduarda Gabriel, Houlihan Lokey
To: Richard Hale, Senior Partner
Re: Project Lane -- Executive Summary for the Investment Committee

Richard,

Bottom line: the deal is on track to close on March 31 at USD 105 million.

1. What NorthPack accepted is a 12-month escrow (down from 18).

3. Exposure: the supplier case, about USD 2 million. The escrow covers it.

Recommendation: accept the 15% cap and keep the March closing date. I need your green light before the committee on Tuesday.

Best,
Eduarda

Your Real Briefing

Choose a real deal from your work. A senior partner gives you two minutes. Give the bottom line, two or three key points and your recommendation, and end with what you need from the partner. Use at least two sentences with a spotlight ("What ... is...", "It's ... that..."). No script.
