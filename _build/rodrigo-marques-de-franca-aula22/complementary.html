Lesson 22 -- A Very British Email

Video
Pellegrino Riccardi -- "Cross cultural communication" (TEDxBergen)

A British-Italian speaker who lives in Norway shows, with a lot of humor, how the same words and the same behavior can mean very different things in different cultures. A light companion to this lesson's guide.

Tip: write down two moments when one culture reads the other one wrong, and say how a tactful email could have avoided it.

Podcast
BBC Learning English, 6 Minute English -- "How important is politeness?"

Six minutes on why Britain has a reputation for being polite, and a linguist's view that politeness depends as much on the listener as on the speaker. Short, clear and with a full transcript.

Tip: listen once without the transcript, then read it and find two polite phrases you could use in an email this week.
