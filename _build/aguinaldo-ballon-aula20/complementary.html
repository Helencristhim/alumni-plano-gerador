TED -- Joe Kowan, "How I beat stage fright" (9 min)

A musician who was terrified of the stage tells how he used his own stage fright as material, and found a simple way to keep his composure in front of an audience.

Tip: write down the moment he decides to stop hiding the fear, and say what you could do with yours on a panel.
