Grammar Discovery

Which Tool Does Which Job?

"As soon as the panel ends, I will send you my notes."

"Although I was nervous, I accepted the invitation."

"Roughly two thirds of the audience are investors."

"The moderator seems to know the sector very well."

No new rule today. For each sentence, answer two questions: what JOB is the structure doing (sequence, contrast, an approximate number, an impression), and which lesson did it come from?

JobFormExample
Impressions and certainty (lessons 11, 12)seem to + verb · must / may / be likely toThe moderator seems to know the sector.
Linking and dense ideas (lessons 13, 14)Moreover, · this + noun · noun phrasesThis decision changed the market.
Building rapport (lesson 15)question tagsYou are on the second panel, aren’t you?
Numbers and evidence (lesson 17)roughly · the majority of · a fraction ofRoughly two thirds of the audience are investors.
Cause and contrast (lesson 18)due to + noun · although + sentence · despite + nounAlthough I was nervous, I accepted.
Time and sequence (lesson 19)as soon as / once / until + present · by the time + hadAs soon as the panel ends, I will call you.

In one line: on the stage, choose the structure by the job you need to do, not by the rule you remember.

Listening 1 · The Forum Director

What Makes a Good Panelist

The director of an international forum explains what she looks for in a panelist. Sound first — no text.

1. Who are the best panelists, according to her?
The ones with a clear point of view, not the ones with the best English.
2. What question does she ask each panelist before a forum?
What do you want the audience to remember?
3. What should you not do with your opening remarks?
You should not read them.
Listening 2 · The Lawyer

The Second Invitation

A Brazilian lawyer remembers the first panel he turned down, and the second one he accepted. Sound first — no text.

1. Why did he really say no the first time?
He was afraid that somebody would ask a question he could not understand.
2. How did he prepare for the second invitation?
He rehearsed his opening remarks every morning and asked a colleague to play the moderator.
3. What happened after the panel?
A partner from a fund talked to him, and that conversation became his biggest client.
