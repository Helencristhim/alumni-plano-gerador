Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TED -- Nancy Duarte, "The secret structure of great talks" (18 min)

A presentation designer on the structure behind the most memorable speeches: what is, what could be, and the call to action. Exactly what a three-minute opening statement needs.

Tip: use her structure to plan your own three-minute statement: one sentence for what is, one for what could be, one recommendation.

Talk
TED -- Pamela Meyer, "How to spot a liar" (19 min)

A researcher on deception and the questions that reveal it. Useful from the other side of the table: it shows what a skeptical committee is looking for when it listens to a witness.

Tip: note two behaviors she describes, and say how a witness can show the opposite: precision, sources and quick corrections.

Talk
TED -- Julia Galef, "Why you think you're right -- even if you're wrong" (11 min)

A talk about the scout mindset: wanting to see things as they are, not as you want them to be. A good attitude for scrutiny, and clear, careful English to imitate.

Tip: summarize her main idea in one sentence with a noun phrase, and attribute it: according to Galef, ...
