Chapter 4: The Code

Telling Giulia What He Said

How to report someone else’s words

Grammar Discovery

His Words or Your Report?

"Davide said that the reports were not flexible."

"He told Paola that he would choose us again."

"He said that he could vouch for the people."

"Paola asked if there was a drawback."

Find Davide’s and Paola’s real words in the transcript. What happened to each verb?

He saidYou reportExample
is / arewas / wereHe said the reports were not flexible.
use / needused / neededHe said they used it in three warehouses.
will / canwould / couldHe said he would choose us again.
say / tellsaid (that) · told + person (that)He told Paola that... / He said that...
Is there...? Can I...?asked if + subject + verbShe asked if there was a drawback.
What is...? How long...?asked + wh- + subject + verbShe asked what the main drawback was.
Neversaid + person, or question orderNot: he said me, he told that, she asked what is

In one line: one step back in time, told + person, and no question order after asked.

The Note to Giulia

Subject: Reference call with Ferrante Logistica
From: Flávio Kuroda · To: Giulia Romano, Vetra Group

Hi Giulia,

Davide Conti called me after his call with Paola. He said it was a candid conversation. Paola asked about the go-live, and he told her about the hiccup with the connector. She also asked what the main drawback was, and he said the reports were not flexible.

The good news: he said he would choose us again, and he told her that he could vouch for our team. Paola asked if she could visit one of his warehouses. He said yes.

Best,
Flávio

Every fact comes from Davide. Nothing added, nothing hidden: the bad news is in the note too.
1. What were Paola's exact words about the drawback, in the transcript?
Is there a drawback I should know about?
2. Why does Flávio put the hiccup and the drawback in the note?
Because Giulia must hear the bad news from him first, before Paola tells her.
3. What will probably happen next, according to the note?
Paola will visit one of Ferrante's warehouses.
Chapter 6: Your Turn

Two Recordings, Then Giulia Calls

Listening 1 · The Customer Success Director

When the Customer Sells for You

A customer success director on how she prepares a reference. Sound first — no text.

1. What does she never do before a reference call?
She never writes the customer's answers.
2. What three things does she tell the customer?
Who the buyer is, what the buyer is worried about, and that a candid answer is better than a perfect one.
3. Why is a small problem good to mention?
Because buyers do not trust a reference with no drawback, and a hiccup fixed fast is worth more than a testimonial.
Listening 2 · The Voice Message

Davide Calls Back

A voice message from Davide Conti, right after the call with Paola. Sound first — no text.

1. What did Paola ask about first?
If the connector was ready on time.
2. What did Paola ask at the end of the call?
If she could visit one of Ferrante's warehouses in person.
3. When will Paola be ready to decide?
In two weeks.
