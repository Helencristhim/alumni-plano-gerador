Lesson 26 -- The Reference Call

Talk
Rachel Botsman -- "We've stopped trusting institutions and started trusting strangers" (TED, 17 min)

Why people today trust the experience of other users more than the message of a company. It explains why one call with a real customer can be worth more than any brochure.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Pick two things she says and report them in English: she said that... / she told the audience that...

Podcast
6 Minute English (BBC Learning English) -- "Is this the era of distrust?"

What makes people trust a person or a company: being honest and competent, again and again. A good way to think about what a reference really proves. Full script on the same page.

Tip: read the script and report two ideas from the presenters with he said that or she explained that.

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Feedback"

Short, useful phrases to ask for feedback and to pass it on at work. This is what you do after every reference call: you collect what the customer said and share it with your team.

Tip: write three sentences about a real customer's feedback, each one with told me that or asked if.
