Chapter 4: The Code

His, Her, Their

Whose is it?

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"Her office is in New York."

"His manager is Anil."

"Sarah’s boss is in London."

"Their department is Technology."

Look at the blue words. "Her office": whose office, a man's or a woman's? And how do we say "o chefe da Sarah"?

JobFormExample
I / youmy / yourMy boss is the CEO. Who is your manager?
He (a man)hisRaj is an engineer. His office is in Pune.
She (a woman)herSarah is the manager. Her team is big.
We / theyour / theirOur clients are banks. Their department is Technology.
A namename + ’sSarah’s team. Raj’s manager.
The traphis / her = the ownerSarah’s boss is a man, but it is her boss (Sarah is a woman).

In one line: his for a man, her for a woman, their for many people. For a name, add 's: Sarah's team.
