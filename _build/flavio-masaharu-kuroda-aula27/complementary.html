Lesson 27 -- The First Concession

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) -- "4 negotiation tactics to try in everyday life"

Four simple tactics for any negotiation, from asking for more to staying calm when the other side pushes. Every one of them works on a price call with a CFO. Transcript on the same page.

Tip: choose one tactic and write it as a trade with if: if you..., we will...

Talk
Adam Galinsky -- "How to speak up for yourself" (TED, 15 min)

A professor of negotiation explains the "range of acceptable behavior" and why the person with more options can ask for more. Very useful before you decide how small your first concession is.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down one idea and say how you will use it in your next price call.

Podcast
TED Radio Hour (NPR) -- "Getting what you want: A guide to negotiating"

A mediator, a linguist and other guests on how to negotiate with more clarity and less fear. Good listening practice at real speed, with many voices.

Tip: listen for the moments when a guest gives something and asks for something back. Repeat one of those sentences out loud.
