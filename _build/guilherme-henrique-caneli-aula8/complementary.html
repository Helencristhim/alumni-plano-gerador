Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Freakonomics Radio — “How to Change Your Mind”

Why people and institutions struggle to update their beliefs, and what helps them do it. The question behind every summit that wants its commitments to hold.

Tip: listen to the last ten minutes twice and notice how the episode itself closes: recap, idea, next step.

Listen on Freakonomics ↗
Talk
Adam Grant — “The surprising habits of original thinkers” (TED)

An organizational psychologist on how original ideas actually get done, including why the people who follow through are rarely the first to move.

Tip: retell his three habits in the future perfect, as commitments for your next year at GRI.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
Amy Cuddy — “Your body language may shape who you are” (TED)

On presence under pressure: how the body affects the way a speaker feels and is seen on stage. A final piece for a moderator who wants to stop preparing every sentence.

Tip: watch it before your next opening, and notice what changes in the first thirty seconds.

Watch on TED ↗
