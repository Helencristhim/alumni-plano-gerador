Lesson 15 -- The Client Who Is Wrong

Talk
Julia Dhar -- "How to disagree productively and find common ground" (TED, 13 min)

A former world debating champion on how to separate the idea from the person, and how to talk to somebody who is sure they are right. It is the other half of this lesson: what to do when the person in front of you does not want to hear it.

Tip: write down every sentence she uses to change the frame of a conversation, and try one of them in your next difficult call.

Talk
William Ury -- "The walk from no to yes" (TED, 19 min)

The co-author of Getting to Yes on what he calls going to the balcony: stepping back from the anger in the room before you answer it. Full of stories from very difficult negotiations, told in calm, clear English.

Tip: pause each time he tells a story and summarize it in one cleft sentence: What he did was...

Podcast
Freakonomics Radio -- "How to Change Your Mind"

Why people hold on to a belief even when the facts are against them, and what actually helps them let it go. Useful for any lawyer whose client is sure they are right -- and for anybody who has ever been that client.

Tip: the page has a full transcript. Find two sentences where a guest corrects a common misunderstanding, and rewrite each one as It's... that..., not...
