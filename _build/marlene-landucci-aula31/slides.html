Chapter 3 -- You Have To

Language Discovery

You have to take off your shoes. / You don't have to pay.

Discovery

Listen and Notice

Which sentences say it is necessary? Which ones say it is NOT necessary?

"You have to take off your shoes."

"Women have to cover their hair."

"You don't have to pay. It is free."

"Do I have to bring a gift?"

The Rule

Rules and Customs

UsePatternExample
It is necessaryI / you / we / they have to + verbYou have to take off your shoes.
It is necessary (he / she)he / she / it has to + verbShe has to cover her hair.
It is NOT necessarydon't have to + verbYou don't have to pay.
Ask about a ruleDo I have to + verb?Do I have to bring a gift?
Short answerYes, you do. / No, you don't.No, you don't. It is free.

Key idea: have to means it is necessary. don't have to means it is NOT necessary -- you can, but you do not need to. With he or she, say has to. To ask, say Do I have to...?

Chapter 4 -- A Turkish Home

Dinner with Emre's Family

A conversation, a sign and two messages

Dialogue

Before the Dinner

E

"Hi Marlene! My mother is very happy. You are our guest on Friday."

M

"Thank you, Emre. I want to be polite. Do I have to take off my shoes?"

E

"Yes, you have to take them off at the door. It is a custom in Turkey. We have slippers for you."

M

"OK. And do I have to bring a gift?"

E

"No, you don't have to, but a small box of sweets is very nice."

M

"Perfect. Is it rude to say no to tea?"

E

"A little. My mother always offers tea, so say yes, please!"

M

"Of course! And on Saturday I want to visit the Blue Mosque."

Comprehension

About Emre

1. What does Emre say Marlene has to do at the door?
She has to take off her shoes -- it is a custom in Turkey.
2. What does Emre say about a gift?
She does not have to bring one, but a small box of sweets is very nice.
3. What does Emre's mother always offer?
Tea.
4. What does Emre want Marlene to say when his mother offers tea?
Yes.

Listening 1

A Message from Emre

Read the questions first, then listen. Sound only -- no text.

1. What do guests have to do when they arrive?
They have to take off their shoes at the door -- there are slippers.
2. What gift does Emre say is perfect?
A small box of sweets or some flowers.
3. Why is it polite to say yes to tea?
Because Emre's mother always offers tea -- it is the custom.
4. Does Marlene have to eat everything at the table?
No, she doesn't -- but she should try a little of each dish.
Listening 2

At the Door of the Mosque

Read the questions first, then listen. Sound only -- no text.

1. When is the mosque closed to visitors?
At prayer times, five times a day.
2. Do visitors have to pay?
No -- entrance is free.
3. What do women have to do, and what if they do not have a headscarf?
Cover their hair; they can borrow a headscarf at the door.
4. What does the guide say about photos?
Do not use a flash.
