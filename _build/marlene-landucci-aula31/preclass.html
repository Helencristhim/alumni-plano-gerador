Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. Toque em Listen para ouvir. / Listen to each word and read the example.

Custom -- something people usually do in a place (costume (tradição))
"In Turkey, it is a custom to offer tea." (Na Turquia, é costume oferecer chá.)
Polite -- kind, with good manners (educado)
"It is polite to say thank you." (É educado dizer obrigado.)
Rude -- not polite (grosseiro / mal-educado)
"It is rude to wear shoes in the house." (É falta de educação usar sapatos em casa.)
Mosque -- a building where Muslims pray (mesquita)
"The Blue Mosque is in Istanbul." (A Mesquita Azul fica em Istambul.)
Headscarf -- cloth that covers the hair (lenço de cabeça)
"Women wear a headscarf in the mosque." (As mulheres usam lenço na mesquita.)
Guest -- a person you invite (convidado / visita)
"We have slippers for guests." (Temos chinelos para as visitas.)
Gift -- a thing you give to someone (presente)
"Sweets are a nice gift." (Doces são um bom presente.)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto e responda às perguntas. / Read the text and answer the questions.

Marlene is in Istanbul. Her friend Emre invites her to dinner, and she wants to be polite. She asks: "Do I have to take off my shoes?" Emre says: "Yes, you have to take them off. It is a custom." Marlene asks about a gift. "You don't have to bring one, but sweets are nice." On Saturday she visits the Blue Mosque. She doesn't have to pay, but she has to cover her hair with a headscarf. (Neste texto, have to = ter que; don't have to = não precisa; has to = tem que, com he/she.)

1. What does Marlene have to do at Emre's door?

C Pay for dinner.
2. Does Marlene have to bring a gift?
A No, she doesn't have to.
B Yes, a big gift.
C Yes, money.
3. What does Marlene have to do in the mosque?
A Pay ten euros.
B Take photos.
C Cover her hair.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- have to / don't have to

GRAMMAR

Use have to + verbo para o que é obrigatório (ter que). Use don't have to para o que NÃO é necessário (não precisa -- mas pode). Com he/she: has to. Pergunta: Do I have to...? / Use have to for rules; don't have to when it is not necessary.

RuleExample (EN)Tradução (PT)
ObrigatórioYou have to take off your shoes.Você tem que tirar os sapatos.
Obrigatório (he / she)She has to cover her hair.Ela tem que cobrir o cabelo.
Não é necessárioYou don't have to pay.Você não precisa pagar.
PerguntaDo I have to bring a gift?Eu preciso levar um presente?
Resposta curtaYes, you do. / No, you don't.Sim, precisa. / Não, não precisa.

Key point / Ponto-chave: don't have to NÃO é "é proibido" -- é "não precisa". Para proibido, use Don't + verbo (Don't use a flash). E cuidado com o falso cognato: "costume" em inglês é fantasia; o costume de um país é custom. Em italiano: devo = I have to.
