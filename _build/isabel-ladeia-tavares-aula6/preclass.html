Lesson 06 -- Pre-class

What Was Done, and In What Order -- The Methods Section

The methods section is the only part of a paper written so that a stranger can repeat it, and everything odd about its English comes from that. Key words: a procedure, to carry out, to obtain, a replicate, a control group, to store, apparatus, to pool, to thaw, to calibrate, reproducible, subsequently. Structure: had been for the step that had already finished, and prior to, following and subsequently for the order.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Ten of these you do with your hands. Two you only ever read.

A procedure -- the exact set of actions a study followed, written so that somebody else can run it again
"The procedure was repeated three times on separate days."
To carry out -- to do a piece of work from beginning to end
"All measurements were carried out at room temperature."
To obtain -- to get something as the result of a process, rather than by chance
"The samples were obtained from three separate collection sites."
A replicate -- one of several identical samples run at the same time, so that a single result is never trusted alone
"The replicates were pooled, three into one, before the first measurement."
A control group -- the group that receives no treatment at all, kept only so that the others have something to be compared with
"The control group was not treated at any point."
To store -- to keep material in set conditions until it is needed again
"The samples were stored at minus twenty degrees between collection and analysis."
Apparatus -- the equipment used to run an experiment, taken together as one set
"Once the apparatus had been calibrated, measurements were taken every six hours."
To pool -- to combine several separate samples into one before the next step
"Whether the replicates were pooled before or after the first measurement is not stated."
To thaw -- to let frozen material come back to liquid, slowly and under conditions you choose
"The tubes were thawed on the bench rather than in the water bath."
To calibrate -- to set an instrument against a known value, so that the numbers it gives you can be trusted
"The apparatus had been calibrated before the first run."
Reproducible -- written clearly enough that another laboratory can run it and get the same result
"Take the order words out and the section is still true, and no longer reproducible."
Subsequently -- after that, later in the same sequence. The word a paper uses instead of then
"The plates were photographed and subsequently discarded."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Here is the second section of a paper, and the whole thing is written for one reader: a person in another building who has to run the procedure again next year without asking you anything. Sixty samples were obtained from three separate collection sites in March. Each one was labeled and sealed. All of them were stored at minus twenty degrees. Notice who is missing. Nobody writes I collected the samples, because next year the person will not be you, and only what was done can be repeated.

The second thing the section has to carry is order, and the verbs alone will not carry it. Prior to transport, each sample had been sealed — that step was finished before the transport began, and had been is what says so. The tubes were thawed on the bench for forty minutes. Following thawing, the replicates were pooled, three into one, and the samples were subsequently returned to the freezer. Take those words out and every sentence is still true, and nobody can run it.

Two more things and the section is done. Once the apparatus had been calibrated against a known standard, measurements were carried out every six hours. And the sentence people forget: a control group of twelve samples was not treated at any point, and was kept under exactly the same conditions as the rest. A method that leaves that out is not reproducible, however true it is.

1. "The samples were obtained from three sites." Why is the person not in the sentence?
A Because naming yourself is considered impolite in academic writing.
B Because next year the person will be somebody else, and only what was done can be repeated.
C Because the writer did not do it personally and does not want to say so.
2. "Prior to transport, each sample had been sealed." Which happened first?
A The transport. The sealing was done on arrival.
B The sealing. Had been marks the step that was already finished before the other one.
C They happened at the same time, and the order does not matter.
3. The text says a method can be true and still not reproducible. What makes the difference?
A The order. Without the words that place each step, a reader who was not there cannot run it.
B The length. A method has to be long enough to be taken seriously.
C The vocabulary. A method has to use technical words rather than everyday ones.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Step That Had Already Finished

GRAMMAR

Had been marks what was already done. Prior to and following take a noun, never a verb.

UseFormExample
A finished stepwas / were + third formThe samples were collected in March.
A step that had already finished before ithad been + third formThey had been stored at minus twenty.
Before, in a paperprior to + noun, never a verbPrior to analysis, the tubes were thawed.
After, in a paperfollowing + noun, never a verbFollowing incubation, the plates were photographed.
Later in the same sequencesubsequently + the verbThe plates were subsequently discarded.
What was not donewas / were not + third formThe control group was not treated at any point.

Never: Prior to analyze the samples · Following incubate the plates · The apparatus had calibrated · The samples were subsequent stored · The control group was not treat.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the sentence a methods section would actually use.

You want to say that the tubes were thawed before you measured anything. You write:
A "Prior to measure, the tubes were thawed."
B "Prior to measurement, the tubes were thawed."
C "Prior to I measured, the tubes were thawed."
The calibration was finished before the first reading. You write:
A "The apparatus had been calibrated before the first reading was taken."
B "The apparatus had calibrated before the first reading was taken."
C "The apparatus was calibrating before the first reading was taken."
You need to say what happened to the plates after the incubation. You write:
A "Following incubate, the plates were photographed."
B "Following incubation, the plates were photographed."
C "Following that we photographed the plates."
A reader in another building cannot tell whether the replicates were pooled before or after the first measurement. This means the method is:
A Untrue, and the results should not be published.
B Possibly true, but not reproducible, which is what this section exists to prevent.
C Fine, because the reader can email the author and ask.
