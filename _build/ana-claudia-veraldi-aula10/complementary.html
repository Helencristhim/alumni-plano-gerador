Lesson 10 -- I Used to Live in the City

Video
Kirsten Dirksen -- "They left for city. Offspring came back to restore farmstead"

A family that moved to the city two generations ago, and the children who came back to rebuild the old farmstead by hand. It is your lesson in real life: an entire way of living that people used to have, told by the people who went back for it.

Tip: write down every "used to" and every "would" you hear about the old days. You will notice that states take used to and repeated actions take both.

Listening
NPR Life Kit -- "Techniques to help you figure out how to make a big life change"

Twenty minutes on the moment somebody decides that the old routine is over, and on what actually happens next. Fast, natural American English, with the full transcript free on the same page.

Tip: listen once with the transcript hidden and write the gist in one sentence. Then read along and check how much you already had.

Talk
TED -- Matt Cutts, "Try something new for 30 days"

Three minutes on how a habit is built and how a habit is broken. He describes the person he used to be and the small changes that replaced it, which is exactly the contrast you practiced tonight.

Tip: it is short, so watch it twice. The second time, say his sentences out loud after him and copy the rhythm of "I used to".
