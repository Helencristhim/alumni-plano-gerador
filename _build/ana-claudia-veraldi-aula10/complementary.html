Lesson 10 -- I Used to Live in the City

Documentary
Norway’s tips for achieving happiness -- DW English

Norwegians explaining, in English, the small everyday rituals that organize their week: the walk, the weather, the outdoors whatever the season. It is a whole country describing its own second nature.

Tip: this is the accent of Listening 1. Norwegian English keeps every vowel clean and almost never reduces a word, so play it once at full speed and notice how much you catch without trying.

Podcast
Hidden Brain -- Creatures of Habit, with Wendy Wood

Fifty minutes on why almost half of what you do every day was never decided by you. The title is one of tonight’s expressions, and the whole episode is people describing routines that are over.

Tip: count five sentences with used to and five with would. Write down the three words that come immediately before every would — they are almost always a time marker.

Archive Film
Could You Start a New Life in the Country? -- In Our Nature, 1978, BBC Archive

British families in 1978 turning their back on the city for the Welsh countryside, and saying out loud what they expect to lose. Your own decision, filmed forty years before you made it.

Tip: this is the accent of Listening 2, and the speakers are ordinary people, not presenters. Listen for the moment somebody says what they used to do — that sentence is always where the real reason is.
