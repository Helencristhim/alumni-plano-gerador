Two Documents, One Course

Bring two documents. Nothing to study.
▼

Before class

In class we will work with two documents about the same course — what it promised, and what it later did. You do not need to prepare them: they are in the lesson.

What you can bring is one real case. Something at work that was written down once and is now done differently. You do not have to bring the papers — just be ready to say what changed and how you know.
