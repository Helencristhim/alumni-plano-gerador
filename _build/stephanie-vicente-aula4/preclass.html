Lesson 04 · Block 1 — Build

The Feedback You Have to Give

Nothing to prepare. Come as you are — that is the point.
▼

Before this one, do nothing

There is no pre-class for this lesson, and that is deliberate. In the first part of the class you are going to give a piece of feedback with no phrases, no model and no preparation — just the way you would give it today.

That first attempt is what the whole lesson is built on. If you learn the phrases beforehand, you will produce the phrases instead of your own English, and neither of us finds out what you already do well.

So: nothing to read, nothing to memorise. Come and talk.
