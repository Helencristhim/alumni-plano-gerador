Grammar Discovery

Number one

Casa Verde is the cheapest restaurant on the list.
The Municipal Market is the most famous market in the city.
Bella Mesa is the best restaurant in Pinheiros.
Six o'clock is the worst time for traffic.

What do the orange words have in common?

short word: cheap → the cheapest · long word: the most famous

Number one in a group of three or more: always the. A short word gets -est, a long word gets the most before it. Special: good → the best, bad → the worst.

The Code

Two things or the group?

How it changes

cheap → cheaper → the cheapest
big → bigger → the biggest (gg)
noisy → noisier → the noisiest (y → i)
famous → more famous → the most famous
good → better → the best
bad → worse → the worst

Never

It's the most big park.
short word: the biggest
It's most beautiful park.
number one needs the
It's the most good restaurant.
special: the best

Question: What is the best restaurant in Pinheiros? — Bella Mesa. It's the best.

Sound Check

"-est" is soft

It's the cheapest restaurant.

CHEAP-ist: strong on the first part

It's the most famous market.

FA-mous: strong on the first part

Six o'clock is the worst time.

"worst" sounds like WURST, one short sound
