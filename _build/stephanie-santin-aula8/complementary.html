Lesson 8 -- The Doctor You Used To Be

Talk
Quyen Nguyen -- "Color-coded surgery"

A surgeon shows how a dye makes tumors glow in the operating room. Short, visual, and full of the vocabulary of the room you used to work in.

Tip: after watching, describe in four sentences what you used to do in the operating room and what you would do before every operation.

Talk
Sandeep Jauhar -- "How your emotions change the shape of your heart"

A cardiologist tells his grandfather's story and his own. Half the talk is about a medicine that used to be practised differently, which is exactly today's grammar in use.

Tip: count the sentences with used to and would. Then say three of your own about your first year in the hospital.

TED-Ed -- "How do cigarettes affect the body?"

A short animated lesson with comprehension questions. Ordinary patient-facing vocabulary about something you have explained a thousand times in Portuguese.

Tip: answer the questions out loud. Then say what a patient who used to smoke would notice in the first year after stopping.
