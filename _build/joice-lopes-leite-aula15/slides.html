Chapter 3: The Code

Same Form, New Job

now · and the future that is already decided

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am working from home right now."

"We are leaving on the fourth."

"I am presenting on the fifth, at three."

"Next week I am travelling to Recife."

JobHow you knowExample
NOWnow, right now, at the momentI am working right now.
SET FUTUREa date or a future wordI am presenting on the fifth.
negativeis / are + not + verb-ingI am not coming on Tuesday.
questionAre you + verb-ing + when?Are you coming in November?
Key ideaUse this form for the future only when it is ALREADY DECIDED: a date, a room, a ticket. If it is only an idea, it is another form (Lesson 20).

Future words that need no preposition: tomorrow, next week, next month, tonight.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"In the next week I am travelling to Recife."
"Next week I am travelling to Recife."
"I present on the fifth, at three."
"I am presenting on the fifth, at three."
"We will leave on the fourth. The tickets are ready."
"We are leaving on the fourth. The tickets are ready."

When the ticket is bought and the room is booked, English says I am leaving -- not I will leave.

Chapter 4: The Itinerary

Four Days in Belém

and one of them is yours

Dialogue

When Am I Presenting?

O
Hi, Joice. Are you coming to Belém in November?
J
Yes, I am coming. We are leaving on the fourth, right?
O
Exactly. We are arriving in the afternoon, and we are visiting two schools on the fifth.
J
Good. And when am I presenting? Is the plan ready?
O
You are presenting on the fifth, at three, in the small room.
J
Perfect. My material is almost ready. I am finishing it this week.
O
Great. And are you coming back with us on the seventh?
J
Yes. But next week I am not here: I am travelling to Recife.
O
No problem. Can you send me your slides soon?
J
I am sending them tomorrow morning. They are almost ready.
Comprehension

About Omar

1. When is the group arriving in Belém?
On the fourth, in the afternoon.
2. What is the group doing on the fifth in the morning?
They are visiting two public schools.
3. What does Omar ask Joice to send?
Her slides, soon.
