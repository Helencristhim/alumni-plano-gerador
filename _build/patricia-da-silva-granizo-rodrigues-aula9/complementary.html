Why the best hire might not have the perfect resume -- Regina Hartley, TED

Ten minutes on hiring judgement, from an HR director. Directly useful when you are about to write eleven job descriptions for a market you do not know.

Tip: after it, describe one of your eleven roles out loud in English -- what you would actually be looking for, not what the template says.
