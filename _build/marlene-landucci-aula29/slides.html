Chapter 3 -- A Kilo Of

Language Discovery

A kilo of tomatoes, please. / Do you have any pecorino?

Discovery

Listen and Notice

Look at the orange words. What comes before the food?

"I would like half a kilo of tomatoes, please."

"Can I have a piece of pecorino?"

"I want some olive oil -- a bottle of oil for my family."

"Do you have any fresh basil?"

The Rule

Quantities and Some / Any

UsePatternExample
Weighta kilo of / half a kilo of + foodHalf a kilo of tomatoes, please.
One part of a thinga piece of + foodA piece of pecorino, please.
A containera bottle of / a jar of + foodA bottle of olive oil.
Positive, no exact numbersome + foodI want some cheese.
QuestionDo you have any + food?Do you have any fresh basil?
Price (review)How much is + it?How much is a kilo?

Key idea: Put of after the quantity: a kilo of tomatoes, a piece of cheese, a bottle of oil. Use some in a normal sentence and any in a question: Do you have any basil?

Chapter 4 -- At San Lorenzo

At the Market

A seller, a receipt, and two messages

Dialogue

At the Leather Stall

L

"Good morning, madam. Are you looking for a bag? This one is real leather."

M

"Good morning. It is beautiful. Is it handmade?"

L

"Yes, it is handmade here in Florence. It is sixty euros."

M

"Hmm, it is a little expensive. Do you have any small bags?"

L

"Yes, this small one is forty-five. And I have some silk scarves, too."

M

"I would like the small bag and one scarf as a souvenir. What is your best price?"

L

"For the two, fifty-five euros. It is a bargain, believe me."

M

"OK, fifty-five. Thank you very much. Can I have a receipt, please?"

Comprehension

About Luca

1. Where does Luca say the big bag is made?
Here in Florence -- it is handmade.
2. How much is the big bag, and how much is the small bag?
Sixty euros; the small one is forty-five.
3. What else does Luca sell?
Silk scarves.
4. What is Luca's best price for the small bag and one scarf?
Fifty-five euros.
