Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Celeste Headlee: “10 ways to have a better conversation” (12 min)

A radio host who has talked to thousands of strangers gives ten simple rules. Rule number five is the lesson: ask open questions, and then follow what the other person says.

Tip: pick the two rules you break most often. Try one of them at your next party or with your sailing team, and tell your teacher what happened.

TED Talk
Julian Treasure: “How to speak so that people want to listen” (10 min)

A sound expert on why some voices make people lean in and others make them look away. The part about the music of your voice connects with the questions you practiced today.

Tip: listen to the part about pitch and pace. Then record your five party questions and listen: does your voice go down at the end of the wh- questions?

TED Talk
Marc Pachter: “The art of the interview” (15 min)

A museum director who interviewed famous people for years on how to get anybody to open up: the right question, at the right moment, and then silence while the other person talks.

Tip: write down the question he likes best. Use it with a real person this week, and bring the answer you got to class.
