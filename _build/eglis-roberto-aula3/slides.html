Three Strangers

One Party, Three Conversations

Your mission with each guest: find two things you have in common, and ask one question that starts with Can I ask or Do you know.

Marco
An architect from Milan. He answers every question, but he never asks one back.
Julia
An engineer. Very quiet. She answers in one word, until you find the right topic.
Ben
A musician. He loves to talk about himself. You have to find a way in.

Under Pressure

The Host and the New Guy

Round 1 is a loud party in somebody’s apartment. Round 2 is the first day of a new person in your sailing team. In both, the goal is the same: by the end, you are not strangers anymore.

  • Round 1: the host introduces you to people, talks fast and changes the subject. You keep up.
  • Round 2: you welcome a new crew member. You ask the questions and make him feel at ease.
