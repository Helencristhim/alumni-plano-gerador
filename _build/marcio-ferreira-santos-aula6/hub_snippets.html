The Official Version -- Passive Voice at the Top

How pricing decisions are made, reported and challenged in writing. Key words: benchmark, threshold, variance, compliance, procurement, disclosure, accountability, to sign off. Structure: the passive voice in the present, the past, the present perfect and with modals.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Benchmark -- a reference number you compare your own result against
"A new benchmark was set for eleven categories."
Threshold -- the point where a rule starts to apply
"Every change above the threshold is approved by two people."
Variance -- the difference between the result and what was expected
"Any variance above ten percent is reviewed by compliance."
Compliance -- following the rules that the company or the law requires
"The change was reviewed by the compliance team."
Procurement -- the work of buying what the company needs from suppliers
"The contract was signed by the procurement team."
Disclosure -- information a company publishes because it has to
"The full disclosure is on page forty-one."
Accountability -- answering for a decision, whatever the result is
"Accountability for the price sits with the commercial director."
To sign off -- to formally approve something so that it can go ahead
"Every change above the threshold must be signed off by two people."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

During the year, the pricing model was rebuilt at SKU level and a new benchmark was set for eleven categories. A gain of two hundred and forty million was reported in commercial efficiency. This figure has been audited by an external firm and is included in the full disclosure. No store was closed to reach it. A new sign-off rule was introduced in the second half of the year: every price change above the threshold must be signed off by two people, and any variance above ten percent is reviewed by the compliance team.

1. Why does the report say "the model was rebuilt" instead of "the team rebuilt the model"?
A Because the focus is the decision and the number, not the person who did it.
B Because nobody in the company knows who rebuilt the model.
C Because the active voice is not correct in English reports.
2. How do we build the passive?
A With the verb "be" plus the past participle.
B With the verb "have" plus the base verb.
C With the past simple and the word "by".
3. Which sentence is correct?
A "Every change must be sign off by two people."
B "Every change must be signed off by two people."
C "Every change must signed off by two people."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Passive Voice

GRAMMAR

The formula never changes: be plus the past participle. Only "be" moves with the tense.

TenseFormExample
Presentam / is / are + past participlePrices are set every week.
Pastwas / were + past participleThe model was rebuilt last year.
Present perfecthas / have been + past participleThe figure has been audited.
With a modalmodal + be + past participleIt must be signed off by two people.
The "by" partOptional. Use the passive for the process in a company document, and the active voice in your CV or in an interview, where the author matters.
