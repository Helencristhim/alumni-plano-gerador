Chapter 3: The Code

The Passive Voice

The gain was reported · prices are set · it must be signed off

Grammar Discovery

Read and Notice

"Prices are set by the commercial team."

"The model was rebuilt at SKU level."

"The figure has been audited by an external firm."

"Every change must be signed off by two people."

In all four sentences, who did the action? And where is that person in the sentence?

TenseFormExample
Presentam / is / are + past participlePrices are set weekly.
Pastwas / were + past participleThe model was rebuilt last year.
Present perfecthas / have been + past participleThe figure has been audited.
With a modalmodal + be + past participleIt must be signed off by two people.
The "by" partOptional. Add by the team only when the person matters. Leave it out when the decision matters more than the author.

The formula never changes: be + past participle. Only the first word moves with the tense.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"The model was rebuild at SKU level."
"The model was rebuilt at SKU level."
"The prices reviewed in all four regions."
"The prices were reviewed in all four regions."
"Every change must be sign off by two people."
"Every change must be signed off by two people."

The passive always needs be plus the past participle. Rebuild becomes rebuilt, sign off becomes signed off, and the verb be can never disappear.

Detail

Whose Result Is It?

Company document → passive  ·  your CV or interview → active

REPORT: "A gain of 240 million was reported." → INTERVIEW: "My team ___delivered a gain of 240 million."
REPORT: "The model was rebuilt at SKU level." → INTERVIEW: "I ___rebuilt the model at SKU level."
INTERVIEW: "We changed the sign-off rule." → REPORT: "A new sign-off rule ___was introduced."
INTERVIEW: "An external firm audited the figure." → REPORT: "The figure ___was audited by an external firm."

That no store was closed to reach the number.

Listening 2

The New Sign-Off Rule

An internal briefing about how prices are approved. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What changes from Monday?
Every change above the threshold must be signed off by two people, and the reason must be recorded.
2. Why is the rule not about trust?
It is about disclosure: the regulator must find the answer in one place, not in six email threads.
3. What happens to a variance above ten percent?
It is reviewed by the compliance team.
