Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
United Nations — “What is the International Court of Justice? The Role and Activities of the ICJ” (YouTube)

The official United Nations video about the court in today’s reading: who can use it, how the judges are chosen and what an advisory opinion is.

Tip: watch once and write down every relative clause you hear, like the court which or states that.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Series
The Good Fight — Season 1, Episode 1, “Inauguration”

A Chicago law firm, courtrooms, rulings and appeals, in fast, natural American English. Not international law, but the vocabulary of today’s lesson is in almost every scene.

Tip: when a judge speaks, pause and say the decision again in plain English.

See the episode on TVmaze ↗
Talk
Bryan Stevenson — “We need to talk about an injustice” (TED)

A lawyer who has spent his career in courtrooms on what justice means when the system fails the people it should protect. One of the most watched TED talks about law.

Tip: listen for how he uses stories to back up his argument, and summarize one story in three blocks.
