Grammar Discovery

Five questions, five tenses

What time do you get up on school days?
What are you doing right now?
What did you do last Saturday?
How long have you been here?
What are you going to do at the weekend?

The question tells you which tense to use

Nothing new here. Every one of these five you have already learned, one at a time. What changes today is that they arrive in random order, and the answer has to match the question. Listen to the auxiliary at the front — do, are, did, have, are going to — and give it back.

The Code

Match the auxiliary

The question starts with

Do you...? / What time do you...?
Are you ...ing?
Did you...?
Have you...? / How long have you...?
Are you going to...?

So the answer is

I get up at six. (routine)
I am doing an assignment. (now)
I went to the beach. (finished)
I have been here for a month. (still true)
I am going to work. (decided)

If you lose the thread, buy time in English: Sorry, say that again? Never switch to Portuguese to think.

Z
Zoe: You surf before school every day? Even in winter?
R
Renato: Every day. Well, I did not go on Tuesday, because I had two assignments to catch up on.
Z
Zoe: Fair. What are you doing this weekend?
R

Z
Zoe: You have been here a month and you already have a job. Right, you have settled in.

Listening 1

A normal week

1. What time does she get up, and why?
Half past five, because the good waves are before school and school starts at nine.
2. What is she doing at the moment, and what is the problem?
An assignment on Australian history, due Friday, and she has not started it properly yet.
3. What is she going to do this weekend?
Work at the surf shop again, and then sleep for about eleven hours.
Listening 2

The first month, week by week

1. What does he say about week two?
It is horrible, and that is when most students call home crying.
2. What has changed by week four?
They have a routine and they have stopped translating everything in their head.
3. What are his two pieces of advice?
Join something in the first seven days, and say yes to every invitation for a month.
