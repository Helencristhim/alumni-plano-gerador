Lesson 4 -- English Around the World

Talk
Speak it like you are playing a video game -- Marianna Pascal, TEDxPenangRoad (12 min)

The single best talk ever given about what you are doing wrong when you speak English. Her point: people who communicate well are not the ones with the best grammar, they are the ones focused on the other person instead of on themselves. Watch this one twice.

Tip: she is talking about exactly what we did in class. Count how many times she says the word perfect and notice what she thinks of it.

Podcast
What Is Normal? How Culture Affects Communication Styles

Why a Dutch colleague sounds blunt to you and you sound vague to them, and why neither of you is being rude. This is the theory behind the register scale we practised in class, with real examples from international teams.

Tip: listen once for the gist, exactly as we did with Lars and Bram. Then write down the one idea you want to remember, in one sentence.

Listening practice
Global Language -- Belle Ho, TEDxYouth (8 min)

A short talk on what happens to a language when more people speak it as a second language than as a first. Useful, and short enough to listen to twice in one sitting.

Tip: first time, no subtitles, and only answer one question -- what is she arguing? Second time, with subtitles, and check how much you had already got.
