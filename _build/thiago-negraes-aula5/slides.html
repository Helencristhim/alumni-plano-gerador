Chapter 3: What Happened

Tell It in the Past

Three shapes, and one that surprises everybody

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The airline lost my bag."

"I filled out the form at the desk."

"They did not give me a voucher."

"Did you speak to the manager?"

ShapeFormExample
Positive, regularverb + -edI filled out the form.
Positive, irregulara new word to learnThe airline lost my bag.
Negativedid not + base verbThey did not give me a voucher.
QuestionDid + subject + base verbDid you speak to the manager?

The past is marked once. As soon as did appears, it carries the past alone and the main verb goes back to its base form.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"I did not received a refund."

✓ RIGHT

"I did not receive a refund."

✗ WRONG

"Yesterday I go to lost and found."

✓ RIGHT

"Yesterday I went to lost and found."

One past mark per sentence. With did, the main verb stays in the base form; without did, the verb itself changes.

Chapter 4: At the Desk

The Belt Stopped

Now you have to tell the story

Dialogue

The Missing Bag

T
Good evening. My bag is missing. It did not come off the plane.
A
I am sorry. Which flight did you take?
T
Flight 214 from Chicago. I waited at the belt for forty minutes.
A
Did you check the bag in Chicago or in Sao Paulo?
T
In Sao Paulo. Nobody told me anything in Chicago.
A
Please fill out this form with your address and your hotel.
T
Here you are. I have a meeting tomorrow and all my clothes were in that bag.
A
I found it. It stayed in Chicago and it arrives at six in the morning.
T
And tonight? Do I get a voucher for what I need to buy?
A
Yes, one hundred dollars. Keep the receipts, because your insurance may pay the rest.
T
Thank you. Where do I file a complaint if it does not arrive?
Did You Catch It?

About the Agent

1. What two questions does she ask before she helps?
Which flight he took, and where he checked the bag in.
2. What does she find out about the bag?
That it stayed in Chicago and arrives at six in the morning.
3. Why does she tell him to keep the receipts?
Because his insurance may pay whatever the voucher does not cover.
Listening

The Baggage Announcement

An airport announcement. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. Where do passengers with a missing bag have to go, and what do they do there?
To the lost and found office on the arrivals level, to fill out a delayed baggage form.
2. How long did yesterday's delayed bags take?
They were all delivered within twenty-four hours.
3. Why should passengers keep their receipts?
Because the airline does not refund what the travel insurance already covers.
Listening

The Manager Calls

A voicemail about your room. Catch what he offers.

0:000:00
1. What is the problem with the room tonight?
The hotel was overbooked and they could not keep his room.
2. What did the hotel already do about it?
They booked a room at a sister hotel four blocks away and paid for the taxi.
3. What two things will he receive?
A voucher for dinner and a full refund of tonight's rate.
