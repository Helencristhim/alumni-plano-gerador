The question of the day: when did you decide? Before, with a date = -ing. Before, in your head = going to. Right now = will.

Fashion show mistakes

0 / 5 errors found

"I'm going to sewing the dress tonight."
"I'm going to sew the dress tonight. — going to + the base form."
"We presenting our collection on Friday."
"We are presenting our collection on Friday. — the present continuous needs am / is / are."
"I will to bring the sequins."
"I will bring the sequins. — no to after will."
"Have you ever designed an outfit? Yes, I had."
"Yes, I have. — your life up to now: have, not had."
"Tomorrow I meet Ruby at the store at three."
"Tomorrow I'm meeting Ruby at three. — a time and a person in the calendar: present continuous."
