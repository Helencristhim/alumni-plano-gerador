Lesson 16 — Pre-class

The Fashion Show Plan

About ten minutes. Eight fashion words — sketch, fabric, sew, outfit, runway, mood board, sequins, stitch — and three ways to talk about the future: we're presenting on Friday, I'm going to use old jeans, I'll ask my grandma.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example out loud.

Sketch -- a quick drawing that shows your idea
"Mrs. Lee is looking at our sketches tomorrow."
Fabric -- the material that clothes are made of
"I'm going to buy blue fabric on Saturday."
Sew -- to join pieces of fabric with a needle and thread
"Don't worry, I'll sew it for you."
Outfit -- all the clothes you wear together
"We're going to make five outfits for the show."
Runway -- the long path where models walk in a fashion show
"Look at that runway. It's going to be amazing!"
Mood board -- a board with pictures and colors that show the feeling of your ideas
"Our mood board is full of colors from Rio."
Sequins -- small shiny circles you sew on clothes
"I'm sewing sequins on an old T-shirt tonight."
Stitch -- one small line of thread that goes in and out of the fabric
"The machine stopped in the middle of a stitch."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read Sophia's messages about the school fashion show. The words in bold talk about the future in three different ways.

Monday — Big news: we're presenting our outfits on Friday at ten, in the gym. The teacher is meeting us tomorrow after class.

Tuesday — I have decided: I'm going to make a skirt with old jeans. My sketch is ready!

Wednesday — Ruby: “I don't have any sequins!” Me: “No problem, I'll bring mine.”

"We're presenting our outfits on Friday at ten." Why the present continuous?
A Because it is happening right now, at this moment.
B Because it is in the calendar, with a day, a time and other people.
C Because she is not sure about it.
"I'm going to make a skirt with old jeans." When did Sophia decide?
A Before, and it is her plan.
B Right now, while she is writing the message to her friends.
C She has not decided yet.
"No problem, I'll bring mine." Why will here?
A Because it is in the school calendar.
B Because Sophia had planned this a long time before the message.
C Because she decides and offers it right now.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

When did you decide?

  • present continuous = it is in the calendar, with a time or other people: We're presenting on Friday at ten.
  • going to = your plan, decided before: I'm going to make a skirt. Also when you can see it coming: It's going to rain!
  • will = you decide right now, as you speak (offers and promises too): I'll bring mine!

Remember: after will and going to, the base form: I'm going to sew, I'll bring.

Reflection
