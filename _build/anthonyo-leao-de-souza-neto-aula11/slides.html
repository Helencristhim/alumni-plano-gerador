Chapter 3: Was and Were

Two Words, and the Past Is Open

it is → it was · they are → they were

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The flight was long."

"The hotel was comfortable."

"The streets were crowded."

"We were lucky."

NowBeforeExample
I am · he is · she is · it iswasI was tired · the flight was long
you are · we are · they arewerewe were lucky · the streets were crowded
One thing or many thingsOne = was (the hotel, the city, my room). Many = were (the streets, the people, my bags).
Portuguese has four, English has twoera, foi, estava, estive — all of them are was or were. You never choose by meaning. You choose by how many.

Two words. That is the whole past of to be, and it is the most useful thing you will learn this month.

The flight was · the streets were · I was · we were — five lines, and the whole week is told.

1. How was your trip?

2. How was the hotel?
The hotel was comfortable, and my room was quiet.
3. And the city? Was it nice?
The streets were crowded, but the city was beautiful and the food was great.

2. What two things were wrong for her?

Her weekend was good. She was at her sister's house.

Give the Question Back

Scenario: Same breakfast, but now every answer you give ends with a question for the other person. Nobody stops talking, and nobody talks about work.

Keywords:

And you? How was your week? Where were you? Was it cold here?
Role-Play — Free

Tell Me About Your Last Trip

Scenario: One question, and then two minutes with nobody helping you. A real trip: where you were, how the flight was, how the hotel was, what was good and what was terrible.

No keywords this time. Two minutes, no stopping. Different from the model is not wrong — if the week is clear to the other person, it worked.
