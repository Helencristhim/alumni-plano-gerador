"How to use the third conditional" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on exactly the structure of this lesson, drilled by two native speakers. Pay attention to how fast they say "would have" -- it comes out as one syllable.

Tip: after each of their examples, say your own version out loud about a real decision at work. Contract the modal every time: should've, would've, could've.

Booth research with an uncomfortable finding: people learn far less from their own failures than from other people's, because their own are threatening. Short, and directly about the exercise you did in class.
