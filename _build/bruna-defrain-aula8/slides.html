Listen and Notice

"I like the treadmill."

"I don't like weights."

"Do you train every day?"

How do we say no? How do we ask?

To do thisSayExample
Say what you doI / you + verbI train every morning.
Say noI don't + verbI don't like weights.
AskDo you + verb?Do you like yoga? Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

The Gym Sign

Park Hotel Fitness Room — Welcome!

Our gym is open every day, from six in the morning to ten at night.

Please bring a towel and water. A locker for your bag is free: the key is at the front desk.

We have three treadmills, two bikes, and free weights. Please put the weights back after use.

Our yoga class is on Monday and Wednesday at seven. Do you want help? Our trainer, Mike, is here every morning.

No children under sixteen, please. Have a great workout!

Sign — Park Hotel, London
