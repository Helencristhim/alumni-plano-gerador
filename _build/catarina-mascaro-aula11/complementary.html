Series
Down to Earth with Zac Efron — Season 1, Episode 1 (Iceland)

Two friends travel to places they have never been and try things for the first time — hot springs, glaciers, very strange food. Every few minutes somebody says "I’ve never seen anything like this".

Tip: watch with English subtitles and count the "I’ve never" sentences. Which of their experiences would go on your bucket list?

6 Minute English — "Microadventures!"

Six minutes about small adventures close to home — you do not need a plane ticket to do something you have never done. Free, with the full transcript.

Tip: listen once, then plan one microadventure you have never tried. Tell your teacher about it next class.

YouTube
Creedence Clearwater Revival — "Have You Ever Seen The Rain"

A classic song built on the question of this lesson. The chorus repeats "I want to know, have you ever seen the rain?" — perfect for practicing the "have you ever" sound.

Tip: sing the chorus along with the video. Then change it: "Have you ever seen the snow?", "Have you ever..." — make three new lines.
