have / has + been, seen, tried...

This is about your life so far, not about a day on the calendar. We do not say when. The moment you say when — "last year", "when I was nine" — you go back to the past simple.

Listening 1

A bucket list, out loud

1. What has she never seen, not even once?
Snow.
2. Why doesn’t the beach count?
She has been there about a hundred times.
3. What is number one on her list, and what happened the last time she tried something like it?
A really big roller coaster. She went on a small one twice when she was nine, and she screamed the whole time.

Welcome to the museum

2. What have they seen so far?
Only the entrance.
3. What is the one rule, and why is the last sentence funny?
Do not touch the moon rocks. The rocks have been to space, and the students have not.
