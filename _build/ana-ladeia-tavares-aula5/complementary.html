TED-Ed -- "The best way to apologize (according to science)"

Five animated minutes on what the research actually shows: which parts of an apology change how it is received, which parts change nothing, and why offering to repair the harm matters more than the words around it. Clear narration at a speed you can follow without subtitles.

Tip: pause at each element it names and say a sentence with wish that would carry that element. You should end up with four sentences, two about the past and two about now.
