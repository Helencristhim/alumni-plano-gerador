Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Where do you live?
What do you do on the weekend?
When does the meeting start?
How do you go to work?

Look at the orange words. What comes right after the question word, every time?

UseFormExample
PlaceWhere + do / does + ...Where do you live?
Thing / activityWhat + do / does + ...What do you do on the weekend?
TimeWhen + do / does + ...When does the meeting start?
PersonWho + do / does + ... + withWho do you work with?
WayHow + do / does + ...How do you go to work?
CarefulThe question word comes FIRST, then DO or DOES. "Where you live?" is not a question in English. With he, she or it it is DOES, and the verb loses the S: "Where does she live?"
