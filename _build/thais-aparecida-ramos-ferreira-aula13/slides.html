Chapter 3: Like and Would Like

Always, or Tonight?

I like watching movies · I'd like to watch a movie

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I like watching series in English."

"My daughter likes playing tennis."

"I don't like waking up early."

"I would like to see a French movie tonight."

FormMeaningExample
I like + verb-ingalways, in generalI like watching series.
She likes + verb-ingthe S goes on LIKE, never on the second verbShe likes reading.
I don't like + verb-ingwhat you avoidI don't like waking up early.
I would like to + verbnow, tonight, this tripI would like to see a movie.

One word tells you which one it is: if the second verb ends in -ing, it is your life. If it comes after to, it is tonight. And I'd like is just the short way of saying I would like.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"I like watch series."

✓ RIGHT

"I like watching series."

✗ WRONG

"She like playing tennis."

✓ RIGHT

"She likes playing tennis."

✗ WRONG

"I would like watching a movie tonight."

✓ RIGHT

"I would like to watch a movie tonight."

Portuguese uses de ver in both sentences, so the ear does not hear the difference. English marks it: -ing for your life, to for tonight.
