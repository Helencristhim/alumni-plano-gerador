Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
How I Built This — “KAYAK: Paul English”

A founder who built a company, ran it for years and sold it, telling the whole story in the first person. This is the grammar of today for an hour without a break, and the interviewer keeps asking exactly the question you practised: what did you used to do back then?

Tip: write down every sentence you hear that starts with I used to or contains would for a past habit. Aim for ten. You will get there before minute thirty.

Video
Ben Horowitz — “Delegation for a Startup CEO”

Short and blunt, from somebody who ran a company of six hundred people before he started investing. He describes the exact moment a founder has to stop doing the thing he is best at, which is the hardest sentence in this lesson to say out loud about yourself.

Tip: after watching, say his argument back in your own words, out loud, using I used to three times. Do not write it first.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Talk
WorkLife with Adam Grant — “Burnout Is Everyone's Problem”

The organizational psychologist on why exhaustion is a design problem and not a personal failure. Clear, unhurried American English, and one of the eight words from this lesson is the subject of the whole episode.

Tip: listen once for the argument and once for the stories. In the second pass, note how often he describes a past habit rather than a single event — that choice is the grammar of this lesson.
