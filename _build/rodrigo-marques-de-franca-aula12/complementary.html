Lesson 12 -- Checkpoint: Taking Stock

Podcast
NPR Short Wave -- "Learning a second language as an adult can be hard, but it's not impossible"

A short science episode on what actually changes when adults learn a language, and why the idea that it is too late does not survive the evidence. A good companion to the plateau text from this lesson.

Tip: after listening, say in one minute which part of the episode describes you, and which part does not. Use although at least once.

Talk
Lýdia Machová -- "The secrets of learning a new language" (TED)

A polyglot on the habits that separate learners who keep improving from learners who stop. Practical, specific, and very close to the benchmark idea you used today.

Tip: pick one habit from the talk and turn it into a plan for the next block. Say it out loud as a first conditional: if I do this every week, I will...

Series
The Queen's Gambit (Netflix) -- Episode 1, "Openings"

A limited series about talent, practice and the long stretches where nothing seems to improve. The first episode is the story of somebody building a skill alone, long before anybody measures it.

Tip: watch with English subtitles, then describe the first episode in two minutes using the narrative tenses from lesson 10: what was happening, and what had happened before.
