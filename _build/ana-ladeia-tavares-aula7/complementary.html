NPR Life Kit -- "4 negotiation tactics to try in everyday life"

Short, practical and entirely free of jargon: four moves that work whether the table is a mediation or a flat-share. The most useful part for you is the section on asking the question that makes the other side name what they actually want. Transcript on the page.

TED -- Margaret Heffernan, "Dare to disagree"

A former chief executive on why the people who agree with you are the least useful people in the room, told through the story of a doctor who spent twenty-five years being contradicted on purpose. Twelve minutes, clear delivery, and directly relevant to a study group where you feel like the least advanced person.

Tip: watch it once and then say out loud, in three sentences, what you would disagree with in your study group this Friday if disagreeing were free. Start one of them with "What I actually think is".
