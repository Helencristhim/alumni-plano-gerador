"The Galata Tower is very tall." (A Torre de Gálata é muito alta.)
Century -- one hundred years (século)
"It was built in the 15th century." (Foi construído no século XV.)
Empire -- many countries under one ruler (império)
"Istanbul was the capital of an empire." (Istambul era a capital de um império.)
Emperor -- the ruler of an empire (imperador)
"The emperor lived in the palace." (O imperador morava no palácio.)
Ruins -- what is left of an old building (ruínas)
"We saw old ruins near the walls." (Vimos ruínas antigas perto das muralhas.)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto e responda às perguntas. / Read the text and answer the questions.

Marlene visits the old part of Istanbul with Emre. First they see Hagia Sophia. It was built in 537, and it is the oldest of the three wonders. For almost one thousand years it was the biggest church in the world, and its dome is one of the most beautiful in the city. Then they visit Topkapi Palace. It was built in the 15th century, and the emperors of the Ottoman Empire lived there. In the evening they go up the Galata Tower: it has the best view of Istanbul. Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Superlatives (the biggest / the most beautiful) + It was built in

GRAMMAR

O comparativo compara DUAS coisas (bigger than -- aula 26). O superlativo compara com TODAS: palavra curta = the + -est (the oldest); palavra longa = the most (the most beautiful). Para história: It was built in + ano. / Superlatives compare one thing with all the others.

RuleExample (EN)Tradução (PT)
Palavra curtaIt is the oldest building.É o prédio mais antigo.
big: dobra a letraIt was the biggest church.Era a maior igreja.
Palavra longaIt is the most beautiful dome.É a cúpula mais bonita.
Irregularthe best view / the worst daya melhor vista / o pior dia
Um entre muitosone of the oldest towersuma das torres mais antigas
HistóriaIt was built in 1348.Foi construída em 1348.

Key point / Ponto-chave: não esqueça o the: "It is oldest" está errado -- é It is THE oldest. Nunca "the most big": é the biggest. E depois de one of the, a palavra vai no plural: one of the oldest towers. Em italiano: il più bello = the most beautiful.
