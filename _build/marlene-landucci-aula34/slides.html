Chapter 4 -- The Biggest

Language Discovery

the biggest church / the most beautiful dome / It was built in 537

Discovery

Listen and Notice

Compare "bigger than" (Lesson 26) with the orange words. What changes?

"Hagia Sophia is the oldest of the three."

"It was the biggest church in the world."

"It is one of the most beautiful domes in Istanbul."

"It was built in 537."

The Rule

The Biggest and the Most

UsePatternExample
Short adjectivethe + adjective-estthe oldest / the tallest
big, hot: double letterthe + biggestthe biggest church
Long adjectivethe most + adjectivethe most beautiful dome
Irregulargood -> the best / bad -> the worstthe best view of the city
One in a groupone of the + superlative + pluralone of the oldest towers
When (history)It was built in + year / centuryIt was built in 1348.

Key idea: Comparatives compare two things (bigger than, Lesson 26). Superlatives compare one thing with ALL the others: the biggest, the most beautiful. Always use the. For history, say It was built in + the year.

Dialogue

In the Palace Gardens

E

"Here we are, Marlene: Topkapi Palace. It was built in the 15th century."

M

"It is huge! Is it the biggest palace in Istanbul?"

E

"It is one of the biggest. The Ottoman emperors lived here for about four hundred years."

M

"Amazing. And that great dome over there -- is it Hagia Sophia?"

E

"Yes. It is the oldest of all these buildings. It was built in 537, in the Byzantine Empire."

M

"For me, it is the most beautiful building in the city. And where is the Galata Tower?"

E

"On the other side of the water. It has the best view of Istanbul. Near the old walls, you can see ruins too."

M

"Then tonight we go to the tower. I want the best photo of my trip!"
