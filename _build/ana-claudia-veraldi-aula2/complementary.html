Lesson 2 -- A Town of 40,000

Series
I Escaped to the Country -- Season 9, Episode 1: Somerset Revisit (full episode)

The BBC revisits families who left a British city for a small town, years after the move. They walk through the houses they bought and say plainly what is better, what is worse, and what they did not expect. It is your own decision, told by strangers, in British English.

Tip: this is the accent from tonight's dialogue with Tom. Count how many times somebody says far, much or nowhere near before a comparison -- it happens constantly, and that is the point.

Podcast
The Urban Exodus Podcast, E66 -- A tech entrepreneur leaves San Francisco for rural Maine

Grace Chen ran a company in one of the most expensive cities on earth and moved to a small town in Maine. She compares the two places honestly: what she gained, what she gave up, and what she would tell somebody considering the same move.

Tip: she names her trade-off out loud, more than once. Write down the exact sentence she uses, then say your own version of it about your town.

Talk
City Quitters: why the future belongs to the countryside -- Karen Rosenkranz, TEDxBergamo (15 min)

A researcher who interviewed people all over the world who left cities on purpose. She argues that the move is not an escape but a choice, and she has the arguments you will want the next time somebody asks why you did it.

Tip: the speaker is not a native speaker of English, and she is easy to follow. This is exactly the English of your own meetings. Listen once without subtitles before you turn them on.
