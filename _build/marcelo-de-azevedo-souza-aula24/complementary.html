Lesson 24 -- Midpoint Review

TEDx Talk
Josh Kaufman: The first 20 hours -- how to learn anything

A short, funny talk about how adults learn new skills, and why the first hours feel so slow. Good material for your audit: what does he say about practice that you already do, and what do you not do?

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Pause after each of his four steps and say whether you do it in your English, with one example.

TED Talk
Eduardo Briceño: How to get better at the things you care about

Eleven minutes on the difference between doing something and practicing it. It is the article of this lesson in one talk: deliberate practice, feedback, and the comfort zone.

Tip: write down his two zones and say which one you spend more time in with English, and why.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Angela Lee Duckworth: Grit -- the power of passion and perseverance

A psychologist explains why consistency over a long time predicts success better than talent. Six minutes, clear and well organized.

Tip: after the talk, say one area of your life, cycling or work, where you have shown grit, and one where you could raise the bar.
