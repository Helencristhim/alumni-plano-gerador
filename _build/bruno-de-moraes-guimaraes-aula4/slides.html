Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"What do you study?"

"Where do you live?"

"When does the bus come?"

"Who is your coach?"

What is the same in all four questions?

WordIt asks aboutExample
Whata thing"What do you study?"
Wherea place"Where is the bus stop?"
Whena time"When does the camp start?"
Whoa person"Who is your coach?"
The orderquestion word + do (I, you) or does (he, she) + person + verb: "Where do you live?" · "When does he practice?"
Him and herAfter the verb, he becomes him and she becomes her: "I know him." · "Ask her." · "Call me."

Four words. With them you can get anywhere, in any city, without a map.

Dialogue

At the Bus Stop

B
Excuse me. Where does the bus number twelve stop?
G
Right here, on this corner. Are you going to the sports center?
B
Yes. When does it come?
G
Every ten minutes. It's a short trip. The center is near the library.
B
What does a ticket cost?
G
Two dollars, and you need coins. The driver doesn't give change.
B
Thank you. I am new here and I don't want to be lost.
G
Don't worry. I take the same bus. Come with me and I show you the stop.
Comprehension

About Grace

1. How often does the bus come?
Every ten minutes.
2. What does she say about the ticket?
It costs two dollars and you need coins, because the driver doesn't give change.
3. Where is the sports center?
Near the library.
4. What does she do at the end?
She takes the same bus and she shows him the stop.
Listening

On the Bus

The driver talks to the passengers. Sound first — no text.

1. What is the next stop?
Fifth Street.
2. How do you pay for the ticket?
Two dollars, and only in coins.
3. Which stop does the bus skip today, and why?
The park, because the street is closed.
Listening 2

Two Minutes on Foot

A voice message with directions. Sound first — no text.

1. What is the big blue building?
It is the library.
2. Left or right, and for how long?
Right, and two minutes on foot.
3. What is next to the sports center?
A small coffee shop.
