The Message You Pass On

Relaying a director's feedback, an auditor's warning and a BPO instruction, with better verbs than said. Key words: relay, feedback, stress, remind, warn, urge, acknowledge, word for word. Structure: reporting verbs and their patterns (remind somebody to, stress that, suggest -ing).

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

To relay -- to pass a message or information from one person to another
"I relayed the director's feedback to the BPO team the same afternoon."
Feedback -- comments about how good or bad a piece of work is, so that it can improve
"The director gave us feedback on the closing pack."
To stress -- to say that something is especially important
"She stressed that the deadline could not move."
To remind -- to tell somebody to remember something that they need to do
"Can you remind the BPO to send the bank statements?"
To warn -- to tell somebody about a possible problem before it happens
"The auditor warned us that the sample would be bigger this year."
To urge -- to ask somebody very strongly to do something
"The controller urged everybody to close the open items by Friday."
To acknowledge -- to accept or say that something is true
"He acknowledged that the forecast had been too optimistic."
Word for word -- using exactly the same words that another person said
"You do not need to repeat it word for word; give the main message."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

The director ends the call with three points, and you have to pass them on to the BPO. You could write: She said the pack is important. She said the statements are missing. She said maybe two parts. All true, but the BPO still does not know what to do.

A better relay chooses the verb. Some verbs take a person and to: She reminded the BPO to send the bank statements. She asked us to close the open items. Other verbs take that and a full sentence, and the tense usually goes back: She stressed that the pack had to go out on Friday. The auditor warned that the sample would be bigger.

And one verb has its own rule: suggest takes -ing. She suggested splitting the review into two parts. Never she suggested us to split it. With the right verb, three short lines tell the team what is fixed, what is a request, and what is only an idea.

1. Why does the first relay in the text not help the BPO?
A Because it uses said for everything, so the BPO does not know what to do.
B Because it is written in the past tense, and the BPO team prefers instructions that are written in the present tense.
C Because it is too long.
2. Which sentence follows the pattern of remind?
A She reminded that the BPO sends the statements before the regional call on Wednesday.
B She reminded sending the statements.
C She reminded the BPO to send the statements.
3. What comes after suggest?
A A person and to, like suggested us to split, exactly the same as with ask and remind.
B -ing, like suggested splitting.
C Only a noun.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Reporting Verbs

GRAMMAR

Choose the verb that says what the person wanted, then use its pattern.

VerbThe patternExample
ask, remind, urge, tellverb + person + to + verbShe reminded us to send it.
warn (an action)verb + person + not to + verbHe warned us not to close the period.
stress, acknowledge, explain, warn (a fact)verb + that + sentence; the tense goes backShe stressed that the deadline could not move.
suggestsuggest + -ing, or suggest that we + verbHe suggested reviewing it weekly.
explainexplain something to somebodyHe explained the variance to me.

Order
