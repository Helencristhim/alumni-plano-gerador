Chapter 4: What They Actually Said

Better Verbs Than Said

Every verb comes with its own pattern

Grammar Discovery

What Comes After the Verb?

“Don't forget the bank statements.” → She reminded the BPO to send the bank statements.

“The deadline cannot move.” → She stressed that the deadline could not move.

“Yes, the forecast was too optimistic.” → He acknowledged that the forecast had been too optimistic.

“Why don't we split the review?” → He suggested splitting the review.

What comes after the orange verb? After the blue ones? And after the green one?

VerbThe patternExample
ask, remind, urge, tellverb + person + to + verbShe reminded the BPO to send the statements.
warn (an action)verb + person + not to + verbHe warned us not to post it twice.
stress, acknowledge, explain, warn (a fact)verb + that + sentence (the tense goes back)She stressed that the deadline could not move.
suggestsuggest + -ing, or suggest that + we + verbHe suggested splitting the review.
explainexplain something to somebodyHe explained the variance to me.
relaying, not repeatinggive the main message, not every wordShe asked us to send it by Friday.

In one line: choose the verb that says what the person wanted, then use its pattern.

Chapter 5: Real Speed

Both Ends of the Message

The team lead who receives it, and the controller who sends it

Listening 1 · A BPO Team Lead

What I Need Is the Verb

A team lead at the BPO on the instructions she receives. Sound first — no text.

1. How do most of her instructions arrive?
Second hand: from the director, to the client, to her contact, and then to her.
2. Why did the message she received last month not help her?
It only said the deadline was important; it did not have the right verb.
3. What does she need from a relay?
The main message and the right verb, not the call word for word.
Listening 2 · A Controller

Three Lines, Not a Transcript

A controller on the people who relay his feedback.

1. How many countries does he give feedback to every month?
Three.
2. What do the people who relay well write?
Three lines, never a transcript.
3. What does it mean when he sees the word said five times?
That the team will have to call him again to ask what he actually wanted.
Real Document

The Relay to Lucia

KEITMA GOMES → BPO BUENOS AIRES
Teams · After the regional call · 4:40 PM

Hi Lucia, a quick relay from the regional call with the director.

She acknowledged that the intercompany entries arrived on time this month. Thank you to the team.

She stressed that the closing pack must go out on Friday, and she asked us to send the bank statements by Wednesday at noon.

She also suggested reviewing the accruals together every Monday next month. Could you check if that works for your team?

Note: four reporting verbs, and the word said does not appear once.
1. What did the director acknowledge?
That the intercompany entries arrived on time this month.
2. What must the BPO send, and by when?
The bank statements, by Wednesday at noon.
3. What did the director suggest for next month?
Reviewing the accruals together every Monday.
