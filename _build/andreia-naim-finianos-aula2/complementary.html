Lesson 2 -- Beyond Six Words

Podcast
NPR -- How I Built This: "Spanx: Sara Blakely"

An hour of somebody telling the story of a company from nothing to everywhere, in exactly the tense this lesson trained. She has no slides and no script: she has years, milestones and a turning point, and she keeps the answer alive for a full hour. American English at conversational speed, with a transcript on the page.

Tip: stop after the first fifteen minutes and count how many times she says have or has plus a verb. Then tell the story of your own company for two minutes, out loud, with the same shape.

Talk
Ricardo Semler -- "How to run a company with (almost) no rules" (TED, 22 min)

A Brazilian executive telling twenty years of company history to an international audience, in English, without ever sounding like a report. Useful for two reasons at once: the story-telling shape you are practising, and a Brazilian accent that carries a room without apologising for itself.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down the three sentences where he tells you how long something has been true. Then write the same three about your own company.

Series
The Playlist -- Episode 1 (Netflix)

The story of a company crossing its own border, told once per episode by a different person who was there. Episode one is the founder version: what they had built up, what they had never done, and the deal they had to land before anything else could happen. English and Swedish, with the business scenes in English.

Tip: watch the first episode with English subtitles. Note one moment where somebody explains the company to a room that does not know it yet, and how many sentences it took them.
