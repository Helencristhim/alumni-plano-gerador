The Pattern

When did each of these happen?

I joined the bank in 2001.
I spent six months in New Zealand.
I took over the team in 2015.
Eventually, I became head of operations.

Finished time → past simple

If the moment is over (in 2001, last year, six months ago), use the past. Most verbs add -ed; a few become a new word — joined, but spent, took, became.

The Pattern

Two ways to tell twenty-three years

A finished moment

I joined in 2001.
I became head in 2015.
I lived in New Zealand for six months.

It happened. It is over. Past simple.

Still true today

I have been with the bank for 23 years.
I have worked here since 2001.
I have led this team since 2015.

It started before and it has not stopped.

for + how long (for 23 years) · since + when it started (since 2001). Never "I work there since 2001".
