Chapter 4: Can and Can't

I can · I can't · Can you...?

ability, limits and requests

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I can read the report in English."

"I can't speak fluently yet."

"My manager can sign the form next week."

"Can you send the checklist today?" -- "Yes, I can."

FormUseExample
subject + can + base verbability: you are ableI can read the report in English.
subject + can't (cannot) + base verba limit: you are not ableI can't speak fluently yet.
Can + subject + base verb?a question or a requestCan you send the checklist today?
Short answersyes / noYes, I can. / No, I can't.
Soundcan = short and weak ("I c'n read") -- CAN'T = long and strong

Same form for everybody: he can, she can, we can -- no -s, no "to". / Igual para todos: sem -s e sem "to" depois de can.

Dialogue

Before the Audit Visit

E
Hi Carlos. The European auditor is visiting your office next month. Can I ask you some questions?
C
Of course, Emma. I am available now.
E
Thank you. Can your team provide the maps of all the farms?
C
Yes, we can. The maps are ready in the IMS system.
E
Great. Can you translate the reports into English?
C
I can translate the short reports, but I can't translate the long ones this month.
E
That's OK. The team in Geneva can do the long reports. Can you speak with the auditor in English?
C
Yes, I can, but I can't speak fluently yet. I can explain the criteria slowly.
E
That's perfect. Auditors like clear answers, and your skill with the data is very good.
C
Thank you, Emma. I can send you the documents tomorrow.
