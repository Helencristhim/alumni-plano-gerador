Real Speed

Tax, Not Text

One open vowel changes the whole meeting. Listen, then say it back at the same speed.

The tax reform replaces five taxes with two.
It works a bit like a VAT.
The new tax is charged where the product is consumed.
Please send me the text of the new law.

The Ladder

Four Steps, Forty-Five Seconds

One sentence on each step. Then say it again without looking.

1. What changes
One sentence. No history, no list of taxes.
2. It works like…
One comparison with something they already know.
3. The impact
What it costs us, saves us, or changes for us.
4. What I need
A decision, a resource, or just their attention.

Language you can use

In short, …It works like your…, with one difference:For us, the impact is…What I need from you is…

Under Pressure

So What?

A call with Zurich. Karin has ten minutes before the board meeting. She is polite, she is in a hurry, and every sentence without an impact gets the same answer: “So what?”

  • Round 1: you explain the reform. After every sentence without an impact, Karin asks: so what?
  • Round 2: an analyst on your team explains it badly. You interrupt and ask for the short version.
