Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
CNBC Explains -- "What’s next for oil?"

The first video of this block, and worth ending on it. In lesson one it was six minutes about your market. Watch it again now and you will hear something different: a headline in the first ten seconds, three points, a projection given as a range, and a close. It is the structure you spent ten lessons building, done by professionals.

Tip: watch with a pen. Mark where the headline is, where each point starts, and where the range appears. Then compare with your own dry run.

Podcast
NPR Planet Money -- "Is dynamic pricing coming to a supermarket near you?"

Twenty minutes on a decision that will land on your desk sooner than you think: prices that move during the day, and what that does to the shelf, the shop and the brand. Everything in it is a projection given honestly, as a range.

Tip: the page has the full transcript. Listen once with it closed, then write your own headline for the episode. One sentence, the way you did in class.

Listen on NPR ↗
Series
Mad Men -- Season 1, Episode 1, "Smoke Gets in Your Eyes"

Where this block started. Watch the pitch scene at the end again, ten lessons later, and listen for the headline: one sentence, said slowly, before any of the reasoning. It is the same move you practised today, in the same industry, sixty years earlier.

Tip: pause right after the headline and say your own out loud in English, about your own quarter. If it takes more than one sentence, cut it.
