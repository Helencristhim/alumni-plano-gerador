Which Lesson Is Each One From?

"Coverage is lower than the target, and the gap is the widest in the south."

Every one of these came from a different lesson, and the last one carries two at once. Name the structure and say what job it is doing in the sentence.

Where in the meetingStructureExample
The numberscomparatives and superlativesCoverage is lower than the target.
The recommendationshould · must not · have toWe should hold the price. We must not discount.
What happenedpast simple, and past continuous for the backgroundWe were visiting when the supplier stopped.

What comes nextgoing toWe are going to hold the price.

Listening 1 · A Director

Three Points and One Decision

A director opening a quarterly board. Sound first — no text.

1. What is his headline, and why does he say he gives it first?
Coverage is at nineteen against a target of twenty-two, three points behind and behind on purpose. He gives it first because he would rather the board heard it from him.
2. What reason does he give for the slower quarter on quarter movement?
The first visit to a new workshop is the expensive one, and they underestimated the number of visits.
3. What is the one decision he asks for, and what is the deadline?
The budget, and the deadline is the fifteenth.
Listening 2 · A Board Member

What a Board Remembers

Twenty years of sitting on boards, in three observations. Sound first — no text.

1. What is the first thing she says the memorable presentations have, and how does she test it?
A headline. She tests it by what a board member repeats in the corridor afterwards.
2. Why does she prefer a range to a single number?
Because a single number for a period that has not happened is either luck or a guess. She would rather be told between twenty-two and twenty-five than find out in February that the number was decoration.
3. What is the third thing, and what is her line about it?
Somebody who flags the bad news themselves, early, in their own words. A board can work with a problem it heard about in October, and cannot work with the same problem in February.
