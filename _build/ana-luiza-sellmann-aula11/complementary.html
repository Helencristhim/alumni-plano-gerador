Grammar Video
"Verb patterns" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on exactly today's problem: which verbs are followed by -ing and which are followed by to. It also covers the verbs that take both shapes with a change of meaning.

Tip: pause after each example and say the pair out loud twice. The goal is not to understand the rule — there is no rule. The goal is for the wrong version to start sounding strange.

Podcast
"The Choices Before Us" -- Hidden Brain

Why having more options often makes us decide less, and why we keep postponing the choice that matters most. Calm American English, one speaker leading the story, which is the easiest kind of listening to follow. The transcript is on the page.

Tip: while you listen, note every time you hear "decided to", "avoided", "kept" or "refused to". Then use three of them to describe a decision of your own out loud.

Talk
"Inside the mind of a master procrastinator" -- Tim Urban, TED (14 minutes)

The funniest and most honest talk about the gap between deciding to do something and actually doing it. Fast but very clear American English, and the last three minutes are about the things nobody has a deadline for — which is where your ceramics course and your month off live.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Afterwards, answer his final question out loud in English: what is on your own calendar that has no deadline, and what have you decided to do about it?
