Lesson 4 -- The Two Minutes Before the Real Conversation

Talk
Celeste Headlee -- "10 ways to have a better conversation"

A radio host explains what makes people keep talking to you. Every one of the ten rules is a decision you make in the first two minutes of a coffee break.

Tip: pick the two rules you break most often in Portuguese. Then say out loud, in English, what you will do instead next time.

Talk
Julian Treasure -- "How to speak so that people want to listen"

Not about grammar: about pace, pitch and pauses. In a second language the temptation is to speak fast to hide the hesitation, and this talk shows why that works against you.

Tip: record yourself saying your survival card twice: once fast, once with pauses. Listen to both and choose.

Guided Study
TED-Ed -- "Why do we dream?"

A short animated lesson with comprehension questions. Deliberately outside your field: small talk is often about something you do not work with.

Tip: after watching, invent three follow-up questions about it — the kind you would ask a stranger who had just told you this. Say them out loud.
