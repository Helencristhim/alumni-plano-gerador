Chapter 4: The Code

The Causative

Have and get something done

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I had my bike serviced before the trip."

"I got the brakes checked in the next town."

"I had the mechanic look at the front wheel."

"I got my neighbor to water the plants."

In all four, somebody else does the work. Only the shape changes.

You name...FormExample
the thinghave + object + past participleI had my bike serviced.
the thing (informal)get + object + past participleI got the brakes checked.
the personhave + person + base formI had the mechanic look at it.
the person (informal)get + person + to + base formI got my neighbor to water them.
bad newshave + object + past participleI had my wallet stolen.

The object always comes before the participle: "I had my bike serviced", never "I had serviced my bike".

Language Focus

Thing or Person?

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"I had serviced my bike at the workshop."

✓ RIGHT

"I had my bike serviced at the workshop."

✗ WRONG

"I got my sister drive me to the shop."

✓ RIGHT

"I got my sister to drive me to the shop."

Put the object first, then the participle. And after get plus a person, you always need to.

Dialogue

Back Home with Kaia

K
Three months on those roads. Please tell me you had the bike checked before you rode it home.
M
I did not dare. I dropped it off at the workshop the next morning and asked for a quote. After that much wear and tear, a tune-up was never going to be enough.
K
So you had the whole thing overhauled?
M
Every bearing. It is more roadworthy now than the day I bought it. The problem is me. I came back to a backlog of four hundred emails and I was swamped before I had even unpacked.
K
That is the part nobody warns you about. I always get my brother to run my errands for the first week. Reverse culture shock is real, and it takes me a month to readjust.
M
That is exactly it. So this week I am having the photographs printed, and then I am going to sit still and recharge my batteries.

Self-Assessment

What I Learned

I can say what somebody did for me: "I had my bike serviced".
I can use "get" the same way: "I got the brakes checked".
I can name the person: "I had the mechanic look at it" or "I got my sister to drive".
I put the object before the participle, never after "had".
I can talk about coming home and what I still need to get done.
