Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Severance — Season 1, Episode 1

An office drama in which almost every line is somebody setting something up, putting something off or refusing to bring something up. The English is American, calm and clear, and the phrasal verbs are the everyday ones from this lesson.

Tip: English subtitles on. Every time you hear a phrasal verb, pause and say the formal verb it replaces. Aim for ten in the first episode.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English — “What English phrases really mean”

Six minutes, two British speakers, on how British people say one thing and mean another. It is the soft no from the second recording of this lesson, explained by the people who use it. Free transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen once with no text. Then say out loud two phrases you could hear from your client that sound friendly and probably mean no.

Talk
David Grady — “How to save the world (or at least yourself) from bad meetings” (TED)

Six minutes on the meetings nobody needed, and on how to turn down an invitation politely. Full of the verbs people use to set up, put off and get out of meetings.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles. Then say in three sentences how you would turn down a meeting invitation from your client without sounding rude.
