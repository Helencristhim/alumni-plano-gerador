Chapter 3: The Comma That Adds Context

Something More, Between Two Commas

Take it out, and the sentence still stands

Grammar Discovery

What Can You Take Out?

"The close, which I oversee end to end, takes five business days."

"My manager, who leads the R2R team for Latin America, asked me to prepare the talking points."

"SAP, where we post every entry, is the main system for the close."

"The report that triggered the finding was late." — "The Q3 report, which we submitted on Friday, has already been approved."

Cover the orange part. Is the sentence still complete? What is around it on both sides? And why does the blue clause have no commas at all?

ShapeWhat it doesExample
thing, which ... ,extra information about a thing or a processThe close, which I oversee, takes five days.
person, who ... ,extra information about a personMy manager, who leads the team, asked me.
place or system, where ... ,extra information about a place or a systemSAP, where we post every entry, is the main system.
..., which ... (at the end)a comment on the whole idea before itWe closed two days early, which had never happened before.
the comma testtake the clause out: the sentence is still complete and trueThe close takes five days.
no commas (lesson 10)the clause tells you WHICH one — it cannot goThe report that triggered the finding was late.
the trapnever that after the commaNot the close, that I oversee, takes five days.

In one line: the commas say “this is extra” — so the listener gets the context without you starting a new sentence.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

The Night Before the Introduction

One rehearsal, two voice notes and one profile

Dialogue

Title First, Then the Scope

M
Tomorrow you meet Daniel, the new controller for Canada. He will ask what you do. What are you going to say?
K
I am a senior accountant in the R2R team, and I work on Brazil.
M
That is your title. He can read your title. Give him the scope, in the same sentence.
K
I look after the Brazil close, which covers several entities, and I oversee it end to end.
M
Better. Now the audit. He will want to know who to call when the auditors have a question.
K
I am also the liaison with the external auditors, who usually come to me first when a number needs explaining.
M
Good. Last one, and he will ask it. Does the BPO report to you?
K
Not formally. The BPO team, which prepares most of the entries, takes its instructions from me — so I have ownership of the result, but not of the people.
Comprehension

About Mark

1. Who does Mark say Keitma is meeting tomorrow?
Daniel, the new controller for Canada.
2. What does Mark say about her first answer, and what does he ask her to add?
That it is only her title, which Daniel can read. He asks her to add the scope, in the same sentence.
3. What is the last question Mark says Daniel will ask?
Whether the BPO reports to her.
Listening 1 · An Audit Partner

The Answer With No Follow-Up

An audit partner on the first ten minutes of every kickoff. Sound first — no text.

1. What question does she ask at the start of every kickoff?
“Walk me through your close.”
2. What is the problem with answering with a list?
It leaves her with twenty follow-up questions.
3. What three things does she learn from the sentences with context in the middle?
Who does what, how big it is, and where the risk sits.
Listening 2 · A Controller

One Out of Thirty

A new controller on the only introduction he remembered.

1. How many people did he talk to in his first two weeks, and how did most of them introduce themselves?
About thirty, and most of them gave a job title and a team name.
2. What three things did the one memorable sentence tell him?
Her scope, her ownership, and when to call her.
3. What does he say a title tells him, and what does the extra clause tell him?
A title tells him where you sit; the extra clause tells him why he should remember you.
Real Document

The Profile Draft

KEITMA GOMES · ABOUT
Senior Accountant · Record to Report · Financial Shared Services

I look after the Brazil close, which covers several company codes and runs on a five-day calendar.

I coordinate the external audit end to end, from the pre-audit review to the final meeting with the partner, who signs the opinion.

Before that, I supported the Canadian controller for nine months, which taught me to explain a balance sheet tab by tab, in English, to people who were not in the room when the numbers were built.

My expertise is the part between the ledger and the director: the reconciliation, the variance and the rationale.

Note: four sentences, three of them with extra context between commas or at the end.
1. What extra information does the first sentence give about the Brazil close?
That it covers several company codes and runs on a five-day calendar.
2. Who is the partner, according to the profile?
The person who signs the opinion.
3. What did the nine months with Canada teach her?
To explain a balance sheet tab by tab, in English, to people who were not in the room when the numbers were built.
