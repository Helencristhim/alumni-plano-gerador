"Good conversations take time and attention" -- Life Kit, NPR

Twenty minutes of calm American English on what actually makes a conversation work, including how to keep following when your attention starts to slide. Connection to Lesson 4: this is the listening drill in real conditions -- a real conversation, real speed, and a transcript on the page when you want to check.

Tip: do the three-pass drill from class on the first minute. Listen once and write only the words you are sure about, listen again and fill in around them, and only then open the transcript.

Grammar Video
"Grammar challenge: Wish and the past perfect" -- BBC Learning English

A short, direct lesson on the structure you built today: wish plus the past perfect for regrets about a past you cannot go back to. Connection to Lesson 4: this is the difference between I wish I studied, which describes a habit, and I wish I had studied, which is the regret you actually mean.

Tip: pause after each example and say one true sentence about your own English -- one with wish and the past simple, one with wish and the past perfect. Then say the same thing again without wish, in five words.
