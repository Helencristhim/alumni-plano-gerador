Chapter 4: The Code

From Actions to Events

Nominalization in a career story

Grammar Discovery

Action or Event?

"His appointment as CEO led to a turnaround."

"The restructuring of the company took two years."

"His decision to start a doctorate surprised his colleagues."

"After the sale of the company, he returned to law."

Each blue word was a verb before: appoint, restructure, decide, sell. Now it is a noun, and it is the subject of the sentence, or comes after after and following. What small word comes after each noun?

JobFormExample
Choose for a jobhis / her appointment as + jobHis appointment as CEO ...
A process or changethe + noun (-ing, -tion, -ment) + of + thingThe restructuring of the company ...
A decision or a planhis / her decision to + base verbHis decision to start a doctorate ...
A time markerafter / following / during + the nounAfter the sale of the company ...
Common pairsappoint → appointment · decide → decision · leave → departure · sell → sale · grow → growthfollowing his departure from the board
The trapdecision to, not decision of; not too many nouns in one sentenceNot his decision of returning · not the appoint.

In one line: turn the key actions into nouns: his appointment as, the restructuring of, his decision to; keep simple verbs for the rest.

Listening 1 · The Forum Organizer

Nobody Reads a List

The organizer of an international forum explains what makes a good speaker bio. Sound first — no text.

1. What is the problem with most bios, according to her?
They are lists.
2. How should a good bio start?
With what the speaker does today, not with the university.
3. What is the maximum length she asks for?
Under one hundred words.
Listening 2 · The Former CEO

Why I Went Back to Law

A former CEO explains why he returned to legal practice. Sound first — no text.

1. What happened to the company during his tenure as CEO?
It went through a difficult turnaround: they sold two businesses and rebuilt the rest.
2. Which part of the job did he enjoy most?
The regulatory side: the negotiations with the agencies and the drafting of rules.
3. What did he start after his return to practice?
His doctorate.

Self-Assessment

What I Can Do Now

I turn key actions into events: his appointment as, the restructuring of.
I use decision to + base verb, never decision of.
I use after, following and during + a noun.
I mix nouns and simple verbs, so my bio is clear, not cold.
I know the words: an appointment, tenure, a turnaround, a career path, to oversee, to spearhead, a stint, a legacy, a transition, hands-on.
