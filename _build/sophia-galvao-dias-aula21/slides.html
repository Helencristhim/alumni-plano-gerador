Kitchen mistakes

0 / 5 errors found

"Yesterday I must bake three batches."
"Yesterday I had to bake three batches. — must has no past: had to."
"We didn't had to buy eggs."
"We didn't have to buy eggs. — after didn't, the base form."
"I needn't have make so much frosting."
"I needn't have made so much frosting. — have + past participle."
"I needn't to wake up early yesterday."
"I didn't need to wake up early yesterday. — not necessary, not done: didn't need to."
"Have you ever sold anything? Yes, I had."
"Yes, I have. — your life up to now: have, not had."
