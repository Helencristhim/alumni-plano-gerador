Chapter 3: The Code

The Other Future

I am presenting · I am going to speak

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am presenting on the fifth, at three."

"I am going to speak in every meeting."

"We are going to prepare it together."

"I am not going to use the translator."

FutureWhen you use itExample
am / is / are + verb-ingthe plan is SET: date, room, ticketI am presenting on the fifth.
going to + verbyou DECIDED it, inside your headI am going to speak in every meeting.
negativeam / is / are + not + going toI am not going to use the translator.
questionAre you going to + verb?Are you going to be nervous?
Key ideaThe room and the ticket give you the first one. A decision in your head gives you the second one. Both are real futures.

In speech, going to sounds like gonna. You will hear it much more than you read it.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I am going speak in every meeting."
"I am going to speak in every meeting."
"I going to prepare three minutes."
"I am going to prepare three minutes."
"We go to prepare it together."
"We are going to prepare it together."

The full shape has three pieces: am / is / are + going to + the clean verb.
