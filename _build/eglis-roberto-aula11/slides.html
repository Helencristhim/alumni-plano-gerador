Lesson 11 · The Question

Did You Hear What He Said?

A message travels around a party: what people said, what they asked, and what they told you to do.

The Question

Did the Story Change?

You arrive late at a friend’s party. Someone says: “Did you hear what Jake said? He’s leaving the country!” Later you find out that Jake only said he was taking a trip. Has this ever happened to you? What did people say, and what was the real story?

The Conversation · You

Your Messages

1. Think of a message that got lost or changed on the way to you. What did the person say, and what did you hear?
2. What is the best piece of advice someone gave you on a trip or at sea? What did they tell you to do?
3. Has a stranger at a bar ever told you something surprising? What did he or she say?

The Conversation · The World

Gossip, News and the Truth

1. Is gossip always bad, or can it help people? Give one example.
2. Today a rumor travels on WhatsApp in minutes. Is that worse than word of mouth at a bar?
3. Before you pass a story on, what should you do? What did your parents tell you about this?

Closing

One Minute, One Message

The party is over and your friend calls you the next morning: “What did I miss?” Tell him what three people said, what one person asked you, and what someone told you to do. One minute. Go.

I can report what people said with said, told me and a tense one step back.
I can report a question with asked if and asked where, in the normal word order.
I can pass on a request or an order with told me to and asked me not to.
