Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Elizabeth Loftus: How reliable is your memory? (17 min)

A psychologist shows how a story changes in our memory when other people talk to us about it. It is the same thing that happens to a rumor at a party.

Tip: after the talk, pass on two things she said, with She said that... and She told us to...

Film
Doubt (2008)

Meryl Streep and Philip Seymour Hoffman in a small church school in New York. A suspicion becomes a story, and the story travels. Watch for the famous sermon about gossip and a pillow full of feathers.

Tip: after the film, tell a friend what the priest said in the sermon, and what Sister Aloysius told the young nun to do.

TED Talk
Julian Treasure: How to speak so that people want to listen (10 min)

A sound expert lists the habits that make people stop listening to us. Number one on his list is gossip. Clear, slow and very practical for conversations at a bar or a party.

Tip: write down the seven habits he told us to avoid, and pass them on to a friend with He said that...
