Before class 1: The glossary

Vocabulary

Tap Listen and say each word out loud. Eight words — you will need all of them in class.

Deadline — the day when the work must be finished
"We will still meet the deadline."
Budget — the money available for a project
"It stays inside the same budget."
Blueprint — the technical drawing of a building
"The client approved the first blueprint."
Revision — a change made to a plan
"The new layout means a revision."
Approve — to officially agree to something
"He did not approve the first plan."
Delay — extra time that a job takes
"That caused a two-week delay."
Layout — the way rooms and spaces are arranged
"The new layout puts the kitchen next to the living room."
Supplier — a company that provides materials
"Our supplier could not deliver on time."

Before class 2: The reading

Input

Read it once, without a dictionary. In class we will work on it — you only need the general idea now.

The email nobody wants to write

Marina is an architect in Porto Alegre. Last Tuesday she opened her laptop and found a problem. The glass supplier for the Ribeiro house had just written to her: the special windows would arrive three weeks late.

She had two options. She could keep the original blueprint and accept a long delay, or she could change the layout of the back wall and use a different window. The second option was faster, but it meant a revision, and the client had already approved the first plan.

Marina did not send a long email. She called. She told the client what had happened, explained why she was suggesting the change, and said what it would cost: nothing extra, because the new windows were inside the same budget. She also said the deadline would move by four days, not three weeks.

The client asked two questions and then said yes. Later he told her that what he liked was not the solution. It was that she called before he had to ask.
