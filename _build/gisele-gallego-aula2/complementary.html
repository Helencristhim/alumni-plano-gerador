Lesson 02 -- What Does This Actually Do?

Documentary
The Story of Stuff -- Annie Leonard (full film, 21 min)

Where the things in your cart come from, what is really inside them, and why the box is designed to tell you as little as possible. Narrated in clear, slow American English.

Tip: watch it once with subtitles on and once with them off. Write down every word you hear about products and packaging.

Podcast
NPR Life Kit -- How Do I Get This Out? Your Guide to Stain Removal

A scientist explains, in fifteen minutes, why grease behaves differently from coffee, what water alone can and cannot do, and which product to reach for. Exactly tonight’s vocabulary, at real speed.

Tip: listen with the transcript open on the same page. First without reading, then again while you follow the text.

Talk
It’s a Wash: The Chemistry of Soap -- NBC News Learn

Five minutes on what actually happens when soap meets grease, and why the sentence on the back of the box says it breaks down fat. The answer to the line you could not read.

Tip: short and clearly spoken. Watch it once, then say the main idea back in three sentences of your own.
