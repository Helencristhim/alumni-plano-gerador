What the Speakers Said -- Reporting a Conference

You tell your team what the speakers said at a conference. Key words: a keynote, a speaker, a trend, a takeaway, to highlight, findings, to mention, accessibility, a summary, to share. Structure: reported speech.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A keynote -- the main talk at a conference, often by a famous speaker
"The keynote was about the future of design."
A speaker -- a person who gives a talk to an audience
"The speaker was a design director from Paris."
A trend -- a change or a new direction that many people follow
"Voice interfaces are a big trend."
A takeaway -- an important idea you remember and use after a talk or a meeting
"My main takeaway was simple: talk to users more."
To highlight -- to show that something is important
"She highlighted three numbers from her research."
Findings -- the results of research or a study
"The findings were surprising."
To mention -- to say something quickly, without many details
"He mentioned a new tool, but he didn’t explain it."
Accessibility -- how easy it is for everybody, including people with disabilities, to use a product
"Accessibility was the topic of the best talk."
A summary -- a short text that gives the main ideas of something longer
"I wrote a one-page summary for my team."
To share -- to tell other people about something, or give it to them
"I want to share three ideas from the conference."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

After a design conference, Juliane writes a summary for her team. The keynote speaker said that most teams designed for the perfect user.

Another speaker highlighted some findings about accessibility. He told us that accessibility was not a feature. He also mentioned a free tool, but he didn’t explain it.

The last speaker explained that AI would change the work of designers. The trend was clear, and Juliane wants to share it with everybody.

1. What did the keynote speaker say?
A That most teams designed for the perfect user.
B That users were always happy.
C That AI was not important.
2. The speaker said: “AI will change our work.” In the report, it becomes:
A “She explained that AI will changed our work.”
B “She explained that AI would change our work.”
C “She explained me that AI would change our work.”
3. Which sentence is correct?
A “He told us that accessibility was not a feature.”
B “He said us that accessibility was not a feature.”
C “He told that accessibility is not a feature.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Reported Speech -- What the Speaker Said

GRAMMAR

To report what someone said, use said that or told + person + that. The verb takes one step back in time: is to was, are to were, will to would, can to could, present to past.

JobFormExample
is / arewas / were“It is new” → she said it was new.
is / are + -ingwas / were + -ing“AI is changing design” → was changing.
will / canwould / could“It will grow” → he said it would grow.
Present simplepast simple“Users want speed” → users wanted speed.
Say or tell?said (that) / told + person (that)She said that... She told us that...
The trapno person directly after saidNot she said me. Say she told me.

Never: She said me · He said the trend will be voice · She told that... (no person) · He said users are... (no step back)
