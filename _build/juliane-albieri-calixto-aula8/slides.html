Chapter 4: The Code

She Said That...

From the speaker’s words to your words

Grammar Discovery

From the Speaker to the Team

"She said that AI was changing design."

"He told us that accessibility was not a feature."

"She said that every designer would use AI."

"He explained that small teams could move faster."

The speakers said: “AI is changing design”, “accessibility is not a feature”, “every designer will use AI”, “small teams can move faster”. When you report it later, the blue verb takes one step back in time: is becomes was, will becomes would, can becomes could. And look at the red words: told needs a person (us), said does not.

JobFormExample
is / arewas / were“It is new” → she said it was new.
is / are + -ingwas / were + -ing“AI is changing design” → was changing.
will / canwould / could“It will grow” → he said it would grow.
Present simplepast simple“Users want speed” → users wanted speed.
Say or tell?said (that) / told + person (that)She said that... She told us that...
The trapno person directly after saidNot she said me. Say she told me.

In one line: said that / told us that, and the verb takes one step back: is to was, will to would, can to could.

Listening 1 · The Keynote

A Speaker on the Stage

Part of a keynote by a design director at a conference. Listen, and then report what he said. Sound first — no text.

1. What does he say most teams design for?
For the perfect user, with time and a good connection.
2. What does he say real users are like?
Tired, busy, and often on a bad connection.
3. What will his team do next year?
They will test every feature with users on their worst day.
Listening 2 · Daniel Reports

Daniel Calls

Daniel, the speaker from Chicago, calls Juliane after a conference and reports the best talk. Sound first — no text.

1. Who gave the best talk, according to Daniel?
A designer from a bank in Singapore.
2. What did she say about bank apps?
She said that most bank apps were designed for experts, not for normal people.
3. What did she tell the audience to do?
To read their app out loud to a person who is not a designer.
