Chapter 4: The Code

I am attending... · We are meeting...

talk about your plans for the event

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am flying to Orlando on Monday."

"We are meeting three clients on Tuesday."

"Mike is coming to our booth."

"Are you attending SkyExpo?" -- "Yes, I am."

FormUseExample
I am + verb-inga plan with a day or timeI am flying on Monday.
he / she is + verb-inganother person's planMike is coming on Tuesday.
we / you / they are + verb-inga group planWe are meeting three clients.
Are you + verb-ing...?ask about plansAre you coming to the airshow?
I'm not + verb-ingnegativeI'm not working on Wednesday.

Plans with a day or a time: am / is / are + verb-ing. / Planos ja combinados, com dia ou hora: am / is / are + verbo com -ing.

Dialogue

A Call with Mike

M
Hi, Karina! I hear you are attending SkyExpo in Orlando next week.
K
Yes! We are bringing our new brochures. Our booth is in Hall B.
M
Great. When are you arriving?
K
I'm flying to Orlando on Monday. What are you doing on Tuesday?
M
I'm meeting two clients in the morning, but I'm free in the afternoon.
K
Perfect. Are you coming to the airshow on Wednesday?
M
Yes, I am. I'm watching it with my partners. Do you want to come with us?
K
I'd love to! Do I need a ticket?
M
No. Exhibitors don't need a ticket. Just show your badge.
K
Great. See you at the booth on Tuesday afternoon, Mike!
Did You Catch It?

About the Conversation

1. What is Mike doing on Tuesday morning?
He is meeting two clients.
2. Who is Mike watching the airshow with?
With his partners.
3. Do exhibitors need a ticket, according to Mike?
No. They just show their badge.
