Lesson 13 -- I See It Differently

Talk
Julia Dhar -- "How to disagree productively and find common ground" (TED@BCG)

A world debate champion on how to disagree with the idea and not the person, and how to start from the common ground. It is exactly the method of this lesson, in clear American English.

Tip: watch it with English subtitles. Write down the first sentence she uses to disagree, and use it in your next meeting.

Talk
Margaret Heffernan -- "Dare to disagree" (TEDGlobal)

A former CEO on why good companies need people who push back, and why disagreement is a way of thinking together. Good for a director who wants his team to say what they really think.

Tip: listen for the story about the doctor and the data. Then tell it in three sentences, with one soft phrase.

NPR Life Kit -- "This is the right way to tackle workplace conflict"

A short NPR episode with practical tips on conflict at work, in natural American English. There is a full article with the main tips on the same page.
