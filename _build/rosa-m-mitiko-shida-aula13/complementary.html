Reading & Audio
"Verbs That Change with Gerunds or Infinitives" -- VOA Learning English, Everyday Grammar

A short American lesson, with audio and the full text on the page, about the verbs that change meaning: remember, stop, try and a few more. Connection to Lesson 13: it is exactly the pair from the warm-up, stopped to check and stopped checking.

Tip: listen once without reading, then read along. Write one true sentence about your work for each verb -- one with to and one with -ing -- and read them out loud.

Talk
"Inside the mind of a master procrastinator" -- Tim Urban, TED

About fourteen minutes, and very funny, on why people keep putting things off even when the deadline is close. Connection to Lesson 13: it is full of the verbs from today -- put off, manage to, avoid doing, keep doing -- in natural American speech.

Tip: watch it with English subtitles. Then describe one task you keep putting off at work, using I keep + -ing and I need to, and say what you will do about it this week.

A clear grammar lesson on which verbs take -ing, which take to, and why some take both. Connection to Lesson 13: it reviews the same three groups as the Grammar Tip, with many examples.
