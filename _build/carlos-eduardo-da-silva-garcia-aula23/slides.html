Five Visit Phrases

Ask about problems -- Did you find any problems?

Answer no -- No, I didn't. Everything was OK.
Give a detail -- I met the producer on Thursday.
Chapter 3: Did You...?

Did you meet him? · What did you find?

questions about the past

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Did you meet the producer?"

"Yes, I did. I met Mr. Silva and his son."

"What did you find at the farm?"

"I didn't find the training record."

FormUseExample
Did + subject + base verb?yes / no questionDid you meet the producer?
Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.short answerYes, I did. I met Mr. Silva.
What / When / Where / Who + did + subject + base verb?information questionWhat did you find? When did you leave?
subject + didn't + base verbnegativeI didn't find the training record.
CarefulThe past is in did. After did / didn't, use the base verb: Did you find? (not "Did you found?")

Did + base verb. / Depois de did e didn't, o verbo volta para a forma base (find, meet, write).
