Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. Toque em Listen para ouvir. / Listen to each word and read the example.

Allergic -- your body reacts badly to a food (alérgico)
"Paul is allergic to nuts." (O Paul é alérgico a nozes.)
Ingredient -- one of the foods in a dish (ingrediente)
"Tomato is the main ingredient." (O tomate é o ingrediente principal.)
Nuts -- small hard food from a tree (nozes / castanhas)
"The pesto has nuts in it." (O pesto tem nozes.)
Seafood -- food from the sea, like shrimp (frutos do mar)
"The soup has a lot of seafood." (A sopa tem muitos frutos do mar.)
Dairy -- milk, cheese and butter (laticínios)
"The risotto has dairy in it." (O risoto tem laticínios.)
Spicy -- hot in your mouth, like red pepper (apimentado)
"The chicken is a bit spicy." (O frango é um pouco apimentado.)
Gluten-free -- with no wheat or gluten (sem glúten)
"Do you have gluten-free pasta?" (Vocês têm massa sem glúten?)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto e responda às perguntas. / Read the text and answer the questions.

Tonight Marlene and Paul have dinner at a small osteria in Florence. Paul is allergic to nuts, so Marlene asks the waiter: "Does the pesto have nuts in it?" The answer is yes, so Paul orders the tomato pasta. Marlene would like to try the risotto, but it has dairy in it. She orders it without cheese. The chicken is a bit spicy, and Marlene says: "I can't eat very spicy food." The waiter checks every ingredient with the chef. (Neste texto, allergic to = alérgico a; Does it have ... in it? = Isso tem ...?; without = sem; can't eat = não posso comer.)

1. Who is allergic to nuts?
A Marlene.
B Paul.
C The waiter.
2. How does Marlene order the risotto?
A With extra cheese.
B With seafood.
C Without cheese.
3. Why does Marlene ask about the chicken?
A Because she can't eat very spicy food.
B Because it has nuts.
C Because it is cold.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Dietary needs (allergic to / can't eat / Does it have...?)

GRAMMAR

Para falar do que você NÃO pode comer, use I am allergic to + comida (alérgico A) ou I can't eat + comida. Para perguntar o que tem no prato: Does it have ... in it? Para tirar um ingrediente: without. / Say what you cannot eat, and ask what is in a dish.

RuleExample (EN)Tradução (PT)
Alergia (sempre com TO)I am allergic to nuts.Eu sou alérgica a nozes.
Não posso comerI can't eat seafood.Eu não posso comer frutos do mar.
PerguntaDoes it have dairy in it?Isso tem laticínios?
RespostaYes, it does. / No, it does not.Sim, tem. / Não, não tem.
Tirar um ingredienteThe pasta without cheese, please.A massa sem queijo, por favor.
Revisão (aula 23)I would like to try the risotto.Eu gostaria de provar o risoto.

Key point / Ponto-chave: em português dizemos "alérgico A"; em inglês é allergic TO, nunca "allergic nuts". Depois de can't o verbo vem sem to: I can't eat. E a pergunta precisa do Does no começo -- "It have cheese?" não funciona. Para quem fala italiano: without = senza.
