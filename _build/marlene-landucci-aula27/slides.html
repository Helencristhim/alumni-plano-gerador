Chapter 3 -- Can I Eat It?

Language Discovery

I am allergic to nuts. / Does it have cheese in it?

Discovery

Listen and Notice

Two sentences say what you cannot eat. Two sentences ask or order. What do the orange words have in common?

"I am allergic to nuts."

"I can't eat seafood."

"Does it have cheese in it?"

"I would like to try the pasta, without cheese, please."

The Rule

Say It and Ask It

UsePatternExample
An allergyI am allergic to + foodI am allergic to nuts.
Food you cannot eatI can't eat + foodI can't eat seafood.
Ask about a dishDoes it have + food + in it?Does it have dairy in it?
Take one thing outdish + without + foodThe pasta without cheese, please.
Order (review)I would like to try + dishI would like to try the risotto.

Key idea: allergic always goes with to: allergic to nuts. After can't the verb has no to: say I can't eat, never "I can't to eat". To ask, start with Does it have and finish with in it?

Chapter 4 -- At the Osteria

Dinner at Osteria del Ponte

A waiter, a menu, and two messages

Dialogue

Ordering at the Osteria

W

"Good evening. Here is the menu. Would you like some water to start?"

M

"Yes, please. One question first: my friend Paul is allergic to nuts. Does the pesto have nuts in it?"

W

"Yes, it does. Our pesto has pine nuts. For Paul, I recommend the tomato and basil pasta. It has no nuts."

M

"Perfect. And I would like to try the risotto. Does it have dairy in it?"

W

"Yes, it has butter and cheese. But we can make it without cheese. Is that OK?"

M

"Yes, without cheese, please. And is the chicken spicy? I can't eat very spicy food."

W

"It is a bit spicy. The chef can make it with no red pepper. Just tell me."

M

"Great. Thank you for checking every ingredient. You are very kind."

Comprehension

About the Waiter

1. What does the waiter say about the pesto?
It has nuts in it -- pine nuts.
2. Which pasta does the waiter recommend for Paul?
The tomato and basil pasta -- it has no nuts.
3. What does the waiter say about the risotto?
It has butter and cheese, but they can make it without cheese.
4. What can the chef do with the chicken?
He can make it with no red pepper, so it is not spicy.
