Podcast
Short Wave (NPR) -- "This week in science: Small talk..."

A short science episode on why people dread small talk, and on a study showing that conversations people expect to be boring are usually more interesting than they predicted. Two hosts, natural American speed, and a transcript on the page.

Tip: listen once for the main idea, then a second time and write down every question tag and every softened sentence you hear.

Talk
Celeste Headlee -- "10 ways to have a better conversation" (TED)

A radio host with years of interviews explains how to keep a conversation going: ask open questions, do not repeat yourself, and listen more than you talk. It is the second half of small talk, after the ice is broken.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and pause after each of the ten rules. Say one sentence you could use with a colleague at the hospital.

Talk
Kio Stark -- "Why you should talk to strangers" (TED)

A short, friendly talk on the small exchanges with people we do not know, and why they matter in a new city. It is exactly the situation of the first weeks in the United States.

Tip: write down two openers she uses and turn each one into a sentence with a question tag.
