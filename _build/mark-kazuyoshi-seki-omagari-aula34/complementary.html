Travel video
Tokyo vs Kyoto: Which City is Better for First-Time Visitors? (travel vlog)

A traveler who has spent time in both cities compares them point by point: the crowds, the food, the temples and what there is to do at night. It is the same comparison you made in class, from somebody who has already made the choice.

Tip: every time the video compares the two cities, pause and say the same idea with a quantifier from the lesson: "Tokyo has plenty of...", "Kyoto has hardly any...", "Neither city..."

Travel with Amateur Traveler -- Travel to Kyoto and the Kansai Region of Japan

A long conversation about Kyoto and the cities around it, Osaka and Nara included: what to see, how many days to give each place and which day trips are worth it. It is the region Daisuke was talking about in the second listening.

Tip: write down every number of days and every day trip they recommend, then say in one sentence how your own plan is different.

engVid -- Learn English: LESS, FEWER, FEW, LITTLE, A FEW, A LITTLE, MORE

A clear lesson on the quantifiers that change meaning with one small word, including the difference between few and a few, and fewer and less. Watch it after the lesson, so that it confirms what you worked out in class.

Tip: for each quantifier in the video, say one sentence about Tokyo or Kyoto, and decide whether it is good news or bad news.
