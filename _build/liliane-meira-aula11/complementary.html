Lesson 11 -- Talking About Time and Money

Podcast
Art Problems -- "How to Transform Your Grant Applications" with Kimberlee Koym-Murteira

A conversation about what makes a grant application convincing, including the budget pages most artists write last and in a hurry.

Tip: every time a number or an amount of time comes up, say it again yourself with roughly, around or just under.

Talk
Amanda Palmer -- "The art of asking" (TED, 14 min)

An artist talks openly about money, and about asking people to pay for work. Useful for the part of today’s lesson that most artists avoid: putting their own time into the budget.

Tip: note two sentences where she talks about amounts without exact numbers, and reuse them about your own project.

Video
Sydney Executive Plus -- "How Big Things Get Done: a conversation with Professor Bent Flyvbjerg"

The researcher who studied thousands of projects explains why almost all of them run over in time and money, and what the few that finish on time do differently.

Tip: listen for how he gives numbers: almost all, just over, the bulk of. Then describe one overrun you have lived through.
