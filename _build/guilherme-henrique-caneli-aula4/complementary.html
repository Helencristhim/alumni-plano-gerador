Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Freakonomics Radio — “Regulate This!”

What regulation really does to an economy, from people who have written and fought rules for a living. Natural-speed English on the exact tension your investors worry about.

Tip: open the transcript on the same page and mark every reporting verb. Which ones signal certainty, and which ones doubt?

Listen on Freakonomics ↗
Talk
Rachel Botsman — “We’ve stopped trusting institutions and started trusting strangers” (TED)

How trust moves between institutions, markets and people — the same question behind every regulatory framework and every investor who asks whether a contract will survive.

Tip: retell her main argument to yourself in reported speech, using three different reporting verbs.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
Tim Harford — “Trial, error and the God complex” (TED)

An economist on why complex systems, from markets to public policy, work better when rules can be tested and corrected — the thinking behind every regulatory sandbox.

Tip: listen for the examples he uses and note one you could bring to a panel on regulation.

Watch on TED ↗
