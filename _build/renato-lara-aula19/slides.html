Grammar Discovery

Not what happened. How.

The current pulled him quickly.
Renato talked to him calmly.
Mick cleaned the cut carefully.
Ollie walks very slowly now.

What do the orange words have in common?

verb (+ object) + adjective + -ly

These words answer the question how? They describe the action, not the person. Most of them are the adjective + -ly: quick → quickly, calm → calmly. They usually go after the verb, or after the verb and its object: "He cleaned the cut carefully."

The Code

The rule, and three rebels

Add -ly

quick → quickly
calm → calmly
bad → badly
careful → carefully
easy → easily (y → i)

The rebels

good → well
"You surf really well." Never "surf good".
fast → fast
"The current pulled him very fast."
hard → hard
"He paddled hard." ("Hardly" means almost not.)

The question: How did he paddle? · How does she surf?

The Code

The person, or the action?

An adjective describes a person or a thing. An adverb describes how somebody does something.

The person

Harper is a good surfer.
Renato was calm.
Mick is a careful driver.

The action

Harper surfs well.
Renato talked calmly.
Mick drives carefully.
Harper is a good surfer. She surfs really well.

Same idea, two jobs: good for the person, well for the action.

Sound Check

Three blocks, one ending

The current pulled him really quickly.

"-ly" is short: QUICK-lee

Mick cleaned the cut carefully.

three syllables: CARE-fuh-lee

You did really well on Saturday.

"well" ends with a dark L: the tongue goes back

K

R

K
Kai: I know. I was at the other end of the beach, but I saw you paddle hard. You did really well.

K
Kai: That's the right thing. When people panic, they swim badly. Did he twist it on the rocks?
R
Renato: Yeah. A wave pushed him and he landed badly. Mick did first aid on the sand and put a bandage on his foot.
K
Kai: Good on him. At training tomorrow I want you to tell the story to the club. Slowly, so the grommets understand.
R
Renato: Sure. My English isn't perfect, but I can speak clearly.

Listening 1

A message for the parents

1. What happened to Ollie near the rocks?
The current pulled him very fast, a wave pushed him onto the rocks, and he cut his foot badly. He twisted his ankle too.
2. What did Mick do with the first aid kit?
He cleaned the cut carefully and put a bandage on it. Then they drove Ollie to the hospital.
3. What did the doctor say?
The ankle is swollen, but it isn't broken. Ollie can't surf for two weeks.
Listening 2

A thank-you from Harper

1. How did Renato act on Saturday, according to Harper?
He stayed really calm, he paddled very fast, and he talked to Ollie quietly, so Ollie didn't get more scared.
2. What does Ollie's foot look like now?
It's purple, with a huge bruise, and it still bleeds a little at night.
3. Is Kai angry? What does he want?
No. He wants everybody to know the rules well.
