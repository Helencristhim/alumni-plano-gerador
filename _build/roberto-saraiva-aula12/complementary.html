Lesson 12 -- The Minutes

Talk
Celeste Headlee -- "10 ways to have a better conversation" (TEDxCreativeCoast)

A radio interviewer on how to really listen to the other person. You cannot report what somebody said if you were only waiting for your turn to talk, and that is the skill under every good set of minutes.

Tip: watch it with English subtitles, then tell somebody what she said in three sentences, with said and told.

Talk
Amy Edmondson -- "How to turn a group of strangers into a team" (TEDxHGSE)

A Harvard Business School professor on teams that form and work together fast, the way your export call does every week with people in another country. Clear, slow American English.

Tip: listen for what she says good teams do after a meeting. Compare it with your own action log.

Podcast
NPR Life Kit -- "Good conversations take time and attention. Here's how to have better ones"

A short NPR episode with practical advice on listening and asking good questions, in natural American English. Good for the car, and there is a full transcript on the page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then report two pieces of advice out loud, starting with: She said that...
