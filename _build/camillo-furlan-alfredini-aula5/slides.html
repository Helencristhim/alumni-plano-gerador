Chapter 3: The Code

Can I...? Can You...?

Ask, offer, say what is possible

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"Can I check in now?"

"I'm sorry, you can't check in before three."

"Can you keep my bag, please?"

"She can speak Spanish."

Look at the orange words. What comes after can? Does can change with she?

JobFormExample
Ask for permissionCan I + verb...?Can I check in now?
Ask for helpCan you + verb...?Can you keep my bag?
Not possible / not allowedcan't + verbYou can't smoke in the rooms.
Abilitycan + verbShe can speak Spanish.
Short answersYes, you can. / No, I can't.Can I pay by card? Yes, you can.
The trapno "to", no -sNot She cans speaks or I can to check. Say She can speak.

In one line: can + simple verb, the same for everybody. For a question, can goes first: Can I...? Can you...?

Chapter 4: At the Hotel

At the Front Desk

One conversation, two recordings, one key card

Dialogue

Can I Check In Now?

H
Good afternoon, welcome to the Hotel Spree. How can I help you?
C
Hi. My name is Camillo Furlan. Can I check in now?
H
I'm sorry, Mr. Furlan. You can't check in before three. Your room isn't ready.
C
No problem. Can you keep my bag, please?
H
Of course. And at three, your room key is here at the front desk.
C
Great. What time is breakfast?
H
From six thirty to ten, on the first floor. The elevator is on the left.
C
And can I check out late on Sunday?
H
Yes, you can. Check-out is at eleven, but you can stay until one. Enjoy Berlin!
Comprehension

What Does Hannah Say?

1. Why can't he check in now, according to Hannah?
Because his room isn't ready. Check-in is at three.
2. Where is breakfast, according to Hannah?
On the first floor.
3. Until what time can he stay on Sunday?
Until one.
