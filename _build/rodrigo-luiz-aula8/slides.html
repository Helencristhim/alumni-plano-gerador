Chapter 3: The Pattern

Two Words Open the Past

was · were · there was · there were

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Last month was difficult.
The last two weeks were easy.
There were two problems with the machines.
It was not our fault.

When do we say "was", and when do we say "were"?

SubjectPastExample
I / he / she / it / one thingwasLast month was difficult.
You / we / they / many thingswereThe last two weeks were easy.
There is → there wasone thingThere was a problem on Tuesday.
There are → there weremany thingsThere were two problems with the machines.
Negativewas not / were notIt was not our fault. The tanks were not full. (Formas curtas: wasn't, weren't.)
