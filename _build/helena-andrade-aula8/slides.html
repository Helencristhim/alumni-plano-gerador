Chapter 4: The Code

Finished Before, or In Progress At

will have done / will be doing

Grammar Discovery

Two Futures, One Deadline

"By the time a ship reaches the pilot station, the crew will have completed the safety round."

"The deck team will be standing by thirty minutes before the pilot arrives."

"By the end of the quarter, the company will have replaced every card."

Which actions are finished before the deadline? Which one is in progress at that moment? Write the word that shows the deadline.

FormWhat it doesExample
will have + past participlefinished BEFORE a moment in the futureBy the time she arrives, the crew will have completed the round.
will be + -ingin progress AT a moment in the futureThe deck team will be standing by at the bow.
by / by the time / by the end ofthe deadline. Look for it firstBy the end of the quarter, the company will have replaced...
The reading trapwill have completed = finished before. will complete on arrival = at the moment of arrival. A wrong option in the exam often changes one into the other.

You do not need to write these tenses. Find the deadline first (by...), then ask: finished before it, or in progress at it?

Four Minutes in a Machinery Space

The chief engineer talks about the February fire. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. How long did the fixed system take to control the fire?
Four minutes.
2. Why could he not open the forward locker?
Somebody had painted it shut at the last dry dock.
3. Why did the two problems not cost more?
Because the order to abandon ship was never given.
Writing

Write What He Said

Task: Write three short sentences about the chief engineer. Use one starter for each sentence.

Start with:

The fixed system...Two people...The forward locker...
The fixed system controlled the fire in four minutes. Two people went to the wrong muster station. The forward locker was painted shut.
Writing

Before the Pilot Boards

Task: Write three sentences for the new instruction: two things that will be finished before the pilot station, and one thing that will be in progress.

Start with:

By the time the ship reaches the pilot station, the crew will have...By then, the officers will have...At that moment, the deck team will be...
By the time the ship reaches the pilot station, the crew will have completed the safety round. By then, the officers will have checked every life jacket by name. At that moment, the deck team will be standing by at the bow and the stern.
