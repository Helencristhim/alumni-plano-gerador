Abandon Ship Drill using Lifeboats

The drill itself, filmed on board: muster, life jackets, boarding and lowering, in the order SOLAS requires it. The words of this lesson are the commands you will hear.

Tip: pause at the muster and say out loud what would have to be complete BEFORE that moment for the drill to work. That is the future perfect, in its natural habitat.
