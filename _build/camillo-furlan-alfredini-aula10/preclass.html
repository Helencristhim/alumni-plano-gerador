Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto em voz alta, devagar, e depois responda as perguntas.

In March, Laura is going to travel abroad. She is going to visit Brazil for the first time. Her flight is twelve hours long.

She is going to have a meeting with her customers. She isn't going to stay in a big hotel: she is going to stay in a small hotel near the fair.

On the last day, she is going to do some sightseeing and buy souvenirs. Her suitcase is going to be very heavy!

1. Quando a Laura vai viajar? (When is Laura going to travel?)
A Na semana que vem, de carro. (Next week, by car.)
B Em março. (In March.)
C Ela não vai viajar. (She isn't going to travel.)
2. Como se diz "Eu vou visitar a Alemanha" em inglês?
A "I go to visit Germany one day with my friends."
B "I going to visit Germany."
C "I'm going to visit Germany."
3. Por que o texto diz "is going to"? (Why "is going to"?)
A Porque é um plano para o futuro. (Because it is a plan for the future.)
B Porque ela está indo para o aeroporto agora, de táxi. (Because she is going to the airport now.)
C Porque é passado. (Because it is the past.)

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Be going to (vou fazer)

GRAMMAR

Para planos, use am / is / are + going to + verbo. É o "vou visitar" do português e o "voy a visitar" do espanhol. / For plans: am / is / are + going to + verb.

Uso / UseEstrutura / PatternExemplo / Example
Plano / Planam / is / are + going to + verboI'm going to visit Germany. (Eu vou visitar a Alemanha.)
Negativa / Negativeam not / isn't / aren't + going toShe isn't going to stay long. (Ela não vai ficar muito.)
Pergunta / QuestionAre / Is + sujeito + going to...?Are you going to travel? (Você vai viajar?)
Quando / Whennext week, next year, in MarchI'm going to travel next year. (Vou viajar no ano que vem.)
Ideia-chave / Key ideaNão esqueça o am / is / are: "I going to" está errado. E depois de "going to" o verbo fica simples: "going to opens" está errado. / Don't forget am / is / are; the verb after "to" has no -s.
