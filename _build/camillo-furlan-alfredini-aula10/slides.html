Chapter 4: The Code

Going To

Plans for the future

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"She is going to visit Brazil."

"I'm going to open a second shop."

"She isn't going to stay in a big hotel."

"Are you going to visit the fair?"

Look at the orange words. Past, now or future? What three pieces do you see?

JobFormExample
A planam / is / are + going to + verbI'm going to visit Germany.
Negativeam not / isn't / aren't + going toShe isn't going to stay long.
QuestionAre / Is + subject + going to...?Are you going to travel?
Short answersYes, I am. / No, she isn't.Is she going to come? Yes, she is.
Time wordstomorrow / next week / next year / in MarchI'm going to travel next year.
The trapdon't forget am / is / areNot I going to travel. Say I'm going to travel.

In one line: for plans, am / is / are + going to + verb. Just like Spanish: voy a visitar.
