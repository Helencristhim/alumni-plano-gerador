Lesson 05 -- Working the Floor

Talk
Celeste Headlee -- "10 ways to have a better conversation" (TEDxCreativeCoast)

Eleven minutes on the skill this lesson trains: asking instead of performing. Her rules are made for exactly the situation you are in at a fair — a stranger, very little time, and a conversation that dies if you only talk about yourself.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Then pick the two rules you break most often in Portuguese, and try them in your next call.

Podcast
NPR How I Built This -- "Starbucks: Howard Schultz" (live episode)

A recorded live conversation, which is a different listening exercise from a studio interview: an audience, interruptions, and two people speaking at natural speed. He tells the story of a trade fair in Milan that changed the whole company — the exact scene of this lesson.

Tip: good for the car. Count the questions the host asks and notice how short they are. Short questions are what keep a conversation moving.

Series
Shark Tank (US version)

Each segment is a person with ninety seconds to explain a business to strangers who then attack it with questions. It is the fair, with a camera. American English, business vocabulary, and the fastest question-and-answer training you can watch.

Tip: one segment is enough. Write down every question the investors ask, and notice how many start with a helper: do, did, are, can.
