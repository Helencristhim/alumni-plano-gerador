Chapter 3: The Code

There Is, There Are

One place, two places

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"There is a pharmacy on Ocean Street."

"There are two supermarkets."

"Is there a pharmacy near here?"

"Are there restaurants on this street?"

Look at the blue words. When is it is, and when is it are? In a question, where is the verb?

JobFormExample
One thingThere is (There’s)There is a pharmacy on Ocean Street.
Two or moreThere areThere are two supermarkets.
Question (one)Is there...?Is there a pharmacy near here?
Question (two or more)Are there...?Are there restaurants here?
Short answerYes, there is. / No, there are not.Is there a bank? Yes, there is.

In one line: There is: one thing. There are: two or more. Never “have” for a place.

Chapter 4: On the Street

On the Street

One conversation, two messages, one guide

Dialogue

Excuse Me

E
Excuse me. Is there a pharmacy near here?
L
Yes, there is. There is a pharmacy on Ocean Street.
E
Where is Ocean Street?
L
Go to the corner and turn left.
E
Left at the corner. Okay. And is there a supermarket?
L
Yes, there are two supermarkets. One is on the right, next to the bank.
E
Great. Is the pharmacy open now?
L
Yes, it is. It is open until ten p.m.
E
Thank you so much!
L
You are welcome. Have a nice day!
Comprehension

About Luis

1. Where is the pharmacy, according to Luis?
On Ocean Street, left at the corner.
2. How many supermarkets are there, according to Luis?
Two.
3. The pharmacy is open until what time?
Until ten p.m.
