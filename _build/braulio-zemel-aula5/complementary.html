Grammar Video
"Inversion 1: after negative or limiting adverbs" -- BBC Learning English, Masterclass

A short masterclass on the structure you built today: never, rarely, seldom and not only at the front of a sentence, and the question order that follows. Connection to Lesson 5: this is the difference between I have never seen that and never have I seen that -- same fact, and only the second one makes a table look up.

Tip: pause after each example and say one true sentence about a trip of your own. Then say it again the ordinary way and listen to the difference in weight.
