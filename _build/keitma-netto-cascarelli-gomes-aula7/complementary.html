Talk
Margaret Heffernan — Dare to disagree (TED)

Thirteen minutes on what happens in organisations when nobody asks the uncomfortable question — and on the people whose job is to ask it anyway. It is the audit relationship, described from the outside.

Tip: listen for how often she describes a process rather than a person. Write down three of those sentences and check the shape: what is the verb doing?

Talk
Ricardo Semler — How to run a company with (almost) no rules (TED)

Twenty minutes from a Brazilian executive speaking English about controls, trust and what a process is really for. His accent is one you already understand, so your ear can work on the structures instead of the sounds.

Tip: he describes a lot of processes. Every time he does, note whether he names a person or a role — and try saying the same sentence the other way.

Podcast
The Indicator from Planet Money — Did you know companies can vote in Delaware?

Nine minutes about entities, filings and who is allowed to do what — the legal side of the world your company codes live in. It is dense with passive sentences, because that is how rules are described in English.

Tip: listen once for the story. On the second pass, write down five sentences in the passive and mark whether they name an agent after by or leave it out.
