Chapter 3: The Code

The Small Verb

I am, you are, it is

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I am Eliane."

"You are in seat 14A."

"It is a window seat."

"We are on the flight to Miami."

Look at the blue words. They are one verb. What changes: I, you, it, we?

JobFormExample
II am / I’mI am Eliane. I’m from Brazil.
Youyou are / you’reYou are in seat 14A.
He, she, it (the flight, the seat)is / ’sThe flight is eight hours.
We, theyare / ’reWe are on the plane.

In one line: I am, you are, it is, we are. The verb is always there.

Chapter 4: On the Plane

On the Plane

One conversation, two messages, one boarding pass

Dialogue

Welcome On Board

M
Good evening. I am Mark. Welcome to the flight to Miami.
E
Good evening. I am Eliane. This is my ticket.
M
Thank you, Eliane. And your passport, please.
E
Here it is. I am from Brazil, from São Paulo.
M
Great. You are in seat 14A. It is a window seat.
E
Thank you. My bag is small.
M
Perfect. It is okay under the seat. The flight is eight hours.
E
Okay. Miami is my second home.
M
Wonderful. Have a good flight, Eliane!
Comprehension

About Mark

1. What is the man’s name?
His name is Mark.
2. What seat is Eliane in, according to Mark?
Seat 14A, a window seat.
3. How long is the flight, according to Mark?
It is eight hours.
