Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Ted Lasso — Season 1, Episodes 1 and 2

An American lands in England and takes every polite English sentence at face value. Half the jokes in the first two episodes are exactly the thing you heard in this lesson: somebody says something mild and means something firm, and only one person in the room misses it.

Tip: English subtitles on. Every time a British character is very polite, pause and say out loud what they actually mean.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English — “Why are we all so stressed?”

Six minutes, two British speakers, normal speed, on where the pressure at work actually comes from. It is the human half of this lesson: the language of a moved deadline is easy once you stop treating the delay as a personal failure. Free transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen once with no text, then write two sentences with if and one with unless about your own week. Check them against the transcript.

Talk
Tim Urban — “Inside the mind of a master procrastinator” (TED)

Fourteen minutes on what actually happens between the day you take the work and the day it is due. He is funny and he speaks fast, which makes this the hardest listening on your list this week and the one worth repeating.

Tip: watch it once at normal speed with no subtitles, and only then with them. Notice how much you understood the first time — it is usually more than it felt like.
