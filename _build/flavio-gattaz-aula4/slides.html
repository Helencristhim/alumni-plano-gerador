Chapter 4: The Code

The Purpose, and the Result

Two ways to answer why, and they take different shapes

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Share?

"I am applying in order to close a gap I can already see."

"I chose an online format so that I could keep running the company."

"The residencies are short, so most participants keep working."

"The company grew faster than I did, which is why I am applying now."

One of the red parts is followed by a bare verb. The other three are followed by a full sentence with its own subject. That is the first thing to see. The second is that two of them give the reason, and two give the consequence.

JobFormExample
Purpose, neutralto + base formI am applying to close a gap.
Purpose, formalin order to / so as to + base formI am applying in order to close a gap.
Purpose, subject changesso that + subject + can / could / will / wouldI chose it so that my uncle could stay on site.
Negative purposeso as not to / in order not toI moved the trip so as not to miss the residency.
Result, so + full sentenceThe weeks are short, so most people keep working.
Result, pointing back, which is why + full sentenceThe company grew faster, which is why I am applying.
The trapfor never introduces a verb of purposeNot for improve my English. Say to improve.

In one line: same subject takes to; a new subject takes so that; and the consequence takes so.

Chapter 5: Real Documents

One Page, Two Voices

The fact sheet, the admissions office and somebody who did it

Real Document

The Fact Sheet, One Page

EXECUTIVE PROGRAM — FACT SHEET
online format · intake beginning next year
Cohort70 participants, around 30 countries
Formatonline, plus 2 residencies a year
Prerequisite10 years of management experience
Admissionsinterviews from October, one round only
Tuitionpayable in four instalments; travel not included
Deferralonce only, on request, before enrollment
Candidates are shortlisted on the strength of the written application and are then invited to a thirty-minute interview. The faculty teaching on the program are drawn from the school and from industry. The final capstone is built around a real problem inside the participant's own organization, which is why applicants are asked to name that problem at the point of application.
1. Two lines on this sheet decide whether you can apply this year at all. Which two, and why?
The prerequisite, ten years of management experience, and the admissions line, because there is only one round and the interviews start in October.
2. What does the sheet say about the deferral, and what is the limit on it?
It is granted once only, on request, and it has to be asked for before enrollment.
3. According to the paragraph, why are applicants asked to name a real problem when they apply?
Because the final capstone is built around a real problem inside the participant's own organization.
Listening 1 · Admissions

The Answer We Hear Too Often

An admissions director on the difference between why and why now. Sound first — no text.

1. What answer does she say she hears too often, and what is wrong with it?
That the candidate wants to grow and broaden their horizons. It is true of everybody who applies, so it makes the case for nobody in particular.
2. What does she say the question why now is really asking?
What changed. Something moved in the last year or two, and if the candidate cannot name it, the committee assumes nothing did.
3. What does she say about candidates who have been on the fence for years?
That being on the fence is not a problem as long as they can say what pushed them off it. The ones who cannot are usually the ones who are not shortlisted.
Listening 2 · An Alumnus

What Nobody Tells You About the Online Format

Somebody two years out, on what the brochure leaves out. Sound first — no text.

1. What does he say about the ten to fifteen hours a week the page mentions?
That the number is honest, but nobody tells you which hours they are: they come out of the evening and out of Sunday, and the family notices before you do.
2. What does he say the residency weeks are actually for?
Not the teaching, which he says you could do online. They are where the cohort turns into people you would call, and that is the part that lasts after the program ends.
3. What is his advice about the capstone?
To choose the problem you are already avoiding at work, because you are going to spend a year on it either way.
