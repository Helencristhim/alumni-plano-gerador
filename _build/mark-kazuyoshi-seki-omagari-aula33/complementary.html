Lesson 33 -- Because of the Rain...

Film
The Terminal (2004)

Tom Hanks plays a man who lands in New York and cannot leave the airport, because while he was in the air his country stopped being recognized. One cause, and months of knock-on effects: the whole film is a chain of results, and he decides to make the best of it.

Tip: after the first twenty minutes, pause and explain his situation out loud in three sentences, each with a different link -- because of, so ... that, which is why.

Radio story
NPR -- Flight Overbooked? Perfect: These Frequent Flyers Want To Get Bumped

A short radio story, under five minutes, about travelers who are happy when a flight is overbooked, because giving up their seat gets them vouchers and compensation. It is the same vocabulary as the lesson, told from the side of the people who win.

Tip: press play at the top of the page, and every time you hear a result, say its cause out loud with "because of" or "which is why".

YouTube
engVid -- Conversational English: So & Such

A whiteboard lesson on exactly the trap from class: when it is "so" and when it is "such", with adjectives, nouns and "that". Watch it after the lesson, so that it confirms what you already worked out.

Tip: pause before each example is completed and say it yourself first -- then add a "that" and a result to every one.
