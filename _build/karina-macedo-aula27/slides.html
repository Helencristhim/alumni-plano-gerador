Chapter 3: I Want To

I want to... · My dream is to...

how to talk about your goals

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I want to grow my company."

"I would like to expand to the United States."

"My dream is to have an office in Miami."

"I don't want to wait."

FormUseExample
want to + verbsay what you wantI want to grow my company.
would like to (I'd like to) + verbsay what you want, more politeI'd like to visit more fairs.
My dream / My goal is to + verbpresent your big goalMy dream is to have an office in Miami.
don't want to + verbnegativeI don't want to wait.
What do you want to + verb?ask about plansWhat do you want to do next year?

want to = querer · would like to = gostaria de. Always to + verb: want to grow, not want grow. / Sempre to + verbo.

Chapter 4: Karina's Future

Five Years from Now

a goal card · a call with Mike

Real Artifact

Karina's Goal Card

5-Year Plan

Karina Macedo -- Aircraft Sales, Sao Paulo

My dream: an office in Miami

Step 1 (this year): improve my English

Step 2 (next year): visit three aviation fairs in the United States

Step 3 (in three years): expand to Miami with a partner

Success in 2030: twenty aircraft sales a year

Look at this card every week. One step at a time.

Dialogue

A Call About the Future

M
Hi Karina! My partners in Miami want to know about your plans. What are your goals for the future?
K
Good question, Mike. My big dream is to have an office in Miami.
M
That's great! Why do you want to expand to the United States?
K
Many of my clients are there. I want to grow my company and sell more aircraft.
M
I see. And what is your first step?
K
This year, I would like to improve my English. Then I want to visit more fairs.
M
Good plan. What does success mean for you?
K
For me, success is happy clients and twenty aircraft sales a year.
M
You can achieve that, Karina. Would you like to work with my partners in Miami?
K
Yes, I'd love to! Let's talk about the next steps.
