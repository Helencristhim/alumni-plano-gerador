Grammar Discovery

Two cities, one sentence

São Paulo is bigger than Boston.
Boston is colder than São Paulo.
The park is more beautiful than the avenue.
The food here is better than the food in Boston.

What do the orange words have in common?

short word: big → bigger than · long word: more beautiful than

To compare two things: a short word gets -er, a long word gets more before it. Then than. Special: good → better, bad → worse.

The Code

-er or more?

How it changes

small → smaller
big → bigger (gg)
noisy → noisier (y → i)
beautiful → more beautiful
good → better · bad → worse

Never

São Paulo is more big.
short word: bigger
Boston is colder that São Paulo.
compare with than
The food is more good.
special: better

Question: Is Boston bigger than São Paulo? — No, it isn't. It's smaller.

Sound Check

"than" is small

São Paulo is bigger than Boston.

"than" is short and soft

The park is more beautiful.

BEAU-ti-ful: strong on the first part

The food here is better.

in American English, "better" sounds like BED-er

A message from Mark
