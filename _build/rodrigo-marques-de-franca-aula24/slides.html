Detective

Spot the Error

0 / 4 errors found

"Not only they missed the deadline, but they also changed the price."
"Not only did they miss the deadline, but they also changed the price." (after not only, the auxiliary comes first)
"The deposit shall returned within thirty days."
"The deposit shall be returned within thirty days." (modal + be + past participle)
"Who did tell you that the deal was off?"
"Who told you that the deal was off?" (who is the subject, so no did)
"They must have forget to sign the second page."
"They must have forgotten to sign the second page." (must have + past participle)
Listening 1 · An Arbitrator

The First Ten Minutes

An arbitrator on what makes her trust an argument. Sound first — no text.

1. What does she know within the first ten minutes?
Whether counsel understands where the burden of proof lies.
2. What does she want lawyers to do with the other side's argument?
Say the strongest thing the other side will say, and then explain why it is still wrong.
3. What happens when a lawyer adds a point only to distract her?
She trusts the other points a little less.
Listening 2 · A Debate Coach

Swap the Papers

A university debate coach on his first exercise. Sound first — no text.

1. What do the new students do after they write their best argument?
They swap papers and spend fifteen minutes trying to find the weak points in somebody else's argument.
2. How had some students cherry-picked their evidence?
They used three studies that agreed with them and none of the ten that did not.
3. What question destroys a slippery slope?
Why would the second step follow from the first?
