Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Memorandum -- a short document that describes what each side will do
"We signed a memorandum with the agency in Bologna."
Obligation -- something that you must do because you agreed to do it
"Our obligation is to organize the farm visits."
Clause -- one part of a written agreement, about one point
"The memorandum has a clause about the pilot."
Leverage -- to use something you have to get a better result
"We can leverage the results for new projects."
Alignment -- when two sides agree on the same goals
"Alignment on the goals is more important than size."
Win-win -- good for both sides at the same time
"This pilot is a win-win for the producers."
Stakeholder -- a person or group that is interested in a project and affected by it
"The producers are the main stakeholders."
Pilot -- a small first project to test an idea
"The pilot starts in July with ten producers."
Expertise -- special knowledge or skill in one area
"The agency has expertise in export rules."
Resource -- something useful that you can use, like money, people, or time
"Our main resource is our team of consultants."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Flavia talks with Giorgio, from an agricultural agency in Bologna. Giorgio asks about the details of the partnership. Flavia says: "What we are offering is access to producers who have a geographic indication. In return, we would expect your expertise in export rules." Giorgio asks about the pilot. Flavia explains: "If we sign the memorandum in May, the pilot will start in July. This partnership would allow us to test the idea with low risk. One of the key benefits is that our producers can reach new markets." Giorgio likes the idea. For him, it is a win-win situation.

1. What is Flavia offering?
A Money for a big food fair in Bologna, with the travel and the hotels for the Italian team.
B Access to producers who have a geographic indication.
C A new office in Italy.
2. What would Flavia expect in return?
A Giorgio's expertise in export rules.
B A visit to all the agencies and producers in the north of Italy next year.
C A free dinner in Milan.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A "This partnership would allow us test the idea with low risk, and grow together later."
B "This partnership allow us to testing the idea."
C "This partnership would allow us to test the idea."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Describing a Partnership

GRAMMAR

How to say what you offer, what you expect, and why the partnership is good for both sides.

FunctionPhraseExample
What you giveWhat we are offering is + nounWhat we are offering is access to our producers.
What you want backIn return, we would expect + nounIn return, we would expect your expertise.
The resultThis partnership would allow us to + verbThis partnership would allow us to test the idea.
A benefitOne of the key benefits is that + sentenceOne of the key benefits is that both sides share the costs.
Extra detailswho (people) / that, which (things) + ifWe work with producers who have a GI, and if we sign in May, we will start in July.
RuleAfter allow us, use to + verb: "allow us to test" (not "allow us test"). After one of the, use a plural noun: "one of the key benefits". Use who for people and that / which for things. After if, use the present: "If we sign" (not "If we will sign").
