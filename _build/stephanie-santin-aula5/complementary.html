Lesson 5 -- If I Pass, I Will Apply

Talk
Atul Gawande -- "How do we heal medicine?"

A surgeon argues that medicine fails less because of knowledge and more because of systems and checklists. Useful language and a useful idea for someone about to face a six-item requirements list.

Tip: while you watch, write three first conditional sentences he could have said about the systems he describes.

Talk
Tim Urban -- "Inside the mind of a master procrastinator"

Funny, fast and full of deadlines. The speed is the exercise: this is closer to how people really talk than any recording made for learners.

Tip: watch it once at normal speed with no subtitles, then again with them. Note the three sentences you only understood the second time.

Guided Study
TED-Ed -- "How does your body know what time it is?"

A short animated lesson with comprehension questions, on circadian rhythm. Content you know, in the register of a patient explanation.

Tip: answer the questions out loud. Then say three things that will happen if a person on night shifts ignores that rhythm.
