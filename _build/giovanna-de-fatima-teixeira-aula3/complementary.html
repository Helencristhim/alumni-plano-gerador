Easy English Conversations -- Episode 4: "How to talk about your daily routine"

Five minutes from BBC Learning English. Two presenters tell each other what they do every day, slowly and with subtitles on the screen. It is the same conversation you had in class, with two other people.

Tip: listen for one thing only -- the word that comes before each verb. Every time you hear always, usually, often, sometimes or never, say the whole sentence out loud after them.

Podcast
5-Minute English Practice -- "[A2] My Daily Routine in English"

Anna tells her whole day, from the alarm to the evening, in five minutes and at a calm speed. She uses the same four words you used in class to join the parts: first, then, after that and finally.

Tip: good for the commute, when you cannot read or write but you can listen. Play it once without stopping, then again and count how many times you hear "then".

Animated English -- "My Daily Routine (for Learners of English)"

Under three minutes. A man tells his day from morning to night, and the video puts on the screen exactly the two things you learned today: the words for how often, and the words for what comes next.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause after each sentence and say the same sentence about yourself, out loud.
