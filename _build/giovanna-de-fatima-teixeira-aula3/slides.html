Chapter 3: The Pattern

Two Small Words

do · not

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

I work on Monday.
I do not work on Saturday.
Do you work on Saturday?
What time do you wake up?

Which word appears in the last three sentences, but not in the first?

TypePatternExemplo
AfirmativaI + verbI work on Monday.
NegativaI + do not + verbI do not work on Saturday. (= I don't work)
PerguntaDo + you + verb?Do you work on Saturday?
HorasA hora vem sempre com at: at seven, at five thirty, at twelve. Nunca "in seven" nem "on seven". O portugues nao tem o "do" da negativa e da pergunta — e ele que costuma sumir.
Clock Time

Always With At

"I wake up at five thirty."

at + hour — the start of your day

"I have lunch at twelve."

have lunch — never "take lunch"

"What time do you arrive home?"

the question you can ask anyone

Chapter 4: A Long Day

Coffee at Seven

two people, two very different days

Dialogue

You Look Tired

D
Good morning. You look tired today.
G
I am. I wake up at five thirty every day.
D
Five thirty? That is very early. I wake up at seven.
G
You are lucky. I leave home at six and I arrive at the site at seven.
D
I do not drive here. I take the train from the hotel.
G
And what time do you have lunch?
D
At one. I do not eat at the site. I go to a small place near the station.
G
Do you work in the afternoon too?
D
Yes, I do. I finish at six, and then I walk to the hotel.
G
I arrive home at eight, and I am very tired. But I do not work on Saturday.
Comprehension

About Daniel

1. What time does Daniel wake up?
At seven.
2. How does Daniel come to the site?
He takes the train from the hotel. He does not drive.
3. Where does Daniel have lunch?
At a small place near the station, at one.

Listening 2

A Day That Starts at Night

Ana works when the city sleeps. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What time does Ana start work?
At seven at night. She finishes at seven in the morning.
2. Why does Ana not have lunch?
Because she sleeps in the day. She has dinner at six, before work.
