Chapter 3: How Often, In What Order

Two Families of Small Words

always · usually · never  |  first · then · finally

Grammar Discovery

How Often?

I always wake up at five thirty.
I usually get home at eight.
I sometimes work overtime on Friday.
The traffic is often heavy at six.

Where do the orange words go — before or after the verb?

WordHow many daysExample
alwaysevery dayI always wake up at five thirty.
usuallyalmost every dayI usually get home at eight.
oftenmany daysThe traffic is often heavy.
sometimesa few daysI sometimes work overtime.
neverzero daysI never relax before dinner.
PositionBefore the verb: I always wake up. · But after am / is / are: I am never late. The traffic is often heavy.
Grammar Discovery

In What Order?

First, my alarm goes off at five thirty.
Then I get ready and I leave home at six.
After that, I sit in the traffic for one hour.
Finally, I get home at eight and I relax.

Which word opens the day, and which one closes it?

WordWhere in the storyExample
First,the beginning — only onceFirst, my alarm goes off.
Thenthe next thing — use it many timesThen I get ready.
After that,the next thing, a bit slowerAfter that, I leave home.
Finally,the end — only onceFinally, I relax.
Quick trickThey all come at the start of the sentence. First and Finally open and close the day; Then and After that carry the middle.

Dialogue

So, Tell Me About Your Day

G
So, this is our catch up. My day here starts at eight, and I never wake up early. And you?
Gi
First, my alarm goes off at five thirty. I always wake up before the sun.
G
Five thirty! In Seattle I usually wake up at seven. And then?
Gi
Then I get ready in twenty minutes, and I leave home at six.
G
Six in the morning. Is the traffic bad at that time?
Gi
It is often heavy, yes. After that, I arrive at the site at seven and I read the schedule.
G
And your lunch break? Mine is always one hour, at my desk.
Gi
Mine is at twelve, but it is short. Thirty minutes, usually.
G
Do you ever work overtime?
Gi
Sorry, could you say that again, please? The machines are loud.
G
Overtime — do you sometimes stay after five?
Gi
Ah, yes. Sometimes on Friday. Finally I get home at eight, and I relax. That is my routine.
Comprehension

About Greg

1. What time does Greg's day start here?
At eight. He never wakes up early.
2. What time does he usually wake up in Seattle?
At seven.
3. How long is Greg's lunch break, and where?
Always one hour, at his desk.
