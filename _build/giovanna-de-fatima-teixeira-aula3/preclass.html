Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Leia o texto em voz alta e depois responda as perguntas. Repare em quantas vezes aparece a palavra "at".

My day is long. I wake up at five thirty. That is very early, but I like the morning. I leave home at six, and I arrive at the site at seven.

I have lunch at twelve. I do not have lunch at home — I eat at the site office with my team. I finish at six and I arrive home at eight. At eight I am very tired.

I do not work on Saturday and I do not work on Sunday. And you? What time do you wake up?

1. Como se diz "eu não trabalho no sábado" em inglês? (How do you say it?)
A "I not work on Saturday."
B "I do not work on Saturday."
C "I no work on Saturday."
2. Qual preposição vem antes da hora? (Which preposition?)
A in — "in seven"
B at — "at seven"
C on — "on seven"
3. Onde ela almoça? (Where does she have lunch?)
A At home. (Em casa.)
B At the site office, with her team. (No escritório da obra, com a equipe.)
C She does not have lunch. (Ela não almoça.)

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- As duas palavras que faltam

Grammar

O português não tem o "do" da negativa e da pergunta — por isso ele some. / Portuguese has no "do" in negatives and questions, and that is why it disappears.

Tipo / TypeEstrutura / PatternExemplo / Example
Afirmativa / AffirmativeI + verboI work on Monday. (Trabalho na segunda.)
Negativa / NegativeI + do not + verboI do not work on Saturday. (Não trabalho no sábado.)
Pergunta / QuestionDo + you + verbo?Do you work on Saturday? (Você trabalha no sábado?)
Pergunta com hora / What timeWhat time do you + verbo?What time do you wake up? (Que horas você acorda?)
Ideia-chave / Key ideaA hora vem sempre com at: at seven, at five thirty, at twelve. Nunca "in seven" nem "on seven". Forma curta da negativa: I do not = I don't. / Clock time always takes "at". Short form: don't.
