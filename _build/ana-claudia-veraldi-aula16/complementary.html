Lesson 16 -- It Must Be...

Series
Sherlock — Sherlock and John’s First Meeting (A Study in Pink, BBC)

The most famous detective in English meets Dr. Watson and, in two minutes, figures out where he has been from a few small clues. Fast British English and the best example of evidence, not a hunch.

Tip: watch it once for fun. Then pause after each deduction and say it with must: he must have been...

Podcast
Hidden Brain — How Others See You

A psychologist explains why we are so bad at guessing what other people think of us after a first conversation — our deductions about ourselves are often wrong too.

Tip: listen for might and must. Notice how often the guest says what people might think, not what they must think.

YouTube
Why Do Americans Smile So Much? — The Atlantic

A short video on why smiling at strangers is normal in the United States and not in many other countries — the other side of Elodie’s story in tonight’s listening.

Tip: after watching, make three guesses about a smiling stranger in New York and a serious waiter in Paris, using must, might and can’t.
