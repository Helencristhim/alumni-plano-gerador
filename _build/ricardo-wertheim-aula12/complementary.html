Grammar Video
"English Grammar Lesson: How to Use Used to for Past Habits" -- MK's English

A short lesson on the exact structure of this class: used to for habits that are not true now, the negative with didn't use to, and the question with Did you use to. Connection to Lesson 12: every example is a then-and-now sentence, like the ones you will record this week.

Tip: pause after each example and say the same sentence about your own trips. Then change it into a question for another traveler: Did you use to...?

Two presenters talk about how travel has changed and what visitors do to the places they love. Connection to Lesson 12: the same question as the reading, from the side of the city: the traveler who collects places, and the one who blends in.

Two comedians try to see Ljubljana in 48 hours: a very structured, very fast trip in the same city as the guide from Listening 1. Connection to Lesson 12: it is the opposite of a slow pace, and a good start for your own opinion.
