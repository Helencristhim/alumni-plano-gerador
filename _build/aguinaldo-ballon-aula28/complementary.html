Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TED -- Alan Siegel, "Let's simplify legal jargon!" (5 min)

A brand strategist on why contracts, tax forms and credit card agreements are written so that nobody can read them, and what happens when they are rewritten in plain language. Short, clear, and directly about today's lesson.

Tip: pause after each example and say how you would explain the same rule to an investor, with has to and as long as.

Talk
TED -- Philip K. Howard, "Four ways to fix a broken legal system" (18 min)

A lawyer on how too many detailed rules make people stop thinking. Strong opinions, a clear structure, and a good model of a lawyer speaking to a general audience without legal jargon.

Tip: note one sentence where he paraphrases a rule instead of quoting it, and say it back in your own words.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
TED -- Jim Yong Kim, "Doesn't everyone deserve a chance at a good life?" (12 min)

The former president of the World Bank on infrastructure, investment and development. Useful for the vocabulary of your field, and for listening to an institutional leader explain complex policy in plain words.

Tip: write down two sentences with have to, can or only if, and use them to describe a project of your own.
