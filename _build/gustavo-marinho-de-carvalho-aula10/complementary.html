Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Chef’s Table — any single episode

Every episode is one person describing what they kept on doing for years, what they gave up, and what they eventually managed to do. It is the grammar of this lesson told as a life, and each episode stands on its own in about fifty minutes.

Tip: English subtitles on. At the end, describe the person in four sentences: what they gave up, what they kept on doing, what they managed to do, and what they are looking forward to.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English — “Should we cycle more?”

Six minutes, two British speakers, normal speed, on a change that most people plan, start, and give up within a month. Half the episode is verb followed by verb, which is the structure you spent today on. Free transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen once with no text, then say four sentences about a habit of your own, using end up, keep on, manage to and be reluctant to.

Talk
Lydia Macháová — “The secrets of learning a new language” (TED)

Ten minutes from somebody who speaks nine languages and refuses to say she has a gift. Her argument is the same as the recording you heard in class: the method matters far less than the number of minutes, and the people who succeed are the ones who found a way to enjoy the minutes.

Tip: watch it at the end of block one, not before. Then say out loud which of her four principles you already do and which one you have been avoiding.
