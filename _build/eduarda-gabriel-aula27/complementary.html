Lesson 27 -- Due Diligence

Documentary
Skandal! Bringing Down Wirecard

A German payment company was worth billions, until a team of journalists dug into its accounts and found that a large part of the money did not exist. Connection to Lesson 27: it shows what happens when auditors and investors do not ask the right questions.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down three questions that an auditor should have asked Wirecard, as indirect questions ("Could you tell me whether...").

"10 ways to have a better conversation" -- Celeste Headlee, TED

A radio interviewer shares what she learned from thousands of interviews: ask open questions, listen, and don't pretend to know. Connection to Lesson 27: her advice is exactly what the consultant says about due diligence questions.

Tip: listen once without subtitles. Then choose two of her ten rules and explain how you could use them in a meeting with a CFO.
