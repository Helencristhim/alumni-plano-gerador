Chapter 3: Reporting the Past

There was · There were · There was no

what existed on the farm -- and what did not

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"There was a gap in the documentation."

"There were two missing documents."

"There was no record of the training."

"The producer did not have the map."

FormUseExample
There was + one thingone problem existedThere was an issue in the warehouse.
There were + two or moretwo or more problems existedThere were two critical findings.
There was no / There were nonothing was thereThere was no record of the training.
did not havesomeone did not have somethingThe producer did not have the map.
was not / were notsomething was not correctThe farm was not compliant.
today: there is / there are the visit: there was / there were

One thing = there was. Two or more = there were. Nothing = there was no / there were no. / Uma coisa = there was. Duas ou mais = there were. Nada = there was no / there were no.

Common Mistake

Wrong vs Right

✗ WRONG

"There was two problems on the farm."

✓ RIGHT

"There were two problems on the farm."

✗ WRONG

"The producer not had the documents."

✓ RIGHT

"The producer did not have the documents."

Plural takes "were". The past negative takes "did not have". / Plural pede "were". A negativa do passado pede "did not have".

Chapter 4: A Real Finding

One Corrective Action Plan

Criterion · finding · severity · corrective action

Real Artifact

Corrective Action Plan

Corrective Action PlanCarlos Eduardo · Sucafina Brazil
Criterion: 7 -- Storage and processing records
Finding: There was no record of the storage area.
Severity: Critical. The farm was not compliant.
Second finding: There were two names missing in the training list. Minor.
Corrective action: The producer sends the storage records in thirty days.
Dialogue

Emma Asks: Were There Any Issues?

E
Hi Carlos. How was the verification visit on Monday? Were there any issues?
C
Yes, Emma. There were two findings. One was minor, and one was critical.
E
Tell me the critical one first, please.
C
There was no record of the storage area. The producer did not have the storage documents.
E
That is a non-compliance with criterion 7. For me, that is the critical finding.
C
The minor one was a small gap in the training list. Two names were missing.
E
Good. In the report, please write the criterion, the finding, and the corrective action. I need it by Friday.
C
Understood, Emma. Thank you for the help.
