Aula 17 -- Pre-class

Describing Problems and Non-Compliance -- Reporting Issues Found During Verification

Reporte os problemas encontrados numa visita de verificacao: o que faltava, qual criterio nao foi cumprido, a gravidade do achado e a acao corretiva exigida. Gramatica: there was / there were e a negativa do passado (there was no, did not have, was not). Seis palavras de relatorio e uma pagina modelo do corrective action plan.

Etapa 1.1: Cartoes de Vocabulario

Vocabulary

Ouca cada palavra e leia o exemplo. Sao as palavras que voce usa para reportar um problema. Toque Listen para ouvir. (Listen = Ouvir)

Issue -- problema (algo que voce encontra no trabalho)
"We found an issue in the storage area." (Encontramos um problema na area de armazenamento.)
Gap -- falha / lacuna (algo que esta faltando)
"There was a gap in the documentation." (Havia uma falha na documentacao.)
Finding -- constatacao / achado (o que voce descobre numa verificacao)
"This is a critical finding." (Esta e uma constatacao critica.)
Non-compliance -- nao conformidade (quando a fazenda nao cumpre uma regra)
"This is a non-compliance with criterion 7." (Isto e uma nao conformidade com o criterio 7.)
Corrective action -- acao corretiva (o que o produtor precisa fazer para corrigir)
"A corrective action is required." (Uma acao corretiva e exigida.)
Critical -- critico (muito serio, precisa ser resolvido agora)
"The missing record is a critical finding." (O registro que falta e uma constatacao critica.)

Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Leia o relatorio da visita e responda as perguntas. (Read the visit report and answer.)

Last Monday, Carlos verified a coffee farm in the south of Minas Gerais. He found two issues. There was no record of the storage area, and the producer did not have the storage documents. The farm was not compliant with criterion 7, so this is a non-compliance and a critical finding. There were also two names missing in the training list. That gap is a minor issue. For each problem, Carlos wrote a corrective action in the plan.

1. What was missing on the farm? (O que faltava na fazenda?)
A The map of the region. (o mapa)
B The record of the storage area. (o registro da area de armazenamento)
C The email of the producer. (o email)
2. Which finding is critical? (Qual constatacao e critica?)
A The gap in the training list. (essa e minor)
B The non-compliance with criterion 7. (essa e critica)
C The date of the visit. (nao e um problema)
3. How do we say that two things were missing? (Como dizemos que duas coisas faltavam?)
A There was two names missing. (singular com plural)
B There were two names missing. (plural = were)
C There is two names missing. (presente)

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- There Was / There Were

Grammar

Como dizer o que existia e o que NAO existia na fazenda. / How to say what was there and what was not there.

Form / FormaUse / UsoExample / Exemplo
There was + singularuma coisa existia (havia)There was an issue in the warehouse. (Havia um problema no armazem.)
There were + pluralduas ou mais coisas existiamThere were two critical findings. (Havia duas constatacoes criticas.)
There was no / There were nonao havia nadaThere was no record of the training. (Nao havia registro do treinamento.)
did not havealguem NAO tinha algo (negativa do passado)The producer did not have the map. (O produtor nao tinha o mapa.)
was not / were notalgo NAO estava correto: The farm was not compliant with criterion 7. (A fazenda nao estava em conformidade com o criterio 7.)

Lembre: na aula 13 voce usou there is / there are para hoje. Para a visita que ja aconteceu, use there was / there were. / Remember: there is / there are is for today; there was / there were is for the past.

Etapa 4: Quiz Situacional

Quiz

Escolha a melhor resposta para cada situacao.

The coordinator asks: "Were there any issues?" You found one problem. (Voce encontrou UM problema.)
A "Yes, there were one issue."
B "Yes, there was one issue."
C "Yes, there is one issue yesterday."
The producer had no training list on the visit day. (O produtor nao tinha a lista.)
A "The producer not had the training list."
B "The producer did not have the training list."
C "The producer does not had the training list."
You report a very serious non-compliance. (Voce reporta algo muito serio.)
A "It is a small gap, no action."
B "This is a critical finding, and a corrective action is required."
C "There is nothing in the report."
