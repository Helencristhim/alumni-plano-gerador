Lesson 08 -- The Spec Sheet

Talk
Tom Wujec -- "Build a tower, build a team" (TED2010)

Six minutes on why the teams that build a prototype early beat the teams that plan first, told through an experiment run with thousands of people. Short, clear American English and the exact argument the plant manager makes in this lesson.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Then say out loud, in three sentences, what your company tests too late.

Podcast
NPR How I Built This -- "Dyson: James Dyson"

Five thousand one hundred and twenty-seven prototypes before one worked. Forty minutes of natural English on specifications, testing and the distance between something that works in a workshop and something a factory can produce every day.

Tip: good for the car. Count how many times he says a version of not yet, and notice that the whole story is told in the tense you learned tonight.

Series
Abstract: The Art of Design -- any episode

Forty minutes inside the head of somebody who decides what a product has to be before anybody makes it. Slow, deliberate American English, and every episode is essentially a specification being argued about.

Tip: one episode is enough. Write down five sentences where somebody describes a requirement, and compare them with the way your own documents say the same thing.
