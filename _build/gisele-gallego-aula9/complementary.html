Lesson 9 -- Back to Work

Film
The Intern (2015) -- Nancy Meyers, with Robert De Niro and Anne Hathaway

A seventy-year-old man goes back to work after years at home, and lands in a company run by a woman half his age. The whole film is made of the words you learned tonight: the interview, the role, the onboarding, and what a track record is actually worth.

Tip: start with the trailer below, then watch the film with English subtitles. Write down every sentence you hear with would.

Podcast
Taking a Career Break with Katrina McGhee -- Episode 63, with Lyndall Farley

Two women talk for an hour about what a career break really looks like from the inside: leaving a job, losing the title, and going back afterwards. Free, and exactly the vocabulary of this lesson at natural speed.

Tip: seventy-five minutes, so listen in two parts. On the second part, write down three sentences you could use about your own break.

Talk
Why the best hire might not have the perfect resume -- Regina Hartley, TED

A hiring executive explains why she picks the candidate with the broken line on the page over the one with the perfect one. Fifteen minutes, and it is about people whose track record does not look like anybody else’s.

Tip: clear American English with subtitles available. Watch once without them, then say the main idea back in three sentences.
