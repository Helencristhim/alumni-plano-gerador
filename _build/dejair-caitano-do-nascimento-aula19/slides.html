Chapter 3: The Code

Finished, or Still Going?

we tested / we have tested

Grammar Discovery

When Did It Happen?

So far, we have tested three inflammatory markers.
Last year, we tested two markers in a smaller group.
Have you published anything yet? — Not yet.
Because of this, we have decided to start a second study.
Click to reveal the rule
The present perfect (have / has + past participle: have tested, has gone up, have found) connects the past to now: results up to today, in a study that is not finished. We use it with so far, yet, already, ever, and with no finished time. When you say when it happened (last year, in 2024, in March, two years ago), use the past simple: we tested. Questions: Have you published...? Negative: We haven't found a clear pattern yet. A research story usually moves from the past simple (what happened) to the present perfect (where we are now).

Lunch, day two

What Have You Found So Far?

E
I missed your talk this morning. So, what have you found so far?
D
So what happened was this. We were measuring pain in a cohort of eighty patients when we noticed something unexpected.
E
Unexpected in what way?
D
The patients who had the most pain didn't have the most nerve damage. The pattern was different from what we expected.
E
Interesting. Have you looked at inflammation?
D
Yes, we have. So far, we have tested three inflammatory markers, and one of them is very high in the patients with more pain.
E
That could be a real shift in how we think about this pain.
D
It might be. It's a preliminary finding, but because of this, we have decided to start a second study.
E
Have you published anything yet?
D
Not yet. But if you're interested, I'd be happy to share the data with you.

Chapter 5: Practice

The Cheapest Intervention

A story with a surprising ending

Listening 2

Something Shifted

Read the questions, then listen.

1. What problem did the clinic have?
Treatment adherence: many patients stopped their medicine after two or three months.
2. Why did they start calling the patients?
Only to collect data; they were not trying to change anything.
3. What has happened since they started the calls?
Adherence has gone up from fifty to almost eighty percent.
