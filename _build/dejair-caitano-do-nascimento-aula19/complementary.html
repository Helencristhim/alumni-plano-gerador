Lesson 19 -- From Data to Story

Conference Talk
"TED's secret to great public speaking" -- Chris Anderson, TED

The head of TED explains the one thing every great talk has: a single idea, built step by step, with stories and examples. Short and practical -- exactly what you need for your finding story.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down his four steps and match each one with a move of your story card.

Watch on TED →
Podcast
"The fear of numbers" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters talk about why so many people are afraid of numbers, and how to talk about data so that people understand. Six minutes, with a transcript and a vocabulary list.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then say one number from your research as a story: what it means for a patient, not only the percentage.

Listen on BBC Learning English →
Video
"The beauty of data visualization" -- David McCandless, TED-Ed

A data journalist turns big numbers into pictures that tell a story you can see in one second. A model of how to make data memorable for a mixed audience.

Tip: choose one of his images and describe it out loud in thirty seconds with "so far" and "have": "So far, the data have shown..."
