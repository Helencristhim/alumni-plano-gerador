Documentary
The Social Dilemma

People who built the biggest social media apps explain what their products were designed to do, and the consequences nobody planned. The whole film is one long chain of causes and knock-on effects.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and note every sentence with lead to, result in or because of. Then explain one chain from the film in your own words.

Talk
Adam Alter — “Why our screens make us less happy” (TED)

A psychologist explains why the time we spend on our phones keeps growing, and what it pushes out of the day. Clear examples of causes, effects and a vicious circle, at a calm speed.

Tip: pause after each example and say the chain out loud with which in turn.

Talk
Tristan Harris — “How a handful of tech companies control billions of minds every day” (TED)

A former designer at Google on how small design decisions bring about big changes in how billions of people spend their attention. Faster and denser than the other talk: good listening practice at real speed.

Tip: write down one unintended consequence he describes, and explain its root cause in two sentences.
