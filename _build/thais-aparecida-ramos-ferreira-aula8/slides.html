PatternWhere the word goesExample
I / you / we / theyBEFORE the verbI never take the metro.
he / she / itBEFORE the verb, and the verb takes SShe always arrives early.
am / is / areAFTER the verbShe is always late.
The questionHow often do you travel? — I travel two times a month.

One rule for everything: the small word sits before the action, and after am / is / are.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"I take never the metro."

✓ RIGHT

"I never take the metro."

✗ WRONG

"She always arrive early."

✓ RIGHT

"She always arrives early."

In Portuguese the small word can go anywhere. In English it has one home: before the action. And with she, he and it, the verb keeps its S — the small word does not take it away.

A Table at Breakfast

P
Good morning. Is this table free?
T
Yes, of course. Good morning.
P
Thank you. I am Peter. I am from Boston.
T
Nice to meet you. Are you here with your family?
P
Yes. My wife and my daughter. She never gets up before ten.
T
My daughter too. She always sleeps late on vacation.
P
And you? You are here very early.
T
I always wake up early. I usually start work at six.
P
On vacation too? I always have breakfast at seven, every day.
T
On vacation I never open my email. That is my rule.
P
A very good rule. How often do you come to Paris?
T
This is our first time. We sometimes take the metro, but we walk a lot.
