Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Video
TOEFL Tutor Tatiana — “TOEFL 2026 Complete the Words: Strategy, Tips, Examples” (YouTube)

A clear walkthrough of the new Complete the Words task, with examples of how context clues give you the missing letters.

Tip: pause before each answer and say which context clue helped you.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Series
Gilmore Girls — Season 1, Episode 1, “Pilot”

A fast-talking student who reads everything and wants a demanding school. The dialogue is famously quick, which makes it excellent listening practice.

Tip: after each scene, say one thing that must be true about a character, even if nobody says it.

See the episode on TVmaze ↗
Talk
Carol Dweck — “The power of believing that you can improve” (TED)

A psychologist on why the students who improve most see a bad result as “not yet”. Ten minutes that change how you read a practice score.

Tip: summarize her main argument in three blocks, and say what must be true about students who say not yet.

Watch on TED ↗
