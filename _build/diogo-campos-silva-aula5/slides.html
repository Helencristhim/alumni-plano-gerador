Chapter 3: The Code

Happening Now

am, is, are + -ing

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I am sharing my screen now."

"The page is loading."

"The VPN is not working at the moment."

"What are you doing now?"

Look at the blue words. You know the first part from Lesson 1. What is new at the end of the verb?

JobFormExample
Now (I)I am + verb-ingI am sharing my screen.
Now (he, she, it)is + verb-ingThe page is loading.
Now (you, we, they)are + verb-ingWe are installing an update.
Negativeis not / are not + verb-ingThe VPN is not working.
QuestionAre you...ing? / What are you...ing?Is the user receiving messages?
The trapnever forget am / is / areNot I sharing. Say I am sharing.

In one line: for an action happening now: am / is / are + verb-ing.

Chapter 4: On the Phone

On the Phone

One live call, two recordings, one remote session

Dialogue

What Are You Doing Now?

K
Hi Diogo, it’s Karen from Microsoft Support, about ticket four five two. Are you at your computer now?
D
Hi, Karen. Yes, I am. I’m sharing my screen now.
K
Great, I can see it. What are you doing now?
D
I’m opening the admin center. It’s loading, but it’s very slow today.
K
No problem. I’m checking the account on our side. Please wait.
D
Okay, I’m waiting. What are you doing exactly?
K
I’m installing an update on the server. Don’t close the window.
D
Understood. The page is loading again. Now I can see the account.
K
Perfect. Is the user receiving messages now?
D
Yes, she is. She’s reading her emails right now. Thank you, Karen!
