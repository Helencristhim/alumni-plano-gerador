Which Word Do You Need?

1. The jacket fits badly. It is too small.
Size. "Do you have a bigger size?"
2. You want to say no without saying no.
Expensive. "That is a little expensive for me, thank you."
3. You are leaving the shop and you may want to bring it back.
Receipt. "Can I have the receipt, please?"
4. You paid with a big note and the person is counting.
Change. "Here is your change." — "Thank you."
Chapter 3: How Much, How Many

Two Questions, One Difference

how much · how many

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"How much is it?"

"How much money do you have?"

"How many bags do you have?"

"How many days are you here?"

FormWhenExample
How muchfor the PRICE, alwaysHow much is it? — It is forty.
How much + word with no pluralmoney, water, time, coffeeHow much money do you have?
How many + word with an Sbags, days, people, roomsHow many bags do you have?

The shortcut that works almost every time: if the word takes an S in the plural, use MANY. If it does not, use MUCH. And for a price, it is always how much.

Comprehension

What Did Nina Say?

1. What is the price of the first jacket, before and after the discount?
Two hundred and twenty before, and one hundred and fifty-four after — thirty percent off.
2. How much is the black jacket?
Three hundred and ten.
3. What does she say about the receipt?
You can change the jacket in fourteen days.
