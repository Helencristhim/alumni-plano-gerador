Podcast
"Can apps teach you a language?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters discuss what is better for learning a language, an app or a class, and what the research says. Six minutes, with a transcript -- a good start for your routine after the program.

Tip: after you listen, plan your English week after Bangkok in three sentences, out loud, with "I'm going to...".

Conference Talk
"4 reasons to learn a new language" -- John McWhorter, TED

A linguist from Columbia University gives four reasons why learning a language is worth the effort, even in a world where English is everywhere. A talk with a clear structure: four points, one after the other.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write his four reasons, and then add your own fifth reason, from your experience in this program.

Video
"The benefits of a bilingual brain" -- Mia Nacamulli, TED-Ed

A short animated lesson about what happens in the brain of a person who speaks two languages, with a bit of neuroscience that a pharmacologist will enjoy. Five minutes.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, explain the main idea in one sentence with a booster, and one detail with a hedge.
