Chapter 3: The Code

Your Program in Five Sentences

The past, the result, the plan, the promise and the claim

Grammar Discovery

Five Tools, One Story

"In July, I lost the thread when people asked me fast questions."
"Since July, I have learned to repair in English."
"I'm going to take part in the workshop on Wednesday."
"If I miss a word, I'll ask for repair."
"My listening is clearly better than in July."
Click to reveal the rule
The past: past simple (lost, asked) for a finished time. The result until now: present perfect, have + past participle, with since or so far. The plan: am / is / are going to + verb. The possible future: if + present, will + verb (no will after if). The strong claim: a booster, clearly / definitely, when you can back it up. Five tools, and together they tell your story.
