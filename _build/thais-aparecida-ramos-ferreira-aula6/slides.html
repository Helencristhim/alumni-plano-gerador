Chapter 3: The Magic Words

Three Ways to Ask

Can I have...? · Can you bring...? · I'd like...

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Can I have the menu, please?"

"Can you bring some water, please?"

"I'd like a table for three."

"Would you like a coffee?"

PatternUseExample
Can I have + thing?You ask for a thing, for youCan I have the bill, please?
Can you bring + thing?You ask the waiter to do itCan you bring some water?
I'd like + thingYou say what you want, politelyI'd like a coffee.
Would you like...?The waiter asks YOUWould you like a dessert?
pleaseOne word, and everything is politeA table for three, please.

I'd like = I would like. Two words that sound like one: ID LIKE.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"I want the menu."

✓ RIGHT

"Can I have the menu, please?"

✗ WRONG

"Bring me the bill."

✓ RIGHT

"Can you bring the bill, please?"

"I want" and "bring me" are not wrong English — they are just very direct. In a restaurant in Europe, can and please do all the work for you.

Chapter 5: At the Table

Good Evening, Madame

one waiter, one table, three people who are hungry

Dialogue

Dinner at Le Petit Bistro

T
Good evening. Can I have a table for three, please?
J
Good evening, madame. Of course. This table, near the window?
T
Yes, perfect. Thank you. Can I have the menu, please?
J
Here you are. The menu in English is on the second page.
T
Thank you. Can you bring some water for the table, please?
J
Of course. A carafe of water is free. Are you ready to order?
T
Yes. I'd like the chicken, and two fish, please.
J
Very good. And would you like a dessert tonight?
T
Yes. One chocolate cake for my daughter, and one coffee for me.
J
Perfect. I bring the water and the bread now.
T
Thank you. And can I have the bill after the coffee, please?
J
Of course, madame. And the service is in the bill, so no tip is necessary.
Comprehension

What Did Julien Say?

1. Where is the table?
Near the window.
2. Where is the menu in English?
On the second page of the menu.
3. Why is no tip necessary?
Because the service is already in the bill.
Listening

The Restaurant Answers

You call the bistro and a recorded message answers. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What time does the restaurant close?
At eleven. It is open every day, from twelve to eleven.
2. How many tables are there inside?
Twelve. That is why a big family calls before six o'clock.
3. What is free at every table?
Water. And service is included in the bill.
Listening 2

A Message from the Reception

The receptionist recommends a restaurant for tonight. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What time does Claire recommend for the table?
Seven thirty. At nine o'clock the bistro is very busy.
2. What does Claire say about the coffee?
Coffee in France is very small. For a big one, ask for a coffee with hot water.
3. Is a tip necessary?
No. Service is in the bill.
Chapter 6: Your Turn

Three Scenes

Guided > Semi-free > Free

Role-Play — Guided

At the Door

Scenario: You arrive at Le Petit Bistro with your husband and your daughter. Ask for a table, ask for the menu, and ask for water.

Keywords:

Can I have a table for three, please? Can I have the menu? Can you bring...? water
Role-Play — Semi-free

Dinner for Three

Scenario: You have the menu. Your husband and your daughter do not speak English. Order dinner for the three of you, and answer the waiter when he asks you a question.

Keywords:

I'd like... chicken chocolate cake coffee
Role-Play — Free

The End of the Night

Scenario: Dinner is finished. Ask for the bill, ask one question about the tip, say one thing about the dinner, and say goodbye to the waiter.

No keywords this time. Two minutes, no stopping. Different from the model is not wrong — if he understood you, it worked.
