English Words

Words That Fool You

These words look like other words you know, but they mean something else. Say the sentence with the English word.

#Not thisSay this
1I pretend to finish it today. (to act as if)I intend to finish it today.
2Let me resume the meeting. (to start again)Let me sum up the meeting.
3The deadline is exquisite. (beautiful)The deadline is strange.
4We need to attend the client better. (to be at)We need to serve the client better.
5I will realize the project. (to understand)I will carry out the project.

Words to Be Direct

Short adjectives that make your point faster. Use one in a sentence about your work.

#Long wayDirect way
1It is a thing that is very important for us.It is critical.

4This is the most important thing now.This is the priority.
5It was a decision that was very fast.It was a quick decision.
