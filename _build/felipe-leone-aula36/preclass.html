Lesson 36 -- Pre-class

Speaking Directly: Fewer Detours -- Decide and Succeed

Ten to twenty minutes before class. Two word families, decide and succeed, and the words that help you get to the point. Key words: a decision, decisive, success, successful, to sum up, a priority.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A decision -- a choice that you make after you think about it
"We made the decision in ten minutes."
Decisive -- able to make choices quickly and with confidence
"A good manager is decisive when there is a problem."
Success -- the good result that you wanted
"The new product was a big success in Mexico."
Successful -- having the result that you wanted
"It was a successful quarter for the whole team."
To sum up -- to say the most important points in a few words
"To sum up, we need more time and one more analyst."
A priority -- the thing that is more important than the others
"Our priority this month is the new client."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Ana leads a risk team. Her manager likes short answers. “When she asks about a decision, I say it in one sentence. I try to be decisive, and I answer decisively.”

Last year her team succeeded with a new model. “The project was a success because we explained it clearly. Everybody knew the priority.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- One Family, Four Jobs

GRAMMAR

The same idea changes form: a thing, an action, a quality, a way of doing it.

JobFormExample
A thingnouna decision, a success
An actionverbto decide, to succeed
A qualityadjectivedecisive, successful
A way of doing itadverbdecisively, successfully

Never: a successful project done successful · we made a decide · he is very decision

Stage 2: Put the Conversation in Order

Order

Listen first, then put the lines in order. A manager asks for a quick update. Put the answer in the most direct order.

Step 1: listen to the whole conversation. Step 2: put the lines in the order you heard.
?"To sum up, the project is still on track."
?"The reason is simple: the data arrived late."
?"So the next step is to finish on Wednesday."
?"The main point is that we are two days late."
?"We have already decided to add one more analyst."
