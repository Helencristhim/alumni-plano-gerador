Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Simon Sinek: “How great leaders inspire action” (18 min)

A famous talk about starting with why: a simple way to explain any idea clearly and directly.

Tip: explain one project of your team in three sentences: why, how, what.

TED Talk
Ruth Chang: “How to make hard choices” (14 min)

A philosopher on why some decisions are hard, and how to decide decisively when no option is clearly better.

Tip: write down every word from the decide family that she uses.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Barry Schwartz: “The paradox of choice” (19 min)

A psychologist on why more options can make decisions harder and people less satisfied.

Tip: sum up his idea in 30 seconds, out loud, without notes.
