Lesson 8 -- Numbers on the Page

Film
Moneyball (Moneyball - Jogada de Risco)

A baseball manager decides to trust the numbers instead of the opinions of experienced scouts. It is a story, not a lesson, but half the dialogue is people describing data out loud: what went up, by how much, compared with whom.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time somebody says a number, repeat the sentence with by or to.

Podcast
Short Wave — The sound of climate change: hearing heat data with science

A short episode about turning temperature data into sound, so that you can hear a trend instead of seeing it. It is a clever way into today’s idea: the shape of the data comes first, and the explanation comes after.

Tip: listen once. Then describe the trend they talk about in two sentences, with rose by or rose to.

Talk
Hans Rosling -- The best stats you’ve ever seen

Twenty minutes of a scientist describing moving graphs in real time, with energy, humor and very clear English. Watch how he always says what is on each axis before he says anything else.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Pause on any graph and describe it before he does: axes, trend, comparison.
