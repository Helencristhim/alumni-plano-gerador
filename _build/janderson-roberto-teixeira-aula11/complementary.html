Grammar Video
"'Have to' and 'must'" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on today's exact pair, including the part that costs most: why must not and do not have to are opposites and not variations. Connection to Lesson 11: it drills the same distinction with different examples, which is the fastest way to make it automatic.

Tip: pause after each example and produce the opposite version out loud. If the sentence forbids, make it optional; if it is optional, make it forbidden.
