Lesson 7 -- Something Went Wrong at the Bench

Series
Ted Lasso

A comedy about a coach who is wrong about almost everything at the start and says so out loud, again and again. Half-hour episodes, easy to fit in, and full of people owning up, covering things up, and trying to make up for what they did.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Every time a character admits a mistake, pause and say what they should have done, with should have.

Podcast
Life Kit — How to say sorry

A psychologist on what makes an apology work and what ruins it. The point that matters for today: the moment you add a but, the apology becomes a defense. It is the same trap as the forty minutes in the first listening.

Tip: listen once. Then write down the two mistakes she says people make most, and say each one out loud with should not have.

Talk
Kathryn Schulz -- On being wrong

Eighteen minutes on why being wrong feels exactly like being right until the moment you find out. Clear, funny and spoken at a calm pace, which makes it good training for an ear that has been away from English for a while.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Note every sentence about the past that is not certain, and decide whether it is a must have, a might have or a should have.
