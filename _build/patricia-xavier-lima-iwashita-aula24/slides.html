Chapter 4: Language Focus

Usage Has Been Falling

The present perfect continuous — has been / have been + -ing

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Usage has been falling for three months."

"The finance team has been asking for a comparison since April."

"We have been running weekly sessions since May."

"The adoption plan has been stalling since the outage."

All four started in the past and are still going on today. What is the same in every green part — and what decides between for and since?

StructureUseExample
has / have been + -ingStarted in the past, still going on nowUsage has been falling for three months.
for + a length of timeHow long it has lastedfor three months, for six weeks
since + a point in timeWhen it startedsince April, since the outage
has / have + past participleThe finished result and the numberUsage has gone up by eleven per cent.
How long has / have + subject + been + -ing?Ask how long something has been going onHow long has this been going on?
AvoidAfter been the verb always ends in -ing: not "has been fall" → "has been falling". Never use the present continuous for this: not "they are talking to another vendor since March" → "they have been talking to another vendor since March". And it is for three months, never "since three months".

The present perfect continuous is the tense of work that has not stopped. Has been + -ing says it is still going on; has + past participle gives the number it has already produced.

"What Have You Been Doing?"

P
I know, and thank you for telling me. Let me start with the part that is on us. Usage has been falling since March, and two of your teams have been off the dashboard for eleven weeks.
D
So you have been looking at the same numbers I have.
P
We have. And we have been working on them since May: weekly sessions with both teams, and their usage has already gone up by eleven per cent. It is not fixed, but it has stopped falling.
D
That is the first good sentence I have heard about this account in two months. What do I take to my director?
P
One page: what has been done, what has changed, and what the next twelve months look like. Your new sponsor is on it too, because he has never seen a review from us. You will have it on Thursday.

Real Artifact

Renewal One-Pager — Northwind

Northwind Logistics · Renewal June 30

One page for the director · Patrícia Xavier Lima Iwashita

What has been happening

Usage has been falling since the March outage, and two of the five teams have been off the dashboard for eleven weeks. Active users: 400 in February, 260 in May.

What has been done since May

Weekly sessions have been running with both inactive teams since the first of May. Usage in those two teams has gone up by 11 per cent, and it has stopped falling.

The next twelve months

A business review for the new executive sponsor on June 12, the two workshops delivered before June 30, and a monthly usage report to the director. One owner and one date on every line.

1. How many active users has the account lost since February?
A hundred and forty: from four hundred in February to two hundred and sixty in May.
2. What has been running since the first of May, and what has it produced?
Weekly sessions with both inactive teams. Usage in those two teams has gone up by eleven per cent and has stopped falling.
3. What is promised for the new executive sponsor, and when?
A business review, on June the twelfth.
