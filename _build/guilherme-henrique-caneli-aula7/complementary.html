Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Freakonomics Radio — “How to Make Meetings Less Terrible”

What research says about running meetings that work: clear rules, strict time and the right people. A panel is a meeting with an audience.

Tip: write down the one rule you would add to the ground rules of your next panel.

Listen on Freakonomics ↗
Talk
Celeste Headlee — “10 ways to have a better conversation” (TED)

A radio host who interviewed people for decades explains how to ask better questions and follow up without dominating. Everything a moderator does between the first and the last question.

Tip: choose the two rules that matter most on stage and use them in your next panel.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
Simon Sinek — “How great leaders inspire action” (TED)

One of the most watched talks ever, built on a single clear idea repeated in precise language. Listen to how he describes organizations: short noun phrases, in the natural order.

Tip: note three noun phrases he uses and check the order of the words in each one.

Watch on TED ↗
