"The panel has simultaneous interpretation into Spanish and Mandarin."

"The licensing debate continues in the breakout session after lunch."

"We have two minutes, so let me wrap up."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Advanced speakers rarely make grammar mistakes in front of a noun. They make rhythm mistakes. “A bilateral long-term robust infrastructure framework” is built from correct words, and a native listener still stops, because English puts those words in a fixed order.

The order is roughly this. Opinion comes first: robust, credible, ambitious. Then size, age or time: new, long-term, early-stage. Then origin or scope: Brazilian, regional, bilateral, multilateral. Closest to the noun comes the word that says what kind of thing it is: infrastructure, rail, pension. So: “a credible long-term regional infrastructure plan,” “two experienced Canadian pension funds.”

Two details matter. Compound words take a hyphen before the noun (“a long-term plan”) but not after the verb (“the plan is long term”). And the word next to the noun is never plural: a pension fund, not a pensions fund, even when there are many pensions.

The last rule comes from the interpretation booth. If you stack five words before the noun, simultaneous interpretation falls behind, because the interpreter cannot know what the adjectives describe until the noun arrives. Stop at three or four, and put the rest in a clause. The rapporteur, the lineup and every listener in a second language will thank you.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Adjective Order in Executive Noun Phrases

GRAMMAR

Opinion, then time, then origin, then the word that says what kind -- closest to the noun.

PositionKind of wordExample
1. Opinioncredible, robust, ambitiousa credible plan
2. Size, age or timenew, long-term, early-stagea credible long-term plan
3. Origin or scopeBrazilian, regional, multilaterala credible long-term regional plan
4. Type (next to the noun)infrastructure, rail, pensiona credible long-term regional infrastructure plan
Hyphensbefore the noun onlya long-term plan · the plan is long term
Singular modifierthe word next to the noun is not plurala pension fund

Never: a Brazilian new ambitious concession · two pensions funds · an infrastructures plan · a round of lightning
