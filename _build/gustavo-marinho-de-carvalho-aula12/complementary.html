Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
The Morning Show — Season 1, Episode 1

A drama set inside an American news program, where every argument has two sides and nobody is completely right. The dialogue is fast and educated, and it is full of although, however and despite in real conversation.

Tip: English subtitles on. Pause after one argument between two characters and summarize it out loud in one sentence, with whereas.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English — “What is the future of work?”

Six minutes, two British speakers, on what is changing at work, including the four-day week trials from this lesson. It is the same debate as the text, from the other side of the Atlantic. Free transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen once with no text. Then give your opinion on the four-day week in three sentences: one with although, one with despite and one with however.

Listen on BBC Learning English →
Talk
Julia Galef — “Why you think you're right -- even if you're wrong” (TED)

Eleven minutes on the difference between defending an opinion and looking for the truth, and on why people who can change their minds sound more credible. It is the man with the street with no cars, as a way of thinking.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles. Then say in ninety seconds something you were wrong about, and the part you still doubt.
