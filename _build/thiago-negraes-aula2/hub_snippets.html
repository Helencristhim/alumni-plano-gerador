Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Room 812, eleven at night. "There is a safe in the wardrobe," the receptionist said, "and there are two lifts on your left." Upstairs, the room is quiet, but the desk is empty: there is no water and there are no towels. You pick up the phone and you have two jobs. First you describe what is missing. Then you ask for it, and the whole difference between rude and polite is one word: "Could you send someone up, please?" Notice that after could the verb never changes: could you send, not "could you to send".

1. Which sentence is correct?
A "There is two lifts on your left."
B "There are two lifts on your left."
C "Have two lifts on your left."
2. What happens to the verb after "could"?
A It stays in the base form: "Could you send someone up?"
B It takes "to": "Could you to send someone up?"
C It takes "-ing": "Could you sending someone up?"
3. You want to know whether the hotel has a gym. What do you say?
A "Have a gym in the hotel?"
B "There has a gym in the hotel?"
C "Is there a gym in the hotel?"

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- There is / there are and could

GRAMMAR

One structure says what exists in a place. The other asks for what you want.

StructureUseExample
There is + singularOne thing existsThere is a safe in the room.
There are + pluralTwo or more things existThere are two beds and one sofa.
Is there / Are thereAsk whether it existsIs there a pool on the roof?
Could I / Could you + base verbAsk politelyCould I have a late check-out?

Portuguese uses one verb for both ideas, and that is the trap. In English, have is only for what somebody owns ("I have a reservation"). For what exists in a place you always need there is or there are.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the best thing to say in each situation.

You arrive and the room is not ready yet. You want to leave your suitcase.
A "I want to leave my bags here."
B "Could I leave my bags here until three, please?"
C "Have a place for my bags?"
You want to know what the extra services cost.
A "Are the amenities complimentary?"
B "The amenities is free?"
C "Have free the amenities?"
The room has no towels and you call the front desk.
A "Not have towels in my room. Bring now."
B "There are no towels in the room. Could you send someone up?"
C "There is no towels. You can to bring?"
You do not understand why the hotel is holding your money.
A "Why you take my money?"
B "Could you explain what the deposit covers?"
C "Explain me the deposit."
