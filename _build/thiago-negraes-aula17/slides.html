Chapter 3: What Was Done

The Voice of the Report

The passive, one step back in time

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The excavation was flooded last night."

"Two of the new pumps were damaged by the water."

"The pipe was hit by an excavator."

"Nobody was hurt."

FormUseExample
was / were + past participlewhat happened, when the action matters more than the personThe excavation was flooded.
... + by + agentonly when it is important to say who or what did itThe pipe was hit by an excavator.
wasn't / weren't + past participlenegativeThe client wasn't told yesterday.
Was / Were + subject + past participlequestionWas anybody hurt?

It is the passive from lesson 14, one step back in time: is / are becomes was / were, and the past participle does not change.

Chapter 4: The Report

Bad News from Station 1

A call with Houston the morning after

Dialogue

The Morning After

R
Hi Thiago. I got your message. What happened at Station 1?
T
A water main burst last night, and the excavation was flooded.
R
Oh no. Was anybody hurt?
T
No, nobody was hurt. The crew wasn't on site at night.
R
Good. Was there any damage to the equipment?
T
Yes, two of the new pumps were damaged by the water.
R
Do you know what the root cause is?
T
Not yet. We think the pipe was hit by an excavator yesterday, but we need to confirm it.
R
And is the station shut down now?
T
Yes, it was shut down at three, and the water was pumped out this morning. I'm sending the incident report by tomorrow.
R
Thanks. Let's go over it with the client on Friday.
