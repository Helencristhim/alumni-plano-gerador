Talk
Melissa Baese-Berk — “Learning to understand accented English” (TED)

A linguist explains the research behind this lesson: understanding an unfamiliar accent is not a talent, it is a skill that improves with short, regular exposure. It is the scientific version of what happens to your ear on the second afternoon of a conference.

Tip: listen once for the gist and write it in one sentence. Then listen again and note one inference you made that she never said directly.

Speed, cost and certainty in international payments, and why the point where a payment starts changes the result. The same topic as the panel in listening 2, with no American voice to lean on: perfect material for a cold listen.
