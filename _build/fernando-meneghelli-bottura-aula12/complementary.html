Lesson 12 -- The Live Wire: Second Simulation

Real Interview
HARDtalk -- Akinwumi Adesina, African Development Bank

The same interview twice is the exercise here: watch the first fifteen minutes today, and the same fifteen minutes again in three days. Connection to Lesson 12: you are not watching the content, you are watching whether HIS answers change when the interviewer comes back to the same point.

Tip: write down three figures he gives in the first pass. In the second pass, check whether they came back identical. That is exactly the line your second evaluator is scoring.

Talk
"How to stay calm when you know you will be stressed" -- Daniel Levitin, TED

Preparing the decision before the pressure arrives, so the brain does not have to decide under cortisol. Connection to Lesson 12: your three figures and your deal-breaker are decided the day before, never in the room.

Tip: write the three figures on one card the night before the simulation, and do not look at it during. The point is that you already decided.

Grammar Video
"Linking words of contrast" -- BBC Learning English, English Class

The structure from Lesson 11, revised before the simulation: although, whereas, despite, in spite of. Connection to Lesson 12: under pressure, conceding and keeping your position in one sentence is what stops an answer from wandering.

Tip: before the simulation, say five sentences out loud about your own results, each one with a concession and a contrast. Five is enough to make the structure automatic for forty minutes.
