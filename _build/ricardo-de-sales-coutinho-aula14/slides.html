PatternExampleRemember
a + word + persona friendly colleaguethe word comes FIRST
an + word with a, e, i, o, uan experienced manageran before a vowel sound
two or more peopletwo new colleaguesno -s on the word: new, not news

Hi Ricardo. Welcome to the Miami team! Here are three people you meet on Monday.

Paul is an experienced manager. He is tall and very quiet, but he is funny at lunch. His office is on the fourth floor.

Diego is a new colleague from Colombia. He speaks English and Spanish, and he is very good with numbers.

See you on Monday! Linda
