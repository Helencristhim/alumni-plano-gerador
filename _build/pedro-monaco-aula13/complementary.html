"Sound More Polite and Professional in English | Hedging Language Explained" — English Speaking 360

A clear walk-through of hedging: the modal verbs, the softening adverbs and the phrases that turn a flat statement into a professional one. Connection to Lesson 13: these are the tools from the Grammar Discovery table, with more examples than we had time for in class.

Tip: for every example in the video, say the blunt version first and then the hedged one. Then check the fact inside it: if it is a date or a number, it should not have moved.
