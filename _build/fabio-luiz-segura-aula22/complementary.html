Talk
David Grady — “How to save the world (or at least yourself) from bad meetings” (TED)

A short and funny talk about meetings without a clear goal. It is the reason the first minute of a suite meeting matters so much: say the goal, or the meeting belongs to somebody else.

Tip: write one sentence you can use to ask for the goal of a meeting. Put it in your follow-up email.

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart (Stanford GSB) — “The Art of Negotiation: How to Get More of What You Want”

An episode about the common mistakes people make in high-stakes conversations, and how to prepare for them. Very close to a vendor meeting with a term sheet on the table.

Tip: listen for one mistake you have seen in a real negotiation. Describe it with It was... that.

Video
William Ury — “The power of listening” (TEDxSanDiego, YouTube)

One of the authors of Getting to Yes explains why the best negotiators listen more than they talk. Useful before any meeting where the other side arrives with a strong pitch.

Tip: write down one idea about listening and use it in the next vendor meeting at work.
