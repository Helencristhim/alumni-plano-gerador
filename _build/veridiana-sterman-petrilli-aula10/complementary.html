Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Street Food: USA — Season 1, "Miami, Florida" (Netflix)

Thirty minutes in the city you land in this October, told by the people who cook there. Cuban sandwiches, Haitian food, strong accents and nothing heavy in it at all.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Write down five places you would actually go, and say why for each one.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "How to tell a captivating story, from a wedding toast to a job interview"

The directors of The Moth on how anybody learns to tell a stronger story: find the moment that surprised you, keep it tight, go in order, and end somewhere different from where you began. That is exactly the sixty-second trip of this lesson.

Tip: the transcript is on the page. Apply their four rules to your own ten days and record it again.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Adam Galinsky — "How to speak up for yourself" (TED)

Six minutes on why some people speak at the desk and others wait, and what makes the difference. It is the same idea as the first listening of this lesson, from a social psychologist instead of a trip guide.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Afterwards, report three of his points back, all of them starting with he said or he told us.
