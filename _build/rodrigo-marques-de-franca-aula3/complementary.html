Lesson 3 -- Say That Again

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) -- "Taking up space at work isn't easy. Here's how employees can speak up for one another"

What to do when you are interrupted, talked over, or simply not heard in a meeting. It is the same problem you worked on in class, seen from the other side: not how to ask again, but how to get back into a conversation that moved on without you. Transcript on the same page.

Tip: write down every phrase somebody uses to come back into the conversation. Then say each one out loud twice, until it sounds like something you would actually say.

Talk
Celeste Headlee -- "10 ways to have a better conversation" (TED, 12 min)

A radio host explaining what makes a conversation work, and the rule that matters most for you: if you did not hear it, say so. She speaks fast and clearly, which is exactly the speed of the calls you are training for.

Tip: watch once without subtitles. Then watch again and count how many of her ten rules you already follow in Portuguese but not in English. That gap is the lesson.

Watch on TED ↗
Series
The Diplomat -- Season 1, Episode 1 "The Cinderella Thing" (Netflix)

Professionals interrupting each other at full speed, in a register very close to yours: fast, formal, and full of people managing what they can and cannot say. The plot is a bonus. What you are watching for is how somebody comes into a conversation that is already moving.

Tip: pick one scene and watch it twice. The second time, pause every time a character interrupts and write down the exact words they used to get in.
