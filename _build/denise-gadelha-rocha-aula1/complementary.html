Complementary Activities

Optional extras for the week between classes. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 1 -- Taking the Floor

Series
Suits -- Season 1, Episode 1 (Pilot)

A firm where everything is decided in meetings, and where the person who speaks first usually sets the frame. The register is exactly the one you need with international executives: direct, fast and never rude. Listen for how the senior partners open a conversation — they never start with background.

Tip: watch with English subtitles, never Portuguese ones. Pick two moments where a character leads with the conclusion instead of the explanation, and write down the exact sentence.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart -- Stanford Graduate School of Business

Free episodes from the Stanford business school on the exact problem you brought to us: sounding like yourself under pressure, opening with the point, and handling the question you did not expect. Episodes are short, the English is clear American, and every one of them ends with something you can use on Monday.

Tip: start with an episode on speaking spontaneously. Listen once for the idea, then again with a pen — write down three phrases you would actually say to your global head.

Listen on Stanford GSB ↗
YouTube
Nancy Duarte -- The Common Structure of the Greatest Communicators (TEDxEast)

Eighteen minutes on the shape of a talk that moves a room. She takes speeches apart and shows the same pattern underneath all of them: where you are now, where you could be, and the gap between the two. It is the grown-up version of the four moves you built in class today.

Tip: watch it once for the argument, then map your own quarterly opening onto her structure. Where is your "what is" and where is your "what could be"?
