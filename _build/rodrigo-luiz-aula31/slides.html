Chapter 4: The Forum

From Start to Finish

the global forum, one more time

Dialogue

Brazil at the Global Forum

C
Good morning, everybody. Brazil is first today. Rodrigo, the floor is yours.
R
Thank you, Claire. I'm Rodrigo, Operations Director for Brazil. Today I'm going to talk about two things: our KPIs and our plan.
C
Great. Let's start with the KPIs.
R
As you can see, delivery increased to ninety-six percent. It was a big challenge, and the team is very proud.
C
Congratulations! And what is the plan for next year?
R
We are going to open a new line. In my view, we should keep our local suppliers, because they are faster.
C
Interesting. And how much will the new line cost?
R
Good question. Sorry, do you mean the machines or the total cost?
C
The total cost.
R
Let me check and come back to you on Friday. To sum up: a good year, and a new line. What do you think?
