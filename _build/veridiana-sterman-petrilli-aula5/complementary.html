Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Emily in Paris — Season 1, Episode 2 "Masculin Féminin" (Netflix)

Twenty-five light minutes of one person asking strangers for things in a country whose rules she does not know. Half the episode is a request that goes wrong and gets repaired, which is exactly today’s lesson with better clothes.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on and write down every question that starts with Could, Would or Do you think.

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "How to travel on a budget"

Most of the money-saving advice in this episode is really advice about asking: who to ask, when to ask, and what to ask for. Listen for how often the answer to "how did you get that?" is simply "I asked".

Tip: the full transcript is on the same page. Find three requests and rewrite each one in a softer form.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Jia Jiang — "What I learned from 100 days of rejection" (TED)

Fifteen minutes from a man who spent a hundred days asking strangers for absurd things on purpose, to stop being afraid of no. It is the other half of this lesson: the form is easy, and the fear is the real work.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Then say out loud, in two sentences, one thing you have never asked for because you were sure the answer was no.
