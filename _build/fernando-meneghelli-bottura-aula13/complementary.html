Lesson 13 -- The Negotiation Room

Grammar Video
"Linking words of contrast" -- BBC Learning English, English Class

Revision before the offer call: although, whereas, despite. Connection to Lesson 13: the counter-offer sentence is a concession plus a condition, and both halves have to be right.

Tip: build three sentences that start with a concession about their number and end with your condition. Say them out loud until none of them needs a second attempt.

Talk
"How to stay calm when you know you will be stressed" -- Daniel Levitin, TED

Deciding in advance what you will do, so the decision is not made under pressure. Connection to Lesson 13: your walk-away line and your three conditions are decided before the call, never during it.

Tip: write your walk-away line on the same card as your three figures. If you would not say it out loud to a recruiter, it is not your real line yet.

Real Interview
HARDtalk -- Akinwumi Adesina, African Development Bank

An executive answering about money, mandate and scope without ever sounding defensive. Connection to Lesson 13: notice how often he answers a question about resources by describing the job instead of the person.

Tip: count the times he moves the conversation from the person to the mandate. That move is exactly what turns a salary conversation into a scope conversation.
