Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Jennifer 8. Lee: “The hunt for General Tso” (17 min)

A journalist follows a famous dish around the world and finds out how food changes when it travels to another culture.

Tip: count how many times she says there is or there are, and note one sentence with a lot of.

TED Talk
Taiye Selasi: “Don’t ask where I’m from, ask where I’m a local” (16 min)

A writer on how places, rituals and relationships, not countries, make us feel at home when we travel.

Tip: pause after each of her three questions and answer it in one sentence about a city you know.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie: “The danger of a single story” (19 min)

A novelist on what happens when we know only one story about a country or a culture.

Tip: think of one country you visited for work: what single story did you have before the trip?
