Structure Hunt

There Is, There Are

Find the structure in the audio. Then say the sentence again, changing the noun.

#From the audioFind
1There is a lot of food on every street.a lot of + uncountable noun
2There aren't many menus in English.many + countable noun
3There wasn't much time.much + uncountable noun
4There are a lot of rules at the table.a lot of + countable noun
5There isn't much talking during the meal.much in a negative sentence
