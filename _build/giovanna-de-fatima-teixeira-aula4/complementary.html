Extra Practice

None of this is homework and nothing has to be delivered. It is English coming in through your ears while you do something else. Tick the box when you watch or listen, just so you remember what you have already seen.

Series
Extra English -- Episode 6: "Bridget Wins the Lottery"

The same sitcom, one episode later. Bridget wins the lottery, and the four friends spend the whole episode talking about their flat and about where things are: in the kitchen, on the table, next to the door. English subtitles are in the picture.

Tip: watch one scene twice. The first time only listen. The second time write down every sentence with in, on, under, next to or there is / there are.

Podcast
Learn English with Bob the Canadian -- "Prepositions of Place, and Other Ways to Describe Location and Motion"

Bob is a Canadian teacher and he speaks slowly. In eight minutes he explains exactly the two families of this class: the words that say where something is (on, under, between) and the words that say which way you go (through, across, into).

Tip: stop the audio after each preposition and say one sentence out loud about your own site before you press play again.

YouTube
HS2 -- "80 Metres Below Ground with the Engineers Building HS2's Longest Tunnel"

Five minutes inside a real tunnel in England, eighty meters under the ground, with two giant boring machines and the engineers who work next to them. This is not a graded lesson: it is your own subject, at normal speed, in British English.

Tip: do not try to understand every word. Listen only for the words you already know -- tunnel, underground, machine, ground -- and then write three sentences with under, through and below.
