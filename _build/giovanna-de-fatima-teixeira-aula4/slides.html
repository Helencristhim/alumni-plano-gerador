Chapter 3: The Pattern

One Thing, Many Things

there is · there are

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

There is one entrance for people.
There are three buildings on the site.
There is a big machine in the tunnel.
There are two doors near the gate.

When do we say "is" and when do we say "are"?

How manyPatternExemplo
One thingThere is + a/one + coisaThere is a machine in the tunnel.
Two or moreThere are + número + coisasThere are three buildings on the site.
Nothing thereThere is no / There are noThere is no living room.
CuidadoEm português a gente diz "TEM três prédios". Em inglês não se usa "have" aqui: "It has three buildings" muda o sentido. Para dizer o que EXISTE num lugar, e sempre there is / there are.
Prepositions of Place

The Small Words That Place Things

"The machine is in the tunnel."

in — inside a closed space

"Our room is on the first floor."

on — on a surface, on a level, on the site

"The office is next to the canteen."

next to — right beside, touching

"The park is behind the building."

behind — on the other side
