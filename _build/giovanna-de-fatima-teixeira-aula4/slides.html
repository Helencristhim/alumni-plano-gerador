What do the orange words have in common?

FormWhenExample
There isone thingThere is one entrance for people.
There aretwo or more thingsThere are three containers next to the gate.
Negativethere is no / there are noThere is no ramp on this side.
QuestionIs there...? / Are there...?Is there a gate on the north side?
Place wordsunder · above · between (two things) · next to · around — they all answer where?
Grammar Discovery

Which Way Do You Go?

The trucks come in through the main gate.
We walk along the fence to the office.
You go across the area to the container.
The crew goes down the ramp into the tunnel.

These words do not say where something is. So what do they say?

WordThe pictureExample
throughin one side, out the other sideThe trucks come in through the gate.
alongfollowing a line, end to endWe walk along the fence.
acrossfrom one side to the other sideYou go across the area.
intofrom outside to insideThe crew goes into the tunnel.
The one rulePlace words answer where? — these words answer which way? The verb tells you which family you need: is asks for where, go asks for which way.
