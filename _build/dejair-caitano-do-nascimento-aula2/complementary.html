Lesson 2 -- Your Research Story

Research Talk
"Three Minute Thesis (3MT) Winner 2025" -- Students' Union UCL

A whole doctoral study explained out loud in three minutes, with one slide and no jargon. This is exactly the format of the ninety seconds you practised today, done by someone under real pressure.

Tip: watch it once for pleasure. Watch it a second time with a pen and write down the first sentence she uses to say what she investigates. Then say your own version of that sentence out loud, five times.

Listening
"A vaccine for cancer" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes about a clinical trial, at a speed built for learners. Every word you met today comes back here: trial, treatment, side effect, to publish.

Tip: listen once without the transcript and count how many of today's eight words you hear. Only then open the transcript, and only to check the ones you missed.

Conference Talk
"Can the damaged brain repair itself?" -- Siddharthan Chandran, TED

A neurologist talks about nerve damage and nerve repair to an audience that is not from his field. Your subject, and the register you need in Bangkok: serious science, ordinary words.

Tip: watch with English subtitles the first time. Note the three verbs he uses most to describe his own work -- then compare them with the four you learned today.
