Chapter 3: The Code

Asking, and Saying No

Do you...? Does she...? We do not... She does not...

Discover the Rule

What Do These Have in Common?

Do you collaborate with other centres?
Does your group publish in English?
We do not develop new drugs.
She does not work with children.
Click to reveal the rule
In questions and negatives the present simple needs a helper: do for I, you, we, they, and does for he, she, it. The helper takes the -s, so the main verb goes back to its plain form: Does she publish? — never Does she publishes?

Yelena, from Kazan
