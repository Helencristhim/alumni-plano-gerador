Chapter 3: Used To It

Normal for Me

I'm used to · I'm getting used to · I'm not used to

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"In São Paulo, I am used to driving everywhere."

"I am not used to walking so much. My feet hurt!"

"The grid was strange at first, but I am getting used to it."

"Ten years ago, I used to take a cab everywhere. Now I take the subway."

What do the orange words have in common? Which sentence is about the past?

FormUseExample
be used to + -ing / nounit is normal for you, not strangeI'm used to crowded trains.
get used to + -ing / nounit is becoming normal (a change)I'm getting used to the subway.
not be used to + -ing / nounit is still strange for youI'm not used to jaywalking.
used to + base verba past habit, not true now (Lesson 20)I used to take a cab.

After be used to and get used to, the word "to" is a preposition: use -ing or a noun, never the base verb.

Chapter 4: Times Square

Ask a New Yorker

Lucimara is a little lost; Tony knows the way

Dialogue

Near Times Square

L
Excuse me. I have a meeting on Fifth Avenue, but I think I got lost.
T
No problem. What's the address? Do you know the cross street?
L
Yes, Forty-Second Street. In São Paulo, I'm used to driving everywhere. Here, everybody walks!
T
You'll get used to it. It's a ten-minute walk, or one stop on the seven train. It's off-peak, so it won't be crowded.
L
Great. Where do I get off?
T
At Fifth Avenue. There's a service change this weekend, but today the trains are normal.
L
Thank you. And that restaurant over there? It looks very busy.
T
Honestly? It's a tourist trap. Walk two blocks, and you'll find better food for half the price.
L
Good to know! People are jaywalking everywhere here. I'm not used to that yet.
Comprehension

About Tony

1. What two ways to get to Fifth Avenue does Tony give?
A ten-minute walk, or one stop on the seven train.
2. Why does Tony say the train won't be crowded?
Because it is off-peak.
3. What does Tony say about the busy restaurant?
It is a tourist trap. Two blocks away, the food is better and half the price.
