Lesson 31 -- By Then I Will Have...

Documentary series
Somebody Feed Phil

Every episode is one city and one trip: Phil Rosenthal lands in Tokyo, Rio de Janeiro, Bangkok or Amsterdam, eats what the locals eat and talks to the people who cook it. It is an itinerary happening in real time, which is exactly what you planned in this lesson.

Tip: pick the Tokyo episode, and halfway through pause it and say out loud what he will have done by the end of that day. Three sentences, all with "will have".

Podcast
Amateur Traveler -- Otaku Guide to Tokyo (Episode 880)

A host and a guest build a Tokyo trip day by day through gaming, anime and arcade culture: Akihabara, the retro game shops, the themed cafes, and how many of them fit into one afternoon. It is a real itinerary being decided out loud, which is the conversation you had in class.

Tip: the full day-by-day plan is written on the page under the player. Listen once for the shape of the trip, then read the plan and write three deadlines of your own: "By the second morning I will have..."

The same structure you worked out in class, on a whiteboard: the form, the negative, the question, the contractions and the past participles that people get wrong. Watch it after the lesson, not before, so that it confirms what you already discovered.
