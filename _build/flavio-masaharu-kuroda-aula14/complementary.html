Lesson 14 -- The Partner Agreement

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Formal or informal?"

A short episode on choosing between formal and everyday English at work. It is the same move you make with a contract: the document says one thing in formal language, and on the call you say it in simple words. Full script on the same page.

Tip: read the script and make two columns, formal and informal. For each formal phrase, write the version you would say on a call with a partner.

Talk
Alan Siegel -- "Let's simplify legal jargon!" (TED, 5 min)

A short talk arguing that contracts and official documents should be written so that normal people can understand them. It is the reason you summarize the agreement for your team and explain it to the partner in plain English.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down one complicated phrase he shows and rewrite it with have to, get or can.

Watch on TED ↗
Film
The Founder (2016)

A salesman signs a partnership agreement with two brothers who own a restaurant, and the whole story depends on what the contract says and what it does not say. Watch how a handshake and a written clause end up meaning very different things.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time the contract is mentioned, ask: who has to do what, and who gets what?
