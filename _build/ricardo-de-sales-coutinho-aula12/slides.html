Your first month in Miami

Hi Ricardo. Here is the calendar for your first month in Miami. Your first day is on Monday, October fifth.

The team meeting is every week, on Monday, at nine in the morning. On Wednesday, you have a call with São Paulo, in the afternoon.

In November there is a big holiday: Thanksgiving. It is on a Thursday, and the office is closed. Many people travel to see the family.

Your calendar is on the computer. Look at it every day, in the morning. See you in October! Amy

Email -- Amy, Miami office
