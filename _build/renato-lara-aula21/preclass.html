Lesson 21 — Pre-class

Who Said What?

Eight words for a team selection (trials, squad, coach, announce, pick, rumor, gossip, chance) and how to report what people said: said / told me, with the verb one step back.

Stage 1.1: The Team Selection

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Eight words, two minutes. Here "pick" means choose.

Trials — a test where a coach watches you to choose the best players
"The trials are next Saturday."
Squad — the small group of players that the coach chooses for a team
"The squad has four surfers."
Coach — a person who trains players and helps them get better
"The coach works with the state team."
Announce — to tell everybody some official news
"Kai will announce the names on Monday."
Pick — to choose somebody or something from a group
"The coach picks the squad."
Rumor — a story that many people tell, but nobody knows if it is true
"Did you hear the rumor about the trials?"
Gossip — talk about other people's lives, often things that are not true
"That's just gossip."
Chance — a possibility that something good can happen
"You have a good chance."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read Renato's message to his friend Pedro in Brazil. Then answer the questions.

Pedro, crazy week. The club has trials for the interschool team, and everybody is talking. Ollie said the trials were this Saturday, but that was just a rumor. Kai told us they were next Saturday. Tom said the coach was from the state team, and this time it wasn't gossip: it's true. Her name is Dana Reid, and she picks the squad. Only four surfers! Jess told me I had a good chance, and Harper said she was nervous. Kai said he would announce the names on Monday. Wish me luck!
Kai's words were: "The trials are next Saturday." Why does Renato write "were"?
A When you repeat what somebody said, the verb usually goes one step back: are becomes were.
B Because the trials are finished.
Which sentence is correct?
A Jess said me that I had a good chance.
B Jess told me that I had a good chance.
Kai said: "I will announce the names." How does Renato report it?
A Kai said he would announce the names.
B Kai said he will announced the names.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Said / told me + the verb one step back

When you repeat what somebody said, the verb usually goes one step back, and I / you change to the right person.

One step back

is / are → was / were
can → could
will → would
have → had
surf → surfed

Said or told?

He said (that) ...
He told me (that) ...
He said me
She told that

Positive: Kai said the trials were next Saturday.  ·  Negative: Harper told me she couldn't come.  ·  Question: What did the coach say?
People change too: Ollie: "I can't surf." → Ollie said he couldn't surf.
That is optional: "She said (that) she was nervous."

Quiz
