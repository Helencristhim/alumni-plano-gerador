Grammar Discovery

What happens when you repeat it?

Kai: "The trials are next Saturday." → Kai said the trials were next Saturday.
Harper: "I can surf on Thursday." → Harper told me she could surf on Thursday.
Kai: "I will announce the names." → Kai said he would announce the names.
Jess: "You have a good chance." → Jess told me I had a good chance.

What do the orange words have in common?

said (that) + sentence · told + person (that) + sentence

When you repeat what somebody said, the verb usually goes one step back: is → was, are → were, can → could, will → would, have → had. I and you change too: "I can" → she could. Told always needs a person: told me, told us. That is optional.

The Code

One step back

They said → you repeat

is / are → was / were
can → could
will → would
have / has → had
surf / surfs → surfed

Never

He said me that ...
"said" has no person: he told me / he said
She told that ...
"told" needs a person: she told me that ...
Kai said he will announce it.
one step back: he would announce it

The question: What did he say? · What did she tell you?

The Code

Fact, or gossip?

When you hear news, repeat it with the name of the person. Then you know if it is a fact or just gossip.

You hear

"The coach is from the state team."
"You need a new board."
"The trials are this Saturday."

You check

"Kai said she was. That's a fact."
"Who told you that?"
"Tom said they were. Just gossip."
Kai told us the trials were next Saturday.

A name plus what they said: that is how you stop a rumor.

Sound Check

Three blocks, one story

Kai said the trials were next Saturday.

"said" is short: SED

Jess told me I had a good chance.

"told me" is one block: TOLD-me

He said he would announce the names on Monday.

"would" has no L sound: WUD

Listening 1

The facts, from Kai

1. When and where are the trials?
Next Saturday, not this Saturday, at their home beach, at seven.
2. Who picks the squad, and how many surfers are in it?
Dana Reid, the coach who works with the state team. Four surfers and one reserve.
3. Do they need a new board? When will Kai announce the names?
No, they surf their normal board. He will announce the names on Monday, on the club group.
