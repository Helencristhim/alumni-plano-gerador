Grammar Video
"Gerund or infinitive?" — Ask BBC Learning English

Four minutes on what happens when two English verbs meet: when the second one becomes -ing, when it takes to, and why there is no rule you can reason your way to. Connection to Lesson 11: this is the pattern that runs through every sentence you produced in the interview.

Tip: pause at every example and say it back with a verb from your own week -- involves reviewing, decided to escalate, responsible for drafting. The pattern only sticks attached to your own sentences.

A short workplace-English episode on talking about your own work without sounding either apologetic or inflated — the exact register an interview asks for. Connection to Lesson 11: it is the language of Stage 5 and of the guided role-play, used at conversational speed.
