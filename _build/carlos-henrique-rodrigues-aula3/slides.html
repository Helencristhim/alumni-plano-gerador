Chapter 3: The Code

I Need, Do You Have?

The present simple with I and you

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I need a quiet room."

"I do not need a big bed."

"Do you have breakfast?"

"What time do I check out?"

Look at the blue words. Sentences 2, 3 and 4 have a small helper word. What is it? Where is it?

JobFormExample
AffirmativeI / you + verbI need a room. You have a nice hotel.
NegativeI / you + do not (don’t) + verbI don’t need a big bed.
Yes / no questionDo + you + verb?Do you have breakfast?
Wh- questionWhat / Where / What time + do + I / you + verb?What time do I check out?
Short answersYes, I do. / No, I don’t.Do you need help? Yes, I do.
The trapno "do" with the verb to beNot Do you are ready? Say Are you ready?

In one line: for most verbs, questions and negatives need do: Do you have...? I don't need...

Chapter 4: At the Front Desk

At the Front Desk

One conversation, two recordings, one card

Dialogue

Good Evening!

L
Good evening, and welcome to the Hudson Hotel. How can I help you?
C
Good evening. I want to check in, please. The reservation is for Rodrigues.
L
Yes, Mr. Rodrigues. Four nights, a double room. Do you need two keys?
C
Yes, I do. And I need a quiet room, please.
L
No problem. Your room is on the tenth floor. The elevator is on the left.
C
Thank you. Do you have breakfast?
L
Yes, we do. Breakfast is from seven to ten, on the second floor.
C
Great. And what time do we check out?
L
At eleven. Enjoy New York!
Comprehension

About Linda

1. What is the name of Linda's hotel?
The Hudson Hotel.
2. Where is the elevator, according to Linda?
On the left.
3. What time is breakfast, according to Linda?
From seven to ten.
4. What time is check-out, according to Linda?
At eleven.
