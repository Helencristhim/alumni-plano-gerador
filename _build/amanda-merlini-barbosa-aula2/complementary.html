Series
The Good Wife -- Season 1, Episode 1: Pilot

A lawyer goes back to work after thirteen years away and has to explain, in one room, who she used to be and who she is now. It is a career story told under pressure, which is exactly the task of this lesson.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down every sentence where a character says how long they have done something. Check the tense each time.

Podcast
How I Lawyer -- #150: Heather Stevenson, General Counsel, Former Litigator, and Ex-Juice Bar Owner

A general counsel tells her whole career in one conversation: the firm, the exit, the years in between and the way back in. The tenses do the work, and you can hear them doing it.

Tip: listen to the first ten minutes twice. The first time for the story, the second time only for "I have" and "I did". Write down three sentences of each.

Sarah Ellis and Helen Tupper: The best career path isn't always a straight line

Thirteen minutes on why a career that changes direction is stronger than one that does not, and how to say that out loud without sounding lost. The English is clear and the subtitles are there.

Tip: pause at the end and say your own version in one sentence, starting with "My career has not been a straight line, because...".
