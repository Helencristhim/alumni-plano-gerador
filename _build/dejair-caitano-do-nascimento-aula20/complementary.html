Lesson 20 -- Bangkok Ready

Podcast
"Chatting to new people" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters talk about why we feel anxious when we speak to people we don't know, and what a social psychologist found about it. Six minutes, with a transcript -- the small talk of your first scene in Bangkok.

Tip: listen once without the transcript and write the key words. Then write three small talk questions you can use in the elevator or in the coffee line.

Conference Talk
"Your body language may shape who you are" -- Amy Cuddy, TED

A social psychologist talks about how our posture changes how confident we feel before a difficult moment, like an interview or a Q&A. A researcher presenting her own study -- and a good talk to watch before your congress.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Note one sentence she uses to describe her results, and one sentence where she is not 100% sure (hedging).

Video
"Why do we sleep?" -- Russell Foster, TED

A neuroscientist from Oxford explains the body clock, what happens when we don't sleep, and why a long flight is so hard for the brain. Useful science before your flight to Bangkok -- and a clear British speaker to train your ear.

Tip: watch the part about the body clock twice. Then explain your plan for jet lag in Bangkok in three sentences, out loud.
