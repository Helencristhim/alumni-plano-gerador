Chapter 3: The Code

Your Toolbox

One tool for each scene of the day

Grammar Discovery

Which Tool for Which Scene?

In the elevator: "I was looking for the coffee when I saw your badge."
In the Q&A: "Could you tell me what you mean by chance?"
Under pressure: "The effect might be related to the dose."
At the poster: "So far, we have tested sixty patients."
At the goodbye: "If you send me your protocol, I'll share our data."
Click to reveal the rule
Every scene of the day has its tool. Small talk tells short stories: past continuous for the background, past simple for the event (I was looking for... when I saw...). The Q&A needs polite repair: indirect question, subject + verb (what you mean, not what do you mean). A hard question needs hedging: might / may / could + verb, probably. The poster needs results that are not finished: present perfect with so far and yet. The goodbye needs a promise about a possible future: if + present, will + verb.
