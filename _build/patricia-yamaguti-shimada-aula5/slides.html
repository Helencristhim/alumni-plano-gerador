Chapter 3: What Is There

What There Is, and What There Is Not

there is · there are · there is no

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"There is one big bed."

"There are two small beds."

"There is no towel in the bathroom."

"Is there a hair dryer in the room?"

PatternUseExample
There is + one thingONE thing existsThere is one big bed.
There are + two or moreTWO OR MORE existThere are two small beds.
There is no / There are noit does NOT existThere is no towel.
Is there...? / Are there...?you ask if it existsIs there a hair dryer?

One thing = is. Two or more = are. To ask, the two words change places: there is → is there.

Dialogue

A Call from Room 304

T
Good evening, reception. How can I help you?
P
Good evening. This is room 304. There are no towels in the bathroom.
T
I am very sorry. How many towels do you need?
P
Four, please. I am here with my family.
T
Certainly. Would you also like me to send up some extra pillows for the little ones?
P
Sorry, I don't understand. Can you speak more slowly, please?
T
Of course. Pillows — for the children. Do you want more?
P
Yes, please. Two pillows. And is there a hair dryer in the room?
T
No, there is not. But there is one at the reception, and it is free.
P
Thank you. One more thing: the elevator on my floor is not working.
T
Thank you for telling me. I send someone now. Use the stairs, or the second elevator.
P
Perfect. And what time is breakfast?
T
From seven to ten, on the ground floor. Your key opens the door. Good night.
Comprehension

What Did Tom Say?

1. Where is the hair dryer, and how much is it?
At the reception, and it is free.
2. What does Tom say about the elevator?
He sends someone now, and she can use the stairs or the second elevator.
3. What time is breakfast, and where?
From seven to ten, on the ground floor.
