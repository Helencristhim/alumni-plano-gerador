Lesson 29 -- If We Had Known...

Film
Sliding Doors (1998)

A woman in London runs for a train. The film shows two versions of her life: one where she catches the train, and one where she misses it. Everything changes because of one small moment. Connection to Lesson 29: the whole film is a third conditional — if she had caught the train, her life would have been different.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. After the film, write five sentences about the two lives with if + had + past participle and would have + past participle.
