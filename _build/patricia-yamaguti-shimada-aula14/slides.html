Chapter 3: Do and Does

The S Moves to the Front

do · does

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Do you have this in a large?"

"Do they have it in the other shop?"

"Does it come in blue?"

"Does this model have pockets?"

The question is aboutFirst wordExample
you, we, theyDoDo you have this in a large?
he, she, it, this modelDoesDoes it come in blue?
after do / doesthe verb is NAKED, no SDoes it come...? (never comes)
short answersthe same word comes backYes, we do. / No, it does not.

It is the SAME S from lesson 13. In a question it leaves the verb and jumps to the front: she comes → does she come? One S per sentence, never two.

Dialogue

She Does Not Go Away After the First No

P
Excuse me. Do you have this dress in a large?
O
Let me look. In blue? No, I am sorry, we do not.
P
Do you have it in stock, in the back?
O
I checked the screen. Blue in large: nothing, not here.
P
Does it come in another color, in large?
O
It does, actually. We have two in black, size large.
P
Is it the same model? Or a different one?
O
Exactly the same, same brand, same price. Just a different colour.
P
Sorry, I don't understand. Can you say that again, please?
O
Sorry. Same dress. Same price. Only the colour changes.
P
Thank you. But my daughter wants blue. Do you have it in another shop?
O
The King Road shop has three in blue, size large. Do you want me to call them?
P
Yes, please. Can you ask them to keep one until tomorrow?
Comprehension

What Did Oliver Say?

1. What does the shop have in size large?
Two in black — nothing in blue.
2. What does Oliver say about the black one?
Exactly the same dress, same brand, same price — only the colour changes.
3. What does the other shop have, and what does he offer?
Three blue in large at King Road. He offers to call them.

Role-Play — Free

Four Nos in a Row

Scenario: You need one specific thing and the shop says no four times. Do not go away: stock, other color, other model, other shop. Get to a yes, or to a real no.

No keywords this time. Two minutes, no stopping. Different from the model is not wrong — if they understood you, it worked.
