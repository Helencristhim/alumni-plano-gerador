Talk
TED -- William Ury, "The walk from no to yes" (19 min)

A negotiation expert tells stories of conflicts and meetings, and he constantly reports what people said to him and what he said to them. Great listening practice for reported speech in a real story.

Tip: listen for "he told me that" and "they asked if". Then retell one of his stories in five sentences.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English -- "Rude emails"

Six minutes on why some work emails sound rude and how to write messages that people read well. Useful before you write the next debrief to a partner or a client.

Tip: listen once, then read the transcript and find two phrases you could use in a debrief email.

Video
engVid -- "Grammar: Learn to use REPORTED SPEECH in English"

A teacher explains, step by step, how to report statements and questions, and why the verb moves back in time. A clear review of today's structure.

Tip: pause after each example and report something a client or a regulator said to you this week.
