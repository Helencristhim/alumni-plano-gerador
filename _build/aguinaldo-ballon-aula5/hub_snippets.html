What They Told Us -- Reporting a Meeting

After a meeting, you tell people who were not there what was said. Key words: to state, to confirm, to warn, to acknowledge, a counterpart, a briefing, a statement, to recommend, off the record, a debrief. Structure: reported speech.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

To state -- to say something clearly and formally
"The agency stated that the deadline would not change."
To confirm -- to say that something is true or will happen
"The regulator confirmed that the hearing was on Tuesday."
To warn -- to tell somebody about a possible problem or danger
"Our lawyer warned us that the rule could change."
To acknowledge -- to accept or admit that something is true
"The agency acknowledged that the process was slow."
A counterpart -- a person with the same job or role in another organization
"I called my counterpart at the London firm."
A briefing -- a short meeting to give people the information they need
"We had a briefing before the hearing."
A statement -- an official, public text or declaration
"The ministry published a statement this morning."
To recommend -- to say that something is a good idea
"The consultant recommended a new timeline."
Off the record -- expression: said privately, not to be repeated officially
"Off the record, he told us the rule would be delayed."
A debrief -- a meeting after an event to report and discuss what happened
"Let's do a short debrief after the call."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

On Tuesday, Aguinaldo met the director of the transport agency. After the meeting, his counterpart in London asked for a debrief.

The director had said, "We will publish the rule in May." In the debrief, Aguinaldo wrote that the director had confirmed that the rule would be published in May. The director had also said, "The penalties can be higher." Aguinaldo reported that he had warned them that the penalties could be higher.

Finally, one adviser asked, "Can your client send data on accidents?" In the email, Aguinaldo wrote that she had asked if the client could send the data. One comment was off the record, so it did not go in the email.

1. What did the director actually say about the rule?
A "We would publish the rule in May."
B "We will publish the rule in May."
C "We had published the rule in May."
2. Why does the report say "could be higher" and not "can be higher"?
A Because in reported speech the verb moves one step back in time.
B Because the penalties are not important.
C Because can is only used in questions.
3. Why is one comment not in the email?
A Because Aguinaldo forgot it.
B Because it was said off the record.
C Because it was a question.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Reported Speech

GRAMMAR

When you report what somebody said, the verb usually moves one step back in time. Tell needs a person; say does not.

JobFormExample
will becomes wouldsaid (that) + would"We will publish it." → He said they would publish it.
present becomes pastsaid (that) + past simple"It is slow." → She said it was slow.
present perfect becomes past perfecttold us (that) + had + past participle"We have received it." → They told us they had received it.
can becomes couldsaid (that) + could"We can wait." → He said they could wait.
Yes / no questionsasked + if + normal word order"Can you send it?" → She asked if we could send it.
say or tell?say something / tell somebody somethingHe said that... / He told us that... (never "said us")
The trapreporting verbs that are more precise than saidHe confirmed / warned / recommended that...

Never: He said us that... · She asked if could we send it · They told us they have received it yesterday · He said that the deadline will not change (in a report written later)

Stage 2: Put the Debrief in Order

Order

You are calling your counterpart in London after the meeting. Put your debrief in a logical order.

?"The meeting with the agency went well."
?"The director confirmed that the rule would be published in May."
?"He said the transition period would be twelve months."
?"But he warned us that the penalties could be higher."
?"He also asked if our client could send data on accidents."
?"I will send a short debrief in writing this afternoon."

Quiz

A "She asked if our client could send the data."
