As work gets more complex, 6 rules to simplify

Yves Morieux argues that companies answer complexity by adding reporting lines, dashboards and meetings, which makes work slower rather than clearer. His six rules are a direct argument about who must talk to whom, and when.

Tip: listen for the modal verbs he uses to give rules, such as you have to and managers must, and copy the pattern.

6 Minute English -- Rude emails

The presenters look at what makes a work email feel uncivil and what it costs a team. It is a short, very usable lesson in the register you need for progress updates, escalations and follow-ups.

Tip: pick one email you sent this week and rewrite its first line using the softer phrases from this episode.

5 tips for dealing with meeting overload -- The Way We Work, a TED series

A short episode on which meetings need to exist, who really needs to be in them, and how to end them with clear next steps. Exactly the problem of progress calls, minutes and action items.

Tip: choose one recurring call in your project and write, in English, the rule you would propose for it.
