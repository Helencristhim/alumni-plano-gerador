Lesson 03 -- Pre-class

Who Talks to Whom -- The Communication Flow of a Project

Once the scope is divided, information needs a route. Key words: a point of contact, to escalate, an escalation path, a progress call, to copy someone in, the minutes, an action item, a follow-up, a decision log, a distribution list. Structure: must, have to, need to and should.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A point of contact -- the one person you write to about a specific topic
"For anything on the buildings, I am your point of contact."
To escalate -- to take a problem to a higher level when it cannot be solved where it is
"If we do not have an answer by Friday, we escalate."
An escalation path -- the agreed order of people a problem goes to when it is not solved
"The escalation path is lead engineer, project manager, then director."
A progress call -- a short regular meeting to check how the work is going
"The progress call is every Tuesday at three, thirty minutes."
To copy someone in -- to add a person to an email so that they can read it
"Please copy the piping lead in when you answer."
The minutes -- the written record of what a meeting decided
"I will send the minutes before the end of the day."
An action item -- one task from a meeting, with an owner and a date
"We closed the call with six action items."
A follow-up -- a message or a meeting that continues something you started
"This is just a follow-up on the question from Tuesday."
A decision log -- the list of decisions taken on a project, with dates
"If it is not in the decision log, it did not happen."
A distribution list -- the fixed group of people who receive a document
"Every revision goes to the same distribution list."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Before the design starts, the two offices agree how they will talk. Each topic gets one point of contact. The progress call is every Tuesday at three, and it lasts thirty minutes. The team has to send the minutes within twenty-four hours, because the contract says so.

Two rules have no exception. Every change to the interface must go through the decision log. And nobody must share a drawing outside the distribution list.

The rest is advice. You should copy the piping lead in when you answer a question about the equipment base. You do not have to attend every technical call — but if you do not, you need to read the action items the same day.

1. Why does the text say the team “has to send” the minutes?
A Because it is only a recommendation.
B Because the obligation comes from outside: the contract says so.
C Because it is forbidden to send them later.
2. What does “you do not have to attend every technical call” mean?
A It is forbidden to attend.
B Attending is optional.
C You must attend every call.
3. Which sentence is a rule with no exception?
A “You should copy the piping lead in.”
B “Every change must go through the decision log.”
C “You do not have to attend the call.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Must, Have To, Need To and Should

GRAMMAR

Four verbs for four different levels of pressure. The question that separates them: who decided this, and what happens if you do not do it?

JobFormExample
A rule with no exception, often writtenmust + verbEvery change must go through the decision log.
An obligation that comes from outside youhave to / has to + verbWe have to send the minutes within 24 hours.
Something necessary for the work to moveneed to + verbI need to know the equipment load before Friday.
Advice, good practiceshould + verbYou should copy the piping lead in.
Not necessary, optionaldo not have to + verbYou do not have to join the whole call.
The trapmust not is a prohibition, not an absence of obligationYou must not send drawings outside the distribution list = it is forbidden. You do not have to send them = it is optional.

Never: We must to deliver it · You should to escalate · Do I must copy him in? · You must not send the minutes (when you mean it is optional)

A "You must not join the technical call."

A "You must not share the drawings outside the distribution list."
