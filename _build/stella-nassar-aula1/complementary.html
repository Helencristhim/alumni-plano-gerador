Mona Lisa Smile (2003)

An art history professor arrives at a women's college in 1953 and refuses to teach the slides the way the textbook says. Julia Roberts, Kirsten Dunst and Julia Stiles, and a classroom that argues about what makes a painting good.

Tip: watch the classroom scenes twice. Every time a student defends or attacks a painting, ask yourself whether she is describing the work or only saying how she feels about it.
