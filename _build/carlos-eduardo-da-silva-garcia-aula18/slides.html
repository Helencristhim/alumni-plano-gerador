Chapter 4: Going To

Am, Is, Are + Going To

How do we talk about a plan?

Grammar Discovery

Read and Notice

"I am going to follow up with the producers."

"Emma is going to train the team on April tenth."

"We are going to close all the corrective actions."

"I am not going to wait until the last week."

FormHowExample
Affirmativeam / is / are + going to + base verbI am going to send the report.
Negativeam not / is not / are not + going toWe are not going to wait.
QuestionAm / Is / Are + subject + going to...?Are you going to visit the farm next week?
will (contrast)a decision you make NOW, not a planThe phone is ringing. I will answer it.
Time wordsnext week, next month, in two weeks, by April 30th, before the deadline, soon

Use going to for a plan you decided BEFORE. Use will for a decision you make at that moment. / Use going to para um plano ja decidido; use will para uma decisao tomada na hora.

Common Mistake

Wrong vs Right

✗ WRONG

"I going to send the report tomorrow."

✓ RIGHT

"I am going to send the report tomorrow."

✗ WRONG

"We are going to closed the actions."

✓ RIGHT

"We are going to close the actions."

Never drop am / is / are before going to, and always use the base verb after it. / Nunca esqueca am / is / are antes de going to, e use sempre o verbo base depois.
