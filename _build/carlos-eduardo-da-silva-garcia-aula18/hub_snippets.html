Aula 18 -- Pre-class

Talking About the Future -- Plans, Deadlines and Next Steps in Sustainability Projects

Depois de reportar os problemas da visita, diga o que vem DEPOIS: o plano dos proximos 30 dias, com prazos e prioridades. Gramatica: futuro com going to (afirmativa, negativa e pergunta), com will apenas como contraste. Seis palavras de planejamento e um plano de acao modelo.

Etapa 1.1: Cartoes de Vocabulario

Vocabulary

Ouca cada palavra e leia o exemplo. Sao as palavras que voce usa para falar de um plano de acao. Toque Listen para ouvir. (Listen = Ouvir)

Deliver -- entregar (enviar no prazo o que voce prometeu)
"I am going to deliver the final report by April thirtieth." (Vou entregar o relatorio final ate 30 de abril.)
Implement -- implementar (comecar a usar um sistema ou plano novo)
"We are going to implement the new checklist in April." (Vamos implementar o novo checklist em abril.)
Follow up -- dar continuidade (falar de novo com alguem para checar o proximo passo)
"I am going to follow up with the producer next week." (Vou falar de novo com o produtor na proxima semana.)
Timeline -- cronograma (as datas e as etapas do projeto, em ordem)
"Please put the new date in the timeline." (Por favor coloque a nova data no cronograma.)
Target -- meta (o resultado que voce quer alcancar)
"Our target is to close every corrective action." (Nossa meta e fechar todas as acoes corretivas.)
Priority -- prioridade (o mais importante, o que voce faz primeiro)
"The four farm visits are our top priority." (As quatro visitas sao nossa prioridade maxima.)

Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Leia o plano de acao da equipe e responda as perguntas. (Read the team action plan and answer.)

This is the plan for the next thirty days. The target is simple: the team is going to close all the corrective actions. First, Carlos is going to follow up with the six producers. This is the top priority. Without the documents, the team is not going to close anything. After that, Emma is going to implement the new checklist in the IMS system, and the training is in the timeline for April tenth. Carlos is going to deliver the final report by April thirtieth.

1. What is the first step of the plan? (Qual e o primeiro passo do plano?)
A The training on April tenth. (isso vem depois)
B To follow up with the six producers. (falar de novo com os produtores)
C To deliver the final report. (isso e o ultimo passo)
2. Who is going to implement the new checklist? (Quem vai implementar o novo checklist?)
A The six producers. (nao, eles enviam documentos)
B Emma. (ela implementa no sistema IMS)
C Nobody. (o plano tem um responsavel)
3. How do we say a plan in English? (Como dizemos um plano em ingles?)
A "I going to send the report." (falta o verbo am)
B "I am going to send the report." (am + going to + verbo base)
C "I am going to sent the report." (depois de going to o verbo e base)

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Going To (Futuro)

Grammar

Como falar de um plano que voce ja decidiu. / How to talk about a plan you already decided.

Form / FormaUse / UsoExample / Exemplo
am / is / are + going toum plano ja decidido (afirmativa)I am going to send the report. (Vou enviar o relatorio.)
am not / is not / are not + going toum plano que NAO vai acontecer (negativa)We are not going to wait. (Nao vamos esperar.)
Am / Is / Are + sujeito + going to...?pergunta sobre o plano do outroAre you going to visit the farm next week? (Voce vai visitar a fazenda na proxima semana?)
will (contraste)decisao tomada NA HORA, sem planoThe phone is ringing. I will answer it. (Vou atender.)
Time wordsnext week, next month, in two weeks, by April 30th, before the deadline, soon (na proxima semana, no proximo mes, em duas semanas, ate 30 de abril, antes do prazo, em breve)

Atencao: nunca esqueca am / is / are antes de going to, e use sempre o verbo BASE depois (send, close, deliver -- nunca sent ou closed). / Never drop am / is / are, and always use the base verb after going to.

Etapa 4: Quiz Situacional

Quiz

Escolha a melhor resposta para cada situacao.

The coordinator asks: "What are you going to do this week?" (Voce ja decidiu: falar com os produtores.)
A "I going to follow up with the producers."
B "I am going to follow up with the producers."
C "I am going to followed up with the producers."
You want to know the plan of your colleague. (Voce quer saber o plano do colega.)
A "You are going to visit the farm next week?"
B "Are you going to visit the farm next week?"
C "Do you going to visit the farm next week?"
The timeline is short and you want to start now. (O cronograma e curto e voce quer comecar agora.)
A "We are going to wait until the last week."
B "We are not going to wait until the last week."
C "We not going to wait until the last week."

Etapa 5: Producao Livre

Reflection

Grave voce respondendo a pergunta abaixo. Nao ha resposta certa ou errada.

Present your action plan for the next thirty days. Say your target, your top priority, one date in your timeline, and when you are going to deliver the final report. Use going to in every step. Take your time. (Apresente seu plano de 30 dias: a meta, a prioridade, uma data do cronograma e quando vai entregar o relatorio final.)
