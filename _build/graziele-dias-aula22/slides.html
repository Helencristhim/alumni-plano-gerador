Chapter 3: The Offer Code

Offer Help

I'll + simple verb

Discover the Pattern

What's the Pattern?

You need a taxi? I'll call the driver.

You want to see the ICU? I'll show you.

You have a lot of bags? I'll help you.

Don't worry. I won't be late.

What do the orange words have in common?

FormUse
I'll + verban offer or a decision now: "I'll call the driver."
I'll = I willthe short form, for speaking
I won't + verba promise with no: "I won't be late."

After I'll / I won't: the simple verb, with no "to".
