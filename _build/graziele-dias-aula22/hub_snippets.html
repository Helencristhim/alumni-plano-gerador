Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Leia o plano da visita e responda às perguntas. Ele usa I'll, will e won't. (Read and answer.)

Dear Doctor Carter, here is the plan for Thursday. Our driver will meet you at the airport. At ten thirty, I'll meet you at reception, and I'll give you your badges. Then I'll give you a tour of the hospital. We will have lunch with the team. Don't worry, I won't be late. I promise!

1. Who will meet the doctors at the airport? (Quem vai encontrar os médicos no aeroporto?)
A "Graziele, with the badges and the coffee." (A Graziele.)
B "The team." (A equipe.)
C "The driver." (O motorista.)
2. What will Graziele give the doctors at reception? (O que a Graziele vai dar aos médicos na recepção?)
A "Lunch." (Almoço.)
B "Their badges." (Os crachás.)
C "A room for the presentation in the morning." (Uma sala.)
3. "I won't be late" is a... (A frase é uma...)
A "promise" (promessa)
B "question about the time of the flight" (pergunta)
C "rule" (regra)

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Will for Offers and Promises (I'll / I won't)

Grammar

Como oferecer ajuda e fazer promessas em inglês. / How to offer help and make promises in English.

Forma / FormUso + exemplo
I'll + verbooferta ou decisão na hora: "I'll call the driver." (Eu ligo para o motorista.)
I'll = I willI'll é a forma curta, usada na fala: "I will help you" = "I'll help you". (Eu te ajudo.)
I won't + verbopromessa com não: "I won't be late." (Não vou me atrasar.)
Atenção / Watch outDepois de will / I'll vem o verbo simples, sem to e sem -s: "I'll to call" (errado) -> "I'll call" (certo). Para o não, use won't, e não "don't will".

I'll = oferta / decisão · I won't = promessa com não · + verbo simples. (I'll = offer · I won't = promise with no · + simple verb.)
