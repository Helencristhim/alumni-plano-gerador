Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Lesson 1 -- Flagging the Problem

Series
Suits -- Season 1, Episode 1 (Pilot)

A New York law firm from the inside. Ignore the plot for a moment and listen only to how the lawyers deliver bad news to a client: they name the problem first, then the consequence, then what they want to do about it. That is the same three-line shape you practiced today.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down three sentences where a lawyer flags a problem. Rewrite each one using your own clause and your own client.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Podcast
Slate's Amicus with Dahlia Lithwick -- any recent episode

Lawyers and journalists explaining complex legal reasoning to a non-legal audience, in clear American English. The value for you is the register: technical content, plain delivery, no jargon for its own sake. That is exactly the balance a contracts manager wants from you on a call.

Tip: pick one argument you hear and try to say it back in three lines -- the claim, the consequence, the recommendation. Record yourself.

Listen on Slate ↗
YouTube
LegalEagle -- "Real Lawyer Reacts" series

A practising American attorney breaks down contracts and legal scenes, saying out loud what is wrong with them and why. It is the closest thing on YouTube to the task you did today: reading a clause and flagging the problem in spoken English.

Tip: pause every time he says "the problem here is..." and write the sentence down. You are building a bank of openers for your own calls.
