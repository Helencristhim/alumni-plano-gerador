Film
The Trial of the Chicago 7

A courtroom film built almost entirely out of argument: objections, cross-examination, concessions and closing statements, at natural professional speed. Watch it for the same thing you practised in this lesson -- how somebody keeps control of a room while being interrupted, and how a position is defended without overstating it. Connection to Lesson 15: the hedge, the pause and the recovery after an interruption, performed by people whose whole case depends on getting them right.

Tip: pick one cross-examination scene and write down every phrase used to buy time before answering. Then say your own version of three of them, about the reform.

Podcast
Cross-border Tax Talks -- "The Global Tax Journey: 2025 to 2026"

A free, recurring podcast on international and corporate taxation: Pillar Two, digital services taxes, and where global tax policy is going next. This episode reviews the year and what is coming, in exactly the vocabulary you already work with in Portuguese every day. Connection to Lesson 15: this is the one live thing to keep after the program ends -- new episodes arrive on their own, so the habit does not depend on you remembering to look for material.

Tip: this is your listening habit, not a lesson. One episode a fortnight, no notes. If you can follow it at normal speed by December, your listening is exam-ready.

Video
Cambridge English -- "C1 Advanced Speaking test: Raphael and Maude"

The official Cambridge demonstration of a full speaking test, filmed with real candidates and real examiners across all four parts. You see exactly what is asked, how long each part lasts, and what a strong performance actually sounds like -- free, from the exam board itself, with no course to buy. Connection to Lesson 15: this is your certification step, and the same channel has the B2 First version, so one source covers both levels.

Tip: watch once as a viewer, then again with a timer, answering Part 1 out loud yourself before the candidates do. That comparison tells you more about your readiness than any practice book.
