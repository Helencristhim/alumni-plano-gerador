Lesson 6 -- Better, Bigger, More Stylish

Series
Emily in Paris — Netflix

Every episode has at least one scene where two people argue about which idea, which campaign or which piece is better. Watch for the argument, not for the clothes.

Tip: pick one disagreement and write down the two positions in English, one sentence each. Then say out loud which side you are on and why.

Listening
"Making old things last longer" — 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on repairing instead of replacing — the same argument as the reading text of this lesson, from another angle and with a different vocabulary.

Tip: listen once without subtitles. Then say three sentences comparing repairing and replacing, using a comparative in each one.

Grammar
"Comparatives and superlatives" — 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

The whole rule in six minutes, including the spelling changes for short adjectives and the two irregular pairs.

Tip: after the video, compare three pairs of things from your own life out loud. If you say more with a short adjective, you will hear it immediately.
