"How to use reported speech" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on the structure you practiced in class: passing on what another person said. Connection to Lesson 18: the presenters explain say and tell, and how the verb moves back in time -- is becomes was, will becomes would.

"5 ways to listen better" -- Julian Treasure, TED

Eight minutes on why we listen badly and five simple ways to listen better. Connection to Lesson 18: you can only pass on what people said if you really heard it -- in a meeting, on a call or in a voicemail like the one from Tom.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then tell somebody two things the speaker said, starting with "he said that".
