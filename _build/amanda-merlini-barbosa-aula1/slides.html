Chapter 3: The First Meeting

Three Minutes With a New Director

A conversation in your office, line by line

Dialogue

Meeting the New Operations Director

A
Amanda, good morning. I am Anton Larin. I start today, and I am meeting every department this week.
A
Good morning. I am the legal manager here, so I look after the legal department.
A
Tell me what that means in practice. I am an economist, not a lawyer.
A
My team handles three kinds of matter: contracts, litigation and advisory work.
A
Start with the contracts. Who writes them?
A
We draft them here. I am the lawyer who reviews every contract before the commercial team signs it.
A
And litigation? Do you go to court yourself?
A
Not usually. We choose the firm that represents us in court, and my team controls the strategy.
A
One last question. What is not your responsibility?
A
Employment law. An outside firm takes care of that, and I deal with everything else.
Comprehension

About the Conversation

1. Why does Anton ask for an explanation in practice?
Because he is an economist, not a lawyer, so the words of the legal department are new to him.
2. In litigation, what does the internal team do and what does the outside firm do?
The internal team chooses the firm and controls the strategy; the outside firm represents the company in court.
3. Which area is outside the legal department, and who takes care of it?
Employment law. An outside firm takes care of it.
Chapter 4: The Code

Two Short Sentences, One Long One

who · which · that

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I am the lawyer who reviews every contract."

"We work for a group that exports grain to forty countries."

"Litigation is the work which ends up in court."

"The managers who sign the contracts are in three plants."

Two of the colored words point to a person. Two point to a thing. Which is which?

WordWhat it points toExample
whoa person: the lawyer, the manager, the buyerthe lawyer who reviews the contract
whicha thing: the work, the clause, the documentthe clause which protects the seller
thata person or a thing. It is the natural choice when you speakthe group that exports grain
Watch outthe word already IS the subject, so you do not repeat it: say "the lawyer who reviews", never "the lawyer who she reviews".
Why it matters"I am a lawyer. I work for an agribusiness group." becomes "I am a lawyer who works for an agribusiness group." One sentence, more information, same breath.

Sound Check

Say It Clearly

Word stress · the words of this lesson

Why this one is hard for a Portuguese speaker

In Portuguese: these words look almost the same on paper, so the mouth keeps the Portuguese stress, and it falls on a different syllable.

In English: the stress moves. li-ti-GA-tion, com-PLI-ance, ad-VI-so-ry, a-GRI-bu-si-ness, de-PART-ment.

One pair to keep: the noun is CON-tract, the verb is con-TRACT. "I sign the CON-tract" and "the muscles con-TRACT" are not the same word.

Chapter 5: Real Speed

Two Messages, One Meeting

A voice message from the group office, and the new director introducing himself

Listening 1

A Voice Message from the Group Office

Irina leaves a message about next week. Sound first, no text.

1. How much time does each manager have with Anton, and why so little?
Three minutes each, because he has one afternoon for the whole site.
2. What three things does Irina ask Amanda to tell him?
Which matters her team handles, who she works with inside the company, and one thing that is not her responsibility.
3. What does Irina need by Thursday at noon, and what for?
Two lines from Amanda, so that she can put them in Anton's file.
Listening 2

The New Director Introduces Himself

Anton records a short message for the whole site. Sound first, no text.

1. What did Anton do before this job, and why does that matter here?
He looked after the grain business in Eastern Europe for six years, so South America is completely new for him.
2. What does he want to understand in his first month?
How each department really works day to day, not the organisation chart.
3. What three things does he ask each manager to bring to the meeting?
What the team handles every week, who depends on them, and one problem that comes back again and again.
