Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
The Diplomat — Season 1, Episode 1

An American who says exactly what she means arrives in London, where almost nobody does. Every scene is a professional conversation in which the words on the surface and the meaning underneath are two different things. That gap is the whole reason this lesson exists.

Tip: watch the first fifteen minutes twice. The second time, stop whenever a British character is polite and ask yourself what the answer would sound like in Portuguese.

Watch on Netflix ↗
Talk
Celeste Headlee — “10 ways to have a better conversation” (TED)

Eleven minutes on the thing you asked for: conversations that feel natural instead of correct. She is a professional interviewer, and almost everything she recommends is about listening rather than about language.

Tip: English subtitles on the first time, off the second. Write down the two rules you already follow and the two you never do.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English — “Rude emails”

Six minutes, two British speakers, normal speed, on what makes a work email sound cold or sharp to the person reading it. It is the written half of exactly what this lesson practices out loud, and the free transcript is on the same page.

Tip: listen once with no text. Listen again with the transcript open and mark every phrase you understood by ear but would never have written yourself.
