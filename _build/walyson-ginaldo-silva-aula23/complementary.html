Lesson 23 -- Giving and Receiving Feedback

Talk
LeeAnn Renninger -- “The secret to giving great feedback” (TED)

A cognitive psychologist shows a simple, research-based way to give feedback that people accept. It is short and very practical -- the perfect partner for your homework.

Tip: write down her steps, and compare them with the feedback sandwich from class.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
Kim Scott -- “How to lead with radical candor” (TEDxPortland)

A former manager at Google and Apple explains how to care about people and still tell them the truth. Clear examples of feedback that is too blunt and feedback that is too soft.

Tip: listen for two things her bosses said to her, and report them with said or told.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
Joe Hirsch -- “The joy of getting feedback” (TED)

A speaker and author talks about receiving feedback without fear, and about “feedforward”: advice for the future, not only comments about the past.

Tip: notice how he receives criticism. Write one sentence you can use when your director gives you feedback.
