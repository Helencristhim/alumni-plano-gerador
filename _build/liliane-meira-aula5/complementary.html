Lesson 5 -- From Photograph to Practice

Talk
David Epstein -- "Why specializing early does not always mean career success" (TED, 16 min)

The argument you will want the next time somebody treats your first decade as a detour: people who change direction late tend to bring something the specialists never had. Clear American English, and a useful set of phrases for talking about a career that turned.

Tip: find the two moments where he tells somebody else's story with a past perfect, and write your own version of each sentence.

Podcast
A brush with... Catherine Opie (The Art Newspaper)

An artist who has spent decades in photography and keeps moving inside it, talking about what formed her and what she left behind. It is exactly the interview you practiced today, conducted by somebody very good at asking the question you are learning to answer.

Tip: stop the episode after each of her long answers and summarize it in one sentence containing had. You will be practicing the whole lesson while you listen.

Listen on The Art Newspaper ↗
Film
Louisiana Channel -- Yu Hong: "Questions We Need to Ask Ourselves"

A short studio film in which a painter describes how her subject found her over thirty years. Louisiana Channel films are the closest thing to sitting in another artist's studio, and this one is built entirely on the two layers of past you worked with today.

Tip: watch it once without subtitles and once with. Then say, out loud, the one sentence of hers you would like to be able to say about your own work.
