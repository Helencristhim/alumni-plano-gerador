Lesson 7 -- Used to vs. Now

Podcast
NPR Planet Money -- "Episode 760: Tax Hero"

Twenty minutes on your own market, in another country: how tax filing used to work in the United States, who tried to change it, and why it did not change. Fast American English, two narrators handing the story back and forth, and a transcript on the page. Almost every sentence about the past uses the form you learned today.

Tip: listen once without reading, then read the transcript and mark five sentences with used to. Then say the equivalent five about Brazil, out loud.

Talk
Knut Haanaes -- "Two reasons companies fail, and how to avoid them" (TED, 12 min)

Exactly the argument of the second recording in class: companies do not fall behind by standing still, they fall behind by doing very well the thing that used to work. Twelve minutes, clear delivery, and one idea you will be able to repeat to a partner the same week.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down the two reasons in one sentence each, and then say which of the two your own company is closer to right now.

Series
Explained -- any episode (Netflix, 18 min each)

Every episode does the same thing in eighteen minutes: here is how this used to work, here is what changed it, here is where it is going. It is the exact shape of the three minutes you practised, done by professionals, in fast American English with strong visual support.

Tip: pick any episode and write the four movements down as you watch: how it used to be, what changed it, what is the same, what is next. Then do the same four about your market, out loud, in three minutes.
