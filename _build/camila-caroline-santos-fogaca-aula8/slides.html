Chapter 4: The Code

The Connectors of an Update

First and foremost, moving on to, last but not least

Grammar Discovery

What Does Each Connector Do?

"First and foremost, the audit is closed."

"As far as Chile is concerned, the process is finished."

"Moving on to Argentina, we have one open position."

"Last but not least, the works council meets next week."

Each expression tells the listener where you are in the update. Which one opens, which two change subject, and which one closes?

FormWhat it doesExample
first and foremostthe most important point, and only onceFirst and foremost, the audit is closed.
as far as ... is concernedmove to one specific subject or countryAs far as Chile is concerned, the process is finished.
moving on tomove to the next item on your listMoving on to Argentina, we have one open position.
last but not leastthe final item, still importantLast but not least, the works council meets next week.
Watch outusing them in the wrong order or twice. Two "first and foremost" in the same update tell the listener that you have no order at all.

Sound Check

Say It Clearly

T and D without "tchi" and "dji" (point 2 of 14) — before an i sound, the tongue stays behind the teeth

Why this one is hard for a Brazilian speaker

In Portuguese: T and D before an i sound become "tchi" and "dji" (tia, dia, vinte).

In English: The tongue stays flat behind the teeth: the T stays a T and the D stays a D, whatever vowel follows.

If it slips: "Update" becomes "updeitchi" and "digital" becomes "dijitau".

Chapter 5: Real Speed

The Notes and the Update

Two recordings from the same Friday

Listening 1

Notes, Spoken Out Loud

A colleague reads his raw notes from the week. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. How many subjects does he mention, and are they in any order?
Four subjects, and they are not in order: he moves from the audit to holidays, back to Chile and then to the works council.
2. Which piece of information is the most important, according to what he says?
That the audit is closed with no findings.
3. What does he say about the credit team?
That the workload is still high and he is worried about it, but there is no decision yet.
Listening 2

The Same Week, as an Update

The same information, organised for a manager. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. What is the headline of the update?
That the audit is closed with no findings and the quarter ends with one open position.
2. What is presented as a concern, and why?
The workload in the credit team, because the volume grew and no decision has been taken yet.
3. What is the last point, and what is asked about it?
The works council meeting next week, and the manager is asked whether headquarters should be represented.

The first line, the headline: the audit is closed and the quarter ends with one open position.
2. What is the difference between the highlight and the concern here?
The highlight is the onboarding process that went live; the concern is the workload in the credit team, which has no decision yet.

Three lines: if the update does not fit in three lines, it is not an update.
