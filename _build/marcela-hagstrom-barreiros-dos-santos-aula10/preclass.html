Teaching What I Know -- Have You Ever Taught?

At the end of your master’s, you want to teach. Key words: a lecture, a syllabus, a guest speaker, a workshop, a case study, an audience, a slide deck, to take questions. Structure: the present perfect for experience, with ever and never.

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Professor Hill invites guest speakers every semester. He asks two questions: “What have you done?” and “Have you ever taught?” Many of his best guests have never taught before.

Priya has taught three times. Her first workshop was last March. She prepared too many slides, but the case study worked very well.

She has given many presentations at work, but students are a different audience. She has never given a full course, and she hasn’t written a syllabus yet.

1. What does Professor Hill ask his guests?
A Where they studied.
B What they have done and if they have ever taught.
C How old they are.
2. When was Priya’s first workshop?
A Last March.
B Last year in December.
C She has never given a workshop.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A “I have given a workshop last year.”
B “I gave a workshop last year.”
C “I have gave a workshop last year.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Present Perfect -- Experience With Ever and Never

GRAMMAR

Use have or has + the third form of the verb for experiences in your life, with no date. Use have you ever...? to ask. When you give a date (last year, in March), use the past simple.

JobFormExample
Experience (no date)have / has + third formI’ve taught marketing. She has worked abroad.
QuestionHave you ever + third form?Have you ever given a lecture?
Neverhave never + third formI’ve never given a lecture in English.
Short answersYes, I have. / No, I haven’t.Have you ever taught online? Yes, I have.
With a datepast simpleI gave a workshop last year.
Third forms to learnregular = -ed · irregular = memorizework → worked · teach → taught · give → given · be → been

Never: I’ve teached · I have given a workshop last year · I never have given · Did you ever teach? (for experience)
