Chapter 4: The Code

Have You Ever? I’ve Never.

Experience with the present perfect

Grammar Discovery

Four Sentences About Experience

"I’ve taught growth marketing to graduate students."

"Have you ever given a lecture in English?"

"No, I’ve never given a lecture in English, but I gave a workshop in Portuguese last year."

"She has worked with companies from many countries."

Look at the red words: have or has + a verb in the third form (taught, given, worked). They talk about experience in your life, with no date. Now the blue word in sentence 3: gave, with last year. When there is a date, it is the past simple.

JobFormExample
Experience (no date)have / has + third formI’ve taught marketing. She has worked abroad.

In one line: have or has + third form for experience with no date; past simple when you say when.
