"Where Is Home?" — Pico Iyer, TED

A travel writer who has spent his life in transit on what it means to belong somewhere when you are always arriving. Connection to Lesson 10: the register is exactly what you are training — reflective, unhurried, and full of participle clauses used naturally. English transcript on the page.

Tip: this one rewards listening rather than studying. Watch it once for pleasure, then go back and find three sentences that begin with a participle.
