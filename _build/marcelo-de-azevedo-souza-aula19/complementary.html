Lesson 19 -- Booking It Yourself

Lesson theme: planning and booking the next trip yourself, and choosing the right future form (will, going to, present continuous). Extra practice outside class -- tick each one when you finish it.

Film
The Bucket List (2007) -- directed by Rob Reiner

Two men write down everything they still want to do and then go and do it. The first half of the film is nothing but future forms: what they are going to do, what they will do if there is time, and where they are flying next week. Slow, clear dialogue between two actors who never rush a line, and the perfect companion to tonight's grammar.

Tip: pause after the scene where the list is written and say five things they are going to do, then five things you are going to do.
