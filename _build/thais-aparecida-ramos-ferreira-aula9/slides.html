Chapter 3: Can and Can't

One Word, Three Jobs

I can swim · Can I take a photo? · Can you help me?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I can speak a little English."

"She can help you with a map."

"I can't understand the menu."

"Can I take a photo here?"

FormUse it forExample
I / you / she can + verbwhat you are able to doI can speak a little English.
I can't + verbwhat you are not able to doI can't understand the menu.
Can I + verb?asking if it is allowedCan I take a photo here?
Can you + verb?asking a person for helpCan you speak slowly, please?

Two things never change: no TO after can, and no S with she or he. Same word for everybody: I can, you can, she can, we can.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"I can to speak English."

✓ RIGHT

"I can speak English."

✗ WRONG

"She cans help you."

✓ RIGHT

"She can help you."

✗ WRONG

"Do you can help me?"

✓ RIGHT

"Can you help me?"

Portuguese says consigo falar with a small word in the middle. English takes it out. And can is already the question — it needs no help from do.

Chapter 4: Want and Can

I Want To, and I Can

the wish, and the permission — two different sentences

Want and Can

The Wish, and the Answer

"I want to see the paintings."

the wish — the TO stays

"Can I see the paintings today?"

the permission — the TO goes away

"I want to take a photo of my daughter."

the wish

"So: can I take photos in the museum?"

the wish first, then the question — this is how a real person talks

In Portuguese: quero VER keeps the small word, consigo ver drops it. English does exactly the same thing.

Dialogue

The Museum Window, 9:30 a.m.

M
Good morning. How can I help you today?
T
Good morning. Can I buy three tickets, please?
M
Of course. Three tickets for the museum. That is forty-five euros.
T
I'm sorry. Can you speak slowly, please? I can't understand.
M
No problem. Three tickets. Forty. Five. Euros.
T
Thank you. Now I can understand. And can we take photos inside?
M
Yes, you can take photos. But you can't use a flash.
T
Perfect. My daughter always takes photos. And the line — is it long?
M
Not now. In the morning the line is short. At two o'clock it is very long.
T
Good. One more question: can my husband pay with a credit card?
M
Yes, he can. You can pay here, or at the machine on the left.
