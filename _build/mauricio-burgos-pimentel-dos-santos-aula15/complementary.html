Dirty Money — Season 1, Episode 1: “Hard NOx”

A real business case on screen: the executives of a car company made a risky decision to cut costs, and the stakeholders paid for it. Every scene asks the question of this lesson: what would you do if you were the CEO?

Tip: stop the episode after the first decision. Say out loud: “If I were the CEO, I would ...” and back it up with one fact from the episode.
