Chapter 4: Who Does It Matters Less

What Is Done, Not Who Does It

The voice of every technical document

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The plant is operated by the city water utility."

"Two pumping stations are built on the east side."

"The flow rate is measured every hour."

"The design is approved by the utility before construction."

FormUseExample
is / are + past participlethe action matters more than who does itThe flow rate is measured every hour.
... + by + agentonly when it is important to say who does itThe plant is operated by the utility.
isn't / aren't + past participlenegativeMaintenance isn't included.
Is / Are + subject + past participlequestionIs the design approved first?

One question decides it: is the action more important than the person who does it? In a technical document it almost always is, which is why scopes, specifications and reports are full of is / are plus the past participle.

Dialogue

Questions About the Scope

K
I read the scope last night, but some parts are not clear to me. Who operates the plant today?
T
The plant is operated by the city water utility. We only design the upgrade.
K
And what exactly is included in the scope?
T
The capacity is increased to six hundred liters per second, and two pumping stations are built.
K
Where are the technical details? I could not find them.
T
All the equipment is described in Annex B. Every requirement there is mandatory.
K
Mandatory? Even the brand of the pumps?
T
No, only the specification. The brand is chosen by the contractor.
K
Good. And how is the flow rate controlled?
T
It is measured every hour, and the design is approved by the utility before anything is built.
K
Last question. Is maintenance included? The board is going to ask me that.
