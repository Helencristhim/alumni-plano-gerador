Grammar Video
"Grammar: Giving emphasis in English" -- BBC English Masterclass

A short, direct lesson on the structure you built today: how English moves one idea to the front of the sentence with it was ... that and what ... was. Connection to Lesson 6: these are the two forms on your survival card, explained by a teacher at a speed you can slow down.

Tip: pause after each example and say the same idea twice about your own week -- once with It was ... that, once with What ... was. Then say it a third time in five plain words, so you can hear what the cleft is buying you.
