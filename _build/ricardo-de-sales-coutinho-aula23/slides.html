Chapter 3: No -ED Here

Some Verbs Change

go, have, get — in the past

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I go to the bank every day."

"In 1998, I went to the bank."

"Today I have a team of twenty-five."

"In 1998, I had a great boss."

NowBeforeExample
gowentI went to university at night.
have / hashadI had a great boss.
getgotI got a promotion.
taketookI took an MBA.
makemadeI made a lot of friends.
beginbeganMy career began in 1998.

No -ED. The same word for I, you, he, she, we and they.

Now and Then

Change the Verb

"I take the subway. In 1998, I took the train."

take → took

"I get coffee at eight. In 2003, I got a job."

get → got

"I make calls every day. I made my first trade in 1999."

make → made

"The meeting begins at nine. My career began in 1998."

begin → began
