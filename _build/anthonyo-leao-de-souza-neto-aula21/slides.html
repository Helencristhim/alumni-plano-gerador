Chapter 3: Going To

What You Decided to Do

I am going to · she is going to · are you going to...?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Tomorrow I am going to rent a bike."

"We are going to take the ferry at ten."

"Claire is going to visit the museum."

"What are you going to do on Saturday?"

PersonFormExample
Iam going to + verbI'm going to rent a bike.
he / she / itis going to + verbShe's going to visit the museum.
you / we / theyare going to + verbWe're going to take the ferry.
Noam / is / are + not going toI'm not going to work on Saturday.
QuestionAre you going to...? · What are you going to do?Are you going to come? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

Am, is, are from lesson 1 + going to + the simple verb. It is a plan: you decided before.

The Sound

How Going To Sounds

"I'm going to rent a bike."

careful speech: I'm GO-ing to RENT a BIKE

"We're going to take the ferry."

fast speech: we're GONNA take the FERRY — you will HEAR gonna everywhere

"I'm not going to work on Saturday."

the NOT is strong: I'm NOT going to WORK

"What are you going to do tomorrow?"

the question that starts every plan with the people you travel with
