Chapter 4: My, His, Her

My, His, Her

Small words for people and things

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"My key is in my bag."

"His name is Tom."

"Her family is from Cuba."

"This is our apartment."

Look at the blue words. Who is it? I, he, she or we?

JobFormExample
ImyMy key is in my bag.
YouyourIs this your key?
He (a man)hisHis name is Tom.
She (a woman)herHer family is from Cuba.
WeourThis is our apartment.
TheytheirTheir pool is on the roof.

In one line: his = of a man; her = of a woman. The thing does not change the word.
