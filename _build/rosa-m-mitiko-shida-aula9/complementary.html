Talk
"The secret structure of great talks" -- Nancy Duarte, TED

Eighteen minutes on how a message is built so that people actually receive it. Connection to Lesson 9: the passive and the active are two ways of building the same sentence, and her argument is that the shape of a message decides whether it lands.

Tip: watch it once with English subtitles. Then take the last announcement you had to explain to a client and say the first thirty seconds of it twice — once as the memo wrote it, once as you would say it on the phone.

Grammar Video
"Present and past passives" -- BBC Learning English, 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on the two tenses you will meet most often in a corporate email. Connection to Lesson 9: only be changes tense, and the third form of the verb never moves — which is the whole mechanism.

Tip: pause after each example and say the same idea in the active, naming a person. Then decide out loud which of the two versions you would send to a client, and why.
