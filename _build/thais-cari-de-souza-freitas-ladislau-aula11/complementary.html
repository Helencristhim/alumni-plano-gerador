Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Tom Wujec -- "Got a wicked problem? First, tell me how you make toast" (TED)

Nine minutes on one simple exercise: draw how toast is made. Everybody draws a different process, and that is the point. It is the best short argument you will hear for writing a method down step by step, exactly what you did today with the credit decision flow.

Tip: after the talk, describe how toast is made in five steps, out loud, with the passive: the bread is taken out, it is put in the toaster...

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Mashable -- "What is a Credit Score? Kal Penn Explains"

A short, light explanation of how an American credit score is built and which variables are weighed most. You know the content better than the presenter does, so all your attention can go to the English: how a method is explained to a general audience.

Tip: pause after each factor and say how the same thing is done at your bank, starting with "At our bank, ... is calculated by...".

Watch on YouTube ↗
Radio
NPR -- "Credit Scores Even More Key In A Tight Economy"

A short radio story from the 2008 crisis on where the credit score came from and how lenders used it when credit got tight. Clear, slow enough to follow, and with the passive you practised today: the data behind the score is compiled and constantly updated.

Tip: open the transcript on the page, underline every is or are followed by a participle, and read those sentences out loud.
