Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Simon Sinek — “How great leaders inspire action” (TED)

Eighteen minutes on exactly what this lesson was about: the order in which you say things about your company. His argument is that most founders open with what they do and never reach why it matters — which is the mistake Ana Beltran took nine years to fix.

Tip: write down the three-word structure he draws on the board, then rewrite your Dataside opening sentence in that order. Say both versions out loud and choose one.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Graham Weaver — “How to Win Without Crushing Your Soul” (Stanford GSB)

A private equity founder telling his own fifteen-year story to a room of students. He uses the present perfect the way this lesson taught it, constantly and without thinking about it, and the English is unscripted at native speed.

Tip: count how many times he says I have plus a verb in the first ten minutes. Every one of those is a count he is telling you is still running.

Watch on YouTube ↗
Podcast
In Good Company — Andrea Orcel, CEO of UniCredit

A European bank CEO answering the same two questions you practised today: what the company has already done, and what it has not done yet. He is careful with dates in a way that is worth copying.

Tip: the first fifteen minutes are enough. Write down the two sentences where he names a year, and the two where he deliberately does not.
