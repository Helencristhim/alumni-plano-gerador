Talk
"What makes a good life?" -- Robert Waldinger, TED

Twelve minutes on the longest study ever run on adult health and happiness, and what it found about the second half of a life. Connection to Lesson 16: well-being here is not blood pressure, which is exactly the wider sense of the word you learned today. The page carries the full English transcript beside the video.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles and once without. Then say out loud, in two sentences, whether his finding matches what you have seen in your own family.

"Stop buying or stop to buy?" -- English Masterclass, BBC Learning English

A short lesson on the exact trap you worked on today: verbs whose meaning changes completely depending on what follows them. Connection to Lesson 16: it drills stop, regret and go on until the choice stops being a decision.

Tip: pause after every example and say your own version out loud BEFORE the presenter does. Use your own tablets, your own walk, your own screening.
