Culture, History and City Tours -- Who, Which, Where

A walking tour of the oldest city in the United States. Key words: a fort, a lighthouse, to found, a settler, a guide, a century, historic, a ghost, a shell, to preserve. Structure: relative clauses with who, which, that and where.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A fort -- a strong building made to protect a city
"There is an old fort on the hill."
A lighthouse -- a tall tower with a light that helps ships at night
"You can see the lighthouse from the beach."
To found -- to start a city, a school or a company
"The school was founded in 1920."
A settler -- a person who goes to live in a new place where few people live
"The settlers built small wooden houses."
A guide -- a person whose job is to show a place to visitors
"The guide spoke English and Spanish."
A century -- a period of one hundred years
"This church is two centuries old."
Historic -- important in history, and often very old
"We stayed in the historic center."
A ghost -- the spirit of a dead person that some people believe they see
"My son is afraid of ghosts."
A shell -- the hard outside part of some sea animals
"The children collect shells on the beach."
To preserve -- to keep something old in good condition
"It is important to preserve old buildings."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Ouro Preto is a city which is famous all over Brazil. It is in Minas Gerais, a state where gold was found in the eighteenth century.

The people who visit Ouro Preto walk up and down steep stone streets. They see churches that are full of gold, and the houses where rich families lived three centuries ago.

Aleijadinho is the artist who made many of the statues in the churches. Today, the city preserves its historic center, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Who, Which, That, Where

GRAMMAR

Use these words to give more information about a person, a thing or a place, in one sentence.

WordUse it forExample
whopeopleThe man who built the hotel was rich.
whichthingsThe fort which you see is very old.
thatpeople or thingsIt is a stone that is made of shells.
whereplaces + subject + verbThis is the street where I live.
One sentencedo not repeat he, she, it or therethe beach where I walk my dogs (not: where I walk my dogs there)

Never: the guide which told the story · the street who has no cars · the people who they climb
