Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Park website
National Park Service — Castillo de San Marcos National Monument

The official page of the fort from this lesson: its history, the cannons and the visitor information.

Tip: read the first page and write three sentences with which or that about the fort.

Open on NPS.gov ↗
Museum website
St. Augustine Lighthouse & Maritime Museum

The museum of the lighthouse from the listening: the 1874 tower, the keepers’ house and the stories of the people who lived there.

Tip: find one person or one thing on the website and describe it with who or which.

Open the museum website ↗
Article
Atlas Obscura — "The Ponce de Leon Hotel"

A short article about the luxury hotel which Henry Flagler opened in 1888, and which is now Flagler College.

Tip: read it, then tell a friend about the building in three sentences with who, which and where.
