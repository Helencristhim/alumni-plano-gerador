Lesson 21 -- Pre-class

Expressing Opinions with Confidence

Say what you think and let people hear how sure you are: strong opinions with "I am convinced (that)...", softer ones with "I would say" and "it depends", and polite disagreement with "I am not so sure" and "That is a fair point, but...". Key words: convinced, doubtful, strongly, admit, overrated, fair point, it depends, assumption.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Convinced -- Completely sure that something is true
"I'm convinced that this is the right call."
Doubtful -- Not sure about something; thinking it is probably not true
"I'm doubtful about that plan."
Strongly -- With a lot of force or certainty
"I strongly believe we should ask the team first."
Admit -- To accept that something is true, even when it is hard
"I have to admit you were right."
Overrated -- Praised more than it deserves
"Open offices are overrated, in my opinion."
Fair point -- A reasonable thing the other person said
"That's a fair point, but I still disagree."
It depends -- The answer changes with the situation
"It depends on the client and the deadline."
Assumption -- Something you believe without proof
"That's an assumption, not a fact."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

In yesterday's meeting, Rafael said that open offices make teams more creative. I am convinced that this idea is overrated. I strongly believe that most people need quiet to think. Marina disagreed, and honestly, she made a fair point: brainstorming really is better in a room full of noise. So it depends on the task. I am still doubtful about the idea that noise equals energy — for me, that is an assumption, not a fact. But I have to admit one thing: I never asked the new people what they prefer. I would say we should ask them before we decide.

1. How sure is she that the idea is overrated?
A She has no opinion about it yet.
B She is convinced — she has no doubt about it.
C She agrees completely with Rafael.
2. What fair point did Marina make?
A That brainstorming is better in a room full of noise.
B That the office is too far from her home.
C That nobody in the team needs quiet.
3. Which sentence gives a SOFTER opinion?
A "I am convinced that this idea is overrated."
B "I would say we should ask them before we decide."
C "That is an assumption, not a fact."

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- How sure are you?

GRAMMAR

The same opinion sounds very different depending on how much weight you put behind it.

StructureUseExample
I'm convinced (that) + sentenceStrong: you have no doubtI'm convinced that this is the right call.
I'd say / I think + sentenceNeutral: easy to soften or change laterI'd say it depends on the client.
I'm not so sure / I'm doubtful aboutPolite disagreementI'm not so sure about that.
That's a fair point, but...Agree with one part, then add your viewThat's a fair point, but the numbers say something else.

'Agree' is a verb: say 'I agree', never 'I am agree'. After 'I am convinced', use that plus a full sentence, not 'of that'. And with it / he / she, the verb takes the -s: it depends.
