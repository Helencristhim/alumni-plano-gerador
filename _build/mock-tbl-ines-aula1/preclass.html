Lesson 01 — Before class (priming)

The Clause That Broke

Exposure, not instruction. Meet the six negotiating moves and the text before class — in class you will not practise them, you will use them under pressure.

Priming 1: Eight moves

Exposure

Read and listen. Do not memorise — you only need to recognise them when you reach for one.

I'd want to understand what sits behind that number. — probes without accusing
That's workable in principle, provided that... — conditional acceptance -- yes, with a price
Let me push back on one thing. — signals disagreement before making it
Where does that leave my client if...? — tests the downside out loud
I can live with the number if we fix the definition. — trades a concession for what you want
Can we park that and come back to it? — defers without conceding
Help me understand the rationale. — asks for reasoning, not position
On that basis, yes. — closes, and marks what the yes depends on

Priming 2: Which move is it?

Quiz
The other side gives you a number with no reasoning. What do you say first?
A I'd want to understand what sits behind that number.
B That number does not work for us.
You want to accept the price but fix the definition of profit. Which move?
A Can we park that and come back to it?
B I can live with the number if we fix the definition.
You need time on a point without signalling agreement. Which move?
A Can we park that and come back to it?
B On that basis, yes.

Priming 4: Say them once

Speaking

At C1 the words are not the problem — the tone is. Record and listen back: does it sound firm without sounding hostile?

Let me push back on one thing.
That's workable in principle, provided that we fix the definition.
Where does that leave my client if the buyer opens a new market?
I can live with the number if we fix the definition.

Priming 5: Bring a real one

Reflection
Think of a negotiation in English where you conceded something you would not have conceded in Portuguese. Record 40 seconds: what was on the table, and where the English ran out.
