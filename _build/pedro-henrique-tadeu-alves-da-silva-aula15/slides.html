Listening 1 · The Night It Went Wrong

A Small Disaster

A vocal coach remembers one live broadcast. Sound first — no text.

1. What happened an hour before the live broadcast?
The lead singer lost her voice, and the producer asked the speaker, a backing singer, to sing her part.
2. Why was that night a setback?
She forgot a few words in the second verse, and the next day the show was criticized in two newspapers.
3. What did the songwriter tell her, and what happened next?
She told her that her voice sounded honest and asked her to sing on her demo. That demo was her stepping stone.
Listening 2 · Too Comfortable

Clean Is Not Enough

A sound editor and the sentence that changed his career. Sound first — no text.

1. What did the director tell him about his work?
He told him that his work was clean, but that it had no personality.
2. How was he different from the younger students in the first month?
He was much slower and hardly understood their jokes, but he had a lot of experience, and very few of them did.
3. What does he say about being comfortable for too long?
It is a sign: it is probably time to get out of your comfort zone.
