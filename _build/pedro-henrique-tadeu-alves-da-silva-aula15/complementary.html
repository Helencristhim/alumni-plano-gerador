Twenty Feet from Stardom (2013) -- Official Trailer

An Oscar-winning documentary about backing singers: people who sang on the biggest records in history, and the turning points that took a few of them to the front of the stage. For somebody who works on The Voice, every story will sound familiar.

Tip: after the trailer, tell one singer’s story in three sentences: one with the past continuous, one with reported speech (she said that...), and one with a turning point.

Tip: pause three times and report what she said, one step back in time: “She said that she was...”.

The Power of Believing That You Can Improve -- Carol Dweck

Carol Dweck explains the power of “not yet”: a problem is not a failure, only a step you have not finished. A good talk to watch at a checkpoint, when you look back on how far you have come.

Tip: write two sentences about your English: one with “I used to...” and one with “I have come a long way since...”.
