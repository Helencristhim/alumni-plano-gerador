How to use the present perfect continuous tense -- 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on exactly the structure you practiced tonight: have or has, then been, then the -ing verb, with for and since carrying the duration. Two BBC presenters build the sentences live, at natural speed, and drill them with quick examples.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause after each example and say one sentence about your own program: "We have been piloting..."

Present perfect simple or continuous? -- Ask BBC Learning English

A short answer to the one decision you will make in every status update: the score or the clock. A teacher explains when the result and the number take the simple form, and when the activity and its duration take the continuous.

Tip: after watching, take three sentences from your own work and say each one twice -- once as a number, once as a duration.

Angela Lee Duckworth: Grit -- the power of passion and perseverance

Six minutes on why sustained effort over years predicts success better than talent does. It is the natural context for tonight's language: everything she describes is work that has been running for a long time and has not finished yet.
