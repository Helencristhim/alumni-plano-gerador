Lesson 03 -- Beyond Chicken or Beef

Documentary
Julia Child’s Kitchen Wisdom -- full documentary (54 min)

A woman who moved abroad at thirty-six, could not speak the language, could not read a menu, and learned the whole thing through food. Her actual kitchen is on display at the Smithsonian, a short ride from your apartment.

Tip: watch the first twenty minutes with no subtitles and write down every word about flavor you hear -- savory, mild, herb, seasoning. Then watch it again with subtitles.

Podcast
Gastropod -- How Chilis Conquered the World

Why one small fruit ended up in the seasoning of almost every kitchen on the planet, and what actually happens in your mouth when a dish is not mild at all. Two American hosts, relaxed speed, a lot of food vocabulary.

Tip: listen once while you walk the dog, with nothing in your hands. Then listen again and count how many times you hear a word from this lesson.

Talk
The Beginner’s Guide to Cooking with Spices -- Ethan Chlebowski

Every jar on the shelf at the supermarket, named out loud, one by one: what each spice tastes like, which herbs go with which food, and why a dish tastes flat when the seasoning is wrong.

Tip: keep a pen next to you and write the name of every spice he says. Take that list to the store this week and read the labels.
