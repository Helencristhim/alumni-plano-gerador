Chapter 4: The Code

Do It, Don’t Do It

How every instruction starts

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"Open the VPN app."

"Click Settings."

"Don’t change the port number."

"Don’t close the window."

Look at the blue words. Where is the person (I, you, he)? How does the sentence start?

JobFormExample
Do itverb + ...Restart the computer.
Don’t do itdon’t + verb + ...Don’t turn off the firewall.
Politeplease + verbPlease send me a screenshot.
Order of stepsfirst, then, after thatFirst, check the cable. Then open the app.
The trapno “you”, never “no”Not No close the window. Say Don’t close the window.

In one line: an instruction starts with the verb. To stop an action: don’t + verb.
