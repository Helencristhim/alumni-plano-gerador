Lesson 15 -- Pre-class

Have You Ever...?

The experiences of a lifetime: what you have done, and what is still on the list. Key words: bucket list, once-in-a-lifetime, highlight, adventurous, to give it a try, to cross off, to regret, experience. Structure: present perfect for experiences -- have you ever been to Croatia? I have never tried octopus.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Bucket list -- a list of things you want to do before you die
"Seeing the northern lights is on my bucket list."
Once-in-a-lifetime -- so special that it happens only one time in your life
"The night swim in Dubrovnik was a once-in-a-lifetime experience."
Highlight -- the best or most exciting part of a trip or an event
"The highlight of the trip was dinner with a local family."
Adventurous -- happy to try new and exciting things
"I am more adventurous with food than with sports."
To give it a try -- to try something new, to see if you like it
"I have never eaten octopus, but I will give it a try."
To cross off -- to take something off a list because you did it
"I walked the city walls, so I crossed it off my list."
To regret -- to feel sorry about something you did, or did not do
"I regret that I never learned to swim well."
Experience -- something that happens to you and stays with you
"Living in Lisbon for a month was a great experience."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Elena works at a small guesthouse in Dubrovnik, and she loves asking her guests one question: "Have you ever swum in the sea at night?" Most guests say no. Elena has done it many times. She did it for the first time when she was sixteen, with her brother.

Her guests have been to many places. One man from Sao Paulo has seen Rome, Venice and Florence, but he has never been to Croatia before this trip. Last night he finally gave it a try. This morning he said: "It was the highlight of the whole trip."

1. "Elena has done it many times." Does the sentence say WHEN she did it?
A No. It is an experience in her life, with no date.
B Yes. It says she did it last night, with the man from Sao Paulo and his family.
C Yes, this morning.
2. Why does the text say "she did it for the first time when she was sixteen"?
A Because "did" is the polite form of "has done" in a story.
B Because it says WHEN, so it is the past simple.
C Because the present perfect is only for questions with ever, never for sentences about a person.
3. What is true about the man from Sao Paulo?
A He has been to Croatia many times, and he swims in the sea every night.
B He has never been to Italy.
C This trip is his first time in Croatia.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Present Perfect for Experiences

GRAMMAR

The present perfect talks about an experience at some time in your life. It does not say when.

FormRuleExample
have / has + past participlean experience, no dateI have been to Italy. · She has tried octopus.
QuestionHave / Has + person + ever + participleHave you ever seen the northern lights?
Negativehaven't / hasn't, or neverI haven't tried it. · He has never been to Japan.
Short answersYes, I have. · No, I haven't. Never "Yes, I have ever".
Participlesbe → been · see → seen · eat → eaten · swim → swum · take → taken · try → tried
The trapWith a date, use the past simple: I went to Lisbon in 2019 — never I have been to Lisbon in 2019.

A natural conversation uses both: "Have you ever been to Portugal?" "Yes, I have. I went there in 2019."

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

You are talking about experiences on a trip. Choose the best thing to say.

At dinner, a traveler asks: "Have you ever been to Japan?" You never went. You say:
A "No, I haven't, but it is on my bucket list."
B "No, I have ever been there, but maybe one day in the future."
C "No, I didn't go."
Someone asks about Lisbon. You were there in 2019. You say:
A "Yes, I have been there in 2019, in the summer, with two friends from work."
B "Yes, I go there in 2019."
C "Yes, I have. I went there in 2019."
A waiter recommends a fish you have never eaten. You want to try it. You say:
A "I regret it."
B "I have never tried it, but I will give it a try."
C "I have already crossed it off my list, so I will order the same fish again tonight."
A friend asks about the best part of your trip to Croatia. You say:
A "The bucket list was a night swim under the walls of the old town in Dubrovnik."
B "The experience was adventurous."
C "The highlight was a night swim under the walls."

Stage 5: Free Production

Reflection

Record yourself answering the question below. There is no right or wrong answer.

What is on your bucket list? Take about ninety seconds. Say three things you have already done in your life (and, for one of them, when you did it), and two things you have never done but want to do. Use at least three words from this lesson, and end with "What about you?" Do not stop to correct yourself.
