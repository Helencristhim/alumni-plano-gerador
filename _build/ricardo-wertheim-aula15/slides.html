Chapter 3: The Code

Your Life, No Date

have been · have tried · have never seen

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Orange Words Have in Common?

"I have been to Italy many times."

"Have you ever swum in the sea at night?"

"She has never seen the northern lights."

"I have tried black risotto. I tried it on Tuesday, in Dubrovnik."

What do the orange words have in common? Do they say WHEN? And in the last example, why does the second sentence change?

FormRuleExample
have / has + past participleAn experience at some time in your life. No date.I have tried black risotto. · She has been to Japan.
ever / neverever = at any time in your life (questions). never = at no time.Have you ever been to Norway? · I have never seen snow.
Short answersYes, I have. · No, I haven't. Never "Yes, I have ever".
Past participlesbe → been · see → seen · eat → eaten · swim → swum · take → taken · ride → ridden · try → tried
been toFor places, say been to: I have been to Croatia = I went and came back.
The date changes itWhen you say WHEN, go back to the past simple: Yes, I have. I went there in 2019. Never I have been there in 2019.

First the experience, with no date. Then the story, with the past simple.

Chapter 4: The Last Night in Dubrovnik

One More Thing for the List

A guesthouse, a last evening and a very good question

Dialogue

The Last Evening

E
So, this is your last night in Dubrovnik. Have you ever been to Croatia before?
R
No, never. It is my first time. But I have been to Italy many times.
E
And have you crossed everything off your list here?
R
Almost. I have walked the city walls, and I have tried the black risotto. That was the highlight of the week.
E
Have you ever swum in the sea at night? Locals say it is a once-in-a-lifetime experience.
R
No, I haven't. I am not very adventurous after dark!
E
You should give it a try. I did it last summer, and the water was so warm.
R
All right. I don't want to regret it when I am back in Sao Paulo.
E
Then tonight, at ten. I will show you the little beach under the walls.
Comprehension

About Elena

1. Has Elena ever swum in the sea at night?
Yes, she has. She did it last summer, and the water was very warm.
2. What do locals say about swimming at night, according to Elena?
They say it is a once-in-a-lifetime experience.
3. What does Elena offer to do, and when?
She offers to show him the little beach under the walls, tonight at ten.
