Grammar Video
"Grammar Andy: Present Perfect" -- American English (U.S. Department of State)

A short, clear lesson on the present perfect, built around a game where people say what they have and have never done. Connection to Lesson 15: it is the same game as the wine tasting role-play, with "Have you ever...?" and "I have never...".

Tip: every time the video gives an example, pause and say a true sentence about your own life with "I have..." or "I have never...".

Podcast
"Should we have a bucket list?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters discuss if a bucket list helps you live better, or makes life less spontaneous. Connection to Lesson 15: it is the key word of the class, and the question from Listening 1, the man with the notebook at seventy.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then give your own opinion in three sentences: are you for or against a bucket list, and why?

"Dubrovnik and Balkan Side Trips" -- Rick Steves' Europe, full episode

Rick Steves walks the walls of Dubrovnik and then travels to Mostar and the Bay of Kotor. Connection to Lesson 15: it is the city of the dialogue, and a list of places you can check against your own.
