Chapter 3: Like and -ing

One Ending, and You Can Talk About Anything

like + verb-ing

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I like walking in the park."

"My son likes swimming."

"My daughter likes drawing."

"I don't like waiting in queues."

PatternWhere the S goesExample
I like + verb-ingno S anywhereI like walking in the park.
He / she likes + verb-ingthe S is in LIKES onlyMy son likes swimming.
I don't like + verb-ingwhat you avoidI don't like waiting in queues.
He doesn't like + verb-ingthe S is already in DOESN'THe doesn't like waiting.

The second verb ALWAYS ends in -ing and NEVER takes an S. The S lives in likes, and only there — the same rule as lesson 13, one step further.

Dialogue

Nobody Is Solving Anything

S
They get on well, don't they? Mine never stops.
P
Mine too! My son likes running everywhere.
S
How old is he?
P
He is eight. And my daughter is six — she likes drawing more than running.
S
Ah, a quiet one. Mine would draw on the walls if I let him.
P
Sorry, I don't understand. Can you say that again, more slowly?
S
Sorry. My son draws on the walls of the house. Not on paper.
P
Oh! Mine too, when he was four. Do they come to this park often?
S
Every day after school. There is swimming on Wednesday, if yours likes water.
P
He does. He likes swimming a lot. Is it far?
S
Two minutes, next door. It is fun, and it is free.
P
That is good to know. And you? What do you like doing, when they are playing?
S
Honestly? Nothing. I sit here and I do nothing, and it is the best part of my day.
Comprehension

What Did Sean Say?

1. What does Sean say his son does with the walls?
He draws on the walls of the house, not on paper.
2. What is on Wednesday, and where?
Swimming, at the pool next door, two minutes away — and it is free.
3. What does Sean like doing while the children play?
Nothing at all — he sits there, and he says it is the best part of his day.

Twenty Minutes, and Nothing to Solve

Scenario: The children are playing and you are on the bench with a stranger. No problem, no question, no task. Just talk, and keep it going.

No keywords this time. Two minutes, no stopping. Different from the model is not wrong — if they understood you, it worked.
