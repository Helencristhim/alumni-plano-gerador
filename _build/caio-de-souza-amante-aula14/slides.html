The Core

The Investor Call

I am the investor. I speak at natural speed, with signals. You take notes in short chunks, and you check before you answer.

  • Round 1: the call. One minute, fast. Then you say what you understood: “If I understood correctly…”
  • Round 2: the cut. I stop after a signal; you say what comes next.
  • Round 3: swap. You are the investor, with three signals.
