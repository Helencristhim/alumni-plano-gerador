Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Coaching for Leaders, Episode 656: “How to Understand People Better” (with Heather Younger)

A conversation about listening to understand, not listening to answer. Two American speakers at natural speed, with many of the signals from this lesson. It is good practice for your ear and useful for your meetings.

Tip: about forty minutes. Do not try to catch every word. Write down three signposts you hear, and what came after each one.

Listen on Coaching for Leaders ↗
Talk
Julian Treasure: “5 Ways to Listen Better”

A short talk about why we are losing our listening, and five simple exercises to get it back. Clear British English, and a good contrast with the American voices of this lesson.

Tip: eight minutes. Watch once without subtitles, then once with English subtitles, and compare what you understood.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Rachel’s English: “Fast English: The #1 Secret Is Reductions”

An American teacher shows how gonna, wanna and other reductions really sound in fast conversation. You do not need to speak like this, but you need to hear it.

Tip: watch the examples twice. Every time you hear a reduction, say the full form out loud: going to, want to, kind of.
