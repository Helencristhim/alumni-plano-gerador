Chapter 3: Me, Him, Her

The People After the Verb

he → him · she → her · they → them

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"That is Paul. Do you know him?"

"I met her in Lisbon."

"Can you introduce me to them?"

"They invited us to dinner."

Before the verbAfter the verbExample
ImeShe called me.
hehimI know him.
sheherI met her in Rome.
weusThey invited us.
theythemIntroduce me to them.
you / itYou and it do NOT change: I see you. I like it.

After a verb (know, meet, call) or after to, with, for: me, him, her, us, them.

The Sound

Him, Her and Them Are Small

"Do you know him?"

fast, the H almost disappears: "do you KNOW-im?"

"I met her in Lisbon."

her is weak: "I MET-er in LISBON"

"Tell them my name."

them is weak: "TELL-em my NAME"

"Can you introduce me to them?"

the question that opens every table

Dialogue

Can You Introduce Me?

C
Leão! You are at our table. Come, sit with us.
L
Thank you. Claire, the man in the blue shirt... I remember him, but I don't remember his name.
C
That is Paul, from Chicago. You met him in Rome last year.
L
Of course! Paul. I didn't recognize him with the beard.
C
And the couple at the end of the table are the Millers. Do you know them?
L
No, I don't. Can you introduce me to them?
C
Sure. And my sister Nina is here too. I want to introduce her to you.
L
Great. Is she the woman with the red dress? She waved at us.
C
Yes, that is her. Oh, and they all call me Clara here. It is my nickname from school.
L
Clara? Then you can call me Leão. Nobody calls me Anthonyo.
C
Leão is perfect. Come, the Millers are waiting for us.
L
Let's go. But first, give me two minutes with Paul.
