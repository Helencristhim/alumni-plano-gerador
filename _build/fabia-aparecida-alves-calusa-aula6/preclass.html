Stage 1: The same six words

Review

Nothing new here. Read each one and say it out loud. If it comes back on its own, that word is yours.

Audit trail — the record of who changed what, and when
"Every change leaves an audit trail."
Breach — when data gets out and it should not have
"In a breach, we must notify you within 24 hours."
Regulatory — required by the law, not by the company
"That is not our policy, it is regulatory."
Confidentiality — the promise that you do not share what you see
"Everyone must sign a confidentiality agreement."
Notify — to tell someone officially, on the record
"We must notify you within twenty-four hours."
Read-only access — they can look at it, they cannot change it
"We can give your auditors read-only access."

Stage 3: Law, advice or ability?

Grammar

The rule, one line: must = the law · should = we recommend · can = we are able to. The verb after a modal never changes: must notify, never "must to notify".

"We ______ notify you within 24 hours. That is regulatory."
A must
B should
"You ______ keep a copy on your side too — not obligatory, just sensible."
A must
B should
Which line is correct?
A We must to keep the audit trail for five years.
B We must keep the audit trail for five years.
The client asks if the auditors can check it themselves. Which one?
A Yes, we can give them read-only access.
B Yes, they must give us read-only access.
