Documentary
American Factory

A company that used to work one way and has to change — told by the people living the transition, not by a narrator. Every interview is somebody saying what things used to be like, which is precisely the grammar of this lesson.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down five sentences with "used to". Notice that nobody adds "but not anymore" — the form already says it.
