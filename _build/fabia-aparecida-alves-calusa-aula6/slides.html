The Pattern

Which one is no longer true?

We used to do everything by hand.
We replaced the system in 2018.
I used to work only in bookkeeping.
I moved to back office in 2016.
Clients used to come to the office.
The pandemic ended that in one week.

used to = a habit or a situation that has ENDED

The left column says "and we do not do that anymore" without you having to add it. The right column is one finished event, with a date — that is past simple. A good story alternates the two.

The Pattern

Used to, in three shapes

+ We used to do everything by hand.
− We did not use to work with foreign clients. (no d after did not)
? Did you use to handle payroll internally? (a very useful discovery question)

The shape of a good story:
used to → how it was · past simple + date → what changed · present → how it is now.
"We used to do it by hand. We replaced the system in 2018. Now we run payroll for two hundred companies."

Careful: used to only talks about the past. For a habit today, use the present simple.

Listening 1

How the firm got here

1. What did the firm use to do that it does not do now?
Everything on paper, and only for Brazilian clients.
2. What single event changed the direction?
Their first foreign client, in 2015, who needed reports in English.
3. Why does she tell the story at all?
Because a prospect wants to know the firm has changed before — and survived it.
Listening 2

What the client used to do

1. Who used to run payroll in his company?
One person, who has now retired — and nobody replaced her.
2. What happened every December?
She worked weekends, and he says nobody ever questioned it.
3. What is he really asking for, without asking?
That it never depends on one person again.
