The Pattern Again

Which one is the law?

In a breach, we must notify you within 24 hours.
You should keep a copy of the register on your side too.
We can give your auditors read-only access.
Could you send us the file by Friday?

must = no choice · should = we recommend · can = we are able to · could = asking politely

The verb after a modal never changes: must notify, never "must to notify" and never "must notifies".
