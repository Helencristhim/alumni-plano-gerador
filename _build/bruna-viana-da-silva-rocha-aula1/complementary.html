Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Documentary series
Our Great National Parks — Episode 1, "A World Of Wonder"

Barack Obama narrates five hours of national parks around the world, and the first episode goes from Africa to the Great Barrier Reef. Slow, clear American English over the kind of scenery you drove seventeen days to see.

Tip: watch the first ten minutes twice. The first time, just listen. The second time, write down every sentence where the narrator says what something does every year — that is the same bare verb you practised in this lesson.

Podcast
NPR Life Kit — "Secrets to a successful couple's trip" (21 min)

Two American hosts and their guests talking about travelling with the person you live with: who plans, who decides, and what happens when one of you wants the museum and the other wants the road. Real conversational speed, and the full transcript is on the same page.

Tip: listen once with the transcript closed. Then open it and find three sentences where somebody tells a story about one finished trip. Read those three out loud at the same speed as the speaker.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
Accurate English — "The Rhythm for English Speaking (How Americans Really Speak)"

The exact thing you said you wanted: not the English of the book, but the rhythm an American actually uses. Which words get the beat, which ones almost disappear, and why a sentence you understand on paper can go past you on the street.

Tip: pick one sentence from the video and say it along with her four times, until your mouth moves at her speed. One minute, four times — that is the whole method.
