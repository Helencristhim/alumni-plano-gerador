Talk
"Try something new for 30 days" -- Matt Cutts, TED

Three minutes, and almost all of it in the past simple: he tells you what he did for thirty days and what happened. Connection to Lesson 2: it is the shortest possible model of the thing you practised today -- one small story, told in finished verbs, with a surprise at the end.

Tip: it lasts three minutes, so watch it twice. The second time, write down every past-tense verb you hear. There are more than twenty and none of them are difficult.

Podcast
"Do you need to declutter your home?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes about the thing everybody says they will do at the weekend and nobody does. Connection to Lesson 2: the presenters keep moving between what they usually do and what they actually did, which is the exact choice you made all through today's class. Audio, vocabulary and full transcript are free on the page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then open it and underline every verb in the past. Notice how many of them are irregular and how ordinary they are.

Grammar Video
"The past simple tense" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on exactly today's structure: regular verbs with -ed, the irregular ones you cannot avoid, and the negative and the question with did. Connection to Lesson 2: it drills the one mistake that costs you most -- did not went -- until it stops sounding right.

Tip: pause after each example and say one true sentence about your own last weekend. Then say the negative version of the same sentence out loud.
