The author of The First 90 Days on the questions a new leader has to answer before acting. Connection to Lesson 18: his questions are the diagnosis behind any recommendation you put forward to a board.

A short class on recommend that, suggest that and it is essential that, with the base form after each one. Connection to Lesson 18: this is the structure of every recommendation in a board paper.
