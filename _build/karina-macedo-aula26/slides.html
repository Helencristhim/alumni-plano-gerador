Chapter 4: The Code

You should... · You shouldn't...

give advice about customs

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"You should arrive on time."

"You should give your card with two hands."

"You shouldn't write on a business card."

"Should I bring a gift?" -- "Yes, you should."

FormUseExample
should + verba good idea, adviceYou should be punctual.
shouldn't (should not) + verbnot a good ideaYou shouldn't be late.
Should I / we + verb...?ask for adviceShould I bring a gift?
same form for I, you, he, she, we, theyno -s with he / sheShe should accept the coffee.

After should: the verb with NO to (should arrive). / Depois de should, o verbo vem sem to.

Traveler: Karina Macedo

Chapter 5: Practice

Culture in Action

Dialogue · Listening · Detective · Speak · Build

Dialogue

A Call with Kenji

K
Hello, Karina. Thank you for the call. Our meeting in Tokyo is next month. Are you ready?
K
Almost! Can I ask you a question? How do you greet clients in Japan?
K
We usually bow. But a handshake is OK with foreign clients, too.
K
Great. Should I bring a gift?
K
Yes, a small gift is a nice custom. You shouldn't give an expensive gift.
K
OK. And what time should I arrive?
K
You should arrive ten minutes early. In Japan, people are very punctual.
K
That's interesting. In my country, meetings sometimes start a little late.
K
Really? How do you do business in Brazil?
K
In my country, people are very warm. We usually talk about family first, and then about business.
Did You Catch It?

About the Conversation

1. How do people greet clients in Japan, according to Kenji?
They usually bow. A handshake is OK with foreign clients, too.
2. What kind of gift shouldn't Karina give?
An expensive gift.
3. What time should Karina arrive, according to Kenji?
Ten minutes early.
