Chapter 3 -- The Code

So Good That They Remember

Adding impact to your proof

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Purple Words Have in Common?

"It was such a complex deal that we needed six months."

"The market is so small that everyone knows everyone."

"We had so many creditors that we used two data rooms."

"It was such good news that the client called us at midnight."

What comes after "so"? What comes after "such"? And what comes after "that"?

FormExample (+ that + result)
so + adjective / adverb"The market is so small that everyone knows everyone."
so many + countable noun / so much + uncountable noun"We had so many creditors that we used two data rooms."
such a / an + adjective + singular noun"It was such a complex deal that we needed six months."
such + adjective + plural or uncountable noun"It was such good news that the client called us at midnight."
NeverNot "so a complex deal" / "such complex deal" / "so much creditors"
Grammar Rule

A Fact or a Story?

Neutral (a fact)With impact (a fact + its result)
"The deal was complex.""It was such a complex deal that we negotiated with forty creditors."
"The market is small.""The market is so small that everyone knows everyone."
"We had a lot of cases last year.""We had so many cases that we hired three new analysts."
"The client was happy.""The client was so happy that he hired us again."
The investor hears a fact.The investor remembers a story.
Grammar Practice

So or Such?

Say it first, then click to check

"It was ___such (so / such) a long negotiation that we lost two weekends."
"The process was ___so (so / such) fast that the client was surprised."
"We had ___so many (so many / so much) creditors that the meeting took all day."
"It was such ___a (a / -) difficult case that it went to court."
"Brazilian restructurings are ___so (so / such) complex that few banks do them well."
"We received ___such (such / so) good feedback that the client hired us again."
Chapter 4 -- Getting There

The Coffee Break

One investor, two recordings, one program

Dialogue

Eduarda and Nathan

N
Great keynote, wasn't it? I'm Nathan Cole, from Bluewater Capital.
E
It was. Nice to meet you, Nathan. I'm Eduarda Gabriel, from Houlihan Lokey in Sao Paulo.
N
So what do you do at Houlihan Lokey?
E
I help Brazilian companies survive their worst year. I specialize in restructurings, mostly mid-market companies.
N
Interesting. Is that a big market in Brazil?
E
It's a niche, but it's growing. Last year we had so many cases that we hired three new analysts.
N
What kind of cases?
E
My last deal was such a complex case that we negotiated with forty creditors. And we closed it in six months.
N
That's impressive. Are you speaking here?
E
Yes, at the breakout session on Latin America this afternoon. What is Bluewater looking at in Brazil?
