Lesson 31 -- Conference Introduction and Elevator Pitch

Film
The Pursuit of Happyness (2006)

A salesman without money or contacts fights for a place in a stockbroker training program. Connection to Lesson 31: in the famous interview scene, he has a few seconds to make the managers remember him. It is a pitch with a hook.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. In the interview scene, pause: what is his hook, and why do the managers remember him?

Talk
"How great leaders inspire action" -- Simon Sinek, TED

A famous talk about why people remember the why, not the what. Connection to Lesson 31: your hook is your why. "I help companies survive their worst year" is more memorable than a job title.

Tip: listen once without subtitles. Then write your hook in one sentence: the problem that you solve, not your title.
