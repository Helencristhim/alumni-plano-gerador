Documentary
"Remaking the Middle East: The U.S., Israel & Iran" -- FRONTLINE, PBS

A two-hour FRONTLINE documentary on how the region got where it is, free on the official PBS channel. Connection to Lesson 9: it is the long version of every twenty-second item you hear on the radio, and it gives you the background that makes the short reports listenable.

Tip: do not watch it in one sitting. Take fifteen minutes at a time, with English subtitles on, and write down the names of the people and the places before you write anything else. That is the skeleton exercise from class.

Fifty minutes of American English on the man who put the question of Palestine into the American mainstream, and on how a story gets told. Connection to Lesson 9: history and current affairs in the same episode, which is exactly the combination you said you wanted. The full transcript sits under the player.

Grammar Video
"Future perfect, or future perfect continuous?" -- Ask BBC Learning English

A short, direct answer to the one question today's grammar leaves open: when the prediction is about a finished event and when it is about a duration. Connection to Lesson 9: it is the difference between they will have signed and they will have been talking, which is the difference on your survival card.

Tip: pause after each example and make the same prediction about a story you are following, once with will have and once with will have been. Say the date out loud both times.
