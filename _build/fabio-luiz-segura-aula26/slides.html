Chapter 4: Same Verb, Two Meanings

Remember Meeting, Remember to Send

Verbs that change meaning with -ing or to

Grammar Discovery

Same Verb, Two Meanings

"I remember meeting her at the startup area."

"Please remember to send the pilot results by Friday."

"We stopped testing the old format in June."

"On the way to the airport, she stopped to answer my message."

In which sentences did the action happen before? In which is it a task, or a purpose?

UseFormExample
A memory of the pastremember / forget + verb-ingI remember meeting her. · I’ll never forget seeing that demo.
A task you must not forgetremember / forget + to + verbRemember to send the deck. · I forgot to reply.
End an activitystop + verb-ingWe stopped testing the old format.
Pause in order to do somethingstop + to + verbShe stopped to answer my message.
An experiment vs an efforttry + verb-ing · try + to + verbTry calling her. · I tried to reach her three times.

In one line: with remember, forget, stop and try, -ing looks at an action that happens or happened; to looks at a task or a purpose ahead.

Chapter 5: Real Speed

Two Voices After the Event

A business developer, and a voicemail from a fintech founder

Listening 1 · The Rule

Seventy-Two Hours

A business development lead explains what happens after a conference. Sound first — no text.

1. According to him, when are conference deals really lost?
Not at the event, but in the three weeks after it.
2. What does he do on the plane home, and how long are his messages?
He makes a list: every person, one next step and a date. His messages are short: three lines and one clear question.
3. What does he do if somebody does not answer?
He does not give up. He tries sending something useful, like an article or a warm introduction.
Listening 2 · The Voicemail

A Message from Priya

A fintech founder follows up after Sibos. Sound first — no text.

1. Who is Priya Nair, and where did she meet Fabio?
She is from Clearline, a fintech. They met in the startup area at Sibos.
2. What does she remember, and what has she just done?
She remembers talking to Fabio about real-time settlement for trade finance. She has just emailed him the pilot results she promised.
3. What does she suggest, and why?
A short call with his innovation team in the next two weeks, because things can easily fall through the cracks after a big event.
