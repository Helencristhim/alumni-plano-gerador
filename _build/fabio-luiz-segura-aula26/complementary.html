Frances Frei — “How to build (and rebuild) trust” (TED)

A business school professor explains the three parts of trust: authenticity, logic and empathy. After a conference, every follow-up is a small test of trust.

Tip: choose one of her three parts of trust. Check your next follow-up email against it.

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart (Stanford GSB) — “Talk Less, Say More: How to Kick the Habit of Over-Talking”

An episode about saying more with fewer words. Exactly the rule for a follow-up: three lines and one clear question.

Tip: after listening, cut your next follow-up email in half. Keep only one question.

Video
Adam Grant — “Are you a giver or a taker?” (TED, YouTube)

A psychologist explains why people who help others first often build the strongest professional networks. A good idea for re-engagement: send something useful before you ask.

Tip: think of one useful thing you could send to a dormant lead this week.
