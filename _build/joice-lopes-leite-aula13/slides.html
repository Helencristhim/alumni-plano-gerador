Chapter 3: The Code

Two Places, One Shape

a clear screen · the screen is clear

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"It is a very useful platform."

"The first screen is really clear."

"The report page is difficult."

"They are interesting sessions."

PositionPatternExample
before the nounadjective + nouna useful platform · an important question
after to beis / are + adjectiveThe screen is clear. The videos are simple.
strongervery / really + adjectiveIt is very useful. It is really difficult.
no pluralthe adjective NEVER changesinteresting sessions -- never interestings
Key ideaIn Portuguese the adjective comes AFTER the noun and agrees with it. In English it comes BEFORE and never changes.

Very and really mean the same here. Really sounds a little more spoken.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"It is a platform very useful."
"It is a very useful platform."
"They are interestings sessions."
"They are interesting sessions."
"The report page has difficult."
"The report page is difficult."

The adjective never takes an -s in English, and it goes before the noun.

Chapter 4: The Evaluation

The Evaluation

three good words, one real problem

Dialogue

What Is the Platform Like?

E
Hi, Joice. You tested the new platform. What is it like?
J
It is very useful. The first screen is really clear.
E
Good. And is it easy for a teacher on the first day?
J
The videos are simple and short, so yes. But the report page is difficult.
E
Difficult how? Too many buttons?
J
Yes. Many teachers do not find it. That is an important problem.
E
You are right. And the general idea of the tool?
J
The idea is very powerful, and the project is really interesting.
E
So: useful, clear, but the report page is a problem.
J
Exactly. Three good words, and one important question.
