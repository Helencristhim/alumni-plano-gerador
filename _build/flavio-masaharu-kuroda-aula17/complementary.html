Lesson 17 -- The Cold Call

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Small talk"

A short episode on the first friendly minute of a conversation with someone you do not know well. It is the minute that decides if a cold call becomes a conversation. Full script on the same page.

Tip: read the script and mark every question that asks permission or checks the moment. Add two of them to your opener card.

Listen on BBC Learning English ↗
Talk
Jia Jiang -- "What I learned from 100 days of rejection" (TED, 15 min)

A man asks strangers for strange things for one hundred days to lose his fear of hearing no. He discovers that asking politely, and asking why, turns many noes into yeses. Very useful for the brush-off on a cold call.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down the exact words he uses when somebody says no, and use one of them on your next call.

Watch on TED ↗
Series
Better Call Saul -- Season 1, Episode 1 "Uno"

A small-time lawyer needs clients, so he calls and approaches people who do not know him and did not ask for his help. Watch how he tries to get past the brush-off and earn a second meeting.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Each time he approaches a stranger, notice if he asks permission first, and what small next step he asks for.
