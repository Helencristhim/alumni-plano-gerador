Lesson 1 -- The Cost of Choosing

Talk
How to Make Hard Choices -- Ruth Chang, TED (15 min)

A philosopher argues that hard choices feel hard for a reason, and that the reason is not a lack of information. Her answer sits very close to the last line of tonight’s text: some decisions are not about finding the better option, they are about deciding who you are.

Tip: she speaks slowly and structures everything with signposts. Notice how many times she says on the one hand and on the other -- that is the concession language you practised.

Video
The Paradox of Choice -- Barry Schwartz, TED (20 min)

The other half of tonight’s argument, and the evidence behind the advice to cut the number of options before you start weighing them up. More choice does not make people happier; past a certain point it makes them worse off and more likely to regret what they picked.

Tip: watch the first eight minutes with subtitles off. Write down the gist in one sentence, then watch again with subtitles and check how close you were.

Talk
Inside the Mind of a Master Procrastinator -- Tim Urban, TED (14 min)

The funniest fourteen minutes on this list, and the one closest to the verb you practised tonight: to postpone. He is fast, informal and full of the natural speech you will need in a real conversation -- so treat it as listening training, not just entertainment.

Tip: this one is fast. Do not try to catch every word -- that is exactly the habit we are unlearning. Aim for the story and the punchlines.
