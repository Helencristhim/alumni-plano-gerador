Lesson 16 -- Pre-class

It Was Decided -- The News and the Process

How to say what happened without saying who did it, the way news and bank announcements do. Key expressions: to announce, to launch, a board, to appoint, a shareholder, to merge, to raise, a headline, to cut, behind closed doors. Structure: the passive in the present simple and the past simple.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each expression and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it. These ten stay here in your material -- they do not disappear after the class.

To announce -- to tell people something officially, often in public
"The deal was announced at seven this morning."
To launch -- to start or introduce something new, like a product or a fund
"A new fund is launched almost every month."
A board -- the group of people who control and direct a company
"The decision was made by the board last night."
To appoint -- to choose somebody officially for a job
"A new director was appointed in March."
A shareholder -- a person who owns part of a company
"The shareholders were told about it by letter."
To merge -- to join two companies together to make one
"The two banks are going to merge next year."
To raise -- to make something higher, like a price or a rate
"Interest rates were raised again this week."
A headline -- the title of a news story, in large letters
"Everybody read the headline and nobody read the report."
To cut -- to make something smaller or lower, like costs or jobs
"Two hundred jobs were cut after the deal."
Behind closed doors -- privately, where the public cannot see or hear
"The real decision was made behind closed doors."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Harbor Capital and Northgate Bank are going to merge. The news was announced at seven on Monday morning, and by eight it was the main headline on every financial website in the country.

The deal was not decided in a day. It was discussed behind closed doors for almost a year, and it was approved by both boards last Thursday. The shareholders were told by letter on Sunday night, a few hours before the public.

For clients, very little changes at first. Accounts are managed by the same teams, fees are kept at the same level for twelve months, and no new products are launched until the two systems are joined. A new chief executive is expected to be appointed in the spring.

Janderson read the story on his way to work. Two of his clients have accounts at Northgate, and he called both of them before ten, because a client who hears the news from you trusts you more than a client who reads it in a headline.

1. "The deal was approved by both boards." Why is was used here?
A Because the approval happened at a finished time in the past, and it is one deal.
B Because the boards approved it, and boards are always singular.
C Because it is a question.
2. When were the shareholders told?
A On Monday morning at seven, together with the public and the clients.
B On Sunday night, a few hours before the public.
C Last Thursday.
3. What changes for clients at first, according to the text?
A Very little: the same teams manage the accounts and the fees stay the same.
B All the fees are raised immediately and every account is moved to a new team.
C The accounts are closed.
4. Why did Janderson call his two clients before ten?
A Because the bank asked him to call every client.
B Because a client who hears the news from him trusts him more than one who reads it.
C Because their accounts were closed on Monday morning by the new board.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Passive

GRAMMAR

The passive puts what happened at the start of the sentence. You use it when the action is more important than the person who did it.

FormWhat it saysExample
am / is / are + past participlePresent: what happens regularly, or is true now."The accounts are managed by the same team."
was / were + past participlePast: what happened at a finished time."The deal was approved last Thursday."
Negativenot after the verb be."A new director was not appointed."
Questionbe moves before the subject."Was the deal approved?"
"When was it announced?"
by + the personOnly when the person is important or new information."It was approved by both boards."
Irregular verbsUse the past participle, not the past simple."made, told, cut, chosen, written"

The five-second test: is the sentence about the person, or about what happened? If it is about what happened, start with that, and use be + the past participle.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

You are telling people about news at work. Choose the best thing to say.

You want to tell a colleague about the merger:
A The merger announced this morning.
B The merger was announced this morning.
C The merger was announce this morning by somebody.
You want to say who manages the accounts now:
A The accounts are managed by the same team.
B The accounts is managed by the same team as before the deal.
C The accounts are manage by the same team.
A client asks when the decision happened. You ask your manager:
A When the decision was made?
B When was the decision made?
