"Unbreaking the Bank" -- This American Life, episode 377, act two

A reporter follows what really happens when a bank fails and is taken over by the government, from the first phone call to the Monday morning. Connection to Lesson 16: it is a news story told from the inside, and you will hear the passive constantly -- the bank was closed, the accounts were moved, the staff were told. Free to listen on the page.

Tip: listen once for the story. The second time, write down five sentences in the passive and say who did the action in each one.
