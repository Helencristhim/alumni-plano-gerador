Film
Minimalism: A Documentary About the Important Things

Ninety minutes about the word you learned today: enough. It is not a film about throwing things away — it is a film about people who worked out how much was actually enough for them, and what happened to the rest of their week once they did. It is the same argument as today’s second recording, told by fifteen different people.

Tip: English subtitles on. Every time somebody says how much or how many of something they have, write the sentence down. You will end the film with a page of today’s grammar in real speech.

Video
BBC Learning English -- "Live English Class: the difference between much and many"

A full class from the BBC teachers, on exactly today’s split. It is longer than the other videos here, and that is the point: they take real questions from real learners, and almost every question is a mistake you have made or are about to make.

Tip: it is half an hour, so do not try to finish it in one sitting. The rule itself is explained in the first ten minutes — watch that much, and come back for the questions another day.

Talk
Laura Vanderkam -- "How to gain control of your free time" (TED)

She studied how busy people actually spend their weeks, hour by hour, and her conclusion is uncomfortable and useful: we do not build the lives we want and save the leftovers. We save the leftovers and hope a life turns up in them. Twelve minutes, clear speech, and full of the numbers this lesson is made of.

Tip: English subtitles on. She gives one instruction near the end — write down next week’s priorities before the week starts. Do it, in English, in six sentences.
