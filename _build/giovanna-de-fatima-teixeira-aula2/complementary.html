Extra Practice

None of this is homework and nothing has to be delivered. It is English coming in through your ears while you do something else. Tick the box when you watch or listen, just so you remember what you have already seen.

Series
Extra English -- Episode 5: "A Star Is Born"

The same sitcom as last week, one episode later. Four friends share a flat in London and spend the whole episode talking about each other: what Hector does, what Bridget wants, where Nick goes. That is exactly the grammar of this class, in a story.

Tip: pick one character and listen only for the sentences about that person. Every time you hear a verb with a final s, say the sentence out loud after the actor.

Podcast
Lovely English Stories -- "Sophie's Daily Routine"

A short A2 story about one day in the life of a girl called Sophie, told from the outside: she gets up, she works, she does not have much free time. Same shape as the text you read in class, but spoken slowly, so your ear gets the final s instead of your eyes.

Tip: listen once for the story and a second time only for the verbs. Then tell Sophie's day out loud in five sentences, without notes.

YouTube
Indeed -- "A Day in the Life of a Civil Engineer"

A real civil engineer, filmed through one working day, in normal American English. This is not a graded lesson: it is the speed you complained about, on your own subject. Every word of this class is in there -- site, schedule, task, report, colleagues.

Tip: turn the subtitles on and watch only the first five minutes. Write down three words you already knew before today. That is the point of this one.
