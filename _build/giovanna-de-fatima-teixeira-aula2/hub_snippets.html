Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen as many times as you want.

Field engineer -- an engineer who works outside, on the site, not at a desk
"The field engineer walks eight kilometers a day."
Supervisor -- the person who checks your work and gives you instructions
"My supervisor reads the report every evening."
Colleague -- a person who works in the same company as you
"She inspects the wall with two colleagues."
Schedule -- the plan that says when each thing happens
"The schedule says the crew starts at seven."
Task -- one piece of work that you have to finish
"Her first task is the tunnel wall."
Report -- a short written text about what happened at work
"She writes the report at four o'clock."
Inspect -- to look at something carefully, to see if it is correct and safe
"She inspects the tunnel wall every morning."
Office -- the room or the building where people work with computers and papers
"He works in the office, not in the tunnel."
Daily -- happening every day, without exception
"The daily report goes to the supervisor."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the text out loud, then answer the questions below. Read slowly -- nobody is timing you.

Karen Booth is a field engineer on a metro project. She works outside most of the day, so she is not in the office very often. Her day starts at six thirty, before the crew arrives at the site. First she reads the schedule with her supervisor. Then she gets her first task of the day: she inspects the tunnel wall with two colleagues. She does not write the daily report in the morning. She writes it at four o'clock, and her supervisor reads it the same evening.

1. Why do we say "she works" and not "she work"?
A Because the sentence is about the morning.
B Because he, she and it add a final s to the verb.
C Because Karen is a field engineer.
2. The text says "she does not write". Why is there no s on "write"?
A Because the sentence is negative, so verbs never take an s.
B Because "write" is an irregular verb.
C Because the s is already inside "does", and it appears only once.
3. What is Karen's first task of the day?
A She writes the daily report.
B She inspects the tunnel wall with two colleagues.
C She stays in the office with her supervisor.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Third Person S

Grammar

One little letter, and it appears only once in the sentence.

FormHow it worksExample
Affirmativehe / she / it + verb + sShe inspects the tunnel wall.
Negativehe / she / it + does not + basic verbShe does not write the report in the morning.
QuestionWh- + does + he / she + basic verbWhere does she work?
Spellingverbs in -ch, -sh and -o take -esHe watches the crew. She goes to the gate.
The one ruleThe s appears once. If it is inside does, the next verb goes back to its basic form.
