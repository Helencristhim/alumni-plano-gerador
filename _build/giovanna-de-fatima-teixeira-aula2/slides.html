Chapter 3: She Works, She Starts

One Letter, Two Rules

she works · she does not write · what does she do?

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Karen works on a metro project.
She starts at six thirty.
Her supervisor reads the daily report.
She does not write the report in the morning.

What do the orange words have in common?

SubjectVerbExample
I / you / we / theywork, start, readI work on a metro site.
he / she / it / Karenworks, starts, readsKaren works on a metro project.
Negative (he / she / it)does not + verbShe does not write the report in the morning.
Spelling-ch, -sh, -o take -esShe watches the crew. He goes to the gate.
The one ruleThe S appears once in the sentence. If it is in does, the next verb has no S.
Grammar Discovery

What Does She Do?

What does she do?
Where does she work?
When does her shift start?
Who does she talk to?

Where is the S in these questions?

Question word+ does +subject + basic verb
Whatdoesshe do? — She is a field engineer.
Wheredoesshe work? — She works on a metro project.
Whendoesher shift start? — It starts at six thirty.
Whodoesshe talk to? — Her supervisor and two colleagues.
The one ruleThe S is inside does, so the verb after it is basic: Where does she work?
