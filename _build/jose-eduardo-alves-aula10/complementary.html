Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Planet Money (NPR) — "What’s up with all the ads for law firms?"

American lawyers used to be forbidden to advertise, and one man took that rule to the Supreme Court in 1977. The whole episode is a profession changing shape, told through the grammar of this lesson: what the job used to be, the turning point, and what it is now.

Tip: after listening, say out loud three sentences with used to about how the legal market changed after 1977.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Emilie Wapnick — "Why some of us don’t have one true calling" (TED)

Twelve minutes on the question behind this lesson: what happens to a person whose career changes direction in the middle, and why the culture treats that as a failure of focus rather than as an asset.

Tip: subtitles and a full transcript are on the page. Note every sentence where she contrasts what somebody used to be with what they are now.

Watch on TED ↗
Documentary
FRONTLINE (PBS) — "In the Age of AI (full documentary)"

Two hours on work that used to exist and does not any more, including a long section on document review in law firms. Slow, clear American English, and the interviews are people describing their own before and after.

Tip: two hours is a lot. Watch the first twenty minutes only, and write down five things the speakers say people used to do.
