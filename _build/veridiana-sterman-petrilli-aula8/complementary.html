Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
The World’s Most Extraordinary Homes — Season 1, Episode 1 "Mountains" (Netflix)

Two presenters walking through houses and comparing them out loud for forty-five minutes: bigger, brighter, more expensive, the best view. It is an hour of exactly the structure you practised today, and it is calm from beginning to end.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on and write down ten comparisons. Mark which ones take -er and which take more.

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "How to travel on a budget"

Half of this episode is one long comparison: this way against that way, and what you actually give up for the cheaper option. Listen for the moment they name the trade-off out loud, which is the move you practised today.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Find three trade-offs and say each one back in one sentence.

Listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Barry Schwartz — "The paradox of choice" (TED)

Twenty minutes on why two good options are harder than one, and why naming the trade-off out loud is what stops the choice from paralysing you. Funny, and it is the reason the last sentence of today’s lesson exists.

Tip: turn the English subtitles on. Then describe, in three sentences, one decision you put off longer than it deserved.
