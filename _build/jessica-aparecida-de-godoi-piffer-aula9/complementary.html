Talk
John McWhorter — “Txtng is killing language. JK!!!” (TED)

Fourteen minutes from a linguist on the difference between how people speak and how they write — which is exactly the gap this lesson works in. He is fast, funny and builds long sentences you can hear the structure of.

Tip: pick two of his longer sentences and rewrite each one as three short ones. Then decide which version you would rather read.

Radio
99% Invisible — “Octothorpe”

The history of a single symbol, and of the arguments about what it is allowed to mean. It is the same idea as this lesson’s second recording, at the level of one character rather than one comma.

Tip: the episode is full of non-defining clauses between commas. Write down three and take the middle part out — check that the sentence still stands.

Podcast
Criminal — “A Man to Be Afraid Of” (episode 367)

An episode in which identifying the right person out of several is the entire problem. The narration is built almost entirely from the structure you practised today: the man who, the woman whose, the house where.

Tip: every time a new person appears, note the clause that identifies them. Then try describing somebody at your own work in the same shape.
