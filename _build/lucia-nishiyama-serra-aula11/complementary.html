The past perfect tense -- 6 Minute Grammar

Six minutes on exactly what you practiced tonight: how "had" plus a past participle marks the earlier of two past moments, and why the second action stays in the past simple. Two BBC presenters build the contrast live, at natural speed, with the same signal words you drilled in class.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause after each example and say one real sentence from your own career: "By the time I took over, ..."

The Past Perfect Tense -- The Grammar Gameshow, Episode 13

Ten minutes in quiz format: contestants build past perfect sentences under pressure while the host corrects every mistake out loud. It covers the two traps from class -- dropping "had" after "by the time", and putting both verbs of a sentence in the past perfect.

Tip: answer out loud before the contestants do. If you beat them to "had already left", the form is already yours.

The leader of X, Google's moonshot factory, explains how his teams look for the root cause early, treat every setback as information, and kill the projects that cannot be turned around.
