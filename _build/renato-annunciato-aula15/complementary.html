Lesson 15 -- Reporting Progress

Series
Inventing Anna (Inventando Anna) -- Episode 1

A journalist investigates a young woman who pretended to be a rich heiress in New York. Most of the episode is people telling the journalist what Anna said and did, and the journalist reporting it to her editor. It is a full hour of reported speech in real conversations.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time someone says "she told me" or "he said that", pause and notice the verb after it: is it one step back in time?

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart -- Meetings With a Point: How to Design For Better Decisions (Episode 261)

A free episode from Stanford Graduate School of Business. Matt Abrahams talks with Rebecca Hinds about why so many status updates fill our calendars, and which updates should be a short message instead of a meeting. It connects directly with today's four-line update.

Tip: listen once at normal speed. Then write one sentence about what Rebecca Hinds said, starting with "She said that...".

YouTube
Linda Raynier -- Stop Being Quiet in Meetings: Speak Clearly at Work

A career coach explains how to speak up in meetings with a clear structure, including a simple framework for giving a progress update: what is done, what you are working on, and what comes next.

Tip: watch it once with subtitles. Then give your own update for this week out loud, using her structure and the four lines from class.
