Chapter 3: The Manager Who Asks Why

Explaining the Rule to a Local Manager

A conversation about a policy that arrives from headquarters

Dialogue

The Policy and the Local Manager

R
Camila, I read the policy on remote work. Honestly, I do not understand why headquarters changed it.
C
It changed so that the three countries have the same rule. Today each entity decides alone, and that creates problems in compliance.
R
And when does it come into effect? My team asked me this morning.
C
The effective date is the first of March. Until then, nothing changes for your team.
R
The policy says three days in the office. Here we agreed two days last year. Do I have to change it?
C
Yes, from March. But we send a local adaptation to headquarters, in order to explain the agreement you already have.
R
Good. And what do I tell the team? They will ask me the same question I asked you.
C
I pass the guideline on to you this week, so that you have the same words in front of you. It has five questions and five short answers.
R
That helps. One more thing: the first of March is a Sunday.
C
Thank you for pointing that out. I bear that in mind and I check the date with compliance today.
Comprehension

About the Conversation

1. Why does Camila say the policy changed?
So that the three countries have the same rule, because each entity deciding alone creates compliance problems.
2. What is the problem with the rule in Rafael's team, and what is the solution?
His team agreed two days in the office last year and the policy says three. They send a local adaptation to headquarters explaining the existing agreement.
3. What does Rafael point out at the end, and what does Camila do about it?
That the first of March is a Sunday. She says she will check the date with compliance the same day.
Chapter 4: The Code

Saying Why: So That and In Order To

The structure that answers the only question that matters

Grammar Discovery

What Comes After So That?

"I send the guideline so that the managers have the same words."

"We change the policy in order to have one rule in three countries."

"I call the local team so that they understand the effective date."

"We publish it in February to give everybody time to prepare."

Two of these structures are followed by a person and a verb. Two of them are followed only by a verb. Which are which?

FormWhat it doesExample
so that + subject + verbwhen the purpose belongs to somebody elseI send it so that the managers have the same words.
in order to + verbwhen the purpose belongs to the same subject, formalWe change the policy in order to have one rule.
to + verbthe short, everyday version of in order toWe publish it in February to give everybody time.
so that + can / willvery common when you talk about a future possibilityI send the file today so that you can prepare the meeting.
Watch outputting a person after to. "to her understand" and "to the team know" do not exist: use so that she understands, so that the team knows.

Listening 1

Headquarters Announces the Policy

Claire records the announcement for the regional HR teams. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. What is the effective date of the policy, and what happens before that?
The first of March. Until then nothing changes, and February is used to prepare and to explain it.
2. Why was the policy changed, according to Claire?
So that the whole group has one rule, because today each entity decides alone and that creates problems with compliance.

The local adaptation, with anything that does not work in that country, by the tenth of February.
Listening 2

A Country Answers Back

A manager in Argentina leaves a message after reading the policy. Sound first, no text.

0:000:00
1. What is the manager's main problem with the policy?
Not the three days in the office, but the fact that his team agreed something different last year with the union.
2. What does he ask Camila to do?
To include the existing agreement in the local adaptation, and to tell him what he can say to his team this week.
3. What does he say about the communication?
That his team read the policy before he did, because it was sent to everybody at the same time.

The objective, the participants by role and the decision.

What has to be done, who does it, and by when.
