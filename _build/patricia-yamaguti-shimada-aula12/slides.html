Chapter 3: What Time

At the Clock, On the Day, Until the End

at · on · until · in

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"We open at nine."

"We are closed on Mondays."

"We are open until five."

"Breakfast is in the morning."

WordYou use it withExample
at + a time on the clocknine, half past seven, middayWe open at nine.
on + a dayMonday, Saturday, 12 OctoberWe are closed on Mondays.
until + the endwhen something stopsWe are open until five.
in + a part of the daythe morning, the afternoon, the eveningBreakfast is in the morning.

Clock = at. Day = on. End = until. Part of the day = in. And days of the week always start with a CAPITAL letter in English: Monday, not monday.

Dialogue

A Call to the Museum

G
Good morning, City Museum. How can I help?
P
Good morning. What time do you open, please?
G
We open at ten, every day except Monday.
P
And what time do you close?
G
Five in the afternoon, but the last entry is at half past four.
P
Sorry, I don't understand. Can you say that again, please?
G
Of course. Last entry: four thirty. After that, no more people come in.
P
Thank you. Are you open on Sunday?
G
We are, but only until three. And how long is your visit? Most families take about two hours.
P
So: Tuesday, open at ten, last entry half past four, and two hours inside. Is that right?
G
Exactly right. Do you want to book for Tuesday?
P
Yes, please. Two adults and two children, Tuesday morning.
G
Booked. See you on Tuesday, and please arrive fifteen minutes early.
Comprehension

What Did George Say?

1. What time is the last entry?
Half past four — four thirty. After that nobody comes in.
2. What is different about Sunday?
They are open, but only until three.
3. What does George ask her to do on Tuesday?
Arrive fifteen minutes early.
Listening

The Recorded Message

The message you hear when you call and nobody answers. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. Which days are they closed?
All day on Monday, and also on the first of January and the twenty-fifth of December.
2. What time is the last entry on a normal day, and on Sunday?
Half past four normally, and half past two on Sunday.
3. When do groups of more than ten people call?
In the morning, between nine and ten.
Listening 2

A Change of Plan

The tour company calls to change the time. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What was the old time, and what is the new one?
It was at half past two; now it starts at four.
2. What time does the tour finish now?
At twenty past five — it is still one hour and twenty minutes.
3. What happens if she does not call?
They keep her on Thursday at four.
