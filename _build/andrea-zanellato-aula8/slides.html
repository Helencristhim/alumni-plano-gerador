Chapter 4: The Code

Have You Ever...? I Have Never...

Experiences in your life, with no date

Grammar Discovery

Four Sentences About Concerts

"I have seen U2 live two times."

"Have you ever been to a stadium concert?"

"She has never heard that song."

"I saw Coldplay in 2023."

Look at the blue verbs. Have seen, has heard: have / has + a past form. We use it for experiences in our life, with no date. In sentence 4 there is a date (2023), so the verb is the simple past: saw.

JobFormExample
Experience (no date)have / has + past participleI have seen U2 live.
he / she / ithas + past participleShe has heard that song.
QuestionHave you ever + past participle?Have you ever been to a stadium concert?
Negativehave never / haven’t + past participleI have never seen Dua Lipa.
Past participlessee → seen · be → been · hear → heard · go → gone / beenI have been to London.
With a dateuse the past simpleI saw them in 2017.

In one line: have / has + past participle for experiences with no date; with a date, the past simple.
