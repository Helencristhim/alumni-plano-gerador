Lesson 21 — Pre-class

What the Fee Covers

Four new words for the engagement letter — cover, bookkeeping, tax return, additional. And one thing only: saying what is NOT in the fee with "however" and "although". Around ten minutes.
0%

Stage 1: Four new words

Vocabulary

You write engagement letters every month. What is new is only the English word and the sound. Read each one and say it out loud.

Cover — to include something in a price or a service
"The monthly fee covers payroll."
Bookkeeping — keeping the daily records of what a company pays and receives
"We do the bookkeeping every month."
Tax return — the form a company sends to the government about its taxes
"We send your tax returns on time."
Additional — extra; more than what was agreed
"Additional work has its own price."

Stage 3: What is in, and what is not

Grammar

One structure this week. To say that something is NOT in the fee after you said what is, use however at the start of a new sentence: Our fee covers bookkeeping. However, it does not cover audits. Or put although before the first idea, then a comma: Although we do not do audits, we work with a partner firm. One contrast word is enough: never "although..., but...".

"Our fee covers payroll. ______, it does not cover audits."
A However
B Although
"______ we do not do audits, we work with a partner firm."
A However,
B Although
Which sentence is correct?
A Although the fee is fixed, but additional work like a new branch has its own price every time.
B Although the fee is fixed, additional work has its own price.
The client reads the engagement letter. Which answer tells her what is not in the fee?
A Our fee covers bookkeeping. However, it does not cover audits.
B The fee covers the usual things, you know, and some other things are not in it.

Stage 5: Say it out loud

Speaking

Make a small stop after "however" and after the "although" idea. That small pause is what makes the contrast sound clear.

Our monthly fee covers bookkeeping and payroll.
However, the fee does not cover audits.
Although we do not do audits, we work with a partner firm.

Stage 6: Before class

Speaking
Choose one real client of your firm (no name needed). Record three sentences as if you were explaining the engagement letter: what the monthly fee covers, one thing it does not cover (However, ...), and what you offer instead (Although we do not..., we...). One sentence each — no more. In class you will use this client.
