Answer B. "It covers bookkeeping, payroll and your tax returns" and "it does not cover audits" give her the full list.

One sentence: "Answer ______ is better, because ______ ."

Discover the shape

What is in, and what is not

Our monthly fee covers bookkeeping. However, it does not cover audits.
Although we do not do audits, we work with a partner firm.
The fee is the same every month. However, additional work has its own price.

Idea one. However, idea two. · Although idea one, idea two.
Never "although..., but...". One contrast word is enough.
