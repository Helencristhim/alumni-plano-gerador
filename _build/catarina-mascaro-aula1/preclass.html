Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read the chat. Then answer the questions.

Bia: Hi! Do you live near the school?
Sam: Yes, ten minutes on foot. What do you do after class?
Bia: I play volleyball on Mondays. How often do you play basketball?
Sam: Twice a week. My sister plays too — she is very competitive.
Bia: Ha! Where does she train?
Sam: At the club near the park. She is there every day. She is so energetic.
How often does Sam play basketball?
A Every day.
B Twice a week.
Why does the chat use "does" and not "do" in "Where does she train?"
A Because the question is about "she".
B Because the question starts with "Where".
Who asks more questions in this chat?
A Bia.
B Sam.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Questions in the present — you always need do

Use do with I / you / we / they. Use does with he / she / it.

Do

Do you play sports?
What do you watch?
How often do they train?

Does

Does she play sports?
What does he watch?
Where does Bia live?

+ I play volleyball on Mondays.
− I do not play volleyball on Fridays. (short: don't)
? Does she play volleyball? (not "Does she plays")

Stage 4: In the conversation

Quiz
A new classmate says: "I love volleyball." You want to keep the conversation going. What do you say?
A "Ok."
B "Nice! How often do you play?"
You do not know the word "stubborn". What do you say?
A "I do not know that word. What does it mean?"
B Say nothing and wait.
Someone asks: "Are you competitive?" Which answer is better?
A "Yes."
B "Yes, I am. I hate losing games, even with my family."
