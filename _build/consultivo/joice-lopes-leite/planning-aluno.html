Your planning

Block 1 — lessons 9 to 12

Four lessons about the same thing: saying more than one sentence, and asking something back.

LessonTopicWhat you will do
9The invitationRead an invitation to an international meeting and tell someone what it is, when it is, and what you will do there — without the text in front of you.
10Two people say who they areListen to two people introduce themselves in an online meeting, ask for the part you missed, and ask each of them one question.
11Six questions, one conversationAsk six questions in a row, each one coming from the answer you have just heard.
12Your first two minutesCome into an international meeting: say who you are, ask two questions, and answer one you did not expect.
