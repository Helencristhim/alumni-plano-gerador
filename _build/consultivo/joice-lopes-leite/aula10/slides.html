7 · On the call

Now it is your turn

Your teacher is Marta. She has just introduced herself.

Say who you are in two sentences, and ask her one question. Nobody will call your name.

Say your name and what you do — two sentences.
Ask her one question about what she said.
If you missed something, ask for it again first.
Use her answer in your next sentence.

Which one do you spend it on?

Speed
One question, well spent
Spend it on a name and you can write it down. Spend it on what someone does and you have something to ask about later — which is how a conversation continues after the introductions are over.
