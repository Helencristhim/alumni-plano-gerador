Block 1 — lessons 4 to 7

Four lessons on the question that was not in your notes: understanding what someone is really asking, checking it, and giving the right number, with where it comes from.

LessonTopicWhat you will do
4Fact or forecast?Read a news article about a closed shipping route and its effect on warehouse jobs, and tell someone who has not read it what is certain and what is not: who says each part, and what up to or at least does to a number.
5What exactly are you asking?Follow a call where a finance director interrupts a presentation with questions nobody prepared, and check what a question means before you answer it.
6Since when?Give an update on three indicators, with the verb that the time period needs, and answer questions about when things changed.
7What turnover costs, in dollarsPresent the cost of a turnover problem to leaders in the US, from number of people to dollars, and answer questions you did not see coming.
