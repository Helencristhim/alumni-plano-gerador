Lesson 07

What Turnover Costs,
in Dollars

In this lesson you present the cost of a turnover problem to leaders in the US,
and you answer the questions you did not prepare for.

Who outside Brazil sees the numbers you prepare, or will see them?

1 · The review

A leadership review, and one decision

The quarterly review of the São Paulo sites, on a video call with the US.

Your role
HR director for the Brazil operation.
Who is listening
The vice president of HR and the chief financial officer, both in the US.
What they know
That turnover has gone up. Not what it costs.
What they will do
Ask questions you did not prepare for.
What you want by the end
Approval for one action, with the cost on the table.

What do they need to hear first: how many people left, or how many dollars it cost?

4 · Easy to follow by ear

The same total, said two ways

Both versions are correct. Which one could a CFO repeat after hearing it once, and what makes the difference?

Version 1: “The total cost of the voluntary exits at the two sites in the second quarter, at an estimated $4,870 per exit, was $345,770.”
Version 2: “We lost 71 people in the second quarter. At about $4,870 each, that comes to roughly $346,000.”
A number the listener can keep Version 2 gives one number per sentence, says what each number is before the number, and gives the rounded total first. The exact figure can come next, if it matters.
“We lost 71 people…” — what the number is, then the number.
“…that comes to roughly $346,000.” — the rounded total first.
“$345,770, to be exact.” — the exact figure, in its own sentence.
