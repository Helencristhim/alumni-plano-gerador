Your planning

Block 1 — lessons 1 to 4

Your first four lessons in English. Everything happens in a situation you already know: a trip.

LessonTopicWhat you will do
1Your name on the bookingRead a hotel booking and say four things out loud: your name, the city, the dates, and what is included.
2Three questions at the deskListen to the three questions they ask at a hotel desk, answer each one, and ask them to repeat when you do not understand.
3I am, you areSay six sentences about you and about your trip, using the verb you already used in lessons 1 and 2.
4At the front deskDo a whole hotel check-in: say who you are, confirm the dates, ask for one thing, and answer a question you did not expect.

Your next lessons will appear here as you finish each block.
