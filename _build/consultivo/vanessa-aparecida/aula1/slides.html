The situation

7 · Your four sentences

Your turn at the desk

Your teacher is at the hotel desk. You have just arrived.

Say your four sentences, one after the other. O professor faz o papel da recepção. Diga as suas quatro frases, uma depois da outra.

Say your name. Diga o seu nome.
Say the city. Diga a cidade.
Say the two dates. Diga as duas datas.
Say what is included. Diga o que está incluído.
