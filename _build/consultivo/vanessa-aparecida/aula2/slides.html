7 · Your turn at the desk

The whole check-in, with your teacher

Complete the hotel desk conversation with your teacher. Answer freely. If you need support, open your booking details from lesson 1. If you do not understand a question, ask your teacher to repeat it. Faça a conversa de check-in com o professor. Responda livremente. Se precisar de apoio, abra os dados da sua reserva da aula 1. Se não entender uma pergunta, peça para o professor repeti-la.

8 · What just happened

What worked, and once more

Let’s put down what worked and one thing to build on. Vamos anotar o que funcionou e uma coisa para desenvolver.

Once more, at a natural pace

Try the conversation once more. This time, your teacher will ask the questions at a more natural pace. You may ask for a repeat once. Faça a conversa mais uma vez. Agora, o professor fará as perguntas em um ritmo mais natural. Você pode pedir para repetir uma vez.

Asking is normal
At a hotel desk, in every language, people ask for things to be repeated all day. The person who asks is the person who is following — not the one who is lost.
