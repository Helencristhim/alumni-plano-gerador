Lesson 03

I Am,
You Are

The verb you said you did not know.
You have used it nine times. O verbo que você disse não saber. Você já o usou nove vezes.

1 · Say it once

Three sentences about you

Anything. No notes. O que você quiser. Sem consultar nada.

Who are you, where are you, and what do you do? Quem você é, onde você está e o que você faz?

2 · Your own sentences

From lessons one and two

What you said

These are your sentences, from the first two lessons.

1. My name is Vanessa.
2. I am in Lisbon.
3. It is from 14 October to 18 October.
4. Breakfast is included.
5. It is a room for two people.
6. Four nights, and they are paid.

How many different forms are there in these six? Quantas formas diferentes do verbo aparecem nestas seis frases?

3 · What changed

Three of these are right

4 · The form under it

One table, after you tried

I am  ·  you are  ·  he / she / it is  ·  we are  ·  they are

Negative: add not after the verb — I am not, it is not.
Question: change the order — are you…?, is it…?

My children ___ at home. And breakfast ___ included? Complete as duas: am, is ou are?

5 · Twelve, fast

Am, is, or are

Your teacher gives you the subject. You finish the sentence, immediately.

6 · Six of your own

With the table still open

Three positive, two negative, one question. Três afirmativas, duas negativas e uma pergunta.

About you, your family, or your next trip. Sobre você, sobre a sua família ou sobre a sua próxima viagem.

I am…  ·  My name is…  ·  They are…
I am not…  ·  It is not…  ·  Are you…?
7 · Say them together

Six sentences, nothing on screen

Your teacher is someone you met at the hotel. O professor faz o papel de alguém que você conheceu no hotel.

Three things you are. Two things you are not. And one question back. Três coisas que você é. Duas que você não é. E uma pergunta de volta.

Start with who you are and where you are. Comece por quem você é e onde você está.
Say two things that are not true about you. Diga duas coisas que não são verdade sobre você.
Ask one question with are you. Faça uma pergunta usando are you.
8 · Someone else

The same six, about your daughter

Now the sentences are not about you. Agora as frases não são sobre você.

What changes, and what stays the same? O que muda e o que continua igual?

Where everyone slips
I am becomes she is — and the rest of the sentence does not move. Getting that right while thinking about what you want to say is the whole lesson.
