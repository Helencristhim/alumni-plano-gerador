3 · What changed

Which form matches?

Read the six sentences. Mark the three that use the correct verb form. Leia as seis frases. Marque as três que usam a forma correta do verbo.

4 · The form under it

The verb to be

I am  ·  you are  ·  he / she / it is  ·  we are  ·  they are

Negative: add not after the verb — I am not, it is not.
Question: the verb comes first — are you…?, is it…?

Complete the two sentences with am, is or are. Complete as duas frases com am, is ou are.

My children ___ at home. Os meus filhos estão em casa.
___ breakfast included? O café da manhã está incluído?
5 · Choose the form, then say it

Am, is, or are

First, choose the form that goes in each sentence. Primeiro, escolha a forma que entra em cada frase.

Now out loud: your teacher gives you the beginning of a sentence, and you finish it with am, is, or are. Agora em voz alta: o professor diz o começo de uma frase e você termina com am, is ou are.

I am  ·  you are  ·  he / she / it is  ·  we are  ·  they are
6 · Six of your own

Six sentences about you

Say six sentences about you, your family or your next trip: three affirmative, two negative, and one question. Diga seis frases sobre você, sua família ou sua próxima viagem: três afirmativas, duas negativas e uma pergunta.

I am…  ·  My name is…  ·  They are…
I am not…  ·  It is not…  ·  Are you…?
