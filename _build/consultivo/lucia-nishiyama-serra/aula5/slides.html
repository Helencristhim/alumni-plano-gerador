7 · Hold the room

Ninety seconds, and one disagreement

Argue your own decision. Your teacher disagrees once, in the middle.

Open with a cleft — announce what the point is.
Park one thing you are not arguing today.
When your teacher disagrees, concede and hold.
Close with the ask, at the end of the sentence.

8 · One more

Again, and I disagree twice

Same argument. Two objections instead of one.

What two objections test
Conceding once is politeness. Conceding twice, and still arriving at the same ask, is the thing that makes a room believe you have thought about it.
