7 · Your answers

Three questions, one at a time

Your teacher is Diane, then Ray, then Nadia.
Each question comes once, out loud.

Start with a check or with four seconds — never with the answer to a question you guessed.
For the fact you do not have: say so, and say when it arrives.
For the vague one: make her choose between two readings.
For the hostile one: name the assumption she is making, then disagree with it.
Close each answer. Do not let it trail off into the next question.
That is a fair question  ·  Let me take that in two parts  ·  If I understand you correctly…
Short answer…  ·  I would push back on that  ·  I do not have that with me
8 · Twenty seconds less

Nadia again, in twenty seconds

Same objection. A third of the time.

What survives when there is no room for the two parts?

What twenty seconds is for
Not a shorter version of the same answer. One sentence that names her assumption, and one that disagrees with it. Everything else can go — including, if you have to, the promise to send the wording.
