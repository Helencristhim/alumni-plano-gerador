6 · Say the move

The time is yours

Your teacher asks you four quick questions. Start every answer with one of these — a different one each time.

Going quiet ends your turn. Any of these keeps it, and buys you the time you need.

Let me think…
That is a good question.
Honestly? …
I mean, …
6 · React to me

Ask it back

Your teacher says what they think about three things. Every time, do two things: react, and then ask them why.

Really? Why do you say that?
What made you think that?
Wait, really? I do not agree.
And what about the ending?
The person asking is not the person who is lost In the conversation you just heard, Maya asks Chris to explain, and at the end Chris asks her the same thing back. Neither of them is behind. Asking is how they both stay in it.

7 · Change the subject

Now you choose again

Second round, new subject — and this time you start. Ask your teacher what they think about something, and go after the reason.

You ask first.
Go after the reason at least twice.
Say what you think too — do not only ask.
