Your planning

Block 1 — lessons 19 to 22

Four lessons about one thing: keeping the conversation going after the activity ends. You say what you think, you say why, you ask back — and you choose the subject in every lesson.

LessonTopicWhat you will do
19Two reviews, one filmRead two short reviews of the same film — one good, one bad — and then recommend something you have watched, with two reasons and nothing on the screen.
20Really? Why?Listen to the same two people disagreeing, and then keep a conversation going for six to eight turns: answer, buy yourself time, and ask the question back.
21The order of the questionPut six scrambled questions back together against the clock, and then ask your teacher six questions of your own to find out something you did not know.
22What I want to doTalk for two minutes about you, what you study and what you want to do next — to someone your age who has never been to Brazil, and who asks you something you did not expect.
