3 · Because or so

The same information, in a different order

These two sentences give the same information. What changes in the order? What comes after because, and what comes after so?

“The installation took longer because two laptops had a problem.”
“Two laptops had a problem, so the installation took longer.”
The reason and the result change places After because comes the reason. After so comes the result. The facts are the same; you choose the order by what you want to say first.
result + because + reason
reason + so + result
4 · The words that connect

Five words, five jobs

Read the table. Then look at your explanation from the beginning of the lesson: which of these words did you use?

WordWhat comes after itExample
becausethe reason: subject + verbIt took longer because two laptops had a problem.
because ofthe reason: a nounIt took longer because of the password problem.
sothe result: subject + verbThey arrived at five, so Nora started that evening.
butsomething different: subject + verbNora fixed them, but one laptop is still not working.
whenthe time: subject + verbI will ask for a new one when they call me.
How it sounds In fast speech, because is often short and weak: /bɪkəz/. Before so and but, people often make a small pause, like the comma in writing.
