Lesson 01

The Update
Before the Meeting

In this lesson you read a short update about a problem at work,
and you explain it to your manager before the meeting starts.

Think of a meeting you have at work. Who is there, and what do you usually need to explain?

3 · Read it once

Read it once, for the main point

Read the email once. What is the problem, and what does Ryan need?

From: Ryan Reyes · To: Operations team · Subject: Update: new laptops for the sales team

Sample document.

Hi all,

A quick update before our meeting on Monday. The new laptops for the sales team did not arrive last Friday. The supplier had a problem at their warehouse, and the delivery is now next Thursday.

This is a problem because the sales team has training on the new system next Tuesday. Without the laptops, they cannot practice with the system in the training.

Ryan

5 · The words that open a part

Before the information, the first words

Look at the first words of these three lines. What do they tell Paula before she hears the rest?

“This is a problem because the sales team has training next Tuesday.”
“We have two options: we move the training, or they use their old laptops.”
“I need a decision by Monday.”
An update in four parts The first words of each part tell the other person what kind of information comes next. In a meeting, they can help people follow you, and they can give you a moment to find the next words.
“A quick update: …” — what happened.
“This is a problem because …” — why it matters.
“We have two options: … or …” — the options.
“I need a decision by …” — what you need, and when.
