Your planning

Block 1 — lessons 1 to 4

Four lessons about work meetings, in the same team and the same week: an update you read, the meeting where it is discussed, how to connect your ideas, and your own update.

LessonTopicWhat you will do
07The update before the meetingRead a short update about a problem at work, and explain it to your manager before the meeting. Then answer her questions.
08Sorry, could you say that again?Follow part of a team meeting, and ask questions when something is not clear.
09One idea to the nextConnect the ideas when you explain a problem: the reason, the result, the contrast and the time.
10The weekly check-inGive a short update on your own work in a meeting, answer questions, and ask one.
