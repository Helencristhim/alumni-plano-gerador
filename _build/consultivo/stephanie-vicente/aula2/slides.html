Lesson 02

Three People,
One Material

In this lesson you follow a call about your coursebook,
and you come into it to check the one thing you need.

When were you last on a call where other people disagreed and you did not say anything?

1 · Who is in the call

A call you are on, and not in

The regular call with the publisher’s team. Three people are talking about the unit your group starts next month.

Who is there
Marta, who chairs. Peter and Rachel, who wrote different parts of the unit.
Where you are
On the call. Camera on, and nobody has asked you anything.
What you need
To know whether the second reading text is staying, because your group starts in four weeks.
What nobody will do
Ask you. If the question gets asked, you ask it.

What do you need to know before the call ends?

2 · What to expect

Before you hear it, what would you expect?

Answer with what you expect. You check your answers when you listen to the call.

When two people who wrote a unit disagree about it, what do they usually disagree about?
What does the person chairing normally do when that happens?
What would make you decide to speak?
3 · Listen once

Listen once, for the big picture

Listen to the whole call once, and answer the two questions below.

Speed
How many people are talking, and what is the call for?
Is anyone deciding anything?
4 · Listen again

Who is arguing for what

Listen again and say who says each thing. Open the transcript after you check, to find the line.

Speed
5 · What each move does

Not what they say — what it does

Six lines from the call. What is the speaker doing in each one?

5 · The line that moved the call

The line that moved the call

Before this line, the call was about which part of the unit to cut. After it, it was about something else. What changed, and how did Rachel do it?

Marta: “So we agree the unit is too long. We disagree about which part goes.”
Rachel: “I am not sure we agree it is too long.”
Refuse the summary, not the person Rachel does not argue with Peter’s proposal. She does not accept Marta’s summary of it, and the call moves from what to cut to what order the unit is in.
“I am not sure we agree that…” — refuse the summary.
“It is too long the way it is sequenced.” — accept the fact, change the cause.
“That is a different proposal.” — say what just happened before you answer it.
6 · Coming in

Name what you heard, then ask

Your teacher says a line from the call. Come in: first say what you heard, then ask one thing about it.

“Can I check one thing before we move on?”
“Sorry to jump in — I heard you say the text might move.”
“Just to be clear — is that decided, or is it a proposal?”
“My group starts in four weeks, so it changes what I plan.”
“That was all I needed — sorry, you were saying?”
7 · Your call

Come in, and hand it back

Choose option A or option B. The call plays in three parts and stops after each one. At each stop, decide whether to come in and ask what you need.

Option A — Your group

Your group starts Unit 7 in four weeks. You need to know whether the second reading text is staying.

Option B — The plan

You look after the lesson plan the teachers use. You need to know whether the new order of the unit is decided, and who approves it.

Part
Come in by naming something that was said.
Ask the one thing you need.
Say why it matters to you, in one sentence.
Answer what comes back, and hand the call back to whoever was speaking.
Can I check one thing…
I heard you say…
Just to be clear…
Sorry, you were saying?
7 · After the call

Marta calls you after the call

Marta asks you two questions about what you asked in the call. Answer each one, and then ask her one question back.

Answer the question, and give your reason.
Say which of the two proposals you would go with.
Ask Marta one question about what happens next.
For my group, the better option is…, because…
What I would need from you is…
When will we know if it is approved?
8 · What just happened

What worked, and once more

Let us put down one thing that worked and one thing to build on.

The same stop, and Peter answers

Come in again at the stop where you waited longest. This time Peter answers you, and he wants the text out. Keep your question, and hand the call back.

I hear you, Peter. What I still need to know is…
