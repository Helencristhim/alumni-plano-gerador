Aula 02 · Listening / L1 — Lesson 02 — Before the call starts

4 · Two lines, once

Two lines from the beginning of the call. Listen once, without stopping.

What is the speaker doing?

Giving her own opinion on the coursebook
Opening the item and handing the turn to someone
Asking Peter to change his view
She names the item, names the person and gives the turn away. That is chairing, and it is the whole of what she does.

5 · The same line, listening for something else

Now a line from later in the call. Listen for the numbers and what they belong to.

Speed
eighteen
twenty
seven
Two numbers for the same thing
Eighteen hours, twenty on paper — one is what happens, the other is what a document says. Which is which is not in the words: it is in on paper.
