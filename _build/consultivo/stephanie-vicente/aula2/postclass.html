Speak More

About 60 to 90 seconds

Record a short voice message, if you would like to.

The situation Think of a meeting you were in recently where two people disagreed and you did not speak.

What you are trying to do: say what you would have said, now, with the call already over. Start with what you heard.

You could cover:

  • who was arguing for what;
  • the one thing you needed settled;
  • why it mattered to your own work;
  • what you would have asked, in one sentence.

Write More

About 60 to 90 words

Write the note you would send after that call, if you would like to.

ReaderThe person who chaired, who has three other calls today.
PurposeGet one thing confirmed in writing, without reopening the discussion.
LengthA subject line and one short paragraph.
0 words
Language you might use Just to confirm what we said…  ·  My understanding is that…  ·  If that is not right, let me know before…
