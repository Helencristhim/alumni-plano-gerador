Course syllabus — General English B1, second semester
The semester is divided into four units. Unit 7 is allocated 20 teaching hours, and no unit may run beyond its allocation without approval from the coordination. Written work produced at the end of a unit is corrected by the teacher, with written feedback returned to the student within one week. Each unit is assessed in the week after it ends. This syllabus states what the course commits to; it does not list every task a teacher may use.
