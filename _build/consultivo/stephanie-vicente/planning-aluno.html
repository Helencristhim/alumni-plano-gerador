Your planning

Block 1 — lessons 1 to 4

Four lessons on the conversations your job is made of: documents, calls, observations and feedback.

LessonTopicWhat you will do
1Two documents, one courseFind the three points where the lesson plan and the course syllabus disagree, and brief the coordinator on them.
2Three people, one materialFollow a three-voice call with no transcript, work out who argues what, and come in to check one point.
3The scene and the verdictSeparate what was happening from what happened, and the description of a lesson from the verdict on it.
4The feedback you have to giveGive feedback on an observed lesson so that the teacher can disagree with it — and hold it when he does.
