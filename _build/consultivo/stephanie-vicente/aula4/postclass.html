Lesson recap

A quick reference to the conversation and the language you used.

SituationObservation feedback to a teacher who works in English only, and who is free to disagree.
Opening with what you sawWhat I noticed was… · From where I was sitting…
Recommending so he can refuseOne thing you might try is… · It might be worth… · Have you thought about…?
Holding it under disagreementThat is fair — and the part I would still look at is… · I might be wrong about the reason. What I saw was…
Asking, and meaning itWhat were you trying to do at that point? · How did that feel from where you were?
Key vocabularyto push back · that is fair · to have a point · to act on something
The test that settles it Could he say no, because… and still be talking about the same thing?
If yes, you left him room. If the only answers are yes or a fight, you gave an instruction — however politely it was worded.

Optional practice

Speak More

About 60 to 90 seconds

Record a short voice message, if you would like to.

The situation Think of feedback you have to give in the next few weeks — to a teacher, or to anyone whose work you have watched.

What you are trying to do: say it in a way the person could answer. Start with what you saw, and finish with something they choose.

You could cover:

  • what you saw, without saying whether it was good;
  • what you would ask them before recommending anything;
  • one thing they might try, in a form they can refuse;
  • where you might be wrong.
