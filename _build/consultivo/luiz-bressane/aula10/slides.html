6 · Your question

Quote her, then ask

I give you the claim. You ask the question — in one sentence.

You said… — does that hold for…?
You mentioned… — is that with the same definition of…?
I might be missing something, but…
Before I ask about the data — can I check what counts as…?
7 · The follow-up

Ask it, then hold it

Your teacher is the speaker.
She will present a claim and open the floor.

One question. No writing first.

Quote something she said, in her words.
Ask where that claim stops being true.
If the answer goes somewhere else — say so, and ask again.
Keep it to one sentence. Both times.
Does that hold for…?  ·  Is that with the same definition of…?
That answers… — what I was asking about was…
Sorry — I meant the first year, not the second.
8 · What just happened

Once more, shorter

You heard him do it. Now say yours again.

Shorter is the test
If the second version is longer than the first, the form has not landed yet. If it is shorter and still asks the same thing, it has.
