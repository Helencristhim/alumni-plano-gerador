Lesson 12

Three Minutes,
One Objection

She is a lawyer.
She has never heard of your system.

1 · What she does not know

Three minutes, starting now

Your interlocutor

Sample setting, written for this lesson.

A defence lawyer from another jurisdiction, at a conference. Competent, interested, and with no knowledge of how criminal defence works where you practise.

She has read nothing about the case. She has three minutes before the next session.

Tell her your position. Go.

2 · How it sounds

She interrupts three times

Claire, at the same conference. Listen for what each interruption is doing.

Speed
Which of the three is a real objection, and which two are her catching up?
Is the objection correct?
3 · What she needs first

In, out, or later

Six things you could tell her.

Classify each one, then check.

4 · Micropractice

She is right, and you still hold it

“If the delay explains the inconsistencies, it explains them for both sides.”

Now write one sentence
Your opening line — the first thing she hears. One sentence. Then read it aloud once and put it away: you will not have it in front of you.
5 · Round one

Three minutes, and she will interrupt once

Your teacher is Claire. Nothing in front of you.

Open with what she needs, and only that.
Chain the consequence — do not leave her a list of facts.
When the objection comes: grant it, then give her what she does not have.
Do not go back to the beginning.
In our system…, which means that…
That is fair — it does cut both ways. The difference is…
Where I was going with that is…
6 · Feedback

What she got, and what it cost

What did she need that she did not get?
What did you spend time on that she did not need yet?
When the objection came — did you grant it, deny it, or start again?
7 · Round two

Sixty seconds, and she already knows

Same position. Same objection. She now knows how your system works.

What goes, and what stays?

This is the measure of the block
Cutting the explanation means you read her. Cutting the argument to keep the explanation means you did not.
