Street Food: Latin America — Season 1, Episode 1: São Paulo

Forty minutes inside the streets of your own city, in English. The narrator describes one neighborhood after another: what there is on each corner, how crowded it gets, and what the traffic does to it.

Tip: write down every sentence that starts with there is or there are. You will get more than five in the first ten minutes.
