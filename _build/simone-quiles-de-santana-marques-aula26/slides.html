Chapter 3 -- The Argument Code

In Addition · What Is More · Therefore

Small words that turn reasons into a request

Discovery

Listen and Notice

Two of the orange words add another reason. Two show a result. Which are which? What comes right after each one?

"The files are not clean. Therefore, we need three more weeks."

"The review protects the price. In addition, it protects our credibility."

"The target had a problem with the regulator. As a result, its track record is weak."

"Three weeks is a short delay. What is more, the clearance takes longer anyway."

ConnectorJobExample
in addition, / furthermore,adds a second reason"In addition, it protects our credibility."
what is more,adds the strongest reason, usually last"What is more, the clearance takes longer anyway."
therefore, / as a result,shows the result of what came before"Therefore, we need three more weeks."
that is why + sentenceshows the result, more spoken"That is why I am asking for a review."
so (inside one sentence)a short result, no new sentence"The files are old, so we need a review."

Key idea: these connectors start a new sentence and take a comma: "Therefore, we need ..." The fixed forms are in addition (never "in addition of that") and as a result (never "as result"). Only so lives inside one sentence.

Practice

Add or Conclude?

Say it first, then click to check

"The review protects the price. ___In addition, it protects our credibility." (a second reason)

"The exposure is the whole deal. ___That is why I am asking for a review." (a result, spoken)
The Structure

Reason, Reason, Request

The same three steps in every pitch. Only the facts change.

Step 1 · The reason

"The property files have not been checked since 2019."

One fact. Back it up with a date, a number or a document.

Step 2 · In addition / What is more

"In addition, the target had a problem with the regulator two years ago."

A second reason makes the first one stronger.

Step 3 · Therefore / That is why

"Therefore, I am asking for three more weeks before closing."

Now the request. It sounds like a result, not like a favor.

A pitch that ends with therefore sounds like logic. A pitch that ends with please sounds like a favor.

Artefact

Pitch Note

PITCH NOTE -- DEAL COMMITTEE

Campinas acquisition · Simone Quiles, Legal · Thursday 10:00

Request: three more weeks of review on the property files.

1. Rationale

Property files not checked since 2019. Target had a problem with the regulator two years ago.

2. Evidence

Data room index, page 4. Letter from the regulator, two years ago.

3. Cost of waiting

Three weeks. No change to the closing date: antitrust clearance takes about 90 days.

4. Cost of not waiting

Exposure: the full transaction value, if a hidden liability is in the files.

5. Owner and deadline

Simone Quiles. Review closed by the 30th.

Your line: "Three weeks does not change the date. Therefore, the benefit clearly outweighs the cost."

Dialogue

The Pitch to Javier

J

"You have five minutes. Why should we wait three more weeks?"

S

"Because the property files have not been checked since 2019, and I can back up that point with the data room index."

J

"Old files are normal. That is not a compelling reason for my stakeholders."

S

"By itself, no. In addition, the target had a problem with the regulator two years ago. Its track record is not clean."

J

"And the board? They want to close in March, and New York has already agreed."

S

"The antitrust clearance takes about ninety days anyway. Therefore, three weeks of review does not move the closing date."

J

"So the delay costs us nothing. What does it protect?"

S

"The whole value of the deal. The cost of three weeks does not outweigh that, and I am asking for your buy-in, not only a yes."

Spot the Error

Find the Mistake

0 / 4 errors found

"The files are not clean. Therefore we need more time."
"The files are not clean. Therefore, we need more time." (a comma after the connector)
"The files are old. In addition of that, the target has a weak record."
"The files are old. In addition, the target has a weak record." (in addition, -- no of that)
"The review is cheap. As result, the board can approve it."
"The review is cheap. As a result, the board can approve it." (as a result -- keep the a)
"The review protects the price, what is more it protects our credibility."
"The review protects the price. What is more, it protects our credibility." (a new sentence and a comma)
