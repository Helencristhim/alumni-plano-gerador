Stage 1.1: Words for a company profile

Vocabulary

These are the words that fill an "About us" page. Tap Listen and say each one out loud.

Established — started, and still running — used with a year
"Established in 1994, the firm is now in its third decade."
Track record — everything you have already delivered, as proof you can do it again
"We have a thirty-year track record in payroll."
Workforce — all the people who work for a company, taken together
"Our workforce has grown to a hundred people."
Headquartered — having its main office in a particular city
"We are headquartered in São Paulo."
Compliance — following the rules and the law, and being able to prove it
"Compliance is the first thing a foreign client asks about."
Headcount — the number of people a company employs, as a figure
"Our headcount went from sixty to a hundred in four years."
Set up — to create something and get it running (past is also "set up")
"We set up the payroll division in 2018."
Serve — to work for a group of clients and look after their needs
"We serve clients across every service industry."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

A short company profile. Read it once, then answer.

Meridian was established in 2003 and is headquartered in Lisbon. The firm has been serving international companies for more than twenty years.

Since 2019 its workforce has doubled, and its headcount now stands at eighty. Meridian serves a hundred and forty clients, and most of them have been with the firm for over five years.

Compliance sits at the centre of the business: every process has been audited every year since 2015. The firm set up its Brazilian desk in 2021.
Is Meridian still serving international companies today?
A Yes — "has been serving" means it started before and has not stopped.
B No — it served them for twenty years and then stopped.
Why is it "for more than twenty years" but "since 2019"?
A Because one is about the firm and the other about the staff.
B Because for measures how long, and since marks when it started.
Why is "set up its Brazilian desk in 2021" in the past simple?
A Because it happened once, on a date, and finished.
B Because the Brazilian desk has already closed.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

Still true → have been. Finished → past simple.

On a call, this is not a grammar point. It is whether the client thinks you are still in business.

for + how long

for thirty years
for over two decades
for six months

since + the starting point

since 1994
since we opened
since the pandemic

Say "we worked in payroll for thirty years" and a prospect hears that you closed that division. Say "we have worked" and thirty years becomes an argument instead of an obituary.

+ We have been in this market for over thirty years.
− We have not had a single payroll error this year.
? How long have you been in this market? (not "How long are you")
⚠ One finished moment with a date: We set up the BPO division in 2018. (past simple, not "have set up")

Stage 4: On the call

Quiz
A prospect asks how long you have been in the market. Which answer helps you win the deal?
A "We worked in this market for thirty years."
B "We’ve been in this market for over thirty years."
You want to say when the BPO division started. It opened in 2018 and still runs.
A "We set up the BPO division in 2018."
B "We have set up the BPO division in 2018."
Which sentence answers a compliance question best?
A "Yes, we do compliance too."
B "Every process we run has been audited every year since 2015."
