The Pattern

Still true, or finished?

We have been in this market for over thirty years.
I have worked in accounting since 1994.
Our workforce has grown to a hundred people.
We set up the BPO division three years ago.

Started before, still true now → have / has + past participle

One finished moment with a date or "ago" → past simple. Say "we worked in payroll for thirty years" and the client hears that you stopped.

The Pattern

For measures. Since marks the start.

for + how long

for thirty years
for over two decades
for six months

since + when it started

since 1994
since the pandemic
since we opened

Listening 1

"How long have you been doing this?"

1. How long has his company been in Brazil?
Eighteen months — and he says it feels like five years.
2. What went wrong with their last provider?
The provider had only been running for two years and could not handle the volume.
3. What does he say he cares about more than price?
How long the firm has been doing it, and whether it has been audited.
Listening 2

Somebody else’s two minutes

1. When was the firm established, and where is it headquartered?
In 2003, headquartered in Lisbon.
2. What has happened to their headcount?
It has doubled since 2019 — from forty to eighty.
3. What does she offer at the end?
To send two client references before any pricing conversation.
