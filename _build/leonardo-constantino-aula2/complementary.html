A Los Angeles defense lawyer takes on a case that arrives out of nowhere, and the whole first episode is people explaining what they have done and when they did it. Connection to Lesson 2: this is fiction you can enjoy, but the client meetings are full of background questions and career stories in the two tenses you studied today.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down three sentences with "have / has + past participle" and three in the past simple, then say why each one uses that tense.
