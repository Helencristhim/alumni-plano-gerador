Lesson 7 -- Must Not Is Not the Same as Do Not Have To

Talk
Leana Wen -- "What your doctor will not disclose"

An emergency physician on what doctors tell patients and what they keep to themselves. The talk is built on the difference between what a doctor must do and what a doctor should do.

Tip: write down three sentences she says with should, must or have to. Then say each one to an imaginary patient.

Talk
Stefan Larsson -- "What doctors can learn from each other"

On why outcomes differ so much between hospitals doing the same procedure. Clear, slow English, and a subject that sits exactly between your two careers.

Tip: after watching, say out loud what a hospital should do, what it must do, and what it does not have to do.

TED-Ed -- "What causes headaches?"

A short animated lesson with comprehension questions on a complaint you hear every day. The vocabulary is the patient's, not the specialist's.

Tip: answer the questions out loud. Then give an imaginary patient three instructions about it: one must not, one should, one might.
