Talk
Sheena Iyengar — How to make choosing easier (TED)

Sixteen minutes on how people decide, and on why too many options make us avoid deciding at all. It is a talk about decisions, so listen for the verbs from this lesson: decide to, choose to, avoid -ing, keep -ing.

Tip: write down every verb that is followed by another verb, and put it in one of two columns: to or -ing. Then add one sentence about your own close to each column.
