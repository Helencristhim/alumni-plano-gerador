The Call · Round 1

Ten Minutes With Somebody Who Did It

Marina picked up on the second ring and she has ten minutes before a meeting. She has done this six years and she will answer anything — but only what you ask. You are yourself.

  • Block 1: the process — the application, how long, what she would do differently.
  • Block 2: the money — the rent, the commute, what she could afford in year one.
  • Block 3: the part nobody puts in a spreadsheet.

The Call · Round 2

Now You Are the One Who Left

Same call, other chair. You left five years ago and it worked. I am Juliana, twenty-nine, from Campinas, with an offer on the table and six weeks to answer. I am scared and I am asking you to tell me the truth.

  • Block 1: I ask, you answer — and every answer has a consequence in it.
  • Block 2: three pieces of advice, each one from something you actually lived.

Closing

One Minute, No Notes

Your husband asks you tonight: “So — are we doing this or not?” You have one minute. Go.

I can say what I will do if something happens, without putting will in the wrong half.
I can ask somebody a real question about their life abroad and keep asking for ten minutes.
I can give advice with a consequence in it, instead of just saying it will be fine.
