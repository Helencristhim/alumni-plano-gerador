Series
Emily in Paris — Season 1, Episode 1, "Emily in Paris"

A thirty-something takes a job in a city where she knows nobody and does not speak the language. The first episode is one long lesson in what actually happens in week one: the apartment, the neighborhood, the colleagues who do not make room for you, and the moment you realize that being good at your job is not the same as fitting in.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down every sentence with if in it. Then write your own version of three of them, about your own year.

Podcast
NPR Life Kit — "These journal prompts will help you figure out your next big life move"

An American psychology professor on how people actually decide to change their lives: the questions that separate a plan from a daydream, and the ones that stop you from moving for the wrong reason. Normal conversational speed, transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen once with the transcript closed. Then answer two of his questions out loud in English, in full sentences, starting each one with If.

Rachel's English — "Intro to Linking: American English Pronunciation"

Why if I get a job there comes out as one sound and not six words. She takes the joins apart one at a time, so you can hear where a word ends and the next one starts — which is exactly what stops being obvious at full speed.

Tip: after the video, say "If I get the offer, I will take it" ten times, faster each time, until the first five words stop being five words.
