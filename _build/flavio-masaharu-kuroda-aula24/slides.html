ANNEX A · MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS
Invent Match · response to the Vetra Group RFP · draft by Flávio Kuroda
2.1 Data stored in the EUWe confirm that all data is stored in Frankfurt.
2.2 Integration with SAP S/4HANAWe confirm that the connector is included.
2.3 Support in English and ItalianRegarding 2.3, support is provided in both languages.
3.2 Pricing schedule in eurosCompleted in accordance with Annex B.
5.1 ReferencesPlease find attached two references in Europe.
Left column: the requirement and its number. Right column: one formal sentence, in the order of the RFP. No adjectives, no marketing, no contractions.
1. Read the answer to 2.1 as you would say it to Giulia on the phone.
Yes, all our data is in Frankfurt, so it's in the EU.
2. Why does every answer repeat the number of the requirement?
Because the evaluator scores each requirement in order, and must find each answer fast.
3. What could disqualify this submission, even with a good price?
A mandatory requirement without an answer, a change in the format of the pricing schedule, or a submission sent by email.
Chapter 6: Your Turn

Two Recordings, Then the Clarification Call

Listening 1 · The Procurement Director

Thirty Bids a Year

A procurement director on how she scores formal offers. Sound first — no text.

1. How do most suppliers lose, according to her?
Not on price, but on the rules.
2. What two mistakes does she describe?
Changing the format of the pricing schedule, and answering a mandatory requirement with brochure text.
3. What advice does she give about clarification questions?
Send them early, because every bidder sees the answer and a good question can change the process.
Listening 2 · The Bid Manager

Forty Minutes Late

A bid manager on the first RFP his company lost. Sound first — no text.

1. Why did they lose their first RFP?
They read the deadline in Sao Paulo time and were forty minutes late.
2. What do they do on the first day now?
One person reads the whole RFP and lists every mandatory requirement.
3. When do they upload the submission now?
One full day before the deadline, in the customer's time zone.
