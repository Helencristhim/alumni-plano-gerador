The RFP -- Answering a Formal Request for Proposal

The document a large customer publishes to compare suppliers, and how to answer it in the formal register of a written bid. Key words: an RFP, a bidder, mandatory, a submission, evaluation criteria, to disqualify, eligible, to award, a pricing schedule, a clarification question. Structure: formal register in written bids (we confirm that, are required to, in accordance with, regarding).

A bidder -- a company that sends an offer to win a contract

Mandatory -- that you must do or have, with no exception
"Support in Italian is a mandatory requirement."
A submission -- the complete offer that a supplier sends
"Our submission was uploaded two days before the deadline."
Evaluation criteria -- the points that are used to score and compare offers
"Price is only thirty-five percent of the evaluation criteria."
To disqualify -- to remove a company from a process because it broke a rule
"They can disqualify a bidder that changes the format."
Eligible -- allowed to take part because you meet the conditions
"Only suppliers with an office in the EU are eligible."
To award -- to officially give a contract to one company

"The pricing schedule must be completed in euros."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

On the phone, you can say: yes, we do that. In a written bid, the same idea becomes: we confirm that all customer data is stored in the EU. The register changes because the reader is an evaluator, not a friend, and every sentence can be scored.

The RFP also uses formal English to give rules. Bidders are required to submit through the portal. The pricing schedule must be completed in accordance with Annex B. When you answer, you use the same words: the table was completed in accordance with Annex B.

Regarding introduces the topic of your answer: regarding requirement 2.3, support is provided in Italian. Please find attached introduces a document. And when you cannot do something, you write we are unable to, not we can't.

In a bid there are no contractions and no chat words. Brazilian speakers often write in accordance to, or are required to submitting, or please find attach. All three are wrong.

1. Which sentence is correct for a written bid?
A "We're confirming that our data is in the EU, no problem at all."
B "We confirm that all customer data is stored in the EU."
C "Yes, we do that."
2. Which sentence is correct?
A "The table was completed in accordance to Annex B, as the RFP asks."
B "Bidders are required to submit through the portal."
C "Bidders are required to submitting through the portal."
3. Why does a bid avoid contractions like we're and don't?
A Because the reader is an evaluator, and the formal register is expected.
B Because contractions are always grammatically wrong in English, in emails, in calls and in every other situation.
C Because the portal cannot read the apostrophe.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Formal Register in a Bid

GRAMMAR

The same idea, in the voice of a written bid. No contractions.

On the phoneIn a bidExample
yes, we do thatWe confirm that + clauseWe confirm that data is stored in the EU.

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the answer a real speaker would give.

The RFP asks if your data is stored in the EU. You write:
A "Yes, we do that, and our data center is also very modern and secure."
B "We're in the EU, no problem."
C "We confirm that all customer data is stored in Frankfurt, in the EU."
A rule in the RFP is not clear to you. You:
A "Send a clarification question through the portal, before the date in the RFP."
B "Call Giulia and ask her to tell you the answer privately, so the other bidders do not know."
C "Change the rule in your submission."
You cannot offer a free trial. You write:
A "We can't do trials, sorry."
B "We do not believe a free trial is a good idea for a project of this size and importance."
C "Regarding requirement 4.2, we are unable to offer a free trial."
When do you upload the submission?
A "Five minutes before the deadline in Sao Paulo time, because then the competitors cannot see our price."
B "One full day before the deadline, in the customer's time zone."
