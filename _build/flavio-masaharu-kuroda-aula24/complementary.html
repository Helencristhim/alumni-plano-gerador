Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Writing notes"

How to write short, clear notes at work: what to keep, what to cut, and how the tone changes when the reader is more formal. Full script on the same page.

Tip: take one informal sentence from the script and rewrite it for a formal bid, with we confirm that or regarding.

Podcast
6 Minute English (BBC Learning English) -- "Rhetoric: How persuasive are you?"

The old art of convincing people with words, and why facts and structure persuade more than big adjectives. Exactly the style an evaluator wants to read.

Tip: read the script and write down two words that a bid should never use, and two that it should.

Talk
Barry Schwartz -- "The paradox of choice" (TED, 20 min)

Why more options can make people choose worse. It explains why procurement teams use fixed evaluation criteria to compare bidders.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write one formal sentence that starts with Regarding about one idea of the talk.
