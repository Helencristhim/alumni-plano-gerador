Chapter 4: The Code

There Is, There Are

Two phrases for every place

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"There is an office in São Paulo."

"There are many clients."

"There is not a lot of English every day."

"Is there an office in New York?"

Look at the blue words and the word after them. When is it is, and when is it are?

JobFormExample
One thingthere is + a / anThere is an office.
Two or more thingsthere are + pluralThere are many clients.
Negativethere is not / there are noThere are no stores in New York.
QuestionIs there...? / Are there...?Are there banks in your list?
Short answersYes, there is. / No, there are not.Is there an office in São Paulo? Yes, there is.
The trapnever "have" for a placeNot Have many banks. Say There are many banks.

In one line: one thing: there is; two or more: there are. Never "have" to say what exists in a place.
