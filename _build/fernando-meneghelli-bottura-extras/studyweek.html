Your Study Week

Your plan for about five hours a week outside class, in blocks of twenty minutes. Every block has one clear job. If a day goes wrong, skip it and follow the next day. Do not try to catch up.

The one rule. Do the Pre-class of a lesson BEFORE the class of that lesson. The class then uses what you prepared, instead of starting from zero. If you are not sure which lesson comes next, ask your teacher at the end of each class.

After every class

  • Open the class summary tab (the last tab at the top of this page). Read three parts: the words you did not understand, pronunciation, and corrections.
  • Do the Word Review of that lesson on the same day or the next day.
  • Anything from the summary that is still hard goes into your next Word Review 04 session.

How to keep new words

  • Day 1: the full Word Review of the lesson.
  • Day 3: the same review again, only the items you got wrong.
  • Day 7: only the speaking stage. Say each sentence without reading it first.
  • Seeing a word at the right time works better than seeing it ten times in one day.

How to train your ear

  • Real-Speed English teaches the sounds an interviewer really makes. Start at 0.75x and always finish at 1x.
  • Accents: read the questions before you listen. Open the transcript only at the end.
  • Series & Films: three passes, English subtitles, no subtitles, then speak along for one minute.
  • Your brain fills in the words it already knows. Every block here adds to that stock.

The week of a real interview

  • Two days before: the Accent card closest to your interviewer, twice.
  • The day before: Real-Speed English 01 and 04, then record your career story from Word Review 01.
  • One hour before: listen to one Accent card at 1x. No new words on the day.
