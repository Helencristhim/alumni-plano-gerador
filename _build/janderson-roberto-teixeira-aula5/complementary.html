"Where is home?" -- Pico Iyer, TED

Fourteen minutes from a travel writer who has spent his life between airports and countries. Connection to Lesson 5: almost every sentence he says about the future is one of the two forms you practised today, and he is a model of pace -- slow, clear, and never in a hurry. The page carries the full English transcript beside the video.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles on and once without. Write down two sentences about your own next trip in the same shape as his.

Podcast
"Are you flourishing?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Six minutes about plans, goals and what people say they are going to do with their lives. Connection to Lesson 5: it is full of both futures, used naturally and side by side, which is the only way to hear the difference between a plan and a promise. Audio, vocabulary and full transcript are free on the page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then open it and underline every going to and every will. Ask yourself, for each one, when the decision was taken.

Grammar Video
"How to talk about the future" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on today's exact choice: the arrangement you already made against the decision you take while speaking, plus the evidence rule. Connection to Lesson 5: it drills the one distinction that decides whether you sound organised or vague in a meeting.

Tip: pause after each example and say one true sentence about your own diary. Then say one promise you would make right now, out loud.
