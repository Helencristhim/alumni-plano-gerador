Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart -- Episode 109, "Simplify! How to Communicate Complex Ideas Simply and Effectively"

A Stanford professor on why experts lose the room: they explain the mechanism when the listener only needs the decision. Connection to Lesson 8: this is exactly what the general counsel asked you for -- where we stand, what you recommend, numbers instead of adjectives.

Tip: after listening, take the last client email you wrote in Portuguese and rewrite the first paragraph in English in three sentences. If it takes more than three, cut until it does not.
