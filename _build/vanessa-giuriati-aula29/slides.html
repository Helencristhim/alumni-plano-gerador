Chapter 3

Been Feeling

For things that started before and are still going

Grammar Discovery

Finished, or Still Going?

"I've been feeling a bit run down lately."

"She's been sleeping badly for weeks."

"How long have you been dealing with this?"

"I've been trying to look after myself more."

FormUseExample
have / has been + -ingStarted in the past and still going (or just stopped)I've been feeling run down lately.
How long have you been + -ing?Ask about the length of something ongoingHow long have you been dealing with this?
for / since / lately / recentlyTypical time wordsShe's been sleeping badly since March.
know, like, want (no -ing)Some verbs are not used in the continuousI've known her for years.
⚠ CarefulThe continuous shows the PROCESS: 'I've been working late'. For a RESULT or a number, use the simple form: 'I've had three coffees today'. With 'since' or 'for', never the simple present: 'I have been feeling tired since Monday', not 'I am feeling tired since Monday'. And 'take a toll on something'.

Listening

Recharging Is a Skill

A different voice, a different message. Listen carefully.

1. What did the speaker use to think about rest?
That rest was what happened when the work was finished.
2. What three things has he been doing for about a year?
He walks for twenty minutes after lunch, switches off his work phone at seven, and cooks on Sundays.
3. What is his lesson at the end?
Pace yourself, or your body will do it for you.
