Chapter 3: The Code

It Was Done

When the thing matters more than the person

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The house was built in the nineteen forties."

"The floors were sanded down by hand."

"The kitchen is being painted this week."

"A builder knocked down that wall."

The green ones start with the thing. The blue one starts with the person. Which two words appear in every green sentence?

FormUseExample
Past passive
was / were + past participle		Something that happened to the thing. Finished.The house was built in 1946.
Present passive
is / are + past participle		A general fact or a current state.Old beams are treated against damp.
Happening now
is / are being + past participle		The work is going on right now, unfinished.The kitchen is being painted.
by + agentOnly when WHO did it is interesting or surprising.The floors were sanded by hand.
Active vs passiveActive starts with the person. Passive starts with the thing.A builder knocked it down. / It was knocked down.

The rule in one line: be + past participle, and the thing comes first.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"The house built in 1946."
"The house was built in 1946."
"The pipe was broke last winter."
"The pipe was broken last winter."
"The wall was knocked down for a builder."
"The wall was knocked down by a builder."

The passive always needs be plus the past participle, never the simple past. And the person who did the work comes after by, never after "for".

Detail

Keep the by, or Drop It?

Keep it only when who did the work is interesting

"The floors were sanded down ___by me, over three weekends -- keep it"
"The letters are delivered ___by the postman -- drop it, it is obvious"
"The table was made ___by my grandfather -- keep it"
"My car was stolen last night ___by someone -- drop it, nobody knows"

T
