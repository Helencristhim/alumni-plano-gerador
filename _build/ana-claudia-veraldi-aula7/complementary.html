Lesson 7 -- The House I Am Restoring

Series
Restoration Home -- Stoke Hall, Episode Three (full episode)

A house that had been left to fall down, taken apart and put back together over a year. Every single thing that happens to that building is described in the exact structure you worked on tonight.

Tip: the narration is almost all passive. Write down six sentences you hear with was, is being or has been plus a participle.
