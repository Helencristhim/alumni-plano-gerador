Lesson 7 -- The House I’m Restoring

Video
Ash Mazzina -- "3 Years (in 15 Minutes) Renovating an Old London Victorian Terraced House"

Three years of a real renovation, told from start to finish: the damp, the wiring, the beams, the floors that were stripped and sanded down. It is your house, in English, with a British accent to train your ear.

Tip: every time she shows a finished room, say the sentence out loud yourself: "The floor was sanded down", "The wall was knocked down".

Listening
BBC Learning English -- "Building a better world with wood?" (6 Minute English)

Six minutes on why we are building with wood again, and what that does to the buildings we live in. Two natural British speakers, at a speed you can follow, on exactly tonight’s topic.

Tip: the presenters use the passive constantly ("timber is treated", "the frame was made"). Count five of them, then write your own five about your house.

Listen on YouTube ↗
Talk
TED -- Corentin Fivet, "Let’s reuse building parts!"

An architect argues that old beams, doors and windows should be salvaged and used again instead of thrown away. It is the idea behind your whole workshop, taken to the scale of a city.

Tip: turn on the English subtitles the second time. Catch one sentence with "is reused" or "was built" and repeat it until it feels easy.
