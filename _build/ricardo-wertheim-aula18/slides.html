Chapter 4: The Code

Was Built, Was Designed

When the building is more important than the builder

Grammar Discovery

Who Did It, or What Was Done?

"The Triple Bridge was designed by Plecnik."

"Two footbridges were added in the 1930s."

"The city was hit by an earthquake in 1895."

"Fresh fruit is sold at the market every morning."

What comes first: the person who did it, or the thing that was done? Now look at the orange words: which verb comes first, and what form is the second one?

TimeFormExample
present (always, every day)is / are + participleFresh fruit is sold every morning.
past (finished)was / were + participleThe bridge was designed in 1929.
who did it (if it matters)+ by + personIt was designed by Plecnik.
questionsWhen was it built? · Who was it designed by?When was the library completed?
irregular participlesbuild → built · make → made · hit → hitIt was built in stone.
the trapNot it was build and not it built in 1932. Say it was built in 1932.

In one line: when the building matters more than the builder, put the building first and use was or is + participle.
