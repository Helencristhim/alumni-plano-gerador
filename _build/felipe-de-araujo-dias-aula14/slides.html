Chapter 4: If, Unless, As Soon As

The Present That Talks About Tomorrow

The first conditional

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"If the ship leaves on Thursday, it will arrive on the 26th."

"If it doesn't leave, you will miss your date."

"Unless I call, the plan stays the same."

"I will call you as soon as the port confirms."

FormUse it forExample
If + present, will + verbA real possibility in the future, and its result.If it leaves on Thursday, it will arrive on the 26th.
will + verb + if + presentThe same idea, result first. No comma.We will miss the launch if it arrives late.
unless + presentIf not. Only the exception.Unless I call, the plan stays the same.
as soon as / when + presentThe moment something happens.I will call you as soon as it arrives.
might / can / imperativeOther results are possible too.If it is late, call me. If it rains, it might slow down.

Never will after if. The if half is the condition, in the present. The will half is the result.

Grammar Practice

Condition and Result

Say the full sentence, then click to compare

1. If the container ______ (arrive) after the 28th, we ______ (miss) the launch.
"If the container arrives after the 28th, we will miss the launch."
2. ______ we pay for air freight, the prints will not be in the stores.
"Unless we pay for air freight, the prints will not be in the stores."
3. I ______ (call) you as soon as the port ______ (confirm).
"I will call you as soon as the port confirms."
4. If we ______ (not have) enough buffer stock, the stores ______ (notice).
"If we don't have enough buffer stock, the stores will notice."
