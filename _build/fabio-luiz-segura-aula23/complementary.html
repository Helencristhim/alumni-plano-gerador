Talk
Daniel Levitin — “How to stay calm when you know you’ll be stressed” (TED)

A neuroscientist explains why the brain thinks less clearly under stress and fatigue, and how to plan ahead for those moments. It is the science behind cognitive load on day two of a conference.

Tip: write down his idea of a “pre-mortem”. Use it to plan the afternoon of day two in Las Vegas.

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart (Stanford GSB) — “Um, Like, So: How Filler Words Can Be Effective in Communication”

A linguist explains what filler words really do in a conversation, and when a short pause works better. Perfect for this lesson: buy time without sounding lost.

Tip: notice when the guest recommends a pause instead of a filler word. Compare it with your two-second pause.

Matt Abrahams — “Think Fast, Talk Smart: Communication Techniques” (Stanford GSB, YouTube)

A Stanford lecturer shows how to answer well when you are under pressure and have no time to prepare. Many of his techniques work as recovery language.
