Chapter 3: The Rule

One Word Does All the Work

E o verbo pode descansar.

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

O que vem logo depois de didn't?

I didn't sleep the night before.

I didn't apply for the job.

They didn't ask about my English.

She didn't decide that day.

Depois de didn't o verbo está pelado: sleep, apply, ask, decide. Mas as frases são do passado… então onde está o passado?

Afirmativo (aula 14)Negativo (hoje)
I slept well.I didn't sleep well.
I applied for the job.I didn't apply for the job.
They asked about my English.They didn't ask about my English.
A regra em uma linha: o passado está no did. Como o didn't já carrega o passado, o verbo volta à forma pelada: didn't sleep, didn't apply, didn't go. Um passado por frase — nunca dois. E vale para I, you, she, we, they: o didn't é o mesmo para todos.
The Relief

In the Negative, the Irregulars Disappear

Os seis verbos que você decorou na aula 14 — e o que acontece com eles hoje

Aula 14 — afirmativoAula 15 — negativo
I beganI didn't begin
I gotI didn't get
I becameI didn't become

Repare no que isto significa na prática: no negativo você não precisa do irregular. Se a forma do passado não vier à cabeça, a frase negativa te salva — e ela está certa, não é atalho.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"I didn't went to the office."
"I didn't go to the office."
"I not applied for the job."
"I didn't apply for the job."

O primeiro erro é pôr dois passados na mesma frase: o didn't já é passado, então o went sobra — fica go. O segundo é usar o not sozinho, como em português (“eu não me candidatei”). Em inglês o not precisa de um ajudante na frente, e no passado esse ajudante é sempre o did.

Chapter 4: The Questions

Now — Did you…?

A mesma peça, virada do avesso.

The Three Shapes

Negative, Yes-No, and Wh-

NEGATIVO → DIDN'T

"I didn't apply."

"They didn't ask."

verbo pelado depois

SIM / NÃO → DID…?

"Did you apply?"

"Did they hire her?"

Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

WH- → WHAT DID…?

"What did they ask?"

"When did you decide?"

as palavras da aula 8, + did

É uma regra só, usada três vezes. Em todas elas o did carrega o passado, e por isso o verbo fica pelado: didn't apply, Did you apply?, What did they ask? Nada de didn't applied, nada de Did you asked?

Comprehension

What Did Greg Learn?

1. Greg asks how Paula came to Zurich. What is the answer?
She didn't apply. Sofia called her in March last year, after a broker gave Sofia her name. She didn't even know the company.
2. What happened the night before the interview?
She didn't sleep — not one hour. She was very nervous.
3. Greg asks what they asked her. What did they ask — and what did they NOT ask?
They asked about large risks for twenty minutes. They didn't ask about her English — not one question.
4. Why did she say yes in the end?
Because they didn't want perfect English — they wanted a real story, and she had one. The offer came two weeks later, and she didn't decide that day: she took a week.
The Artifact

The Panel's Notes

CandidatePaula Morales

1. How did Paula reach this interview?
She didn't apply. The record says "referral" — someone gave her name to Sofia, and the company called her.
2. Did the panel test her English?
No, they didn't. The line says NO. They spent twenty minutes on large risks and ten on her career — and zero on English.
3. When did the offer arrive?
Two weeks after the interview. And she didn't decide that day — she took one week to answer.
4. Look at the line "English tested: NO". What does that tell you about why they hired you?
There is no wrong answer here — say it out loud, in English, with a negative. For example: "They didn't hire me for my English. They hired me for my work."
