Lesson 15 -- What Happened? What Didn't Happen?: The Past in the Negative, and the Zurich Interview

YouTube
Simple Past Negative — English Tenses for Beginners (engVid, with Ronnie, free)

A short, direct class about the past negative only — exactly the piece you learned today. The teacher writes on the board and hammers the one thing that matters: after didn't the verb goes back to its simple form. It is the same rule as your lesson, in another voice and with other examples, and hearing it twice from different people is what makes a rule automatic.

Tip: watch it with English subtitles (the video's own CC). Do one thing only: every time she writes a sentence with didn't, pause and say ONE sentence of your own in the same mould, about the Zurich interview (“I didn't apply”, “They didn't ask about my English”). By the end of the video you should have said about five. Open channel, no sign-up.

Podcast / audio
Past Simple — Negative and Interrogative Form + QUIZ (Sparkle English, free)

The other half of your lesson: here the explanation puts the negative (didn't) and the question (Did you…?) in the same place, which is exactly how you will use them in a real conversation — a question, a negative answer, another question. And there is a quiz at the end, so you leave knowing whether you got it. The voice is calm and the pace is slow, made for beginners.

Tip: listen twice. The first time, just listen. The second time, pause at each Did question and answer out loud with a FULL sentence (“No, I didn't…”), never just “no”. Leave the quiz for last and do not pause during it — it measures what actually stuck. Open and free, no sign-up.

Let's Learn English — Level 2, Lesson 2: The Interview (VOA Learning English, free)

It is not a grammar class: it is a job interview acted out, with subtitles on screen and American English spoken slowly on purpose. Pete is interviewed by Ms. Weaver, and you hear the real questions a panel asks — including the past ones, with did. It is the same scene you lived through at Zurich a year ago, now with the text on screen so you can READ and HEAR at once.

Tip: watch it once only and do one thing: write down every question that starts with Did or with What / When / Why did. Then close the video and answer those same questions out loud, about YOUR Zurich interview. You do not have to understand every word; the goal is to get your mouth answering in the past without freezing. VOA is an American public service, free and with no sign-up.
