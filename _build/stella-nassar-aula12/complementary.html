Modern Art & Ideas -- The Museum of Modern Art on Coursera

A free online course from MoMA built around themes such as places and spaces, identity and everyday objects, with videos and interviews with artists. A natural next step after this program.

Tip: take one module per week and write a 150-word response in your Writing Studio document, using one style move from the program each time.

Talk
The power of believing that you can improve -- Carol Dweck, TED

A Stanford psychologist on the difference between thinking of ability as fixed and thinking of it as something that grows, and why the word yet matters.

Tip: after watching, write the two style moves you still need to practice as sentences that contain the word yet.

Film
Ferris Bueller's Day Off (1986)

A classic comedy about three friends who skip school in Chicago, including a famous, silent sequence inside an art museum that ends with a slow walk toward a pointillist painting.

Tip: watch the museum sequence closely. What does the camera do with the painting, and what does that say about looking?
