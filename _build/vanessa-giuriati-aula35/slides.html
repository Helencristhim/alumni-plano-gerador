Chapter 3

This Time Tomorrow

A photo of your future, in the middle of the action

Grammar Discovery

In the Middle of It

"Don't call at ten tomorrow. I'll be flying to Recife."

"This time next week, we'll be presenting the project."

"Will you be using the meeting room this afternoon?"

"Sorry, on Friday I won't be working. It's a holiday."

FormUseExample
will be + -ingAn action in progress at a moment in the futureAt ten tomorrow, I'll be flying.
won't be + -ingSay what you will not be doingI won't be working on Friday.
Will you be + -ing...?Ask about someone's plans politelyWill you be joining us for lunch?
this time next week / at ten tomorrowTime expressions that go well with itThis time next week, we'll be presenting.
⚠ CarefulNever forget 'be': 'I will be traveling', not 'I will traveling'. Verbs of state (know, like, need, believe) don't usually take -ing: 'I'll know tomorrow', not 'I'll be knowing'.

Comprehension

Did You Get It?

1. Who does Daniel want to have a call with?
The Lisbon team.
2. Why can't Daniel do the call at two on Wednesday?
He double-booked: at two he'll be meeting the auditors.
3. What will Daniel do if he is running late?
He'll send Vanessa a message.
Listening

The Softest Question

Listen first. No text. Sound only.

1. Which of the two questions is softer?
"Will you be using the meeting room this afternoon?"
2. Why does it sound softer?
It asks about your plan, not about a decision. It does not sound like the speaker wants something.
3. Who uses this kind of question all the time?
Receptionists, flight attendants and good colleagues.

Detective

Spot the Error

0 / 4 errors found

"This time tomorrow, I will flying to Recife."
"This time tomorrow, I will be flying to Recife." (don't forget "be")
"Will you be join us on Friday?"
"Will you be joining us on Friday?" (be + -ing)
"I'll be knowing the answer tomorrow."
"I'll know the answer tomorrow." (know is a verb of state)
"Can we bring forward it to Monday?"
"Can we bring it forward to Monday?" (the pronoun goes in the middle)
