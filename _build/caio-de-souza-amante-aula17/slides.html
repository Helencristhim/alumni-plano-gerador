The Core

The Negotiation Table

I am the buyer’s lead negotiator, and I write down every number you say. Give me numbers with their limits: a source, a range, a condition.

  • Round 1: the numbers. Five questions about revenue, margin and price.
  • Round 2: the push. I try to turn your range into a promise; you keep the limits without sounding weak.
  • Round 3: swap. You are the buyer, and you push me.

Precise, Not Promised

A buyer asks for one number you are not sure about yet. What is your first sentence?

I can give a number with a source, a range or a condition.
I can say that something is not final: indicative, preliminary, provisional.
