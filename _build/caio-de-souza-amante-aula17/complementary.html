Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart: “Communicating Uncertainty” (Stanford GSB)

How to speak to an audience when the answers are not clear yet, and why being transparent about what you do not know earns trust. It is the listener’s side of every hedge in this lesson.

Tip: write down two phrases the guest uses to talk about uncertainty, and use each one in a sentence about Dataside’s numbers.

Listen on Stanford GSB ↗
Talk
Kathryn Schulz: “On Being Wrong”

A writer explains why being wrong feels exactly like being right until the moment we discover it. A good reason to give your numbers their limits before a buyer does it for you.

Tip: about eighteen minutes. After watching, write one firm sentence about Dataside and rewrite it with a hedge.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Julia Galef: “Why You Think You’re Right, Even If You’re Wrong”

A short talk about the difference between defending a position and trying to see clearly. Listen to how she qualifies her own claims: she is precise without being absolute.

Tip: twelve minutes. Note every time she uses a hedge (I think, it seems, probably) and decide if it makes her stronger or weaker.
