Chapter 4: Have It Done

Who Does the Work?

have / get + something + done

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I'd like to have my suit pressed before the fair."

"Could I get my shirts cleaned by tomorrow morning?"

"We'll have the thermostat checked right away."

"I had my bags stored at the front desk."

Look at the orange words. Who does the work: the guest, or the hotel? What is the order of the words?

FormMeaningExample
have + object + past participlesomeone else does the work for youI'd like to have my suit pressed.
get + object + past participlethe same, a little more informalCould I get my shirts cleaned?
had + object + past participlethe pastI had my bags stored.
will have + object + past participlethe hotel promises a serviceWe'll have it checked.

Order: have + the thing + done. Compare: "I pressed my suit" (I did it) and "I had my suit pressed" (the hotel did it).
