Chapter 3: The Pattern

The Adjective Goes First

friendly · a friendly leader

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

Claire is friendly.
She is a friendly leader.
The office is modern.
It is a modern office.

Two sentences say it after IS. Two say it before the thing. Where does the adjective go?

UseFormExample
After bebe + adjectiveClaire is friendly.
Before the nouna / the + adjective + nouna modern plant, a funny man
Pluralthe adjective never takes -Stwo tall buildings — never "talls"
Two adjectivesadjective + and + adjectiveThe streets are small and quiet.
Strongervery + adjectiveHe is very funny.
CarefulNever "a leader friendly" or "an office modern". To ask for a description, say "What is he like?" — "How is he?" asks if he is OK.
