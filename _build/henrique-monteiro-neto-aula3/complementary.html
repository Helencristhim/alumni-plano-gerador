Lesson 3 -- The Art of Disagreeing Well

Talk
"How to Disagree Productively and Find Common Ground" — Julia Dhar, TED

Twelve minutes from a three-time world debate champion on why most disagreements fail before the first argument is made. Connection to Lesson 3: her three techniques are the spoken version of today's vocabulary — common ground, conceding, and refusing the straw man. The full English transcript is on the page.

Tip: watch once with subtitles, then pick the single technique you use least and try it in your next real disagreement, in Portuguese. The habit transfers before the language does.
