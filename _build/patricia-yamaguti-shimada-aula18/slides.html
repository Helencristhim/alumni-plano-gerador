Chapter 3: What You Prefer

Not What You Want Now — What You Always Choose

I prefer · I like it better · because

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"I would like the salmon." (tonight)

"I prefer fish to meat." (always)

"I like the salmon better."

"I prefer it because it is not spicy."

PatternWhat it saysExample
I would like + thingwhat you want TONIGHTI would like the salmon. (from lesson 3)
I prefer + A + to + Bwhat you ALWAYS chooseI prefer fish to meat.
I like + A + betterthe easy way to say the sameI like the salmon better.
..., because + reasonthe WHY — new in this programmeI prefer it because it is not spicy.

Prefer joins the two things with to, never with than. And it never takes more: the word already means "more than the other".

Dialogue

She Knows the Food Better Than the Waiter

K
Good evening. Have you been to a Japanese restaurant before?
P
Good evening. Yes, many times. In Brazil we eat a lot of Japanese food.
K
Ah, São Paulo, of course. Then you know more than most of my customers.
P
To start, two miso soups, please.
K
Of course. And for the main? The sushi set is very popular this evening.
P
Is the fish raw in the set? My son is eight.
K
It is, yes. For the little ones I would suggest the chicken katsu.
P
Then chicken katsu for the children. For me the grilled salmon — I prefer it, because it is not spicy.
K
A good choice. Would you like anything else with that at all?
P
Sorry, I don't understand. Can you say that again, more slowly?
K
Sorry. Anything more? Rice? Vegetables?
P
Extra rice, please. And a fork for the children — they cannot use chopsticks yet. Try the salmon with lemon, it is delicious like that.
K
With lemon? I will tell the kitchen. Thank you — I learn something every day.
Comprehension

What Did Kenji Say?

1. What does Kenji suggest for the children, and why?
The chicken katsu — because the fish in the sushi set is raw.
2. What does he say about her knowledge of the food?
That she knows more than most of his customers.
3. How does he react to her suggestion about the lemon?
He says he will tell the kitchen, and that he learns something every day.
