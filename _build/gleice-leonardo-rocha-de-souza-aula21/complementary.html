Lesson 21 -- Pitching Your Project

Talk
Bill Gross -- "The single biggest reason why start-ups succeed" (TED, 7 min)

A man who started many companies explains what makes a new business work. He talks about ideas, money, the team and the right moment. Perfect for your own project.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down the five reasons he talks about, and say which one is the most important for your business.

Podcast
BBC Learning English -- 6 Minute English: "Working for yourself"

Two presenters talk about having your own business and being your own boss, with simple English and the full script on the page.

Tip: read the script while you listen. Underline every sentence with will, and say two of them about your own project.

Listen on BBC Learning English ↗
YouTube
BBC Learning English -- "English Class: will and be going to"

A short, clear class on the two ways to talk about the future: will for promises and predictions, and going to for plans. It is exactly the grammar of this lesson.

Tip: watch at 0.75x with English subtitles. Then record a 90-second pitch of your project: one plan with going to, three promises with will, and one next step.
