Aula 21 -- Pre-class

Pitching Your Project -- Will for Promises and Predictions

A new block: your own business. Pitch your representation project to a foreign brand. Use will to make promises (I will send you the plan) and predictions (Your revenue will grow), and going to for a plan you already have (I am going to open my company in March). New words: partner, investment, revenue, profit, opportunity, deliver, expand, promise.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Learn the eight new words for a business plan. Tap Listen to hear each one.

Partner -- a person or company that works with you in a business
"Brightleaf is looking for a partner in Brazil." (parceiro)
Investment -- money you put into a business to make more money later
"The investment for Brazil is fifty thousand dollars." (investimento)
Revenue -- all the money a company gets from its sales
"Our revenue will grow in the second year." (faturamento)
Profit -- the money you keep after you pay all the costs
"We will make a profit from month twelve." (lucro)
Opportunity -- a good chance to do something or to win something
"The Brazilian market is a big opportunity." (oportunidade)
Deliver -- to take products to a place, or to do what you said you will do
"I will deliver a sales report every month." (entregar)
Expand -- to grow bigger, for example to new cities or new countries
"We want to expand to Campinas next year." (expandir)
Promise -- to say that you will really do something
"I promise I will call you on Friday." (prometer)

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Will for Promises and Predictions

GRAMMAR

Read the explanation and the examples.

Use will + verbo para PROMETER (I will send you the plan = Vou te mandar o plano, prometo) e para PREVER o futuro (The market will grow = O mercado vai crescer, eu acho). Use going to para um PLANO que você já tem, com data (I am going to open my company in March). Depois de will, o verbo vem SEM to e SEM -ing: I will send (nunca I will to send). / Use will + verb to make a promise or a prediction. Use going to for a plan you already have.

Use / UsoStructure / EstruturaExample / Exemplo
Promise / PromessaI + will + verbI will send you the plan. (Vou te mandar o plano.)
Prediction / Previsãoit / they + will + verbYour revenue will grow. (Sua receita vai crescer.)
Plan / Plano com dataam / is / are + going to + verbI am going to open my company in March.
Negative / Negativawill not (won't) + verbYou will not pay a fixed fee.
Question / PerguntaWill + subject + verb?Will the market grow?

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

A "I send you the numbers yesterday."
B "Sure, I will send you the numbers today."
C "I will to send you the numbers today, before the meeting."
You already have a date to open your company. What do you say?
A "I am going to open my company in March."
B "Maybe I open my company some day, I am not sure about it."
C "I will opening my company."
Mark is worried about the money. What is the best answer?
A "I do not know about the money."
