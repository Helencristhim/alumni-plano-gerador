Lesson 20 -- The Road So Far

Lesson theme: taking stock of a long journey and putting two ideas in one sentence with participle clauses. Extra practice outside class -- tick each one when you finish it.

Film
The Straight Story (1999) -- directed by David Lynch

A seventy-three-year-old man crosses two American states on a riding lawnmower to make peace with his brother. It moves at the speed of the machine, which means the English is slow, plain and completely clear, and almost every conversation on the way is somebody taking stock of a long life out loud.

Tip: stop after each roadside conversation and say in one sentence what that person is looking back on.
