CRISTIANE’S CARD
eight words to hold the floor
To buy a secondLet me think · Sort of
To say it again, betterI mean · Actually
To get back on trackAnyway · The thing is
To keep them talkingGo on · That reminds me
Print it. Put it next to the screen. Nobody on the call can see it, and you only need it for about a month.
1. Which two words are for the moment the word does not come, and why do they work?
Let me think and sort of. They fill the pause with sound, and sound means you have not finished, so nobody takes the floor.
2. What is the difference between the second group and the third?
I mean and actually repair the sentence you are inside. Anyway and the thing is bring you back after you have gone somewhere else.
3. Why is the last group on a card about holding the floor?
Because keeping the other person talking gives you time to build your next turn, and that reminds me is how you take it back when you are ready.

Listening 1 · Sorry, What Is the Word

The Worst Speaker in Three Countries

A man on the two seconds after the sentence stops. Sound first — no text.

1. What did he used to do when he could not finish a sentence, and what happened?
He stopped, looked at the table and waited for somebody to rescue him. Somebody always did, and then the conversation moved on without him and he had said nothing.
2. What does he do now, and what are his two examples?
He says: sorry, what is the word, and he describes it. The thing you put the coffee in. The room where you wait before the flight.
3. What does he say people actually mind?
Not the missing word — nobody has ever minded that. They mind the silence, because they do not know whether to speak.
Listening 2 · Two Tenths of a Second

You Do Not Have to Be Fast. You Have to Not Be Silent.

A woman on what conversation analysts actually measured. Sound first — no text.

1. What did they measure, and what is the average?
The gap between one person finishing and the next person starting, across a lot of very different languages. About two tenths of a second.
2. What does that number mean about how we listen to each other?
We are not waiting for people to finish. We are predicting the end of the sentence and starting on top of it.
3. Why does she say the small words work, and what is her conclusion?
They fill the gap with sound, and sound is read as: not finished yet. You do not have to be fast. You have to not be silent.

Role-Play 2 of 3 — Semi-free

Keep Me Talking, Then Take It Back

Scenario: I tell you a story and I stop in the middle several times. Keep me going with go on and one question, and then, when you are ready, take the turn with that reminds me and tell me one of your own.

Keywords:

Role-Play 3 of 3 — Free

Two Minutes, No Card, No Silence

Scenario: Tell me about the last month of your life — anything in it. Compare two things, give me one amount, tell me one thing that was going on when something happened, and give me one piece of advice for somebody in the same situation. Then ask me a question.

No card, no notes, two minutes. Nobody will interrupt you and nobody will rescue you.
