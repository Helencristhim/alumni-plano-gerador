Series
My Next Guest Needs No Introduction with David Letterman -- any episode

One hour, two people, no script, and nothing else. It is the closest thing on television to what you are practicing: a long conversation where somebody has to keep a turn alive, hand it over, and take it back. Pick whichever guest you like — the shape is the same in all of them.

Tip: English subtitles on, and do one thing only — every time either of them pauses, notice what they say instead of nothing. You will hear this week’s ten words in the first fifteen minutes.

Video
BBC Learning English -- "Scared to speak English?" (6 Minute English)

Six minutes on the exact thing that brought you here. Two teachers talk about why people who read and write well still freeze the moment they have to open their mouth, and what actually helps. Listening to it in English is itself part of the answer.

Tip: listen once with no subtitles, then once with them. The gap between what you understood the first time and the second is smaller than you think, and that gap is the whole fear.

Talk
Celeste Headlee -- "10 ways to have a better conversation" (TED)

She was a radio interviewer for years, which means her job was keeping strangers talking for an hour. Her ten rules are about conversation itself, not about English — but they are the reason the small words on your card work, and several of them are things you already do in Portuguese without noticing.

Tip: English subtitles on. Pick the two rules you break most often and try them in your next call in Portuguese first. What transfers to English afterwards is the habit, not the vocabulary.
