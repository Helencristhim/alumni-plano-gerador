Grammar Discovery

Who? What? Nobody knows

Kai: "Nobody caught anything for ten minutes."
Kai: "Everybody on the beach was watching you."
Renato: "Did anybody film my last wave?"
Kai: "Somebody next to me screamed your name!"

What do the orange words have in common? Which ones are about people, and which are about things?

-body = people · -thing = things

some- in positive sentences · any- in questions and negatives · no- with a positive verb · every- = all, with a singular verb.

The Code

Four families

some-: positive

Somebody called my name.
I saw something in the water.

any-: questions, negatives

Did anybody film it?
I didn't hear anything.

no-: positive verb

Nobody caught a wave.
Nothing changed.

every-: all

Everybody was watching.
Everything is ready.
Watch out: only ONE negative: "Nobody caught anything", not "Nobody didn't catch". "I didn't see anybody", not "I didn't see nobody". Every- is singular: "Everybody was", not "Everybody were".
Sound Check

Three sounds

Nobody caught anything for ten minutes.

"nobody": the strong part is NO

Everybody on the beach was watching.

"everybody" has three quick parts: EV-ree-body

I didn't hear anything out there.

"anything" starts like "EN": EN-ee-thing
