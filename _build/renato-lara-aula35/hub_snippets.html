Stage 1.1: Heat Day

Vocabulary

Tap Listen, then say the word out loud. Eight words, two minutes.

Scoreboard — a big board that shows the points in a game or contest
"Look at the scoreboard. You're in second place!"
Timer — a clock that shows how much time is left
"The timer said twenty seconds, and I took off."
Spectator — a person who watches a sports event
"The spectators on the beach were screaming."
Tie — when two people or teams have the same points
"It was almost a tie with the surfer in white."
Shaky — moving a little because you are nervous, cold or tired
"My legs were shaky the whole time."
Relief — the good feeling when a worry or a problem ends
"When I saw my score, what a relief!"
Interview — a meeting where somebody asks you questions
"The commentator wants a quick interview at the tent."
Hug — when you put your arms around somebody
"Kai gave me a big hug on the sand."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Read Renato's message to his mom after the semifinal. Then answer the questions.

Hi Mom! The semifinal is over. At eight o'clock the ocean was flat, and nobody caught anything for ten minutes. My legs were so shaky. Then the sets came, and Jack, the local surfer, got two great waves. I waited. With twenty seconds on the timer, I took off on my last wave. I didn't hear anything out there, but Kai says everybody on the beach was screaming. The spectators went wild! After the heat, something was wrong with the scoreboard, and we waited for ten long minutes. I was afraid of a tie with Leo. Then the results came: I'm second, and I'm in the final! What a relief! Kai gave me a big hug, and I did my first interview at the tent. Is anybody at home watching the live stream tomorrow?
What happened in the first ten minutes of the heat?
A Everybody caught a wave, and Jack took the best one on the inside.
B Nobody caught anything. The ocean was flat.
Why did everybody wait for ten minutes after the heat?
A Something was wrong with the scoreboard.
B Because the judges wanted to watch the video of Renato's last wave again.
"I didn't hear anything out there." What does Renato mean?
A He heard some people on the beach, but he didn't know who they were.
B In the water, he heard no sounds from the beach.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip

Grammar

somebody, anybody, nobody, everybody

Use -body for people and -thing for things. Use some- in positive sentences, any- in questions and negatives, no- with a positive verb, and every- when you mean all.

some-

Somebody called me.
I saw something.

any-

Did anybody film it?
I didn't hear anything.

no- / every-

Nobody caught a wave.
Everybody was watching.

Positive: Somebody screamed my name.  ·  Negative: I didn't see anybody.  ·  Question: Did anybody film my wave?
Remember: only ONE negative. Say "Nobody caught anything", not "Nobody didn't catch". Say "I didn't hear anything", not "I didn't hear nothing". Every- is singular: "Everybody was there".
Your voice: "everybody" has three quick parts: EV-ree-body. "Anything" starts like "EN": EN-ee-thing.

Stage 4: Heat day

Quiz
You forgot your sunscreen. Ask the squad.
A Does anybody have sunscreen?
B Does nobody have sunscreen for me in the van or in the tent?
Tom asks: "Did you see Jack's first wave?" You were looking at the scoreboard.
A No, I didn't see nothing, because I was looking at the scoreboard.
B No, I didn't see anything.
All the surfers in the club came to the beach. Tell Bev.
A Everybody was on the beach.
B Everybody were on the beach, and they were screaming for me.
The ocean was flat at eight o'clock. What do you say?
A Nobody didn't catch a wave in the first ten minutes of the heat.
B Nobody caught a wave.
You hear a voice on the beach, but you can't see who it is.
A Somebody is calling my name.
B Anybody is calling my name from the beach, but I can't see.

Stage 5: My big moment

Reflection
Think about a big moment in your life: a heat, a game or a test. Record 30 seconds: what happened at the start, who was there, and how you felt at the end. Use nobody, everybody and something. Check one thing before you send it: only one negative (nobody caught, not nobody didn't catch).
