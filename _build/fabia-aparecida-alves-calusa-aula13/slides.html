How others answer

What B said

Revenue was nine percent higher than last quarter.
The margin was three points lower than last year.
Overhead is the highest since twenty twenty-three.

higher than = two things · the highest = all of them · 12.5 = twelve point five
Never "more higher". A number alone says nothing; next to another one, it says a lot.
