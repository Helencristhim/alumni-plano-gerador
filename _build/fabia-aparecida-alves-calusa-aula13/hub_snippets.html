Lesson 13 — Pre-class

Twelve Point Five

Four new words from the management report — revenue, margin, forecast, overhead. And one thing only: putting one number next to another, so the figure becomes analysis. Around ten minutes.
0%
▼

Stage 1: Four new words

Vocabulary

You know all four concepts better than most native speakers. What is new is only the word and the sound. Read each one and say it out loud.

Revenue — all the money a business takes in, before costs
"Revenue was up nine percent on last quarter."
Margin — what is left after you take the costs away
"The margin is thinner than last year."
Forecast — what you expect the numbers to be, before they happen
"We are ahead of forecast for the third month."
Overhead — the cost of running the business, whatever you sell
"Overhead is the highest it has been since 2023."

Stage 3: Two things, or all of them

Grammar

One structure this week. Two things: short word + -er + than — higher than, lower than; long word: more profitable than. All of them: the + -est — the highest since 2023. Never both: "more higher" does not exist. And out loud, 12.5 is twelve point five, never "comma".

"Revenue is nine percent ______ last quarter."
A more higher than
B higher than
"Overhead is ______ it has been since 2023."
A the highest
B the higher
How do you say 12.5 on a call?
A twelve comma five
B twelve point five
The client asks if 22% is a good margin. Which answer is analysis?
A It is three points lower than last year.
B It is twenty-two percent.

Stage 4: Complete the line

Practice

These four lines are the report. You will say them in class.

"Revenue is nine percent than last quarter."
"The margin is three points lower a year ago."
"Overhead is the since 2023."
"We are ahead of this quarter."

Stage 5: Say it out loud

Speaking

Say the whole figure slowly, before the comparison. The number is the part that trips people, not the grammar.

Revenue closed at one point four eight million.
That is nine percent higher than last quarter.
Overhead is the highest since twenty twenty-three.

Stage 6: Before class

Speaking
Take one number from your own firm this month — revenue, a margin, a cost. Record three sentences: the number, the number compared with last month or last year (higher than / lower than), and one thing that is the highest or the lowest since a date. One sentence each — no more.
