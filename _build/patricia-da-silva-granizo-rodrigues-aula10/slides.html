Chapter 4: The Code

Agreeing and Disagreeing in the Same Sentence

The linkers that make a claim survive a room full of objections

Grammar Discovery

Granted, But...

"Although the budget is flat, we are opening two sites this year."

"Attrition is flat in the plants, whereas it is rising in the office."

"Granted, benchmarks lag. The leavers under one year are still on market pay."

"Attrition is down. Having said that, two of the leavers were irreplaceable."

Each of these holds two ideas that pull in opposite directions. What is each coloured word doing to the balance between them?

FormWhat it doesExample
although / even though + clauseconcedes and continues in ONE sentenceAlthough the budget is flat, we are opening two sites.
whereascontrasts two facts of equal weight, without either one winningAttrition is flat in the plants, whereas it is rising in the office.
having said that / that saidconcedes AFTER a full stop, at the start of the next sentence... Having said that, the trend is not the biggest problem.
granted, ... butconcedes your opponent point OUT LOUD before you answer itGranted, benchmarks lag. But the leavers are on market pay.
the trapAlthough ... but ... in the same sentence. Portuguese allows the pair; English uses one or the other, never both. Same for despite of, which does not exist: it is despite or in spite of.

Conceding is not weakness and it is not politeness. It is what makes the part you do not concede impossible to dismiss.

Chapter 5: The Ear

Two Ways to Sum Up Nine Months

A director summarising a year, and an executive saying what he wanted instead

Listening 1

Nine Months in Two Minutes

A director opens a mid-year review. Sound first -- no text.

0:000:00
1. What does she put in the first thirty seconds, and why?
What has not gone well -- the engineering pipeline gap, which is exactly where it was in January -- because that is the part the room will remember anyway.
2. What has worked, and how does she describe the contrast?
The first ninety days: attrition under one year has come down eleven points since March, whereas attrition over three years has not moved, which is what they expected and wanted.
3. What is the qualification she attaches to the onboarding redesign?
That it has worked in three sites out of five, and the two missing are the two that need it most.
Listening 2

What I Wanted From That Review

The executive who was in the room. Sound first -- no text.

0:000:00
1. Why does he say starting with the failure works on him?
Not because of honesty or humility, but arithmetic: if somebody spends two minutes on good news, he spends the rest of the meeting working out what was left out, and he is not a generous auditor.
2. What was the second thing she did well?
She conceded one point and refused to let it do more work than it deserved -- granted, they lost two people, and that does not solve anything.
3. What was the third, and what was the result?
She stopped talking at two minutes. He asked three questions and every one was about what to do next.
