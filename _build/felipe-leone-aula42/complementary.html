Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Carol Dweck: “The power of believing that you can improve” (10 min)

A psychologist on the power of the word yet: the difference between I can’t and I can’t yet.

Tip: say three sentences about your English with yet and already.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Matt Cutts: “Try something new for 30 days” (3 min)

A short, simple talk on how small habits, repeated for 30 days, change what you are able to do.

Tip: choose one habit for your English over the next 30 days and tell your teacher what you are going to do.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Eduardo Briceño: “How to get better at the things you care about” (11 min)

An educator on the difference between the zone where we perform and the zone where we learn.

Tip: pause and say, in one sentence, what you are going to practice in the learning zone next year.
