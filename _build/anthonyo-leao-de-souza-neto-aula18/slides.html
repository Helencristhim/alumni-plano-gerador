QuestionWas there music?Were there many people?
Short answerYes, there was. / No, there wasn't. · Yes, there were. / No, there weren't.

Was for one, were for many — the same rule as lesson 11 (it was, they were).

The Sound

How It Sounds

"There was a band."

fast and weak: "there-w'z a BAND" — the stress goes on the thing

"There were forty guests."

were is short: "there-wer FORTY GUESTS"

"There wasn't a free table."

the no is strong: WAZ-nt

"Was there any music?"

the question that brings back the whole night

Dialogue

Was There Music?

C
Good morning! So, how was the dinner at Casa Lume?
L
Wonderful. There were forty guests, at one long table in the garden.
C
Forty! Was there music?
L
Yes, there was a band from Lisbon. People danced until midnight.
C
And you? Did you dance?
L
No, I didn't. But I ate a lot. There were three desserts.
C
Three desserts! I went to a dinner in Paris last year, and there wasn't any dessert.
L
No dessert? That is terrible. Was the food good?
C
Very good, but there were only twelve people, and there was no music. Very quiet.
L
At Casa Lume there was a toast for the chef. It was his birthday.
C
That is lovely. Were there candles on the tables?
L
Everywhere. And the waiter remembered my name. A night to remember.
Comprehension

What Did Claire Say?

1. Where was Claire's dinner, and when?
In Paris, last year.
2. What was missing at her dinner?
There wasn't any dessert, and there was no music.
3. How many people were there?
Only twelve people. It was very quiet.
