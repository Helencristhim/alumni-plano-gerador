Lesson 22 -- The First-Month Review

Talk
Tom Wujec -- "Build a tower, build a team" (TED, 7 min)

Teams build a tower with spaghetti and a marshmallow, and the results show why testing early beats planning everything first. A short, clear talk about what teams learn after each try.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then say two sentences: what teams used to do in the test, and what the best teams do.

Talk
Tim Harford -- "Trial, error and the God complex" (TED, 20 min)

An economist explains why complex systems improve through small tests and honest reviews, not through one perfect plan. A good companion to any retrospective.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down one example he gives and say how it compares with your migration project.

Video
Brian Christian -- "How to manage your time more effectively (according to machines)" (TED-Ed, 5 min)

A short animated video that uses ideas from computer science to explain bottlenecks and priorities in everyday work. Simple English and very familiar ideas for an IT manager.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then name one bottleneck in your week and say how you used to handle it.
