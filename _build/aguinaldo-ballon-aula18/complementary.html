Talk
TED -- Tim Harford, "Trial, error and the God complex" (18 min)

An economist argues that complex problems are rarely solved by one expert with a perfect plan, and that trial and error works better. Many clear examples of cause and effect.

Tip: write down two cause-and-effect sentences he uses, and say them again with due to or as a result.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English -- "Why are we all so stressed?"

Six minutes on the causes and the consequences of stress at work and in modern life. Good practice for the language of cause and effect.

Tip: after listening, say two causes and two consequences of stress with because of and as a result.

English with Emma (engVid) -- "Learn English: Cause and Effect"

A clear lesson on the words that express cause and effect, such as so, since, due to and as a result, with examples and a short quiz.
