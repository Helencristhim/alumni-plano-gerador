Chapter 4: The Code

Due To, As a Result, Despite

Linkers of cause, consequence and contrast

Grammar Discovery

Cause, Consequence or Contrast?

"The project was delayed due to a shortage of engineers."

"The tariff was frozen. As a result, investors lost confidence."

"Although traffic fell, revenue increased."

"Despite the crisis, the port doubled its exports."

The blue words link a cause and its effect. The red words show a contrast: something happened, but something else happened anyway. Now look at what comes after each one: a noun (a shortage, the crisis) or a full sentence (traffic fell)?

JobFormExample
Cause + noundue to / because of + noundelayed due to a shortage of engineers
Cause + sentencebecause / since / as + subject + verbbecause the license was late
Consequence, new sentenceAs a result, / Therefore, / Consequently,As a result, investors lost confidence.
Contrast + sentencealthough / even though + subject + verbAlthough traffic fell, revenue increased.
Contrast + noun or -ingdespite / in spite of + noun or -ingDespite the crisis · despite losing traffic
The trapdespite never takes a full sentence or of; although never takes butNot Despite traffic fell or Although it fell, but.

In one line: due to and despite + a noun; because and although + a sentence; as a result starts a new sentence.

DELAY REPORT — BR HIGHWAY DUPLICATION, LOT 3
Monthly report to the lenders · prepared by the independent engineer
Root causeIncomplete design at the time of the tender
TriggerState license 7 months late · agency short of staff
Direct consequenceMain works started in the rainy season
Knock-on effectLots 4 and 5 delayed by 5 months
Extra costRoughly R$ 180 million (just over 9% of the budget)
ContrastTraffic on finished lots 18% above forecast
RecommendationRenegotiate the deadline, not the tariff
The independent engineer sends this report to the lenders every month. The last line is the one the lawyers read first: it decides the next negotiation with the state.
1. What is the root cause of the delay?
The design was incomplete at the time of the tender.
2. Why was the state license late?
Because the agency was short of staff.
3. What is good news despite the delay?
Traffic on the finished lots is 18% above the forecast.
Listening 1 · The Project Director

Why the Airport Opened Late

The director of an airport project explains a two-year delay to the press. Sound first — no text.

1. What was the root cause of the delay, according to her?
The design of the terminal, which was not complete when they signed the contract.
2. What triggered the eight-month delay?
The review of the environmental license, because the design changed three times.
3. What happened despite everything?
Passenger numbers in the first year were higher than expected.
Listening 2 · The Economist

The Side Effect of a Popular Decision

An economist talks about what happens when a government freezes tariffs. Sound first — no text.

1. Why does the concessionaire cut investment in maintenance?
Because it receives less money after the tariff freeze.
2. What is the knock-on effect on new projects?
Investors ask for higher returns everywhere in the country.
3. What is his opinion of tariff freezes?
The long-term costs usually outweigh the short-term benefits.

What I Can Do Now

I use due to and because of with a noun, and because with a sentence.
I start a consequence with As a result or Therefore.
I use although with a sentence and despite with a noun or -ing.
