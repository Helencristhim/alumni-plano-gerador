Chapter 4: The Code

Have You Ever?

Experience and results, without a date

Grammar Discovery

Four Sentences About the Award

"I have entered three projects this year."

"I have never been to an award ceremony."

"Have you ever entered a design award?"

"The award has existed for more than seventy years."

Look at the blue verbs: two parts, have or has plus the third form of the verb (entered, been). Now look for a date: there is none. What matters is the experience or the result now. The red words help: ever in questions, never for zero times, for and since for a period that has not finished.

JobFormExample
Experience, no datehave / has + third formI have entered three projects.
Zero timeshave never + third formI have never been to a ceremony.
QuestionsHave you ever + third form?Have you ever entered an award?
Done / not donealready / not ... yetWe have already submitted it. We haven’t heard yet.
Still true todayfor + a period / since + a startI have worked here for five years.
The trapwith a date, use the past simpleNot I have submitted it in May. Say I submitted it in May.

In one line: have / has + third form for experience and results, with no date; with a date, past simple.

Listening 1 · Inside the Jury

How the Jury Decides

Lena has worked with design awards for ten years. She explains how a jury looks at the entries. Sound first — no text.

1. How long does a jury member often look at an entry the first time?
About one minute.
2. What do the best entries show first?
The problem, in one or two sentences.
3. What mistake has Lena seen many times?
Entries with too much text and no clear story.
Listening 2 · The Night in Berlin

A Winner Remembers

A British designer has won a design award. He remembers the ceremony night. Sound first — no text.

1. How many times has he been to the ceremony?
Twice: once as a guest and once as a winner.
2. What did the staff tell him before he went up to the stage?
To wait at the side of the stage and walk up when they called his name.
3. What is his advice for the night?
Bring business cards and talk to people from other countries.
