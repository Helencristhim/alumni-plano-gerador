Chapter 4: The Code

Don’t, Doesn’t

Two small words for every problem

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"I don’t receive messages."

"The website doesn’t open."

"Do other users have this problem?"

"Does the error appear on every computer?"

Look at the blue words. When is it don’t, and when is it doesn’t? What happens to the verb after them?

WhoFormExample
I, you, we, theydon’t + verbI don’t receive messages.
He, she, it (the email, the website)doesn’t + verbThe website doesn’t open.
Question: I, you, we, theyDo...?Do other users have this problem?
Question: he, she, itDoes...?Does the error appear on every computer?
Short answersYes, it does. / No, it doesn’t.Does the email work? No, it doesn’t.
The trapno -s after doesn’t / doesNot doesn’t opens. Say doesn’t open.

In one line: I don’t, it doesn’t. Do you...? Does it...? After don’t, doesn’t, do and does, the verb is simple.
