Chapter 3: The Code

It Was Great, We Walked a Lot

One tense for finished stories

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Show?

"The trip was amazing."

"The museums were free."

"We visited the monuments and walked a lot."

"The tour wasn’t expensive."

Look at the blue words. Was goes with one thing, were with two or more. Regular verbs in the past end in -ed: visited, walked. For the negative of be, look at the red word: wasn’t.

JobFormExample
I / he / she / itwasThe trip was amazing.
you / we / theywereThe museums were free.
Negativewasn’t / weren’tThe tour wasn’t expensive.
Regular verbsverb + ed (for everybody)We visited the monuments.
Verbs that end in -e+ dWe arrived on Monday.
The trapthe past is the same for I and sheNot she visiteds. Say she visited.

In one line: was for one, were for two or more; regular verbs take -ed for everybody.

Chapter 4: Telling the Story

Telling the Story

One conversation, two travelers, one itinerary

Dialogue

Monday Morning, After the Trip

K
Hi, Marcela! Welcome back. How was the trip?
M
Hi, Kevin! It was great, thank you. We visited Washington and Chicago, and then New York.
K
Nice! I lived in Chicago for two years. How was it?
M
I loved it. We booked a guided tour on the river, and the architecture was amazing.
K
That tour is the best. And Washington? It is always very crowded in October.
M
Yes, the museums were crowded, but they were free. We walked around the National Mall all day.
K
Did you see the view from the Washington Monument?
M
No, there were no tickets that day. Next time! But the Lincoln Memorial was my favorite landmark.
Comprehension

About Kevin

1. Where did Kevin live for two years?
In Chicago.
2. Which tour does Kevin think is the best?
The river tour in Chicago.
3. What does Kevin say about Washington in October?
It is always very crowded.
