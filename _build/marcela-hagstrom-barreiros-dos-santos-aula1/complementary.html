What's the point of museums? -- 6 Minute English

Two BBC presenters talk about museums: what they are for and why we visit them. A good match for the Smithsonian museums in Washington.

Tip: listen once without the text. The second time, write down every verb in the past that you hear.

Washington DC Travel Guide -- National Mall Things To See and Do

A four-minute tour of the National Mall: the monuments, the memorials and the museums. Many of the words of this lesson are here: landmark, monument, crowded, view.

Tip: pause after each place and say one sentence in the past about it, like: we walked around the Mall.
