Lesson 25 -- Chairing a Session

Podcast
"The art of conversation" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters discuss whether we are losing the art of conversation: listening, taking turns and not talking too much. Six minutes, with a transcript -- the skills a good chair protects in a session.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Write down two phrases the presenters use to change the topic or bring the conversation back.

Conference Talk
"How to disagree productively and find common ground" -- Julia Dhar, TED

A world debate champion explains how to disagree about ideas without attacking people, and how to find common ground. Useful when two panelists in your session do not agree.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Note one sentence you could use as a moderator when two panelists disagree.

Video
"Dare to disagree" -- Margaret Heffernan, TED

A business leader tells the story of a doctor who questioned the medical consensus of her time, and explains why good teams need people who disagree. A clinical story with a lesson for every chair.

Tip: listen for the discourse markers she uses to move from the story to her main point, like "that said" or "anyway".
