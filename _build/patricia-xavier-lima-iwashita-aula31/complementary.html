Lesson 31 -- Before the Big Meeting

Film
Up in the Air (2009)

A man who travels all year for work meets a young colleague with a new idea that could change his job. The film is full of fast, polite American business conversations, where people check agreement all the time — often with question tags. Connection to Lesson 31: listen to how people prepare difficult conversations before they happen.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time you hear a question tag (isn't it, don't you, can't we), pause and repeat it with the same intonation.

BBC Learning English Grammar -- "Question tags"

A short BBC episode about question tags: how to make them with any verb, and how intonation changes the meaning. Connection to Lesson 31: exactly the grammar of your pre-meeting call with Victor.

Tip: listen twice. The second time, repeat every tag out loud and check your intonation: down to check, up to ask.
