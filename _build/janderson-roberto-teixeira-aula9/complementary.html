Six minutes about why a message that is grammatically fine can still land badly. Connection to Lesson 9: it is today's class in writing. The same request, sent two ways, and only one of them gets an answer. Audio, vocabulary and full transcript are free on the page.

"Make polite requests" -- English at Work, BBC Learning English

Four minutes of a short office drama, built entirely around the ladder you climbed today. Connection to Lesson 9: you watch somebody ask for the same thing three times, badly and then well, and you hear exactly where the request stops sounding like an instruction.

Tip: pause every time somebody makes a request and say your own version out loud before the video gives you theirs.

Talk
"Are you a giver or a taker?" -- Adam Grant, TED

Thirteen minutes on what happens inside a company when people ask each other for favors and when they do not. Connection to Lesson 9: today was the language of asking; this is the argument for doing it more often. His central point is that the people who ask are not the weak ones.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Then write down one favor you have been putting off asking for, and ask for it this week using the language from class.
