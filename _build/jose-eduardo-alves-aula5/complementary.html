Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
Dan Gilbert — "The psychology of your future self" (TED)

Seven minutes on the person you are going to be in ten years, and why everybody predicts that person badly. A Harvard psychologist, speaking slowly, in the exact tense of this lesson.

Tip: at the end, say three sentences out loud about who you are going to be after the master's. Then say one thing you are not going to change.

Watch on TED ↗
Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "A smarter way to pick the right college"

How to choose a program without choosing a brochure: what to compare, what to ignore, and which questions to ask the admissions office before you commit. The examples are American undergraduates, and the method is the same for a master's at fifty-one.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Write down the three questions you are going to ask when you arrange your own call.

Listen on NPR ↗
Documentary
DW Documentary — "Hope, homesickness, new beginnings: how years abroad shape young people"

People leaving their country for a year, filmed before they go and after they arrive. They are younger than you and the calendar is identical: the plan, the paperwork, the first month, and the sentence everybody uses to explain the decision.

Tip: every time somebody says why they went, pause and answer the same question about yourself, out loud, in two sentences.
