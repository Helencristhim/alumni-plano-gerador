Talk
TED -- Julian Treasure, "How to speak so that people want to listen" (10 min)

A sound expert on the voice habits that make people stop listening, and the simple tools that make a short introduction sound confident. Clear and well paced, with English subtitles.

Tip: watch once with English subtitles. Then record your one-minute introduction again and use one of his tools.

Podcast
BBC 6 Minute English -- "Have you ever seen a whale?"

Two presenters talk for six minutes about a single topic, and the title is the grammar of this lesson: "Have you ever...?" Listen for how they move between experiences with no date and stories with a date.

Tip: listen once without the transcript. Then read the transcript on the page and underline every have + past participle.

Video
Stanford GSB -- Matt Abrahams, "Think Fast, Talk Smart: Communication Techniques"

A Stanford lecturer on speaking well when you have no time to prepare: how to reduce anxiety and give a clear answer in a structure the audience can follow. Exactly the situation of the panel you did not take.

Tip: it is long. Watch the first twenty minutes and write down one technique you can use at the next event.
