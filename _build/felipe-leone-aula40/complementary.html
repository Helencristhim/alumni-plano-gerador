Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
William Ury: “The walk from no to yes” (19 min)

One of the authors of the most famous book on negotiation explains how to get to yes, even in the hardest deals.

Tip: write down two sentences he says with already or yet, or with a mix of tenses, and say them out loud.

TED Talk
Margaret Heffernan: “Dare to disagree” (13 min)

A business leader on why good teams and good deals need people who say no, and how to disagree well.

Tip: use one of her ideas in the 4/3/2 answer in class.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Adam Galinsky: “How to speak up for yourself” (15 min)

A social psychologist on how to speak with confidence in difficult conversations, like asking for a better deal.

Tip: pause after each idea and say, in one sentence, if you have already used it in a negotiation.
