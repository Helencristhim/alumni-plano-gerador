Lesson 22 -- Your Bangkok Moment

Podcast
"What's in a footballer's brain?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters talk about how footballers cope with high-pressure moments, like a penalty, when the whole stadium is looking at them. Six minutes, with a transcript -- your high-stakes moments in Bangkok are not so different.

Tip: listen once without the transcript and write the key words. Then say in two sentences what a footballer and a researcher in a Q&A have in common.

Conference Talk
"Why we choke under pressure -- and how to avoid it" -- Sian Leah Beilock, TED

A cognitive scientist explains what happens in the brain when we fail in an important moment, and she shares simple strategies from her research to stay calm. A researcher talking about her own data, in clear American English.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down one strategy you can use before your workshop in Bangkok, and say it out loud with "If I..., I'll...".

Video
"The science of stage fright (and how to overcome it)" -- Mikael Cho, TED-Ed

A short animated lesson about why our body reacts when we have to speak in front of people, and what we can do about it. Five minutes -- useful the night before your Bangkok moment.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause after each tip and repeat the sentence that explains it.
