Series
Woodward English -- "Present Simple vs. Present Continuous Tense"

Eleven minutes, one idea: the same verb in two different sentences. The teacher puts both forms side by side on the screen, exactly like the two columns you saw in class, and says every example twice.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Every time a sentence with -ing appears, say out loud what you are doing at that minute.

Podcast
Go English -- "How to Use Present Continuous in English"

Twenty minutes of real conversation about what is happening now. Two speakers describe scenes they are looking at, so you hear the form you learned today in the middle of a normal talk, not in a list.

Tip: good for the commute, when you cannot read or write but you can listen. Play it once without stopping, then again and count how many times you hear "at the moment".

Oxford Online English -- "Talking on the Phone in English"

Twelve minutes on the exact call you practiced today: how to answer with your name, how to ask the other person to wait, how to take a message, and what to say when the line is bad.

Tip: watch it twice. The second time, pause after each model call and say the same lines out loud, as if the phone were in your hand.
