Friday Afternoon

Dialogue

Are You Free on Friday?

D
Giovanna, are you busy right now?
G
A little. I am on the plans of the tunnel. Is it urgent?
D
Not urgent. Are you free on Friday afternoon? The report is almost ready.
G
On Friday I am at the site in the morning, but in the afternoon I am free.
D
Perfect. Two o'clock, in the meeting room on the first floor.
G
I am there. And you? Are you busy this weekend?
D
No, I am not. My wife is here from London, so we are together on Saturday.
G
Nice. I am with my boyfriend on Saturday. We are together at the cinema.
D
Is there a good cinema near your home?
G
Yes, there is one behind my building. On Sunday night I am tired, but I am ready for Monday.
Comprehension

About Daniel

1. When is the meeting, and where?
Friday at two, in the meeting room on the first floor.
2. Is Daniel busy this weekend?
No, he is not. His wife is here from London.
3. What does Daniel want to know about the cinema?
If there is a good one near her home.
