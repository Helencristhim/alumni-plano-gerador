Series
Queer Eye — Netflix, any episode

Five Americans spend a week with somebody, and almost every scene is one of them telling another what the person said in the kitchen an hour ago. It is warm, it is fast, and it is forty minutes of he told me that, she said she wanted and I asked him if at real conversational speed.

Tip: write down eight sentences where one of them reports what somebody else said. Circle the reporting verb in each — you will find at least four different ones.

What actually happens between what somebody says and what you walk away with, and why the part you drop first is the part that mattered. American hosts, normal speed, full transcript on the same page.

Tip: listen once with the transcript closed. Then report three things the guest said, out loud, in full sentences, using three different reporting verbs.

Rachel's English — "The H Reduction in American English Pronunciation"

Why you never hear the h in told her, asked him or gave her. This is the exact reason a reported sentence at full speed sounds like one long word to an ear trained on the written form.

Tip: after the video, say "she told her the gate had changed" and "he asked him where I was from" five times each, with no h at all in the middle.
