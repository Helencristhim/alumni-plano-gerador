Chapter 3 -- The Polite Code

One Small Word: Would

Polite requests, offers and wishes

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Orange Words Have in Common?

"I would like to represent NatureCare in Brazil."

"I would like a meeting with your team in July."

"Would you send me the price list, please?"

"Would you like a call next week?"

All four use would. Why not I want, or You send me?

UseFormExample
What you want + a thingI would like + nounI would like a meeting.
What you want + an actionI would like to + verbI would like to represent your brand.
Polite requestWould you + verb...?Would you send me the list, please?
Polite offerWould you like + noun / to + verb?Would you like a call?
Short formI would = I’dI’d like to confirm the date.
Neverwould like + verb without to / would you + toNot I would like receive. Not Would you to send.

In one line: I would like (to) for what you want; Would you...? to ask politely; Would you like...? to offer.

Grammar Practice

Wish, Request or Offer?

Say it first, then click to check

A wish, an action: "I ___would like to send (send) you my proposal."
A wish, a thing: "I ___would like (like) a meeting in July."
A polite request: "___Would you confirm (confirm) the territory, please?"
A polite offer: "___Would you like (like) a copy of the plan?"
Polite, not direct: not "Send me the catalog." but "___Would you send me the catalog, please?"
Chapter 4 -- The Proposal

Before You Click Send

One call, two recordings, one formal email

Dialogue

Gleice and Thomas

T
Hi Gleice, this is Thomas Grant from NatureCare. Jennifer told me about your plan.
G
Hello, Thomas. Thank you for calling. I would like to send you my proposal this week.
T
Great. Please send it by email. It has to be formal, because our board will read it.
G
Of course. Would you confirm the territory? Is it only Sao Paulo state?
T
Yes, only Sao Paulo state for the first two years. Do you have any other request?
G
Yes, one. Would you send me the price list for the pharmacy chains, please?
T
Sure, I will send it today. Would you like a call next week, after we read the proposal?
G
Yes, I would like that very much. I really appreciate your time, Thomas.
Comprehension

About Thomas

1. Why does the proposal have to be formal, for Thomas?
Because the NatureCare board will read it.
2. What territory does Thomas confirm?
Only Sao Paulo state, for the first two years.
3. What does Thomas offer at the end?
A call next week, after they read the proposal.

Listening 2 -- The Coach

Five Parts of a Formal Email

A communication coach explains how to write a formal email. Sound first -- no text.

1. What is part one of a formal email?
A clear subject.
2. How do you start the first sentence?
With I am writing to..., to say why you are writing.
3. What is better in a request: I want or I would like?
I would like, because it is more polite.
Real Document

The Proposal Email

From Gleice Leonardo Rocha de Souza
To Jennifer Lowe, NatureCare Skin
Cc Thomas Grant
Subject Proposal for Brazil -- Gleice Souza

Dear Ms. Lowe,

I am writing to send you my proposal for NatureCare Skin in Brazil. I would like to represent your brand in Sao Paulo state.

In the first year, I will visit thirty pharmacy chains. The plan and the list of chains are in the PDF.

I have one request: would you confirm the commission of eight percent? I would also like a call with Mr. Grant next week.

I appreciate your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
Gleice Leonardo Rocha de Souza

1. What is the subject of the email?
Proposal for Brazil -- Gleice Souza.
2. What is Gleice's request in the email?
She asks Jennifer to confirm the commission of eight percent.
3. Find the polite closing sentence before Best regards.
I appreciate your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.
