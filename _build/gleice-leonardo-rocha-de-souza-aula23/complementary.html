Lesson 23 -- Professional Emails

Podcast
BBC Learning English -- Office English: "Emails"

Two presenters talk about how formal or friendly an email has to be: to a colleague, to your boss, or to a person at another company. Many useful phrases for work emails.

Tip: listen once without the script. Then write down three phrases you can use in your proposal email to a foreign brand.
