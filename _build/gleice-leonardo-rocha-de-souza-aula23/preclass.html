Aula 23 -- Pre-class

Professional Emails -- Would Like and Would You for Polite Requests

Write a formal proposal email to NatureCare. Use I would like (to) to say what you want politely (I would like to represent your brand), Would you...? to ask (Would you confirm the territory?) and Would you like...? to offer (Would you like a call?). New words: proposal, request, subject, formal, polite, confirm, appreciate, regards.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Learn the eight new words for a professional email. Tap Listen to hear each one.

Proposal -- a written plan or offer that you send to someone for a decision
"I am sending you my proposal for Brazil." (proposta)
Request -- when you ask for something in a polite way
"I have one request: a meeting in July." (pedido)
Subject -- the short title of an email, on the first line
"The subject is: Proposal for Brazil." (assunto)
Formal -- serious and correct, for work and for people you do not know well
"A proposal email has to be formal." (formal)
Polite -- kind and respectful when you speak or write to people
"Would you is more polite than you send." (educado)
Confirm -- to say that something is true or that it is decided
"Could you confirm the date of the meeting?" (confirmar)
Appreciate -- to be thankful for something that someone does for you
"I appreciate your time and your attention." (agradecer)
Regards -- a polite word before your name at the end of an email
"Best regards, Gleice." (atenciosamente)

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Would Like and Would You

GRAMMAR

Read the explanation and the examples.

Use I would like no lugar de I want para dizer o que você quer de forma EDUCADA (I would like a meeting = Eu gostaria de uma reunião). Antes de um verbo, use would like to (I would like to represent your brand = Eu gostaria de representar a sua marca). Para PEDIR, use Would you + verbo? (Would you send me the list? = Você poderia me mandar a lista?). Para OFERECER, use Would you like...? (Would you like a call? = Você gostaria de uma ligação?). Nunca: I would like receive (falta o to) / Would you to send (sobra o to). / Use I would like (to) to say what you want politely, Would you...? to ask, and Would you like...? to offer.

Use / UsoStructure / EstruturaExample / Exemplo
Wish + thing / Desejo + coisaI would like + nounI would like a meeting. (Eu gostaria de uma reunião.)
Wish + action / Desejo + açãoI would like to + verbI would like to represent your brand. (Eu gostaria de representar a sua marca.)
Request / PedidoWould you + verb...?Would you confirm the territory? (Você poderia confirmar o território?)
Offer / OfertaWould you like + noun?Would you like a call? (Você gostaria de uma ligação?)
Short form / Forma curtaI would = I'dI'd like to confirm the date.
