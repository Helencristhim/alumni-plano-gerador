Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

TED Talk
Julian Treasure: “How to speak so that people want to listen” (10 min)

A sound expert on the habits that make people stop listening, and the tools that make a short, direct answer work in any meeting.

Tip: choose one tool he gives and use it in class when the new director asks about your career.

Watch on TED ↗
TED Talk
Amy Cuddy: “Your body language may shape who you are” (21 min)

A social psychologist on how the way you stand and speak changes how others see you at work.

Tip: count how many times she says what she has done (no date) and what she did (with a date).

Watch on TED ↗
TEDx Talk
Larry Smith: “Why you will fail to have a great career” (15 min)

An economics professor, fast and funny, on the excuses people give for not doing the work they love.

Tip: pause after each excuse and say, in one sentence, if you have ever used it.
