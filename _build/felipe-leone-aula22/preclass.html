Lesson 22 -- Pre-class

Felipe Leone, Professional -- Your Career in Two Tenses

Ten to twenty minutes before class. How to talk about your career: what you did, with a date, and what you have done, with no date. Key words: to join, a role, credit risk, to manage, a background, to be in charge of.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

To join -- to start working at a company or in a team
"I joined the credit team after five years in sales."
A role -- the job and the responsibilities a person has
"In my current role, I lead a team of eight analysts."
Credit risk -- the chance that a person or a company does not pay back money
"Our models help banks measure credit risk."
To manage -- to be responsible for people or for a project
"She has managed the Mexico account since March."
A background -- the studies and the jobs a person had before
"My background is in economics and banking."
To be in charge of -- to have the responsibility for something
"I am in charge of the relationship with our biggest clients."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Ana works for a data company in Chicago. When a new director asks about her career, she starts with her experience: “I have worked in credit risk for twelve years, and I have managed teams in two countries.” There is no date here. She is talking about her experience until today.

Then she gives the details: “I joined the company in 2019. Before that, I worked for a bank in Toronto for five years.” These are finished moments, so she uses the past simple.

"I have worked in credit risk for twelve years." What does it mean?
A She is going to work in credit risk for twelve years.
B She worked in credit risk in the past, and she stopped.
C She started twelve years ago, and she still works in credit risk.
Why does she say "I joined the company in 2019" and not "I have joined the company in 2019"?
A Because 2019 is a finished moment.
B Because the director asked a question.
C Because she does not like the company.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Did or Have Done?

GRAMMAR

Experience until today, no date: have + third form. A finished moment: the past simple.

JobFormExample
Experience until todayhave / has + third formI have worked in finance for twenty years.
A finished momentpast formI joined it three years ago.
Never in my lifehave never + third formI have never worked in the US.

Never: I have joined the company in 2021 · I work here since 2021 · I never have been to Atlanta

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

A "I worked in credit since 2015."
B "I have worked in credit since 2015."
C "I work in credit since 2015."
He asks: "When did you join the company?" You say:
A "I joined it three years ago."
B "I have joined it three years ago."
C "I am joining it three years ago."

A "I never have worked in the US office."
