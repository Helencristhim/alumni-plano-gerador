Drill 1

How Long Have You…?

Full sentence, real numbers. for + a period, since + a starting point.

How long have you worked in finance?
How long have you been at the company?
How long have you managed your team?
How long have you worked with American colleagues?
How long have you had this role?
Drill 2

When Did You…?

A finished moment: the past simple. Full sentence, real dates.

When did you join the company?
When did you start working in finance?
When did you first lead a team?
When did you last travel for work?
When did you have your first meeting in English?
