Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Series
Emily in Paris — Season 1, Episode 1 "Emily in Paris" (Netflix)

Light from the first minute, and the whole episode is one person discovering that the rules in another country are not the rules she knows. Twenty-five minutes, easy American English, and half the story happens at a desk where somebody has to explain a policy.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Write down every sentence where somebody says what she can and cannot do.

Podcast
Life Kit (NPR) — "Flight cancelled again? Try these tips."

Exactly the small print of this lesson, but for flights: what an airline has to give you, what it is allowed to offer you instead, and what you do not have to accept. Useful before October, and it is fifteen minutes long.

Tip: the full transcript is on the same page. Listen once without it, then read it and find every must, have to and are allowed to.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
Wolters World — "Travel Tips with Mark"

A traveller who has been to about eighty countries, talking the way people actually talk: clear American English, no script, and a lot of practical rules about hotels, bookings and money. Good ear training, because nothing is slowed down for you.

Tip: watch it once with subtitles and once without. Write down three pieces of advice as rules, using must, have to or are allowed to.
