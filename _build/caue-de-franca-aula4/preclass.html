Subtitles On -- Stop Watching or Stop to Watch?

An article about learning English with movies, series and cartoons. Key words: subtitles, dubbed, closed captions, to pick up, to rewatch, to keep up with, the plot, to catch, to get the gist, to binge-watch, a cliffhanger, background noise. Structure: gerund or infinitive with a change in meaning.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Subtitles -- the words at the bottom of the screen that show what people say
"I watch everything with subtitles."
Dubbed -- with the original voices replaced by voices in another language
"I never watch dubbed movies."
Closed captions -- subtitles that also describe sounds, like [door slams] or [music]
"Closed captions help when there is a lot of noise."
To pick up -- to learn something without studying it formally
"I picked up a lot of English from cartoons."
To rewatch -- to watch something again
"I like to rewatch cartoons I already know."
To keep up with -- to go at the same speed as someone or something
"The actors speak so fast that I can’t keep up with them."
The plot -- the story of a movie, a series or a book
"I know the plot, so I can focus on the words."
To catch -- to hear or understand something that someone says
"Sorry, I didn’t catch that. Could you say it again?"
To get the gist -- to understand the general meaning, not every word
"I don’t understand every word, but I get the gist."
To binge-watch -- to watch many episodes of a series one after another
"We binge-watched the whole season in one weekend."
A cliffhanger -- an exciting end of an episode that makes you want to see the next one
"The first season ends with a big cliffhanger."
Background noise -- the sounds you hear behind the main voices
"With background noise, I miss half of the dialogue."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Cauê stopped watching dubbed movies a long time ago. He always watches with subtitles. This month he decided to try switching them to English.

On Saturday he rewatched a cartoon he remembers watching as a kid. He knew the plot, so he could focus on the words. In one scene he stopped to write down an expression he did not catch the first time.

He could not keep up with every word, especially with the background noise, but he got the gist. Now he has a list on his phone: “Remember to choose English, every time.”

1. What does “he stopped to write down an expression” mean?
A He quit writing expressions because it took too much time and he wanted to enjoy the cartoon.
B He paused the cartoon in order to write the expression.
C He forgot to write the expression, so he had to watch the whole scene again later that night.
2. Why could Cauê focus on the words?
A Because he already knew the plot.
B Because the cartoon was dubbed into Portuguese, so he could understand the story easily.
C Because there was no background noise at all in any of the scenes he watched.
3. Which sentence is about a memory?
A “Remember to choose English.”
B “He remembers watching it as a kid.”
C “He tried to remember to choose English subtitles every night.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Gerund or Infinitive -- Stop Watching or Stop to Watch?

GRAMMAR

Some verbs can take -ing or to, and the meaning changes. -ing looks at the activity or a memory; to looks forward, to a purpose or a task.

JobFormExample
Stop + -ingyou quit an activityI stopped watching dubbed movies.
Stop + toyou pause one activity to do anotherShe stopped to write down a word.
Remember / forget + -inga memory of something in the pastI remember watching it as a kid.
Remember / forget + toa task: something you need to doRemember to choose English.
Try + -ing / try + to-ing: an experiment · to: an effort, often difficultTry switching the subtitles. I tried to follow the jokes.
The trapstop + to does not mean quitNot I stopped to watch dubbed movies. Say I stopped watching.

Never: I stopped to watch dubbed movies (= quit) · Remember switching the subtitles (= task) · I enjoy to watch · I can’t keep up them
