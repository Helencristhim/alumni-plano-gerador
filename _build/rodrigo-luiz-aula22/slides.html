Chapter 3: The Pattern

Advice, Not Orders

should · shouldn't

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

You should call the customer today.
You shouldn't promise a date you cannot keep.
We should update the forecast every week.
Should we work on Saturday?

Is this an order, or advice? And what comes after should?

UseFormExample
Advice: yesshould + base verbYou should call the customer.
Advice: noshouldn't + base verbYou shouldn't promise a date.
QuestionShould + subject + verb?Should we work on Saturday? — Yes, we should.
Ask for adviceWhat should we do?What should we do about the customer?
CarefulAfter should: no TO and no -S. "He should to call" and "he shoulds" are wrong: "he should call".
