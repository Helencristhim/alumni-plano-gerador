Podcast
How I Built This -- Planet Labs: Will Marshall and Robbie Schingler

Two founders describing a plan that ran for years against dates that kept moving, and the discipline of saying what would be true by when. The future language is everywhere in it, in ordinary spoken American English.

Tip: listen to any twenty minutes. Every time somebody says what will have happened by a date, write the sentence down.

TED -- Dan Gilbert, "The psychology of your future self" (7 min)

A psychologist on why people are confident about a version of themselves twelve months ahead that never quite arrives. It is the argument behind the March check in this lesson: the plan is not the problem, the certainty is.

Tip: seven minutes and very clear. Then answer his question about your own year: what will you be doing that you cannot imagine changing?
