Lesson 3 -- Checking In, Speaking Up

Video Lesson
"Let's Learn English at a Hotel" — Bob the Canadian

A full hotel stay filmed from the outside in: the front desk, the key card, the floor, the room. Slow, clear North American English, with subtitles.

Tip: pause every time he names an object and say the word out loud before he does. That is the difference between recognising a word and owning it.

Listening
"Booking a hotel room" — English at Work, BBC Learning English

A short workplace story where somebody books a room on the phone. Listen for the requests, not for the vocabulary: notice how often they ask instead of demand.

Tip: write down every question you hear. Then use three of them out loud, with your own dates and your own name.

Listen on YouTube →
Grammar
"5 linkers for natural conversation" — BBC Learning English

Five small words that hold a story together. Three of them are exactly the ones from this lesson.

Tip: after the video, tell the story of your last trip in five sentences, using a different linker in each one. Record it and listen back — the story will sound twice as organised.
