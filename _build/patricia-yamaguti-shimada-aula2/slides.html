Chapter 3: Asking Questions

One Word Changes Everything

where · what · what time

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"Where is the gate?"

"What is my seat?"

"What time is my flight?"

"Where are the bags?"

Question wordYou want to knowExample
Where is + the thinga placeWhere is the gate?
What is + the thinga number, a nameWhat is my seat?
What time is + the thingan hourWhat time is my flight?
Where are + two or morea place, pluralWhere are the bags?

One thing = is. Two or more = are. And please at the end makes it soft.

Chapter 6: Your Turn

From Guided to Free

Role-Play — Guided

Check In, With Two Bags

Scenario: You are at the check-in desk with your family and two bags. Give your ticket and your passport, say how many bags you have, and ask for a window seat.

Keywords:

Here is my ticket two bags a window seat please
Role-Play — Semi-free

Find the Gate Yourself

Scenario: You are in the middle of the terminal with the children. You do not know the gate, the time or your seat. Stop a member of the staff and get the three answers.

Keywords:

Where is...? What time is...? What is...? BA 247
Role-Play — Free

The Gate Changed and Nobody Told You

Scenario: You arrive at gate twelve with your family and your flight is not there. Find a member of the staff and solve it. You have your boarding pass in your hand.

No keywords this time. Two minutes, no stopping. Different from the model is not wrong — if he understood you, it worked.
