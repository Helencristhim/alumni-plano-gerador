Online Shopping -- Comparatives (cheaper, faster, more expensive)

Comparar dois produtos numa loja online e escolher um, explicando por quê (cheaper than, faster than, more expensive than, better). Vocabulário novo: cart, order, delivery, discount, brand, review, fast, light.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Ouça cada palavra e leia o exemplo. / Listen to each word and read the example.

Cart -- the place on a shopping website for the things you want to buy
"I put the headphones in my cart." (carrinho)
Order -- the things you buy online, in one purchase
"My order arrives on Friday." (pedido)
Delivery -- when a company brings your order to your home
"The delivery is free this week." (entrega)
Discount -- a lower price, for a short time
"This laptop has a ten percent discount." (desconto)
Brand -- the name of the company that makes a product
"This brand makes very good keyboards." (marca)
Review -- what a buyer writes about a product: good or bad
"I always read the reviews before I buy." (avaliação)
Fast -- it works or moves quickly; not slow
"My new computer is very fast." (rápido)
Light -- not heavy; easy to carry
"My laptop is light, so I take it to class." (leve)

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Leia o texto e responda. / Read the text and answer the questions.

Estephano needs a new laptop for college. He has two laptops in his cart: the NovaBook and the ByteBook. The NovaBook is cheaper than the ByteBook, and its screen is bigger. Today it has a ten percent discount. But the ByteBook is faster and lighter, and the reviews are better. The ByteBook is more expensive, but Estephano prefers a light laptop for college. He places his order, and the delivery takes only two days.

1. Which laptop is cheaper?
A The ByteBook, because it has better reviews.
B They have the same price today.
C The NovaBook.
2. Why does Estephano choose the ByteBook?
A It is cheaper and it has a discount.
B He prefers a light laptop for college.
C Its screen is bigger than the NovaBook screen.
3. How long does the delivery take?
A Two days.
B Five days, because the delivery is free.
C One week.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Comparatives

GRAMMAR

Para comparar duas coisas: palavra curta + -er + than; palavra longa: more + palavra + than. / To compare two things: short word + -er + than; long word: more + word + than.

Form / FormaUse / UsoExample
short word + -er + thanmais barato / mais rápido queThe NovaBook is cheaper than the ByteBook.
big → biggerdobra a consoante finalThe screen is bigger.
more + long word + thanmais caro queThe ByteBook is more expensive than the NovaBook.
good → bettermelhor (forma especial)The reviews are better.
Which ... is cheaper?PerguntaWhich laptop is cheaper?
Atenção: nunca more cheaper (só cheaper) e nunca cheaper that (o certo é than).
