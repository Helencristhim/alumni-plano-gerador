Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TED -- Celeste Headlee, "10 ways to have a better conversation" (12 min)

A radio host on how to listen and talk better. Slow, clear English, and full of reported speech: she tells what people said to her and what she said back. Perfect for today's lesson.

Tip: choose one story from the talk and tell it to your husband in reported speech: she said that...

Talk
TED -- Julian Treasure, "5 ways to listen better" (8 min)

A short talk about listening: how we filter what people say, and how to listen more carefully. Useful when you have to pass on a message exactly.

Tip: after the talk, write down two things he said, and say them back with he said that or he explained that.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
TED -- Abraham Verghese, "A doctor's touch" (18 min)

A doctor tells stories about patients and what they said to him. You will hear many examples of a doctor reporting a conversation, in clear, beautiful English, from your own field.

Tip: pick one patient story and tell it in reported speech: the patient told him that...
