Chapter 3: The Code

Is Becomes Was

One step back in time

Grammar Discovery

What Happens to the Verb?

Mike: "The park is closing." → Mike said that the park was closing.

The vet: "Your puppy needs a vaccine." → The vet told me that my puppy needed a vaccine.

Carol: "I will call you." → Carol said that she would call me.

Mike: "Are you coming?" → Mike asked if I was coming.

Look at the blue words. What happens to is, needs and will? And when you tell a question, what word comes after asked?

They saidYou tellExample
am / is / arewas / were"It is late." → She said that it was late.
present simplepast simple"I need help." → He said that he needed help.
willwould"I will call." → She said that she would call.
say vs tellsay (that) · tell + person (that)He said that... · He told me that...
a yes / no questionasked + if + subject + verb"Are you coming?" → He asked if I was coming.
I / you / mychange the person"I like your dog." → She said that she liked my dog.

In one line: when you pass it on, the verb goes one step back, and the people change.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

Did You Hear?

A rumor at the dog park, and what people really said

Dialogue

A Rumor at the Dog Park

M
Ana Paula! Did you hear the rumor? People are saying that the dog park is going to close.
A
Really? Who told you that?
M
I overheard two women at the gate. One of them said that the city wanted to build a parking lot here.
A
That sounds like gossip. Yesterday I talked to Carol, the organizer. She told me that the park was only closing for two weeks.
M
Two weeks? Why?
A
She explained that they were going to fix the fence. And she said that the city would announce it on Monday.
M
So she denied the parking lot story?
A
Completely. She laughed. And she asked if you could help with the new fence. Can I pass on your number?
Comprehension

About Mike

1. What rumor did Mike hear?
That the dog park was going to close.
2. How did Mike hear it?
He overheard two women at the gate.
3. What did one of the women say, according to Mike?
That the city wanted to build a parking lot there.
Listening 1 · The Meeting

What They Said at the Meeting

Carol, the dog park organizer, after a neighborhood meeting. Sound first — no text.

1. What did the man from the city say about the fence?
He said that the old fence was not safe, and that they would build a new one.
2. What did some neighbors complain about?
They complained that the dogs were too loud in the morning.
3. What did the city ask the volunteers?
They asked if the volunteers could paint the new benches.
Listening 2 · A Voice Message

What the Vet Said

Mike tells you about his puppy's visit to the vet. Sound first — no text.

1. What did the vet say about the puppy's health?
She said that he was very healthy, but a little too heavy.
2. What did the vet tell Mike to do?
She told him to give the puppy less food and more walks.
3. What does Mike ask Ana Paula at the end?
He asks if she wants to walk the dogs together on Saturday.
