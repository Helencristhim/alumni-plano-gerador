Chapter 4: Place and Movement

Where It Is, Where You Go

Two families of small words

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

"The station is on the corner of Fifth and Main."

"The crosswalk is between the bank and the hotel."

"Walk across the park to the museum."

"Get off at the second stop and go along the avenue."

FamilyWordsExample
Place: where it isin, on, at, between, opposite, next toThe station is on the corner.
Movement: where you goto, into, across, along, through, up, downWalk across the park.
Public transportget on / get offGet off at the second stop.
Cars and taxisget in / get out ofGet into the taxi at the door.

One test decides everything: if nothing moves, you need a place word. If something travels from A to B, you need a movement word.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

✗ WRONG

"I get in the train at Union Square."

✓ RIGHT

"I get on the train at Union Square."

✗ WRONG

"We arrived in the station at six."

✓ RIGHT

"We arrived at the station at six."

You stand on a train, a bus or a plane, because you can walk around in them. You sit in a car or a taxi. And you arrive at a point on the journey, in only for a city or a country.

Dialogue

Asking a Local

T
Excuse me. Is there a subway station near here?
L
Sure. Go along this avenue for three blocks.
T
Three blocks. And then?
L
At the third corner there is a crosswalk. Cross there. The entrance is on the other side.
T
Thank you. How much is the fare?
L
Three dollars, and it is the same to any station.
T
I need the green line. Do I pay again for the transfer?
L
No. You change at Union Square and it costs nothing.
T
Perfect. How long does it take at this time?
L
Twenty minutes. But it is rush hour, so give yourself thirty.
T
Thirty. That is still better than my commute at home. Thank you.
Did You Catch It?

About the Local

1. What are the three steps she gives to reach the station?
Go along the avenue for three blocks, cross at the crosswalk on the third corner, and the entrance is on the other side.
2. What does she say about the price of the transfer?
That he changes at Union Square and it costs nothing.
3. Why does she add ten minutes to the journey?
Because it is rush hour.
