Lesson 7 -- The Cross-Functional Meeting

Series
The Diplomat (A Diplomata) -- Season 1, Episode 1

Every episode is a room full of people from different areas who need each other and do not report to each other. That is exactly your cross-functional meeting, with higher stakes and better lighting. Listen for how the characters ask for something without ever sounding like they are begging for it.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time somebody makes a conditional promise, write it down: "if you do this, I will do that". You will collect six or seven in one episode.

Podcast
Think Fast, Talk Smart -- Communicating Through Conflict: How to Get Along with Anyone

A free episode from the Stanford business school on the moment somebody in the room says no. The argument is that disagreement is not the problem, and that most meetings go wrong before the conflict, not during it. Around thirty minutes, clear American English, transcript on the page.

Tip: listen once without the transcript, on the way to work. Then pick one phrase for pushing back politely and say it out loud, in English, three times.

Listen on Stanford GSB ↗
YouTube
Celeste Headlee -- 10 Ways to Have a Better Conversation (TED)

Eleven minutes from a radio interviewer on the habits that quietly ruin a conversation, and almost all of them show up in meetings: finishing other people's sentences, waiting to speak instead of listening, and answering a question that nobody asked. She speaks fast and clearly, which is useful training on its own.

Tip: watch it once with subtitles and once without. Then choose the single habit you recognise in your own meetings and try dropping it for one week.
