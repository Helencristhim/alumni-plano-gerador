Chapter 3: The Code

Polite Requests & Offers

could you · would you be able to · I would like you to

Grammar Discovery

Read and Notice

"Could you raise the ceiling to our benchmark?"

"Would you be able to give us a six-month grace period?"

"I would like you to send the revised package before Thursday."

"We would be happy to accept the fee in return."

What comes right after the orange words?

MovePatternExample
AskCould you + base verb?Could you raise the ceiling?
Ask, softerWould you be able to + base verb?Would you be able to give us six months?
Ask, most indirectWould it be possible to + base verb?Would it be possible to drop the fee?
State the askI would like to + base verb · I would like you to + base verbI would like you to send the package.
Offer / tradeWe would be happy to + base verb · We could + base verb · If you raise the ceiling, we would sign this week. Never "could you to raise", never "able to giving", never "I would like that you send".

The polite shape is not the weak shape. It is the shape the other side can take to their committee and defend.

Common Mistake

Right vs Wrong

"Could you to raise the ceiling?"
"Could you raise the ceiling?"
"I would like that you send the revised package."
"I would like you to send the revised package."
"Would you be able to giving us a grace period?"
"Would you be able to give us a grace period?"

A modal like could takes the bare verb, with no to. After able to and after would like you to, the verb stays in its base form — never -ing.

Same number · four heights

Hard: "Raise the ceiling." → Ask: "___Could you raise the ceiling?"
Softer, when the answer is not obvious: "___Would you be able to raise the ceiling?"
Most indirect, when you know it is difficult: "___Would it be possible to raise the ceiling?"
With something in return: "___If you raise the ceiling, we would sign this week."

Listening

Marina Sets the Trade

A voicemail before the call. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. Which ask does Marina say Sergio cannot drop?
The ceiling, and only the ceiling. The grace period is already granted and the fee is small.
2. What does Marina want Sergio to give in return?
The one percent fee — accept it, and ask for the ceiling at the benchmark plus the grace period in writing on signature day.
3. What does Marina say about the words "walk away"?
Not on this call. It stays in his pocket, and only comes out on Friday if the number has not moved.
Listening 2

Paulo on How to Ask

A colleague from the credit desk. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. Why does Paulo prefer "Would you be able to take the ceiling to two hundred?"
Because it lets the other side argue for you with their committee, instead of defending a position against an order.
2. What does Paulo say about asking for three things?
Never one at a time — they say no three times and nothing is left to trade. Put them on the table together and say which one matters most.
3. What should Sergio do before he pushes on the ceiling?
Thank the other side for the grace period. It costs nothing and it sets the tone for the rest of the call.
