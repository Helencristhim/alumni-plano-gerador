"Know your worth, and then ask for it" -- Casey Brown (TED)

A nine-minute talk on why good people undercharge and never make the ask, and on the words that turn a number into a request the other side can accept. Connection to Lesson 14: this is the half of the negotiation you practiced today -- not choosing the number, but asking for it out loud.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Every time she describes a request, stop and say the same request in two shapes: "Could you...?" and "Would you be able to...?"

"4 negotiation tactics to try in everyday life" -- NPR Life Kit

A free twenty-minute episode in clear American English, with the full transcript on the same page. Connection to Lesson 14: the four tactics are exactly what you did today -- open with what you already have, put the asks together, offer something in return, and know the one point where you hold firm.

Tip: listen once without the transcript, then read it and underline every request. Rewrite two of them as "Would you be able to...?"

"Make polite requests" -- English at Work, BBC Learning English

A short, free workplace-English episode that drills the exact shapes you learned today: Could you...?, Would you be able to...?, and I would like you to... Connection to Lesson 14: it shows the same request going wrong when it is too direct, and right when it gives the other person room to say yes.

Tip: watch once, then pause after each request and repeat it out loud. Finish by asking for three things at your own desk, one in each shape.
