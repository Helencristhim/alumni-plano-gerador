Lesson 23 -- The Follow-up That Works

Podcast
Office English (BBC Learning English) -- "Chasing people"

A short episode on how to remind colleagues and clients about something you are waiting for, politely and without sounding annoyed. Full script on the same page.

Tip: copy two phrases you can use with a customer who went quiet, and say each one with the present perfect.

Podcast
6 Minute English (BBC Learning English) -- "Why do we procrastinate?"

Why people leave things for later, even important things. It explains a lot about why customers go quiet, and it is not about you.

Tip: read the script and write two sentences about a customer who has not answered you, with have not and since.

Talk
Tim Urban -- "Inside the mind of a master procrastinator" (TED, 14 min)

A very funny talk about why people delay decisions until a deadline appears. After it, you will think about follow-ups and deadlines differently.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write one follow-up sentence that gives your customer a small deadline in a polite way.
