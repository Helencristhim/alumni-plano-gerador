Chapter 3: The Code

Right Now

am / is / are + -ing

Grammar Discovery

What Do These Four Sentences Have in Common?

"We are moving the traffic to server B."

"I am turning off the old service."

"We aren’t using the new server."

"Are the payments working?"

Look at the blue words. You know am, is and are from lesson 1. What is new after them? Is it every day, or now?

JobFormExample
Iam + verb-ingI am testing the system.
He, she, itis + verb-ingRaj is turning off the service.
We, they, youare + verb-ingWe are moving the traffic.
Negativeam not / isn’t / aren’t + -ingWe aren’t using the new server.
QuestionAm / Is / Are + subject + -ing?Are the payments working?
Every day x nowpresent simple x present continuousI usually test on Mondays. Now I am testing.

In one line: am / is / are + verb-ing for what is happening now. For every day, use the present simple.

Chapter 4: Live on the Call

Live on the Call

One conversation, two recordings, one status board

Dialogue

The Window Is Open

R
Okay, everyone. The change window is open. Right now, we are moving the traffic to server B.
E
Thank you, Raj. What are you doing now, exactly?
R
I am turning off the old service. My team is watching the system.
E
Okay. Here in São Paulo, my team is testing the payments.
R
Great. Are the payments working?
E
Yes, they are. But one store is having a problem.
R
Hmm. Is that store using the new server?
E
No, it isn’t. It is using the old one. Wait... Okay, now it is using server B.
R
Perfect. Now I am turning on the new update. Ten more minutes.
