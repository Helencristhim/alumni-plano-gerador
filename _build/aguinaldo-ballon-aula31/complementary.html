Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Talk
TED -- William Ury, "The walk from no to yes" (19 min)

One of the authors of Getting to Yes on how to negotiate when the stakes are high. Clear, calm English, many real stories, and the idea of the third side, which connects with the mediator in today's first listening.

Tip: note one of his stories and tell it with a conditional offer: if they had..., they would...; if you could..., we would...

Talk
TED -- Margaret Heffernan, "Dare to disagree" (13 min)

A former CEO on why good organizations need conflict, and how to disagree without destroying the relationship. Useful for the moment when you say no at the table and keep the door open.

Tip: write down one sentence she uses to disagree, and turn it into a conditional offer with unless or what if.

Watch on TED ↗
Talk
TED -- Simon Sinek, "How great leaders inspire action" (18 min)

A very famous talk about starting with why. In a negotiation, starting with the reason before the request is the same idea. Clear structure and English that is easy to follow.

Tip: prepare one offer for a real negotiation of yours, starting with the why and finishing with if you could..., we would...
