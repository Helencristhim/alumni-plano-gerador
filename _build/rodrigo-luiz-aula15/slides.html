Chapter 3: The Pattern

Eight Verbs That Break the Rule

went · had · saw · took · ate · bought · made · got

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

I went to a museum on Saturday.
We had dinner in Brooklyn.
I saw the city from the bridge.
Did you take a lot of photos?

In lesson 9 the past ended in -ED. Look at the orange words. What happened to these verbs? And in the question?

UseFormExample
go / havewent / hadI went to a museum. We had dinner.
see / takesaw / tookI saw the bridge. I took a lot of photos.
eat / buyate / boughtI ate pasta. I bought a souvenir.
make / getmade / gotI made a new friend. I got a lot of questions.
Question / negativeDid / didn't + normal verbDid you see it? I didn't go to the park.
CarefulOnly the AFFIRMATIVE changes. After DID the verb goes back to normal: never "Did you saw", never "I didn't went".

Chapter 4: The Last Day

Friday, the Last Day at the Office

twenty days later, Nora asks one question

Dialogue

How Was New York?

N
So, Rodrigo, it's your last day. How was New York?
R
It was great. On Saturday I went to a museum, the Met.
N
Oh, I love the Met. I went there with my kids last month. What did you see?
R
The Egyptian rooms. And on Sunday I walked across the Brooklyn Bridge. The view was fantastic.
N
Did you take a lot of photos?
R
More than three hundred. And I bought a souvenir for my kids.
N
My family did something nice last weekend. We took the ferry to Staten Island. It's free, and you see the Statue of Liberty.
R
I didn't know that! Next time. And I ate very well here, every night.
N
And the plant visit? Tom said you asked great questions.
R
Really? I had a very good day there. I made a new friend.
Comprehension

About Nora

1. Who did Nora go to the Met with, and when?
With her kids, last month.
2. What did Nora's family do last weekend?
They took the ferry to Staten Island — it is free, and you see the Statue of Liberty.
3. What did Tom say about Rodrigo?
That he asked great questions at the plant.
