Grammar Video
"Present perfect and past simple" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on the only question that decides it: is there a date in the sentence? Connection to Lesson 2: this is the difference between "I have sent the form" and "I sent it on Monday", which you used all through the office dialogue.

Tip: after each example, say one sentence about your own preparation for Canada — one with a date, one without. Two sentences, then stop.

Podcast
"Living Abroad" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two hosts talk about what actually changes when you move to another country: the paperwork, the food, the moment the novelty wears off. Slow, clear British English with the key words explained as they come up. Connection to Lesson 2: the vocabulary of settling into a system that is not yours.

Tip: listen once without stopping. Then write down the three words they explain and use each one in a sentence about your own move.

Vlog
"High School Diary in Canada" -- an international student's vlog

A day inside a Canadian high school filmed by a student who arrived from another country: the lockers, the cafeteria, the timetable, the walk between buildings. Connection to Lesson 2: every word from today's list appears somewhere in this video, spoken by teenagers.

Tip: pause whenever you see a sign, a form or a noticeboard on screen and read it out loud. That is exactly the reading you practised today.
