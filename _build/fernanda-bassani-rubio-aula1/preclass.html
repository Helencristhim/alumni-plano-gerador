Lesson 01 -- Pre-class

Who I Am on the Call -- Your Job in Four Sentences

A Teams call starts and people from three countries say who they are. Key words: a key account, to be in charge of, to deal with, a supplier, a car maker, a time zone, to be based in, to join a call. Structure: present simple, affirmative and negative.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A key account -- a very important customer for your company
"Our key accounts are big car makers."
To be in charge of -- to be the person responsible for something
"I am in charge of the automotive segment."
To deal with -- to work with someone or take care of something
"I deal with global customers every day."
A supplier -- a company that sells materials to another company
"We are a supplier of adhesives for cars."
A car maker -- a company that builds cars
"We sell to car makers in many countries."
A time zone -- an area of the world with the same clock time
"My colleague in China is in a different time zone."
To be based in -- to have your main office in a place
"I am based in Brazil, and my manager is based in the US."
To join a call -- to enter an online meeting
"I join a call with the global team every Tuesday."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

It is Monday morning, and Fernanda joins a call with the global team. First, Anna speaks. She works in Germany, and she is in charge of two German car makers. She doesn’t work with the American market.

Then Wei speaks. He is based in Shanghai, and he plans the production of adhesives. He is in a different time zone, so for him it is nine at night.

Then it is Fernanda’s turn: “I am a commercial manager. I deal with our key accounts in South America. I don’t travel every month, but I talk to our customers every day.”

1. Who is in charge of two German car makers?
A Wei
B Anna
C Fernanda
2. Why is it nine at night for Wei?
A Because he works at night.
B Because he is in a different time zone.
C Because the call is late.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A “Anna work in Germany.”
B “Anna works in Germany.”
C “Anna is work in Germany.”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Present Simple -- Affirmative and Negative

GRAMMAR

Use the present simple for your job and for things you do every day. With he and she, the verb gets an -s.

JobFormExample
I / you / we / theyverbI deal with global car makers.
he / she / itverb + sDaniel works in Detroit.
Negative: I / you / we / theydon’t + verbI don’t travel every month.
Negative: he / she / itdoesn’t + verb (no s)Wei doesn’t speak Portuguese.
Your job titleI am + jobI am a commercial manager.
The trapno -s after doesn’tNot he doesn’t works. Say he doesn’t work.

Never: She work in Germany · He don’t deal with Asia · I am work in sales · He doesn’t speaks Portuguese
