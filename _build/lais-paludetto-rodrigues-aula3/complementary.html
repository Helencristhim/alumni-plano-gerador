Lesson 3 -- Living With the Kanes

Grammar Video
"'Have to' and 'must'" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

Six minutes on obligation, and on the pair that trips everybody up: "you don't have to" and "you mustn't" are opposites, not versions of the same idea. Connection to Lesson 3: this is exactly what your host family will say to you in the first week.

Tip: after the video, say four sentences out loud about your own house in Sao Paulo — one with have to, one with don't have to, one with mustn't and one with am allowed to.

Lesson
"How to Ask For Permission in English: CAN, COULD, MAY, DO YOU MIND?" -- engVid

A full lesson on the politeness ladder you built today, with a teacher who explains exactly when each form sounds friendly and when it sounds like you are asking for too much. Connection to Lesson 3: the difference between "Can I?" and "Would it be okay if I?" decides how your requests land.

Tip: pause and write down three real requests you will have to make in Burnaby. Then choose the right level for each one.

Vlog
"What You Should And Shouldn't Do As A Host Family" -- IAmLaura

A former exchange student lists what worked and what did not in the houses she lived in. It is aimed at host families, which is exactly why it is useful: you get to hear the other side, and half of it is about communication, not rules.

Tip: while you watch, write down the three things she says students should do. Those are the three you will actually need in September.
