Lesson 14 -- Asking Nicely -- and Who You Are Asking

Video
Norway culture shock — Asian in Norway (YouTube)

A YouTuber who moved to Norway talks about the things that surprised them most in everyday life. English as a lingua franca again: a non-native speaker, speaking clearly, about a culture that is not their own.

Tip: every time something sounds strange or rude, ask yourself: is it rude, or just straightforward? Then say it as a polite question: do you know why Norwegians...?
