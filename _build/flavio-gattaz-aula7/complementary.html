Podcast
Wisdom From The Top -- Best Buy (bonus episode on How I Built This)

A chief executive narrating a turnaround from the inside: what he inherited, what had already gone wrong before he arrived, and which of his own moves he now calls a misstep. It is a whole career answer at full length, and the past perfect is everywhere in it.

Tip: listen for every "by the time" and "had already". Write down three of them and rewrite each with a year from your own company.

Stanford GSB -- Application Week 2026: Letters of Recommendation

Your career story is also told by other people, in writing, to the same committee. This is the admissions team explaining what a recommender is actually asked and what makes a letter useless. Worth watching before you decide who to ask.

Tip: after watching, write down in English the two moments you would want a recommender to describe, and check that both of them are in your own three-move answer too.

Talk
TED -- Emilie Wapnick, "Why some of us don't have one true calling" (12 min)

An argument about careers that do not look like a straight line, and about what connects them anyway. Useful here for one reason: it gives you a vocabulary for a through-line that is not "I always knew", which is the version nobody believes.

Tip: twelve minutes, clear delivery, no subtitles on the second viewing. Then say your own three moments out loud without using the word career once.
