Lesson 17 -- The Culture Question

Talk
"How to design and build a healthy company culture" -- Melissa Daimler, TEDx

A people leader on culture as the behaviors a company actually rewards, not the values on the wall. Connection to Lesson 17: it is the same test the interviewer runs on you, the moment and not the adjective.

Tip: write down one behavior she describes and say, out loud, a moment from your own career that shows it. One name, one date.

Real Interview
"Nike's CFO on Developing Necessary Leadership Skills" -- UCLA Anderson

A CFO of a global company talking about how he leads, with almost no numbers in it. Connection to Lesson 17: listen for how he talks about people by what they did, not by adjectives.

Tip: write down one sentence in which he talks about his team without an adjective. Then say a sentence of your own in the same shape.

Grammar Video
"How to use prefer, would rather and would prefer" -- BBC Learning English, Live English Class

A live class on how to say what you prefer, including would rather with another person. Connection to Lesson 17: it is the structure that shows a preference without saying the other way is wrong.

Tip: after the video, say three sentences about how you lead with I would rather, and one condition with we had better.
