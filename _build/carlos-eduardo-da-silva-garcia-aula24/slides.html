Part of the meetingStructureExample
Who you are / every weekto be + present simpleI am an analyst. Every Monday I check the IMS system.
Nowam / is / are + -ingRight now I am preparing the EUDR maps.
Yes and nocan / can't + base verbWe can provide the maps. We can't provide the records.
The last visitpast simple (-ed / irregular) + didLast month I visited three farms. Did you find any issues?
The planam / is / are + going to + base verbNext month I am going to present the results.

Look at the time word first: every Monday, right now, last month, next month. / Olhe primeiro a palavra de tempo -- ela escolhe a estrutura.
