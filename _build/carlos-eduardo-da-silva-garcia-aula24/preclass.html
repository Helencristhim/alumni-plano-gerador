Etapa 1.3: Grammar in Context

Grammar

Leia as notas do Carlos para a reunião e responda às perguntas. (Read Carlos's notes and answer.)

My name is Carlos, and I am a sustainability analyst at Sucafina. Every Monday I check the farm data in the IMS system. This month I am very busy: right now I am preparing the EUDR maps for twelve farms. It is a big project. Our team can provide the maps, but we can't provide the training records this month. Last month I visited three farms. I met the producers and I found two small issues. Next month I am going to visit three new farms. On Tuesday I am available for the meeting at eleven.

1. What is Carlos doing right now? (O que o Carlos está fazendo agora?)
A He is visiting three new farms with the producers in the south of Minas. (isso é o plano)
B He is preparing the EUDR maps. (preparando os mapas)
C He is checking the training records. (eles não estão prontos)
2. What can't the team provide this month? (O que a equipe NÃO pode fornecer este mês?)
A The training records. (os registros de treinamento)
B The maps of the twelve farms in the EUDR project. (os mapas eles podem)
C The farm data. (não está no texto)
3. Which sentence is about the PAST? (Qual frase é sobre o PASSADO?)
A "Every Monday I check the farm data." (rotina)
B "Next month I am going to visit three farms." (plano)
C "Last month I visited three farms." (passado)

Etapa 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Review: One Meeting, Six Structures

Grammar

A palavra de tempo escolhe a estrutura / The time word chooses the structure

Part / ParteForm / FormaExample / Exemplo
Quem você é / toda semanato be + present simpleI am an analyst. Every Monday I check the data. (Sou analista. Toda segunda confiro os dados.)
Agora (now)am / is / are + -ingRight now I am preparing the maps. (Agora estou preparando os mapas.)
Sim e não (yes and no)can / can't + verbo baseWe can provide the maps. We can't provide the records. (Podemos / não podemos fornecer.)
A última visita (the past)past simple + didLast month I visited three farms. Did you find any issues? (Visitei três fazendas. Encontrou problemas?)
O plano (the plan)am / is / are + going to + verbo baseNext month I am going to visit new farms. (No mês que vem vou visitar fazendas novas.)

Dica: every Monday = rotina; right now = agora; last month = passado; next month = plano. / Tip: look at the time word first.
