Chapter 3: The Pattern

The Second Verb Changes

like · love · hate + -ing

Grammar Discovery

Listen and Notice

I like watching series.
She loves going to parties.
He hates waiting in traffic.
I do not like long movies.

What happens to the second verb after like, love and hate?

StrengthPatternExemplo
love (+++)love + verbo-ingI love watching series.
like (++)like + verbo-ingI like listening to music.
do not like (-)do not like + verbo-ingI do not like waiting.
hate (- - -)hate + verbo-ingI hate waiting in traffic.
Cuidado"I like watch" nao existe. Depois de like, love e hate, o segundo verbo ganha -ing. Com substantivo nao muda nada: I like parties, I like music.
The Scale

From Love to Hate

"I love going to parties."

love — the strongest yes

"I like listening to music."

like — the everyday yes

"I do not like waiting."

do not like — the polite no

"I hate waking up at five thirty."

hate — the strongest no
