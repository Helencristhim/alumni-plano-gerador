Talk
TED -- Lera Boroditsky, "How language shapes the way we think" (14 min)

A cognitive scientist shows how the language we speak changes the way we think about time, space and responsibility, with examples from many countries. A perfect talk for a lawyer who thinks in three languages.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down three definitions she gives. Notice how many use who, which or where.
