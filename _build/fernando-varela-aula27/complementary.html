A clear lesson on how much and how many, with things you can count and things you can't count. Connection to Lesson 27: this is exactly "How many families stayed here? How much money did it cost?"

Podcast
"Do you need to declutter your home?" -- 6 Minute English, BBC Learning English

Two presenters talk about how much stuff we keep at home and how much space we really need. Connection to Lesson 27: furniture, space and a lot of things, with much, many and a lot of in real conversation.

Tip: listen once without the transcript and write down every much, many, a few and a little that you hear. Then listen again with the transcript and check them.
