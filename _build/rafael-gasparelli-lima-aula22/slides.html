Grammar Discovery

Good Idea or Bad Idea?

We should find the root cause first.

We shouldn’t wait too long.

Why don’t we call the port in Santos?

How about sending a short email now?

Look at the verb after each colored word. What form is it?

UseStructureExample
A good ideashould + base verbWe should check the records.
A bad ideashouldn’t + base verbYou shouldn’t blame the team.
Asking for adviceWhat should + we / I + base verb?What should we do first?
A suggestion (1)Why don’t we + base verb?Why don’t we call the client?
A suggestion (2)How about + verb-ing?How about sending an email?

After should and why don’t we: the base verb (no to, no -s). After how about: -ing.

Chapter 4: The Problem Arrives

A Call From Claire

One call, two recordings, one real complaint

Dialogue

A Problem in Rotterdam

C
Rafael, we have an urgent issue. The client in Rotterdam sent a complaint this morning.
R
Oh no. What happened? Was the shipment late?
C
No, it arrived on time, but twenty boxes were damaged. The mangoes were too soft.
R
That is serious. I think we should find the root cause first.
C
I agree. The client thinks the cold container was too warm.
R
Then we should check the temperature records. Why don't we call the port in Santos?
C
Good idea. But we shouldn't wait too long. The client wants an answer today.
R
You're right. How about sending a short email now, and a full report on Friday?
C
Perfect. And we should have a backup plan for the next shipment.
R
Yes. If there is a shortage of containers, we can use a second company. We will fix this.
Comprehension

About Claire

1. What did the client send this morning, according to Claire?
A complaint.
2. What does Claire say was wrong with the shipment?
It arrived on time, but twenty boxes were damaged. The mangoes were too soft.
3. Why does Claire say they shouldn’t wait too long?
Because the client wants an answer today.
Listening 1 · When a Client Complains

Five Steps After a Complaint

A business consultant gives advice. Sound first — no text.

1. What is the first mistake the speaker talks about?
Hiding the problem. Clients always find out, and then the issue is bigger.
2. What should you say in the first call?
Say sorry, and say that you are checking. You shouldn’t blame other people.
3. Which step do most companies forget?
Having a backup plan.

Real Document

The Complaint

From: Anna de Vries, Quality Manager · Van Dijk Fresh, Rotterdam
To: Rafael Gasparelli Lima · Picunha Group
Subject: Complaint — Shipment 118, damaged boxes

Dear Mr. Gasparelli Lima,

Shipment 118 arrived on Monday, on time. However, 20 of the 400 boxes were damaged: the mangoes were too soft. We think the container was too warm.

Please send us the temperature records and tell us your plan before Friday. We would also like a partial refund for the damaged boxes.

Kind regards,
Anna de Vries

1. How many boxes were damaged, out of how many?
Twenty boxes, out of four hundred.
2. What does Anna think is the cause of the problem?
She thinks the container was too warm.
3. What does Anna ask for, and by when?
The temperature records and a plan before Friday, and a partial refund for the damaged boxes.
