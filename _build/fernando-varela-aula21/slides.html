Chapter 3: The Code

The Long Action and the Short One

was presenting · were waiting · when · while

Grammar Discovery

What Do the Orange Words Do?

"I was presenting the results when the system crashed."

"Sarah was taking notes when the screen went black."

"While the IT team was fixing the server, I called Sarah."

"What were you doing at seven o'clock?"

Look at the orange words. Which action was longer, and which action stopped it? How do we make the orange form?

FormUseExample
was / were + verb-ingAn action in progress at a moment in the past.At 7 p.m., I was presenting the results.
long action + when + short actionThe long action is the background. The short action (past simple) interrupts it.I was presenting when the system crashed.
while + long actionWhile introduces the background action.While IT was fixing the server, I called Sarah.
Negative and questionwasn't / weren't + -ing · was / were + subject + -ing?We weren't recording the call. What were you doing?
The trapVerbs like know, want, need, believe are not used in the continuous: I knew the numbers — never I was knowing.

The past continuous paints the background. The past simple tells the event.

Chapter 4: The Morning After

Sarah Calls Back

Wednesday, 8 a.m. Sarah wants to know what happened last night.

Dialogue

The Morning Call

S
Good morning, Fernando. Are you OK? Your screen went black last night, and nobody explained anything.
F
Good morning, Sarah. I am really sorry about that. It was an unexpected incident.
S
What happened? I was showing your numbers to our director when you disappeared.
F
What happened was, the hospital system crashed. At that moment, I was presenting the budget for next year.
S
Oh no. And what were you doing while the system was down?
F
Meanwhile, the IT team was working on the server, and my team was printing the slides.
S
Smart. Our director noticed that you stayed calm. He was very impressed.
F
Thank you. In the end, we finished the presentation by phone.
S
Next time, let us have a plan B before the meeting. Can you send me a short incident report today?
F
Of course. I will send it before lunch.
Comprehension

About Sarah

1. What was Sarah doing when Fernando disappeared from the call?
She was showing his numbers to her director.
2. What did Sarah's director notice?
He noticed that Fernando stayed calm, and he was very impressed.
3. What does Sarah ask for, and what does she suggest for next time?
A short incident report today, and a plan B before the next meeting.
