"Past simple and past continuous" -- 6 Minute Grammar, BBC Learning English

A short, clear lesson on the two tenses of this class: the long action in the background and the short event that interrupts it. Connection to Lesson 21: this is exactly "I was presenting the budget when the system crashed."

Tip: pause after each example and say one sentence about your own work with the same structure: "I was ... when ..."

Film
Apollo 13 (1995)

The true story of a space mission that goes wrong, and of the team on the ground that handles the crisis without panic. Connection to Lesson 21: "Houston, we have a problem" is the most famous incident report in history.

Tip: watch the mission control scenes with English subtitles. Notice how the leaders say what happened, what everyone was doing, and what the plan is.
