Never Have I Ever — Season 1, Episode 1

The whole first episode is a teenager announcing her plan for the school year, out loud, in order. It is "going to" from beginning to end — and then life happens anyway.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down three plans she announces. Did any of them actually happen?

6 Minute English — "Why do we procrastinate?"

Six minutes about the gap between the plan you make and the thing you actually do. If you have ever said "I’m going to study tonight" and then did not, this one is about you. Free, with the full transcript.

Tip: listen once, then say out loud one plan you made this month and did not do — and one you did.

YouTube
Easy English — "What Are Your Plans Today?"

Real people on a real street, answering the exact question of this lesson. Nobody is speaking slowly for you, and almost nobody says "going to" — they all say gonna. Subtitles on screen the whole time.

Tip: count how many times you hear "gonna" in the first two minutes. Then answer the same question about your own day.
