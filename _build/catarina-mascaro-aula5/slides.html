Already decided → going to

It is the same am / is / are you learned last lesson, plus going to plus the normal verb. You decided BEFORE you said it.

The Code

When did you decide?

Decided before → going to

I am going to study tonight.
We are going to travel in July.
She is not going to come.

Deciding right now → will

Your bag is heavy — I’ll help you.
Okay, I’ll text you later.
I’ll have the same, thanks.

Same future. The difference is when the decision happened, not when the action happens.

Hear it, yes. Write it, no. On paper it is always going to.
