Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Documentary
CNBC — "How Shipping Containers Control Global Trade"

Fifteen minutes of pure quantity: how many boxes fit on a ship, how much a delay costs, how many days a port can hold the whole chain. Every sentence in it is the grammar of this lesson, said by people who work in your market.

Tip: write down eight numbers you hear, and next to each one write the question that would get it — how much, or how many.

Podcast
Planet Money (NPR) — "What happened to U.S. farmers during the last trade war"

Soybeans, corn and pork counted in tons, in dollars and in farms that did not survive. A tariff is only a number until somebody measures what it moved, and this episode measures it.

Tip: the transcript is on the same page. Listen once, then find three uncountable nouns in the text — the ones that never take a plural.

Listen on NPR ↗
Video
The Wall Street Journal — "How China's $285M Export Hub in Brazil Will Cost U.S. Billions"

Your ports, your grain, your clients, explained to an American audience in numbers. Terminals, tonnage, days at sea and the quota behind all of it — the whole vocabulary of this lesson, on a map you already know by heart.

Tip: watch it once and then say out loud, in four sentences, what you would correct or add. This is the one video in the block where you know more than the narrator.
