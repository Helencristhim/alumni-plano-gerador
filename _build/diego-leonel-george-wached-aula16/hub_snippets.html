Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Sensitivity -- how well a test finds the people who have the disease
"Dermoscopy has a high sensitivity for melanoma."
Specificity -- how well a test finds the people who do not have the disease
"Low specificity means many unnecessary biopsies."
A false positive -- a result that shows a disease when there is no disease
"Every false positive means one more anxious patient."
A false negative -- a result that shows no disease when the disease is there
"A false negative can delay the diagnosis for months."
Prevalence -- how common a disease is in a population at one time
"The prevalence of psoriasis is about three percent."
Incidence -- the number of new cases in a group during a period of time
"The incidence of melanoma has risen in older adults."
A rate -- how often something happens, usually given as a percentage
"The biopsy rate dropped after the training."
A confidence interval -- the range where the true result is probably found
"The confidence interval was narrow, so the result is precise."
A threshold -- the level at which something starts to happen or to count
"Above this threshold, the lesion goes to biopsy."
To outweigh -- to be more important or bigger than something else
"Here, the benefits outweigh the risks."
To double -- to become two times bigger
"The number of referrals doubled in one year."
Roughly -- not exactly; about
"Roughly one in five lesions was malignant."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

A research article almost never says that a result is simply good. It compares. When it compares two methods, it uses a comparative with than: dermoscopy was more sensitive than the naked eye, specificity was lower than expected.

When it chooses one from a group, it uses a superlative: biopsy is the most accurate test, this was the largest study of the three. And it tells you how big the difference is: much higher, slightly lower, twice as many melanomas as dermoscopy.

Brazilian doctors often say more high or more sensitive that, because in Portuguese the same comparison word goes with every adjective, short or long. In English, short adjectives take -er, long adjectives take more, and the word after the comparative is always than.

1. "Ninety percent is ___ seventy percent." Which form completes the sentence?
A more high than, because the numbers are percentages
B more higher that, because the difference is big
C higher than
2. Why does the article say "the most accurate test"?
A Because it compares only two tests with than.
B Because it chooses one test from a group of tests.
C Because accurate is a short adjective.
3. Which sentence is correct?
A The visual exam missed twice as many melanomas as dermoscopy.
B The visual exam missed two times more melanomas than the dermoscopy exam in the clinic.
C Dermoscopy is more sensitive that the naked eye.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Comparatives and Superlatives

GRAMMAR

Than compares two things. The most and -est choose one from a group. Much and slightly tell how big the difference is.

JobFormExample
Short adjective-er than / the -esthigh → higher than → the highest
Long adjectivemore ... than / the most ...more sensitive than · the most accurate
Lessless ... than / the least ...less specific than · the least reliable
Size of the differencemuch / far / slightly + comparativemuch larger · slightly lower
Equal or timesas ... as · twice as many ... asas accurate as · twice as many cases as
Irregulargood, better, best · bad, worse, worstThe new test is better, but biopsy is the best.

Never: more high · more sensitive that · the more accurate of all · two times more than · more better

Stage 2: Put the Summary in Order

Order

You are presenting a study at a journal club. Put the sentences in the order of a clear summary: the question, the method, the results, the precision and the conclusion.

?"In my opinion, the benefits of dermoscopy outweigh the cost."
?"Specificity was only slightly lower with the naked eye."
?"The confidence interval was wide, because the study was small."
?"Two hundred lesions were examined with both methods, and all of them went to biopsy."
?"Dermoscopy was much more sensitive than the naked eye."
?"This study compared dermoscopy with the naked eye for melanoma."
