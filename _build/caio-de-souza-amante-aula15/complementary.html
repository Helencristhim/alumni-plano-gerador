Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Podcast
PowerSpeaking Podcast: “Cracking the Code to Executive Presentations”

A CEO shares what she learned from interviews with senior leaders: what they want from a presentation, and why most presentations to executives fail. It is the listener’s side of today’s pitch.

Tip: listen for three things executives want, and write one sentence about how your pitch gives each one.

Listen on Buzzsprout ↗
Talk
Nancy Duarte: “The Secret Structure of Great Talks”

A presentation designer shows that great speakers move between “what is” and “what could be.” It is exactly the move from part two to part three of your pitch: from what Dataside has done to where it is going.

Tip: eighteen minutes. After watching, write one “what is” sentence and one “what could be” sentence about Dataside.

Watch on TED ↗
Video
Steve Jobs introduces the iPhone (Macworld 2007)

One of the most famous product presentations ever. Notice how little time he spends on the past, and how he makes the future sound simple and certain.

Tip: about ten minutes. Write down every sentence he says about the future, and check: is it will, going to, or the present continuous?
