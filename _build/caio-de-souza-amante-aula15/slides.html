The Core

The Investor Pitch

I am the investor. You have five minutes for the five parts, and then three questions. No script, no slides, only your notes.

  • Round 1: the pitch. Five minutes, without interruption.
  • Round 2: the questions. Three investor questions, answered in real time.
  • Round 3: the repair. Two sentences from your pitch, said again, stronger.
