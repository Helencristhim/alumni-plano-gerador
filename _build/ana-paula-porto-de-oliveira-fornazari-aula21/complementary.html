Complementary Activities

Optional extras to enjoy outside class. Mark each one as done after you watch or listen to it.

Radio story
NPR Shots — "Dog Owners Walk 22 Minutes More Per Day, And Yes, It Counts As Exercise"

A short radio story about a real study, very close to Tom’s study in this lesson: who was measured, how, and what was found. You can read it and listen to it.

Tip: listen once, then say three things that were done in the study, in the passive.

Read and listen on NPR ↗
Talk
Ben Goldacre — "Battling bad science" (TED)

A doctor explains how health research can be done well, or badly. The English is fast, so this is a challenge: watch the first four minutes only.

Tip: turn on English subtitles. Write down two sentences from the talk with was or were + past participle.

Watch on TED ↗
Grammar page
British Council LearnEnglish — "Passives"

A clear page about the passive voice, with examples and two short online tests. Read the present simple and past simple parts first.

Tip: do Grammar test 1, then write three sentences in the passive about your own city.
