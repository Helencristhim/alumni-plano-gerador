Chapter 3: The Code

What Was Done?

The passive voice

Grammar Discovery

Who Did It? Does It Matter?

"The study is done at the university."

"The participants are chosen from local vet clinics."

"Ninety owners were invited last year."

"The first finding was published in March."

Who does the action in these sentences? What do the red and blue verbs have in common?

FormHowExample
Present simple passiveis / are + past participleThe steps are measured every day.
Past simple passivewas / were + past participleThe study was published in March.
Who did it? (optional)by + the personIt was done by Tom's team.
Negativeis not / was not + past participleNames are not used.
QuestionIs / Was + subject + past participle?Was the study published?
Why use it?the action and the result are more important than the person who did it

In the passive, the thing that receives the action comes first: be + past participle.

Grammar Practice

Be + Past Participle

Say it first, then click to check

"The steps ___are measured every day." (measure, now)
"The study ___was published last March." (publish, past)
"Every participant ___is given a number." (give, now)
"Ninety owners ___were invited last year." (invite, past)
"The names ___are not used in the study." (not use, now)
"The watches ___were collected at the clinic." (collect, past)

Active to Passive

3. They chose sixty owners.
"Sixty owners were chosen."

Chapter 4: Real Speed

Dinner With a Researcher

One conversation, two recordings, one summary

Dialogue

A Study About Dogs

T
Ana Paula, I heard you have two border collies. Did you know dog owners are part of my study?
A
My dogs? Really? What is the study about?
T
It is about dog owners and exercise. The study is done at the university, and the steps of every participant are measured with a small watch.
A
Interesting! And how are the participants chosen?
T
They are chosen from local vet clinics. Last year, ninety owners were invited, and sixty said yes.
A
And was anything published?
T
Yes. Our first finding was published in March. Dog owners walk about twenty minutes more every day.
A
That makes sense. My dogs are walked twice a day, in the rain or in the sun!
Comprehension

About Tom’s Study

1. Where is Tom's study done?
At the university.
2. How are the participants chosen?
They are chosen from local vet clinics.
3. When was the first finding published?
In March.
