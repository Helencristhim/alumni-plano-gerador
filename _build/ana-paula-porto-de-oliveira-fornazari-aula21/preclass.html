Lesson 21 -- Pre-class

Talking About Research -- How It Is Done

How research is done and what was found. Key words: a study, a researcher, a participant, to measure, to collect, a finding, to publish, evidence, to test, a sample. Structure: the passive voice -- is / are and was / were + past participle.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A study -- a piece of research about one question
"A new study about sleep was done in Florida."
A researcher -- a person whose job is to find new facts
"The researcher explained the study at dinner."
A participant -- a person who takes part in a study
"Every participant was given a number."
To measure -- to find the size or amount of something
"My blood pressure was measured twice."
To collect -- to bring things together from different places
"The answers are collected every Friday."
A finding -- something new that a study discovers
"One finding was a surprise for the doctors."
To publish -- to print a text so many people can read it
"The article was published online last week."
Evidence -- facts that show something is true
"We need more evidence before we change the rule."
To test -- to try something to see if it works
"The new medicine was tested for two years."
A sample -- a small group chosen from a bigger group
"A sample of fifty patients was chosen."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Every year, many health studies are done in Florida. Last spring, a small study about coffee and sleep was done at a clinic in Tampa.

Fifty adults were chosen for the study. They were asked to stop drinking coffee after two in the afternoon. Their sleep was measured with a small watch, and their answers were collected every week.

The finding was published in June: the participants slept better and felt less tired. But the researchers say the sample was small, so the study is now repeated with five hundred people.

1. Where was the study done?
A At a university in Orlando.
B At a clinic in Tampa.
C At a hospital in Miami.
2. What were the participants asked to do?
A Drink more coffee in the morning.
B Go to bed at nine every night.
C Stop drinking coffee after two in the afternoon.
3. Why is the study repeated now?
A Because the sample was small.
B Because the finding was wrong.
C Because the watches were lost.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- The Passive Voice

GRAMMAR

Use the passive when the action is more important than the person who does it.

FormHowExample
Present simpleis / are + past participleThe steps are measured every day.
Past simplewas / were + past participleThe study was published in June.
Negativeis not / was not + past participleThe names were not used.
QuestionIs / Was + subject + past participle?Was the study done in Florida?
Who did it?by + person (only if it is important)It was done by a team from Tampa.

Never: the study was publish · the steps measured every day · the results was published
