Lesson 05 -- Pre-class

Interview Day -- You Ask the Questions

You interview candidates every month: today, in English. Key words: a job interview, experience, a skill, a strength, a weakness, a team player, a shortlist, to follow up. Structure: questions with what, where, why and how, in the present and in the past.

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

A job interview -- a meeting where a company asks a candidate questions about a job
"The job interview is on Thursday at ten."
Experience -- the knowledge you get from doing a job for some time
"She has fifteen years of experience in HR."
A skill -- something you can do well because you learned it
"Communication is an important skill for a manager."
A strength -- something that you are very good at
"Her biggest strength is organization."
A weakness -- something that you are not very good at
"My weakness is that I talk too fast."
A team player -- a person who works well with other people
"We need a team player for this position."
A shortlist -- a small list of the best candidates for a job
"We have three people on the shortlist."
To follow up -- to contact somebody again after a meeting
"I always follow up two days after the interview."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text, then answer the questions.

Oliver is an HR director. He does about twenty job interviews a month. His first question is always: What do you do in your current job? He wants to hear about the person’s experience and skills.

Then he asks about the past: What happened in a difficult moment, and what did you do? He wants to know if the person is a team player. He also asks about one strength and one weakness.

Ingrid was a candidate. The interviewer asked: Why did you leave your last company? She was not ready. The next day she sent an email to follow up. She was on the shortlist, but she didn’t get the job.

1. What is Oliver’s first question?
A What do you do in your current job?
B Why did you leave your last company and your last boss?
C What is your weakness?
2. What did Ingrid do the day after the interview?
A She called the interviewer and asked for a second interview the same week.
B She sent an email to follow up.
C She started a new job.
3. Which question is correct?
A “Why you left your last company?”
B “Why did you leave your last company?”
C “Why did you left your last company in 2024?”

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip -- Wh- Questions -- Present and Past

GRAMMAR

A question has a fixed order: question word + do / does / did + person + simple verb. Use do / does for now, and did for the past.

JobFormExample
The orderquestion word + do / does / did + person + verbWhat do you do?
Present, he / shequestion word + does + person + verbWhere does she work?
Past, everybodyquestion word + did + person + verbWhy did you leave?
With bequestion word + be + personWhat was your biggest challenge?
Question wordswhat · where · when · why · how · how many · how longHow long did you work there?
The trapthe helper comes before the personNot Why you left? Say Why did you leave?

Never: Why you left? · Where she works? · What did you studied? · How many people you managed?

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

You want to know about the candidate’s job now. You ask:

A "Why did you leave your last company?"

C "Why did you left the company in the last year?"

A "a shortlist of good candidates"

C "a weakness"
You want to end the interview politely. You say:
A "Thank you. We will follow up by Friday."
