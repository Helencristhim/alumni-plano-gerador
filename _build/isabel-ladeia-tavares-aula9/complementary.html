Lesson 9 -- Asking for Help Without Apologising

Series
Grey’s Anatomy — Season 1

Five first-year surgical interns who do not know where anything is, and have to ask people who are tired and busy. Watch who asks well and who gets sent away: the difference is almost always how precise the question is.

Tip: watch with English subtitles on. Every time an intern asks a direct question, pause and say it again as an indirect one, starting with could you tell me or do you know if.

Podcast
HBR IdeaCast — Getting People to Help You

A social psychologist on the right and wrong ways to ask colleagues for help. One of her points is the same as the first listening: an apology at the start of a request makes it harder, not easier, for the other person to say yes.

Tip: listen once. Then write down two of her wrong ways to ask, and turn each one into a precise, indirect question you could use at the lab.

Talk
Heidi Grant -- How to ask for help, and get a yes

A short talk on why people are more willing to help than we think, and why vague requests fail. Clear, well organized and spoken at a comfortable pace.

Tip: watch with English subtitles. Write down every polite opening she uses before a request, and decide which ones you could say to your supervisor tomorrow.
