Chapter 3: The Code

A Question Inside a Question

Where the politeness really comes from

Grammar Discovery

Polite, Precise, Indirect

"Could you tell me what spin briefly means?"

"Do you know if anyone has found a workaround?"

"I was wondering whether we dilute before or after the spin."

"Could you tell me where the buffers are kept?"

The direct questions would be: What does it mean? Has anyone found one? Do we dilute first? Where are they kept? Compare them with the words in color. What changed?

Direct questionIndirect questionWhat changes
Where are the buffers kept?Could you tell me where the buffers are kept?subject before verb
What does it mean?Could you tell me what it means?do / does / did disappear
Is it done on ice?Do you know if it is done on ice?yes / no question: add if or whether
Do we dilute first?I was wondering whether we dilute first.the softest one: no question mark at all
Can you check my plan?Would you mind checking my plan?would you mind + -ing

In one line: the polite opening is the question. What comes after it is a statement.

Chapter 4: Real Speed

The Question Worth Asking

One conversation, two recordings, one message

Dialogue

Ten Minutes on Step Four

D
You have been looking at that protocol for ten minutes. Everything all right?
I
Almost. I would rather ask than guess. Could you tell me what spin briefly means in step four? It is a bit ambiguous.
D
Fair question. About ten seconds, just to bring the liquid down to the bottom. Anything else?
I
Yes. I was wondering whether we dilute before or after the spin. I tried to figure it out from last year’s notes, but they say both.
D
After. And that one has caught out everybody, so do not feel bad about asking it.
I
Thanks. One more thing. Do you know if anyone has found a workaround for the plate reader? It keeps freezing on me.
D
Paulo has. Ask him. And run your plan for Thursday by me before you start, so we know you are on the right track.
I
I will. Would you mind if I sent it to you tonight, so you can look at it in the morning?
Comprehension

About Daniel

1. What has he noticed before she asks anything?
That she has been looking at the protocol for ten minutes.
2. According to him, what does spin briefly mean, and why is it done?
About ten seconds, just to bring the liquid down to the bottom.
3. Who does he send her to about the plate reader, and what does he want to see before Thursday?
Paulo, who has found a workaround. He wants her to run her plan for Thursday by him before she starts.
Listening 1 · Three Sorrys, Eight Words

The Question Behind the Apology

A researcher on the habit she had to unlearn. Sound first — no text.

0:000:00
1. What did her supervisor count, and what was the result?
How many times she said sorry. Three times in one question, and the question itself was eight words long.
2. What does an apology at the start of a question tell the other person?
That you think you are wasting their time, and after a while they start to believe it.
3. What is her rule before asking, and what really annoys people in a lab?
Spend fifteen minutes trying to figure it out yourself, then ask precisely. What annoys people is the mistake that happens because somebody was too embarrassed to ask.
