will + the simple verb: I'll have, I'll get, I won't forget — the same form for I, you, he, she, we, they.

The Sound

How It Sounds

"I'll have the fish."

I'll sounds like AIL — one short sound, almost no W

"I'll pay this time."

the stress goes on the action: PAY

"I won't have dessert."

WOUNT, with O — not WANT. won't = no, want = quero

"I'll call a taxi for you."

the sentence that helps a person in one second
