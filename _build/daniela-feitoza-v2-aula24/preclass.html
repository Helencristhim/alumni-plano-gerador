Warm-up — From Lesson 23

Review

In Lesson 23 you wrote a technical report: a bug was detected, a patch was approved, the servers were restored. Reports say what happened. Results say how much. The third quarter is over, and on Thursday Daniela presents the numbers of the Brazilian stores to the US directors. Today you will learn the words of a results meeting and how to describe a trend: up or down, by how much, fast or slow. (Directors trust numbers more than adjectives.)

Stage 1.1: Vocabulary Cards

Vocabulary

Listen to each word and read the example. Tap Listen to hear it.

Quarter -- a period of three months in a business year
"July, August and September are the third quarter."
Percentage -- a number that shows a part of one hundred
"A big percentage of our customers pay with the app."
Target -- a number or a result that you plan to reach
"Our target for the quarter was fifteen percent."
Average -- the usual number, when you look at all the numbers together
"The average checkout time is now forty-five seconds."
Increase -- to go up or become bigger
"Online sales increased by eighteen percent."
Drop -- to go down or become lower
"Support tickets dropped by thirty percent."
Steady -- changing slowly and regularly, a little every time
"We saw a steady increase in app users."
Sharp -- very big and fast (about a change)
"There was a sharp drop in the checkout time."

Stage 1.3: Grammar in Context

GRAMMAR

Read the text and answer the questions.

Last week, Daniela prepared the numbers of the Brazilian stores for the third quarter. Most of the results were very good. Online sales increased by eighteen percent, and the target was fifteen percent, so the team was higher than the target. With the new checkout system, the average checkout time dropped from ninety seconds to forty-five. That was a sharp drop: customers now wait half the time. Support tickets dropped by thirty percent, to seven hundred a month. Only one number was a problem. App users increased steadily every month, from two hundred thousand to two hundred and fifty thousand, but the target was three hundred thousand. On Thursday, Daniela will present these numbers to the US directors.

1. What was the target for online sales?
A Fifteen percent.
B Eighteen percent, the same number as the result in the third quarter.
C Thirty percent.
2. What changed for the customers at checkout?
A They wait ninety seconds now, because the old system was faster than the new one.
B They wait half the time now.
C They wait a little more.
3. Which result was lower than the target?
A Online sales.
B Support tickets, because they dropped only to seven hundred a month in September.
C The number of app users.

Stage 1.4: Grammar Tip — Describing Trends

GRAMMAR

A trend is the direction of a number: up or down, fast or slow. Three small words and two patterns do most of the work.

UseFormExample
The differenceverb + by + numberSales increased by eighteen percent.
The start and the endverb + from ... to ...The time dropped from ninety to forty-five seconds.
How it changed (verb)verb + adverb (-ly)App users increased steadily. Tickets dropped sharply.
How it changed (noun)there was + a + adjective + nounThere was a sharp drop. There was a steady increase.
Compare with the targethigher / lower thanSales were higher than our target.
RememberUse by, not "in": "increased by ten percent". Percent never takes -s: "eighteen percent". Adjective before a noun (a sharp drop), adverb after a verb (dropped sharply).

Stage 4: Situational Quiz

Quiz

Choose the best answer for each situation.

A director asks how online sales did this quarter. Which sentence is correct?
A "Online sales grew in eighteen percent, compared to the second quarter of the year."
B "Online sales grew by eighteen percent."
C "Online sales grew eighteen percents."
The change in the checkout time was big and fast. How do you describe it?
A "The checkout time went down sharply."
B "There was a sharply drop in the checkout time after the new system went live."
C "The checkout time went down steady."
Greg asks for the final number of support tickets per month. What do you say?
A "They went down by seven hundred tickets a month, from one thousand, in the third quarter."
B "They went down of seven hundred."
C "They went down to seven hundred a month."
A director asks about the app. You want to be honest about the target. What do you say?
A "The app grew fast, and we were much higher than our target for the whole quarter."
B "The app grew steadily, but we were below our target."
C "The app users were lower that the target."
