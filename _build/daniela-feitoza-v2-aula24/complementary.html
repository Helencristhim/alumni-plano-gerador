Lesson 24 — Talking About Data, Metrics and Results

Content to hear how people talk about numbers, results and trends — at work and in the real world. Mark each one as done when you finish.

Film
Moneyball (2011) — official trailer

A true story about a baseball manager who changes his team with data and numbers, not opinions. Direct connection to Lesson 24: people who do not trust the numbers, and a manager who has to present them anyway.

Tip: watch with English subtitles and write down every number word you hear. Then say one sentence about the team with "increased" or "dropped".

Business English Pod — BEP 115: Presentations, Describing Trends

A finance director presents the market share of three companies over six quarters, and the lesson explains the language of trends in a presentation. Direct connection to Lesson 24: the same job as your Thursday meeting.

Tip: listen once without the text. Then listen again and write down three trend sentences, like "rose steadily" or "a sharp drop".

YouTube
BBC — Hans Rosling’s 200 Countries, 200 Years, 4 Minutes

A famous statistician shows two hundred years of data in four minutes, with a moving chart. Direct connection to Lesson 24: the best example of numbers that tell a story, not a list.

Tip: watch twice. The second time, pause and describe one trend in your own words: what increased, from what, to what.
